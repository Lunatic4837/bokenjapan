  • Isedotai Stone Circles

    Akita

  • Satake Shiryo Kan

    Akita · 018-827-5075

    Displays materials on the Satake clan, lords of Akita Domain and descendants of the Minamoto. Reopened on 25 October 2025; the terrace overlooks Kubota Castle's front gate and Senshu Park's Ninomaru Plaza.

  • Ani Ijinkan Densho Kan

    Ani Ijinkan and Densho Hall

    Akita · 0186-82-3658

    Nationally designated Important Cultural Property, built in 1882 in colonial style as foreign lodging for the government-run Ani Mine. The adjacent Densho Hall holds materials and cultural resources from the former copper mine.
