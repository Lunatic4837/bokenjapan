  • Zenkoku Hanabi Kyogitaikai (Omagari no Hanabi)

    National Fireworks Competition (Omagari Fireworks)

    Daisen, Akita · 0187-88-8073

    Fireworks artists from across Japan compete on a summer night with their own displays and fireworks provided by the event. Hospitality events called Omagari Fireworks Week begin one week before.

  • Nara Oka Yaki Togei Experience

    Naraoka-yaki Pottery Experience

    Daisen, Akita · 0187-73-1018

    Naraoka-yaki dates to the late Edo period and is known for the blue depth of namako glaze. That color is used on Akita Shinkansen Komachi Green Car seats. Reservations allow kiln visits and pottery.

    Iwakura Onsen

    Daisen, Akita · 0187-74-2345

    Open for more than 300 years and recorded by Edo traveler Sugae Masumi. Rock-salt source water is said to ease fatigue and help sleep, and fireflies appear in early summer.

  • Fuku no Tomo Shuzo

    Fukunotomo Brewery

    Daisen, Akita · 0187-72-4141

    Near Jinguji Station, the brewing floor cannot be seen for hygiene reasons, but the entrance, main house, and storehouse can be viewed. Tastings cover sake, amazake, and brewing water.

  • Okuda Shuzo Mise

    Okuda Sake Brewery

    Daisen, Akita · 018-892-3001

    Founded in 1673–1681, it makes Chiyomidori sake with Ou mountain water, heavy snow, cold climate, and quality rice. The Seiichi Shirai-designed shop is a registered tangible cultural property; tours require advance reservation.

  • Daisen Ei Kyowa Ski Area

    Daisen Municipal Kyowa Ski Area

    Daisen, Akita · 018-893-2050

    Two pair lifts and nine courses cover beginners through advanced levels. Ski and snowboard schools, badge tests, and events such as the Kyowa Snow Festa are held.

  • Kawa Wo Wataru Bonden

    Bonden Crossing the River

    Daisen, Akita

    For over 160 years, bonden have crossed the Omono River by ferry, still used after a bridge was built, and climbed to Izusan Shrine to pray for safety and harvest.
