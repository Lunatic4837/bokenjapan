  • Gannin Yo

    Gannin Dance

    Hachirogata, Akita · 018-875-5803

    Said to derive from gannin monks' door-to-door art, reaching Hachirogata about 300 years ago. Rhythmic dance includes a Kabuki skit of Sadakuro and Yoichibei, offered at Ichinichi Shrine on May 5, then at homes.
