    Hachimantai Dragon Eye (Kagaminuma)

    Kazuno, Akita

    Dainichido Bugaku

    Kazuno, Akita · 0186-32-2706

  • Kyu Kan Zen Saketen

    Former Kanzen Sake Shop

    Kazuno, Akita · 0186-23-7799

    Founded in 1856, this Meiji merchant house keeps a komose, a long eave with a passage beneath, said to remain in Akita only in Kazuno. About 300 hina dolls are displayed from January through March.

  • Kemanai Bon'odori

    Kemanai Bon Odori

    Kazuno, Akita · 0186-30-3939

    Bon dance around bonfires in Kemanai: drum-and-flute Dainosaka plus unaccompanied Jinku and Jonkara. Said to date back about 450 years; held August 21–23. National Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property and 2022 UNESCO Furyu Odori listing.
