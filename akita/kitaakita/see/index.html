  • Taihei Mizumi

    Taihei Lake

    Kitaakita, Akita · 0186-76-2107

    Artificial lake formed when Moriyoshi Dam was completed, with 13 gorges and streams flowing in. Cherry salmon, char, carp, and pond smelt live here. Shuttle boats run to Komata Gorge through the October foliage season.

  • Moriyoshi Sanroku Guest House ORIYAMAKE

    Moriyoshi Sanroku Guest House Oriyamake

    Kitaakita, Akita

    Renovated 70-year-old farmhouse at the foot of Mount Moriyoshi in Nemorita, Kitaakita, retaining Matagi culture. One group a day from two-night stays; seasonal activities include wild-plant picking, gorge trekking, mushroom hunting and snowshoeing.

  • Kuinsu Moriyoshi

  • Omata Kyo

    Omata Gorge

    Kitaakita, Akita · 0186-62-1851

    Gorge of about 100 waterfalls, rock potholes, and deep pools among mixed conifer and broadleaf trees. Autumn foliage peaks from early to mid-October.
