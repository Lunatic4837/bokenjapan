Sights in Noshiro, Akita

Noshiro · Akita · 8 places

See

  • Kimimachi Saka Prefectural Nature Park

    Yoneshiro River Canoe Touring

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-73-5075

    Canoe descent of the Yoneshiro River in handmade canoes, with Japan Canoe Federation-certified instructors from land training to landing. At Roadside Station Futatsui, an about-45-minute petit canoe session takes walk-ins until one hour before start.

  • Kyu Ryotei Kin Isami (Kaneyu)

    Kinyu Kimono Dressing Experience

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-55-3355

    Visitors wear kimono, or yukata in summer, for a building tour and photos at a historic former restaurant. Staff assist with dressing.

  • Noshiro Tanabata (Tenku no Fuyajo)

    Noshiro Tanabata: Sleepless Castle in the Sky

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-74-5109

    Revived in 2013, the festival parades two castle lanterns: Chikasue (24.1 m), for Hiyama Castle lord Ando Chikasue, and Karoku (17.6 m), for the carpenter who first made Nagoya Castle-style lanterns. Held August 2–3, 2025.

  • Komedai Kawa Kanutsuringu

    Yoneshiro River Canoe Touring

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-88-8815

    Canoe descent of the Yoneshiro River in handmade canoes, with Japan Canoe Federation-certified instructors from land training to landing. At Roadside Station Futatsui, an about-45-minute petit canoe session takes walk-ins until one hour before start.

  • Noshiro Basuke Museum

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-88-8876

    A museum in Noshiro, Akita prefecture.

  • Yu Raku no Yado no Shiro

    Yuraku no Yado Noshiro

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-54-2121

    Natural hot-spring inn in Noshiro with alkaline simple spring water, a binchotan charcoal bath, and a sauna. Rooms range from Japanese-style to special rooms, with meal plans, amid black pine woods.

  • Noshiro Enajiamu Park

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-52-2955

    A park in Noshiro, Akita prefecture.

  • Kaze no Matsubara

    Kaze no Matsubara

    Noshiro, Akita · 0185-89-2179

    Black-pine coastal windbreak in Noshiro, 1 km wide and 14 km long (760 ha), with trees over 100 years old. A Japan Heritage direction stone and a five-ring pagoda stand inside.
