Sights in Ugo, Akita

Ugo · Akita · 3 places

See

  • Nishimonai Soba

    Nishimonai Soba

    Ugo, Akita · 0183-55-8635

    Soba said to date from 1818, when a craftsman trained in Osaka Sunaba style opened in Ugo. Thin nihachi noodles—two wheat, eight buckwheat—use funori and are often eaten as hiyagake with cold sauce.

  • Yukitopia Shichi Kyoku

    Yukitopia Nanamagari

    Ugo, Akita · 0183-62-2111

    Winter event at Nanamagari Pass recreating bridal processions of the 1950s–60s. Newlywed couples in wedding attire ride horse-drawn sleighs about 14 km from central Ugo into the mountains.

  • Bon Yado U

    Bon Yado U

    Ugo, Akita · 0183-56-7615

    Renovated 180-year-old merchant-house lodging in Ugo, where Nishimonai Bon Odori culture remains. Themed storehouse rooms, a Bon-dance sauna, a cafe-bar in a former rice storehouse, and a community space are on site.
