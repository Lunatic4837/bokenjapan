    Roadside Station Sannai Woody Land

    Yokote, Akita · 0182-56-1600

    Roadside station at Akita's eastern gateway, in mountainous Sannai on the old Heiwa Kaido. Known for local foods such as imonoko taro, handmade soba, iburigakko, grapes, carrots, and Sannai Toji sake, plus onsen and streams.

  • Akita Sukaifesuta

    Akita Sky Festa

    Yokote, Akita · 080-3145-5371

    Balloons from across Japan float in clear spring skies for three days. Tethered rides rise about 20 meters, and night Balloon Glow turns the envelopes into lanterns. Free flights run only in calm early mornings.

  • Tezu Kuri Experience

    Handmade Experience

    Yokote, Akita · 0182-33-8870

    Cloisonne accessories and small boxes with mountain cherry bark attached can be made, along with traditional Akita crafts. Sessions are limited to 10 people.

  • Tenka Mori Ski Area

    Tenkamori Ski Area

    Yokote, Akita · 0182-45-9816

    Ski area in Masuda, Yokote, for beginners, intermediate skiers, and families. Night lighting is provided, snowboarding is allowed on all courses, and night sessions run on Fridays and Saturdays.
