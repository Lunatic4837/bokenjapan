  • Sai Ya Shuzo Mise

    Saiya Brewery

    Yurihonjo, Akita · 0184-22-0536

    The master brewer and staff grow Akita Sake Komachi rice and brew without stirring, added water, or filtration. Across the road, Hakko Koji Taya opened in July 2019 with a shop, cafe, and gallery.

  • Nihonkai Yojo Hanabitaikai

    Nihonkai Offshore Fireworks Festival

    Yurihonjo, Akita · 0184-73-2014

    Summer tradition in Iwaki, Yurihonjo. About 5,000 fireworks are launched offshore from an island-style fishing-port park, a format uncommon across Japan.

  • Chokai Kogen Yajima Ski Area

    Chokai Kogen Yajima Ski Area

    Yurihonjo, Akita · 0184-56-2182

    Skiing is possible from about December to March. Courses include a 1,300-meter panorama run and a snow-park run, with full equipment rental for beginners through advanced skiers.

  • Safin Experience (glassy (Gurasshii))

    Surfing Experience (glassy)

    Yurihonjo, Akita

    Veteran instructors teach surfing to adults and children, including first-timers. A folk house over 125 years old has been renovated as a guesthouse, and a campsite is available.

  • Anraku Onsen

    Anraku Onsen

    Yurihonjo, Akita · 0184-22-0637

    Founded in 1916, this ryokan in Yurihonjo has operated for over 100 years. Spring water is drawn from 800 meters underground as free-flowing onsen water, and the inn hosts ceremonies and local banquets.
