Sights in Yuzawa, Akita

Yuzawa · Akita · 15 places

See

  • Kawara Ke Jigoku

    Kawarage Jigoku

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-55-8180

    Sulfur gas still vents, leaving the slopes bare of plants and covered in strange rock. Counted with Osorezan and Tateyama among three sacred places in Japan; the road closes from early November to early May.

  • Sho An Kyo Dai Fun Yu

    Oyasukyo Daifunto

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-47-5080

    Hot water and steam erupt from steep rocks at the base of a 60-meter gorge. Spring greenery, autumn leaves, and winter icicles called shigakko mark the valley; the trail closes in winter.

  • Kawara Ke Oyu Taki

    Kawarage Oyu Falls

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-55-8180

    Hot-spring water falls about 20 meters into a basin used as a natural open-air bath. Bathing runs from early July to late September, with swimsuits required and a simple changing room only in summer.

  • Yuzawashi Kanko Kuri Sono

    Yuzawa City Tourist Chestnut Orchard

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-73-0415

    About 400 chestnut trees stand on a roughly 3-hectare mountain slope. In autumn, early Tanzawa and mid-season Tsukuba chestnuts ripen, and picking is available without a reservation.

  • Inaniwa Udon Isshisoden no Waza Kokoro Rekishi

    Inaniwa Udon: Inherited Craft, Heart, and History

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-43-2911

    Visitors can watch craftspeople work up close at Sato Yosuke Sohonten, then eat standard or seasonal dishes. The shop sells Inaniwa udon and other Akita specialties.

  • Ryokan Taro Be

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-47-5016

    Local sight in Yuzawa, Akita.

  • Yamamo Misoshoyu Jozomoto / Taka Shigeru Gomeigaisha

    Yamamo Miso Soy Sauce Brewery / Takashige Gomei Kaisha

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-73-2902

    Miso and soy sauce have been brewed in Iwasaki since 1867, continuing through seven generations. A factory store, Suijin strolling garden, garden cafe, and I.L.A. Gallery adjoin the brewery.

  • Aki no Miyayama So

    Akinomiya Sanso

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-56-2400

    Colorless, odorless water is called Kagami-yu for its clarity and light feel. This Akinomiya Onsen inn has a highland-style exterior, Heian-period interior colors, seven baths, and Japanese-Western meals.

  • Interiashoppu & Kafe momotose (Momotose)

    Interior Shop & Cafe Momotose

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-55-8839

    Shop and cafe selling Japanese-made wooden furniture, tableware, and household goods that visitors can handle. Meals use local vegetables, miso, soy sauce, Silk Pork, and Minase beef; coffee and hot-spring-pasteurized dairy drinks are also served.

  • Kawara no Yu Kko

    Kawara no Yukko

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-55-8180

    Hot-spring water wells up on the Yakunai River bank in the Akinomiya Onsen area. Shovels at a nearby hut are for digging a personal footbath, and ramen from Mako Shokudo can be eaten there.

  • Oyado Tsurumine Kan

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-47-5335

    Local sight in Yuzawa, Akita.

  • Sennen Park

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-73-2904

    A park in Yuzawa, Akita prefecture.

  • Ishi Mago Main Shop

    Ishimago Main Shop

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-73-2901

    Brewery making miso and soy sauce by traditional methods for over 160 years, using ingredients from Akita Prefecture. Warehouse tours show the koji room, wooden aging vats, and old tools; experience activities are also offered.

  • Medaki Sawa Torekkingu

    Medakisawa Trekking

    Yuzawashi, Akita

    Beech woods include trees over 100 years old along a sloping trail. A Japanese ash ranked fourth in size nationwide stands here, and the route can be walked through seasonal scenery.

  • Somoku Momotose

    Somoku Momotose

    Yuzawashi, Akita · 0183-55-8839

    Whole-house rental in Iwasaki's Takahashi family house, once used by a sake brewery owner and renovated while keeping Meiji-era character. An open kitchen is the focus downstairs; two straw-bed tatami rooms are upstairs.
