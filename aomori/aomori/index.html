Sights

  • Jigoku-numa Pond

    Aomori · 017-738-6002

    Strongly acidic pond in an old explosion crater about 5 minutes' walk from Sukayu Onsen, with steaming vents nearby.

  • Asamushi Onsen Matsunoyu

    Aomori · 017-752-3628

    Public bathhouse about 6 minutes' walk from Asamushi Onsen Station, with sodium-rich hot spring water and family baths.

  • Kayano Highlands

    Aomori · 017-738-2428

    Grassy highland at the foot of the Hakkoda Mountains, green in early summer and framed by autumn foliage in fall.

  • Popeye Onsen

    Aomori · 0172-62-7515

    Hot spring bathhouse with a slightly cool outdoor bath, a kids' bath and family baths, greeted by the resident cat.

  • Wanoyu Natural Hot Spring

    Aomori · 0172-62-7322

    Namioka hot spring known for its tea-black water; family baths are refilled for each group.

  • Gappo Park

    Aomori · 017-741-6634

    Seaside park in central Aomori City, completed in 1894 and listed among Japan's 100 best city parks.

  • Berjaya Hakkoda Ski Resort

    Aomori · 017-738-2233

    Resort hotel at the foot of the Hakkoda Mountains in Towada-Hachimantai National Park, next to powder-snow ski slopes.

  • Asamushi Tourist Information Center

    Aomori · 017-752-3259

    Tourist information for Asamushi Onsen, a hot spring town with over 1,200 years of history about 30 minutes from Aomori.

  • Namioka Hachimangu Shrine

    Aomori · 0172-62-2108

    Historic shrine in Namioka said to have been founded in the late 8th century and later a prayer site of the Tsugaru clan.

  • Shimizu Farm

    Aomori · 090-9744-3601

    Organic JAS-certified farm in Aomori's Tsukimino area offering strawberry picking and vegetable planting and harvest experiences.
