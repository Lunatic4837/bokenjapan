Sights

  • Lake Jusanko

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-38-1515

    Lake Jusanko is seven kilometers from north to south, five kilometers from east to west, and 31.4 kilometers around its shoreline, making it the third largest lake in Aomori Prefecture.

  • Kawakura Sai-no-Kawara Jizo Festival

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-53-3282

    Annual festival at a hilltop Jizo hall near Ashino Park, known for itako mediums and about 2,000 Jizo statues.

  • Kintaro Onsen

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-34-7715

    Hot spring bathhouse decorated with Kintaro paintings, popular for its cold plunge and alternating hot-cold bathing.

  • Hirota Onsen

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-34-6385

    Hot spring with a hot bath and cold plunge for alternating bathing, plus separate family baths.

  • Wakimoto Iwakisan Shrine Grand Festival

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-62-3536

    Mountain pilgrimage on Mt. Moya each lunar August 1 to pray for good harvests and catches; the public can join.

  • Mt. Moya

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-62-3536

    152 m hill in Wakimoto worshipped as Wakimoto Iwakisan Shrine and said to be the sister of Mt. Iwaki.

  • Onuma Park

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-35-2111

    Nature park below the Karakawa Castle ruins with a lakeside path and a long covered bridge.

  • Dazai Osamu Memories Plaza

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-35-2111

    Small rest plaza in Kanagi on the road from Dazai's birthplace Shayokan to his old school, with plaques of his works.

  • Nandaiji Temple

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-52-2661

    Family temple of Dazai Osamu's Tsushima family, which once ran a Sunday school and lent books to children.

  • Kase Yakko Odori Dance Lesson

    Goshogawara, Aomori · 0173-52-3232

    Short lesson in Kase's traditional Yakko dance taught by local women, with a drink and souvenir.
