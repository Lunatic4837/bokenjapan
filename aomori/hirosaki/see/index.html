Sights in Hirosaki, Aomori

Hirosaki · Aomori · 9 places

See

  • Atago Onsen

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-82-5885

    Hilltop hot spring with views of Mt. Iwaki and Mt. Hakkoda and a popular cold plunge.

  • Gumonji Temple (Mt. Iwaki)

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-83-2373

    Temple at the foot of Mt. Iwaki enshrining the zodiac ox and tiger, with a Kokuzo Bodhisattva main image.

  • Zenringai

    Hirosaki, Aomori

    Zenringai in Hirosaki is a unique district in Japan as where is packed with only Soto Zen temples.

  • Taga Shrine

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-86-2952

    Shrine in Higashimeya enshrining the zodiac rat, also called Kiyomizu Kannon after its Kyoto-style main hall.

  • Choshiro Park

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-40-3022

    Large park in Joto with unique play equipment, walking paths and lawns.

  • Kumano Okuteru Shrine

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-32-7663

    Historic shrine whose main hall, rebuilt in 1613 by the second Tsugaru lord, is said to be Hirosaki's oldest building.

  • Former Hirosaki Kaikosha

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-82-1642

    Large Renaissance-style wooden Western building with ornate details.

  • Tenmangu Shrine (Nishishigemori)

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-32-5796

    Shrine on the site of the former Daigyoin mountain-ascetic temple, enshrining Sugawara Tenjin since 1872.

  • Kakuoin Temple

    Hirosaki, Aomori · 0172-84-2127

    Shingon temple and former prayer site of the Hirosaki domain, enshrining the Namikiri Fudo.
