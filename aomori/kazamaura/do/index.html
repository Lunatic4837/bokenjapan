Do in Kazamaura

Do

  • Shimofuro Onsenkyo Hot Spring Village

    Kazamaura, Aomori · 0175-35-2010

    Shimofuro Onsen Hot Spring is the northern-most hot spring in Honshu, and there are about dozen each hot spring hotels and Japanese guest homes.

  • Kuwahata Onsen Yun Yun

    Kazamaura, Aomori · 0175-32-6045

    Village-run day hot spring on a former school site 30 m above the Tsugaru Strait, with outdoor bath and sauna.

  • Tsugaru Strait Light Fishing Experience

    Kazamaura, Aomori · 0175-31-1270

    Squid-style night fishing just 5 minutes from port, with local fishermen teaching beginners.
