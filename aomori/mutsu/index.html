Sights

  • Yunokawa Onsen

    Mutsu, Aomori · 0175-23-1311

    Yunokawa Onsen is an idyllic hot spring located 14 km from the mountainous town of Kawauchi, which served as the setting for Tsutomu Mizukami's novel Kigakaikyo.

  • Roadside Station Kawauchiko (Nodai Kogen Center)

    Mutsu, Aomori · 0175-38-5108

    Log-house center of the Kawauchi Lakeside Park on the way to Hotokegaura.

  • Hayakakenuma Park

    Mutsu, Aomori · 0175-22-1591

    Mutsu's busiest cherry-blossom spot, a scenic park opened to the public in 1964.

  • Wakinosawa Morning Market

    Mutsu, Aomori · 0175-44-2111

    Specialty-products fair held with the Wakinosawa fishermen's cooperative, featuring cod and other local seafood.

  • Wakinosawa Distribution Center

    Mutsu, Aomori · 0175-44-2233

    Shimokita tourist information and rest stop near Wakinosawa Port, also selling Mutsu Bay ferry tickets.
