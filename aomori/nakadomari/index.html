  • Chokandai Observatory

    Nakadomari, Aomori · 0173-64-2111

    Lookout at the highest point of the Tappaku Line (Route 339), with views of Hokkaido and Mt. Iwaki.

  • Nanatsu Falls

    Nakadomari, Aomori · 0173-57-2111

    21 m waterfall on the Tappaku Line that drops over seven rock steps.

  • Kintamamejo Puppet Theater

    Nakadomari, Aomori · 0173-58-3573

    Theater for the traditional Tsugaru puppet play created in 1907.

  • Tsugaru Novel Statue Memorial Hall

    Nakadomari, Aomori · 0173-64-3588

    Memorial to Dazai Osamu's novel "Tsugaru" overlooking the school ground where he reunited with his nanny Take.

  • Roadside Station Kodomari Pontomari

    Nakadomari, Aomori · 0173-27-9300

    Roadside station on the Tappaku Line with local squid and rockfish, a restaurant and a market.

  • Fudo Falls

    Nakadomari, Aomori · 0173-57-2111

    7 m waterfall once used by mountain ascetics, about 30 minutes' walk from Takinosawa Sabo Island.

  • Orikoshinai Auto Campground

    Nakadomari, Aomori

    Campground opposite Roadside Station Kodomari next to Orikoshinai beach.

  • Yurapark Nakadomari

    Nakadomari, Aomori · 0173-23-0132

    The town's only hot spring, opened in 2024, with sauna, cold bath and outdoor bath.
