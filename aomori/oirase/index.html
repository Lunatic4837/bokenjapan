Sights

  • Japan's Largest Statue of Liberty

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-52-6744

    Statue of Liberty replica in Icho Park, on the same 40°40' north latitude as New York.

  • Great Ginkgo of Negishi

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-4276

    Prefectural natural monument ginkgo, 32 m tall and 16 m around, estimated over 1,100 years old.

  • Icho Park

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-52-6744

    Park with spring cherry blossoms, hydrangeas and autumn ginkgo, home to the Statue of Liberty replica.

  • Kawayo Green Farm

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-4111

    A 50-hectare farm looking out over the Hakkoda Mountains. Hosts events, concerts, exhibitions, etc.

  • Momokawa Sake Brewery

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-52-2241

    Brewery founded in 1889 using Oirase River underground water and Aomori sake rice.

  • Agurinosato Oirase

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-2884080-2839-6061

    Agurinosato Oirase is a pick-your-own farm and hands-on facility offering not only all sorts of fruits and vegetables but also various delights.

  • Kehi Shrine

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-2591

    Shrine on the site of the Nanbu domain's largest horse ranch, known for its guardian deity of horses.

  • Shimoda Park

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-4702

    Park also called Swan Park, one of Aomori's main swan wintering spots.

  • Mukaiyama Station Museum

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-4703

    Tiny museum inside Mukaiyama Station run by local fans, with old railway equipment.

  • Oyama Shogi Memorial Hall

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-52-1411

    Museum about shogi master Oyama Yasuharu, the 15th Lifetime Meijin.
