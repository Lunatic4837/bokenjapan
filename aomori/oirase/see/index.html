  • Japan's Largest Statue of Liberty

  • Great Ginkgo of Negishi

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-4276

    Prefectural natural monument ginkgo, 32 m tall and 16 m around, estimated over 1,100 years old.

  • Icho Park

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-52-6744

  • Kehi Shrine

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-2591

  • Shimoda Park

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-4702

  • Mukaiyama Station Museum

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-56-4703

    Tiny museum inside Mukaiyama Station run by local fans, with old railway equipment.

  • Oyama Shogi Memorial Hall

    Oirase, Aomori · 0178-52-1411

    Museum about shogi master Oyama Yasuharu, the 15th Lifetime Meijin.
