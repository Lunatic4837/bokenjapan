  • Suiren-numa Pond

    Towada, Aomori · 0176-75-2425

  • Kimigara Slippers

    Towada, Aomori · 0176-28-3611

    Traditional slippers woven from dent corn husks, a craft of horse-breeding Towada.

  • Utarube Campsite

    Towada, Aomori · 0176-75-2477

    The campsite in the quiet and rich forest, located on the Ogura Peninsula at the Towada Lakeside, have beautiful view of the Lake Towada in front and Mount Hakkoda at some distance.
