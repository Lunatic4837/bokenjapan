Sights in Eiheiji, Fukui

Eiheiji, Fukui: things to do, food and stays · Fukui · 31 places

See

  • Daihonzan Eihei-ji Temple

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-3102, Fukui

    Head temple of the Soto Zen school founded by Dogen in 1244, in the mountains of Eiheiji town.

  • Benzaiten Hakuryu-o Daigongen

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-64-2566, Fukui

    Shrine hall on land opened in 717 as a sub-temple of Heisen-ji Hakusan Shrine.

  • ESHIKOTO

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-1030, Fukui

    Eiheiji town brand created by Ishidaya Nizaemon, owner of the Kokuryu sake brewery.

  • Maeda Seika (Habutae Mochi Shop)

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-2100, Fukui

    Confectioner known for habutae mochi, a Fukui specialty supplied to Eihei-ji.

  • Eiheiji Shinzen no Yado Hakujukan

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-1188, Fukui

    Temple-lodging style inn at the Eihei-ji entrance offering Zen meditation and vegetarian shojin cuisine.

  • Yoshimine-ji Temple

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-64-2536, Fukui

    About 800-year-old temple where Dogen first set up a training hall on entering Echizen in 1243.

  • Eiheiji Exchange Facility Eibokan

    Eiheiji, Fukui, Fukui

    Town facility on the theme of Zen, food and sake, with lunch, café and hire space.

  • Echizen Railway Eiheiji-guchi Station

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0120-840-508, Fukui

    Station on the Echizen Railway for access to Eihei-ji.

  • Eiheiji Midori no Mura Fureai Center

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-4222, Fukui

    Community and lifelong-learning centre built around a gymnasium, opened in 1993.

  • Jakko-en

    Eiheiji, Fukui, Fukui

    Memorial garden at Eihei-ji with the statue of the young Dogen and a tower for his master Nyojo.

  • Hokkyo-so

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-3311, Fukui

    Roadside restaurant and souvenir shop on the Eihei-ji approach with coach parking, serving Fukui sauce katsu-don and grated-radish soba.

  • Shihi Line-Carved Cliff Buddhas

    Eiheiji, Fukui, Fukui

    Buddhist figures carved into the rock wall at the entrance to the Shihi district, beside Sogen Bridge.

  • Kuzuryu River Museum Waku Waku RiverCAN

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-7100, Fukui

    River-themed museum and disaster-prevention centre for the Kuzuryu River basin.

  • Matsuoka Juni Magari

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-3400, Fukui

    Stretch of the old Katsuyama highway in the former Matsuoka castle town with a dozen right-angle bends from the Edo period.

  • Kamishihi Cultural Hall Sunsun Hall

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-64-3170, Fukui

    Cultural hall of Eiheiji town.

  • Eiheiji Dam

    Eiheiji, Fukui, Fukui

    Dam completed in 2001 on the Eiheiji River, built for flood control and water supply.

  • Tenryu-ji Temple

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-0471, Fukui

    Soto temple under Eihei-ji, built in 1653 on the order of Matsudaira Masakatsu, first lord of Matsuoka domain.

  • Ozeki (Folk Craft Shop)

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-3131, Fukui

    Folk-craft and local-product shop in the Eihei-ji temple-gate town.

  • Basho Monument, Tenryu-ji Grounds

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-0471, Fukui

    Monument at Tenryu-ji to a poem Matsuo Basho wrote there during his journey recorded in Oku no Hosomichi.

  • Omiyage Nakamura

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-3035, Fukui

    Souvenir shop selling sesame tofu, habutae mochi, dried radish and Eiheiji morning-porridge goods.

  • Omiyage-zaka

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-3097, Fukui

    Souvenir shop selling additive-free sesame tofu and Eiheiji-only mizu-yokan.

  • Matsuoka River Park

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-3400, Fukui

    Riverside park on the Kuzuryu River with one of the prefecture's largest mallet-golf courses.

  • Kogyo-ji Temple Shinshu Mirai-kan Museum Hana no Kura

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-64-2707, Fukui

    Temple museum in Eiheiji town.

  • Yamakawaya

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-3029, Fukui

    Shop selling prayer beads and carved Shaka Nyorai statues near Eihei-ji.

  • Matsuoka Park

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-3948, Fukui

    Hilltop park with views over Matsuoka's streets and the Kuzuryu River, including the old twelve bends.

  • Waku Waku RiverCAN (Kuzuryu River Basin Disaster Prevention Center)

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-7100, Fukui

    River education and disaster-prevention facility for the Kuzuryu River basin.

  • Arakawa Fireflies (Yoshino District)

    Eiheiji, Fukui, Fukui

    Yoshino district, called the "village of fireflies", with the Ara River as the best firefly-viewing spot.

  • Mt Daibutsuji

    Eiheiji, Fukui, Fukui

    807 m peak on the ridge trail from Yoshimine-ji to Eihei-ji, the route Dogen is said to have walked.

  • Goto

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-63-3007, Fukui

    Souvenir shop selling ceramics, washi paper, chopsticks and traditional gifts.

  • Joboji Mountain Landslide Site

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-3921, Fukui

    Mountain in Eiheiji town; landslide-control area.

  • Eiheiji Town Hall

    Eiheiji, Fukui · 0776-61-1111, Fukui

    Municipal office of Eiheiji town.
