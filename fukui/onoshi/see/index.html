Sights in Onoshi, Fukui

Onoshi, Fukui: things to do, food and stays · Fukui · 51 places

See

  • Echizen-Ono Castle (Castle in the Sky)

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-66-1111, Fukui

    Hilltop castle on a 249 m hill above the Ono basin, nicknamed the "castle in the sky".

  • Lake Kuzuryu

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-66-1111, Fukui

    Dam lake in Ono, one of Fukui's best stargazing spots for seeing the Milky Way.

  • Yuiraku-za Tenugui Corridor

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-65-5521, Fukui

    Corridor of about 200 Japanese tenugui towels in around 50 designs.

  • Rokuroshi Highlands

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-65-5521, Fukui

    Highland straddling Katsuyama and Ono, with nature and seasonal activities.

  • Karikomi Pond

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-66-1111, Fukui

    Mountain pond with autumn colours reflected, backed by Mt Sannomine, linked to the monk Taicho.

  • Ono Fossil Excavation Center HOROSSA!

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-78-2070, Fukui

    Facility where visitors can dig for fossils from the Paleozoic (about 440 million years ago) to the Cretaceous.

  • Former Tamura Family Samurai Residence

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-65-6212, Fukui

    City-designated cultural property: reconstructed house of Tamura Mataemon, senior retainer of Ono domain.

  • Katsuhara Campground

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-66-1111, Fukui

    Campground in Katsuhara, Ono.

  • Kurotani Snow Wall

    Onoshi, Fukui, Fukui

    Snow-wall road feature at Kurotani in Ono.

  • Yume no Kakehashi

    Onoshi, Fukui, Fukui

    266 m bridge over Lake Kuzuryu, known as the prototype for the Seto Ohashi bridge.

  • Kuzuryu Gorge

    Onoshi, Fukui, Fukui

    Gorge cut by the Kuzuryu River from Katsuhara to Izumi, with a parallel road.

  • Oshimizu Spring

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-66-1111, Fukui

    Famous spring in Ono, one of Japan's Hundred Famous Waters.

  • Hokyo-ji Temple

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-65-8833, Fukui

    Ancient Soto temple in dense cedar forest, opened about 700 years ago by Jakuen and ranked next to Eihei-ji.

  • Auto Campers Kuzuryu

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-78-2345, Fukui

    Campground at the eastern end of Lake Kuzuryu, some sites with AC power.

  • Hotoke-no-Omae Falls

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-66-1111, Fukui

  • Former Uchiyama Family Samurai Residence

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-65-6122, Fukui

    Residence commemorating Uchiyama Shichirouemon and his brother, who rebuilt Ono domain's finances at the end of the shogunate.

  • Teramachi Street (Ono)

    Onoshi, Fukui · 0779-65-5521, Fukui

  • Hongan Shimizu Itoyo
