Sights in Aizuwakamatsu, Fukushima

Aizuwakamatsu · Fukushima · 6 places

See

  • Hatsune Toy Flutes

    初音

    #3 ranked in Aizuwakamatsu

    A lucky Aizu New Year charm, sold only on the morning of New Year's Day from the Edo period until about 1965, which children blew as a toy.

  • Aizu Cotton

    会津木綿

    #4 ranked in Aizuwakamatsu

    Said to date from 1627, when lord Kato Yoshiaki brought weavers from Iyo-Matsuyama to teach the craft in Aizu.

  • Aizu Tojin Kites

    会津唐人凧

    #5 ranked in Aizuwakamatsu

    Kites that reached Nagasaki from the continent about 400 years ago; how they came to faraway Aizu is a mystery.

  • Aizu Keizan Pottery

    会津慶山焼

    #6 ranked in Aizuwakamatsu

    Began when Aizu lord Gamo Ujisato invited potters to make roof tiles for Kurokawa Castle, the forerunner of Tsuruga Castle.

  • Aizu Painted Candles

    会津絵ろうそく

    #7 ranked in Aizuwakamatsu

    Candle-making from lacquer-tree wax began in the late 16th century and was raised to a folk craft by Gamo Ujisato.

  • Pinwheels

    風車

    #8 ranked in Aizuwakamatsu

    A popular good-luck charm for prosperous business.
