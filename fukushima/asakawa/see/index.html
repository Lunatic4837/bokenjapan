  • Shirayama Hime Shrine

    白山比咩神社

    #1 ranked in Asakawa

    Founded in 1146 when the lord of Asakawa invited Hakusan worship from Kaga.

  • Kumogoro Iris Garden

    雲五郎あやめ園

    #3 ranked in Asakawa

    A residents' handmade iris garden on 50 ares of idle rice paddies.

  • Asakawa History and Folklore Museum

    浅川町歴史民俗資料館

    #4 ranked in Asakawa

    Displays on Asakawa's fireworks history, secret recipes and handmade tools.

  • Yoshida Tomizo Memorial Hall

    吉田富三記念館

    #5 ranked in Asakawa

    On the life of Asakawa-born, world-renowned cancer researcher Yoshida Tomizo.

  • Kanshuji Mummified Monk Kochi Hoin Yutei

    貫秀寺 即身仏「弘智法印　宥貞」

    #8 ranked in Asakawa

    Fukushima's only self-mummified monk, who entered meditation in 1683 for the villagers.

  • Tsuiji no Mori Seven Monks

    ついじの森七人坊主

    #9 ranked in Asakawa

    Five Wisdom Buddhas and two guardian stone statues in a grove of great cedars.
