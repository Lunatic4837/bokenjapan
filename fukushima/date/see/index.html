Sights in Date, Fukushima

Date · Fukushima · 6 places

See

  • Ryozen Shrine

    霊山神社

    #2 ranked in Date

    A former imperial-ranked shrine enshrining Kitabatake Chikafusa, Akiie, Akinobu and Morichika.

  • Peaches of the Takako Twenty Views

    高子二十境の桃

    #3 ranked in Date

    Mid-Edo poet Kumasaka Haryo named 20 scenic spots in Takako village and wrote Chinese poems about them.

  • Komahime Cherry

    駒ヒメ桜

    #4 ranked in Date

    An Edo higan cherry estimated to be about 500 years old.

  • Cherry Blossoms of Chausuyama

    茶臼山の桜

    #5 ranked in Date

    Park on the ruins of Chausuyama Castle, seat of the Kakeda clan in the 14th to 16th centuries.

  • Beniyatoge Senbonzakura Forest Park

    紅屋峠千本桜森林公園

    #6 ranked in Date

    About 1,000 cherry trees of 10 varieties around a park overlooking northern Date.

  • Saito Orchard

    斎藤果樹園

    #8 ranked in Date

    Peach picking of various varieties (reservation required), with peaches to take home.
