See

  • Fukushima City Photo Art Museum (Hana no Shashinkan)

    福島市写真美術館（愛称：花の写真館）

    #1 ranked in Fukushima

    Built in 1922 as the former Japan Electric Meter Inspection Office and handed over to Fukushima City in 2000.

  • Lito Leaf Art Museum Fukushima

    リト　リーフアート　ミュージアム　福島

    #2 ranked in Fukushima

    The world's first museum of leaf-cut art, opened in Iizaka, Fukushima City.

  • Skunk Cabbages of Otokonuma

    男沼の水芭蕉

    #3 ranked in Fukushima

    Delicate skunk cabbages bloom in profusion at Otokonuma and the Akebono wetland.

  • Takakurayama

    高倉山

    #4 ranked in Fukushima

    Little-known spot where cherry blossoms frame the "snow rabbit" on Mt. Azuma on clear days; spacious grounds, pets welcome.

  • Local sight in Fukushima

    堤ヶ平のヒメサユリ

    Romaji: Himesayuri Lilies of Tsutsumigadaira

    #5 ranked in Fukushima

    Pale pink himesayuri lilies sway across the mountainside.

  • Jododaira

    浄土平

    #6 ranked in Fukushima

    Midway along the Bandai-Azuma Skyline, an area often called the Arizona of Japan.

  • Hanayashiki Park

    花やしき公園

    #7 ranked in Fukushima

    A park where local residents planted some 7,000 flowering trees and shrubs such as flowering peach and forsythia.
