Sights in Furudono, Fukushima

Furudono · Fukushima · 10 places

See

  • Shiozawa Fudo Falls

    塩沢の不動滝

    #2 ranked in Furudono

    A waterfall in Okuda on Prefectural Road 135.

  • Shimotaki Fudo Grove

    下滝不動様の杜

    #3 ranked in Furudono

    A grove of old trees in Sanjo on Route 349.

  • Furudono Hachiman Shrine

    古殿八幡神社

    #6 ranked in Furudono

    Said to have been founded in 1064 by Minamoto no Yoriyoshi and Yoshiie after a victory.

  • Michi-no-Eki Furudono Ofukuro no Eki

    道の駅ふるどの　おふくろの駅

    #8 ranked in Furudono

    On Route 349 between central Fukushima and southern Iwaki.

  • Nyudo Onsen Nyudo no Yu

    入道温泉　入道の湯

    #9 ranked in Furudono

    Also called the deer spa, from a legend of a wounded deer healing here.

  • Samegawa River

    鮫川

    #10 ranked in Furudono

    River through the town centre lined with cherry blossoms in spring and autumn colours.

  • Mt. Mikabu

    三株山

    #11 ranked in Furudono

    Furudono's highest mountain, with about 80 wild cherry trees blooming on its plateau.

  • Gannyuji Temple Site

    如信上人願入寺跡

    #12 ranked in Furudono

    Historic site of the Oku no Gobo, said to have been founded by Nyoshin, grandson of Shinran.

  • Yoshida Shoin Visit Monument

    吉田松陰遊跡記念碑

    #13 ranked in Furudono

    Inscribed with his diary entry from a night in Takanuki in early 1852.

  • Koshiro Cherry

    越代のサクラ

    #14 ranked in Furudono

    A 400-year-old mountain cherry about 20 m tall, one of Japan's 100 forest giants.
