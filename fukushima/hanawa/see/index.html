Sights in Hanawa, Fukushima

Hanawa · Fukushima · 21 places

See

  • Autumn Leaves of Yujimata Onsen

    湯岐温泉郷の紅葉

    #1 ranked in Hanawa

    A spa known since Edo times, with cycling and driving routes through November color.

  • Triangle Road

    三角形の道

    #2 ranked in Hanawa

    A cycling course, the fourth certified in Japan by the Runners Information Lab.

  • Kappa no Suribachi Trail

    河童のすり鉢遊歩道

    #3 ranked in Hanawa

    A handmade trail maintained by Katagai residents.

  • Nagura River Gorge

    那倉川渓谷

    #4 ranked in Hanawa

    A gorge along Prefectural Road 27.

  • Tominaga Ichiro Hanawa Manga Gallery

    富永一朗はなわ漫画廊

    #6 ranked in Hanawa

    In the community plaza at JR Iwaki-Hanawa Station, easy to visit while waiting for a train.

  • Abukuma Kogen Art Museum

    塙町立あぶくま高原美術館

    #7 ranked in Hanawa

    Shows works by Hanawa-born painter Sugi Saburo and calligrapher Suzuki Seisui.

  • Wood Museum

    木の博物館

    #8 ranked in Hanawa

    Displays 300 items from 60 broadleaf species.

  • Furoyama Park

    風呂山公園

    #9 ranked in Hanawa

    Mountain azaleas planted after the Russo-Japanese War and tended by townspeople for 120 years.

  • Hanawa Community Plaza

    塙町コミュニティプラザ

    #10 ranked in Hanawa

    A building with eight forest-like roofs along Iwaki-Hanawa Station, symbol of the wood town.

  • Yuyu Land Hanawa Dahlia Garden

    湯遊ランドはなわダリア園

    #11 ranked in Hanawa

    About 5,000 dahlias of 300 varieties bloom from August to October.

  • Cherries of Mukaigaoka Park

    向ヶ岡公園の桜

    #12 ranked in Hanawa

    A park for all classes said to have been built in 1793 by the respected magistrate Teranishi Jujiro.

  • Kuji River Cycling Road Cherries

    久慈川サイクリングロードの桜並木

    #13 ranked in Hanawa

    At peak bloom, 5 km of the Kuji River become a splendid tunnel of cherry blossoms.

  • Otomehime no Yu (Tokoyo Onsen)

    常世温泉　乙女姫の湯

    #15 ranked in Hanawa

    Said to be a beauty spa because Princess Otome of Shibahara used its spring water.

  • Hanawa Yujimata Onsen Yuyu Land Hanawa

    はなわ湯岐温泉 湯遊ランドはなわ

    #16 ranked in Hanawa

    Overnight stays and day-use hot-spring bathing.

  • Hanawa Fureai Forest

    塙ふれあいの森

    #17 ranked in Hanawa

    A 10.6 ha forest with 26,000 trees including cherry and dogwood.

  • Historic site in Hanawa

    陸奥代官塙陣屋跡

    #20 ranked in Hanawa

    Site of the shogunate office for 50,000 koku of direct-rule land, moved to Hanawa in 1729.

  • Tojoji Yakushido

    東浄寺薬師堂

    #21 ranked in Hanawa

    A hillside sacred site to which Tojoji was moved at some point.

  • Tanaka Genzo Execution Site

    田中愿蔵刑場跡

    #22 ranked in Hanawa

    Site linked to the 1864 Mito-domain uprising in the turbulent last years of the shogunate.

  • Daijuku Yakuoji Yakushido

    台宿薬王寺薬師堂

    #23 ranked in Hanawa

    A hall built with donations as Yoneyama Yakushi worship spread around Mount Yamizo from the 1670s.

  • Tokoyo Kannon Hall

    常世観音堂

    #24 ranked in Hanawa

    Kannon hall named after Tokoyo, one of the few local place names recorded in ancient chronicles.

  • Furujuku Kannon Hall

    古宿観音堂

    #25 ranked in Hanawa

    A hall of an Eleven-faced Kannon with an ink dragon on the ceiling painted by Kano Masunobu in 1742.
