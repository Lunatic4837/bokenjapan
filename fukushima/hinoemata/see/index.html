Sights in Hinoemata, Fukushima

Hinoemata · Fukushima · 20 places

See

  • Kotsunagi Line

    小繋ライン

    #2 ranked in Hinoemata

    A pass road to Minamiaizu with superb autumn scenery.

  • Numayama Pass

    沼山峠

    #3 ranked in Hinoemata

    Terminus of the Oze-numa shuttle bus; about an hour's walk to the Oe Marsh.

  • Autumn Leaves at Mokake Falls

    モーカケの滝の紅葉

    #4 ranked in Hinoemata

    Falls on the Mokake stream, a tributary of the Hinoemata River.

  • Numata Kaido Road

    沼田街道

    #5 ranked in Hinoemata

    An old trade route linking Numata in Gunma via Ozenuma to Aizuwakamatsu.

  • Ozenuma Pond

    尾瀬沼

    #6 ranked in Hinoemata

    At 1,665 m, dammed by an eruption of Mount Hiuchigatake, about 6 km around and 5 m deep; Japan's highest lake of its kind.

  • Hiraname Falls

    平滑ノ滝

    #7 ranked in Hinoemata

    A rare slide waterfall 70 m wide and 500 m long flowing gently over the Tadami River's bedrock.

  • Sanjo Falls

    三条の滝

    #8 ranked in Hinoemata

    A symbol of Oze: a great waterfall 100 m high and 30 m wide.

  • Hinoemata History and Folklore Museum

    檜枝岐村歴史民俗資料館

    #9 ranked in Hinoemata

    Near the village office, showing village history and Hinoemata Kabuki costumes.

  • Nakadoai Park

    中土合公園

    #10 ranked in Hinoemata

    On the Funamata River, for rice cooking, barbecues (reservation), river play and walks.

  • Mini Oze Park

    ミニ尾瀬公園

    #11 ranked in Hinoemata

    Compact botanical garden recreating Oze's flora by season, with a memorial hall to naturalist Takeda Hisayoshi.

  • Oze National Park

    尾瀬国立公園

    #12 ranked in Hinoemata

    Spanning Fukushima, Tochigi, Gunma and Niigata, designated Japan's 29th national park in 2007.

  • Autumn Leaves of Bunadaira

    ブナ平の紅葉

    #13 ranked in Hinoemata

    Seen from the shuttle bus between Miike and Numayama Pass.

  • Oze Hinoemata Onsen

    尾瀬檜枝岐温泉

    #14 ranked in Hinoemata

    The northern gateway to Oze, where all 3 ryokan and 26 minshuku have hot-spring water.

  • Oze Hinoemata Onsen Ski Area

    尾瀬檜枝岐温泉スキー場

    #15 ranked in Hinoemata

    An advanced slope with two courses on Route 352.

  • Mount Hiuchigatake

    燧ヶ岳

    #19 ranked in Hinoemata

    North of Ozenuma, Tohoku's highest peak and one of Japan's 100 famous mountains, with Aizu's best views.

  • Kamitashiro

    上田代

    #20 ranked in Hinoemata

    A gentle wetland on Hiuchigatake's north slope dotted with ponds and plants.

  • Mount Aizu-Komagatake

    会津駒ケ岳

    #21 ranked in Hinoemata

    A famous Aizu peak with views over Oze, Minamiaizu and Nikko.

  • Oze no Sato Exchange Center

    尾瀬の郷交流センター

    #22 ranked in Hinoemata

    A restaurant serving Hinoemata's traditional mountain cuisine and tachi soba.

  • Six Jizo Statues

    六地蔵

    #24 ranked in Hinoemata

    Six stone statues standing beside Route 352.

  • Atago and Chinju Shrines

    愛宕神社・鎮守神社

    #25 ranked in Hinoemata

    In the village center, always watching over the villagers.
