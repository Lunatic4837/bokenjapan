Sights in Hirata, Fukushima

Hirata · Fukushima · 6 places

See

  • Yamadori Falls

    山鶏滝

    #1 ranked in Hirata

    A photo spot where an 8 m drop contrasts with vivid autumn leaves.

  • Iwakura Cherry

    岩倉桜

    #2 ranked in Hirata

    A lone Edo higan cherry about 500 years old against a cedar forest.

  • Michi-no-Eki Hirata

    道の駅ひらた

    #3 ranked in Hirata

    At the junction of Route 49 and the Hirata IC, near Jupia Land Hirata.

  • Jupia Land Hirata Hydrangea and Lily Gardens

    ジュピアランドひらたのあじさい園・ゆり園

    #4 ranked in Hirata

    Set a world record in 2018 for the most hydrangea varieties, 372.

  • Mount Yomogita

    蓬田岳

    #6 ranked in Hirata

    A 952 m mountain with a first-order triangulation point, one of Tohoku's 100 mountains.

  • Moss Phlox of Jupia Land Hirata

    ジュピアランドひらたの芝桜（蓬田岳森林公園）

    #7 ranked in Hirata

    About 250,000 moss phlox bloom on 2.3 ha at the foot of Mount Yomogita.
