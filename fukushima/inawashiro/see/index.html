Sights in Inawashiro, Fukushima

Inawashiro · Fukushima · 26 places

See

  • Saishoji Temple

    西勝寺

    #1 ranked in Inawashiro

    Its Funki Fudo, one of Aizu's five-color Fudo, is said to grant courage and strength.

  • Kobirakata Tenmangu

    小平潟天満宮

    #2 ranked in Inawashiro

    In pine woods on Lake Inawashiro's north shore, counted among Japan's three great Tenjin shrines.

  • Iwahashi Shrine

    磐椅神社

    #3 ranked in Inawashiro

    Moved here from the summit of Mount Bandai about 1,300 years ago.

  • Kamegajo Park

    亀ヶ城公園

    #4 ranked in Inawashiro

    Ruins of a hill castle of the Inawashiro clan, built at the tip of a mudflow ridge below Mount Bandai.

  • Shiraito Falls

    白糸の滝

    #5 ranked in Inawashiro

    Seen 5 minutes from the Numajiri trailhead, a ribbon of hot-spring water falling over lava cliffs.

  • Tatsusawa Fudo Falls

    達沢不動滝

    #6 ranked in Inawashiro

    A popular, beautiful fall with a Fudo shrine, 5 minutes by car and 10 on foot from Nakanosawa Onsen.

  • Enmusubi Cherry

    えんむすび桜

    #8 ranked in Inawashiro

    A mountain cherry sprouting from an 800-year-old cedar at the entrance to Iwahashi Shrine.

  • Inawashiro Ski Resort

    猪苗代スキー場

    #10 ranked in Inawashiro

    From 42-degree expert runs to a gentle central slope, set in magnificent nature.

  • Kannonji River Cherry Trees

    観音寺川の桜並木

    #11 ranked in Inawashiro

    About 1 km of cherry trees along the Kannonji River, including Somei-Yoshino and weeping cherries.

  • Shibuki Ice

    しぶき氷

    #12 ranked in Inawashiro

    In deep winter, wind-sprayed ice sculptures form near the Nagase River mouth south of Tenjinhama.

  • Hakucho Beach

    白鳥浜

    #13 ranked in Inawashiro

    Lake Inawashiro beach where hundreds of swans winter from November to mid-April.

  • Tenjinhama Beach

    天神浜

    #14 ranked in Inawashiro

    On Lake Inawashiro's north shore along Route 49.

  • Shidahama Beach

    志田浜

    #15 ranked in Inawashiro

    White sand and green pines on Lake Inawashiro's northeast shore along Route 49.

  • Midori no Mura

    緑の村

    #16 ranked in Inawashiro

    At the foot of Mount Bandai, with a kingfisher aquarium, craft workshops, fishing pond and barbecue house.

  • Minamigaoka Ranch

    南ケ丘牧場

    #17 ranked in Inawashiro

    A ranch in Bandai-Asahi National Park with horse and donkey riding, putter golf and archery.

  • Inawashiro Craft Beer Hall

    猪苗代地ビール館

    #18 ranked in Inawashiro

    Craft beer brewed German-style with Bandai spring water and select German ingredients.

  • Inawashiro Herb Garden

    猪苗代ハーブ園

    #19 ranked in Inawashiro

    An herb garden in front of the Listel Inawashiro hotel.

  • Oshika Cherry

    大鹿桜

    #21 ranked in Inawashiro

    A late-blooming cherry, one of Aizu's five famous cherries, at Iwahashi Shrine.

  • Yokomuki Onsen

    横向温泉

    #24 ranked in Inawashiro

    At the western foot of Tsuchiyu Pass, used since old times as an inn and healing spa.

  • Listel Ski Fantasia

    リステルスキーファンタジア

    #25 ranked in Inawashiro

    Six varied courses up to a mogul World Cup course, plus a snow playland for families.

  • Bonari Pass

    母成峠

    #26 ranked in Inawashiro

    Battlefield where the new government army clashed with Hijikata Toshizo's Shinsengumi in the Boshin War.

  • Mount Bandai

    磐梯山

    #27 ranked in Inawashiro

    A 1,816 m stratovolcano made up of three peaks.

  • Local sight in Inawashiro

    白津からかさ松

    #28 ranked in Inawashiro

    25 m old pine with a perfect umbrella-shaped crown and a Sanbo Kojin shrine at its base.

  • Former Takamatsu-no-miya Okinashima Villa (Guest House)

    旧高松宮翁島別邸（迎賓館）

    #29 ranked in Inawashiro

    A Japanese-style annex of Tenkyokaku, built in 1922 for Princess Arisugawa's convalescence.

  • Kannonji Temple

    観音寺

    #30 ranked in Inawashiro

    Founded in the 1190s, valuable evidence of Muromachi-era stone Buddhist culture in Aizu.

  • Annonji Temple

    安穏寺

    #31 ranked in Inawashiro

    Holds the oldest cast Amida Buddha in Aizu.
