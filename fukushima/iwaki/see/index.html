Sights in Iwaki, Fukushima

Iwaki · Fukushima · 5 places

See

  • 末続のアジサイ

    Hydrangeas of Suetsugi

    #1 ranked in Iwaki

    Hydrangeas planted at the junction from Route 6 to JR Suetsugi Station and along the roadside slopes.

  • いわき和紙

    Iwaki Washi Paper

    #2 ranked in Iwaki

    A craft from the Edo period: local paper-mulberry fiber is carefully hand-made into paper in the harsh winter cold.

  • いわき絵のぼり

    Iwaki Painted Banners

    #3 ranked in Iwaki

    Painted banners handed down since the Edo period as charms for Boys' Day and a son's healthy growth.

  • いわき回転やぐら盆踊り大会

    Iwaki Rotating Yagura Bon Dance

    #5 ranked in Iwaki

    A summer tradition in Uchigo dating from the days of the Joban coal mines.

  • いわきおどり

    Iwaki Odori Dance

    #7 ranked in Iwaki

    A dance created in 1981 to mark the 15th anniversary of Iwaki City.
