Sights in Izumizaki, Fukushima

Izumizaki · Fukushima · 10 places

See

  • Summit of Karasu Pass

    烏峠頂上

    #1 ranked in Izumizaki

    The village's highest peak at 486 m, with an Inari shrine long worshipped.

  • Izumizaki Museum

    泉崎資料館

    #2 ranked in Izumizaki

    Finds from village sites and a diorama of the whole village.

  • Weeping Cherry of Joganji

    常願寺のしだれ桜

    #3 ranked in Izumizaki

    An old tree over 600 years old, planted in the Kamakura period.

  • Weeping Cherry of Shokenji

    昌建寺のしだれ桜

    #4 ranked in Izumizaki

    A 230-year-old village natural monument watching over temple visitors.

  • Autumn Leaves of Karasu Pass

    烏峠の紅葉

    #5 ranked in Izumizaki

    The village symbol turns color with oak, maple and mountain cherry.

  • Weeping Cherry of Atago Shrine

    愛宕神社のしだれ桜

    #6 ranked in Izumizaki

    A 120-year-old weeping cherry guarding an unattended shrine.

  • Karasu Pass Trails

    烏峠（烏峠遊歩道）

    #8 ranked in Izumizaki

    Ten walking trails with sweeping views from the summit and Enmandaira.

  • Izumizaki Cave Tomb

    泉崎横穴

    #9 ranked in Izumizaki

    A decorated cave tomb about 1,400 years old, found in 1933, the first in Tohoku.

  • Sekiwaku Government Office Site

    関和久官衙遺跡

    #10 ranked in Izumizaki

    Remains of the ancient Shirakawa district office, in use for 250 years from about 1,300 years ago.

  • Karasu Pass Inari Shrine

    烏峠稲荷神社

    #11 ranked in Izumizaki

    Founded in 828 and revered as a shrine that grants wishes.
