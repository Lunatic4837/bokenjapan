Things to do in Kagamiishi, Fukushima

Kagamiishi · Fukushima · 3 places

Do

  • Iwase Farm

    岩瀬牧場

    #2 ranked in Kagamiishi

    Japan's first Western-style ranch and the model for the song Bokujo no Asa.

  • Torimiyama Park

    鳥見山公園

    #3 ranked in Kagamiishi

    Kagamiishi's top cherry spot, with about 400 trees in 18 ha, lit up at night in season.

  • Niida Twin Amida Raigo Rock Carving

    仁井田双式来迎三尊図像磨崖板碑

    #7 ranked in Kagamiishi

    The only twin Amida descent relief in Japan, carved into a cliff on the Shakado River.
