Sights in Kagamiishi, Fukushima

Kagamiishi · Fukushima · 6 places

See

  • Iwase Agricultural High School Garden

    岩瀬農業高校前庭園

    #4 ranked in Kagamiishi

    A 7,000 m2 landscape garden around a large pond with 117 kinds of trees.

  • Kobo Fudo no Yu

    弘法不動の湯

    #5 ranked in Kagamiishi

    Legend says hot water gushed out where Fudo, appearing in a dream, pointed.

  • Saikoji Temple

    西光寺

    #6 ranked in Kagamiishi

    Said to have been founded in the 1560s by Kagaminuma Tonai, a retainer of the Nikaido lords of Sukagawa Castle.

  • Oguriyama Kannon Hall

    小栗山観音堂

    #8 ranked in Kagamiishi

    A cave in the hall enshrines an Eleven-faced Kannon said to be by the priest Gyoki.

  • Kagaminuma Pond

    鏡沼

    #9 ranked in Kagamiishi

    Legend tells of a wife who walked here after her exiled husband, found him dead, and threw herself into the pond.

  • Kasa Jizo Bunna Stone Monument

    笠地蔵文和の碑

    #10 ranked in Kagamiishi

    Jizo statues kept in a hall built by villagers; fairs on April 23 and August 23 each year.
