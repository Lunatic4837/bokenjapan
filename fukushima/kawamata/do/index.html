Do in Kawamata

Kawamata · Fukushima · 6 places

Do

  • 常泉寺のシダレザクラ

    Weeping Cherry of Josenji

    #4 ranked in Kawamata

    A 250-year-old twin-trunk weeping cherry that cascades with pale pink blossoms.

  • 女神山

    Mount Megami

    #6 ranked in Kawamata

    A well-trailed mountain rich in wildlife and history, straddling Kawamata and Tsukidate, popular with hikers.

  • 口太山

    Mount Kuchibuto

    #7 ranked in Kawamata

    A mountain between Kawamata and Towa with maintained trails and summit, enjoyed in every season.

  • 高太石山

    Mt. Takadaishi

    #10 ranked in Kawamata

    Quiet mountain climbed in about an hour on beginner-to-intermediate trails, with beech woods and granite.

  • かわまたおりもの展示館　からりこ館

    Kawamata Textile Museum Karariko-kan

    #12 ranked in Kawamata

    Inside Michi-no-Eki Kawamata, introducing silk history and techniques with weaving and dyeing workshops.

  • 川俣中央公園

    Kawamata Central Park

    #17 ranked in Kawamata

    Hilltop park in the town centre with a Japanese garden, outdoor stage and about 150 Somei-Yoshino cherry trees.
