Sights in Kawamata, Fukushima

Kawamata · Fukushima · 12 places

See

  • Kawamata Shamo Chicken

    川俣シャモ

    #1 ranked in Kawamata

    Enjoy Kawamata Shamo, a Fukushima brand-certified local chicken and a town specialty.

  • Chojusen Cherry Road

    長寿泉の桜街道

    #2 ranked in Kawamata

    Cherry trees nourished by the Chojusen spring on Mount Choju, said to add ten years to your life if you drink it.

  • Kotehime Cherry of Hataori Shrine

    機織神社の小手姫桜

    #3 ranked in Kawamata

    About 1,400 years ago Otomo no Koteko, consort of Emperor Sushun, came here seeking her son and taught weaving.

  • Otsu Zaru Chrysanthemum Garden

    大津ざる菊園

    #5 ranked in Kawamata

    About 1,400 dome-shaped zaru chrysanthemums bloom in red, white and yellow.

  • Mount Choju and Mount Tarobo

    長寿山、太郎坊山

    #8 ranked in Kawamata

    Mount Tarobo (about 551 m) to the north and Mount Choju (about 566 m) to the south, either side of Niwasaka Pass.

  • Mount Hanatsuka

    花塚山

    #9 ranked in Kawamata

    Kawamata's highest peak, straddling Kawamata and Iitate.

  • Fukusawa Hayama

    福沢羽山

    #11 ranked in Kawamata

    A 607 m hill in the southwest of town with superb summit views.

  • Kawamata Specialty Hall Silkpia

    かわまた銘品館シルクピア

    #13 ranked in Kawamata

    Displays and sells Kawamata's famous products inside Michi-no-Eki Kawamata.

  • Hayama no Mori Art Museum

    川俣町「羽山の森美術館」

    #14 ranked in Kawamata

    A museum in the renovated former Fukusawa Elementary School, closed after 133 years.

  • Koma Cherry of Akiyama

    秋山の駒ザクラ

    #15 ranked in Kawamata

    A giant Edo higan cherry over 500 years old, about 21 m tall, on a slope at the foot of Mount Megami.

  • Great Wisteria of Haneda Kasuga Shrine

    羽田春日神社の大フジ

    #18 ranked in Kawamata

    Three wisteria vines climbing cedar and hackberry trees beside the shrine approach, blooming in early summer.

  • Kasuga Shrine

    春日神社

    #19 ranked in Kawamata

    Legend says it was founded by Lord Yamakage, saved from mountain monkeys by a white deer, on divine instruction.
