See

  • 平伏沼

    Heifukunuma Pond

    #1 ranked in Kawauchi

    A small pond atop 842 m Mount Heifuku, ringed by broadleaf forest home to forest green tree frogs.

  • 高塚高原

    Takatsuka Highlands

    #3 ranked in Kawauchi

    East of Mount Otakine, with azaleas and rhododendrons, fresh greenery and autumn colors.

  • 平伏沼モリアオガエル

    Forest Green Tree Frogs of Heifukunuma

    #4 ranked in Kawauchi

    They lay white foam-wrapped eggs on waterside branches about 10 days into the rainy season.

  • 八幡神社

    Hachiman Shrine

    #5 ranked in Kawauchi

    Shrine founded in the 1070s, said to enshrine the Iwashimizu Hachiman deity brought by Minamoto no Yoriyoshi.

  • 天山文庫

    Tenzan Bunko

    #6 ranked in Kawauchi

    A thatched library given by Kawauchi villagers to the poet Kusano Shinpei, an honorary resident.
