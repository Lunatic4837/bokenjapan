Sights in Kitakata, Fukushima

Kitakata · Fukushima · 8 places

See

  • 蔵のまち喜多方

    The Warehouses of Kitakata

    #1 ranked in Kitakata

    About 4,200 traditional kura storehouses stand in Kitakata, most still in everyday use as shops, homes and sake or miso storehouses.

  • 喜多方蔵の里

    Kitakata Kura-no-Sato

    #2 ranked in Kitakata

    Open-air museum of relocated storehouses and old houses around a courtyard, a good introduction before touring the kura in town.

  • 撫木のカタクリ群生地

    Dogtooth Violets of Nadeki

    #3 ranked in Kitakata

    A 1 ha colony of delicate dogtooth violets about 3 km from Nadeki village.

  • 雄国の根まがり竹細工

    Oguni Nemagari Bamboo Craft

    #4 ranked in Kitakata

    Bamboo craft made since the Edo period.

  • 会津桐下駄

    Aizu Paulownia Geta

    #5 ranked in Kitakata

    Aizu paulownia's toughness, luster, straight grain and resistance to splitting make it ideal for geta sandals.

  • オープンガーデン「愛の花園」

    Open Garden Ai no Hanazono

    #6 ranked in Kitakata

    Over 300 kinds of flowering plants from spring to autumn, with photos of the flower beds on display.

  • 雷神山花の森

    Raijinyama Flower Forest

    #7 ranked in Kitakata

    About 2,000 trees of 60 kinds, including cherry, azalea and forsythia, with colorful blooms and views.

  • 棚田の桜

    Terraced-Field Cherry Trees

    #9 ranked in Kitakata

    Three cherry varieties planted on the Numanotaira terraces bloom at different times for a long season.
