Things to do in Kitashiobara, Fukushima

Kitashiobara · Fukushima · 6 places

Do

  • Urabandai Ski Area

    裏磐梯スキー場

    #4 ranked in Kitashiobara

    Ski area on the back side of Mt. Bandai with powder snow.

  • Hoshino Resorts Nekoma Mountain (North Area)

    星野リゾート ネコマ マウンテン【北エリア】（旧猫魔スキー場）

    #5 ranked in Kitashiobara

    The former Alts Bandai and Nekoma ski areas, linked by lift into one resort.

  • Grandeco Snow Resort

    グランデコスノーリゾート

    #6 ranked in Kitashiobara

    Resort with excellent snow and a long season from early December to early May.

  • Snowshoe Tours, Urabandai

    スノーシュー体験（裏磐梯）

    #8 ranked in Kitashiobara

    Experience powder snow said to be Japan's finest around Mount Bandai.

  • Aizu Yamashio Mountain Salt

    会津山塩

    #12 ranked in Kitashiobara

    Rare salt made by boiling down Oshio Urabandai Onsen water over a wood fire.

  • Oshio Urabandai Onsen

    大塩裏磐梯温泉

    #23 ranked in Kitashiobara

    Quiet mountain onsen village said to date back some 1,200 years to Kobo Daishi.
