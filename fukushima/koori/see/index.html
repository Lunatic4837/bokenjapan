See

  • 王林

    Orin Apples

    #1 ranked in Koori

    Born in Koori, the Orin is a classic green apple crossed from Golden Delicious and Indo.

  • 至福の桃飲むこんにゃくゼリー

    Shifuku no Momo Drinkable Konjac Jelly

    #2 ranked in Koori

    The third product in Koori's Shifuku no Momo peach series.

  • 至福の桃グミ

    Shifuku no Momo Gummies

    #3 ranked in Koori

    The second product in the Shifuku no Momo peach series.

  • 至福の桃ソルベ

    Shifuku no Momo Sorbet

    #4 ranked in Koori

    Made only with Koori-grown Akatsuki peaches, with 75% fruit juice.

  • 福桃ちゃん絵付け体験

    Fukumomo-chan Painting Experience

    #5 ranked in Koori

    Paint your own peach-like kokeshi doll, Fukumomo-chan.

  • 陣屋の杜公園

    Jinya no Mori Park

    #6 ranked in Koori

    Site of a shogunate magistrate's office, once the political center of Koori.

  • 産ヶ沢川ホタル自然公園

    Ubugasawa River Firefly Park

    #7 ranked in Koori

    A Genji firefly habitat where fireflies swarm from mid-June to early July.

  • ハートレイク半田沼

    Heart Lake Handanuma

    #8 ranked in Koori

    Seen from near the 863 m summit of Mount Handa, the pond looks heart-shaped.

  • 種徳美術館

    Shutoku Art Museum

    #10 ranked in Koori

    Shows some 250 Edo- and early Meiji-era paintings and calligraphy, including a prefectural Important Cultural Property screen.

  • 旧伊達郡役所

    Former Date District Office

    #11 ranked in Koori

    A romantic Western-style building erected in 1883.

  • 桃の郷ポケットパーク

    Momo no Sato Pocket Park

    #12 ranked in Koori

    A pocket park with the Koori Togenkyo peach-country lookout and restrooms.

  • こおり桃源郷の桃の花

    Kori Peach Blossom Valley

    #13 ranked in Koori

    Peach orchards along the Abukuma River that turn the valley pink in spring; memorial to the 2015 imperial visit.

  • 万正寺の大カヤ

    Great Torreya of Manshoji

    #14 ranked in Koori

    Said to have been planted by Date Tomomune to mark taking up residence at Takadate in 1189.

  • 御蔭の松

    Okage Pine

    #15 ranked in Koori

    Named by the Emperor during his 1881 tour of Tohoku.

  • 桑折西山城跡

    Koori Nishiyama Castle Ruins

    #16 ranked in Koori

    A mountain castle said to have been built by Date Tomomune, founder of the Date clan, after Minamoto no Yoritomo's 1189 northern campaign.

  • 桑折寺山門

    Koori-dera Temple Gate

    #17 ranked in Koori

    A gate from Nishiyama Castle, moved here when the castle was demolished in 1548 and Date Harumune moved to Yonezawa.

  • 観音寺

    Kannonji Temple

    #18 ranked in Koori

    Family temple of Date Yoshihiro, third head of the Date clan, and one of the five Date temples.

  • 伊達朝宗墓所

    Tomb of Date Tomomune

    #19 ranked in Koori

    A five-tiered stone stupa for the Date clan founder, erected by the Sendai domain in the Edo period.
