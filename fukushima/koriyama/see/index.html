Sights in Koriyama, Fukushima

Koriyama · Fukushima · 5 places

See

  • Sakurazato Chrysanthemum Garden

    桜里の菊楽園

    #1 ranked in Koriyama

    Beside the Benishidare Jizo cherry in Nakata, Koriyama, about 3,000 zaru chrysanthemums bloom across 30 ares.

  • Choshigataki Falls

    銚子ヶ滝

    #2 ranked in Koriyama

    An approximately 48 m waterfall on the Ishimushiro River, fed from Mount Adatara, framed by red and yellow foliage.

  • Gotomakita Cherry Tree

    五斗蒔田桜

    #3 ranked in Koriyama

    A weeping cherry about 150 years old on a low hill, believed to be a descendant of the Miharu Takizakura.

  • Ebine Traditional Handmade Washi

    海老根伝統手漉和紙

    #5 ranked in Koriyama

    Papermaking in the Ebine district has been handed down since the Edo period.

  • Aizu Tojin Kites

    会津唐人凧

    #6 ranked in Koriyama

    Kites that reached Nagasaki from the continent about 400 years ago; how they came to faraway Aizu is a mystery.
