Sights in Minamiaizu, Fukushima

Minamiaizu · Fukushima · 36 places

See

  • Weeping Cherry of Nansenji

    南泉寺のしだれ桜

    #1 ranked in Minamiaizu

    A weeping cherry about 120 years old behind Nansenji's tower gate.

  • Local sight in Minamiaizu

    シラネアオイ園

    Romaji: Shiraneaoi Garden

    #2 ranked in Minamiaizu

    A wildflower garden with Japan's only colony of 10,000 white Shiraneaoi.

  • Weeping Cherry of Former Nakaarai Branch School

    旧中荒井分校のしだれ桜

    #4 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Known locally as the Hundred-Year Cherry, a designated landscape tree.

  • Jorakuin Temple

    常楽院

    #5 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Its Seishun Fudo, one of Aizu's five-color Fudo, is said to grant youthful passion and vitality.

  • Jionji Temple

    慈恩寺

    #6 ranked in Minamiaizu

    One of Aizu's five-color Fudo; praying to its Jiai Fudo is said to awaken compassion.

  • Tajima Banko Ware

    田島万古焼

    #7 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Pottery known for lucky frog teacups and teapots with rattling dice inside.

  • Michi-no-Eki Banya

    道の駅番屋

    #8 ranked in Minamiaizu

    A small oasis at the gateway to Tohoku.

  • Roadside Station Tajima

    道の駅たじま

    #9 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Southern gateway to Aizu selling seasonal vegetables, fruit and local crafts, with sansai soba.

  • Furumachi Festival

    古町のまつり

    #10 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Held each September since 1681 as the festival of Hirose Shrine, continuing about 340 years.

  • Mount Karakura

    唐倉山

    #11 ranked in Minamiaizu

    A sacred mountain said to enshrine Izanagi and Izanami, once climbed by ascetics and pilgrims.

  • Mount Mitsuiwa and Mount Madoake

    三ツ岩岳・窓明山

    #12 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Mount Mitsuiwa is named for the three rocks at its summit.

  • Mt. Daihakata

    大博多山

    #13 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Mountain with a first-order triangulation point, beech forest and a 360-degree view across Aizu.

  • Mt. Ojiro

    尾白山

    #14 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Sacred symbol of the Ina district, once crowned by a shrine for rain prayers; beech forest and views over the Ina River.

  • Mount Nanatsugatake

    七ヶ岳

    #15 ranked in Minamiaizu

  • Mount Saito

    斎藤山

    #16 ranked in Minamiaizu

    The symbolic mountain of the Nagano district of Minamiaizu.

  • Aizu Tajima Asparagus

    会津田島アスパラ

    #17 ranked in Minamiaizu

    At about 550 m, big day-night temperature swings make Tajima asparagus juicy, sweet and crisp.

  • Nango Tomatoes

    南郷トマト

    #18 ranked in Minamiaizu

    Firm tomatoes with well-balanced acidity and sweetness.

  • Aizu Kogen Onsen Yume no Yu

    木賊温泉　岩風呂

    Named for the horsetail plants that grew here; found about 1,000 years ago and called the hidden spa of Aizu.

  • Hisakawa Castle Ruins

    久川城跡

  • Shigiyama Castle Ruins

  • Takatsue Buckwheat Fields

    高清水自然公園　ひめさゆり群生地

    #37 ranked in Minamiaizu

    A million wild himesayuri lilies on 7 ha at 850 m, said to be Japan's largest colony.

  • Nagata Embankment Cherries

    永田の堰堤桜

    Said to be the finest cherry avenue in Minamiaizu.
