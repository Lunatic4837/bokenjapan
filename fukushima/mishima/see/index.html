Sights in Mishima, Fukushima

Mishima · Fukushima · 6 places

See

  • Obayashi Furusato no Yama

    大林ふるさとの山

    #2 ranked in Mishima

    One of the prefecture's largest dogtooth-violet colonies, flowering as the snow melts.

  • Aizu Paulownia Chests

    総桐箪笥

    #3 ranked in Mishima

    Minamiaizu is a source of Aizu paulownia wood, prized as Japan's finest for chests.

  • Mishima Life Craft Center (Okuaizu Basketry)

    三島町生活工芸館（奥会津編み組細工）

    #4 ranked in Mishima

    Baskets woven from hiroro grass, wild grape and silvervine, a winter handicraft passed down for daily use.

  • Michi-no-Eki Oze Kaido Mishima-juku

    道の駅尾瀬街道みしま宿

    #5 ranked in Mishima

    The first rest stop at the gateway to Okuaizu.

  • First Tadami River Bridge Viewpoint

    第一只見川橋梁ビューポイント

    #7 ranked in Mishima

    The most iconic view of the JR Tadami Line, famous for autumn leaves.

  • Mount Shizukura

    志津倉山

    #8 ranked in Mishima

    A sacred mountain long worshipped for rain-praying.
