Sights in Motomiya, Fukushima

Motomiya · Fukushima · 7 places

See

  • Takamatsuyama Kannonji Temple

    高松山観音寺

    #1 ranked in Motomiya

    Mount Takamatsu, where priest Tokuitsu is said to have carved and enshrined a Buddha in 807.

  • Prince William's Park English Garden

    プリンス・ウィリアムズ・パーク/英国庭園

    #2 ranked in Motomiya

    An English garden built after Prince William's 2015 visit as a symbol of friendship and recovery, with roses and seasonal flowers.

  • Great Cherry of Shionosaki

    塩ノ崎の大桜

    #4 ranked in Motomiya

    A 600-year-old Edo higan cherry on a hilltop, about 18 m tall with a 7 m trunk.

  • Iwatsunosan Iwatsunoji Temple

    岩角山岩角寺

    #5 ranked in Motomiya

    A scenic mountain designated a prefectural natural monument, home to a temple founded in 851.

  • Weeping Cherry of Jonouchi Battlefield

    城ノ内古戦場の枝垂桜

    #6 ranked in Motomiya

    A 250-year-old weeping cherry said to be an offspring of the Miharu Takizakura.

  • Mizuiro Park

    みずいろ公園

    #7 ranked in Motomiya

    A waterside park with play equipment where the Adatara and Abukuma rivers meet.

  • Red Weeping Cherry of Nichirinji

    日輪寺の紅枝垂桜

    #8 ranked in Motomiya

    A giant twin-trunk tree whose history is carved on a stone lantern at the Suwa family grave.
