See

  • Jouroku Park

    丈六公園

    #1 ranked in Namie

    Known for cherries and azaleas, crowded with visitors in bloom season.

  • Lucky Park in Namie

    ラッキー公園 in なみえまち

    #2 ranked in Namie

    Japan's first park themed on Chansey (Lucky), Fukushima's support Pokemon, opened at Michi-no-Eki Namie in 2021.

    震災遺構浪江町立請戸小学校

    #3 ranked in Namie

    A school severely damaged by the 11 March 2011 earthquake, tsunami and nuclear accident.

  • Benifusa Cherries at Namie Town Hall

    浪江町役場の紅房桜

    #4 ranked in Namie

    Deep pink blossoms that hang downward like bells.

  • Ohirayama Cemetery (Community Plaza)

    大平山霊園（コミュニティ広場）

    #5 ranked in Namie

    Site of a memorial to the victims of the Great East Japan Earthquake.

  • Ikoi no Mura Namie

    いこいの村なみえ

    #6 ranked in Namie

    Lodging with a large bath and karaoke rooms.

  • Ukedo Port New Year Ceremony

    請戸漁港出初式

    #8 ranked in Namie

    About 30 fishing boats gather at Ukedo port to pray for safety and good catches.

  • Ukedo River Line

    請戸川リバーライン

    #10 ranked in Namie

    About 120 Somei-Yoshino cherries along 1.5 km of the Ukedo River bank.

  • Kabocha Manju

    かぼちゃ饅頭

    #11 ranked in Namie

    Pumpkin bun from the Tsushima district made with Namie's Kujuri pumpkins.

  • Shirasu Whitebait

    しらす

    #12 ranked in Namie

    Whitebait landed at Ukedo, the town's only fishing port, a Namie specialty.

  • Namie Yakisoba

    なみえ焼そば

    #14 ranked in Namie

    Thick noodles with only pork and bean sprouts, coated in a rich special sauce.

  • Obori Soma Ware

    大堀相馬焼

    #15 ranked in Namie

    A national traditional craft born in the Genroku era and preserved in the Obori district.

  • Anba Festival

    安波祭

    #17 ranked in Namie

    Festival of Kusano Shrine, whose main hall was swept away by the tsunami.
