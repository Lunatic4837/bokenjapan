Sights in Nishigo, Fukushima

Nishigo · Fukushima · 18 places

See

  • Horikawa Dam

    堀川ダム

    #1 ranked in Nishigo

    A multipurpose rockfill dam completed in 2000 in Nishigo.

  • Weeping Cherry of Ensho Jizo

    延生地蔵のしだれ桜

    #2 ranked in Nishigo

    A Jizo invited from Tochigi in 1851, worshipped for safe childbirth and child-rearing.

  • Daijo Cherry Avenue

    台上地区の桜並木

    #3 ranked in Nishigo

    500 Somei Yoshino and 100 Oyamazakura line the 3 km Daijo road.

  • Takasuke Weeping Cherry

    西郷村高助地区のしだれ桜

    #4 ranked in Nishigo

    Lone weeping cherry standing in the middle of rice fields in Nishigo.

  • Dogtooth Violets of Kashi

    甲子地区のカタクリ

    #5 ranked in Nishigo

    Colonies along Route 289 in the Kashi district.

  • Nasu Shirakawa Forest Springs

    那須白河フォレストスプリングス

    #6 ranked in Nishigo

    Nature fishing grounds, among the largest in Tohoku.

  • Yukiwari Bridge

    雪割橋

    #7 ranked in Nishigo

    Steel bridge about 60 m above the Abukuma gorge, famous for autumn colours.

  • Rakuokei Gorge

    楽翁渓

    #8 ranked in Nishigo

    Named because lord Matsudaira Sadanobu praised the gorge's beauty.

  • Shin-Kashi Promenade

    新甲子遊歩道

    #9 ranked in Nishigo

    A riverside walk by the Abukuma's source, listening to the stream.

  • Nishigo History and Folklore Museum

    西郷村歴史民俗資料館

    #10 ranked in Nishigo

    Opened in 1992 in the former army remount office, a village cultural property.

  • Cherries of Horikawa Waterside Park

    堀川親水公園の桜

    #11 ranked in Nishigo

    About 700 cherry trees around the park and the neighboring welfare facility.

  • Kashi Bridge

    甲子大橋

    #12 ranked in Nishigo

    A bridge at 1,000 m on the Kashi Road (Route 289) linking southern Fukushima and Aizu.

  • Kashi Onsen

    甲子温泉

    #14 ranked in Nishigo

    Named for being found in a kinoe-ne (kashi) year more than 600 years ago.

  • Mount Akazura

    赤面山

    #17 ranked in Nishigo

    A 1,701 m mountain east of the Nasu range.

  • First Torii of Nasu Gongen

    那須権現一之鳥居

    #19 ranked in Nishigo

    Nasu Gongen sits on Chausudake and was widely worshipped as a mountain deity.

  • Tadogairi Skunk Cabbage Habitat

    田土ケ入水芭蕉自生地

    #20 ranked in Nishigo

    A remnant of once-plentiful colonies reduced by rice fields and housing.

  • Japanese Yew of Kabuto Shrine

    甲神社のキャラボク

    #21 ranked in Nishigo

    Named for the helmet Minamoto no Yoshiie offered here in 1056 praying for victory.

  • Tsurugi Katsura

    剣桂

    #22 ranked in Nishigo

    A twin-trunk katsura tree over 300 years old and 35 m tall.
