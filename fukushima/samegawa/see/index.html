Sights in Samegawa, Fukushima

Samegawa · Fukushima · 22 places

See

  • Mametachi Gozen

    まめ達御膳

    #1 ranked in Samegawa

    A set meal rich in Samegawa soybeans and seasonal vegetables, served only at Temamekan.

  • Weeping Cherry of Shimizubata

    清水端のしだれ桜

    #2 ranked in Samegawa

    A 150-year-old weeping cherry with a gazebo nearby.

  • Mount Asahi

    朝日山

    #3 ranked in Samegawa

    The village's highest peak at 796.9 m, on the Iwaki border.

  • Kanodaira Cross-Country Course

    鹿角平クロスカントリーコース

    #4 ranked in Samegawa

    A 2.5 km, 3 m-wide course in Kanodaira's nature, ideal for all-round fitness.

  • Temame Cafe

    手まめカフェ

    #5 ranked in Samegawa

    Handmade natural-yeast bread and cakes with good coffee.

  • Great Maple of Ochiai

    落合の大もみじ

    #7 ranked in Samegawa

    The symbol tree of Ochiai, watching over the district for generations.

  • Higan Cherries of Eryuda

    江竜田のひがん桜

    #8 ranked in Samegawa

    Three weeping cherries standing together, symbol of the Eryuda district.

  • Higan Cherry of Nidanda

    二段田のひがん桜

    #9 ranked in Samegawa

    Said to have been planted around 1652 when a shared cemetery was made, over 350 years old.

  • Weeping Cherry of Kanzawa Jizo

    官沢の地蔵様のしだれ桜

    #10 ranked in Samegawa

    Thought to have been planted around 1799, when the neighboring Jizo hall was built.

  • Weeping Cherry of Choonji

    長遠寺のしだれ桜

    #11 ranked in Samegawa

    A cherry over 200 years old in the grounds of a temple founded in 1532.

  • Hot House Samegawa

    ほっとはうす・さめがわ

    #12 ranked in Samegawa

    A quiet spot among woods, rice fields and streams for a relaxed stay.

  • Kanodaira Observatory

    鹿角平天文台

    #13 ranked in Samegawa

    An observatory at 700 m on a grassy hill.

  • Tengu Bridge

    天狗橋

    #14 ranked in Samegawa

    A stone bridge said to have been built by a tengu, surrounded by wildflowers.

  • Weeping Cherry of Hiuchiishi

    火打石のしだれ桜

    #15 ranked in Samegawa

    A huge weeping cherry said to be over 400 years old.

  • Eryuda Falls

    江竜田の滝

    #16 ranked in Samegawa

    Six falls of various sizes in the gorge where the Watase and Oto-Nakagawa rivers meet.

  • Kowataki Rapids

    強滝

    #17 ranked in Samegawa

    Samegawa's best-known sight: 2 km of rapids along Route 349 sculpted by rock and water.

  • Kanodaira Tourist Ranch

    鹿角平観光牧場

    #18 ranked in Samegawa

    A grassland ranch with barbecue house, bungalows, observatory and cross-country course.

  • Tateyama Park

    舘山公園

    #19 ranked in Samegawa

    Site of Akasaka-date, said to have been built by the Akasaka clan in the 14th to 16th centuries.

  • Samegawa Farm Products Center Temamekan

    鮫川村農産物加工・直売所「手まめ館」

    #21 ranked in Samegawa

    Soy sauce, miso, tofu and kinako made from Samegawa soybeans, plus fresh vegetables.

  • Sanno no Sato

    鮫川村農村体験交流施設「山王の里」

    #22 ranked in Samegawa

    A self-catering lodge in the converted former Watase nursery school.

  • Tomita Yakushido

    富田薬師堂

    #23 ranked in Samegawa

    A hall built before 1668 housing a standing Yakushi and guardian kings, prefectural cultural properties.

  • Watase Famine Relief Monument

    渡瀬村救荒修祭記碑

    #24 ranked in Samegawa

    Recalls the 1783 famine when long rains devastated the cold highland village.
