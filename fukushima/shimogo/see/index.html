Sights in Shimogo, Fukushima

Shimogo · Fukushima · 31 places

See

  • Roadside Station Shimogo

    道の駅しもごう

    #1 ranked in Shimogo

    Roadside station on Route 289 with mountain views over Minamiaizu, food and local products.

  • To no Hetsuri

    塔のへつり

    #2 ranked in Shimogo

    The finest scenery on the Okawa Line, with strange rocks standing like towers.

  • Sarugaku Plateau Buckwheat Fields

    猿楽台地のそば畑

    #3 ranked in Shimogo

    About 40 ha of buckwheat fields at 600 to 700 m that burst into white bloom.

  • Yunokami Onsen Station

    湯野上温泉駅

    #4 ranked in Shimogo

    A rare thatched-roof station on the Aizu Railway.

  • Mountain Cherries of Toaka

    戸赤のやまざくら

    #5 ranked in Shimogo

    Oyamazakura cherries carefully protected by locals as boundary trees since the Meiji era.

  • Kagaminuma Pond

    鏡ケ沼

    #6 ranked in Shimogo

    A 3 ha hand-mirror-shaped pond west of Mount Sudate.

  • Higurashi Falls

    日暮の滝

    #7 ranked in Shimogo

    Falls on the upper Katoyagawa in two drops of about 40 m and 15.6 m.

  • Onogawa Gorge

    小野川渓谷

    大川ダム西公園

    #13 ranked in Shimogo
