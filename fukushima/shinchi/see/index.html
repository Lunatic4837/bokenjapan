Sights in Shinchi, Fukushima

Shinchi · Fukushima · 22 places

See

  • Shinchi Garland Maaru Garden

    新地Garland まぁるの庭

    #1 ranked in Shinchi

    About 200 kinds of roses plus seasonal trees and flowers.

  • Shinchi Pump Track

    しんちパンプトラック

    #2 ranked in Shinchi

    A loop course with wave-like bumps of various sizes.

  • Tsurishi Disaster Prevention Green Park

    釣師防災緑地公園

    #3 ranked in Shinchi

    A park themed on disaster mitigation, exchange and earthquake archives.

  • Shinchi Sea Fishing Park

    新地町海釣り公園

    #4 ranked in Shinchi

    Reopened in 2019 for safe, easy fishing for anglers and families.

  • Shinchi General Park

    新地町総合公園

    #5 ranked in Shinchi

    A gym, baseball field, track and tennis courts, plus a children's forest with play equipment.

  • Soma Regional Development Memorial Green

    相馬地域開発記念緑地

    #6 ranked in Shinchi

    Aquatic plant garden, walking paths and a free barbecue area (reservation required).

  • Waku Waku Land

    わくわくランド

    #7 ranked in Shinchi

    A facility on energy and the environment with lawns and sea breezes.

  • Yakuso no Mori Shinchi Park Golf Course

    やく草の森 新地パークゴルフ場

    #8 ranked in Shinchi

    A strategic 18-hole park golf course with night play year-round (reservation required).

  • Shinchi Local Market Agri-ya

    しんち地場産市場 あぐりや

    #9 ranked in Shinchi

    Fresh produce, flowers and goods mostly from Shinchi, including fig and strawberry ice cream.

  • Fukuda Suwa Shrine

    福田諏訪神社

    #10 ranked in Shinchi

    Preserves the Fukuda Twelve Kagura, a prefectural intangible folk cultural property.

  • Kurume Gasuri House

    くるめがすりの家

    #11 ranked in Shinchi

    Built in 1931 by Shinchi-born architect Endo Arata, a disciple of Frank Lloyd Wright.

  • Komagamine Castle Ruins

    駒ヶ嶺城跡

    #12 ranked in Shinchi

    Built by the Soma clan in the 1570s; taken by the Date in 1589 as a border castle.

  • Mayumi Shimizu Spring

    真弓清水

    #13 ranked in Shinchi

    One of Shinchi's three springs, with two outlets of different water quality.

  • Ippai Shimizu Spring

    いっぱい清水

    #14 ranked in Shinchi

    One of Shinchi's three springs, once used for the local Karosan sake.

  • Ginkgo of Shirahata

    白幡のいちょう

    #15 ranked in Shinchi

    A prefectural natural monument about 260 years old.

  • Ukon Shimizu Spring

    右近清水

    #17 ranked in Shinchi

    One of Japan's 100 Heisei-era famous waters, named after Date Masamune's grandson Ito Ukon.

  • Anko Jizo

    あんこ地蔵

    #18 ranked in Shinchi

    A Jizo whose mouth is smeared with sweet bean paste each August in memory of priest Kazan.

  • Mount Karo

    鹿狼山

    #19 ranked in Shinchi

    A 430 m peak on the Miyagi border, Shinchi's symbol, with five gentle trails.

  • Shinchi Shell Mound and Tenaga Myojin Shrine Site

    新地貝塚附手長明神社跡

    #20 ranked in Shinchi

    A Late Jomon shell mound tied to the legend of the giant Tenaga Myojin.

  • Karo no Yu

    鹿狼の湯

    #21 ranked in Shinchi

    A single inn at the foot of Mount Karo, in business since the early Meiji era.

  • Sangaiji Shell Mound

    三貫地貝塚

    #22 ranked in Shinchi

    One of Tohoku's leading shell mounds, which yielded over 100 human skeletons from the Late to Final Jomon.

  • Komine Shrine

    奥の相善宮　子眉嶺神社　

    #24 ranked in Shinchi

    A shrine founded in 702 and listed in the Engishiki, long revered as a guardian of horses.
