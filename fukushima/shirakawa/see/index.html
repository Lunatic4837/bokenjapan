Sights in Shirakawa, Fukushima

Shirakawa · Fukushima · 5 places

See

  • Komine Castle History Museum

    小峰城歴史館

    #1 ranked in Shirakawa

    A guidance facility for Komine Castle, opened in April 2019.

  • Nanko Dango

    南湖だんご

    #4 ranked in Shirakawa

    Dumplings said to have been served to workers by lord Matsudaira Sadanobu when Nanko Park was built.

  • Shirakawa Ramen

    白河ラーメン

    #5 ranked in Shirakawa

    Famous among ramen fans for soup made from whole chicken, chicken and pork bones with flat curly noodles.

  • Shirakawa Daruma

    白河だるま

    #6 ranked in Shirakawa

    Said to be modeled on paintings by Tani Buncho, official artist to lord Matsudaira Sadanobu.

  • Mount Gontakura

    権太倉山

    #7 ranked in Shirakawa

    Shirakawa's highest peak, one of Fukushima's 100 beautiful mountains, with views of the Nasu range, Lake Hatori and Mount Bandai.
