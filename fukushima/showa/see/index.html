Sights in Showa, Fukushima

Showa · Fukushima · 21 places

See

  • Dogtooth Violets of Oashi

    大芦地区のカタクリ

    #1 ranked in Showa

    Quiet colonies of small, pale purple flowers in the Oashi district.

  • Karamushi Ramen

    からむしラーメン

    #2 ranked in Showa

    Noodles kneaded with leaves of karamushi, the ramie plant used for Showa's famous weaving.

  • Yanohara Plateau Soba

    矢ノ原高原そば

    #3 ranked in Showa

    Fields at about 700 m where big day-night temperature swings grow tasty crops.

  • Baby's Breath

    カスミソウ

    #4 ranked in Showa

    A Mediterranean flower that thrives in Showa's cool summers.

  • Karamushi Weaving

    からむし織

    #5 ranked in Showa

    Cloth woven from karamushi, a perennial of the nettle family also called ramie.

  • Daikan Shimizu Spring

    代官清水

    #6 ranked in Showa

    A spring so delicious that a shogunate inspector ordered the magistrate to protect it.

  • Mountain Stream Fishing

    渓流釣り

    #7 ranked in Showa

    The clear Nojiri River and its tributaries along Route 400 are a mecca for sweetfish angling.

  • Tsunagi Gorge

    綱木渓谷

    #8 ranked in Showa

    A gorge of the Nojiri River, a Tadami tributary sourced in Oze, seen from Route 400.

  • Mount Hakase

    博士山

    #9 ranked in Showa

    The name is said to come from samurai who climbed the ridge wearing their swords.

  • Tamagawa Gorge

    玉川渓谷

    #10 ranked in Showa

    An 18 km gorge sourced from the Komado wetlands, on the Shin-Toriipass road (Route 401) to Nango.

  • Sakura Kannonji Temple

    慈眼山観音寺（佐倉観音寺）

    #12 ranked in Showa

    Seventh temple of the Okurairi 33 Kannon pilgrimage, founded in 1616 as a branch of Shoboji.

  • Michi-no-Eki Karamushi-ori no Sato Showa

    道の駅 からむし織の里 しょうわ

    #14 ranked in Showa

    Seasonal mountain produce delivered by careful village hands.

  • Kuimaru School Exchange Center

    交流・観光拠点施設「喰丸小」

    #15 ranked in Showa

    A two-story wooden school built in 1937.

  • Reiko Sacred Spring

    冷湖の霊泉

    #16 ranked in Showa

    A pure spring tied to a legend of a tengu who revealed water in a village head's dream during a drought.

  • Kasuga Shrine Cherry Blossoms

    春日神社の桜

    #17 ranked in Showa

    Hilltop shrine in Nakamukai with cherry blossoms and a view over the hamlet.

  • Tohachi Falls

    藤八の滝

    #18 ranked in Showa

    Near the site of a poor woodturners' settlement that made bowls and trays from mountain timber.

  • Skunk Cabbage and Birch Forest

    水芭蕉としらかばの杜

    #19 ranked in Showa

    A natural birch forest on Route 401 below Mount Hakase, for walks to birdsong.

  • Yanohara Wetland

    矢ノ原湿原

    #20 ranked in Showa

    A village natural monument since 1973 and prefectural nature conservation area since 1976.

  • Pheasant's Eye

    福寿草

    #21 ranked in Showa

    Yellow pheasant's-eye flowers cover a roadside slope in Shimonakatsugawa near the Yamazaki bus stop.

  • Komado Wetlands (Showa)

    駒止湿原（昭和村）

    #22 ranked in Showa

    A wetland straddling Minamiaizu and Showa; 148 ha with surrounding forest is a national natural monument.

  • Mount Gozengatake

    御前ヶ岳

    #23 ranked in Showa

    A 1,234 m mountain tied to the tragic love of Prince Mochihito and Lady Kobai.
