Sights in Sukagawa, Fukushima

Sukagawa · Fukushima · 6 places

See

  • 牡丹こけし

    Peony Kokeshi Dolls

    #2 ranked in Sukagawa

    Kokeshi painted with Sukagawa's famous peonies, first made about 100 years ago.

  • 江戸小紋

    Edo Komon Dyeing

    #3 ranked in Sukagawa

    A fine-pattern dyeing technique perfected in the Edo period, made in Sukagawa for about 100 years.

  • 須賀川絵幟

    Sukagawa Painted Banners

    #4 ranked in Sukagawa

    Began about 230 years ago with banners of Shoki painted on paper or cloth to celebrate boys' growth on Boys' Day.

  • 須賀川かっぱ麺

    Sukagawa Kappa Noodles

    #5 ranked in Sukagawa

    Noodles celebrating Sukagawa's open-field cucumbers, among the top in Japan for harvest and shipments.

  • 須賀川特撮アーカイブセンター

    Sukagawa Tokusatsu Archive Center

    #6 ranked in Sukagawa

    Honors special-effects filmmaking, founded by Sukagawa-born director Tsuburaya Eiji, the god of tokusatsu.

  • 風流のはじめ館

    Furyu no Hajime Museum

    #7 ranked in Sukagawa

    The Sukagawa Basho Memorial Museum reborn as the Furyu no Hajime Museum.
