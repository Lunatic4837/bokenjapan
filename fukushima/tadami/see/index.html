Sights in Tadami, Fukushima

Tadami · Fukushima · 22 places

See

  • Iwana no Sato

    いわなの里

    #1 ranked in Tadami

    Since 1986: a fishing pond and restaurant where you can catch char raised in clear mountain water.

  • Iyashi no Mori Forest

    癒しの森

    #2 ranked in Tadami

    A walk through oak and cedar to a plaza with the great beech on the old provincial border.

  • Vine Craft

    つる細工

    #3 ranked in Tadami

    Basketry woven from wild silvervine and akebi vines, long used daily in Tadami and now a folk craft.

  • Hitoppuro Machiyu

    ひとっぷろまち湯

    #4 ranked in Tadami

    A day-use bathhouse with a diner, loved by locals and visitors.

  • Furusato-kan Tagokura

    ふるさと館田子倉

    #6 ranked in Tadami

    A museum preserving memories of Tagokura village, submerged by the Tagokura Dam.

  • Tadami Information Center

    只見町インフォメーションセンター

    #7 ranked in Tadami

    Ask here about sights, routes, lodging and food in town.

  • Gamo Dogtooth Violet Park

    蒲生カタクリ公園

    #8 ranked in Tadami

    Dogtooth violet fields tended by Gamo villagers at the Mount Gamo trailhead.

  • Mount Yogai

    要害山

    #9 ranked in Tadami

    A steep hill of about 700 m behind JR Tadami Station.

  • Toki no Sato Yurari

    季の郷湯ら里

    #11 ranked in Tadami

    A relaxing wooden lodge surrounded by nature.

  • Mitsuishi Shrine

    三石神社

    #12 ranked in Tadami

    A matchmaking shrine halfway up Mount Yogai behind Tadami Station, worshipping a huge rock.

  • Rokujurigoe Yukiwari Road

    六十里越雪割り街道

    #13 ranked in Tadami

    A pass linking Tadami and Uonuma, with Lake Tagokura and outstanding beech forests.

  • Megumi no Mori Forest

    恵みの森

    #14 ranked in Tadami

    A stream through beech forest, lovely for stream walks among autumn leaves.

  • Lake Tagokura

    田子倉湖

    #15 ranked in Tadami

    Reservoir created by the Tagokura Dam, with fresh green, autumn colours, snow camellias and a sightseeing boat.

  • J-POWER Tadami Exhibition Hall

    J-POWER只見展示館

    #16 ranked in Tadami

    A water-themed exhibition hall by the Tadami Dam lake on Route 252.

  • Kawai Tsugunosuke Memorial Museum

    河井継之助記念館

    #17 ranked in Tadami

    Nagaoka retainer Kawai Tsugunosuke died here in 1868 after being wounded in the Boshin War.

  • Tadami Beech and River Museum

    ただみ・ブナと川のミュージアム

    #18 ranked in Tadami

    Dioramas and specimens of Tadami's beech forests and wildlife.

  • Fukasawa Onsen Murayu

    深沢温泉　むら湯

    #19 ranked in Tadami

    A bathhouse next to Yurari with abundant free-flowing spring water.

  • Tagokura Dam

    田子倉ダム

    #21 ranked in Tadami

    A dam on the Tadami River, sourced from Ozegahara, in operation since May 1959.

  • Mount Asakusa

    浅草岳

    #22 ranked in Tadami

    A mountain on the Niigata border north of Lake Tagokura.

  • Mount Gamo

    蒲生岳

    #23 ranked in Tadami

    An 828 m sharply pointed rock peak called the Matterhorn of Aizu, with exposed scrambling.

  • Mount Aizu-Asahi

    会津朝日岳

    #24 ranked in Tadami

    A 1,624 m mountain southeast of Lake Tagokura, a rugged Aizu classic.

  • Jobo-ji Kannon Hall

    成法寺観音堂

    #25 ranked in Tadami

    A fine Zen-style hall, a national Important Cultural Property valuable for medieval temple architecture.
