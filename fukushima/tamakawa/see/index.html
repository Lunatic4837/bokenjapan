Sights in Tamakawa, Fukushima

Tamakawa · Fukushima · 15 places

See

  • Blueberries

    ブルーベリー

    #2 ranked in Tamakawa

    Hand-picked, fully ripe berries, very sweet eaten fresh or on yogurt and cakes.

  • Shibori Tomatoes

    しぼりトマト

    #3 ranked in Tamakawa

    Tomatoes grown with minimal water so their own strength makes them sweet.

  • Sarunashi Fruit

    さるなし

    #4 ranked in Tamakawa

    A nutrient-rich relative of the kiwi, also called kokuwa.

  • Wakuwaku Land Tamakawa

    わくわくらんどたまかわ

    #7 ranked in Tamakawa

    Tamakawa's indoor play area inside Fukushima Airport.

  • Higashino Stream

    東野の清流

    #8 ranked in Tamakawa

    At over 500 m on the western Abukuma slopes, with fresh plateau breezes year-round.

  • Fukushima Airport

    福島空港

    #11 ranked in Tamakawa

    Opened in 1993 on hills straddling Tamakawa and Sukagawa, convenient for Nikko and Nasu.

  • Minamisugama Nenbutsu Odori

    南須釜の念仏踊り

    #13 ranked in Tamakawa

    A nationally selected folk dance handed down since the early Edo period.

  • Osho-ya Cherry

    大庄屋桜

    #14 ranked in Tamakawa

    Cherry tree in the Sugama district east of the airport, linked to a former village headman.

  • Konpira Cherry

    金毘羅桜

    #15 ranked in Tamakawa

    A 300-year-old red weeping cherry in the Naka district.

  • Michi-no-Eki Tamakawa

    道の駅たまかわ

    #16 ranked in Tamakawa

    Near Fukushima Airport, selling fresh produce and village specialties like sarunashi and shibori tomatoes.

  • Upside-Down Cedar of Kawabe Hachiman Shrine

    川辺八幡神社のさかさ杉

    #17 ranked in Tamakawa

    A 1,000-year-old cedar, 26 m tall, with twisted drooping branches; a prefectural natural monument.

  • Tofukuji Reliquary Stupa

    東福寺舎利石塔

    #18 ranked in Tamakawa

    Stone reliquary stupa in the Tofukuji grounds dated 1205.

  • Kawabe Hachiman Shrine

    川辺八幡神社

    #19 ranked in Tamakawa

    A prefectural cultural property said to have been founded in 1053 by Minamoto no Arimitsu.

  • Stone Five-Tiered Stupa

    石造五輪塔

    #20 ranked in Tamakawa

    A 180 cm national Important Cultural Property, the late-Heian grave of lord Minamoto no Motomitsu.

  • Ganpoji Founding Monument

    岩法寺開山碑

    #21 ranked in Tamakawa

    A fine 2.85 m memorial stone erected in 1318.
