Things to do in Tenei, Fukushima

Tenei · Fukushima · 6 places

Do

  • 道の駅羽鳥湖高原

    Michi-no-Eki Hatoriko Kogen

    #1 ranked in Tenei

    A roadside station stocked with Ten-ei village specialties.

  • 天栄温泉

    Ten-ei Onsen

    #3 ranked in Tenei

    A therapeutic hot spring said to date from the Meiji era, with a single facility.

  • エンゼルフォレスト白河高原

    Angel Forest Shirakawa Kogen

    #6 ranked in Tenei

    A resort on vast natural grounds with cottages and many facilities to enjoy with your dog.

  • 岩瀬湯本温泉

    Iwase Yumoto Onsen

    #13 ranked in Tenei

    A hot spring said to have been found in the early 9th century to cure Emperor Saga's illness.

  • グランディ羽鳥湖スキーリゾート

    Grandee Hatoriko Ski Resort

    #15 ranked in Tenei

    Mostly gentle slopes, so families and beginners can ski from the top with confidence.

  • 羽鳥湖畔オートキャンプ場

    Lake Hatori Auto Campground

    #16 ranked in Tenei

    A campground at 700 m inside Okawa-Hatori Prefectural Nature Park.
