Sights in Tenei, Fukushima

Tenei · Fukushima · 19 places

See

  • British Hills

    ブリティッシュヒルズ

    #2 ranked in Tenei

    A lodging modeled on British culture of the 12th to 18th centuries, with authentic afternoon tea in its Ascot Tea Room.

  • Matsuzaki Brewery

    松崎酒造

    #4 ranked in Tenei

    Brewer of Hirotogawa Daiginjo, a sake with fresh aroma and crisp finish.

  • Suzunoi Brewery

    寿々乃井酒造店

    #5 ranked in Tenei

    Brews sake with spring water from the hill behind and the local climate.

  • Kobayashi Family Weeping Cherry

    小林家のしだれ桜

    #7 ranked in Tenei

    Weeping cherry about 270 years old, the first to bloom in Ten-ei village.

  • Route 294 Cherry Avenue

    国道２９４号の桜並木

    #8 ranked in Tenei

    About 2 km of Somei Yoshino cherries along Route 294.

  • Weeping Cherry of Daihoji

    大方寺のしだれ桜

    #9 ranked in Tenei

    Towering weeping cherry, with a yew estimated at 350 years old in the same temple grounds.

  • Lake Hatori

    羽鳥湖

    #10 ranked in Tenei

    A man-made lake at 600 m on the Pacific-Sea of Japan divide, 16 km around and up to 31.2 m deep.

  • Autumn Leaves of Lake Hatori

    羽鳥湖の紅葉

    #11 ranked in Tenei

    When autumn wind stirs the pampas grass, colored leaves brighten Lake Hatori.

  • Futamata Onsen

    二岐温泉

    #12 ranked in Tenei

    In a gorge at 800 m at the foot of Mount Futamata, with open-air baths amid virgin beech forest.

  • Ski Resort Ten-ei

    スキーリゾート天栄

    #14 ranked in Tenei

    Forest courses averaging 10 degrees, ideal for beginners.

  • Mount Futamata

    二岐山

    #17 ranked in Tenei

    Two peaks, Otoko-dake and Onna-dake, rise side by side.

  • Ryugazuka Kofun

    龍ヶ塚古墳

    #18 ranked in Tenei

    A keyhole-shaped tomb built about 1,400 years ago.

  • Manganji Temple

    満願寺

    #19 ranked in Tenei

    A Shingon Chisan-school temple thought to be of ancient foundation.

  • Itagoya Site

    板小屋遺跡

    #20 ranked in Tenei

    Remains of a woodturners' settlement that lasted about 160 years in the Edo period.

  • Yumoto Yakushido

    湯本薬師堂

    #21 ranked in Tenei

    In Manganji's grounds, said to enshrine a Yakushi Buddha placed by Kobo Daishi in 821 to pray for health.

  • Yusen Hachiman Shrine

    湯泉八幡神社

    #22 ranked in Tenei

    Records say it began when Hachiman appeared in the dream of the gravely ill Emperor Saga in 818.

  • Osato Castle (Ushigajo) Ruins

    大里城（牛ヶ城）跡

    #23 ranked in Tenei

    A mountain castle built about 500 years ago in the Muromachi period.

  • Onabe Shrine

    御鍋神社

    #24 ranked in Tenei

    About 1,000 years ago Princess Kikyo, wife of the defeated Taira no Masakado, is said to have hidden here.

  • Weeping Cherry of Kisshoin

    吉祥院のしだれ桜

    #25 ranked in Tenei

    In the grounds of Kisshoin, a Shingon temple founded in 1559.
