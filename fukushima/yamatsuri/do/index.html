Things to do in Yamatsuri, Fukushima

Yamatsuri · Fukushima · 5 places

Do

  • Yuzu Sherbet

    ゆずシャーベット

    #2 ranked in Yamatsuri

    Made with 100% fresh Yamatsuri-grown yuzu.

  • Higashidate Onsen Yupal Yamatsuri

    東舘温泉ユーパルやまつり

    #3 ranked in Yamatsuri

    Hot spring in the centre of town, the inner retreat of Okukuji Prefectural Natural Park.

  • Yazawa Sake Brewery

    矢澤酒造店

    #5 ranked in Yamatsuri

    Brewery founded in 1833 using Kuji River underground water and traditional methods.

  • Ayu Suspension Bridge

    あゆのつり橋

    #8 ranked in Yamatsuri

    A red bridge 62 m long near Yamatsuriyama Station, a long-loved town symbol.

  • Saigyo Poem Monument

    西行の歌碑

    #12 ranked in Yamatsuri

    A stone carved with Saigyo's poem praising Yamatsuriyama's autumn scenery.
