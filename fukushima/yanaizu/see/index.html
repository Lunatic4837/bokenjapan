Sights in Yanaizu, Fukushima

Yanaizu · Fukushima · 14 places

See

  • Aizu-Yanaizu Station

    東北の駅百選「会津柳津駅」

    #1 ranked in Yanaizu

    One of Tohoku's 100 stations, with an akabeko painting workshop and a cafe.

  • Yanaizu-Nishiyama Geothermal Power Station

    柳津西山地熱発電所

    #3 ranked in Yanaizu

    Drills magma-heated steam from kilometers underground to drive turbines.

  • Mount Iiya

    飯谷山

    #4 ranked in Yanaizu

    A 783 m mountain in Tokorozawa for beginners to intermediate hikers.

  • Waterfall in Yanaizu

    つむじ倉滝（ツムジクラ滝）

    Romaji: Tsumujikura Falls

    #5 ranked in Yanaizu

    The biggest falls on Mount Hakase, in two drops of 25 m and 60 m.

  • Yanaizu-Nishiyama Geothermal PR Center

    柳津西山地熱発電所PR館

    #6 ranked in Yanaizu

    Next to Japan's highest-output geothermal plant, which uses magma heat.

  • Saito Kiyoshi Art Museum

    やないづ町立斎藤清美術館

    #7 ranked in Yanaizu

    Works by world-renowned printmaker and honorary townsman Saito Kiyoshi (died 1997).

  • Yanaizu Onsen

    柳津温泉

    #11 ranked in Yanaizu

    A temple town around Enzoji whose pilgrim lodgings became the largest spa town on the Tadami River.

  • Nishiyama Onsen

    西山温泉

    #12 ranked in Yanaizu

    A hot-spring village on the Takiya River beside Japan's top geothermal plant, among birdsong.

  • Mount Hakase

    博士山

    #14 ranked in Yanaizu

    Yanaizu's highest peak, straddling Yanaizu, Showa and Aizumisato.

  • Tsukimigaoka Community Center

    つきみが丘町民センター

    #15 ranked in Yanaizu

    On high ground with views of Enzoji to the east and the Tadami River to the west.

  • Fukuman Kokuzo Bosatsu Enzoji

    福満虚空藏菩薩圓藏寺

    #16 ranked in Yanaizu

    Founded by Tokuitsu in 807; its principal image is said to be carved by Kobo Daishi.

  • Takehisa Yumeji Romance Monument

    竹久夢二ロマン記念碑

    #17 ranked in Yanaizu

    On the Tadami River promenade near Kiyohime Park.

  • Kubota Thirty-Three Kannon

    久保田三十三観音

    #18 ranked in Yanaizu

    Thirty-three Kannon statues line a 300 m loop path on 501 m Kannon hill.

  • Okunoin Bentendo

    奥之院弁天堂

    #19 ranked in Yanaizu

    A thatched mid-Muromachi hall south of Enzoji, a national Important Cultural Property.
