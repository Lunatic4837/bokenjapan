Things to do in Ena, Gifu

Ena, Gifu: things to do, food and stays · Gifu · 13 places

Do

  • Ena-kyo Cruise

    Ena Gorge Cruise

    #2 ranked in Ena

    30-minute high-speed jet-boat cruise through the gorge departing from the dock beyond the souvenir path.

  • Ena Skate Rink

    Gifu Prefecture Crystal Park Ena Skating Rink

    #9 ranked in Ena

    Inline skating and futsal can be enjoyed from spring to autumn, and speed skating and public skating in winter.

  • Ena-kyo Sightseeing Boat Birdwatching

    Ena Gorge Sightseeing Boat Birdwatching

    #23 ranked in Ena

    Sightseeing boats cruise Ena Gorge in Ena, Gifu, with window views of strange rocks and a vast seasonal panorama.

  • Hara Farm

    Little Farm Hara Farm

    #36 ranked in Ena

    Visitors may eat their fill of rich, ripe strawberries grown in Iwamura castle town, such as Benihoppe and Mino Musume.

  • KASAGI BASE CAMP

    Kasagi Base Camp

    #39 ranked in Ena

    A long-loved campground at 1,000 meters on Mt. Kasagi is being reborn as a place to gather and play.

  • Sasayuri-no-yu

    Sasayuri no Yu

    #41 ranked in Ena

    A day-trip onsen on a 464-meter summit, proud of open-air baths overlooking magnificent nature.

  • Hourakuen

    Horakuen

    #46 ranked in Ena

    Fruit orchard in Iwamura where visitors can pick grapes, pears and apples in season.

  • Ishikawa Farm

    Ishikawa Farm

    #56 ranked in Ena

    This Kamiyahagi farm grows tomatoes and strawberries and offers 45-minute all-you-can-eat picking, with parfait-making and nine tomato varieties.

  • Akechi Ringo Park

    Akechi Apple Park

    #78 ranked in Ena

    Hilltop orchard in Taisho-mura, Akechi-cho, Ena, growing apples with plenty of organic fertilizer and offering a tree-owner program.

  • Kasagiyama Climbing Area

    Kasagiyama Climbing Area

    #81 ranked in Ena

    A bouldering and route-climbing rock area in Ena, Gifu, and the official site of the Kasagiyama Climbing Association.

  • Meshi Chi Kogen Shizen Tento Village

    Iiji Kogen Nature Tent Village

    #86 ranked in Ena

    A lesser-known campsite for first-time family or solo camping, managed by a relocated resident.

  • B.C.G.E Bushcraft Gifu Ena

    Bushcraft Gifu Ena

    #89 ranked in Ena

    Many bushcraft fields in Ena, Gifu, for beginners through experienced participants.

  • Shirataka Campground

    Shirataka Campground

    #90 ranked in Ena

    Varied campsites include an open group and family site by an old house on a hill overlooking a basin village.
