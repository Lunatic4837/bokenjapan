  • Iwamura Castle Ruins

  • Nakasendo Hiroshige Museum of Art

    Museum in Ena dedicated to ukiyo-e artist Utagawa Hiroshige and his successors.

  • TOP

    Roadside station in Ena offering local produce, crafts, and lodging options such as Mongolian tents and cottages.

  • Kamiyahagi La Foret Fukuju no Sato

    Roadside station in Ena selling local vegetables, rice, and handmade souvenirs with a cafeteria serving set meals.

    #19 ranked in Ena

    A history museum on the Iwamura domain lord's former residence, at the foot of Iwamura Castle ruins.

  • 岐阜の複合施設なら恵那 銀の森

    #20 ranked in Ena

    This complex, surrounded by seasonal nature, offers tasty experiences.

  • Benkei Cedar

    Benkei Cedar

    #21 ranked in Ena

    In the Tenpyo era, Todaiji high priest Roben climbed the mountain, recited the Lotus Sutra, and founded Kakurinbo.

  • Enakyo Sazanami Plaza Cherry Blossoms

    Ena Gorge Sazanami Plaza Cherry Blossoms

    #26 ranked in Ena

    During bloom, Ena Gorge Park's Sazanami Plaza is lit from sunset until 10 p.m., best viewed early to mid-April.

  • Ryugo-ji Temple

    Ryugoji Temple

    #27 ranked in Ena

    Ryugoji, family temple of the Akechi Toyama clan, has Akechi Mitsuhide's memorial tower, split by a large diagonal crack.

  • Ochiai Fort (Senjojiki Park)

    Ochiai Fort (Senjojiki Park)

    #28 ranked in Ena

    Ochiai Fort, said to predate Akechi Castle built in 1247, is now Senjojiki Park with good views from its outwork.

  • Grave of Omaki no Kata

    Grave of Lady Omaki

    #29 ranked in Ena

    Grave of Omaki no Kata, Akechi Mitsuhide's mother, offered as a hostage when Yakami Castle would not fall in 1575.

  • Tengu Shrine and Mitsuhide Study Site

    Lord Akechi Mitsuhide's Study Hall and Tenjin Shrine

    #30 ranked in Ena

    In Mangahora, young Akechi Mitsuhide is said to have studied under a Tenryu-ji monk at this shrine to Sugawara no Michizane.

  • Kinheisha Hachioji Shrine

    Kinpeisha Hachioji Shrine

    #31 ranked in Ena

    Founded in the Heian period, its 1678 hall is a prefectural Important Cultural Property; the karamon was reportedly moved from Akechi Castle.

  • Akechi Castle Ruins

    Akechi Castle Ruins

    #32 ranked in Ena

    A mountain castle in southern Ena, second in scale to Iwamura Castle and the Akechi Toyama clan's successive seat.

  • Sakaori Rice Terraces

    NPO Ena City Sakaori Rice Terrace Preservation Society

    #33 ranked in Ena

    This society conserves Ena's Sakaori rice terraces, selected among Japan's 100 best, and seeks to revitalize mountain farming.

  • Kori River Dam

    Oragawa Dam

    #34 ranked in Ena

    A multipurpose dam on the Ena-Mizunami border for flood control, river conservation, and power generation, with its interior open to visitors.

  • Iwamura Brewery

    Iwamura Brewery

    #35 ranked in Ena

    Sake brewery in Iwamura, Ena, offering tours and tastings of local rice wine.

  • Taisho-mura Roman-tei

    Taisho Village Romantei

    #43 ranked in Ena

    Taisho-era museum in Ena showcasing early 20th-century culture and design.

  • Former Miyake House

    Former Miyake House

    #44 ranked in Ena

    An Ena City designated cultural property, donated in 1991 and relocated and restored the following year.

  • Former Akechi Town Hall

    Nihon Taisho-mura Village Hall (Former Akechi Town Hall)

    #51 ranked in Ena

    Built in 1906, this ultra-modern two-story wooden Western-style hall served as Akechi Town Hall until 1957 and was designated a tangible cultural property in 1999.

  • Ukare Yokocho

    Chuma Kaido Ukare Alley

    #52 ranked in Ena

    Part of the Chuma Kaido, this nostalgic alley once had sake and udon shops for travelers and packhorse drivers, and some houses span the road.

  • Nakatsugawa-Shinshu Kaido Crossing

    Old Highways Crossing (Chuma Kaido and Nanboku Kaido)

    #53 ranked in Ena

    This Akechi crossing of the Chuma and Nanboku Kaido, once busy with packhorse drivers and Ise pilgrims, led toward Shiojiri, Owari, Okazaki, and the Nakasendo.

  • Maple Planted by Akechi Mitsuhide

    Lord Mitsuhide's Hand-Planted Maple

    #54 ranked in Ena

    Tradition says Lord Mitsuhide enshrined Hitomaro at Hachioji Shrine and planted this maple before the Kakinomoto no Hitomaro Shrine.

  • Ishikawa Tomato Farm

    Ishikawa Tomato Farm

    #55 ranked in Ena

    Ishikawa Tomato Farm grows tomatoes at 650 meters in Kamiyahagi using Yahagi River headwaters, and sells them by mail order.

  • Enakyo Gorge Cherry Blossoms

    Ena Gorge Cherry Blossoms

    #57 ranked in Ena

    Geographer Shiga Shigetaka named this gorge in 1920; about 200 cherry trees bloom each spring at Sazanami Plaza in Ena Gorge Park.

  • Mansho-ji Temple

    #58 ranked in Ena

    A hidden Senju Kannon said to be made by Jikaku Daishi Ennin is enshrined here and revered locally for warding off misfortune.

  • Meiji Emperor's Rest House Ooi

    #59 ranked in Ena

    The former Ito inn at Oi-juku on the Nakasendo lodged Emperor Meiji in 1880 and still preserves its rooms, bath, and toilet.

  • Kakashi Street

    Scarecrow Street

    Varied, realistic scarecrows stand near the Kiriyama bus stop in Kasagi-cho's Himeguri district, an area called Scarecrow Street.

  • Nakasendo Ooi-juku

    #62 ranked in Ena

    Historic post town on the Nakasendo, once the busiest station in Gifu Prefecture.

  • Hishiya Archives

    Typical grand townhouse of the Furuyama family, leading Oi-juku merchants and village headmen from the mid-Edo period.

  • Iwamura Basin Farmhouses

    Traditional tiled, white-walled farmhouses and storehouses scatter across the gently sloping Iwamura Basin, framed by hills and mountains.

    Late-Edo merchant house known as Matsuya, comprising two wooden two-story buildings, opened in August 2003.

  • Iwamura Castle Site Park

    Park on about two hectares of former samurai land below Iwamura Castle, with a museum, drum turret, and study hall.

  • Iibama Castle Ruins

    Ruins of a small Iwamura branch castle of the Toyama Iibama clan, said to have linked defenses with Naegi and Akechi.

  • Kimura Residence

    Imposing townhouse of the Kimura family, a former wholesaler on Iwamura castle town's main street, prosperous from mid to late Edo.

  • Tosaya

  • Kano House

  • Tozan Cherry Tree

  • Gomozaza

  • Yamaoka Station Kantenkan

  • Ichiyajo Castle Ruins

  • Okuyahagi Lake Cherry Avenue
