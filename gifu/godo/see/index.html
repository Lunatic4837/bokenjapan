  • Wajū Embankment Cherry Trees

    Waju Tsutsumi Cherry Blossoms

    #3 ranked in Godo

    Built to shield homes and paddies from floods, about 700 cherry trees now form a crowded blossom tunnel from late March to early April.

  • Hakusan Shrine

    Hakusan Shrine

    #4 ranked in Godo

    Shrine in Godo where Tokugawa Ieyasu rested during the Sekigahara campaign in 1600.

  • Gōdo Rose Park

    Godo Rose Park

    #5 ranked in Godo

    This free, 24-hour, 250-meter walking path north of Hirogodo Station has about 1,400 rose bushes by the Yoro Railway and peaks in May.

  • Zenkoin

    Zengakuin Temple

    #6 ranked in Godo

    The main hall is Fujiwara-period shinden-zukuri, and the storehouse holds many Important Cultural Properties, including Buddhist statues.
