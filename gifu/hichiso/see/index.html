Sights in Hichiso, Gifu

Hichiso, Gifu: things to do, food and stays · Gifu · 7 places

See

  • Hissui Gorge

    Hisuikyo Rock Garden

    #1 ranked in Hichiso

    Hisuikyo is a 12-kilometer Hida River gorge from Hichiso to Shirakawa, included in Hida-Kisogawa Quasi-National Park since 1964.

  • Japan's Oldest Stone Museum

    Japan's Oldest Stone Museum

    #3 ranked in Hichiso

    Displays 2-billion-year-old gneiss, Japan's oldest stone, Earth's oldest stone, and stromatolites.

  • Sorafusagari

    Sorafusagari

    #4 ranked in Hichiso

    Huge rocks crowding in from both sides nearly meet overhead, hiding the sky that gives this rugged spot its name.

  • Hissui Gorge Pot-holes

    Hisuikyo Pothole Group

    #6 ranked in Hichiso

    Potholes carved over ages by the Hida River's rapids, this group is a national Natural Monument of worldwide value.

  • Kambuchi Shrine

    Kanbuchi Shrine

    #7 ranked in Hichiso

    Said to have been founded in 770 when Susanoo-no-Mikoto's spirit was brought from Izumo, it stands near the summit.

  • Two-billion-year-old Stone Discovery Site

    Two-Billion-Year-Old Stone Discovery Site

    #8 ranked in Hichiso

    A rock once famed as Japan's oldest stone, believed formed about two billion years ago, is an important geological find.

  • Ryumon-ji Temple

    Ryumonji Temple

    #9 ranked in Hichiso

    Founded in 1308 by Neiissan Kokushi, a monk naturalized from Song China, with a gate dragon said to be by Hidari Jingoro.
