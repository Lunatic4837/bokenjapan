    An ancient weeping cherry with a very beautiful branch shape suits the temple gate and bell tower and is lit nightly in late April.

    #5 ranked in Hida

    Hundreds of nostalgic everyday glass items from the Bakumatsu through early Showa, including mame lamps and uranium glass, are displayed.

  • 黒内果樹園｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Kurouchi Orchard

    #8 ranked in Hida

    Kurouchi Orchard grows Hida apples and peaches and sells 100% apple juice, grated apple, and fruit mix pie.

  • 山田川の桜　｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Yamada River Cherry Blossoms

    #9 ranked in Hida

    A cherry avenue along the Yamada River in Kamioka is a citizens' walking course, with night blossoms lit near Shinwaso.

  • 柏原の紺屋桜｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Koya Cherry Tree in Kashiwabara

    #10 ranked in Hida

    This late-blooming 10-meter weeping cherry, over 100 years old, peaks in early May and was named for a former dye shop.

  • 慈眼寺の鎮護桜　｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Chingo Cherry Tree at Jigenji Temple

    #11 ranked in Hida

    Designated a prefectural natural monument, this two-trunk cherry at Jigenji Temple is kept short after nearly breaking under snow.

  • 神岡振興事務所の桜｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Kamioka Promotion Office Cherry Blossoms

    #12 ranked in Hida

    Early-blooming cherries bloom in front of the Kamioka Promotion Office in central Kamioka, with parking right there.

    Tomorrow's Treasure: Rice Terrace and Itakura Landscape and Mountain Village Culture

    #13 ranked in Hida

    #14 ranked in Hida

    Route 360, formerly Hida's important Etchu Nishi Kaido, follows a V-shaped valley above the Miyagawa, with giant and strange rocks.

  • 坂巻公園の桜｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Sakamaki Park Cherry Blossoms

    #15 ranked in Hida

    A citizens' relaxation spot, Sakamaki Park has a cherry-lined path around its baseball field and later purple mountain wisteria.

  • 千代の松原公園の桜　｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Chiyo no Matsubara Park Cherry Blossoms

    #16 ranked in Hida

    This small waterfront park on the clear Miyagawa's right bank has Hamaguri footbridge, where large carp and summer ayu are seen.
