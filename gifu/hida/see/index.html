Sights in Hida, Gifu

Hida, Gifu: things to do, food and stays · Gifu · 18 places

See

  • Kamioka Common Water House

    Kamioka Communal Water Houses

    #2 ranked in Hida

    Before city waterworks, residents formed associations that drew mountain and spring water and supplied it to each house.

  • Kannon Yama

    Kannon Yama

    #3 ranked in Hida

    Hill in Hida Kamioka with 33 stone Kannon statues, a large Daruma statue, and the ruins of Kasamatsu Castle.

  • Hineno Art Museum & Cafe

    Hineno Art Museum & Cafe

    #5 ranked in Hida

    A small museum in a traditional Hida Furukawa townhouse displays Japanese paintings, old Imari, Kutani ware, and maki-e.

  • Saikoji Cherry Blossoms

    Saikoji Temple Cherry Blossoms

    #6 ranked in Hida

    An ancient weeping cherry with a very beautiful branch shape suits the temple gate and bell tower and is lit nightly in late April.

  • Komma Glass Museum

    Koma Glass Art Museum

    #7 ranked in Hida

    Hundreds of nostalgic everyday glass items from the Bakumatsu through early Showa, including mame lamps and uranium glass, are displayed.

  • Kuronai Orchard

    Kurouchi Orchard

    #10 ranked in Hida

    Kurouchi Orchard grows Hida apples and peaches and sells 100% apple juice, grated apple, and fruit mix pie.

  • Yamada River Cherry Blossoms

    Yamada River Cherry Blossoms

    #11 ranked in Hida

    A cherry avenue along the Yamada River in Kamioka is a citizens' walking course, with night blossoms lit near Shinwaso.

  • Konya Cherry Blossom

    Koya Cherry Tree in Kashiwabara

    #12 ranked in Hida

    This late-blooming 10-meter weeping cherry, over 100 years old, peaks in early May and was named for a former dye shop.

  • Jigenji Chinju Cherry Blossom

    Chingo Cherry Tree at Jigenji Temple

    #13 ranked in Hida

    Designated a prefectural natural monument, this two-trunk cherry at Jigenji Temple is kept short after nearly breaking under snow.

  • Kamioka Regional Office Cherry Blossoms

    Kamioka Promotion Office Cherry Blossoms

    #14 ranked in Hida

    Early-blooming cherries bloom in front of the Kamioka Promotion Office in central Kamioka, with parking right there.

  • Tanada and Itakura Landscape

    Tomorrow's Treasure: Rice Terrace and Itakura Landscape and Mountain Village Culture

    #15 ranked in Hida

    Tanekura's rice terraces, itakura, and mountain-village culture in Miyagawa were newly certified as a Gifu Tomorrow's Treasure.

  • Etchu Nishi Kaido Hydrangea Road

    Etchu Nishi Kaido Hydrangea Road

    #16 ranked in Hida

    Route 360, formerly Hida's important Etchu Nishi Kaido, follows a V-shaped valley above the Miyagawa, with giant and strange rocks.

  • Sakamaki Park Cherry Blossoms

    Sakamaki Park Cherry Blossoms

    #17 ranked in Hida

    A citizens' relaxation spot, Sakamaki Park has a cherry-lined path around its baseball field and later purple mountain wisteria.

  • Chiyo no Matsubara Park Cherry Blossoms

    Chiyo no Matsubara Park Cherry Blossoms

    #18 ranked in Hida

    This small waterfront park on the clear Miyagawa's right bank has Hamaguri footbridge, where large carp and summer ayu are seen.

  • Rinsho-ji

    Rinshoji Temple

    #19 ranked in Hida

    Former Anegakoji family temple rebuilt in 1589 by Kanamori Arishige; a gate said to be from Mashima Castle is city-designated.

  • Fukahora Marsh

    Shindo Marsh

    #21 ranked in Hida

    Shindo Marsh and its primeval spruce, thuja, oak, horse chestnut, and beech forest require entry permission; ordinary vehicles cannot enter.

  • Hodasan Tounji

    Tounji Temple

    #23 ranked in Hida

    Soto Zen temple said to date from the Muromachi Oei era, known as the Daruma Temple with over 3,000 daruma.

  • Nakagawa Yoichi Literary Museum

    Nakagawa Yoichi Literary Materials Room

    #26 ranked in Hida

    Literary materials room for Nakagawa Yoichi, author of the 1938 bestseller Ten no Yugao, at Okuhida Yamanomura Farm in the novel's setting.
