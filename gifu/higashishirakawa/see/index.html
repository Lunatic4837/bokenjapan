Sights in Higashishirakawa, Gifu

See

  • Tsuchi no Ko Kan

    Tsuchinoko Hall

    #2 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    Museum in Higashishirakawa devoted to the Tsuchinoko, a cryptid reported in the area.

  • Ontakesan Observatory

    Mount Ontake Viewpoint

    #4 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    A prime viewpoint of Mount Ontake, with seasonal views from fresh green to snow on clear days.

  • Oguchi Fudo Waterfall

    Oguchi Fudo Falls

    #7 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    A wide, beautiful waterfall with little drop, easily reached beside Prefectural Route 62 in lush greenery.

  • Shimoshindani Fudo Falls

    Shitaoyada Fudo Falls

    #9 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    A beautiful, powerful waterfall in Shitaoyada, seen head-on from a stream path near the overlook.

  • Gosuke-no-taki Park

    Gosuke Falls Park

    #10 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    A park by Gosuke Falls, near the cave where the village's legendary phantom thief Gosuke is said to have lived.

  • Koshihara Hananoki Habitat

    Etsuhara Hananoki Habitat

    #11 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    The Etsuhara hananoki habitat is a national natural monument for a rare maple found only in parts of Gifu and Aichi.

  • Otome Falls

    Otome Falls

    #14 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    A beautiful waterfall flows over large rocks in a tree-ringed, park-like space where only the falls and brook are heard.

  • Birthplace of Shirakawa Tea

    Birthplace of Shirakawa Tea

    #15 ranked in Higashishirakawa

    Shirakawa tea reportedly began about 600 years ago when Osawa's Banryuji priest brought tea seeds from Uji and encouraged cultivation.
