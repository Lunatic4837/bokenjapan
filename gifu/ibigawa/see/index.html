Sights in Ibigawa, Gifu

Ibigawa, Gifu: things to do, food and stays · Gifu · 23 places

See

  • Tenku no Yuhodo

    Sky Promenade (Gifu's Machu Picchu)

    #1 ranked in Ibigawa

    Reputedly the Nanbokucho-period site of Toki Yoriyasu's residence and Kojima Castle, called Japan's Machu Picchu.

  • Tokuyama Dam

    Tokuyama Dam

    #2 ranked in Ibigawa

    Japan’s largest reservoir dam holding 660 million cubic metres behind a 427-metre crest.

  • Yokoji Temple

    Ryokaisan Yokokura Temple

    #3 ranked in Ibigawa

    Called the Shosoin of Mino, it houses 22 National Important Cultural Properties and Priest Myoshin's roughly 200-year-old mummy.

  • Kegonji Temple

    Tanigumisan Kegon Temple

    #5 ranked in Ibigawa

    The 33rd and final Saigoku Kannon pilgrimage temple, nicknamed Tanigumi-san, enshrines an Eleven-faced Kannon; the route is Japan Heritage.

  • Kegonji Temple Autumn Leaves

    Autumn Leaves at Tanigumisan Kegon Temple

    #6 ranked in Ibigawa

    Chosen among the Hida-Mino 33 autumn-leaf spots, it holds a maple festival, with best viewing from mid-November to early December.

  • Koi no Tsuribashi

    Koi no Suspension Bridge (Miyayama Bridge)

    #10 ranked in Ibigawa

    A swaying wooden suspension bridge with superb views, named for growing ties between people on both banks.

  • Ibigawa Gorge Autumn Leaves

    Ibikyo Autumn Leaves

    #13 ranked in Ibigawa

    Man-made lake from Nishihira Dam in Ibi-Sekigahara-Yoro Quasi-National Park, reflecting autumn mountains in jade-green water.

  • Kanmuriyama Autumn Leaves

    Kanmuriyama Autumn Leaves

    #15 ranked in Ibigawa

    Oku-Mino's only rock peak at 1,256.6 meters on the Gifu-Fukui border, selected among 33 Hida-Mino autumn foliage sites.

  • Meoto no Taki

    Fufu Falls

    #20 ranked in Ibigawa

    Named for two falls side by side, it is beautiful amid greenery, autumn leaves, and winter snow.

  • Zuiganji Temple

    Zuiganji Temple and Kojima Tongu Monument

    #23 ranked in Ibigawa

    Graves of Toki Yoriyasu and father Yorikiyo, and a monument marking Emperor Gokogen's Kojima Tongu.

  • Ibiden Toyoko-yama Power Station Cherry Blossoms

    Ibiden Toyoko Yama Power Station Cherry Blossoms

    #26 ranked in Ibigawa

    Cherry-tree lined path beside the power station near Hoshi-no-Furusato Fujihashi roadside station.

  • Asukagawa Fireflies

    Asuka River Fireflies

    #27 ranked in Ibigawa

    Fireflies illuminate the Asukagawa river from early to mid June.

  • Ukita Hideie Yukari no Chi

    Site Associated with Ukita Hideie

    #29 ranked in Ibigawa

    Site in Ibigawa where Ukita Hideie is said to have been sheltered after the Battle of Sekigahara in 1600.

  • Inaba Ittetsu's Grave

    Inaba Ittetsu no Haka Gekkei In | Kanko Spot | Gifuken Kanko Koshiki Saito Gifu no Tabi Gaido

    #31 ranked in Ibigawa

    Inaba Ittetsu, lord of Sone Castle in Mino, served the Toki, Saito, Oda, and Toyotomi houses and led the Western Mino trio.

  • Konishi Shrine

    Konishi Shrine

    #32 ranked in Ibigawa

    After his 1600 Sekigahara defeat, Konishi Yukinaga was enshrined because Nakayama feared repeated fires were his curse.

  • Katsura River Fireflies

    Katsuragawa Fireflies

    #34 ranked in Ibigawa

    Many Genji fireflies dance along Katsuragawa from early to mid-June.

  • Ohira Eight Falls

    Ohira Eight Falls

    #36 ranked in Ibigawa

    Yamaguchi of Kasuga Mitsuka discovered eight falls at the Kasukawa headwaters and named them Ohira Eight Falls.
