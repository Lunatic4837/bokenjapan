  • Kakamigahara Aerospace Museum

    Museum in Kakamigahara displaying over fifty real aircraft and spacecraft.

  • Aqua Totto Gifu

    World Freshwater Aquarium Aqua Toto Gifu

    #3 ranked in Kakamigahara

    One of the world's largest freshwater aquariums, in Kakamigahara, Gifu.

    #5 ranked in Kakamigahara

    Museum in Kakamigahara exhibiting some two thousand items on the history of medicine.

    #6 ranked in Kakamigahara

    About 70 ginkgo trees stretch 200 meters in Manabi no Mori Park and turn color together in mid to late November.

  • Sohara Nature Park

    Sohara Nature Park

    #7 ranked in Kakamigahara

    Red and white plum blossoms peak in spring; a trail runs from Ibuki Falls, and summit views are exceptional.

    Kasekiko Park

    #8 ranked in Kakamigahara

    This precious park preserves Teppo River riparian vegetation and flood traces, with paths, a viewing deck, and old levees.

  • Kawakami Villa

    #11 ranked in Kakamigahara

    Kawakami Sadayakko, known as Japan's first actress, built this 1933 villa in front of Teishoji Temple, overlooking the Kiso River.

  • Robata Ruins

    Robatake Ruins

    #12 ranked in Kakamigahara

    Excavation of this middle-to-late Jomon settlement found ten pit dwellings, pottery, and stone tools; it is now a green park.

  • Ogase Pond

    Ogase Pond

    #15 ranked in Kakamigahara

    This 2 km-circumference pond has colorful carp, long-held local faith and dragon-god legends, plus a July festival with fireworks from the center.

  • Kagamihara Natural Heritage Forest

    #18 ranked in Kakamigahara

    Forest park preserving native plants, birds and insects of the local satoyama.

  • Hinode Fudoson

    One of Mino's Three Fudo, said to originate when a traveling monk enshrined it after a divine oracle.

  • Hofuku-ji

  • Kasami Shrine
