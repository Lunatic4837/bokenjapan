    Officially Mount Atago, this 261-meter peak in Kawabe's Fukushima area is a beloved town symbol.

    Kawabe Lakeshore Promenade

    #5 ranked in Kawabe

    A 3.3 km loop from the town hall parking lot, with views of Yoneda Fuji and Yamakawa Bridge.

    Nanten Falls

    #6 ranked in Kawabe

    A straight waterfall with a drop of about 20 meters on a tributary of the Hida River.

    #7 ranked in Kawabe

    Famous for cherry blossoms, with over 500 trees blooming from late March to early April, about 20 minutes west of Nakakawabe Station.

    Myounji Temple

    #11 ranked in Kawabe

    More than a thousand Shaka Nyorai statues, donated in the Edo period, are unveiled each year on August 28.

  • 東光寺公園｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    Tokoji Park

    #12 ranked in Kawabe

    A park a five-minute walk from Kawabe Town Hall, near Yamakawa Bridge, with parking, benches, and toilets.

  • 八坂山｜観光スポット｜岐阜県観光公式サイト 「岐阜の旅ガイド」

    #13 ranked in Kawabe

    A Muromachi-period castle site with stone walls, stone statues, and a still-filled well on the summit above Kawabe.
