    The honjin monument and lattice houses recall the past, beside a marker once noting Owari domain land east of Nishizaka.

    Praised as a passionate Heian poet, Izumi Shikibu is said to have fallen ill here, sought a cure at Oniiwa Onsen, and died.

    A late-15th-century flatland castle first called Edo Castle, later Kamiedo Castle, whose lord plotted against Mori Nagayoshi around 1582.

    #21 ranked in Mitake

    Edo-period sake brewers' residence site; only a stone wall remains, once a Nakasendo rest stop for daimyo and travelers.

    Small castle remains survive behind Hakusan Shrine in Kohara; the Ogura lords lived west of the shrine and used the castle only in emergencies.

    #24 ranked in Mitake

    Named because the slope was so steep that cows and horses scraped their noses on the ground; a national historic site since 2016.
