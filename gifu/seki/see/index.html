    Large park in Seki established to mark the centenary of Gifu Prefecture, featuring walking trails and autumn foliage.

  • Kaore Gorge

    Kaore Gorge

    #3 ranked in Seki

    Kaore Gorge in Itadori, Seki, has emerald-green water, towering cliffs, and healing water sounds.

  • Feather Museum

    Feather Museum

    Feather Museum is the world's first comprehensive blade museum.

  • Teraogahara Senbonzakura Park

    Teraogahara Sembonzakura Park

    #10 ranked in Seki

    About one thousand cherry trees line a two-kilometer prefectural road.

  • Seki Enku-kan

    Seki City Enku Museum

    #14 ranked in Seki

    Museum in Seki devoted to the Edo-period monk-sculptor Enku.

  • Yoshidagawa Cherry Blossoms

    Yoshida River Cherry Blossoms

    #15 ranked in Seki

    Along the Yoshida River in spring, Somei-Yoshino cherries bloom overhead, with blue skies by day, illuminated blossoms at night, and windblown petals.

  • Horado Enku Memorial Hall

    Horado Enku Memorial Museum

    #20 ranked in Seki

    Museum in Horado displays wooden statues carved by Enku.

  • Gifu Prefectural Museum

    Gifu Prefectural Museum

    #21 ranked in Seki

    Museum exhibits natural history, fossils and local artifacts.

  • Sekigawa Cherry Blossoms

    Sekigawa Cherry Blossoms

    #22 ranked in Seki

    Two hundred fifty Somei-yoshino trees line the Sekigawa in central Seki.

  • Seki Fireflies

    Seki Fireflies

    #27 ranked in Seki

    Seki has many firefly-viewing spots; the best season is late May to early June, and they fly most beautifully around 8 to 9 p.m.

  • Kanzei-ji Temple

    Seki Zenko Temple (Shukyu Temple)

    #28 ranked in Seki

    It offers prayers for safe childbirth, conception, and warding off evil, plus memorial rites; Kannon Reibyo, Seki's first non-sectarian ossuary, opened in November 2013.

  • Koga Gorge

    Koga Gorge

    #31 ranked in Seki

    Chosen among Gifu's 50 famous waters, with water clear to the riverbed, smooth white boulders, and 4 km for forest bathing and photography.

  • Mount Koga

    Mount Koga

    #32 ranked in Seki

    Elevation 1,224.2 m; long known as a sacred mountain, with splendid summit views of Mount Ontake, Mount Hakusan, and the Okumino mountains.

  • Mount Kabura

    Kaburayama

    #33 ranked in Seki

    Kaburayama, at 1,068 meters northeast of 21st Century Forest Park, offers a nature observation trail for satoyama hikes to the summit.

  • Nichiryubuji Temple (Takazawa Kannon)

    Nichiryubuji Temple (Takazawa Kannon)

    #37 ranked in Seki

    Gifu Prefecture's oldest temple has a stage-style main hall called Mino Shimizu and a nationally designated Important Cultural Property pagoda.

  • Hokiyama Katakuri Flowers

    Hokiyama Dogtooth Violets

    #38 ranked in Seki

    North-facing slope in Seki’s Ashihara district where thousands of wild katakuri bloom from late March to mid-April.

  • Seibei-buchi Pool

    Seibei Fuchi

    #39 ranked in Seki

    Deep river pool in Seki with clear spring water, known for spring cherry blossoms and autumn foliage.

  • Tsukahara Ruins Park

    Tsukahara Ruins Park

    #40 ranked in Seki

    Archaeological park in Seki displaying reconstructed Jomon pit dwellings and a small exhibition of excavated artifacts.

  • Hazama Fudoson

    Hazama Fudoson

    #41 ranked in Seki

    Historic temple complex in Seki with 1,200 years of tradition and monthly fire rituals on the 28th.

  • Nichiryubuji Temple (Takazawa Kannon)

    Nichiryuhoji Temple (Takazawa Kannon)

    #42 ranked in Seki

    Gifu Prefecture's oldest temple, with a stage-style main hall called Mino Shimizu and a nationally designated Important Cultural Property pagoda.

  • Oni ga Otoshita Oishi

    #43 ranked in Seki

    Two large stones are found here.

  • Shin-Chokokuji Temple (Kita Kannon)

    Shinchokukuji Temple (Kitta Kannon)

    #45 ranked in Seki

    Temple in Seki noted for its rare Muromachi-period guest hall, a National Important Cultural Property, and historic gardens.

  • Koga Shrine

    Takaga Shrine

    #46 ranked in Seki

    Shrine in Seki first founded in the Nara period and rebuilt three times.

  • Iwato no Taki

    Iwamon Falls

    #51 ranked in Seki

    Waterfall about 20 meters high in Seki, beside a 10-meter rock gate.

  • Kasuga Shrine

    Kasuga Shrine

    #57 ranked in Seki

    Kasuga Shrine in Seki, Gifu, has rituals, annual events, nationally designated Important Cultural Properties, and Doji Yagyo.

  • Momijibafu and Tokaede Tree-Lined Road

    #58 ranked in Seki

    For about 4 km from Wakakusa Tunnel toward Seki Techno Highland and north of City Hall, Momijibafu and Tokaede turn red and yellow in autumn.

  • Teraogahara Senbonzakura Park

    Autumn Leaves at Teraogahara Senbonzakura Park

    #59 ranked in Seki

    This park, known for full spring cherry blossoms, also shows striking autumn color as trees along about 2 km of road turn red and orange.

  • Kasuga Shrine Autumn Leaves

    Autumn Leaves at Kasuga Shrine

    #60 ranked in Seki

    Beloved as guardian of Seki swordsmiths, Kasuga Shrine has maples and ginkgo that turn red and yellow along the approach and Noh stage in autumn.

  • Mirokuji

    Ryugezan Miroku-ji Temple

    #61 ranked in Seki

    Miroku-ji in Ikejiri, Seki, is known for ties to Edo-period monk Enku, who is said to have spent his later years here.

  • Seki Zenkoji

    Seki Zenko-ji Temple

    #62 ranked in Seki

    Seki Zenko-ji in Seki offers prayers for safe birth, conception, and warding off evil, plus memorial services, and completed a non-sectarian ossuary in November 2013.

  • Mirokuji Cherry Blossoms

    Cherry Blossoms at Miroku-ji Temple

    #63 ranked in Seki

    At Miroku-ji in Seki, cherry blossoms usually peak in early April amid the spacious government-office ruins, while dandelions also bloom underfoot.

  • Gifu Prefectural Hyakunen Park

    Gifu Prefecture Hyakunen Park

    #64 ranked in Seki

    Hyakunen Park in Seki, Gifu, is a 100-hectare satoyama park with typical Chuno woodland and many plants and animals through the seasons.
