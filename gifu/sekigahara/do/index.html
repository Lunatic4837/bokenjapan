  • Sekigahara Japanese Umbrella Story

    Sekigahara Japanese Umbrella Story

    #9 ranked in Sekigahara

    Now held year-round, it features a Japanese umbrella art wall, an umbrella tunnel, and lights from more than 300 umbrellas.

  • Goma no Sato

    Sesame Village

    #13 ranked in Sekigahara

    A theme park beside a sesame-product factory, with a museum on sesame history and culture and a shop of sesame foods and sweets.

  • Sekigahara E-Bike Rental

    Electric-Assist Bicycle Rental in Sekigahara

    #26 ranked in Sekigahara

    Limited electric-assist bicycles can be reserved at the tourism center in front of Sekigahara Station.

  • Sekigahara Historic Site Guide

    Sekigahara Historic Sites Guide

    #32 ranked in Sekigahara

    Guides who completed two years of town historic-site courses lead Sekigahara visits at chosen times between 9:30 and 16:30.

  • Sekigahara Oshi Trip – Learning Journey

    Sekigahara Favorite Trip: Learning Journey

    #46 ranked in Sekigahara

    A specialist-guided day trip to Ishida Mitsunari, Tokugawa Ieyasu, or Otani Yoshitsugu sites, with memorial-museum study and battlefield atmosphere.

  • Sekigahara Oshi Trip – Role-Play Journey

    Sekigahara Favorite Trip: Immersive Journey

    #48 ranked in Sekigahara

    A premium Sekigahara experience to become an admired warlord, highlighted by authentic armor try-on at Sasaozan Exchange Hall.
