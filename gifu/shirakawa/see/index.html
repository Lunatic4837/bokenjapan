Sights in Shirakawa, Gifu

Shirakawa, Gifu: things to do, food and stays · Gifu · 10 places

See

  • Hisu Gorge

    Hisuikyo Gorge

    #4 ranked in Shirakawa

    About 12 kilometers of Hida River gorge between Shirakawa and Hichiso, with potholes, June rock azaleas, and a 2007 geological listing.

  • Oyamashirayama Jinja Cedar

    Great Cedar of Oyama Hakusan Shrine

    #5 ranked in Shirakawa

    A national natural-monument cedar on Hakusan Shrine's steps measures 8.9 meters around, 36 meters tall, and 1,200 years old.

  • Sakami River Gorge Autumn Leaves

    Samigawa Gorge Autumn Leaves

    #9 ranked in Shirakawa

    A roughly 6 km gorge along Route 256 in Shirakawa, especially famous for autumn leaves near Momiji Bridge.

  • Shirakawa Bridge

    Shirakawa Bridge

    #11 ranked in Shirakawa

    This steel suspension bridge, completed in 1926 when Shirakawaguchi Station opened, has largely kept its form for over 100 years.

  • Futatsumori-yama

    Mount Futatsumori

    #13 ranked in Shirakawa

    Near the summit is a prefectural natural-monument oak said to be Japan's second largest, with views of the Alps and Mount Ontake.

  • Mino Shirakawa Tea Plantation

    Mino Shirakawa Chachaen

    #14 ranked in Shirakawa

    Grown in mountains called tea's northern limit, Mino Shirakawa tea fields are spectacular when new shoots appear in May and June.

  • Shirakawa Tea Plantation

    Mino Shirakawa Chachaen Landscape

    #15 ranked in Shirakawa

    These tea fields spread across Shirakawa Town's mountains, said to be the northern limit of tea cultivation.

  • Hissui Gorge Rock Azalea

    Shirakawa Town Flower Rock Azalea

    #16 ranked in Shirakawa

    At Hisuikyo, one of Japan's 100 geological sites, the town flower rock azalea blooms brilliant pink among strange rocks in early summer.

  • Kakihan Rural Landscape

    Kakitan Rural Landscape

    #17 ranked in Shirakawa

    A rural landscape used as a location for The Woodsman and the Rain, especially soothing and moving in morning mist.

  • Onodera-jinja Tenno Cherry Tree

    Tenno Zakura at Ono Tsushima Shrine

    #18 ranked in Shirakawa

    This roughly 250-year-old higan cherry, designated a prefectural preserved tree in 1926, was named Tenno Zakura in 1995 and blooms pure white.
