Things to do in Shirakawamura, Gifu

Shirakawamura, Gifu: things to do, food and stays · Gifu · 6 places

Do

  • Shirakawa-go Gassho-zukuri Folk Village Autumn Illumination

    Shirakawa-go Gassho-zukuri Minkaen Autumn Leaves Light-up

    #6 ranked in Shirakawa-go

    The 17th autumn-leaves light-up is a night opening held while foliage turns red and yellow; the 2025 event has ended.

  • Toyota Shirakawa-go Nature School

    Toyota Shirakawa-go Nature School

    #12 ranked in Shirakawa-go

    Offers nature experiences for adults and children in Shirakawa-go, backed by Hakusan National Park and overlooking the World Heritage gassho village.

  • Hirase Onsen

    Oshirakawa no Yu Hirase Onsen

    #15 ranked in Shirakawa-go

    A quiet mountain hot-spring area about 20 minutes by car from Ogimachi, known for very hot water called Kodakara no Yu.

  • Shiraimizu no Yu

    Oshirakawa Onsen Shiramizu no Yu Day-Use Hot Spring

    #16 ranked in Shirakawa-go

    A free-flowing day-use hot spring 15 minutes by car from World Heritage Shirakawa-go, set in greenery, with meals available.

  • Jinriki Shin-chan

    Rickshaw Shinchan

    #18 ranked in Shirakawa-go

    Shirakawa-go's only rickshaw, with sightseeing tips, Hida beef croquettes, and sarubobo keychain and postcard souvenirs.

  • Jiba Workshop

    Shirakawa-go, Gifu

    Workshop located in Shirakawa-go.
