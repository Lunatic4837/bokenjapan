  • Bodai-yama Castle Site Hiking Course

    Bodaiyama Castle Ruins Hiking Course

    #2 ranked in Tarui

    Hiking trail in Tarui leading to the hilltop ruins of Bodai-yama castle, once home to Takenaka Hanbei.

  • Omote Sata Tsuzumi Odori

    Omote Sata Tsuzumi Dance

    #5 ranked in Tarui

    Traditional drum dance in Tarui performed with 60 kg drums during festivals and rituals.

  • Ōishi Matsuri

    Oishi Festival

    #6 ranked in Tarui

    It is held yearly on October's second Sunday, with about 150 fireworks and traditional Tachibi launched from bamboo poles.

  • Tarui Hikiyama Matsuri

    Tarui Hikiyama Festival

    #8 ranked in Tarui

    This Tarui festival, over 660 years old, reportedly began in 1353 when villagers pulled three flower floats to comfort Emperor Gokogon.

  • Aikawa Carp Streamer Festival

    Aikawa Koinobori Mass Flight

    #11 ranked in Tarui

    From late March to early May, about 300 carp streamers fly over the Aikawa, a spring tradition in Tarui.

  • Nangu Taisha

    Nangu Taisha Flower Chozu Tea Seat

    #20 ranked in Tarui

    Nangu Taisha, Mino's chief shrine, enshrines Kanayamahiko, deity of iron ore and mines.

  • Nangu Taisha Reisai

    Nangu Taisha Annual Grand Festival

    #28 ranked in Tarui

    Three portable shrines proceed to Otabi Shrine in Fuchu, with a 13-meter snake-mountain tower raised en route.

  • Hotaru Matsuri Iwate

    Firefly Festival (Iwate)

    #34 ranked in Tarui

    Around early June, many fireflies glow along the river in Iwate because conservation has kept the water clean.
