Things to do in Yamagata, Gifu

Yamagata, Gifu: things to do, food and stays · Gifu · 6 places

Do

  • Ijira Lake Wakasagi Fishing

    Pond Smelt Fishing at Ijira Lake

    #1 ranked in Yamagata

    Winter smelt fishing on scenic Ijira Lake in Yamagata city.

  • Yamagata Ichigo Paradise Miyabi

    Yamagata Ichigo Rakuen Miyabi

    #8 ranked in Yamagata

    A strawberry-picking farm in Yamagata City, Gifu, grows Akihime and Benihoppe in raised beds with organic fertilizer, beside Tenkomori produce shop.

  • Hajikami Trailhead

    Hajikami Course to Okuwa Castle (Hajikami Forest Road Trailhead)

    #23 ranked in Yamagata

    A mostly gentle climb from the Hajikami forest-road pass to the Kojo-yama summit, about 750 m and 20 to 30 minutes.

  • Kenkyaku Trailhead

    Kenkyaku Course to Okuwa Castle (Kojo-yama Trailhead)

    #24 ranked in Yamagata

    A longer, partly steep climb from the Kojo-yama trailhead past Okuwa Castle remains, about 2.1 km and 60 to 90 minutes.

  • Mitoca Farm

    Mitoka Experience Farm

    #27 ranked in Yamagata

    This experience farm, Gifu's largest by area, offers greenhouse harvests and a cafe and shop using its fruit.

  • Gifu Satoyama Experience

    Chestnut Picking and Satoyama Experience

    #29 ranked in Yamagata

    Programs include chestnut picking and children's nature experiences, plus sales of local produce and processed goods.
