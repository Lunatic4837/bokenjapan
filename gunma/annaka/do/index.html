Things to do in Annaka, Gunma

Annaka · Gunma · 4 places

Do

  • Megumi no Yu Sand-Salt Bath

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-329-6203

    A sand bath using salty mineral sand from Isobe Onsen warms the body within ten minutes.

  • Ayu Salt-Grilling Experience

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-385-6959

    Visitors grill fresh live sweetfish at Isobe Yana, then eat an ayu meal with local dishes.

  • Jishoji-yaki Satoaki Kiln

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-5560

    Annaka pottery workshop offering kiln tours, exhibitions, and hand-building experiences.

  • Wintersweet Village

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-1111

    About 1,200 wintersweet plants fill 3.2 hectares, and a festival is held in early January.
