Sights in Annaka, Gunma

Annaka · Gunma · 22 places

See

  • Haisen Uoku

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-329-6203

    Event walking the closed Usui Line between Yokokawa and Karuizawa, including unlit tunnels and the Megane Bridge; hours 9:00-17:00, varying by course, usually about 120-480 minutes.

  • Lovers' Sanctuary

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-329-6203

    Isobe Onsen was certified a Lovers' Sanctuary in July 2019, with a silk-cord tying spot at Akagi Shrine.

  • Isobe Onsen

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-329-6203

    This historic hot-spring town north of Isobe Station is known as the birthplace of the onsen symbol.

  • Usui Pass (Spectacles Bridge)

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-1111

    Completed in 1892, Japan's largest brick arch bridge is a National Important Cultural Property.

  • Usui Pass Railway Heritage Park

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-380-4163

    This hands-on theme park displays 40 vehicles tracing pass and railway history.

  • Usui Silk Reeling Co., Ltd.

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-393-1101

    It manufactures raw silk and offers explained tours of the production process.

  • Ote Confectionery

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-385-6224

    Manufactures Isobe rice crackers and offers viewing and sorting experiences.

  • Isobe Fish Weir

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-385-6959

    A place beside the Usui River serving live sweetfish and local cuisine.

  • Matsuida Rose Garden

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-393-3962

    Owners created this natural rose garden, where about 1,000 bushes of 200 varieties bloom.

  • Gunma Flower Highland

    Annaka, Gunma · 090-8818-9221

    Visitors can see 600 winter plums, azaleas, cherries, daffodils, and wintersweet across this large area.

  • Akima Plum Grove

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-1111

    This is Kanto's largest plum grove, with about 35,000 red and white trees on 500,000 square meters.

  • Chimase Adonis

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-1111

    Wild adonis turns a small hill near Chimase in Kami-Masuda golden from early February to early March.

  • Akima Plum-Viewing Park

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-1111

    Wintersweet, plums, cherries, azaleas, and poppies grow here; May poppies cover about 3,000 square meters, entry free.

  • Former Maruyama Substation

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-1111

    Brick substation ruins built in 1912 when the Usui Line was electrified, designated a national Important Cultural Property.

  • Usui Checkpoint Site

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-7622

    Established in 1623 at Yokokawa on the Nakasendo to police incoming guns and outgoing women; east gate restored.

  • Former Residence of Niijima Jo

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-7622

    In 1874 Niijima Jo reunited with his parents here in Annaka after ten years in America; a city historic site.

  • Former Usui District Office

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-7622

    Gunma's only surviving district office was rebuilt in modern Japanese style after a 1910 fire.

  • Chimase Adonis Habitat

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-382-1111

    Adonis grows wild over about 1,500 square meters, with a legend of buried gold blooming as flowers.

  • Kitanoji Temple

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-329-6203

    Temple where Ii Naotaka, son of Ii Naomasa and later Hikone lord, spent his childhood.

  • Annaka Smile Park

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-380-2525

    Children through seniors gather around a circular lawn, a walking course, and play space.

  • Iris Hill Flower Paradise

    Annaka, Gunma · 027-385-5822

    A three-hectare hillside garden where about 100,000 German irises bloom from early May.

  • Melodic Light Walk

    Annaka, Gunma

    A two-kilometer night walk on an abandoned railway in Annaka, with lanterns, black light, and sound.
