Sights in Chiyodamachi, Gunma

Chiyodamachi · Gunma · 6 places

See

  • Akaiwa Ferry

    Chiyodamachi, Gunma · 0276-49-5200

    Linking Akaiwa and Kumagaya, this powered ferry is the Tone's only water prefectural road, carrying over 20,000 people yearly.

  • Akaiwa Koonji Temple

    Chiyodamachi, Gunma · 0276-86-2157

    Said to be Chiyoda's oldest temple, opened about 1,200 years ago, it holds cultural treasures and biannual festivals.

  • Daiichi Sankyo Nakasato Park

    Chiyodamachi, Gunma · 0276-86-7003

    A green park themed on water and wind has a Tone overlook, a 60-meter slide, baseball, and barbecue facilities.

  • Chiyoda Town Cycling Road

    Chiyodamachi, Gunma · 0276-86-7003

    An 11-kilometer Tone River cycling road passes Koonji Temple, Atago Shrine, and other spots blending nature and history.

  • Eastern Sports Park

    Chiyodamachi, Gunma · 0276-86-3012

    A large park with soccer and baseball fields, a playground, heated pool, and an air-conditioned barrier-free gym.

  • Nakajima Cherry Blossom Avenue

    Chiyodamachi, Gunma

    About 60 cherry trees line 350 meters north to south, forming a noted blossom spot.
