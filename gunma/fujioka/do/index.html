Do in Fujioka

Fujioka · Gunma · 6 places

Do

  • Fujioka Wisteria Festival

    Fujioka, Gunma · 0274-22-8111

    This festival is held in a 2.3-hectare park with 45 wisteria varieties and a 350-meter trellis.

  • Yashio Hydrangea Village

    Fujioka, Gunma · 0274-52-3111

    Some 5,000 hydrangeas bloom around Yashio Onsen, where paths offer an Eight Lucky Gods walk, late June to early July.

  • Fujioka Cherry Blossom Festival (Sakurayama Park)

    Fujioka, Gunma · 0274-52-3111

    About 7,000 winter cherries, a national scenic site, and 3,000 Yoshino cherries turn Sakurayama pale pink in spring.

  • Tezukuri Tosen (Matsuya Brewery)

    Fujioka, Gunma · 0274-22-0022

    Handmade local sake using Ayukawa spring water that won national gold in 2000.

  • Iwao (Takai Brewery)

    Fujioka, Gunma · 0274-24-0011

    Brewery founded in 1729 and named for Field Marshal Oyama Iwao.

  • Former Hino-nishi Elementary School

    Fujioka, Gunma · 0274-28-0220

    Each October it hosts the Hino Autumn Festival, with student performances and over 100 scarecrows.
