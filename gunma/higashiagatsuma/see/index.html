  • Iwabitsu Castle Ruins

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-68-2111

    These cliffside castle ruins, where young Sanada Yukimura lived, are among Takeda's three strongholds.

  • Kanekaya Confectionery and Bakery

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-67-2105

    Its karinto manju appears in Gunma's voted favorite-souvenir selection.

  • Daffodil Fields

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-68-2111

    Trumpet daffodils bloom in Higashiagatsuma from late March to mid-April, especially by Iwai park.

  • Mitsuba Azaleas of Scenic Agatsuma Gorge

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-68-2111

    National scenic Agatsuma Gorge stretches about 3.5 kilometers, with peak viewing in mid-April.

  • Omaki Cherry at Former Iwashima No. 2 Elementary School

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-68-2111

    The town's earliest cherry, blooming in mid-April, signaled hemp-sowing time and is called the Omaki Cherry.

  • Hakoshima District Firefly Viewing

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-68-2111

    Wild fireflies along Hakoshima Spring are viewed from about 8 p.m. between mid-June and late July.

  • Yakushi Hot Spring (temporarily closed as of August 31, 2024)

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-69-2422

    A relocated thatched house offers a self-flowing beauty spring, waterfall bath, and spacious indoor bath.

  • Agatsuma Fureai Park

    Higashiagatsuma, Gunma · 0279-67-3193

    A park beside Agatsuma Gorge roadside station, with trails, 14 play units, and a footbath.
