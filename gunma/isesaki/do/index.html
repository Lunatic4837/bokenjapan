Do in Isesaki

Isesaki · Gunma · 5 places

Do

  • Kezoji Park

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-24-5111

    The park has seasonal flowers, sports facilities, and a free-entry amusement park with a Ferris wheel.

  • Jomo Shimbun Printing Center

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-23-8600

    Prints daily newspapers and offers rotary-press tours with explanatory videos.

  • Koizumi Inari Shrine

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-24-5111

    Gunma's tallest great torii marks this shrine, which has about 300 donated torii and spring cherry blossoms.

  • Kasukawa Carp Streamers

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-24-5111

    About 600 carp streamers fly over the Kasukawa River each spring, with 150 near Kazoji Park.

  • Isesaki Mirai Park (Isesaki Citizens' Forest Park)

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-20-3333

    Universal-design park with rock fountains, streams, and about 130 early Kawazu cherry trees.
