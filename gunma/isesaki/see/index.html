Sights in Isesaki, Gunma

Isesaki · Gunma · 12 places

See

  • Chuji Teahouse Honpo

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-32-0124

    This shop was relocated from the last hideout where Kunisada Chuji was arrested.

  • Akabori Iris Garden

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-62-1151

    Some 25,000 irises bloom in June among the nationally designated Onnabori canal remains.

  • Isesaki Meiji Hall

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-40-6885

    This Japanese-Western building displays seasonal Isesaki meisen and sells handmade meisen souvenirs.

  • Koizumi Inari Great Torii Cosmos Fields

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-62-1311

    Colorful cosmos carpet about 6.6 hectares around Gunma's tallest Koizumi Inari torii in mid to late October.

  • Isesaki Monja

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-24-2211

    Merchants from textile town Isesaki brought this snack from Tokyo; sweet uses strawberry syrup and spicy uses curry.

  • Azuma Suisei Shokubutsu Park

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-62-1311

    Park where about 7,000 kakitsubata and shobu bloom together, usually from early to mid-June, with a gazebo and walking paths.

  • Isesaki Kikka Taikai

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-24-5111

    Event of chrysanthemums grown by enthusiasts, lined up on the stone pavement of Kezouji Park, held annually from early to late November.

  • Sansui Eatery

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-25-6202

    Chewy Joshu-flour noodles in aged tomato sauce; baked mozzarella and five cheeses recommended.

  • Soba Shin

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-74-7467

    Popular Kaminari miso and ingredient-rich chanko are signature tastes; limited handmade nihachi soba is praised.

  • Asami Udon

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-74-2445

    Traditional kneaded, trodden, and cut udon shop with okkirikomi plus 33 udon varieties.

  • Edomasa

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-23-6126

    Handmade soba and udon shop offering healthy tasty dishes; okkirikomi appeals to all ages.

  • Guriguri Dog Cafe

    Isesaki, Gunma · 0270-75-5878

    Two minutes from Kezoji Park, it welcomes all dog sizes indoors, on the terrace, and in a run.
