Things to do in Itakura, Gunma

Itakura · Gunma · 4 places

Do

  • Gunma Water Country Agefune Yatagawa Cruise

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-1111

    Itakura event where a boatman poles an agefune along about two kilometers of the Yatagawa River.

  • Catfish Cuisine (River Fish Cuisine)

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-0032

    Itakura specialty of river fish, especially catfish tempura and loach fritters, long served to shrine visitors.

  • Hayashiya

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-0031

    A historic river-fish restaurant beside Raiden Shrine, known for its catfish set meal.

  • Hanareyama Park

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-1111

    Cherry blossoms in spring and red spider lilies in autumn bloom at this park in Ebise.
