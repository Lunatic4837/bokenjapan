Sights in Itakura, Gunma

Itakura · Gunma · 9 places

See

  • Raiden Shrine

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-0007

    Head Kanto Raiden shrine; its halls are prefectural, and Gunma's oldest wooden sub-shrine is a national cultural property.

  • Watarase Retarding Basin

    Itakura, Gunma · 0282-62-1161

    A 3,300-hectare flood basin across four prefectures, noted for reed beds, rare wildlife, and water sports.

  • Eat And Kanto Factory

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-70-4100

    It produces frozen gyoza, boiled gyoza, wrappers, fillings, and noodles.

  • Udon Cafe Harada

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-0063

    This shop serves Itakura cucumber cold-broth udon, consecutive champion of the first two Men-1 Grand Prix contests.

  • Uotoshi

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-0054

    This Itakura restaurant has served local catfish, crucian carp, and eel for 120 years.

  • Kobayashiya

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-0032

    A catfish restaurant in front of Raiden Shrine for over a century, famous for tempura.

  • Nozawaya

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-1018

    This reservation-only family restaurant serves carp stew, eel, and catfish tempura.

  • Tanomoshi Weeping Cherry

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-1111

    This estimated 250-year-old weeping cherry, about 16 meters tall, blooms pale pink-white from late March into early April.

  • Takatori Tenmangu Shrine

    Itakura, Gunma · 0276-82-0122

    A shrine to Sugawara no Michizane whose 1848 hall is a town-designated important cultural property.
