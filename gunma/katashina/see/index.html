Sights in Katashina, Gunma

Katashina · Gunma · 13 places

See

  • Konsei Shrine

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-2250

    Konsei Shrine on Konsei Pass enshrines a stone phallic deity of family prosperity.

  • Hariyama Tenno Cherry Tree

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-3222

    Hariyama's estimated 300-year-old Tenno Cherry is designated a Gunma natural monument.

  • Oze National Park

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-3222

    Oze, Honshu's largest high moor, preserves lakes, alpine plants, and primeval forest nearly untouched.

  • Sugenuma SUP

    Katashina, Gunma · 080-7826-0815

    Easy stand-up paddling is offered on Sugenuma, Honshu's clearest lake, in Katashina.

  • Oku-Nikko Family Land Shirane Fish Garden

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-2250

    Visitors catch stream-raised trout and eat them immediately salt-grilled.

  • Shigehachi Indigo Workshop

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-25-3443

    Offers formal traditional indigo dyeing without synthetic dyes or chemicals, using natural lye fermentation.

  • Shobuzawa Eastern Skunk Cabbage Colony

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-3222

    Eastern skunk cabbage emerges in late April at Shobuzawa and Hariyama in Hanasaku, Katashina, signaling spring.

  • Oshimizu Asian Skunk Cabbage Colony

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-2112

    Ten thousand Asian skunk cabbages grow on one hectare, best from mid to late May.

  • Katashina Asian Skunk Cabbage Forest

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-3222

    About 5,000 Asian skunk cabbages cover 0.75 hectares and are lit at night from late April to early May.

  • Nikko Shirone Yama Ropue Sancho Ekishuhen

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-2211

    Rock garden at the summit station, first planted with 20,000 komakusa that formed a red carpet at full bloom; numbers have since stabilized, and many now grow wild.

  • Buson Ranch

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-3757

    Ranch whose summit has about 10,000 renge azaleas, including a colony designated a Gunma Prefecture natural monument in 1958, usually best from mid to late June.

  • Lake Sugenuma

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-2112

    Honshu's most transparent lake, at 1,730 meters, formed when Nikko-Shirane lava dammed a river.

  • Konsei Pass

    Katashina, Gunma · 0278-58-3222

    A 2,024-meter pass between Katashina in Gunma and Nikko in Tochigi.
