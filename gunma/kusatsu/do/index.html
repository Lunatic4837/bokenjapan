Things to do in Kusatsu, Gunma

Kusatsu · Gunma · 8 places

Do

  • Kusatsu Onsen Yumomi

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-0800

    Netsunoyu presents a daily yumomi and dance show that tempers Kusatsu's scalding hot-spring water.

  • Kusatsu Hot Spring BanZip TENGU

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-8111

    Since April 2019, a zip-line glides about 500 meters from Tenguyama at Kusatsu Onsen Ski Area.

  • Otaki no Yu

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-2600

    Famed Nigawa spring water fills this bath, where Kusatsu's traditional combined baths can be enjoyed.

  • Kusatsu Hot Springs

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-0800

    One of Japan's three famous hot springs, Kusatsu discharges over 32,300 liters per minute.

  • Kusatsu Onsen Ski Resort

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-8111

    A century-old ski resort known for Japan's first commercial lift and a 4.3-kilometer course.

  • Goza-no-Yu

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-9000

    A rebuilt communal bath linked to Minamoto no Yoritomo has wooden and stone baths alternating daily.

  • Nishinokawara Street

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-0800

    Kusatsu Onsen's main street from Yubatake, lined with shops for sweets, snacks, and souvenirs.

  • Kusatsu Onsen Rich Tonkotsu Ramen Buta Rinno

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-6211

    A rich Jiro-style pork-bone ramen shop on the grounds of Matsunoi Ryokan in Kusatsu Onsen.
