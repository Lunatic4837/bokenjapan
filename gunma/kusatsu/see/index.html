Sights in Kusatsu, Gunma

Kusatsu · Gunma · 7 places

See

  • Sainokawara Open-air Bath

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-6167

    One of Japan's largest open-air baths, about 500 square meters, with seasonal views all around.

  • Joshu Local-Flour Udon Matsumoto

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-2678

    Homemade flat udon about two centimeters wide uses Gunma Kinunonami wheat.

  • Yubatake Soan Footbath Cafe

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-89-1011

    First-floor cafe before Kusatsu's Yubatake where you foot-bathe with drinks and soft-serve.

  • Maeguchi Eastern Skunk Cabbage Park

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-0001

    Northern Kanto's largest colony lines a forest stream, with a boardwalk, and peaks in early April.

  • Sesshogawara (Rhododendrons)

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-0001

    Colonies of Azuma and Hakusan rhododendrons bloom from late April to early July.

  • Sainokawara Park

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-7188

    Springs gush at 1,400 liters a minute across this Kusatsu riverbed, forming a stream, falls, and ponds.

  • Snowshoeing (Kusatsu Town)

    Kusatsu, Gunma · 0279-88-8111

    Snowshoeing through white snow reveals winter birds and tracks of foxes, hares, and serows.
