Things to do in Maebashi, Gunma

Maebashi · Gunma · 7 places

Do

  • Seiryo (Machida Sake Brewery)

    Maebashi, Gunma · 027-266-0052

    Founded in Meiji 16, it pursues clear, rounded sake brewed with Komagata's Tone River well water.

  • Katsuragawa (Yanagisawa Sake Brewery)

    Maebashi, Gunma · 027-285-2005

    Since Meiji 10, this Mount Akagi brewery has specialized in sweet sake that keeps rice's flavor.

  • Ai no Yama no Yu

    Maebashi, Gunma · 027-264-3030

    Day-trip baths fed by a spring from 1200 meters underground overlook Mount Akagi's foothills and Maebashi.

  • Akagi Kogen Onsen

    Maebashi, Gunma · 027-283-5211

    A single inn with an open-air bath overlooking the Kanto Plain and slow-cooling, iron-rich water.

  • Ogo Onsen

    Maebashi, Gunma · 027-283-2508

    Heated indoor baths at this low-temperature spring are said to ease pain and warm the body deeply.

  • Takizawa Onsen

    Maebashi, Gunma · 027-283-5711

    A hot spring in central Gunma.

  • Snow Peak Akagi Camp Field

    Maebashi, Gunma · 027-289-0482

    A 1,350-meter campground on Mount Akagi, cooler than the city, near caldera lakes and marshland.
