  • Tanakaya Honten

    田中屋本店

    Dining in Maebashi

    Yaki-manju shop in Maebashi, the main store of three Tanakaya shops, selling plain and bean-paste-filled manju topped with white sesame; closed Mondays, near Route 17 and Shikishima Park.

  • Tanakaya Yakimanju-ten Asahimachi-ten

    たなかや焼饅頭店　朝日町店

    Dining in Maebashi

    Yaki-manju shop in Asahimachi, Maebashi, founded in 1935 on Route 50, selling mixed skewers of filled and plain manju plus black-sesame filling; open 10:00-17:00, closed Wednesdays.

  • Tanakaya Seika

    田中屋製菓

    Dining in Maebashi

    Takeaway yaki-manju shop in Maebashi selling homemade plain and filled manju and sauce, plus gift manju to grill at home; closed Wednesdays, on former Saikawacho street.

  • Wadashiki Yakimanju

    和田式焼きまんじゅう

    Dining in Maebashi

    Yaki-manju shop in Maebashi grilling manju to white, brown, or black for different tastes and textures, with eat-in and takeaway; open from 10:00 until sold out, closed Mondays.

  • Wanukiya

    わぬき屋

    Dining in Maebashi

    Yaki-manju shop in Maebashi, first at a roadside station, with five items including soft serve and original miso sauce; open 10:00-17:30 (18:00 weekends and holidays), closed year-end and New Year.

  • Katahara Manju

    片原饅頭

    Dining in Maebashi

    Manju shop in Maebashi, opened in October 2023, making additive-free handmade sake-seed manju with rice koji; open 10:00-15:00, closed Mondays and Sundays.
