See

  • Sayado-Ohata Flowering Peach Road

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-46-7289

    About 1,500 weeping peaches, planted by locals over 30 years, bloom along two kilometers in early to mid-April.

  • Watarase Keikoku Railway

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-73-2110

    A 44.1-kilometer local railway from Kiryu to Mato, slowly winding along the Watarase River through seasonal scenery.

  • Lake Kusaki Canoeing

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0278-72-5070

    Nature canoeing using easy, stable boats for beginners, with kind instruction from guides.

  • Kusaki Dam Tour

  • Shikanogawa Pond

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-46-7289

  • Water Plaza (Cherry Blossoms)

    Midorishi, Gunma

    This plaza lies directly below Kusaki Dam and is ringed by cherry trees popular in mid-April.

  • Nagame Park and Nagame Theater

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-72-1968

    Nagame Park hosts an autumn chrysanthemum show and contains a 1937 wooden kabuki-style theater.

  • Odaira no Sato Botanical Garden (Wintersweet)

    About 400 wintersweet trees bloom here in early February, spreading a sweet scent.

  • Iwajuku Village Dogtooth Violets

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-46-7289

  • Konaka Great Falls (Kesakake Bridge)

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-46-7289

    A rare stepped suspension bridge, graded up to 44 percent, leads to scenic Konaka Great Falls.

  • Kesa Maruyama

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-46-7289

    Mountain (Mae-Kesamaru 1,878 m, Ato-Kesamaru 1,903 m) on the Japan 300-mountain list, with early-summer azaleas and fresh green and autumn leaves; Oriba ridge trail and Tonosawa trail via Neshaka.

  • Konakao Falls

    Midorishi, Gunma · 0277-46-7289

    One of Gunma's largest waterfalls, with a 96-meter drop, is most spectacular in autumn foliage.

  • Sori Station (Hydrangeas)

    Midorishi, Gunma

    From late June to mid-July, mountain hydrangeas bloom on the slope from the station to the Watarase riverbed.

  • Kanto Chrysanthemum Exhibition (Omama, Midori City)

  • Train Restaurant Seiryu (Godo Station)

  • Iwajuku Museum

  • Midori City Tourism Promotion Video "Four Seasons of Midori"
