Things to do in Minakami, Gunma

Minakami · Gunma · 6 places

Do

  • Minakami Highlands Ski Resort

    Minakami, Gunma · 0278-75-2222

    The resort has twelve courses, kids' parks, and Honshu's only full-scale dog-sledding.

  • Tsukiyono-yaki Yutaro Kiln

    Minakami, Gunma · 0278-72-5307

    Produces everyday and ornamental pottery and offers tourist pottery experiences.

  • Yamaki Minakami Factory

    Minakami, Gunma · 0278-62-1231

    It produces Japanese soup bases and offers group factory tours and optional bonito-shaving experiences.

  • Minakami Fruit Land Mogitore

    Minakami, Gunma · 0278-64-2800

    Experience-based fruit theme park growing varied fruit year-round on about 65,000 square meters.

  • Mount Tanigawa Yohho by Hoshino Resorts (former Mount Tanigawa Ropeway)

    Minakami, Gunma · 0278-72-3575

    Year-round scenic spot with spring greenery, summer flowers, autumn leaves, and skiing.

  • Hodaigi Ski Area

    Minakami, Gunma · 0278-75-2557

    A dog-friendly ski resort with shared lifts, a dog restaurant, gear shop, rescue center, and sled course.
