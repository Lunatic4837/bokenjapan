Sights in Naganohara, Gunma

Naganohara · Gunma · 7 places

See

  • Yamba Dam

    Naganohara, Gunma · 0279-83-2560

    This gravity concrete dam on the Agatsuma River was completed in March 2020.

  • Gunma Prefectural Asama Livestock Breeding Ranch

    Naganohara, Gunma · 0279-84-2074

    Vast prefectural ranch grazing about 800 cattle, with views of Mount Asama.

  • Yanba Teahouse

    Naganohara, Gunma

    Souvenir and dining facility near Yanba Dam, serving ramen, limited dam curry, and cake sets.

  • Yanba Lake Station Maruiwa

    Naganohara, Gunma · 0279-82-5056

    Amphibious-bus terminal modeled on the old Naganohara town hall, with dining, gifts, and a lake park.

  • Naruhodo! Yanba Museum

    Naganohara, Gunma · 027-251-2021

    Museum of Yanba Dam history and workings inside the dam office, with a second-floor viewing balcony.

  • Naganohara-Kusatsu-Kuni Station

    Naganohara, Gunma · 0279-82-1515

    Beside Naganohara-Kusatsuguchi Station, with a restaurant and souvenirs below and multipurpose space above.

  • Yanba Tenmei Mudflow Museum

    Naganohara, Gunma · 0279-82-5150

    Museum on the 1783 Asama mudflow that swallowed Yanba villages about 20 kilometers away.
