Things to do in Numata, Gunma

Numata · Gunma · 8 places

Do

  • Oigami Onsen

    Numata, Gunma · 0278-56-3013

    Legend links this gentle alkaline hot spring to healing wounds and smoothing skin.

  • Tambara Ski Park

    Numata, Gunma · 0278-23-9311

    About two hours from the capital, open late November to Golden Week on easier runs.

  • Machidaya

    Numata, Gunma

    Visitors can try scooping yuba tofu skin and shop.

  • Nango Hot Spring Shakunage no Yu

    Numata, Gunma

    This public bath free-flows pH 9.4 alkaline simple spring water from the grounds at nearly 400 liters a minute.

  • Sadaijin (Otone Brewery)

    Numata, Gunma · 0278-53-2233

    Small handmade-sake brewery at the foot of Oze, embraced by the Okutone mountains.

  • Shirasawa Plateau Hot Spring

    Numata, Gunma · 0278-53-2010

    Alkaline pH-10 hot spring on hills overlooking Numata's river terraces; a drinking spring is also available.

  • Yumoto Katei

    Numata, Gunma · 0278-56-4126

    Four outdoor baths along the Katashina Valley overlook the scenery of each season.

  • Tambara Highland Nature Guide

    Numata, Gunma

    Experienced guides lead up to ten people through Tambara beech forests and wetlands from April to November.
