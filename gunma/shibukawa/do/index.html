Things to do in Shibukawa, Gunma

Shibukawa · Gunma · 8 places

Do

  • Altenade

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-3151

    This is the nickname of a 9-kilometer road from Shibukawa to Ikaho Onsen.

  • Kajika Bridge

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-3151

    This vermilion drum bridge near Ikaho Onsen has autumn foliage peaking in early November.

  • Kanto no Hana (Hijiri Sake Brewery)

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-52-3911

    Founded in 1841 on Mount Akagi's southwest foot, its daiginjo won a tenth national gold award in 2009.

  • Ikaho Hot Springs

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-3151

    A hot-spring resort of over 400 years, with iron-brown Golden Water and clear Silver Water.

  • Onogami Hot Spring

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-2111

    Beside the Agatsuma Line, this alkaline pH 9 spring smooths skin and retains moisture.

  • Kokyuya

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-3322

    Hot-spring inn in Ikaho Onsen town; okkirikomi is served with every dinner plan.

  • IKALIN Ikaho Hot Spring Rental Bicycles

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-3151

    Electric-assist rental bicycles for easy rides around hilly Ikaho Hot Spring town and nearby areas.

  • Shibukawa Skyland Park

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-20-1589

    A scenic hilltop amusement park near Ikaho Onsen with 20 attractions for all ages.
