Sights in Shibukawa, Gunma

Shibukawa · Gunma · 28 places

See

  • Akagi Nature Park

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-56-5211

    Nature park on the western slope of Mount Akagi for experiencing four seasons.

  • Yumeji Takehisa Ikaho Memorial Museum

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-4788

    Shows works by Taisho Romantic painter Yumeji Takehisa, who loved Ikaho and Haruna.

  • Shiroi-juku Double Cherries

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-2111

    Large vivid-pink double cherries along the post town peak from late April to early May.

  • Rokkayama Studio Glass Workshop

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 070-7462-3179

    Visitors can watch glassblowing and make tumblers, bowls, mugs, and paperweights from start to finish.

  • Gunma Glass Craft Museum

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-20-1101

    It exhibits Art Nouveau glass and furniture and offers glass-painting and accessory workshops.

  • Akagi Apple Cooperative

    Shibukawa, Gunma

    Apple picking is available from early September to early December.

  • Komochi Apple Cooperative

    Shibukawa, Gunma

    Apple picking runs from late August through December.

  • Shibukawa City Fruit Growers Association

    Shibukawa, Gunma

    Apple picking runs from early September to late November.

  • Shibukawa Comprehensive Park

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-24-0535

    A 57.2-hectare park around 600 meters high, overlooking Mount Akagi and the Tanigawa peaks.

  • Saku Power Station

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-2873

    About 100 seventy-year-old Somei-Yoshino cherries contrast with a 75-meter surge tank.

  • Komochi Wintersweet Village

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-2073

    About 320 wintersweet plants and 30 maples grow on a hillside owned by Sorinji Temple.

  • Shorenji Temple (Peonies)

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-0838

    This famous peony temple's garden has 1,500 colorful peonies; adults pay 300 yen only during peony season.

  • Gyoko Ta Nambara Chiku Soba Hatake

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-2111

    Soba-field of Toyoaki soba, covered with white soba flowers, usually viewed from late September to early October.

  • Tsutsujiga Oka (Ikaho Forest Park)

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-5210

    Prefectural forest park east of Mount Haruna, centered on Futatsudake and spanning 2.9 km east-west by 1.9 km north-south, with azalea, maple and birch; yamatsutsuji usually peak in late May.

  • Nagamine Park

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-2873

    Vivid mountain azalea colonies glow against Mount Haruna and Ikaho, peaking mid to late May.

  • Shibukawa City General Park (Hydrangeas)

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-24-0535

    Some 35,000 hydrangeas are scattered through the park, peaking from mid to late June.

  • Shibukawa Skyland Park

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-20-1589

    This amusement park on Mount Haruna's hills has rides and a Ferris wheel reaching 510 meters.

  • Onoike Hydrangea Park

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-2111

    About 8,000 hydrangeas of roughly 20 kinds bloom west of Shibukawa from mid-June to mid-July.

  • Mizusawa Kannon Temple

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-3619

    Its main hall enshrines Eleven-faced Thousand-armed Kannon, and its Genroku-era hexagonal pagoda is a prefectural cultural property.

  • Hara Museum ARC

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-24-6585

    Five minutes from Ikaho Onsen, it shows modern art and Oriental antiquities, including National Treasures.

  • Japan Chanson Museum

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-24-8686

    The world's first museum devoted to French chanson, displaying singers' costumes, records, and instruments.

  • Ikaho Forest Park

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-72-5210

    A 224-hectare park in central Gunma, famous for azalea colonies and wind caves.

  • Hokkitsu Hot Spring

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-20-4126

    A yellow-green spring on a former castle hill overlooks the Tone River and Mt. Haruna.

  • Wakutama Noodle Shop

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-56-8885

    About two minutes from Akagi IC, serving generous handmade udon, soba, and set meals.

  • Kemuriya

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-22-1602

    Pure handmade soba and udon shop keeping traditional flavors for over 150 years.

  • Haruna Pavilion

    Shibukawa, Gunma · 0279-23-4217

    Enjoy the panorama of Mount Akagi, the Nikko mountains, and the Kanto Plain.

  • Shirai Post Town (Double Cherry Blossoms)

    Shibukawa, Gunma

    Viewing peaks from late April to early May along a 900-meter street with wells and storehouses.

  • Ikaho Stone Steps

    Shibukawa, Gunma

    Formed in 1576, this steamy inn-lined stone-step street is the setting of Tokutomi Roka's The Cuckoo.
