Sights in Shimonita, Gunma

Shimonita · Gunma · 18 places

See

  • Shimonita Hydrangea Garden

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-67-7500

    One of Kanto's leading hydrangea gardens, with about 20,000 hydrangeas and 1,200 crape myrtles on three hectares.

  • Nakanotake Shrine

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-67-7500

    Popular goshuin offer protection from the deities enshrined at Nakanotake Shrine and Okuni Shrine.

  • Restaurant Hiro

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-3325

    Shop directly opposite Shimonita Station, recommended for katsu curry and katsudon.

  • Nisshoken Western Restaurant

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2285

    Long-established Western restaurant founded in 1919, also serving Shimonita pork katsudon.

  • Shokutei Eight

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2371

    Restaurant preserving inherited flavors for about forty years and serving lean katsudon.

  • Yasubei

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2516

    Restaurant serving nostalgic Western plates, ramen, French dishes, and Shimonita katsudon.

  • Tokiwakan

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2216

    1912 inn where Yumeji Takehisa stayed, serving winter Shimonita leek sukiyaki.

  • Sakura no Sato Prefectural Forest Park

    Shimonita, Gunma

    About 15,000 trees of over 50 cherry varieties bloom in succession beside a mushroom hall.

  • Abuda Adonis Village

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-67-7500

    Volunteers protect long-established adonis and planted red plum that announce the arrival of early spring.

  • Mount Arafune

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2111

    Its flat summit resembles a warship plowing rough seas, and it ranks among Japan's 200 famous mountains.

  • Kiyoshiya Diner

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-3176

    A famous Shimonita diner fries branded local pork cutlets to order and serves them over hot rice.

  • Nabeya

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2028

    It is known for soy-sauced pork cutlet bowls and sweet egg-simmered pork sukiyaki set meals.

  • Brasserie Chacha

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2030

    This Shimonita cutlet-bowl association restaurant serves thick herb-pork cutlets, cafe dishes, and drinks.

  • Toyoken

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-3177

    This evening-only Chinese restaurant is known for generous fried chicken and gyoza wrapped to order.

  • Ichiban

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-2594

    This 1964 local diner, loved for tanmen and gyoza, became nationally famous through Solitary Gourmet.

  • Mount Myogi Stone Gate Tour

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-67-7500

    Closed since a 2020 rockfall, this chain route passes Mount Myogi's uniquely named rocks.

  • Curry and Coffee Simon Hood

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-67-5651

    Spice curry in a renovated rice storehouse, with specialty coffee recommended afterward.

  • Sukiyaki Columbia

    Shimonita, Gunma · 0274-82-3128

    Year-round Gunma sukiyaki, especially Shimonita pork featured on the drama Solitary Gourmet.
