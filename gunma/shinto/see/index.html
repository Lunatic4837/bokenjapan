Sights in Shinto, Gunma

Shinto · Gunma · 6 places

See

  • Chikyu Ya

    Shinto, Gunma · 0120-198-867

    Japanese goods shop displaying 13,556 handmade lucky charms about 10 cm each, made from old silk, in a Guinness-recognized hanging ornament; also sells recycled kimono; free entry, open 10:00-17:00.

  • Chikyuya Bakery

    Shinto, Gunma · 0279-70-8000

    An additive-free bakery using house-cultured natural yeast, where visitors can eat lunch and fresh-baked bread.

  • Usaburo Kokeshi Co., Ltd.

    Shinto, Gunma · 0279-54-6766

    Visitors can tour the kokeshi factory and try paid kokeshi painting.

  • Chikyuya Haruna Glass Craft Workshop

    Shinto, Gunma · 0279-67-8877

    A large craft studio offering more than thirty hands-on activities, including glass, pottery, and candles.

  • Hachiman Firefly Village

    Shinto, Gunma · 0279-54-2211

    Volunteers raise fireflies at Hachiman Shrine; Genji appear mid-June and Heike mid-July.

  • Shinto Hometown Park

    Shinto, Gunma · 0279-54-2211

    Two park areas linked by a bridge, with a 31-meter roller slide and popular rides.
