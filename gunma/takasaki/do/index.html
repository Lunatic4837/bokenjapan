Things to do in Takasaki, Gunma

Takasaki · Gunma · 7 places

Do

  • Daruma Painting Experience

    Takasaki, Gunma · 027-386-5770

    Visitors paint eyes and whiskers on daruma at workshops in Takasaki, Japan's leading daruma-producing city.

  • Taihai (Makino Brewery)

    Takasaki, Gunma · 027-378-2011

    Gunma's oldest sake brewery, making sake since 1690 at Mount Haruna's western foot.

  • Kurabuchi Aimagawa Hot Spring

    Takasaki, Gunma · 027-378-3834

    This earth-colored, strongly salty chloride spring has an oily scent and lasting warmth.

  • Yubata Hot Spring

    Takasaki, Gunma · 027-384-8602

    Heated from a cold spring, this weakly alkaline chloride bath leaves skin warm and moist.

  • Takasaki Nakao Hot Spring

    Takasaki, Gunma · 027-362-1726

    Popular hot spring near Maebashi Interchange, open 23 hours, with free-flowing rock-style outdoor baths.

  • Kurabuchi Hot Spring

    Takasaki, Gunma · 027-378-2311

    On old Shinshu Kaido, now Route 406; used 300 years ago and affectionately called Tamago-yu.

  • Lake Haruna Onsen

    Takasaki, Gunma · 027-374-9211

    A sulfate-chloride hot spring at 1,100 meters by Lake Haruna, with an open-air bath.
