Sights in Tamamura, Gunma

Tamamura · Gunma · 4 places

See

  • Tamamura Hachimangu Shrine

    Tamamura, Gunma · 0270-65-2305

    Historic shrine founded by Minamoto no Yoritomo; its vermilion main hall is a National Important Cultural Property.

  • Cherry Rows in Front of the Prefectural Women's University

    Tamamura, Gunma · 0270-64-7707

    About 360 cherry trees bloom along both sides of the town road in early April.

  • Tamamura Town Historical Museum

    Tamamura, Gunma · 0270-30-6180

    Introduces, through materials and video, Tamamura's history as an Edo post town on the Nikko Reiheishi Road.

  • Shigeta Family Residence

    Tamamura, Gunma · 0270-30-6180

    An Edo-period doctor's residence, nationally registered, with a recreated clinic for doctor role-play.
