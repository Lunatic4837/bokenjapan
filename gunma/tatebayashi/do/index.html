Things to do in Tatebayashi, Gunma

Tatebayashi · Gunma · 4 places

Do

  • Tsutsuji-gaoka Park

    Tatebayashi, Gunma · 0276-74-5233

    Nationally noted azalea park with over 10,000 shrubs of 100 varieties, ancient trees, and a 4D theater.

  • Toa Pharmaceutical Tatebayashi Factory

    Tatebayashi, Gunma · 0276-72-4941

    It manufactures medicines, veterinary drugs, and feed, with explained tours for groups of up to 20.

  • Prefectural Tatebayashi Museum of Art

    Tatebayashi, Gunma · 0276-72-8188

    Built in swan-visited Tataranuma Park, it collects works about relationships between nature and people.

  • Chiaki Mukai Memorial Children's Science Museum

    Tatebayashi, Gunma · 0276-75-1515

    Memorial to Tatebayashi-born astronaut Chiaki Mukai, offering a planetarium and lunar gravity experience.
