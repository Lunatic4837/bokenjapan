Do in Tomioka

Tomioka · Gunma · 4 places

Do

  • Takada Strawberry Garden

    Tomioka, Gunma · 090-2327-7522

    Tomioka's only strawberry-picking garden, five minutes by car from Tomioka Silk Mill, grows several varieties.

  • Lake Oshio

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-6001

    About 1,000 cherry trees and 2,000 hydrangeas grow around this four-season lake.

  • Tomioka Nyu District Sunflower Fields

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-6001

    Over 110,000 sunflowers bloom on a hill beside Lake Nyu from late July to early August.

  • Tomioka Northern Sports Park (Momijidaira Comprehensive Park)

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-1511

    A hilltop park beside a natural history museum and city art museum, with playgrounds and sports fields.
