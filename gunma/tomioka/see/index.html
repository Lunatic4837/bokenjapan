See in Tomioka

Tomioka · Gunma · 16 places

See

  • Tomioka Seishi Ba

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-67-0075

    Silk mill registered as a World Heritage site in 2014, founded in 1872 by the Meiji government as Japan's first full-scale mechanized silk-reeling factory; open 9:00-17:00, adults 1,000 yen.

  • Myogi Shrine

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-73-2119

    Midway up Mount Myogi, this ornate shrine has a vermilion gate and April cherry arches.

  • Marui Orchid Development Center

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-67-0828

    A phalaenopsis greenhouse where groups can view blooms and receive cultivation lectures.

  • Gunma Prefectural World Heritage Center Sekaito

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-67-7821

    The center introduces Tomioka Silk Mill and Gunma silk culture that changed the world.

  • Ozawaya

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-0341

    Tomioka shop founded in 1936 serving traditional handmade okkirikomi year-round.

  • Tokiwaso Japanese Restaurant

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-0090

    Restaurant built in 1938 and registered as a cultural property, serving sukiyaki by a garden.

  • Tomioka Shakuyaku Sono

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-63-0772

    Peony garden with about 50 varieties and 5,000 plants on about 5,000 square meters, usually in bloom from early-mid to late May; open 10:00-18:00, 400 yen (junior high and under free).

  • Kannari Ancient Lotus Village

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-6001

    These lotuses were root-divided in 2010 from plants raised from seeds about 2,000 years old.

  • Ichinomiya Nukisaki Shrine

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-2009

    One of Japan's three descending shrines; its main hall, worship hall, and gate are nationally designated.

  • Gunma Museum of Natural History

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-60-1200

    Five zones present 4.6 billion years of evolution and Gunma's nature, with impressive life-size dinosaur models.

  • Mount Myogi

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-6001

    One of Japan's three scenic wonders, it offers bizarre rock formations and views over the Kanto Plain.

  • Myogi Sky Park

    Tomioka, Gunma · 090-3112-2203

    A JHF-registered paragliding area and school in Tomioka offers tandem flights and license courses.

  • Oshima Mineral Spring

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-1490

    Firewood heats this retro mineral spring, faintly forest-scented, warming body and mind deeply.

  • Suginoya Soba Restaurant

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-1734

    Okkirikomi is available year-round, with soba, udon, and rice dishes also served.

  • Kawaraya

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-62-2359

    Kappo three minutes from Tomioka Silk Mill, named for an ancestor who tiled the mill roof.

  • Gunma Safari Park

    Tomioka, Gunma · 0274-64-2111

    Free-ranging lions, tigers, and other animals, plus Japan's only exhibited Sumatran elephants.
