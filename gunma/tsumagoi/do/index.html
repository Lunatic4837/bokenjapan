  • MUJI Campagna Tsumagoi Campground

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 03-5950-3660

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 090-7012-2789

  • Lake Baragi

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 0279-96-1150

    A lake two kilometers around on 1,300-meter Baragi Plateau, for walking, fishing, and camping.

  • Ushi Pond

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 0279-97-4000

    A small quiet acidic pond near Manza Onsen is clear enough to see the bottom and has trails.
