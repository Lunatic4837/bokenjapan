Sights in Tsumagoi, Gunma

Tsumagoi · Gunma · 4 places

See

  • Onioshidashi Garden

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 0279-86-4141

    Lava from Mount Asama's 1783 eruption formed this garden, named for demons pushing rocks from the crater.

  • Mount Azumaya

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 0279-97-3721

    A 2,354-meter peak on the Gunma-Nagano border and one of Japan's 100 Famous Mountains.

  • Karuizawa Toy Kingdom

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 0279-86-3515

    Northern Kanto play area with about 120 athletic elements, all-age rides, and indoor toy rooms.

  • Free Time Log Cottages

    Tsumagoi, Gunma · 0279-80-6000

    Seven log cottages in Tsumagoi offer views of Mount Asama and Mount Azumaya.
