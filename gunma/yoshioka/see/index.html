Sights in Yoshioka, Gunma

Yoshioka · Gunma · 16 places

See

  • Gunma Maitake Center

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-8976

    Sells fresh in-house maitake and processed goods; tastings, factory tours, parking, and accessible toilets are available.

  • Ikaho Toy, Doll and Automobile Museum

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-55-5020

    This all-day complex near Ikaho Onsen displays vintage toys, dolls, teddy bears, and classic cars.

  • Morita Family Main House

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 080-3462-8797

    Former honjin on the Ikaho Road at Noda-juku, visited by noted scholars and artists.

  • Urushihara Weeping Cherry Avenue

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-55-4126

    About fifteen weeping cherries along a cycling road bloom best from early to mid-April.

  • Gunma Maitake Center

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-8976

    Offers free window tours of its maitake mushroom factory and tastings.

  • Ogura Grape Village

    Yoshioka, Gunma

    Delaware, Kyoho, and many grapes grow from early August to mid-October; picking is not all-you-can-eat.

  • Funao Falls

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-3111

    A noted waterfall where Haruna spring water drops more than 60 meters from a cliff.

  • Mitsuya Burial Mound

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-9443

    A rare octagonal mound of imperial-tomb type, restored and opened to the public as a historic park.

  • Shiroyama Overlook Park

    Yoshioka, Gunma

    Opened in April 2019, the park gives 360-degree views of Mt. Akagi, Mt. Haruna, and the Kanto Plain.

  • Ankodo

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-8505

    Local-gourmet restaurant selling hygienically made in-house souvenirs, including Yunohana manju and yaki manju.

  • Yamaichiya

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-6088

    Enjoy noodles infused with the owner's special blended flavor.

  • Ankodo Gunma Gourmet and Specialties

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-8505

    You can taste homemade udon and okkirikomi.

  • Okkirikomi Hometown

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-4217

    Rare prefectural okkirikomi specialist cooking it for 46 years, with a simple flavor.

  • Miyabi at Food Station Gunma Yoshioka

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-30-6111

    Noodle shop in Food Station; udon and soba are handmade and broth is prepared daily.

  • Yoshioka Town Shiroyama Overlook Park

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 0279-54-3111

    Opened in 2019 on Momoi Castle ruins, with plant-themed areas and views of Maebashi.

  • INU-MUSUBI Dog Cafe

    Yoshioka, Gunma · 080-2067-1776

    Dogs can run freely indoors and outdoors in a space designed to relax their owners.
