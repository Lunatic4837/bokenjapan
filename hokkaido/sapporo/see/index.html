Sights in Sapporo, Hokkaido

Sapporo · Hokkaido · 7 places

See

  • Sapporo Clock Tower

    Sapporo Clock Tower

    Sapporo, Hokkaido · 011-231-0838

    Sapporo Clock Tower, a wooden landmark at North 1 West 2, Chuo-ku, and a major city tourist attraction.

  • Sapporo Maruyama Zoo

    Sapporo Maruyama Zoo

    Sapporo, Hokkaido · 011-621-1426

    Opened in 1951 as Hokkaido's first zoo, Maruyama Zoo in Maruyama Park keeps and exhibits about 140 animal species.

  • Horo Ken Pass Lavender Garden

    Horo Ken Pass Lavender Garden

    Sapporo, Hokkaido · 011-622-5167

    Lavender fields on hills overlooking Sapporo adjoin a viewing parking lot certified as a Japan Night View Heritage site.

  • Sapporo Hitsujigaoka Lookout

    Sapporo Hitsujigaoka Lookout

    Sapporo, Hokkaido · 011-851-3080

    Hitsujigaoka Observation Hill in Toyohira-ku, known for its William S. Clark bronze statue and city views.

  • Hokkaido Museum of Modern Art

    Hokkaido Museum of Modern Art

    Sapporo, Hokkaido · 011-644-6882

    In central Sapporo, this quiet green museum shows famous Japanese and foreign works, with leading sculptors' works in the front garden.

  • Moyuk SAPPORO

    Moyuk SAPPORO

    Sapporo, Hokkaido · 011-213-0037

    Opened in Sapporo's Odori in 2023, moyuk SAPPORO is themed on city-nature coexistence, with a mall, restaurants, and offices.

  • Tenjin Mountain Green Space (Tenjin Fuji)

    Tenjin Mountain Green Space (Tenjin Fuji)

    Sapporo, Hokkaido · 011-851-1681

    This 85-meter hill park overlooking Sapporo is known for two centuries of Tenjin wisteria, best from late May to early June.
