Sights in Anamizu, Ishikawa

Anamizu, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 20 places

See

  • Noto Sakura Station

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Station on the Noto Railway in Anamizu.

  • Bora-machi Yagura

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Fishing lookout towers on Anamizu Bay used to watch for shoals of mullet, described by the astronomer Percival Lowell in his book "Noto".

  • Noto Railway

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-4422, Ishikawa

    The Noto Railway, the local line running up the Noto Peninsula to Anamizu.

  • Anamizu Bussankan Shikisai-sai

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3333, Ishikawa

    Souvenir and local-products shop beside Anamizu Station, the terminus of the Noto Railway.

  • Anamizu Castle Ruins

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Castle site on a plateau about 60 m high at the mouth of the Omata River, overlooking Anamizu.

  • Raigo-ji Temple

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-0709, Ishikawa

    Old Shingon temple founded in 814 (Konin 5) at the prayer of Emperor Saga.

  • Noto Nakai Casting Museum

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-56-1231, Ishikawa

    Museum of the Nakai metal-casting tradition in Anamizu (currently closed).

  • Meisen-ji Temple

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-57-1353, Ishikawa

    Temple in Anamizu, Ishikawa.

  • Anamizu Folk History Museum

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-2231, Ishikawa

    History and folk museum of Anamizu, the gateway to Oku-Noto (currently closed).

  • Anamizu Bay

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Name that Anamizu residents fondly use for the northern part of Nanao Bay.

  • Satori no Michi

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Path through the Nakai district of Anamizu, which prospered on the Kitamaebune trade in the Edo period, passing temples and historic buildings.

  • Shiosai no Michi

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Promenade of about 2 km along Anamizu Bay.

  • Hasebe Shrine

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3010, Ishikawa

    Shrine in Anamizu enshrining the Kamakura samurai Hasebe Nobutsura, ancestor of the Cho clan of Sengoku warlords.

  • Chifuku-in Temple

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-56-1678, Ishikawa

    Temple in a scenic spot overlooking the Nakai Eight Views, originally named Kaisei-zan Kotei-ji and later Zuiho-zan Chifuku-in.

  • Keyaki Kogei Taniguchi (Anamizu Factory)

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-1255, Ishikawa

    Workshop making crafts from zelkova wood that grows wild in the Noto hills, with factory visits and experiences.

  • Noto Railway Rental Cycles

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-0900, Ishikawa

    Rental bicycles run by the Noto Railway (service currently suspended).

  • Nakai Tatara-uta

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Work song of the Nakai metal casters, handed down to the present day in a district that prospered on casting.

  • Nakai Madara-uta

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Celebration song sung in ports across Japan, said to have spread from Kyushu, and kept alive in Nakai.

  • Anamizu Town Office

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-0300, Ishikawa

    Official site of Anamizu, a town on the Noto Peninsula.
