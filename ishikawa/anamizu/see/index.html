Sights in Anamizu, Ishikawa

See

  • Noto Wine

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-58-1577, Ishikawa

    Winery making 120000 bottles a year from Noto grapes, offering free tastings and guided tours.

  • Noto Sakura Station

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Noto-Kashima Station lined with cherry trees that form a tunnel over the platforms each spring.

  • Bora Machi Yagura

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Wooden watch towers once used in Anamizu to spot schools of bora fish for net fishing.

  • Anamizu Bussan Kan Shiki Saisai

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3333, Ishikawa

    Roadside station beside Anamizu Station selling local oysters, Noto wine, seafood, sake and crafts.

  • Anamizu Castle Ruins

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Ruins of Anamizu Castle on a 60-metre hill overlooking the port, seat of the Hasebe clan for 390 years.

  • Raigo-ji Temple

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-0709, Ishikawa

    Shingon temple founded in 814, later the prayer temple of the Hasebe clan.

  • Mei Izumi-ji Temple

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-57-1353, Ishikawa

    Temple founded in 652 whose five-storey stone pagoda is a national important cultural property.

  • Anamizu Folk History Museum

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-2231, Ishikawa

    Museum in Anamizu that researches and preserves the town’s history as a crossroads of land and sea routes.

  • Anamizu Bay

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Calm ria coast bay surrounded by land and islands, offering views of Bessho-dake and Futago-yama.

  • Satori no Michi

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Walking course linking eight temples and shrines along the coast in the Nakai district of Anamizu.

  • Shiosai no Michi

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Two-kilometre seaside promenade along Anamizu Bay popular for strolling, jogging, and cycling.

  • Hasebe Shrine

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3010, Ishikawa

    Shrine in Anamizu dedicated to the Kamakura warrior Hasebe Nobutsura, ancestor of the Naga clan.

  • Jifuku-in

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-56-1678, Ishikawa

    Temple in Nakai, Anamizu, housing a seated Maitreya statue and a twin-headed Aizen Myo-o statue, both town cultural assets.

  • Keyaki Kogei Taniguchi

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-1255, Ishikawa

    Woodworking workshop in Anamizu that uses locally grown zelkova trees.

  • Nakai Tatara Uta

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Traditional work song from Nakai once sung by ironworkers operating tatara bellows.

  • Nakai Madara Uta

    Anamizu, Ishikawa · 0768-52-3790, Ishikawa

    Ritual boat-launching song performed in Nakai on the eleventh day of the first lunar month.
