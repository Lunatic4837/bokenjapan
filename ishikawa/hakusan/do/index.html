Things to do in Hakusan, Ishikawa

Hakusan, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 22 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Sena

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-256-7172, Ishikawa

    Roadside station and local-products centre in the Sena area of Hakusan, with information on nearby sports facilities, lodges and mountain huts.

  • Nanryu Sanso Mountain Lodge

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-259-2022, Ishikawa

    Mountain lodge and campground at Nanryu-ga-baba, on the Mt. Hakusan climbing route.

  • Michi-no-Eki Megumi Hakusan

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-276-8931, Ishikawa

    Roadside station in Hakusan, Ishikawa.

  • Shiramine Onsen Soyu

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-259-2839, Ishikawa

    Public hot-spring bathhouse in the centre of Shiramine, built with local timber at the foot of Mt. Hakusan.

  • Hakusan Seymour Ski Area

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-273-0331, Ishikawa

    Ski area in the Hakusan foothills, operated by SAM Hakusan.

  • Michi-no-Eki Ikko-ikki no Sato Seseragi

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-254-2888, Ishikawa

    Roadside station in Hakusan, Ishikawa, named for the Ikko-ikki uprising of the Sengoku period.

  • Matto Kaihin Onsen

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-274-5520, Ishikawa

    One of five citizens' hot-spring facilities in Hakusan, open to day visitors.

  • Mikawa Okaeri no Kai Guided Tours

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-278-4768, Ishikawa

    Local guide group introducing the sights, historic spots and specialities of Mikawa, once the first prefectural capital of Ishikawa and a port of the Kitamaebune trade.

  • Mikawa Onsen Motoyu Honda

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-278-3434, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring facility in the Mikawa district of Hakusan.

  • Hakusan Ichirino Onsen

    Hakusan, Ishikawa, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring village at Ichirino, in the foothills of Mt. Hakusan.

  • Senjo Onsen Seiryu

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-273-3483, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring inn with a restaurant beside the Nomidani River at the foot of Mt. Hakusan.

  • Yoshino Auto Campground

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-255-5353, Ishikawa

    Campground and barbecue area in the Hakusanroku Theme Park, a seasonal garden park in the Hakusan foothills.

  • Hakusan Shirakawa-go White Road

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-256-7341, Ishikawa

    Fully paved 33.3 km toll road linking Ishikawa and Gifu, climbing from 500 m to 1,450 m above sea level.

  • Hakusan Chugu Onsen Yuyado Kuroyuri

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-256-7955, Ishikawa

    Traditional hot-spring retreat in the mountains of Mt. Hakusan.

  • Hakusan Ichirino Onsen Ski Area

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-256-7412, Ishikawa

    Ski area at Ichirino Onsen in Hakusan, operated by SAM Hakusan.

  • Chida Kobo (Kaga Lion-Head Workshop)

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-272-1696, Ishikawa

    Workshop in Hakusan making hand-carved wooden Kaga lion heads, finishing every step from carving to colouring by hand.

  • Daimon Onsen Center

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-255-5833, Ishikawa

    Public hot-spring facility in Hakusan, Ishikawa.

  • Meotoiwa Onsen Rakuyo

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-272-4126, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring facility in Hakusan, Ishikawa.

  • Senjo Onsen Seiryu Campground

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-273-3483, Ishikawa

    Campground beside the Senjo Onsen Seiryu inn in the Hakusan foothills, among seasonal greenery along the Nomidani River.

  • Iyashi no Yu Tenryo

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-256-7846, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring bath facility in Hakusan, run by SAM Hakusan.

  • Hakusan Suginoko Onsen

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-255-5926, Ishikawa

    Natural hot spring in the Hakusan foothills with 100% free-flowing source water, neither heated nor diluted.

  • Shin-Chugu Onsen Center

    Hakusan, Ishikawa · 076-256-7730, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring bathhouse in Hakusan with a wide washing area and rock baths fed by flowing spring water.
