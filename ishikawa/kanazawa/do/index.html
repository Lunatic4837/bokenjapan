Things to do in Kanazawa, Ishikawa

Kanazawa, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 19 places

Do

  • Yuwaku Onsen

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-235-1040, Ishikawa

    Quiet hot-spring village of eight inns in the hills, 20 minutes by car from Kenrokuen.

  • Shiinoki Guest House

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-261-1111, Ishikawa

    Prefectural memorial guest house in Kanazawa, a hospitality and event space.

  • Kinjo Onsen Motoyu

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-268-2601, Ishikawa

    Bathhouse in Kanazawa with 100% free-flowing hot-spring water in the main bath (a mild 38 °C) and private family baths; the weakly alkaline spring is a hydrogen-carbonate type.

  • Asanogawa Onsen Yuraku

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-235-1126, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring facility in Kanazawa fed by two different sources, with free-flowing baths and views of the seasons.

  • Yuwaku Onsen Zenigame

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-235-1426, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring bath in Yuwaku Onsen, about 30 minutes by car from central Kanazawa.

  • Yuwaku Onsen Soyu Shirasagi-yu

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-235-1380, Ishikawa

    Public bathhouse in Yuwaku Onsen, Kanazawa.

  • Io no Sato Auto Campground

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-229-1312, Ishikawa

    Forest campground in Kanazawa.

  • Daijoji Temple Zazen Experience

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-241-2680, Ishikawa

    Zen meditation experience at Daijoji temple in Kanazawa.

  • Kanazawa Katani Gold Leaf Experience

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-231-1566, Ishikawa

    Gold-leaf crafts experience at Kanazawa Katani, a gold-leaf maker founded 120 years ago.

  • Yachiya Shuzo Sake Brewery Experience

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-252-7077, Ishikawa

    Sake brewery experience at Yachiya Shuzo (Jizake Taniuchi-ya) in Kanazawa.

  • Futamata Honsen-ji

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-236-1004, Ishikawa

    Temple in the Futamata area of Kanazawa.

  • Iozan Ski Area

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-229-1947, Ishikawa

    Municipal ski area on Mt. Ioh run by the Kanazawa Sports Foundation.

  • Miroku Onsen Motoyu

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-221-8096, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring bath in Kanazawa.

  • UIGI Kanazawa Morning Zazen

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-248-3847, Ishikawa

    Morning zazen experience with the local-experience travel service UIGI Kanazawa.

  • Hakuza Gold-Leaf Making Experience

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-251-8941, Ishikawa

    Hands-on workshop at Hakuza on making engake gold leaf, a rare traditional gold-leaf method.

  • Kanazawa Buried Cultural Properties Centre Brewery Display

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-240-2371, Ishikawa

    Cultural-heritage display about sake-brewing remains, run by the Kanazawa City cultural properties office.

  • Yachiya Shuzo (Jizake Taniuchi-ya)

    Kanazawa, Ishikawa · 076-252-7077, Ishikawa

    Local sake brewery in Kanazawa.
