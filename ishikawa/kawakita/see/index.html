Sights in Kawakita, Ishikawa

Kawakita, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 6 places

See

  • Basho no Watashi

    Kawakita, Ishikawa · 076-277-1111, Ishikawa

    Ferry crossing where the haiku master Matsuo Basho stopped on 7 September 1689 (Genroku 2) during his Kaga journey and composed a verse.

  • Fureai Kenko Center

    Kawakita, Ishikawa · 076-277-1110, Ishikawa

    Day-trip hot-spring and leisure facility with a spacious communal bath.

  • Kato Washi

    Kawakita, Ishikawa · 090-4689-6055, Ishikawa

    Washi paper workshop making gampi paper, which is fine, strong, smooth and glossy; experiences and souvenirs.

  • Kusabuka Jinshiro Monument

    Kawakita, Ishikawa · 076-277-1111, Ishikawa

    Monument to the swordsman Kusabuka Jinshiro, who was active in the Sengoku period.

  • Sakura-zutsumi

    Kawakita, Ishikawa · 076-277-1111, Ishikawa

    Cherry-blossom embankment at the foot of Tatsunokuchi Bridge by the Tedori River.

  • Tomita Sukunabikona Shrine Stone Shrine

    Kawakita, Ishikawa · 076-277-1111, Ishikawa

    Stone shrine in the Tachibana-shinden district, designated a cultural property of Kawakita.
