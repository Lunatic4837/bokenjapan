Things to do in Nomi, Ishikawa

Nomi, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 6 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Shirayamasan

    Nomi, Ishikawa · 076-273-4851, Ishikawa

    Roadside station at the gateway to the Hakusan foothills in Wasadani, Nomi, on Route 157, with a multipurpose rest building, an information building and a restaurant.

  • Tatsunokuchi Onsen Soyu Satoyama no Yu

    Nomi, Ishikawa · 0761-51-2183, Ishikawa

    Nomi City hot-spring exchange hall that promotes health and local exchange through the use of hot-spring water.

  • Kutani Togei Mura (Kutani Pottery Village)

    Nomi, Ishikawa · 0761-58-6102, Ishikawa

    Kutani ware pottery complex in Izumidai, Nomi, with an annual pottery-village festival.

  • Kutani Ware Painting Experience (Ultraman Series)

    Nomi, Ishikawa · 0761-58-6300, Ishikawa

    Kutani ware painting experience in Nomi featuring Ultraman-series designs.

  • Miyamoto Shuzo Shop

    Nomi, Ishikawa · 0761-51-3333, Ishikawa

    Sake brewery shop in Nomi, Ishikawa.

  • Tatsunokuchi Onsen Footbath

    Nomi, Ishikawa · 0761-58-2251, Ishikawa

    Footbath in Nomi's Tatsunokuchi Onsen area.
