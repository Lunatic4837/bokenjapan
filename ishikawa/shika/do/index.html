Things to do in Shika, Ishikawa

Shika, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 5 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Togi Umi Kaido

    Shika, Ishikawa · 0767-42-0975, Ishikawa

    Roadside station on the Noto coast, a one-minute walk from Masuho-ura beach, where sakura shells wash up.

  • Michi-no-Eki Koro Kaki no Sato Shika

    Shika, Ishikawa · 0767-32-4831, Ishikawa

    Roadside station whose Shunsaikan market sells morning-picked vegetables from 246 local farms, as well as seafood and folk crafts.

  • Noto Resort Area Masuho-ura

    Shika, Ishikawa · 0767-42-2125, Ishikawa

    Outdoor resort on the Masuho-ura coast of Shika, facing a designated bathing beach.

  • Oshima Campground and Beach

    Shika, Ishikawa · 090-4329-3134, Ishikawa

    Campground and swimming beach on the Noto Peninsula in Shika.

    Shika, Ishikawa · 0767-32-5566, Ishikawa

    Resort facility in Shika, Ishikawa.
