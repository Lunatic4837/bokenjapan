Things to do in Suzu, Ishikawa

Suzu, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 6 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Suzu Shioda Village (Oku-Noto Shioda Mura)

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-87-2040, Ishikawa

    Preserves the age-old agehama salt-field method of salt making, the only one left in Japan, with salt recipes and an online shop.

  • Michi-no-Eki Noroshi

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-86-2525, Ishikawa

    Roadside station at the northern tip of the Noto Peninsula, known for tofu made from local Ohama soybeans.

  • Suzu Onsen Notoji-so

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-84-1621, Ishikawa

    Hot-spring inn in Suzu combining natural surroundings, quality hot spring and local cuisine, without luxury facilities.

  • Sogen Shuzo

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-84-1314, Ishikawa

    Sake brewery at the tip of the Noto Peninsula in Suzu, founded in 1768 (Meiwa 5).

  • Suzu Ceramics Centre

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-3221, Ishikawa

    City-run ceramics centre in Suzu, home of Suzu ware pottery.

  • Sashi-ami Gill-Net Fishing Experience

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-4688, Ishikawa

    Experience of gill-net fishing, in which a net is set across the path of fish so they become tangled in the mesh.
