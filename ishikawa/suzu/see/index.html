  • Mitsukejima (Gunkanjima)

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-7776, Ishikawa

    Battleship-shaped rock island off Suzu, one of the best-known sights of the Noto Peninsula.

  • Rokkosaki Lighthouse (Noroshi Lighthouse)

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-7776, Ishikawa

    Lighthouse at the tip of the Noto Peninsula in Noroshi-machi, Suzu.

  • Suzu Misaki Capes

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-7776, Ishikawa

    Collective name for the capes of Rokkosaki, Kongosaki and Nagatesaki, with Himejima, Onijima, Kamijima and other islets offshore.

  • Kinoura Village

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-86-2014, Ishikawa

  • Godzilla Rock

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-7776, Ishikawa

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-7776, Ishikawa

  • Suzu Shrine

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-88-2772, Ishikawa

    Shrine with two halls, Takakura-gu and Kinbun-gu, enshrining a divine couple.

  • Ni-Shichi Morning Market

    Suzu, Ishikawa, Ishikawa

    Iida morning market held for about 400 years since the Muromachi period, on days ending in 2 and 7.

  • Matsunagi Coast

    Suzu, Ishikawa · 0768-82-7776, Ishikawa
