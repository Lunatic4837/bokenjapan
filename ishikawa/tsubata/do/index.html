Things to do in Tsubata, Ishikawa

Tsubata, Ishikawa: things to do, food and stays · Ishikawa · 3 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Kurikara Genpei no Sato

    Tsubata, Ishikawa · 076-288-8668, Ishikawa

    Roadside station and Kurikara-juku centre selling local vegetables and regional specialities.

  • Kurikara Pass (Takehashiguchi)

    Tsubata, Ishikawa · 076-288-1054, Ishikawa

    Historic Kurikara Pass on the Hokuriku Road, an official highway since ancient times, with a roadside station entrance, experiences and souvenirs.

  • Kuze Shuzo Shop

    Tsubata, Ishikawa · 076-289-2028, Ishikawa

    Sake brewery shop in Tsubata, whose motto is that good sake needs good rice.
