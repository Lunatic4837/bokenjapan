Things to do in Hirono, Iwate

Hirono · Iwate · 3 places

Do

  • Hirono Fisheries Hall Uniiku

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-5161

    Opened on March 26, 2014 as a new tourism information and experience exchange base for Hirono Town.

  • Taneichi Seaside Park Campsite

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-5916

    A campsite located at Taneichi Seaside Park. The calm sea with white sand stretches out in front of you.

  • Mt. Kujihira

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-77-2111

    Kujihiradake is 706m above sea level. This is a great hiking course that is 5km from the trailhead to the top and takes about an hour to walk.
