Sights

  • Hamanasu-tei Main Store

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-2981

    Hamanasutei (main store) is open for business in an orange prefabricated store.

  • Edogahama Donburi

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-53-4989

    A new local cuisine certified as a "Food Master" in 1998. A rice bowl full of seafood, made with plenty of sea urchin, a specialty of Hirono Town.

  • Kiriya Seafood Restaurant

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-2971

    The restaurant's popular diver set meal is a restaurant where you can enjoy sea squirt in a variety of ways, including sashimi, grilled, and fried sea squirt.

  • Yume Milk / Yume Yogurt

  • Yuuki Center

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-77-4379

  • Kohachiya

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-5222

    The sea in Taneichi is lively! The luxurious delicacies of sea urchin and abalone are specialties, and you can eat them fresh.

  • Hirono Fisheries Hall Uniiku

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-5161

  • Taneichi Farm-Direct Fureai Plaza

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-3910

  • Taneichi Seaside Park Campsite

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-5916

  • Konbu-maki (Kelp Rolls)

    This is a traditional dish from Hirono Town.

  • Gardening Workshop

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-77-3202

  • Mt. Kujihira

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-77-2111

    Kujihiradake is 706m above sea level. This is a great hiking course that is 5km from the trailhead to the top and takes about an hour to walk.

  • Tainai Kuguri Rock Passage

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-77-2111

    Tainai Kuguri, located at the 8th station of Mt. Kujihiradake, has strangely shaped rocks measuring 10 meters square and lined up at 50 centimeter intervals, and it is said that walking through these rocks will relieve your lower back pain.

  • Nakano Shirataki

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-5916

    Nakano Shirataki is a large waterfall with two tiers, the upper waterfall is 27 meters high and 8 meters wide, and the lower waterfall is 6 meters high and 12 meters wide.

  • Takizawa Otaki

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-2111

    A 20-meter-high, 15-meter-wide waterfall surrounded by virgin forest behind Takizawa Shrine.

  • Window Rock

    Hirono, Iwate · 0194-65-2111
