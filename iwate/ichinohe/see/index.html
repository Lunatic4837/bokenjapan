Sights in Ichinohe, Iwate

Ichinohe · Iwate · 10 places

See

  • Old House Restaurant Urara-tei

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-32-3551

    This restaurant was opened in an old house that was built in the early Meiji period and is over 100 years old, and is located on a small hill near Goshono Jomon Park.

  • Okunakayama Highland Gelato Yukiakari

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-35-3131

    Gelato carefully made with fresh milk from Okunakayama Highlands.

  • Bandaikan

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-33-2211

    It was founded in 1909 as a puppet theater in a converted storehouse, and began screening movies in the Taisho era.

  • Ichinohe Town Tourism Observatory

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-33-1211

    There is a reflecting telescope with a diameter of 50 centimeters, and you can use a computer to search for the stars you want to see.

  • Takamori Plateau

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-33-2111

    Altitude: 668m. It is covered with approximately 500 hectares of vast pastureland, and the contrast between the sparkling sun, the bright green grass, and the blue sky is impressive.

  • Goshono Ruins [World Heritage Site]

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-32-2652

    On July 27, 2021, "Jomon Sites in Hokkaido and the Northern Tohoku Region," including the Goshono Ruins, were decided to be inscribed on the World Heritage List at the 44th session of the World Heritage Committee (held online).

  • Canaan Ranch

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-35-3505

    A handmade bread factory located on a refreshing plateau. They also sell their products at a factory that makes handmade breads using Nishidake's spring water, 100% ground flour, millet grains, and handmade jams.

  • Ichinohe Handicraft Museum

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-33-3993

    On display are wooden and bamboo accessories, furniture, and sakiori made by craftsmen in Ichinohe Town.

  • Torigoe Kannon

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-33-2111

    This is an old temple that is said to have been founded by Jikaku Daishi in the early Heian period (early 9th century).

  • Fujishima Wisteria

    Ichinohe, Iwate · 0195-33-2111

    A huge Nodafuji tree with beautiful branches is located in Kotoridani in the town.
