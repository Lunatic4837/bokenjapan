Sights

  • Millet Park Handmade Soba

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-47-1452

    At Millet Plaza in Millet Park, the handmade soba noodles made from 100% local buckwheat flour ground in a stone mill are exquisite.

  • Uchimaruya

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-2318

    You can enjoy locally produced dishes such as cereal set meals and Dattasoba.

  • Senbonmatsu (Local Sweet)

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-2528

    "Senbonmatsu" was named after the town's designated natural monument.

  • Tartary Buckwheat Soba

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-2318

    Tartary soba is said to contain 100 times more rutin, which strengthens capillaries, than Japanese soba.

  • Tartary Buckwheat Soba and Dried Grain Noodles

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-2301

    Made at a long-established noodle factory in Karumai town, using traditional natural drying methods.

  • Soba Mooncakes

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-2528

    Mooncakes are a sweet that originated in China and are said to have been served during celebrations of the Mid-Autumn Harvest Moon.

  • Sarunashi (Hardy Kiwi)

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-43-4666

    The fruit, which ripens to a light green color in autumn, is high in vitamin C and is said to be effective as a tonic and to stimulate the appetite.

  • Harutaka Dondon Market

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-3115

    A farm direct sales store with freshly harvested vegetables and handmade sweets.

  • Gokoku Hojo Cake

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-2528

    Pound ke0ki is made with butter, flour, sugar, and eggs. To the ingredients of the cake, millet, buckwheat, amaranth, green beans and black beans containing savonin and linoleic acid are added to lock in the power of minor grains for a luxurious finish.

  • Heartful Sportsland

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-3880

    Heartful Sports Land, surrounded by a rich natural environment full of greenery, was established as a place for intergenerational interaction where everyone from infants to the elderly can enjoy health promotion together.

  • History and Folklore Museum / Hall of History and Folk Tales

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-4232

    History and Folklore Museum/Hall of History and Folklore

  • Tokurakuji Temple

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-46-2944

    Tokuraku-ji Temple (Soto sect) was founded in 1558 as a branch temple of Hoko-ji Temple in Nagawa-cho, Aomori Prefecture.

  • Mill Miru House

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-47-1030

    Located at the confluence of National Routes 340 and 395, this facility not only sells special products from Karumai Town and sells agricultural products directly, but also serves as a restaurant and rest area.

  • Yukiyagawa Dam Forest Park Karumai

    Karumai, Iwate · 0195-45-2444

    A beautiful forest park where water and greenery are in harmony with the forest and dam landscape.
