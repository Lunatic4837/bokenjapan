Sights

  • Machi no Eki Masazane-kan

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-2226

    Masazane-kan, a town station, opened in July 2011.

  • Nanbu Kakke (Soba Kakke)

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-3148

    "Nanbu Kakke" is a local dish that has been passed down from ancient times in the village of Kunohe in the southern part of Iwate, a soba restaurant.

  • Amacha (Sweet Hydrangea Tea)

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-4400

    Although it is said to be 200 times sweeter than sugar, it is calorie-free and has beauty and health benefits such as suppressing allergies, ulcers, and bad breath, purifying the blood, diuretic, and regulating the intestines.

  • Yamabundo Wild Grape Juice

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-4400

    Since ancient times, wild grapes have been said to be good for recovering from fatigue, fortifying the body, and because they contain a lot of iron, they are also good for anemia.

  • Soba-Making Experience

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-4001

    We are holding a soba making experience at the Furusato Sozokan.

  • Koropokkurland

    A play zone built at the foot of Mt. Orizume.

  • Orizume Five Falls

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-2111

  • Pocket Park Fureai Plaza

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-2111

    It is divided into a relaxing space with benches, an outdoor stage, and play equipment, and a parking space with a bus waiting room and restrooms.

  • Toiryosawa Skunk Cabbage Park

    Kunohe, Iwate · 0195-42-2111

    Along the clear stream of the stream, skunk cabbage and Japanese staghorn cabbage bloom with pretty flowers, and there is a promenade where you can observe the flowers up close.

  • Furusato Sozokan

  • Himebotaru Firefly Habitat on Mt. Orizume

    Kunohe, Iwate

    The area near the summit of Mt. Oritsume has abundant spring water and a Quercus forest, making it one of the largest habitats for fireflies in Japan.
