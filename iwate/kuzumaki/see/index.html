  • Mori no Sobaya

    Kuzumaki, Iwate · 0195-66-4111

    Handmade soba noodles are made by grinding locally produced buckwheat flour using a water wheel.

  • Kuzumaki Nabe (Hot Pot)

    Kuzumaki, Iwate · 0195-66-2111

    This is a single-serve hotpot made with Kuzumaki-cho's abundant local specialties such as meat, vegetables, dairy products, and red wine, and topped with flat noodles produced in the town, "Hibogahat".

  • Kuzumaki Wine

    Kuzumaki, Iwate · 0195-66-3111

    Wild grapes are rare because of their small fruits, cluster volume, and low yield, and have been said to be effective in nourishing and tonicizing, increasing blood flow, and relieving fatigue since ancient times.
