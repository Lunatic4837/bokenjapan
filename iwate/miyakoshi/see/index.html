  • Masaki Kaigan Kominato Beach

    Miyako, Iwate · 0193-62-2111

    A cape that juts out to the north of Taro Bay, it offers a panoramic view from Myojinzaki to Todogasaki, the easternmost tip of Honshu, and is also suitable for rock fishing.

  • Aneyoshi Campsite

    Miyako, Iwate · 0193-68-2316

    This campground is located at the starting point of the promenade to Todokasaki Lighthouse, located on the easternmost tip of Honshu.

  • Janome Main Store

    Miyako, Iwate · 0193-62-1383

    A fish restaurant located right in front of Miyako Station. Nigiri and sashimi sushi using fresh seafood caught in the Sanriku region.

  • Yakushi River Gorge

    Miyako, Iwate · 0193-76-2165

    Many yamame trout and char live in the mountain stream, which stretches for about 10km from its confluence with the Oguni River.

  • Kukai Kogen Walking Center

    Miyako, Iwate · 0193-77-2216

    A facility that provides information about the nature of Kukai Kogen, including walking routes and flora and fauna, to visitors to Kukai Kogen.

  • Gakkura no Sato Pickles

    Miyako, Iwate · 0193-65-7735

    This pickled radish is called "Gakkurazuke" because of the way it is cut off with a machete.
