Sights

  • Kitagawa Shokudo Gochiso-don

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-34-2251

    The "5-layer rice bowl", which was created after the geological formations in Tanohata, is a popular menu item with rice, strawberry stew, seaweed, rice, and a seafood delicacy piled up in five layers.

  • Kitayamazaki Rest House

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-33-2021

    Kitayamazaki Rest House has a souvenir shop on the first floor and a restaurant on the second and third floors.

  • Loreol Tanohata

    Tanohata, Iwate · 080-9014-9000

    The sea and vast land of Sanriku. Iwate, blessed with great nature, is truly a treasure trove of ingredients.

  • Banya Cooking Experience

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-37-1211

    Enjoy freshly caught sea urchin, wakame seaweed, sea squirt, and more by cutting them yourself at the banya or on the Sappa boat.

  • Tanohata Dairy Products

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-34-2555

    Tanohata milk, ice cream, and yogurt are popular for their rich flavor and are made from milk produced with great care by Tanohata dairy farmers, and are the specialty products of Tanohata Village.

  • Shii Ohashi Bridge

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-34-2111

    With a total length of 315m and a height of 120m, it is one of the largest "Reverse Rose Bridges" in Japan, spanning the Matsumae swamp.

  • Kitayamazaki Cliff Cruise Tourist Boat

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-33-2113

    You can enjoy the impressive view of Kitayamazaki, a scenic spot representing Sanriku Fukko National Park, from the ocean.

  • Kitayamazaki

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-33-3248

    "Kitayamazaki", which has large cliffs approximately 200m high that extend for approximately 8km, boasts the most impressive coastal beauty among all the coastal scenery in Japan, and was ranked in the coastal category of the tourism resource evaluation…

  • Sugakubo deer dance

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-34-2226

    The Sugakubo Deer Dance, which has been designated as an Iwate Prefecture Intangible Folk Cultural Property, is a performing art in which participants wear a deer head that resembles a real deer and dance bravely like a wild deer.

  • Haipe Koikorobe Cretaceous Fossil Site

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-33-3248

    Hype Koikorobe Cretaceous Fossil Locality

  • Tanohata Village Folklore Museum

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-33-2210

    This museum focuses on materials from the Sankei Uprisings of 1847 and 1853, which are said to be the pinnacle of the peasant uprisings in terms of logic, leadership, and organizational skills.

  • Kitayamazaki Sappa Boat Adventures

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-37-1211

    This is a thrilling tour of Kitayamazaki's cliffs and sea caves aboard a sappa boat, a small rock boat still in use by fishermen.

  • Unosu Cliff

    Tanohata, Iwate

    The Unosu Cliff, which offers a view of five layers of cliffs approximately 200m high, has a raised coast unique to the northern Rikuchu coast.As the name suggests, the cliffs are nesting sites for sea cormorants and eagles.

  • Omiya Kagura

    Tanohata, Iwate · 0194-34-2226

    Omiya Kagura, which descends from Yamabushi Kagura, is designated as an Intangible Folk Cultural Property by Iwate Prefecture, and is dedicated to the lion head of Omiya Gongen, celebrating people's health and praying for warding off troubles.
