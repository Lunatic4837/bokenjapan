Things to do in Aira, Kagoshima

Aira · Kagoshima · 12 places

Do

  • Starland AIRA

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-68-0688

    Experience facility with a planetarium and a 40-centimeter telescope for stargazing.

  • Shigetomi Coast Beach

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-66-3145

    White sand and pines stretch hundreds of meters in Kirishima-Kinkowan National Park, facing Sakurajima across tidal flats.

  • Saezuri no Mori Campground and Takakura Observatory

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-6666

    From 125 meters, greenery, Kajiki town, Kinko Bay, and Sakurajima fill one frame; camping is available.

  • Takaoka Park

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-66-3111

    155-meter hilltop park lit up in cherry season, with Sakurajima views and a carrot slide.

  • Sumiyoshi Pond Park Camp Village

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-66-3145

    Tents and bungalows by Sumiyoshi Pond in Imuta Pond Prefectural Natural Park; 4 km around, 30 m deep.

  • Kamo Country Club

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-52-0381

    Opened over 40 years ago, a strategic course using natural terrain, the prefecture's only 27-hole course.

  • Kagoshima Takamaki Country Club

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-52-1603

    LPGA T-Point Ladies tournament course. Flat strategic layout using nature. Attached lodging has 28 rooms.

  • Fontana Hill Kamou

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-52-1218

    Blends three springs including Kyushu's finest sulfate spring. Kagoshima lunches, salt-free sugar-free meals, and day-use bathing.

  • Ryumon Pottery and Health Village Tomu Land

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-1000

    Choose hand-building, wheel, or painting courses for all ages. Plaza offers fitness equipment, gear rentals, slides, and jungle gyms.

  • Ryumon Falls Onsen

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-2488

    Closed after August 2025 rain damage. Onsen overlooks Ryumon Falls, one of Japan's 100 waterfalls.

  • Kinko Bay Tidal Flat Creature Observation Tour

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-67-6042

    Expert-guided creature observation at Shigetomi Coast, Kinko Bay's largest inner tidal flat.

  • Sakurajima Service Area (Inbound) Dog Run

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-66-5180

    Service area with views of active volcano Sakurajima and a mini off-leash dog run for small and medium dogs.
