Sights in Aira, Kagoshima

Aira · Kagoshima · 19 places

See

  • Shirogane-zaka Historic Road

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-65-1553

    Stone-paved slope of about 2.7 km, a former difficult section of the Satsuma Kaido.

  • Yamada Triumphal Arch

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-65-1553

    Rare Japanese triumphal arch, built in 1906 to mark safe return from the Russo-Japanese War.

  • Ryumon Falls

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-67-6052

    A 46-meter-high, 43-meter-wide falls, long famed in Satsuma and selected for Japan's 100 Waterfalls in 1990.

  • Ryumonji Slope

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-67-6052

    Stone-paved road begun in 1635; 486.8 meters remain. Saigo's army passed in 1877. Chosen among 100 Historic Roads.

  • Muku Hatoju Literature Memorial Museum

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-4800

    Museum of Muku Hatoju, Japan's first serious animal-literature writer, opened in 1990 with manuscripts and relics.

  • Great Camphor Tree of Kamo

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-52-8400

    Japan's largest camphor, about 1,600 years old; root 33.5 m, eye-level trunk 24.22 m, height about 30 m.

  • Kamo Hachiman Shrine

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-52-8400

    Founded in 1123; holds one national Important Cultural Property bronze mirror plus 116 others.

  • Ryuga Castle Sanskrit Cliff Carvings

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-2111

    About 1,700 Sanskrit characters across 120 m; called Japan's most in one place; city-designated cultural property.

  • Shirokane Shuzo Stone Storehouse Museum

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-67-1496

    Meiji-era handmade shochu brewery with process viewing, sweet potato shochu history, Saigo Takamori ties, VR, and workshops.

  • Nanpo Bunshi Grave

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-2111

    Grave of monk Bunshi, born in 1555, who invented the Bunshi-ten reading method. National historic site since 1936.

  • Kuwashihoko Shrine

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-5716

    Shrine to 17th Shimazu lord Yoshihiro. Seasonal cherry blossoms; basin and mill brought from the Bunroku campaign.

  • Nagisa Park Aira

    Aira, Kagoshima

    Sakurajima views, sea breeze, and clear water with small fish. Sunday evening fresh-fish market behind; refreshing promenade for walks.

  • Shigetomi Coast Nature Interaction Hall Nagisa Museum

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-73-3146

    Introduces Shigetomi Coast in Kirishima-Kinkowan National Park. At low tide, tidal flats appear for creature encounters.

  • Kamo Okariya Gate

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-2111

    Main gate of Kamo's administrative and military steward residence, with bell tower, curved tile roof, and riveted double doors.

  • Okariya Inumaki Tree

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-2111

    Garden inumaki said to be 400 years old. Called hitotsuba in Kagoshima for its shape, it evokes Edo foothill scenery.

  • Kamo Castle Ruins

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-67-6052

    Kamo clan castle on 160m Mt. Ryuga: 1,700 cliff Sanskrit characters, called Japan's most, and 200 spring cherries.

  • Kakehashi Slope

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-62-2111

    Edo-period local road linking Imuta and Kedoin with Kamo, known as the hardest stretch for tribute-rice hauling.

  • Kamo Fumoto

    Aira, Kagoshima · 0995-66-3145

    A functional town layout remains between two rivers, with many samurai gates and Japan's largest camphor tree.

  • Nanohana Road along the Hikiyama River

    Aira, Kagoshima

    Rapeseed flowers bloom about 800 meters along the Hikiyama River in Kajiki, forming a yellow carpet.
