Day to day

  • Akune Roadside Station

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    Roadside station on National Route 3 facing the East China Sea, with local products, a restaurant, and EV charging.

  • Iso no Aji Kuro-no-seto

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-75-0023

    Pumps fresh seawater 24 hours to keep tanks sea-like; seats overlook the sea and Kuronoseto Bridge.

  • Furusato Jusan

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-72-1420

    Hosts Akune sea urchin bowl festival in April-May and spiny lobster festival in September-October; kibisuki year-round.

  • Nigiwai Exchange Hall Akune Station

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-4850

    Station renovated in 2014 by Eiji Mitooka, with wooden hall, kids space, cafeteria, book corner, cafe, and specialty shop.

  • Sunset & Coffee Akune Roadside Station

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-74-1400

    Coffee stand at Akune Roadside Station serves coffee, craft beer, and frappuccino with East China Sea views.

  • Iwashi Building

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-3104

    Renovated dried-fish shop founded in 1939, with a cafe, shop, processing floor, and hostel.

  • Terrace Cafe Sora

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-68-1110

    Cafe in Farm Garden Sora serving open sandwiches and parfaits made with homegrown fruit, vegetables, and herbs.
