Things to do in Akune, Kagoshima

Akune · Kagoshima · 11 places

Do

  • Wakimoto Beach

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    Shallow 3 km sandy beach for surfing and marine sports, where sea turtles come to nest.

  • Akune Onsen

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    Nearest onsen to crane grounds; a salty radon spring known for beauty and health.

  • Akune Oshima Campground

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-72-5920

    Uninhabited island 4 km around, about ten minutes by boat, with marine sports and wild deer.

  • Banshogaoka Park

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-3777

    Go-kart, putter golf, roller skating and ground golf facilities. Known for spring cherry blossoms and flower beds.

  • Okawashima Beach

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    Calm inlet beach safe for families. Nearby ria coast. Recommended for swimming and strolling amid grand natural views.

  • Akune Oshima Park

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    Pine-covered island about 10 minutes by boat, where roughly 120 wild deer can be touched.

  • Kimiyoshi Onsen

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-72-1174

    Akune Onsen beauty-and-health bath; strong saline spring with indoor and day-visit baths.

  • Somen Nagashi Onoan

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-79-2130

    Opened in 2020 after renovating Akune's beloved Tashiro somen nagashi. Enjoy noodles by a mountain stream; shop sells soft serve.

  • Farm Garden Sora

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-3685

    Retreat space in citrus-producing Akune with seasonal garden walks, fruit and herb harvesting, and a cafe.

  • wag Tail SUP Experience

    Akune, Kagoshima · 080-5802-4872

    SUP on Akune's coast from late April to late October, including adventure and dog courses.

  • Sea Urchin Shell Art Experience

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-72-3646

    Paint and decorate removed inedible sea urchin shells into unique art. Anytime, 90 minutes, ages 5 and up.
