Sights in Akune, Kagoshima

Akune · Kagoshima · 4 places

See

  • Kuronoseto Strait and Bridge

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    One of Japan's three great tidal rapids between Akune and Nagashima, with whirlpools and fishing.

  • Kasayama Tourist Farm

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    Hilltop view of hydrangeas, greenery, and the East China Sea, with former terrace stone steps.

  • Ushinohama Coast

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    East China Sea coast of odd rocks, with Koshikijima views, sunsets and fishing lights. Red torii tops a 20-meter rock.

  • Terashima Munenori Memorial Museum

    Akune, Kagoshima · 0996-73-1114

    Former home of Meiji Restoration figure Terashima Munenori, father of Japanese telegraphy, and sole surviving Satsuma Student house.
