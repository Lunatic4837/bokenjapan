Things to do in Amagi, Kagoshima

Amagi · Kagoshima · 3 places

Do

  • Waidomu Amagi

    Amagi, Kagoshima · 0997-85-5149

    Traditional culture and tourism complex with Unbuki cave experiences and a Tokunoshima produce shop.

  • Amami Rabbit Observation Hut

    Amagi, Kagoshima · 0997-85-5178

    Watch the national natural monument Amami rabbit on a large monitor; cameras record from 8 p.m. to 6 a.m.

  • Senma Coast

    Amagi, Kagoshima · 0997-85-5382

    Representative Tokunoshima dive site: three points, caves, arches, coral, fish watching, sea turtles, and winter cuttlefish spawning.
