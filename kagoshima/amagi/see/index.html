Sights in Amagi, Kagoshima

Amagi · Kagoshima · 3 places

See

  • Mushirose

    Amagi, Kagoshima · 0997-85-5382

    Grand granite coast rare in the south, with mat-like rocks, winter spray, fishing, and sharinbai dye plants.

  • Inu no Monbuta

    Amagi, Kagoshima · 0997-85-5382

    Raised coral forms odd rocks, cliffs, caves, and Glasses Rock. Named for wild dogs thrown into the sea during famine.

  • Saigo Nanshu Landing Monument

    Amagi, Kagoshima

    Saigo stayed here two and a half months in 1862 before exile to Okinoerabujima. A monument marks the site.
