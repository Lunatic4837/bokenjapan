Do in Amami

Amami · Kagoshima · 11 places

Do

  • Ohama Seaside Park and Kohama Campground

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-55-6000

    Naze's top beach with emerald-green sea, white coral sand, snorkeling, camping, sunsets, and turtle nesting.

  • Kinsakubaru

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-52-1111

    Amami Oshima forest retaining subtropical trees and rare wildlife; a certified eco-tour guide is required.

  • Honba Amami Oshima Tsumugi Mud-Dyeing Park

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-54-9088

    Preserved mud fields where Oshima tsumugi mud-dyeing can be viewed and original T-shirts made.

  • Kuroshio Forest Mangrove Park (Roadside Station Amami Oshima Sumiyo)

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-56-3355

    Second-largest mangrove forest in Japan; canoe tours through tunnels are possible for beginners after instruction.

  • Yoan Coast

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-52-1111

    A 1.9 km white-sand coast in northern Amami Oshima, popular for swimming and marine leisure.

  • Ayamaru Cape Tourist Park

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1170

    Northern tip cape of Amami Oshima with coral reefs, cycad jungle, and many leisure activities.

  • Kenmun Village

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1178

    Island song and pottery experiences by reservation, about ten minutes by car from Amami Airport.

  • Kagoshima Prefectural Amami Youth Nature House

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-53-1032

    Only prefectural youth education facility in the Oshima district, offering training, marine programs, and group sports camps.

  • Amami Resort Basha Yamamura

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1178

    Pacific-facing resort with a white beach, hotel, campsite, marine activities, and Amami experiences.

  • Island Cuisine Gintei

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-52-9646

    Amami local-cuisine restaurant on Yanigawa Street with brown-sugar shochu and island-song live shows for clapping and dancing.

  • Amami Tourism Habu Center

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-52-1505

    Screens the world's only habu and mongoose show, explains habu ecology and bite prevention, and sells products.
