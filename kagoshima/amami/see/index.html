Sights in Amami, Kagoshima

Amami · Kagoshima · 12 places

See

  • Amami Ocean Exhibition Hall

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-55-6000

    Exhibits Amami's sea, people, nature, and unique fish and shellfish; coral viewing and handicrafts.

  • Tanaka Isson's Final Home

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-52-1111

    House where painter Tanaka Isson lived and painted Amami nature until his death in 1977 at 69.

  • Tsuchimori Coast

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1111

    Deep green sea nicknamed Blue Angel, near the airport on the way to Ayamaru Cape.

  • Amami Park

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-55-2333

    Introduces Amami nature, history, culture, and industry, centered on Amami no Sato and Tanaka Isson Memorial Art Museum.

  • Tanaka Ichi Village Kinen Art Museum

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-55-2635

    Museum in Amami, Kagoshima.

  • Kamozaki Tourist Park

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1111

    Northern Amami Oshima park; parking-lot cherries greet arrivals; observatory views East China Sea and mountains.

  • Amandee

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1111

    Said to be where gods Amamiko and Shinireku first descended; views of villages and the Pacific.

  • Suno Dam

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1111

    Kasari's most famous cherry-blossom spot; a three-kilometer cherry avenue encircles the dam for walks.

  • Yo Observatory

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-63-1111

    Northernmost observatory on Amami Oshima, on a hill overlooking Yo village and the Pacific.

  • Amami Museum

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-54-1210

    Unique Amami culture, based on Japanese culture, absorbed Ryukyu and Kagoshima influences. Exhibits highlight differences from Okinawa.

  • Ashikabe Nanshu Shrine

    Amami, Kagoshima

    Shrine to Saigo Takamori built in 1940 with a divided spirit from Kagoshima City's Nanshu Cemetery.

  • Amami Oshima World Heritage Center

    Amami, Kagoshima · 0997-69-2281

    Opened July 2022 after July 2021 World Natural Heritage registration, with forest exhibits and conservation displays.
