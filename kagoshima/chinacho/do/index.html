Things to do in Chinacho, Kagoshima

Chinacho · Kagoshima · 3 places

Do

  • Okinoerabu Island Caving Tour

    Chinacho, Kagoshima

    Tour exploring natural caves and limestone caverns on Okinoerabu Island, said to hold about 300 caves.

  • Yakomo Beach

    Chinacho, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3162

    Swimming beach with white sand and coral, showers and toilets; Yoron and Okinawa visible on clear days.

  • Okidomari Seaside Park

    Chinacho, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3162

    Large Okinoerabu beach; tide pools hold tropical fish and crabs. Godzilla filmed here. Camping possible; rip currents make swimming unsafe.
