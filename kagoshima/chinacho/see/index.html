  • Shoryudo Cave

    Chinacho, Kagoshima · 0997-93-4536

  • Ujiji Beach

    Chinacho, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3162

  • Okinoerabu Island Bashofu Hall

    Chinacho, Kagoshima · 0997-93-4753

    They handweave bashofu from ito-basho stem fiber using only island plant dyes, fully organic.

  • Jikkyonuho Spring

    Chinacho, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3162

  • Oyama Botanical Park and Observatory

    Chinacho, Kagoshima

    Observatory nearly 240m high overlooks the island; clear days may show Tokunoshima, Yoron, and Okinawa. Trails, lodge, camping.

  • Tamina Cape

    Chinacho, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3162

    Cliff of raised coral, 51m, at the island's northwest tip, with cobalt sea and nearby karst.
