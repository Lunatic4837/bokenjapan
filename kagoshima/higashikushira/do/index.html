Things to do in Higashikushira, Kagoshima

Higashikushira · Kagoshima · 3 places

Do

  • Maruyama Park Fureai-no-Mori Campground

    Higashikushira, Kagoshima · 0994-35-1881

    No-fee unpartitioned sites; vehicles can park beside every tent. Toilets, showers, and wooden play structures on site.

  • Kashiwabara Coast

    Higashikushira, Kagoshima · 0994-63-3122

    Beautiful white-sand, green-pine coast at Nichinan Coast Quasi-National Park's south end; April lupine festival and clamming.

  • Community Plaza

    Higashikushira, Kagoshima · 0994-63-3122

    Ikenohara Toyosaka playground and plaza for children and families. Roadside cherries peak late March to early April, lit at night.
