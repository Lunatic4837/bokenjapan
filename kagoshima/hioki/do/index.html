Things to do in Hioki, Kagoshima

Hioki · Kagoshima · 23 places

Do

  • Miyama Pottery Experience Hall

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-274-5778

    Hands-on Satsuma ware practiced for over 420 years, with display and sales of local kiln works.

  • Yunomoto Onsen

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-248-9409

    Discovered around 1640 and visited by Shimazu lords. Colorless simple and sulfur springs with many family baths.

  • Ijuin Forest Park

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-273-9253

    Forest park with 360-degree views of Sakurajima, Kinko Bay and Kirishima, plus a treehouse and camping.

  • Fukiage Onsen

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-248-7380

    Onsen near Fukiage Beach along Yunoura River, loved by Saigo Takamori; some springs change color by season.

  • Yupuru Fukiage Health Exchange Hall

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-296-6022

    Japanese and Western onsen baths rotate daily by gender, with sauna, open-air bath, and pool.

  • Hioki City Ijuin General Sports Park

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-272-2525

    Spacious park with a baseball field, eight tennis courts, a 400m track, a 2km jogging course, and a summer pool.

  • Higashimine Farm

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 090-3736-6665

    Enjoy picking mini tomatoes, blueberries, grapes, and mandarins. Cafe menu uses seasonal fruits and vegetables.

  • Yukaida Onsen Tsurezure no Yu

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-274-5905

    Simple alkaline sulfur onsen; wooden and sky baths switch genders weekly, with seasonal views.

  • Eguchihama Seaside Park

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-248-9409

    First artificial beach on Fukiagehama, facing the East China Sea and famous for sunsets, beside a product hall.

  • Seseragi no Yu Hanamizuki

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-273-1718

    100 percent free-flowing simple onsen by the Konokawa River, with about 2,000 maples.

  • Katahira Tourist Farm

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 090-3323-8123

    Strawberry-picking farm in Hioki with all-you-can-eat and take-home courses, plus soft serve and jam.

  • Ocean Resort Eguchiya

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-295-0130

    Every room has an ocean view. Onsen, restaurant, and terrace overlook the East China Sea. Horizon sunsets are stunning.

  • Seika Foods Hioki Ice Cream Factory Tour

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-272-0030

    Daily ice cream production on a vast site, built for tours of most processes, then tasting. Nearly 200 kinds.

  • Yuu-Yuu Family Bath

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-274-0526

    Private family baths, plus large private and open-air baths for groups. Simple sulfur spring; two day-rest rooms.

  • Tanoyu Onsen

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-274-2219

    Baths split into hot and moderate. Popular smooth beauty bath. Fully free-flowing simple sulfur spring.

  • MUGIUDA BASE

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-273-9105

    Opened August 11, 2022 in Hioki City; electric go-karts reach 37 km/h, plus indoor kids' karts.

  • AGRICIA

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-213-9466

    LED climate-controlled strawberry greenhouse with weigh-and-buy picking and mini-tomato harvests November to May.

  • Forest Adventure Fukiagehama

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 080-2112-1154

    Outdoor park for tree adventures using black pines on Fukiagehama, one of Japan's three great sand dunes.

  • Kajurabo Tourist Farm

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 090-6778-5501

    Cultivates Fujiminori, Shine Muscat, and Queen Nina grapes; Fujiminori is seedless and easy to peel.

  • Yagie's Land Petting Ranch

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 090-7294-0465

    Reservation-only friendly goat petting and feeding for children and adults, plus live goat sales and rentals.

  • Tsukihi Terrace

    Hioki, Kagoshima

    Catch Hioki tsukihi shellfish from tanks and beach-grill them while viewing Eguchi Beach along Fukiagehama.

  • PERI.Garden

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-295-6543

    One of few Kagoshima bouldering gyms, opened July 2023 in Miyama, Hioki, with lessons and a youth school.

  • Hioki City B&G Higashi-Ichiki Marine Center

    Hioki, Kagoshima · 099-274-2401

    Year-round sports facility with a six-lane 25m heated pool, gymnasium, martial arts hall, tennis courts, and sumo ring.
