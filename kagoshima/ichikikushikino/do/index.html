Do in Ichikikushikino

Ichikikushikino · Kagoshima · 7 places

Do

  • Mount Kanmuridake

    Sacred mountain known for mountain Buddhism, medicinal herbs, odd rocks, and a walking course.

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-33-5640 · Official / source link

  • Ichiki Fureai Onsen Center

    Free-flowing sodium chloride-sulfate onsen with indoor and open-air baths, a sauna, and family baths.

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-21-5022 · Official / source link

  • Arakawa Area Fireflies

    Naturally occurring Genji fireflies can be seen at Arakawa River, mid-May to early June, 18:00-20:30.

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-8809 · Official / source link

  • Kushikino Dam Area

    Kushikino Dam on the upper Gotanda River has spring cherries, a playground, and walking trails.

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-3111 · Official / source link

  • Kanmuridake Onsen

    Day-trip onsen in Ichikikushikino amid Kanmuridake nature, with open-air baths, sauna, and an udon restaurant.

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-21-2626 · Official / source link

  • Satsuma-age Factory Tour

    Kanba Kamaboko, Hidaka Fisheries Processing, and Takahama Kamaboko offer factory tours with process explanations.

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · Official / source link

  • Reimei Hashima Sightseeing Boat

    Glass-boat cruises to see island monkeys and fish, retrace young students' route, or follow the wind and waves. Reservation required.

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-31-0050 · Official / source link
