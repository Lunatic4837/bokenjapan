Sights in Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima

Ichikikushikino · Kagoshima · 18 places

See

  • Kanmuridake Garden

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-21-5113

    Chinese-style garden depicting Mount Kanmuridake and the Jofuku legend, suited to strolling.

  • Chikabi Exhibition Hall

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-4747

    Hall at a former tunnel entrance of Japan's first underground oil-storage base, explaining oil and storage.

  • Terushima Coast

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-33-5640

    About 3 km white-sand, pine-lined dune coast at the north end of Fukiagehama Prefectural Natural Park.

  • Departure Site of Satsuma Students for Europe

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-21-5128

    In March 1865, 19 people including Japan's first 15 overseas students secretly sailed from Hashima Port to Europe.

  • Birthplace of Satsuma Ware

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-21-5128

    In 1598, 43 Korean potters landed here and made pottery, later moving to Naeshirogawa.

  • Kannon-ga-ike Citizens' Forest

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-36-2207

    One of 70 prefecture-recommended forest-bathing forests, with a thousand cherries, azaleas, hydrangeas, and night illuminations.

  • Kanmuridake Hanakawa Erosion Control Park

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-0760

    Chinese-style garden at sacred Kanmuridake's foot with spring cherries, summer water lilies, autumn leaves, and rare green Gyoiko.

  • Chingoku-ji Chobo-in Temple

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-21-2255

    Shingon temple on sacred Mount Kanmuridake, revived in 1983 after Meiji suppression.

  • Kushikino Sunset Park

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-5256

    Panoramic East China Sea views; Koshiki Islands on clear days. A spectacular sunset spot.

  • Gotanda Riverside

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-3111

    About 200 Someiyoshino cherries bloom along a 500-meter promenade beside the Gotanda River through the city.

  • Satsuma Domain British Students Memorial Hall

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-35-1865

    Exhibits on Satsuma students who secretly went to Britain and helped modernize Japan, plus a deck, cafe, and library.

  • Nishidake Shrine

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima

    516 m summit of Mt. Kanmuridake in Ichikikushikino; Sakurajima and Kirishima views; New Year sunrise and first shrine visit.

  • Hashima Hamanaka Port Cobblestone Breakwater

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-33-5640

    Breakwater that young Saigo is said to have worked on; one of 100 fishing-village heritage sites.

  • Jitokariya Ruins

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-21-5113

    A Japan Heritage property. Stone walls use part of Kushikino Castle's outer bailey; remaining walls date to around Meiji 30.

  • Fumoto History Museum

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-5256

    Opened in 2014 as a renovated home museum of Kushikino Castle history, keeping samurai-residence atmosphere and a stone storehouse.

  • Satsuma Kushikino Tuna Museum

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-29-5515

    Tuna museum with restaurant and products hall, run by Shinyo Suisan, owner of four tuna fishing boats.

  • Kushikino Fumoto

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima · 0996-32-5256

    Formed around Kushikino Castle; magistrate residence stone walls and a dignified samurai gate remain.

  • Ichiki Osako Family Residence

    Ichikikushikino, Kagoshima

    1916 house registered as a national tangible cultural property in 2018, with a Satsuma samurai-style garden. Reservations required.
