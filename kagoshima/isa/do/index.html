  • Sogochi Youth Travel Village

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-22-0959

    Lakeside campground by Sogochi Pond with play areas, a stone pizza kiln, and a Goemon bath.

  • Kusumoto River Stream Nature Park

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-26-4636

    Nature-rich park in national forest northeast of Hishikari; stream fishing, stargazing, camping, bungalows, and a grass ski slope.

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-22-0313

    Isa farm with over 4,000 pesticide-free blueberry bushes of seven varieties for picking in July and August.

  • Okuchi Distillery Second Distillery Isa Tradition Hall Eiroku

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-26-5500

    Merged from 11 Isa breweries, Okuchi Distillery offers shochu factory tours and tastings by email reservation.
