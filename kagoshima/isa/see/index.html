Sights in Isa, Kagoshima

Isa · Kagoshima · 10 places

See

  • Sogi Falls Park

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-28-2600

    Falls 210 meters wide and 12 meters high, with towering rocks, spring blossoms, and autumn foliage.

  • Koriyama Hachiman Shrine (Shochu Graffiti)

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-23-1311

    National Important Cultural Property; 1559 graffiti is Japan's oldest shochu writing, tied to Okuchi's claim as birthplace.

  • Kedoin Family Residence

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-22-1613

    Mid-Edo Satsuma rural samurai house, estimated around 1653; National Important Cultural Property since 1975.

  • Mount Takaguma

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-22-1613

    Northeast of the Okuchi Basin, this city overlook was a fierce 1877 Satsuma Rebellion battlefield.

  • Okusogochi Gorge

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-22-1111

    Cool 500-meter gorge northwest of Okuchi, with over ten waterfalls and virgin forest.

  • Tadamoto Park

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-29-4113

    Hilltop park overlooking the city, with about two kilometers of cherries lit by a thousand lanterns.

  • Sogi Power Station Ruins

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-23-1311

    Power station ruins 1.5 kilometers downstream from Sogi Falls, still strongly showing the Meiji era.

  • Hakozaki Shrine Main Hall and Attached Palace

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-26-1111

    Main hall regarded as early Muromachi; a prefectural important cultural property and national registered tangible cultural property.

  • Former Fuke Elementary School

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-29-4113

    Wooden school in Isa's northernmost district, closed in 1979 and open to visitors with a borrowed key.

  • Ota Red Spider Lilies (Isa City)

    Isa, Kagoshima · 0995-29-4113

    Rice-field banks near Koriyama Hachiman Shrine bloom red with spider lilies for about 500 meters in early autumn.
