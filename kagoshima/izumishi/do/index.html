Things to do in Izumishi, Kagoshima

Izumishi · Kagoshima · 12 places

Do

  • Izumishi Tsuru Museum Kurein Park Izumi

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-8915

  • Tokozan Park

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    Near town, a 160m summit park overlooking the Shiranui Sea and Izumi Plain, with playgrounds and 330 cherries.

  • Takanoyama Park and Campground

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-82-0543

    Recreation park overlooking Izumi Plain and Yatsushiro Sea, with a 70-meter roller slider and camping.

  • Izumi Sightseeing Ox Cart

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-79-3030

    Guided ox-cart circuit of the Izumi Fumoto samurai district, about 1 km in 20-30 minutes.

  • Cranes and First Sunrise

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-85-5151

    Watch cranes and the first sunrise; warm food and drinks, limited can badges, and calligraphy experience offered.

  • Nukumori no Yu Natural Onsen

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-79-3726

    Natural hot spring in Izumi City with spacious baths, dining, and overnight rooms.

  • Hirari Bicycle Rental HIRARIN

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-79-3030

    Bicycle rental at Hirari tourist office in Izumi Station, offering assist-gear and electric-assist bikes.

  • 2026 New Year's Day Mochi Hospitality at a Samurai Residence Hearth

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-6142

    Only on New Year's Day, mochi is served at the lit hearth of the public Saisho Residence.

  • Orange Olle 2026

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-4777

    Izumi course follows Komenotsu River from a shrine to samurai residences; advance registration required.

  • World Wetlands Day Symposium and Izumi Bird Festa

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-8915

    Held January 31 to February 1, 2026 for Izumi's 20th anniversary, featuring crane tours and a symposium.

  • 2025 3rd Fumoto Asobi Market

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-68-1390

    Held in the plaza beside Izumi Fumoto History Museum, with food, traditional play, and ox carts.

  • 2026 Obon Evening Cool Gathering

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-62-5505

    Annual Obon gathering in the garden of open samurai residence Takezoe, with barley tea, watermelon, and fireworks.
