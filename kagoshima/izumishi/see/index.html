Sights in Izumishi, Kagoshima

Izumishi · Kagoshima · 16 places

See

  • Izumi City Crane Observation Center

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-85-5151

    Winter observatory by reclaimed crane roosts. From mid-October, over 10,000 cranes from Siberia stay until March.

  • Izumi Fumoto Samurai Residence District

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    Nationally selected samurai district with grid roads, stone walls, hedges and gates. Restored Takezoe House opens year-round.

  • Noma Barrier Ruins

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    A city-designated historic site: Satsuma's strict first-class border checkpoint with Higo, established around 1600.

  • Uwaba Plateau (Cosmos Garden)

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    Three-hectare, 500-meter plateau on the Kumamoto border, with rapeseed, peonies, hydrangeas, and about 250,000 cosmos.

  • Hakozaki Hachiman Shrine (Japan's Largest Bell)

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-62-2219

    Features Japan's largest bell, 4 meters high, 3.4 meters in diameter and 5 tons, carved with dancing cranes.

  • Kashikuri Shrine

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-67-2800

    Said to date from the Nara period, long revered as Satsuma's second shrine, with a spring harvest rite.

  • Kannozen-ji

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-84-2075

    Prefecture's oldest Zen temple, founded in 1187 by Shimazu Tadahisa and opened by Eisai in 1194.

  • Izumi Crane Wintering Grounds

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    Over 10,000 cranes from Siberia winter in Izumi from mid-October to March. A national special natural monument.

  • Takagawa Dam Area Park

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    Cherries bloom on the circuit road and lakeside park around Takagawa Dam lake.

  • Special Attack Monument Park

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    Former Izumi naval airfield monument lists sortie members; April 16 memorial and cherry trees.

  • Shinonome no Sato Hydrangea Garden

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-68-2133

    40,000-tsubo garden on mountain slopes with paths. June hydrangeas, November foliage, pottery gallery, and teahouse.

  • Suwa Shrine

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-3340

    Shrine in Izumi Fumoto, a national traditional-buildings district, five minutes from the history museum and open samurai houses.

  • Izumi Fumoto History Museum

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-68-1390

    Strolling base for nationally designated Izumi Fumoto samurai houses, with dioramas, videos, and exhibits.

  • Izumi Fumoto Samurai Residences: Saisho Residence

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-6142

    Saisho samurai house, said to be the oldest in Izumi Fumoto, with archery place, hidden room, and escape route.

  • Yasaka Shrine

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-2111

    Yasaka Shrine grounds hold Japan's largest single-carved Jizo, 4.15 m including the base, built in 1925.

  • Izumi Crane Wintering Grounds

    Izumishi, Kagoshima · 0996-63-8915

    More than 10,000 cranes winter at Izumi Crane Wintering Ground, and many wild birds visit yearly.
