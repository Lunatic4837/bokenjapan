Sights in Kagoshima, Kagoshima

Kagoshima · Kagoshima · 117 places

See

  • Arimura Lava Observatory

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1327

    1946 lava-field observatory on a 1 km trail; close views of Sakurajima's active Minamidake crater and Kinko Bay.

  • Former Kagoshima Spinning Mill Engineer's Residence

    Kagoshima · 099-247-3401

    Residence for British engineers invited for Japan's first Western-style spinning mill. Important Cultural Property and World Heritage.

  • Meiji Restoration Hometown Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-239-7700

    History tourism facility on late-Edo Satsuma and the Meiji Restoration, in Saigo and Okubo's hometown Kajiya-cho.

  • Okubo Toshimichi Bronze Statue

    Kagoshima

    Monument to Okubo Toshimichi, called one of the Meiji Restoration's three great figures, made by Shinya Nakamura in 1979.

  • Kagoshima Prefectural History and Art Center Reimeikan

    Kagoshima · 099-222-5100

    Prefectural historical materials center built on Kagoshima Castle's main bailey site, rich in Restoration-era materials.

  • Kagoshima City Museum of Art

    Kagoshima · 099-224-3400

    City art museum since 1954, with Kagoshima-born painters, Western art from Impressionism onward, Satsuma ware and cut glass.

  • Io World Kagoshima Aquarium

    Kagoshima · 099-226-2233

    Aquarium facing Sakurajima with about 800 species, including Kyushu's only whale sharks in a large tank.

  • Ishibashi Memorial Park and Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-248-6661

    Park with three restored late Edo arch stone bridges and a museum on bridge techniques and history.

  • Saigo Takamori Statue

    Kagoshima

    Eight-meter bronze of Saigo Takamori in army general uniform, completed in 1937 at Shiroyama.

  • Satsuma Gishi Monument

    Kagoshima

    Monument built in 1920 honoring 88 Satsuma samurai who died in the 1753 Horeki flood-control works.

  • Xavier Park

    Kagoshima

    Park with remains of Xavier Church and statues of Francis Xavier, who landed in Kagoshima in 1549.

  • Shoko Shuseikan

    Kagoshima · 099-247-1511

    Shimazu family history museum in Japan's oldest surviving Western-style factory, a World Heritage site since 2015.

  • Shiroyama Park

    Kagoshima · 099-298-5111

    107-meter hill with trails and an observatory viewing Sakurajima, Kinko Bay, and the city; Satsuma Rebellion sites.

  • Sengan-en

    Kagoshima · 099-247-1551

    Shimazu family villa garden built in 1658, treating Sakurajima as a hill and Kinko Bay as a pond.

  • Hayashi Fumiko Literary Monument and Furusato Park

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1366

    Furusato Park has two bronze statues and Hayashi Fumiko's literary monument; her mother was from Sakurajima.

  • Kurokami Buried Torii

    Kagoshima

    The 1914 Sakurajima eruption buried Kurokami Shrine's torii, left unexcavated as a prefectural natural monument.

  • Minato-odori Park

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1354

    Lawn-belt park from City Hall to the coastal road, with a lit stone monument, flat fountain, and year-end illumination.

  • Xavier Landing Monument

    Kagoshima

    Stone monument in Gion-no-su Park facing Kinko Bay, commemorating Xavier's first Christian mission in Kagoshima in 1549.

  • Kagoshima Prefectural Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-223-6050

    Natural history museum founded in 1953 introducing Kagoshima nature spanning 600 kilometers.

  • Kagoshima City Science Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-250-8511

    Science museum on volcanoes and rockets, with a giant Sakurajima model and planetarium.

  • Nagashima Art Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-250-5400

    Hilltop art museum among subtropical trees, overlooking Sakurajima, with about 1,000 works.

  • Oasis Care Yoshino Park (Kagoshima Prefectural Yoshino Park)

    Kagoshima · 099-243-0155

    A 30-hectare lawn park in northeast Kagoshima with an observatory facing Sakurajima.

  • Kodama Art Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-262-0050

    Kagoshima City's first private art museum, opened in 1985 on a wooded 85,600-square-meter site.

  • Miyake Art Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-266-0066

    Displays Kagoshima-related paintings, Satsuma and Ryukyu ceramics, and contemporary Satsuma ware.

  • Kagoshima Shimazu Family Cemetery (Fukusho-ji Ruins)

    Kagoshima

    Ancestral temple of the Shimazu family, preserved as their cemetery and abolished in 1869.

  • Xavier Memorial Cathedral

    Kagoshima · 099-222-3408

    Completed in 1999 in Kagoshima to mark 450 years since Xavier's landing. Exterior evokes trading ships; seats 750.

  • Terukuni Shrine

    Kagoshima · 099-222-1820

    Shrine to Shimazu Nariakira, 28th Shimazu head. Kagoshima City's largest, with a free museum and bronze statues.

  • Karasujima Observatory

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1327

    Observatory overlooking Sakurajima and Kinko Bay on barren Taisho lava that buried the island of Karasujima.

  • Sakurajima Visitor Center

    Kagoshima · 099-293-2443

    Exhibits on Sakurajima's eruptions, growth, plant succession, tourism, and disaster prevention, plus theater and dioramas.

  • Yunohira Observatory

    Kagoshima

    Highest public viewpoint on Sakurajima at 373 meters, overlooking Minamidake, Kinko Bay, and Kagoshima.

  • Sakurajima

    Kagoshima

    Kagoshima's symbol, a rare active volcano combining Kitadake and Minamidake, still emitting smoke and ash.

  • Senganbira Nature Park

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1327

    At 577 meters, views of Sakurajima, Kaimondake, and a glimpse of Lake Ikeda; famous for azaleas.

  • Shinya Nakamura Art Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-246-7070

    A rare sculpture museum in Japan beside sculptor Shinya Nakamura's studio, with about 1,000 works.

  • Irisa Field God

    Kagoshima

    A 1727 field god statue backed by the huge Imoishi rock, a prefectural cultural property.

  • Hanao Hidden Nenbutsu Cave

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1327

    Cave used for secret Jodo Shinshu practice while the Satsuma domain banned the sect.

  • Hanao Shrine

    Kagoshima · 099-298-7669

    Shrine founded in 1218 by Shimazu Tadahisa and called Satsuma Nikko.

  • Tango no Tsubone Tomb

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1327

    Dozens of stone pagodas at Hanao Shrine, including Tango no Tsubone's 1227 tomb.

  • Statue of Lord Shimazu Tadayoshi

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1344

    29th head of the Shimazu family, sculpted in 1917 by Fumio Asakura.

  • Statue of Lord Shimazu Hisamitsu

    Kagoshima

    Bronze statue of Hisamitsu, Nariakira's brother and Tadayoshi's father, at Terukuni Shrine, made in 1917 by Fumio Asakura.

  • Bronze Statue of Hirata Yukie

    Kagoshima · 099-298-5111

    Commissioner of the 1753 Kiso River flood control; 3-meter statue made in 1954 by Ando Shi.

  • Bronze Statue of Admiral Togo Heihachiro

    Kagoshima · 099-298-5111

    Combined Fleet commander who defeated Russia's Baltic Fleet in 1905; 1.8-meter statue made in 1957 by Kohei Amada.

  • Statue of Godai Tomoatsu

    Kagoshima

    Bronze statue of Godai, first head of Osaka's chamber of commerce, made in 1961 by Masakatsu Sakagami.

  • Sakamoto Ryoma Honeymoon Trip Monument

    Kagoshima

    Bronze statue by Shinya Nakamura, installed in 1980, marks Ryoma and Oryo's 1866 Kirishima visit, called Japan's first honeymoon.

  • Young Satsuma Statue Group

    Kagoshima

    Statues of Satsuma students sent to Britain in 1865, at Kagoshima-Chuo Station east exit plaza.

  • Yozan Art Museum

    Kagoshima · 099-239-5555

    Currently closed. Opened in 1999; collection centers on Ebihara and Hirano, and it owns Van Gogh's Paris apartment.

  • Jigen-ryu Heihojo Historical Museum

    Kagoshima

    Displays Jigen-ryu materials including Togo family documents; visitors can view the dojo, watch videos, or book experiences.

  • Kagoshima Castle (Tsurumaru Castle) Goromon

    Kagoshima

    Imposing main gate of Tsurumaru Castle, burned in 1873 and restored in 2020 as Japan's largest castle gate.

  • Shigakko Ruins

    Kagoshima

    Private school Saigo Takamori founded in 1873 for samurai; bullet marks remain in the walls. Prefectural historic site since 1968.

  • Kagoshima Prefectural Office Observation Lobby

    Kagoshima · 099-286-3798

  • Terayama Park

    Kagoshima · 099-216-1366

    Park at Yoshino Plateau's highest point with a Kinko Bay panorama observatory near World Heritage Terayama charcoal kiln ruins.

  • Statue of Lord Shimazu Nariakira

    Kagoshima

    Bronze statue of 28th Shimazu head Nariakira, who began Shuseikan, promoting military, education, science, and industry. Fumio Asakura, 1917.

  • Akamizu Observation Plaza

    Kagoshima · 099-298-5111

    Sakurajima looks wide and close from a lawn slope. The lava Portrait of the Scream marks Nagabuchi's 2004 all-night concert.

  • Kagoshima Ken Gokoku Shrine

  • Birthplace of Okubo Toshimichi
