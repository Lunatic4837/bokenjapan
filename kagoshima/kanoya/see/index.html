Sights in Kanoya, Kagoshima

Kanoya · Kagoshima · 20 places

See

  • Osumi Lake

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1121

    Lake below seven peaks over 1,000 meters, with spring cherries, early-summer hydrangeas, autumn leaves, and winter birds.

  • Kanoya Air Base Museum

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-42-0233

    Displays Navy history, special-attack portraits and wills, a restored Zero, and the world's only Type 2 flying boat.

  • Kanoya City Folk Museum

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-45-2872

    Displays about 600 ethnic items, including costumes, instruments, toys, and dolls, from 14 Asian countries.

  • Kanoya City Narinoo Ranch

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-46-3247

    City pasture of 71 hectares at 500 meters on Takakuma's western foothills, with grazing Holsteins.

  • Kihoku Uwaba Park and Kihoku Tenkyukan Observatory

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 099-485-1900

    Hilltop park at 550m with 360-degree views, top starry skies, and a space-themed observatory.

  • Otsukayama Park

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1148

    108.9m hill in Kushira farmland with 360-degree views, popular for sunrise.

  • Aira Sanryo

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-58-7257

    Rock-cave mausoleum of Emperor Jimmu's parents, called Little Ise, with cherry blossoms and autumn leaves.

  • Sugawara Shrine (Arahira Tenjin)

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1121

    Shrine to Sugawara no Michizane, god of learning, stands on Tenjin Island; sandbar path sinks at spring high tide.

  • Kanoya Rose Garden

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-40-2170

    One of Japan's largest rose gardens, with about 35,000 bushes on 8 hectares and themed gardens.

  • Kushira Peace Park

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1148

    Some 5,000 flight trainees trained at former Kushira Naval Air Base; the runway is now a cherry-lined road.

  • Kanoya City Railway Memorial Hall

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-40-0078

    Former JNR Osumi Line museum, closed 1987, with 400 exhibits; a diesel car can be ridden.

  • Oka Monument and Nozato National School Site

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1121

    Near Kanoya Air Base, monument enshrining Jinrai members who sortied south in spring 1945.

  • Kozuka Park (Former Kanoya Air Base Special Attack Memorial Tower)

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1148

    Eleven-meter tower on Kozuka Hill honors 908 members who flew from Kanoya Base to Okinawa, topped by a white dove.

  • Shintoku Inari Shrine

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-36-0303

    Photogenic shrine with a transparent torii and red thousand torii gates.

  • Damask Wind

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-46-5627

    One of few organic, pesticide-free rose gardens in Japan, with damask roses and herbs. Cafe sells rose soft-serve and cosmetics.

  • Ikoi-no-Sato Hydrangea Garden

    Kanoya, Kagoshima

    Hydrangeas bloom mid-May to mid-June on a private hillside with paths and Kinko Bay viewpoints.

  • Kannonbuchi Medieval Memorial Towers

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1121

    About 90 medieval stone memorial towers in a natural cave, with famous spring water visitors collect.

  • Minamishimizugawa Irrigation Association Cosmos Garden

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-49-3098

    About 800,000 cosmos bloom on 2 hectares near Minami Elementary School, with unique seasonal scarecrows.

  • Kushira Base Ruins First Telegraph Room Bunker

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1121

    Concrete bunker about 7 meters deep and 51 meters long received special attack telegrams. Kanoya cultural property since 2015.

  • Kawahigashi Aircraft Shelter at Kasanohara Base Ruins

    Kanoya, Kagoshima · 0994-31-1121

    Concrete shelter disguised as a small hill to protect Zero fighters and supplies. Only nearly complete one of about 200.
