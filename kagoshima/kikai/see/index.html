Sights in Kikai, Kagoshima

Kikai · Kagoshima · 10 places

See

  • Straight Road Through Sugarcane Fields

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-1111

    Roughly 3 km straight road through endless sugarcane fields under blue sky to the sea, popular with tourists.

  • Tekutsuku Giant Banyan

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-3683

    A 17.7 m banyan in Tekutsuku village, the island's top power spot, estimated over 100 years old.

  • Combat Command Post Ruins

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-3683

    Underground reinforced concrete Pacific War bunker by the Miyamoto unit, Japan's only surviving combat command post.

  • Hyakunodai National Park

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-1111

    Kikai's highest plateau at 203 meters, with Pacific and East China Sea views; one of Amami's ten scenic spots.

  • Coral Stone Walls

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-1111

    Many traditional coral stone walls remain in Aden village; high walls and banyan trees line every central road.

  • Ufuyaguchi Limestone Cave

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-1111

    About 30-meter cave with a large entrance and mysterious dome. Some stalactites destroyed during wartime military use.

  • Shinyo Storage Bunker Ruins

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-3683

    Ruins of bunkers said to store fifty wooden Shinyo ramming boats; none ever sortied.

  • Nanatsujima Cape (Point 211)

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-1111

    Kikai Island's highest point at 211 m, with former Japanese military communications ruins and a paraglider launch site.

  • Araki-Nakazato Promenade

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-1111

    Coastal path over 2km from Nakazato Beach to Araki, formed of raised coral in a quasi-national park, with beautiful sunsets.

  • Aircraft Shelter

    Kikai, Kagoshima · 0997-65-3683

    Only concrete aircraft shelter among fifty-plus on the island; maintained kamikaze planes flew south.
