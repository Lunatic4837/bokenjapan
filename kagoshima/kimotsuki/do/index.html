Do in Kimotsuki

Kimotsuki · Kagoshima · 8 places

Do

  • Yabusame no Sato Park and Takayama Onsen Dome

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-31-5711

    Over 10 baths and saunas, private family baths, welfare baths, far-infrared and steam saunas, and a 30 cm children's bath.

  • Doyadoyasa (Demon Fire Ritual)

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima

    Designated intangible folk cultural demon-fire rite in Uchinoura, Kimotsuki, begun in the Satsuma period.

  • Nagoshidon

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-67-2888

    Traditional local event, nationally called the Nagoshi Festival, held August 14 with shrine rites, coastal dance, and purification.

  • Kishira Coast

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-67-2888

    Toilets, showers, and shelters; early-summer turtle nesting and first-class rock fishing. Swimming not recommended.

  • Toji Yu no Sato Yunotani Onsen

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-34-6161

    Alkaline simple onsen known since the Shimazu domain period for neuralgia, cuts, and burns.

  • Tekotendon

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima

    Ancient New Year tradition in Kishira, Kimotsuki: a climb to a huge rock atop 750m Kitadake.

  • Futamata River Campsite

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-65-2594

    Enjoy nature beside a beautiful stream, with bungalows, tents, showers, a goemon bath, and gear rental for beginners.

  • Fireflies at Shijukusho Shrine

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-67-2888

    Fireflies naturally occur around Shijukusho Shrine, famous for horseback archery rites, about 18:00-20:00 in mid-May.
