Sights in Kimotsuki, Kagoshima

Kimotsuki · Kagoshima · 11 places

See

  • JAXA Uchinoura Space Center

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 050-3362-3111

    Rare rocket launch site on mountain terrain that launched Japan's first satellite Ohsumi in 1970.

  • Todoroki Falls

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-67-2888

    Three-tier falls, 6 meters high and 30 meters wide, on the lower Arase River. Crowded in summer.

  • Shijukusho Shrine

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-65-2938

    Founded in 984; horseback archery, a nearly 900-year tradition, is held the third Sunday of October.

  • Nikaido Family Residence

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-31-5252

    Former Representative Susumu Nikaido's home, a large southern Kagoshima farmhouse. National Important Cultural Property.

  • Tsukazaki Camphor Tree

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-65-0170

    Camphor tree over 1,200 years old on Tsukazaki Kofun No. 1. Legend of a giant snake at the roots.

  • Hetsuka Coast

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-67-2888

    Scenic white-sand inlet; turtles nest in May and June. Swimming is not recommended.

  • Honjo Giant Ginkgo

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-65-2594

    Giant ginkgo at the old Honjo school site; nationally designated Takayama Castle ruins stand just behind.

  • Doryu-ji Temple Ruins

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima

    Doryu-ji ruins in woods hold stone pagodas and statues; former hall is rice paddies, with autumn maples.

  • JAXA Space Science Museum (Uchinoura Space Center)

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 050-3362-3111

    Five-story museum beside the launch site displaying rocket and satellite models in a star-shaped building.

  • Tsukazaki Kofun Group

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-65-0170

    Five keyhole and 54 round tombs. Tomb 51, 52 meters long, is Japan's southernmost keyhole tomb. National historic site.

  • Uchinoura Kaizo Fortress Ruins

    Kimotsuki, Kagoshima · 0994-67-2888

    Battery and observation post in Kaizo; seven cannons stood at six Shibushi Bay sites. Observation post entry is prohibited.
