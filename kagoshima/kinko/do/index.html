Things to do in Kinko, Kagoshima

Kinko · Kagoshima · 6 places

Do

  • Kamikawa Great Falls Park

    Kinko, Kagoshima · 0994-25-2511

    Park with 30m-wide, 25m-high falls, a smaller fall, suspension bridges, play areas, and a 130m aerial walk at 68m.

  • Oku-Hanase Rainbow Trout Fishing Ground

    Kinko, Kagoshima · 0994-25-3376

    Rainbow trout fishing from early spring to autumn, 5 km from Hanase Park; rods, bait, and take-home catch.

  • Kagoshima Prefecture Laurel Forest and Inao-dake Visitor Center

    Kinko, Kagoshima · 080-6417-6518

    One of western Japan's largest laurel forests, with walking and hiking courses, a free campsite, and nature learning.

  • Kamikawa Campground (Kamikawa Coast)

    Kinko, Kagoshima · 0994-22-1446

    Seaside campground facing a sandy beach, with Sakurajima and Kaimon-dake views, auto sites, fishing, and sunset shadow art.

  • Kamikawa Beach Shadow Picture Festival

    Kinko, Kagoshima · 0994-28-2488

    Views of Mount Kaimon and Sakurajima, golden sunsets, changing shadow art, and nearby camping.

  • Classic Grape Hamada Farm

    Kinko, Kagoshima · 0994-25-2584

    Grapes raised to classical music; grape and blueberry picking, mango sales, and fruit sauces.
