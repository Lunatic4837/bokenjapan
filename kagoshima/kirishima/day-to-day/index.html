Day-to-day services in Kirishima, Kagoshima

Day to day

  • Saigo Park

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-73-3828

    Free park in front of Kagoshima Airport with a 10.5-meter Saigo Takamori statue and tourist information.

  • Kirishima Onsen Market

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-78-4001

    Tourist information, souvenir shops and eateries, onsen-steamed eggs and vegetables, and a paid foot bath.

  • Black Vinegar Garden Pot Field Shop & Restaurant

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-54-7200

    Sakamoto Brewing has made pot-brewed black vinegar for 200 years; the shop and restaurant overlook pot fields and Sakurajima.

  • Kakushida Black Vinegar Village

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-55-3231

    Japan's first black-vinegar restaurant with pot-field tours and a shop, using home-grown JAS organic vegetables.

  • Kurobuta no Yakata Farm-Direct Restaurant

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-57-0713

    Serves Kagoshima black pork from partner cooperatives, including tonkatsu and shabu-shabu; local information base.

  • Koji Village Barrel Valley Praha & GEN

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-58-2535

    Learn, taste, and buy exclusive koji products and shochu. Czech village offers Pilsner and meals; GEN serves koji-raised black pork.

  • JR Kirishima-Jingu Station

    Kirishima, Kagoshima

    Nearest station to national-treasure Kirishima Jingu, renewed March 2024 with Kagoshima cedar, food, crafts, and nearby cafe.

  • Kagoshima Airport Tourist Information Center

    Kirishima, Kagoshima

    First-floor information desks in domestic and international arrival lobbies guide tourism, transport, hotels, and taxis.

  • Kagoshima Airport Multifunction Service Counter (Domestic Flights 1F)

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-55-1488

    Counter for domestic and overseas airport users, with English support and WiFi router rental.

  • Kimamana Kitchen

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-78-8118

    Hideaway shop in a Kirishima forest serving homegrown vegetable lunches and homemade sweets, with hammocks on site.

  • Yokogawa Kito

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-73-3574

    Cafe, select shop, and guesthouse in a renovated 1931 house, serving local handmade spice curry.

  • CAFE510

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-73-7445

  • Kirishima Fumoto Station

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-76-2888

    Local products hall with fresh vegetables, bento, and Kagoshima specialties, plus a cafe serving gelato and drinks.

  • Toki Kirishima

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-55-8039

    Japanese tea cafe at the foot of the Kirishima mountains serving tea-based lunches, seasonal sweets, and Kirishima tea.

  • Obama Village

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-73-7739
