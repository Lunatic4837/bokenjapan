Kirishima · Kagoshima · 41 places

See

  • Maizuru Castle Ruins

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    Stone walls, moat, and two stone bridges remain; Shimazu Yoshihisa resided here seven years from 1604.

  • Uenohara Jomon Forest

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-48-5701

    Preserves Uenohara, Japan's oldest and largest-class Jomon settlement site, with pottery, stone tools, and dioramas.

  • Takaya-no-yama-no-e Mausoleum

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    Mausoleum of Hikohohodemi no Mikoto atop a 390-meter peak among cedar trees, near the airport.

  • Iwado Kannon

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    Nanbokucho-period tuff niche near Yokogawa with seated Amida and standing Kannon and Seishi.

  • Ara Shrine

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    One of the five Osumi shrines, founded around 708, where deities and Kannon are enshrined together.

  • Maruo Falls

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    A 23 m high, 16 m wide hot-spring waterfall along Route 223, milky blue and lit up at night.

  • Inukai Falls

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    36-meter-high, 22-meter-wide white falls against Takachiho Peak, viewed by Sakamoto Ryoma.

  • Wake Shrine

    Kirishima, Kagoshima

    Dedicated to Wake no Kiyomaro, with a Sakamoto Ryoma honeymoon monument.

  • Kirishima Mountains

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-57-2505

    Volcanic mountains across Kagoshima and Miyazaki with caldera lakes, seasonal flowers, and many hikers.

  • Kirishima Jingu

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-57-0001

    Vermilion shrine enshrining Ninigi-no-Mikoto; main halls designated a National Treasure in 2022.

  • Mount Takachiho

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-57-2505

    A 1574-meter cone volcano with a heavenly descent legend, a summit spear, superb views, and hiking trails.

  • Kagoshima Jingu

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-42-0020

    Hilltop shrine to Hikohohodemi overlooking Sakurajima; 1756 halls are Important Cultural Properties.

  • Hayatozuka

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    National historic site with three five-story pagodas and four guardian statues, restored near its form from 800 years ago.

  • Kumaso Cave

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-77-2111

    Cave said to be the home of Kumaso chief Kawakami Takeru, 22 m deep. Closed after August 2025 heavy rain.

  • Miyaura Shrine (Fukuyama Married Ginkgo Trees)

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    Historic shrine listed in the Engishiki, with two ginkgo trees over 1,000 years old, a prefectural cultural property.

  • Former Tanaka Family Villa

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-56-2012

    Villa completed in 1922 by Tanaka Shozo, now a prefectural tangible cultural property, with a Japanese garden.

  • Matsushita Museum of Art

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-55-3350

    Founded in 1983 with about 3,000 artworks, from Kuroda Seiki to Monet and Picasso, plus a Japanese garden.

  • Sakamoto Ryoma and Oryo Honeymoon Onsen Monument

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-76-0007

    Marks Ryoma and Oryo's Shiohitashi Onsen stay on Saigo's advice; called Japan's first honeymoon. In Ryoma Park.

  • Hayatozuka Historic Site Museum

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-43-7110

    Guidance hall for national historic site Hayatozuka, showing excavation photos, a Heian Buddha, and a restored stone tower.

  • Inariyama Park

    Kirishima, Kagoshima · 0995-45-5111

    Famous for about 100 Yoshino cherry trees, best viewed from late March to early April.

  • Yamagano Gold Mine

  • Wake Park Wisteria

  • Osumi-Yokogawa Station

  • Kareigawa Station
