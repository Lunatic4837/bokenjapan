Do in Makurazaki

Makurazaki · Kagoshima · 5 places

Do

  • Hinokami Park and Campground

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-76-1668

    Cape park on the East China Sea with Tategami Rock, camping, picnics, rock fishing, and a summer pool.

  • Satsuma Shuzo Kegogawa Distillery Meiji-gura

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0120-467-355

    Shochu still made in clay pots used over 100 years, with tastings and guided or self-guided tours.

  • Seto Park

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-72-1111

    Views of the East China Sea, islands, and Kaimondake; playground; 170 Somei-Yoshino cherries in spring.

  • Makurazaki Nagisa Onsen

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-72-5080

    Natural Makurazaki onsen. Rock open-air bath views Tategami Rock, East China Sea sunsets, nightscape, and fishing-boat lights.

  • Hinokami Distillery Visitor Center

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0120-467-386

    Opened January 2026 with shop, tours, and anechoic room tasting, overlooking Mt. Kaimon and the East China Sea.
