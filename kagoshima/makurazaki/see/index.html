Sights in Makurazaki, Kagoshima

Makurazaki · Kagoshima · 5 places

See

  • Makurazaki Cultural Center Nanmeikan

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-72-9998

    Wooden art museum in Katahirayama Park overlooking the East China Sea, displaying local and famous artists' works.

  • Peace Prayer Observatory in Hinokami Park

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-76-1668

    Has a memorial monument for the Second Fleet, including battleship Yamato, bound for the Okinawa naval special attack.

  • Makurazaki Station

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-76-1668

    Station completed in April 2013, Japan's only one built mostly with private donations including from citizens.

  • Myoken Shrine

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-72-2629

    Officially Amenominakanushi Shrine, one of the area's oldest, with a stone said to grant wishes when touched.

  • Minamikata Shrine

    Makurazaki, Kagoshima · 0993-72-2629

    Rare side-by-side torii shrine enshrined by Shimazu Ujihisa, dedicated to Takeminakata and Yasakatome.
