Do in Minamikyushu

Minamikyushu · Kagoshima · 13 places

Do

  • Mori no Kawanabe Auto Camp

    Forest auto camp 35 minutes from Kagoshima, with trailer camping and a 400-meter mountain-bike course.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 050-3529-6424 · Official / source link

  • Chiran Tennis Forest Park

    Seventeen sand-filled artificial-turf courts with racket and shoe rentals and a clubhouse with showers and lounge.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-1030 · Official / source link

  • Tomoike Park

    Edo-period farm reservoir park with lawns, paths, bird calls, and 800 mountain cherry trees.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2511 · Official / source link

  • Hata no Sato Midori-kan

    Hand-roll Chiran tea into sencha or learn to brew it with a Japan Tea Advisor.

  • Seahorse House

    Japan's only tourist farm for observing seahorses, offering guided viewing, lucky goods, workshops, and a cafe.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-38-1883 · Official / source link

  • Chiran Country Club

    A flat course seen from tee to green, designed so anyone can enjoy bold shots.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-4711 · Official / source link

  • Kawanabe Buddhist Altar Craft Hall

    Teaches nationally designated Kawanabe Buddhist altar techniques and offers artisan-guided making experiences.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-56-0240 · Official / source link

  • Chaya at the Foot of Mount Ono

    Enjoy lodging and a premium Chiran tea experience amid tea fields at the foot of Mount Ono.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 050-3567-1850 · Official / source link

  • Mori no Kawanabe Adventure Park

    Play-and-learn activity park with tree courses to about 10 meters and a 100-meter zipline.

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 050-3529-6424 · Official / source link

  • Maeda Strawberry Farm
