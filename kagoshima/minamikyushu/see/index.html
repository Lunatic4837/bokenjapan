Sights in Minamikyushu, Kagoshima

Minamikyushu · Kagoshima · 19 places

See

  • Sehira Park

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2511

    Scenic spot on Route 226 viewing Mount Kaimon among rocks and old pines, with distant island views on clear days.

  • Bandokoro Natural Park

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2511

    Scenic spot praised by Ino Tadataka, with Kaimondake views and Japan's only seahorse farm.

  • Onodake Shrine

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-36-1111

    Shrine atop Onodake, a scenic spot jutting 450 meters above the sea.

  • Shimizu Cliff Buddhas

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-4433

    About 200 cliff Buddhas, 20 meters high and 400 meters long, a prefectural historic site.

  • Shimizu Spring

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2511

    One of Japan's 100 famous waters, yielding about 6,000 tons daily beneath the Shirasu plateau.

  • Chiran Samurai Residence Gardens

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-58-7878

    Seven open samurai gardens in Chiran, designated national scenic sites in an 18.6-hectare district.

  • Chiran Peace Museum for Kamikaze Pilots

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2525

    Displays relics of Army special-attack members who rammed bomb-laden planes into enemy ships during the Battle of Okinawa.

  • Museum Chiran

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-4433

    History museum on southern Satsuma culture, Chiran's Ryukyu-Yamato blend, and suppressed hidden Buddhist chanting.

  • Toyotamahime Shrine

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-4335

    Local shrine known for safe-birth prayers; each July, water-powered puppet theater selected as a national folk cultural property.

  • Chiran Peace Park

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2511

    Adjacent to Chiran Special Attack Peace Hall, with many cherry trees and an entrance tunnel; night light-up is held.

  • Itatehyozu Shrine (Kamabuta Shrine)

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-38-2127

    Shrine on an inlet reef, dedicated to Susanoo, known for evil-warding, fortune, and victory prayers.

  • Onodake Park

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2511

    Gentle 466 m mountain in Ei-cho with drive-up 360-degree views of tea fields, Kaimon-dake and the East China Sea.

  • Ochaya-no-ba Park

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-83-2511

    View Diamond Satsuma Fuji, sunrise from Kaimon-dake, early December to mid-January; New Year's Day is crowded.

  • Hotaru-kan Tomiya Cafeteria

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-58-7566

    Museum faithfully recreating the army-designated Tomiya Cafeteria from late World War II at its original location.

  • Kinkodai Observation Park (Ibusuki Skyline)

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 099-213-0231

    Called Sky Palette, with a suspension bridge, walking paths, and varied Sakurajima views.

  • Chiran Observatory (Ibusuki Skyline)

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 099-213-0231

    Wider Kinko Bay views than other skyline spots; Sakurajima looks like a floating island.

  • Kori Cosmos in Ei Town

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-58-7577

    Volunteer-planted cosmos and sunflowers with an Ibusuki-Makurazaki Line overpass behind, mid-October to early November.

  • Okubo Sunflowers

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 090-5489-8690

    About 120,000 sunflowers in Minamikyushu City, free tea, produce sales, and free flowers to take home.

  • Tashiro Cosmos and Sunflowers

    Minamikyushu, Kagoshima · 0993-78-3093

    Sunflower and cosmos fields before Tashiro Community Center, Minamikyushu. Take up to five each. Best late October to early November.
