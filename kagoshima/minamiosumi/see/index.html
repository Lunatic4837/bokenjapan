Sights in Minamiosumi, Kagoshima

Minamiosumi · Kagoshima · 6 places

See

  • Ogawa Falls

    Minamiosumi, Kagoshima · 0994-24-3115

    46 m drop, 60 m wide, about 1,200 m trail from parking. Filming site; accessible path completed in 2023.

  • Cape Sata Park

    Minamiosumi, Kagoshima · 0994-27-3151

    Mainland Japan's southernmost cape on the 31st parallel, with one of Japan's oldest lighthouses and subtropical paths.

  • Suwa Shrine Parallel Torii

    Minamiosumi, Kagoshima · 0994-24-3115

    Shrine with a rare hall and two parallel torii said to bring matchmaking blessings, popular with couples.

  • Misaki Shrine

    Minamiosumi, Kagoshima · 0994-24-3115

    Founded in 708 along the Sata Cape walkway; red-roofed amid cycads and popular for matchmaking.

  • Panorama Park Nishiharadai

    Minamiosumi, Kagoshima · 0994-24-3115

    439 m viewpoint of Sakurajima and Kaimondake; popular paragliding point near Ogawa Falls.

  • Daiba Park

    Minamiosumi, Kagoshima · 0994-24-3115

    Granite coastal battery built against foreign ships in the late Edo period, never fired, overlooking Kinko Bay.
