Sights in Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima

Minamisatsuma · Kagoshima · 20 places

See

  • Takeda Shrine and Inishie no Michi (Nisshinko Iroha Poem Monuments)

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-52-2841

    Shrine to Shimazu Tadayoshi lined with 47 iroha poem steles and a 200-meter inumaki avenue.

  • Bansei Tokko Peace Memorial Hall

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-52-3979

    Memorial hall on Bansei Airfield, the army's last special-attack base, showing a recovered seaplane, letters, and relics.

  • Junji Yoshii Art Museum

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-6778

    Art museum opened in 1992 amid cedars, showing Yoshii oils, drawings, prints, and about 140 associated artists' works.

  • Nansatsu Railway Memorial Hall

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2102

    Opened in 1994 in Kaseda, preserving six Nansatsu Line vehicles including German-built steam locomotives.

  • Sunset Bridge

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    405-meter bicycle-only cable-stayed bridge over the Manose River estuary, with East China Sea sunset views.

  • Beppu Castle Ruins (Kaseda Castle)

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    Heike clan castle from about 1100; only a stone monument remains, no ruins.

  • Hokino-yama Park

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    Overlooks the East China Sea, islets, and mountains; memorial to 597 Sino-Japanese War dead.

  • Kasasa Art Museum (Kurose Observatory Museum)

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-63-0990

    Views of the East China Sea and complex rocks; observatory overlooks Biro Island.

  • Oto Stone Wall Village

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    Stone-walled houses on a slope, reportedly a million stones, and a 1,250-meter stone path.

  • Mimitori Pass

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    Summit poem monument of a Meiji court poet, overlooking Mount Kaimon and Makurazaki city.

  • Bonotsu Historical Materials Center Kishinkan

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-67-0171

    Exhibits donated Bonotsu materials, including an important cultural property Nirvana painting replica and Ming porcelain.

  • Ganjin Memorial Hall

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-68-0288

    Built in Akime, where priest Ganjin landed in 753; displays a statue replica, exhibits, and dioramas.

  • Kinpo History Exchange Hall

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-58-4321

    Displays materials, models, and videos on seafaring people's history and culture.

  • Minamisatsuma Sea Road Eight Views

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    Eight representative landscapes along Route 226, with grand natural scenery and cultural heritage.

  • Hill Where Whales Sleep and Stranded Whale Monument

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    Preserves a sperm whale skeleton from 14 stranded in 2002 at former Oura Town.

  • Mount Takasaki

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    One of the Eight Sea Road Views, with bow-shaped Fukiage Beach, foreground islands, and distant Sakurajima.

  • You Only Live Twice Filming Location

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    1967 You Only Live Twice location in Akime. The 1990 monument is engraved with Connery and Tamba signatures.

  • Kaseda Fumoto Samurai Residences

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 090-4512-6325

    Former Satsuma outer-castle town retaining samurai houses, stone walls, inumaki trees, and gates along canals.

  • Soken-seki

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima · 0993-53-2111

    Sword-like twin rocks, 27 m and 21 m tall, near Ajiro Beach; designated part of scenic Bonotsu.

  • Takaebi Monument

    Minamisatsuma, Kagoshima

    Pink heart monument of Minamisatsuma's Takaebi specialty, designed by Shoko Nakagawa to express eternal love.
