Things to do in Minamitane, Kagoshima

Minamitane · Kagoshima · 6 places

Do

  • Tanegashima Mangrove Park

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Rare dwarf Kandelia under one meter, not found elsewhere in the Nansei Islands; boardwalk and sea kayaking offered.

  • Minamitane Town Kawauchi Onsen Center

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-2510

    Gentle alkaline simple onsen with skin-smoothing effects, an indoor bath, sauna, and day-visit bathing.

  • Tanegashima Kayak Exploration

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Tanegashima's mangrove habitat, considered Japan's northern limit, is rich in rare plants and animals; kayaking is possible.

  • Uchugaoka Park

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Poem monument from the 1982 crown prince and princess visit, rocket viewing, fine views, and camping.

  • Hamada Beach

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    White-sand shallow beach known for its beauty, busy with beachgoers in season. Showers and toilets available.

  • Tanegashima Shinsaku Farm Tankan Harvest Experience

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-7108

    Sunny seaside tankan farm in Hirayama, Minamitane. Harvest mid-February to mid-March; other farm work available; guests get tankan.
