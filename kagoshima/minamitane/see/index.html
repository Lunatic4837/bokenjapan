See in Minamitane

Minamitane · Kagoshima · 9 places

See

  • Tanegashima Space Center Space Science and Technology Museum

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-9244

    Japan's first full-scale space development exhibition hall at JAXA Tanegashima Space Center, free to the public.

  • Senzano Iwaya Cave

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Tanegashima's largest sea-eroded cave, traditionally said to seat a thousand. Enter only around low tide.

  • Cape Kadokura

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Southernmost point of Tanegashima, with a 1543 firearms-introduction monument, observatory, shrine, and Yakushima views.

  • Hirota Site Museum and National Historic Site Hirota Site Park

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-24-4811

    Guidance museum for national historic Hirota burial site, showing shell grave goods and shell-accessory making.

  • Tanegashima Red Rice Museum

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-7444

    Introduces red rice preserved at Homan Shrine, related folk rites, and local rice-farming culture.

  • Homan Shrine

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Long-cherished shrine to Tamayorihime, linked with local rice farming through red-rice rites, near legendary Homan Pond.

  • Takezaki Coast

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Coast around Tanegashima Space Center, one of Minamitane's eight scenic views, contrasting space center and nature.

  • Nagatani Park

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    The most visited rocket-launch viewing spot, with countdown audio and a live launch broadcast.

  • Nanairo Observatory

    Minamitane, Kagoshima · 0997-26-1111

    Overlooks paddies, windbreaks, and Maenohama Beach; the space center launch pad is visible on clear days.
