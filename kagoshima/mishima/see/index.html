  • Bell of Hope and Bell of Happiness

    Mishima, Kagoshima · 09913-2-2370

    Erabu Cape, called Lovers' Cape, is a high-cliff viewpoint with two bells rung against Mount Io.

  • Shunkan-do

    Mishima, Kagoshima · 09913-2-2101

    Hall enshrining Shunkan, exiled to Iojima in 1177, who died there in 1179; Bon fires still burn.

  • Lovers' Cape

    Mishima, Kagoshima · 099-222-3141

    Legendary cape on southern Iojima where couples are said to unite. Views of Iodake, Yakushima, Kuchinoerabujima, and Tanegashima.

  • Nagahama Bay

    Mishima, Kagoshima · 099-222-3141

    Central crater cone of Kikai Caldera; mystical coastline dyed seven colors by constantly gushing hot springs.

  • Sawako Ariyoshi Literary Monument

    Mishima, Kagoshima · 099-222-3141

    Monument to author Sawako Ariyoshi, inscribed with the title of her 1959 novel set on Kuroshima.

  • Kuroshima Peace Park

    Mishima, Kagoshima · 099-222-3141

    Kamikaze peace Kannon statue built by an emergency-landed member and comrades, plus a peace bell; memorial service each May.
