Things to do in Nagashima, Kagoshima

Nagashima · Kagoshima · 5 places

Do

  • Taiyo-no-Sato Onsen Center Tosenbo

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-2626

    Hilltop onsen center in Nagashima overlooking Ikara Bridge, Unzen, and Yatsushiro Sea islands.

  • Shishijima Fossil Park

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1111

    Many fossils were excavated around the park on Shishijima; excavation experience next door.

  • Japan Mandarin Center

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-2011

    Birthplace of Unshu mandarins, with exhibits, about 300 citrus varieties, fresh juice, and picking.

  • Nagashima Youth Travel Village (Obama Beach and Campground)

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1137

    July-August town campground at Nagashima's only natural beach, with clear water and sea-turtle nesting.

  • Matsugahira Campground and Azuma Beach

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1137

    Campground and calm artificial beach on the Shiranui Sea inside Unzen-Amakusa National Park.
