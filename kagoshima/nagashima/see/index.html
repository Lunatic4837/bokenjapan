Sights in Nagashima, Kagoshima

Nagashima · Kagoshima · 8 places

See

  • Nagasakibana Lighthouse Park

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1111

    Scenic park in southwest Nagashima with a white lighthouse, East China Sea sunsets, and summer sunflowers.

  • Nagashima Bougainvillea Hill

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-87-1600

    White, red, and pink greenhouse bougainvillea from about November to May. Cafe chiffon cake with amanatsu jam is popular.

  • Gyonin-dake

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1111

    A 394m mountain in central Nagashima with island views and summit halls of Fudo Myoo and Zao Gongen.

  • Ikara Bridge

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1111

    Japan's longest cable-stayed bridge, 675 meters, opened in 1996 linking Nagashima and Ikara Island.

  • Nagashima Windmill Park

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1137

    Park around a huge wind power station overlooking Amakusa and the East China Sea, hosting flower and shochu festivals.

  • Hario Park

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1111

    Cliffside park on a former Miyaji clan fort site, overlooking Amakusa and Mount Unzen.

  • Nagashima Town History and Folklore Museum

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-88-5160

    Introduces Nagashima history, over 200 ancient tombs, gold rings, and the Nagashima clan.

  • Shichirozan

    Nagashima, Kagoshima · 0996-86-1111

    Highest point on Shishijima at 393 m, with 360-degree views of Nagashima and Unzen-Amakusa National Park.
