Things to do in Nakatane, Kagoshima

Nakatane · Kagoshima · 3 places

Do

  • Nature Recreation Village (Kumano Beach)

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-8785

    Campsite next to Kumano coast near native plant areas, emerald and white beach, and a panoramic town onsen.

  • Nakatane Town Onsen Recreation Center

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-9211

    Onsen in front of Kumano Beach: weakly alkaline simple spring, bubble and therapeutic baths, and sauna.

  • Nagahama Beach

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-1111

    The island's largest sandy beach, 12 kilometers long, visited by surfers and nesting sea turtles, with sunset views beside Yakushima.
