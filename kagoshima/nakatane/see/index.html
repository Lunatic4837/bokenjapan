Sights in Nakatane, Kagoshima

Nakatane · Kagoshima · 9 places

See

  • Male and Female Dragon Rocks

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-1111

    Close-standing rocks said to be a couple cast into the sea on a stormy night; right male dragon, left female.

  • Furuichi Family Residence

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-3711

    House built in 1846, among the oldest surviving on Tanegashima, designated a national Important Cultural Property in 1994.

  • Otokobuchi and Onnabuchi Falls

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-1111

    Rare two-tier falls on Tanegashima; the upper is men's pool, the lower women's, flowing even in drought.

  • Japan's Largest Cycad (Sakai Shrine)

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-1111

    A female cycad estimated over 600 years old, 8 meters tall, standing in Sakai Shrine and braced by iron pillars.

  • Kumano Shrine

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-9004

    Many people across Tanegashima visit; enshrines couple Izanagi and Izanami, said to bless matchmaking and safe childbirth.

  • Inujo Observatory

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-1111

    Overlooks lush evergreen forest, wave-eroded strange rocks, and the open sea.

  • Tachikiri Site

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-3711

    Late Paleolithic site about 31,000 years old, found in 1997, with stone-steam cooking traces and about 50 tools.

  • Inujo Coast and Umatate Cave

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-1111

    Scenic east coast of Tanegashima with caves and giant rocks; Umatate Cave named for a waiting horse.

  • Nakatane Town History and Folklore Museum

    Nakatane, Kagoshima · 0997-27-2233

    Museum along Route 58 with recreated Taisho-Showa homes, sugarcane press, last dugout canoe, and 31,000-year-old artifacts.
