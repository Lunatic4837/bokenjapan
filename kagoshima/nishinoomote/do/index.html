Things to do in Nishinoomote, Kagoshima

Nishinoomote · Kagoshima · 5 places

Do

  • Urata Beach

    Nishinoomote, Kagoshima · 0997-22-1111

    One of Japan's 88 selected beaches at northern Tanegashima, with white sand, clear water, fishing, diving, and camping.

  • Yokino Beach

    Nishinoomote, Kagoshima · 0997-22-1111

    Beach along Route 58 lined with birou palms, suited to families, surfing, fishing, and sunsets.

  • Appo Land

    Nishinoomote, Kagoshima · 0997-24-3611

    Waterfront park around Saikyo Dam with lawn, wading pool, play equipment, outdoor stage, and sports-camp facilities.

  • Kanehama Beach

    Nishinoomote, Kagoshima · 0997-22-1111

    Eastern Nishinoomote beach named for abundant iron sand, now famous for surfing big waves.

  • Tanegashima Onsen Akaogi-no-Yu

    Nishinoomote, Kagoshima · 0997-22-0019

    Sodium-bicarbonate beauty spring, Tanegashima's first free-flowing source onsen, with a free foot bath.
