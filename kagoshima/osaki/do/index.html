Things to do in Osaki, Kagoshima

Osaki · Kagoshima · 6 places

Do

  • Shinodan Onsen (Kotobuki-yu)

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-478-3778

    Heated cold spring kept rather hot, long said to help cuts and skin diseases. Atmospheric retro communal bath.

  • Kunino Matsubara Segway Guided Tour

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-3611

    Sixty-minute guided Segway ride through pine woods in Nichinan Coast Quasi-National Park.

  • Kuni-no-Matsubara Campground

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-3611

    Faces Masumaru Coast in Nichinan Coast Quasi-National Park. Bungalows, fixed tents, car camping sites, and Segway experiences.

  • Kunino Matsubara Masumaru Pool

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-3611

    Popular town-run pool for children, with toddler pools, a slide pool, and benches for parents.

  • Osaki Food and Agriculture Exchange Hall

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-0883

    Restaurant serving organic vegetables from natural-cycle farming, with soba-making and processing experiences.

  • Mochidome Firefly Village

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-477-2400

    Naturally occurring Genji fireflies appear in Mochidome rice fields from mid-May to early June, around 19:30-21:00.
