  • Yokose Kofun

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-1111

    A national historic site: huge mid-5th-century keyhole tomb, 129 meters long and 15.4 meters high.

  • Tsuma Shrine (Osaki Ichinomiya)

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-0588

    One of the five Hyuga grand shrines, enshrining Konohanasakuya-hime, treasured as Osaki's ichinomiya.

  • Setomabushi Bridge

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-477-2400

    Stone bridge over the Setomabushi River. The 1921 span is closed to traffic and seen from the 1969 bridge.

  • Teruhi Shrine

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-1111

    Deity enshrined from Ise Grand Shrine in 1688; March festival includes sacred dance.

  • Fureai no Sato Park Japanese Garden

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-1111

    Someiyoshino cherries, irises, and hydrangeas bloom by season; about 2,000 irises on the south side.

  • Four Seasons Forest Pillboxes

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-1111

    Central Osaki lookout over Shibushi Bay and Kanoya, with seasonal trees and nearby wartime pillboxes, trenches, and closed tunnels.

  • Hishida Cosmos

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 090-1583-3384

    Cosmos planting expanded yearly to over 35,000 square meters, best from late October to November.

  • Nyakuichi Shrine (Osaki Sannomiya)

    Osaki, Kagoshima

    Formerly Nyakuoji Shrine, a Kumano branch. Shimazu clan crest remains; Osaki's third five-shrine pilgrimage stop.

  • Mochidome Shrine (Osaki Ninomiya)

    Osaki, Kagoshima

    Formerly Ninomiya Daimyojin Shrine, founded before 1526. One of Osaki's five-shrine pilgrimage sites.

  • Osaki Town Central Community Center Local History Exhibition Room

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-0548

    Displays pottery and mirrors from Yokose and Jinryo tombs, Kofun-period pumice coffins, kagura masks, and wartime items.

  • Ikoi-no-Mori (Kusano-oka)

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-1111

    Observation area on 268 m Kusano-oka in Osaki, with wide views and a barrier-free slope.

  • Bronze Mirror with Fence, Two Chrysanthemums and Twin Sparrows Design

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-0548

    A 21.1 cm Kamakura-period bronze mirror designated an Important Cultural Property in 1918, owned by Tsuma Shrine.

  • Kariyado Nagedon (Japanese Bay Tree)

    Osaki, Kagoshima · 099-476-1111

    Prefecture's largest tabunoki, about 400 years old, with an 8-meter trunk. Horse-headed Kannon is enshrined at its base.

  • Mochidome Todoroki Falls

    Osaki, Kagoshima

    About 10 meters high on the Mochidome River, this powerful waterfall is visible from Prefectural Road 64.
