Things to do in Satsuma, Kagoshima

Satsuma · Kagoshima · 9 places

Do

  • Shibi Onsen

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-53-0525

    Quiet basin at Mount Shibi's southeast foot, suited to rest. Water under the shrine hall is called God's Bath.

  • Niterra Nittoku Sparktec WKS Park

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-21-3939

    Adventure playground modeled on former Torai Castle, plus toddler areas, bungalows, camping, an onsen, and seasonal crafts.

  • Tezuka Ryokan

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-55-9311

    Quiet inn about 80 minutes from Tenmonkan and Kirishima Shrine, blending Asian resort ease with Japanese hospitality.

  • Umashiki Sato Kirara School

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-53-4760

    Former elementary school closed in March 2016, with play, learn, eat, and stay experiences, a cafe, and workshops.

  • Miyanojo Onsen

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-53-0525

    Seven inns and ward bath along the Sendai River, long a spa town. Simple sulfur spring said to beautify skin.

  • Hatago Shibi-so

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-59-8001

    Two highly alkaline springs praised for skin; riverside open-air bath with early-summer fireflies.

  • Miyanojo Traditional Crafts Center

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-52-1313

    Bamboo-craft experience tourism is offered, and local specialties are displayed and sold together.

  • Shibi Ward Public Bath

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-59-8975

    Sulfur spring welling at Shibi Shrine, called god's water. Indoor bath, foot bath, and drinking spring.

  • Satsuma Vidro Crafts

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-58-0141

    Factory beside Kannon Falls Park offering Satsuma kiriko tours and sales, plus other glass products.
