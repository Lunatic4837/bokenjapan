  • Tsuruda Dam Park

    Left-bank park overlooks Lake Otsuru. Its 117.5 m gravity concrete dam is among Kyushu's largest.

  • Mount Shibi

    Highest elevation in the Hokusatsu area; you can drive to the summit and see Amakusa and Unzen on clear days.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-53-1111 · Official / source link

  • Cherry Blossom Avenue at Nagano Railway Memorial Museum

    Switchback rails and conductor cars remain; seasonal flowers, fields, and illuminated night cherry blossoms.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-24-8950 · Official / source link

  • Hiiragino Spider Lilies

    Spider lilies, including white ones, bloom near a pavilion by the old Hiiragino Elementary School. Peak late September.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-24-8950 · Official / source link

  • Shibi Shrine

    Said founded 1,500 years ago by Priest Kugaku; long the head shrine of Kedoin's seven villages.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-59-3111 · Official / source link

  • Sungo Cosmos Fields

    Pink, purple, and white cosmos bloom across three fields; sunflowers also bloom at the third. Peak early November.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-24-8950 · Official / source link

  • Hirose Lotus Flower Field

    White and pink lotus in four plots along Route 504 bloom about 7-10am; peak around mid-July.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-24-8950 · Official / source link

  • Miyanojo Cultural Center

    Famous cherry blossom spot, illuminated at night.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-53-1732 · Official / source link

  • Miyanojo Historical Materials Center

    Shows archaeological finds from the Paleolithic to Edo, plus armor and swords.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-52-3340 · Official / source link

  • Taishakuten Park

    Cherries, satsuki azaleas, and azaleas are planted, becoming a noted flower spot at peak bloom.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-53-1111 · Official / source link

  • Tahara Rice Terrace Water Lilies

    Abandoned farmland became water lily ponds blooming yellow, pink, and white from early July to early August, 9am-1pm.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-24-8950 · Official / source link

  • Sokoji Park

    Former Miyanojo Shimazu family temple site with 33 huge lord tombs, Kyushu's largest group, and cherry blossoms.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-53-1111 · Official / source link

  • Nagano Gold Mine Ruins (Gomame Pit Entrance)

    Nagano Gold Mine pit mouth, from a mine discovered in 1640 that yielded gold and silver until 1953.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-57-0970 · Official / source link

  • Soko-ji Gravestones and Historical Materials Center

    Only the cemetery of Soko-ji, Miyanojo Shimazu family temple, remains, beside a history center. Prefectural historic site since 1975.

    Satsuma, Kagoshima · 0996-52-3340 · Official / source link
