Things to do in Satsumasendai, Kagoshima

Satsumasendai · Kagoshima · 25 places

Do

  • Terayama Recreation Plaza

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-31-4477

    Hilltop park at 247 meters overlooking the Sendai River, with a go-kart course, rabbits, cafe, and night views.

  • Marutake Armor Workshop

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-26-3113

    Armor theme park with samurai and period-drama armor displays, workshop viewing, and armor-wearing experiences.

  • Karahama Beach

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-22-1866

    White-sand beach about 2 km long northwest of Satsumasendai, shallow, with views of the Koshiki Islands.

  • Nishikata Beach

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-28-1061

    Beach near Nishikata Station on Route 3 with broad East China Sea views and Ningyo Rock scenery.

  • Ikoi no Mori Maruyama Park

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-37-2300

    Mid-slope park with a free plaza, outdoor stage, play equipment, and spring cherry blossoms.

  • Mukaiyama Nature Park

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-2371

  • Iriki Onsen

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-5200

    Onsen managed by the Irikiin lords and visited by Shimazu lords, formerly called Soeda Onsen.

  • Togo Goshiki Waterside Park and Campground

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-42-1813

    Campground on the Takaigawa, a Sendai River tributary, mainly for river play, fishing, bungalows and tents.

  • Kamikoshiki Nature Park Camping Village (Koshiki Islands)

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-2-1112

    Coastal leisure facility on Kamikoshiki, northern Koshiki Islands, with campsites, fishing pier, tennis, and go-karts.

  • Kanoko Sightseeing Boat

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-2-1150

    West-coast cruise past 100m cliffs and odd rocks; Cretaceous strata rank among Japan's 100 geological sites.

  • Teuchi Beach

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    About 1.5 km white sand beach at Koshikijima's southernmost tip, lively with anglers and families in summer.

  • Suwa Onsen

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-3472

    Single inn northeast of Imuta Pond Prefectural Park, long loved for medicinal chloride baths.

  • Maruyama Onsen

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-38-1589

    Quiet riverside inn overflowing with natural hot water and a weakly alkaline simple spring.

  • Yanagiyama Agri Land

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-20-3832

    Volunteer tourist park at 389 m with 360-degree views, seasonal flowers, a cafe, and a 15-minute hike to Kirishima Shrine.

  • Ichihino Onsen

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-38-2254

    Historic public hot-spring town since the Edo period, known for weakly alkaline simple spring water.

  • Koshikijima High-Speed Ferry

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-32-6458

    Elegant ferry designed by Eiji Mitooka; reservations recommended, with two daily sailings and a port shuttle.

  • Sendai Takashiro Onsen

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-28-1362

    Mountain onsen about 12 km from Satsumasendai, over 800 years old, chosen among Japan's 100 famous hot springs in 1990.

  • Kimono Town Walk Experience

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-5200

    Stroll the Iriki Fumoto samurai residence district in kimono; arrive empty-handed and enjoy casually.

  • Momotsugi Firefly Village

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-23-5111

    About 10 minutes by car from Sendai Station; two kinds of naturally occurring fireflies, mid-May to early June.

  • Kedoin Golf Club

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-55-1160

    18-hole course on flat hills beside 14-story Hotel Kedoin, a southern Satsuma resort.

  • Kyodo-yu Communal Bath

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-28-0884

    Beside Futabaya; pay at the fee box. Saigo Takamori liked soaking curled in a corner. Alkaline simple sulfur spring.

  • Shimonoyu Onsen

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima

    Onsen since early Showa, visited for cures after busy farming season. Water kept at 43 degrees; opens early morning.

  • Koshikijima Cliff Cruise

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-2-1150

    Cruise Koshikijima's west-coast cliffs over 100 meters on boat Kanoko, seeing Kashima Cliff and Napoleon Rock.

  • Iriki Fumoto Tourist Information and Samurai Tourism

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-5200

    Tourist office at Iriki Fumoto samurai houses offers armor, kimono, crafts, iaido and guided walks.

  • Mountain Strawberry Shop Higashi Farm

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 090-5925-4541

    Strawberry farm near Ichihino Onsen offering picking and direct sales of large berries for over 45 years
