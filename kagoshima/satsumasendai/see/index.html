Sights in Satsumasendai, Kagoshima

Satsumasendai · Kagoshima · 46 places

See

  • Sendai Space Museum

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-31-4477

    Hands-on astronomy facility with a 50cm reflecting telescope, daytime solar viewing, and space computer games.

  • Nitta Shrine (Eno Mausoleum)

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-22-4722

    Shrine to Ninigi no Mikoto on 70-meter Kamikameyama, beside the Eno Mausoleum managed as his tomb.

  • Satsumasendai City Sendai History Museum

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-20-2344

    Museum of Sendai archaeology, history, folklore, and art, with an outdoor garden and a Satsuma-style boat.

  • Sendai Nuclear Power Plant Exhibition Hall

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-27-3506

    Japan's first full-scale reactor model, 12 meters high and 4.5 meters in diameter, plus nuclear displays.

  • Ningyo Rock

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-25-4700

    Strangely shaped rock on the Nishikata coast along Route 3, natural art in evening backlight.

  • Shochu Museum

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-38-0345

    Japan's first shochu museum in an Edo-period brewery, showing tools and books, with tasting and sales.

  • Omiya Shrine (Birthplace of Kimigayo)

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-2390

    Head shrine of the Irikiin clan; Kimigayo is recited in its Twelve-Person Sword Dance.

  • Iriki Fumoto Samurai Residences

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-5200

    Medieval-to-Edo samurai district with round-stone walls, hedges, gates, and other historic remains.

  • Fujikawa Tenjin Shrine (Garyubai)

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-42-0753

    Shrine to learning deity Sugawara no Michizane; about 300 plum trees, 55 sprawling dragon-like Garyubai.

  • Bronze Statue of Saigo Takamori's Dog Tsun

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-42-0753

    Bronze statue of Saigo Takamori's Satsuma dog Tsun, erected in 1990 beside the Fujikawa Tenjin approach.

  • Imuta Pond Nature Park

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-56-0085

    Pond about 1 km across, 3.5 m deep, nearly 300 floating peat islands; natural monument and Ramsar site.

  • Tombolo Observatory

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    A tombolo about 1500 m long and up to 1000 m wide links islands and holds Sato Town's central village.

  • Koshiki Daimyojin Bridge

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    420m, 8m-wide bridge between Kamikoshiki and Nakakoshiki at the rock shrine said to originate the Koshiki name.

  • Ota-no-hama Seaside Park

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    Autumn sunsets that silhouette Koshiki Daimyojin are supremely romantic.

  • Maenohira Observatory

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Observatory facing azure East China Sea and Napoleon Rock, symbol of Shimokoshiki, with Sesenoura village behind.

  • Tsurikakezaki Lighthouse

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Lighthouse opened in 1896 on a 146 m cliff, guiding East China Sea ships; light reaches about 55 km.

  • Teuchi Fumoto Samurai Residence Ruins

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    About 700 m of former samurai houses in central Teuchi; stone walls remain. Japan Heritage, 2019.

  • Yajiri Observatory

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Observatory on a forest road overlooking both coasts, Kashima mountains, Naka-Koshiki, and Satsuma Peninsula amid birdsong.

  • Nagame-no-hama Observatory

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    About 10 minutes by car from Sato Port on Kamikoshiki, overlooking a 4 km sandbar.

  • Tanoshiri Observatory

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    Overlooks Namako Pond, Nagame-no-hama, and the East China Sea.

  • Mirage Hill

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Doctor's mirage poem monument, also in Dr. Koto's Clinic. Napoleon Rock and East China Sea sunsets visible.

  • Sato Fumoto Samurai Residence Street / Teuchi Samurai Residence Street

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    Samurai streets in Teuchi and Sato remain from Ogawa rule near Kamejo, with stone walls and hedges. Japan Heritage, 2019.

  • Sendai Magokoro Literature Museum

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-25-5580

    Displays Arishima brothers materials, especially Satomi Ton, Shirakaba works, and Kaizo magazine manuscripts and letters.

  • Kanoko Bridge

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    A 240-meter bridge linking Naka-Koshiki Island and Nakajima, named for wild kanoko lilies, ideal for sunsets.

  • Kedoin Joryu Tokoro

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-31-8115

  • Mukoda Park

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-25-8282

    A town oasis with Yosano Tekkan's poem monument and a fountain; spring cherries color the town.

  • Tenjin Ike Tembo Tokoro

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-25-8282

  • Nakago Ue Ike

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-25-8282

  • Seo Kannon Three Falls Park

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    55 m three-stage falls on southern Shimokoshiki Island, 10 minutes by car from Nagahama Port; Kannon statue at the basin.

  • Torinosuyama Observatory

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Observatory below a lighthouse on northern Shimokoshiki, enjoyable year-round; flowers bloom June to August; fine Koshiki Bridge view.

  • Ryuseki

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-25-4700

    Rock at 462 m with a female-dragon-to-stone legend; ten-minute walk to the summit.

  • Tsukiyayama / Sendaigawa Arashi

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-26-3849

    160 m hill near Sendai Port where the Sendai River meets the East China Sea, with a summit observatory.

  • Koshikijima Islands

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-25-1140

    Three islands about 35 km long with contrasting terrain, cliffs, diving, and overlooks.

  • Nagano Falls

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-5200

    Beautiful falls from a rocky gap, viewable from a deep cave behind the basin. Filming site for 2018 drama Segodon.

  • Koshiki Museum

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-4-2211

    Reopened April 2025, showing about 1,000 Koshiki fossils and rocks from land and sea.

  • Kiyoshiro Castle Ruins Trench

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-5200

    Entrance trench of the Irikiin clan castle, with steep shirasu dry moats blocking invaders.

  • Former Masuda Family Residence

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-44-4111

    Main house and stone storehouse preserved; roof restored from tile to original thatch.

  • Kedoin Ecosystem Conservation Facility Aquaim

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 0996-56-0085

    Freshwater-fish exhibits and books on Imuta Pond, a key site for rare dragonflies.

  • Kinokuchi Observatory

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    Southern Nakakoshiki lookout over Shimokoshiki Island and 1,533-meter Koshiki-Oshima.

  • Koshiki Bridge

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    Kagoshima Prefecture's longest bridge at 1,533 m, linking upper and lower Koshiki and viewable from observatories.

  • Kamejo Castle Ruins

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    Hilltop castle ruins built by Ogawa Suenao after the 1221 Jokyu War, with a view over the town.

  • Sesenoura Village

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-6-3930

    Odd rocks rise in the sea just in front, a unique landscape. A Heike refugee legend is also told.

  • Solitary Island Wild Dog Monument

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Monument to Muku Hatoju's children's story about Koshiki mountain dogs, called ancestors of Saigo Takamori's dog Tsun.

  • Christian Martyrdom Site

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Memorial at Tsurikakesaki, Shimokoshiki's southern tip, for those who gave their lives for faith. Sad legend remains.

  • Tsuguchi Guardhouse Ruins

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Built in the Edo period for maritime control and monitoring foreign ships.

  • Mitchiri Grassland

    Satsumasendai, Kagoshima · 09969-5-1800

    Early summer yellow Nishinohama daylilies and later pink Kanoko lilies bloom against blue sea and sheer cliffs.
