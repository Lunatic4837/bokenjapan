Things to do in Setouchicho, Kagoshima

Setouchicho · Kagoshima · 10 places

Do

  • Yadorihama Campground

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1001

    Emerald sea and arcing white sand with banyan shade, facilities, diving, windsurfing, and camping.

  • Marine Viewer Seto

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1326

    Whale-shaped semi-submersible boat viewing Oshima Strait coral reefs and tropical fish from Koniya Port.

  • Pearl Extraction Experience

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-4061

    Free experience extracting rare mabe pearls from shells, farmed only in Setouchi on Amami Oshima.

  • Saneku Beach

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Sea called Saneku Blue is exceptionally clear, with fine scenery, coral stone walls, and tropical plants.

  • Surihama Coast

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Beautiful beach called White Village, a marine-sports mecca for diving, jet skiing, fishing, windsurfing, and swimming.

  • Tokohama Coast

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Beautiful open-sea beach with a lion-shaped rock, steep cliffs, coral, shells, sea urchins, and tropical fish.

  • Katoku Beach

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1111

    Three-sided mountain coast on the rough Pacific, ideal for surfing, with 4,000-year-old Katoku ruins.

  • Nishikomi Gate

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 070-9009-4133

    Town-run campsite opened in 2024 on a former school site, with campsites, lodging, and a large bath.

  • Doren Beach

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Emerald-blue Kakeroma Island beach with pandanus shade and white sand; camping, rental boats, showers, toilets.

  • Aratonton House

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-76-3374

    Inn, eatery, and shop; otherwise only sea, banyan trees, and islands.
