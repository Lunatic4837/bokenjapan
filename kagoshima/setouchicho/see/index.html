Setouchicho · Kagoshima · 14 places

See

  • Honohoshi Coast

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1111

    Pacific coast of wave-washed cobbles that rumble like drums, with odd rocks, caves, and fishing.

  • Kochiyama Observatory

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1001

    Overlooks Oshima Strait, an Amami ten-view, Kakeroma Island, Koniya, and January Taiwan cherries.

  • Coral Stone Walls

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1115

    Selected for the transport ministry's 100 Island Treasures. About 350 coral walls on Yoro Island total about 880 meters.

  • Osei Banyan Tree

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Large banyan and Otoko wa Tsurai yo film-location sign; cool in the shade or play on the swing.

  • Ankyaba Battle Site Park

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Eastern Kakeroma park with ammunition depot and defense station remains that guarded the Combined Fleet anchorage.

  • Shodon Deigo Avenue

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Seaside row of deigo trees over 300 years old, with brief May-June red blooms and a film-location house.

  • Sesou Port

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-75-0430

    Shell monument greets visitors at Sesou Port near central Kakeroma Island, a film location for Otoko wa Tsurai yo.

  • Yuidake Observatory

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Overlooks Oshima Strait between Oshima and Kakeroma, with views of Agina and Isu Bay.

  • Nishiamuro Sunset

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    A Kakeroma Island spot with an especially beautiful sunset view that makes you lose track of time.

  • Nishikomi Covered Observation Post

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Occasioned by the Russo-Japanese War, fortresses and batteries were built at strategic Oshima Strait.

  • Toshio Shimao Literary Monument

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Monument to Toshio Shimao, wartime special-attack commander at Nominoura, with restored Shinyo boats in a bunker.

  • Katetsu Heart Bay

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Viewpoint between Honohoshi Coast and Katetsu, marked by a Kagoshima Romantic Road heart-scenery sign.

  • Yoroshima

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Coral stone walls that invite coolness, chosen among 100 Island Treasure Views. Traditional walls remain; history lives on.

  • Sukomo Coast

    Setouchicho, Kagoshima · 0997-72-1199

    Vivid cobalt-blue, highly transparent Sukomo Coast; the village retains ritual asage, god paths, and many folk features.
