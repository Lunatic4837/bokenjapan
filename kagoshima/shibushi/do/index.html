Do in Shibushi

Shibushi · Kagoshima · 7 places

Do

  • Daguri Cape Beach

    Shibushi, Kagoshima · 099-473-1061

    A beach at Daguri Cape on Shibushi Bay, surrounded by subtropical plants and overlooking Biro Island.

  • Daguri Cape Amusement Park

    Shibushi, Kagoshima · 099-473-1061

    The Osumi Peninsula's only amusement park, at Daguri Cape in Nichinan Coast Quasi-National Park.

  • Yomogi no Sato

    Shibushi, Kagoshima · 099-475-2626

    Core tourist facility of former Ariake Town, with warm baths, a restaurant, and a waterfront park.

  • Tanoura Yamamiya Shrine Dago Festival

    Shibushi, Kagoshima · 099-472-1111

    Harvest-prayer festival offers colorful dago-flower rice cakes on bamboo; eating them is said to bring year-long health. Kagura is performed.

  • Ariake Kaiden no Sato Park

    Shibushi, Kagoshima · 099-471-4343

    Stroll to nearby Takenoyama. From the observatory in cherry season, see 1,000 blossoms, Ariake fields, and distant Shibushi Bay.

  • Kaseda Tourist Farm

    Shibushi, Kagoshima · 099-487-2100

    All-you-can-eat strawberry picking late December to late April; sweets and jam sold in the garden.

  • Handmade Hojicha Roasting Experience (Wakoen / Horiguchi Tea)

    Shibushi, Kagoshima · 099-475-1091

    Roast stems and leaves over a direct flame until brown, brew the hojicha, and take it home.
