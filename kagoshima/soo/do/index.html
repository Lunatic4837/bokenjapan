Things to do in Soo, Kagoshima

Soo · Kagoshima · 8 places

Do

  • Takarabe Onsen Health Center

    Soo, Kagoshima · 0986-72-3553

    Sodium bicarbonate onsen from 1,261 m underground, famous as a skin-smoothing beauty bath, with sauna.

  • Okawara Gorge Campsite

    Soo, Kagoshima · 0986-74-2555

    Okawara Gorge campsite, one of the Soo Eight Views, with cabin tents and an August stream festival.

  • Yukyu Forest

    Soo, Kagoshima · 0986-76-8810

    Conifer and broadleaf forest with a clear stream, ideal for forest bathing, with a November walking event.

  • Hanabusa Gorge Recreation Forest Campsite

    Soo, Kagoshima · 0986-78-1100

    Campsite in Anraku River gorge laurel forest with go-karts, putter golf, tennis, and a walking trail.

  • Mesena Sumiyoshi Exchange Center (Mesena Onsen)

    Soo, Kagoshima · 0986-76-7898

    Weak alkaline simple onsen from 1,600 meters underground, with sauna, shop, restaurant, and lodging.

  • Forest Adventure Osumi

    Soo, Kagoshima · 080-8555-1324

    One-hectare forest courses for climbing, crossing, and sliding from elementary first grade, plus laser tag and trampolines.

  • Takarabe Forest School

    Soo, Kagoshima · 0986-28-6120

    Former junior high site with experience lodging and programs such as farming, crafts, pizza, wood-splitting and stargazing.

  • SKLV South Kyushu Livestock and Veterinary Base

    Soo, Kagoshima · 0986-36-6087

    Soo City facility for livestock and veterinary learning, with viewing, lunch, horseback riding, and lodging.
