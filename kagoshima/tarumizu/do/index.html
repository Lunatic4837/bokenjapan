Things to do in Tarumizu, Kagoshima

Tarumizu · Kagoshima · 8 places

Do

  • Enoshima Onsen

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-0765

    Public bath on the Kinko Bay coast; alkaline simple sulfur spring, pH 9.3, constantly flowing.

  • Sarugajo Valley Forest Station Tarumizu

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-9601

    Campground and climate-controlled cottages with onsen by Sarugajo Valley, for overnight or day use and river activities.

  • Sarugajo Radon Onsen

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-38-6117

    Radon onsen in Tarumizu's Sarugajo Valley; source has 621 Bq/kg radon, with sulfur family baths and private baths.

  • Tarumizu Kaigata Onsen Koyokan

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-0505

    Founded in 1932, this simple sulfur spring is known as a beauty bath.

  • TM Farm Onsen (Masakari Onsen)

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-35-3520

    A little-known onsen full of wild charm whose minerals create a unique landscape.

  • Canyoning and Shower Climbing Experience

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-9601

    July-September Takakuma stream programs include canyoning, shower climbing, camping, and cottages.

  • Tarumizu Fishery Experience

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-1165

    Feed farmed amberjack before Sakurajima, enter a minus-20 freezer, and watch filleting.

  • LiveMax Resort Sakurajima Seafront

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-9195

    Opened August 1, 2024. Glamping with three dome-tent types, alkaline simple sulfur spring, indoor and open-air baths, and sauna.
