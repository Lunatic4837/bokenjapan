See in Tarumizu

Tarumizu · Kagoshima · 12 places

See

  • Takatoge Tsutsujigaoka Park

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-1111

    550 m hilltop with 360-degree views of Sakurajima and Kirishima among wild Sata azaleas.

  • Tarumizu Thousand Ginkgo Trees

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-1111

    Over 1,200 ginkgoes turn golden in season. Created over 40 years and won the first Kagoshima Landscape Award.

  • Ushine-Sakai Railway Memorial Park

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-1111

    Yoshino cherries bloom and color this railway memorial park in Tarumizu.

  • Tenuki Shrine (Kaminomiya Shrine)

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-1486

    Popular local power spot for victory, warding off evil, safe birth, child-rearing, and prosperity.

  • Birthplace of Modern Shipbuilding

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima

    Commemorates a shipyard between Tarumizu and Sakurajima; Shoheimaru monument is at the roadside station.

  • Onagaya Ruins (Hayashinojo Castle Ruins)

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima

    Tarumizu Shimazu castle site from 1611; wooden Onagaya and stone walls still remain.

  • Kaigata Sakura Park and Hotaru Park

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-1111

    Two parks near Tarumizu port, known for farmed amberjack. Hotaru Park, a film location, offers Sakurajima views.

  • Futozaki Kannon

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima

    Small Kannon shrine where the highway curves into a cape, with Sakurajima seen through tree branches.

  • Ushine-fumoto Buried Torii (Inari Daimyojin)

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-1111

    Torii buried by 1914 Sakurajima ash, now excavated about 1.45 meters. Designated a Tarumizu natural monument in 2012.

  • Arasaki Parking Area

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-8839

    Seaside spot on Route 220 where northern palm trees accent Sakurajima.

  • Tarumizu Shimazu Family Cemetery

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-0224

    Precious historic site lined with gravestones of successive Tarumizu Shimazu lords and family, who ruled Tarumizu about 260 years.

  • Tarumizu Fumoto

    Tarumizu, Kagoshima · 0994-32-7551

    Only Shimazu-branch heritage district, with orderly grid blocks centered on former castle grounds.
