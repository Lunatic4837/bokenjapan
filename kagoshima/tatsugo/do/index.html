Things to do in Tatsugo, Kagoshima

Tatsugo · Kagoshima · 4 places

Do

  • Kurasaki Coast

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-62-3111

    Most beautiful coast in Japan's first known meteorite crater, with coral reefs; popular for swimming, camping, and diving.

  • Mud Dyeing Experience and Process Tour (Oshima Tsumugi Village)

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-62-3100

    Tour scattered workshops to watch white silk thread become cloth. Mud-dyeing experiences and woven goods sold.

  • Amami Nature Observation Forest

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-54-1329

    2.31-hectare forest park for observing Amami plants, birds, and insects, with trails, athletics, and two observatories.

  • Tebiro Coast

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-62-3111

    High-wave Pacific coast for surfing and bodyboarding, with showers, toilets, odd rocks, and a low-tide heart-shaped pool.
