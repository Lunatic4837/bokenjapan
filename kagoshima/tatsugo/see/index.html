Sights in Tatsugo, Kagoshima

Tatsugo · Kagoshima · 7 places

See

  • Saigo Nanshu Exile Site

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-62-3368

    Saigo Takamori lived here over three years in exile; the wooden house and Katsu Kaishu monument remain.

  • Heart Rock

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-69-4512

    A natural heart-shaped tide pool on a white sand beach, appearing only at low tide, popular with couples.

  • Ganganbana Tunnel

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-57-7215

    Sunset enters the 29-meter tunnel in En, Tatsugo, for days before the spring equinox and after the autumn equinox.

  • Ryugaku-kan (Saigo Pine)

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-62-3110

    Entrance shows wooden statues of Saigo and Aikana made from the damaged Saigo Pine.

  • Aikana Spring

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-69-4512

    Well said to have been used by Aikana, in front of a field Saigo prepared for her.

  • Agiyaba Sotetsu and Basho Grove

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-62-3111

    Spectacular Agiyaba grove of cycads and Japanese bananas, once used as famine food and daily materials.

  • Saigo Pine Site

    Tatsugo, Kagoshima · 0997-69-4512

    Ryukyu pine where Saigo tied the stern rope of the Fukutoku Maru in 1859; only the trunk remains.
