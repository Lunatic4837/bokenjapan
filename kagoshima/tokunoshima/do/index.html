Things to do in Tokunoshima, Kagoshima

Tokunoshima · Kagoshima · 4 places

Do

  • Aze Prince Beach Seaside Park

    Tokunoshima, Kagoshima · 0997-83-4111

    White sand runs 1.5 km amid coral. Named after the 1972 imperial visit. Free campground, plaza, stage, lawn slider.

  • Bullfighting Interaction Experience

    Tokunoshima, Kagoshima · 080-3903-2704

    Meet Tokunoshima bull owners, brush fighting bulls, and learn the island's bullfighting culture from a guide.

  • Tetehama Seaside Park

    Tokunoshima, Kagoshima · 0997-83-4111

    Campground among cycads and other trees, with boulders, sea, parking, toilets, showers, and outdoor hearths.

  • Tokunoshima Town Leisure Pool

    Tokunoshima, Kagoshima · 0997-82-2904

    Affordable municipal pool with a toddler pool and a flowing pool for the whole family.
