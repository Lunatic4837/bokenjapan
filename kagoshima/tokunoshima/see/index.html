Sights in Tokunoshima, Kagoshima

Tokunoshima · Kagoshima · 3 places

See

  • Kanamizaki Cycad Tunnel

    Tokunoshima, Kagoshima · 0997-83-4111

    Cycads form a tunnel-like arch about 200 meters long. An observatory views Tonbara Rock and Amami Oshima.

  • Satokuhama Coast

    Tokunoshima, Kagoshima · 0997-83-4111

    Especially scenic Tokunoshima coast with views of Kakeroma Island and Amami Oshima on clear days.

  • 46th Yokozuna Asashio Taro Memorial Statue

    Tokunoshima, Kagoshima · 0997-83-4111

    Memorial statue of Tokunoshima-born yokozuna Asashio Taro, five-time makuuchi champion with four Outstanding Performance awards.
