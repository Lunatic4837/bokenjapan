Things to do in Toshima, Kagoshima

Toshima · Kagoshima · 4 places

Do

  • Kodakara Island Yudomari Onsen

    Toshima, Kagoshima · 09912-4-2001

    Seaside open-air bath where water springs from coral reef cracks, with three pools of different temperatures.

  • Nakanoshima East District Onsen

    Toshima, Kagoshima · 09912-2-2101

    Tin-roof bath with pale salty sulfur water, used daily by islanders, anglers, and divers.

  • Nakanoshima Nishiku Onsen

    Toshima, Kagoshima · 09912-2-2101

    Simple tin-roof bath whose water clears on sunny days. Sulfur spring used by islanders, anglers, and divers.

  • Ogomori Beach

    Toshima, Kagoshima · 09912-4-2129

    Inlet beach about five minutes from Maekago fishing port, with white sand, emerald sea, camping, and showers.
