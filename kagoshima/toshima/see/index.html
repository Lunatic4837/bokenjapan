See

  • Mt. Otake

    Highest peak of the Tokara Islands (979m), nicknamed Tokara Fuji. Hike and view islands from the summit on clear days.

  • Toshima Village History and Folklore Museum

    Museum introducing Tokara islands' history, culture, and life, with pottery, dugout canoes, and folk tools on display.

  • Nakanoshima Observatory

    Observatory with a 60cm Cassegrain reflecting telescope and monitors for group viewing at the foot of Ontake.

    Toshima, Kagoshima · 09912-2-2338 · Official / source link

  • Takarajima Giant Mural

    Colorful 10-meter-high, 50-meter-wide mural depicting an imagined underwater city at Maekago fishing port.

    Toshima, Kagoshima · 0991-24-2129 · Official / source link

  • Yaruse Lighthouse

    Lighthouse in a ranch behind Jinnyomu Peak, with a viewpoint over Nanatsuyama coast, Ontake and Kuchinoshima.

    Toshima, Kagoshima · Official / source link
