Things to do in Uken, Kagoshima

Uken · Kagoshima · 4 places

Do

  • Yakeuchi no Sato

    Uken, Kagoshima · 0997-67-2295

    Semi-natural onsen with a large bath, far-infrared sauna, and mist sauna in a relaxed atmosphere.

  • Yadon Beach

    Uken, Kagoshima · 0997-67-2211

    Representative Amami beach in coral-rich Yakiuchi Bay, with colorful tropical fish, toilets, showers, and parking.

  • Taen Beach

    Uken, Kagoshima · 0997-67-2211

    Emerald-green clear beach with coral reefs; snorkeling, kayaking, SUP, breakwater fishing, and sometimes dolphins.

  • Funakoshi Beach

    Uken, Kagoshima · 0997-67-2218

    Swimming and snorkeling beach where reefs spread at spring low tide, with doughnut-shaped microatolls.
