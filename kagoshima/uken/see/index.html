Sights in Uken, Kagoshima

Uken · Kagoshima · 3 places

See

  • Mount Yuwan

    Uken, Kagoshima · 0997-67-2211

    Highest peak of the Amami Islands at 694 meters, with superb observatory views.

  • Taken Bus Stop and Taken Kenmun Park

    Uken, Kagoshima · 0997-67-2218

    Third Kenmun park in the village, behind the bus stop, with a banyan, tables, benches, and a folktale board.

  • Minedayama Park

    Uken, Kagoshima · 0997-67-2211

    Summit has an 8m observatory overlooking the village. About 10,000 azaleas bloom in April like a pink carpet.
