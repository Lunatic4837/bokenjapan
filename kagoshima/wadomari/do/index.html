Things to do in Wadomari, Kagoshima

Wadomari · Kagoshima · 3 places

Do

  • Wanjo Beach

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-92-1111

    Most popular white-sand beach on the island, shallow and easy to swim, with showers and a campground.

  • Thalasso Okinoerabu

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-92-3002

    Authentic French thalassotherapy facility using warm marine climate and abundant seawater, facing the sea to refresh body and mind.

  • Kasaishi Seaside Park

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3512

    Year-round subtropical flowers, spectacular lilies from mid-April to early May, a wide sandy beach, and a tower observatory.
