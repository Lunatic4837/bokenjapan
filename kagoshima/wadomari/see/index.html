Sights in Wadomari, Kagoshima

Wadomari · Kagoshima · 8 places

See

  • Japan's Number One Banyan

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3512

    Fuji-like banyan spreading across an elementary schoolyard, planted over 120 years ago by the first graduates.

  • Saigo Nanshu Memorial Hall

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-92-0999

    Recreates Saigo's lattice exile cell on Okinoerabu; see his emaciated statue and learn about life then.

  • Fucha Blowhole Cave

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3512

    Wave-eroded vertical cave; spray rises 20-70 meters in seasonal winds or typhoons, and green turtles are often seen.

  • Sea Turtle Viewpoint

    Wadomari, Kagoshima

    Turtle observation spot from Wanjo Beach to Hanzaki. At high tide, many green turtles feed in shallows; free binoculars.

  • Nanshu Shrine (Wadomari Town)

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-92-0999

    Shrine enshrining Saigo Takamori on Okinoerabu, with bronze statues of him and his dog Tsun.

  • Hanzaki

    Wadomari, Kagoshima

    Scenic cliffs on the island's north. A flat grassy cape offers open views and superb sunsets, especially spring to summer.

  • Yononushi Tomb

    Wadomari, Kagoshima · 0997-92-1111

    Stone-gated tomb of Okinoerabu's king from about 1400, holding urns of the king, his wife, children, and retainers.

  • Sotetsu Jungle

    Wadomari, Kagoshima

    Cycad grove and about 600-meter path exiting near Wanjo Beach, with East China Sea views and tropical cycad scenery.
