Things to do in Yakushima, Kagoshima

Yakushima · Kagoshima · 19 places

Do

  • Shiratani Unsuikyo (Drum Rock)

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-42-3508

    Yakushima recreation forest where even visitors unsure of their stamina can easily see cedars over 1,000 years old.

  • Yakusugi Land

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-42-3508

    Vast forest 15 km from Anbo with five hiking courses of 30, 50, 80, 150, and 210 minutes.

  • Hirauchi Undersea Onsen

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-47-2953

    Rare sulfur onsen rising from the sea, open about two hours around low tide twice daily; swimwear forbidden.

  • Mount Miyanoura

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    Kyushu's highest peak, 1,936 meters, in central Yakushima, nicknamed the Alps of the Sea and popular with climbers.

  • Nagata Inakahama Beach

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    An 800-meter white beach of weathered granite sand where Pacific sea turtles come ashore to lay eggs.

  • Yudomari Onsen

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-48-2806

    Rustic open-air simple sulfur onsen on Yudomari village's rocky shore, facing the ocean, said to help rheumatism and neuralgia.

  • Trolley Road

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    About 8 km trolley track once used to haul timber, still used for night soil, leading to Jomon Cedar.

  • Nishinoyu Onsen, Kuchinoerabu Island

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    Rustic natural open-air bath by the sea, with a slightly hot sodium chloride spring.

  • Shiratani Unsuikyo Trekking, Yakushima

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-42-3508

    Guided trek past Yakusugi, laurel forest, moss, ferns, waterfalls and streams. Yaku monkeys or deer may appear.

  • Isso Beach

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    Beach in Yakushima's northernmost port town, a cove by Yahazu-zaki known for coral diving.

  • Kuchinoerabu Island Honmura Onsen

    Yakushima, Kagoshima

    Bath views of active volcano Shindake and sunsets. Iron-rich simple spring, light brown. Currently unavailable.

  • Mount Motchomu

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    A 940 m granite peak called the Matterhorn of the East, with 3000-year-old cedars and a steep course.

  • Yakushima Village Tour

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-42-2900

    Local storytellers guide history, culture, nature, and industry of 24 lowland villages. Reserve three days ahead.

  • Kuchinoerabujima Yumugi Onsen

    Yakushima, Kagoshima

    Praised for abundant water and floating yellow bath flowers. Island residents' rest spot. Currently closed for planned repairs.

  • Harutahama Beach

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    On Yakushima's largest uplifted coral reef coast: swimming, snorkeling, tide pools with tropical fish and crabs, and a children's pool.

  • Yakushima Environmental Culture Training Center

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-46-2900

    Training facility for learning about Yakushima nature, with programs, a library, and lodging.

  • Kozue Kairo Canopy

    Yakushima, Kagoshima

    Zip-line between trees and walk treetop canopy corridors, viewing the forest from a bird's perspective.

  • Kurio Beach

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    Shallow white-sand beach famous for sea turtle nesting, suited to families with children.

  • Yawaraka

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-42-0109

    Yakushima scent-themed space. Attached workshop extracts aroma from Yakushima materials. Make an aroma spray choosing your scent.
