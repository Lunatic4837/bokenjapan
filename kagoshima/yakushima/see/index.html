    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

  • Kigen Sugi

  • Senpiro Falls

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

  • Yakushima Fruit Garden Papaya no Sato

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-48-2468

    About 1,600-2,000 species of tropical plants and fruits, with tastings and jam sales.

  • Western Forest Road

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

  • Masuku Shrine

  • Wilson's Stump

    Yakushima, Kagoshima · 0997-43-5900

  • Toroki Falls

  • Yodogawa
