Things to do in Yamatomura, Kagoshima

Yamatomura · Kagoshima · 5 places

Do

  • Amami Onsen Yamato Hanahana Beach Resort

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-55-8711

    Opened in April 2024 near an East China Sea beach; Amami Oshima's only natural hot spring.

  • Kuninao Beach

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2117

    White beach of crushed coral sand where sea turtles nest from May; swimming, SUP, and kayaking are popular.

  • Kuninao Sea Turtle Park

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima

    Park adjoining popular swimming spot Kuninao Coast, with age-divided play equipment, gazebo, turtle monument, and stage.

  • Amami Forestpolis

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-58-3166

    About 20-hectare forest park below Mt. Yuwan, with Materiya Falls, a playground, camping, and cottages.

  • Gajutogura

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-55-8711

    Open-air restaurant inside Amami Onsen Yamato Hanahana Beach Resort, serving seasonal BBQ courses and a la carte.
