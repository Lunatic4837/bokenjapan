    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2117

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-58-3166

    About 20-hectare forest park below Mt. Yuwan, with Materiya Falls, a playground, camping, and cottages.
