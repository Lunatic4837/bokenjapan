Sights in Yamatomura, Kagoshima

Yamatomura · Kagoshima · 12 places

See

  • Materiya Falls

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2111

    Named for the sun reflected in its basin; the stream feeds mangroves within a World Natural Heritage area.

  • Miyakozaki Cape

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2117

    Scenic cape in Amami Gunto National Park facing the East China Sea, with Ryukyu bamboo and winter whale sightings.

  • Amami Rabbit Museum Quru Guru

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-1196

    Hands-on museum opened April 2025 to protect the Special Natural Monument Amami rabbit, plus a cafe.

  • Amami Wildlife Conservation Center

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-55-8620

    Opened in 2000 as Japan's seventh wildlife conservation center, exhibiting rare Amami species such as the Amami rabbit.

  • Yamatohama Boregura

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2111

    Five traditional Amami grain storehouses over 100 years old, clustered at the village edge; a prefectural cultural property.

  • Tokuhama Cliffs

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2111

    172-meter sea cliffs above Naon Tunnel, said to have formed in a Kamakura-period earthquake, with an observatory.

  • Mount Yuwan

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima

    694-meter sacred peak between Yamato and Uken, a World Natural Heritage forest with boardwalk and observatory.

  • Kuninao Fukugi Avenue

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2117

    Fukugi trees long planted between houses for wind and fire protection form a breezy scenic photo spot.

  • Imazato Tachigami

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima

    Rises before Imazato village; worshipped as the first stop for gods from Neriyakanaya. In Amami Islands National Park.

  • Kainyo Shrine

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2111

    Dedicated to Naokawa Chio, who brought sugarcane seedlings and brown-sugar techniques from China in early Edo.

  • Toen Fureai Park

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2153

    Benched observatory views Toen Coast, Hien Beach, Ayamazaki, and the Amami-style village from mountain to shore.

  • Mineyama Park

    Yamatomura, Kagoshima · 0997-57-2111

    Observatory on a cape near central Oshima views the west, with May azaleas and beautiful sunsets.
