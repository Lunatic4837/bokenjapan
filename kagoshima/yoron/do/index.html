Things to do in Yoron, Kagoshima

Yoron · Kagoshima · 7 places

Do

  • Yurigahama Beach

    Yoron, Kagoshima · 0997-97-5151

    Phantom sandbar 1.5 km off Oganeku Beach, visible only at low tide from spring to autumn.

  • Akasaki Coast and Fishing Port

    Yoron, Kagoshima · 0997-97-5151

    Clear white-sand coast with rocks, suited to swimming and skin diving, plus a family-friendly fishing port.

  • Yunnu Experience Hall

    Yoron, Kagoshima · 0997-84-3661

    Hands-on facility for learning Yoron life, local cuisine, and culture, with sanshin, folk songs, and salt-making.

  • Oganeku Beach and Campground

    Yoron, Kagoshima · 0997-97-5151

    Coral-fringed white-sand beach on Yoron Island's east coast, good for marine sports, with a cool campground.

  • Udonosu Beach

    Yoron, Kagoshima · 0997-97-5151

    Calm swimming beach a walk from Chabana, used for school meets and popular with islanders.

  • Yoron Town B&G Marine Center Boathouse

    Yoron, Kagoshima · 0997-97-5033

    Snorkeling, canoe and board paddling, and jet skiing, with resident instructors for beginners.

  • Kanebo Beach

    Yoron, Kagoshima · 0997-97-5151

    Sunset beach near Yoron Airport where the setting sun turns the coastline orange.
