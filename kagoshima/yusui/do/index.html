Do in Yusui

Yusui · Kagoshima · 11 places

Do

  • Uono Flight Area

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Paraglider takeoff; cool months may bring early basin cloud seas and sunrise over Kirishima.

  • Takenakaike Park (Town Pool and Somen Nagashi)

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Spring-fed park with flowing somen, a cold town pool, water slide, and fireflies from late May to early June.

  • Tsurumaru Onsen

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-75-2858

    Rare coffee-colored moor spring of plant origin, a base for Kurinodake climbing and river fishing, with an outdoor bath.

  • Kirishima Art Farm

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-1333

    Horse ranch in grand scenery offering beginner rides, a campground, and dog-friendly cottages.

  • Mount Kurinodake

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    A gentle 1,102 m peak for families and beginners, with autumn foliage and summit views.

  • Mori no Yakata Yuttari Hall

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-75-4142

    In Yusui, a noted Kagoshima onsen area. Fully free-flowing natural simple onsen, high in ions, praised for skin.

  • Horse Trust Ranch Hiking Experience

    Yusui, Kagoshima

    Hiking among about 150 retired horses on a Kirishima ranch with views toward Sakurajima.

  • Kurinodake Recreation Village

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Park adjacent to Kirishima Art Forest with two large new play structures finished in March 2026.

  • Maruike Spring Fireflies

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    At Maruike, one of Japan's 100 Famous Waters, natural Genji fireflies can be seen in early summer.

  • Ikehira Park

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Hilltop park east of town with Kirishima and city views, cherry blossoms, forest bathing, playground, and lantern-lit night sakura.

  • Kurinodake Log Camp Village

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-54-1020

    Beside Kirishima Art Farm at 780 meters on Mount Kurino. Former school with pool and lawn in a national park.
