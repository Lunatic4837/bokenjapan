See in Yusui

Yusui · Kagoshima · 12 places

See

  • Maruike Spring

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Selected among Japan's 100 famous waters. About 60,000 tons daily from Kirishima foothills supply the town. Spring blossoms, summer fireflies.

  • Kirishima Art Forest

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-5945

    Outdoor art museum at about 700 meters on Mount Kurino, north of Kirishima, with 23 works including Yayoi Kusama.

  • Hachiman Daijigoku

    Yusui, Kagoshima

    Kurinodake, said to be the oldest volcano in the Kirishima range, has Kyushu's largest fumarole area and sources Kurinodake Onsen.

  • Mount Kurino Railroad Tie Stairs (Mount Kurino Observatory)

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Summit stairs using 7000 ties from the 1988-closed Yamano Line, Japan's number-one railroad-tie stairs.

  • JR Yoshimatsu Station

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Once a railway-town hub, with 115 m of pale-pink moss phlox, C5552 display, railway museum, and SL hall.

  • Railway Museum (Tourist Steam Locomotive Hall)

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-54-2177

    Museum covering Hisatsu Line construction history, with steam locomotive photos, signals, uniforms, a heritage C55 locomotive, and a stone warehouse.

  • Katsukuri Shrine

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Built in the Wado era over 1,260 years ago; bronze mirror is a prefectural Important Cultural Property.

  • Matsuo Castle Ruins

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-4313

    Only mountain castle ruins in southern Kyushu. Kitahara seat; Shimazu Yoshihiro entered 1590. Stone walls, steps remain; main gate restored.

  • Sawahara Plateau

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Second only to Aso in Kyushu: grassland beside primeval forest, biodiversity-certified, with a late-July daylily evening.

  • Koda District Rice Terraces

    Yusui, Kagoshima

    Stone-walled terraces in a quiet forest valley, designated one of Japan's 100 best rice terraces.

  • Southernmost Habitat of Wild Higan Cherry

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    About 60 wild higan cherries, a national natural monument as Japan's southernmost habitat.

  • Tabu Primeval Forest and Japan's Largest Tabu Tree

    Yusui, Kagoshima · 0995-74-3111

    Japan's only tabu primeval forest, in Kawazoe National Forest, contains the largest tabu, 30 meters and about 500 years old.
