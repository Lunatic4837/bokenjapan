Do in Aikawa

Aikawa · Kanagawa · 8 places

Do

  • Hattori Farm

    Kanagawa's largest leisure ranch, 90 minutes from central Tokyo, with dairy cows, other animals, and farm experiences.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Aikawa Park

    A 51-hectare park beside Miyagase Dam with azalea slopes, play equipment, a craft village, and a local history museum.

    Aikawa, Kanagawa · 046-281-3646 · Official / source link

  • Traditional Craft Experience at Aikawa Park

    Try weaving, indigo dyeing, papermaking, pottery, and wood and bamboo crafts in Aikawa Park.

    Aikawa, Kanagawa · 046-281-2438 · Official / source link

  • Nakatsugawa Trout Fishing Spot

    Aikawa, Kanagawa · 046-281-3292 · Official / source link

  • Rainbow Plaza (Aikawa Textile Hall)

    Aikawa's Hanhara prospered in textiles since the Edo period; try hand weaving, indigo dyeing, and papermaking.

    Aikawa, Kanagawa · Official / source link

  • Sakamoto Youth Plaza

    About 200 Somei-Yoshino cherries span 200 m along the Nakatsu River, drawing late-March blossom viewers.

    Aikawa, Kanagawa · 046-285-2111 · Official / source link

  • Hidamari Strawberry Farm

    Spacious strawberry-picking farm using beneficial insects and reduced pesticides, where you can compare each variety's flavor.

    Aikawa, Kanagawa · Official / source link

  • Aikawa Solar Park

    Solar-power spot with a free azalea garden, grass paths, viewing plaza, and free EV fast charger.

    Aikawa, Kanagawa · 045-210-7294 · Official / source link
