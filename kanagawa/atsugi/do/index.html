Things to do in Atsugi, Kanagawa

Atsugi · Kanagawa · 20 places

Do

  • Sagami River Riverbed

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-225-2820

    Ayu fishing is possible; the three-river confluence is the Atsugi Ayu Festival fireworks site.

  • Atsugi Children's Forest Park

    Atsugi, Kanagawa

    Satoyama learning park with wildlife, rice farming, and Japan's longest 106-meter polyethylene slide.

  • Atsugi Herabuna Center

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-245-1063

    Easy herabuna, carp, and goldfish fishing, with free rod rental and a dining facility.

  • Iiyama Hakusan Forest Park

    Atsugi, Kanagawa

    Atsugi forest park on Mount Hakusan with summit views to Yokohama and Shinjuku, plus hiking courses.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Nanasawa Forest Park

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-247-9870

    Chosen among Japan's 100 urban parks; rhododendrons, barbecue, foliage, pottery, and Mt. Oyama views.

  • Atsugi Glass Studio

    Atsugi, Kanagawa

    Workshop in Atsugi City with bright, fun classes and experiences about glass beauty and glassmaking.

  • Thunder Snake ATSUGI

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-226-1281

    A facility equipped with practice studios and a live venue.

  • Nanamagari Pass

    Atsugi, Kanagawa

    A winding pass road partway along the Hinata Yakushi hiking course.

  • Higashi-Tanzawa Green Park

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-241-1427

    Lush outdoor grounds covering 50,000 square meters, with athletic hike, fishing pond, barbecue, and popular Atsugi burgers.

  • Tree Cross Adventure

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 080-3306-7738

    Leisure facility with 42 obstacles 5-15m up in trees, Tarzan ropes, and a zip line up to 173m.

  • Bosai no Oka Park

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-270-1035

    Lush park overlooking Tanzawa and Oyama ranges, with seasonal activities and evacuation facilities for 20,000.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Comprehensive Disaster Prevention Center

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-227-1700

    Disaster information and experience floors with earthquake and storm-flood simulations. Reservations currently required.

  • Sagami Sato Kagura Kakizawa Troupe

    Atsugi, Kanagawa

    Masked silent myth drama, also called Jindai Kagura, influenced by Mibu Kyogen and transmitted as Aiko Kagura since 1912.

  • Sweet Potato Digging at Blueberry Hills Atsugi

    Atsugi, Kanagawa

    Autumn sweet-potato digging and tasting of five varieties grown without pesticides or chemical fertilizers.

  • Ogino Sports Park

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-225-2900

    Sports park with gym and fields, plus 80,000 wild plants, nemophila, and cosmos.

  • Kabuto-yu Onsen Sansuiro

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-248-0025

    Secluded one-inn onsen with free-flowing water that welled up in the Great Kanto Earthquake. Day baths often unavailable.

  • Kenjo Hiking Course

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-248-1102

    Hiking course from Hirozawa-ji Onsen parking past a rock over 40 meters, with Sagami Plain views.

  • Wakamiya Park

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-225-2410

    Park with lawns, an outdoor stage, and tennis courts, a D51 locomotive display, and carp streamers from April to May.

  • Bosai no Oka Park Play Pond

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-270-1035

    Shallow water-play area with fountains and bubbles; nearby arbor for parents. Roller slide and playground equipment on site.

  • Ogino Sports Park Pool

    Atsugi, Kanagawa · 046-225-2900

    Year-round indoor heated pool, shallow toddler pool, and summer outdoor 50-meter pool, plus plaza, slide, and wildflower garden.
