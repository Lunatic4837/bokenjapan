Sights in Ayase, Kanagawa

Ayase · Kanagawa · 4 places

See

  • Kanzaki Ruins Museum

    Ayase, Kanagawa

    Late Yayoi moated settlement, a national historic site, backfilled and developed as Kanzaki Ruins Park with explanation boards.

  • Gosha Shrine

    Ayase, Kanagawa · 0467-77-5310

    Yamato Takeru enshrined five earth deities here. Sitting stone, huge chinquapin tree; tree, hall and plaque are city cultural properties.

  • Kanigaya Park

    Ayase, Kanagawa · 0467-70-5685

    Terrain-based waterfront park. Hilltop lawn views Fuji and Tanzawa; wetland garden has kingfishers. Irises June-July, lotus July-August.

  • Ayase Rose Garden

    Ayase, Kanagawa · 0467-38-3090

    New-era rose garden of 11 world-themed gardens, with about 680 roses of 150 kinds on 7,700 square meters.
