Do in Chigasaki

Chigasaki · Kanagawa · 8 places

Do

  • Eboshi Rock Sightseeing Cruise

    Forty-minute cruise around Eboshi Rock, reefs 1.4 km off Chigasaki Beach, viewing Enoshima and Mt. Fuji.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-82-6946 · Official / source link

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Chigasaki Satoyama Park

    Park with seasonal flowers, ponds, slides, trampolines, Mount Fuji views, and barbecue areas.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-50-6058 · Official / source link

  • Suzuki-en Fruit Picking

    Low-tree flat tourist farm; children, seniors, and wheelchair users pick organic grapes, pears, chestnuts, persimmons, and kiwi.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-51-0283 · Official / source link

  • Matsugaoka Rathgen Street Park

    Park on German trader Rudolf Rathgen's former villa site, with cherry trees and play equipment.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · Official / source link

  • Southern Beach Chigasaki

    Beach with parking in front of the sea and good car access, with Shonan Chigasaki roadside station along the coast.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-81-7144 · Official / source link

  • Kakizawa Farm

    Farm in Chigasaki where people and cows can smile, at Amanuma.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-51-0940 · Official / source link

  • Noten Yumoto Yukai Sokai Chigasaki

    Chloride day-trip onsen from 1500m with nationwide top component content, unique baths, and running exit service.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-82-4126 · Official / source link

  • Daiichi Cutter Yellow Park (Central Park)

    About 150 cherry trees form a tunnel, popular for walks, running, events, and weekend family visits.

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · Official / source link
