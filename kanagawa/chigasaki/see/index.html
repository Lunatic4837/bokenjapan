Sights in Chigasaki, Kanagawa

Chigasaki · Kanagawa · 10 places

See

  • Chigasaki Southern C

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa

    Symbol of Southern Beach Chigasaki, featured on a Southern All Stars CD jacket and famous as a matchmaking spot.

  • Left Fuji of Nango

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-82-1111

    Rare Tokaido spot where Mount Fuji is seen on the left from Toriido Bridge, depicted by Hiroshige.

  • Headland Boardwalk

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa

    T-shaped jetty and about 1 km promenade for walking and jogging, overlooking Enoshima, Mt. Fuji, and Sagami Bay.

  • Himuro Camellia Garden

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-82-2823

    Donated to Chigasaki and opened in 1991, with 1,300 trees and over 200 camellia varieties.

  • Kaiko Ken Memorial Museum

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-87-0567

    Novelist Kaiko Ken lived here in Chigasaki from 1974 until 1989; his home is now a memorial museum.

  • Nanko-in Memorial Taiyo no Sato Garden

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-82-1111

    Partly open former Meiji tuberculosis sanatorium garden with wooden Western buildings, a fountain pond, and Fuji views.

  • Chigasaki City Museum of Art

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-88-1177

    Opened in 1998 as a local museum collecting Chigasaki-related works and holding themed shows from 2,000 pieces.

  • Shorai-an

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-87-5258

    Strolling Japanese garden from a former villa, with pond, stone bridge, plum grove, and rentable tea room.

  • Chigasaki Museum of Associated People

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa · 0467-81-5015

    Exhibits Chigasaki-related people and works; workshops held. Deepens knowledge of thinking, living, history, and local character.

  • Old Sagami River Bridge Piers (Historic Site and Natural Monument)

    Chigasaki, Kanagawa

    Bridge piles appeared in rice fields after the 1923 Great Kanto Earthquake; historic site and natural monument.
