Sights in Ebina, Kanagawa

Ebina · Kanagawa · 9 places

See

  • Otani Neighborhood Park

    Ebina, Kanagawa · 046-235-8439

    Observation plaza views of Tanzawa, Mt. Oyama and Mt. Fuji, with benches and seasonal greenery.

  • Ryuho-ji

    Ebina, Kanagawa

    Early Muromachi Kencho-ji branch temple; Kannon hall, Nio gate and statues are Ebina City cultural properties.

  • Sagami Sankawa Park

    Ebina, Kanagawa · 046-236-5008

    River park upstream of the Sagami, Nakatsu and Koayu confluence; clear days show Tanzawa and Oyama.

  • Akihayama Kofun Group

    Ebina, Kanagawa · 046-235-4925

    Kofun group built about 1700 years ago in the Yayoi-Kofun transition; national historic site since 2005.

  • Ebina City Onkokan

    Ebina, Kanagawa

    First floor shows Ebina history, Sagami Kokubunji model and excavated pottery; folk materials upstairs.

  • Sagami Kokubun-ji and Bronze Bell

    Ebina, Kanagawa · 046-232-8212

    Kamakura-period bronze bell donated by Kokubun Sueyori, a nationally designated Important Cultural Property, and a Nyoirin Kannon statue.

  • Imafuku Yakuimon Park

    Ebina, Kanagawa · 046-235-4925

    Park on land donated by the Imafuku family, Edo-period headmen, with an Edo yakuimon gate and book storehouse.

  • Great Zelkova of Ebina

    Ebina, Kanagawa · 046-235-4925

    A giant zelkova estimated at 570 years old, grown from a stake driven upside down to moor a boat.

  • Romancecar Museum

    Ebina, Kanagawa · 046-233-0909

    Railway museum with Odakyu trains, Romancecar exhibits, a driving simulator, and a giant diorama.
