Things to do in Fujisawa, Kanagawa

Fujisawa · Kanagawa · 29 places

Do

  • Shonan Monorail

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa

    Suspended monorail from Ofuna to Enoshima in 14 minutes, max 75 km/h, viewing Ofuna Kannon and Mt. Fuji.

  • Enoden (Enoshima Electric Railway)

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-24-2713

    A green electric railway linking Fujisawa and Kamakura, with views of Enoshima, Mt. Fuji, mountains, and sea.

  • Sakai River Cycling Road

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-50-3531

    Cycling road beside the Sakai and Hikichi rivers, which flow through Yamato and Fujisawa to Enoshima.

  • Satsumaya Main Store Sushi Making Experience

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-23-2487

    Sushi class a 15-minute walk from Fujisawa Station. Chef and bilingual hostess teach in English.

  • Shinrin Park (Strolling and Birdwatching)

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-27-4437

    A 16.2-hectare park with a 1.5 km ridge trail, play area, spring-fed pond for birdwatching, and an Edo-period house.

  • Prefectural Shonan Coast Park Surf Village

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-34-9912

    Open for sea contact and understanding, beach sports and lifesaving support, and general visitor use.

  • Enoshima Island Spa

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-29-0688

    Day-use natural onsen and spa at Enoshima's entrance; Shonan and fair-weather Fuji views. Hotel wing opened March 2020.

  • Enoshima Yacht Harbor

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-22-2128

    Public yacht harbor created for the 1964 Tokyo Olympics and the Tokyo 2020 sailing venue.

  • Yunochi Karasawa

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-26-2614

    Super sento with 11 baths, including a Cleopatra carbonated spring and hinoki bath, and two saunas.

  • Enoden Enoshima Station

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa

    Enoshima Station is operated by Enoshima Electric Railway, a private railway company.

  • Akibadai Cultural Gymnasium

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-88-1111

    Sports facility opened in October 1984, the model for the match venue in manga Slam Dunk.

  • Yakult Honsha Shonan Cosmetics Factory

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-25-8960

    Five-senses factory tour with original ingredients, production-line viewing, and cosmetics try-on.

  • Fruit Park Chogo

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-43-5868

    Chogo, Fujisawa orchard: fruit picking, direct sales, delivery. Plums from May; pears, grapes, Western pears, persimmons, figs August-December.

  • Miyahara Blueberry Picking Farm

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa

    Blueberry picking experience available late July to late August. Please check opening days before visiting as a family.

  • Enoshima Escar

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-23-2444

    Japan's first outdoor escalator to Enoshima's summit; visitors can reach the top without many stairs.

  • Miyaji Pork BBQ

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa

    Rare pork from a Shonan farm family; BBQ once or twice monthly; reservation required.

  • Shonan Nagisa Park Bicycle Rental

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa

    Third-sector firm funded by prefecture and city began 2014 bicycle rentals from Enoshima to Kamakura or Chigasaki.

  • Tsujido Seaside Park

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-34-0011

    Summer jumbo pool has seven pools, including a wave pool and water slides, plus a lawn and traffic park.

  • Shonandai Park

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-46-7788

    Busy park about 10 minutes from Shonandai Station, with paid tennis courts, lawns, playgrounds, and benches.

  • Shonan Coast Park

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-55-2543

    Park stretching about 2 km along Kugenuma and Katase coasts, with surf village, slides, and sea-view lawns.

  • Enoshima Rocky Shore Play

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-22-4141

    About 4 km around, 60 m high. Southern rocky shores face open sea; shore play and snorkeling near low tide.

  • Katase Beach

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa

    Coastal view of Mount Fuji, especially large and clear on sunny winter days.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Sports Center

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-81-2570

    Established in 1968; renewed in 2020 with a 50m pool and wheelchair-accessible rooms.

  • Kanagawa Sea Ride

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa

    Enjoy sea-breeze views of Mt. Fuji and Enoshima seen only from the sea.

  • Robolink

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-90-3490

    Robot-industry exchange site where anyone can view and try robots and VR; exhibits may change.

  • Tsujido Beach

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-24-4141

    Calm family beach; area popular for surfing, near Prefectural Tsujido Seaside Park with a pool.

  • Katase Nishihama and Kugenuma Beach

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-24-4141

    Representative Shonan beach about 1 km long, calm and shallow, with Enoshima, Fuji, Hakone, Izu views and volleyball.

  • Katase Higashihama Beach

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-24-4141

    Among Japan's older beaches and a marine-sports mecca, with green Enoshima and Fuji views, calm and shallow.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Tsujido Seaside Park Jumbo Pool

    Fujisawa, Kanagawa · 0466-35-7347

    Flowing pool, wave pool, water slides, a shallow toddler pool, and pools for children in swim diapers.
