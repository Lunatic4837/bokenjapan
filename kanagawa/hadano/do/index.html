Things to do in Hadano, Kanagawa

Hadano · Kanagawa · 17 places

Do

  • Mount Takatori

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Nature-rich summit reachable by hiking, with fine views and benches suited to lunch or rest, via a varied up-and-down trail.

  • Koboyama Park

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-9648

    Prefectural park of three low mountains for year-round hiking, with Fuji views from Gongen-yama.

  • Hadano International Riding Club

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-81-5621

    Relaxed horseback riding amid greenery, Mount Fuji to the northwest, and full spring cherry blossoms.

  • Mount Gyojagatake

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Narrow summit once had an En no Gyoja statue, now a monument with his image. Chain sections toward Shin-Dainichi.

  • Sannoto

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Summit offers a full view of the Omoteone ridge and distant Mount Fuji, plus a rest area and toilets.

  • Mount Tonodake

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Most popular peak in Omote Tanzawa; a 4-5 hour climb with summit views of Mount Fuji and Sagami Bay.

  • Mount Ninoto

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    1122 m peak on the Tanzawa Omoteone; one name origin is a Karako Shrine legend.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Hadano Togawa Park

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-87-9020

    Year-round flowers against Tanzawa mountains, with summer river play, barbecue, a tea room, playground, and sports facilities.

  • Omote Tanzawa Citizens' Forest

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Paths from Shijuhasse River to Kurinokibora at 908 m, with cedar and cypress plantations, broadleaf woods, shelters, and a lawn.

  • Kuzuha House Nature Observation Facility

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-84-7874

    Opened in 1998 as a nature observation base in Kuzuha Green Space, Kanagawa's first National Trust green space (1987).

  • Kobo no Sato-yu

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-69-2641

    Day-trip onsen with two springs: an outdoor bath plus indoor and private baths. Calcium-rich water warms the body.

  • Meisui Hadano Fujimi-no-Yu

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-1026

    Day-trip onsen with Mount Fuji views. Water from 100m down is rich in metasilicic acid, said to beautify skin.

  • Hadano Tanzawa Climbing Park

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-63-2630

    Bouldering facility in Hadano Togawa Park, plus adjacent lead and speed walls for all three sport-climbing events.

  • Mount Tonodake (Yabitsu Pass Trailhead)

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Trailhead pass for Tanzawa and Oyama ridges, said to be a Warring States battlefield.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Mountain Sports Center

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-87-9025

    Mountaineering and nature activity base in Hadano Togawa Park, with 12 rooms for camps, climbing, and recreation.

  • Totonoi no Sato Hadano Yukaraku

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-84-4126

    Day-trip onsen surrounded by Tanzawa nature, rebranded May 2025, with Hadano's only medicinal carbonated spring.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Hadano Togawa Park Minashi River

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-87-9020

    A shallow river small children can enjoy, with a downstream section dogs can also use.
