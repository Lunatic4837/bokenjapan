See in Hadano

Hadano · Kanagawa · 27 places

See

  • Minamoto no Sanetomo Head Mound

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Said mound where Minamoto no Sanetomo's head was secretly buried in Hadano after his 1219 assassination. Festival each November 23.

  • Izumo Taisha Sagami Branch Shrine

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-81-1122

    1888 branch of Izumo Taisha enshrining Okuninushi, revered as Kanto's Izumo, with a thousand-year zelkova.

  • Zenba Pass

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-8833

    Border pass of Hadano and Isehara, used until the Zenba Tunnel was completed in early Showa.

  • Mount Nenbutsu

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-8833

    Ridge mountain from Mount Kobo to Mount Takatori; Buddhist chant gatherings lasted until early Showa.

  • Hadano Springs (Kobo no Shimizu)

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Hadano water ranked first nationwide for taste in the Selected 100 Famous Waters poll; spring hiking is popular.

  • Ryokusui-an

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Early Showa tobacco farmhouse relocated to Hadano and registered as a national tangible cultural property.

  • Mount Karasuoyama

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Overlook Hadano City beyond the Sannoto ridge and enjoy the Omoteone range, including Sannoto and Tonodake.

  • Lake Shinsei

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-9648

    Lake formed when the 1923 earthquake dammed Ichikizawa; a national registered monument since 2021.

  • Entsu-ji Temple

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Hadano temple with a city-designated important cultural property, a wooden Eleven-Faced Kannon.

  • Kokuryu Falls

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    15 m waterfall that never dries, cool even in summer. Name likely comes from a nearby prayer master's hermitage.

  • Sakuradote Kofun Park

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-87-5542

    By Hadano's Mizunashi River, 35 late-7th-century circular mounds were excavated and six preserved, among the prefecture's largest groups.

  • Jotoku-in Iris Garden

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Early June to early July, naturally occurring Genji and Heike fireflies sometimes fly at night, evoking a paradise.

  • Imaizumi Firefly Park

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Green park near Hadano Station with Mukaihara spring stream, protected as a firefly habitat by volunteers.

  • Nakogi Terraced Rice Fields

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Selected as excellent terraced rice fields. Farmers and an NPO conserve them and offer farming exchanges, preserving biodiverse rural scenery.

  • Imaizumi Meisui Sakura Park

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-73-8612

    Built in 2005 to mark 50 years since Hadano became a city. Cherry-blossom and famous-water park for local rest.

  • Maimai Spring

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Named after an ancient maimaizu well. Groundwater quality improved, so water now flows artesian from 20 meters down.

  • Nanohana-dai

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-8833

    Clear days give 360-degree views from Boso to Fuji, including Enoshima, Izu, and Hakone. Narrow passes have tight curves.

  • Tsurumaki Hydrangea Walking Path

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-8833

    A hydrangea path 20 minutes on foot from Tsurumaki Onsen Station. Rainy-season blooms stand out vividly against green fields.

  • Ryujin Spring

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Upper Mizunashi River spring. A dragon-shaped rock is said to house the dragon god, where ascetics and hunters rested.

  • Shirasasa Inari Shrine

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-81-0256

    Said to be one of Kanto's three great Inari shrines. Ikkanida Spring, Hikarimo pond, free ceiling viewing, antique markets.

  • Hadano Cherry Blossom Road

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    About 6.2 km of cherry trees from Nishi-Otake to Horiyamashita, Kanagawa's longest, forming beautiful arches each spring.

  • Dainichi-do

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Said to have been founded in 742 by Gyoki. Its 175 cm Dainichi Nyorai is a prefectural Important Cultural Property.

  • Shungoku Spring

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    About 30 minutes uphill from Minoge bus stop along Kaname River. Soft, low-hardness spring also used as a water source.

  • Higeso Falls

    Hadano, Kanagawa

    Named for monk Shungoku, who had a chest-length beard and trained here. Mixed woods surround the mystical falls.

  • Minoge Nature Observation Forest

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-82-9618

    Deciduous oak forest offering spring greenery, autumn foliage, winter bare trees, birds, and animals.

  • Hadano Ukiyo-e Gallery

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-86-6309

    Gallery in Hadano City Library showing about 1,900 ukiyo-e donated in 1998 by collector Otsu Maruko.

  • Hadano Municipal Miyanaga Takehiko Memorial Art Museum

    Hadano, Kanagawa · 0463-78-9100

    Displays Miyanaga Takehiko's diverse works, changing exhibitions about every six months.
