Things to do in Hakone, Kanagawa

Hakone · Kanagawa · 25 places

Do

  • Hakone Pirate Ship

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-7722

    Three pirate ships cruise Lake Ashi, with views of seasonal nature, Mount Fuji, and an underwater torii.

  • Hakone Ropeway

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0465-32-2205

    Ropeway between Owakudani and Togendai stations touring Hakone from about 1,000 meters, with Mount Fuji views.

  • Tenzan Tojigo

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-86-4126

    Day-trip onsen in a quiet Sukumo River valley with baths, sauna, meals, and lodging from three nights.

  • Hakone Komagatake Ropeway

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    A 1,783-meter ropeway in a national park links Lake Ashi to Komagatake summit in about seven minutes, with 360-degree views.

  • Hakone Lake Ashi Sightseeing Cruise

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-6351

    Since 1920, regular and circular cruises link three ports, with panoramic deck views of Lake Ashi.

  • Hakone Craft House

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-82-9210

    Hands-on craft hall in Hakone Gora Park with seven making experiences and one-on-one instruction.

  • Forest Adventure Hakone

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    French-born outdoor park with adventure and canopy courses, about 120m zip slide, and nearby putter golf.

  • Hakone Kowakien Yunessun

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-82-4126

    Onsen theme park: swimsuit Yunessun and nude Mori no Yu, coffee bath, and Hakone's only water slide.

  • Hakone-en

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-1151

    Hakone resort complex for leisure, dining, and shopping, with Lake Ashi cruises and the Komagatake Ropeway departing on site.

  • Hakone Yuryo

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-8411

    Large day-trip onsen in Hakone Yumoto with 19 private open-air baths, among the largest in greater Tokyo, plus full meals.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Onshi Hakone Park

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-7484

    Prefectural park on the former Meiji-era Hakone Imperial Villa site, overlooking Mount Fuji and Lake Ashi.

  • Forest Adventure Hakone / Odawara / Yokohama

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    French-born outdoor park using forest trees, where you operate safety gear and advance yourself.

  • Ninja Bus Water Spider

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-1151

    New amphibious bus dives into Lake Ashi for views of Hakone's outer mountains, Mount Fuji, and seasonal scenery.

  • Fun Space Lake Ashi Camp Village Lakeside Villa

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-8279

    Campground in Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park with rental villa cabins, tent sites, and auto sites.

  • Hakone Tozan Railway (Hydrangea Train)

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0465-32-6823

    Hydrangeas bloom from mid-June along the climbing line; a reserved-seat night train passes illuminated spots.

  • Hakone Mountain Bike Cruising

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-9222

    Mountain bike tours through forests full of negative ions around Lake Ashi.

  • Saien

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Renovated old house in Miyanoshita serving monk-made Buddhist vegetarian breakfast by reservation from 8 a.m.

  • Hakuundo Tea Garden

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Said to have been started by Don'o Masuda Takashi, a tea master since Rikyu. Tea preparation and interior viewing available.

  • Lake Ashi Shore (Smelt Fishing and Lunch)

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-7361

    A simple natural spawning method allows more smelt to be hatched and released. Smelt swim to the shore.

  • Amimoto Oba

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-8984

    Eat Lake Ashi smelt caught by the owner, or fish at the attached Lake Ashi Fishing Center.

  • Mount Kintoki

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Stratovolcano active from 350,000 to 270,000 years ago and the highest peak of Hakone Dairinzan.

  • Kai Sengokuhara

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 050-3134-8092

    All 16 guest rooms have open-air baths for enjoying Hakone Sengokuhara and changing scenery.

  • Hakone-en Golf Course

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-6555

    Offers refreshing shots toward Lake Ashi.

  • Sengokuhara Yutate Lion Dance

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Yutate kagura from 1776. Rare lion hot-water rite for health, safety, and harvest. National and Kanagawa intangible cultural property.

  • Miyagino Yutate Lion Dance

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Transmitted in 1776. Lions rarely perform this hot-water rite, praying for health and harvest. Prefectural and national cultural property.
