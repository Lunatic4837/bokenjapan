Sights in Hakone, Kanagawa

Hakone · Kanagawa · 53 places

See

  • Sengokuhara Pampas Grassland

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Vast pampas grass on Mount Daigatake is green in spring and golden in autumn; one of Kanagawa's 50 scenic spots.

  • Owakudani Park

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-5201

    Sulfurous volcanic valley of white smoke and dead trees, once called Hell Valley, reached by Hakone Ropeway.

  • Lake Ashi

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Hakone volcano caldera lake with pirate-ship cruises, possible reflected Fuji, and summer festival fireworks.

  • Hakone Shrine and Kuzuryu Shrine

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-7123

    Founded in 757 by imperial order and revered by Yoritomo and Ieyasu. Kuzuryu Shrine honors Lake Ashi's guardian deity.

  • Hakone Gora Park

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-82-2825

    Japan's first French-style formal garden, over 110 years old, with symmetrical fountains, seasonal flowers, and roses.

  • Hakone Open-Air Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-82-1161

    Open-air museum showing about 120 masterpieces by modern and contemporary sculptors against Hakone mountains.

  • Hakone-en Aquarium

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-1151

    Forest aquarium with saltwater and freshwater halls, a 1,255-ton seawater tank, otters, penguins, and Baikal seals.

  • Hakone Glass Forest Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-86-3111

    Displays Venetian glass that captivated Renaissance European royalty, plus Italian dining and an imported glass shop.

  • Hakone Shrine

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-7123

    Shrine founded in 757 beside Lake Ashi, revered by samurai for luck, wish fulfillment, and traffic safety.

  • Hakone Museum of Art

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-82-2623

    Oldest Hakone museum, opened 1952 in Gora. Japanese ceramics plus moss, maple, and rock gardens, a national scenic site.

  • Hakone Karakuri Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-7826

    Museum of Hakone yosegi works by master craftsmen. Visitors can touch trick pieces and make boxes, coasters, or banks.

  • Hakone Lalique Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-2255

    Sparkling jewelry and glass works by French craftsman Rene Lalique, spanning his lifetime.

  • Pola Museum of Art

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-2111

    Opened 2002 in Hakone. Impressionist to 20th-century Western paintings and contemporary art; seasonal forest trails.

  • Pola Museum of Art: A New Eye - Monet and 21st Century Art

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-2111

    Exhibits all 19 Monet oils, Asia's largest collection, plus 18 contemporary artist groups, through April 2027.

  • Moto-Hakone Old Highway Cedar Avenue

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Said to be planted in 1618, cedars up to 350 years old line 500 meters of the old highway.

  • Hakone Checkpoint

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-6635

    Edo-period checkpoint restored using period techniques from 1983 materials. Adjacent museum and Mount Fuji views from the lookout.

  • Hakone Botanical Garden of Wetlands

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-7293

    Japan's first wetland plant garden in Hakone Sengokuhara, with about 1,700 species blooming through the seasons.

  • Tsubaki Line (Tsubakidai)

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0465-63-2111

    One of Kanagawa's 50 scenic spots, a hill on the camellia-lined Tsubaki Line, best February to April.

  • Mount Taikan

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    On the Yugawara-Hakone border, with views of Mount Fuji and Lake Ashi. Said to be named after Yokoyama Taikan.

  • cu-mo Hakone

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0465-32-2205

    A 2020 Sounzan Station spot with a viewing terrace, free foot bath, and sweets shop.

  • Hakone Sekisho Travel Story Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-6355

    Beside Lake Ashi cruise pier and Hakone Checkpoint; cedar avenue is walkable. Serves homemade 100-percent soba.

  • Okada Museum of Art

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-87-3931

    Shows about 450 artworks from ancient to modern Japan, China, and Korea, with a seasonal garden and footbath cafe.

  • Lake Ashi Skyline

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    A toll road spanning Kanagawa and Shizuoka prefectures, famous for Mount Fuji views.

  • Narukawa Art Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-6828

    On Lake Ashi in Hakone, opened 1988, focusing on contemporary Japanese painting with over 4,000 works.

  • Hakone Samurai Village Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-84-8177

    Displays Edo-to-Kamakura armor, helmets, matchlocks, swords, and paintings; armor dressing in Hakone Sengokuhara.

  • Hakone Geomuseum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-8140

    Museum where you can encounter the mysteries and appeal of Hakone's unique volcanic terrain.

  • Hakone Ekiden Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-7511

    Introduces 100 years of Hakone Ekiden history from the first race in 1920, with race topic videos inside.

  • Choan-ji

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Popular for autumn leaves and 500 Arhat statues; a flower-rich Soto Zen temple with purple iwashajin blooms around October.

  • Miyagino Hayakawa Embankment Cherry Blossoms

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    About 120 Somei-Yoshino trees line about 600m of the Hayakawa embankment; peak late March to mid-April.

  • Hakone Museum of Photography

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-82-2717

    Permanent Mount Fuji photographs by Hakone photographer Kei Endo; opened in 2002 in Gora.

  • Mount Komagatake

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-1151

    Only Hakone view of seven Izu islands, Suruga and Sagami bays, plus Fuji and the outer rim.

  • Nicolai Bergmann Hakone Gardens

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-83-9087

    Unique Hakone gardens by Danish flower artist Nicolai Bergmann, combining nature and flower art, with seasonal blooms and a cafe.

  • Hiryu Falls

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Kanagawa's largest waterfall, falling 15 then 25 meters; Kamakura-period ascetics trained beneath it.

  • Honma Yosegi Art Museum

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5646

    Museum of traditional Hakone yosegi since the Edo period, with about 500 works and 200 on display.

  • Hakone Kuzuryu Forest

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Lush nature park by Lake Ashi with wild trees and flowers, Hakone Kuzuryu Shrine, a nature trail, and lawns.

  • Hakone Old Highway

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Part of the early Edo Tokaido over Hakone Pass; west-slope stone pavement was laid in 1680.

  • Kanrei Domon

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    1931 shelter under a Great Kanto Earthquake-collapsed cliff, protecting vehicles from rockfall. National Important Cultural Property since 2015.

  • Kojiri Water Gate

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Built near Ashinoko Camp Village at Sakasagawa mouth to regulate lake water level.

  • Sansho Roses near Komagatake Summit

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Pale pink one-day sansho roses bloom mid-May to mid-June near Hakone Komagatake's about 1,356 m summit.

  • Kousa Dogwood around Lake Ashi

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    White kousa dogwood blooms around caldera Lake Ashi in June, with red fruit in September and November foliage.

  • Hakone-Yumoto Firefly Park

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-7751

    Fireflies restored by a local group glow in early June; now over 500, with a stream and hydrangeas.

  • Shojin Pond Area

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Near Route 1's highest point on Kamakura-period Yusaka Road, with Motohakone stone Buddhas and April bean cherries.

  • Chitose Bridge

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Built in 1930 with foreign tourists in mind; wave-linked newel posts; National Important Cultural Property since 2015.

  • Motohakone Stone Buddhas

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Near Shojin Pond, stone Buddhas centered on 3.2m Rokudo Jizo are designated National Important Cultural Property and historic site.

  • Yao Bikuni's Tomb

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Part of the Moto-Hakone stone Buddha group near Shojingaike; the surrounding area is a National Historic Site.

  • Mount Kanmurigatake

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    A 1,409-meter volcano in Hakone formed as a lava dome after a steam explosion and collapse about 3,000 years ago.

  • Rokudo Jizo

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    Late Kamakura to early Muromachi Jizo stone Buddhas and pagodas; a National Important Cultural Property.

  • Tada no Mitsunaka's Tomb

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    A late Kamakura stone treasure-seal pagoda near Shojingaike, designated a National Historic Site and Important Cultural Property.

  • Mount Kamiyama

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-7410

    Highest Hakone peak overlooking Lake Ashi, from Sagami Bay to Boso, with Mount Fuji views.

  • Asahi Bridge

    Hakone, Kanagawa

    A concrete arch bridge on National Route 1 at the gateway to Hakone.

  • Twenty-five Bodhisattvas

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Stone Buddhas and pagodas are National Important Cultural Properties of medieval Jizo faith; the area is a National Historic Site.

  • Graves of the Soga Brothers and Tora Gozen

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    Three stone five-ring pagodas along National Route 1 toward Ashinoyu, near Shojin Pond; National Important Cultural Properties.

  • Ocho Jizo

    Hakone, Kanagawa · 0460-85-5700

    A cliff-carved Buddha west of National Route 1 near Shojingaike, a National Important Cultural Property.
