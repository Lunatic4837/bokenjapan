Things to do in Hayama, Kanagawa

Hayama · Kanagawa · 9 places

Do

  • Hayama Marina

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-875-0002

    Marina opened in 1964 at Hayama, Japan's yachting birthplace, overlooking Mt. Fuji.

  • Chojagasaki Coast

    Hayama, Kanagawa

    A coast known for views of Enoshima, the Izu Peninsula, and Mt. Fuji, and famous as a swimming beach.

  • Lydlys Atelier and Showroom

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 080-6625-0127

    Jewelry brand atelier on the third floor above Hayama Isshiki Post Office, with ring and bangle workshops.

  • SCAPES THE SUITE Afternoon Tea

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-877-5730

    Mt. Fuji and Enoshima views, Hayama-inspired seasonal sweets, savories, and complimentary drinks.

  • Beach Hayama Outdoor Fitness Club

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-854-4046

    Hayama outdoor fitness club offering surfing, SUP, beach yoga, outrigger canoe, and trail running.

  • Shonan Beach Yoga

    Hayama, Kanagawa

    On Shonan beach, stand barefoot on warm sand, feel the sea breeze, and look up at an open sky.

  • Hayama Ocean Sports

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-874-4796

    Stand-up paddleboard club at Hayama beach, with a beach boathouse and old-house clubhouse one minute from the sea.

  • Hayama Port

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-875-1504

    Northernmost Kanagawa Prefecture facility on Hayama Coast, Japan's yachting birthplace and marine sports hub.

  • Hayama Sangaokayama Green Space

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-876-4601

    About 140m urban forest hiking course with flowers, birds, and sea and Mt. Fuji views.
