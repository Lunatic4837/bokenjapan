Sights in Hayama, Kanagawa

Hayama · Kanagawa · 6 places

See

  • Morito Daimyojin

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-875-2681

    Superb evening scenery chosen among Kanagawa's 50 views. Ancient purification site, Kamakura-period disaster-averting place, and fertility sacred site.

  • Hayama Shiosai Park

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-876-1140

    Park on the former Imperial Villa annex with a Japanese garden, Fuji and Izu views, and marine displays.

  • The Museum of Modern Art, Hayama

    Hayama, Kanagawa

    Holds several exhibitions a year, with a sculpture path, restaurant, shop, and Isshiki Coast views.

  • Hayama Park

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-876-4601

    Former Imperial Villa riding ground beside the villa, with views of Sagami Bay, Mount Fuji, and Enoshima.

  • Hoshun Yamaguchi Memorial Museum

    Hayama, Kanagawa · 046-875-6094

    Former home of Japanese painter Hoshun Yamaguchi, now a museum with works, Yoshida studio, and garden.

  • Moriyama Shrine

    Hayama, Kanagawa

    Founded in 749, enshrining Kushinadahime, reputedly transferred by High Priest Roben, born in Kamakura.
