Things to do in Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

Hiratsuka · Kanagawa · 14 places

Do

  • Hiratsuka City General Park

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-2233

    A large citizen park marking the city's 50th anniversary, with 100,000 trees, cherry blossoms, a petting zoo, and sports facilities.

  • Tokei Security Hiratsuka General Gymnasium

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-2255

    Used for B.League professional basketball, national martial arts tournaments, and individual use.

  • Shonan Sky Sports School

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    A facility offering paragliding experiences.

  • Shonan Coast Park (SunSun Marche Venue)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-20-5110

    Opened in 1964 on leased municipal and national land, with a sports ground, pool, and beach center.

  • ABEMA Shonan Bank (Hiratsuka Keirin Track)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-21-3935

    A vast venue for keirin, gourmet food, and events, with night shows of lights, fountains, and music.

  • F.Dream Hiratsuka

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-24-3786

    Casual circuit experience in Shonan Hiratsuka: easy rental karts with 100 km/h speed and thrills.

  • Arai Darumaya Daruma Painting Experience

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-21-6096

    Hiratsuka specialty Soshu daruma, handmade by traditional methods. Painting experience available; inquiry required.

  • Darumaya Kyozome Main Store (Kimono Wash-and-Stretch Experience)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    See kimono structure explained and the wash-and-stretch method, and try part of that cleaning.

  • Fureai Petting Zoo

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-2266

    Birds, squirrel monkeys, and chipmunks; free to hold and feed guinea pigs and rabbits. Bad weather may cancel.

  • Lemon Gas Stadium Hiratsuka

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-33-4455

    Shonan Bellmare's home and a Japan athletics federation Class 2 stadium for J.League and national-to-school meets.

  • Kaname Shinsui Park

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-21-9852

    Nature park with spring-fed streams and ponds for watching Genji fireflies, insects, and plants, best in May to early June.

  • Kiiroi Ouchi Farm

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-79-5156

    No-fertilizer, pesticide-free farm offering seasonal food-making programs for all ages through picture books.

  • Hiratsuka Sea Terrace Seaside Park

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Seaside park on Route 134 with a market building, BBQ restaurant, lawn, sports field, and coastal path.

  • Parent-Child Real Fisherman Experience

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-21-0146

    Half-day real fishing from Hiratsuka Tamasaburo Port in Sagami Bay, with staff support and an optional fish-cleaning class.
