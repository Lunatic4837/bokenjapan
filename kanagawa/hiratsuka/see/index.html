See in Hiratsuka

Hiratsuka · Kanagawa · 36 places

See

  • Kana Garden

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-73-6170

    Photogenic rose garden and panorama beds, with vegetable-harvest and rice planting and harvesting experiences.

  • Hiratsuka City Art Museum

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-2111

    Holds special exhibitions of Shonan-related works under the theme Art and Light of Shonan.

  • Yoshizawa Eight Views

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-20-5110

    Beautiful satoyama scenery in Yoshizawa, Shonan Hiratsuka, selected to preserve for posterity.

  • Tobiyatsu Hill (Majestic Mount Fuji)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-20-5110

    Top Fuji viewpoint among Yoshizawa Eight Views; sometimes diamond Fuji; spring embankment cherries.

  • Shonan-daira (Komayama Park)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-21-9852

    Green park spanning Hiratsuka and Oiso; about 180m observatories offer a 360-degree Shonan panorama and famous night views.

  • Hinomiya Shrine and Standing Stone

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-20-5110

    Roughly 4-meter-square natural rock called Hinomiya-san, with a Yamato Takeru legend and counted among Yoshizawa Eight Views.

  • Komyo-ji Kannon Hall (Kaname Kannon)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-58-0127

    Hiratsuka Eight Views and 7th Bando 33 site; Kaname Kannon's miniature shrine is a national Important Cultural Property.

  • Shogan-ji Temple

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    After the stone steps, a view of the Miura Peninsula. Founded in 1502, long a Soto Zen training temple.

  • Tsukagoshi Park

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Rare in the prefecture: a front-square rear-square burial mound with a cherry tree on top and good views.

  • Yatsurugi Shrine and Kanman Fudo

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Guardian of Yoshizawa; large zelkova at upper shrine, nationally designated wooden Fudo statue at lower shrine.

  • Myoen-ji (Tsuchiya Coin-Washing Benzaiten)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-58-1436

    Tendai temple in Tsuchiya, Hiratsuka, enshrining Benzaiten, with a cave and coin-washing pond.

  • Tentoku-ji

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Linked to Sanada Yoichi Yoshitada of the Ishibashiyama battle; later an Ueda clan castle in Sagami.

  • Tsuchiya Basket Chrysanthemum Garden

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-20-5110

    Best viewing is from late October to mid-November.

  • Hiratsuka Offshore Experiment Tower (University of Tokyo Ocean Alliance)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-73-7690

    Yellow observation and marine experiment tower 1 km offshore in 20 m of water, run by the University of Tokyo.

  • Ishikkusu Banyu Flower Field

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Three hectares of seasonal flowers on the Sagami River riverbed: tulips, poppies, cornflowers, lavender, zinnias, and cosmos.

  • Hiratsuka Hachimangu Shrine

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-23-3315

    One of Sagami's six shrines, reputedly founded by Emperor Nintoku in 68, hosting several festivals.

  • Hachimanyama Park

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-21-9852

    Central-city oasis with cherry, roses, hydrangea, and bush clover, monuments, and a Western-style building.

  • Hachimanyama Western-style House (Former Yokohama Rubber Hiratsuka Factory Memorial Hall)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-7114

    Registered as a national tangible cultural property as a historic building.

  • Kirifuri Falls (Hiratsuka Eight Views)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-8107

    Hiking course around marble-like falls and a stone reportedly visited by Yamato Takeru. Also called Maou Falls.

  • Shichikoku Pass and Tohara

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    City's westernmost 182 m pass, said named for seven-province views. Mountain ridges and seasonal flowers welcome hikers.

  • Hiratsuka Coast

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    About 4 km coast. Fuji's silhouette appears behind Shonandaira TV tower, a Hiratsuka symbol. Popular year-round for views.

  • Banyu Bridge Area (Shonan Itako)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-8107

    Hiratsuka waterside district called Shonan Itako, with Banyu Fureai Park, arena and soccer field upstream of Banyu Bridge.

  • Yoshizawa Eight Views (Village Pond)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Selected village landscape of the Yoshizawa Eight Views, with terraces and wooded traditional farmhouses against Mount Oyama.

  • Yoshizawa Eight Views (Yoshizawa Elementary School)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Yoshizawa Eight Views site. In spring the main gate road shows cherry trees and the school's red triangular roof.

  • Yoshizawa Eight Views (Yurugi Hill)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Selected Yoshizawa Eight Views site. A farm road from Kami-Yoshizawa Yatsurugi Shrine reveals fan-shaped terraces backed by Mount Fuji.

  • Yoshizawa Eight Views (Megumigaoka)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Selected as Yoshizawa Eight Views for local revitalization. Azaleas and dogwoods line Megumigaoka's outer road, best in late April.

  • Hiratsuka City Museum

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-33-5111

    Hiratsuka museum themed on Sagami River basin nature and culture, with a popular children's planetarium.

  • Okiku Mound

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Grave site of Okiku, heroine of the Edo ghost story Bancho Sarayashiki, in Beniya-cho Park.

  • Edo-guchi Mitsuke Site

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Restored Edo-side entrance of old Tokaido Hiratsuka-juku, based on a photo an early Meiji Italian minister took home.

  • Hiratsuka-juku Waki-Honjin Site Monument

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Hiratsuka-juku had one honjin and one waki-honjin, auxiliary lodging for officials and daimyo.

  • Official Notice Board Site

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Boards of basic shogunate or lord laws were posted here; Hiratsuka also showed fares to Fujisawa and Oiso.

  • Former Hiratsuka-juku Honjin Site

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Tokaido post-town inn for daimyo, nobles, and officials; 163 tsubo, 20 sitting rooms, gate and raised room.

  • Kyoto-side Mitsuke Site

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Said to be Hiroshige's Hiratsuka Nawate-michi view, with Komayama, the path, and Hanamizu River.

  • Hiratsuka Mound

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa

    Monument off the old Tokaido. Legend says Taira Masako's flat mound became the name Hiratsuka.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Environmental Science Center

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-24-3311

    Conducts environmental survey research, monitoring, and learning support to preserve and create safe living conditions.

  • Namiki Maki-e Workshop (PILOT)

    Hiratsuka, Kanagawa · 0463-35-7069

    Taisho brick former naval powder factory exhibits maki-e fountain pen history and lacquer works; advance reservation required.
