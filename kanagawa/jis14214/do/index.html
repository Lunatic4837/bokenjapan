Do in Isehara

Isehara · Kanagawa · 7 places

Do

  • Oyama Cable Car

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-95-2040

    Links Oyama Cable Station to Afuri Shrine Station in about six minutes; won the 2016 Good Design Award.

  • Kurhaus Yamagoya (Isehara)

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-92-7750

    BBQ and camping in Oyama Quasi-National Park; restaurant serves river fish and venison; faces the Mount Oyama trail.

  • Hinata Valley Trout Fishing Ground

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-94-7220

    Managed fishing and barbecue area in Tanzawa-Oyama Quasi-National Park, with stream fishing, a beginners' pond, and summer trout grabbing.

  • Mount Hinata

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    A 404-meter mountain on the Isehara and Atsugi border, where beginners and families can easily hike.

  • Oyama-dera Station

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    Oyama Cable Car intermediate station where passing cars look like a kiss, a popular sacred spot for couples to meet.

  • Togakubo

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-95-2038

    Shinto lodging for Oyama pilgrims with sutra copying, post-hike baths, and homemade Oyama tofu kaiseki.

  • Japan Clay Target Shooting Association Kanagawa Prefectural Isehara Shooting Range

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-92-7882

    Clay and rifle shooting ranges in lush greenery within the Tanzawa mountain area.
