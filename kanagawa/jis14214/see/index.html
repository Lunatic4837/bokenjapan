Sights in Isehara, Kanagawa

Isehara · Kanagawa · 22 places

See

  • Kamikasuya Shrine

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    Shrine in Isehara. Former name Sanno Shrine is recorded for Kamikasuya Village in an Edo-period gazetteer.

  • Oyama Afuri Shrine

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-95-2006

    Mountain-worship shrine said founded over 2,200 years ago, with a summit shrine, lower shrine, and seasonal views.

  • Tosho-in Temple

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-93-0308

    Muromachi prayer temple of the Kanto Kanrei Uesugi clan, also known as Guzo-ji.

  • Hinata Yakushi (Hinatayama Hojo-bo)

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-95-1416

    Founded by Gyoki in 716, one of Japan's three Yakushi, with many nationally designated Important Cultural Property statues.

  • Utake Bunko (Yamaguchi Family Residence)

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-95-0002

    1998 national cultural property; former magistrate office with late-Edo and civil-rights exhibits, and spring plum and rapeseed fields.

  • Hinata District Red Spider Lilies

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-73-7373

    Red spider lilies bloom from mid-September amid green mountains and golden rice in Hinata.

  • Miharashidai Observatory (Oyama Hiking)

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    A 770-meter observatory overlooking Isehara and Atsugi.

  • Niju Falls

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    One of Oyama's six falls, also called the rain-prayer falls.

  • Oyama Temple

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-95-2011

    One of Kanto's three great Fudo, founded in 755. Iron Fudo Myoo and two attendants are national Important Cultural Properties.

  • Iyori Pass

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    A pass on the old Sakamoto road connecting Hadano City and Oyama.

  • Fudo Myoo

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    Fudo Myoo at Iyori Pass in Tanzawa, said to have once served as a guidepost.

  • Mizunomi Jizo

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    Located before Iyori Pass along the Sengen-yama forest road.

  • Stone Monument at the Foot of the Bridge

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    From opposite the stone monument at this bridge's foot, one enters the Oyama Road.

  • Mujozan Johotsugan-ji

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-94-3954

    Head temple of the Tendai Tansei school, opened by Priest Tansei, founder of an ascetic wandering practice.

  • Fujimidai Viewpoint

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-73-7373

    On the main trail to Oyama summit, where Mount Fuji is visible on clear days.

  • Husband and Wife Cedars

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    A mystical 600-year-old auspicious tree on the way up to Fujimidai.

  • Isehara Daijingu Shrine

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-96-1611

    Shrine near Isehara Station, called Daijingu-san. Setsubun festival, spring cherries, and a summer flower market.

  • Sannomiya Hibita Shrine

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-95-3237

    Heian-recorded Engishiki shrine, Sagami's third, revered by Yoritomo and Tokugawa shoguns. Holds wooden guardian dogs said to be Kanto's oldest.

  • Isehara Shibuta Riverbank

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-94-4729

    About 600 meters of moss phlox bloom along the Shibuta riverbank, a Kanagawa flower spot.

  • Yamazakura at Oyama Afuri Shrine

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-94-4729

    Four mountain cherries over 400 years old dot the back hill. Seeing all four needs about a one-hour hike.

  • Okame Cherries along Oyama Shindo

    Isehara, Kanagawa · 0463-94-4729

    About 160 deep pink Okame cherries line 300 meters along Oyama Shindo, blooming from early to mid-March.

  • Mount Oyama

    Isehara, Kanagawa

    Western Kanagawa mountain popular for hiking, a six-minute cable car, and Michelin two-star views.
