Things to do in Samukawa, Kanagawa

Samukawa · Kanagawa · 8 places

Do

  • Kirin Beverage Shonan Factory (Afternoon Tea Tour)

    Samukawa, Kanagawa · 070-4235-870

    Tour experiencing Kirin Afternoon Tea through videos and activities for adults and children; no production-line visit.

  • Peace Trampoline Gym Samukawa

    Samukawa, Kanagawa · 0467-38-6992

    Freely open from children to adults, with beginner, competition, and gymnastics trampolines.

  • Kozen-ji (Zazen Experience and Viewing)

    Samukawa, Kanagawa

    Zazen experience offered, with English support. Founded in the late Sengoku period; Momoyama-period principal image.

  • Samukawa Central Park

    Samukawa, Kanagawa · 0467-74-1111

    Vast central park with playgrounds, a stream, pumptrack, Mount Fuji views, and a late March cherry blossom festival.

  • River Interaction Park (Lucky Mount Fuji Spot: Inverted Fuji)

    Samukawa, Kanagawa · 0467-74-1111

    Riverbed park with a lawn plaza, soccer and baseball fields, and a Sagami River Eight Views Fuji scene.

  • Anraku-ji (Sutra Copying Experience)

    Samukawa, Kanagawa

    Sutra copying on the second Sunday each month from 15:00, closed in May and August; chairs available.

  • Ichinomiya Park

    Samukawa, Kanagawa

    A park nicknamed Log Plaza, integrated with Ichinomiya Greenway and selected among Kanagawa's 50 parks.

  • Samukawa Station Front Park

    Samukawa, Kanagawa · 0467-75-9051

    Park hosting Samukawa Mikoshi Festival, where local mikoshi gather, and a Mountain Day summer festival, plus winter illuminations.
