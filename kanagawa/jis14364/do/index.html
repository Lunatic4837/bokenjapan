Things to do in Yamakita, Kanagawa

Yamakita · Kanagawa · 8 places

Do

  • Lake Tanzawa

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    Beautiful lake with Mount Fuji views, cherry blossoms, autumn leaves, boating, cycling, fishing, and camping.

  • Nakagawa Onsen Buna-no-Yu

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-75-3646

    Day-trip alkaline simple onsen near Lake Tanzawa, called Shingen's hidden bath and a beauty bath.

  • Mount Tonodake

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    Most popular peak in Omote Tanzawa; a 4-5 hour climb with summit views of Mount Fuji and Sagami Bay.

  • Nishi-Tanzawa Nakagawa Onsen Uozantei Yamabuki

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-78-3911

    Hinoki baths over 2 meters in diameter with soft pH 10.6 water. Nakagawa Onsen, known as Shingen's hidden spring.

  • Yamakita Town Health and Welfare Center Sakura no Yu

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-75-0819

    Multipurpose facility attached to the health and welfare center. Open to the public, with spring cherry views.

  • Ryugen Glass Workshop

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-20-3636

    Rebuilt from a house left outdoors over 20 years. Visitors blow colored glass cups and collect them the next day.

  • Hidamari no Sato

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-77-2777

    Forest campsite with power, a cooking area, four tennis courts, and early-to-late June firefly viewing.

  • Therapy Road (Forest Therapy)

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    Forest walks and nature strolls for mental and physical health, with stress checks by survey and blood pressure.
