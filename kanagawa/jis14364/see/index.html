Sights in Yamakita, Kanagawa

Yamakita · Kanagawa · 13 places

See

  • Shasui Falls

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-75-2717

    Three-tier waterfall selected among Japan's 100 waterfalls and 100 famous waters, dropping 69, 16 and 29 meters.

  • Yamakita Railway Park

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-75-2717

    Preserves working steam locomotive D52 No. 70, south of Yamakita Station.

  • Miho Dam

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    Multipurpose dam where three Tanzawa rivers meet, with seasonal scenery and a selected Kanto Mount Fuji view.

  • Around Lake Tanzawa

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    Lovely autumn leaves reflected on the lake around Chiyonosawa garden and Yaizu boat landing parking.

  • Kawamura Castle Ruins Historical Park

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    Kawamura clan castle developed into a historical park and the prefecture's first designated mountain castle historic site.

  • Tanzawa Lakeside Chiyo-no-Sawa Garden Observatory

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    Selected among Kanto's 100 Fuji Views, overlooking beautiful seasonal Mt. Fuji and Lake Tanzawa.

  • Lake Tanzawa Memorial Hall and Miho House

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-78-3415

    Hall built with the dam shows the lake's birth and Jomon pottery; restored late-Edo Miho House offers free visits.

  • Mount Ono

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-75-3646

    Summit 360-degree views of Hakone mountains, Mount Fuji, Ashigara Plain, and Tanzawa. Clear days show sunrise over Sagami Bay.

  • Yoritomo Sakura (Cherry Blossom Viewing)

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-75-3649

    A mountain cherry designated a town natural monument. Tradition holds that Minamoto no Yoritomo planted it.

  • Hinokiboramaru

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 042-769-8236

    A mountain famous for beautiful azaleas, in season from late May to early June.

  • Hoki Cedar

    Yamakita, Kanagawa

    National natural monument cedar near Hokizawa, Yamakita. About 12m chest, 18m roots, 45m tall; estimated age about 2,000 years.

  • Kumasasa Peak

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 042-769-8236

    On the border of Sagamihara and Yamakita, designated Tanzawa-Oyama Quasi-National Park.

  • Entsu-ji

    Yamakita, Kanagawa · 0465-77-2738

    A Zen temple in nature-rich Yamakita Town holding zazen, sutra copying, and tea on the first Saturday monthly.
