Things to do in Kamakura, Kanagawa

Kamakura · Kanagawa · 22 places

Do

  • Kamakura Japanese Sweets Making Experience Temari

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Japanese sweets embody awe and gratitude toward nature, and making them deepens understanding.

  • Fueta Park (Mount Fuji View)

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-32-0559

    Large park on Kamakurayama's mid-slope with paths, green views to Shichirigahama, and Mount Fuji on clear days.

  • Ebisu-ya Rickshaw Kamakura

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 090-5366-789

    Experience ancient capital Kamakura, birthplace of the samurai government, by rickshaw amid seasonal flowers.

  • Meigetsu Valley

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    An entrance to the Ten-en Hiking Course in a quiet residential area.

  • Kamakura Seaside Park Yuigahama Area

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-45-2750

    Park with three areas for sea views, watching windsurfers, and relaxing in the sun.

  • Kita-Kamakura Takara Garden

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-25-5742

    About a 10-minute walk from Kita-Kamakura Station. An 85-year-old house offers pottery, cooking, dyeing and yoga by reservation.

  • Hase-to Experience Workshop

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Instructors teach electric wheel use; beginners make an original in about an hour, dried, fired, and mailed.

  • Kita-Kamakura Hoan Tea House

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    1934 tea house in Jochi-ji valley; usually closed except monthly reserved matcha events.

  • Kamakura Owl Forest

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-8977

    Owl garden by Kamakura Station; interact with 20 owls of 14 kinds. No time limit; reservation not required.

  • Yuigahama Beach

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-61-3884

    Famous beach since the Meiji era, with a long sandy shore, sunrise views, and quiet early-morning coastal walks.

  • Hase Villa

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    About 30-year-old residence midway between the Great Buddha and Hase Kannon, with a large lawn garden.

  • Kamakura Arts Hall

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-48-5500

    Kamakura cultural facility with a resonant hall, bamboo-grove lobby views, and a buffet.

  • Kamakura Samurai Experience Fukasawa Fort

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Try traditional Japanese costumes and walk Kamakura historic sites in costume with a local samurai guide.

  • Shichirigahama Beach

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    A 2.9 km beach between Enoshima and Kamakura, popular for surfing and Mount Fuji views.

  • Zaimokuza Beach

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-61-3884

    Kamakura's easternmost beach, shallow well offshore, recommended for families with children.

  • Ishigama Garden Terrace

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-8851

    Jomyo-ji, fifth of Kamakura's Five Mountains; elegant Western house and Scottish-designed garden for lunch and tea.

  • Kamakura Kokon

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-81-4435

    A fully renovated 170-year-old folk house in Kamakura's Nikaido area, rented exclusively as a unique venue.

  • Inamuragasaki Onsen

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-7199

    Rare moor spring expected to help fatigue and cold sensitivity, overlooking Kamakura sea, Fuji, and Enoshima.

  • Arashi-yu Kamakura Bettei

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Arashi-yu's first Kanto shop on Komachi-dori offers flower foot baths and authentic foot massage.

  • Choya Ume Experience Shop Kamakura

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Blend five ume types, five sugars, and four liquors into original ume syrup or ume liquor year-round.

  • Raitei

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Japanese restaurant in a relocated Edo-period house with a vast garden in quiet Kamakurayama, Kamakura.

  • Yuigahama Beach

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-61-3884

    Central Kamakura beach, busiest for station access and varied beach houses, with barrier-free boardwalks and a wheelchair slope.
