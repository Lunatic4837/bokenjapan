See

  • Kamakura Central Park

  • Koshigoe Fishing Port

    Southwestern Kamakura fishing port landing Sagami Bay seafood, with public access and Enoshima and Mount Fuji views.

  • Myohon-ji Temple

    Said to be the oldest Nichiren temple, a ten-minute walk from Kamakura Station East Exit.

  • Hongaku-ji

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-0490

    Founded in 1436 and donated by Ashikaga Mochiuji to Nichijitsu; called Nichicho-sama and Higashi Minobu.

  • Ankokuron-ji Temple

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-4825

    Founded at Nichiren's hermitage; closed cave where Rissho Ankoku Ron was written; views of Kamakura and Fuji.

  • Anyo-in Temple

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-0806

    Temple linked to Hojo Masako, moved here after burning in 1333, now famous for azaleas.

  • Egara Tenjin Shrine

  • Engaku-ji

  • Sugimoto-ji

  • Ofuna Kannon-ji

  • Enno-ji

  • Amanawa Shinmei Shrine

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Shrine said founded in 710 by Someya Taro Tokitada. Minamoto no Yoritomo, Masako, and Sanetomo reportedly visited.

  • Kamakura Museum of Literature

    Kamakura, Kanagawa

    Closed for major repairs; FY2029 reopening planned. 1936 Maeda villa with literary materials and 250 roses in spring and autumn.

  • Kencho-ji

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-0981

    Rinzai Kencho-ji school's head temple, first of Kamakura's Five Mountains, founded by Hojo Tokiyori; birthplace of kenchin soup.

  • Jomyo-ji Kisen-an

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-8638

  • Zeniarai Benzaiten Ugafuku Shrine

    Kamakura's foremost money-luck shrine where washing coins is said to increase wealth. Its spring is one of five famous waters.

  • Dankazura

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-0315

    Raised approach about 500m from the second torii to Tsurugaoka Hachimangu, improved by Yoritomo in 1182, with cherries and azaleas.

  • Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine

    Kamakura, Kanagawa · 0467-22-0315

  • Hasedera Temple

  • Grave of Minamoto no Yoritomo

  • Kotoku-in Temple

  • Hokoku-ji

  • Kita-Kamakura Old Folk House Museum

  • Genjiyama Park
