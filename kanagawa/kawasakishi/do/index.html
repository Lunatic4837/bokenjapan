Things to do in Kawasaki, Kanagawa

Kawasaki · Kanagawa · 37 places

Do

  • Daishi Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-276-0050

    Park beside Kawasaki Daishi Heiken-ji with baseball, tennis, pool, and lawn. Shenyang's 1987 Chinese garden has seasonal plants.

  • Promenade along the Nikaryo Canal

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-200-2380

    Tama River flow and riverbed play, with upstream sweetfish and birds near the weir. Seseragi Hall shares river information.

  • Club Citta

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-246-8888

    Large live hall in La Cittadella, five-minute walk from Kawasaki Station. Opened in 1988; Japanese and foreign artists perform.

  • La Cittadella

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-223-2333

    Italian hill-town entertainment complex a five-minute walk from Kawasaki Station, with movies, dining, shopping, and events.

  • Kawasaki Factory Night View Houseboat Cruise

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 050-3498-0748

    Houseboat tour from Shiohama along canals to view factory nightscapes, with a navigator and shuttle bus.

  • Todoroki Ryokuchi

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Sports park with track, baseball, tennis, soccer, and Todoroki Arena; the stadium is Kawasaki Frontale's home.

  • Kawasaki Racecourse

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-233-6701

    Popular night racing from April through December; last race around 8:50 p.m.

  • Kashima Garden

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-777-0923

    Sells traditionally dug pansies and violas; visitors may dig flowers. Also sells vegetables and offers harvests.

  • Kawasaki Racecourse Barbecue Area

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-233-6708

    A new barbecue plaza inside Kawasaki Racecourse with a large screen for races and playground equipment.

  • Tamagawa Unane Park Golf

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-833-0115

    Park golf, a golf-like sport for all ages and genders, at a low-fee facility owned by Kawasaki City.

  • Kappa Park Saginuma (Saginuma Fureai Square)

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-200-3356

    Children's play area with lawn, grassy field, and dirt ground zones.

  • Sugo Green Space

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-877-1661

    East area has slope athletics and large play equipment for children; west suits forest bathing and walks.

  • Tokaraku Pottery Studio

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-722-3993

    Pottery classroom in Kawasaki offering hand-building experiences, six minutes on foot from Musashi-Kosugi Station.

  • Kureha Environment Wastec Kanagawa

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-280-3810

    Kawasaki facility offering tours of waste intake, crushing, incineration, and residue treatment.

  • Kawasaki Large-Scale Solar Power Plant

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Since 2011, about 100,000 panels produce up to 20,000 kW. Tours from Eco Mirai-kan include Haneda Airport views.

  • Kawasaki Citizens Soap Plant

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-276-0739

    Collects used cooking oil to make and sell recycled soap and biodiesel. Each manufacturing process can be toured.

  • Minaminogawa Fureai Forest

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Woodland kept in its original natural state, with walking paths and benches for experiencing rich nature.

  • Bornelund World of Play Grand Tree Musashi-Kosugi

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-982-3221

    Fourth-floor indoor parent-child play area at Grand Tree Musashi-Kosugi with baby garden, large wooden equipment, and slide ball pool.

  • FISH ON Ozenji

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-959-0037

    Lure, fly, and bait ponds stock stream fish, with barbecue, dog run, cafe, and night facilities.

  • Yumemigasaki Animal Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Free year-round Kawasaki municipal animal park on a hill, with about 50 species and a playground.

  • Candy Cutting Experience

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-277-7711

    Cut soft candy sticks at Matsuya Sohonten, the area's first candy shop on Kawasaki Daishi's shopping street.

  • Kawasaki City Art Center

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-955-0107

    Cultural promotion hub opened in 2007 in Shin-Yuri, housing a 195-seat theater and 113-seat screening hall.

  • Muza Kawasaki Symphony Hall

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-520-0100

    Opened in 2004 as a symbol of music-town Kawasaki, with vineyard seats praised for acoustics.

  • Teatro Giglio Showa

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-953-9875

    Comprehensive theater at Showa University of Music, with European tradition, advanced stage equipment, and natural acoustics.

  • Kaguchi-no-ike and Keshomen-dani Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-200-2327

    Cherry trees surround the pond; April blossom viewing is lively. The park can be enjoyed with children.

  • Tachibana Onsen Tachibana-yu

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-788-4399

    An onsen public bath with the latest facilities and a spacious, clean community space.

  • Sakura Tobo Pottery Class

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Pottery class run by ceramic artist Michiko Kamo. Accepts orders for original pottery, gifts, favors and celebrations.

  • Motoki Farm Strawberry Picking

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 080-1984-9293

    Strawberry picking January to mid-May, web reservations only. Nine varieties, direct sales and kids space, 7-minute walk from Kurokawa Station.

  • Ozenji Furusato Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-954-0505

    A park themed on water and greenery in the Tama Hills, with woodland paths and a Mt. Fuji viewing plaza.

  • Yomiuriland

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-966-1111

    Lush amusement park with thrill rides, bungee jumping, a Ferris wheel, character shows, and sea lion shows.

  • Kawasaki Velodrome

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-233-5501

    A 400m bank with a steep incline, plus a BMX and skateboard park opened in summer 2022.

  • BOULCOM Kawasaki

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Bouldering gym for children and beginners, five minutes on foot from Kawasaki Station; no reservation needed.

  • Miyamaedaira Gensen Yukemuri no Sho

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-860-2641

    Day-trip natural onsen with carbonated amber baths, one of Kanto's largest bedrock baths, dining, and healing.

  • Mizonokuchi Onsen Kirari

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-741-4126

    Weakly alkaline bicarbonate day-trip onsen with an open-air beauty bath and free-flowing source spring.

  • Sauna House

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Opened January 23, 2025. Six-story sauna house with five saunas per gender, cold and warm baths, restaurant, and relaxation.

  • Kurokawa Sunflower Field Opening and Picking Experience

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Free no-reservation sunflower field opening and picking in Kurokawa, Kawasaki; schedule may change with growth.

  • Yomiuriland Pool WAI

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-966-1111

    Yomiuriland opens Pool WAI, a leading Tokyo leisure pool, from June 27 to September 23, 2026.
