Sights in Kawasaki, Kanagawa

Kawasaki · Kanagawa · 69 places

See

  • Inage Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-222-4554

    Kawasaki guardian shrine of victory and harmony, with a sacred ginkgo said to be over 1,000 years old.

  • Kawasaki Daishi Heiken-ji

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-266-3420

    Famous temple drawing many nationwide New Year visitors, known for warding off misfortune, with daily goma prayers.

  • Kawasaki City Nihon Minka-en

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-922-2181

    Twenty-five relocated eastern Japan folk houses, watermills, and a kabuki stage in Ikuta forest.

  • Kawasaki City Fujiko F. Fujio Museum

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 0570-055-245

    Cultural facility sharing manga artist Fujiko F. Fujio's works and messages through original art.

  • Kawasaki City Greening Center

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-911-2177

    Urban greening botanical garden offering seasonal plant displays, a greenhouse, and a lawn plaza for rest.

  • Nikaryo Canal Kuji Cylindrical Diversion

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-544-8229

    1941 irrigation divider, a 1998 registered tangible cultural property, siphons under Hirase River, metering by cylinder angle.

  • Taro Okamoto Museum of Art, Kawasaki

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-900-9898

    Mostly underground museum fused with nature, centered on the Tower of Mother and experiential displays.

  • Kawasaki Marien

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-287-6000

    51m port observation room, gym, tennis courts, and one of the world's largest great white shark specimens.

  • Kawasaki River Port Water Gate

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Completed in 1928 on the Tama River, with a fruit-basket tower ornament; registered cultural property since 1998.

  • Tokaido Kawasaki-juku Exchange Hall

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-280-7321

    Exhibits on Kawasaki history and its Edo-period Tokaido post town, with costume photos and classical performances.

  • Futago Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Near the Tama River's Futago Bridge; reportedly founded in 1641 as old Futago Village's shrine.

  • Nitta Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Shrine enshrining Nitta Yoshisada, a famous general of the Kamakura period.

  • Choja-ana Horizontal Cave Tomb

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Horizontal cave tombs cut into a hillside in the 7th-century Asuka period; interior entry is closed for safety.

  • Hisamoto Yakuimon Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Former Oka family site, eight generations of doctors, said to model the sanatorium in Kurosawa's Red Beard.

  • Suenaga Kumanomori Green Space (Edomi Cherry and Tarzan Tree)

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Mountain cherry found and put into verse by Minamoto no Yoshiie, later called Edomi Cherry, plus a Tarzan Tree.

  • Kuji Plum Grove Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    About 3,800 sqm with 40 plum trees of 20 species; red and white blooms mid-February to early March; poetry monument.

  • Egawa Seseragi Promenade

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-788-2311

    Eight themed zones with plants and stones; stream uses highly treated sewage water from Todoroki Water Treatment Center.

  • Chidori Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-287-6034

    Park built directly above Kawasaki Port undersea tunnel; observation plaza overlooks Higashi-Ogishima factory night views.

  • Kotohira Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-988-0045

    Old main hall burned in 2007; rebuilt in 2011 with restored paintings, after 1826 merger with Kotohira-gu.

  • Shirahata Hachiman Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-977-5639

    Said to be Genji-linked. Hatsu-u Festival has archery and a straw serpent; grand festival shows city-designated Negi dance.

  • Tsukiyomi Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 042-735-5617

    Founded in 1534 for harvest prayers; later became Aso Shrine, then Tsukiyomi Shrine again.

  • Joraku-ji (Manga Temple)

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Main hall built around 1668-1703; nicknamed Manga Temple after manga drawn on sliding doors in 1968.

  • Saimyo-ji

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Shingon temple revered by the shogun family; enshrines Daikokuten, one of Kawasaki's Seven Lucky Gods.

  • Aso Fudo-in (Tokusa Fudo)

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Officially Myoozan Fudo-in Hannyabo, famous for a daruma market praying for health and family safety.

  • Migawari Fudoson Kawasaki Head Temple

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-865-8111

    Hall built to enshrine a miraculous image after an epidemic ended. Visitors pray year-round for evil-warding and traffic safety.

  • Jokei-ji

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-988-1223

    A hilltop temple known as Kakio's hydrangea temple, with over 1,000 hydrangeas and unique arhat statues.

  • Hayano Sacred Park and Seven Ponds

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-987-6120

    Seven ponds said to store spring water for fields. Cemetery and park preserve scenery; paths for observing plants and animals.

  • Lord's Tomb

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Stone five-ring pagoda near Kaio-ji on Odoyama in Hayano, called the Lord's Tomb by locals.

  • Sato Honjin Site

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Called Sozaemon Honjin, where 14th shogun Iemochi stayed en route to Kyoto; poet Sato Sonosuke born here in 1890.

  • Kotoro Bridge Ruins

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Former drainage bridge; channel filled around 1931, only newel posts kept beside an intersection.

  • Daishigawara Tidal Flat Museum

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-287-7882

    Part of Daishigawara flood center; since 2008 shows Tama River estuary environment, history and culture, plus workshops and events.

  • Basho's Haiku Monument

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Rare precious monument where poet Matsuo Basho, heading to Iga, composed a farewell haiku to disciples at Hatchonawate.

  • Okamoto Kanoko Literary Monument: Pride

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-900-9898

    1962 Pride monument by son Okamoto Taro honoring poet-novelist Okamoto Kanoko.

  • Togaku-in (Azalea Temple)

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-866-4573

    Kawasaki temple known for about 2,000 azaleas, red and pink around Golden Week.

  • Ishi Kannon Hall

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Tendai Meicho-ji's detached hall, opened in 1665 by monk Benyu; principal image is a stone Nyoirin Kannon.

  • Kunikida Doppo Monument

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    A monument inscribed with a famous poem by Japanese poet and novelist Kunikida Doppo.

  • Shinshu-ji Kawasaki Cemetery Bell Tower

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-965-0965

    Jodo Shinshu Honganji-ha temple bell tower with the Happiness Bell, painter Masuo Ikeda's posthumous work.

  • Oishi Bridge

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    In the Edo period a large stone bridge stood here, prospering as Mizonokuchi-Futago post town.

  • Kamei Rokuro Residence Ruins

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Site of Kamei Rokuro's residence; descendants reportedly lived here until the early Showa period.

  • Tachibana Ancient Hill Green Space

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Green space preserving Tachibana government-office ruins designated historic in March 2015; Nara-Heian early storehouses remain underground.

  • Tachibana Shrine and Fujimidai Burial Mound

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Circular mound 17.5 meters across and 3.7 meters high; legend says Oto Tachibana Hime's robe and crown were buried here.

  • Shiboguchi Shell Mound

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Prefecture-designated historic site; Tama Hills' oldest shell mound, about 7000 years old, type site for Shiboguchi pottery.

  • Mizoguchi Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    An ancient Kawasaki shrine enshrining Amaterasu, known for safe birth, child-rearing, matchmaking, and family safety.

  • Kakio Local History Museum

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Opened in 2010 inside Kakio Junior High to exhibit history of former Kakio and Okagami villages.

  • Sakuramoto Nine Lucky Gods

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Nine lucky-god statues installed on Sakuramoto shopping street in 1999, all facing upward, including a locally loved beckoning cat.

  • Estuary Tidal Flats and Embankment Cherry Blossoms

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Tidal flats and reeds at the Tama River mouth, with embankment cherry blossoms.

  • Midorigaoka Cemetery

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-813-1182

    Northwestern Kawasaki cemetery where several hundred cherry trees bloom in spring.

  • Kawasaki Sky and Greenery Science Museum (Planetarium)

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-922-4731

    Learn about Kawasaki nature and enjoy a planetarium; new projectors show realistic stars, and binoculars are available.

  • Kawasaki Port Undersea Tunnel

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-287-6034

    Japan's longest walkable undersea tunnel, 2180 m by 3 m; pedestrian section opened 2004, links Chidori Park and Higashi-Ogishima.

  • Mitutoyo Measurement Museum (Numata Memorial Hall and Measuring Instruments Hall)

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-813-8204

    Museum of precision measuring instruments, comprising Numata Memorial Hall and Measuring Instruments Hall; holdings certified as Modern Industrial Heritage.

  • Higashi-Ogishima East Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-287-6034

    Kawasaki park with an artificial beach, popular for viewing planes, ships, and factory night views.

  • Kawasaki City Environmental Research Institute

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-276-9001

    Introduces Kawasaki's pollution history and environmental technology through videos, tapestries, touch panels, and exhibits.

  • Kawasaki Eco-Living Future Museum

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-223-8869

    A facility to enjoyably learn environmental protection, centered on global warming, renewable energy, and resource circulation.

  • Tonmori Yato

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Satoyama in Ikuta Ryokuchi Hatsuyama with firefly river, farm paddies and fields, and former firewood woodland.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Higashitakane Forest Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Kawasaki's only prefectural park, with a natural-monument shirakashi forest, Yayoi settlement ruins, flowers, and a wetland plant garden.

  • Kawasaki City Oyama Kaido Hometown Museum

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-813-4705

    Exhibits historical and folklore materials on Oyama Kaido and the Takatsu area, with special or permanent displays.

  • Keiko Oguro Children's Song Memorial Museum

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-381-3490

    Displays songs and records of Keiko Oguro, known for affectionate children's works including NHK Minna no Uta.

  • Okkoshiyama Fureai Forest

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-200-2327

    Kawasaki greenery conservation area since 1995, up a path at Jokei-ji. Kakio Tunnel site nearby now has only a sign.

  • Fujiya Site in Front of the Station

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-965-5113

    Former site of charcoal wholesaler Fujiya. Nearby farmers' Edo-period Kurokawa charcoal was sent overland to Edo.

  • Kanayama Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-222-3206

    Shrine in Kawasaki's Wakamiya Hachimangu. Metal phallus deity Kanamara-sama guards childbirth and lower-body illness. Spring festival has unusual portable shrines.

  • Oyama Kaido

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Road from Edo's Akasaka Gate to Oyama Afuri Shrine, famed for rain prayers, also called Atsugi Kaido.

  • Ozenji Eco-Lifestyle Environment Center

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-712-4637

    Learn enjoyably about resource circulation, global warming measures, and coexistence with nature. Events held regularly.

  • Ozenji View Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-965-5113

    Small 128 sq m park with excellent views, on high ground in rolling terrain from Ozenji to Yurigaoka.

  • Keihin Fushimi Inari Shrine

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-411-4110

    108 stone fox statues sit in the grounds, with vermilion torii modeled on Kyoto Fushimi Inari.

  • Kawasui Kawasaki Aquarium

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-201-7921

    Aquarium themed on the world's watersides, from the Tama River to the Amazon.

  • Sumiyoshi Cherry Blossoms

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-744-3161

    About 2 km of Somei-Yoshino cherries along Shibukawa near Motosumiyoshi Station, best late March to early April.

  • Ikuta Ryokuchi Park

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa · 044-933-2300

    Northwest Kawasaki green space with an 84-meter hill, observatory views, seasonal nature, and museums.

  • Ikuta Ryokuchi Rose Garden

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Tama Hills woodland rose garden, open spring and autumn, closing after 2025 autumn for planned redevelopment.

  • Myoraku-ji

    Kawasaki, Kanagawa

    Tendai temple on Nagao Hills overlooking the Tama River, known for about 1000 hydrangeas.
