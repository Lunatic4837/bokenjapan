Do in Kiyokawa

Kiyokawa · Kanagawa · 8 places

Do

  • Miyagase Lakeside Park

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa · 046-288-3600

    Lakeside park in Kiyokawa amid Tanzawa mountains, with lawns, a road train, and a 315-meter suspension bridge.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Miyagase Lake Canoe Course

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa · 046-288-3600

    Canoe racing venue for the 1998 National Sports Festival, now a residents' canoe sports base.

  • Mount Tonodake

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa

    Most popular peak in Omote Tanzawa; a 4-5 hour climb with summit views of Mount Fuji and Sagami Bay.

  • Kiyokawa Village Fureai Center Bessho no Yu

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa · 046-288-3900

    Not a hot spring: day baths use Kiyokawa's Tanzawa headwater, with outdoor, sauna, and cypress baths.

  • MAMURO Camp Base

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa · 080-4773-4321

    Spacious powered camp field near Create drugstore and Kiyokawa roadside station, with hot water and free showers.

  • Miya Maru Sightseeing Boat

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa · 046-288-3600

    Sightseeing boat connecting three Miyagase areas for travel, nature watching, and lake views.

  • Higashi-Tanzawa Campground Richland

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa · 046-288-1031

    Family-friendly campground in Kiyokawa Village beside the Nakatsu River, with stream play, cottages, and an open-air bath.

  • Shin-Dainichi

    Kiyokawa, Kanagawa

    Wide summit with benches. Reach Tonodake summit in under an hour. Named for a once-enshrined Dainichi Nyorai statue.
