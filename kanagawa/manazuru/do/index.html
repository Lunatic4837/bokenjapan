Things to do in Manazuru, Kanagawa

Manazuru · Kanagawa · 5 places

Do

  • Manazuru Nature Trail

    Manazuru, Kanagawa

    Three promenades of about 300, 850, and 450 meters, walkable year-round amid fresh air and sea breeze.

  • Kotogahama Beach

    Manazuru, Kanagawa

    Manazuru beach said to be Japan's diving birthplace, offering snorkeling, rock pooling, fishing, and summer bioluminescence.

  • Ohayashi Observation Park

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-5501

    Park in Manazuru surrounded by sea and forest, with park golf and barbecue, viewing Hatsushima and Oshima.

  • Iwa Beach

    Manazuru, Kanagawa

    Small sandy beach about 200 meters long, known as Yoritomo's departure shore, with free swimming.

  • Orange Floral Farm

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-69-2239

    Pesticide-free organic orchard with lemon and amanatsu picking plus BBQ from late November to early June.
