Sights in Manazuru, Kanagawa

Manazuru · Kanagawa · 11 places

See

  • Manazuru Cape

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-1131

    Sagami Bay cape known for sunrise, symbolized by Mitsuishi, with pines over 350 years and clam digging.

  • Manazuru Port

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-3827

    Port for fishing and stone-transport boats. Hosts the Kibune Festival sea procession. Nearby same-day fish shop sells out.

  • Cape Manazuru

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-1112

    Drive-in at Manazuru Peninsula's tip with lawn, panoramic sea views, cherry blossoms, restaurant, and souvenirs.

  • Manazuru Municipal Nakagawa Kazumasa Art Museum

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-1128

    Art museum of Nakagawa Kazumasa paintings and calligraphy; closed for renovation, reopening planned March 2026.

  • Mitsuishi

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0460-85-9560

    Symbol of Manazuru Peninsula, also called Kasashima, famous for sunrise and joined only at low tide.

  • Arai Castle Ruins Park

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-1131

    Quiet park in a bamboo grove; spring weeping-cherry banquet, water reflections, and nighttime illumination.

  • Kifune Shrine

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-0066

    Held two days every July, its famous bold splendid boat festival is a national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property.

  • Shiosai Promenade

    Manazuru, Kanagawa

    A roughly 700m promenade beside the sea at Manazuru Peninsula's tip, for walks viewing Sagami Bay and Mitsuishi.

  • Mount Tomyo

    Manazuru, Kanagawa

    A 96-meter mountain on the Manazuru Peninsula.

  • Manazuru Town Endo Shell Museum

    Manazuru, Kanagawa · 0465-68-2111

    Permanent shell exhibits from Haruo Endo's lifetime collection, gathered in Japan and worldwide, inside Manazuru Peninsula Nature Park.

  • Kotori Pond

    Manazuru, Kanagawa

    A pond that appears only at limited times, such as heavy rain, near the center of Ohayashi.
