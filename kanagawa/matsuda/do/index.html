Do in Matsuda

Matsuda · Kanagawa · 5 places

Do

  • Yori Clear Stream Trout Fishing Ground

    West Tanzawa fishing spot on the Nakatsu River for cherry salmon, rainbow trout, and char, with gear rental.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-89-2321 · Official / source link

  • Tashiro-en Mikan Picking

    A mikan farm with barbecue terrace overlooking Sakawa River, Ashigara Plain, Sagami Bay, and Mt. Fuji.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · Official / source link

  • Matsudayama Mandarin Picking Naito Garden & Agri Park Sagayama Garden

    South-slope farms offer mandarin picking with Ashigara Plain and Mt. Fuji views; you can harvest your own tree.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-82-1423 · Official / source link

  • Yadoriki Seven Stars Dog Run & Cafe

    Spacious dog run amid seasonal nature, with seven areas, covered rain space, water play, and a dog-menu cafe.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-89-3113 · Official / source link

  • Mount Nabewari

    1,272.5 m peak with beautiful beech trees; summit views reach Hadano, West Tanzawa, and Mount Fuji.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · Official / source link
