See in Matsuda

Matsuda · Kanagawa · 8 places

See

  • Kochia Village

    Matsuda spot with 8,000 nemophila in early May and kochia reddening from late September, plus clear-day Fuji views.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-83-1228 · Official / source link

  • Matsudayama Herb Garden

    South-slope herb garden overlooking Sagami Bay and Ashigara Plain, with restaurant, workshop, and shop.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-85-1177 · Official / source link

  • Nishihirahata Park

    On Matsuda Mountain's mid-slope, views of Mt. Fuji, Sagami Bay, and Odawara Castle; Kawazu cherries bloom around February.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-85-1177 · Official / source link

  • Yadoriki Wintersweet Garden (Yadoriki Wintersweet Festival)

    One of Japan's largest wintersweet gardens in Matsuda, with over 20,000 fragrant yellow blooms in early spring.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-83-1228 · Official / source link

  • Saimyo-ji Historic Site Park

    Quiet park near Matsuda Mountain's summit with spring flowers, cherries, and views of Fuji and Sagami Bay.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-83-1228 · Official / source link

  • Weeping Cherries along the Nakatsu River

    220 weeping cherries bloom along the Nakatsu River in Yoriki, Matsuda, from late March to early April.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-83-1228 · Official / source link

  • Ninomiya Sontoku Monument

    Monument to Edo-period rural reformer Ninomiya Sontoku, installed to revitalize Matsuda Town and tourism.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · Official / source link

  • Fukusho-in

    Soto Zen temple founded in the Sengoku period; its mid-Edo gate is called Yori's oldest wooden building.

    Matsuda, Kanagawa · 0465-89-2535 · Official / source link
