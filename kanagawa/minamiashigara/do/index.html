Things to do in Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

Minamiashigara · Kanagawa · 3 places

Do

  • Prefectural Ashigara Fureai Village

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa · 0465-72-2010

    Outdoor education facility for group or family camping and nature classes, including firewood BBQ. Year-round camping events. Reservation required.

  • Ashigara Forest Park Maruta no Mori

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa · 0465-74-4510

    Minamiashigara forest park at the foot of Myojingatake with trails, forest bathing, camping, and day-use barbecue.

  • Modern Toji Only You

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa · 0465-72-1126

    Day-trip forest onsen at 250 meters on Daiyuzan in Minamiashigara, with lodging, high-pH beauty water, and sauna.
