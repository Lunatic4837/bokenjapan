Sights in Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

Minamiashigara · Kanagawa · 10 places

See

  • Daiyuzan Saijoji Temple

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa · 0465-74-3121

    Soto Zen temple founded in 1394, one of three great Soto prayer temples.

  • Bunmei Levee

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

    Levee combining natural terrain and structures so the once-straight Sakawa River flows zigzag to prevent floods.

  • Kintaro's Birthplace Site and Play Stones (Jizodo)

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

    Mansion site said to be Kintaro's birthplace, and giant stones young Kintaro is said to have played on.

  • Minamiashigara City Folk Museum

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa · 0465-73-4570

    In Ashigara forest of tengu legends. Permanent Minamiashigara history and culture exhibits, plus collection shows and three yearly special exhibitions.

  • Ashigara Manyo Park

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa · 0465-73-8053

    Park on Ashigara Pass at the Kanagawa-Shizuoka border, overlooking Hakone, Ashigara Plain, and Sagami Bay, with Manyoshu plants.

  • Ashigara Pass

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

    Pass on the Kanagawa-Shizuoka border, long an important route linking east and west Japan, with checkpoint ruins and Ashigara Myojin.

  • Yuhi Falls

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

    23 m high and 5 m wide, said to be Kintaro's first bath, named for its beauty in the sunset.

  • Haruki Path and Happiness Road (Harumeki Cherry Blossoms)

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

    Harumeki cherries line both Kari River banks along a 1.3 km walk, with Mount Fuji behind; early bloom peaks mid-March.

  • Ichinoseki Harane (Harumeki Cherry Blossoms)

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

    116 Harumeki cherry trees on a hillside, best from mid-March, near JA Kanagawa Seisho Fukuzawa sorting facility.

  • Fukuzawa Park (Harumeki Cherry Blossoms)

    Minamiashigara, Kanagawa

    Harumeki cherries planted in Fukuzawa Park, a relaxation spot for residents, best from mid-March.
