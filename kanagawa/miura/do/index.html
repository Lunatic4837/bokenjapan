Do in Miura

Miura · Kanagawa · 19 places

Do

  • Jogashima Cycling (Miura Rental Cycle)

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-888-0588

    Miura bicycle rentals, including electric-assist family bikes, for leisurely tours of scenic spots like Fuji and Misaki.

  • Koajiro Forest

    Miura, Kanagawa · 045-210-4310

    Kanto's only continuous forest, wetland, tidal flat, and sea, with a 1.6-kilometer walking trail.

  • Nijiiro Sakana Underwater Sightseeing Boat

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-6721

    Colorful semi-submersible boat around Misaki Port with underwater windows and seabird and fish feeding.

  • Miura Bicycle Rental

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Three-speed electric-assist bicycle rental for Miura's varied terrain, returnable at several tourist hubs.

  • Araihama Beach

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-2738

    Sandy and rocky beach in Miura's Aburatsubo area, selected as one of Japan's 88 bathing beaches.

  • Marujumaru Fishing Boat

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-0100

    Fishing boat at Edo-era Koajiro Port for casual fishing and views of Sagami Bay, Aburatsubo, and Koajiro Bay.

  • Aburatsubo Keikyu Marina

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-882-2720

    Japan's oldest marina, in a prime location overlooking the Miura Peninsula and Mount Fuji.

  • Doami Coast

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Calm waves suit small children. Fishing and rocky-shore play are available on both sides.

  • Jogashima Sea Cage Fishing Pond J's Fishing

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-854-9891

    Beginners can easily fish without snags or net tangles. All gear is provided, and barbless hooks are used.

  • MIURA FUN BEACH

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-888-0588

    Wide sandy beach, easy by train or car, family-friendly, with lifeguards, an event stage, beach sports, and local food trucks.

  • Miura Kaigan Horse Trekking Farm

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-887-1088

    Horse riding a walk from Miura Kaigan Station; one staff per horse, plus summer sea bathing with horses.

  • Nagatsuro Rocky Shore

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Deformed rocks spread here, fun for rocky-shore play. Once home of Hojo Soun's retainer Nagatsuro Nizaemon.

  • KEIKYU OPEN TOP BUS MIURA

    Miura, Kanagawa · 045-225-9696

    Roofless double-decker bus for Miura Peninsula views, with a guide aboard.

  • Misaki Port Ferry Sanshiro

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-0533

    Ferry linking Misaki and Jogashima through Misaki fishing port; bicycles allowed.

  • Jogashima Hiking Course

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-888-0588

    Hiking course on Jogashima where about 300,000 double daffodils bloom mid-January to mid-February.

  • Autumn Mikan Picking in Miura

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Miura mikan farms hold all-you-can-eat picking from mid-October to late November, with about one kilogram souvenir.

  • Wada Beach

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-2738

    About 325 m of sand on Sagami Bay; shallow, calm water popular with families with young children.

  • Miura Kaigan Cherry Blossom Festival

    Miura, Kanagawa

    About 1,000 Kawazu cherries and rapeseed line 1 km from Miura-Kaigan Station to Komatsugaike Park.

  • Jogashima Diving Center

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-882-0700

    Marine activity facility at Miura Peninsula's southern tip, 90 minutes from central Tokyo, offering diving and snorkeling.
