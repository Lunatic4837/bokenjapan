Sights in Miura, Kanagawa

Miura · Kanagawa · 16 places

See

  • Ena Bay Tidal Flats

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Precious natural tidal flat at Ena Bay's inner end on the Miura Peninsula, with reeds, eelgrass, waterbirds, and diverse life.

  • Jogashima Lighthouse

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-6640

    White-tiled lighthouse on Jogashima at the Miura Peninsula tip, rebuilt in 1926 and an island symbol.

  • Shirahige Shrine

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-7676

    Enshrines Choan Jurojin, one of the Miura Seven Lucky Gods and guardian of maritime safety.

  • Miyagawa Park

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-888-0588

    Two wind turbines mark this park, whose observatory overlooks Miyagawa Bay, Izu Oshima, and sometimes Mount Fuji.

  • Kainan Shrine

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-3038

    Miura Peninsula tutelary shrine near Misaki Port, known for enshrining a food deity.

  • Tsurugisaki Lighthouse

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-861-8374

    Built in 1866 and rebuilt after the Great Kanto Earthquake; rocky reefs and tide pools; one of Kanagawa's scenic spots.

  • Honzui-ji

    Miura, Kanagawa

    On a hill overlooking Misaki, known as the site of Minamoto no Yoritomo's Sakura Palace.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Jogashima Park

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-6640

    Park on eastern Jogashima with narcissus blooms, views to Fuji and Izu Oshima, and Awasaki Lighthouse.

  • Chakkirako Misaki Showa Museum

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-881-6721

    Exhibits Showa-era life in Misaki, Michizo Komura's handwritten scores, and Chakkirako heritage materials.

  • Enoki Terrace

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Located in Koajiro Forest, called Kanto's only setting where forest, wetland, tidal flats and sea facing Sagami Bay continue together.

  • Abosaki Lighthouse

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Vegetable-motif green-gradient design lighthouse near Jogashima Park; 16 m tall, light reaches 7 km.

  • Umanose Sea Cave

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-888-0588

    Rock carved by seawater over many years, with mysterious natural beauty and grand Pacific Ocean views.

  • Umiu Observatory

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-888-0588

    Overlooks cormorant breeding cliffs, preserved greenery and wave-cut reefs, with views of Boso, Izu, Fuji-Hakone, and Tanzawa.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Fisheries Technology Center

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-882-2312

    Studies fish, shellfish, marine environment, and processing technology. Free to view; no guidance or exhibit explanations.

  • Kaigai-cho Slump Structure

    Miura, Kanagawa · 046-888-0588

    Kanagawa Prefecture designated natural monument with a typical fold-type slump structure on a roadside cliff.

  • Nusuttogari

    Miura, Kanagawa

    Sheer cliffs where waves crash into white spray; legend says thieves froze there and were easily caught.
