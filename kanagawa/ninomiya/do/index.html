    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-68-2522

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-43-1611

    Restaurant and wedding complex overlooking Sagami Bay, rooted in a 1988 former villa, serving French cuisine with local ingredients.
