Things to do in Ninomiya, Kanagawa

Ninomiya · Kanagawa · 3 places

Do

  • Azumayama Park

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-72-3611

    Park overlooking Mount Fuji and Sagami Bay, with seasonal flowers, a 20-minute hike, and a 102-meter slide.

  • Shonan Beach Seine Fishing Ichigoromaru

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-68-2522

    Beach seine experience a seven-minute walk from JR Ninomiya Station; private charters limited to two groups daily.

  • Shihantei Pine Tree Resort

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-43-1611

    Restaurant and wedding complex overlooking Sagami Bay, rooted in a 1988 former villa, serving French cuisine with local ingredients.
