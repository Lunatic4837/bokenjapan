Sights in Ninomiya, Kanagawa

Ninomiya · Kanagawa · 8 places

See

  • Radian Flower Hill Park

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-71-3311

    About 20,000 spider lilies of various colors are planted in two main areas.

  • Ninomiya Town Futami Memorial Museum

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-72-6981

    Exhibits works and materials of painter Futami Toshitoki, born and raised in Ninomiya, west of Azumayama Park.

  • Kawawa Shrine

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-71-0709

    Shrine that gave Ninomiya its town name. Quiet forest setting with spring cherry blossoms and autumn leaves.

  • Ninomiya Seseragi Park

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-72-6112

    Famous for Japanese irises in early to mid-June; natural fireflies in late May, plus hydrangeas and lotus.

  • Azuma Shrine

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0438-22-7711

    In Ninomiya's Azumayama Park. Ototachibana-hime leapt into the sea; her washed-ashore comb was buried on the summit here.

  • Shinmei Shrine

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-72-6912

    Isshiki tutelary shrine in Miyane enshrining Ohirume no Mikoto; annual festival on July 15-16.

  • Ninomiya Fruit Tree Park

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-71-5956

    Remaining pear, grape, and persimmon trees flower in spring and fruit from early summer, with hillside trails.

  • Glass Rabbit Statue

    Ninomiya, Kanagawa · 0463-71-3315

    Built in 1981 at Ninomiya Station south exit to wish lasting peace after August 1945 strafing deaths.
