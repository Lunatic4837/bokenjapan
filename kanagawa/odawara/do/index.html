Things to do in Odawara, Kanagawa

Odawara · Kanagawa · 14 places

Do

  • Hakone Tozan Railway

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-32-6821

    Connects Odawara and Gora and climbs Japan's steepest wheel-powered 80-permil grade using three switchbacks.

  • Forest Adventure Odawara

    Odawara, Kanagawa

    French-origin outdoor park where visitors wear harnesses and ride ziplines, including one over 100 meters.

  • Odawara Ohoribata Manyo no Yu

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-23-1126

    Day-trip onsen in front of Odawara Station, with places for bathing, dining, and resting.

  • Oranger Garden

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-29-0703

    Orchard overlooking Sagami Bay grows low-pesticide mikan; picking from late October to December is all-you-can-eat.

  • Okawa Woodworking Workshop (Odawara Lacquerware)

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-22-4630

    Lathe workshop selling lacquerware, with polishing experiences you can take home the same day.

  • Odawara Castle Adventure (in Wanpaku Land)

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-24-3189

    Grounds about three Tokyo Domes have roller slides and play equipment. Highlight: rare athletic course themed on a local landmark.

  • Odawara Castle Information Center (Armor Dressing Experience)

    Odawara, Kanagawa

    Become a samurai, princess, or ninja and photograph against Odawara Castle. Armor, outer kimono, and ninja costumes for rent.

  • Sakawa River Cycling Road

    Odawara, Kanagawa

    Flat easy riverside course popular with beginners; about 17 km round trip from Odawara Arena in two hours.

  • Odawara Castle Ninja Hall

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-22-3818

    Hands-on facility themed on Fuma ninja who secretly supported the Sengoku Hojo, with missions to save Toyotomi-besieged Odawara Castle.

  • Sororito Odawara

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 090-4055-1543

    Workshop cafe near Odawara Castle for underglaze plate painting and scent sachets from nine natural fragrances.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Seisho Sports Center

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-48-2650

    Sports center renamed in April 2020 that provides a gymnasium, sports plaza, and tennis courts.

  • Hilton Odawara Resort & Spa

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-29-1000

    Resort by Hakone mountains and Sagami Bay, all rooms ocean view, onsen, spa, and ten pools, one hour from downtown.

  • Enoura Beach

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-29-0027

    A calm family beach inside a fishing port that small children can also enjoy.

  • Miyuki-no-hama Beach

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-33-1521

    A scenic beach overlooking the Izu, Miura, and Boso peninsulas, with beach houses.
