Sights in Odawara, Kanagawa

See

  • Odawara Castle Ruins Park

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-23-1373

    Castle ruins park with keep exhibits, observation deck, samurai and ninja halls, seasonal flowers, and an amusement park.

  • Hotoku Ninomiya Shrine

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-22-2250

    Shrine enshrining Ninomiya Sontoku, born in Odawara, who worked to revive farming villages in many regions.

  • Ishigakiyama Castle

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-23-1373

    Hideyoshi built a fully stone-walled castle in 1590 as his Odawara siege headquarters. National park area and national historic site.

  • Odawara Castle

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-22-3818

    National historic site; keep presents castle history and top deck overlooks Sagami Bay.

  • Soga Shrine

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-42-1282

    Said to be founded in 1028 by Soga Harima no Kami Yasuyoshi, enshrining Soga ancestors; Soga's main shrine since Meiji.

  • Chokozan Shotai-ji

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-22-7760

    About 360-year-old weeping cherry reportedly planted by Inaba Masanori, an Odawara City natural monument.

  • Suzuhiro Kamaboko Museum

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0120-07-4547

    Hands-on exhibits on fish cake history and nutrition, craftsman viewing, and making classes.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Museum of Natural History

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-21-1515

    Museum of 4.6 billion years of natural science, with dinosaurs, a 2.5-ton meteorite, ammonites, and butterfly specimens.

  • Odawara Literature Museum

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-22-9881

    Exhibits materials on Odawara-born and associated writers in Tanaka Mitsuaki's Spanish-style villa.

  • Former Matsumoto Gokichi Villa and Uko-tei Teahouse

  • Matsunaga Memorial Hall

  • Soga Plum Grove

    Odawara, Kanagawa · 0465-20-4192

    Plum grove in Odawara with about 35,000 trees, the Odawara Plum Festival, and views of Mount Fuji.

  • Odawara Blueway Bridge

    Odawara, Kanagawa

    Bridge directly above Odawara Port, built using extradosed technology to secure the shipping lane below.

  • Koki-an

  • Sakawa River Open Levee
