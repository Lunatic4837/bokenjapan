Things to do in Oimachi, Kanagawa

Oimachi · Kanagawa · 4 places

Do

  • me-byo valley BIOTOPIA

    Oimachi, Kanagawa · 0465-85-1113

    A facility where all ages enjoy me-byo improvement through food, exercise, and healing, plus forest therapy and a market.

  • Oi Town Shiki no Sato Agricultural Experience Facility

    Oimachi, Kanagawa · 0465-82-3751

    Experience rice planting, harvesting, and pizza making amid rich nature; shop sells fresh local vegetables and specialties.

  • Konkonto Field (Blueberry Garden Asahi)

    Oimachi, Kanagawa

    Pesticide-free, fertilizer-free blueberry picking farm with all-you-can-eat fruit and takeaway. Cafe offers healthy dishes and handmade ginger ale.

  • Oimatsuda Riding Club

    Oimachi, Kanagawa · 0465-82-7093

    Riding club founded in 1972, offering trial rides, recreational riding, and lessons from beginner to experienced.
