Do in Oiso

Oiso · Kanagawa · 7 places

Do

  • THERMAL SPA S.WAVE

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-8381

    Day-trip onsen inside Oiso Prince Hotel with an infinity pool, saunas, jacuzzis and winter-to-spring Mt. Fuji views.

  • Oiso Long Beach

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-7726

    Pool resort at Oiso Prince Hotel with nine pools including 500m flowing pool, wave pool, diving pool and water slides.

  • Oiso Beach Seine Experience

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    Experience traditional beach seine fishing from April to October; BBQ sets can be rented to cook your catch on site.

  • Hijikata Citrus Garden

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-72-0238

    All-you-can-pick-and-eat mikan grown in mild Oiso, open only weekends and holidays from late October to late November.

  • Koyurugi Beach

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    Ancient poetic shore where Meiji-era political and literary figures later built seaside villas.

  • Oiso Beach

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-4100

    Japan's first sea-bathing beach, opened in the Meiji era, with Sagami Bay and Mount Fuji views.

  • Oiso Port Design Lighthouse and Green Pigeon Watching

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    Watch green pigeons on Terugasaki Coast rocks and the design lighthouse at Oiso Port.
