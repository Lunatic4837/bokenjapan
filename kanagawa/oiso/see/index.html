Sights in Oiso, Kanagawa

Oiso · Kanagawa · 20 places

See

  • Terugasaki Coast

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-4100

    Kanagawa natural monument where green pigeons arrive at dawn from May to November to drink seawater.

  • Oiso Shiroyama Park

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-0355

    Prefectural park on former Mitsui villa and Shigeru Yoshida residence sites, with tea rooms, a museum, and bay views.

  • Former Residence of Shigeru Yoshida

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-4700

    Stately Oiso sukiya-style mansion with vast gardens, a rose garden, and Mt. Fuji views on clear days.

  • National Route 134

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    National highway from Yokosuka to Oiso with views of the Shonan coast and Mount Fuji.

  • Oiso Guest House (Former Kinoshita Villa)

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 050-3385-0013

    1912 nationally registered wooden Western-style villa on an Oiso hill, overlooking Sagami Bay, with Italian dining.

  • Meiji Memorial Oiso Villa Garden

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-0101

    Open former Okuma and Furukawa villas and Mutsu villa site in historic Oiso, with gardens and Taisho glass.

  • Shigitatsu-an

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-6926

    Haiku hermitage over 360 years old, with a thatched hut and wabi-sabi garden.

  • Jifuku-ji

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-4100

    Shingon To-ji school temple in Oiso. Shimazaki Toson couple's grave among about 20 old plum trees blooming from early February.

  • Shigitatsu-an

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-6926

    Thatched-gate garden where Saigyo is said to have composed verse, and a cultural source of the word Shonan.

  • Old Tokaido Pine Avenue (Oiso)

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-3300

    Edo-period pine rows on the Tokaido that rested travelers and still retain that era's atmosphere.

  • Yokoku-ji Cave Tombs

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    Prefectural historic site: Oiso's most famous cave tombs, over 20, facing the Shonan-daira nature trail.

  • Former Shimazaki Toson Residence

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-3300

    Five-minute walk from Oiso Station. Toson's single-story former home has cedar-bark walls, Taisho glass and an August 22 memorial.

  • Kesho Well

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    Said to be the makeup well of Tora-gozen, heroine of the Soga Monogatari, who lived in Oiso.

  • Former Mitsui Villa District

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-0355

    Site of a Meiji Mitsui zaibatsu head's villa, once with gardens and a national treasure tea room, now a park.

  • Sawada Miki Memorial Hall (Hidden Christian Museum)

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-4888

    Renewed March 2014. Memorial museum for Sawada Miki, Elizabeth Saunders Home founder, showing Hidden Christian materials.

  • Rokusho Shrine

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-71-3737

    Ancient Oiso shrine jointly enshrining six major Sagami shrines; May 5 Konomachi festival.

  • Takaku Shrine

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-1146

    Stone monument by Tokugawa Yorimichi; main-hall plaque by Kabayama Sukenori. Edo daimyo reportedly dismounted to visit.

  • Kewaizaka Pine Avenue

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    Said to be Hiroshige's Oiso view, depicting rain of tragic love and a pine avenue.

  • Kojima Honjin Ruins and Onoe Honjin Ruins

    Oiso, Kanagawa

    Oiso post town, busy in the Edo period, had 66 inns and three honjin along about 1.3 km.

  • Oiso Town Local History Museum

    Oiso, Kanagawa · 0463-61-4700

    Permanent archaeology, history, folklore, and nature exhibits on Oiso and nearby areas, with three to five special shows yearly.
