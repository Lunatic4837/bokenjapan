Things to do in Sagamihara, Kanagawa

Sagamihara · Kanagawa · 28 places

Do

  • Sagamihara Asamizo Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Park of greenery and colorful flowers, with clematis, hydrangeas, squirrel monkeys, and a petting corner.

  • Swan-shaped Sightseeing Boat Swan Maru

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-684-2131

    Lake internationally known as the 1964 Tokyo Olympics canoe venue; enjoy boating, fishing, camping, hiking.

  • Sagami Lake MORI MORI (formerly Sagami Lake Resort Pleasure Forest)

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 0570-037-353

    A nature resort with outdoor activities and an amusement park, including camping, barbecue, athletics, and winter illuminations.

  • Lake Sagami Sightseeing Boat or Boating

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Sister ship of Japan's first swan-shaped pleasure boat; 16-ton, 100-person cruise, plus pedal, row, and motor boats on Lake Sagami.

  • Fujino Art House Experience Workshop

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-689-3030

    Art experience facility in rich nature for pottery, woodwork, and glass, with lodging rooms, a studio, and a hall.

  • Sagami-ko Onsen Ururi

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-685-2641

    Day-trip onsen in a forest outdoor resort by Sagami Lake, with carbonated and alkaline springs.

  • Dobogawa Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-776-6484

    Scenic park conserving Dobogawa headwaters and Yokoyama hills; rest, bird and plant observation, outdoor education.

  • Fujino Horticultural Land

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    A hands-on tourist farm run by 15 local farm households. Enjoy sweet potato digging, chestnut picking, and shiitake foraging.

  • Sagami River Nature Village Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-769-8243

    Corner park of Sagami River Nature Village, lively for blossom viewing and camping; stream and pond for children's water play.

  • Wada no Sato Experience Center Mura no Ie

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-687-5151

    Experience center below Mount Jinba with bamboo-craft and udon-making events using local resources.

  • Green Holiday Village Aone Campground

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-787-1380

    Northwestern Kanagawa campground on the Doshi River for camping, hiking, barbecue, river play, and fishing.

  • Yada Ridge Junction

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Just before the Kumasasa Peak summit, after crossing Higashizawa-no-atama.

  • Fujino Yamanami Onsen

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-686-8073

    Day-trip hilltop onsen with an unscreened open-air bath, seasonal views, stars and cherry blossoms.

  • PICA Sagamiko (Former Paddington Bear Campground)

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Campground at Sagamiko Resort Pleasure Forest with setup tent sites, trailer houses, log cabins, and auto camp sites.

  • Fujino Satoyama Experience Tour

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-684-9503

    Satoyama experience tour in Fujino homes for bamboo tableware, handmade udon, and indigo dyeing; apply after checking the official site.

  • Muscle Monster 2 (Sagamiko Morimori)

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 0570-037-353

    Asia and Japan's first giant climbing attraction with 93 activities, views, and thrills.

  • Sagamiko Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-684-3276

    Overlooks man-made Sagami Lake, with lawns, about 2000 cherry trees, and seasonal flowers.

  • Fujino Kabuki Performance

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Annual Fujino village kabuki revived about 25 years ago, mostly by children taught by senior actors.

  • Fujino Club

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-689-6105

    Agricultural experience site with farm restaurant, BBQ, glamping, harvest experiences, miso-making, and Jomon and Edo cooking.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Sagami Lake Exchange Center

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-682-6121

    A 10-minute walk from Sagami-ko Station. Cultural facility for exchange activities by Lake Sagami, with hall, gallery, and cafe.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Lake Sagami Rowing Course

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-684-2339

    Lake Sagami rowing venue since 1963; 1964 Olympics canoe site, with a 2000m course from 2019.

  • Jindani Onsen (Sagamiko Area)

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 0426-87-2363

    Hidden onsen with a famous cypress bath and seasonal views from Mt. Jinba's foot. Day visits depend on lodging.

  • Sagamiko MORI MORI Splash Carnival 2026

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 0570-037-353

    One of Kanto's largest outdoor water-play areas, Splash Carnival, opens June 27, 2026 at Sagamiko MORI MORI.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Sagamihara Park Seseragi-no-Sono Wading Zone

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-778-1653

    Wading zone for water play, very popular with children. Other ponds are off-limits.

  • Kami-Oshima Campground

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-760-6066

    On the Sagami River bed, also famous for fishing and cherry blossoms; 180 tent sites.

  • Mochi Benten Campground

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-760-6077

    30 tent sites. Open March 1 to October 31, plus November weekends and holidays.

  • Kubota Sake Brewery

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Tour a sake brewery continued since the Edo period, then taste sake in an 1864 house.

  • Mount Jinba

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    855-meter mountain on the Sagamihara-Hachioji border, with broad summit views and a white horse statue.
