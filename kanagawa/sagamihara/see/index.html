Sights in Sagamihara, Kanagawa

Sagamihara · Kanagawa · 48 places

See

  • Shiroyama Dam

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 045-210-7239

    Dam in Sagamihara completed in 1965 for drinking and industrial water, power generation, and flood control.

  • Sagami Dam

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 045-210-7239

    Dam in Sagamihara City, completed in 1947 to secure water for supply, power generation, and irrigation.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Sagamihara Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-778-1653

    Large lawn park beside Asamizo Park, with an iris garden, French-style formal garden, and tropical greenhouse.

  • Miyagase Dam

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 046-281-6911

    One of the capital region's largest concrete gravity dams, in eastern Tanzawa, northwestern Kanagawa, with six-minute tourist releases.

  • Taimasan Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-769-8243

    About 1,000 hydrangeas planted, a noted spot for Sagamihara City's flower. Best viewing in late June.

  • Hirugatake Summit

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Kanagawa's highest peak: spring azaleas, golden larch foliage, summer constellations, and a 360-degree winter night panorama.

  • Ogura Bridge and Sagami River Stream Walk

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Arched road bridge over the Sagami River, Shiroyama's symbol and civil engineering heritage, with summer lantern floating and illumination.

  • Sagami Giant Kite Center (Renge no Sato Araiso)

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 046-255-1311

    Japan's largest handmade kite, about 14.5 meters square and 950 kg, from a Sagamihara tradition since around 1830.

  • Sagami River Fureai Science Museum Aquarium Sagamihara

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-762-2110

    Aquarium displaying Sagami River creatures from upstream to the mouth, with feeding, keeper guides, and craft workshops.

  • Ozaki Gakudo Memorial Hall

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-784-0660

    Built in 1957 at Yukio Ozaki's birthplace, with photos, portraits, relics, and a Gakudo cherry tree.

  • JAXA Sagamihara Campus (Space Science Exploration Exchange Building)

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-759-8008

    JAXA space science visitor facility with an M-V rocket, satellite test models, Hayabusa2 exhibits, and guided tours.

  • Obara-juku Honjin

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-684-4780

    Only surviving official inn of Kanagawa's former 26, a grand pure-Japanese building designated a prefectural important cultural property.

  • Komorebi Forest

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Enjoy seasonal nature on maintained paths and lawns; selected among Kanagawa's 50 beautiful forests.

  • Kanuma Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-755-9781

    A former reed marsh called Kanuma. Legend: giant Deirabotchi's footprint became the marsh. Carp, ducks, local rest spot.

  • Seiryu no Sato

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-760-2711

    Enjoy the Sagami River's rich nature and seasonal changes.

  • Sagamihara City Old Folk House Garden

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-760-1130

    Estimated mid-Edo Former Aoyagi-ji quarters, relocated and restored here, is a Kanagawa Prefecture Important Cultural Property.

  • Joshibi Art Museum (Joshibi University of Art and Design)

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-778-6801

    Joshibi University Sagamihara campus museum with about six exhibitions, lectures, gallery talks, and workshops yearly.

  • Kawajiri Stone Age Site

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Middle to late Jomon settlement site with varied artifacts and pebble-built remains, including stone-paved dwellings.

  • Tsukui Castle Ruins

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-780-2420

    Mountain castle built by reshaping Shiroyama, a major Sengoku-era Odawara Hojo border castle, now a prefectural park with trails.

  • Katsusaka Ruins

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-769-8371

    Restored Jomon pit dwellings, post-abandonment hollows, a stone-paved house replica, and on-site artifact displays.

  • Tana Mukaihara Paleolithic Learning Center

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-777-6371

    Rare Paleolithic learning center; the national historic site has Japan's only traces of a building from about 20,000 years ago.

  • Lake Tsukui Shiroyama Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-780-2420

    Vast 95-hectare park by Shiroyama Dam with flower gardens, cherry blossoms, seasonal blooms, and Tsukui Castle ruins.

  • Sandan Falls Observation Plaza

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Observatory overlooks the Sagami River; a downstream riverbed plaza hosts sports and events.

  • Sagamihara City Hall Sakura Street

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-769-8236

    About 300 Someiyoshino cherries line 1.6 km, blooming from late March to early April, with night illumination.

  • Shiroyama Katakuri Village

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-782-4246

    Private woodland open to the public only March-April, with about 300,000 purple katakuri and a short hike.

  • Sagamihara City Museum

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-750-8030

    Museum of Sagamihara history and nature since 1995, with Hayabusa capsule exhibits and a planetarium.

  • Hakkei no Tana and River Terraces

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-704-9046

    Selected as a Sagami River Eight View, with a panorama of the river and Tanzawa mountains.

  • Mount Sekiro

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-684-2633

    Collective name for mountains southeast of Lake Sagami, rich in huge strange rocks of Tertiary conglomerate.

  • Inukoeji

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Selected among Kanagawa's 50 scenic spots, named for a legend of Takeda Shingen leading dogs over a pass.

  • Hinokiboramaru

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-769-8236

    A mountain famous for beautiful azaleas, in season from late May to early June.

  • Niji no Ohashi Bridge

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    330 m reverse Lohse bridge linking Toriihara and lakeside areas at Miyagase Dam; a Kanagawa 100 Bridges selection.

  • Fujino Art Village Gallery

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Nature-rich forest-and-lake countryside with nine galleries of local artists for viewing, interaction, making, and demonstrations.

  • Sagamihara North Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-779-5885

    Park using on-site woodland, designed to feel like a botanical garden, famous for hydrangeas and autumn ginkgo.

  • Green Tower Sagamihara

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-777-3451

    55-meter observation tower in Sagamihara Asamizo Park overlooking Tanzawa, Tama Hills, and Yokohama.

  • Yoshino-juku Fujiya

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-750-8030

    Former Koshu Kaido post-town inn rebuilt around 1897; city cultural property with sericulture and charcoal displays.

  • Taima Ferry Site

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Old hub with ferry at Showa Bridge to Echi. Oyama pilgrimages increased use, and Taima thrived as a post town.

  • Muryoko-ji

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-778-0284

    Ji-sect temple of founder Ippen. Scenic grounds chosen in Kanagawa's 50; a pond said to reflect him carving his statue.

  • Showa Bridge

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Sagami River bridge built in 1931 after the nearby Taima ferry was damaged by floods.

  • Takada Bridge

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-707-7050

    Sagami River bridge built in 1924, said to take its name from Takamine and Tana villages; a Kanagawa 100 Bridge.

  • Mitsui Bridge

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-780-1415

    A 212-meter bridge near the center of Lake Tsukui, a vermilion symbol of the lake, in Kanagawa's 100 Bridges.

  • Ogino Bread

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-780-8121

    Makes nostalgic fried bread and mild Tanzawa bean-paste buns. Fresh bread and factory tours are available at the main factory.

  • Lake Tsukui

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Artificial lake created in 1965 by damming the Sagami River; cherry blossoms, foliage, hiking, and historic sites.

  • Lake Miyagase

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Large dam lake created in 2000, with seasonal scenery, leisure facilities, and restaurants.

  • Toriihara Garden

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 046-288-3600

    Lakeside scenic spot in a prefectural park with seasonal views and autumn foliage.

  • Hyogippara

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa

    Maple forest on a hiking course around Shiroyama Lake, popular with hikers for late-November red and yellow leaves.

  • Kotohira Shrine

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-783-8065

    Founded in 1804 by locals enshrining Shikoku Kotohira's spirit; long worshipped for warding off evil and good fortune.

  • Ryukagoyama Observatory

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-783-8065

    Hidden panorama east of Shiroyama Lake; clear days show Landmark Tower, Tokyo Tower, and Skytree.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Sagamihara Park

    Sagamihara, Kanagawa · 042-778-1653

    Park with early-blooming Harumeki cherries, a vast lawn, iris garden, formal garden, and greenhouse.
