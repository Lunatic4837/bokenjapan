Things to do in Yamato, Kanagawa

Yamato · Kanagawa · 6 places

Do

  • Izumi Forest

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-264-6633

    Yamato's largest green space around the Hikiji River source, with forest, waterside, cherry blossoms, birds, and a free campsite.

  • Fureai Forest

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-263-8711

    Yamato park of about 9 hectares themed on water, greenery, and flowers, with a restored Hikichi River.

  • F. Marinos Seijo Ishii Park (Yamato Yutori no Mori)

    Yamato, Kanagawa

    Sports and recreation park opened in 2007 south of Atsugi Airfield, with Mount Fuji views.

  • Sirius Indoor Children's Plaza

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-259-7592

    Bornelund-produced indoor children's plaza for ages 0-2 and 3-8, plus temporary childcare up to four hours daily.

  • Yamato Art and Culture Hall

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-259-7591

    Main hall in 2016-opened Sirius hosts ballet, musicals, classical music, and jazz.

  • Gaiso Water Park Hikichidai

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-260-5757

    Heated pool with a 25-meter pool, flowing pool, water slides, toddler pool, and jacuzzi.
