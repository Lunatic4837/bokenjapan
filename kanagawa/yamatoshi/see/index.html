See in Yamato

Yamato · Kanagawa · 5 places

See

  • Yamato City Folk House Garden (Izumi no Mori)

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-260-5790

    Two relocated old farmhouses, one the oldest surviving in the city, surrounded by seasonal flowers and trees.

  • Seiryuzan Josen-ji

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-267-8789

    Known as the flower and kappa temple; year-round flowers, famous mitsumata and white spider lilies, over 300 kappa.

  • Yamato City Shimotsuruma Hometown Museum

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-272-6556

    Restored Ogura main house (1856) and storehouse are city-designated important cultural properties; rare post-town merchant house.

  • Hikiji River Thousand Cherry Blossoms

    Yamato, Kanagawa

    About 500 cherry trees bloom along 1.3 km of both Hikiji River banks, forming a tunnel at full bloom.

  • Yamato City Library at Sirius

    Yamato, Kanagawa · 046-263-0211

    Central library of Sirius, opened in 2016, with daily events, concerts, films, manga, and a children's library.
