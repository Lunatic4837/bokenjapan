Do in Yokosuka

Yokosuka · Kanagawa · 25 places

Do

  • YOKOSUKA Naval Port Cruise

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-825-7144

    A roughly 45-minute local cruise to see US Navy and Maritime Self-Defense Force ships up close.

  • Uraga Ferry

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-825-7144

    Ferry linking east and west Uraga Port, nicknamed ponpon boat. Three-minute rides view former shipyard cranes and docks.

  • Tokyo Bay Ferry

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-830-5622

    Links Kurihama, Kanagawa and Kanaya, Chiba in about 40 minutes, with views of ships and Mount Fuji.

  • Kurihama Flower Park

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-833-8282

    Closed for now after typhoon damage. Free park with poppies, cosmos, herb garden, and adventure land.

  • Kurihama Flower Land Pool

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-835-7754

    Indoor heated pool enjoyable by adults and children, also with a training room.

  • Mount Takatori

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-822-8333

    Yokosuka mountain nicknamed Shonan Myogi, known for sheer vertical rocks, where you can hike.

  • Tokyo Bay Ferry Terminal

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-830-5622

    About a 40-minute ferry from Kurihama to Kanaya across Tokyo Bay, with southern Boso views.

  • Kayama Farm Strawberry Picking Experience

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 090-3237-1583

    Soil-grown strawberries with organic fertilizer; pick Benihoppe and Tochiotome from early January to early June; daifuku and jam sold.

  • Seaside Bath and Fisherman's Cuisine Yokosuka

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-838-4809

    850-seat ocean-view facility in Kurihama Port for fresh seafood, banquets, bathing, rest, and shopping.

  • Tatara Beach

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-822-8294

    One of the few white-sand beaches on the Miura Peninsula. Original Godzilla appeared here in 1954; footprints remain.

  • Maeda River Promenade

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-822-8510

    A clean 1.4 km stream walk with trees, birds, and aquatic life; part of a beginner hike. Partly closed.

  • Kanagawa Prefectural Kannonzaki Park

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-843-8316

    Vast park covering most of Kannonzaki on Tokyo Bay, with hydrangeas, forest walks, barbecue and Japan's first Western-style lighthouse.

  • Heiwa Central Park

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-823-3222

    Yokosuka rest park for praying for peace, with Tokyo Bay views and a monument beaming light 16 times a year.

  • Sano Natural Onsen Noborigumo

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-851-2617

    Sodium chloride natural onsen from 800 meters underground, called a beauty spring, with bedrock baths and a rock salt sauna.

  • Beach Park Living

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 080-7019-022

    Barbecue facility in Kannonzaki Park Tatarahama overlooking Tokyo Bay, serving domestic meat; 2022 Good Design Award winner.

  • Tsukuihama Tourist Farm

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-857-9656

    Strawberry, mandarin, and sweet potato picking; seasonal vegetables sold at the information office.

  • Yokosuka Onsen Yura-no-Sato

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-845-1726

    Salty seawater-like spring that moisturizes and stays warm, called a heat bath. Includes open-air bath and runner's spa.

  • Yokosuka Natural Onsen Umibe-no-Yu Kurihama

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-838-4126

    Sister shop of a Futtsu facility beside Kurihama ferry port. Open-air bath views Tokyo Bay and the Boso Peninsula.

  • Touch Uocchi Aquarium, Soleil Hill

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-857-2500

    Experience aquarium opening April 25, 2025 at Soleil Hill, gathering 12 aquatic species to touch or feed.

  • Tondemi Yokosuka

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-854-4531

    Indoor athletic facility with trampolines, climbing, and digital content; night illumination from 19:00.

  • Mynavi Japan Cup Yokosuka

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa

    Ended for 2025. Free freestyle BMX performances with music and an MC.

  • Nagahama Beach

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-822-8301

    Relaxed beach locals call Nahama, suited to families and snorkeling, popular for year-round marine sports.

  • Yokosuka City Umikaze Park

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-826-2899

    Park facing Sarushima with barbecues, sports facilities, playground equipment, and Third Sea Fort remains.

  • #Jihangun Yokosuka

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-822-8533

    Photo spot at Nobi Beach with vending-machine objects and a six-meter swing against sky and sea.

  • Yokosuka YY Vehicle Festival 2026

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa

    Ended event enjoying Yokosuka through JR East and Keikyu trains, JMSDF Yokosuka ships, and cars.
