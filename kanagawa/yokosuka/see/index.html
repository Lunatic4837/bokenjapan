  • Kannonzaki Lighthouse

  • Kinugasayama Park

  • Hashirimizu Shrine

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-844-4122

  • Kano Shrine

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-841-5300

  • Tsukayama Park

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-823-2439

  • Verny Park

    Yokosuka, Kanagawa · 046-845-6660

  • Sarushima Island

  • Memorial Ship Mikasa

  • Awashima Shrine

  • Kaigan Garden
