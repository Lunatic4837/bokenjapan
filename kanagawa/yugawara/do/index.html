Do in Yugawara

Yugawara · Kanagawa · 8 places

Do

  • Chibori Yugawara Sweets Factory

    Chibori direct store and cookie factory opened in 2017, with cookie buffet, oven viewing, and sweets making.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-63-0404 · Official / source link

  • Yugawara Mikan Picking

    Seasonal spot to enjoy sweet Yugawara mikan overlooking Sagami Bay, ripe from early October to late December.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-63-1920 · Official / source link

  • Yoshihama Beach

    Shallow calm beach with clear water and black sand containing iron sand; AA water quality and JLA-certified.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-63-2111 · Official / source link

  • Yutoro Sagasawa-no-Yu

    Two daily gender-switched baths from a private source. Smooth water warms from the core and can be drunk.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-62-2688 · Official / source link

  • Yugawara Onsen Kogome no Yu

    Day-trip onsen with Yugawara source water. Large and open-air baths surrounded by nature; water changed daily.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-62-2744 · Official / source link

  • Manyo Park Yugawara Soyu Books and Retreat

    Manyo Park has a hot-spring poem monument, Doppo monument and Kumano Shrine, plus onsen, sauna, cafe and library.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-64-1234 · Official / source link

  • Yugawara Mirakuan

    Famous for mandarin-orange wafer cakes, Yugawara's Mirakuan offers hands-on Japanese sweets making.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · Official / source link

  • Y.S.C. Hakone Paraglider School

    In 1972 it made Japan's first homemade hang-glider flight and has trained many flyers as Japan's oldest school.

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-62-5776 · Official / source link
