Sights in Yugawara, Kanagawa

Yugawara · Kanagawa · 17 places

See

  • Fudo Falls

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-64-1234

    15m falls with rich water; substitute Fudo enshrined left, Shusse Daikoku right, teahouse nearby.

  • Tsubaki Line (Tsubakidai)

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-63-2111

    One of Kanagawa's 50 scenic spots, a hill on the camellia-lined Tsubaki Line, best February to April.

  • Mount Taikan

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    On the Yugawara-Hakone border, with views of Mount Fuji and Lake Ashi. Said to be named after Yokoyama Taikan.

  • Anest Iwata Sky Lounge

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-23-0381

    360-degree scenic spot at Anest Iwata Turnpike Hakone's end, viewing Mt. Fuji, Lake Ashi, Hatsushima, Oshima and peninsulas.

  • Yugawara Town Art Museum

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-63-7788

    Art museum of Yugawara-related painters, opened in 1998 in a renovated inn.

  • Yugawara Plum Grove (Makuyama Park)

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-63-2111

    About 4,000 red and white plums bloom on Makuyama slopes from early February to mid-March.

  • Gosho Shrine

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-62-5869

    Yugawara shrine said to enshrine Amaterasu as guardian, with a longevity deity and an 850-year-old sacred tree.

  • Ikemine Maple Village

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-63-2111

    A new Yugawara spot with about 540 Japanese maples, a vivid walking path at its November peak.

  • Neno Shrine

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    Main deity Onamuchi, a child-blessing god said to have had 181 children; called Kanto's oldest.

  • Hakuun Falls

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    Waterfall near Tenshozan Shrine with a drop of about 30 meters, beautiful like flowing silk threads.

  • Chitose River Cherry Blossom Avenue

    Yugawara, Kanagawa · 0465-62-5135

    Forty Someiyoshino and twenty Oshima cherries on the Chitose River's Atami side peak late March to early April.

  • Godan Falls

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    Large falls spanning about 100 meters in five tiers, with atmospheric views amid onsen steam.

  • Daruma Falls

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    Falls looked down on from Seiho Bridge, named for painter Takeuchi Seiho; central rock juts out like a daruma.

  • Yugawara Station Plaza

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    Station plaza designed by Kengo Kuma and Associates as a warm wooden community space.

  • Kyorai Falls

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    Waterfall beside Tenshozan Shrine, a yamabushi training falls for reflecting on life's comings and goings.

  • Hinoki Charity Concert Hall

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    World's only all-hinoki concert hall in Yugawara, built with traditional wood joinery and white plaster.

  • Morishita Park

    Yugawara, Kanagawa

    Park in a residential area away from Yugawara Onsen, offering a glimpse of local town life.
