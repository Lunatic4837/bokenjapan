Sights in Zama, Kanagawa

Zama · Kanagawa · 12 places

See

  • Nissan Heritage Collection

    Zama, Kanagawa · 046-298-4355

    Houses about 400 commemorative Nissan vehicles from 1930s cars to race cars; about 300 always displayed.

  • Prefectural Zama Yatoyama Park

    Zama, Kanagawa · 046-257-8388

    Satoyama inhabited since the Jomon period, with springs that feed ponds and rice fields.

  • Zama Sunflower Field

    Zama, Kanagawa · 046-205-6515

    About 550,000 sunflowers in one of greater Tokyo's largest fields, a popular photo spot.

  • Suzuka Myojin Shrine

    Zama, Kanagawa · 046-256-1122

    Said to date from Emperor Kinmei's reign (539-571), with Saito Shozo's haiku stone and a Zama-designated Jomon site.

  • Zama Shrine

    Zama, Kanagawa · 046-251-0245

    Known as Sagami's Izuna-sama; head shrine was in Nagano. Cherry park views Oyama, Tanzawa, and Hakone; pet prayers offered.

  • Banjinsui Park

    Zama, Kanagawa

    Spring said to gush where Nichiren struck the ground with his staff. Used as daily water until about 1956.

  • Ryugen-in Spring

    Zama, Kanagawa

    Only water source for the Suzuka Jomon ruins. Later used as wasabi fields and to power water wheels.

  • Suzuka Izumi Spring

    Zama, Kanagawa

    Spring supplied six households and fire water until 1956; wasabi reportedly grown on borrowed land for about 30 years.

  • Shingan-ji Spring

    Zama, Kanagawa

    Said to have supplied local water since prehistoric times; now fills a pond and once irrigated paddies.

  • Kameido Spring

    Zama, Kanagawa

    Long used as drinking water and still for washing vegetables; now about one-tenth its former area.

  • Zama City Hall Observation Corridor

    Zama, Kanagawa · 046-255-1111

    One of Zama Eight Views; Yokohama Landmark Tower visible on clear days. Filming site of the drama Zeicho.

  • Sagamigaoka Nakayoshi Path

    Zama, Kanagawa

    Walking path for commuting and strolls, lined with 65 cherry varieties in spring.
