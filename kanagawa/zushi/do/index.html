Things to do in Zushi, Kanagawa

Zushi · Kanagawa · 7 places

Do

  • Kotsubo Fishing Port and Fisherman Taxi

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-873-1111

    Western Zushi fishing port with winter seaweed harvests, a January 2 mandarin toss, and fisherman-boat cruises.

  • Zushi Beach

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-873-1111

    Blue Flag-certified shallow, calm family beach named the half-mile beach where the sun was born.

  • OCEANS.Club

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-872-8911

    A stand-up paddle surfing school in Shonan Zushi that also lends equipment for experiences.

  • Fly Field Zushi Beach

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 090-1617-583

    Experience aquaboard, flying by water pressure from a personal watercraft, where Mt. Fuji is visible behind Sagami Bay and Enoshima.

  • EVER RESORT

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-876-5710

    Seaside resort a ten-second walk from Zushi Beach with yoga, a cafe, and marine sports.

  • Shonan Small Atelier Nature Living

    Zushi, Kanagawa

    Reservation-only private salon in Zushi for aroma perfume, herbarium, and flower candle making.

  • CINEMA AMIGO

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-873-5643

    Renovated old house cinema cafe in an alley leading to Zushi Beach, a pioneer of cinema cafes in Japan.
