Sights in Zushi, Kanagawa

Zushi · Kanagawa · 9 places

See

  • Osaki Park

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-873-1111

    76.7 m Zushi cape summit park with clear-day Mount Fuji, Izu-Hakone, and Miura Peninsula views; designated temporary tsunami evacuation site.

  • Hiromeyama Park

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-871-4941

    Popular observatory with Mt. Fuji views on clear days, small animals, spring cherries, and a hiking course.

  • Jimmu-ji

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-871-4565

    Tendai temple in Zushi City surrounded by vast nature.

  • Mandara-do Yagura Group

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-872-8153

    Over 150 well-preserved cliff burial caves at Nagoe Kiridoshi, a rare layered sight in Kamakura.

  • Iwadono-ji

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-871-2268

    Ancient temple said founded by Tokudo and Gyoki, second Bando 33 Kannon site with hilltop views.

  • Koyo-ji (Namiko Fudo)

    Zushi, Kanagawa

    Temple formerly called Namikiri Fudo or Shirataki Fudo, later named Namiko Fudo after heroine Namieko.

  • Zushi Beach Season of the Sun Monument

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-873-1111

    Built for the 50th anniversary of Ishihara Shintaro's Akutagawa Prize-winning novel Season of the Sun.

  • Roka Memorial Park

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-873-1111

    Opened in 1984 for Zushi's 30th city anniversary on Sakurayama land linked to Meiji writer Tokutomi Roka.

  • Nagara Sakurayama Kofun Group

    Zushi, Kanagawa · 046-873-1111

    Two keyhole-shaped burial mounds built from the mid to late 4th century; the first is 91.3 meters long.
