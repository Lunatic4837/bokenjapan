Things to do in Amakusa, Kumamoto

Amakusa · Kumamoto · 39 places

Do

  • Ariake Onsen Sazanami no Yu

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-53-1126

    Onsen center by Roadside Station Ripple Land, with open-air baths viewing Shiroga Beach, the Ariake Sea, and Mount Fugen.

  • Shimoda Onsen

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3239

    Onsen on Amakusa Shimoshima with East China Sea views and undiluted 40-42 degree natural water.

  • Sumoto Onsen Center Kappa Romance Hall

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-54-5526

    Kappa-faced onsen with cypress scent. Radon-rich alkaline simple spring, family baths, sauna, outdoor bath, and Japanese garden.

  • Natural Onsen Aimuri

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-76-1526

    Spanish-style sodium bicarbonate onsen. Large, outdoor, and four family baths, plus indoor pool and restaurant. Fully barrier-free.

  • Dolphin Watching

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-33-1600

    See about 200 wild Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins off Amakusa year-round, with over 90 percent encounters.

  • Nagomiyado Shinwaso Kaishin

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-22-3653

    Small 10-room seaside inn at Amakusa Shimoshima entrance, with fresh fish dinners and a hiba-wood private bath.

  • Gunpokaku Gracia

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3316

    Taisho-romantic Japanese-modern Shimoda Onsen ryokan with five rooms, stained glass, and three free-flowing hot springs.

  • Ajinoyado Kaiotei

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3211

    Inn at Amakusa Shimoda Onsen with plentiful hot water; the owner, an active fishmonger, serves live fish.

  • Amakusa Shimoda Onsen Boyokaku

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3111

    Ocean-view hotel facing Amakusa's west coast, with free-flowing natural onsen and seafood dinners.

  • Relaxation Inn Marco

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3306

    Onsen inn a five-minute walk from the sea, with free-flowing baths and Amakusa seafood.

  • Igaya

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3011

    Meiji-founded ryokan in Shimoda Onsen with smooth free-flowing baths and seasonal seafood.

  • Amakusa Shimoda Onsen Yunohana Travel Inn

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3180

    Inn overlooking the East China Sea, with free-flowing onsen and dolphin-watching package plans.

  • Amakusa Olive Farm AVILO

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-32-0366

    Experience olive farm on a hill in Itsuwa, growing ten varieties, with tours, hand-pressing, and tastings.

  • Ishiyama Rikyu Gosoku no Kutsu

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-45-3633

    All-detached onsen inn in Unzen-Amakusa National Park overlooking the East China Sea, with 15 villas and Amakusa kaiseki.

  • Ebisu Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-64-3111

    Quiet 267-meter white-sand beach facing the Shiranui Sea, watched over by four Ebisu statues.

  • Shirogahama Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-53-1111

    515-meter white-sand beach with gentle waves, popular with women and families, facing Unzen.

  • Demon Castle Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-32-1111

    Demon theme park in Itsuwa, Amakusa, with a 13-meter observatory tower overlooking Hayasaki Strait.

  • Takahama Ware Juho Kiln

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-1114

    Historic kiln started in 1762. Takahama ware is white, thin, and translucent. Offers visits, painting, and a museum.

  • Ushibuka Marine Park Glass Boat Submarine

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-1173

    Semi-submersible boat from Ushibuka fishing port, five trips daily, about 80 minutes. View the seabed; feed seagulls in winter.

  • Ubushima Campground and Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 080-8369-7356

    Uninhabited-island white-sand beach and campground, five minutes by boat from Kamihira Port. Bungalows but no electricity.

  • Kurosaki Beach Kurosaki Campground

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-32-1111

  • Hondo Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    Quiet, shallow beach looking toward Unzen. Popular with families, with showers, changing rooms, and a rest area.

  • Kotakahama Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-76-1111

    Small, gentle beach inside Yokaku Bay, recommended for children. Toilets, showers, changing rooms, and a first-aid station without lifeguards.

  • Satsuki Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2114

    Crescent beach with one kilometer of sand and a wheelchair ramp. Family-friendly, with a beach festival in season.

  • Shiratsuruhama Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-0407

    A 1.3 km white beach like a crane's wings, one of Japan's 100 best. Clear water, surfing, and sunsets.

  • Mogushi Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2111

    Natural beach 15 minutes from central Ushibuka. Clear color-changing water; turtles nest May-June. 2003 NHK drama site.

  • Onikizaki Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2111

    East China Sea beach with superb sunsets and clear water. A sandy shore appears from the sea at low tide.

  • Tachiumi Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-46-2111

    Shallow, calm southeast Shimoshima beach facing Yatsushiro Sea. Fish visible. Popular with young children. Wooden toilets and showers.

  • Gosoku no Kutsu Literary Promenade

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-1111

    A 3.2 km trail on the 1907 route of five Myojo poets, about two hours, with viewpoints and poem boards.

  • Kuroshima Uninhabited Island

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-67-2111

    Uninhabited island 10 minutes by charter boat from Goshoura. White beach, camping, toilets; a spring well is not drinkable.

  • Ushibuka Haiya Festival

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2111

    April's third Saturday and Sunday festival, with about 5,000 Haiya dancers and a fisheries fair.

  • Wakamiya Park Beach

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-32-1111

    Calm family beach backed by Mount Unzen, with Kame Island walkable at low tide.

  • Tsujishima

    Amakusa, Kumamoto

    Small island home to wild Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins, with dolphin watching and salt workshops.

  • Perla no Yubune Aibo no Yu

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-24-2626

    Natural hot springs at Hotel Alegria Gardens Amakusa, with sea-view and forest baths plus five private baths.

  • Amakusa Cultural Exchange Center

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-27-5665

    A spot to experience making Amakusa crafts, housed in a Western-style nationally registered tangible cultural property.

  • HIGH BEACH

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 090-6941-9624

    Near Shiratsuruhama Beach, offers Amakusa herb distillation and aroma craft experiences. Opened in 2018 by aromatherapist Miho Kurosawa.

  • Kuratake Sunny Auto Campground

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-64-3221

    Winter-comfortable campground overlooking the Amakusa islands, with hands-free camping sets and gear rental.

  • Amakusa Ushibuka Onsen Yasuragi-so

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-72-6666

    Quiet Ushibuka onsen inn with 14 rooms, fresh local fish, Amakusa Daio chicken, and natural hot springs.

  • Amakusa West Coast Holiday Park Furaibo

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-3911

    Campsite, whole-house guesthouse, and cottages on an Amakusa west-coast inlet, seven minutes from Shimoda Onsen, with powered RV sites.
