See in Amakusa

Amakusa · Kumamoto · 36 places

See

  • Ushibuka Kaisaikan

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-3818

    Sea-culture complex under Ushibuka Haiya Bridge by the ferry terminal, with shops, exhibits, a 1,000-fish tank, and a restaurant.

  • Catholic Sakitsu Church

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-78-6000

    Symbol of Sakitsu village, a 2018 World Heritage Hidden Christian site; Gothic church rebuilt in 1934 with rare tatami floors.

  • Catholic Oe Church

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-22-2243

    Earliest Amakusa church after Christianity's ban ended; 1933 building by Father Garnier and local believers.

  • Ebisu Statue Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-64-3115

    Park with Japan's largest marble Kuratake Great Ebisu statue, weighing 320 tons.

  • Thirteen Buddhas Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-22-2243

    Park famed for sunset views from Myokenura to Shiratsuruhama Beach, with Yosano poetry monuments.

  • Myokenura (Elephant Rock)

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    Nationally designated scenic natural monument reef on Amakusa's west coast, linked by sea caves.

  • Tsuten Park Ai yori Aoku Monument

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2111

    Park just past Tsuten Bridge, with a monument to the 1972 NHK drama set in Ushibuka.

  • Tomiyama Narcissus Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2114

    Summit park over Ushibuka Port with about 450,000 narcissus, one of Amakusa's eight sunset views.

  • Tsuruhayama Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2111

    Park on Amakusa's southernmost cape, overlooking Nagashima in Kagoshima, with about 1,000 cherries.

  • Gion Bridge

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    One of Japan's largest stone girder bridges, a national Important Cultural Property built in 1832.

  • Ushibuka Haiya Bridge

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2114

    883-meter bridge over Ushibuka fishing port, designed by Renzo Piano and lit at night.

  • Ushibuka Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2111

    Circuit-style Japanese garden around a former reservoir, also one of Japan's 100 urban parks.

  • Martyrdom Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    Park on former Hondo Castle site overlooking Hondo, with a memorial mound and spring cherry blossoms.

  • Meitoku-ji

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    Amakusa's first Zen temple, built after the rebellion by magistrate Suzuki Shigenari to spread Buddhism.

  • Amakusa Collegio Museum

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-76-0388

    Museum showing Nanban materials, including a restored Gutenberg press from the Tensho embassy.

  • Amakusa Kirishitan Museum

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-22-3845

    Museum of Amakusa Christian history in four zones, showing about 200 items including Shiro's battle flag.

  • Itsuwa History and Folklore Museum

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-33-1645

    Displays stone tools and pottery from Okinohara Shell Mound, plus Edo fishing gear and Jomon salt-making tools.

  • Ogase and Kogase

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    Dozens of wave-carved reefs about 1 km off Shimoshima. Ogase holds tropical sea life; Kogase draws shore anglers.

  • Oidake Observatory

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    Observatory on Oidake, Amakusa's second-highest peak at 582 meters. Views of islands, Aso, and Fugendake. Drive-up access.

  • Tsujishima Wind Power Plant

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-3498

    Clean-power wind turbines on Tsujishima symbolize Itsuwa Town. Traditional seawater salt-making continues, and dolphins visit.

  • Nigaki Fossil Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-67-2325

    About 20 touchable shell fossils from 100-million-year-old Goshoura strata. Iron dinosaur sculpture; nearby ammonite layer.

  • Amakusa Rosary Museum

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 096-942-5259

    Museum of Amakusa hidden Christians' life and faith, with Maria Kannon, relics, a recreated hidden room, and films.

  • Ueda Family Residence and Ueda Museum

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-1114

    Historic Ueda headman-house museum of Amakusa documents and old Takahama ware; interior closed.

  • Seaside Maria Statue

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-76-1111

    Maria statue near Sakitsu where fishermen pray; its sunset is one of Amakusa's Eight Sunset Views.

  • Sakitsu Village

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-78-6000

    Hidden Christian heritage fishing village where Buddhism, Shinto and Christianity coexisted, with a Gothic church.

  • Nishinokubo Park

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-23-1111

    Rare iris garden on terraced fields, with 250,000 irises and 6,000 hydrangeas in June, plus about 480 cherry trees.

  • Kuratake Shrine

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-64-3111

    Shrine atop 682-meter Mt. Kuratake, Amakusa's highest peak, built to pray for safe fishing.

  • Nishihira Camellia Park (Laputa Tree)

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-42-1111

    About 20,000 wild camellias and a 20-meter tree rooted on a giant rock, called Amakusa's Laputa.

  • Tsukinoshima

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-73-2111

    Uninhabited island with a crescent white beach, popular for diving in Ushibuka Marine Park.

  • Kuroishi Coast

    Amakusa, Kumamoto

    Coast where orange sunsets sink into the sea, selected for Japan's Top 100 Sunsets.

  • Makishima Island

  • Kamikake Falls

  • Helmet Plum of Enkei-ji

    Plum tree over 500 years old, linked to a late-1500s battle legend and said still to bear no fruit.

  • Sakitsu Suwa Shrine

    Amakusa, Kumamoto

    A shrine traditionally founded in 1647 for bountiful catches and maritime safety, where hidden Christians secretly prayed.

  • Amakusa City Sakitsu Museum Minatoya

    Amakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-75-9911

  • Amakusa Municipal Goshoura Dinosaur Island Museum
