Sights in Arao, Kumamoto

Arao · Kumamoto · 8 places

See

  • Yotsuyama Shrine

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-62-1390

    Shrine with a late 6th-century burial mound, festivals on February 13 and September 13, and over 300 cherry trees.

  • Manda Coal Mine Museum

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-64-1300

    Museum on coal-industry history and town life, displaying miners' tools and mine photo panels.

  • Birthplace of the Miyazaki Brothers

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-63-2595

    Birthplace of the four Miyazaki brothers, with a traditional house, garden visited by Sun Yat-sen, and a museum.

  • Iwamoto Bridge

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-63-1421

    32-meter stone arch bridge, a prefectural important cultural property said to be by Hashimoto Kangoro.

  • Manda Pit

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-57-9155

    Miike Coal Mine pit that supported Japan's modernization, a World Heritage site since 2015 with preserved buildings.

  • Arao Tidal Flat

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-57-7444

    One of Japan's largest single tidal flats, a Ramsar wetland since 2012, with wildlife, migratory birds, and sunsets.

  • Omoyai Citizens Flower Garden

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-57-7059

    Citizen-maintained rose garden: about 4,000 square meters, about 100 varieties, 1,900 plants. Rose Festival every May.

  • Arao City Library

    Arao, Kumamoto · 0968-57-7747

    Circular shelves like tide pools and round lights like waterbirds evoke Arao tidal flats. Second floor of Arao City Mall.
