Do

  • Asagiri Yakushi Onsen Healthy Land

    Asagiri, Kumamoto · 0966-47-0755

    Named for nearby Tanimizu Yakushi; alkaline sulfur onsen with jet bath, electric bath, and sauna.

  • Shiragadake

    Asagiri, Kumamoto · 0966-45-7220

    1417 m mountain bordering Miyazaki. Gentle ridges suit beginners; beech forest is said to be Japan's southern limit.

  • Asagiri Meishukai

    Asagiri, Kumamoto · 0966-45-1055

    Experience planting, harvesting, and brewing your own Kuma shochu from pesticide-free aigamo rice.

  • Kumamoto Crown Golf Club

    Asagiri, Kumamoto · 0966-45-5611

    Relatively flat golf course in Asagiri with about 35 meters of elevation difference and 300 cherry trees.
