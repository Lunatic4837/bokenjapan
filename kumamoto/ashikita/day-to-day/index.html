Day to day

  • Roadside Station Tanoura Higo Urara

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-87-2230

    Roadside station offering local Amanatsu oranges, Dekopon, fresh vegetables and seafood, with a restaurant of Egami Culinary Academy dishes.

  • Da Rope-tei

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-86-1929

    Mountaintop yakiniku restaurant serving rare fillet blocks on a teppan; up to 400 visitors come daily in peak season.

  • Tabakuman Restaurant

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0968-87-2230

    Restaurant inside Tanoura roadside station famous for towering cutlassfish rice bowls in sweet-spicy sauce.

  • Ashikita Utase Direct-Sales Diner Ebi-an

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-83-8888

    Harbor diner serving fresh natural Shiranui Sea seafood; top dish is red-leg shrimp tempura rice bowl.

  • Bistro Pazapa

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-82-3399

    Bistro facing Ashikita Bridge and the Shiranui Sea, serving Italian with ashiaka shrimp and Ashikita beef.

  • Roadside Station Ashikita Dekopon

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-61-3020

    Roadside station selling local produce, Ashikita beef, and sashimi, noted for dekopon and Sara Tama-chan.

  • Kajihara Tea Farm

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-84-0608

    Pesticide-free farm growing rare pan-fired tea with a clean aftertaste. Its Benifuuki black tea won Best in Show in 2022.
