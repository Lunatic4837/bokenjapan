Things to do in Ashikita, Kumamoto

Ashikita · Kumamoto · 13 places

Do

  • Ono Onsen Center

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-61-7300

    Mountain onsen in Ashikita with cypress baths, rock outdoor bath, sauna, and family baths. Lunch buffet and farm shop.

  • Healthy Park Ashikita

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-86-0259

    Onsen with exercise facilities and 10 kinds of baths. Weak-alkaline simple spring. Gallery of Tomihiro Hoshino's poem-paintings.

  • Sightseeing Utase Boat

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-82-2066

    Traditional Ashikita sailboat fishing pulls bottom nets by wind, mainly catching prawns, crabs, and mantis shrimp. Currently stopped.

  • Kameiso

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-86-0063

    Praised for abundant free-flowing source water and plentiful seafood from the Shiranui Sea.

  • Tsurugahama Beach

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-82-2511

    Artificial white-sand beach with pines behind and the Yatsushiro Sea ahead. Calm waves; showers, changing rooms, and toilets.

  • Otachimisaki Campsite Marine House

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-87-0737

    Campsite in Otachimisaki Park with two campgrounds, fixed tents for six, Marine House cottages, and a beach.

  • Otachimisaki Park Beach

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-87-0737

    One of the prefecture's largest beaches, with white sand and clear water west of Otachimisaki Park. Swimming June to August.

  • Ashikita Marine Park Beach

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-82-2511

    Leading artificial white-sand beach with a resort feel, beach volleyball, events, showers, toilets, and changing rooms.

  • Otachimisaki Park

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-87-0737

    Leisure park overlooking sea and Amakusa islands, with beach, camping, onsen, and a roadside station.

  • HOTEL AZ Kumamoto Ashikita

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-68-3301

    Affordable business hotel with large-vehicle parking and free Wi-Fi in every room.

  • Ashikita Seaside Park

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-82-5588

    Park beside Tsurugahama Beach with family play equipment, roller luge, ZORB, skate park, and lawn.

  • OONO-CAMP

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 090-7148-6841

    Ashikita Ono campground with two sites, each for one group a day; one seasonal, one year-round.

  • Ashikita Seaside Park

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-82-5588

    Open seaside park beside Tsurugahama Beach with a skate park and Japan's largest roller-luge course, totaling 2,000 meters.
