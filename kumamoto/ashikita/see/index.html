Sights in Ashikita, Kumamoto

Ashikita · Kumamoto · 4 places

See

  • Nosaka no Ura

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-87-1171

    Scenic spot where Nara-period official Osada no Okimi composed waka; a poem monument stands. Popular for sunsets.

  • Seishoko Rock

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-87-1171

    Huge rock mountain on the Kuma River between Ashikita and Kuma, tied to legends of Kato Kiyomasa.

  • Yunoka Bridge

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-82-2511

    Bridge over the Yunoura River crossing Route 3, part of Kumamoto Artpolis, and lit up at night.

  • Sashiki Castle Ruins

    Ashikita, Kumamoto · 0966-87-1171

    Castle ruins restored as a historical park after 1993 excavation, overlooking Sashiki Port and mountains toward Hitoyoshi.
