Things to do in Aso, Kumamoto

Aso · Kumamoto · 32 places

Do

  • Uchinomaki Onsen

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-3330

    Century-old onsen visited by writers, with over 130 springs and about 17 inns offering free-flowing private water.

  • Azalea 21

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-5311

    All-weather dome heated pool, training center, and onsen from 950 meters underground. Pool use suspended.

  • Frontier Horse Group El Patio Ranch

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3861

    Lodging amid endless grassland at El Patio Ranch, with Native American and cowboy rooms, Aso views, stars, and horse-trekking stays.

  • Daiaso

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-0157

    Low-priced free-flowing day bath at Uchinomaki Onsen, long popular with locals.

  • Sozankyo, Aso Uchinomaki Onsen

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-0515

    Traditional Japanese inn linked to Yosano Tekkan and Akiko, with a thousand-year-old cedar room and free-flowing hot spring.

  • Yumeoiso, Aso Uchinomaki Onsen

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-0622

    Uchinomaki Onsen inn with a lavish seasonal buffet, private family baths, and nightly bingo and festival games.

  • Yunoyado Irifune

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-0781

    Inn with three free-flowing onsen, five private baths, and creative cuisine using homemade rice and vegetables.

  • Chambre d'Hote Alm Forest

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-4500

  • Aso no Tabiyado Takanosho

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-5880

  • Ogasawara Inn

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-2000

  • Aso no Shiki Wildflower Inn

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3618

    Inn at the foot of Mt. Aso amid wildflowers, with four free private baths using mineral spring water.

  • Aso Cuddly Dominion

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-2020

    Theme park to meet world bears and animals, offering cub cuddling, a petting zone and animal trainer shows.

  • Nakae Iwato Kagura

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3174

    33-part kagura about 240 years old, performed on the first Sunday monthly from April to November except October.

  • Aso Ichinomiya Fruit Village

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-2167

    About 100 pesticide-free kiwi trees since 1982; kiwi and apple picking from late October to early November.

  • Kodai no Sato Camp Village

  • Aso Nature Land

  • Aso Kusasenri Riding Club

  • Namino Plateau Yasuragi Exchange Center

    Former elementary school converted for lodging and training, with plateau nature-experience programs.

  • Mizuki Tour and Monzenmachi Stroll

    Fourteen spring drinking spots on the Ichinomiya temple-town street, serving water revered for long life.

  • Mount Kishima

  • Fruit Country COUPLES

    Year-round fruit picking facing Mount Aso, with a cafe offering handmade apple pie and strawberry soft serve.

  • Dog Palace Resort Aso Otohime

  • ASO BASE BACKPACKERS
