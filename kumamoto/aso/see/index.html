Sights in Aso, Kumamoto

Aso · Kumamoto · 25 places

See

  • Aso Nakadake Crater

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    Active Nakadake crater, 600 meters in diameter and 130 meters deep; crater viewing is currently closed.

  • Kusasenrigahama

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    Highland at about 1,100 m where grazing horses walk, with views of smoking Aso Nakadake crater.

  • Daikanbo

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    Peak about 936 m above Aso Valley offering 360-degree views to the Five Peaks and Kuju Mountains.

  • Aso Shrine

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-0064

    Head shrine of about 500 Aso Shrines, over 2,000 years old, with six national important cultural properties.

  • Aso Mountain Summit Area

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3174

    One of the world's largest calderas, with over 1,500 plants and Miyamakirishima blooms.

  • Futae Pass

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    Stone-paved Bungo Kaido section built by Kato Kiyomasa, once used for Hosokawa alternate attendance.

  • San Shrine

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    Shrine to Toyotamahime, goddess of milk, reputedly built about 200 years ago; many pregnant women visit.

  • Myogyo-ji

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-1960

    Linked to Natsume Soseki's The 210th Day; centuries-old weeping cherry and municipal natural-monument ginkgo.

  • Kokuzo Shrine

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-4077

    One of Aso's twelve shrines, also called the North Shrine, preserving a cedar trunk over 2,000 years old.

  • Aso Volcano Museum

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-2111

    Learn about active Mount Aso, with the world's largest caldera, via models and live crater broadcasts.

  • Kamimikura and Shimomikura Kofun

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3229

    Among Kyushu's largest kofun, 60 meters west of Aso Kokuzo Shrine, with a two-chamber corridor-style stone chamber.

  • Aso Shrine Approach

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-0064

    Rare side approach facing Aso Shrine's tower gate, lined with shops, springs, and April cherry blossoms.

  • Bochu Yaeijo

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-0351

  • Komezuka

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    About 80 meters high and 3,300 years old; an inverted-bowl cone covered in soft grassland.

  • Fukunokami Jizo

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    Lucky Jizo unearthed by chance sits on a rice bale holding a lottery ticket; people pray to win.

  • Kabutoiwa Observatory

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-32-1910

    Viewpoint of the Aso Five Peaks and Aso Valley, sometimes with a sea of clouds at dawn.

  • Sunasenrigahama

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    Old crater southeast of Aso Nakadake, with barren rock and black sand unlike green Kusasenrigahama.

  • Hakoishi Pass

    Aso, Kumamoto

    A switchback pass facing Nekodake, popular with motorcyclists and cyclists, with scenery likened to the Great Wall.

  • Kusasenri Observatory

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    785,000 square meters of grassland beside a pond said to come from rain, viewing Nakadake, Komezuka, and Aso Valley.

  • Rime Ice at Kusasenri

    Aso, Kumamoto

    At Kusasenri in Aso, rime ice can be seen until about mid-February, with a frozen pond and white snow.

  • Koga Falls

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3174

    Twin falls about 80m and 100m high become ice cascades in midwinter, with lighting from mid-January to late February.

  • Sensuikyo Gorge

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-34-1600

    About 900 m gorge at the north foot of Aso Nakadake and Takadake. Miyama Kirishima turn it pink in mid-May.

  • Tagoyama Observatory (Sorafune Pier)

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3174

    Wooden deck projecting from 653m Mount Tago, overlooking Uchinomaki, Aso Valley, and the Five Peaks of Aso.

  • Usopp Statue

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3174

    Sniper Usopp statue in Aso, planned for fiscal 2019, helping restore damaged grasslands.

  • Nihyakutoka Monument

    Aso, Kumamoto · 0967-22-3174

    Monument to Soseki's novel Nihyakutoka, based on getting lost climbing Aso, near his presumed lost spot.
