Sights in Gyokuto, Kumamoto

Gyokuto · Kumamoto · 4 places

See

  • Takatsuki Government Army Cemetery

    Gyokuto, Kumamoto · 0968-85-3111

    National Historic Site park with 980 graves of government soldiers killed at three Satsuma Rebellion battlefields.

  • Usoura Government Army Cemetery

    Gyokuto, Kumamoto · 0968-85-3609

    Cemetery of 398 government soldiers and sword-unit police; designated a prefectural historic site in 1977.

  • Kichiji Pass (Kichiji Park)

    Gyokuto, Kumamoto · 0968-85-3111

    One of the fiercest Satsuma Rebellion battlefields and the Satsuma line's last stronghold, feared as Hell Pass.

  • Prince Arisugawa Battle Command Site

    Gyokuto, Kumamoto · 0968-85-3111

    Site where commander Prince Arisugawa Taruhito viewed the Tabaruzaka battle; a monument stands in a spring azalea park.
