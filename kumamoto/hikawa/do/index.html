Things to do in Hikawa, Kumamoto

Hikawa · Kumamoto · 4 places

Do

  • Sakuragaoka Park

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-62-2315

    About 1,000 cherry trees of 10 varieties, with walking paths and a late-March festival.

  • Tachigami Gorge Rural Park

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-62-1543

    Nature park with a 75m gorge cliff, camping, lodges, and a 71m suspension bridge.

  • Ryuhoku Park

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-62-2999

    Hilltop park with a 126m roller slide, athletic play equipment, and a festival square.

  • HOTEL AZ Kumamoto Yatsushiro Miyahara

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-62-1300

    Affordable business hotel about 10 minutes by car from Yatsushiro IC, with large-vehicle parking and Wi-Fi.
