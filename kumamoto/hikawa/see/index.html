Sights in Hikawa, Kumamoto

Hikawa · Kumamoto · 5 places

See

  • Hikawa Town Community Development Information Bank

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-62-1600

    Former Taisho-era Iseri Bank, now a community hub with an Akiyama Koji gallery.

  • Ono Shell Mound

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-52-5860

    Middle to late Jomon shell mound that yielded pottery, stone tools, and bones.

  • Notsu Burial Mound Group

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-52-5860

    Four early to mid-6th-century keyhole tombs about 100m long, a national historic site.

  • Tategami Gorge

    Hikawa, Kumamoto · 0965-62-1543

    Popular camping gorge where limestone cliffs contrast with autumn leaves, and cliffs and a suspension bridge are lit year-round.

  • Onoana Kofun

    Hikawa, Kumamoto

    Once thought round, excavation showed a late 6th-century keyhole tomb over 100 meters, with a 6.48-meter chamber.
