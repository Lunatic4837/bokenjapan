Do in Hitoyoshi

Hitoyoshi · Kumamoto · 15 places

Do

  • Hitoyoshi Onsen

    Since 1910, with 50-plus sources of gentle weak-alkaline carbonated water. Ryokan on the Kuma River and 20-plus public baths.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-1370 · Official / source link

  • Kuma River Descent

    Over-100-year wooden-boat descent of the Kuma River, one of Japan's three great rapids, with winter kotatsu boats and rafting.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-5555 · Official / source link

  • Marue Annex

    Family-run homey ryokan in central Hitoyoshi, about 10 minutes from Hitoyoshi IC, with hearty local cuisine.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-3948 · Official / source link

  • Marue Honkan

    Inn originating from Chitoseya, a rice and kimono merchant to the Hitoyoshi Sagara clan, with hearty local cuisine.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2291 · Official / source link

  • Hitoyoshi Onsen Ayu no Sato

    Inn on the Kuma River, one of Japan's three great rapids, viewing Hitoyoshi Castle ruins.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2171 · Official / source link

  • Hitoyoshi Onsen Nabeya

    Historic inn where every room overlooks the Kuma River and Hitoyoshi Castle site.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-3131 · Official / source link

  • Shirasagiso, Hitoyoshi Onsen

    Century-old Hitoyoshi inn for five groups, serving ayu by a selected spring pond, with free-flowing baths.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-3420 · Official / source link

  • Murayama Park

    Plateau park overlooking Hitoyoshi with an observatory, tennis courts, jogging course, and spring cherries.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111 · Official / source link

  • Kuma River Rafting

    Rafting on the Kuma River, one of Japan's three fastest, with one instructor per boat.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 090-2394-9098 · Official / source link

  • Unsun Karuta

    Japanese remake of mid-16th-century Portuguese cards, kept only in Hitoyoshi-Kuma, a prefectural intangible folk property.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2566 · Official / source link

  • Sengestu Shiromi Brewery

    Kuma shochu brewery since 1903. Staff-guided tours, free tasting of about 30 shochu, and an on-site hot-spring foot bath.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-3207 · Official / source link

  • Hitoyoshi Onsen Motoyu

    Public bath founded in 1934 near Kuma River boat boarding and Hitoyoshi Castle ruins, loved by locals.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-32-8632 · Official / source link

  • Tsurukame Onsen

    Early Showa public bath with attendant counter, quality water, and Ebisu and Daikoku spouts.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-3221 · Official / source link

  • Hassenba Hitoyoshi Kumagawa

    Hitoyoshi-Kuma base for Kuma River boat trips, rafting, and e-bike tours, with cafe and shop.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-5555 · Official / source link

  • Coworking Space osoto Hitoyoshi

    Paid-membership coworking renovated from a public lodging, with indoor turf, tents, free Wi-Fi, and drinks.

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 050-1743-1594 · Official / source link
