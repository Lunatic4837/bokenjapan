Sights in Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto

Hitoyoshi · Kumamoto · 35 places

See

  • Aoi Aso Shrine

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2274

    Founded in 806; main halls built in 1610-1613 are National Treasures. A Kengo Kuma hall opened on site in 2023.

  • Eikoku-ji

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2458

    Soto Zen temple founded in 1408, famous for a ghost scroll and as Saigo army headquarters.

  • Ganjo-ji

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-24-4161

    Sagara family temple founded in 1233; its seated Amida is a national Important Cultural Property.

  • Hitoyoshi Castle Ruins

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2324

    One of Japan's 100 famous castles and a national historic site, Sagara center for about 700 years.

  • Sekisui-ji

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-4411

    Temple with a round gate hollowed from a huge stone, plus a spectacles bridge and spring.

  • Kannon-ji Kannon (Kyushu Sagara 33 Kannon)

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-3793

    13th stop of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, at a Rinzai temple founded in 1385.

  • Kiyomizu Kannon (Kyushu Sagara 33 Kannon)

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-24-4161

    First Sagara 33 Kannon stop; Thousand-armed Kannon invited from Kyoto Kiyomizu-dera in 1603.

  • Sekishitsu Kannon (Kyushu Sagara 33 Kannon)

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-4411

    Seventh Sagara 33 Kannon stop, moved from Sekishitsu-ji after a 1502 quarrel left that temple abandoned.

  • Murayama Kannon (Kyushu Sagara 33 Kannon)

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-24-4911

    Ninth Sagara 33 Kannon stop, whose wooden Thousand-armed Kannon is said to understand people's troubles.

  • Hitoyoshi Castle History Museum

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2324

    History museum of Hitoyoshi Castle.

  • Nagata (Ashihara) Kannon Hall (Kyushu Sagara 33 Kannon)

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-23-3234

    11th Sagara 33 Kannon stop; image kept in a nail-free, vividly colored late-Edo cabinet.

  • Kuma Shochu Museum Hakutake Densho-gura

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-32-9750

    Museum on 400 years of Kuma shochu history and culture, with exhibits and tasting.

  • Benitoriyama Observatory

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Overlooks the Hitoyoshi-Kuma Basin, with fantastical mist views in winter. Maintained roads allow access by car.

  • Hitoyoshi Loop Bridge

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Loop bridge on National Route 221 near the prefecture border, opened in 1977. Length 1,190 m; highest pier 60 m.

  • Kaname Falls

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Selected among Japan's 100 waterfalls: male about 36 m, female about 30 m, and a gentle fall. Summer retreat.

  • Nuno Falls

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Named for water wrapping the rock face like a large cloth. Late-July festival offers somen noodles and sweetfish catching.

  • Akaike Kannon

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    33rd Sagara 33 Kannon site at Akaike Castle ruins. Sho-Kannon, said to be Muromachi, is a city-designated important cultural property.

  • Iwaya Kumanoza Shrine

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2324

    Formerly called Iwaya Gongen, a shrine retaining Shinto-Buddhist syncretism. Its tuff torii and main hall are National Important Cultural Properties.

  • Yunomoto Kannon

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Eighth Sagara 33 Kannon site near Hayashi Onsen source. The statue was lost in the 1965 flood and replaced.

  • Yasegatsuru Kannon

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Third Sagara 33 Kannon site. The Eleven-faced image was carried out and enshrined here after the 1862 fire.

  • Mikkahara Kannon

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Fourth Sagara 33 Kannon site, worshipped since the Edo period. Breast-shaped offerings are said to improve milk production.

  • Sagasato Kannon

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Sixth Sagara 33 Kannon site, an Eleven-faced image dated 1752. Said to protect children and houses from fire.

  • Sebara Kannon

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-3550

    Tenth stop of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, founded in 1653 as a retirement temple.

  • Oikami Shrine

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2324

    Deity enshrined in 807; the 1628 hall built by Sagara Nagatsune is a National Important Cultural Property.

  • Indo Kyukei Monument

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Built November 1, 1953 to honor Hitoyoshi-born lyricist Indo Kyukei, writer of Kokyo no Haika and Ryoshu.

  • Yatake Station

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-4011

    Closed. Highest station on the Hisatsu Line mountain section, at 536.9 m, with a Meiji building and D51-170 display.

  • Okoba Station

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto

    Closed, including the restaurant. Japan's only station with both a loop and switchback; many business cards are posted.

  • Hitoyoshi Plum Garden

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2111

    Historic plum garden on Okoba hill south of Hitoyoshi. White and red plums bloom from mid-February; famous plum festival.

  • Nishize Bridge

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto

    Model for the anime Natsume's Book of Friends, with Kuma River sunset views and crested kingfishers.

  • SL Observatory

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto

    Viewpoint for Japan's only working stone engine shed, built in 1911, with a nearby turntable viewing zone.

  • Hitoyoshi Railway Museum Mozoca Station 868

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-23-2000

    Railway museum by Eiji Mitsuoka introducing the Hisatsu Line, with dioramas, mini trains, rail bikes and a library.

  • Hitoyoshi Station Automaton Clock

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto

    Castle-style automaton clock at JR Hitoyoshi Station; 17 dolls appear hourly to a Kuma folk melody.

  • Misogi Bridge

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2274

    1921 three-arch bridge called the prefecture's oldest concrete bridge, before Aoi Aso Shrine.

  • Kajiyamachi Street

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto · 0966-22-2566

    Hitoyoshi castle-town street of stone paving and white-walled merchant houses, with one remaining blacksmith.

  • Buke-gura Samurai Residence

    Hitoyoshi, Kumamoto

    Closed residence of Sagara chief retainers; gate from Hitoyoshi Castle, once Saigo Takamori's lodging.
