Things to do in Itsuki, Kumamoto

Itsuki · Kumamoto · 5 places

Do

  • Itsuki Onsen Yumeuta

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 0966-37-2301

    Weak-alkaline natural spring with indoor and outdoor baths viewing Itsuki nature. Finnish deep-sea stone sauna and charcoal steam bath.

  • Hashiuno Nature Forest Park

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 080-2670-3811

    Pine-surrounded 1,000-meter park with trails, forest-road mountain-bike courses, lodging houses, bungalows, and tent rental.

  • Itsuki Village Bungee Jump

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 0278-72-8133

    66m jump from Koyae Bridge into Kawabe River, one of Japan's three most beautiful rivers. Over 2000 jumpers since 2015.

  • Tankaino Campsite

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 080-2670-3811

    Rare 1,000-meter campsite in Itsuki, cool in summer, with starry skies, sauna, and fishing.

  • Itsuki Outdoor Club

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 080-2670-3811

    Activity plans using Itsuki nature at a 1,000-meter campsite beside the Kawabe River.
