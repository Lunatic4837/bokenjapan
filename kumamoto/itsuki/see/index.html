Sights in Itsuki, Kumamoto

Itsuki · Kumamoto · 5 places

See

  • Otaki Nature Forest Park

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 0966-37-2611

    Forest park with a 35-meter waterfall, over-100-year red oak and beech forest, and many rare plants and animals.

  • Hirase Gorge

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 0966-37-2247

    Clear winding mountain stream; summer greenery and autumn foliage; prime village yamame trout waters for fishing and river play.

  • Shirataki Park

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 0966-37-2211

    Roadside park with a 70m-high, 200m-wide limestone cliff like a waterfall, emerald stream, small cave, and suspension bridge.

  • Shimokajiwara Area, Itsuki Village

    Itsuki, Kumamoto · 0966-37-2211

    One of the prefecture's top autumn foliage spots, best late October to mid-November. Mountain cherries bloom in April.

  • Higashimata and Nishimata Aso Shrine

    Itsuki, Kumamoto

    Mid-Edo lord valuing Itsuki ties reportedly sent magistrates to worship; a 600-year sacred tree stump is dedicated.
