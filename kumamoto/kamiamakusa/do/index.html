Things to do in Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto

Kamiamakusa · Kumamoto · 19 places

Do

  • Komatsuya Nagisakan

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-59-0111

    Amakusa Pearl Line setting with endless blue sea, green islands, and kind hospitality.

  • Gokyoen

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0967-56-1417

    Small family onsen ryokan by the sea, serving fresh Amakusa seafood, with fishing-gear rental.

  • Amakusa Nagisatei

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-56-3232

    Hotel beside Amakusa Matsushima with outdoor-bath detached rooms and courses rich in Amakusa ingredients.

  • Ai no Misaki Inn

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-59-0010

    Onsen inn on Yagyu Island with sea and mountain cuisine, mineral-rich Shioyu baths, and a guest-only beach.

  • Yurakutei Great Cave Inn

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-0536

    Amakusa hillside inn known for a hand-dug cave bath, two spring sources, and seafood, near Yumigahama Beach.

  • Kamiamakusa Onsen Area

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-5602

    Oyano and Matsushima onsens with cave baths, a foot-bath bar, and Japan's 100 Best Sunsets views.

  • Nishime Beach (Calm Beach)

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-5602

    About 300m of white sand with calm waves, popular with families, and a beach house with lodging.

  • Hiai Beach (Pearl Sun Beach)

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-5602

    About 300m of white sand, Mt. Unzen on clear days, and a low-tide walk to Takamoku Island.

  • Amakusa Treasure Island Line

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-56-2458

    Scheduled 20-minute ferry between Misumi and Maejima, with views of the Five Bridges and Amakusa Matsushima.

  • Suwa Beach

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-5602

    Calm cove beach safe for small children, with ample shade and an attached campground.

  • Kojima Beach

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-5602

    Roughly 300m beach along a breakwater on the Shiranui Sea, calm enough for small children.

  • Nokamajima Happuku Campground

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-3883

    Seaside campground past Nokama Bridge, with Ariake views, swimming, rock fishing, and a July-August beer garden.

  • Amakusa Fishing Pond Leisure Land

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-59-0188

    Fishing pond one minute by boat from the pier, with 15 fish species and March-June clam digging.

  • Shiradake Forest Park

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-26-5512

    50ha nature park in Unzen-Amakusa National Park with camping, Fudo Falls, Yatake Shrine, megalith ruins, and wetlands.

  • Sea Cruise

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0969-56-2458

    Luxury cruiser tours include Five Bridges, dolphin watching reportedly at 98% encounter rate, and Matsushima sunsets.

  • Spa Thalasso Amakusa

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-56-1126

    One of Japan's few full-scale thalassotherapy facilities, using Kamiamakusa seawater for activities, care, and sunsets.

  • Tsuriichi Sea Fishing Pond

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-57-0734

    Family fishing pond for red sea bream, black sea bream and large horse mackerel, with private hire and raft barbecues.

  • L'isola THE BIRD

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 0964-27-5757

    Stay resort near Yumigahama Beach in Kamiamakusa with cottages, infinity pool, sauna, and mini golf.

  • TAYUTA

    Kamiamakusa, Kumamoto · 050-1706-5284

    Ocean-view suite villas with hot springs on Hiai Island, Kamiamakusa, offering Amakusa cuisine dinners.
