Do in Kikuchi

Kikuchi · Kumamoto · 15 places

Do

  • Kikuchi Onsen

    Beauty-skin onsen said found about 60 years ago after a white dragon's message, certified among Japan's 100 famous baths.

  • Kikuchi Gorge Onsen Iwakura
