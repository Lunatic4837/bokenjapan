Things to do in Kikuchi, Kumamoto

Kikuchi · Kumamoto · 15 places

Do

  • Shikinosato Kyokushi

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-37-3939

    Active-tourism complex midway up Kuradake, with a zoo, log houses, campground, BBQ house, summer pool, and spring rhododendron garden.

  • Kikuchi Onsen

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-2926

    Beauty-skin onsen said found about 60 years ago after a white dragon's message, certified among Japan's 100 famous baths.

  • Shichijo Onsen Dome

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-26-4800

    Castle-style onsen with lodging, pool, and bungalows. Ten bath types, two saunas, and a wide family bath with handrails.

  • Kikuchi Gorge Onsen Iwakura

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-27-0026

    Single riverside inn with free-flowing onsen rooms and creative kaiseki from Kikuchi ingredients.

  • Shiroyama-so

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-2400

    Quiet Kikuchi Onsen inn proud of its natural spring, called makeup water and popular for beautiful skin.

  • Horai-kan

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-2703

    Kikuchi Onsen inn since 1955 with free-flowing beautiful-skin spring water and seasonal cuisine.

  • Kikuchi Tourist Grape Orchard

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-27-0514

    Grape orchard for picking from mid-August to early October, with picnics allowed and a product shop.

  • Kikuchi Park

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-7242

    Hilltop park over Kikuchi popular with families, offering athletic gear, azaleas, and about 3,000 cherries.

  • Ryumon Dam

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-27-1120

    Large dam whose Madaraguchi Lake is used for boats, canoes, and events, plus learning exhibits.

  • Kikuchi Plateau Family Campground

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-27-1228

    Highland auto campsite with wide lawns, hot showers, a fishing pond, suited to stargazing.

  • Inn Among the Trees Seiryuso

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-24-2155

    Inn a little apart from Kikuchi hot-spring town, with four baths including a handmade outdoor bath overlooking a stream.

  • Kunosato

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-7123

    Inn where you can stay with pets in rooms and the restaurant; each room has indoor and outdoor baths.

  • Dragon Campground

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-27-0011

    Campground beside large Ryumon Dam for overnight or day camping, picnics, rentals, and light meals.

  • Madajakuchi Lake (Ryumon Dam) Kayak and SUP Experience

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 096-221-9869

    Go Nature canoe and kayak tours on Madajakuchi Lake, with waterfalls not visible from land.

  • Kikuchi Mirai Base Work and Life Space

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-2501

    Renovated 100-year farmhouse with workspace, guest rooms, a rotating cafe, private sauna, and spring water.
