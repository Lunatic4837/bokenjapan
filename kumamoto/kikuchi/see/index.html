Sights in Kikuchi, Kumamoto

Kikuchi · Kumamoto · 9 places

See

  • Kikuchi Gorge

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto

    1,193-hectare recreation forest with waterfalls and springs, listed among Japan's famous waters and falls.

  • Kikuchi Shrine

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-2549

    Shrine founded in 1870 honoring three Kikuchi lords, with a hall displaying family cultural properties.

  • Kitamiya Aso Shrine

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-2685

    Former village shrine that enshrined Aso Kitamiya in 1378, with prefectural cultural-property statues.

  • Lord Kikuchi Takeshige's Grave

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-7232

    Grave of the 13th Kikuchi head, known for the family code and the thousand-spear design.

  • Kikuchi Matsuhayashi Noh Stage

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-7232

    Noh stage where Matsuhayashi is dedicated every October 13 at Kikuchi Shrine's autumn festival; rebuilt in the Edo period.

  • Watarase River and Nikashiki River

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-7223

    Popular firefly spot in Obara and Takayanagi, Kikuchi. Peak late May to early June, especially along the Watarase River.

  • Shichijo Cosmo Bridge Area

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-7223

    About 1.5 million cosmos bloom like a pink carpet along the riverbed around Cosmo Bridge in Shichijo.

  • Not Found

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-37-3719

  • Kikuchi City Central Library

    Kikuchi, Kumamoto · 0968-25-1111

    About 200,000 books in KiCROSS. Book River shelves evoke Kikuchi River, children's books at the source, adult genres downstream.
