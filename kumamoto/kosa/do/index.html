Do in Kosa

Kosa · Kumamoto · 8 places

Do

  • COMMON IDOE Campground

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-282-8591

    Empty-handed glamping with beds, air conditioning, and amenities; local BBQ and bonfires. Tent sites and a cafe too.

  • Nakako Bridge Green Park

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-1154

    Midori River right-bank park linking Kosa and Misato. Picnic on grass and dip hands and feet in summer.

  • Tsushida Riverside Nature Park

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-1154

    Called Otome Kawara; camping, barbecue, and river play, with spring flowers and autumn ginkgo.

  • Kawahira Campsite

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-2447

    Quiet mountain riverside campsite with cooking areas and toilets, suited to small training groups.

  • Kosa Town Yana

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-0125

    Fresh Midori River sweetfish dishes served in a thatched pavilion; winter menus use Kumamoto produce.

  • NIPPONIA Kosa Sosui no Sato

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-8871

    About 130-year-old renovated inn, three groups daily, with canal-side veranda, garden annex, and sushi breakfast.

  • COMMON IDOE

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-282-8591

    Glamping and tent sites renewed in 2020, with hands-free barbecue, showers, cafe, and gear shop, under an hour from Kumamoto.

  • Tsushida Riverside Nature Park

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-1154

    Kosa park on the Midori River for river play and barbecue, with rapeseed, cherries, ginkgo, forest, and lawn.
