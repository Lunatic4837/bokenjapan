Sights in Kosa, Kumamoto

Kosa · Kumamoto · 3 places

See

  • Asobaru Fragrant Olive

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-2447

    Said to exceed 770 years and stand 18m tall. National natural monument blooming twice from late September to late October.

  • Kosa Shrine

    Kosa, Kumamoto · 096-234-1111

    One of four Aso shrines, enshrining Kosa Myojin and housing the Mongol Invasion Scrolls.

  • Jinnouchi Castle Ruins

    Kosa, Kumamoto

    Outstanding medieval Higo castle ruins on the Midorikawa, with a large dry moat; thought built by Konishi Yukinaga.
