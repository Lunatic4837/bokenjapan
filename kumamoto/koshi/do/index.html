Things to do in Koshi, Kumamoto

Koshi · Kumamoto · 4 places

Do

  • Kumamoto Agricultural Park Country Park

    Koshi, Kumamoto · 096-248-7311

    Park for learning farming and playing on pastures and athletic equipment, with 3,400 roses of 550 varieties and rose festivals.

  • Bentenzan Park

    Koshi, Kumamoto · 096-248-3855

    Park selected among Kumamoto's 100 Green Views, with loop roads for jogging and athletic equipment.

  • You Palace Benten

    Koshi, Kumamoto · 096-348-2626

    One of the prefecture's largest health complexes, with onsen, heated pools, a restaurant, and training rooms.

  • Mova Villa Koshi

    Koshi, Kumamoto · 070-8958-5160

    Nine villas for 4-8 in quiet Koshi, about 25 minutes by car from Aso Kumamoto Airport, with elevators.
