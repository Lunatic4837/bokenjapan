Sights in Kuma, Kumamoto

Kuma · Kumamoto · 7 places

See

  • Oninokuchi Rice Terraces

    Kuma, Kumamoto · 0966-32-1114

    Selected among Japan's 100 rice terraces: 68 steps over 100 m, with regular early-Edo stone walls along the Imo River.

  • Kyusendo Cave

    Kuma, Kumamoto · 0966-32-0080

    About 4800 m limestone cave, second longest in western Japan; columns and stalagmites still grow after 300 million years.

  • Uguchi Kannon Hall

    Kuma, Kumamoto · 0966-33-0921

    Fifth stop on the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, said to bring sound health and freedom from illness.

  • Matsudani Rice Terrace Observatory

    Kuma, Kumamoto · 0966-32-1114

    Observatory over fan-shaped irregular rice terraces at 150-250 meters, dotted with huge stones and cedars, changing with the seasons.

  • Maidoko Great Cherry Tree

    Kuma, Kumamoto · 0966-32-1115

    A lone 15m mountain cherry in cedar woods, trunk over 3m, about 300 years old, night-lit, sometimes heart-shaped.

  • Konose Sumiyoshi Shrine

    Kuma, Kumamoto

    Right-bank Kuma River shrine for navigation and traffic safety, once revered by lords and boatmen.

  • Hashizume Bridge

    Kuma, Kumamoto

    Stone arch bridge over Imo River, a Kuma River tributary, completed in 1955 and still used.
