Things to do in Kumamoto, Kumamoto

Kumamoto · Kumamoto · 50 places

Do

  • Rikka no Yu, Dormy Inn Kumamoto

    Kumamoto · 096-311-5489

    Business hotel a three-minute walk from Sakuramachi Bus Terminal, with rooftop natural onsen indoor and outdoor baths.

  • Kinpo Forest Station Michikusa Hall

    Kumamoto · 096-277-2727

    Hiking base below Mount Kinpo, with history exhibits, spring flowers, and early-summer fireflies.

  • Ueki Onsen

    Kumamoto · 096-274-6830

    Onsen over 130 years old near central Kumamoto, offering simple sulfur and weakly alkaline simple springs.

  • Takuma Onsen Kamizono Sanso

    Kumamoto · 096-380-2511

    Ryokan on low Kamizono Mountain by Kumamoto Interchange; clear views reach Kumamoto City and distant Amakusa islands.

  • Koyoi no Yuyado Yuzen

    Kumamoto · 096-215-3177

    All eight rooms are detached with large free-flowing alkaline open-air onsen baths.

  • Kumamon Square

    Kumamoto · 096-327-9066

    Kumamon's activity base with a daily 360-degree stage, photo spots, AR games, limited goods, and a local-sweets cafe.

  • Kumamoto Ken Shuzokenkyujo

    Kumamoto · 096-352-4921

  • Kumamoto Port

    Kumamoto · 096-329-4411

    Artificial-island port on the Ariake Sea for ferries to Shimabara and Amakusa, with shops and seasonal events.

  • Hakenomiya Park

    Kumamoto · 096-245-5050

    Northern Kumamoto retarding pond used as a city water source, popular for water play and cherry blossoms.

  • Kichijien Tourist Farm

    Kumamoto · 096-273-2544

    Fruit-picking farm offering grapes, apples, mandarins, pears, and strawberries, plus barbecue and a fruit shop.

  • Yuhoen Fruit Land

    Kumamoto · 096-288-1545

    Year-round fruit picking with a fishing pond, flowing somen, and a cafe serving fruit tarts and coffee.

  • Aquadome Kumamoto

    Kumamoto · 096-358-2711

    Indoor pool complex in Minami Ward; the main pool is a winter ice rink and event venue.

  • Eto Boathouse

    Kumamoto · 096-362-5266

    Boathouse founded in 1877 with pedal boats, rowboats, canoes, and roofed party boats on Lake Ezu, plus a cafe.

  • Stone-paved Path (Kusamakura Hiking Course)

    Kumamoto · 096-328-2393

    Former main road, the model for Soseki's Kusamakura, now a hiking course with stone pavement and bamboo.

  • Tatsudayama

    Kumamoto · 096-346-5090

    152-meter hill northeast of Kumamoto City with forest paths, a wetland garden, fishing pond, athletic course, and lawn.

  • Ishigamiyama Park

    Kumamoto · 090-7398-5951

    At Kinpozan's eastern foot; the city's only barbecue park, with dog run, sky plaza, and observatory views.

  • Tsuboigawa Green Space

    Kumamoto · 096-245-5054

    Large park beside the Tsuboigawa basin, with sports grounds, a castle-inspired deck, and Higokko Jungle.

  • Kumamoto Wasukishikan

    Kumamoto · 096-352-5101

    Japanese creative hotel five minutes' walk from Kumamoto Castle, with a large bath and breakfast buffet.

  • Extol Inn Kumamoto Suizenji

    Kumamoto · 096-364-3111

    Modern business hotel a one-minute walk from Suizenji Park Station and four minutes from Suizenji Jojuen.

  • Extol Inn Kumamoto Ginza-dori

    Kumamoto · 096-351-2111

    Hotel in the middle of downtown, a three-minute walk from Hanabatacho tram stop.

  • Toyoko Inn Kumamoto Sakuramachi Bus Terminal

    Kumamoto · 096-322-1045

    Casual business hotel a two-minute walk from Shinshimachi tram stop and three minutes from downtown.

  • Toyoko Inn Kumamoto Station Front

    Kumamoto · 096-351-1045

  • Daifuku-yu

    Kumamoto · 096-343-7868

  • Yoan-yu

  • Takano-yu

  • GuestHouse426

  • Sumitsugu House Nishi

  • Kumamoto City Zoological and Botanical Gardens

  • Matsuya Annex

  • Ryotei Matsuya Honkan Suizenji
