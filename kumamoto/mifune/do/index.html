Things to do in Mifune, Kumamoto

Mifune · Kumamoto · 4 places

Do

  • Yoshimuta Plateau Green Village

    Mifune, Kumamoto · 096-285-2210

    Plateau at the foot of Aso's outer rim offering walks, stargazing, BBQ, camping, and fossil digging.

  • Higurashi Kiln

    Mifune, Kumamoto · 096-285-2426

    Pottery kiln deep in Yoshimuta Plateau offering one-day pottery experience classes.

  • Ichido Kiln

    Mifune, Kumamoto · 096-282-0578

    Pottery kiln known for soft white ware made with three techniques, offering hand-wheel experiences.

  • Toen Mifune Kiln

    Mifune, Kumamoto · 096-282-6783

    Pottery studio between the Mifune and Midori rivers, with a gallery and hand-wheel pottery experiences.
