Do in Minamata

Minamata · Kumamoto · 16 places

Do

  • Eco Park Minamata

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-62-7501

    Seaside park in Minamata with playground, rose garden, sports facilities, and Minamata Disease Museum.

  • Yunoko Onsen

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-63-2079

    Onsen in scenic Ashikita Coast Prefectural Park, said discovered about 1,900 years ago, now a developed hot-spring village.

  • Yunotsuru Onsen

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-63-2079

    Hidden onsen of about 700 years in Minamata near Kagoshima, mostly simple sulfur springs, with four inns.

  • Shoyokan

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-63-4121

    Popular onsen inn with free-flowing scenic open-air baths, ocean-view rooms, and local seafood, notably live-fish sashimi.

  • Hiranoya

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-63-2161

    Yunoko Onsen inn overlooking the Shiranui Sea, with private family baths and barrel baths; business use welcome.

  • Yunoko Sea and Sunset

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-62-6262

    Ocean-view inn overlooking the Shiranui Sea, with a skin-friendly free-flowing onsen and local buffet.

  • Cutlassfish Fishing

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-62-4210

    Hand-line cutlassfish fishing off Yunoko Onsen; the skipper teaches from baiting to technique.

  • Yunoko Fishing Park

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-63-3870

    Coastal fishing pier at Yunoko Onsen where children and women can fish easily.

  • Yunoko Island Park

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-61-1629

    Uninhabited three-island park linked by suspension bridge to Yunoko Beach for summer swimming.

  • Yunoko Beach

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-63-2079

    White-sand beach near Yunoko Onsen with showers, a boat bath, SUP, and diving.

  • Japan's Longest Athletic Field

    Minamata, Kumamoto

    Seventeen-kilometer path on the former JR Yamano Line for cycling and walking, with bike rentals.

  • Yunotsuru Onsen Town

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-61-1629

    Quiet mountain hot-spring town with inns and day baths along the river, long used for cures, colored by autumn leaves.

  • Iwasaki Onsen

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-62-3354

    Small free-flowing onsen inn run by a fishing-boat captain couple, serving freshly caught local fish.

  • Yunotsuru Onsen Health Center Hotaru no Yu

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-68-0811

    Municipal day-visit onsen with free-flowing hinoki baths, a free rest space, and local produce.

  • Yunotsuru Onsen Asahi-so

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-68-0111

    Ryokan over 70 years old with free-flowing alkaline simple spring, seasonal dishes, and day bathing.

  • Nagisa no Koban HIMETATSU

    Minamata, Kumamoto · 0966-83-8120

    Community base at Yunoko Beach offering year-round kayaking, diving, snorkeling, workshops, and a cafe.
