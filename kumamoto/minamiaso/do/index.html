Things to do in Minamiaso, Kumamoto

Minamiaso · Kumamoto · 28 places

Do

  • Aso Farm Land

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2100

    Unusual health theme park supervised by experts, with exercise woods, restaurants, onsen, a dome hotel, and animal encounters.

  • Aso Monkey Village Sarumawashi Theater

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-35-1341

    Entertainment park where monkeys perform handstands, stilts, and cycling in a 600-seat climate-controlled theater.

  • Minami-Aso Onsen Village

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2222

    Area of five onsen, Tochigi, Tarutama, Jigoku, Daiaso Hinoyama and Hakusui, each with its own character.

  • Hakusui Onsen Takenokura Lodge

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2980

    All rooms have open-air baths at Hakusui Onsen, with a large bath viewing the Aso outer rim.

  • Minami-Aso Kugino Onsen Shiki no Mori

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2212

    Weakly alkaline beauty onsen with Aso views; every guest room overlooks Aso's Five Peaks.

  • Aso Farm Land

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto

    Over 400 unique dome rooms attached to Aso Farm Land, with a large open-air bath.

  • Jikusha

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-63-3250

    Only five fully detached rooms on a 1,000-tsubo site at 650 meters on Aso's foothills.

  • Minami-Aso Plateau House Noah

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-62-3010

    Pension in Minami-Aso Village for inexpensive room-only stays, with no meals or baths.

  • Jigoku Onsen Seifuso

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-0005

    Onsen midway up Mt. Eboshi, one of Aso's five peaks, with bluish milky outdoor bath Suzume no Yu.

  • Minamiaso Luna Observatory Auberge Forest Atelier

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-62-3006

    Auberge with observatory and twin planetarium in the Aso caldera, founded in 1986, offering four stargazing tours.

  • Aso Hakusui Onsen Ruri

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-62-9999

    Onsen overlooking the Aso outer rim, with 56-degree sodium bicarbonate water, large baths, open-air baths and family baths.

  • Aso Farm Land Genki no Mori

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2100

    Exercise facility in a natural forest and valley, with a rolling funnel, giant maze and twisted paths for all ages.

  • Minamiaso Luna Observatory

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-62-3006

    At 600 meters, staff guide viewing on Kyushu's largest reflector; rain brings a digital planetarium.

  • Kagura no Sato Park

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-1111

    Minamiaso park where 330-year-old nationally designated Nagano Iwato Kagura is performed twice a month.

  • Kumamoto Prefectural Outdoor Theater Aspecta

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-1161

    Among the world's largest outdoor stages on the southern Aso outer rim, hosting youth-centered cultural events.

  • Sugi Bee Garden Aso Honeybee Farm

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2148

    Beekeeping site with over a hundred hives, bee exhibits, and a shop selling popular comb honey.

  • Aso Tourist Grape Garden North Grape Farm

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-62-9051

    All-weather greenhouse grape farm; pesticide use is under one-fifth of commercial levels, with no herbicide.

  • Trial Golf and Resort ASO Course

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-1616

    Hilly Aso foothill course crossed by a river, with Aso and Kumamoto views and clear-day Mount Fugen.

  • Jizo Pass

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-1112

    Pass between Minamiaso and Nishihara with three Jizo statues, golden autumn pampas, foliage, and a beech-forest trekking course.

  • Jigoku-Tarutama Onsen

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-1112

    Secluded hot spring around 700 meters up the southwest slope of Eboshi-dake in Minamiaso, with colorful autumn leaves.

  • Wakka

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-65-8114

    Small inn for handmade soba using on-site natural water, with Aso views; also usable as a guesthouse.

  • Bettei Soan

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-4555

    Villa overlooking Aso's five peaks, with 16 unique rooms, Aso cuisine, and in-room open-air baths.

  • BED & PANYA Uminekoya 1987

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 090-9407-1987

    Woody inn for three groups a day, with shop Ravi selling morning-baked natural-yeast bread.

  • Garden Villa Minamiaso

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2654

    One-group-per-day villa atop Natural Garden Minamiaso, with Mount Aso views, self-catering, and terrace barbecue.

  • Kinouchi Farm

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-68-0552

    Tateno strawberry farm reopened in January 2020, with picking, soft serve, and a shop.

  • Glass Studio Matsu

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-65-8108

    Small stained-glass workshop in Minamiaso with hands-on workshops making hand mirrors and ornaments.

  • THE ACONCAGUA RESORTS

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 0967-67-2211

    Resort hotel with infinity pool, private natural hot-spring baths, campsite, BBQ sites, and 12 rooms.

  • OHANA

    Minamiaso, Kumamoto · 080-9060-0518

    Lodging amid Aso nature, a former apartment reborn after the Kumamoto earthquake as a homey Minamiaso inn.
