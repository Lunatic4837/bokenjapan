Things to do in Minamioguni, Kumamoto

Minamioguni · Kumamoto · 38 places

Do

  • Nonohana Inn

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-0595

    Secluded Okukurokawa mountain inn with six detached rooms, unique baths, one barrier-free, and outdoor, stone, and barrel onsen.

  • Kurokawa Onsen

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-0076

    Popular onsen village at 700 meters, treating 30 inns and the mountain landscape as one inn, with seven spring types.

  • Yamadori no Mori Auto Campground

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-0997

    Nature-rich campground with summer pool, bungalows, fishing pond, and harvest farm. On-site natural onsen free for campers.

  • Manganji Onsen

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1444

    Quiet onsen along the Manganji River in Minamioguni, with an open concrete communal bath in the river.

  • Detached Inn Yamazaki

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-0945

    Small inn at secluded Oda Onsen with eight sukiya-style detached rooms and 24-hour hot springs.

  • Sato no Yu Waraku

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-0690

    Hidden adults' inn in Oku-Kurokawa; all detached rooms for two have semi-open-air hot springs.

  • Yuyu Higurashi

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-1938

    Homey inn in vast nature, serving grilled ayu and yamame, with private baths and a dog bath.

  • Backpackers Sanko

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1718

    Budget pension at 700-year-old Manganji Onsen, ten minutes by car from Kurokawa Onsen.

  • Oda Onsen Shiki no Sato Hanamura

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-0088

    All-detached Oda Onsen inn in a quiet village; every room has a free-flowing indoor bath.

  • Headwaters Inn Hoyamatei

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-44-0059

    All-detached inn at uppermost Kurokawa Onsen; the Chikugo River source flows beside every room.

  • Yoshiwara Gonbee Village

  • Aso Ogi Onsen Ogiso

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1019

  • Ko no Yu

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-48-8700

  • Inn Genka

  • Iyashi no Sato Kiyashiki

  • Kurokawaso
