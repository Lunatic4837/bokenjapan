Sights in Minamioguni, Kumamoto

Minamioguni · Kumamoto · 7 places

See

  • Hikyo Seven Falls

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1111

    Seven falls on the lower Oda River, set in a steep gorge and natural forest, viewable from a maintained trail.

  • Takenokuma Giant Zelkova

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1111

    About 1,000 years old, 18-meter trunk girth and 32 meters tall, at Takenokuma Sugawara Shrine. National natural monument since 1935.

  • Sparrow Hell

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1111

    Cold-spring area where sulfurous gas vents, named because the gas kills small birds and animals. Desolate and mountain-ringed.

  • Mazeno Gorge

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1444

    Chikugo River headwaters called a secret gorge, open to the public only a few days twice a year.

  • Oshidoishi Hill

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1444

    Said to be an ancient prayer site near Mazeno Gorge, with arranged stones and a 360-degree panorama.

  • Meoto Falls

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1444

    Two small falls where the Oda and Tanohara rivers meet, famous as a matchmaking falls.

  • Sanai Plateau

    Minamioguni, Kumamoto · 0967-42-1444

    Plateau spanning eastern Minamiaso and Kuju, Oita, offering hiking and spectacular spring and autumn views.
