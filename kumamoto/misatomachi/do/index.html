Things to do in Misatomachi, Kumamoto

Misatomachi · Kumamoto · 6 places

Do

  • Japan's No. 1 Stone Steps (Shakain Misaka Promenade)

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Japan's number-one stone steps, 3,333 steps to Shakain, with rest areas and a November climbing event.

  • Misato Forest Campsite Garden Place

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-48-0158

    Lakeside campsite by Midorikawa Dam with lodges, bungalows, tents, a BBQ house, and a children's plaza. Wi-Fi equipped.

  • Genki no Mori Kajika

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-46-3993

    Closed elementary school lodging an hour from Kumamoto, with nature workshops and parent-child countryside programs.

  • Forest Adventure Misato

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 080-8387-3310

    Lakeside outdoor park with adventure courses, Japan's first dam-lake zip crossing, and a 510-meter zip slide.

  • Nihonsugi Pass

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Pass between Yatsushiro and Misato, entrance to Gokanosho, with seasonal views and a hiking course.

  • Misato Footpath Association

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-27-9850

    Eighteen countryside walking courses in Misato, with maps, local guides, and regular events to choose from.
