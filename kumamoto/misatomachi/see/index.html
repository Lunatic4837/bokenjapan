Sights in Misatomachi, Kumamoto

Misatomachi · Kumamoto · 14 places

See

  • Reitai Bridge

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    A 90-meter stone bridge over Funatsu Gorge, among Japan's largest single arches, built in 1847 and lit at night.

  • Kozaki District Rice Terraces

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Muromachi-period stone-walled rice terraces, nicknamed Misato's Machu Picchu, with seasonal rural scenery.

  • Midorikawa Dam

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-48-0216

    Multipurpose dam completed in March 1971, with 1,600 cherry trees, a museum, and Misato pudding.

  • Makado Bridge

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Misato cultural property: a 27-meter downward-curving stone bridge over the Tsuru River, with a waterfall view.

  • Okubo Bridge

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Stone bridge over the Tsuru River, a Midori tributary, built by Shinohara Zenbei. Elegant arch and spring photo spot.

  • Futamata Bridge

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Called Twin Bridge, two stone spans meet at right angles. November to March, late morning sun casts a heart.

  • Okedaki Bridge

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Built in 1817 as Japan's second aqueduct bridge, said to model Tsujun Bridge. Prefectural important cultural property.

  • Naidaijin Bridge

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Arch bridge at Naidaijin Gorge, 86 meters high and 199.5 meters long, once called the East's greatest.

  • Katashida Castle Ruins

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-46-2038

    Aso mountain castle first recorded in 1523, fallen to the Shimazu in 1585. National historic site.

  • Shimofukura Rice Terraces

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Layered paddies in Horadake follow the rural terrain. Flooded fields mirror sunset and colors change by season.

  • Shiraishino Rice Terraces

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Near the September equinox, red spider lilies contrast with golden rice, attracting tourists and photographers.

  • Toshine Bridge

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-46-2038

    Misato Town cultural property, the town's newest recorded stone arch bridge: 60 meters long and 24 meters high.

  • First Tsuru River Bridge Piers

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Impressive Yuen Railway piers about 400 meters past the Octagonal Tunnel, on the old line to Tomochi.

  • Octagonal Tunnel

    Misatomachi, Kumamoto · 0964-47-1111

    Yuen Railway rockfall shelter remaining after the 1964 closure, a mysterious forest photo spot.
