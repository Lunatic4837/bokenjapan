Things to do in Mizukami, Kumamoto

Mizukami · Kumamoto · 6 places

Do

  • Ichifusa-an Naruo

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-46-0221

    Mizukami inn with silky free-flowing alkaline sulfur onsen from 160 meters down; weekend day baths and seasonal cuisine.

  • Yukidomari Quiet Inn Hyoman-tei

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-46-0122

    Self-catering inn providing rice and vegetables; firewood-cooked pot rice is the treat, and a cypress bath refreshes you.

  • Inari's Inn Yuan

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0229

    Deep-mountain Mizukami inn: cook rice in a kamado, soak in a firewood Goemon bath, and grill yamame at the hearth.

  • Yuyama Onsen Motoyu

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-46-0555

    Ancient 100 percent free-flowing spring below Mount Ichifusa, said to have flowed since the Yayoi period.

  • Asenohara Waterfront Park

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0312

    Park at the entrance to Yuyama Onsen shopping street, with lawn, streams, and spring cherries.

  • Mount Ichifusa Campground (outsideBASE mizukami)

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-46-0768

    Kumamoto's largest campground, in Kyushu Central Mountains Quasi-National Park. Log houses, bungalows, tent sites, and rentals.
