Sights in Mizukami, Kumamoto

Mizukami · Kumamoto · 12 places

See

  • Ichifusa Lake Great Fountain

    Mizukami, Kumamoto

    An 80-meter fountain sprays about 10 minutes and can form a rainbow in suitable weather.

  • Ichifusa Thousand-Year Cedars

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0312

    About 50 cedars on the shrine path include 50-meter trees roughly 1,000 years old.

  • Kawauchi District, Mizukami Village

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0312

    Around the autumn equinox, spider lilies dye both banks of the Ogouchi River red.

  • Shozen-in (Cat Temple)

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0068

    Built to pacify a cat spirit, with gate cats and a national Important Cultural Property Kannon hall.

  • Cherry Encyclopedia Garden

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0312

    About 70 varieties and 100 cherry trees, best early March to early May, with rapeseed flowers.

  • Ichifusa Dam

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0304

    Multipurpose dam 78.5 meters high, famous for about 10,000 cherry trees around the lake.

  • Ryusen-ji Kannon

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0312

    No. 24 of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage; its hall was moved here in 1904.

  • Shiramizu Falls Suspension Bridges (Hakuryuhi Bridge and Hakuryuo Bridge)

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0314

    Kumamoto's longest suspension bridges span paired falls near the Miyazaki border, one with acrylic flooring 150 meters above.

  • Mount Ichifusa

    Mizukami, Kumamoto · 0966-44-0314

    1,722-meter mountain called Otake-san, with autumn views from the summit and huge cedars said to be 1,000 years old.

  • Ichifusayama Jingu Main Shrine

    Mizukami, Kumamoto

    Shrine to Ichifusayama, Hitoyoshi-Kuma's highest peak, at the fourth station. Sagara clan and locals often made Otakesan pilgrimages.

  • Ichifusayama Jingu Middle Shrine (Ichinomiya Shrine)

    Mizukami, Kumamoto

    Enshrines a Kirishima Jingu deity on the 807 temporary-hall site from Ichifusayama Jingu's construction.

  • Ichifusa Cedars

    Mizukami, Kumamoto

    Cedars on sacred Mount Ichifusa, said 1,000 years old, trunks several meters around, a mystical solemn landscape.
