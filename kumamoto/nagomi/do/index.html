Do in Nagomi

Nagomi · Kumamoto · 5 places

Do

  • Mikawa Onsen Nagomi no Yu

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-2249

    Alkaline simple onsen, pH 10.09, with smooth water, large, open-air and family baths, plus a product hall.

  • Kuriyama Onsen Beni Sanzashi

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-4233

    Mountain onsen whose 13 private rooms each have a different open-air bath, including cypress, tea-bowl, and 200-year camphor baths.

  • Nagomi Eta River Canoe Campground

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-82-8414

    Campground along the Eta River offering beginner lessons and a 4 km, about three-hour canoe trip upstream.

  • Yutei Jogen no Tsuki

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-42-4126

    A private-bath onsen in Nagomi's Mikawa Onsen area, with 13 rooms each having indoor and outdoor baths.

  • Shichirogami Grand Festival

    Nagomi, Kumamoto

    Each early-April Sunday, locals dedicate a portable shrine shaped like male genitalia, praying for harvests.
