Sights in Nagomi, Kumamoto

Nagomi · Kumamoto · 12 places

See

  • Higo Folk House Village

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-86-4564

    Open-air museum restoring seven old folk houses, with a history museum, art museum, and paid hands-on experiences.

  • Ear Deity (Grave of Yufu Oisuke)

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-5725

    Tomb of Yufu Oisuke, worshipped as the Ear Deity; fire-blowing bamboo is offered and used in worship.

  • Eye Deity

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-5725

    Believed to grant health and family safety by drinking spring water, and especially effective for eye disease.

  • Tooth Deity

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-5725

    Tooth-shaped gravestone worshipped as a rare toothache deity; white sand or rice is offered when teeth ache.

  • Shichirojin, Deity of Sex and the Waist

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-5725

    Deity of fertility, safe birth, and marital harmony, enshrined for spreading farming techniques.

  • Life Deity

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-5725

    A stone worshipped as a life-saving deity; one prayer in a lifetime for critical illness is said to be granted.

  • Stomach Deity

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-5725

    Hilltop stone shrine said to restore vigor to people with weak stomachs; visiting is impossible due to the Kumamoto Earthquake.

  • Tateyama Hand and Foot Deity

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-5725

    Said to heal limb pain and quicken the feet; a divided spirit of Kashima's deity, kept in its original state.

  • Tanaka Castle Ruins

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-3047

    Medieval castle of Wani Chikazane, called among Kumamoto's largest of its era; now a park and National Historic Site.

  • Tonkararin

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-3047

    Five stone tunnels of differing structure and size, named for the sound of stones falling; purpose unknown.

  • Eta Funayama Kofun

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 0968-34-3047

    Large keyhole tomb from the late 5th century; its chamber can be viewed, and all 92 artifacts are National Treasures.

  • Omusubi Library

    Nagomi, Kumamoto · 090-9655-6322

    Library in Higo Minka Village's relocated old houses, selling rice flour, sweets, coffee, and donuts.
