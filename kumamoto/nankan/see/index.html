Sights in Nankan, Kumamoto

Nankan · Kumamoto · 4 places

See

  • Kanaguri Shizo Road

    Nankan, Kumamoto

    Twelve-kilometer round-trip school road of Japan's marathon father Kanaguri Shizo, with its original landscape remaining.

  • Old Shodai Village Park

    Nankan, Kumamoto · 0968-53-1111

    Birthplace of Shodai ware with preserved Edo kiln sites, 150 plum trees, and a mid-March pottery and plum festival.

  • Firefly Village Park

    Nankan, Kumamoto · 0968-53-1111

    Late May fireflies can be seen around Biwase River, a selected firefly site with a viewing plaza, toilets, and parking.

  • Nankan Teahouse Ruins on Buzen Kaido

    Nankan, Kumamoto · 0968-57-8507

    Former daimyo rest house completed in 1852, a national historic site with a pond park and autumn maples.
