Do in Nishihara

Nishihara · Kumamoto · 9 places

Do

  • Rakuno Mothers Aso Milk Farm

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 096-292-2100

    Dairy farm with milking, animal contact, sausage and bread making, a milk shop, and autumn cosmos.

  • Mount Tawarayama

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 096-279-3111

    1,095-meter Aso outer-rim peak spanning Nishihara and Minamiaso. Looks like a rice bale; popular for trekking.

  • Kanmurigatake

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 096-279-3111

    1,154m crown-like mountain west of the south outer rim. Fine trails, good spring and autumn summit views.

  • Drive-in Theater Aso

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 080-6401-5560

    Drive-in theater amid Aso's grand nature, watched from the car with sound via FM radio.

  • Nishihara Village Youth Forest Kaze no Sato Campground

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 096-279-2884

    Campground about 50 minutes from Kumamoto City for solo or family camping, with a 70m slide and observatory views.

  • Takayubaru Plane Campers

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 070-9139-1692

    Campsite 30 minutes from central Kumamoto near the airport, with woodland and ground sites; opening planned July 2023.

  • Aso Maron no Ki Auto Campground

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 096-279-1588

    Auto campground beside the dog-run restaurant Aso Maron no Ki, with dog-free sites and a cafe.

  • Rakuno Mothers Aso Milk Farm

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 096-292-2100

    Aso milk farm at 430 meters offering cow milking, animal contact, and handmade butter, cheese, and ice cream.

  • Lassie Inn

    Nishihara, Kumamoto · 096-279-4879

    Whole-building rental for two or more, about 10 minutes by car from Kumamoto Airport; balcony BBQ even in rain.
