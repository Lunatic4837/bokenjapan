Do

    Onsen next to Nishiki Town Hall for all ages and disabilities. Weak-alkaline simple spring with low-frequency, bubble, and jet baths.

  • Nishiki Kurando Park

    6.5-hectare park with lawns, a play area, koi pond, statues, a roadside station, and produce shop.

    Nishiki, Kumamoto · 0966-38-4419 · Official / source link

    The only onsen campground in Hitoyoshi-Kuma, with varied auto sites, Wi-Fi, and pets allowed.

    Sengoku swordsmanship founded by Sagara instructor Marume Kurando no Suke Nagayoshi, later learned by domain samurai.

    Nishiki, Kumamoto · Official / source link
