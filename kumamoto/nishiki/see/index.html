Sights in Nishiki, Kumamoto

Nishiki · Kumamoto · 7 places

See

  • Tsuchiya Kannon

    Nishiki, Kumamoto · 0966-38-4419

    31st Sagara 33 Kannon stop, enshrining wooden Kamakura Sho-Kannon and late Heian Jizo statues.

  • Nishiki Secret Base Museum

    Nishiki, Kumamoto · 0966-28-8080

    Museum at the former Hitoyoshi Naval Air Base, showing history panels, relics, and Zero fighter remains, plus underground tours.

  • Shinguzen-ji

    Nishiki, Kumamoto · 0966-38-0251

    Southernmost Obaku Zen temple in Japan, with Six Kannon statues and autumn foliage.

  • Kimoto Shrine

    Nishiki, Kumamoto

    On Hirakawa Iwanjo castle ruins; 13th lord Sagara Nagatsune enshrined Ichifusa Gongen's divided spirit here.

  • Iwanjo Castle Ruins

    Nishiki, Kumamoto

    Local Hirakawa clan castle; after losing to the Sagara at Chishikibaru they took their lives inside.

  • Arata Daio Shrine

    Nishiki, Kumamoto

    Late Edo shrine to Hirakawa Uemon Yoshitaka, said to have slain village vipers; prayer said to heal bites.

  • Shaka Nyorai Statue of Arata Kannon Hall

    Nishiki, Kumamoto

    Late Heian wooden seated Shaka Nyorai in Arata Kannon Hall, with an inner inscription dated Hoen 7.
