Sights in Oguni, Kumamoto

Oguni · Kumamoto · 10 places

See

  • Amida Cedar

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-2111

    Kumamoto's largest cedar, over 1,300 years old and designated a national natural monument.

  • Shimojo Great Ginkgo

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-2113

    The prefecture's largest ginkgo, over 1,000 years old, a national natural monument lit up in autumn.

  • Keyaki Spring

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-2111

    Spring near Oguni Ryo Shrine, welling from the roots of a zelkova said to be 1,000 years old.

  • Oguni Ryo Shrine

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-32-8535

    Shrine enshrining two deities who pioneered Oguni. July Gion Festival and a 250-year-old autumn festival with a procession.

  • Kitasato Shibasaburo Memorial Museum

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-5466

    Museum at Kitasato Shibasaburo's birthplace, preserving part of his birth home, a visiting residence, guest house, and library.

  • Ichii no Mori Art Museum

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-5682

    Art museum in an oak forest, in a striking 50-meter single-story building founded by Fukuoka painter Yoshimura Ikuo.

  • Sakamoto Zenzo Art Museum

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-5732

    Museum in Oguni displaying works of abstract painter Sakamoto Zenzo, grand prize winner at a Paris print exhibition.

  • Nabegataki Falls Park

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-4440

    Popular falls about 10 meters high and 20 meters wide, formed by Aso's eruption about 90,000 years ago.

  • Shimojo Falls

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-2111

    Falls 28 meters high and 10 meters wide near the nationally designated Shimojo giant ginkgo, with a trail above.

  • Kagami Pond

    Oguni, Kumamoto · 0967-46-2111

    Clear spring along the approach to Oguni Ryo Shrine, welling from the roots of a large zelkova.
