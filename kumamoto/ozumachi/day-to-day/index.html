    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 096-285-5573

    Owner-roasted coffee shop with popular beans, handmade sweets from Japanese ingredients, and seasonal jam.

  • Musubiya En

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 080-8563-6650

    200-year-old rice-ball farmhouse cafe; popular sets use Kikuchi rice, Tsujishima salt, Ariake seaweed and pork soup.

  • Edo Yashiki Kakinoha Sushi Shop

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 096-293-9191

    Renovated 186-year-old house selling persimmon-leaf sushi with mackerel, shrimp, and salmon.

  • Restaurant Cafe Iroha

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 096-283-9000

    Cafe attached to a Tenpo 3 old house, with lunch and shaved ice. Same-day reservations fill immediately.

  • Sweet Potato Kitchen Car MAIMOTTO

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 080-3376-4466

    Kitchen car using Beniharuka sweet potatoes, offering various sweets besides baked sweet potatoes.

  • Karaimoya-san

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 096-294-1618

    Wagashi shop in Roadside Station Ozu on Route 57, using Ozu sweet potatoes for dango and taiyaki.
