Do in Ozumachi

Ozumachi · Kumamoto · 4 places

Do

  • Enman-ji Kiln

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 090-3197-4941

    Kiln making varied ceramics, including clay with wood or cloth textures; pottery experience available.

  • HSR Kyushu (Honda Safety and Riding Plaza Kyushu)

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 096-293-1370

    Facility inside Honda's Kumamoto factory with on-road and off-road courses, plus bike and course rentals.

  • Hatago Hashimoto

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 096-293-2141

    Inn with relaxing tatami rooms, large baths alternating by gender, a local handmade buffet, many outlets, and Wi-Fi.

  • Mova Villa Ozu

    Ozumachi, Kumamoto · 070-8958-5160

    Private villa in Ozu at the foot of Aso's outer rim, for 4-6, five minutes by car from JASM.
