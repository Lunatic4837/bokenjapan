Things to do in Reihoku, Kumamoto

Reihoku · Kumamoto · 3 places

Do

  • Reihoku Town Onsen Center Rinsen no Yu

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-3770

    Onsen center viewing Chijiwa-nada and Amakusa-nada, with floating, bubble, sauna, and family baths; silky alkaline simple spring.

  • Tomioka Beach

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Outstanding clarity on Amakusa-nada, a 2006 Japan 100 best beach, with about 300m of calm shore.

  • Shiraiwasaki Campground

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-31-1136

    Campground on Tomioka Peninsula in Unzen-Amakusa National Park, offering Amakusa-nada sunsets and tent rental.
