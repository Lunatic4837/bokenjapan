Sights in Reihoku, Kumamoto

Reihoku · Kumamoto · 9 places

See

  • Mt. Tenjiku

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Highest peak on Amakusa Shimoshima at 538m, views of Yatsushiro and Nomozaki, and an April azalea festival.

  • Tomioka Castle Park

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Built in 1604 by Terasawa Hirotaka as Amakusa's base; a sea-bound natural fortress also called Garyu Castle.

  • Oppai Rock

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Rock thought formed by seawater, shaped like a breast, 1.5m across, and visible only at low tide.

  • Tomioka Marine Park Observatory

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Observatory overlooking Japan's first underwater park, with clear water, coral, fish, reefs, and sand spits.

  • Shikisaki Cape Park

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-3332

    Park at Tomioka Peninsula's tip with seasonal wildflowers, a white lighthouse, and an observation deck.

  • Shiki Castle Ruins

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Said built after 1205 by Shiki Mitsuhiro; ruined in 1589 by Konishi and Kato forces, now a park.

  • Amakusa Shiro Embarkation Site

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Said boarding place of Amakusa Shiro's rebels bound again for Shimabara after failing to take Tomioka Castle.

  • Shiraiwasaki Coast

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-1111

    Pure white coast of Amakusa pottery stone, contrasting with deep blue sea and orange sunsets.

  • Reihoku Town History Museum

    Reihoku, Kumamoto · 0969-35-0712

    Museum in Tomioka Castle Park covering Tomioka Castle and the Amakusa-Shimabara Rebellion.
