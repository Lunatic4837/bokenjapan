Sights in Sagara, Kumamoto

Sagara · Kumamoto · 8 places

See

  • Amemiya Shrine

    Sagara, Kumamoto · 0966-35-1034

    Hilltop rain-praying shrine in 35-meter forest called Totoro's Forest, with a giant stone tunnel.

  • Fukami Kannon

    Sagara, Kumamoto · 0966-35-1030

    No. 18 of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, prayed to for water and as the tooth god.

  • Minomo Kannon

    Sagara, Kumamoto · 0966-35-1030

    No. 15 of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, where people turning 60 pray for longevity.

  • Toshima Kannon

    Sagara, Kumamoto · 0966-35-1030

    No. 14 of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, enshrining Sho-Kannon in a hall built in 1995.

  • Kamizono Kannon

    Sagara, Kumamoto · 0966-35-1030

    No. 17 of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, said to protect children, cattle, and horses from illness.

  • Mawari Kannon

    Sagara, Kumamoto · 0966-35-1030

    No. 18 of the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, long known as the god of moles.

  • Toshima Sugawara Shrine

    Sagara, Kumamoto

    Shrine to Sugawara no Michizane, god of learning, the area's largest Tenjin; named for ten islands in its pond.

  • Izawa Kumanoza Shrine

    Sagara, Kumamoto

    Sung in Kuma no Rokuchoshi, near the Kawabe-Kuma confluence; built by Hirakawa and restored by Sagara Yoshishige.
