Day-to-day services in Takamori, Kumamoto

Takamori · Kumamoto · 12 places

Day to day

  • Takamori Dengaku Preservation Society

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-0234

    Originator of Takamori dengaku, charcoal-grilled hearth cuisine served in a Meiji-era old house.

  • Takamori Dengaku Village

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1899

    Charcoal-grilled local dengaku in a 200-year-old farmhouse, with Takamori taro and views of Nekodake.

  • Tokumaru Restaurant

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-0096

    Eatery of 50-year pickle shop Tokumaru, serving a pickle lunch with half-milled multigrain rice and varied pickles.

  • Grill de Kato

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1020

    Curry shaped like symbol Mt. Nekodake, using red beef and specialty tomatoes, won the Aso Geopark brand top prize.

  • Yamayasu

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-0888

    Specialty restaurant for traceable Aso akaushi beef, including steak and beef bowls.

  • Yamamura Sake Brewery

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-0001

    Kumamoto's oldest sake brewery, founded in 1762 and famous for Reizan; brewery tours and tastings by reservation.

  • Aso Cafe Sosui

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1771

    Takamori cafe serving five-hour drip coffee, just four cups, plus limited tomato roll cake.

  • Daiaso Blueberry Farm Asoburu

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-2030

    Small cafe in a blueberry tourist farm, open mid-March to early November, serving farm blueberry soft serve and drinks.

  • Aso Cafe SOSUI

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1771

    Nordic-style cafe with creative sweets using local ingredients, including fruit shortcake, moist Aso brandy cake, and summer shaved ice.

  • Tanoshika-tei

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1188

    Renovated old house opposite Reizan brewery; three local women serve traditional kaitenyaki, plus okonomiyaki, gyoza, takoyaki, and summer shaved ice.

  • Nantetsu Cafe

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1373

    Cafe at Takamori Station serving Aso-milk lattes, banana smoothies, and light meals, plus monthly markets and night events.

  • Fukumame

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1200

    Takamori shop with gently sweet bean sweets and Japanese confectionery, good for snacks or gifts.
