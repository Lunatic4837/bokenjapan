Do in Takamori

Do

  • Kyukamura Minamiaso

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-2111

    Hotel in Aso-Kuju National Park facing the five Aso peaks, with skin-friendly private-source onsen Shikimi no Yu.

  • Takamori Spring Water Tunnel Park

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-3331

    Former railway tunnel, 2055m long, with 32 tons of spring water flowing per minute.

  • Bluegrass Horseback Riding

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-3366

    Western-style riding club in Aso offering lessons, trail rides, lodging, and camps.

  • Tsukimawari Park

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1628

    Viewpoint of Aso mountains with lawns, putt golf, go-karts, and small animals.

  • Minamiaso Railway

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-0058

    17.7 km railway inside the Aso caldera, including a slow windowless trolley train.

  • Reshuku Forest Brick Hall

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-3158

    Quiet green inn limited to four rooms a day, serving seasonal French-based full-course meals.

  • Minamiaso Fureai Zoo Fairytale

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-2613

    Small Takamori zoo on a former cattle barn site, with about 25 species and 200 animals you can touch.

  • Okuaso Fruit Garden

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 090-3197-3898

    Tourist farm at 800 meters offering nearly year-round picking of about 20 varieties.
