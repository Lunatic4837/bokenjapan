Sights in Takamori, Kumamoto

Takamori · Kumamoto · 11 places

See

  • Takamori Pass Thousand Cherry Trees

    Takamori, Kumamoto

    Varied cherry trees line this pass, with a summit panorama of Aso's five peaks in spring.

  • Mount Nekodake

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1111

    1433-meter peak named for a catlike summit, oldest of the five Aso volcanoes and symbol of Takamori.

  • Takamori Pass

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1111

    Mountain pass.

  • Anasako Inari Shrine

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1111

    About 400-year-old shrine revered for cattle and horses, with a lunar February festival.

  • Takamori Tono no Sugi

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1111

    Giant cedar pair over 400 years old along the Kyushu Nature Trail, called couple cedars, girth over 10m.

  • Ugetoiwa

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-2913

    Large wind cave behind Kamishikimi Kumanoza Shrine, reputed for passing exams and victory.

  • Upside-Down Nekodake

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1111

    Reflection of cat-shaped Nekodake, a Takamori symbol and Aso Five Peak, visible only in spring when paddies are flooded.

  • Kamishikimi Kumanoza Shrine

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-2300

    A shrine with 97 stone lanterns along the approach and a huge 10-meter wind cave called Ugetoiwa beyond the hall.

  • Franky Statue

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1111

    Shipwright Franky statue in Takamori, installed in fiscal 2020, cheering Minamiaso Railway's full reopening.

  • Kamishikimi Kumanoimasu Shrine

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-2300

    Setting of the anime film Into the Forest of Fireflies' Light; enshrines Izanagi, Izanami, and Ishigimi Taishogun.

  • Takamori Spring Water Tunnel

    Takamori, Kumamoto · 0967-62-1111

    Unfinished railway tunnel now open as a waterfront park, with 36 tons of spring water per minute.
