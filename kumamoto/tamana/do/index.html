Things to do in Tamana, Kumamoto

Tamana · Kumamoto · 18 places

Do

  • Kusamakura Onsen Tensui

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-82-4500

    Hilltop onsen among mandarin orchards, with baths overlooking the Ariake Sea and Mount Unzen Fugen.

  • Tamana Onsen

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-74-2961

    Weakly alkaline radium simple onsen near Hino Shrine, flowing for 1,300 years after a white-heron healing legend.

  • Shogen Sanso

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-2266

    Healing inn with a seasonal Japanese garden, Tamana Onsen, and kaiseki; all 12 rooms overlook the garden.

  • Mountain Maple Inn Happoen

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-2161

    Century-old luxury ryokan in Tamana Onsen amid autumn leaves; every room has a semi-open-air bath and in-room meals.

  • Satsuki Inn

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-3157

    Hilltop Tamana Onsen inn with unheated free-flowing spring water, a large flower garden, and four room types.

  • Kinpoen

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-2501

    Small Tamana onsen ryokan with round-the-clock free-flowing baths, family baths, and a spacious group room.

  • Oama Onsen Nagoikan

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-82-2035

    Oama Onsen ryokan founded in Meiji 1, tied to Soseki's Kusamakura, renovated in 2018 retaining late-Edo beams.

  • Jagatani Park

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-5655

    Large park near Tamana Onsen with sports grounds, a lookout, a 130-meter slide, cherries, and azaleas.

  • Mizumoto Orange Garden

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-82-2126

    Mikan orchard over 120 years old, with seasonal citrus picking and barbecue facilities.

  • Mount Shodai Hometown Nature Park

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-73-3900

    Recreation area on Mount Shodai with camping, hiking trails, picnic grounds, and views of Ariake Sea.

  • Genyama Observation Park

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-75-1122

    Hilltop park at 198 meters with play equipment, lawns, flowers, and views of the Ariake Sea.

  • Okunoin Giant Tea Ceremony

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-74-3533

    Tea ritual using a 40-cm bowl weighing about 6 kg, transmitted from Saidaiji in Nara.

  • Tamana Onsen Tsukasa-no-Yu

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-7777

    Among western Japan's largest onsen resorts, with cypress and family baths, a mildly alkaline simple spring and women's salt sauna.

  • Tamana Flower Park

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-51-2727

    Hilltop flower farm, free to enter, with 400 varieties, BBQ, camping, and wide views.

  • Nagoikan Bettei Tamayura Family Bath

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-71-2615

    Family bath opened in 2022 in Tamana Onsen, with 10 free-flowing rooms and Finnish saunas in five.

  • Private Spa Kurage

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-79-7726

    Family bath east of Tamana Onsen with seven indoor-only rooms, free-flowing spring, and a large VIP room.

  • Tamana Onsen Family Bath Jun no Toki Yugan

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-73-1126

    Family bath in Tamana Onsen with fresh water each visit; all 10 different rooms have indoor and outdoor baths.

  • Kusamakura Sanso Campsite

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-82-4500

    Campsite on Kusamakura Onsen Tensui grounds, on terraced mandarin fields with Unzen sunsets, toilets, and a private sauna.
