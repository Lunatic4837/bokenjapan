Sights in Tamana, Kumamoto

Tamana · Kumamoto · 15 places

See

  • Maeda Family Villa

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-82-4511

    Retreat of Meiji politician Maeda Kakashi, setting of Soseki's Kusamakura; the detached room and bath remain.

  • Rengein Tanjo-ji Okunoin

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-74-3533

    Shingon Ritsu temple enshrining Koen Daibosatsu, known for the world's largest bell, Hiryu no Kane.

  • Tamana City History Museum Kokoro Pia

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-74-3989

    Museum on Tamana's development along the river, with exhibits and 16 huge rooftop display towers.

  • Takase Spectacles Bridge

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-75-1136

    Double-arch bridge 19 meters long, built in 1848 and designated a prefectural historic site.

  • Tamana Observatory

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-75-1314

    Observatory in Momoda Sports Park overlooking the city, Kikuchi River, Mount Shodai, and Ariake Sea.

  • Kofuku-ji

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-74-9011

    Temple founded in 1357 by Kikuchi Takesumi; its old documents are national Important Cultural Properties.

  • Tamana Daijingu

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-74-1955

    Shrine first dedicated to Tamayorihime, later also enshrining Aso's twelve gods, Emperor Keiko, and Amaterasu.

  • Daibo Kofun

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-75-1136

    Decorated keyhole tomb over 40 meters long with painted interior; a national Important Cultural Property.

  • Yamada Wisteria (Yamada Hiyoshi Shrine)

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-5313

    Prefectural natural monument: estimated 200-year-old wisteria with pale purple blooms from mid-April to early May.

  • Uragawa Waterside Green Park

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-5313

    Park with the 1848 Takase Spectacles Bridge and stone bridges, and about 6,300 Higo irises blooming in June.

  • Firefly Village

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 090-1875-7143

    Fireflies dance at the southern foot of Mt. Shodai from mid-May to early June.

  • Kusamakura Exchange Hall

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-82-4511

    Museum on Natsume Soseki's Kusamakura and Maeda villa, closed for renovation until February 20, 2026.

  • Shizo Kanakuri Residence and Museum

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-57-7548

    Home in Oda, Tamana, where Shizo Kanakuri and wife Suya lived, showing their belongings.

  • Taimei Kaishoro

    Tamana, Kumamoto

    A low-tide-only road into the Ariake Sea toward Unzen, lined with utility poles; sunsets are recommended.

  • Hikino Shrine

    Tamana, Kumamoto · 0968-72-2917

    Near Tamana Onsen, an Engishiki shrine with the prefecture's largest white torii and spring water to bottle.
