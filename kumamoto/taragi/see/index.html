Sights in Taragi, Kumamoto

Taragi · Kumamoto · 9 places

See

  • Nakayama Kannon

    Taragi, Kumamoto · 0966-42-1267

    28th Sagara 33 Kannon stop. Sho-Kannon and Four Heavenly Kings are estimated Heian works and prefectural cultural properties.

  • Seizan Kannon

    Taragi, Kumamoto · 0966-42-1267

    23rd Sagara 33 Kannon stop up 108 steps. Thousand-armed Kannon, largest in Kuma County, is a prefectural important cultural property.

  • Shoren-ji

    Taragi, Kumamoto · 0966-42-2637

    Founded in 1298 by Sagara Yorimune. Thatched Amida hall shows Kamakura style; Amida triad is a national important cultural property.

  • Ogu Shrine Tower Gate

    Taragi, Kumamoto · 0966-42-2529

    Said founded in 1416 by Sagara Yorihisa. Kumamoto's oldest Chinese-style thatched tower gate is a prefectural important cultural property.

  • Taragi Town Cultural Properties Center Kodai no Kaze Kuro no Kura

    Taragi, Kumamoto · 0966-42-7520

    Restored 1942 tuff rice storehouse displaying unusual stone tools, pottery, and obsidian.

  • Ota Family Residence

    Taragi, Kumamoto

    Said built in late Edo, with hook-shaped expansion for space under strict rules. Shochu production recorded at era's end.

  • Taragi Sagara Clan Historic Sites

    Taragi, Kumamoto

    After entering, the Sagara main family was the Taragi Sagara clan from Kamakura to mid-Muromachi. Residence and irrigation sites remain.

  • Kume Haruyori Shrine

    Taragi, Kumamoto

    Said built in the Sengoku period to memorialize exiled rebel Sagara Haruyori and his mother.

  • Kobo Daishi Statue at Tsukinoki Daishi-do

    Taragi, Kumamoto

    A hall in mountains on the Miyazaki border. Early Muromachi Kobo Daishi statue commissioned by the Sagara clan.
