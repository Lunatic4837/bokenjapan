Sights in Tsunagi, Kumamoto

Tsunagi · Kumamoto · 6 places

See

  • Juban Rock

    Tsunagi, Kumamoto · 0966-78-3111

    Odd rock at the town center on medieval Tsunagi Castle ruins. Reportedly Kato Kiyomasa's lookout; Japanese flag on top.

  • Maizuru Castle Park Observatory

    Tsunagi, Kumamoto · 0966-78-3111

    Observatory on medieval Tsunagi Castle ruins atop Juban Rock. Views of Tsunagi and the Shiranui Sea; sculpture Boka on display.

  • Tsunagi Art Museum

    Tsunagi, Kumamoto · 0966-61-2222

    Town-run art museum in Tsunagi, a hub for local art projects and artist residencies.

  • Tsunagi Castle Ruins

    Tsunagi, Kumamoto · 0966-78-3111

    Castle ruins on a hill about 130 meters high in central Tsunagi, also called Maizuru Castle.

  • Tatsubutsu

    Tsunagi, Kumamoto · 0966-61-2222

    Thirty-three Buddha statues carved from living trees by artist Tatsu Nishino in a Tsunagi forest.

  • Former Akasaki Elementary School

    Tsunagi, Kumamoto · 0966-78-3111

    Featured as Japan's only school on the sea; Hadakajima is walkable at low tide, with sunset silhouettes.
