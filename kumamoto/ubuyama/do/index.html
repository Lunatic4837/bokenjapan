Things to do in Ubuyama, Kumamoto

Ubuyama · Kumamoto · 6 places

Do

  • Yamanami Oku-Aso Inn

    Ubuyama, Kumamoto · 0967-25-2414

    Ubuyama Village inn with pure free-flowing spring water from about 1000 meters and homemade country cooking.

  • Oyubune Onsenkan

    Ubuyama, Kumamoto · 0967-25-2654

    Quiet mountain hot spring with low fees and sulfur sodium-magnesium bicarbonate water, plus self-catering lodging Yasuragi.

  • Ubuyama Place

    Ubuyama, Kumamoto · 0967-25-2029

    Renewed in July 2024, with grassland views of Kuju, Aso, and Sobo, plus park golf.

  • Higotai Park

    Ubuyama, Kumamoto · 0967-25-2777

    Ubuyama wildflower park with seasonal flowers, mountain views, camping, trails, and a river pool.

  • Ubuyama Farm Village

    Ubuyama, Kumamoto · 0967-25-2210

    Complex of lodges, auto camping, and rental fields, overlooking Aso's five peaks and Kuju.

  • Shikicho Mountain Lodge

    Ubuyama, Kumamoto · 0967-25-3232

    Small four-room plateau guesthouse at 850 meters; exclusive for six or more, with optional horse sashimi and red beef.
