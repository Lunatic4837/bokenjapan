See in Uki

Uki · Kumamoto · 13 places

See

  • Misumi West Port

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-53-0010

    One of three great Meiji ports, opened in 1887 and listed as World Cultural Heritage in 2015.

  • Matsuai Local History Museum

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-42-3560

    Displays relics of locals including playwright Ken Miyamoto and clairvoyant Chizuko Mifune, plus merchant goods and Shiranui materials.

  • Ishiuchi Dam Museum

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-53-1111

    Modern museum beside Ishiuchi Dam. A 4.4m hall on dams and water use; a floating cylindrical lobby overlooks the dam.

  • Ogawa Aso Shrine

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-43-1111

    Founded in 1040 from Aso Shrine. City natural-monument camphor, about 1,300 years, 35m tall, 13m around.

  • Moshigi Monju-do

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-32-1111

    Hall of learning deity Manjusri, called the West's Dazaifu. Busy in exam season; giant November-yellow ginkgo beside a red torii.

  • Einootsurugi Shrine

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-32-1604

    Said founded in 713 for Wadatsumi. Stomach benefits, stingray plaques, and a sea torii whose appearance changes with tides.

  • Odara Kofun

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-32-1111

    Fifth-century decorated tomb, reburied. Remaining 2m screen has circles, quivers, and shields; replica at the prefectural museum.

  • Shiranui Mysterious Fire

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-32-1111

    Mirage on the Shiranui Sea on lunar August 1, designated a national scenic site. Crowds gather yearly at Eino Shrine.

  • Matsuai White-Walled Storehouse District

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-42-3560

    Historic white-walled storehouse streets remain in Matsuai, built against fires in a dense port town.

  • Ukiuki Toyono Sunflower Garden

    Uki, Kumamoto · 080-1771-0183

    Sunflower field suddenly appears in calm scenery, grown six years by locals to revitalize Toyono, Uki. Still a hidden spot.

  • Law Hall (Former Misumi Summary Court)

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-53-1111

    Former summary court in World Heritage Misumi West Port, open as Law Hall; currently closed after the earthquake.

  • Omi Ishidatami Park

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-32-1111

    Scenic spot where a stream flows over a 150-meter lava pavement, popular for hiking and summer retreats.

  • Koonoura Tenjin Camphor Tree

    Uki, Kumamoto · 0964-32-1111

    Prefectural natural monument, a roughly 1,000-year-old camphor about 23 meters tall, enshrined as a sacred tree.
