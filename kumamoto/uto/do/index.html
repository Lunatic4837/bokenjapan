Things to do in Uto, Kumamoto

Uto · Kumamoto · 5 places

Do

  • Ajisai no Yu

    Uto, Kumamoto · 0964-24-3456

    Natural alkaline simple spring from 900 m underground near JR Sumiyoshi. Large bath, sauna, jet bath, and 52-mat hall.

  • Business Inn Komeya

    Uto, Kumamoto · 0964-22-0261

    Japanese-modern hotel with study-like rooms and home-cooked meals for business or long stays.

  • Tatsuoka Nature Park

    Uto, Kumamoto · 0964-22-1111

    About 2000 cherry trees ring ponds dug by Kato Kiyomasa, with a sports center and campground.

  • Sumiyoshi Nature Park

    Uto, Kumamoto · 0964-22-1111

    Scenic corner of Misumi-Oyano Seaside Prefectural Park. About 2,000 hydrangeas; mid-June Sunday mandolin concert and fair.

  • Sodo Kiln

    Uto, Kumamoto · 0964-27-0777

    Kiln that presses wild plants onto flat ceramic plates, transferring their forms, then colors the works.
