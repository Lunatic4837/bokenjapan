Sights in Yamae, Kumamoto

Yamae · Kumamoto · 8 places

See

  • Mae River and Yamada River Area

    Yamae, Kumamoto · 0966-23-3112

    Fireflies dance in the quiet Mae and Yamada river basins from mid-May to early June.

  • Maruoka Park

    Yamae, Kumamoto · 0966-23-3112

    Panoramic view of the Hitoyoshi-Kuma Basin, with 300 cherries, 50,000 azaleas, an April azalea festival, and a campsite.

  • Yamada Daio Shrine

    Yamae, Kumamoto · 0966-23-3665

    Three halls and one stone torii are national Important Cultural Properties; the shrine was built in 1405.

  • Kassenmine Kannon

    Yamae, Kumamoto · 0966-23-3604

    12th stop on the Sagara 33 Kannon pilgrimage, built in 1198 and said to aid competitions.

  • Koji-in

    Yamae, Kumamoto · 0966-23-3051

    Oldest temple in Kuma District, with 365 steps, eight Bishamonten images, and an Azuchi-Momoyama wooden pagoda.

  • Yamae Village History and Folklore Museum

    Yamae, Kumamoto · 0966-23-3665

    Museum using local cedar, showing excavated stone tools, pottery, folk tools, and Ikkoshu paintings.

  • Eleven-Faced Kannon Statue of Shiroyama Kannon-do

    Yamae, Kumamoto

    Eleven-Faced Kannon commissioned by a Sengoku Sagara lord, cherished for generations, permanently shown at Yamae Village museum.

  • Nagatome Sagara Clan Castle Group

    Yamae, Kumamoto

    Yamada Castle and Takajo, built by Nagatome Sagara descendants of Sagara Yorichika before moving to Hitoyoshi Castle.
