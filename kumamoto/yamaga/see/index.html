Sights in Yamaga, Kumamoto

Yamaga · Kumamoto · 20 places

See

  • Kumamoto Prefectural Decorated Tumulus Museum

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-36-2151

    Japan's only prefectural museum specializing in decorated tombs, designed by Tadao Ando, with finds and workshops.

  • Yamaga Lantern Folk Craft Museum

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-43-1152

    Built in 1925 on the Buzen highway, showing Yamaga lanterns and Yamaga Onsen history, with a wheelchair ramp.

  • Yachiyoza and Yumegura

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-44-4004

    Working playhouse built in 1910 and a national Important Cultural Property; Yumegura displays its costumes and props.

  • Takema Gorge

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-32-3111

    Gorge with unusual named rocks, waterfalls including Kinbara Falls, trails, a suspension bridge, and a campground.

  • Yadani Gorge

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-48-9156

    Gorge with a thrilling water chute, hiking, fishing, river play, and an on-site nature campground.

  • Bansho Rice Terraces

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-48-3111

    Rice terraces in Bansho, Kikuka, selected among Japan's 100 best, famous for mid-September spider lilies.

  • Fudo Rock

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-43-1579

    Roughly 80-meter-high rock on a gentle hill, with Yamaga views and a nearby Kyushu walking trail.

  • Omiya Shrine Lantern Hall

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-44-1257

    Shrine to Emperor Keiko; Yamaga lanterns from the mid-August festival are displayed for a year in the Lantern Hall.

  • Meditation Forest Park

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-32-3111

    Peaceful riverside park where 10,000 irises bloom, with a 300-year-old Suiten-gu water deity shrine.

  • Sagara Kannon (Sagara-ji Temple)

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-48-9144

    Japan's largest seated wooden Kannon at Sagara-ji, revered for safe birth, conception, and matchmaking.

  • Kikuchi Castle Ruins

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-48-3178

    Late 7th-century mountain castle ruins spanning an area 12 times Tokyo Dome, with 67 building sites and a museum.

  • Katoda Higashibaru Archaeological Site

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-43-1651

    Large late Yayoi to early Kofun settlement with over 100 dwellings and rare bronze and iron artifacts.

  • Chibusan Kofun and Benkei-ga-ana Kofun

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-43-1145

    National historic tombs; Chibusan is known as the breast deity, and Benkei-ga-ana is closed to visitors.

  • Iwahara Futagozuka Kofun

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-36-2151

    One of the prefecture's largest keyhole tombs, the center of a national historic tomb group in a green park.

  • Kiyoura Memorial Hall

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-46-5127

    Memorial to Kiyoura Keigo, Kumamoto's first prime minister, displaying his life, writings, belongings, and photos.

  • Yamaga City Museum

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-43-1145

    Museum of archaeology, history, and folk materials from the Kikuchi River basin, in a history park.

  • Nichirin-ji Temple

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-43-5802

    Temple known for the Ako ronin hair-relic tower, a famed gate bell, 200 cherries, and 35,000 azaleas.

  • Airatobikazura

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-48-3111

    Evergreen vine over 1,000 years old, a national special natural monument, with grape-like reddish-purple flowers.

  • Ancient Lotus Garden

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-36-3838

    About 20 lotus varieties, including 2,000-year-old Oga lotus, bloom from late June to mid-August.

  • Kuginohana Falls

    Yamaga, Kumamoto · 0968-43-1579

    Little-known Yamaga waterfall with emerald water, called Kahoku's Niagara, wider after rain, beautiful spring through autumn.
