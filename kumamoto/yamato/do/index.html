Things to do in Yamato, Kumamoto

Yamato · Kumamoto · 7 places

Do

  • Aobanose Exchange Facility

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-82-3511

    A nature leisure facility for village-life experiences, with camping, pottery, and local cuisine.

  • Seiwa Plateau Observatory and Inn

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-82-3300

    A sliding-roof observatory with a 50-centimeter Newtonian telescope; lodging with a kitchen is on site.

  • Ryokuzankyo Fishing Park Campsite

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-82-3224

    A campsite near the Midori River source, with tent sites, cabins, log bungalows, and gear rental.

  • Utase Campsite

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-83-0554

    An auto campsite in the Kyushu mountains, cool in summer and enjoyable for families.

  • Inashida Plateau Campsite

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-82-3210

    A pine-forest campsite on the south foot of the Aso outer rim, cool even in summer.

  • Fukakakematsu Campground

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-83-0249

    One of western Japan's largest campgrounds, with mountain panoramas, log houses, tent sites, and lodges.

  • Goyururi

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 080-4286-7544

    Nature coworking space in Yamato Town with projector, screen, and desk USB power, plus bonfire and BBQ.
