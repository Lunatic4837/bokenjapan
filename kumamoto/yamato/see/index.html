Sights in Yamato, Kumamoto

Yamato · Kumamoto · 11 places

See

  • Circular Water Divider

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-1158

    Built in 1956 to divide Sasawara River water between Shiraito Plateau and Sasawara districts fairly by farmland area, 7:3.

  • Soyokyo Gorge

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-83-1111

    Called Kyushu's Grand Canyon, a rare U-shaped valley with 200-meter cliffs stretching 10 kilometers.

  • Ryokusenkyo Gorge

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-82-3224

    A Midori River source area famous for autumn leaves, with summer river play, camping, and fishing.

  • Tsujun Bridge

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-3855

    A stone arch aqueduct built in 1854, the only such bridge that discharges water; a National Treasure.

  • Goroga Falls

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-1158

    One of Yamato's largest waterfalls, dropping 50 meters and fully visible from a suspension bridge.

  • Heitate Shrine

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-83-0159

    A shrine in the Kyushu mountains preserving ancient faith, with a sacred cypress and giant cedars.

  • Onari Shrine

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-1158

    Shrine said to date from 640, enshrining Amaterasu and other deities, named after a coming-of-age rite.

  • Ayunose Bridge

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-1158

    A 390-meter bridge over Midorikawa Gorge, about 140 meters high, awarded a top civil engineering prize in 2002.

  • Koichiryo Shrine

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-2377

    Shrine enshrined for over 1,000 years, known for hidden hearts and as a love-fulfillment shrine.

  • Mamihara Bridge

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-1115

    Bridge over the Gokase River between husband-and-wife rocks at former post town Mamihara; lip-shaped design won a 1995 award.

  • Ryugu Falls

    Yamato, Kumamoto · 0967-72-1115

    About 20-meter two-tier falls beside Ryugu Falls Power Station in Yamato Town. Rear cave said to connect to Ryugu.
