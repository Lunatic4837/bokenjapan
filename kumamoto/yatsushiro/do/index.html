Things to do in Yatsushiro, Kumamoto

Yatsushiro · Kumamoto · 16 places

Do

  • Hinagu Onsen

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-38-0267

    Quiet onsen town in Yatsushiro said discovered in 1409, with weakly alkaline simple springs used for hot-spring cures.

  • Select Royal Yatsushiro

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-34-1111

    Long-established city hotel in central Yatsushiro for business, sightseeing, weddings, and authentic cuisine.

  • Kinparo

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-38-0611

    1910 wooden three-story Hinagu Onsen ryokan, a registered cultural property, with free-flowing alkaline baths and winter banpeiyu baths.

  • Tsurunoyu Yatsushiro Branch

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-39-1000

    Low-rate business hotel with repeated main-bath entry, long-stay and student discounts, and meals for camps.

  • Toyoko Inn Shin-Yatsushiro Station

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-31-1045

    Business hotel about 8 minutes by car from Yatsushiro IC, with free breakfast and parking for 80 cars.

  • Higashikata Nature Park

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-4123

    Climb 777 stone steps for views of Yatsushiro Plain, Kuma River, Yatsushiro Sea, Amakusa and Unzen.

  • Gokanosho Nature School

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-5530

    Wooden lodging and training halls for camping and study, with a herb garden, fishing pond and campfire facilities.

  • Gokanosho Mountain Stream Campsite

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-5372

    Campsite by a clear stream in deep mountains, with bungalows, tent sites and rentals. Open March to late November.

  • Ryuhozan Nature Park

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-4123

    A 517-meter mountain overlooking Yatsushiro Plain and Sea; the summit hike takes about 90 minutes.

  • Yatsushiro Myoken Festival

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 070-5819-8246

    Edo-period autumn rite of Myoken Shrine with processions on November 22-23 and many traditional performances, UNESCO-listed in 2016.

  • Sakuraso Mountain Cuisine Inn

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-5267

    Nostalgic inn with warm hospitality, game and wild-vegetable mountain cooking, and a relaxing cypress bath.

  • Momigi Mountain Lodge

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-5211

    Mountain lodge in Heike-legend Gokanosho serving yamame trout, boar, deer, wild plants, and homegrown vegetables.

  • Kumanan Station

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-37-8281

    A spot with limited Kumamon photo booths and vending machines, near scattered Kumamon statues.

  • Matsu-no-Yu

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-38-0573

    Public bath founded in 1931, with differently colored tiles in each bath and a natural hot spring.

  • Higashi-yu

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto

    Public bath in a residential area, open from 6 a.m. and used by local fishermen and farmers.

  • Yume Dream Sports Camp

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-37-1204

    Low-cost Yatsushiro sports-camp lodging where guests handle their own tasks, unlike a hotel.
