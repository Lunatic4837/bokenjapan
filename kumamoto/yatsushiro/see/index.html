See in Yatsushiro

Yatsushiro · Kumamoto · 27 places

See

  • Yatsushiro Shrine (Myoken-gu)

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-32-5350

    Founded in 1186. UNESCO-listed November Myoken Festival draws over 100,000; a prefectural cultural property.

  • Yatsushiro Castle Ruins

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-4533

    Built in 1619 by Kato Masakata. Stone walls and moat remain; a national historic site and cherry-blossom spot.

  • Matsui Shrine

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-0171

    Enshrines Matsui Yasuyuki and Okinaga in the castle's north bailey. Noted for 400-year-old Garyubai plum.

  • Site of Miyazaki Hachiro's Death

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-4533

    Miyazaki Hachiro, 27, died here on April 6, 1877, aiding Saigo's army in the Satsuma Rebellion.

  • Thirteen-story Stone Pagoda

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-32-2209

    National Important Cultural Property built in 1230, now eleven tiers, 6.6 meters, with Buddha and demon carvings.

  • Yatsushiro Municipal Museum Mirai no Mori Museum

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-34-5555

    Designed by Toyo Ito. Exhibits Yatsushiro history, Matsui heirlooms, Yatsushiro ware and festival materials. Currently closed.

  • Former Gunchiku Shinden Sluice Gate A

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-4533

    Sole surviving original Meiji sluice of the Gunchiku reclamation, among Japan's largest. National Important Cultural Property.

  • Shohinken

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-0171

    Tea garden built in 1688 by Matsui Naoyuki for his mother. About 9,000 square meters; a national scenic site.

  • Sakamoto Hachiryu Observatory

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-45-3453

    At about 500 meters elevation, with views of Yatsushiro, Amakusa, Uto Peninsula and Unzen, plus telescopes and a planetarium.

  • Hikawa Dam

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-2730

    About 200 Someiyoshino and 100 mountain cherries bloom by the lake. Bait and lure fishing are possible.

  • Hinagu Onsen Shrine

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-32-5350

    Built in 1420 for hot-spring deity Ichikishima-hime, with fine carvings. Spring cherry spot and summer shiranui viewpoint.

  • Gokanosho Heike Village

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-5372

    Theme facility on the Heike refugee legend, with a museum, Noh stage and thatched restaurant.

  • Saza House

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-5510

    Said to be Sugawara no Michizane's eldest son's hideaway, renamed Saza Taro, with plum-crest openwork.

  • Sekishokan

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-65-2700

    Displays Taneyama stonemason and stone-bridge materials in Toyo, their birthplace. The round tuff building is Artpolis-recognized.

  • Momigi Suspension Bridges

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-2111

    Two suspension bridges in Gokanosho, Ayatori and Shakunage, once local paths, with a nearby viewpoint.

  • Sendan Todoro Falls

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-2730

    One of Japan's 100 waterfalls, falling 70 meters; a suspension bridge reaches its 8-meter basin.

  • Umenoki Todoro Falls

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-2111

    A 38-meter waterfall named for a plum tree dropped while being carried; a bridge reaches the basin.

  • Kasamatsu Bridge Park

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-65-2114

    Toyo's largest stone bridge, 22.75 meters long, said to have been built by Hashimoto Kangoro.

  • Shakain Temple

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-2730

    A Tendai temple famous for Japan's longest stone stairway of 3,333 steps, founded in 799.

  • Ogata House

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-67-5118

    Restored home of a Heike descendant from about 300 years ago, with a hearth room and hidden room.

  • Mizushima

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-33-4115

    A small bridged island near the Kuma River mouth, designated a national scenic site in 2009.

  • Hashirimizu Falls

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0695-45-2211

    A roughly 100-meter waterfall on the Hashirimizu River in Sakamoto, Yatsushiro, also called Shirataki, long known as a scenic spot.

  • Gokanosho

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-36-5800

    Secluded Yatsushiro area ringed by 1,300-1,700 meter mountains, with a Heike warrior legend and vivid autumn foliage.

  • Omatsuri Dendenkan

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-37-8737

    Yatsushiro center presenting local folk performing arts, with a three-screen Myoken Festival theater.

  • Kumamon Port Yatsushiro

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 096-333-2515

    International cruise gateway with about 2.7 hectares of Kumamon park, a 6-meter statue, and 54 chorus statues.

  • Catholic Yatsushiro Church

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-32-4065

    Reportedly 6,000 Christians in 1588. First Edo-period martyr came from Yatsushiro in 1603. Founded in 1890.

  • Yatsushiro Convent of the Sisters of St. Paul of Chartres

    Yatsushiro, Kumamoto · 0965-32-6850

    White and blue building constructed in 1900. Surviving original structure was designated a National Registered Tangible Cultural Property in 2000.
