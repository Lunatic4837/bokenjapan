Sights in Yunomae, Kumamoto

Yunomae · Kumamoto · 9 places

See

  • Ushio Shrine and Sai Shrine

    Yunomae, Kumamoto · 0966-43-4111

    Called the breast deity. Breast-shaped offerings are said to improve milk; visiting both shrines brings marital harmony and descendants.

  • Jozen-ji Amida Hall

    Yunomae, Kumamoto · 0966-43-2050

    Kumamoto's oldest wooden building and national important cultural property, said built in 1222-1224, with a thatched hip roof.

  • Yunomae Manga Art Museum

    Yunomae, Kumamoto · 0966-43-2050

    Museum preserving works of late Yunomae political cartoonist Ryosuke Nasu, about 7000 pieces, with a restored study corner.

  • Yunomae Manga Library

    Yunomae, Kumamoto · 0966-43-4888

    Free manga library of about 4,000 books, with Kumamoto artists' corner, signatures, and a tent facing the local railway.

  • Shimosato Odaishi Hall

    Yunomae, Kumamoto

    Mid-17th-century thatched Kobo Daishi hall on former Kisshoin grounds; repairs finished in 2023.

  • Odaishi-do

    Yunomae, Kumamoto

    Daishi hall kept through faith from lords to commoners. Hinoki single-block Kobo Daishi statue, said to be from Kissho-in Temple.

  • Ichifusayama Jingu Lower Shrine (Satomiya Shrine)

    Yunomae, Kumamoto

    Shrine for people unable to climb to the main shrine on Mount Ichifusa.

  • Shitamachi Bridge

    Yunomae, Kumamoto

    Historic single-arch stone bridge in Yunomae, built in 1906, about 17 meters long and 3 meters wide; town cultural property.

  • Josen-ji (Joshin-ji)

    Yunomae, Kumamoto

    Kamakura-period temple in Yunomae; its Amida Hall is the prefecture's oldest wooden building and a national Important Cultural Property.
