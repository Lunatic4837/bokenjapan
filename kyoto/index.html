Nearby rural towns

Kyoto is a good base for rural Japan. Within a couple of hours by train or bus you reach lakeside villages in Shiga, mountain towns in Nara and Mie, the Tamba and Tajima hills of Hyogo, and the fishing towns of the Wakasa coast in Fukui. These neighbouring town guides make easy day trips or overnight stops.
