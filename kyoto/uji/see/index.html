  • Manpuku-ji Temple

    萬福寺

    Uji, Kyoto

    Manpuku-ji is a Buddhist temple located in Uji, Kyoto Prefecture, approximately a 5-minute walk from JR Obaku Station・Keihan Obaku Station.

  • Ujigami Shrine

    宇治上神社

    Uji, Kyoto

    Ujigami Shrine is a Shinto shrine in the city of Uji, Kyoto Prefecture, Japan. The shrine was built as a guardian shrine for the nearby Byodo-in, and is adjacent to the Uji Shrine.

  • Historic Monuments of Ancient Kyoto

    古都京都の文化財

    Uji, Kyoto

    The UNESCO World Heritage Site Historic Monuments of Ancient Kyoto encompasses 17 locations in Japan within the city of Kyoto and its immediate vicinity.

  • Utoro, Uji

    ウトロ地区

    Uji, Kyoto

    Utoro is a district in Uji, Kyoto Prefecture, Japan. The district has historically been populated by Zainichi Koreans ever since they were compelled to work in difficult conditions in the area in 1943.
