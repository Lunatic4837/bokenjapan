Things to do in Aya, Miyazaki

Aya · Miyazaki · 11 places

Do

  • Aya Teruha Suspension Bridge

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-2055

    A 142m-high, 250m-long pedestrian suspension bridge offering panoramic views of vast lucidophyll forest.

  • Aya Equestrian Park

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-0964

    Town-run riding club in horse-breeding Aya, with beginner to advanced riding, a 28-meter flower clock, lawn, and playground.

  • Aya Nishikihara Soccer Field

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1115

    Winter-grass overseeded soccer field hosting J-League camps, other team camps, and many tournaments.

  • Aya International Craft Castle and Aya Castle

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1223

    Craft hall shows and sells works from 26 local studios; weaving, pottery, and dyeing workshops offered beside reconstructed Aya Castle.

  • Aya Town Odazume Sports Park Athletics Stadium

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1227

    All-weather 400m track with multipurpose plaza and tennis courts, beside Teruha no Mori no Yado lodging.

  • Aya Teruha Dome

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1115

    Arena 60 by 80 meters with an area of 4,800 square meters.

  • Aya Town Teruha Fureai Plaza Soccer Field

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1115

    Winter-grass overseeded soccer field for J-League camps and tournaments, adjacent to Aya Teruha Dome.

  • Aya Town Odazume Sports Park Soccer Field

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1227

    Completed in FY2009. Winter-grass overseeded soccer field beside Teruha no Mori no Yado, with an all-weather track.

  • Aya Nishikihara Baseball Field

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1115

    Every spring, corporate, university, and other teams hold camps. Nature-rich site amid laurel forest, lodging nearby.

  • Forest Ayakawaso Aya Natural Pool

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1227

    Groundwater-fed natural pool with a 25m waterslide and a 30cm-deep toddler pool with a slide.

  • Aya Town Odazume Sports Park Tennis Courts

    Aya, Miyazaki · 0985-77-1227

    Four night-lit sand-filled artificial turf courts near Teruha lodging, used for camps, beside track, soccer field and gym.
