Things to do in Ebino, Miyazaki

Ebino · Miyazaki · 16 places

Do

  • Karakuni-dake

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-3002

    Kirishima's highest peak at 1700m, with a 900m-wide crater and views of Onami Pond and distant peaks.

  • Green Park Ebino

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 050-3139-8760

    Free Coca-Cola factory tours, collection gallery, lawns, and a flower garden.

  • Ebino Plateau Hotel

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-0161

    Hotel at about 1,200 meters on Karakunidake's slopes, with free-flowing bicarbonate hot springs.

  • Shiratori Onsen Kamiyu

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-1104

    Onsen midway up Mount Shiratori, known for Saigo Takamori's three-month stay, with open-air and steam baths.

  • Ebino Plateau Outdoor Ice Skating Rink

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-0161

    Ended: Japan's southernmost outdoor ice rink on Ebino Plateau, backed by snow-covered Mount Karakuni.

  • Kyomachi Tourist Hotel

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-37-1231

    Round-the-clock free-flowing baths at Miyazaki's oldest hot-spring town, plus local kaiseki.

  • Kakuto Onsen

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-35-1230

    Rural onsen raising about 1,000 carp for fresh dishes, five minutes from Ebino IC.

  • Ebino Plateau Foot Bath Station

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-1155

    Tourist information and souvenirs, plus a Kawayu-style foot bath popular with hikers.

  • Nagayama Sports Park

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-48-9677

    Lush sports park and citizens' retreat, one minute by car from expressway IC, parking about 300, soccer and ground golf.

  • Shiratori Onsen Shimoyu

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-3611

    About 1 km below Shiratori Onsen Kamiyu, with a garden open-air bath, sunlit large bath, private baths, and six cabins.

  • Ebino Civic Gymnasium

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-48-9677

    Can fit three volleyball and seven badminton courts for year-round camps. Iino Station-front Gymnasium is about 2 km away.

  • Ojihara Sports Park (Baseball Field)

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-48-9677

    Southern Kyushu center, used from across Kyushu for camps and tournaments of varied ages, near Kyomachi Onsen's scattered inns.

  • Yatake Plateau Belton Auto Campground

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-37-1033

    Auto campground on Yatake Plateau with Kirishima panorama views, showers, a shop, sledding, and rentals.

  • Ebino City Sumo Arena

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-48-9677

    Indoor sumo ring used for Musashigawa stable grand sumo camps and usable in any weather.

  • Nagayama Sports Park (Tennis Courts)

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-48-9677

    Highly convenient, one minute by car from the expressway interchange, with parking for about 300 cars.

  • Ojibaru Sports Park Pitching Practice Field

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-48-9677

    Covered pitching practice field at Ojibaru Ballpark with artificial turf from mound to home, usable in rain.
