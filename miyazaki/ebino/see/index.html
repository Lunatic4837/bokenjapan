Sights in Ebino, Miyazaki

Ebino · Miyazaki · 10 places

See

  • Jin-no-Ike

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-37-2663

    Two clear spring ponds named for an Ito camp in a battle with the Shimazu.

  • Ebino Plateau

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-3002

    A 1,200-meter plateau south of Ebino City in Kirishima-Kinkowan National Park on the Miyazaki-Kagoshima border.

  • Morioka Castle

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-35-1516

    Privately built tourist castle in Ebino, open for viewing, with towers, stone walls, and armor experiences.

  • Sugawara Shrine (Nishikawakita and Tsuru)

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-37-2663

    Two Ebino shrines: Tsuru has a vermilion torii and cultural-property Nio; Nishikawakita holds the Ushikoshi Festival.

  • Kanematsu Honen Shrine

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-35-0987

    Shrine of Honen, the god of shochu, who willed one granted wish for an offering of shochu.

  • Shiratori Shrine

    Ebino, Miyazaki

    Shrine to Yamato Takeru founded in the mid-Heian period, with a main hall rebuilt after an 1809 fire.

  • Rokkannon Miike

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-3002

    Acidic crater lake about 400 meters across, called Kirishima's most beautiful volcanic lake.

  • Ebino Eco Museum Center

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-33-3002

    Visitor center at about 1,200m on Ebino Plateau, exhibiting Kirishima wildlife, plants, and volcanic history.

  • Yatake Plateau

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-37-2663

    Prefectural nature park about 700 meters high, with caldera and Kirishima views, paragliding, and camping.

  • Kuruson Great Bridge

    Ebino, Miyazaki · 0984-35-3838

    325-meter bridge 80 meters above the Sendai River, opened in 2006 near Kuruson Gorge.
