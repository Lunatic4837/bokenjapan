Things to do in Gokase, Miyazaki

Gokase · Miyazaki · 5 places

Do

  • Gokase Highland Ski Resort

    Gokase, Miyazaki

    Winter ski operations ended in 2026, with other uses for the facility being explored.

  • Gokase Town General Park G-Park (Gokase Dome)

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-1710

    At 600m in northern prefecture, cool for summer sports. Membrane-structure dome gymnasium with a training room.

  • Gokase Town General Park G-Park (Athletics Stadium)

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-1710

    At 600m in northern prefecture, cool for summer sports. All-weather 400m eight-lane track; soccer also playable in the infield.

  • Gokase no Sato Camp Village

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 090-4475-5005

    Central Kyushu campground near Aso and Takachiho, with summer river canoeing, bungalows, and tent sites.

  • Gokase Town General Park G-Park (Sports Field)

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-1710

    At 600m in northern prefecture, cool for summer sports. 1.3ha multipurpose field with night lights; many groups practice at night.
