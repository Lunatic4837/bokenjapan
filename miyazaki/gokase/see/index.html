Sights in Gokase, Miyazaki

Gokase · Miyazaki · 7 places

See

  • Unoko Falls

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-1200

    A 20-meter falls with a 5,000-square-meter basin amid columnar jointed rocks on the Mikasho River.

  • Gokase Winery

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-73-5477

    Winery making wine from 100 percent local Gokase grapes, plus a produce shop and restaurant.

  • Gokase White Falls

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-1200

    About 60-meter falls at 900 meters, especially scenic in autumn foliage and winter ice.

  • Gion Shrine

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-83-2008

    Purification shrine on Gion Mountain, called the birthplace of Kyushu Island.

  • Josen-ji

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-0209

    Historic temple founded in 1615, with an about 300-year-old weeping cherry, a Miyazaki Prefecture natural monument.

  • Yuhinosato Bridge

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-1200

    240m bridge completed in 2004 with 89m piers, famous for autumn foliage photography.

  • Mikasho Shrine

    Gokase, Miyazaki · 0982-82-1513

    Shrine to Izanaki and Izanami. The 1818 main hall carvings are a prefectural cultural property.
