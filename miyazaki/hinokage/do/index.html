Things to do in Hinokage, Miyazaki

Hinokage · Miyazaki · 4 places

Do

  • Hinokage Onsen Station

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-87-2690

    Former railway station reused as a natural onsen with gorge views, a shop, railway exhibits, and train guest rooms.

  • Hinokage Camp Village

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-87-2820

    Campground along the Hinokage River, about 30 minutes from Takachiho Gorge, with bungalows and forest therapy.

  • Shikagawa District Exchange Center Tsurigane (Gymnasium)

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-48-0160

    Former Shikagawa Junior High School, closed March 2006, reopened as a lodging social-education facility.

  • Shikagawa District Exchange Center Tsurigane (Soccer Field)

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-48-0160

    Former Shikagawa Junior High closed in March 2006 and reopened in 2010 with lodging for training and sports.
