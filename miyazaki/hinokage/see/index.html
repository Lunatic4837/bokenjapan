Sights in Hinokage, Miyazaki

Hinokage · Miyazaki · 11 places

See

  • Mitate Gorge

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    Foliage gorge on the upper Hinokage River inside a UNESCO eco-park, with rocks and fishing.

  • Seiun Bridge

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    410m truss arch bridge 137m above the water in Hinokage, completed in 1984, then Japan's highest.

  • Former Third Gokase River Bridge

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    Former Takachiho Railway bridge completed in 1939, designated a National Important Cultural Property in 2020.

  • Yato Kannon Falls

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    Roughly 45-meter waterfall in Hinokage, with a Kannon hall long worshipped for women's protection.

  • Tensho Bridge

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    Concrete arch bridge completed in 2000, 463.2m long and 143m above the water, spanning 260m.

  • Bamboo Craft Museum

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-4449

    Displays Kazuo Hiroshima's bamboo crafts, refined for ease, durability and beauty as handmade tools.

  • Togawa Stone Wall Village

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    Stone-wall village, a cultural landscape, with Japan's tallest wall at 11m and terraces in Japan's top 100.

  • Ryuten Bridge

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    Japan's top forest-road bridge and Hinokage's third largest, 100.2 m high and 260 m long.

  • Former Tsunose River Bridge

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    About 418m concrete-arch Takachiho Railway bridge linking Nobeoka and Hinokage, with a then-largest 45m span.

  • Mount Tansuke

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-78-1021

    About 815m peak with a short hike and 360-degree summit views in a quasi-national park special area.

  • British House

    Hinokage, Miyazaki · 0982-89-1220

    Built by Hans Hunter as British engineers' lodging; registered cultural property with Mitate Mine history and original furniture.
