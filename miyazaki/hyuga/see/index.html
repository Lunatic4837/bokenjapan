Sights in Hyuga, Miyazaki

Hyuga · Miyazaki · 10 places

See

  • Cape Hyuga and Umagase

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-54-6177

    Five-kilometer rias cape with columnar joints, a national natural monument, and Pacific views from Umagase.

  • Omi Shrine

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-52-3406

    Ancient Amaterasu shrine beside Isegahama, called Hyuga's Ise, holding one of Japan's largest Sazare-ishi groups.

  • Wish-Granting Cross Sea

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-54-6177

    Eroded reef forms a cross about 200 by 220 meters and is said to grant wishes.

  • Udo Shrine (Dragon Palace)

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-52-3406

    Hand-dug seaside cave shrine east of Omi Shrine, for safe birth and travel, with a dragon silhouette.

  • Mimitsu Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings

    Hyuga, Miyazaki

    Edo-Meiji port town of white-walled storehouses and stone streets, a national preservation district.

  • Kuroda Retainers

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-55-0235

    Tombolo beyond Hososhima Port, walkable at low tide, with graves of three men from the 1862 Teradaya Incident.

  • Kofuku Shrine

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-53-0173

    One minute on foot from Hyuga City Hall; enshrines harvest and fortune deities and has paired camphor trees.

  • Tateiwa Shrine

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-58-1638

    Shrine in Mimitsu where Emperor Jimmu set sail. His sitting rock is enshrined; ancient camphor trees remain.

  • Hososhima Lighthouse

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0987-22-3264

    11m lighthouse at Hyuga Cape, built in 1910, a registered cultural property and one of 50 Love Lighthouses.

  • Kashima Shrine

    Hyuga, Miyazaki · 0982-55-0235

    Quiet shrine past giant rocks facing Hyuga-nada, with many wish stones; pufferfish spawn nearby each July.
