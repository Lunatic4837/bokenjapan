Things to do in Kadogawa, Miyazaki

Kadogawa · Miyazaki · 5 places

Do

  • Kadogawa Onsen Kokoro no Mori

    Kadogawa, Miyazaki · 0982-63-7780

    Natural onsen on a hill overlooking Kadogawa Bay, with varied baths and lodging.

  • Uninhabited Island Otoshima Campground

    Kadogawa, Miyazaki · 0982-57-4110

    Camp on uninhabited Otoshima in Kadogawa Bay: bungalows, tent decks, fishing, trekking, and rugged columnar-jointed rocks. Book the ferry yourself.

  • Kadogawa Seaside Comprehensive Park Baseball Field

    Kadogawa, Miyazaki · 0982-63-4295

    Scenic sea-surrounded sports park; night-lit baseball field hosts yearly adult and university camps; multipurpose ground.

  • Kadogawa Seaside Comprehensive Park Tennis Courts

    Kadogawa, Miyazaki · 0982-63-4295

    Scenic 10.3-hectare park overlooking Kadogawa Bay with sand-filled artificial-turf tennis courts; hosts school tournaments and team camps.

  • Kadogawa Seaside Park Pool

    Kadogawa, Miyazaki · 0982-63-4295

    Outdoor pool with two waterslides, a 110m flowing pool, and a toddler pool no deeper than 60cm.
