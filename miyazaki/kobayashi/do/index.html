Do in Kobayashi

Kobayashi · Miyazaki · 13 places

Do

  • Suki Land

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-48-2480

    Outdoor leisure facility around Mamako Falls and Ono Lake, with activities, natural onsen, and a Finnish sauna.

  • Idenoyama Park

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-23-1174

    Relaxation park around Kobayashi's largest spring-fed pond, famous for fireflies.

  • Sannomiya Gorge

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-27-3280

    Popular one-kilometer hiking path with eleven tunnels, a 32-meter rock wall, and a 7.5-meter waterfall.

  • Nojirikopia

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-44-3000

    Melon-domed leisure park by Nojiri Lake with go-karts, large play equipment, and a summer spring-water pool.

  • Hinamori Auto Campground

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-23-8100

    Top-capacity auto campground in Miyazaki at the Kirishima foothills, with 14 cabins and camper sites.

  • Kobayashi Comprehensive Sports Park (Observation Plaza)

    The Kyushu Soccer League was held here in the past.

  • Miyazaki Kobayashi Country Club

  • Kobayashi Comprehensive Sports Park (Baseball Stadium)

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-22-7911

  • Kobayashi Comprehensive Sports Park (Athletics Stadium)

  • Kobayashi General Sports Park Tennis Courts

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-24-0556

  • Kobayashi City Civic Gymnasium

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-22-7911

  • Nojiri Town Otsukabaru Sports Ground

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-44-1100

    Artificial-turf ground completed March 2018 with good drainage, soccer lines, and a walking and jogging loop.

  • Hinamoridai Cross-Country Course (Yamazakura Course)

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-23-6046
