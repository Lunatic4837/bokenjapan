Sights in Kobayashi, Miyazaki

Kobayashi · Miyazaki · 7 places

See

  • Ikoma Plateau

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-27-1919

    About 90,000-square-meter plateau backed by the Kirishima range, with seasonal flowers, a cafe, and a dog run.

  • Inyoseki

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-22-8684

    Male rock 17.5m high and female rock 5.5m around, rare paired lava rocks below Sannomiya Gorge.

  • Kirishimamine Shrine

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-23-0855

    High-ranking shrine enshrining Ninigi and the Hyuga Three Generations. Moved here in 1874 after merger with Hinamori Shrine.

  • Hagi Teahouse

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-46-0331

    Flower spot with cherries, azaleas, hydrangeas, roses, and four million spider lilies, plus ramen and produce.

  • Mount Shinmoe

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-22-8684

    Active volcano on the Miyazaki-Kagoshima border, with a summit crater about 750 meters across.

  • Sukimurando Great Suspension Bridge

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-25-9500

    155-meter cable-stayed bridge over Ono Lake at Sukimurando, with views of Mamako Falls.

  • Makiba Cherry Blossoms

    Kobayashi, Miyazaki · 0984-22-8684

    About 1,000 Yoshino cherries bloom along a 2 km road near Hosono Junior High; night lights and autumn spider lilies.
