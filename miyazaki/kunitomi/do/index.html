Do

  • Hokkedake Park

    Kunitomi, Miyazaki · 0985-78-1943

  • Arena Kunitomi

    Kunitomi, Miyazaki · 0985-86-6222

    Completed in August 2017. Mirrored multipurpose room for dance, with a walking course on the second floor.

  • Hokkedake Park Campground

    Kunitomi, Miyazaki · 0985-78-1943

    Mountain tent campground about 30 minutes by car from Miyazaki City, with three site types, summer river play, and activities.

  • Miyazaki Golf Club

    Kunitomi, Miyazaki · 0985-75-3131

    Hill course in Kunitomi beside Miyazaki City; moderate elevation adds strategy. 18 holes, 6523 yards, par 72.
