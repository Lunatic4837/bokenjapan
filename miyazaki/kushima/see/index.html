Sights in Kushima, Miyazaki

Kushima · Miyazaki · 6 places

See

  • Cape Toi

    Kushima, Miyazaki · 0987-72-0479

    Southern tip of Nichinan Kaigan Quasi-National Park, with a visitable white lighthouse and wild Misaki horses.

  • Koshima Island

    Kushima, Miyazaki · 0987-72-0479

    Small uninhabited island about 200 meters offshore, a national monument where macaques wash potatoes in seawater.

  • Misaki Shrine

    Kushima, Miyazaki · 0987-72-0479

    Cliffside shrine at Cape Toi's tip, founded in 708, enshrining Watatsumi for maritime safety beside a cycad forest.

  • Toi Cape Lighthouse

    Kushima, Miyazaki · 0987-76-1838

    Kyushu's only climbable lighthouse at Kushima's southernmost cape, completed in 1929, with views from 240 meters.

  • Former Yoshimatsu Family Residence

    Kushima, Miyazaki · 0987-72-6511

    Taisho-era house built by a timber merchant, a national Important Cultural Property open as a city museum.

  • Kushima Shrine

    Kushima, Miyazaki · 090-9792-2652

    Main deity Yamasachihiko, linked to Urashima Taro, is worshipped for prosperity, safe voyages, and matchmaking.
