Things to do in Mimata, Miyazaki

Mimata · Miyazaki · 5 places

Do

  • Asahigaoka Sports Park (Baseball Field)

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-1111

    Near Miyakonojo Interchange, forest-surrounded. Used yearly for adult softball camps. Two softball fields, athletics field, and gateball field.

  • Kamimai Park

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-9085

    Hilltop park in Mimata overlooking the Miyakonojo Basin, with plum and cherry blossoms, playgrounds, and park golf.

  • Mimata Town Central Tennis Courts

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-9312

    Four sand-filled artificial-turf tennis courts near Mimata Junior High School.

  • Mimata Town Multipurpose Sports Center

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-9312

    Artificial turf laid on the gym floor for rainy-day futsal, soccer, and baseball practice.

  • Mimata Town Kyudo Hall

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-9312

    About 15 minutes from Miyakonojo IC, near Mimata Junior High. Nine-person short-range kyudo; straw-target room for basics.
