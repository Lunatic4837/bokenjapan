Sights in Mimata, Miyazaki

Mimata · Miyazaki · 4 places

See

  • Osada Gorge

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-9085

    Roughly 10 km gorge in Wanitsuka Prefectural Natural Park, with waterfall, trail, autumn light-up, and 14 kappa statues.

  • Shiiyae Park

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-9085

    Hill park called Azalea Hill, with about 60,000 Kurume azaleas, 50 double cherries, and an observatory.

  • Shakunage Forest

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-54-1367

    Japan's largest rhododendron park, with 500 species and about 30,000 plants, plus a spring flower festival.

  • Yagafuchi Park (Spectacles Bridge)

    Mimata, Miyazaki · 0986-52-9084

    Downstream park in Nagata Gorge featuring a 1941 double-arch stone Spectacles Bridge.
