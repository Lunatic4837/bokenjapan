Do in Misatocho

Misatocho · Miyazaki · 7 places

Do

  • Nango Onsen Dontaro no Yu

    Onsen said to be a gift from Baekje royalty, rising from 1,500m underground and famed for beautiful skin.

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-59-0100 · Official / source link

  • Misato Lakeland (Ishitoge Lakeland)

    Lakeside leisure complex with cottages, tent sites, an open-air onsen, a restaurant, a pool, and wakeboarding.

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-68-2222 · Official / source link

  • Wakamiya Zojiro Park

    Park near the 733m Zojiro Mountain summit with a 10m local cedar giant swing completed in 2023.

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-68-2522 · Official / source link

  • Kitago General Exchange Center

    Volleyball and badminton courts in a 2002 building with air conditioning and electric movable spectator seats.

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-62-6205 · Official / source link

  • Nango Agriculture and Forestry Workers Training Center

    Volleyball and basketball courts used by high school basketball clubs in the prefecture for camps. Nearby Nango Onsen Yamagiri.

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-59-1605 · Official / source link

  • Mizushidani Hometown Village Auto Campground

    Riverside auto campground on clear Mizushidani River for water play and fishing. Nearby Kaigonto plum garden has about 5,000 trees.

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-59-0613 · Official / source link

  • Saigo Rural Environment Improvement Center

    Volleyball and badminton courts for tournaments and camps. Ishitoge Lakeland with onsen and cottages is five minutes by car.

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-66-3608 · Official / source link
