Sights in Misatocho, Miyazaki

Misatocho · Miyazaki · 7 places

See

  • Kudara Hall

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-59-0556

    Japan-Korea exchange hall modeled on a Buyeo museum guesthouse, displaying Baekje national treasure replicas.

  • Western Shosoin

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-59-0556

    Faithful reproduction of Nara's Shosoin in Kiso cypress, displaying 24 bronze mirrors and 1,003 halberds.

  • Mikado Shrine

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-59-0556

    Shrine founded in 718 enshrining Baekje king Teika. Its 1661 hall is a national important cultural property.

  • Lovers' Hill

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-66-3603

    Hilltop observatory with a Baekhwajeong replica, friendship bells, and about 300 cherry trees.

  • Oseri Falls

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-66-3603

    About 70m three-stage waterfall on the Osako River, said to house a dragon god.

  • Saigo Dam

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-68-2522

    First Mimi River system dam, generating since 1929, with some of Japan's largest roller gates and bell-shaped crane covers.

  • Matsugashita Maple Forest Road

    Misatocho, Miyazaki · 0982-66-3603

    About 400 maples turn red and yellow along a 6.5 km forest road in Kitago, Misato, around November.
