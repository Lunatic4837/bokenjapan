Things to do in Miyakonojo, Miyazaki

Miyakonojo · Miyazaki · 34 places

Do

  • Takachiho Ranch

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-33-2102

    Tourist ranch for animal contact, milking, riding, dining, and a dairy shop at the Kirishima foothills.

  • Kannonike Park

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-58-6139

    About 63-hectare park with the prefecture's only Ferris wheel, a 720m slider, grass skiing, and camping.

  • Aoidake Onsen

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-2177

    Natural silky onsen from ancient sea strata in a Miyakonojo gorge, 40 minutes by car from Miyazaki City.

  • Kurokiri Stadium

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-2800

    Class 1 certified athletics stadium used for J.League, rugby matches, camps, and national events.

  • Miyakonojo Sports Park Tennis Courts

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-23-7502

    16 all-weather sand-filled artificial turf courts opened fully in summer 2024. 2027 National Sports Festival adult men's soft tennis venue.

  • Miyakonojo Sports Park Koala's March Stadium

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-23-7502

    Chiba Lotte Marines camp from February 2025; Phoenix League and pro voluntary training venue.

  • Kirishima Shuzo Sports Land Miyakonojo (Yamanokuchi Sports Park)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-2800

    Park opened April 12, 2025 with a Class 1 certified athletics stadium hosting J.League, rugby, and national meets.

  • Shochu Village Kirishima Factory Garden

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-21-8111

    Kirishima Shuzo garden park with shochu factory tours, a museum, brewery viewing, shop, restaurant, and bakery.

  • Miyakonojo Takazaki Onsen Raspa Takazaki

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-62-5526

    Wellness complex in Takazaki, Miyakonojo, with free-flowing natural hot springs, varied baths, saunas, fitness, and dining.

  • Kanemitake Park

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-39-6727

    About 472 meters high on the Kagoshima border, with summit views of Kirishima and Miyakonojo, and a hang-gliding base.

  • Kannon Sakura no Sato

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-29-2100

    Onsen facility inside Kannonike Park, with 13 baths, sauna, and a year-round indoor pool.

  • Regent Miyazaki Country Club

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-33-1500

    Lee Trevino-designed woodland course with ponds, set below sacred Takachiho.

  • Aoidake Nature Park

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-2177

    Park along the Sakai River gorge with a summer campground and autumn foliage.

  • Miyakonojo Sports Park Pie no Mi Dome

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-23-7502

    Indoor practice field opened January 2025: baseball, one futsal court, nets for up to eight sections. Lotte Marines camp venue.

  • Tachibana Observatory

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-62-4936

    Observatory on a hill in Takasaki Sports Park, with a 50cm telescope and planetarium shows on cloudy days.

  • Miyakonojo City Takajo Sports Park (General Gymnasium)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-58-5514

    Clubhouse in the sports park has showers, changing rooms, and training facilities, often used for camps.

  • Miyakonojo City Takasaki General Park (Baseball Field)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-62-4448

    Used yearly by many out-of-prefecture university and high school camps. Onsen lodging in the park suits training camps.

  • Hayamizu Park Sports and Culture Center (Arena)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-24-6454

    Miyakonojo indoor sports hub for tournaments, camps, and local events. Venue for 2027 National Sports Festival adult basketball.

  • Hayamizu Park Sports and Culture Center (Sub Arena)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-24-6454

    Indoor sports hub with the arena, for daily use, tournaments, and training camps.

  • Miyakonojo City Takajo Sports Park Baseball Field

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-58-5514

    Used for Yomiuri Giants farm camps and pro self-training. Adjacent subground, indoor arena, weights. Many camps, chiefly pro camps.

  • Hayamizu Park Sports and Culture Center (Kyudo Hall)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-24-6454

    Representative kyudo hall of Miyakonojo, known for bamboo bows. National tournament yearly; international tournament every four years.

  • AKAKIRI FIELD (Yamanokuchi Sports Park Athletics Stadium)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-2800

    Class 3 certified auxiliary stadium for KUROKIRI STADIUM, with roofed stands for about 840 and an all-weather 400m track.

  • Yamanokuchi Sports Park Multipurpose Plaza

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-2800

    Located west of AKAKIRI FIELD and usable for soccer and sandlot baseball matches.

  • Hayamizu Park Sports and Culture Center (Budo Hall)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-24-6454

    Indoor sports hub with the arena, for daily use, tournaments, and training camps.

  • Miyakonojo City Takasaki General Park (Athletic Field)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-62-4448

    Multipurpose ground and athletic field in front of Raspa Takazaki onsen lodging host many student camps and youth soccer tournaments.

  • Miyakonojo City Takasaki Comprehensive Park Gymnasium

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-62-4448

    Arena with three volleyball and two basketball courts, often used for training camps, with onsen lodging and an indoor pool.

  • Miyakonojo City Takajo Sports Park Indoor Arena

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-58-5514

    Clubhouse showers, lockers, and training facilities. Used for pro baseball and J-League camps. Partition nets allow up to four areas.

  • Miyakonojo Mochio Country Club

    Mochio hills overlooking Miyakonojo, with views of Takachiho-no-mine. Varied strategic holes. 18 holes, 6058 yards, par 72.

  • Miyakonojo City Takajo Sports Park Multipurpose Field

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-58-5514

    Year-round thorough turf care. Indoor arena; clubhouse with showers, lockers, training. Often used for pro soccer and high-school camps.

  • Miyakonojo City Takasaki General Park (Tennis Courts)

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-62-4448

    Used by children to adults. Camp-ready Raspa Takazaki onsen lodging, 25m six-lane indoor pool with onsen, training and seminar rooms.

  • Miyazaki Prefecture Yamanokuchi Throwing Practice Field

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-2800

    About 14,000 square meters, usable as a practice field for shot put, discus, and hammer throw.

  • Miyakonojo City Takajo Sports Park Tennis Courts

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-58-5514

    Used by a wide range of ages, from children to adults. Clubhouse has showers, locker rooms, and training facilities.

  • Miyakonojo Sports Park Coolish Bullpen

  • Rainbow Sportsland Golf Club
