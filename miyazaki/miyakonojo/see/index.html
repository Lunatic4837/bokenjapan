Sights in Miyakonojo, Miyazaki

Miyakonojo · Miyazaki · 11 places

See

  • Sekinoo Falls

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-38-2460

    About 40m wide with an 18m drop on the Shonai River, one of Japan's 100 Waterfalls.

  • Higashi-Kirishima Shrine

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-62-1713

    One of six Kirishima Gongen shrines, traditionally founded under Emperor Kosho, enshrining Izanagi, with Oniwa stone steps.

  • Kanbashira Shrine

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-23-1395

    Founded in 1026 by Taira no Suemoto as Shimazu-sho's guardian shrine; its treasures are city-designated cultural properties.

  • Miyakonojo Shimazu Residence

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-23-2116

    Shimazu family home since 1879, about 5,000 tsubo, with a nationally registered main house and over 10,000 historical materials.

  • Hayamizu Shrine

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki

    Shrine with Okimizu burial mound and a spring legend of becoming beautiful.

  • Miyakonojo History Museum

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-25-8011

    Museum on medieval Miyakonojo castle ruins showing 30,000 years of local history, including the Shimazu clan.

  • Mochio Park

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-38-2460

    Park of about 2,600 cherry trees with a long history, one of Japan's 100 best blossom spots.

  • Hydrangea Park

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-3113

    Hill park of about 6.9 hectares planted with 28,000 hydrangeas of 84 species.

  • Yamanokuchi Fumoto Bunyabushi Puppet Joruri Museum

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-57-5295

    Museum opened in 1993 for Yamanokuchi Bunyabushi puppet joruri displays and regular performances.

  • Kannonse Waterway

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-23-9547

    Mid-Edo excavation of Oyodo rapids enabled cargo boats from Miyakonojo to Akae Port.

  • Kinokawachi Dam

    Miyakonojo, Miyazaki · 0986-23-2425

    Rockfill dam on the Kinokawachi River for farmland irrigation, completed in 2009 and open to visitors.
