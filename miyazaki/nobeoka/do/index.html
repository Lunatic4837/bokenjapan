Things to do in Nobeoka, Miyazaki

Nobeoka · Miyazaki · 24 places

Do

  • Mount Mukabaki and Mukabaki Falls

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-29-2155

    Scenic 831-meter twin-peak mountain with primeval forest and family hiking trails; Mukabaki Falls is among Japan's 100 waterfalls.

  • Sumie Beach

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-43-0201

    A 150-meter beach with highly clear water, selected among Japan's 100 best bathing beaches.

  • Athlete Town Nobeoka Arena

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki

    Main arena completed in 2026 for the 2027 sports festival, able to host national competitions.

  • Mount Okue

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-29-2155

    A 1,644-meter UNESCO Eco-Park peak with granite cliffs and primeval forest; an advanced climb among Japan's 200 famous mountains.

  • Kumanoe Beach

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-29-2155

    Little-known beach between Sumie and Shimoaso: 800m white sand and clear sea. Swimming suspended from 2026.

  • Shimoaso Beach

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-45-3811

    Calm, clear, white-sand beach often called Kyushu's finest; Shimanoura island is a natural breakwater.

  • Horigawa Campground

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 080-5816-6290

    Simple forest campground near Horigawa Gorge, recommended as a climbing base for Mt. Okue.

  • Nobeoka Waiwai Arena

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-32-5832

    Multipurpose indoor practice facility for baseball, softball, futsal, ground golf, and sports days, with fences and pitching machines.

  • Nishikai Park Baseball Stadium

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-32-5832

    Under redevelopment for a fiscal 2026 opening. Northern prefecture's largest pro-standard ballpark with indoor bullpen and LED lighting.

  • Horigawa Onsen

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-23-3080

    Rustic alkaline onsen at the foot of Mount Okue, Nobeoka's only natural hot spring, with wood-fired Finnish sauna.

  • Healthtopia Nobeoka

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-34-1111

    Year-round health leisure facility with warm flowing pools, water slides, large baths, sauna, and restaurant.

  • Soleil Levant Seaside Campground, Hamayu Village

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-45-3811

    Stay facility facing Shimoaso Beach, once Kyushu's only special pick in Japan's 100 best beaches. Cabins, tents, auto camping.

  • Nishigai Park Athletics Stadium

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-32-5832

    Summer and winter turf hosts J.League, WE League camps and JFL matches. Class 2 blue all-weather track.

  • Sumie Family Travel Village

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-43-0201

    Stay facility with clear beach, tennis, putter golf, family aquarium, and play gear, plus well-equipped auto camping and cabins.

  • Kitakata Golf Club

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-38-0006

    Relatively flat 18-hole course with Gokase and Ose greens; 6,901 yards, par 72.

  • ETO Land Hayahinomine

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-47-2700

    Leisure facility at 868m in Kitakata, with go-karts, an athletic plaza, golf, lodging, and camping.

  • Firefly House

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-27-4740

    Outdoor tourism and UNESCO Eco-Park information hub, reopened April 1, 2025, with lodging, playground, and cycling.

  • Sumie Family Travel Village Tennis Courts

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-43-0201

    Seven sand-filled artificial-turf courts near the beach, used for practice and training camps.

  • Mount Hokodake

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-27-6322

    Raised rock peaks Hokodake and Medake, an akebono azalea habitat visited by many climbers.

  • Myoda Park North and South Plazas

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-21-7093

    Adjacent lawn and clay grounds are used for tournaments and frequently for practice.

  • Nobeoka Golf Club

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-43-0101

    Seaside course overlooking the Pacific and Nippo Coast, comfortable to play in winter.

  • Nishikai Park Tennis Courts

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-21-6719

    Twelve sand-filled artificial-turf courts used for practice and tournaments.

  • Nishigai Park Ball Game Field

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-21-6719

    Maintains summer and winter turf for good ground conditions year-round. Also a Japan Rugby League One camp site.

  • Shishigawa Campground

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-48-0864

    Has bungalows and auto sites, with trails along the stream, surrounded by rich nature.
