Sights in Nobeoka, Miyazaki

Nobeoka · Miyazaki · 19 places

See

  • Nobeoka Castle Ruins and Shiroyama Park

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-22-7022

    Early modern castle ruins built in 1603, with a 19-meter stone wall and over 3,300 camellias.

  • Atagoyama Observatory

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-29-2155

    Observatory on about 251m Mt. Atago with 360-degree views of the city and Hyuga-nada.

  • Pakkun Rock

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-29-2155

    Odd rock about 7m wide resembling Pac-Man, on the Hokodake trail in Nobeoka.

  • Saigo Takamori Lodging Site Museum

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-46-2960

    Preserves the former Kodama house, last Satsuma army headquarters of Saigo Takamori, displaying his belongings and war materials.

  • Mount Kagami

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-46-5010

    Summit views of the Nippo Coast and Okueyama, plus trekking, hang gliding, paragliding, and New Year sunrise.

  • Nobeoka Botanical Garden

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-39-0977

    8.7-hectare garden west of central Nobeoka with seasonal flowers, observatories, and picnic lawns.

  • Shishigawa Gorge

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-47-3600

    Leading Miyazaki gorge of smooth granite and clear water, running about 18km along Shishigawa.

  • Horigawa Gorge

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-27-4740

    Granite gorge carved by Horigawa from Mount Okue, with clear streams, giant rocks, fishing, and water play.

  • Mt. Hiei and Senjojiki

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-29-2155

    Northern Miyazaki granite-porphyry cliffs designated a national scenic beauty and known for rock climbing.

  • Miracle Clear Stream Ogawa

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-27-4740

    Very clear Kitagawa tributary where boats seem to float in midair; gentle summer water play, kayaking, canoeing, and SUP.

  • Shimanoura Island

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-43-0723

    Island 10 minutes by high-speed boat from Nobeoka, with fishing-village scenery, coral, and a panorama.

  • Nobeoka Castle Naito Memorial Museum

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-21-7110

    Preserves Nobeoka history and culture, exhibiting Naito family treasures and folk materials from the Taisho and Showa periods.

  • Imazan Daishi

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-32-5290

    Temple in Nobeoka for the mid-April Daishi Festival, counted among Kyushu's three major spring festivals.

  • Asahi Kasei Nobeoka Exhibition Center

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-22-2070

    Exhibition center on Asahi Kasei history, management, local operations, and community ties, where some products can be touched.

  • Kitagawa Submersible Bridges

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-27-4740

    Bridges built low so they submerge in high water, preventing floods and washout; a low-cost ancestral design now disappearing.

  • Kamisanyama

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-27-4740

    Mysterious cave ruins where 24m and 15m rocks support each other and a pyramid-shaped rock sits in the gap.

  • Ieda Wetland

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-27-4740

    Rare endangered plants and animals, including Japan's largest Saigoku himekohone colony and over 40 dragonfly species.

  • Oni-no-Me Mountain Cedars

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-34-7833

    Only natural cedar habitat on the Kyushu mainland, conserved by local Forest Mantle members.

  • Okashira Shrine

    Nobeoka, Miyazaki · 0982-46-5010

    Shrine to Saeki Koreharu, Warring States lord of Togamure Castle, revered as a god of learning.
