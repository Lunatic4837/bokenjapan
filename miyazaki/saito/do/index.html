Things to do in Saito, Miyazaki

Saito · Miyazaki · 7 places

Do

  • Saitobaru Sports Park Baseball Stadium

    Saito, Miyazaki · 0983-43-3478

    Has an indoor practice field, sub-ground and athletics track; camps include Tokyo Yakult Swallows, Korean pro teams and corporate clubs.

  • Saito Shimizudai General Park

    Saito, Miyazaki · 0983-43-3478

    Three adjacent natural-grass soccer pitches for national, Kyushu tournaments and camps by J-League and school teams. Winter grass January-April.

  • Saitobaru Sports Park Athletics Stadium

    Saito, Miyazaki · 0983-43-3478

    Clay and all-weather tracks suit corporate and student athletics camps, and sub baseball use during events.

  • Saitobaru Sports Park (Tennis Courts)

    Saito, Miyazaki · 0983-43-3478

    Fully renovated in March 2010 into sand-filled artificial turf courts. Ideal for tennis camps and tournaments.

  • Saito West District Athletic Field

    Saito, Miyazaki · 0983-43-3478

    Suitable for university and high school baseball camps beside an athletics sub-field; tournaments possible with Saitobaru Sports Park.

  • Saito Civic Gymnasium

    Saito, Miyazaki · 0983-43-3478

    Used year-round by indoor sports groups; ideal for student camps. Adjacent martial arts hall can host kendo and karate tournaments.

  • Saitobaru Sports Park Indoor Practice Field

    Saito, Miyazaki · 0983-43-3478

    Pro and amateur baseball rain practice in spring, summer and autumn; also tennis, volleyball, badminton and five pitching mounds.
