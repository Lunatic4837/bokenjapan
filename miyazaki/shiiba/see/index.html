Sights in Shiiba, Miyazaki

Shiiba · Miyazaki · 6 places

See

  • Tsurudomi Residence

    Shiiba, Miyazaki · 0982-67-3139

    Shiiba house said to be Nasu Daihachiro and Princess Tsurudomi's tragic-love site; national Important Cultural Property since 1956.

  • Kamishiiba Dam (Hyuga Shiiba Lake)

    Shiiba, Miyazaki · 0982-67-3139

    Japan's first large-scale concrete arch dam, completed in 1955, forming Hyuga Shiiba Lake.

  • Sennin Rice Terraces (Oicho Observatory)

    Shiiba, Miyazaki · 0982-67-3139

    Terraces across the river seem to float in morning clouds, also called Shiiba's Machu Picchu, selected as heritage in 2022.

  • Tonegawa Settlement (Hachimura Cedars)

    Shiiba, Miyazaki · 0982-67-3139

    Steep mountain village near 550m, one of Miyazaki's two national traditional building districts, with nearly 800-year-old cedars.

  • Shiiba Folk Performing Arts Museum

    Shiiba, Miyazaki · 0982-68-7033

    Shiiba traditions including kagura, spring target shooting, autumn mortar-drum dance, and the Heike fugitive legend.

  • Shiiba Itsukushima Shrine

    Shiiba, Miyazaki · 0982-67-3139

    Hilltop shrine to Itsukushima's guardian deity, founded in 1185 by Nasu Daihachiro, who abandoned pursuit of the Heike.
