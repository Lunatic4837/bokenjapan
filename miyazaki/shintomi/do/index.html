Things to do in Shintomi, Miyazaki

Shintomi · Miyazaki · 12 places

Do

  • Shintomi Town Football Center

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-32-1161

    Two artificial turf fields and covered seating, used from amateurs to pros, with a distance-marked walking course around.

  • Ichigo Miyazaki Shintomi Soccer Field

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-32-9555

    One natural grass field seating about 5,300, mainly home ground of Tegevajaro Miyazaki and Viamaterras Miyazaki.

  • Shintomi Town Gymnasium and Kyudo Hall

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-6080

    Year-round meets include Zaronbai Sports Meet. During Zaronbai plum bloom (Jan-Feb), busy with shihanmato, kyudo, judo, kendo, karate, volleyball.

  • Nitta Athletic Field

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-1022

    Adjacent to Nyutabaru Kofun and a park. Mainly used by local junior high students and adult baseball teams.

  • Shintomi Town Hioki Sports Ground

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-1022

    Only hardball and rubber-ball baseball ground in Shintomi Town, good central access, recently used for Little League practice.

  • Nitta Tennis Courts

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-1022

    Five omni courts next to Shintomi Town West Gymnasium and a convenience store; they dry quickly with little weather impact.

  • Shintomi Town West Gymnasium

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-1022

    Near a convenience store, beside Nitta tennis courts. Used by local junior high students and adult mini-volleyball and mini-tennis teams.

  • Miyazaki Zaroume Golf Club

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-35-1311

    Gentle hill 18-hole course near the prefecture center, with two greens and many guard bunkers.

  • Kaminitta Athletic Field

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-1022

    Adjacent to Kaminitta School and a park. Mainly used by local junior high students.

  • Hitotsuse River Prefectural Sports Center

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-5585

    Public course on the Hitotsuse riverbed in Koyu District. Flat, with bunkers and ponds. 18 holes, par 70.

  • Sannodai Sports Park (Sumo Ring)

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-6080

    Grand sumo Michinoku stable camps in Shintomi from early December. Many fans watch morning practice; kids' sumo events too.

  • Workers Sports Center

    Shintomi, Miyazaki · 0983-33-1022

    Near Kami-Nitta Baseball Stadium, mainly used by local elementary and junior high students and district residents.
