Things to do in Takachiho, Miyazaki

Takachiho · Miyazaki · 11 places

Do

  • Takachiho Amaterasu Railway

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-3216

    Open-top cart ride of about 30 minutes on the former line, with views from the 105-meter Takachiho Iron Bridge.

  • Takachiho Kagura

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-2413

    Year-round daily one-hour kagura from 20:00 at Takachiho Shrine, showing four representative dances.

  • Yellow Post of Happiness (Takachiho)

    Takachiho, Miyazaki

    Yellow post at Takachiho Gorge, installed in 2015 to wish happiness for visitors and recipients.

  • Mount Sobo

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1212

    1,756 m peak spanning three prefectures; second highest on mainland Kyushu, a UNESCO eco-park with primeval forest.

  • Takachiho Auto Campground

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-82-2351

    Five minutes from central Takachiho, near Takachiho Gorge, with a dog run, bungalows, and an on-site grill.

  • Shikimibaru Sukoyaka Forest Campground

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-82-2151

    Kyushu's highest campground at 1,200 m, with close stars, mountain views, and a cedar barrel sauna.

  • Mount Futagami

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1212

    Twin-peaked sacred mountain linked to Ninigi's descent, with a gentle hike to an observatory.

  • Takachiho Town Martial Arts Hall

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-4723

    Multipurpose facility evoking mythical Takachiho, with a staged arena and a first-floor archery hall.

  • Takachiho Town General Sports Park (Tennis Courts)

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-4723

    Usable despite rain or frost. Onsen, pool, and training facilities five minutes by car. Sightseeing training camps recommended.

  • Takachiho Town General Sports Park (Baseball Field)

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-4723

    Nearby onsen, pool, and training facilities are five minutes by car. A tourist spot recommended for camps with sightseeing.

  • Orihara Ground

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-4723

    Onsen, pool, and training facilities nearby, five minutes by car.
