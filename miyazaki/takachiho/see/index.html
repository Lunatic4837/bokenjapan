Sights in Takachiho, Miyazaki

Takachiho · Miyazaki · 12 places

See

  • Takachiho Gorge

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1213

    Cliffs 80-100m high stretch about 7km, with boat rides on emerald water near Manai Falls.

  • Takachiho Shrine

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-2413

    Matchmaking shrine and head of Takachiho's 88 shrines, with an Important Cultural Property hall and nightly kagura.

  • Amanoyasugawara

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-74-8239

    Large cave about ten minutes from Amanoiwato Shrine, where gods gathered and visitors stack stones.

  • Kunimigaoka

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1213

    Best viewpoint in the Takachiho Basin at about 513m, with wide views and a seasonal morning sea of clouds.

  • Amanoiwato Shrine

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-74-8239

    Shrine enshrining the heavenly rock cave where sun goddess Amaterasu hid, with west and east main halls.

  • Aratate Shrine

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-2368

    Cedar-forest shrine said to be where married Sarutahiko and Amenouzume lived, blessing harmony and the arts.

  • Kushifuru Shrine

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-72-2413

    Shrine on the sacred descent site of Ninigi-no-Mikoto; its 1694 hall carvings are a Takachiho tangible cultural property.

  • Akimoto Shrine

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1213

    Mountain shrine in Takachiho's Akimoto village, moved here in 1683 and known as a power spot.

  • Tochimata Rice Terraces

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1213

    Gentle, wide terraces on the road from central Takachiho to Amanoiwato Shrine, with rice, tobacco, and seasonal views.

  • Shimono Hachiman Shrine

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1212

    Shrine in Takachiho's Shimono, said founded in 1192, with 900-year-old zelkova and ginkgo, national natural monuments.

  • Gokasho Plateau

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1212

    Cool grassland averaging over 800 meters in northern Takachiho, habitat for rare plants and insects.

  • Odonokuchi Rice Terraces

    Takachiho, Miyazaki · 0982-73-1213

    Gentle, wide terraces on the road from central Takachiho to Amanoiwato Shrine, with rice, tobacco, and seasonal views.
