Do in Takaharu

Takaharu · Miyazaki · 8 places

Do

  • Mount Takachiho (Ama-no-Sakahoko)

    Kirishima's main peak, legendary heavenly-descent site, famous for the treasure spear thrust into the summit.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-4560 · Official / source link

  • Ojibaru Park

    Natural park at the foot of Takachiho Peak, with play equipment, go-karts, animals, and camping.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-3393 · Official / source link

  • Oku-Kirishima Miike Camp Village

    Quiet campground on forest-surrounded Miike lakeshore, with tent sites, elevated bungalows, and outdoor Wi-Fi.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-4038 · Official / source link

  • Takaharu Town General Sports Park Soccer and Multipurpose Fields

    Five minutes from Takaharu IC, within three hours from Fukuoka, with five onsen inns and a campground.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-1484 · Official / source link

  • Oku-Kirishima Ojibaru Park Campground

    Park campground below Takachiho Peak with go-karts, a roller slide, trout fishing, cottages, and tent sites.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-3393 · Official / source link

  • Miike no Yu Auto Campground

    Campground for nature camping and a skin-beautifying onsen, with powered decks, auto sites, and a dog run.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-4088 · Official / source link

  • Takaharu Golf Club

    Kirishima foothills, midway between Takaharu IC and Kobayashi IC. Opened in 1977. 9 holes, 2989 yards, par 36.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-0376 · Official / source link

  • Takaharu Town Gymnasium Annex

    Wooden gassho-zukuri building from 1935, rare in the prefecture, used for kendo and judo practice and camps.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-2111 · Official / source link
