  • Lake Miike

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-4560

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-3838

    One of the six Kirishima shrines, on Takachiho Peak's east slope overlooking Miike, enshrining Izanaki and Izanami.

    Takaharu, Miyazaki · 0984-42-1007

    Kirishima Six Gongen shrine whose cedar approach is a national natural monument.
