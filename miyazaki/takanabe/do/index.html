Things to do in Takanabe, Miyazaki

Takanabe · Miyazaki · 7 places

Do

  • Takanabe Beach (Kaguchihama)

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-22-5588

    Shallow calm western Japan surf spot, selected among Japan's 100 best bathing beaches, a loggerhead nesting site.

  • MASUDA Stadium, Takanabe Comprehensive Sports Park Baseball Field

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-23-3701

    Convenient urban stadium loved by locals, where company and university teams camp each spring and autumn; prefecture's best drainage.

  • Inoue Shoten Sports Center Takanabe Town Gymnasium and Archery Hall

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-23-3701

    One of the prefecture's largest gyms for judo, kendo, volleyball, basketball, badminton and table tennis. Ten-archer hall holds short-range events.

  • Indoor Multipurpose Plaza, Omaru Riverside Sports Park

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-23-3701

    Rainy-day indoor sports base for futsal, tennis, practice and preschool recreation, with baseball nets and batting machines.

  • Omaru Riverside Sports Park Baseball Field

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-23-3701

    Used for youth, rubber-ball and hardball baseball, no rubber fence or nets, lights for night games, company and university camps.

  • Kaguchihama

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-22-5588

    Popular Takanabe surf point with gentle waves for beginners, camping, summer beach, and winter natural oysters.

  • Takanabe Town Sports Center

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-23-3701

    Four sand-filled artificial turf courts with lighting, usable at night.
