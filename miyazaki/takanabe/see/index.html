Sights in Takanabe, Miyazaki

Takanabe · Miyazaki · 3 places

See

  • Maizuru Park

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-22-5588

    Historic park of former Takanabe Castle with a history museum, cherries, and a lantern festival.

  • Mochida Kofun Group and Takanabe Daishi

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-22-5588

    85 ancient tombs from the early 4th to early 7th century on the Omaru River plateau, a Japan Heritage site.

  • Takanabe Wetland

    Takanabe, Miyazaki · 0983-23-3326

    Wetland near Meirin no Yu formed by spring water after dam work, with rare plants, insects, and a suspension bridge.
