Things to do in Tsunomachi, Miyazaki

Tsunomachi · Miyazaki · 6 places

Do

  • Tsuno Town Fujimi Park Athletic Stadium

    Tsunomachi, Miyazaki · 0983-25-5255

    Natural-grass field has hosted JFL. All-weather 400m JAAF Class 3 track, 1,128 seats, full grass; students train on holidays.

  • Tsuno Town Fujimi Park West Multipurpose Ground Soccer Field

    Tsunomachi, Miyazaki · 0983-25-5255

    Hosts Kyushu League, Kyushu meets, and prefectural high-school soccer championships on natural grass. Track stadium and indoor gym adjacent.

  • Tsuno Town Fujimi Park Indoor Gymnasium

    Tsunomachi, Miyazaki · 0983-25-5255

    Artificial turf allows practical practice. Used for many corporate baseball camps and soccer practice.

  • Tsuno Town Fujimi Park Baseball Field

    Tsunomachi, Miyazaki · 0983-25-5255

    Hosts women's corporate softball camps and many rubber-ball baseball prefectural tournaments. Track stadium and indoor gym adjacent.

  • Tsuno Town Fujimi Park East Multipurpose Ground Softball Field

    Tsunomachi, Miyazaki · 0983-25-5255

    Used for many corporate softball and youth baseball tournaments. Baseball field, indoor gym, and track stadium are adjacent.

  • Tsuno Town Fujimi Sports Park Tennis Courts

    Tsunomachi, Miyazaki · 0983-25-5255

    Mainly a practice place for adult club soft tennis.
