Things to do in Achi, Nagano

Achi · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Gessen Onsen no Hanamomo (Hanamomo Village)

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-43-3001

    Flowering peach grove of about 5,000 red, white, and pink trees near Enbara IC, with bloom status announced by phone in season.

  • Hirugami Onsen Sato

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-43-3001

    Hot-spring area with hotels, ryokan, and day-trip baths along the Achi River; the water is strongly alkaline at pH 9.7.

  • Hirugami Onsen Asa City

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-43-3001

    Produce and souvenir shop at a morning-market plaza, displaying seasonal foods and local specialties.

    Hirugami Onsen morning market selling seasonal local produce and Achi village specialties.

  • Jibuzaka Kogen Ski Area

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-47-1111

    Ski area with a family slope for first-time skiers, accessible from the national highway.

    Jibuzaka Kogen ski area at about 1,187 m, directly off Route 153 in southern Nagano.
