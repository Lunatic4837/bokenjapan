Sights in Achi, Nagano

Achi · Nagano · 11 places

See

  • Hanamomo Hassho no Chi (Hanamomo Kaido)

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-46-2001

    Roadside stretch of about 5,000 flowering peach trees along National Route 256, called Hanamomo Kaido and viewed from a car.

    Hanamomo Kaido along National Route 256 between Achi and Nagiso, with about 5,000 flowering peach trees visible from the road.

  • Kosaka Shrine

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-43-3001

    Shrine enshrining the three Sumiyoshi sea deities, with giant sacred Japanese cedars said to be over 2,000 years old, horse chestnut trees, and poem monuments on the grounds.

    Misaka Shrine dedicated to the three Sumiyoshi sea deities, with giant sugi and tochi said to exceed 2,000 years old along the old Tosando.

  • Nippon'ichi no Hoshizora Namiai Park

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-48-8555

    Park at 1,200 m in the mountains with guided stargazing sessions and other star-viewing programs.

    Namiai Park star-gazing site with guided seasonal constellation talks (indoor video program when raining).

  • Tosando Sonohara Bijita Center Hahaki Ki Kan

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-44-2011

    Mini museum with local exhibits and a cafe, Southern Alps views, seasonal red spider lilies, and sales of Mt. Ena and Fujimidai climbing badges and waterfall prayer plates.

    Sonohara Visitor Center Hahakigi-kan mini museum and cafe on the old Tosando, with South Alps views and local hiking badges.

  • Hebunsu So no Hara SnowWorld

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-44-2311

    Ski area five minutes from Sonohara IC, with a gondola to 1,400 m slopes, two beginner courses, snowboard and snowshoe rental, a Kids World snow-play slope, and a glamping site limited to two groups.

  • Koma Tsunagi Cherry Blossoms

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-43-3001

    Cherry tree, a single Edo-higan estimated at over 450 years old, said to be where Minamoto no Yoshitsune tied his horse, and illuminated in flowering season.

    Komatsunagi cherry, an Edohigan estimated over 450 years old, lit during bloom and tied to a Yoshitsune horse-tethering legend.

  • Tsukigawa Onsen

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-44-2321

    Set in historic, legendary Sonohara, it offers silky hot springs amid nature and clear air.

  • Namiai Shrine

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-47-2001

    Built about 250 years ago, it enshrines Prince Tadayoshi and is cherished by local people.

  • Shimo-Seinaiji Suwa Shrine Kagura Music

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-46-2001

    Ancient five-piece kagura music unique to the village is performed at the spring festival.

  • Seinaiji Handmade Fireworks

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-46-2001

    Designated an intangible folk cultural property in 1992, it also took part in the 1998 Nagano Olympics.

  • Araragi Highland Country Club

    Achi, Nagano · 0265-47-2331

    A grand golf course surrounded by nature, enjoyed leisurely amid seasonal scenery.
