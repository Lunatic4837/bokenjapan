Things to do in Aoki, Nagano

Aoki · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Aoki Ei Yokote Campground

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0707

    Campground at 1,285 m on the mid-slope of Mt. Toka, with auto sites and lodging buildings, near Tazawa Onsen, Kutsukake Onsen, and the Shinshu Insect Museum.

  • Shinshu Maruberry Auto Campground

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0001

    The setting amid Shinshu's magnificent nature is superb, and the restaurant serves handmade soba.

  • Tokanzan Trailhead

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0111

    A mountain trailhead for hiking and trekking.

  • Kutsukake Onsen

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0111

    A quiet hot-spring area retaining a therapeutic-bath character, worthy of a hidden onsen.
