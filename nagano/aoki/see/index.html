Sights in Aoki, Nagano

Aoki · Nagano · 12 places

See

  • Shinshu Konchu Museum

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-37-3988

    Insect museum with butterfly and stag-beetle specimens, about 1,000 insect books, nearly all of about 250 Japanese butterfly species, plus jewel beetles, longhorn beetles, weevils, and bees.

  • Aoki Rekishi Bunka Museum

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0303

    History museum displaying manuscripts and books of haiku poet Kuribayashi Isshiro, Edo peasant-uprising records, and pottery, stone tools, and coins from Jomon to Nara-Heian sites.

  • Aoki Folk Museum

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0303

    Museum themed on the Showa era, showing farm tools, folk tools, vehicles, and toys from village life, including three-wheeled trucks and treadle sewing machines.

  • Aoki Kyodo Art Museum

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-3838

    Art museum on Daihoji Temple grounds where the national-treasure three-story pagoda can be seen, with permanent and special galleries and a 500-yen drink-and-admission set.

  • Furusato Koen Aoki

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0111

    Lawn park beside a roadside station, surrounded by the three Aoki mountains, with play equipment.

  • Aoki Tami Puru

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-2224

    Pool with a competition pool, a flowing-water pool, and a children's pool, near Mount Ogami.

  • Aoki Marettogorufu Ba

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-2224

    Golf course using a mountain, with an undulating course on a sunny slope and usable in winter.

  • Aoki Sogo Gym Judo Ba

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-2224

  • Aoki Sogo Gym Kendo Ba

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-2224

    Kendo hall in Aoki.

  • Shunara Pass

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0111

    Amiya Shrine on the Aoki-Chikuhoku border has more than 800 stone Buddhas.

  • Tonodo Pass

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0111

    Historic path linking Daihoji's three-story pagoda and Anrakuji's octagonal pagoda, both National Treasures; pedestrians only.

  • Mount Iizuna

    Aoki, Nagano · 0268-49-0111

    Japanese red pine forest spreads around the mountain, and Daihoji has a National Treasure pagoda.
