Sights in Asahimura, Nagano

Asahimura · Nagano · 12 places

See

  • Korin Tera no Shidare Sakura

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2001

    Weeping cherry viewing spot in Asahimura.

  • Furukawa Tera no Katakuri

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2061

    Dogtooth violet colony in woods west of Furukawa Temple, where the Himegifu butterfly, called the spring goddess, appears when the flowers bloom.

  • Korin Tera

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2309

    Temple with a main hall built in the late 18th century and a late-Muromachi wooden standing Fudo Myoo designated a village treasure.

  • Takei Castle Ruins

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2004

    Castle ruins attributed to the Mimura clan of southwestern Chikuma in the Sengoku period, now a historical park with a tea room reservable for tea gatherings and meals.

  • Asahimura Doso Kami

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2004

    Roadside deity in Asahimura.

  • Asahimura Folk History Museum

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2359

    Folk history museum in Asahimura.

  • Asahimura Art Museum

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2359

    Art museum holding works by village sculptor Kamijo Shunsuke and village-linked Yanagisawa Kanpu and Mimura Yuki, with special exhibitions and an attached history and folklore museum.

  • Midori no Koroshiamu

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-3700

    Multipurpose outdoor facility with a stage that can also be used as a small open-air concert hall.

  • Cherry Blossoms at Asahi Village Central Community Center

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2004

    Old central cherry trees signal spring, and blossoms at the neighboring field are also worth seeing.

  • Kogawa-ji Temple

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2061

    Shingon temple founded in 1096 and said to have been revived during the Eiroku period.

  • Asahi Village Tennis Courts

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2004

    Tennis courts in Asahi Village for outdoor summer activities.

  • Asahi Mallet Golf Course

    Asahimura, Nagano · 0263-99-2001

    A varied 18-hole, par-72 woodland course that also offers forest bathing.
