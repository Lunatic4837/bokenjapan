  • Yakiniku Shokudo Fusan

    やきにく食堂　ふーさん

    Dining in Azumino

    Yakiniku restaurant in an old-house-style single-story building serving charcoal-grilled black wagyu with Azumino wasabi and Shinshu miso, plus horse sashimi, ramen and steak; Nakabusa Onsen coffee is free.

    (株)安曇野 肉のマルト

    Dining in Azumino

    Meat shop selling Shinshu Premium beef, pork and chicken, with an adjoining eatery where meat is served.
