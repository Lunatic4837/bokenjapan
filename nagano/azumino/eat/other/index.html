  • Azumino Bazaar Hotaka Jinja-ten

    あづみ野バザール 穂高神社店

    Green-tent stall at Hotaka Shrine near Hotaka Station selling oyaki, roasted Anno sweet potato, house-roasted coffee, natural-yeast bread, seasonal gelato and souvenirs; open year-round.

    安曇野直売店 たまごの駅

    Dining in Azumino

    Egg shop directly operated by the Aida Joint Poultry Cooperative, selling eggs gathered each morning that can be eaten on site.
