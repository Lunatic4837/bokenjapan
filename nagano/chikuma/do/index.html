Things to do in Chikuma, Nagano

Chikuma · Nagano · 6 places

Do

  • Tsukeba

    Chikuma, Nagano · 026-275-1326

    Fresh Chikuma River dace is served salt-grilled, miso-grilled, fried, or tempura; ayu dishes then continue until late October.

  • Sarashina no Sato Ancient Experience Park and History Museum

    Chikuma, Nagano · 026-276-7511

    A restored Jomon village from about 4,500 years ago offers experiences such as stone-tool making.

  • Chikuma River Manyo Park

    Chikuma, Nagano · 026-275-1753

    Twenty-seven poem monuments include Chikuma River verses and works by literati who visited here.

  • Chikuma River

    Chikuma, Nagano · 0267-92-2167

    Fishing for char and yamame born in the clear Chikuma River opens from February 16, drawing many anglers.

  • Shiroyama Historic Site Park (Arato Castle)

    Chikuma, Nagano · 026-275-6653

    A Sengoku mountain castle restored with attention to methods displays excavated pottery and was an NHK Furin Kazan film site.

  • Ipponmatsu Pass

    Chikuma, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    This junction of Chikuma City and Omi Village also leads to Mount Obasute, famous for the abandonment legend.
