Izakaya & bars in Chikuma, Nagano

Eat in Chikuma · 7 places

Izakaya & bars

  • Izakaya Kohaku

    古波久

    Dining in Chikuma

    Izakaya serving oshibori udon and snacks such as horse sashimi, thick omelette, braised pork and octopus karaage; open 18:00-23:30 (Sun 22:30), lunch May-Oct 11:30-13:30, closed 1st and 3rd Wednesdays.

  • FUJI

    ふじ

    Dining in Chikuma

    Bar serving Scotch whisky and snacks made with local ingredients, plus non-alcoholic drinks; open 19:00 until last order, with irregular closing days.

  • Yoridokoro Fuku Kaeru

    より処 福かえる

    Dining in Chikuma

    Japanese izakaya; open 18:00-22:00, closed Mondays (inquiry required), 30 seats.

  • Ushiwaka

    牛若

    Dining in Chikuma

    Izakaya serving yakitori with optional garlic sauce, plus beef ami, horse sashimi and gizzard sashimi; open 18:00-23:00 (last order 22:30), closed Sundays, or Mondays when Monday is a holiday.

  • Hagoromo

    羽衣

    Dining in Chikuma

    Japanese restaurant serving beef tongue stew, Hagoromo salad, seasonal horse mackerel with myoga, and grilled Hokkaido hokke; open 17:30-21:30 (last order), closed irregularly.

  • Minoya Yondaime

    みのや四代目

    Dining in Chikuma

    Izakaya serving motsu nabe, fugu dishes and horse sashimi; open 18:00-22:00, closed Sundays, Mondays and irregular days.

  • Yamayoshi

    やまよし

    Dining in Chikuma

    Izakaya in Kamiyamada Onsen serving grilled fish among about 80 dishes, with Nagano Koshihikari rice; open 17:00-24:00 (last order 23:30), closed Tuesdays.
