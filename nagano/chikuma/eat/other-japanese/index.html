Other Japanese

  • えんや

    Enya

    Dining in Chikuma

    Serves mixed fried chicken, tonkatsu and chicken sauce cutlet; about 25 seats; open about 16:00-22:00, with irregular closures including 31 Dec-4 Jan.

  • くりや

    Kuriya

    Dining in Chikuma

    Japanese restaurant in Kamiyamada Onsen serving simmered cutlet, simmered-fish set, sashimi set, eggplant dengaku and stewed horse offal; lunch 12:00-15:00, dinner 15:00-20:00 (LO 19:00), closed the 1st and 3rd Wednesdays and Thursdays.

  • 湯の華銭湯 瑞祥 上山田本館

    Yunohana Sento ZUISHO Kamiyamada Honkan

    Dining in Chikuma

    Dining room at a public bath serving ginger-pork sets, loin-katsu sets, katsu curry and gyoza; weekdays 11:00-20:30, Fri-Sun and holidays 10:30-21:30 (last order 30 min earlier); closed the third Tuesday.

  • 河菜

    Kawana

    Dining in Chikuma

    Japanese restaurant in Kamiyamada Onsen cooking seasonal dishes with local ingredients; menu examples include wagyu steak, grilled fish, fried dishes and seasonal fugu; open 17:00-22:00, closed Wednesdays.

  • 田中園

    Tanakaen

    Dining in Chikuma

    Restaurant serving oyaki and inari sushi, including a set with miso soup, simmered dishes, and pickles; open 9:30-14:00, closed Sundays and Mondays.

  • 湯上がり食堂

    Yugari Shokudo (Hakuchoen)

    Dining in Chikuma

    Japanese restaurant on the second floor of the Hakuchoen public bath in Chikuma, serving soba, set meals, rice bowls, ankake yakisoba and yakitori; open 11:00-20:15 (last entry 20:30), closed the second Tuesday.

  • 味のきばらし

    Aji-no-Kibarashi

    Dining in Chikuma

    Serves set meals at lunch and appetizers at dinner, including pork with a sweet-savory sauce; lunch 11:30-14:00, dinner 17:00-21:00, closed Sundays and irregular days.

  • 七福

    Oyaki Shop Shichifuku

    Dining in Chikuma

    Evening oyaki shop in Kamiyamada Onsen serving oyaki with house-made miso, plus ramen, gyoza and drinks; open 18:30-22:00, closed Sundays.
