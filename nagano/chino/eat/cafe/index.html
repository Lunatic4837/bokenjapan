Cafe in Chino, Nagano

Eat in Chino · 13 places

Cafe

  • Peter's Field

    Peter's Field

    Dining in Chino

    Kitchen car mainly at Tateshina lakeside selling light meals and sweets; the menu changes by season and may use garden-grown wild strawberries, raspberries and grapes. Closed irregularly.

  • Flora & Fauna

    八ヶ岳カフェ＆ベーカリー Flora & Fauna

    Dining in Chino

    Cafe and bakery on the Venus Line serving house-made bread, seasonal pasta, coffee and Otari maple-sap tea; open 11:00-15:00 (last order 14:30), closed Wed-Fri in summer and Mon-Fri in winter.

  • Yatsugatake Octet

    八ヶ岳オクテット

    Dining in Chino

    Cafe and bakery in the Chino Station area serving Tateshina beef loco moco with a hamburger steak, runny fried egg and vegetables; open 10:00-19:00; closed Thursdays and certain days.

  • Tateshina Gouter

    たてしなグーテ

    Dining in Chino

    Cafe serving a lunch-only beef stew with salad, bread and a drink, plus cake and drinks; cakes and Jomon cookies are also sold. Open 11:00-18:00, closed Wednesday and Thursday.

  • Shokudo Cafe Field Note

    食堂カフェ フィールドノート

    Dining in Chino

    Cafe serving coffee and handmade dishes; 890-yen lunch includes soup, salad and coffee (not Sundays or holidays), evening a la carte includes bakery gratin (880 yen), open 11:30-15:00 and 17:00-23:00, closed Tuesdays.

  • Tateshina Jiyu Noen 808 Cafe

    たてしな自由農園 808Café

    Dining in Chino

    Farm-shop cafe serving a highland-vegetable salad and bakery goods made with Tateshina and Yatsugatake ingredients; open 10:00-17:30, closed Wednesdays.

  • TOP'S360°

    TOP'S360°

    Dining in Chino

    Cafe-restaurant at the Skyliner lift terminus in Kurumayama Skypark serving oven pizza, taco rice, venison keema curry, desserts and drinks; summer 9:00-16:00, winter meals 11:00-14:00 and cafe 9:00-15:45.

  • Lakeside cafe in Chino

    珈琲&BEANS カリオモン

    Romaji: Coffee & Beans Kariomon

    Dining in Chino

    Lakeside cafe at Lake Shirakaba serving freshly roasted coffee; roasting experiences and coffee classes are offered; open 10:00-17:00; closed Mondays (Tuesdays when Monday is a holiday).

  • Cafe Rest Mokuba

    カフェレスト木馬

    Dining in Chino

    Cafe-restaurant beside Pilatus Ski Resort with glass walls facing the slopes; open in winter only, 10:00-16:00, irregular closures.

  • Tateshina Kogen Barakura English Garden

    蓼科高原 バラクラ イングリッシュ ガーデン

    Dining in Chino

    Garden venue serving afternoon tea, handmade scones and cakes, and British country lunch; open every day, with hours that vary by season.

  • Utsuwa to Cafe Tosenbo Suguridaira

    器とCafe 陶仙房 須栗平

    Dining in Chino

    Gallery cafe in a renovated early-Showa farmhouse in Chino, showing local artists' crafts and serving local seasonal ingredients; hours and closed days change by season.

  • Merry Cafe Snowy

    Merry Cafe Snowy

    Dining in Chino

    Forest cafe in Chino serving house-roasted coffee and homemade lunch; dogs allowed inside and on the terrace, with a dog run. Tue-Wed 11:30-14:00, Thu-Sat 11:30-17:00 (LO 16:00); closed Sun-Mon; hours vary by season.

  • Mori no Kitchen queue

    森のキッチン queue

    Dining in Chino

    Highland cafe in Tateshina, Chino, serving French bread and a chef's handmade menu, plus coffee brewed with Tateshina water; open mid-April to November, 10:00-17:00, closed Thursdays.
