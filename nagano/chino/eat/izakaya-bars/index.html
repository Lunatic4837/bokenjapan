Izakaya & bars

  • Sakura Saku

    さくらさく

    Dining in Chino

    Horse-meat specialty izakaya offering various horse-meat dishes; lunch 11:30-14:00, dinner 17:00-22:30, closed Tuesdays.

  • Bar & Live the Piano Man

    Bar & ライブハウス ピアノマン

    Dining in Chino

    Bar and live house serving food and drinks; open 19:00-25:00, closed Mondays, Tuesdays and certain other days.

  • Shokushuya Umaimonoya Nishiki

    食酒屋 うまいものや にし季

    Dining in Chino

    Izakaya a 3-minute walk from the station serving thick handmade tamagoyaki made with five eggs from designated domestic farms, plus basashi and shio-koji chicken karaage; open 17:00-23:00; closed Sundays.

  • Izakaya in Chino

    おごっつぉう居酒屋 あいのじゅく

    Dining in Chino

    Izakaya on the first floor of Hotel Chino; soft fillet cutlet is the most popular dish. Open 17:30-22:00, closed irregularly.

  • Ishokuya Ao Togarashi

    居食や 青とうがらし

    Dining in Chino

    Izakaya in an old house at the Tateshina entrance on the Venus Line, serving salads with seasonal local ingredients and pasta; open 18:00-24:00, closed Tuesdays (irregular closures December-March).
