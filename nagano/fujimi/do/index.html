Things to do in Fujimi, Nagano

Fujimi · Nagano · 5 places

Do

  • Tachiba Kawa Campground

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-66-3228

    Free-site campground in riverside woods with the Yatsugatake mountains behind it, where barbecue and river play are possible, with flush toilets and a cooking area.

  • Fujimi Highlands Ski Area

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-66-2932

    Ski area 10 minutes from Kobuchizawa IC, where snowboarding is prohibited, with a separate Soli Snow Land for small children.

  • Fujimi Highlands Resort

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-66-2121

    Highland resort that can be enjoyed in spring, summer, autumn, and winter.

  • Fujimi Kogen Paragliding School

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-66-2278

    In summer, visitors take off from the ski area, and anyone can easily try paragliding.

  • Fujimi Kogen Ski Resort

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-66-2932

    A skier-only family resort on Yatsugatake's south slope, with 100-meter-wide runs ideal for beginners.
