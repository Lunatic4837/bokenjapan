Sights in Fujimi, Nagano

Fujimi · Nagano · 9 places

See

  • Fujimi Highlands Hana Village

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-66-2932

    Flower village in Fujimi.

  • Fujimi Highlands Sozo no Mori Chokoku Park

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-62-9342

    Sculpture park at 1,450 m showing 50 outdoor works from a 10-year international sculpture symposium, with three lookouts over Mt. Fuji, the Alps, and Lake Suwa.

  • Hachigagaku Arupaka Ranch

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-75-2554

    Alpaca ranch at the foot of Yatsugatake, where the Northern and Southern Alps are visible and visitors can feed, photograph, and walk with the animals.

  • Mezondobyu Hachigagaku

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-61-1613

    Small hotel from the owner's villa at Mount Nyukasa's foot near Fujimi Panorama (1,000 m), with Yatsugatake and Tateshina views, homemade bread and jam, and pork shabu-shabu.

  • Fujimi Mori no Ofisu Living

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-78-8009

    Guesthouse attached to the Fujimi Mori no Office coworking space, with four rooms, a shared kitchen and living room, and a wood deck.

  • Lilies of the Valley on Mount Nyukasa

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-62-9342

    Mount Nyukasa features 200,000 German and one million Japanese lilies of the valley, best from late May to mid-June.

  • Lotuses at Idojiri Historic Site Park

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-64-2044

    Ancient Oga lotuses, germinated from seeds over 2,000 years old, bloom in mid-July.

  • Idojiri Archaeological Museum

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-64-2044

    Displays pottery, clay figurines, and stone tools from Idojiri sites on Yatsugatake's southern foothills.

  • Fujimi Kogen Athletics Stadium

    Fujimi, Nagano · 0266-66-2931

    A Japan Athletics Federation-certified stadium hosting high-altitude training and various competitions.
