Sights in Hara, Nagano

Hara · Nagano · 10 places

See

  • Hachigagaku Chuo Highlands no Autumn Leaves

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-79-7929

    Autumn leaf viewing spot in Hara.

  • Hara Kyodo Kan

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-79-7930

    Museum of relocated late-Edo to early-Meiji houses reproducing late-1950s rural life, with sericulture tools and a weaving trial free unless you take work home.

  • Sho no Ki So Guraundo

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-74-2311

    Sports ground large enough for two baseball games at once, with mountain cherries around Golden Week and a nearby lodge with a natural hot-spring indoor bath.

  • Hachigagaku (Amida Gaku) Tozan Kosu

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-79-7929

    Day-hike to Amidadake's 2,805 m summit; clear days show Mt. Fuji, the South and Central Alps, Ontake, Norikura, and distant North Alps, with no toilets from Funayama junction; climbing gear needed.

  • Shi no Hara Engei

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-79-5605

    Produce stand selling alstroemeria, mini tomatoes, and corn at 1,100 m elevation, with tomato picking from late July to late September.

  • Resutoran Orudiizu

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-75-3993

    Western restaurant and pension surrounded by nature, serving more than 30 dishes, with dogs allowed.

  • Teuchi Chisoba Ichihatsu (Hitoha)

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-79-6163

    Soba shop serving handmade noodles from buckwheat flour milled in different ways, each with a different flavor and texture.

  • Deri & Kafe (K)

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-74-2684

    Cafe inside Yatsugatake Nature Culture Park serving meals of local vegetables and ingredients, with dog-friendly terrace seats.

  • Minto Garden

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-74-2208

    Cafe in a natural garden serving highland-vegetable salad, naan curry, and chiffon cake with cream; dogs of any breed are allowed if they do not disturb others.

  • Chuodo Hara PA (Parking Area) Kudari

    Hara, Nagano · 0266-72-5335

    Expressway parking area serving market-direct fresh seafood, reachable from local roads via Puratto Park, with a card and e-money shop selling Nagano and Yamanashi souvenirs.
