Things to do in Iida, Nagano

Iida · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Mizuhiki Craft

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851

    Various places in Iida have mizuhiki craft exhibition halls and hands-on experience facilities.

  • Tenpaku Festival Heritage Hall

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-36-2211

    Displays cover the nationally designated Shimotsuki Festival, Akiba Kaido, and Kamimura nature, folklore, and history.

  • Ohira Plateau

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-36-1002

    This 1,300-meter plateau in the Minami Alps UNESCO Eco-Park offers nature observation, lodging, and camping.

  • Matoba Inari Annual Festival

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-36-2211

    Its main deities are Daikokuten and Kodama Daimyojin, honored with masked bow-drawing kagura.
