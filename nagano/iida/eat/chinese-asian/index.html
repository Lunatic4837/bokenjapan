Chinese & Asian in Iida, Nagano

Eat in Iida · 6 places

Chinese & Asian

  • Chugoku Kanton Ryori Sansuiro

    中国広東料理　山水楼

    Dining in Iida

    Cantonese restaurant using Shinshu ingredients, with dishes such as seasonal vegetable and seafood stir-fry and Shinshu beef belly stew; open 11:30-14:30 and 17:00-21:30, closed Sunday lunch.

  • Amakaraya

    甘辛屋

    Dining in Iida

    Iida restaurant serving ramen, set meals, Chinese home cooking and snacks with alcohol; takeout and banquets (private room) available. Open 11:30-14:00 and 17:30-21:00, or to 22:00 Fri-Sat; closed Tuesdays.

  • Hyakkatei

    百香亭

    Dining in Iida

    Dim sum restaurant serving handmade xiaolongbao, ramen, fried rice, stir-fries and Chinese tea; open 11:00-21:30, closed Mondays, or the next day if Monday is a holiday.

  • Hotel Hisai Chinese Cuisine Ryuen

    ホテル久井・中国料理龍苑

    Dining in Iida

    Chinese restaurant at Hotel Hisai; lunch 10:30-14:30 and dinner 17:00-22:00 by reservation, with fruit platters, dessert plates and a children's menu on request.

  • Chinese or Asian restaurant in Iida

    ちゃあしゅう屋 飯田店

    Dining in Iida

    Shop serving three kinds of charcoal-grilled chashu, garlic-seasoned gyoza and karaage; open 11:00-21:00, closing days irregular.

  • HAYAKAWA

    HAYAKAWA

    Dining in Iida

    Bite-size gyoza specialty shop; lunch 11:00-16:00, with evening service 16:00-19:30 about once a month; closed Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday.
