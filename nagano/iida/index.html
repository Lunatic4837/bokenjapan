Sights

  • Omi Village Butai Sakura

    Butai-zakura at former Zagoto Elementary Butai schoolhouse (built Meiji 7): a rare half-double weeping higan cherry about 350 years old.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851 · Official / source link

  • Toyama Sakura Kawazu Sakura

    Toyama-go cherry plantings: about 60-70 Somei-yoshino along 2.5 km of the Toyama River left bank, plus early Kawazu and weeping cherries on the right.

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-34-1071 · Official / source link

  • Ho Hisashi Tera no Shakunage (Shakunage)

    Hojuji Temple rhododendrons on a terrace overlooking the Tenryu River; bloom typically late April to mid-May.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-26-9252 · Official / source link

  • no Soko Yama Forest Park

    Nosokoyama Forest Park with cherry blossoms in spring, maple color in autumn, playgrounds, and camping facilities.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-0915 · Official / source link

  • Myo Kon Park

    Myokin Park for mallet golf, cycling, nearby amago fishing on the Matsukawa, camping, azaleas, and autumn color.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4511 · Official / source link

  • Mitsuishi no Shidarezakura

    Weeping cherry by the Sanseki stones on the old Akiba road in Shimohisakata; about 300 years old and 15 m tall.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851 · Official / source link

  • Sakuramaru no Fufu Sakura

    Meoto-zakura at former Iida Castle Sakuramaru: a weeping cherry and Edohigan said to be about 400 years old and roughly 20 m tall.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851 · Official / source link

  • Kiyohide Sakura

    Seishu-zakura, an Edohigan at Atago Shrine traditionally planted in 1240; Iida oldest cherry and a city natural monument.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4511 · Official / source link

  • Omiya Tori no Sakura Namiki

    About 700 m of cherry trees (about 150, mainly Somei-yoshino) from Omiya Shrine toward Apple Avenue, with blossom festivals and night lighting.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851 · Official / source link

  • Sen Sho Tera no Shidarezakura

    Weeping cherry over 400 years old in front of Senshoji main hall, with night lighting during bloom.

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851 · Official / source link
