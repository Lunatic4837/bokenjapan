See in Iida

Iida · Nagano · 23 places

See

  • Omi Village Butai Sakura

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851

    Butai-zakura at former Zagoto Elementary Butai schoolhouse (built Meiji 7): a rare half-double weeping higan cherry about 350 years old.

  • Toyama Sakura Kawazu Sakura

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-34-1071

    Toyama-go cherry plantings: about 60-70 Somei-yoshino along 2.5 km of the Toyama River left bank, plus early Kawazu and weeping cherries on the right.

  • Ho Hisashi Tera no Shakunage (Shakunage)

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-26-9252

    Hojuji Temple rhododendrons on a terrace overlooking the Tenryu River; bloom typically late April to mid-May.

  • no Soko Yama Forest Park

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-0915

    Nosokoyama Forest Park with cherry blossoms in spring, maple color in autumn, playgrounds, and camping facilities.

  • Myo Kon Park

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4511

    Myokin Park for mallet golf, cycling, nearby amago fishing on the Matsukawa, camping, azaleas, and autumn color.

  • Mitsuishi no Shidarezakura

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851

    Weeping cherry by the Sanseki stones on the old Akiba road in Shimohisakata; about 300 years old and 15 m tall.

  • Sakuramaru no Fufu Sakura

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851

    Meoto-zakura at former Iida Castle Sakuramaru: a weeping cherry and Edohigan said to be about 400 years old and roughly 20 m tall.

  • Kiyohide Sakura

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4511

    Seishu-zakura, an Edohigan at Atago Shrine traditionally planted in 1240; Iida oldest cherry and a city natural monument.

  • Omiya Tori no Sakura Namiki

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851

    About 700 m of cherry trees (about 150, mainly Somei-yoshino) from Omiya Shrine toward Apple Avenue, with blossom festivals and night lighting.

  • Sen Sho Tera no Shidarezakura

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851

    Weeping cherry over 400 years old in front of Senshoji main hall, with night lighting during bloom.

  • Yasutomi Cherry Tree

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-4851

    Prefectural natural monument Edohigan cherry at Iida City Art Museum, estimated 450 years old and 20 meters tall.

  • Hinatsu Konosuke Memorial Museum

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-8118

    Modeled on Iida-born writer Hinatsu Konosuke's later home, it displays his books, letters, and belongings.

  • Iida City Archaeological Museum

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-26-9009

    Displays pre-pottery artifacts from Chuo Expressway excavations and stands beside Kaizenji Temple.

  • Iida City Kawamoto Kihachiro Puppet Art Museum

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-23-3594

    Art museum of puppet artist Kawamoto Kihachiro, showing over 100 puppets from NHK dramas, animation, and commercials.

  • Tenryukyo Autumn Foliage

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-27-2946

    Where the Tenryu River is narrowest, Tenryukyo's autumn colors peak from early to mid-November.

  • Kunio Yanagita Museum

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-22-8118

    This relocated 1927 Tokyo study of folklore pioneer Kunio Yanagita displays his books and Iida connections.

  • Shusui Beauty Painting Museum

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-53-3755

    Active nihonga painter Shusui, pupil of the late Ito Tansui, permanently exhibits his beauty paintings.

  • Kannon Reisui Spring

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-34-1071

    This drinkable Ryuenji spring, long used as household water, is very high in calcium and magnesium.

  • Ryuenji Temple

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-34-1071

    Toyama clan graves lie beneath old cedars, and Kannon Reisui spring rises within the temple grounds.

  • Yanagishima Checkpoint Site

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-34-1071

    Toyama Tosa-no-kami built it on Ieyasu's orders to police Osaka fugitives and control timber.

  • Former Zakouji Omi School Building

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-23-4222

    Built as both kabuki stage and school, it is the prefecture's oldest school and draws spring blossom visitors.

  • Imada Puppets

    Iida, Nagano · 0265-27-3004

    Puppetry began in 1704 to enliven the Omiya Hachiman Shrine festival and is dedicated there each October.

  • Kudagayu Ritual

    Iida, Nagano · 0260-36-2211

    Held at Nakago Hachiman Shrine to divine that year's crop harvest.
