Sights in Iijima, Nagano

Iijima · Nagano · 7 places

See

  • Sennin Tsuka Park

    Iijima, Nagano · 0265-86-6764

    Park and campsite with a lake reflecting the Central Alps, cherry blossoms, an auto campsite and mallet golf; legend holds soldiers from fallen Kitayama Castle were buried here.

  • Yoda Setsu Park

    Iijima, Nagano · 090-4750-1398

    Nature park by the Yodagiri River with camping, barbecue sites, tennis, and a pool; Kosumo no Mizu was selected among Shinshu's 15 noted waters, and a 200 m cherry tunnel is lit at night in season.

  • Ji Fuku In Shidare Sakura

    Iijima, Nagano · 0265-86-3111

    Weeping cherry viewing spot in Iijima.

  • Kosumo no Mizu (Shinshu no Meisui Hi Mizu)

    Iijima, Nagano · 0265-86-6046

    A well in Yodagiri Park pumps soft groundwater recharged over many years around Mt. Kosumo in the Central Alps, and many people come to draw water.

  • Iijima Folk History Museum Iijima Camp Office

    Iijima, Nagano · 0265-86-4212

    Historic building established in 1677 as a shogunate territory office, later the Ina Prefecture office in the Meiji era and restored in 1993.

  • Agurinecha Iijima

    Iijima, Nagano · 0265-86-6072

    Exchange facility meant for contact between people and nature, with lodging attached.

  • Furusato no Aji Iijima

    Iijima, Nagano · 0265-86-6415

    A shop serves Tenryo Gohei gohei mochi with Japanese-pepper and walnut sauce, oyaki filled with bean paste, nozawana, pumpkin, eggplant, mushroom, or daikon, and sakura oyaki.
