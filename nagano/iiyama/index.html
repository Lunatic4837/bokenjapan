  • Na no Hana Park no Himawari

    Nanohana Park spring rape-blossom fields (late April-early May), a viewpoint over the Chikuma River toward the Sekita range.

    Iiyama, Nagano · 0269-62-7000 · Official / source link

  • Hokuryu Mizumi no Na no Hana

    Iiyama, Nagano · Official / source link

  • Madarao Highlands no Ryukinka

    Marsh marigolds at Madarao Kogen Numahara wetland, typically blooming early to late May after the skunk cabbage.

    Iiyama, Nagano · 0269-64-3222 · Official / source link

  • Madarao Highlands no Mizubasho

    Iiyama, Nagano · 0269-64-3222 · Official / source link

  • Nishi Otaki Dam Cherry Blossoms

    Cherry trees at Nishi-Otaki Dam Sakura Plaza, with blossoms reflected on the reservoir in late April-early May.

    Iiyama, Nagano · 0269-62-7000 · Official / source link

  • Nagamine Supotsu Park Cherry Blossoms

    About 550 mountain cherries and Somei-yoshino around Hariko Pond at Nagamine Sports Park (late April-early May).

    Iiyama, Nagano · 0269-62-7000 · Official / source link

  • Chikumagawa Sakurazutsumi

    About 700 cherry trees along roughly 4 km of the Chikuma River embankment, peaking mid- to late April with snowy hills beyond.

    Iiyama, Nagano · 0269-62-7000 · Official / source link

  • Iiyama Castle Park Cherry Blossoms

    Iiyama Castle ruins park with restored gates and walls; a major local cherry spot hosting an April castle sakura festival.

    Iiyama, Nagano · 0269-62-7000 · Official / source link
