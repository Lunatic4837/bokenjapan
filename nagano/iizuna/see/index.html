Sights in Iizuna, Nagano

Iizuna · Nagano · 13 places

See

  • Togyo

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-6646

    Fish (Macropodus chinensis), also called Chosen-buna, about 7 cm long, living in Hiraide rain-fed rice fields and designated a town natural monument.

  • Ue Akashio Chiku no Katakuri no Hana

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-7788

    Colony of reddish-purple katakuri, recommended for early April; temporary parking and toilets are set up for the flower-viewing event.

  • Takaoka Shrine no Osugi

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-6646

    Large cedar trees line the approach to Takaoka Shrine; there were once eight, and two were felled after a lightning strike in 1990.

  • Koke Okina Tera

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-3566

    Temple with a two-story gate, tiled on the lower roof and iron-plate hip-and-gable above, whose Nio statues are cultural properties.

  • Bushu Kashu Dochu Sakai no Hi

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-6646

    Stone monument marking the midpoint between Musashi (Edo) and Kaga (Kanazawa); the Maeda lord is said to have sent an express messenger to Kanazawa from here during sankin-kotai.

  • Koshin Tsuka Burial Mound

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-6646

    Burial mound in Iizuna.

  • Sambonmatsu

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-6646

    Three pine trees in Iizuna.

  • Hana Kenjo Yama

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-4765

    A mountain with castle ruins at its 722.7 m summit was selected in 1987 for Nagano Prefecture's Nature 100 Selections.

  • Basho Haiku Monument

    Iizuna, Nagano · 0267-42-5538

    Built in 1843 by disciple-poet Kobayashi Gyokuho and carved with a Basho haiku.

  • Lake Raisenji

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-3210

    Ranked the prefecture's second-largest artificial lake, it lies at Mount Raisenji's foot with mysterious beauty.

  • Peach Blossoms of Tankakyo

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-4765

    Pale pink peach blossoms cover 10 hectares named Tankakyo by painter Saburosuke Okada in 1933.

  • Eisho Jizo

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-6646

    A 1507 Muromachi stone Jizo in a stone niche is a designated Nagano Prefectural Treasure.

  • Iizuna Apple Museum

    Iizuna, Nagano · 026-253-1071

    It displays materials on the town's specialty apples and holds several special exhibitions yearly.
