Cafe in Ina, Nagano

Eat in Ina · 8 places

Cafe

  • サンタベリーガーデン

    Santa Berry Garden

    Dining in Ina

    Cafe in a berry-picking garden serving tarts, parfaits, soft ice cream and pasta made with the garden's berries and vegetables.

  • サンタベリーガーデン　カフェ・ナータ

    Santa Berry Garden Cafe Nata

    Dining in Ina

    Cafe at a raspberry and blackberry picking farm that serves pasta sets and sweets made with morning-picked berries, with garden seating and takeout, open 11:00-17:00, closed Wednesdays and from 26 December to April.

  • 山荘ミルク

    Sanso Milk

    Dining in Ina

    Mountain cottage at about 1,000 m serving homemade natural-yeast bread made without butter or sugar, and coffee; closed Mondays, with other closures.

  • ハセダイナー

    HASE DINER

    Dining in Ina

    American cafe at Yogoda Stay and Retreat serving handmade-sausage Hase Dogs, burger dogs, baked sweets and tapioca; open 8:00-15:00, closed Mon-Thu, with morning service 8:00-11:00.

  • 蔵ZEN

    Kura ZEN

    Dining in Ina

    Cafe in a 140-year-old storehouse in Ina serving coffee, with jazz; open 11:00-17:00, closed Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

  • 蔵カフェ ぱぷりか

    Kura Cafe Papurika

    Dining in Ina

    Reservation-only cafe in a ~90-year-old storehouse serving vegetable-based creative dishes; choose meat, fish or a hamburg steak with multigrain rice, soup and sides; open 11:00-18:00 Thu-Sun and holidays, closed Mon-Wed.

  • お茶屋 いちえ

    Ochaya Ichie

    Dining in Ina

    Japanese tea shop offering Takato-bo hojicha, lattes and gelato; open 10:00-17:00, closed Mondays and the 1st and 3rd Sundays.

  • 珈琲専門店 ぽえむ・サルサ

    Kohi Senmonten Poemu Sarusa

    Dining in Ina

    Coffee shop in Takato's castle town offering 100 coffees drip-brewed with Tosa washi and handmade cheesecake with blueberry jam; open 10:00-18:00, closed Wednesdays.
