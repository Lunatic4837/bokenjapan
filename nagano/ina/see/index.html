Sights in Ina, Nagano

Ina · Nagano · 23 places

See

  • Katsuma Yakushido no Shidare Sakura

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-96-8100

    Weeping cherry tree estimated at about 140 years old, blooming around a thatched-roof Yakushi hall and stone Buddhas about five days after Takato Castle Ruins Park.

  • Onsho Tera

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-94-3799

    Buddhist temple in Ina.

  • Koen (Shinwa no Oka Rozu Garden)

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-94-2556

    Rose garden facing the Central Alps, with about 270 varieties, 3,000 bushes, a tea house, June and October festivals, and self-driving carts since 2023.

  • Takao Park no Tsutsuji

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-78-4111

    Azalea viewing spot in Ina.

  • Kuwata Yakushido no Shidarezakura

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-98-2009

    Cherry blossom viewing spot in Ina.

  • Nyu Kasa Highlands

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-94-2556

    Highlands about 30 minutes on foot from the Nyugasayama trailhead to the summit, with clear-day views of Mt. Fuji, Yatsugatake, and the Alps, and seasonal flowers in Nyugasa and Oahara wetlands.

  • Hana no Oka Park

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-94-2556

    Park with about 2,000 cherry trees of over 50 varieties and a loop path, where double and mountain cherries bloom after Takato Castle Ruins Park.

  • Shin Myo Tera no Ajisai

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-72-5070

    Temple called a hydrangea temple, with about 200 varieties and 2,500 plants beside 33 Kannon statues and a garden of about 2,000 stone mortars.

  • Miya no Hara no Shidarezakura

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-78-4111

    Weeping cherry about 100-120 years old, 7 m tall and 4 m thick, with drooping branches covering part of a rice field.

  • Kasuga Castle Ruins Park

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-78-4111

    Castle ruins park built in 1534, with cherries and azaleas, play equipment added in spring 2023, and views of Ina and the Southern Alps.

  • Autumn Foliage of the Southern Alps Forest Road

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-98-2821

    Buses cover 22.8 kilometers of autumn foliage from Todai-guchi to Kitazawa Pass in about one hour.

  • Atsuta Shrine

    Ina, Nagano

    Rebuilt in 1763, its small main hall is called Ina Nikko for dynamic carvings.

  • Ina City Creation Hall

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-72-6220

    Renovated 1930 library displaying Jomon artifacts, including a National Important Cultural Property.

  • Shinshu Takato Art Museum

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-94-3666

    Opened in 1992 south of Takato Castle Ruins Park, showing works by Ina-connected artists.

  • Autumn Foliage at Takato Castle Ruins Park

    Ina, Nagano

    Maple colors usually peak from late October to mid-November at this cherry-famous castle.

  • Takato Castle Ruins Park Cherry Blossom Festival

    Ina, Nagano · 問：（一社）伊那市観光協会0265-96-8100

    About 1,500 small reddish Takato-kohigan cherries are long called the finest under heaven.

  • ASOBINA

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-98-0048

    Guided outdoor complex for enjoying Ina Valley nature amid the Southern and Central Alps.

  • Former Izawa Family Residence, Inabe-juku

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-74-8102

    Edo-period residence of wealthy sake brewers, the post town's only gabled house.

  • Nagano Prefectural Ina Cultural Hall

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-73-8822

    Opened in December 1988 and renovated in 2021, with a large hall and planetarium.

  • Oguro River Gorge Campsite

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-74-6886

    A gorge-side campsite on the Oguro River with river play, stargazing, and a fishing pond.

  • Habiro Lion Dance

    Ina, Nagano · （一社）伊那市観光協会0265-96-8100

    Habiro's 400-year-old Little New Year lion dance uniquely pairs male and female lions.

  • Bungui Pass

    Ina, Nagano

    A pass between Ina and Oshika, known as a quiet healing energy spot.

  • Takato Castle Ruins Park

    Ina, Nagano · 0265-78-4111(代)

    It is a national historic site and one of Japan's three great cherry-blossom spots.
