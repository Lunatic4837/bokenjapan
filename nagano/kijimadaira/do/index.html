Things to do in Kijimadaira, Nagano

Kijimadaira · Nagano · 2 places

Do

  • Buna Forest Kaya no Hirataka Hara Campground

    Kijimadaira, Nagano

    Campground at 1,400 m in Joshinetsu Kogen National Park, surrounded by beech forest and grassland, for stargazing and trekking.

  • Hokushin Shu Kijimadaira Ski Area

    Kijimadaira, Nagano · 0269-82-3434

    Ski area on Mount Kosha's north slope with the Sky Course and wide runs, a free children's area with a 50 m moving walkway, sled rental and free parking.
