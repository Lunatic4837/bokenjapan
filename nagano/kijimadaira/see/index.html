Sights in Kijimadaira, Nagano

Kijimadaira · Nagano · 4 places

See

  • Yama no Ie to

    Kijimadaira, Nagano · 0269-82-4188

    Guesthouse three minutes on foot from the ski slope in winter, where guests can ski back to the entrance, with local seasonal meals and a prefecture-designated nonsmoking interior.

  • Kaya no Hirataka Hara Rojji

    Kijimadaira, Nagano

    Highland lodge at 1,400 m surrounded by primeval beech forest, where guests can stay, trek, and watch the stars.

  • Kaya no Taira no Autumn Leaves

    Kijimadaira, Nagano · 090-4547-5188

    Beech forest with walking trails; beech and maple leaves are typically viewed from mid-October to early November, and Nabekura Kogen Mori no Ie offers lodging.

  • Madotsuki Matsushimaya

    Kijimadaira, Nagano

    A former sake warehouse over 130 years old in Kijimadaira, renovated as an adults-only inn for two groups daily.
