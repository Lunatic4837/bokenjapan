Sights in Kisomachi, Nagano

Kisomachi · Nagano · 15 places

See

  • Kyanpingufiirudo Kisomachi Kodo

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-22-2766

    Campsite about 10 minutes by car from central Kiso-Fukushima, with char catching, fishing, and Kiso ancient-road hiking, and Komanoyu Onsen a 10-minute walk away.

  • Ontake Sanza no Ike

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-25-6000

    Alpine lake 13.3 m deep, called goshinsui, about 2.5 hours from the Ontake ropeway end and 1 hour from the summit.

  • Nakasendo Fukushima Yado

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-25-6000

    Post town that prospered at one of Japan's four major checkpoints, where surrounding trees change color in autumn.

  • Kyu Yu

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-22-4000

    Cold carbonated mineral spring along the Nara-period Kiso ancient road, once used for cuts and called Old Bath after Komanoyu opened below.

  • Toku Oto Tera

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-26-2520

    Temple where Kiso Yoshinaka buried his mother in 1168, with graves of Yoshinaka and Tomoe Gozen, spring cherries, and a Bishamonten stamp.

  • Nakasendo Miya no Etsu Yado

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-25-6000

    Post town known as the place where Kiso Yoshinaka was raised, with related historic sites including Tokuonji, Yoshinaka-kan, and Hatage Hachimangu.

  • Yoshinaka Kan

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-26-2035

    Museum built in 1992 to honor Kiso Yoshinaka, reopened in July 2021 after renovation, with history and contemporary art, a touch panel, and a library space.

  • Ontake Ropuei

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-46-2525

    Ropeway between Ontake's fifth and seventh stations, with a 2,150 m observation deck; the 2022 season ran from 25 June to 7 November.

  • Kaido Roman On Yado Tsutaya

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-22-2010

    Ryokan with newly built rooms that have outdoor baths, a Kiso-Fukushima Onsen open-air bath, and Kiso creative Japanese cuisine and kamado cooking.

  • Matsuo Falls

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-25-6000

    A waterfall at the fourth station on Mount Ontake's Kurosawa trail, near many spirit monuments.

  • Aburagi Forest

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-2５-６０００

    A primeval forest of 300-year-old Kiso trees on Mount Ontake is designated an academic reference forest.

  • Hakusan Shrine

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-27-6250

    The main hall has carefully detailed carvings, and ancient kagura is offered every July and October.

  • Mitake General Ground

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-46-2001

    The ground is scenic and equipped with night-game lighting.

  • Kisokoma Forest Park Tennis Courts

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-23-8623

    Tennis courts at the foot of Mount Kisokoma stay cool in summer among surrounding mountains.

  • Kaida Plateau Health Resort Tennis Courts

    Kisomachi, Nagano · 0264-44-2118

    All-weather courts in a 1,320-meter villa area offer tennis with Mount Ontake views.
