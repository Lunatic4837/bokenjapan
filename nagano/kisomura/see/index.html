Sights in Kisomura, Nagano

Kisomura · Nagano · 14 places

See

  • Torii Toge

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    Pass on the Kiso Kaido linking Yabuhara-juku and Narai-juku, once called a Nakasendo hard point, with old stone paving, poet monuments including Basho, and stone Buddhas; Japan Heritage since 2016.

  • Heri Ketsu Shrine

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    Shrine named for enmusubi, tying romantic, family, personal, and place bonds such as jobs and school.

  • Oku Kiso Mizumi

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-3111

    Dam with irregular canoe experiences at Shosawa waterside park, Yanagisawa Ridge Park overlooking the dam and village, and the Kiso River headwaters hall.

  • Toko Nami Falls Toko Nami no Taira Sui

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    Stepped waterfall, with Hiradaru, a stream flowing about 200 m over one large rock; a cool spot in hot summer.

  • Sei Sawa Shinsui Park

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2772

    Park along Okukiso Lake (Misogawa Dam) with a lawn plaza, arbor, and toilets, and green-season canoe trips on the lake.

  • Yanagisawa One Park no Tsutsuji

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    Observation deck overlooking Misogawa Dam and Mount Kisokoma, where azaleas bloom, with peak viewing in mid to late May.

  • Torii Toge Shisan Mi no Tochi

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    Horse chestnut in a Japan Heritage tochi group midway on Torii Pass, with a large hollow and a legend of a family raising an abandoned child inside.

  • Ta no Ue Kannon Do no Shidare Sakura

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    Weeping cherry several hundred years old beside a thatched hall, with pale pink blossoms and autumn foliage.

  • Yoshida Chiku Fukuju Kusa Village

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    Adonis viewing spot in Kisomura.

  • Edohigan Cherry at Suga Ten Kings Hall

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    A 500-year-old Edohigan cherry, a Kiso Village natural monument, fills the sky with pale pink.

  • Mizukisawa Natural Forest

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2772

    A rare mixed conifer and broadleaf forest spreads through the Kiso River headwaters and has three trekking courses.

  • Giant Sawara Cypress of Mizukisawa Natural Forest

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    This is the most prominent giant tree in Mizukisawa Natural Forest, a Heisei famous-water site.

  • Suga-Odaira Iris Park Pond

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2543

    A quiet pond where irises bloom from early June was drained for repairs planned to finish in 2020.

  • Kodama Forest Cottage

    Kisomura, Nagano · 0264-36-2777

    This is a whole-building rental cottage in Kodama Forest.
