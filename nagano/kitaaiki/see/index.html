Sights in Kitaaiki, Nagano

Kitaaiki · Nagano · 12 places

See

  • Ka Kazushi Mizumi

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-92-2167

    Lake in Kitaaiki.

  • Yuki Se Falls

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    Waterfall that sends up white spray, with several smaller falls nearby and fossils from when the area was a coastline.

  • Mitaki Yama

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    Mountain long used as a place of worship, with many stone Buddhas and three waterfalls; Daizen Falls forms a winter ice column visited continually.

  • Gyoza Yama no Shakunage

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    Rhododendron viewing spot in Kitaaiki.

  • Megusuri no Ki

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    Giant tree also called Choja no Ki, with white flowers at the branch tips; bark and leaves were decocted for eye washes and poultices.

  • Yakuyoke Kanzeon

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2422

    Temple said to have opened in the 16th century, No. 17 of the Saku 33 Kannon pilgrimage, with fairs on April 16 and 17.

  • Aiki Castle Ruins

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    Castle ruins in Kitaaiki.

  • Tochihara Iwakage Iseki

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    Cave-like ruins cut by the Aiki River from Yatsugatake mudflow, with artifacts from the Jomon period through the Edo period.

  • Tochihara Shishi Mai

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    Dedication lion dance passed down from old times, said to follow ancient Japanese shishimai (deer dance), and designated a village intangible cultural property.

  • Kananbare (Doll Floating)

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2014

    This old rite transfers impurity onto dolls floated down a river and is held as a school event.

  • Choja Forest

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2726

    Children and adults can play in nature here, and it is very popular with families.

  • Tochihara Rock Shelter Site, Kitaaiki Village Archaeological Museum

    Kitaaiki, Nagano · 0267-77-2111

    It mainly displays Incipient to Early Jomon finds from the Tochihara rock shelter and other village sites.
