Izakaya & bars in Komagane, Nagano

Eat in Komagane · 3 places

Izakaya & bars

  • 南信州ビール（株）味わい工房

    Minamishinshu Beer Ajiwai Kobo

    Dining in Komagane

    Brewery restaurant serving freshly made beer and seasonal dishes using Shinshu ingredients, including miso pizza and yawaraka beer-ni; open 11:30-15:30 (LO 15:00) and 17:00-21:00 (LO 20:00), closed Thursdays.

  • 越百

    Kosumo

    Dining in Komagane

    Izakaya serving reservation-only hand-cut soba, local sake and beer, yakitori, house-made bacon and soba gyoza; open 18:00-22:00, closed Mondays and public holidays.

  • クラフトビールカフェ bine bine

    Craft Beer Cafe bine bine

    Dining in Komagane

    Craft beer cafe serving 11 craft beers, including local Minami-Shinshu beer, four house-roasted coffees, domestic-beef hamburgers, snacks and desserts; closed Mondays and Tuesdays, open 11:30-26:00.
