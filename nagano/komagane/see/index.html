Sights in Komagane, Nagano

Komagane · Nagano · 23 places

See

  • Umami Tsuka Park (Mamizukakoen)

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    Park beside a pond with cherry trees and a mitsuba azalea tunnel that bloom at nearly the same time in spring, distant Central Alps views, and children's play equipment.

  • Chuo Arupusu Komagagaku Ropuei

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-3107

    Ropeway in Komagane.

  • (Sumiyoshiya) no Hanamomo (Nakazawa Chiku)

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    Peach blossom viewing spot in Komagane.

  • Kita no Hara Park (Tomo Rakuen)

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    Park west of Otagiri Station near Katari no Mori, with a wide lawn, a D51 steam locomotive, and play equipment such as a slide and jungle gym; a spring cherry-blossom spot.

  • Komagane Highlands Onuma Mizumi

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    Lake with fukujuso in February-March, cherries in early April, and autumn leaves, Central Alps views from the east shore and Southern Alps views from the west, where canoe and SUP are not allowed.

  • Kise no Shidare Sakura

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    Old weeping cherry whose usual peak viewing is early April, with remaining snow on the Central Alps and surrounding fields.

  • Kozen Tera Tori no Suisen (Suisen)

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    Daffodils in fallow fields and banks along the road to Kozenji, planted by a local farm group from about 30 years ago and said to reach 2 million blooms, peaking late March to mid-April.

  • Suzuran Hausu

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7201

    Agricultural processing facility with factory tours, selling medium-temperature pasteurized Suzuran milk, yogurt, ham, sausage, and bread; a frank dog is 320 yen including tax.

  • Komagane Highlands Komagaike

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    Pond from whose shore Mount Hoken of the Central Alps is visible, with cherry blossoms in spring and autumn leaves.

  • Kozen Tera no Hikarigoke

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-2736

    Luminous moss at a temple in Komagane.

  • Kozenji Temple Autumn Foliage

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-2736

    A national scenic site whose maples turn red and yellow in late October and early November, then are illuminated.

  • Weeping Cherry at Zozeji Temple

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-87-2733

    This temple enshrines Jurojin of the Ina Seven Lucky Gods; its old weeping cherry frames the Central Alps.

  • Komagane City Fun Kappa Museum

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-4767

    Displays Komagane kappa-legend materials and themed art, and holds lectures, workshops, and exhibitions.

  • Komagane City Folk Museum

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-5011

    Built in 1922 as a village office, this rare Taisho Western-style building now displays folk cultural properties.

  • Minamiwari Park

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700(駒ヶ根観光協会)

    It has Alps Stadium, mallet golf, and a dragonfly garden whose pond holds Japan's smallest dragonfly.

  • Utsugidaira Emergency Hut

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-98-8508（駒ヶ根市役所商工観光課）

    Unmanned emergency hut east of Utsugidake summit, intended to protect climbers, not for regular lodging.

  • Autumn Foliage at Senjojiki, Central Alps

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-7700

    From late September to early October, rowan and birch color the cirque, seen from the ropeway and trails.

  • Hoshakuzan Kozenji Temple

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-2736

    One of Nagano's great temples and a leading southern prayer site, with a nationally designated scenic garden.

  • Kozenji Cedar Avenue

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-2736

    Cedars about 300 years old or older guide visitors along the approach from the Nio Gate to the Sanmon.

  • Central Alps Komakko Plaza (formerly Chibikko Plaza)

    Komagane, Nagano

    East of Komaike Pond in Komagane Park, this forest play area was renewed with swings, slides, and athletic equipment.

  • Ruby Village Exterior Garden

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-81-1582

    Open as a park, its paths offer seasonal flowers such as red buckwheat, roses, and hydrangeas.

  • Kamiho Eleven Riders Shrine

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-2736

    In 1614, eleven Chimura retainers secretly joined Sanada Yukimura at Osaka Castle and fought at Sanada-maru.

  • Longevity Water of Kozenji Temple

    Komagane, Nagano · 0265-83-2736

    Spring water beside the main hall is called longevity water because cold water is thought to lengthen life.
