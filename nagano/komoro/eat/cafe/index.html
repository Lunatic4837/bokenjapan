    Winery with a cafe open 10:00-16:00 (closed irregularly) and lunch 11:30-14:00 on Friday-Sunday only.

  • Chabou & Guesthouse Dokusho no Mori

    茶房・ゲストハウス 読書の森

    Dining in Komoro

    Tea room and guesthouse in Komoro listed for meals, sweets and cafe service; open about 10:00-18:30 in summer and until about 18:00 in winter, closed irregularly.

  • Chaya Kumeya

    茶屋くめや

    Dining in Komoro

    Tea house in the nationally registered cultural property Wakihonjin Kumeya, serving onikake udon, soba and komonaka with matcha; open 11:00-16:00, closed Tuesdays, Wednesdays and December-February.

  • Cafe Saihondo

    彩本堂

    Dining in Komoro

    Cafe in Komoro with counter seats where siphon coffee is prepared in view; open 10:00-17:00 (last entry 16:00), closed Thursdays, or the next day if Thursday is a holiday.

  • Cafe Tsugumori

    つぐもり

    Dining in Komoro

    Cafe with garden and lodging in Komoro, with lunch and takeout; open Thursday-Tuesday 10:00-16:30 (last order 16:00, lunch until 14:30), closed Wednesdays.

    カフェ・ガーディアン

    Cafe with four real cats and Kurohime, the origin of the cat manga Nekomachi Soshi; open 10:30-17:00, closed Monday and Tuesday.

  • Komoro-eki no Mado

  • Teishaba Garden Cafe & Shop

    Dining in Komoro

    Cafe and shop in Teishaba Garden in front of Komoro Station serving lunch and sweets using local products, garden herbs and edible flowers; cafe open 10:00-17:00, closed Thursdays and year-end/New Year.

  • Onigiri & Cafe AGEHAYA

    おむすび＆café AGEHAYA
