Sights in Komoro, Nagano

Komoro · Nagano · 25 places

See

  • Komoro Joshi Kaiko Sono Cherry Blossoms

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-0296

    About 400 cherry trees, including Someiyoshino and Komoro Yae-beni-shidare, around a riding ground overlooking the Chikuma River, with bonbori and illumination during the cherry festival.

  • Nunobiki Kannon Cherry Blossoms

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-0568

    Cliff-built Kannon hall about a 15-minute walk up an unpaved path from the Chikuma River, seen with cherry blossoms and linked to the saying of being led to Zenkoji by a cow.

  • Nunobiki Kannon no Katakuri no Hana

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    Katakuri colony around Nunobiki Kannon with pale purple flowers that wilt within a few days of opening.

  • Noma Shusui Gen Chi no Midori Cherry Blossoms

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    Gyoiko double cherries with yellow-green blooms from chloroplasts flower later than Somei-Yoshino near the Cherry Park Line entrance at about 1,000 meters, peaking in early to mid-May.

  • Shobu Sawa no Airisu

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-5545

    Iris viewing spot in Komoro.

  • Umi O In no Komorosumire

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    Double-flowered violet, one of only two such among 96 Japanese varieties, found in late Taisho at Umi O In; deep-purple irregular blooms, named Komoro sumire, city flower since 1973.

  • Shobu Sawa no Shobu

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-5545

    Iris viewing spot in Komoro.

  • Komoro Joshi Kaiko Sono no Komoro Yae Benishidare

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-0296

    Double red weeping blossom viewing spot in Komoro.

  • Nunobiki Gorge

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    This bold gorge, nicknamed Shinano Yabakei, is known for Nunobiki Kannon, cherries, and autumn leaves.

  • Komoro Castle Ruins Kaikoen Observation Deck

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-0296

    It overlooks the Chikuma River and sunsets on Mimakigahara, a selected Shinshu sunset point.

  • Mount Iizuna Park (Komoro City Highland Art Museum and Shiratori Eisetsu Gallery)

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-26-2070

    At 836 meters, it offers a 360-degree panorama and is a selected Shinshu sunset point.

  • Shakusonji Temple (Nunobiki Kannon)

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    Reached by a steep 15-minute climb, its early Kamakura inner palace is an Important Cultural Property.

  • Shogen-in Temple

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-3095

    Said to date from the late Muromachi period, this Soto Zen temple preserves Komoro Castle's relocated Black Gate.

  • Kaio-in Temple

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    Founded in 1536, this Soto temple reportedly mediated the Ueda peace, and Komoro violet was discovered here.

  • Manns Wine Japanese Garden "Mansuien"

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-6341

    This formal Japanese garden at Manns Wine Komoro Winery holds 11,000 trees, a waterfall, pond, and tea house.

  • Station Garden (Komoro Civic Garden)

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-24-2525

    Opened as a civic model for flowers, greenery, and local produce, it has a shop and health cafe.

  • Former Komoro Honjin

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    This Edo-period National Important Cultural Property served as Komoro-juku's honjin and wholesale office.

  • Chikuma River Travel Song Monument

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1130

    Built in 1927, it holds a bronze panel based on Toson's handwritten Chikuma River travel song.

  • Literary Monuments of Kaikoen

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-0296

    Fourteen poetry monuments, including Toson, Kyoshi, and Bokusui, stand throughout the park.

  • Suimeiro

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1511

    This riverside study of Komoro Gijuku head Kimura Kumaji was often visited by Toson and others.

  • Yoakashi Nenbutsu

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    This traditional rite is now held at Yakushi Hall on the spring equinox, with dancing and bead prayers.

  • Sasara Dance

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    This traditional dance at Kensoku Shrine's Gion Festival is said to pray for rich harvests.

  • Lake Otome Park

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-23-8880

    This comprehensive park for lifelong learning includes cultural facilities, sports grounds, woods, and a peony garden.

  • Takamine Highlands

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    These gentle highlands around Kurumazaka Pass offer easy trekking, alpine flowers, and autumn foliage.

  • Fireflies of the Sasazawa River at Omimagahara

    Komoro, Nagano · 0267-22-1700

    This riverside habitat is a noted natural firefly site, best seen from about June 20 to July 20.
