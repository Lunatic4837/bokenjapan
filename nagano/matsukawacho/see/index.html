Sights in Matsukawacho, Nagano

Matsukawacho · Nagano · 15 places

See

  • Enman Bo Cherry Blossoms

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    Cherry-blossom avenue of 400-year-old Edohigan trees, visited for blossom viewing by the Ajima Domain Chiku family in the Edo period and cited as first of the Ina Valley Ten Views in early Showa.

  • Oshima no Ringo no Hana

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    Apple blossom viewing spot in Matsukawacho.

  • Dai Shiro Park no Tsutsuji

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    City park converted from a Sengoku-period castle built by the Takeda clan, with 5,000 red, white, purple, and orange Kirishima azaleas near Ninomaru and an azalea festival every May 3.

  • Harada Cherry Blossoms

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    Old cherry tree said to be 500-700 years old, planted in the Kamakura period by Harada ancestors as a ward against evil from the north.

  • Sugino no no Shidare Sakura (Bu Na Satoyama Shizen Ennai)

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    Edohigan cherry about 200 years old in a satoyama nature garden, set against remaining snow on Mt. Utsugi, where forsythia, narcissus, and katakuri also bloom.

  • Katagiri Dam (Matsukawacho Gorge)

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    Dam lake colored by spring young leaves, summer deep green, and autumn foliage.

  • Dai Shu Shichi Sugi Shrine no Sennen Sugi

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-2018

    Sacred cedar of Shichisugi Shrine, a tree more than 1,000 years old.

  • Matsukawacho Chuo Kominkan

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-2622

    Local public facility in Matsukawacho.

  • Rifuresshutaun Matsukawa no Sato Patagorufu Ba

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-5520

    Highland putter-golf grass course facing the Southern Alps.

  • Lake Matsukawa (Katagiri Dam)

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    This 23.2-meter-deep artificial lake mirrors mountains that turn color in autumn.

  • Cherry Blossoms at Daijo Park

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    Cherries across this park on Takeda-built Oshima Castle ruins bloom fully in early spring.

  • Pear Blossoms of Oshima and Kamikatagiri

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    In late April, pear trellises near the Chuo Expressway bloom fully during artificial pollination.

  • Red Spider Lilies at Reigakuji Temple

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    About 30,000 spider lilies, including rare white ones, bloom at this hilltop temple in autumn.

  • Oshima Castle Ruins (Daijo Park)

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-6320

    Late Heian ruins with complex dry moats; each September, beacons recreate the Sengoku period.

  • Refresh Town Matsukawa no Sato Indoor Sports Facility

    Matsukawacho, Nagano · 0265-36-5520

    Multipurpose indoor sports hall; weekdays cost 700 yen per court-hour plus 500 yen lighting.
