Things to do in Matsukawamura, Nagano

Matsukawamura · Nagano · 2 places

Do

  • Matsukawamura Mara Odaka Hara Campground

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-2481

    Wooded campground on Marao Highland at the foot of Mount Ariake, with flush toilets and a washing area, closed for Ashima River small-hydropower construction and requiring advance reservation.

  • Shinano Kuni Matsukawamura Kyo Gaku Taiko

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-3109

    Creative taiko group formed in 1986 at the foot of Mt. Ariake, with over 50 adult and child members, performing in and outside the prefecture and overseas.
