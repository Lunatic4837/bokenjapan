Sights in Matsukawamura, Nagano

Matsukawamura · Nagano · 11 places

See

  • Azumino Chihiro Park Ohana Dan

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-3109

    Park overlooking the Northern Alps with about 35,000 square meters of lawn, seasonal flower fields in spring and summer, and touchable benches and a pond made by village volunteers.

  • Nezumiana no Shidarezakura

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-3109

    Cherry blossom viewing spot in Matsukawamura.

  • Azumino Chihiro Park

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-85-8822

    Park for hands-on learning about food, farming, and life, with a local-food hall, harvest farm, outdoor cooking zone, Totto-chan plaza, and Northern Alps views.

  • Kuni Shitei Juyo Bunkazai Kinpuku Yama Kan Matsu In Dozo Bosatsu Hanka Zo

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-3366

    Clay half-seated bodhisattva, a National Important Cultural Property considered made on the Korean Peninsula in the early 7th century; viewing requires a reservation.

  • Seicho Azumi Fushi

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-3366

    Folk song created in 1923 by the late village-born physician Shinba Futoo with local enthusiasts; a CD costs 1,000 yen and a DVD 1,200 yen.

  • Azumino Chihiro Art Museum

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-0772

    Art museum showing Chihiro Iwasaki picture-book originals and works by artists from many countries, with a reading room, cafe, shop, and about 53,500 m2 of park; closed in winter.

  • Tamokuteki Koryu Center Suzu no Oto Horu

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-2481

    Multipurpose center with a hall for up to 300 for music and theater, plus a library, exhibition corner, training rooms, and kitchen; advance reservation required.

  • Matsukawamura Shogaigakushu Center Guriinwaku Matsukawa

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-61-1400

    Lifelong learning center with an arena, training rooms, a pottery room, a music room, and Japanese-style rooms for sports and cultural activities; advance reservation is required.

  • Gongen-sama Cherry Blossoms (Matsukawa Shrine)

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-3109

    About 200 Yoshino cherries, some over 80 years old, turn the shrine area pink.

  • Kawanishi Lone Pine

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-2481

    This 14.5-meter red pine, about 200 years old, is Matsukawa Village's first natural monument.

  • Matsukawa Village Wayside Deities

    Matsukawamura, Nagano · 0261-62-3111

    Nearly 100 wayside deities around the village are cherished as protectors from evil and illness.
