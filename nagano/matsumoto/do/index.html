Do in Matsumoto

Matsumoto · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Alps Park

    Matsumoto, Nagano · 0263-34-5640

    This hilltop park northwest of central Matsumoto has Alps views, a nature museum, and a small zoo.

  • Norikura Nature Conservation Center

    Matsumoto, Nagano · 0263-94-2307（松本市アルプスリゾート整備本部）

    Opposite the Norikura Tourism Center, it presents Norikura's nature along with folklore, history, and culture.

  • Utsukushigahara Highlands

    Matsumoto, Nagano · 0263-34-8307

    At 2000 meters east of Matsumoto, cattle graze among flowers beneath panoramic mountain views.

  • Matsumoto City Museum of Mountains and Nature

    Matsumoto, Nagano · 0263-38-0012

    Opened in 2007 in Alps Park, it displays climbing gear and specimens and has a free lookout.
