Sights in Minamiaiki, Nagano

Minamiaiki · Nagano · 14 places

See

  • Gyoza Yama (Kurio Tozanguchi)

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Mountain on the Kitaaiki border, one of Japan's 200 famous mountains, with summit views of Yatsugatake, Mt. Asama, and the North and South Alps, and Azuma rhododendron along the trail in June and July.

  • Tateiwa Mizumi

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Artificial pond created in 1962 on the upper Minamiaiki River, with a circuit walking path and year-round fishing, including shinano yukimasu and wakasagi in winter.

  • Minamiaiki Dam

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Rockfill dam 136 m high and 444 m wide at 1,532 m elevation, completed in 2004 as Lake Okumikawa, upper reservoir of Kannagawa Power Station.

  • Sengataki

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Waterfall falling in one stream just below the Okumikawa Gorge entrance road, with a wide pool.

  • Tateiwa Falls

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Waterfall 15 meters high directly below the rock Tateiwa, which can be seen from close by.

  • Inu Koro Falls

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Waterfall over mossy rocks, seen from the basin after descending trail stairs, with Takimi no Yu hot spring nearby.

  • Omika Falls

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Waterfall designated a prefectural scenic site, formed of three jar-shaped pools and viewable from inside a tunnel.

  • Fudo Falls

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Waterfall on the mid-slope of Mount Goza that collects spring water from the mountains, with a small flow along the hiking trail.

  • Tateiwa

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Rock held as a village symbol, with a legend that a demon carried and stacked the stones in three trips.

  • Tokanya

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-62-3285

    Held on November's second Sunday, children beat the ground with straw guns to expel pests.

  • Minamiaiki Matsutake

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Top-grade local matsutake are sold in village shops and served for a limited time at Takimi no Yu.

  • Minamiaiki Village Folk Museum

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2433

    This 1832 thatched playhouse has a revolving stage and now displays village historical materials.

  • No-War Statue

    Minamiaiki, Nagano · 0267-78-2121

    Built where villagers farewelled departing soldiers, it mourns the war dead and prays for peace.

  • Omika Falls

    Minamiaiki, Nagano

    This is a waterfall called Omika Falls.
