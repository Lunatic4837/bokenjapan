Sights in Minamimaki, Nagano

Minamimaki · Nagano · 13 places

See

  • Yukawa Gorge

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-96-2211

    Gorge where many waterfalls of various sizes fall from the mountainside, with autumn foliage in season.

  • Nobeyama Uchu Denpa Kansokujo

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-98-4300

    Radio observatory where astronomical research is conducted and equipment and data are shared with researchers worldwide, with part of the grounds open for free viewing.

  • Bejitaboru Uizu (Minamimaki no Village Bunkajoho Koryu Kan)

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-91-1771

    Exchange hall with a full-dome theater for a virtual trip through the village's natural scenery.

  • Kiyosato Ariibadogorufu Club

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-98-5070

  • Kottsuanchi

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-98-2817

  • Io Dakeyama So

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0266-73-6673

    Mountain hut between Mt. Iodake and Mt. Yokodake in an alpine-plant area with komakusa, a plant-viewing course, sunrise from the rooms, flush toilets, and a shower room.

  • Shishi Iwa

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-96-2211

    Lion rock in Minamimaki.

  • Nobeyama Highlands

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-96-2211

    Highland with views of the Yatsugatake mountains.

  • Iimori Yama

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-96-2211

    Mountain with a view of Yatsugatake, the Southern Alps, and Mt. Fuji, where daylilies (nikkokisuge), scabiosa (matsumushiso), and other flowers bloom.

  • Hirasawa Pass

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-96-2211

    From this Mount Iimori trailhead with Shishi Rock, views include Yatsugatake, the Southern Alps, Mount Fuji, and sunsets.

  • Senga Falls

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-96-2211

    A waterfall set in a steep rocky valley that stays cool even in summer.

  • JR Railway Highest Point

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-96-2211

    Midway between Nobeyama and Kiyosato on the Koumi Line, this 1,375-meter JR high point has a photo monument.

  • Yatsugatake Highland Music Hall

    Minamimaki, Nagano · 0267-98-2131

    A beautiful hexagon blending into highland mountains, whose wooden hall gives soft natural sound that fully conveys music.
