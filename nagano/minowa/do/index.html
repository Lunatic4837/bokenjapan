Things to do in Minowa, Nagano

Minowa · Nagano · 3 places

Do

  • Kayano Kogen Campground

    Minowa, Nagano · 0265-79-2822

    Plateau campground at 1,200 m; sites are a short walk from parking, with basic gear rental and campfires by advance reservation.

  • Nagata Nature Park Campground

    Minowa, Nagano · 0265-79-0600

    A red-pine forest campground at the Central Alps' foot, facing Mount Senjo and beside Nagata-no-Yu hot spring.

  • Enmei Water

    Minowa, Nagano · 0265-79-3111

    Delicious water from eastern Minowa Town, drawn by residents and campers heading to Nagata or Lake Momiji.
