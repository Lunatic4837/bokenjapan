  • (Shinshu no Meisui Hi Mizu) Onuma no Ike no Yusui

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    Spring-fed pond in Shinrakuji temple grounds, with a dragon figure tied to a dragon-god legend and water used for irrigation.

  • (Shinshu no Meisui Hi Mizu) Chi no Ike to Shuhen Yusui Gun to Nigorikawa

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    Spring, pond, and stream in a Miyota exclave inside Karuizawa, protected as a rice-field irrigation source; iron-rich water turns reddish-brown after it emerges.

  • Makoto Raku Tera

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-2056

    Temple founded by Emperor Yomei to calm Mt. Asama eruptions, with a cedar about 1,000 years old, a three-story pagoda, a Basho monument, and a dragon-god festival on the last Saturday of July.

  • Tonya Yasukawa Ie

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    Gabled building thought to date from the late Edo period that once handled goods transport, with a private interior closed to visits but an exterior that can be viewed.

  • Odai Yado Honjin

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    Former honjin inn of the Yasukawa family that served Odai-juku through the Edo period; envoys and daimyo used a raised formal room, and only the exterior can be viewed.

  • Tsuyukiri Gorge

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    The grandest stretch of Yukawa Gorge, the ravine that follows the course of the Yukawa River.

  • Ichirizuka Weeping Cherry

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    Miyota's ichirizuka on the old Nakasendo is a prefectural historic site cherished by local residents.

  • Odai Post Town on the Nakasendo

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    The 21st Nakasendo post town from Edo's Itabashi, known as the princesses' inn, with surviving lattice-front lodgings.

  • Yamayuri Park

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    A park for sports and walks where Miyota's town flower, the yamayuri lily, blooms from mid-July to early August.

  • Gippa Rock Lookout in Shigesawa Morisenkyo

    Miyota, Nagano · 0267-32-3111

    Panoramic views of Miyota and the Asama Highlands, with moving sunsets over Utsukushigahara and the Japan Alps.
