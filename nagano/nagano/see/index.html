Sights in Nagano, Nagano

Nagano · Nagano · 18 places

See

  • Matsushiro Castle Ruins Cherry Blossoms

    Nagano

    Castle ruins park, former Edo-period Sanada residence, with about 100 cherry trees including Somei-Yoshino; Alps visible on clear days; viewing early to mid-April; lit April 4-13, 2026, 18:00-21:00.

  • Three Cedars of Gaun

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-284-0004（長野市文化財課埋蔵文化財センター）

    Giant cedars beside Gaun-in Temple in Nakajo moved 180 meters in the 1847 Zenkoji earthquake.

  • Kinpusen Hasedera Temple

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-292-2102

    Ancient temple ranked with Nara and Kamakura as one of Japan's three Hase Kannon sites.

  • Nagano Prefectural Art Museum and Higashiyama Kaii Gallery

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-232-0052

    Prefectural art museum in Joyama Park beside Zenkoji, with a gallery of about 960 Higashiyama Kaii works.

  • Nagano City Joyama Park

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-224-5054（長野市公園緑地課）

    Nagano's oldest park, opened in 1900 east of Zenkoji and loved for spring cherry-blossom viewing.

  • Fudo Falls

    Nagano, Nagano · 0268-68-0006

    Waterfall on a tributary, with clear water through deep woods offering varied scenery each season.

  • Kanematsu Vegetables

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-278-1501

    Century-old greengrocer run by three fifth-generation brothers, focusing on traditional Shinshu vegetables.

  • Yoshida Ginkgo Tree

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-224-7013（長野市文化財課）

    Nagano City natural monument about 900 years old, the sacred ginkgo of Yoshida Daimyojingu Shrine.

  • Amidayama Kiyomizu Temple

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-282-3701

    Temple in Wakaho Hoshina preserving nine Important Cultural Properties, known for peonies and autumn leaves.

  • Kusunokigawa Temple Lodging

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-254-2048

    Temple lodging beside Togakushi Shrine's Hokosha, famous for soba overlooking a seasonal courtyard.

  • Giant Kobushi of Tsutsumi

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-284-0004（長野市文化財課埋蔵文化財センター）

    About 300 years old and 25 meters tall, this kobushi magnolia is a Nagano City natural monument.

  • Cherry Blossoms at Kawanakajima Historic Battlefield Park

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-224-5054（長野市公園緑地課）

    About 100 Somei-Yoshino cherries at the site of the fiercest 1561 Kawanakajima battle.

  • Rinshoji Temple

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-278-6542

    Main hall and front gate, built in 1660 as Sanada Nobumasa's mausoleum, are prefectural treasures.

  • Meitokuji Temple

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-278-8621

    Soto Zen temple said to date from 1390, burial place of Kaizu Castle lord Kosaka Danjochu.

  • Zenkoji Temple

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-234-3591

    About 1,400 years old, with an image said to be Japan's oldest Buddha and a National Treasure hall.

  • Senryu Shogunzuka and Himezuka Burial Mounds

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-284-0004（長野市文化財課埋蔵文化財センター）

    Two fourth-century keyhole tombs designated National Historic Sites; grave goods include mirrors and beads.

  • Onda Mokko Poem Monument

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-278-3366（信州松代観光協会）

    Poem monument in Sanada Park, Matsushiro, befitting thrifty reformer Onda Mokko.

  • Fujibakama North of Asakawa Dam

    Nagano, Nagano · 026-244-5165浅川住民自治協議会

    About 250 fujibakama plants attract chestnut tiger butterflies from mid-September to early October.
