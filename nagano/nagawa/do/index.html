Things to do in Nagawa, Nagano

Nagawa · Nagano · 5 places

Do

  • Akakura Forest Oto Campground

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-88-3126

    Campground where the Akakura River flows between the sites and fish live in the river.

  • Buranshu Takayama Sukii Resort

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-69-2232

    Ski resort in Nagawa.

  • Miyashitahiruzuoto Campground

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-88-2915

    Campground with 50 auto-camping plots, 12 with AC power, white-birch forest sites and grassland sites, plus nine lodgings such as bungalows, cottages and log cabins.

  • Miyashita Hills Auto Campground

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-88-2915

    A well-equipped campground just after entering Tobira Pass, with bungalows comfortable even in winter.

  • Utsukushino Pond

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-68-0006

    An artificial lake in a conservation forest reflecting Mount Tateshina, adjoining Himekidaira Campground.
