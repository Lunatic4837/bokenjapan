See in Nagawa

Nagawa · Nagano · 10 places

See

  • Genshi Kodai Roman Taiken Kan

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-41-8050

    History museum with Jomon-period house and ruin displays, offering Jomon pottery-making by reservation.

  • Oyado Wada Yado

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-68-0302

    Stay-only guesthouse renovated from a hatago on the Nakasendo in Wada-juku, with internet-only booking, unmanned smart-lock check-in, and whole-building rental for up to 20.

  • Himeki Taira Howaitobachikyanpufiirudo

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-75-0925

    Campground opened in 2021 at 1,300 m among white birch trees, with a stream and campfires possible year-round; stars fill the night sky.

  • Kuro Yo Ishi Taiken Museum

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-41-8050

    Hands-on museum at the foot of national historic site Hoshikuso Pass obsidian source, showing findings with stone tools, animation, and models, plus arrowhead and magatama making.

  • Hoshi Kuso Kan Kuro Yo Ishi Kozan Tenjishitsu

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-41-8050

    Exhibition room a 50-minute walk from the Obsidian Experience Museum that shows Jomon obsidian mining by projection mapping; open May-November, 9:00-16:00, closed Mondays.

  • Wada Yado Daikokuya

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-41-6123

    Large inn built after the 1861 fire and later moved back to match a lowered road; the first floor is open, with a shared admission ticket at the honjin.

  • Tobira Pass

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-88-2345

    Its summit lies along the Venus Line, where seasonal flowers including Utsukushigahara renge azaleas can be enjoyed.

  • Wada Pass

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-68-0006

    Once called the Nakasendo's hardest stretch, this pass still draws many visitors for mountain walks.

  • Nakasendo Wada-juku

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-41-6123

    A townscape preserving the Nakasendo, with honjin, waki-honjin, and house names hung at each eaves.

  • Ishiai House (Former Honjin)

    Nagawa, Nagano · 0268-41-6123

    So-called palace rooms where daimyo and nobles stayed, including the upper, second, third rooms and corridor, remain.
