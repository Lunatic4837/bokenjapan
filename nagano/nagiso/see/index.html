Sights in Nagiso, Nagano

Nagiso · Nagano · 14 places

  • Nagiso Gaku Ogose Tozanguchi

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    Mountain trailhead in Nagiso.

  • Ta Ritsu Falls

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    Waterfall series whose main fall is 40 m Tenkawa Falls, with Uruu, Rasen, Senshin, Kiriga, Fudo, and many other falls.

  • Nagiso Mitsubatsutsuji

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    Flower site at Tenpaku Park where about 400 mitsuba azaleas bloom in April, with the Nagiso Mitsuba Azalea Festival held around mid-April.

  • Kamikubo Ichiri Tsuka no Ume

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    Historic site in Nagiso.

  • Mitome Nomoto Jin'ato no Shidare Ume

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

  • Kaki Sono Gorge

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    Gorge with waterfalls of various sizes, including Kurobuchi and Ushiga Falls, and emerald-green pools reflecting autumn leaves.

  • Tokaku Tera

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2445

    Old Soto Zen temple with a bell-cherry gate where three Enku statues, including a town-designated Ida-ten figure, can be viewed.

  • Tsumago Yado

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-3123

    Post town, the 42nd of 69 Nakasendo stations from Edo and a junction with the Ina Kaido, where Edo-period houses are kept under a no-sell, no-rent, no-demolish rule.

  • Otoko Falls and Onna Falls

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    It appears in Eiji Yoshikawa's novel Miyamoto Musashi as Musashi and Otsu's romance falls; left men's, right women's.

  • Silver Osmanthus of Tsumago

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-3123

    A giant tree lovingly raised in the Yazaki garden; the family served as hereditary priests of Wachino Shrine.

  • Masugata Site

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-3123

    A post-town fortification bending the road twice at right angles to deter invaders, still in its original form.

  • Momosuke Bridge

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    Also called Peach Bridge, it was completed in September 1922 as a materials route for Yomikaki Power Station.

  • Yomikaki Power Station

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-52-4681

    A power station built by electricity king Momosuke Fukuzawa, a modernization heritage with Momosuke Bridge and Kakizore Aqueduct Bridge.

  • Hakusan Shrine Kagura Lion Dance

    Nagiso, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    A lion dance at Hakusan Shrine whose female lion is danced in women's dress at spring and autumn festivals.
