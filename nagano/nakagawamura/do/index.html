Things to do in Nakagawamura, Nagano

Nakagawamura · Nagano · 2 places

Do

  • Jimba Katachi Yama Campground

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 070-3106-1050

    Campground where camping and day camping are paid and reserved; viewing entry and cooking-area and toilet use are free, and the summit road closes about mid-December to the first Friday of April.

  • Shi Toku Onsen

    Nakagawamura, Nagano

    Hot spring discovered in Genki 3, known as the Shitoku mineral spring, with an attached auto campground.
