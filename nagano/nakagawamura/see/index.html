Sights in Nakagawamura, Nagano

Nakagawamura · Nagano · 14 places

See

  • Jimba Katachi Yama

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Mountain overlooking the Ina Valley; the road is closed to vehicles from mid-December to around the first Friday of April, depending on road conditions.

  • Ori Kusa Toge

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Pass road from Nakagawa Village to Komagane City where Jinbagata summit can be seen and also reached by car.

  • Sakato Kyo

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Historic scenic spot overlooking the Tenryu River, where Sakato Bridge, a national Important Cultural Property since December 2020, has a cherry-blossom tunnel in spring.

  • Jimba Katachi Sancho no Tsutsuji

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Azalea viewing spot in Nakagawamura.

  • Sakato Hashi Cherry Blossoms

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Reinforced-concrete bridge completed in 1932, a nationally registered tangible cultural property, with cherries on both banks forming a tunnel along Route 153.

  • Watashiba no Icho

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Ginkgo avenue set against the Central Alps, whose latest leaf colors are posted on the village website.

  • Minamimukai Hatsudensho

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0120-984-550

    Power station, the last built by Fukuzawa Momosuke, completed in Showa 4 and still operating after 80 years.

  • Iinuma no Tanada

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Terraced rice fields on steep mountain slopes, maintained by local residents, with the Central Alps in front.

  • Kuwabara Falls

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    A three-tier waterfall on the middle Shitoku River mirroring Koshibu Gorge; the crossing bridge has been washed away.

  • Cherry Blossoms at Okusa Castle Ruins Park

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    More than 200 trees of over ten cherry varieties bloom for a long season against remaining Central Alps snow.

  • Beech Tree of Mount Jinbagata

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    A 600-year-old beech with a 6.45-meter girth at 1,280 meters on the trail, only 100 meters from the road.

  • Bee Museum

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-88-2033

    Displays bee ecology and nests, including Tomokazu Tominaga's world's largest nest, 6.6 meters around.

  • Nakagawa Village Art Informel Museum

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-88-1005

    Japan's only Art Informel museum, centered on Takashi Suzuki's collection, like a mountain hideaway.

  • Okusa Castle Ruins Park

    Nakagawamura, Nagano · 0265-96-0658

    Famous cherry-and-Alps park with playgrounds, mallet golf, and 200 trees of 10 varieties blooming until late April.
