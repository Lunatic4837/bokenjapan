  • Chausuyama Highlands

  • Odo Mei

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    Hananoki maples, also called hanakaede, which are dioecious, with red buds before the flowers and autumn foliage.

  • Neba Kyo

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    Gorge where maple and zelkova autumn leaves contrast with a slow clear stream, with rock azaleas in early summer.

  • Tsukise no Osugi

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    Giant tree about 1,800 years old, 13.79 m in trunk circumference and 40 m tall, designated a national natural monument in 1944.

  • Yahashira Shrine

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    Shrine enshrining five male gods and three goddesses, children of Amaterasu, with Amaterasu and Susanoo also enshrined.

  • Sogen Tera

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    Temple Fumonzan Sogenji, once of high rank, founded at Ogawa in 1395, burned in 1754 and rebuilt as a direct branch of Sojiji.

  • Otona Gorge

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    Rock azaleas bloom pale pink on gorge rocks beside the clear stream, photographers' favorite flower.

  • Giant Ceramic Mural

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    The Shingen Cavalry mural on the highway wall uses 100,000 tiles, with Takato ware for the banners.

  • Ikenotaira Tortoise-Shell Rock

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    A splendid basalt columnar joint, 2.4 meters high and 2.5 meters wide, erupted 15 to 9.5 million years ago.

  • Tsukise Circling Drum

    Neba, Nagano · 050-5235-1593

    Seven or eight youths circle and beat one drum at a float's rear with vigorous, lively calls.
