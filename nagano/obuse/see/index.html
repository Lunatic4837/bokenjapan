Sights in Obuse, Nagano

Obuse · Nagano · 7 places

See

  • Shingonshu Toyoyama Ha Joko Tera

    Obuse, Nagano · 026-247-3924

    Shingon temple at the foot of Mount Karita, locally called Karita no Yakushi, whose thatched hip-and-gable Yakushi Hall of 1408 is a National Important Cultural Property.

  • Ko Dai Shrine

    Obuse, Nagano

    Shrine derived from Ise Jingu and jointly enshrining Konpira, Yasaka, and Nishinomiya shrines; Yasuichi market is held in the grounds on January 14-15, with an Issa haiku stone.

  • Obuse Museum Nakajima Chinami Kan

    Obuse, Nagano · 026-247-6111

    Art museum with works by painter Nakajima Chinami and metal sculptor Haruyama Fuminori, a festival-float storehouse, exhibitions, a shop, and a cafe.

  • Chikufu Do Main Shop

    Obuse, Nagano · 026-247-2569

    Shop whose present building was opened in 1988, with an elevator and multipurpose toilets, and the Museum of Japanese Light and Nakano Clay Hina Hall on the grounds.

  • Orai /ourai

    Obuse, Nagano · 026-213-7552

    Complex combining a guesthouse, a work lounge, and dining serving Chinese food, cafe items, and sweets.

  • Obuse Ranch milgreen

    Obuse, Nagano · 026-214-3224

    Gelato shop in the Thousand-Year Forest serving gelato and cheese made with milk from ranch-raised Jersey cows, with views of the forest and calves.

  • Shakaifukushi Hojin Kuri no Mi Sono

    Obuse, Nagano · 026-247-6330

    Farm stand where service users organically farm and keep about 2,000 free-range chickens on fermented feed and seasonal wild grasses, selling eggs, seasonal vegetables, miso, and sweets.
