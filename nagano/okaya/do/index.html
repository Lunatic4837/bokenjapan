Things to do in Okaya, Nagano

Okaya · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Okaya Taiko

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-2345

    At the Okaya Taiko Festival, 300 drummers play together on a 60-meter outdoor stage before visitors from across Japan.

  • Tsurumine Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Central Japan's top azalea spot, with 30-plus varieties and 30,000 bushes peaking mid-to-late May, plus a festival.

  • Okaya Silk Workshop

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-24-2245

    Weaving and dyeing experiences (reservation required) in Okaya, once Japan's top silk town, plus a necktie sales corner.

  • Okaya Hot Springs

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Hot springs beside Lake Suwa; bathe at Romanet's Roman bath, lodging facilities, or the hot-spring stand.
