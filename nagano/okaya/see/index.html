Sights in Okaya, Nagano

Okaya · Nagano · 25 places

See

  • Tsurumine Park no Tsutsuji

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Park azaleas begun in the early Showa era, when three freight-car loads arrived by mistake instead of 300 plants; about 30,000 plants of over 30 kinds bloom red, white, pink, and purple.

  • Shutsu So Park no Katakuri

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Dogtooth violet viewing spot in Okaya.

  • Kosaka Kannon In (Kosaka Park) no Ajisai

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4458

    Hydrangea planting of 800 plants in full bloom in mid-July after the rainy season, in blue, white, and purple, with a view of Lake Suwa.

  • Yokogawa Kawa no Sakura Achi

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4854

    Cherry-blossom arch of about 400 trees, mainly Somei-yoshino, extending about 4 km along both embankments of the Yokogawa River.

  • Former Watanabe Family Residence

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Birthplace of Shinshu's first minister, Kunitake, and sons Chiaki and Chifuyu; called the origin of modern houses. Prefectural treasure.

  • Ilf Children's Art Museum

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-24-3319

    Features Takei Takeo, who coined doga, and permanent works by Maurice Sendak of Where the Wild Things Are.

  • Toriidaira Yamabiko Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-22-6313

    A vast, scenic, nature-rich park on a 1,000-meter rise overlooking Lake Suwa, Yatsugatake, and the Alps.

  • Okaya Lakeside Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    A Lake Suwa fountain rises 55 meters and is lit at night; cherry rows bloom fully in spring.

  • Okaya Civic General Gymnasium

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-22-8800

    A popular civic gymnasium for health promotion and a venue for various sports tournaments.

  • Suwa Lakehill Country Club

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-1224

    Its wooded course, rated 71.1, stirs challenge from average golfers to low-handicap players.

  • Autumn Foliage at Izuhaya Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Peak late October to mid-November; many species yield shifting red, yellow, and orange leaves, also known for spring dogtooth violets.

  • Takabocchi Plateau and Mount Hachibuse

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Refreshing plateau overlooking Lake Suwa, Yatsugatake, Mount Fuji, and the Alps; azaleas bloom in June, daylilies in July.

  • Swans of Lake Suwa

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Swans, a winter symbol since two whooper swans in 1974, now exceed 200 yearly, viewable against Mount Fuji.

  • Prefectural Izuhaya Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Shrine grove is a city natural monument; 240 species color autumn, and the maple festival lights the park at night.

  • Enrei Onodachi Park Lookout

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Famous for autumn leaves and birdsong in Japan's 100 Soundscapes; the only view of Yatsugatake, Fuji, and the Northern Alps.

  • Kosaka Kannon-in Temple

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4458

    Rebuilt in 1715 by fourth Takashima lord Tadataka; Okuma-style work by Ushiyama Zensuke and Hanaoka Isoemon.

  • Nashikubo Ruins

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    A typical mountain settlement ruin on an alluvial fan overlooking Mount Fuji, where Nashikubo-style pottery was found.

  • Former Hayashi Family Residence

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Silk-reeling family's mansion from the industry's peak, with an adjoining tearoom and Western house wrapped in gold leather paper.

  • Dehaya Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Vast dogtooth-violet colonies delight visitors in mid to late April, and autumn foliage in late October to early November.

  • Enrei Goryotachi Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Panoramic views of Lake Suwa, Yatsugatake, Mount Fuji, and the Northern Alps; also known for sunrise and sunset.

  • Okaya City Yamabiko International Skating Center

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-24-5210

    A comprehensive sports facility where elite athletes train and beginners and the public can casually enjoy skating.

  • Yamabiko Skate Forest Ice Arena

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-24-2494

    Beside Yamabiko Park, families skate and curl even in summer on Japan's top-class ice, except mid-July through September.

  • Enrei Outdoor Activity Center

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-8028

    Adjacent to scenic Enrei Goryotachi Park, with a green sports house, campsite, lawn, field athletics, and running course.

  • Wild Birds of Enrei Goryotachi Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    Every Sunday in May and June a bird bus runs; the beautiful songs and scenery rank among Japan's 100 Soundscapes.

  • Autumn Foliage at Enrei Goryotachi Park

    Okaya, Nagano · 0266-23-4811

    A nature park around Shiojiri Pass, designated a prefectural bird forest, offering birdsong and autumn foliage worth seeing.
