Sights in Okuwa, Nagano

Okuwa · Nagano · 13 places

See

  • Ina Kawa Damu Ue Tozanguchi

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-3080

    Trailhead above Inagawa Dam: vehicles cannot go past the lower gate, a hike-registration post stands nearby, and there is no parking, so hikers are asked to use taxis or similar.

  • no Zokido Forest Park

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-3488

    Forest park with Central Alps views, spring azaleas, blueberry picking from summer into autumn, a campground, and star viewing; open late April-late May and mid-July-late September.

  • Atera Gorge

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-4566

    Gorge with high-transparency water called Atera Blue that appears blue or green with the light, plus hinoki forest and steep rocks; private cars cannot enter in the restriction period.

  • Suhara Yado no Mizu Fune

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-4566

    Water troughs hollowed from sawara logs, used since the post-town era for washing rice, vegetables, and dishes and as a resting place.

  • Atera Gorge no Mizu

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-4566

    Gorge of emerald-green Atera Blue water with seasonal flowers, autumn foliage, a suspension bridge, and fences reused from old forest-railway rails; vehicle entry is restricted in summer.

  • Hakusan Shrine

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-4566

    Shrine founded in 1344 with four national Important Cultural Property halls, Zao, Hakusan, Izu and Kumano, in plain one-bay nagare-zukuri, open only during festivals.

  • Sadakatsu Tera

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-4566

    Rinzai Myoshinji-school temple, the oldest of Kiso's three major temples, with nightingale-floor corridors, a garden, spring weeping cherries, and autumn foliage.

  • Okuwa Folk History Museum

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-3550

    Museum displaying forest-resident life and Nakasendo history of Suhara-juku and Nojiri-juku, plus a Nagano Prefecture treasure, perforated flanged pottery with a human face, nicknamed Eternal Smile.

  • Okuwa Tami Gym

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-1020

    Gym with two volleyball courts, two basketball courts, a judo-kendo room and changing rooms; classes are held regularly, and camp use with the adjacent sports park needs a reservation.

  • Koiji Pass

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-57-2727

    A corruption of Koji Pass whose forked red pine shelters welcoming Enmei Jizo; the overlook was Shinshu-certified in 2018.

  • Nozokido Forest Park Azalea Garden

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-3080

    Colonies of three-leaf and mountain azaleas fill the park, purple in late April and red from early to mid-May.

  • Weeping Cherries at Joshoji Temple

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-4566

    Joshoji, an Important Cultural Property and Kisoji's oldest historic temple, has a little-Kyoto garden, beautiful weeping cherries, and autumn leaves.

  • Shinshu Famous and Secret Waters: Atera Gorge Beautiful Face Water

    Okuwa, Nagano · 0264-55-3080

    Drinkable spring from Atera Gorge forest rocks; legend says an Owari official's wife grew fair washing her face with it.
