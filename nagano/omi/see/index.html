  • Sarugababa Toge

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    Mountain pass on the boundary of Chikuma City and Omi Village on the Hokkoku Nishi Kaido (Zenkoji Kaido), now National Route 403 north of Lake Hijiri.

  • Hozen Tera

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2061

    Buddhist temple in Omi.

  • Omi Jinmei Miya

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-3001

    Shrine divided from Ise Inner Shrine in the Heian period; five buildings are national Important Cultural Properties, and a sacred tree said to be 800 years old is a village natural monument.

  • Zendo Tera

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2176

    Branch temple of Kyoto's Chion-in, enshrining Amida with Kannon, Seishi, and a wood-root Kannon, plus 49 bird-and-flower and 54 flower ceiling paintings.

  • Zenkoji Nishi Kaido

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    Old road where Sarugababa Pass, called its hardest point, has the site of Osen's teahouse and a folktale of signboard girl Osen; Omi-juku below retains a few tile roofs and earthen walls.

  • Hironori Shimizu to Sen Chaya Ato

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    Former teahouse site with a Basho haiku monument, where a woman named Osen is said to have run a teahouse.

  • Kawashima Naniwa Yoshiko Besso Ato

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    Site of the Museian villa built at Hijiri by Kawashima Naniwa, who adopted Prince Su's daughter Yoshiko.

  • Omi Yado Honjin, Wakimoto Jin'ato

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-3001

    Honjin inn and camp ruins in Omi.

  • Hijiri Highlands

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    A seasonal highland near Nagano and Chikuma, popular for summer escapes at about 1,000 meters.

  • Lake Hijiri

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    A spring-fed natural lake in Hijiri Highlands, a crucian-carp fishing mecca with beloved duck-shaped boats.

  • Hijiri Highlands (Autumn Foliage)

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2133

    Beautiful autumn leaves around Lake Hijiri and Okuhijiri, especially the large maple at Kawashima Naniwa Lodge.

  • Fukumanji Temple

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-2651

    At Mount Hijiri's foot, a Heian ascetic center and prayer temple housing five Important Cultural Properties.

  • Omi Post Town

    Omi, Nagano · 0263-67-3001

    Prospered before Zenkoji Kaido's hardest pass; traces of old inns remain, with Mount Kamuriki views.
