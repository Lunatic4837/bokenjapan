Things to do in Otaki, Nagano

Otaki · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Ontake Ginga Village Campground

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2332

    Campground at 1,500 m on Ontake Plateau, with cabins, bungalows, tent sites, telescopes, and nightly star viewing from mid-July to late August.

  • Ontake Mori Kichi Oto Campground

    Otaki, Nagano · 080-2382-1569

    Auto campground with 20 AC-powered sites in a natural hinoki forest near the Shirakawa stream, with biological waste treatment and toilets.

  • Renge Azaleas at Ontake Ginga Village Campground

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2257

    Renge azaleas blanket the campground, their colors playing against white birch with Mount Ontake behind.

  • Ontake Ski Resort

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2240

    A snow resort above the clouds at 2,240 meters, open from early December to early April.
