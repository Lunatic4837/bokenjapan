See in Otaki

Otaki · Nagano · 10 places

See

  • Ontake Highlands

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2257

    Highlands at 1,500 m elevation with Mt. Ontake as a backdrop, with walking paths, a forest-bathing area and a campsite; fresh green mid-June, autumn leaves mid-October.

  • Ta no Hara Tennen Park

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2257

    Natural park with views of Mt. Ontake, the Central Alps, Norikura, and Kaida Highlands; the summer Otaki-guchi trail to Kengamine takes about 3 hours, and it closes early November to early May.

  • Nature Mizumi

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2257

    Lake formed when dammed by the 1984 western Nagano earthquake, with standing dead trees and canoe tours; new leaves mid-late May, foliage late October, full freeze Jan-Feb, thaw from early March.

  • Kiyotaki

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2257

    Waterfall where Ontake-kyo believers still practice purification; autumn color mid-October to early November, icing from late December, sometimes fully frozen late January to early February.

  • Otori Izumi Tera no Shidarezakura

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2257

    Weeping cherry more than 350 years old, designated a prefectural monument.

  • (Shinshu no Meisui Hi Mizu) Ontake Jinja Satomiya Ugan Mizu

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2257

    Spring dripping from the cliff behind Ontake Shrine Satomiya and nurturing moss below, taken in the mouth as purification water; 371 stone steps up from the parking lot.

  • Ontake Jinja Satomiya

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2660

    Shrine hall under a standing rock, 449 steps from the road past large hinoki and sawara; formerly Iwato Gongen, later a village shrine of Ontake whose faith spread in the Edo period.

  • Naganoken Seiyaku

    Otaki, Nagano · 0120-100-975

    Factory at the foot of Mt. Ontake that manufactures and develops medicines including Ontake Hyakuso-gan, with video-guided tours of the process, herb and Kampo exhibits, and a shop.

  • Ontake Shrine Satomiya

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-46-2047

    The shrine hall stands beneath a solemn standing rock after 368 stone steps from Mount Ontake's first station.

  • Tanohara Tourist Center

    Otaki, Nagano · 0264-48-2544

    At Mount Ontake's Tanohara trailhead at 2,180 meters, beside Tanohara Lodge, with lodging, meals, and a shop.
