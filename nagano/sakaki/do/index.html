Things to do in Sakaki, Nagano

Sakaki · Nagano · 3 places

Do

  • Sakaki Shrine

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Said to date from 673 and listed in the Engishiki; Takeda Shingen restored it by donating shrine lands.

  • Izayoi Moon-Viewing Hall and Surrounding Scenery

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    From the hall on Murakami's Amikake Izayoi Mountain, the moon and the vast Chikuma River below are splendid.

  • Sakaki-juku Hometown History Museum

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-4193

    Displays materials on Murakami Yoshikiyo's fight with Takeda Shingen and the prosperous Edo post town of Sakaki-juku.
