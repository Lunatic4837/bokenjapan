Sights in Sakaki, Nagano

Sakaki · Nagano · 13 places

See

  • Wahei Kogen Yama no Ie

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Lodging facility for 100 people that provides nature experiences for youth and groups.

  • Wahei Highlands

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Highland at 1,000 m elevation, 20 minutes by car from the town center, with views of the Alps.

  • Sakaki Chikumagawa Bara Park

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Park combining the Chikuma River and roses, the town flower, with more than 330 varieties blooming in large flowers.

  • Ko Kumo Tera Suginami Ki

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Temple approach about 150 meters long with about 80 cedars roughly 300 years old, designated a town natural monument.

  • Kyu Kitaguni Kaido Town Nami

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Old Kitaguni highway townscape in Sakaki.

  • Katsurao Castle Ruins

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Mountain castle ruins of the Murakami clan on a ridge at 800 m, overlooking the Chikuma River and surrounding mountains.

  • Murakami Shi Yakatato

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Site of the Murakami clan residence represented by Sengoku general Murakami Yoshikiyo, with Mansenji Temple built where Takeda Shingen burned it.

  • Kaku Chi Gakko

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3371

    Schoolhouse of pseudo-Western architecture built in 1877, exhibiting founding-period documents and folk cultural properties.

  • Manyo Sakimori Kahi

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Manyo poem monument in a shrine precinct at the southern end of town, erected in the Tenpo era by Takizawa Koan of Nezumijuku.

  • Izayoi Mizuki Do to Basho Kuhi

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3111

    Moon-viewing hall, one of the Sarashina Eight Views, with a monument to a verse Basho composed on his Sarashina Kiko journey.

  • Kakuchi School History and Folklore Museum

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-3371

    This pseudo-Western schoolhouse, built in 1877 and moved here in 1983, displays folk cultural properties.

  • Sakaki Town Iron Exhibition Hall

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-1128

    It shows works by the late Living National Treasure swordsmith Miyairi Yukihira and Sakaki art and history exhibitions.

  • Bingushi no Sato Park

    Sakaki, Nagano · 0268-82-0234

    It has two all-weather tennis courts, an indoor gateball field, and a children's playground.
