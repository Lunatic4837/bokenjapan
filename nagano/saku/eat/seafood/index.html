    Kappo Kagetsu

    Dining in Saku

    Kappo Kagetsu in Saku, founded in 1924, serves Saku carp dishes such as arai, carp soup, simmered carp and salt-grilled carp; lunch 11:30-14:30, dinner orders until 19:00.

  • 寿司割烹 八風

    Sushi Kappo Happu

    Dining in Saku

    Sushi kappo serving seasonal fish as sashimi and nigiri, whole-fish grills and creative dishes, with counter seats and private rooms; lunch 11:00-14:30, dinner 17:00-23:00, irregular closures.
