Sights in Shimosuwa, Nagano

Shimosuwa · Nagano · 15 places

See

  • Yashima Marsh

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-52-7000

    Wetland at 1,630 m designated a national special protection area, with a wide variety of subalpine plants including renge azalea.

  • Kannon Sawa Gorge

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-26-2102

    Gorge from Yashimagahara Marsh that descends from the Misayama ruins to the Ohira settlement, with forest, large rocks and streams, known as a forest where Onbashira are cut.

  • Suwa Mizumi no Ugan Watari

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-26-2102

    Phenomenon when Lake Suwa freezes over completely, said to be the path the Upper Shrine's male deity takes to the Lower Shrine's goddess.

  • Yashima Marsh no Rengetsutsuji

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-52-7000

    Azalea viewing spot in Shimosuwa.

  • Doku Sawa Kosen

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-26-2102

    Hot spring with a legend as Takeda Shingen's hidden bath, long used for cures, and marked by highly acidic red and colored water.

  • Suwataisha Shimosha Togu

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-27-8035

    Shinto shrine in Shimosuwa.

  • Shimosuwa Yado Honjin Iwanami Ie

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-28-7055

    Former honjin inn with a stroll garden of old enkianthus trees and set stones; only part remains, and many daimyo once stayed there.

  • Soraku Tsuka (Kai Tsuka)

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-26-2102

    Mound in Shimosuwa.

  • Shukuba Kaido Museum

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-27-8827

    Folk museum in the center of the hot-spring district, in a vertical-lattice, white-walled building retaining features of Edo-period post-town houses.

  • Manji no Sekibutsu

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-26-2102

    Stone Buddha in rice fields along the Togawa beside Suwa Taisha Shimosha Harumiya, a head set on a hemispherical natural stone over 2 m high.

  • Suwa Grand Shrine Lower Shrine Akimiya

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-27-8035

    Suwa Taisha, one of Japan's oldest shrines, has over 10,000 branch shrines; its Lower Shrine includes Harumiya and Akimiya.

  • Hashimoto Masaya (Former Koshu Kaido Teahouse)

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-26-2102

    A teahouse that looks like an old inn, preserving its original structure and serving Edo-period meals and tea.

  • Shimaki Akahiko Poetry Monument

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-26-2102

    Many poem monuments in Shimosuwa, including Takagi and Suigetsu Park, can be rubbed for copies.

  • Kakiin Sanbo

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-27-1627

    Birthplace of Araragi master Shimaki Akahiko, fronted by a pine over 300 years old.

  • Shimaki Akahiko's Grave

    Shimosuwa, Nagano · 0266-27-1627

    This is the grave of Araragi poet Shimaki Akahiko.
