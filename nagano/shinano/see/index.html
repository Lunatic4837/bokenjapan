  • Tanguramusukiisakasu

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-258-3511

    Ski resort offering skiing and snowboarding for families.

  • Ippan Shadanhojin Shinshu Shina no Town Tourism Association

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-3226

    Station-front tourist office attached to Kurohime Station, renting electric city bikes, electric bicycles, cross bikes, and electric cross bikes.

  • Shinano Sogojoho Center

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-6677

    General information center in Shinano.

  • Dowa no Mori Gyararii

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-6720

    Art museum next to Kurohime Fairy Tale Hall showing works by artists connected with Shinano, closed from December 1 to April 4.

  • LAMP Nojiriko

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-258-2978

    Lodging, sauna, and restaurant complex with seasonal kayaking, SUP, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing, and foraging.

  • Kurohimekogen Su no Park

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-3171

    Ski and snowboard area on the east slope of Mount Kurohime (2,053 m), with natural-snow slopes overlooking Lake Nojiri.

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-3226

    Waterfall on the border with Myoko City, Niigata, also called Earthquake Falls, a 50 m drop listed among 100 selected falls, with May greenery and October foliage.

  • Shinano no Hotaru

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-258-2090

    Firefly viewing from late June to mid-July, especially along the Imogawa canal in the Togusa area.

  • Komazume Stone

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-3226

    While Kiso Yoshinaka rested under a pine, his horse left hoof prints on a stone.

  • Uga Shrine Grand Festival

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-3226

    Annual festival August 26-28, plus a seven-year unveiling rite said to have begun in 1628.

  • Mitoguchi Park

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-3226

    There is a monument to Canadian missionary A.R. Stone.

  • Fishing Land Hanaoka

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-2709

    Enjoy fishing among birdsong and flowers, with handmade play equipment and trampolines for children.

  • Asian Skunk Cabbage at Okubo Pond

    Shinano, Nagano · 026-255-3226

    Asian skunk cabbage blooms near Okubo Pond from late April beside a maintained walking path.
