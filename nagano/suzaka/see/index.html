Sights in Suzaka, Nagano

Suzaka · Nagano · 24 places

See

  • Gomi Ike Hafu Kogen Shizen Sono no Rengetsutsuji

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Renge azalea colony, said to number about one million plants, that blooms all at once in early summer and turns the area red.

  • Chikumagawa Kasenjiki no Momo no Hana

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Peach blossom viewing spot in Suzaka.

  • Naganodentetsu Ensen no Ringo no Hana

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Apple blossom viewing spot along the railway in Suzaka.

  • Benten San no Shidarezakura

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Weeping cherry viewing spot at Benten-san in Suzaka.

  • Cho Myo Tera no Sakura

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Cherry blossom viewing spot at Cho Myo Temple in Suzaka.

  • Ohinata Kannon Do no Shidarezakura

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Two weeping cherry trees, estimated at 250 years old and 20 m tall, called the Kannon-do couple cherries; the right-hand tree fell in snow on 17 January 2017.

  • Enmei Jizo no Edohigan

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Edohigan cherry about 400 years old, 12 m tall, with a trunk circumference of about 5.6 m and an east-west spread of 13 m.

  • Garyo Park Cherry Blossoms

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-245-1770

    Cherry blossom viewing spot in Garyo Park in Suzaka.

  • Autumn Foliage at Yonago Falls

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    Two immense waterfalls and autumn foliage create a spectacular view that moves every visitor.

  • Shinshu Suzaka Fruit Ale

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-214-6225

    This craft beer lavishly uses Suzaka-grown fruit and comes in six sweet and dry styles.

  • Gomiike Happu Plateau

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    You can observe the prefecture's largest lotus azalea colony and rare plants and animals.

  • Yonago Fudoson, Yonago Ryuzan Fudoji Temple

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-245-0972

    This historic temple, Shinshu's oldest Fudo site and one of Japan's three great Fudoson, enshrines Fudo Myoo.

  • Hatcho Yoroizuka Burial Mounds

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-248-9027

    There are five stone-piled burial mounds, which yielded mirrors, jasper magatama, and shell bracelets.

  • Butterfly Folk Museum

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-248-5164

    The museum displays materials on butterflies and people, plus many rare and collection-banned world specimens.

  • Tsubame Nagaden Taxi

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-245-0351

    Recommended for efficient Obuse sightseeing, with local drivers and a taxi stand outside Obuse Station.

  • Kusunoki Winery

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-214-8568

    Shigeyuki Kusunoki learned winemaking in Australia and began brewing in his hometown Suzaka in 2011.

  • Gomiike Happu Plateau Autumn Foliage

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-215-2225

    It features a million early summer azaleas, autumn foliage, and year-round Northern Alps and Zenkoji Plain views.

  • Suzaka Hot Spring

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-245-1460

    This view-rich hilltop hot spring overflows with the poetic charm of northern Shinano.

  • Chie Koike Collection World Folk Doll Museum

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-245-2340

    It displays folk dolls long collected by Chie Koike, former honorary president of Bunka Fashion College.

  • Nobutaka Oka Collection Suzaka Classic Art Museum

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-246-6474

    Housed in a large Meiji townhouse, it displays old folk crafts and popular Meiji and Taisho kimono.

  • Wealthy Merchant's House Tanaka Family Museum

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-248-8008

    The Tanaka main house, Suzaka Domain purveyors since mid-Edo, displays heirloom paintings, calligraphy, and pottery.

  • Suzaka Print Art Museum and Un'ichi Hiratsuka Print Art Museum

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-248-6633

    It presents prints by Suzaka-born Asaji Kobayashi plus other permanent and special exhibitions.

  • Suzaka City Zoo

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-245-1770

    In Garyu Park, famed for cherries and pines, the zoo keeps capybaras, tigers, bears, kangaroos, and penguins.

  • Suko Regional General Pool (Summerland)

    Suzaka, Nagano · 026-245-1173／（シーズン中）026-246-0155

    It has 120m and 70m slide pools, a 180m stream pool, competition, diving, and toddler pools.
