Sights in Takagi, Nagano

Takagi · Nagano · 13 places

See

  • Akaishi Rindo

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-2001

    Forest road in Takagi.

  • A Shima no Oto

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-5126

    Wisteria of 12 vines on about 1,000 square meters, some nearly 2 m long and about 400 years old, with purple, white, and pink flowers; maintenance was underway in 2023.

  • Kurin Kusa

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-5126

    Colony of the village flower kurin-kusa in a wetland in Kujukutani Forest Park, blooming in pink, white, red, yellow, and other colors.

  • Shi Jo no Shidare Sakura

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-2001

    Cherry blossom viewing spot in Takagi.

  • Popii Sono (Iku Kan Hara)

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-3999

    Poppy field of about 40,000 plants on 5,000 square meters, where a 200-yen contribution includes picking 10 stems.

  • Iku Kan Hara Rabenda Sono

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-3999

    Lavender field on a river-terrace slope; cooperation fee 100 yen, and picking is 200 yen per 100 g.

  • Tsukumo Tani Forest Park

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-2001

    Forest park in Takagi.

  • Muku Hatoju Memorial Museum Kinen Library

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-4569

    Memorial museum and library displaying materials on children's author Muku Hatoju from this village, with a cat director working about two days a week.

  • Kagoyoke Falls

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-2001（代表）

    This waterfall east of Yahazu Tunnel freezes completely in winter.

  • Yuhigaoka Park

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-4569

    Beautiful sunsets sink into Mount Ena, reflecting the spirit of Takagi-born children's author Muku Hatoju.

  • Shogetsuan

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-2001

    This two-story teahouse was built in the governing Chiku clan's compound and requires reservations.

  • Takagi Village History and Folklore Museum

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-4508

    Displays center on the Chiku clan, who once governed this area.

  • Takagi Village Sports Park Tennis Courts

    Takagi, Nagano · 0265-33-2001

    These sand-filled artificial-turf courts suit both hard tennis and soft tennis.
