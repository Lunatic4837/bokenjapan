Things to do in Takamoricho, Nagano

Takamoricho · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Yama no Tera Campground

    Takamoricho, Nagano · 0265-35-9406

    Campground reopened in April 2022 at 900 m in a quiet forest, with nature walks and birdwatching.

  • Shinshu Takamori Onsen Yugahora

    Takamoricho, Nagano · 0265-35-8260

    Hot-spring lodging with a natural-radon spring and a viewing bath facing the Southern Alps.

  • Shinshu Takamori Onsen Ontai no Kan

    Takamoricho, Nagano · 0265-35-8270

    Day-use hot spring with a natural radon spring that displays relics of the late Yoshiro Shimaoka, former Meiji University baseball manager.

  • Tonari Matsurigoto Tera (Yama no Tera)

    Takamoricho, Nagano · 0265-35-5362

    Tendai sect temple surrounded by mountains and called Yama no Tera, with 33 Kannon and Kaidan Fudo nearby.
