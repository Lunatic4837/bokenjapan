Sights in Takayamamura, Nagano

Takayamamura · Nagano · 14 places

See

  • YAMABOKU Guriinsaito

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-274-5959

    Campsite at Yamada Ranch at 1,500 m elevation, open for camping from late April through October.

  • Shichimi Otaki

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-214-9296

    Waterfall dropping through three plunge pools; the approach road is rough in places.

  • Yamada Bokujo Haikingukosu

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-214-9296

    Hiking course of two hours one way past grazing cattle, then bamboo grass and shrubs to Kasadake, with 360-degree views of the Northern Alps and Mt. Yokote and a late-May mountain opening.

  • Hachi Taki Lookout

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-214-9296

    Lookout with views of Matsukawa Gorge and Hachitaki Falls, plus spring Oyama cherries and autumn leaves; closed late November to late April.

  • Suparojji Reddoddoin

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-242-2418

    Spa lodge at Yamada Ranch with a day-visit hot spring, built in 1987 from 1,650-year-old redwood imported from Sequoia National Park.

  • Raidahausu Ruto 66

    Takayamamura, Nagano

    Guest house and rider house in central Yamada Onsen, facing the free-flowing non-circulating communal bath Oyu and association-only bath Taki no Yu.

  • YAMABOKU WILD SNOW PARK

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-274-5959

    Snow park on a 1,500-1,750 m pasture with ungroomed slopes and Northern Alps views, a 13 km Takochi course dropping 800 m, and a weekday fee of 2,500 yen for junior-high age and older.

  • Kohii & Gyararii (Santa Ranch)

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-242-2932

    Cafe and gallery at Yamada Ranch serving homemade cake and lunch that change by season, with June-September taco rice, October-May hayashi rice, and wheelchair access.

  • Megumi Mame Kura Shoten

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-242-2605

    Manju shop selling brown, plump, chewy buns, including onsen manju, and other souvenirs.

  • Takayama Village Office Commerce and Tourism Section

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-214-9296

    Open year-round; closed weekends, holidays, and December 29 to January 3.

  • Cycle Oasis

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-242-1122

    Offers bike stands, tool and pump loans, water, and toilets at village sites.

  • Autumn Foliage at Kaminari Falls and Yattaki Falls

    Takayamamura, Nagano · 026-245-1100

    Two Matsukawa Gorge falls, peaking in early October, contrast dynamic white water with maple, beech, and rowan.
