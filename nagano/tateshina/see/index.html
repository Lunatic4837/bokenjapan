Sights in Tateshina, Nagano

Tateshina · Nagano · 13 places

See

  • Tateshinayama Shichi Gome Tozanguchi no Ichi no Torii

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-6201

    First torii at seventh-station trailhead in Tateshina.

  • Tade Sen Falls

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-6131

    Waterfall of about 8 m in Gosensui Nature Garden, fed by the Mizude spring and reached by about a 30-minute walk.

  • O Izumi Shizen Sono no Shakunage

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-6131

    Nature garden at 1,830 m where more than 2,000 Azuma rhododendrons peak from late May to early June, and Hakusan rhododendrons peak in early July.

  • O Izumi Shizen Sono no Rengetsutsuji

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-6131

    Renge azalea viewing spot in Tateshina.

  • Shirakaba Highlands no Autumn Leaves

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-6201

    Autumn leaf viewing spot in Tateshina.

  • Kasatori Toge no Matsunami Ki

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-56-2311

    Row of more than 60 old red pines, 150 to 300 years old, along the former Nakasendo.

  • Ame Sakai Toge Saishi Iseki Gun

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-56-2311

    Ritual site group at 1,580 m Ame Sakai Pass on the north foot of Mt. Tateshina, with 5th-6th century sites and talc imitation magatama found nearby.

  • O Izumi Shizen Sono

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-6131

    Alpine plant garden at 1,830 m reached by gondola from Tateshina Ranch, covering 169 ha with 300 kinds of alpine plants and many wild birds.

  • Shirakaba Kogen Sogo Kanko Center

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-6654

    Tourist information center for Shirakaba Kogen at Tateshina Ranch, with a simple post office.

  • Tateshina Gorufu Kurabu

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-56-3111

    Golf club with a hilly but flat course, riding carts, and a choice of caddie-accompanied or self-play.

  • Former Ashida-juku Honjin Tsuchiya Family Residence

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-56-2311

    Has a yew Kyoto-style raised room and surviving daimyo hospitality rites; unique on the Nakasendo.

  • World Shadow Picture, Paper-Cut, Glass and Music Box Museum

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0266-68-2100

    Displays fantastical color shadow pictures, antique music boxes, and glass, with a well-stocked shop.

  • Tateshina Teddy Bear Museum at Lake Shirakaba

    Tateshina, Nagano · 0267-55-7755

    Over 9,000 teddy bears are displayed across 15 countries and 18 themes.
