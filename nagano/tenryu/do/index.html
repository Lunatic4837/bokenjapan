Things to do in Tenryu, Nagano

Tenryu · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Wachi Nogawa Campground

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-2001

    Campground with clean water for river play in hot summer, and bathing at Ryusen no Yu about 6 km away in Hiraoka.

  • Okochi Forest Park Campground

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-1023

    Quiet park ringed by conifers, with auto campsites and tent areas; nearby Tenryu Onsen bathing.

  • Mukata Okime Festival

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-3206

    Maskless yudate kagura is performed with fans, yachigo, and swords, and it is a national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property.

  • Sakabe Winter Festival

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-3206

    Kumagai family chronicle says it began in 1428 with masked dances and is a national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property.
