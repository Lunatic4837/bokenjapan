Sights in Tenryu, Nagano

Tenryu · Nagano · 12 places

See

  • Hiraoka Dam

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-1023

    Dam built during and after the war, 62.5 m high and 258 m long, generating 101,000 kW.

  • So Kido Kawa Gorge

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-1023

    Gorge on the Hayakido River, a clear Tenryu tributary, with fishing spots for amago, ayu, and other fish, and autumn leaves.

  • Town Hall Okujo no Bupposo

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-2001

    Village bird and prefectural natural monument that arrives in summer, with a red beak and blue-green feathers that show spots when the wings open.

  • Matsushima Sakura Park

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-1032

    Cherry park of about 120 Somei-Yoshino trees planted to mark completion of the Hiraoka Power Station and lit at night.

  • Tenryugawa Gorge (Autumn Leaves)

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-1023

    Gorge where autumn colors on mountains along the Tenryu River begin in mid-October and can be seen from cars and trains.

  • Okochi Ike Daijin Sha Reisai

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-3206

    Shrine festival of maskless yudate kagura conducted by miyabito and negi, and a National Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property.

  • Okochi Shikaoi Gyoji

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-3206

    Ritual in which a male and a female straw deer are made and a hunt is enacted to drive deer and wild boar from village forests and fields.

  • Sakabe no Kake Odori

    Kake odori dance in Tenryu.

  • Ko Ho no Kake Odori

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-3206

  • Mount Kumafuse

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-1023

    A mountain for nature observation, scenic views, trekking, and autumn foliage.

  • Joshin Falls

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-3206

    Long revered as a sacred waterfall, with Fudo enshrined beside the falls.

  • Ina Ozawa Kanzakura

    Tenryu, Nagano · 0260-32-1023
