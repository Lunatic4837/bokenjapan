Things to do in Tomi, Nagano

Tomi · Nagano · 6 places

Do

  • Usukubo Wetland

    Tomi, Nagano · 0268-62-1111

    Boardwalks near Yunomaru Highlands Campground show irises, thistles, and scabiosa; blooms are fewer, but scenery remains.

  • Yuyu Arena (Indoor Gateball Court)

    Tomi, Nagano · 0268-67-3467

    An indoor gateball court inside Roadside Station Mimaki, with a day-use hot spring and restaurants nearby.

  • Yunomaru Ski Resort

    Tomi, Nagano · 0268-64-0400

    Groomed slopes offer beginner runs, a family course, advanced terrain, and a popular snow park.

  • Yunomaru Campground

    Tomi, Nagano · 0268-62-0376

    Back-to-basics camping includes daytime walks at Usukubo Marsh and campfires under the stars.

  • Kanehara Hot Spring, Ota Ward Recreation Village Tobu

    Tomi, Nagano · 0268-63-0261

    Spring-fed scenic baths overlook the Shinshu mountains and warm visitors deeply.

  • OH!LA!HO Beer

    Tomi, Nagano · 0268-64-0001

    Founded in sunny Tomi in 1996, this brewery has grown its own hops since 2010.
