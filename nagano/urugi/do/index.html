Things to do in Urugi, Nagano

Urugi · Nagano · 4 places

Do

  • Nanshin Shu Koiki Park Hoshi no Mori Oto Campground

    Urugi, Nagano · 050-3142-1518

    Beginner-oriented campground at 1,200 m that stays cool in summer, with a hot-water cooking building, children's play equipment, and cottages.

  • Shirakaba Highlands Campground

    Urugi, Nagano · 0260-28-2311

    A free-site campground five minutes by car from Chausuyama and fifteen from Komadori-no-Yu day spa.

  • Iwakura Dam Campground

    Urugi, Nagano · 0260-28-2000

    A quiet campground on the dam lakeshore with no divided sites, so you can camp where you like.

  • Iwakura Dam

    Urugi, Nagano · 0260-28-2311

    Popular for cherry blossoms, autumn leaves, summer carp and amago fishing, and free-site camping.
