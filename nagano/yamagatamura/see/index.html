Sights in Yamagatamura, Nagano

Yamagatamura · Nagano · 8 places

See

  • Shimizu Highlands

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-98-2300

    Highland at 1,300 m with views of the Matsumoto Basin and Azumino, lodging, Kiyomizu Temple, spring greenery, and autumn leaves.

  • Kiyomizudera no Shidarezakura

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-98-5664

    Weeping cherry called Gyoki-zakura after founding priest Gyoki, a large tree in the main-hall garden past the gate and a village natural monument.

  • Jigan Yama Kiyomizudera

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-98-3155

    Old temple at 1,200 m overlooking the Matsumoto Plain; it is said Sakanoue no Tamuramaro moved its principal image to Kyoto, where it became Kiyomizu-dera.

  • Yamagatamura Sotai Doso Kami

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-98-5664

    Roadside deity stones, 40 of them, including paired male and female figures carved by Takato stonemasons.

  • Yamagatamura Tourism Association

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-31-6220

    Local public facility in Yamagatamura.

  • Taka no Kubo Nature Park

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-31-6220

    Nature park with pasqueflower colonies, adonis and other wild plants, cherry blossoms and autumn foliage, plus an upper terraced old park and a sleeping-cow statue.

  • Tobo Takeda Yaki

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-98-4617

    Pottery studio offering hand-building and wheel-throwing; pieces are later fired in a chosen color and delivered.

  • Sukai Land Kiyomizu

    Yamagatamura, Nagano · 0263-98-2300

    Ryokan at 1,300 m above a sea of clouds, with views of the Matsumoto Basin, Azumino, and starry skies, a path to Kiyomizu Temple, and nagaimo dishes.
