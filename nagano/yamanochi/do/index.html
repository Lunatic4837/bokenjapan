Things to do in Yamanochi, Nagano

Yamanochi · Nagano · 7 places

Do

  • Ryuo Maunten Resort

    Yamanochi, Nagano

    Mountain resort with a ropeway, glamping, and a dog run, overlooking Mts. Takayashiro, Madarao, and Myoko, and Sado Island on clear days.

  • Kita Shigakogen Soba Uchi Experience

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-33-6000

    Soba-making experience using 100 percent local soba flour, characterized by a smooth feel and firm texture, and requiring advance reservation.

  • Yudanakaeki Mae Onsen Momiji Baths

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-33-2133

    Day-trip natural free-flowing hot spring across the tracks from Yudanaka Station, named for a large maple by the foot bath said to be 400 years old.

  • SORA GLAMPING RESORT

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-33-6345

    Glamping resort five minutes on foot from the Ryuo ropeway, where dogs can stay, with night-sky viewing.

  • Puraibeto SPA (Bon (Bon)) / Yudanaka Onsen Yoro Zuya

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-33-2111

    Private spa Bon, opened 7 February 2023 in a Kansei-era inn, has a 63 sq m private space with a spring-steam sauna, a Finnish loyly sauna, and a free-flowing hot spring; day use is allowed.

  • Okushiga Kogen Ski Area

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2225

    Ski area with 100% natural snow, wide beginner courses, forest terrain courses, an about 3 km downhill course, and lunch and lodging.

  • Shigakogen Chuo Eria Ski Area

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2301

    Ski area in Yamanochi.
