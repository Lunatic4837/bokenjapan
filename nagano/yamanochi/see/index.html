Sights in Yamanochi, Nagano

Yamanochi · Nagano · 9 places

See

  • Shigakogen Bijita Center (Kyu Naganoken Shigakogen Shizenhogo Center)

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2133

    Visitor center using models and panels on nature guiding, observation lessons, local life and tourism to explain Shiga Kogen's origins, plus trekking and bloom information.

  • X-JAM Takai Fuji

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-33-6252

    Ski and snowboard area with a terrain park and two halfpipes, mainly beginner-intermediate slopes, beginner to advanced park features, and a family sledding plaza.

  • Shiga Highlands Shiroi Onsen Gorge Baths

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2207

    Ryokan in the Shiga Highlands with 12 rooms, a large bath, an open-air sulfur-spring bath, and meals of Shinshu beef and mountain vegetables.

  • Onuma Pond

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2404

    Onuma Pond is Shiga Kogen's largest, 26 meters deep and five kilometers around, dammed by Mount Shiga lava.

  • Sakasa Pond

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2404

    Sakasa Pond is named for looking higher at the west end than the east, with many Hakusan rhododendrons.

  • Moto Pond

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2404

    Mount Shiga volcanism formed this nearly round pond, ringed by northern Japanese hemlock and other conifers.

  • Maru Pond

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2404

    The pond reportedly stays unfrozen in midwinter, with many inns, hotels, boating, and fishing nearby.

  • Shimo-no-Koike

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-34-2404

    This clear high moor pond holds bogbean and newts, and Schlegel's green frogs spawn there.

  • Daihi Hall

    Yamanochi, Nagano · 0269-33-2886

    The hall was rebuilt from 1959 over seven years by more than 50,000 devotees praying for world peace.
