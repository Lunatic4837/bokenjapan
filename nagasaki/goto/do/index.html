Do in Goto

Goto · Nagasaki · 12 places

Do

  • Stained Glass Studio 538

    Goto, Nagasaki · 090-1977-8481

    Sells stained glass and offers workshops, by reservation, for keychains, boxes, and lampshades.

  • Takahama Beach

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-84-3163

    Representative Goto beach of white sand and emerald shallows, about 40 minutes by car from Fukue Port.

  • Kojushi Beach

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-73-5921

    Emerald sea and white sand about 20 minutes by car from central Fukue; a popular easy beach.

  • Sansan Tomie Camp Village

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-86-2920

    Campsite facing Tomie Bay with a white-sand beach, tent sites, cabins, bungalows, and bookable suke-fishing experiences.

  • Nanatsudake

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-82-1111

    431.5 m western Fukue peak, Goto's only Kyushu 100 Famous Mountains selection, with seven sawtooth rock peaks and trekking.

  • Onidake Onsen

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-72-1348

    Brown cloudy natural spring at Goto Concana Kingdom. Outdoor, indoor, and cascade baths plus sauna. Day visits irregular.

  • Koura Beach

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-87-2216

    Calm small-bay beach of striking blue at Tamanoura's far end. Showers provided; busy with islanders and visitors in summer.

  • Goto Country Club

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-72-4526

    The Goto Islands' only golf course, on Onidake's mid-slope, an 18-hole layout with sea views.

  • Goto Ware Masako Kiln

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-73-6697

    Goto ware with camellia and seasonal paintings; husband wheels each piece, wife paints. Pottery experiences offered.

  • Arakawa Onsen Foot Bath

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-87-2216

    Free foot bath at Goto's oldest onsen, open 9:00-17:00 and seating twelve to thirteen people.

  • Sakiyama Seaside Park

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-72-6111

    Seaside park without nearby houses, for swimming, fireworks, and camping, with Sazae Island visible.

  • Tomie Onsen Center Tasshaka Land

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-86-3939

    Facing Tomie Bay, with large bath, sauna, indoor pool, water slide, and a sodium-calcium chloride spring.
