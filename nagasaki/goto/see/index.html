Sights in Goto, Nagasaki

Goto · Nagasaki · 32 places

See

  • Dozaki Church

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-73-0705

    1908 brick church by Father Peru, Goto's early church site, now a museum of persecution history.

  • Onidake Astronomical Observatory

    Goto, Nagasaki

    A 60 cm Newtonian reflecting telescope, among Kyushu's largest, with staff commentary. The Milky Way is visible naked-eye.

  • Osezaki Lighthouse

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-87-2216

    White lighthouse on a cliff at Fukue Island's westernmost point, chosen among Japan's 50 lighthouses.

  • Mizonoura Church

    Goto, Nagasaki

    White wooden church rebuilt in 1938, mixing Romanesque, Gothic, and Japanese architecture.

  • Samurai Residence Street Furusato Hall

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-72-2083

    Single-story wooden tourism base on Fukue samurai house ruins, with exhibits, a garden room, and a cafe.

  • Nanatake Shrine

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-87-2211

    Enshrines seven Heike nobles and retainers who took their lives at Mount Nanatake after fleeing to Goto.

  • Tamanoura Camellia

    Goto, Nagasaki

    Famous Goto camellia mutation found in 1947; the original died from overharvesting, but descendants spread worldwide.

  • Goto Tourism History Museum

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-74-2300

    Keep-style museum at Fukue Castle Ninomaru, displaying Goto history and culture in chronological order.

  • Jotobana

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-72-6111

    Breakwater completed in 1846 by Goto Morishige to ease Fukue Castle works; also served as a lighthouse.

  • Onidake Volcanic Group

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-72-6111

    Five volcanoes of shield then cinder-cone form; grassy Onidake is ideal for hiking and kite flying.

  • Egami Village on Naru Island (Egami Church and Surroundings)

    Goto, Nagasaki

    Sotome Hidden Christians kept their faith in a seaside valley; a later wooden church symbolized the end of hiding.

  • Abunze Lava Coast

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-73-7222

    Black rugged lava shore about 7 km long, formed by an eruption near Onidake some 50,000 years ago.

  • Goto Camellia Garden

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-72-6111

    Six-hectare garden with about 260 varieties and 2,800 camellias, sea views, and a grass ski slope.

  • Prayer Goddess Statue (Hachikuma)

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-87-2211

    Seibo Kitamura statue and bell consoling Pacific War troops who last looked back at Osezaki and never returned.

  • Birthplace of the Haenkaze Floating Offshore Wind Turbine

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-78-2101

    Japan's first floating offshore wind turbine Haenkaze was demonstrated off Kabashima, then moved off Fukue.

  • Sagashima Senjojiki

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-84-3111

    Junction of Medake and Odake, where layered volcanic ash eroded by waves, rain, and wind forms unique scenery.

  • Shigeshiki Dam

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-86-1111

    Nagasaki-managed earth dam for irrigation and water; spring cherry blossoms, greenery, and downstream fireflies.

  • Mt. Medake (Sagashima)

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-84-3111

    Exposed crater where the volcano's cross-section shows gray lava in reddish debris.

  • Ikezuka Hamajincho

    Goto, Nagasaki

    Hamajincho colony near a quiet high-tide line, about 30 minutes northwest of Naru Port. Blooms around December to March.

  • Sairen-ji Temple

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-78-2101

    Kabashima's only temple, built in 1943 and cared for by locals, where the New Year's Eve bell can be rung.

  • Hisakajima Camellia Primeval Forest

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-77-2001

    Goto camellias center on Hisakajima; this representative forest at east-coast Nagahama covers about one hectare.

  • Motokura Townscape

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-87-2111

    Former offshore fishing base in Tamanoura, once home to over 10,000. Two-story houses crowd behind the port.

  • Togi Bridge

    Goto, Nagasaki

    Red arch bridge built in 1979 over Togi Bay, linking Okuura and Togi; also a film location.

  • Miiraku Church Catholic Museum

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-84-2099

    Beside the church, displaying Hidden Christian persecution materials, rebuilding photos, and whaling-era tools.

  • Tamanoura Eighty-Eight Kannon Hall

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-87-2211

    88-site pilgrimage modeled on Shikoku, established in March 1936. The Kannon hall in Fukura cemetery is the start.

  • Shiraishi Mooring Stone (Japan Heritage)

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-82-1111

    Shiraishi was the Kentoshi ships' last port of call; this small mooring stone is worshipped for fishing and maritime safety.

  • Ebisu Shrine

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-78-2101

    Shrine by Ifuki pier enshrining Ebisu, god of big catches. Invited in 1536 and rebuilt in 1945.

  • Amagozen Park

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-84-3111

    Sea-view park near Hasama and Hamasako with a small Amagozen shrine, grave, and sandy beach among rocks.

  • Goto Coral Museum

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-86-0613

    Museum on the second floor of Deguchi Coral Shop, showing Meiji coral, boat models, and world coral.

  • Kentoshi Ship Anchorage Monument

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-82-1111

    Monument on the cape in Uotsugazaki Park to a Kentoshi ship anchorage, visited by many tourists.

  • Hiroari Kasamatsu Memorial Museum

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-64-2209

    Museum in a former elementary school on Naru Island, centered on painter Hiroari Kasamatsu's works.

  • Ashinoura Viewpoint

    Goto, Nagasaki · 0959-78-2101

    Overlook of a small fishing village where hidden Christians once lived; 15 minutes by car from Hongama Port.
