Sights in Hasami, Nagasaki

Hasami · Nagasaki · 4 places

See

  • Hasami Town Pottery Museum

    Hasami, Nagasaki

    Displays 400 years of Hasami ware history, craftsmen's skills, and modern goods, with painting and wheel experiences.

  • World Kiln Plaza Open-air Museum

    Hasami, Nagasaki

    Twelve rare reproduced world kilns on a hill, with Eastern and European kilns placed by era.

  • Hasami Nakaoyama Ceramic Village

    Hasami, Nagasaki

    Hasami kiln district with some of the world's largest Edo climbing-kiln ruins and old alleys.

  • Hasami Town History and Culture Exchange Hall

    Hasami, Nagasaki

    Museum on Hasami history showing about 250 ceramics and documents, including 1650s porcelain exported to Southeast Asia.
