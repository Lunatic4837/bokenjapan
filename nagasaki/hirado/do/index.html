Do in Hirado

Hirado · Nagasaki · 17 places

Do

  • Hirado Onsen

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-23-8600

    Sodium bicarbonate spring said to be good for neuralgia, burns, and beautiful skin, also called the Beauty Spring.

  • Nejishi Beach

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-4111

    Shallow white-sand beach about 1 km long, chosen among Japan's 88 and 100 best beaches.

  • Hitotsuku Beach

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-4111

    Beautiful beach alongside Nejishi Beach, with sea and shore that impress on clear days.

  • Senrigahama Beach

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-4111

    Shallow beach named for its vast sandy shore, easy to swim, with marine sports and showers.

  • Hirado Onsen Arm and Foot Baths

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-4111

    Free arm and foot baths by Hirado Pier bus stop, kept at 40-42C and nicknamed the beauty spring.

  • Ikitsuki Bridge Park

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-53-2927

    Park beside Ikitsuki Bridge with plazas, paths, an outdoor stage, and shop; Nagasaki's first roadside station.

  • Seaview Ranch

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-24-2338

    Western riding club welcoming beginners, with qualified instructors, small lessons, and a sea-view restaurant.

  • Tabira Insect Nature Park

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-57-3348

    Re-created satoyama for observing insects naturally, with guides and 30 to 60 species through the year.

  • Nakase Grassland Campsite

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 090-5772-9101

    Grassland camp overlooking the Genkai Sea; Iki visible on clear days. View, auto, and day-camp sites since 2020.

  • Tabira Park

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-57-0309

    Park with field athletics, tennis courts, a sports field, and a popular 52 m roller slide. Views Hirado Bridge.

  • Ichiroku Beach

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-9140

    Swimming beach open about one month, mid-July to mid-August. Calm shallow water, busy with families in summer.

  • Kameoka Park

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-4111

    Urban park on Hirado Castle's Ninomaru site, with keep, turrets, tennis courts, and cherry blossoms.

  • Hangen Campground

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-4111

    Campground for sea and mountain nature, with bungalows, auto camping, and hot showers.

  • Arasaki Beach

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-4111

    Beautiful white-sand beach next to a municipal campground where barbecues can be enjoyed.

  • Tanoura Onsen

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-2241

    Inn with a simple cold spring said to have welled when Kobo Daishi planted his staff.

  • Daikonzaka Shirahama Beach

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-9140

    Beach on Azuchi-Oshima in Hirado City, surrounded by the sea and mountains.

  • Hayasaki Beach

    Hirado, Nagasaki · 0950-22-9140

    Beach in Ikitsuki, Hirado, beside Ikitsuki Bridge, with a nearby roadside station.
