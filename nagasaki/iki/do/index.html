Things to do in Iki, Nagasaki

Iki · Nagasaki · 5 places

Do

  • Yumoto Onsen

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Onsen village in a northwest Iki inlet, with 13 sources of reddish iron-rich water and 14 varied bath facilities.

  • Iki Bicycle Rental

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Bicycle rentals at Gonoura, Ashibe, Intsuji, and other points, with limited numbers at each location.

  • Nishikihama Beach

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-48-1130

    White-sand beach with emerald sea about three minutes from Iki Airport, a center for jet skis, SUP, and diving.

  • Kushiyama Campsite

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-48-1130

    Campsite on Kushiyama Peninsula in the island's north, with a rest area and showers.

  • Shoni Park Campsite

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-48-1130

    Campsite beside Shoni Park with Genkai Sea views, full facilities, and day camping.
