See in Iki

Iki · Nagasaki · 39 places

See

  • Kojima Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-1263

    Island shrine reached on foot only around low tide, sometimes called the Mont Saint-Michel of Iki.

  • Harunotsuji Ruins

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-2065

    Large multiple-moat settlement identified as the capital of Iki-koku, about 1km square, from the Yayoi to early Kofun period.

  • Sakyo Cape

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-48-1130

    Scenic point at the tip of Yahata Peninsula, with about 1km of cliffs and columnar basalt rocks called Kannon Pillars.

  • Tsukuyomi Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-4145

    Quiet forest shrine to Tsukuyomi, said to hear wishes about the calendar, tides, safety, and safe navigation.

  • Demon's Footprint (Makizaki Park)

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-48-1130

    110m-around natural hole in Makizaki Park, legendarily an ogre's footprint, chosen among Japan's 100 famous caves in 2013.

  • Kumano Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-4145

    Identified with Kii's Kumano Shrine. Tachibana Sadakane is recorded as enshrining it after praying in Empress Jingu's Silla campaign.

  • Katsumoto Castle Ruins

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Castle ruins on a hill overlooking Katsumoto Port and Tsushima, said built in four months in 1591 for Hideyoshi.

  • Otake Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 090-5400-2581

    Shrine where the whole mountain was once off-limits, said to be where Iki's first gods descended, enshrining Sarutahiko.

  • Kakegi Kofun

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-2728

    Round tumulus from the late 6th to early 7th century, retaining Iki's only hollowed house-shaped stone coffin.

  • Amatenagao Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-47-5748

    Iki's first shrine atop 137 mossy steps. Its 1070 Maitreya is Japan's third-oldest stone statue, an Important Cultural Property.

  • Iki Foundation Stone

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Cliffs of Katsumoto Formation at Amagahara Coast: sandstone and mudstone laid down about 25 million years ago, Iki's oldest strata.

  • Matsunaga Memorial Museum

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-44-6688

    Memorial museum for Iki-born Yasuzaemon Matsunaga, showing his belongings, letters, and a 1909 Fukuoka streetcar at the entrance.

  • Haruichiban Tower

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-48-1130

    Ship-shaped tower erected in 1987 at Motoi Park to remember fishermen lost in the strong 1859 south wind.

  • Haze Rock Dike

    Iki, Nagasaki

    About 40 m dike on southern Iki, locally called Shirataki, where black basalt rises through white rhyolite. Prefectural natural monument.

  • Sugoroku Kofun

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-2728

    Nagasaki's largest keyhole tumulus, 91m long on a 100m hill, with a gondola-shaped boat carving in the stone chamber.

  • Hakozaki Hachiman Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 090-5400-2581

    Formerly Kairi Hachiman Shrine; records Iki whaling's start. Peach-shaped basin; elephants and pigeons carved on vermilion hall.

  • Tsushimazuka Kofun

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-2728

    National historic site, Iki's second-largest keyhole tumulus at 63m, with geometric and triangular carvings in the stone chamber.

  • Mitarashimizu Falls

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Falls said to have formed when Kobo Daishi struck a rock with his staff.

  • Hime Shrine / Tobikawa Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Enshrines Emperor Jimmu and three other deities. Annual festival is April 3 with a procession and ritual dance.

  • Hyozu Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-1263

    Priest is not resident; reserve a shrine stamp by phone. Enshrines Susanoo, Onamuchi, and Kotoshironushi.

  • Kokubunji Temple Ruins

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Iki Kokubunji ruins west of Kunikatanushi Shrine, with foundation stones and Heijo-style tiles.

  • Misakito Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Founded in 811; enshrines Yamato Takeru. One of 24 Engishiki shrines. Guardian lions said to be by Yamauchi Rihei.

  • Shichiro Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Enshrines Susanoo and four other deities. The annual festival is November 28, with a ritual dance.

  • Ashibe Sumiyoshi Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Enshrines Amaterasu and five other deities. The annual festival falls on the 9th day of the 9th lunar month.

  • Kuraokami Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Annual festival on the 20th of the 9th lunar month includes a procession and ritual dance.

  • Seibo Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-42-0914

    Protects Katsumoto and enshrines Empress Jingu, with over 1,300 years of history.

  • Grave of Shoni Suketoki

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Grave in Shoni Park of 19-year-old Iki deputy Shoni Suketoki, who fought the 1281 Mongol landing.

  • Ikeda Hachiman Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Enshrines Emperor Ojin. Annual festival is November 21 with ritual dance; deity-welcoming rite is November 29.

  • Kagamitake Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    The hall is on the summit, reached by over 300 stone steps with large rises; climb slowly and carefully.

  • Ankoku-ji

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Rinzai temple renamed from Kaiin-ji in 1338. Holds an Important Cultural Property sutra and a cedar over 600 years.

  • Kunitsuokami Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-47-0675

    Enshrines Susanoo. Guardian lions by Iki master Yamauchi Rihei, made in 1862 at 63, have small heads and gentle form.

  • Takamioya Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-1263

    Said to date from 811. Enshrines Takamimusubi, Izanagi, and Izanami, with two rare pairs of guardian lions in the grounds.

  • Fukae Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Enshrines Izanami and Okunitama. Annual festival is November 11 with a procession and ritual dance.

  • Kifune Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Enshrines Takaokami. The annual festival includes an offering rite and a procession.

  • Kurosaki Battery Ruins

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-48-1130

    Huge battery ruins near Saruiwa, completed in 1933 with two 41cm cannons using main guns from the battleship Akagi.

  • Sui Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    One of Iki's 24 Engishiki shrines. Enshrines Hayaakitsuhi and Sugawara Michizane. Annual festival is November 25.

  • Togami Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-4145

    Enshrines Susanoo and two other deities. Annual festival is October 26, with a procession and ritual dance.

  • Iki Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki · 0920-45-1276

    Iki's newest shrine, main hall built in 1944, dedicated to Shoni Suketoki who died in the 1281 invasion.

  • Ishida Tenman Shrine

    Iki, Nagasaki

    Enshrines Sugawara Michizane and four other deities. Annual festival on October 25 includes a procession and ritual dance.
