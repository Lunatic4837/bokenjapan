Do in Isahaya

Isahaya · Nagasaki · 12 places

Do

  • Sazanka Highland Picnic Park and Herb Garden

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-34-4333

    Highland park with a Ferris wheel, sports slider, and putter golf; herb garden offers shopping, meals, and candle-making.

  • Yuinohama Marine Park

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-48-2213

    Calm fine-sand beach with Unzen and Amakusa views, showers, shops, camping, and one of the prefecture's largest artificial beaches.

  • Ikoi no Mura Nagasaki

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-32-2902

    Lodging midway up Mount Tara near Todoroki Gorge, with day baths, karaoke, and views of Unzen and Ariake Sea.

  • Isahaya Children's Castle at Shirakimine Plateau

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-24-8017

    Isahaya City children's facility on Shirakimine Plateau, with play equipment and crafts for infants to elementary students.

  • Moon Hill Park

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-48-1111

    Hill park with an observatory overlooking the plain, children's slides and play equipment, next to sports facilities and an onsen.

  • Isahaya Yuyu Land Reclamation Village

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-24-6776

    Reclamation museum, playground, small-animal area, and seasonal festivals; free admission from April 2026.

  • Ueyama Park

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-8325

    Isahaya's largest park, 120.3 ha, with 10,000 azaleas; part was Takashiro Castle's main keep.

  • Iimori Moon Hill Onsen

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-28-4141

    Onsen beside Moon Hill Park with large baths, a swimsuit walking bath, family baths, rest space, and a restaurant.

  • Konsenji Mountain Hut

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-8325

    Year-round hut at about 870 meters, just below Mount Tara's summit, used by over 10,000 climbers yearly.

  • Uki Beach

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-1500

    Calm breakwater-enclosed fishing-village beach, popular with families, with Shimabara Peninsula views on clear days.

  • Karako Coast

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-36-1111

    Beautiful coast with views of Unzen and the Amakusa islands, offering swimming and rocky-shore play, though not sandy.

  • Nozomi Park and Hall

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-1500

    About 4 hectares overlooking Omura Bay, with putter golf, roller skating, a stage, and a hall bath.
