Sights in Isahaya, Nagasaki

Isahaya · Nagasaki · 20 places

See

  • Shirakimine Plateau

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-23-9003

    Famous cosmos spot. Many photographers visit in peak season. Sunrise cosmos views are spectacular.

  • Fruit Bus Stop

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-34-2111

    Fruit-shaped Konagai bus stops installed for the 1990 Nagasaki Travel Exposition, inspired by Cinderella's carriage.

  • Isahaya Bay Reclamation Embankment Road (Unzen Tara Sea Line)

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-27-7045

    Weekday daytime closure from October 1 to November 30, 2026, for drainage construction.

  • Todoroki Gorge

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-1500

    Clear stream from Mount Tara with over 30 falls, including Yoryu Falls and Todoroki Falls.

  • Meganebashi (Isahaya Park)

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-1500

    Japan's first stone bridge designated a national Important Cultural Property, built in 1839.

  • Reclaimed Land Flower Zone

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-8325

    Flower plantings on Isahaya Bay reclaimed land, with rapeseed in spring, cosmos in autumn, and views of Mount Unzen.

  • Isahaya Park

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-1500

    National natural monument hills of warm-climate plants, with a 600-year camphor and Spectacles Bridge.

  • Karako Lotus Garden

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-1500

    About two hectares of lotus ponds in Karako Wetland Park, with 13 lotus varieties, water lilies and endangered asaza.

  • Tenyu-ji Temple and Isahaya Family Cemetery

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-0132

    Isahaya family temple whose prefecture-designated cemetery holds graves from the first through eighteenth lords.

  • Isahaya Shrine

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-2073

    Founded in 728 as Shimen-gu, guardian shrine of all Kyushu; its sacred camphor is a prefectural natural monument.

  • Tomikawa Valley (Tomikawa Mannen Forest)

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-1500

    About 12 km north of central Isahaya, featuring prefecture-designated 500 rock-carved arhats and a suspension bridge.

  • Shirakimine Plateau Cosmos Flower Space Museum

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-23-9003

    Four-story hands-on learning facility midway up Gokaharadake at 1,057 m, with large telescopes and art exhibits.

  • Konagai Ogatama Tree

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-34-2111

    Magnolia-family ogatama tree far larger than typical 15-meter specimens, formerly used as sakaki.

  • Isahaya Bay Central Reclamation Inner Embankment

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-0031

    Crimson clover covers the inner embankment like a red carpet, peaking from late April to early May.

  • Isahaya City Art and History Museum

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-24-6611

    Museum of Isahaya art and history, surrounded by castle ruins, Megane Bridge, and a daimyo garden.

  • Ansho-ji Temple

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-0138

    Temple appointed the domain's True Pure Land head temple and used to lodge daimyo and Siebold.

  • Mount Tara Shutara Forest

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-32-2111

    Forest supplying downstream water, with fir woods, trails, and Suijin Pond sought for its famous water.

  • Meotogi Great Cedar

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-8325

    National natural monument cedar about 32 meters tall and 9 meters around, the prefecture's largest.

  • Isahaya Bay Reclamation Eight Views

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-22-8325

    Wide views over Isahaya's reclaimed land and town, suited to rambling past historic sites and birdwatching.

  • Isahaya City Moriyama Folk Museum and Old Folk House

    Isahaya, Nagasaki · 0957-36-1111

    Panels on Moriyama nature, folk culture, and history; a restored middle-class old house used for hands-on learning.
