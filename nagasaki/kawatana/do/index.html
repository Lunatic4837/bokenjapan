Do in Kawatana

Kawatana · Nagasaki · 4 places

Do

  • Osaki Beach (Kawatana Town)

    Kawatana, Nagasaki

    Beach famed as Omura Bay's cleanest, with clear calm water and phoenix and palm trees.

  • Osaki Nature Park Peacock Garden

    Kawatana, Nagasaki

    Free park with about 200 Indian peacocks in a dome, small animals, go-karts, and seasonal flowers.

  • Mount Kokuzo

    Kawatana, Nagasaki

    Known as Kyushu's Matterhorn, with an easy 40-minute hike and Kokuzo Bosatsu at the summit.

  • Kawatana Osaki Onsen Shiosai no Yu

    Kawatana, Nagasaki

    Onsen with a large bath, sauna, open-air bath, and walking pool, open year-round with meals.
