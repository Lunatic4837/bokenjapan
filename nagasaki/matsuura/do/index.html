Things to do in Matsuura, Nagasaki

Matsuura · Nagasaki · 5 places

Do

  • Oasis Village

    Matsuura, Nagasaki · 0955-48-3135

    Campsite at summer-busy Mishiro Beach, with a Mongolian ger, tents, toilets, and showers.

  • Umikaze Park

    Matsuura, Nagasaki · 0950-57-0562

    Park opened in 2025 on reclaimed land at Mikuriya Port, with lawns, paths, and play equipment.

  • Gigigahama Beach

    Matsuura, Nagasaki · 0956-72-1111

    About 100 meters of beach, named for a creaking palanquin when the Matsura founder landed in 1069.

  • Osaki Beach (Matsuura City)

    Matsuura, Nagasaki · 0956-72-1111

    Popular white-sand beach about 150 meters long where windsurfing and camping are also possible.

  • Kaeruhana Park (Fruit Flower Park)

    Matsuura, Nagasaki · 0955-47-4084

    About 5 hectares of herb gardens, flower beds, flowering trees, and orchards, plus a playground.
