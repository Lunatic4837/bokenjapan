Things to do in Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

Minamishimabara · Nagasaki · 11 places

Do

  • Shirahama Beach and Campground

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    White-sand beach with clear water and a pine campground, chosen among Japan's 100 best beaches.

  • Maehama Beach

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    White-sand, green-pine beach for board sailing, with platforms and showers in season. The city's most popular beach.

  • Kuchinotsu Dolphin Watching

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-86-4433

    About 300 wild bottlenose dolphins can be watched swimming and jumping year-round in about one hour.

  • Minamishimabara Dolphin Watching

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-75-1515

    About 200 wild dolphins reside year-round in Hayasaki Strait and are not fed. Two adults minimum to sail.

  • Eco Park Ronshobaru

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-7056

    Campsite and experience farm in Unzen-Amakusa National Park, with cabins, animals, and activities.

  • Marine Park Arie

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Barrier-free artificial beach beside Arie Sports Park; swimming prohibited, with beach sports events.

  • Shirasu (Lithothamnium) Observation Tour

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-85-3155

    Rare plant shoal off Hara Castle visible at lowest tides; observation tours are not currently held.

  • Mount Takaiwa

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-3434

    881m peak south of Unzen Onsen, climbed in 40 minutes to one hour from Hohara Garden.

  • Minamishimabara Guide Association Arima no Sato

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Local guides introduce Christian historic sites, relics, and town walks in Minamishimabara.

  • Kazusa Auto Campground

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Auto sites only, facing mountain and sea in Minamishimabara. Year-round fishing; a beach nearby in July and August.

  • Arie General Sports Park

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-82-1430

    Park with a multipurpose ground, gateball and tennis courts, a 620-meter jogging course, and a lawn plaza.
