Sights in Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

Minamishimabara · Nagasaki · 24 places

See

  • Hara Castle Ruins

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    1496 castle ruins, a natural fortress surrounded on three sides by the Ariake Sea.

  • Debris Flow Damaged House Preservation Park

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-61-0771

    Park preserving nine houses damaged by the August 1992 debris flow to show disaster-prevention importance.

  • Ayugaeri Falls

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

    Waterfall about 14 meters high where sweetfish once returned, with rock play and flowing somen.

  • Former Onokoba Elementary School Damaged Building and Sabo Future Hall

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-72-2499

    School burned by the September 1991 pyroclastic flow, preserved beside a hall of disaster exhibits.

  • Sezumezaki Lighthouse

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

    Southern-tip lighthouse on Shimabara Peninsula; Hayasaki Seto whirlpools reach 20 m across at March-June spring tides.

  • Hara Castle Holy Maria Kannon Hall

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-61-0977

    Ten-meter wooden statue, the world's largest Maria image, carved over 40 years to mourn Shimabara-Amakusa Rebellion victims.

  • Arima Christian Heritage Museum

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-85-3217

    Introduces Hara Castle Ruins, Hinoe Castle Ruins, and Arima Seminary in Nagasaki Christian history.

  • Minamishimabara Sunflower Fields

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Sunflower fields in Minamishimabara, planted by locals after harvest, not run as a tourist farm.

  • Futagoiwa Rock

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

    Head-shaped rock carved by waves from ancient volcanic debris-flow deposits; one of two remains.

  • Harayama Dolmen Group

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Japan's oldest and largest dolmen group, from about the 4th century BC, with over 100 tombs.

  • Hinoe Castle Ruins

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

    Castle ruins of Christian lord Arima Harunobu, who founded Japan's first European secondary school.

  • Kuchinotsu History and Folklore Museum

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6773

    Museum richly displaying materials on Kuchinotsu's past and present as a historic natural harbor.

  • Nanban Ship Arrival Site

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Nagasaki-designated historic site of 1567 Portuguese ship arrivals and a 1579 Christian mission base.

  • Tatsuishi Coast

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-5540

    Coast showing Unzen's earliest layers from 500,000 years ago, above former seafloor deposits and debris-flow layers.

  • Fukae Suwa Shrine Grove

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

    Grove of Suwa Shrine enshrined in 1428, with seven large camphor trees, the largest 6.6 meters around.

  • Seibo Park (Seibo Memorial Hall)

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-85-2922

    Park honoring Peace Statue sculptor Kitamura Seibo, with about 60 works and views of Hara Castle Ruins.

  • Arie Tawaraishi Observatory

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Overlooks Arie from Route 57 between Unzen and Shimabara, with Shiranui Sea, Ariake Sea, and clear-day Mt. Aso views.

  • Tonosumi Falls

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Falls in upper Shimizu River's Tonosumi Gorge, loved for summer coolness and autumn foliage.

  • Hanabusa Observatory

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki

    Panoramic viewpoint over islands, mountains, and Hayasaki Peninsula, with visible ancient volcanic strata.

  • Sunset Hill

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Scenic spot in Eco Park Ronshobaru, Kita-Arima, with fine views of sunsets over Tachibana Bay.

  • Fukae Buried Cultural Properties and Eruption Disaster Museum

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-72-2512

    Displays excavated pottery and other artifacts, plus photos of the Unzen Fugendake eruption disaster.

  • Iwatoyama Grove

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    97-meter mountain; rare plants designated a national natural monument in 1928. Also a Kannon faith site.

  • Gonda Park

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6677

    Hilltop park at 100-140m with views, spring cherries and azaleas, autumn cosmos, and sunsets.

  • Spectacles Bridge (Minamishimabara City)

    Minamishimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-72-3111

    Stone arch bridge built in 1853, called the oldest public stone bridge on the Shimabara Peninsula.
