Things to do in Nagasaki, Nagasaki

Nagasaki · Nagasaki · 44 places

Do

  • Nagasaki Ropeway

    Nagasaki · 095-861-3640

    All-glass gondolas offer 360-degree city and famous night views, climbing in about five minutes to the 333-meter Inasa summit.

  • i+Land nagasaki

    Nagasaki · 095-898-2202

    Entertainment resort on Iojima, about 30 minutes by car from central Nagasaki, with lodges and seafood dining.

  • Kazagashira Park

    Nagasaki · 095-829-1184

    Park on 151.9m Kazagashira Mountain overlooking Nagasaki Port, famous for kite flying, seasonal flowers, and a Sakamoto Ryoma statue.

  • Glover Sky Road

    Nagasaki

    Free inclined elevator with views of Nagasaki Port and Mount Inasa; Minamiyamate Rest House and its garden are free.

  • Nagasaki Inasayama Slope Car

    Nagasaki · 095-861-3618

    Runs about 8 minutes from Inasayama mid-slope parking to the summit observatory; fully glass-walled.

  • Nagasaki Lantern Festival Chuo Park Venue

    Nagasaki

    Lantern and animal objects, many stalls, a lighting ceremony, and daily stage events during the festival.

  • Nagasaki Stadium City

    Nagasaki · 0120-101-477

    New landmark combining gourmet food, shopping, onsen, sauna, and a hotel; popular for stadium visits and families.

  • Agurino-oka

    Nagasaki · 095-801-3232

    European-style countryside on Shikimi hill, about 50 hectares, with animals, pastures, day camping, feeding and harvest experiences, and sea views.

  • Ryoma's Boots Statue

    Nagasaki · 095-823-3400

    Wearable bronze boots near Kameyama Shachu, built in 1995 for the 130th anniversary by local citizens and sculptor Yamazaki Kazukuni.

  • Kakidomari Shirahama Beach

    Nagasaki · 095-829-1268

    Film-location beach in northwest Nagasaki. Swimming area closed since FY2015. Six no-reservation barbecue pits; barbecues on the beach are banned.

  • Agri Dome

    Nagasaki

    All-weather indoor children's play facility opened in 2022, with net play, a climbing wall, and ball pool.

  • Nagasaki Lantern Festival Shinchi Chinatown Venue

    Nagasaki

    Shinchi Chinatown festival venue where red lanterns fill the streets, with many stalls.

  • Minato Park

    Nagasaki

    Park beside Shinchi Chinatown, filled with lanterns, dances, and stalls during the Lantern Festival.

  • Nagasaki Lantern Festival Hamanmachi Venue

    Nagasaki

    Lanterns pack the arcade of a colorful shopping street, with auspicious objects for photos.

  • Ark Land Spa

    Nagasaki · 095-898-2202

    One of Kyushu's largest all-weather spa parks, with a sea-view open-air bath and family baths.

  • Takahama Beach

    Nagasaki · 095-894-2201

    Nearly 1 km of white sand at the Nagasaki Peninsula tip, calm and shallow, with Gunkanjima views.

  • Nagasaki Waterfront Forest Park

    Nagasaki · 095-801-2822

    Canal park in Nagasaki Port bay area with lawns, a water-play area, and an outdoor theater.

  • Takashima Tobishima Rock Fishing Park

    Nagasaki · 095-896-4900

    Fishing park on Tobishima, connected to Takashima by a breakwater and opened in 1997.

  • Nagasaki Lantern Festival Tojin Yashiki Venue

    Nagasaki

    Former Chinese traders' quarter with nostalgic streets and a four-hall candle prayer circuit.

  • Iojima Beach Costa del Sol

    Nagasaki · 095-898-2202

    White-sand beach about 340 m long, 30 minutes by car from Nagasaki, for swimming and sea activities.

  • Takashima Beach

    Nagasaki · 095-896-3511

    Artificial beach of clear cobalt water and white sand, selected among Japan's 88 bathing beaches.

  • Sotome Konoura River Park

    Nagasaki · 0959-24-0211

    Park on Konoura River, once chosen as one of Japan's clearest streams, with a pool.

  • Nagasaki Lantern Festival Nakashima River Park Venue

    Nagasaki

    Yellow lanterns found only here reflect on the river; heart stones are popular for wishing for love.

  • MINATO SPA Bedrock Terrace

    Nagasaki · 095-898-2202

    Spa in i+Land Nagasaki with a carbonated bath, three bedrock baths, and a Thai herb tent.

  • Nagasaki Lantern Festival Doza River

    Nagasaki

    Pink lanterns create a sweet, fantastic atmosphere along the Doza River.

  • Kinkai Akamizu Park

    Nagasaki · 095-814-3410

    Park at about 440 meters beside Prefectural Forest, with Omura Bay views, trails, and camping.

  • Kinkai Central Park

    Nagasaki · 095-814-3410

    Park keeping old field walls and woods, with playground equipment, a 40-meter suspension bridge, and cherry trees.

  • Tateyama Park

    Nagasaki · 095-829-1171

    City park with the most cherry trees, about 700, plus grounds, a long roller slide, playground, and an observatory.

  • Nagasaki Nomozaki Dinosaur Park

    Nagasaki · 095-898-8009

    Dinosaur park opened October 2021 with a dinosaur museum, children's plaza, Gunkanjima museum, and sports facilities.

  • Retro Photo Studio

    Nagasaki · 095-822-8223

    Glover Garden costume photo studio in the old Mitsubishi dock house, including anime dresses.

  • Nagasaki Prefectural Forest

    Nagasaki

    Vast forest park with campgrounds, bungalows, gear rental, an athletic area, and a lodge.

  • Nagasaki Resort Island Passage Kinkai

    Nagasaki · 095-884-3991

    White resort hotel on Omura Bay in Nishisonogi, with view baths, terraces, cottages, and pet-friendly plans.

  • Onohama Seaside Park

    Nagasaki · 0959-24-0211

    Seaside park in Sotome with a large sea plaza, showers, swimming, and sunset views of Sumonada.

  • Takashima Iyashi no Yu

    Nagasaki · 095-896-2345

    Seawater bath using mineral-rich sea water, with a heated pool, mist sauna, and cave bath.

  • Nagasaki City Sakura no Sato Park

    Nagasaki

    Lawns, tennis courts, and a children's playground for year-round sports and recreation; about 8,000 cherry trees bloom in spring.

  • Nagasaki Oe Skate Park

    Nagasaki · 095-822-1257

    Among Kyushu's largest all-concrete parks at 3,280 square meters, with 15 sections for skateboard, BMX, and inline skate.

  • Koyagi General Park

    Nagasaki · 095-871-4111

    Park with tennis courts and a hilltop observatory overlooking the shipyard and port.

  • Peninsula Owners Golf Club

    Nagasaki · 095-886-3535

    Flat 18-hole course on Odo Peninsula, up to 7,023 yards, overlooking Omura Bay islands.

  • Minato Streetcar No. 310

    Nagasaki · 095-845-4113

    Streetcar redesigned by Eiji Mitooka, running since 2017, with a vivid blue port-town exterior.

  • Snorkeling Picnic

    Nagasaki · 095-896-3510

    Instructor-led snorkeling at Takashima Beach to see coral and tropical fish, entering from the shore.

  • Ocean Palace Golf Club & Resort

    Nagasaki · 095-884-1118

    Golf course on hills jutting into Omura Bay, with a unique strait-crossing hole and ocean-view hotel.

  • Citizens' Forest Cabins

    Nagasaki · 095-826-6075

    Forest park built to mark Nagasaki's 400th port opening, with cabins and a campsite.

  • Takashima Fureai Campground

    Nagasaki · 095-896-3511

    Campground beside an emerald-green beach, with permanent tents and rentable barbecue sets.

  • Motomiya Park

    Nagasaki · 095-892-1111

    Sports park of 32,000 square meters with baseball and softball fields and four tennis courts.
