Things to do in Omura, Nagasaki

Omura · Nagasaki · 9 places

Do

  • Glass Beach

    Omura, Nagasaki

    Recycled glass-sand beach created in 2016 to improve Omura Bay water quality.

  • Omata Farm and Nonomi

    Omura, Nagasaki · 0957-55-4588

    Farm for grape, chestnut, and blueberry picking, with a cafe known for homemade apple pie.

  • Nodake Lake Park

    Omura, Nagasaki · 0957-55-8254

    Scenic man-made lake, 3 km around, in Taradake Prefectural Park, with camping and a cycling path.

  • Matsubara Beach

    Omura, Nagasaki · 0957-55-8675

    Omura City's only beach on Omura Bay, with showers, toilets, changing rooms, and a rest area.

  • Kuroki Valley

    Omura, Nagasaki · 0957-53-4111

    River play, forest bathing, autumn foliage, rare alpine plants, stream fishing, camping, and hiking.

  • Boat Race Omura

    Omura, Nagasaki · 0957-54-4111

    Japan's first motorboat-racing venue, holding about 180 race days a year, with a food court and kids' room.

  • Forest Adventure Omura Nagasaki

    Omura, Nagasaki · 080-1403-0552

    France-born forest park, Nagasaki's first Forest Adventure; a zip course over Nodake Lake opens in 2026.

  • Guruun Omura Community Park

    Omura, Nagasaki · 0957-54-1011

    Community park in Omura Boat grounds with the prefecture's largest skatepark, bouldering, and lawn equipment.

  • Kotohira Sky Park

    Omura, Nagasaki · 0957-55-4641

    Nature park at 330 meters with wide views, a 999-step cherry path, roller slider, and play equipment.
