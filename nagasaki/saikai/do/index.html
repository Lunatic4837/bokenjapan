Things to do in Saikai, Nagasaki

Saikai · Nagasaki · 8 places

Do

  • Nagasaki Holland Village (Former Port Hoorn Nagasaki)

    Saikai, Nagasaki

    Closed for equipment maintenance; reopening is undecided.

  • Yukinoura Seaside Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-0064

    Seaside park with a roughly 400-meter beach, hundreds of pines, camping, surfing, and sea turtle nesting.

  • Shirikusari Seaside Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-0064

    Remote untouched beach park for swimming, fishing, and camping, recommended even off-season.

  • Saimono Riverside Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-22-9624

    Riverside park upstream of Yukinoura Dam, popular for playing in the river in summer.

  • Isanoura Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-9087

    Park for cycling, camping, and barbecue, with cottages and 1,500 cherry trees.

  • Yuridake Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-34-2001

    Park with youth campground, azalea garden, outdoor music hall, and an observatory over Saikai National Park.

  • Saikai Genki Village

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-32-2500

    Sustainable farm-life base offering pizza baking, soap making, and old-life experiences.

  • Ogama Beach

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-5833

    Beach with endangered kanoko lilies, wave-cut rocks, and unusual cliffs; shallow and calm for children.
