Sights in Saikai, Nagasaki

Saikai · Nagasaki · 10 places

See

  • 33rd Parallel Observatory

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-34-2208

    Observatory at Sakito Island's tip on the 33rd parallel, offering 360-degree views and sunsets.

  • Tsugane-otoshi Falls

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-22-1111

    About 20-meter falls with no plunge pool; water drops onto a large flat rock.

  • Oshima Bridge

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-32-9779

    White cable-stayed bridge, 1,095 meters long, opened on November 11, 1999. Toll-free since April 1, 2011.

  • Yokoseura Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-0064

    Port agreed with the Jesuits; a Portuguese ship entered in 1562. Now a park with statue, observatory, and museum.

  • Kokuzosan Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-5833

    Summit observatory with a 360-degree panorama of Sasebo Port and Omura Bay on clear days.

  • Sakito History and Folklore Museum / Mitsuharu Inoue Literature Museum and Monument

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-0257

    Museum displaying whaling and coal-mining materials, including a sei whale skeleton and mine dioramas.

  • Hachinoko Island

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-5833

    Island about 300 meters off Yokoseura Port, marked by a white cross restored in 1962.

  • Mugenkyo Kokabo

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-27-0588

    Small hillside art museum displaying ceramics and folk toys, with a mixed-tree garden for walks.

  • Oda Shell Mound Ruins Park

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-5833

    Park showing shell mounds thought to be Yayoi, pit-building traces, and medieval boar-fence replicas.

  • Hirabarugo Kirishitan Gravestone

    Saikai, Nagasaki · 0959-37-5833

    Tombstone with a cross floral design and the letters INRI carved above it.
