Things to do in Sasebo, Nagasaki

Sasebo · Nagasaki · 36 places

Do

  • Huis Ten Bosch

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0570-064-110

    Autumn Huis Ten Bosch offers Miffy Wonder Square, Air Cruise the Ride, and Halloween events.

  • Kujukushima Tourist Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki

    A 4.7-hectare lawn hill with Kujukushima panorama and sunset views, suited to walking, frisbee, and kite flying.

  • Kujukushima Sightseeing Cruise Pearl Queen

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-28-1999

    About 50-minute cruise of southern Kujukushima in Saikai National Park, plus a limited sunset cruise.

  • Kujukushima Aquarium Umi Kirara

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-28-4187

    Pearl Sea Resort aquarium recreating Kujukushima seas, with about 1,000 fish and over 100 jellyfish species.

  • Kujukushima Pearl Sea Resort

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-28-4187

    Resort park gateway to Kujukushima, offering cruises, yacht and kayak outings, an aquarium, shops, and restaurants.

  • Sky Inn Yamanoren (Sasebo Sechibaru Onsen Kunimi no Yu)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-76-2900

    Sky inn at Sasebo Sechibaru Onsen Kunimi no Yu.

  • Kujukushima Zoological and Botanical Garden Mori Kirara

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-28-0011

    Popular Sasebo spot housing 65 species and 264 animals, including Indian elephants, lions, bears, and red pandas.

  • Ohama Beach (Uku Island)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0959-57-3935

    Selected for Japan's 100 Best Bathing Beaches; white sand, cobalt sea, and a free campsite.

  • Kotonoyu Onsen at Hotel Okura JR Huis Ten Bosch

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-58-7111

    Natural onsen Kotonoyu at Hotel Okura JR Huis Ten Bosch.

  • Hiyamizudake Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Overlooks Kujukushima and Hirado, with outdoor stage and playground; mountain azaleas bloom from April to May.

  • Shirahama Beach

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-28-6099

    Sasebo's largest beach, with clear blue sea to the horizon and white sand, popular for sunsets.

  • Jumbo Fishing Village

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-77-4951

    Inlet fishing pond in Saikai National Park where beginners can fish empty-handed.

  • Eboshi Sports Village

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-24-6669

    Family site in Mount Eboshi's untouched nature, with go-karts, mini-golf, and fun bicycles.

  • Nagushiyama Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-77-4111

    About 100,000 azaleas bloom April-May, overlooking the Kujuku Islands, with a roller slide.

  • Shikamachi Onsen Yasuragi Hall

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-66-2617

    Sodium bicarbonate free-flowing spring called a beauty bath. Large baths, saunas, cold bath, and a swimsuit fitness bath.

  • Shirahama Campground

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-28-6006

    Beach campground with fixed tents and auto camping; tents, cookware, and stoves can be rented.

  • Sasebo Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-24-1111

    Lawn park with a huge camphor, Kirara playground, heated pool, and about 150 cherry trees.

  • Daihikan Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Has a softball field, tennis courts, playground, and grass ski slope for families.

  • Arkas SASEBO

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-42-1111

    Nagasaki Prefecture's largest multipurpose hall, with a 2,000-seat hall and a music hall.

  • Shiratake Nature Park Campsite

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-66-9334

    Summer tents, year-round auto camping, field athletics, a lake, and a summit trail.

  • Hirabaru Golf Course (Uku Island)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0959-57-3935

    Seaside grassland course called Japan's St Andrews, with Korai grass, grazing cattle, and ocean holes.

  • Nagasaki Prefectural Sasebo Youth Nature Center

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-23-9616

    Educational facility offering lodging and nature experience programs amid vast nature.

  • Sasebo Back Alleys

    Sasebo, Nagasaki

    Alleys called Shoes Center Street and Sesame Street, the latter lined with foreigner bars.

  • Sugehama Beach (Uku Island)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0959-57-3935

    Second-largest beach after nearby Ohama, popular for marine sports, swimming, and camping.

  • Sasebo Central Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-31-2611

    Long-loved central park with play areas, a stage, lawn, restaurants, camping, and barbecue facilities.

  • Mikawachi Ware Painting Experience

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-30-8080

    Paint your own Mikawachi plate, mug, or cup; groups of ten or more must book a week ahead.

  • Nagushiyama Campsite

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-77-4111

    Reservation-only campsite by Nagushiyama Park: 25 tent sites, rentals, toilets, and showers.

  • Makinodake Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    The one hill whose summit overlooks the whole town, with seasonal scenery and half-day hiking.

  • Kosaza Marine Sports Base

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-68-3378

    Offers canoe, marine jet, water ski, and wakeboard, with resident instructors so beginners can join safely.

  • Mount Kunimi

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    The prefecture's northern highest peak, in Kitamatsu Prefectural Park, with summit views of Hirado, Imari, and Goto.

  • UKUSAUNA

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 090-3072-1137

    Outdoor sauna on Uku Island, northernmost of the Goto Islands, for sauna and open-air bathing with sea views.

  • Mikawachi Ware Openwork Carving Experience

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-30-8080

    Experience highly skilled Mikawachi ware openwork carving. Groups of 10 or more must reserve a week ahead.

  • Kakuidae Park (Walkers Park)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-39-5519

    Kyushu Nature Trail park near 470 m, with an information shelter and free campsite. Azaleas bloom in late May.

  • Shikamachi Marine Sports Base

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-73-2050

    Seaside base facing the Kujuku Islands for kayaking, camping, barbecue, and summer swimming.

  • Sasebo International Country Club Mikawachi Golf Course

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-30-7111

    Eighteen-hole Karako and Akae courses in Saikai National Park, beside pottery towns Arita and Imari.

  • 99TRITON Catamaran Yacht

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-28-1999

    Stable twin-hull yacht for all ages, with October weekend sunset cruises and sushi meal cruises.
