Sights in Sasebo, Nagasaki

Sasebo · Nagasaki · 78 places

See

  • Kozakihana Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Mainland Japan's westernmost point, with a monument, lawn, blue sea, and tide pools.

  • Kujukushima

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    208 islands stretching 25 km north from Sasebo Port to Hirado Strait, called Japan's densest.

  • Hario Radio Towers

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-58-2718

    Three concrete radio towers about 136 meters tall, completed by the Imperial Navy in 1922.

  • Tenkaiho Observatory

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Observatory at 165 meters offering a 180-degree panorama of Kujukushima and Sasebo Port.

  • Saikai Bridge and New Saikai Bridge

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-58-2004

    1955 arch bridge spanning 316m, 43m above Hario Seto rapids, beside a glass-windowed new bridge.

  • JMSDF Sasebo Historical Museum (Sail Tower)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-3040

    Museum explaining Imperial Navy and Maritime Self-Defense Force history, with models and an observation lobby.

  • Jufuku-ji

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-65-2040

    Offers limited inverted autumn-maple and early-summer greenery views reflected in the hall.

  • Ishidake Observatory

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Observatory on 191-meter Ishidake, about 15 minutes from Kujukushima Zoo, with 360-degree views.

  • Mukyudo Cave

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-59-2003

    Huge air-raid shelter dug in 1943 by school teachers and pupils for 500 children.

  • Yumihari-dake Observatory

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Panoramic observatory of Sasebo, Kujukushima and Goto-nada, listed in Japan's 100 night views.

  • Ohashi Kannon Stone Bridge

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    One of Hirado's eight views: a natural stone bridge like a floating bridge beyond an elegant garden.

  • Former Sasebo Naval District Air Defense Command Post Ruins

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Two-level underground air-defense command post of 1,900 square meters, completed in 1942 under the naval district office.

  • Former Sasebo Naval District Triumphal Memorial Hall (Sasebo Civic Cultural Hall)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-25-8192

    Donated and built in 1923; returned from US use in 1977 and a civic cultural hall since 1982.

  • Awashima Shrine

    Sasebo, Nagasaki

    Shrine on a Kujukushima island enshrining three deities who protect women, fertility, and women's illnesses.

  • Sasebo Port

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Long a naval and commercial port, now partly used by the US Navy and Maritime Self-Defense Force.

  • Mikawachi Sarayama

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Pottery district started with kilns in Mikawachi, Eie, and Kihara, where many kilns keep the tradition.

  • Fukui Cave

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-64-3830

    Site with cultural layers of the Paleolithic-to-Jomon transition and tools over 30,000 years old.

  • Senryugataki Falls Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Seventh of Hirado's eight views: about 20-meter men's and women's falls.

  • Megane-iwa

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Rock 10 meters high and 20 wide, naturally pierced like spectacles, one of Hirado's eight views.

  • Fujiyama Shrine

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-46-0762

    Kyushu's leading wisteria site, with prefectural natural monuments and an 800-year-old sacred tree.

  • Funakoshi Observatory

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Lowest Kujukushima Eight Views lookout, so islands feel close. Ink-painting beauty in rain. Step-free for wheelchairs.

  • Utsuwa History Museum

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-30-8080

    Museum of Sasebo pottery from Jomon to Mikawachi ware, with a world-oldest-class bean-pattern replica.

  • Sasebo Flower Garden

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-59-8711

    Year-round flower garden of about 12,000 tsubo with peonies, koi, and mandarin ducks.

  • Haiki Shrine

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-38-3920

    Shrine reached by over 100 steps, overlooking Haiki town and sunsets, and popular for first sunrise.

  • Senpuku-ji Cave

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Cave discovered by local junior-high students, source of pottery about 16,000 years old.

  • Mikawachi Ware Traditional Industry Hall (Mikawachi Ware Museum)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-30-8080

    Closed for major repairs from March 31, 2026 until planned autumn 2028; shows Edo-Meiji white porcelain.

  • Fukui Cave Museum

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-64-3830

    Museum of Fukui Cave, a Paleolithic-to-Jomon site, displaying Important Cultural Property finds and a life-size diorama.

  • Hana no Mori Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Vast park with about 40,000 seasonal flowers and trees, and a hidden cherry blossom site.

  • Nogata Coast (Tsushimase Lighthouse)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0959-57-3935

    Cliff-lined rias coast with grasslands on northern Uku and symbolic Tsushimase Lighthouse.

  • Sasebo Heavy Industries (SSK)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-25-9111

    Historic shipyard, locally called SSK, that built the 1962 tanker Nissho Maru; 1913 crane still works.

  • Sasebo Higashiyama Naval Cemetery (Higashi Park)

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-88-8397

    Cemetery for over 170,000 navy dead, with 62 joint and 437 individual monuments.

  • Red Buckwheat Village

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Local farmers grow red-flowering Takane Ruby buckwheat; flower viewing is usually late October to early November.

  • Dainen-ji Bell Tower Gate

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-38-2503

    City-designated Jodo temple founded in 1541; its Edo bell-tower gate survived a 1991 fire.

  • Uragashira Repatriation Memorial Peace Park and Museum

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-58-2561

    Peace park and museum where about 1.4 million repatriates landed between 1945 and 1950.

  • Fukuseki Kannon Seigan-ji

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-31-8372

    Temple enshrining an Eleven-faced Kannon said to be carved by Gyoki in 710, one of seven in Kyushu.

  • Udokoshi Observatory

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Historic Yumiharidake lookout known since the Taisho era. Views Kujukushima and Pearl Sea Resort. Spring azaleas.

  • Uku Island Jogatake Observatory

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0959-57-3935

    Summit about 259 m overlooks the Goto Islands. Wartime radar remains were designated Japan Heritage in 2017.

  • Iwashita Cave

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Cave at 200 m on Mount Ishimori where over 20 Jomon burials were excavated.

  • Pothole Park

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    500-meter riverside park opened in 1991 along the Saza River, named for its countless potholes.

  • Uku Island Yaku Shrine

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0959-57-3935

    Relocated in the Kamakura period with a spirit from Otokoyama Hachimangu to quell an epidemic.

  • Former Sasebo Naval Barracks Ruins

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

    Facilities in Sasebo Park of a corps that trained over a million youths for 56 years until the war's end.

  • Mount Atago

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 0956-22-6630

  • Iimori Shrine

    Sasebo, Nagasaki · 090-7028-2795

    Ainoura local shrine with a large catfish statue, a healing legend, and white catfish fortunes.

  • Miyajidake Shrine

  • Kuroshima Village

  • Former Residence of Kusumoto Tanzan

  • Marudeyama Observation Post Ruins

  • Uku Island Koshima Shrine

  • Kameyama Hachimangu

  • Sechibaru Coal Mine Museum (Former Matsuura Coal Mine Office)

  • Fuzan Shrine

  • Giant Cycad of Miura Shrine (Uku Island)

  • Former Sasebo Fortress Kokubi Battery Ruins
