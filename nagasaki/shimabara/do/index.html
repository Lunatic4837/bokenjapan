Things to do in Shimabara, Nagasaki

Shimabara · Nagasaki · 18 places

Do

  • Shimabara Onsen Yutorogi no Yu

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1126

    Bright barrier-free onsen with free-flowing baths, a medicinal bath, cold bath, and sauna.

  • Shirahama Beach and Campground

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    White-sand beach with clear water and a pine campground, chosen among Japan's 100 best beaches.

  • Maehama Beach

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    White-sand, green-pine beach for board sailing, with platforms and showers in season. The city's most popular beach.

  • Kuchinotsu Dolphin Watching

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-86-4433

    About 300 wild bottlenose dolphins can be watched swimming and jumping year-round in about one hour.

  • Yutorogi Foot Bath

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    Foot bath opened November 1, 2004, with 100% free-flowing Shimabara Onsen water, available 24 hours.

  • Minamishimabara Dolphin Watching

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-75-1515

    About 200 wild dolphins reside year-round in Hayasaki Strait and are not fed. Two adults minimum to sail.

  • Eco Park Ronshobaru

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-7056

    Campsite and experience farm in Unzen-Amakusa National Park, with cabins, animals, and activities.

  • Shimabara Onsen

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-62-3986

    Seaside castle-town onsen known for Ariake Sea sunrises, with bicarbonate and neutral springs, foot baths, and drinking springs.

  • Marine Park Arie

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Barrier-free artificial beach beside Arie Sports Park; swimming prohibited, with beach sports events.

  • Shirasu (Lithothamnium) Observation Tour

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-85-3155

    Rare plant shoal off Hara Castle visible at lowest tides; observation tours are not currently held.

  • Mount Takaiwa

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-3434

    881m peak south of Unzen Onsen, climbed in 40 minutes to one hour from Hohara Garden.

  • Mount Mai Fureai Road (8888 Steps)

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-68-5888

    About 3 km and 8,888 steps from Mount Mai's mid-slope to the 703-meter summit, roughly one hour.

  • Sengen Park Foot Bath

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    Free foot bath beside Shimabara Port, using 100% free-flowing Shimabara Onsen, open 24 hours except during cleaning.

  • Minamishimabara Guide Association Arima no Sato

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Local guides introduce Christian historic sites, relics, and town walks in Minamishimabara.

  • Kazusa Auto Campground

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Auto sites only, facing mountain and sea in Minamishimabara. Year-round fishing; a beach nearby in July and August.

  • Heisei Shinzan Nature Center

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-6752

    Center 2.5 km from Heisei Shinzan's summit to observe vegetation recovery, with plant walks and trekking.

  • Arie Tawaraishi Nature Sports Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6703

    Sports park with 16 athletic courses, grass skiing, roller skating, and hundreds of cherry trees.

  • Arie General Sports Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-82-1430

    Park with a multipurpose ground, gateball and tennis courts, a 620-meter jogging course, and a lawn plaza.
