Sights in Shimabara, Nagasaki

Shimabara · Nagasaki · 46 places

See

  • Hara Castle Ruins

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    1496 castle ruins, a natural fortress surrounded on three sides by the Ariake Sea.

  • Town Where Carp Swim

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    Shinmachi streams where residents released colored carp into abundant spring water, a highlight of watery Shimabara.

  • Debris Flow Damaged House Preservation Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-61-0771

    Park preserving nine houses damaged by the August 1992 debris flow to show disaster-prevention importance.

  • Shimeiso Spring Garden

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1121

    Late Meiji villa garden with four-direction views, a spring-fed carp pond, pines, and maples.

  • Shimabara Castle

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-62-4766

    Built from 1618 over seven years by Matsukura Shigemasa; also called Moritake Castle.

  • Heisei Shinzan

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-5540

    Unzen lava dome named in 1996 to commemorate the long disaster and new recovery.

  • Ayugaeri Falls

    Shimabara, Nagasaki

    Waterfall about 14 meters high where sweetfish once returned, with rock play and flowing somen.

  • Ariake Forest Flower Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-68-5252

    35,000-square-meter flower park at Mount Mai's foot, with seasonal blooms and views of Ariake Sea, Aso, and Unzen.

  • Former Onokoba Elementary School Damaged Building and Sabo Future Hall

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-72-2499

    School burned by the September 1991 pyroclastic flow, preserved beside a hall of disaster exhibits.

  • Mount Unzen Disaster Memorial Hall Gamadasu Dome

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-5555

    Museum of the Unzen eruption from November 1990 for about five years; renewed March 1, 2025.

  • Shimabara Hibariyama Flower Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-62-3986

    Flower park overlooking Heisei Shinzan, with spring flowers, 10 million cosmos, and 60,000 sunflowers.

  • Omisaki Station

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-62-2231

    Japan's closest station to the sea, an unmanned Shimabara Railway stop with the Ariake Sea directly behind it.

  • Samurai Residences

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    Preserved Shimo-no-cho samurai street, 406.8 meters long and 5.6 meters wide, west of Shimabara Castle.

  • Sezumezaki Lighthouse

    Shimabara, Nagasaki

    Southern-tip lighthouse on Shimabara Peninsula; Hayasaki Seto whirlpools reach 20 m across at March-June spring tides.

  • Hara Castle Holy Maria Kannon Hall

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-61-0977

    Ten-meter wooden statue, the world's largest Maria image, carved over 40 years to mourn Shimabara-Amakusa Rebellion victims.

  • Shimabara Mayuyama Road

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    Eight-kilometer road around Mayuyama near Heisei Shinzan, Japan's newest mountain, for volcanic sightseeing.

  • Arima Christian Heritage Museum

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-85-3217

    Introduces Hara Castle Ruins, Hinoe Castle Ruins, and Arima Seminary in Nagasaki Christian history.

  • Minamishimabara Sunflower Fields

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Sunflower fields in Minamishimabara, planted by locals after harvest, not run as a tourist farm.

  • Futagoiwa Rock

    Shimabara, Nagasaki

    Head-shaped rock carved by waves from ancient volcanic debris-flow deposits; one of two remains.

  • Harayama Dolmen Group

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Japan's oldest and largest dolmen group, from about the 4th century BC, with over 100 tombs.

  • Hinoe Castle Ruins

    Shimabara, Nagasaki

    Castle ruins of Christian lord Arima Harunobu, who founded Japan's first European secondary school.

  • Kuchinotsu History and Folklore Museum

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6773

    Museum richly displaying materials on Kuchinotsu's past and present as a historic natural harbor.

  • Nanban Ship Arrival Site

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Nagasaki-designated historic site of 1567 Portuguese ship arrivals and a 1579 Christian mission base.

  • Tatsuishi Coast

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-5540

    Coast showing Unzen's earliest layers from 500,000 years ago, above former seafloor deposits and debris-flow layers.

  • Fukae Suwa Shrine Grove

    Shimabara, Nagasaki

    Grove of Suwa Shrine enshrined in 1428, with seven large camphor trees, the largest 6.6 meters around.

  • Shimabara City Ariake General Cultural Hall (Green Wave)

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-68-5800

    Complex with a 700-seat hall and galleries; its museum shows Jomon remains, and the library holds over 70,000 books.

  • Hamanokawa Spring

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    Spring with four washing sections used in order by purpose; the traditional custom is still kept.

  • Blue Barbershop

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-64-6057

    Blue wooden barbershop built in 1923, Western in front and Japanese in back, now a rest spot.

  • Seibo Park (Seibo Memorial Hall)

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-85-2922

    Park honoring Peace Statue sculptor Kitamura Seibo, with about 60 works and views of Hara Castle Ruins.

  • Arie Tawaraishi Observatory

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Overlooks Arie from Route 57 between Unzen and Shimabara, with Shiranui Sea, Ariake Sea, and clear-day Mt. Aso views.

  • Reikyu Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    City-center park with about 250 Somei-Yoshino cherries and about 3,000 Hirado azaleas.

  • Hayauo River and Inohara Hardware Store

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-62-3117

    Heisei-era stream from Tsuki well spring water, with fish and wasabi. Bacteria-free, 15 degrees, 150 liters per minute.

  • Tonosumi Falls

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    Falls in upper Shimizu River's Tonosumi Gorge, loved for summer coolness and autumn foliage.

  • Hakariishi Spring Water Spot

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    A concave channel-bed stone splits spring water north and south. Shimabara City tangible folk cultural property.

  • Hanabusa Observatory

    Shimabara, Nagasaki

    Panoramic viewpoint over islands, mountains, and Hayasaki Peninsula, with visible ancient volcanic strata.

  • Shimabara Castle Moat Cherry Blossoms

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-62-4766

    250 Somei-Yoshino cherries bloom along 1.3 kilometers of the castle moat, with red night lanterns.

  • Sunset Hill

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6633

    Scenic spot in Eco Park Ronshobaru, Kita-Arima, with fine views of sunsets over Tachibana Bay.

  • Mayuyama Erosion Control Memorial Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    Memorial park at Mayuyama's foot with Seibo Kitamura's Kanzeon statue, cherry trees, and sports fields.

  • Fukae Buried Cultural Properties and Eruption Disaster Museum

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-72-2512

    Displays excavated pottery and other artifacts, plus photos of the Unzen Fugendake eruption disaster.

  • Iwatoyama Grove

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-6333

    97-meter mountain; rare plants designated a national natural monument in 1928. Also a Kannon faith site.

  • Senbongi Observatory

    Shimabara, Nagasaki

    Overlook where pyroclastic flows and heat blasts devastated the district on June 23-24, 1993.

  • Gonda Park

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6677

    Hilltop park at 100-140m with views, spring cherries and azaleas, autumn cosmos, and sunsets.

  • Spectacles Bridge (Minamishimabara City)

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-72-3111

    Stone arch bridge built in 1853, called the oldest public stone bridge on the Shimabara Peninsula.

  • Amagase Kofun

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-73-6705

    Late Kofun-period corridor-style stone chamber tomb with front and rear rooms, stones now exposed.

  • Shinwa Spring

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-63-1111

    City-center spring across from Yutorogi footbath; people drink the water or take it home.

  • Hayasaki Basalt

    Shimabara, Nagasaki · 0957-65-5540

    One of Shimabara Peninsula's oldest rocks, erupted about 4.3 million years ago, with Hawaii-like black lava.
