Do in Tsushimashi

Tsushimashi · Nagasaki · 20 places

Do

  • Miutahama Beach

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-86-3111

    Extremely fine natural white shell-sand beach, one of Japan's 100 Beaches, near an onsen and campground.

  • Tsushima Hometown Tradition Hall

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-54-8311

    Facility introducing Tsushima tradition, culture, history, specialties, and local food, with soba, pearl, inkstone, and pottery experiences.

  • Kamitsushima Onsen Nagisa no Yu

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-86-4568

    Relax while viewing the deep-blue sea horizon; next to Miuda Beach and enjoyable for the whole family.

  • Komoda Beach

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-52-1566

    White-sand beach near the Sasu River where 30,000 Yuan troops arrived in 1274.

  • Komodahama Shrine Grand Festival

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki

    Honors soldiers killed in the 1274 Mongol attack. Festival on November's second Sunday includes a warrior procession.

  • Isaribi Park

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki

    Hilltop panorama of sky and sea. Tsushima fishing fires sparkle at night. Free foot bath from April to November.

  • Mogihama Beach

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-86-3111

    Tsushima's largest natural beach, over 400 m of shallow sand, and the island's last shore visited by sea turtles.

  • Agata no Sato Soba Dojo

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-84-2340

    Hands-on binder-free Taishu soba from stone-milled Tsushima flour, plus chicken-broth iriyaki soba.

  • Mitsushima Town Beach

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-54-2271

    Twin beaches joined by a 500-meter walkway rare in the prefecture, with first-ranked AA water quality.

  • Shinwa no Sato Nature Park

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-58-1111

    Nature park midway up Mt. Eboshi, with camping, a Japanese garden, and guided sea kayaking.

  • Meboro Dam Equestrian Park

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-85-1113

    Park in northern Tsushima where native Taishu horses can be viewed, fed, and ridden.

  • Aoshio no Sato (Oura Campground and Beach)

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki

    Nature-rich Oura campsite and beach with six-person bungalows, rental tents, and summer swimming.

  • Sago Birdwatching Park

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-84-2311

    Famous stopover where white-naped cranes rest in February-March and rare birds attract birdwatchers.

  • Nishidomari Beach

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-86-3111

    Artificial beach opened in 1995, with a rest house, beside Kamitsushima-so and a hill overlooking Korea.

  • Deai Juku Takumi

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-56-0118

    Make soba from 100 percent Tsushima buckwheat with local instructors, then eat it fresh.

  • Minatohama Beach

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-84-2311

    Natural sandy beach downstream on the Sago River, rare on Tsushima, shallow with calm waves.

  • Nagasaki Prefectural Tsushima Youth House

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-83-0559

    Training center in forested central Tsushima for activities building discipline, friendship, and cooperation.

  • Firefly Viewing

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-52-1566

    Genji fireflies fly along the Segawa from early June. Tsushima-only autumn fireflies are a prefectural natural monument.

  • Matsushima Park Beach

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-86-3111

    A swimming beach with a rest house, used as a place to relax.

  • Birdwatching

    Tsushimashi, Nagasaki · 0920-52-1566

    Tsushima, 49.5 km from Korea, records over 300 wild bird species, more than half of Japan's known birds.
