Things to do in Unzen, Nagasaki

Unzen · Nagasaki · 4 places

Do

  • Unzen Onsen

    Unzen, Nagasaki · 0957-73-3434

    Three-zone hot-spring town with hotels and public baths. Nita Pass shows azaleas, greenery, autumn leaves, and rime.

  • Shimabara Railway (Aino Station-Azuma Station)

    Unzen, Nagasaki · 0957-62-4705

    Joining Aino and Azuma means beloved wife. Popular with lovers. Romanticist honorary headquarters since 2008.

  • Mizuho no Mori Park

    Unzen, Nagasaki · 0957-77-2111

    Park with spring water, a lawn, a woodworking craft house, and a campground popular with families.

  • Unzen Golf Course

    Unzen, Nagasaki · 0957-73-3368

    Planned to develop the prefectural park. Play amid Unzen National Park nature. Fees and hours vary by season.
