Sights in Unzen, Nagasaki

Unzen · Nagasaki · 4 places

See

  • Aino Observatory

    Unzen, Nagasaki · 0957-38-3111

    Route 57 overlook of calm Tachibana Bay and Mount Unzen; Emperor Showa visited three times.

  • Azuma Pasture Village

    Unzen, Nagasaki · 0957-47-7783

    Pasture rest area about 400 meters up Mount Azuma, with grazing cows and a 480-meter view path.

  • Genseinuma Marsh Plant Community

    Unzen, Nagasaki · 0957-73-3636

    Marsh plants in a 1.2-hectare wetland minutes from Unzen Onsen. A rare Kyushu wild site of northern iris.

  • Kirishitan Gravestones

    Unzen, Nagasaki

    Seven gravestones in Obama; six are prefectural cultural properties. Flower crosses; presumed local lords' graves around 1562.
