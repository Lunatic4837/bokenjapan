Things to do in Aga, Niigata

Aga · Niigata · 7 places

Do

  • かのせ温泉 赤湯

    Kanose Akayu Hot Spring

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4186

    Town-run day-trip hot spring in Kanose, Aga, with iron-rich reddish-brown water from a 59°C source and a forest-surrounded semi-outdoor bath.

  • 津川温泉 清川高原保養センター

    Kiyokawa Highland Recreation Center (Tsugawa Onsen)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-5530

    Day-use alkaline simple hot spring heated with local wood pellets, opposite a complex with a coworking space and book cafe.

  • 御神楽温泉 みかぐら荘

    Mikagura-so (Mikagura Onsen)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-2121

    A day-use hot spring at the foot of Mount Mikagura, with a large bath of big windows and an open feel.

  • 七福温泉 七福荘

    Shichifuku-so Hot Spring

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-3550

    Cedar hot-spring inn in Nana, Aga, enshrining the Seven Lucky Gods, with soba-making and a cold sulfur spring.

  • 麒麟山酒造（株）

    Kirinzan Sake Brewery (Kirinzan Traditional Dry)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-3511

    A sake brewery in Aga Town making dry sake since 1843 with rice it grows and clear water.

  • 三川温泉スキー場

    Mikawa Onsen Ski Resort

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-99-3738

    Ski resort in Aga with 10 courses from beginner to advanced, snowboarding throughout, and a 38-degree mogul run.

  • 御神楽温泉

    Mikagura Onsen

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-2211

    Hot-spring area at the foot of Mt. Mikagura with two facilities, including day-trip Mikagura-so, and a weak sodium sulfate spring.
