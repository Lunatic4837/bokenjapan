Sights in Aga, Niigata

Aga · Niigata · 41 places

See

  • 九島観光栗園

    Kushima Orchard (Chestnut Picking)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-3056

    A flat chestnut orchard of about four hectares at the entrance to Aga’s Kamikawa area, where visitors pick reputedly sweet chestnuts and can make chestnut rice or sweets.

  • 角神湖畔青少年旅行村

    Tsunogami Lakeside Youth Travel Village

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4186

    A youth travel village in a wide, quiet forest by Tsunogami Lake, with cottages, bungalows, tent sites, a hot spring, hiking, and birdwatching.

  • つがわ狐の嫁入り行列

    Tsugawa Fox Wedding Procession

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    An Edo-period wedding reenactment in Tsugawa, Aga, in which a fox-makeup bride in white walks from evening into night by torch and lantern light.

  • 三川観光きのこ園

    Mikawa Tourist Mushroom Garden

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-99-3773

    A mushroom garden in Aga where visitors pick cultivated mushrooms and can barbecue or dine.

  • 阿賀野川ライン遊覧船

    Agano River Line Sightseeing Boat

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-99-2121

    A roughly 40-minute sightseeing cruise on the Agano River views the varied Oku-Aga gorge, one of Japan's 100 scenic views, with a boatman guide.

  • 将軍杉

    Shogun Cedar

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-99-2639

    A giant cedar at Byodo-ji Temple, 40 m tall with a 19.31 m trunk, said to mark the burial of Taira no Koreshige.

  • 三川観光やな場

    Mikawa Tourist Fish Weir

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-99-3677

    A summer fish weir on the Shintani River, a tributary of the Agano River, where visitors can eat sweetfish caught there.

  • 芦沢高原ハーバルパーク

    Ashizawa Highland Herbal Park

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-5522

    A three-hectare garden amid rich nature, planted mainly with lavender and other herbs, where seasonal flowers are fragrant and a workshop offers herb-soap making.

  • 中ノ沢渓谷森林公園

    Nakanosawa Valley Forest Park

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-99-3710

    Traditional riverside forest campsite with 60 tent sites, trails among 100 tree species, and activities such as fishing, hiking, and firefly viewing.

  • 麒麟山公園

    Kirinzan Park

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    An 11-hectare park in Aga with about 100 cherry trees, Kirinzan Castle ruins, and a riverbed swimming area by the Tokonami River.

  • たきがしら湿原

    Takigashira Wetland

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-5764

    An artificial wetland in Aga where visitors can see seasonal wetland plants, birds, dozens of dragonfly species, early-summer fireflies, and autumn leaves on the Kamoshika Trail.

  • 狐の嫁入り屋敷

    Fox Wedding House

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-0220

    A free hands-on facility in Aga themed on the Tsugawa fox-wedding procession, with mask painting, crafts, and Agano River views.

  • 阿賀野川ラインの紅葉

    Agano River Line Autumn Foliage

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    Autumn foliage along the Agano River, seen from the riverside national road and also from boat rides or the steam locomotive.

  • 奥阿賀ふるさと館（休館中）

    Oku-Aga Hometown Hall

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4508

    A facility of three boxes in Aga offering electricity experiments, 3D washi TAMAGO making, and washi lighting displays.

  • 麒麟山・鹿瀬登山口

    Mount Kirin (Kanose Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    Beginner trail to 195 m Mount Kirin passes earthworks and stone walls of Tsugawa Castle and overlooks the Agano River.

  • 麒麟山公園

    Kirinzan Park

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A quiet park overlooking the Jōnai River and Mount Kirin, with spring cherry blossoms, summer camping, and autumn foliage.

  • 飯豊山・実川登山口

    Mount Iide (Jitsukawa Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    Advanced trail to 2,105 m Mount Iide, known for alpine flower fields and main-ridge views, taking about 10 hours 30 minutes to the summit.

  • 角神雪椿園

    Tsunogami Snow Camellia Garden

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A garden near Kanose Dam on the Agano River’s left bank protects and grows native snow camellias, peaking in mid-April.

  • 極楽寺野中ザクラ

    Gokuraku-ji Nonaka Cherry Tree

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-2211

    A deep-crimson, large-flowered cherry at Gokuraku-ji, a wild beni-yamazakura variant designated a national natural monument.

  • 阿賀町観光ガイドと町歩き

    Aga Town Guided Town Walk

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    Guides lead about 90-minute walks through former river-port Tsugawa on historic river-port, food-and-alleys, and Kirinzan courses.

  • 薬師清水（やくししみず）

    Yakushi Spring

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A spring at the center of Agano River Line Prefectural Natural Park, flowing about 11 tons a minute at 12°C near Yakushi Hall and Shogun Cedar.

  • 訪春園

    Hoshun-en (Snow Camellia Garden)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-3322

    Snow-camellia garden at Kirinzan Onsen in Aga, where about 2,000 cultivated camellias and cherry trees bloom.

  • 御神楽岳・室谷登山口

    Mt. Mikagura (Muroya Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-2211

    A 1,386 m mountain where kagura offered to the gods can be heard, with an intermediate four-hour hike and no hut.

  • 夫婦桜

    Meotozakura (Couple Cherry Trees)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-99-2311

    Two cherry trees that can look like one from some angles, a photo spot against mountains with lingering snow.

  • 琴平清水（こんぴらしみず）

    Konpira Spring

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A spring in Tsugawa, Aga, with abundant water, a tank bottom curved by long use, and cleaning by local volunteers.

  • 大尾不動滝

    Obi Fudo Falls

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A waterfall of about 15 meters on the forest road to Takigashira Marsh, set among seasonal natural scenery.

  • あが雛めぐり

    Aga Hina Doll Tour

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-0220

    Hina-doll tour in Aga Town, with seven doll sets at Roadside Station Aga no Sato and other sites, including the Fox’s Wedding House.

  • 裏五頭・中ノ沢登山口

    Ura-Gozu Nakanosawa Trailhead

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    Nakanosawa trailhead for Ura-Gozu, the Aga Town side of Mount Gozu; an intermediate climb of about four hours to the 912 m summit, with no mountain hut.

  • 丸渕観光わらび園

    Marubuchi Bracken Garden (Warabi Picking)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-2766

    Visitors harvest fresh bracken in season at this 10-hectare garden in Aga, for salting, tempura, or miso soup.

  • 宝珠山・石間登山口

    Hojusan (Ishima Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    Beginner trail from Ishima to the 559 m summit, about 2 hours 30 minutes, season late May to November, with no mountain hut.

  • 菱ケ岳・石戸登山口

    Hishigatake (Ishido Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    Intermediate trail from Ishido to the 974 m summit, about 3 hours 40 minutes, season late May to November, with no mountain hut.

  • 桂清水（かつらしみず）

    Katsura Spring

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A bedrock spring beside the Agano River among centuries-old zelkova and beech, yielding 100 tons of 11°C water daily; the access road is currently collapsed.

  • 日本平山・谷沢登山口

    Mount Nihondaira (Yazawa Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A 1,081-meter mountain with a gentle summit ridge marking the boundary between two districts; about 4 hours 30 minutes from Yazawa, with no hut.

  • 小瀬ヶ沢洞窟

    Kosegasawa Cave

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-3333

    A cave formed by a rhyolite fissure, dating to the Incipient Jomon about 9,000 years ago, where pottery and over a thousand stone tools were found.

  • 日倉山・五十島登山口

    Mt. Higura (Igashima Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    An 843 m peak for intermediate hikers, about four hours from the Igashima trailhead to the summit, with no mountain hut.

  • 室谷洞窟

    Muroya Cave

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-3333

    A cave formed by lateral erosion beside the Tokonami River, a tributary of the Agano, yielding Jomon tools, pottery, and a flexed female burial.

  • 縄文清水

    Jomon Spring

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A spring along National Route 459 beside the Agano River, used by locals for rest and drinking, with stable year-round water.

  • 馬ノ髪山・綱木登山口

    Mount Umanokami (Tsunagi Trailhead)

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-4766

    A 757-meter peak at the end of a long ridge, with many natural cedars along the trail; about 2 hours 30 minutes to the summit and no hut.

  • 西山日光寺「木造金剛力士立像」

    Wooden Standing Kongorikishi Statues at Nishiyama Nikko-ji

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-92-3333

    A pair of Agyo and Ungyo Nio statues in the gate of Nishiyama Nikko-ji, with restrained movement and gentle robe carving from the Kamakura period.

  • 極楽寺の野中ザクラ

    Nonaka Cherry at Gokurakuji Temple

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-2211

    A wild Beniyamazakura cherry variant in the grounds of Gokurakuji Temple, a national natural monument flowering deep crimson since the early Showa era.

  • どぶろく金よし

    Kaneyoshi Doburoku

    Aga, Niigata · 0254-95-3293

    Doburoku brewed with water and rice from Kamikawa, with a mild sweetness, slight acidity, and a fragrant rustic flavor.
