Things to do in Agano, Niigata

Agano · Niigata · 14 places

Do

  • 華報寺の湯

    Kahoji no Yu Hot Spring Bath

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-3615

    Hot spring bath in Deyu Onsen, Agano, with lukewarm water linked to the Kobo Daishi legend.

  • 出湯温泉（五頭温泉郷）

    Deyu Onsen (Gozu Hot Springs)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Hot-spring area in Agano opened 1,200 years ago, legendarily made to flow when Kobo Daishi struck the ground with his staff.

  • 共同浴場薬師の湯

    Yakushi-no-Yu Communal Bath

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-66-2626

    Source spring of Murasugi Onsen, known as a child-blessing bath, with jet, bubble, and sole-massage baths behind the communal bath.

  • 村杉温泉（五頭温泉郷）

    Murasugi Onsen (Gozu Hot Springs)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Radium-rich hot spring in Agano, traditionally found after an oracle from Yakushi Nyorai and long known as Kodakara no Yu.

  • 安田温泉 保養センターやすらぎ

    Yasuda Onsen Yasuragi

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-1555

    Day-trip hot spring in Agano at the foot of the Gozu mountains, with a sodium chloride spring said to be about 27 times standard concentration.

  • どんぐりの森 キャンプ場

    Acorn Forest Campground

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Campground surrounded by acorn trees at the Mount Gozu Sannomine course trailhead, with 20 tent sites but no drinking water.

  • 赤松山森林公園キャンプ場

    Akamatsuyama Forest Park Campground

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2690

    A forest-park campground beside Hoju Onsen Akamatsu-so, set in preserved Yasuda greenery, with hiking, camping, outdoor cooking and a hilltop gazebo that looks toward Sado in clear weather.

  • 越つかの酒造（株）

    Koshitsukano Sake Brewery (Yoyoizumi)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2011

    Sake brewery in Agano still using the original Tsukano Shuzo building, with reserved tours and tastings.

  • 奥村杉キャンプ場

    Oku-Murasugi Campground

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    A campground on the Yasuno River’s left bank, inland from Murasugi Onsen town, with a cooking area and toilets, drive-in access and spots to play in the river.

  • 瓢湖畔風鈴まつり

    Hyoko Lakeside Wind Chime Festival

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-3786

    Summer wind-chime festival at Suibara Hachimangu Shrine beside Lake Hyoko, with ceramic chimes made from Agano clay and a photo bench.

  • 越後桜酒造（株）

    Echigo Zakura Sake Brewery (Daiginjo Echigo Zakura)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2033

    Sake brewery offering fresh-pressed sake tastings, brewery-exclusive shopping, and 360-degree VR tours of areas not usually open to visitors.

  • 白龍酒造（株）

    Hakuryu Sake Brewery

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2222

    Sake brewery in Agano City that has made sake for 160 years near Lake Hyoko, known for swans.

  • 劇場「かわら座」（安田温泉やすらぎ）　※営業日等は公式サイトhttp://yasudaonsen.com/でご確認ください。

    Kawaraza Theater (Yasuda Onsen Yasuragi)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-1555

    Theater at the Yasuda Onsen Yasuragi facility in Agano.

  • 安田瓦を割る体験

    Yasuda Roof Tile Breaking Experience

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-3802

    Experience throwing or karate-splitting sturdy Yasuda roof tiles, with gloves and helmets provided.
