Sights in Agano, Niigata

Agano · Niigata · 50 places

See

  • Hyoko Waterfowl Park

    瓢湖水きん公園

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2690

    A Ramsar wetland park in Agano where swans winter from early October to late March, with about 5,000 birds at the late-November peak.

  • Asaiino Shrine

    旦飯野神社

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-4755

    A 1,700-year-old shrine in Sasakami, Agano, dedicated to Hondawake-no-Mikoto, with a round sacred stone visitors touch for happiness.

  • Suntopia World

    サントピアワールド

    Romaji: Santopia World

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-3450

    Amusement park in Agano with about 30 attractions, including a dinosaur roller coaster and a large Ferris wheel, plus an indoor play area.

  • Deyu Communal Bath

    出湯共同浴場

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-3863

    A communal bath in Deyu, locally called Shin-yu, offering a retro atmosphere and 100 percent free-flowing spring water.

  • Hojusan and Akamatsuyama Trailhead

    宝珠山・赤松山登山口

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Beginner trailhead for 559 m Hojusan in Agano; the summit takes about two hours, and Hoju Onsen Akamatsuso is at the foot.

  • Urara no Mori Information Center (Mount Gozu Foothills)

    五頭山麓うららの森 情報発信館

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-61-3511

    A food and greenery zone with produce sales, tourism and hot-spring information, bamboo, pottery and tofu-making experiences, coffee, souvenirs, and a lawn.

  • Kojunji Temple (Former Saito Residence)

    孝順寺(旧斉藤邸)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-2434

    A Shinshu Otani-sect temple in the former Saito mansion, linked to Shinran's three-crop chestnut wonder and set in a pond-stroll garden.

  • Yasuda Roof Tile Road

    やすだ瓦ロード（安田瓦協同組合）

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-2112

    Street among Yasuda tile factories in Agano, lined with some 10,000 tile decorations, onigawara art, and brick chimneys.

  • Fromage Cellar (Yasuda Yogurt)

    ヤスダヨーグルト　チーズ専門店「フロマージュセラー」

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-5028

    Cheese shop by Yasuda Yogurt inside Y&Y Garden in Agano, offering cheese chips, cream-cheese omelettes, natural cheeses and cheesecakes.

  • Hyoko Yashiki-no-Mori Brewery (Swan Lake Beer)

    瓢湖屋敷の杜ブルワリー

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-63-2000

    Beer brewery opened in 1997 in the former Igarashi residence near Lake Hyoko, where visitors can see the garden and brewing.

  • Mount Gozu Foothills Recreation Forest

    五頭山麓いこいの森

    Agano, Niigata · 080-6519-0273

    Campground in more than 50 hectares of red pine and mixed forest along the Oarakawa stream, with a terraced river easy for small children.

  • Hachibei Sunflower Field

    八米のひまわり畑

    Agano, Niigata · 025-246-0800

    Sunflower field managed by the Hachibei apiary, with about two hectares of sunflowers and rapeseed planted as bee forage.

  • Yasuda Yogurt

    ヤスダヨーグルト

    Romaji: Yasuda Yogurt

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-5028

    Drinkable yogurt maker in Agano offering advance-booked factory tours through glass windows, with a direct shop and cafe opposite.

  • Yasuda Castle Ruins

    安田城跡

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-3006

    Castle ruins, now a traffic park, retain part of the Hori-period main and second baileys and a castle town facing the southwest highway.

  • Yoshida Togo Memorial Museum

    吉田東伍記念博物館

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-1200

    Museum in the birthplace of Yoshida Togo, pioneer of Japanese historical geography, who compiled the Dai Nihon Chimei Jisho in 13 years.

  • Yasuda Kowa Noji Strawberry Orchard

    安田興和農事イチゴ園

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-4660

    Strawberry orchard.

  • Hyoko Iris Garden

    瓢湖あやめ園

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2690

    Iris garden in Agano that reaches peak bloom around mid-June, with an iris festival and lanterns in the grounds.

  • Uodome Falls

    魚止めの滝

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    An 8-metre waterfall in Gozu Renpo Prefectural Natural Park, with trailheads to Mount Wasabi and Mount Matsudaira and a viewpoint near the plunge pool.

  • Juzukake Cherry Tree at Baigo-ji Temple

    梅護寺の珠数掛ザクラ

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-67-2915

    A cherry tree at Baigo-ji Temple, one of the Seven Wonders of Echigo, whose flowers are said to hang in chains like prayer beads.

  • Suibara Magistrate's Office

    水原代官所

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Magistrate's office restored in August 1995 from the former floor plan, including a tax and civil office and a court for questioning criminals.

  • Hyoko Waterfowl Park

    瓢湖水きん公園

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    A waterfowl park designated a national natural monument, where visitors can feed swans and ducks that come close in winter.

  • Iwase Clear Spring

    岩瀬の清水（いわせのしみず）

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Spring of constant temperature beside Sasaoka Elementary School, with a nearby pavilion, used by children since 1903 and designated a city cultural property.

  • 5yukuri

    5yukuri (Private Sauna)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-4423

    Private Finnish barrel sauna in a villa, where guests pour aroma or plain water on the stones, with a pool lit by LEDs after sunset and optional BBQ.

  • Mirupasu Soft-Serve and Gelato Shop

    ソフトクリーム＆ジェラートショップ みるぱす

    Agano, Niigata · 090-4917-0429

    Gelato shop in Agano with six daily flavors made from Kanda Dairy raw milk and local vegetables and fruit.

  • Shin-e Cherry Blossom Avenue

    新江の桜並木

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    A 4.6-kilometre avenue of 550 Somei-Yoshino cherry trees along the Shin-e irrigation canal, planted by town residents in 1980.

  • Yatsufusa Plum Tree at Baigo-ji Temple

    梅護寺の八房の梅

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-67-2915

    A plum tree at Baigo-ji Temple, one of the Seven Wonders of Echigo, said to bear pale-pink double blossoms and fruit in groups of eight.

  • Kahoji Temple

    華報寺

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    A temple housing a statue Gyoki carved from sacred Mount Gozu wood, a place of Uba-son worship with monuments to Sasaki Shodo and Soma Gyofu.

  • Sasakami Gozu Golf Club

    笹神五頭ゴルフ倶楽部

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-6231

    A 27-hole golf course on gentle hills at the foot of Mount Gozu, with views of the Agano River, Echigo Plain, and Sado.

  • Yamaguchi Kanzan Memorial Monument

    山口坎山頌徳碑

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    A monument honoring Yamaguchi Kanzan, a late-Edo wasan mathematician from former Suibara who traveled across Japan several times.

  • Yasuda Ware

    安田焼

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-2898

    Pottery ranging from everyday utensils to craft works; hands-on experiences require advance contact.

  • Yamabiko Road

    やまびこ通り

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    A forest road of about 5 km lined with more than 250 poetry monuments, with views of the Kanbara Plain.

  • Marusan Yasuda Tile Factory

    丸三安田瓦工業株式会社

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-3802

    Tile factory in Agano making durable Yasuda roof tiles since the Tenpo era, with tours, clay play, and painting experiences.

  • Kuboyama Suikin Park

    久保山水きん公園

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Formerly an irrigation pond for rice paddies, it is now a citizens' park with spring cherry blossoms reflected on the water.

  • East Hill Golf Club

    イーストヒルゴルフクラブ

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-5100

    An 18-hole golf course in Agano covering 117 hectares, with a practice range among the greens.

  • Swan Lake OFFICE

    Swan Lake OFFICE

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-63-1010

    A telework and study space inside Rhythm House Hyoko, with private rooms, open space, Wi-Fi, and baths.

  • Tenchozan Park

    天朝山公園

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    A park remembered as the birthplace of Niigata’s prefectural government after the Boshin War, also known for cherry blossoms and playground equipment.

  • Gozu Foothills Urara no Mori Experience Learning Center

    五頭山麓うららの森体験学習館

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-61-3511

    Experience learning center at Murasugi Onsen entrance in Agano, with advance-booked workshops in bamboo crafts, soba, pottery, miso, gyoza, and tofu.

  • Yasuda Tile-Clay Motifs (1–10 Pieces)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-68-3802

    Make one to ten motifs from Yasuda roof-tile clay by mold or hand-building, then have tea while watching a Yasuda tile DVD.

  • Lake Hyoko Specialty Products Sales Center

    瓢湖特産品販売センター

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2690

    A shop and rest area selling local vegetables, pickles, miso, sweets, and other regional specialties.

  • Suibara Hachiman Shrine

    水原八幡宮

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-3786

    Shrine near Lake Hyoko in Agano enshrining Emperor Ojin, with swan-themed fortune slips and arithmetic amulets.

  • Agano City Suibara Hometown Agricultural History Museum

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-63-1722

    Museum beside Suibara Magistrate's Office that introduces Agano in culture, tourism, and industry zones, with weekend vegetable sales and soba-making.

    In Agano, shape a tea bowl or cup on an electric wheel; it is glazed, fired, and delivered one to two months later.

  • Echigo-fu Site (Tenchozan)

    越後府跡(天朝山)

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2510

    Site on Tenchozan where Niigata-fu was established in 1868, renamed Echigo-fu, before the prefectural office moved to Niigata City.

  • Yaezakura at Hyoko Suikin Park

    Agano, Niigata · 0250-62-2690

    Double cherry blossoms at Hyoko Suikin Park in Agano bloom in late April, after Somei-yoshino and Yui-zakura have leafed out.

  • Agano City Gozu Medicinal Plant Garden

    阿賀野市五頭薬用植物園

    Garden at the foot of Mount Gozu for learning about roughly 300 wild medicinal plants and links between people, herbs, and health.

  • Furusato Park (Park with a Library)

  • Mount Gozu Beech Forest Hike
