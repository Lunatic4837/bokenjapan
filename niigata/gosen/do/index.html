Things to do in Gosen, Niigata

Gosen · Niigata · 6 places

Do

  • Sakihana Onsen

    咲花温泉

    Gosen, Niigata · 0250-43-3911

    Hot-spring area beside the Agano River Line, with 54°C sulfur sodium chloride water and cherry trees planted about 50 years ago.

  • Muramatsu Sakurando Hot Spring

    村松さくらんど温泉

    Gosen, Niigata · 0250-58-1611

    Day-trip hot spring with garden-view baths and an open-air bath; Sakura-no-Yu and Ogon-no-Yu switch genders monthly, plus rooms, a restaurant, and a produce shop.

  • Gosen City Forest Park Campground

    五泉市森林公園キャンプ場

    Gosen, Niigata · 0250-42-5194

    A Gosen forest-park campsite among cedar plantings and native plants, with five grass tent sites, a 3.4 km trail and five-million-year-old strata.

  • 42nd Sakihana Onsen Underwater Fireworks Festival

    第42回 咲花温泉水中花火大会

    Gosen, Niigata · 0250-43-3911

    About 200 underwater fireworks bloom in the Agano River at Sakihana Onsen; the show is postponed if the river rises or weather turns bad.
