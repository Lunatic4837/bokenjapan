Things to do in Itoigawa, Niigata

Itoigawa · Niigata · 12 places

Do

  • Renge Onsen Area

    蓮華温泉周辺

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 090-2524-7237

    Mountain area around Renge Onsen (1,475 m) in Itoigawa, where Shiroike and the 5 km Renge Forest Nature Trail show autumn colors.

  • Auto campsite opened in Itoigawa

    須沢臨海公園オートキャンプ場

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-562-1360

    Auto campsite opened in 2016 where Sea of Japan waves can be heard, with golf practice, putter golf, and a 40-metre roller slide.

  • Arasaki Campground

    荒崎キャンプ場

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-566-3456

    A lawn tent campground near Marine Dream Nou in Itoigawa, with 32 sites and views of the Sea of Japan.

  • Mount Korenge (Renge Onsen Trailhead)

    小蓮華山・蓮華温泉登山口

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-1511

    Renge Onsen is the trailhead for Mount Korenge (2,766 m), Niigata’s highest peak, which shares a ridge with Mount Shirouma in Chubu Sangaku National Park.

  • Shirouma-dake Renge Onsen Lodge

    白馬岳蓮華温泉ロッジ

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 090-2524-7237

    Natural hot-spring lodge at 1,475 m in Chubu Sangaku National Park, with indoor baths and four open-air baths facing the Northern Alps.

  • Kaganoi Sake Brewery

    加賀の井酒造

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-0047

    Sake brewery in Itoigawa founded in 1650, Niigata’s oldest, still brewing with its historic well water; tours available.

  • Charmant Hiuchi Ski Resort

    シャルマン火打スキー場

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-568-2244

    Ski resort.

  • Fudotaki Campground

    不動滝キャンプ場

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-3100

    Forest campground beside Fudotaki, a 70-metre three-stage waterfall in Itoigawa, with 20 grass tent sites, hiking, and stargazing.

  • Renge Onsen Campground

    蓮華温泉キャンプ場

    Itoigawa, Niigata

    Campground in Chubu Sangaku National Park where visitors can hike a two-hour nature trail and relax in Renge Onsen baths.

  • Mount Amakazari (Amakazari Onsen Trailhead)

    雨飾山・雨飾温泉登山口

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-1511

    Trailhead at Amakazari Onsen for the 1,963 m twin-peaked Mount Amakazari in Joshin'etsu Kogen National Park, one of Japan's 100 Famous Mountains.

  • Itoigawa Seaside Valley Ski Area

    糸魚川シーサイドバレースキー場

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-558-2244

    Ski area in Itoigawa with courses mainly for beginners and intermediates, sea-facing runs, and a 360-degree summit view of mountains and the winter Sea of Japan.

  • Maple Syrup Tapping and Making Experience

    メイプルシロップ採取&シロップ作り体験

    Itoigawa, Niigata

    Snowshoe walk in Itoigawa to tap Itaya maple trees, then boil the sap into maple syrup.
