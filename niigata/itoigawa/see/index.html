Sights in Itoigawa, Niigata

Itoigawa · Niigata · 52 places

See

  • ヒスイ海岸

    Jade Coast

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-553-1785

    A coast in Itoigawa covered with colorful stones rather than sand, where visitors can search for natural jade carried down the Hime River.

  • 高浪の池

    Takanami Pond

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-556-2327

    A 13-metre-deep highland pond at 540 metres in the Hakuba foothills recreation area, with giant-fish sightings, camping, and water activities.

  • フォッサマグナミュージアム

    Fossa Magna Museum

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-553-1880

    Museum in Itoigawa with six exhibition areas on jade, the Fossa Magna, geological history, natural resources, and disasters such as earthquakes and landslides.

  • 親不知子不知

    Oyashirazu-Koshirazu Coast

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-553-1785

    A coastline famed as a perilous stretch, spanning about 15 km between Omi Station and Ichiburi Station.

  • 玉翠園・谷村美術館

    Gyokusui-en and Tanimura Art Museum

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-9277

    Museum of Masahiro Sawada’s wood carvings in a Togo Murano building, beside Gyokusui-en, a 1981 viewing garden with an 8-meter stone Kannon.

  • 弁天岩

    Benten Rock

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    Benten Rock, an Itoigawa Geopark geosite, is a coastal reef formed by a Fossa Magna submarine eruption and marked by a lighthouse.

  • 小滝川ヒスイ峡

    Kotakigawa Jade Gorge

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    Kotakigawa Jade Gorge is a national natural monument on the Kotaki River, where jadeite is naturally polished by the stream below Mount Myojo.

  • 糸魚川荒波あんこうVS本ズワイガニ冬の二大激ウマグルメフェア

    Itoigawa Aranami Anglerfish vs Snow Crab Winter Gourmet Fair

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    Winter food fair at participating Itoigawa restaurants featuring local rough-sea anglerfish and snow crab.

  • 大糸線増便バス

    Oito Line Extra Bus

    Itoigawa, Niigata

    Extra buses between Itoigawa Station and Hakuba Station accept railway tickets, fill Oito Line gaps, and connect with Hokuriku Shinkansen trains at Itoigawa.

  • 糸魚川ジオステーション ジオパル

    Itoigawa Geo Station Geopal

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    A promotion base for the Itoigawa Global Geopark under Itoigawa Station, with real trains and large HO and N gauge dioramas you can operate.

  • 天津神社・奴奈川神社

    Amatsu Shrine and Nunagawa Shrine

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-0036

    Amatsu Shrine shares its grounds with Nunagawa Shrine and dedicates bugaku, a national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property, on April 10–11.

  • 翡翠園

    Hisui-en Garden

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-9277

    A stroll garden in Itoigawa centered on a 70-ton cobalt-colored jade boulder, with three waterfalls and ponds, created by Nakane Kinsaku in 1978.

  • ヒスイ探し

    Jade Hunting

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    Beach activity in Itoigawa where visitors search pebble shores for jade and other rare stones; exploration kits cost 800 yen.

  • 能生海水浴場

    Nou Beach

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    A sandy swimming beach symbolized by Benten Rock and its red bridge, with clear shallow water inside a breakwater, popular with families.

  • 安い！早い！うまい！紅ズワイガニ

    Red Snow Crab (Marine Dream Nou)

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-566-3456

    Fresh red snow crab can be bought and eaten on the spot beside Nou fishing port at Marine Dream Nou.

  • 糸魚川市駅北広場キターレ

    Itoigawa Station North Plaza Kitare

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-556-8200

    A plaza linking Itoigawa Station with the Sea of Japan, with a hall, entrance, three kitchens and dining, plus a workspace and children's play space.

  • 糸魚川海水浴場

    Itoigawa Beach

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    Summer swimming beach in Itoigawa, also called Jade Coast because jade is found there, one stop from Itoigawa Station.

  • Live Cafe ひすいの海

    Live Cafe Hisui no Umi

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-556-9100

    A cafe-attached guesthouse with a shared lounge, free Wi-Fi, rooms with desks and hot-tub bathrooms, and buffet breakfast.

  • 親不知海水浴場

    Oyashirazu Beach

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-561-7288

    A pebble swimming beach two minutes from Oyashirazu IC, with clear water where fish can be seen, beside a roadside station popular with families.

  • 街なかレンタサイクル

    Town Rental Cycles

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    Rental bicycles on a roughly 10 km bicycle-only road along the former Hokuriku Main Line, with remaining tunnels, bridges, and Sea of Japan views.

    Itoigawa Black Yakisoba

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-1742

    Squid-ink yakisoba using Niigata squid, with each shop’s own toppings and seasonings, created by Itoigawa restaurants in 2009.

  • no Hakusan Shrine

  • 百川海水浴場（2026年度開設無し）

    Momokawa Beach

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-566-2214

    Sandy swimming beach in Itoigawa that will not open in 2026, with rocky reefs including chicken-shaped Tottoko Rock.

  • 大和川海水浴場

    Yamatogawa Beach

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    Pebble swimming beach in Itoigawa with calm, clear water, a beach hut, and barbecue.

  • 3億年前の神秘！化石発掘体験！

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-553-1880

    On the Fossa Magna Museum grounds in Itoigawa, visitors can dig for roughly 300-million-year-old coral and bryozoan fossils and take home up to three.

  • 鉄道好き必見！ジオラマ鉄道模型で運転手になろう！

    Itoigawa Geo Station Geopal

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    A facility under Itoigawa Station with real trains, models and Plarail, where you can drive a large diorama of Itoigawa’s terrain.

  • 雨飾山麓しろ池の森

    Shiroike Forest at the Foot of Mt. Amakazari

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-558-2002

    Forest at Mt. Amakazari's foot; Shiroike views that peak, one of Japan's 100 Famous Mountains, and nearby Mt. Tokura (975 m) is hikable.

  • 能生漁港昼セリ市場見学

    Nou Fishing Port Daytime Auction Viewing

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    At Nou Fishing Port, visitors can watch a rare daytime fish auction that starts at 3 p.m.

  • 駒ヶ岳・大神堂登山口

    Mt. Komagatake Ogamido Trailhead

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-1511

    Trailhead for the Nechi route up 1,487 m Mt. Komagatake, set among steep cliffs above Umidani Gorge, about three hours to the summit.

    Shiraike Pond

    A pond on the way to Renge Onsen with a mirror-like surface, a surrounding path, spring skunk cabbage, and autumn foliage.

    After viewing Gyokusuien and Tanimura Art Museum, make a bracelet at Gyokusuien's jade table using one jade bead with natural stones or crystal.

  • びびら浜（藤崎）海水浴場

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

    A long sandy swimming beach with shallow water in the Hokuriku region, opening on 1 July 2026.

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-555-7344

  • 大王あじさい園

  • 明星山・ヒスイ峡登山口

    Mount Myojo (Hisui-kyo Trailhead)

    Itoigawa, Niigata · 025-552-1511

    The Hisui-kyo route ascends Mount Myojo (1,188 m), an independent peak that shapes the gorge; the summit takes about three and a half hours, with no mountain hut.
