Sights in Izumozaki, Niigata

Izumozaki · Niigata · 14 places

See

  • Hamayaki (Charcoal-Grilled Fish)

    浜焼き

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2291

    Fish grilled over Uonuma white charcoal, a preserved food from Izumozaki that is also valued as a gift.

  • Ryokan and Sunset Hill Park

    良寛と夕日の丘公園

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2291

    Hill park beside the Ryokan Memorial Hall in Izumozaki, with a Ryokan statue, poem monuments, and a stone marking a selected Sea of Japan and Sado view.

  • Ryokan Memorial Hall

    良寛記念館

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2370

    Memorial hall displaying Ryokan's calligraphy, belongings, and documents, with a replica of the Gogo-an hermitage on the grounds.

  • Izumozaki Paper Balloons

    紙風船

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2045

    Paper balloons have been made in Izumozaki since 1919, including decorative crested ibis and penguin designs and custom orders.

  • Ryokan Hall

    良寛堂

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2250

    Hall completed in 1922 on Ryokan's birthplace site in Izumozaki, with a stone Jizo he carried, his inscribed poem, and a statue facing Sado.

  • Inohana Beach

    井鼻海水浴場

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2291

    Wide sandy swimming beach at the north end of Izumozaki, with views of Sado Island and Mount Yahiko.

  • Basho Garden

    芭蕉園

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2291

    A garden with a carved haiku monument and a Basho statue commemorating the poet’s 1689 stay in Izumozaki on the Narrow Road to the Deep North.

  • Izumozaki Strawberry Farm

    出雲崎いちご畑

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 080-2025-9881

    A farm where visitors can pick and buy Niigata’s Echigo-hime strawberries, then eat them in the rest area or take them home.

  • Tsumairi Streetscape

    妻入りの街並

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2250

    A long streetscape along the Sea of Japan that once thrived as a Hokkoku Kaido post town and port for ships bound for Sado.

  • Fureai no Sato Health and Welfare Center

    保健福祉総合センター ふれあいの里

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-41-7133

    A day-trip hot spring in Izumozaki with baths, assembly halls, Japanese-style rooms, a library, and a cooking classroom.

  • Ryokan Zazo

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2250

  • Ryokan and Basho Tour

    良寛と芭蕉めぐり

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2291

    A short guided historical tour of Izumozaki, Ryokan's birthplace and a place Basho wrote about, visiting sites linked to both.

  • Izumozaki Gourmet Town Walk

    出雲崎『美食』街めぐり

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2291

    A food event in historic Izumozaki where local restaurants serve seasonal dishes and sake, combinable with town sightseeing.

  • Izumozaki Hometown Storyteller Tour

    出雲崎ふるさと語りべ

    Izumozaki, Niigata · 0258-78-2291

    Local guides lead tours of Izumozaki, Ryokan’s birthplace, sharing stories of its Edo-period port town and 4 km coastal tsumairi streetscape.
