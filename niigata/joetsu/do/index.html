Things to do in Joetsu, Niigata

Joetsu · Niigata · 21 places

Do

  • Echigo TOKImeki Resort Setsugekka

    えちごトキめきリゾート雪月花

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-543-8988

    A sightseeing train linking Joetsu-Myoko, Myoko-Kogen, Naoetsu and Itoigawa, where you can enjoy local cuisine amid countryside, mountains and the Sea of Japan.

  • 21st Echigo-Kenshin Sake Festival 2026

    第21回越後・謙信SAKEまつり2026

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-521-2627

    A Joetsu event gathering sake, wine, doburoku and local beer to introduce local sake-making culture, with brewery sales and food booths on Takada Honmachi shopping street.

  • Unohama Onsen

    鵜の浜温泉

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-534-4465

    Hot spring area along the pine-lined Unohama coast, with Sea of Japan sunsets and Sado Island views on clear days.

  • Ogata Campground

    大潟キャンプ場

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-534-4465

    Seaside campsite among pines in Ogata, Joetsu, in a forest-bathing park where waves can be heard, beside a field-athletics course.

  • Yuttari no Sato (Nagamine Onsen)

    ゆったりの郷（長峰温泉）

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-548-3911

    A natural day-trip hot spring overlooking Mount Ogami and Mount Yoneyama, with Japanese and Western baths, an outdoor bath, and a sauna.

  • Shichifuku no Yu Open-Air Bath (Joetsu)

    野天風呂 七福の湯 上越店

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-521-4126

    Sauna in Joetsu with daily löyly sessions in a high-temperature room and monthly sauna events on the 3rd and 17th.

  • Namba Highland Campground

    南葉高原キャンプ場

    Joetsu, Niigata

    Campground on the mid-slope of 949-meter Mt. Namba in Kubiki Prefectural Natural Park, with tent sites, bungalows, and views of the city and Sea of Japan.

  • Unohama Ningyokan (Hot Spring and Pool)

    鵜の浜人魚館（温泉とプール）

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-534-6211

    Natural hot-spring baths and heated pools, including a 25-metre pool, where you can watch the sun set over the Sea of Japan.

  • Kanayayama Ski Resort

    金谷山スキー場

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-525-4295

    Historic ski area known as the birthplace of Japanese skiing, with three courses, a sledding area, and a museum of early ski gear.

  • Itakura-tei Soba Restaurant

    体験交流施設　そば処いたくら亭

    Joetsu, Niigata

    An experience facility and soba restaurant in a private house built in 1923 in Itakura, Joetsu.

  • Kubiki Brewery (Kubiki)

    頚城酒造（株）

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-536-2329

    Sake brewery in Kakizaki, Joetsu, brewing Kubiki in small traditional tanks with Niigata rice polished to about 58 percent.

  • Fukuhei Campground

    伏兵キャンプ場

    Joetsu, Niigata · 090-3343-8856

    A mountaintop campground in Joetsu with 30 auto-camp sites, an observation deck, nature trails, and a campfire plaza.

  • Yukidaruma Hot Spring Kubikino

    ゆきだるま温泉 久比岐野

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-593-2041

    A day-use hot spring expanded from the baths of the inn Onsen no Yado Kubikino, with free-flowing source water near Cupid Valley.

  • Cupid Valley Ski Resort

    キューピットバレイスキー場

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-593-2041

    Ski resort in Yasuzuka, Joetsu, opened in 1990, with a 4,000-metre course, a kids' park, and ungroomed runs.

  • Takeda Sake Brewery (Katafune)

    （資）竹田酒造店

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-534-2320

    Sake brewery in Ogata, Joetsu, producing Katafune, a sake with rounded sweetness balanced by dryness, and offering reserved tours from mid-November to February.

  • Musashino Sake Brewery (Ski Masamune)

    （株）武蔵野酒造

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-523-2169

    Sake brewery in historic Takada, Joetsu, making slightly sweet Ski Masamune, named for Japan’s skiing birthplace, with year-round brewery tours by reservation.

  • Myoko Sake Brewery (Myokosan)

    妙高酒造（株）

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-522-2111

    Sake brewery using ultra-soft underground water from Mount Myoko to make smooth, umami-rich sake; tours require prior consultation.

  • Gochi Campground

    ごちキャンプ場

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-530-7877

    Year-round hilltop campsite in Joetsu that opened in July 2025, on a wide grassland with ocean views and close to city shops and baths.

  • Joetsu Sake Brewery (Echigo Bijin)

    上越酒造（株）

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-528-4011

    Sake brewery making clean, soft-drinking sake such as Echigo Bijin and Koshi no Wakatake; tours are available by reservation.

  • Skytopia Yurand Experience Exchange Center

    スカイトピア遊ランド 体験交流センター

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-547-2221

    Large public bath in a Joetsu lodging and experience facility, with an observation bath containing natural black silica.

  • Joetsu Resort Center Kurumi Kazokuen

    上越リゾートセンターくるみ家族園

    Joetsu, Niigata · 025-544-7440

    A bathing facility using waste heat from a clean center, with a large bath, sauna, osteopathic clinic, gateball field, and Japanese garden.
