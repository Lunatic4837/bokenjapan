Things to do in Kamo, Niigata

Kamo · Niigata · 5 places

Do

  • Kamo Nanatani Onsen Bijin no Yu

    加茂七谷温泉 美人の湯

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-41-4122

    Day-trip hot spring in Kamo overlooking Mount Awagatake, with indoor, open-air and private baths and meals using local vegetables.

  • Charcoal-Making Experience

    炭焼き体験

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    At Awagatake Prefectural Recreation Area in Kamo, visitors remove charcoal burned for three days, a hot task offered each autumn with the foliage.

  • Kayamori Farm (Clay-Pot Rice and Farm Experience)

    新潟のど真ん中で味わう！農家の土鍋ご飯と農業体験

    Kamo, Niigata · 025-333-9555

    Kayamori Farm in Kamo pairs a field experience and clay-pot rice lesson with a meal of vegetables grown using fermented Kamo River salmon as fertilizer.

  • Yukitsubaki Petal Dyeing

    雪椿の花びら染

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-1740

    Hand-dyed goods made one by one with natural snow camellia petals, each with a unique color, sold at the Kamo Chamber of Commerce.

  • Rickshaw Tour of Kamo, the Little Kyoto of Hokuetsu

    人力車で巡る北越の小京都 ～加茂の町並みをめぐる人力車乗車体験～

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0104

    Rickshaw tour of Kamo townscapes, known as the little Kyoto of Hokuetsu, with a meal at a long-established restaurant.
