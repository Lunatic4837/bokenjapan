Sights in Kamo, Niigata

Kamo · Niigata · 20 places

See

  • Kamoyama Park

    加茂山公園

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    Park a five-minute walk from JR Kamo Station, where about 50,000 snow camellias and cherry blossoms can be seen together in April.

  • Koinobori over the Kamo River

    加茂川を泳ぐ鯉のぼり

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    From mid-April to early May, about 500 koinobori fly above the Kamo River in an annual display begun in 1989.

  • Ōmi Shrine

    青海神社

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0671

    Guardian shrine of Kamo on Mount Kamo, about 1,300 years old, known for its nightingale corridor and a May 21 festival procession.

  • Awagadake Prefectural Recreation Area

    粟ヶ岳県民休養地

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    Recreation area with a 27-site campground, hiking and climbing trails, a visitor center, nature observation forest, and charcoal-making beside a reservoir.

  • Kamo City Folk Museum

    加茂市民俗資料館

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0089

    Folk museum in Kamo City.

  • Gejogawa Dam

    下条川ダム

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    Dam green space in Kamo for nature study and recreation, with gardens, camping, fishing, and cherry blossoms in late April and early May.

  • Fuyutorigoe Ski Garden

    冬鳥越スキーガーデン

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-53-6020

    Family ski area in Kamo with gentle slopes, a sled-only course, three T-bar lifts, and wooden railcar Moha 1 on site.

  • Awagadake Central Climbing Trail

    粟ヶ岳・中央登山道

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    Central trail on 1,293 m Awagadake, one of Japan’s 300 famous mountains, with clear-day views of Sado Island and Noto.

  • Gejogawa Dam Green Space

    下条川ダム緑地

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    A dam green space busy with crucian carp anglers, where 700 cherry trees bloom along a 3.8 km circuit road in mid-April.

  • Gejogawa Dam

    下条川ダム

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    Dam recreation area with a 10-site campground, nature learning center, dragonfly and wetland gardens, a suspension bridge, and crucian carp fishing.

  • Seiuntei Yamashige

    清雲亭 山重

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0104

    Machiya-style restaurant in Kamo where guests dine in a quiet space of traditional Japanese atmosphere.

  • ii Office Kamo

    いいオフィス加茂 by 小柳建設

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0008

    Coworking space at Oyanagi Construction in Kamo, also operating as Microsoft Base Niigata-Kamo, with local cafe and bakery pop-ups.

  • Masukagami

    （株）マスカガミ

    Romaji: Masukagami Brewery (Manjukagami)

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0041

    Sake brewery in Kamo, founded in 1892, using Awagadake water and polishing rice to 60% or less even for regular sake.

  • Gejogawa Dam

    下条川ダム

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    A dam lake area for nature education and recreation, with a fishing deck, campsite, wetland garden, and nature learning center.

  • Kamo Folding Screen Workshop

    加茂の伝統工芸 屏風の製作現場を見学

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0040

    Visitors can watch folding screens made from wood and washi in Kamo and assemble a kumiko coaster to take home.

  • Kamo Kiri Tansu

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0445

    Sightseeing spot in Kamo, Niigata.

  • Nagase Shrine

    長瀬神社

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-3101

    Shrine overlooking the Kamo River in Hachiman, Kamo, where a rare hexagonal mikoshi is paraded on June 15.

  • Fuyudorigoe Ski Garden (Multipurpose Plaza)

    冬鳥越スキーガーデン（多目的広場）

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-53-6020

    Year-round plaza that is a grass field from spring to autumn and a family ski slope in winter, displaying historic railcars and a flower clock.

  • Awagadake Prefectural Recreation Area

    粟ケ岳県民休養地

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    Prefectural recreation area at the foot of Mount Awagadake, with a campground, visitor center, and a water source reflecting the mountain in every season.

  • Kamo-jima (Striped Cotton Fabric)

    加茂縞

    Kamo, Niigata · 0256-52-0080

    Cotton cloth woven in Kamo since the late Edo period, valued for indigo insect resistance and durability as farmers' workwear.
