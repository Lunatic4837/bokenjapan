Sights in Kariwa, Niigata

Kariwa · Niigata · 8 places

See

  • Pia Park Torinbo

    ぴあパークとうりんぼ

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-31-8600

    A Kariwa complex with day-use ion and carbonated baths overlooking the plain, sand-dune peach fields, and strawberry and tomato production.

  • Kariwa Pia Park Soccer Field

    刈羽ぴあパークサッカー場

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-31-8600

    Soccer ground with two full-size floodlit pitches and spectator seats, beside a lodging center for sports camps.

  • Kariwa Shinike Nature Observation Park

    刈羽新池自然観察公園

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-45-3913

    Nature observation park in Kariwa with five ponds, a lawn, and streams, where Genji fireflies appear around mid-June.

  • Katsuyama Castle Ruins Walking Trail

    勝山城址遊歩道

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-45-3913

    Green trail at the village-designated Katsuyama Castle ruins, with Sea of Japan and, on clear days, Sado Island views from the summit.

  • Hozoji Temple

    宝蔵寺

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-45-2397

    Temple enshrining Echigo’s fifth-pilgrimage Kannon, also called Futaisan Hozoji after its statues would not leave by boat; azaleas bloom around May.

  • Tofuku-in Temple

    東福院

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-28-2055

    Founded about 540 years ago by Akada Castle lord Saito Shimotsuke-no-kami, one of Uesugi Kenshin’s four chief retainers, it holds many treasures from that time.

  • Joraku-ji Temple

    常楽寺

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-45-3376

    This temple enshrines Echigo’s sixth pilgrimage Kannon, a Nara-period sculpture unveiled every 33 years.

  • Akada Castle Ruins Walking Trail

    赤田城址遊歩道

    Kariwa, Niigata · 0257-45-3913

    A maintained loop of about 2 km at the Akada Castle ruins, chosen among Niigata's 100 forest-bathing forests, with views from a 150 m lookout.
