Things to do in Kashiwazaki, Niigata

Kashiwazaki · Niigata · 6 places

Do

  • Osaki Onsen (Yukiwariso no Yu)

    大崎温泉「雪割草の湯」

    Kashiwazaki, Niigata · 0257-47-2113

    Pale-yellow sodium chloride day-use bath on the Kashiwazaki coast, with Sea of Japan sunset views beside a hepatica colony.

  • Jonnobi Hot Spring (Rakuju no Yu)

    じょんのび温泉「楽寿の湯」

    Kashiwazaki, Niigata · 0257-41-2222

    A brownish natural hot spring on Mount Kurohime, whose indoor bath ceiling represents a thatched-house attic.

  • Yugi Auto Campground (Nishiyama Nature Experience Facility)

    ゆうぎオートキャンプ場(西山自然体験交流施設)

    Kashiwazaki, Niigata · 0257-31-6300

    A forest auto campground in Kashiwazaki with tent and powered sites, log cabins, covered barbecue, and craft experiences, five minutes from Ishiji Beach.

  • Salmon Hand-Catching Experience

    さけつかみ捕り体験

    Kashiwazaki, Niigata · 0257-43-9131

    Visitors catch salmon by hand as they return to the Tannegawa River in Kashiwazaki from early November to early December.

  • Garuru no Oka Campground

    ガルルの丘キャンプ場

    Kashiwazaki, Niigata · 0257-41-3355

    Hilltop campsite in Kashiwazaki overlooking the prefectural Kodomo Shizen Okoku facility, with 15 tent sites, treehouse bungalows, and an all-weather indoor hall.

  • Takayanagi Garuru Ski Area

    高柳ガルルのスキー場

    Kashiwazaki, Niigata · 0257-41-2158

    Family ski area in Kashiwazaki's Takayanagi, attached to a children's nature park, with gentle slopes and a dedicated sled course.
