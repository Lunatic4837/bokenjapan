Things to do in Minamiuonuma, Niigata

Minamiuonuma · Niigata · 34 places

Do

  • Mount Makihata Foothills Campground

    巻機山麓キャンプ場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-0924

    Campground at the foot of Mount Makihata, known for wild birds and alpine plants, with 40 tent sites and hiking.

  • Uenohara Campground

    上の原キャンプ場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-6279

    Lawn campsite in Uenohara Park on Muikamachi’s Uonuma hills, near iris gardens, with fishing and a footbath at Omatsu Pond.

  • Grape Harvest and Wine Festival

    ぶどう収穫祭とワインまつり

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-777-5877

    Grape-harvest festival at Agri-Core Echigo Winery in Yairo no Mori Park, with limited wines, food stalls, and outdoor concerts.

  • Muikamachi Hot Springs

    六日町温泉

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-788-1703

    A hot-spring area in Muikamachi found when springs gushed during natural-gas test drilling in 1957, designated a national recreational hot-spring area in 1964.

  • Hatsukaishi Onsen Ishiuchi Jung Palnas

    ハツカ石温泉 石打ユングパルナス

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-7888

    Day-trip hot spring next to Ishiuchi Maruyama Ski Resort, with a large bath, open-air bath, salt sauna and medicinal-herb bath.

  • Hakkaisan Ropeway (Sea of Clouds Viewing) / Muikamachi Hakkaisan Ski Resort

    雲海鑑賞ロープウェー

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-775-3311

    An 81-passenger ropeway with open aerial views, hiking access to Mt. Hakkai, and early runs for sea-of-clouds viewing.

  • Osaki Dam Park Campground

    大崎ダム公園キャンプ場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-6665

    Campground in a community park at the foot of Mt. Hakkai, beside Osaki Hakkaizan Son Shrine, with seasonal walks around the dam lake.

  • Uonuma Chrysanthemum Exhibition (Urasa Chrysanthemum Festival)

    魚沼菊花展 浦佐菊まつり

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-777-3054

    About 1,000 pots of chrysanthemums, tended for a year by growers from across the region, are exhibited and judged at Fukōji Temple in Urasa.

  • Urasa Hot Springs

    浦佐温泉

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-777-3054

    A simple hot-spring area west of Urasa Station, surrounded by Echigo Sanzan and the Uonuma Hills, and known as a climbing base.

  • Ishiuchi Maruyama Ski Resort

    石打丸山スキー場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-2222

    Ski resort opened in 1949 and linked to GALA Yuzawa and Yuzawa Kogen, with natural snow, varied terrain, and snow glamping.

  • Ikazawa Campground

    五十沢キャンプ場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-774-2142

    Campsite in Minamiuonuma with tent and auto sites, cottages, a fishing pond, and a forestry experience hall.

  • Maiko Onsen Iishi-no-Yu

    舞子温泉 飯士の湯

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-3511

    Day-trip hot spring about two minutes from Shiozawa-Ishiuchi IC, with an onsite simple spring, Uonuma cuisine, disc golf, camping and glamping.

  • Maiko Snow Resort

    舞子スノーリゾート

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-4100

    Ski resort one minute from the Shiozawa-Ishiuchi interchange, with 26 runs, a six-person gondola, a snow park, and an on-site hotel.

  • Cold Ascetic Training and Setsubun Festival

    寒修行・節分祭

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-779-2010

    Setsubun bean-throwing event in Osaki, Minamiuonuma, linked to seven-day cold ascetic training at Fudo Falls that the public may join by application.

  • Itsukamachi Amaike Educational Green Campground

    五日町天池教育グリーンキャンプ場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-776-2032

    Summer campground at Itsukamachi Ski Area with tent sites, birdwatching, and views of Mount Hakkai and Mount Makihata.

  • Chateau Shiozawa Ski Resort

    シャトー塩沢スキー場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-1191

    Ski resort in Minamiuonuma with Ipponsugi and Yoshisato slopes and seven runs, 70 percent of them for beginners.

  • Joetsu Kokusai Ski Resort

    上越国際スキー場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-1028

    Ski resort in Minamiuonuma with four zones and 22 runs for all levels, plus a kids' snow play area and Saturday fireworks.

  • Kabanosawa Hot Spring

    樺野沢温泉（かばんさわおんせん）

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-1030

    A sodium-calcium chloride hot spring in Minamiuonuma, at 47°C, with day-trip bathing at Joetsu Kokusai Ski Resort's Annex.

  • Osawayama Hot Spring

    大沢山温泉

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-2579

    A quiet hot spring surrounded by mountains in Minamiuonuma, said to have opened in 1630, with clear, smooth water good for the skin.

  • Muikamachi Hakkaisan Ski Resort

    六日町八海山スキー場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-775-3311

    Ski area in Minamiuonuma reached by a 2.2 km ropeway, with 11 courses and three pair lifts.

  • Hakkaisanroku Ski Resort

    八海山麓スキー場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-779-3103

    Ski resort with 10 varied runs, a family sled slope, snowboarding on all courses, and elevation from 210 to 420 meters.

  • Muika Snow Resort

    ムイカスノーリゾート

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-3311

    Ski resort in Minamiuonuma with 20 courses, four lifts, a kids' park, and direct access to Muika Onsen Hotel.

  • Yamato City Ski Area Junior Ski Class

    大和市スキー場 ジュニア・スキー教室

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata

    Junior ski lessons for elementary and junior-high students at Hakkaisanroku Ski Area in Minamiuonuma, for residents near Yamato City.

  • Itsukamachi Ski Resort

    五日町スキー場

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-776-2132

    Ski resort in Minamiuonuma with 10 runs, night skiing, four lifts, and slopes from 160 to 510 meters.

  • Uonuma Rice Planting Experience

    自分の手でお米を作る！日本有数の米処、新潟県魚沼で田植え体験

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-2171

    Hands-on rice planting with Koshihikari seedlings in Uonuma, open from elementary-school age, with Uonuma Koshihikari rice as a souvenir.

  • Kimono Experience: Bokushi-dori Stroll in Shiozawa-juku

    着物体験 ～三国街道塩沢宿「牧之通り」散策～

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 080-9415-1501

    In Minamiuonuma, wear Honshiozawa or Shiozawa tsumugi kimono and walk historic Bokushi Street in Shiozawa-juku.

  • Vegetable Harvest and Specialty Farmer Rice Purchase Experience

    野菜収穫体験とこだわり農家お米買付体験

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-0537

    Visitors harvest seasonal vegetables at Kawanaga Farm in Minamiuonuma and can buy Koshihikari rice while talking with the growers; winter offers include mochi pounding and amazake.

  • Snowmobile Experience at Maiko Snow Resort

    スノーモービル体験

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-211-4195

    Snowmobile courses at Maiko Snow Resort let visitors drive 120cc to 200cc machines across the snow after a briefing, with no special driving permit required.

  • Traditional Mochi-Pounding Experience in Uonuma

    「お米のまち」魚沼ならでは！昔懐かし餅つき体験

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-772-6800

    Pound freshly cooked glutinous rice in a large mortar, then eat one serving—the size of three cut pieces—in one of three flavors around a hearth.

  • Snow Banana Boat at Maiko Snow Resort

    雪上バナナボート

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-211-4195

    A snowmobile-towed banana boat runs a dedicated course at Maiko Snow Resort, separate from the ski and snowboard slopes.

  • Uenohara Highland Hot Spring

    上の原高原温泉

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-788-1703

    Highland hot-spring area in Minamiuonuma, opened in 1992 and used year-round for training camps and hot-spring cures.

  • Maiko Snow Resort Course

    白銀のゲレンデをお散歩！舞子スノーリゾートコース

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-4100

    Snowshoe walk on snow-covered ski slopes, including making snow sliders and exploring a treehouse in the forest.

  • Gojitsujo Drum

    御実城太鼓

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-788-1703

    Said to be the battle drum of Sakado Castle's Ueda group, which directed the Uesugi army and was granted the vanguard.

  • Minamiuonuma Hot Spring Village

    南魚沼温泉郷

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-3377

    Hot-spring area in Minamiuonuma with scenic baths, inn meals, autumn foliage, winter sports, and snow-viewing baths in winter.
