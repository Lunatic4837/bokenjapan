Sights in Minamiuonuma, Niigata

Minamiuonuma · Niigata · 80 places

See

  • Minamiuonuma Maji-don (Rice Bowl Campaign)

    南魚沼、本気丼(マジ丼)

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-3377

    A food campaign in which local restaurants and sushi shops serve 54 large rice bowls made with Minamiuonuma Koshihikari.

  • Hakkaisan Ropeway and Observation Deck

    八海山ロープウェー・八海山展望台

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-775-3311

    Ropeway on Mt. Hakkai, one of the Echigo Sanzan, leading to an observation deck with clear-day views of mountains, the Sea of Japan, and Sado Island.

  • Shiozawa-juku Bokushi-dori (Mikuni Kaido)

    三国街道 塩沢宿 牧之通り

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-1206

    Historic street in Minamiuonuma recreating the Edo-period Shiozawa post town and its snow-country covered walkways, named after writer Suzuki Bokushi.

  • Uonuma no Sato

    魚沼の里

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-775-7707

    A rural complex in Nagamori, Minamiuonuma, with a cafe, shops, a snow storehouse, and a soba restaurant, facing Mount Hakkai.

  • Hakkai Shrine

    八海神社

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-775-2693

    Shrine encircled by cedars planted in 1836, where paired trunks symbolize marital harmony and visitors pray for safe childbirth.

  • Mount Hakkai

    八海山

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-6665

    A 1,778 m sacred mountain and symbol of Muikamachi, with three climbing routes and summit scenes used in the 2009 drama Tenchijin.

  • Mount Makihata (Shimizu Trailhead)

    巻機山・清水登山口

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-6665

    Shimizu trailhead for 1,967 m Mount Makihata, with two routes; the recommended Ido Ridge takes about five hours to a summit hut.

  • Ishigami Shrine

    石上神社

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-6665

    A shrine founded as the guardian deity of Sōtō temple Hōrin-ji, whose coffered ceiling displays 69 works donated by Yokoyama Taikan, Yamamoto Isoroku and others.

  • Tsumugi-dori Light Truck Market

    つむぎ通り「軽トラ市」

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-1206

    A pedestrian market on Tsumugi-dori in front of Shiozawa Station, where light-truck vendors sell local produce, food, clothing, antiques, and crafts.

  • Raiden-sama's Water

    雷電様の水（らいでんさまのみず）

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-6665

    Water that wells up in several cascades from a huge rock at the foot of Mount Katsura, at 400 tons a day, and is used for tap water and sake.

  • Uono no Sato (Local Food Complex)

    魚野の里

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-6788

    Uono no Sato has four areas for fresh fish, souvenirs, Echigo sake, and Uonuma cuisine, plus sweets such as sasa-dango soft serve and Uonuma yaki manju.

  • The Veranda Ishiuchi Maruyama

    ザ・ヴェランダ石打丸山

    Romaji: The Veranda Ishiuchi Maruyama

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-2222

    Viewpoint opened in 2022 at Ishiuchi Maruyama Ski Resort, with a terrace overlooking Minamiuonuma rice fields and Mt. Makihata.

  • Ryūkoku-ji Temple

    龍谷寺

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-779-2020

    White-walled temple housing a Hawaii-donated Kannon and eleven-faced Kannon, with openwork transoms by Ishikawa Unchō and a neighboring hall of 120 Buddha statues.

  • Hakkaisan Son Shrine

    八海山尊神社

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-779-2010

    Hakkaisan Son Shrine's village shrine, built in 1979, enshrines Mount Hakkai and has an 8.5-metre granite torii.

  • Kankō-ji Temple

    関興寺

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-2111

    Rinzai Zen temple in Minamiuonuma known for miso said to bring blessings after Uesugi-donated sutras were buried in it and survived a fire.

  • Agricore Echigo Winery

    アグリコア越後ワイナリー

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-777-5877

    Winery offering snow-cellar tours, paid wine tasting, and a restaurant overlooking the vineyards.

  • Untō-an Temple

    雲洞庵

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-782-0520

    Sōtō Zen temple in Minamiuonuma, over 1,200 years old, whose 1707 main hall is a prefectural cultural property and whose treasure hall shows Uesugi and Takeda letters.

  • Uramakihata Gorge

    裏巻機渓谷

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-774-2142

    A 6 km gorge on the north face of Mount Makihata, formed by the Isazawa River, with cliff walls, waterfalls, and a trail in a prefectural conservation area.

  • Uonuma Skyline

    魚沼スカイライン（南魚沼市）

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-3377

    An about 20 km ridge drive on the Minamiuonuma–Tokamachi border, with five lookouts over Uonuma’s mountains and basin and Tsumari’s river terraces; closed in winter.

  • Imaizumi Memorial Hall Art Station

    今泉記念館アートステーション

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-4500

    Art gallery in Minamiuonuma overlooking Mount Makihata, with about 40 of 144 Munakata Shiko works on permanent display.

  • Noborikawa River Park

    登川河川公園

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-3377

    River park with a 6.8-kilometre walking path for barbecues and outdoor activities.

  • Mount Sakado

    坂戸山

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-788-1703

    Mountain in Minamiuonuma, Niigata.

    A 634-meter mountain in Minamiuonuma with Sakado Castle ruins, a national historic site, and spring colonies of dogtooth violets.

  • Bishamondo (Kisshozan Fukoji Temple)

    毘沙門堂（吉祥山普光寺）

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-777-2001

    Hall enshrining Bishamonten, built about 1,200 years ago by Sakanoue no Tamuramaro and venue of the Hadaka Oshiai festival.

  • Sakado Castle Ruins

    坂戸城跡

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-3756

    Castle ruins that were the Ueda Nagao clan's base in the Nanboku-chō period and later a Uesugi garrison guarding the Kantō approach.

  • Yasuo Toyama Nonohana Gallery

    外山康雄 野の花館

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-783-7787

    A gallery in a renovated old folk house that displays paintings of often-overlooked flowers.

  • Minamiuonuma Rafting

    南魚沼ラフティング

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 090-3145-0178

    Rafters descend about 11 km of a river that stays high even in midsummer, over roughly two and a half hours, with a 6 m rock jump and cave exploration.

  • Roku Banki Yama

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-788-1703

  • Mikuni Kaido Shiozawa-juku Warrior Doll Displays

  • Osaki Takiya Spring

    Spring water from the Uonuma range, drawn from a basin at Omae Shrine on Route 291 and used to grow Osaki greens in winter.

  • Mikuni Kaido Shiozawa-juku Hina Snow-Viewing Decorations

    三国街道塩沢宿ひな雪見かざり

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 090-7244-2353

    Since 2006, shops and homes around Shiozawa-juku's Bokushi Street display rare hina dolls from the Edo through Heisei periods, including Echigo-jofu kimekomi dolls.

  • Mount Hakkai Observation Deck

    八海山展望台

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-775-3311

    Observation deck on Mount Hakkai.

  • Tomioka White Art Museum

    Japan's only museum exhibiting and housing about 500 works by Soichiro Tomioka, internationally known for painting snow country.

  • Mount Sakado (Sakado Trailhead)

  • Yairo Forest Park

  • Mount Tango

  • Ikeda Memorial Art Museum

  • Mount Makihata

    巻機山

    Minamiuonuma, Niigata · 025-773-6665

  • Zenibuchi Park Cherry Blossom Viewing

  • Suzuki Bokushi Memorial Museum

  • Omatsu Pond

  • Uonuma Skyline Gokoku Kannon Viewpoint

  • Osaki Dam Lake

  • Kaminohara Park and Omatsu Pond

  • Echigo Jofu
