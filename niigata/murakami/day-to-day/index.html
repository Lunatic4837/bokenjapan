  • Sasagawa Nagare Roadside Station (Yuhi Kaikan)

    道の駅「笹川流れ」 夕日会館

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-79-2017

    Niigata's northernmost roadside station on the Sea of Japan, beside JR Kuwagawa Station, with a bridge to the beach.

  • Iwafune Port Direct Sales Shop

    岩船港直売所

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-56-7107

    Fishery-cooperative market beside Iwafune Port's landing and auction site, selling just-landed local fish and produce, with a fishermen's cafeteria.

  • Roadside Station Asahi (Asahi Midori no Sato)

    道の駅 「朝日（朝日みどりの里）」（国道7号）

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-72-1551

    Roadside station in Murakami's Asahi district with a product shop, restaurant serving Asahi pork, 60°C Mahoroba hot spring, pool, cottages, and a toy history museum.

  • Kamihayashi Roadside Station (Honami no Sato)

    道の駅 「神林（穂波の里）」

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-66-6326

    Roadside station in the Iwafune Koshihikari rice district, selling fresh vegetables, local rice, mochi, rice-flour bread, and sasa dango.

  • Asahi Midori-no-Sato Farm Produce Shop

    朝日みどりの里農産物直売施設

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-72-1570

    Direct-sales shop selling Murakami seasonal vegetables, fruit and processed farm goods, with a wide selection of wild vegetables in season.

  • Yume Farm Arakawa Strawberry Garden

    夢ファームあらかわいちご園

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-62-5338

    A 2,000-square-metre greenhouse growing Echigo-hime, Niigata’s original strawberry, for tasting when fully ripe.
