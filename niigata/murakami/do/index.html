Things to do in Murakami, Niigata

Murakami · Niigata · 20 places

Do

  • Murakami Evening Bamboo Lantern Festival

    むらかみ 宵の竹灯籠まつり

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    About 20,000 bamboo lanterns light Komachi and Anzen-koji streets in Murakami's castle town, with free music at nearby temples and townhouses.

  • Senami Onsen

    瀬波温泉

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-2656

    Coastal hot-spring area in Murakami that began in 1904 when hot water erupted during oil drilling; the source is 95°C.

  • Senami Onsen Beach

    瀬波温泉海水浴場

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2111

    A roughly 1 km swimming beach just behind Senami Onsen, listed among the Environment Ministry's 100 selected bathing beaches, with calm water and sunsets between Sado and Awashima.

  • Miyai Sake Brewery (Shimeharitsuru)

    宮尾酒造（株）

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-5181

    Founded in 1819, this Murakami brewery draws Asahi-range groundwater and polishes local Gohyakumangoku rice for flavorful Shimeharitsuru sake.

  • Yumoto Ryusen (Senami Onsen)

    瀬波温泉 湯元龍泉

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-5251

    Day-use hot spring in Senami Onsen drawing about 94°C sodium chloride water from 180 meters underground, with a sulfur scent near the Sea of Japan.

  • Imagawa Campground

    今川キャンプ場

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    White-sand beach campsite with rocky stretches in Murakami, where swimming, fishing, and walks are possible and camping is also allowed in the parking area.

  • Asahi Mahoroba Onsen

    朝日まほろば温泉（朝日まほろばふれあいセンター）

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-72-6627

    Day-use hot spring in Murakami with soft water from about 1,300 meters underground and open-air baths overlooking seasonal scenery beside Roadside Station Asahi.

  • Campground in Murakami (#2)

    笹川キャンプ場

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    Sandy campsite and swimming beach on the Sasagawa Nagare coast in Murakami, with pine-covered rocks and a path to Itagai Beach.

  • Taiyo Shuzo Nagomigura Exhibition Hall

    大洋酒造（株）

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-3145

    Sake brewery in Murakami with a Nagomigura exhibition of brewing tools and sake history, and brewery tours by reservation.

  • Futagojima Forest Park Auto Campground

    二子島森林公園オートキャンプ場

    Murakami, Niigata · 090-3478-5090

    Auto campground on one of two islands in Miomote Dam Lake, linked by a floating pier, with tent sites, fishing, and rental boats.

  • Seaside campsite in Murakami

    寒川キャンプ場

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    Seaside campsite on a white-sand beach with emerald water in Murakami's Sanpoku district, where visitors can swim, fish, and walk.

  • Itagai Campground

    板貝キャンプ場

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    Campsite on a wide, gently shelving inlet beach in Murakami, where visitors can swim, fish from the rocks, and walk a path toward Sasagawa Beach.

  • Yurihana Onsen Hachiman Exchange Hall

    ゆり花温泉 交流の館 八幡

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-60-5050

    This public inn in Murakami Sanpoku, offering local cuisine and sodium-calcium sulfate baths at Yurihana Onsen, closed at the end of March 2026.

  • Coastal campsite in Murakami

    碁石キャンプ場

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    Coastal campsite in Katsugi, Murakami, where black-and-white pebbles resemble Go stones and are said to have named the place.

  • Senami Onsen Funyu Park

    瀬波温泉 噴湯公園

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-2656

    Hilltop park overlooking the Senami hot-spring town and the Sea of Japan, with a steaming source well where visitors can boil onsen eggs.

  • Kuwagawa Campground

    桑川キャンプ場

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    Campground on a fine sandy beach in Kuwagawa, offering swimming, fishing, and sunset views toward Awashima.

  • New Tea Picking Experience

    新茶のお茶摘み体験

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    May tea-picking in a Murakami tea field, with instruction from a tea-shop owner and leaves you may take home.

  • Northernmost Tea Dyeing Experience

    北限の茶染め体験

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-3570

    A Murakami-tea dyeing experience where you can make an original handkerchief and a stencil-dyed frame.

  • Senami Onsen One-of-a-Kind Vessel Making (Toi Pottery Studio)

    瀬波温泉の思い出に！1点物のお好きな器作り体験（1個）

    Murakami, Niigata · 090-7260-8793

    Make one ceramic vessel, such as a tea bowl or mug, at Toi Pottery Studio; a 28-year instructor assists and firing takes about three months.

  • Sea Kayaking at Sasagawa Nagare

    笹川流れでシーカヤック体験

    Murakami, Niigata · 080-8097-9035

    Sea kayaking along Sasagawa Nagare, a national Place of Scenic Beauty and Natural Monument, passing unusual rocks and entering sea caves, with rentals for beginners.
