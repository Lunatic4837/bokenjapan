Sights in Murakami, Niigata

Murakami · Niigata · 83 places

See

  • 笹川流れ

    Sasagawa Nagare

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    An 11 km coastline of sea-eroded rocks, reefs, and caves, a national scenic site and natural monument seen by sightseeing boat.

  • イヨボヤ会館

    Iyoboya Kaikan

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-7117

    Museum displaying Murakami salmon culture and historic fishing methods, with underground windows onto the Tanegawa, a branch of the Miomote River.

  • 城下町村上 町屋の屏風まつり

    Murakami Castle Town Machiya Folding Screen Festival

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    About 80 houses in Murakami’s old merchant quarter display heirloom folding screens for free and explain their history.

  • 城下町村上 町屋の人形さま巡り

    Murakami Castle Town Machiya Doll Tour

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    Townhouses and shops in Murakami’s old merchant quarter freely display heirloom dolls, and visitors can enter interiors usually closed to the public.

  • 千年鮭 きっかわ

    Kikkawa (Thousand-Year Salmon)

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2213

    A company processing and selling Murakami salmon dishes in a 130-year-old townhouse, a registered cultural property, with about 1,000 salmon hanging inside.

  • 岩船港鮮魚センター

    Iwafune Port Fresh Fish Center

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-1261

    Fresh-fish center selling catch landed at Iwafune Port, homemade dried fish, and Murakami salmon products, with seafood dining on the second floor.

  • 笹川流れ遊覧船

    Sasagawa Nagare Sightseeing Boat

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-79-2154

    A 40-minute sightseeing boat along Sasagawa Nagare, an 11 km coast of wave-eroded rocks, reefs, and caves designated a national scenic site and natural monument.

  • 村上大祭

    Murakami Festival

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    Held since 1633, this Murakami festival features a parade of 19 carved, lacquered oshagiri floats and was designated an Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property in 2018.

  • 千年鮭 井筒屋

    Sennen-zake Izutsuya (Salmon Restaurant)

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-7700

    Salmon restaurant in Murakami, in a national tangible cultural property where Matsuo Basho stayed two nights.

  • 笹川海水浴場

    Sasagawa Beach

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    A swimming beach in central Sasagawa Nagare, a national scenic site and natural monument, with wave-carved rocks and a path to Itagai Beach.

  • 越後村上三ノ丸流鮭塩引き道場

    Echigo-Murakami Sannomaru-style Salted Salmon Workshop

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-7117

    Workshop where participants learn to make shiobiki salted salmon, taught as distinct from aramaki, and can receive rank certificates.

  • 安善小路～黒塀通り～

    Anzen-koji (Kurobei-dori)

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    A castle-town alley in Murakami lined with black fences, temples and restaurants, and an October venue for a festival of about 20,000 bamboo lanterns.

  • 村上市営あらかわゴルフ場

    Murakami Municipal Arakawa Golf Course

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-62-1100

    An 18-hole municipal golf course on the Arakawa riverbed in Murakami, with a practice range and rounds suited to beginners.

  • 笹川流れ・岩船の天然岩ガキ

    Sasagawa Nagare and Iwafune Natural Rock Oysters

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    Natural rock oysters from Sasagawa Nagare and Iwafune in Murakami, grown large by mineral-rich mountain water.

  • 村上城跡

    Murakami Castle Ruins

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-2003

    Castle ruins on 135-meter Mount Gagyu, a National Historic Site with earthworks and stone walls but no remaining buildings.

  • 鮭の酒びたし

    Sake-soaked Salmon (Sake-bitashi)

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

    Thinly sliced salted salmon shade-dried about half a year in Murakami’s wind, eaten with sake or mirin poured over it.

  • 西奈弥羽黒神社

    Senami Haguro Shrine

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-3617

    Shrine in Murakami whose 1690 hall was designated a Niigata Prefecture tangible cultural property in 1969.

  • 南大平ダム湖公園

    Minami-Ohira Dam Lake Park

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-66-6114

    Dam-lake park campsite with cooking facilities and stargazing at the Polar Star Kamihayashi observatory.

  • 石船神社

    Ishifune Shrine

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-56-7010

    An ancient shrine listed first among the eight shrines of Iwafune District in the Engishiki, with a camellia grove designated a prefectural natural monument.

  • 桑川海水浴場

    Kuwagawa Beach

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    Fine sandy beach in Kuwagawa, Murakami, with nearby rocks for fishing and views of the sunset over the sea off Awashima.

  • 碁石海水浴場

    Goishi Beach

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    A beach whose black-and-white pebbles are said to resemble Go stones, with day-trip hot-spring bathing nearby at Yurihana Kaikan.

  • 塩引き鮭・新巻鮭 即売会

    Salt-Cured Salmon and Aramaki Salmon Direct Sale

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3141

    Direct sale in Murakami of salt-cured and aramaki salmon made only from locally landed fish by fishermen’s wives; about 150–200 Sanpoku male salmon (3–6 kg).

  • 縄文の里・朝日 奥三面歴史交流館

    Jomon no Sato Asahi (Okumiomote History Exchange Hall)

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-72-1577

    History exchange hall in Murakami about Jomon-period and village life in dam-submerged Okumiomote, with craft-making experiences.

  • 伊夜日子神社

    Iyahiko Shrine

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-2656

    A shrine beside Senami Onsen's Funto Park enshrining Senami's dragon god, with an inner sanctuary 180 steps up overlooking the town and the Sea of Japan.

  • 茶館きっかわ 嘉門亭で上質なティータイム

    Kikkawa Kamon-tei Tea Salon

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-75-5711

    Tea salon in Murakami, opened in May 2022, where guests taste Murakami tea from first to fourth infusions while viewing the courtyard.

  • 板貝海水浴場

    Itagai Beach

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    Sandy and rocky swimming beach in Itagai, Murakami, where you can swim, fish from the rocks, and walk to Sasagawa Beach.

  • 村上市郷土資料館（おしゃぎり会館）

    Murakami City Local History Museum (Oshagiri Kaikan)

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-1347

    Local history museum exhibiting Oshagiri festival floats from the Murakami Grand Festival, plus armor, helmets, and other historical materials.

  • 岩船海水浴場

    Iwafune Beach

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2111

    A sandy beach in Murakami with free parking, changing rooms and showers, near the Awashima ferry terminal and a fresh-seafood shop.

  • 荒川

    Arakawa River

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-62-3101

    A 73 km first-class river through Yamagata, Niigata, and Fukushima that ranked first in the MLIT water-quality survey for three years (2003-2005).

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-72-6883

    Narumi Gold Mine ruins, said to have produced one-third of the nation's gold in the Keicho era, retain hand-dug tunnels and rough rock faces.

  • 寒川海水浴場

    Samukawa Beach

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

    A white-sand swimming beach in Murakami's Sanpoku district, with an emerald-colored sea unlike rocky shores.

  • 諸上寺公園

    Morokamiji Park

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2111

    Hilltop park between Senami Onsen and Iwafune Port, with about 500 cherry trees and views of the Sea of Japan, Awashima, and Sado Island.

  • 浄念寺

    Jōnen-ji Temple

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-52-4524

  • とれたて野菜市かみはやし株式会社

  • 柏尾海水浴場

    Kashio Beach

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2111

    A 300-metre beach in Murakami facing an inlet, with relatively calm waves, clear water, changing rooms and a beach hut.

  • 村上茶

    Murakami Tea

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-53-2258

  • 重文若林家住宅

  • 村上木彫堆朱

  • 村上どんぶり合戦

  • 今川海水浴場（釜ノ前～上山）

    Murakami, Niigata · 0254-77-3115

  • 茶寮カネエイ

  • 高坪山・高坪山登山口

  • 神林水辺の楽校
