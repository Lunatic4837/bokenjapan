Things to do in Myoko, Niigata

Myoko · Niigata · 26 places

Do

  • Takinoyu Open-air Bath (Akakura Onsen)

    赤倉温泉野天風呂 滝の湯

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-87-2165

    Open-air sulfate-bicarbonate bath at Akakura Onsen, built with about 3,000 rocks totaling 2,500 tons, with a summer mixed swimsuit pool.

  • Akakura Onsen Ski Resort

    赤倉温泉スキー場

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-87-2125

    Ski resort at the foot of Mount Myoko with 17 courses from 720 to 1,500 meters, night skiing, and day baths at Akakura Onsen.

  • Myoko Kogen Liner

    赤倉・池の平・杉野沢の各温泉地を結ぶバス。

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-72-3139

    The Myoko Kogen Liner ran daily without transfers from Joetsu-Myoko Station to Akakura, Shin-Akakura, Ikenotaira and Suginozawa hot springs from 26 December 2020 to 14 March 2021.

  • Akakura Kanko Resort Ski Area (New Year's Sunrise Sky Cable)

    元旦 初日の出早朝スカイケーブル

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-87-2503

    Myoko Kogen Sky Cable at Akakura Kanko Resort Ski Area runs from 6:30 a.m. on New Year's Day for the first sunrise, with drinks at the summit terrace.

  • Fudo Falls (Seki Onsen)

    関温泉 不動滝

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    A roughly 20-metre waterfall under Fudo Bridge in a gorge near Seki Onsen, with Otaki Fudo shrine beside the basin.

  • Seki Onsen Ski Resort

    関温泉スキー場

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-2316

    Ski resort in Myoko's Seki Onsen area with four runs, powder skiing, a snowboard park, and baths, operating into mid-May.

  • Arai Mountain Resort

    アライマウンテンリゾート（旧ロッテアライリゾート）

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    A ski resort on Mount Omunashi in Myoko, with 24 courses, a 7 km longest run, and a hot spring from 1,750 meters underground.

  • Myoko Suginohara Ski Resort

    妙高杉ノ原スキー場

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-6211

    Ski resort in Myoko with a 1,124 m vertical drop and an 8.5 km run, plus a snow park and Mt. Fuji views on clear days.

  • Ikenotaira Onsen Alpenblick Ski Resort

    池の平温泉アルペンブリックスキー場

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-2370

    Ski resort on the lower slopes of Mt. Myoko, with 16 runs, a 4 km course, and views of Lake Nojiri.

  • Kaminomiya Onsen Kawara-tei

    神の宮温泉 かわら亭

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-72-7307

    Hot-spring facility in Myoko with a sauna by advance reservation and a cold bath of metasilicic mineral water.

  • Akakura Kanko Resort Ski Area

    赤倉観光リゾートスキー場

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-87-2503

    Ski area in snowy Myoko Kogen, eight minutes from Myoko Kogen IC, with a 4.5 km downhill from 1,500 m plus sled and tube courses.

  • Madarao Kogen Ski Resort

    斑尾高原スキー場

    Myoko, Niigata · 0269-64-3214

    Snow resort on the Niigata-Nagano border with north-facing slopes, 31 runs for all levels, and night skiing.

  • Kyukamura Myoko Runrun Ski Area

    休暇村妙高ルンルンスキー場

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-3168

    Gentle beginner ski slope between the Kyukamura Myoko hotel buildings, with a 900 m run and sledding space.

  • Myoko Suginohara Ski Resort Snowmobile Experience

    妙高杉ノ原スキー場　 2025-2026 スノーモービル体験

    Myoko, Niigata · 080-3291-7227

    Snowmobile experiences at Myoko Suginohara Ski Resort include a one-group-a-day magic-hour private tour, a short ride, and a forest tour on 200cc machines.

  • Traditional Japanese Craft Experience in Myoko

    妙高で日本の伝統工芸体験

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-77-4860

    A small craft session in Myoko where an embroidery artist teaches tsumami-zaiku and hand-painted yuzen, and finished pieces can be taken home.

  • Hot Air Balloon Experience at Lotte Arai Resort

    気球体験

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    A limited-time, rope-tethered hot-air balloon ride at Lotte Arai Resort, with views of the resort and mountains from evening into night.

  • MYOKO BASE CAMP

    Myoko Base Camp

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-70-2195

    Japan's first telework facility, near Imori Pond in Myoko-Togakushi Renzan National Park, designed with ZVC Japan.

  • Snow Caliber Mini Trip at Myoko Suginohara Ski Resort

    Snow Caliber Mini Trip　雪上車遊覧ツアー

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-6211

    A snow-vehicle tour into the forest at Myoko Suginohara Ski Resort, with winter scenery visible from the windows.

  • Myoko Sake Brewery Tour (Kiminoi Brewery)

    妙高の酒蔵見学～君の井酒造～

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-72-3136

    Kiminoi Brewery tour in Myoko, where three sake houses brew smooth, delicate sake by traditional methods using local water and rice.

  • Snow Drive Tour (Arai Mountain Resort)

    スノードライブツアー

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    A seated snow ride at Arai Mountain Resort that you operate without ski or snowboard experience, lasting about 120 minutes.

  • Snow Tubing Park (Arai Mountain Resort)

    スノーチュービングパーク

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    Visitors slide down dedicated snow-slope courses on tire-tube rings, with more course variations added this year.

  • SpongeBob Kids Park / Arai Mountain Resort

    スポンジ・ボブキッズパーク

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    A SpongeBob-themed snow-play area for children, set up to support families’ first safe experiences on the slopes.

  • Snow Rafting at Arai Mountain Resort

    スノーラフティング

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    A snow-rafting ride that speeds across the snow-play slopes at Arai Mountain Resort, with photo spots on the course.

  • Snow Country Working Vehicle Experience

    雪国はたらく車体験

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1177

    Ride snow-grooming and snow-removal vehicles at Arai Snow Resort; open to ages 7 and over.

  • Strider Park / Arai Mountain Resort

    ストライダーパーク

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    Children glide freely on snow with Snow Striders, Strider bikes fitted with ski attachments, while training balance and core strength.

  • Zip Tour at Arai Mountain Resort

    ジップツアー

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    A winter snow-zipline ride on a 1,501-meter cable from the 950-meter summit, dropping about 240 meters over snowy forest.
