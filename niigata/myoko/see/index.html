Sights in Myoko, Niigata

Myoko · Niigata · 35 places

See

  • Naena Falls

    苗名滝

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    Naena Falls is a 55-meter waterfall on the Seki River at the Niigata-Nagano border, one of Japan's 100 waterfalls.

  • Koyaike Pond

    高谷池（こうやいけ）

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    Mountain pond just before the Mt. Hiuchi summit, with Hakusan kozakura in summer, autumn foliage, and the nearby Koyaike Hut for hikers.

  • Imori Pond

    いもり池

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-5270

    Imori Pond is a 2-meter-deep artificial pond facing Mount Myoko, where over 100,000 Asian skunk cabbages bloom in late April.

  • Naenadaki-en Restaurant

    お食事処 苗名滝苑

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-6536

    Restaurant at the entrance to Naena Falls serving bamboo flowing somen chilled with Myoko spring water, plus salt-grilled iwana.

  • Myoko Kogen Sky Cable

    妙高高原スカイケーブル

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-87-2503

    A cable car on the Myoko Plateau with views toward the Shiga and Nozawa ranges, Mount Madarao and Lake Nojiri, plus a summit cafe and beech forest.

  • Myoko Zip Tour

    全長約1,501mを飛ぶ空中の旅へ出発！ジップツアー

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1177

    A zip-line tour in Myoko that descends from about 950 m to 710 m in roughly three minutes, with views of the Hokushinetsu mountains and Takada Plain.

  • Mount Hiuchi

    火打山

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    A 2,462-metre peak in Myoko, one of Japan's 100 Famous Mountains, with alpine plants and rock ptarmigan along the trails.

  • Sasagamine Plateau

    笹ヶ峰高原

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    A 1,300-meter plateau with trails for spring-to-autumn trekking and, in its pasture, Utana Spring, selected among Heisei's 100 famous waters.

  • Sasagamine Direct Bus

    妙高高原駅と笹ヶ峰高原を結ぶ｢笹ヶ峰直行バス」。

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-72-3139

    Direct bus linking Myoko-Kogen Station to Sasagamine Plateau, a 1,300-meter trailhead for Mount Hiuchi and Mount Myoko.

  • Mount Myoko

    妙高山

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    A 2,454 m mountain in Myoko City, one of Japan’s 100 Famous Mountains, with summit lava rocks and views of the Sea of Japan.

  • Myoko Kogen Visitor Center

    妙高高原ビジターセンター

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-4599

    Visitor center explaining Myoko Kogen wildlife, landforms and geology, with a cafe overlooking Imori Pond and Mount Myoko.

  • Garden Pool & Night Pool

    ガーデンプール＆ナイトプール

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1177

    A two-story garden pool with a waterfall about 4 m high, a 40 cm toddler pool, a heated jacuzzi, and a night pool on weekends and during Obon.

  • Sotaki Falls

    惣滝

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    An 80-metre waterfall beyond Tsubame Onsen, one of Japan's 100 Waterfalls, seen from Sotaki Observatory near the Kogane-no-Yu outdoor bath.

  • Myoko Kogen Beer Tatra-kan

    妙高高原ビール タトラ館

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-2600

    A 400-seat beer restaurant beside Hotel Alpen Blick, modeled on a European townscape, serving fresh Myoko Kogen Beer with buffet dishes.

  • Kanzuri

    かんずり

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-72-3813

    Traditional fermented seasoning from Myoko, made with chili, koji, yuzu, and sea salt over three years.

  • Sekiyama Shrine

    関山神社

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-2464

    Village shrine of Mount Myoko founded in 708, with an 1818 palace registered as a national cultural property in 2018.

  • Heartland Myoko Golden Week Experience Fair

    ハートランド妙高「GWわくわく体験フェア」

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-3935

    A Golden Week fair at Heartland Myoko with potato planting, soft-wax candle making, and ice-making experiences.

  • Sasa Mino Sushi Making

    謙信公が残した食文化～くびき野伝統食・笹箕寿し～

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    Make Myoko sasa mino sushi by folding bamboo leaves into a winnowing-basket shape and topping rice with egg, salmon, shiitake and miso-pickled daikon.

  • Hokkoku Kaido Sekikawa Checkpoint Road History Museum

    北国街道関川関所「道の歴史館」

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3280

    Museum at the former Edo-period Sekikawa checkpoint that recreates the era with CG and life-size figures and explains road history.

  • Myoko Summer Parent-Child Workation

    妙高 夏休み親子ワーケーション

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-3935

    A summer parent-child workation in Myoko with a walk at Naena Falls, one of Japan's 100 Waterfalls, plus barbecue and fireworks.

  • HOT STASH sauna point.

    HOT STASH Sauna Point

    Myoko, Niigata · 050-5539-0253

    Private-group sauna in Myoko with varied loyly, an underground natural-water cold bath, a free-flowing hot spring, and room for eight people.

  • Myoko Ski Park

    妙高スキーパーク

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-4189

    Ski area in Myoko with quality snow and gentle slopes popular with families and beginners, including a 1.2 km forest course.

  • Treetop Adventure

    木の上の大冒険ツリーアドベンチャー

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1100

    A rope-course attraction in a Myoko forest where visitors travel between trees, with three courses for children and adults.

  • MYOKO IKENOTAIRA MTB PARK

    Myoko Ikenotaira MTB Park

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-2370

    An MTB park at the Ikenotaira Onsen Alpenblick ski area, with a beginner-friendly 1.6 km flow trail, Lake Nojiri views and forest riding lines.

  • Shimizugaike Pond

    清水ケ池

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-3911

    A pond on Sasagamine Plateau in Myoko City, about 15 minutes on foot, with views of the adjacent ranch and surrounding mountains.

  • Myoko Kogen Snowshoe Tour

    妙高高原スノーシューツアー

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-77-4860

    A private snowshoe walk through deep-snow primeval forest in Myoko Kogen, guided by local nature guides and followed by a snow picnic.

  • Heartland Myoko

    ハートランド妙高

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-3935

    A Myoko facility where visitors can try farming, straw crafts, cooking, harvesting, and violin making with locals, and also use a coworking space.

  • Myoko-Style Health Resort Program

    妙高型健康保養地プログラム

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-86-4466

    An exercise program pairing climate-therapy walks on forest therapy roads in Myoko-Togakushi Renzan National Park with exercise in a natural hot-spring pool.

  • Infinity Pool and Barrel Sauna

    インフィニティプール・バレルサウナ

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-82-4111

    Infinity pool and two barrel saunas in Myoko with views of Mount Myoko, also open as a night pool.

  • Daily Changing Adventure

    日替わりアドベンチャー

    Myoko, Niigata · 0255-75-1177

    Winter activities in Myoko City that change each day, including snow-hut making, snowball fights, treasure hunts, and giant-snowman building.

  • HIZUME Snow Adventure

    スノーアドベンチャー「HIZUME」

    A snow-play area in Myoko with 15 kinds of items, including sleds and tubes, for children and adults.

    Ride a rubber raft towed by a snowmobile across snowfields; open to ages 4 and over.

    ネイチャートレッキング

    Guided trekking on nature-rich Myoko Kogen in Myoko-Togakushi Renzan National Park, visiting seasonal spots at the foot of Mount Myoko.
