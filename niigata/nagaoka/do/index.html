Things to do in Nagaoka, Niigata

Nagaoka · Niigata · 53 places

Do

  • Kome Hyappyo Festival

    米百俵まつり

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-39-2221

    A Nagaoka festival on the Kome Hyappyo spirit, held with the autumn harvest festival, with stage stories, dress-up, and a word-collecting rally.

  • Nagaoka Festival Grand Fireworks Display

    長岡まつり大花火大会

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0570-082-083

    Fireworks display held on both banks of the Shinano River in Nagaoka, featuring a shell about 650 m across and a phoenix display about 2 km wide.

  • Nagaoka Winter Fest 2026 (Yukishika Festival: New Chapter)

    ながおかウィンターフェス2026－雪しか祭り 新章－

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-32-4500

    Successor to the 40-year Nagaoka Yukishika Festival, this event at Aore Nagaoka offers local food, stages and attractions for adults and children.

  • Izumiya (Yomogihira Onsen)

    よもぎひら温泉 和泉屋

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-23-2231

    Rural hot-spring inn in Nagaoka with an alkaline simple sulfur cold mineral spring and bedrock baths.

  • Hanamizuki Onsen Kiho

    花みずき温泉 喜芳(きほう)

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-42-4126

    Day-use and lodging hot-spring facility in Nagaoka with a 33°C naturally flowing simple spring and open-air baths.

  • Echigo Hillside National Government Park Autumn Fragrance Rose Festival

    国営越後丘陵公園「香りのばらまつり・秋」

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-47-8001

    Autumn roses fill the scent-themed rose garden at Echigo Hillside National Government Park, where about two-thirds of the garden is in bloom.

  • Yomogihira Onsen

    蓬平温泉

    Nagaoka, Niigata

    Yomogihira Onsen is a quiet mountain hot-spring area in Nagaoka, with a simple sulfur spring and three inns near Koryu Shrine.

  • Koshiji Maple Festival

    越路もみじまつり

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-92-5903

    Maple festival at a garden landscaped as the Meiji-era Takahashi family villa, with 150- to 200-year-old maples illuminated from dusk.

  • Asō no Yu Hot Spring

    麻生の湯

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-31-9300

    Natural hot-spring bath in Asoda, Nagaoka, offering a mildly salty chloride spring and outdoor barbecue with night views.

  • Teradomari Tsuwabuki Festival

    寺泊つわぶき祭

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-75-3228

    Yellow tsuwabuki flowers bloom in Teradomari, known as the Kamakura of the north, during a festival with tea ceremonies and weekend fairs.

  • Teradomari Onsen

    寺泊温泉（寺泊温泉郷）

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-75-3363

    Hot-spring area in Teradomari, Nagaoka, with sunset views toward Sado Island, fresh seafood from the port, a fish-market street, and a sodium-chloride spring.

  • Municipal campsite in Nagaoka

    長岡市営キャンプ場（東山ファミリーランド）

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-34-2225

    Municipal campsite near central Nagaoka with ten tent sites, cooking facilities, and hiking.

  • Activity spot in Yamakoshi, Nagaoka

    山古志産業まつり

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-59-2343

    An autumn harvest festival in Yamakoshi with farm-product judging and sales, specialty stalls, the final bull-sumo tournament, and illuminated rice terraces.

  • Teradomari Kinpachi-no-Yu Hot Spring

    寺泊きんぱちの湯

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-75-5888

    Day-use hot spring using deep seawater, with a sauna overlooking the Sea of Japan that opened in March 2024.

  • Washima Auto Campground

    和島オートキャンプ場

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-74-3010

    A hilltop auto campground in Nagaoka overlooking the Sea of Japan, with tent sites, powered pitches, bungalows, and sunset views on clear days.

  • Nagaoka Festival Bus Tour: Asahi Shuzo and Okome to Nakayoshi Park

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0120-865-615

  • Echigo Kawaguchi Onsen Resort

    えちご川口温泉リゾート

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-89-4511

    Day-trip hot spring on a hill in Kawaguchi, Nagaoka, with a sodium chloride spring, cafe, and views of the Shinano and Uono river confluence.

  • Tulip Festival at Echigo Hillside National Government Park

    国営越後丘陵公園「チューリップまつり 」

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-47-8001

    Colorful tulips fill the park, with photo spots such as a swing, door, and windmill, plus Niigata-grown varieties.

  • Oguni Autumn Festival

    おぐに秋まつり

    みしま産業まつり

  • Nagaoka Festival

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-39-2221

  • Echigo Hillside National Government Park Hepatica Festival

    国営越後丘陵公園「雪割草まつり」

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-47-8001

  • Mishima Festival Grand Fireworks Display

    三島まつり大花火大会

    Nagaoka, Niigata · 0258-42-2249
