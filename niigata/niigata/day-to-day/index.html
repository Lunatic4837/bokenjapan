Day-to-day services in Niigata, Niigata

Niigata · Niigata · 6 places

Day to day

  • Roadside Station Niigata Furusato Village

    道の駅「新潟ふるさと村」

    Niigata · 025-230-3030

    A roadside station with about 10,000 local products, regional dishes, sake tasting, crafts, seasonal flower fields, and a year-round artificial snow experience.

  • Toyosaka Roadside Station

    道の駅 「豊栄」（国道7号線）

    Niigata · 025-388-2700

    Barrier-free roadside station serving Niigata rice onigiri and kitsune soba, with a shop selling Koshihikari and local produce beside a nature park.

  • Roadside Station Kamuri Niitsu

    道の駅 　花夢里にいつ

    Niigata · 0250-21-6633

    A roadside station designated in 2002 that displays and sells about 2,000 varieties and 40,000 pots of potted flowers and flowering trees.

  • Fruit Doumu Yamada Orchard

    フルーツ童夢やまだ農園

    Niigata · 025-375-4708

    Orchard offering peach and grape picking, including all-you-can-eat peaches, and also growing pears on 1.1 hectares for up to 100 visitors.

  • Yamanoshita Market

    山ノ下市場

    Niigata · 025-250-2170

    A market in Yamanoshita, Niigata, selling vegetables, fruit and household goods, with an Obon flower market on 12 August.

  • Kameda Regular Stall Market (3-and-9 Market)

    亀田定期露店市場(三・九の市)

    Niigata · 025-383-1000

    Stall market on the central market street in Kameda, open from 8 a.m. to noon on days ending in 3 or 9, continuing since 1694.
