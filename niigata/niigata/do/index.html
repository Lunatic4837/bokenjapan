Things to do in Niigata, Niigata

Niigata · Niigata · 31 places

Do

  • Wara Art Festival

    わらアートまつり

    Niigata · 0256-72-8417

    An annual event in Nishikan Ward, Niigata City, showing five giant rice-straw artworks at Uwasekigata Park, with night lighting on weekends and holidays.

  • Niitsu Onsen

    新津温泉

    Niigata · 0250-22-0842

    A 1954 day-use hot spring in a residential neighborhood, known for petroleum-scented, slightly cloudy sodium-chloride bicarbonate water.

  • New Soba Festival

    新そばまつり

    Niigata · 0256-78-7361

    A reservation-only festival where visitors taste noodles made from locally produced new soba flour from Maki.

  • Fukuju Onsen Jonnobikan

    福寿温泉 じょんのび館

    Niigata · 0256-72-4126

    Day-use natural hot spring and sauna at the foot of Mount Kakuda, with indoor and open-air baths and four saunas.

  • Akiha Onsen Hanamizu

    秋葉温泉 花水

    Niigata · 0250-24-1212

    Day-trip hot spring at the foot of Mount Akiha, with a southern European atmosphere and Japanese- and Western-style baths.

  • Iwamuro Onsen

    岩室温泉

    Niigata · 0256-82-5715

    Historic hot-spring town in western Niigata City, dating to the Edo period and known as the birthplace of Echigo geisha.

  • Kamon! Duck and Leek Festival

    かもん！カモねぎまつり

    Niigata · 0256-86-3123

    Annual December event in duck-hunting Katahigashi, Nishikan Ward, with wild-duck soup, local leeks, free mochi, and negiyaki.

  • Dairo no Yu (Taho Hot Spring)

    多宝温泉 だいろの湯

    Niigata · 0256-82-1126

    Day-use hot spring with three source springs, including a sulfur-scented 50-tatami garden open-air bath and rock and waterfall baths.

  • Kosudo Onsen Hana-no-Yu Health Center

    小須戸温泉健康センター花の湯館

    Niigata · 0250-38-5800

    Day-use hot spring at the foot of oil-seeping hills, with a smooth metasilicic-acid neutral spring and a second-floor cafe using local ingredients.

  • Winter Sake Festival

    冬酒まつり

    Niigata · 025-245-3334

    Twenty-eight Niigata breweries gather at ANA Crowne Plaza Hotel Niigata for reserved-seat sake tastings and talks with brewers.

  • Umibe no Mori Campground

    海辺の森キャンプ場

    Niigata · 025-255-3810

    Campground beside Shimami Beach offering 32 tent sites, four cooking buildings, showers, forest bathing, barbecues, and beach seine-net fishing.

  • Niigata Ichiryu Sake Brewery, Echigo no Sato Kayama-tei

    新潟一粒酒造 越後の里 嘉山亭

    Niigata · 025-387-2025

    Sake tasting room in the brewery's old main house, with Niigata snacks, original goods, and a three-sake comparison set.

  • Kakudahama Campground

    角田浜キャンプ場

    Niigata · 0256-77-2155

    Pine-forest campground near the sea with 70 tent sites, a cooking area, and hot showers; swimming, fishing, and clear-day sunsets toward Sado.

  • Tanoura Onsen

    田ノ浦温泉

    Niigata · 0256-82-5715

    Seaside hot spring in Mase, where open-air baths look toward Sado Island on clear days and sunsets over the Sea of Japan.

  • Dairo no Yu (Taho Onsen)

    多宝温泉　だいろの湯

    Niigata · 0256-82-1126

    Day-trip sulfur hot spring in Nishikan, Niigata, with three unfiltered free-flowing sources and open-air baths; currently closed for construction.

  • Sauna and Natural Hot Spring Jonobi-kan

    サウナと天然温泉じょんのび館

    Niigata · 0256-72-4126

    Hot-spring facility in Niigata City with outdoor baths and a forest sauna whose cold plunge is Kakuda Mountain spring water.

  • Imayo Tsukasa Sake Brewery

    今代司酒造（株）

    Niigata · 025-245-0325

    Sake brewery founded in 1767 in an 1899 building about 15 minutes' walk from Niigata Station, offering guided tours and tastings.

  • Akiha Onsen Kasui (Sauna)

    秋葉温泉 花水（サウナ）

    Niigata · 0250-24-1212

    Sauna at Akiha Onsen Kasui in Niigata City, with a men's dry sauna around 90°C and a women's far-infrared sauna around 77°C.

  • Niigata Furusato Village Golden Week Festival

    新潟ふるさと村 GWフェスティバル

    Niigata · 025-230-3030

    An annual Golden Week event with brewery sake markets, goods selected from about 10,000 items, festival stalls, stage attractions, workshops, and challenge shops.

  • Iwamuro Onsen Town Walk

    岩室温泉まちあるき

    Niigata · 0256-82-1066

    Local guides lead walks through Iwamuro Onsen back streets and lanes, with routes tailored to visitors' requests.

  • Porcelarts Tableware Workshop

    好きな模様のオリジナル食器が完成！手ぶらでポーセラーツ体験

    Niigata · 080-7759-6780

    A one-session porcelarts lesson to decorate two pieces, such as a cup and plate, later fired by the studio at 800°C.

  • Shiokawa Sake Brewery (Koshi no Seki)

    塩川酒造（株）

    Niigata · 025-262-2039

    Sake brewery with a 1912 earthen storehouse, using sand-dune-filtered groundwater and offering reserved tours on Fridays from May to September.

  • Iwamuro Onsen Fuyuzuma Fireflies

    岩室温泉冬妻ほたる

    Niigata · 0256-82-5715

    Firefly viewing near Iwamuro Onsen, named for Fuyuzuma fireflies that grow in clear springs below Mount Taho.

  • Kimono Strolling Experience

    着物散策体験

    Niigata · 090-5751-0123

    Rent a kimono or yukata a two-minute walk from Niigata Station and stroll the streets of Furumachi.

  • Sasaiwai Sake Brewery (Tour and Tasting)

    伝統の酒蔵見学＆県外に殆ど出ない笹祝酒造の唎酒体験

    Niigata · 0256-72-3982

    Founded in 1899, this traditional wooden sake brewery uses only Niigata City rice and offers tours and tastings of sake rarely sold outside the prefecture.

  • Day bus tour in Niigata

    カーブドッチワイナリーとわらアートまつり＜がたバスツアー＞

    Niigata · 025-241-1116

    Day bus tour from Bandai with a guided Cave d'Occi Winery visit and Western lunch, then large straw sculptures at the Wara Art Festival and shopping at Sorano Terrace.

  • Original Pair Guinomi Pottery Experience

    日本酒に合うオリジナルペアぐい呑みを作れる陶芸体験

    Niigata · 025-225-1696

    Make original sake cups from scratch with an instructor who studied pottery in New York; they are reduction-fired and shipped home in one to two months.

  • Gata Bus Tour: Edamame Harvesting and Akiha Onsen Hanamizu

    ＜新潟市の魅力発見！がたバスツアー＞ 枝豆収穫体験と秋葉温泉 花水

    Niigata · 025-241-1116

    A day bus tour with edamame harvesting and tasting, lunch of local produce, a visit to Niigata Prefectural Botanical Garden, and bathing at Akiha Onsen.

  • Ito Family Three-Person Mochi Pounding

    豪農伊藤家 三人餅搗き体験

    Niigata · 025-385-2001

    Visitors pound mochi in the three-person style long served at the wealthy Ito family’s New Year and celebrations, guided by a craftsperson.

  • Hand-Building Pottery Experience

    ぬくもりを感じる器をつくろう！手びねり陶芸体験

    Niigata · 025-225-1696

    Coil-build a teacup, rice bowl, or coffee cup in one to two hours with a New York-trained instructor; pieces are returned after reduction firing.

  • Ajikata Granary Taiko

    味方穀倉太鼓

    Niigata · 025-373-3562

    Taiko group formed in 1990 by Ajikata youth to symbolize pride in village agriculture, performing vigorous drumming at various events.
