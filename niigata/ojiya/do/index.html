Things to do in Ojiya, Niigata

Ojiya · Niigata · 3 places

Do

  • Egami and the Ojiya Hina Festival

    絵紙と小千谷のひいな祭り

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    During the Ojiya Hina Festival, ukiyo-e prints called egami and hina dolls are displayed in the shopping street, reviving a local custom designated in 2019.

  • Takanoi Sake Brewery (Koshi no Hatsuume)

    高の井酒造（株）

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3450

    Sake brewery in Ojiya that stores sake in snow, using contract-grown local rice, with tours and tastings at Yukimizuan.

  • Ojiya Chijimi Handloom Weaving Experience

    トントンカラリトンカラリ♪はた織体験

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-4800

    Make a woven coaster in Ojiya, home of Ojiya-chijimi, a hemp textile listed as UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.
