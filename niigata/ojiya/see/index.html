Sights in Ojiya, Niigata

Ojiya · Niigata · 40 places

See

  • 小千谷市錦鯉の里

    Ojiya Nishikigoi no Sato

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-2233

    Exhibition facility in Ojiya, birthplace of nishikigoi, where about 200 koi of 15 varieties can be seen in a Japanese garden.

  • 山本山高原

    Yamamotoyama Plateau

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A 336 m plateau with a gentle summit for hiking and drives, seasonal flower fields, and views of the Shinano River and Echigo Plain.

  • 錦鯉

    Nishikigoi

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-86-0288

    Ornamental carp originating in Ojiya and Nagaoka, exported worldwide and designated Niigata Prefecture’s ornamental fish in 2017.

  • 慈眼寺

    Jigenji Temple

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-2495

    Temple preserving the room where Kawai Tsugunosuke held 1868 peace talks, plus battle pictures and relics; the hall was rebuilt after the 2004 earthquake.

  • 山本山菜の花畑

    Yamamotoyama Rapeseed and Sunflower Fields

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    Rapeseed and sunflower fields at Yamamotoyama Pocket Park, with hilltop views of the Echigo Sanzan and the Shinano River.

  • おぢゃ～る（市民の家・小千谷信濃川水力発電館）

    Ojaru (Citizens' House and Ojiya Shinano River Hydroelectric Power Museum)

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-2478

    Ojiya facility with a lawn campsite, overnight lodging, a free hydroelectric museum, a dog run, and winter snow play.

  • 池ケ原　はさ木

    Ikegahara Hasagi (Rice-Drying Racks)

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    Rice-drying racks at Ikegahara in Ojiya, a photo spot featured in TV commercials that changes with dawn, starry skies, and snow.

  • 小千谷縮・越後上布

    Ojiya-chijimi and Echigo-jofu

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-2329

    Traditional hemp textiles of the Echigo Uonuma region, a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, with displays and weaving experiences at Ojiya Sunplaza.

  • 小千谷へぎそば

    Ojiya Hegi Soba

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    Ojiya hegi soba is served as bite-sized rolls on a hegi vessel, bound with funori seaweed.

  • オヂヤホタル

    Ojiya Hotaru Illumination

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3566

    About 5,000 bottle lights at Yamamotoyama in Ojiya depict the Shinano River’s bends and the colors of nishikigoi.

  • 小千谷縮雪ざらし

    Ojiya Chijimi Snow Bleaching

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-2329

    A step in making Ojiya Chijimi hemp cloth that bleaches the fabric on snow, done on clear days when the snow is frozen and clean.

  • 小栗山木喰観音堂

    Oguriyama Mokujiki Kannon Hall

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-86-0288

    Hall preserving 33 Kannon statues carved by the monk Mokujiki in 1803, opened on the 17th except from November to April.

  • 料亭 東忠

    Ryotei Tochu

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-2033

    A roughly 280-year-old ryotei in a nationally registered tangible cultural property, retaining the room where Kawai Tsugunosuke lunched after negotiations, and serving seasonal dishes.

  • 小千谷市総合体育館

    Ojiya City General Gymnasium and Civic Pool

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-0077

    Gymnasium and civic pool in Ojiya with arenas, martial-arts and training rooms, children's spaces, and a 50-meter pool with a retractable roof.

  • 馬場清水（姥清水）

    Baba Shimizu Spring

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A spring at the Tokimizu historic-site trailhead in Ojiya, selected as a Niigata famous water that visitors come to draw.

  • 愛宕山ユキツバキ群生地

    Atagoyama Snow Camellia Grove

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A one-hectare grove of snow camellias at Atagoyama, found only on the Japan Sea side from Toyama to Akita and protected under winter snow.

  • 牛の角突き（小千谷・山古志）

    Ushi no Tsunotsuki Bullfighting (Ojiya and Yamakoshi)

    Ojiya, Niigata

    Traditional bullfighting with over 1,000 years of history, a national Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property, held in Ojiya and Yamakoshi and generally ended in a draw.

  • 小千谷豪商の館　西脇邸

    Nishiwaki Residence (Ojiya Wealthy Merchant's House)

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-3000

    Wealthy merchant's residence in Ojiya.

  • 山本山 第一調整池 第二調整池

    Yamamotoyama First and Second Regulating Reservoirs

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-2702

    Regulating reservoirs at the JR Shinano River Power Station; water generates electricity sent to Kanto for the Yamanote Line, and the second reservoir has dam views and cherry blossoms.

  • 白山運動公園

    Hakusan Sports Park

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-0662

    Sports park in Ojiya amid nature, with baseball, tennis, athletics, a 40-meter ski jump, cycling paths, and a barbecue area.

  • 木造愛染明王坐像

    Seated Wooden Statue of Aizen Myoo

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-89-2529

    Late Kamakura cypress principal image of Myokoji Temple, with a fierce three-eyed face, unveiled on the 26th of each month.

  • 新潟銘醸（株）

    Niigata Meijo Sake Brewery (Chojamori)

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-2025

    Sake brewery in Ojiya producing Chojamori, a light, dry Niigata sake with a fine, elegant body and a clean finish.

  • OJIYA SAUNA DEN

    Ojiya Sauna Den

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-84-7444

    A rural sauna in Ojiya with three barrel saunas and a cold bath of Baba Shimizu, a selected Niigata water, plus outdoor air bathing.

  • 郡殿の池

    Kooridon Pond

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A pond in Ojiya named for a legendary local governor, said never to dry and to keep nearby fields watered in drought.

  • 塩殿地区　立野原

    Tatenohara, Shiodono District

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    Just before the Uono joins the Shinano, complex channels and the Echigo Sanzan mountains fill this viewpoint.

  • 小千谷市総合産業会館サンプラザ

    Sunplaza (Ojiya City General Industry Hall)

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-4800

    Multipurpose hall in Ojiya selling local specialties and textiles, with hegi soba restaurants and a weaving experience.

  • 魚沼神社阿弥陀堂

    Amida Hall of Uonuma Shrine

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-2724

    A nationally designated Important Cultural Property, this thatched pyramidal Amida hall has very short eaves for heavy snow and is thought to date from 1558-1570.

  • みまもり岩

    Mimamori Rock

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A boulder beside Ojiya Bullfighting Arena that split in the 2004 Chuetsu earthquake, hung with a 15-meter rope by local bullfighting members.

  • 朝日山古戦場

    Asahiyama Historic Battlefield

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    Historic battlefield where Hokuetsu Boshin War fighting intensified after peace talks failed; French-style trenches and campsite remains survive near the summit.

  • 日帰り錦鯉現地見学

    Day-Trip Nishikigoi Farm Visit

    Ojiya, Niigata · 090-6700-7980

    A day visit to nishikigoi farms and outdoor ponds in Ojiya’s Echigo Nijumurago, the birthplace of colored carp.

  • 金倉山

    Mount Kanakura

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A sacred peak featured in Bakin's Hakkenden, with wildflowers and summit views of the Echigo Plain, rice terraces, and koi ponds.

  • 明石堂

    Akashido Hall

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-0077

    A city-designated zelkova hall built in 1848 to enshrine Ojiya-chijimi founder Hori Jiro Masatoshi, open to the public only on September 12.

  • 小千谷織物工房「匠之座」

    Takumi-no-Za (Ojiya Textile Workshop)

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-2329

    A second-floor shop in Ojiya's Sun Plaza selling Ojiya chijimi, Ojiya tsumugi, Katakai cotton, fabric bolts, clothing, and household goods.

  • 外之沢の棚田

    Sotonosawa Rice Terraces

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    Rice terraces in Ojiya that receive sunlight all day and overlook the Uonuma hills toward the Mikuni Mountains.

  • 朝日山

    Mount Asahi

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A mountain overlooking Ojiya, known as a Boshin War battlefield, with French-style trenches, camp remains, and soldiers' graves near the summit.

  • おぢや街中巡り

    Ojiya Town Walk

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-3512

    A town walk in Ojiya including Nishikigoi no Sato, the only nishikigoi viewing facility in Niigata Prefecture, Akashido, and hegi soba.

  • 小千谷カントリークラブ

    Ojiya Country Club

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-5111

    18-hole, par-72 golf course covering 115 hectares in the Ojiya hills, with views of the Echigo Sanzan mountains.

  • 小千谷織物工房「織之座」

    Orinoza (Ojiya Textile Workshop)

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-83-2329

    Experiential textile gallery at Sun Plaza in Ojiya where visitors can see, touch, and make original textiles guided by traditional craft artisans.

  • 西脇順三郎詩碑

    Junzaburo Nishiwaki Poetry Monument

    Ojiya, Niigata · 0258-82-2724

    Poetry monument to Junzaburo Nishiwaki, born in Ojiya in 1894, whose collections influenced modern Japanese poetry.

    An early-summer warabi-harvesting experience in Shiodono, Ojiya, on relatively flat restored farmland, where locals teach how to remove bitterness.
