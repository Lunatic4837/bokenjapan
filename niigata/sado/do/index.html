Things to do in Sado, Niigata

Sado · Niigata · 45 places

Do

  • Tub Boat Experience (Yajima Experience Exchange Center)

    たらい舟体験（矢島体験交流館）

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-86-2992

    Visitor tub-boat rides in Ogi, Sado, on boats developed from washtubs to gather seaweed and shellfish along a rocky coast.

  • Shosenkaku (Hatano Onsen)

    畑野温泉 松泉閣

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-66-4175

    Day-trip hot spring at Hatano Onsen in Sado, with a 41.8°C sodium-chloride bath and views of the Kuninaka Plain and Mount Kinpoku.

  • Shiizaki Hot Spring

    椎崎温泉

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-27-5000

    Hot spring on a hill by Lake Kamo on Sado, with views of the lake, the Osado mountains, and sunsets, linked to a crested-ibis legend.

  • Niibokatakami Hot Spring

    新穂潟上温泉

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-22-4126

    Day-use hot spring on Sado with an 800-year history, linked to a legend of a white heron healing its wounds in the water.

  • Sado Taiko Experience Exchange Hall Tatakokan

    佐渡太鼓体験交流館 たたこう館

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-86-2320

    Taiko experience hall run by the Kodo Cultural Foundation beside Kodo Village, with a large drum handmade from a 600-year-old zelkova.

  • Futatsugame Campground

    二ツ亀キャンプ場

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-26-2311

    A campsite at Futatsugame Beach in Washizaki, Sado, overlooking Futatsugame and Onogame, two-star sites in the Michelin Green Guide Japon.

  • ist-Sado

    ist-Sado（旧：佐渡関岬オートキャンプ場）

    Sado, Niigata · 070-9046-0615

    A campground on a rise 100 meters from the Seki coast, facing the Sea of Japan, above a trailhead to ancient-cedar forest.

  • Kubota Campground

    窪田キャンプ場

    Sado, Niigata

    Campsite in a pine grove facing Mano Bay, with a calm shallow beach just across the road.

  • Aikawa Hina Doll Festival (Sado)

    佐渡國相川ひなまつり

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-74-2220

    A public viewing of hina dolls and related items kept in homes, shops, and hotels at about 30 venues in Aikawa, a town that prospered from gold mining.

  • Suhama Campground

    素浜キャンプ場

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-9236

    Open-pitch campsite on Suhama Beach's 4 km sandy shore, with roadside and hilltop areas, toilets, showers, and an adjacent bungalow village.

  • Ryotsu Onsen (Ryotsu Hot Spring Area)

    両津温泉（両津温泉郷）

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-27-5000

    A hot-spring area five minutes by bus from Ryotsu Port on Sado, with inns in five districts near Lake Kamo.

  • Sado Island Galaxy Art Festival

    さどの島 銀河芸術祭

    Sado, Niigata · 050-3317-0734

    Artists share Sado’s appeal year-round except summer, with winter sea-and-snow tours and workshops on Noh and local cuisine.

  • Hemmi Sake Brewery

    逸見酒造

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-55-2046

    Sake brewery founded in the Meiji era that makes Shinryo with on-site spring water and offers brewery tours and tastings.

  • Kato Sake Brewery

    加藤酒造店

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-52-6511

    Sake brewery founded in 1915 on Sado, producing Kintsuru and junmai daiginjo Jogen no Tsuki, with free tastings.

  • Obata Sake Brewery

    尾畑酒造

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-55-3171

    Sake brewery founded in 1892 that produces Mano Tsuru and offers brewery tours and tastings.

  • Tsubakiya Pottery Gallery

    椿屋陶芸館

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-63-5555

    Sado pottery shop centered on mumyoi ware made from red clay that cannot leave the island, displaying about 1,000 pieces from 16 kilns.

  • Hokusetsu Sake Brewery

    北雪酒造

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-87-3105

    Sake brewery on Sado offering reserved tours and tastings of handmade daiginjo made from island-grown Yamada Nishiki rice.

  • Sado Koban-Making Experience (with Kirarium Sado Admission)

    佐渡小判作り体験（佐渡金銀山ガイダンス施設「きらりうむ佐渡」入場券付）

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    A Sado koban-making experience including admission to Kirarium Sado, a guidance facility with videos about the Sado gold and silver mines.

  • Hamochi Onsen

    羽茂温泉

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-88-3566

    Hot spring area in Hamochi, Sado, with one bathhouse, Kuaterume Sado, past a red torii, and a B&G gym and pool nearby.

  • Sado Koji Homemade Miso Experience

    初めてでも安心♪佐渡麹で手前味噌体験

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    Make homemade miso using koji from Tsukamoto Kojiya, a Sado maker over 100 years old, then visit the storehouse.

  • Irisaki Campground

    入崎キャンプ場

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-67-7944

    Campground beside fine-gravel, clear Irisaki Beach, with rocky areas for fishing and tide-pool play; sites have no trees.

  • Sea Kayaking at Ogi Kotoura Caves

    佐渡のきれいな海と最高のロケーションでシーカヤック体験（小木琴浦洞窟）

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    Sea kayaking on the Ogi Peninsula for beginners and children, viewing naturally formed caves in a designated marine park.

  • Sado Lake Kamo Onsen

    佐渡加茂湖温泉

    Sado, Niigata

    A hot-spring inn beside Lake Kamo on Sado, popular for skin-beautifying baths and Sea of Japan cuisine, near Ryotsu Port.

  • Hangiri Black Night Cruise (Noctiluca Course)

    はんぎりブラックナイトクルーズ 体験！海にきらめく「夜光虫」を探す旅（夜光虫コース）

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    A limited-date night tarai-bune cruise from Shukunegi on Sado to search for glowing noctiluca.

  • Sumiyoshi Hot Spring

    住吉温泉

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-27-5000

    Coastal hot spring facing Ryotsu Bay near Ryotsu Port on Sado, long known as a healing bath for injured crested ibises.

  • Hangiri Day Cruise B Course Shukunegi Tub Boat Experience

    はんぎりデイクルーズ Bコース（25分）　宿根木でたらい舟体験

    Romaji: Hangiri Day Cruise, Course B (25 Minutes), Shukunegi

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    A 25-minute cruise in a handmade cedar-and-bamboo tub boat past rocky shores, with Shukunegi’s Edo-period-style streets behind you.

  • Sado Sakiori Weaving Experience

    佐渡のお母さんたちと作ろう！佐渡で受け継がれる裂織体験

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    Weave strips of old cloth on a standing loom in Sado to make a placemat, coaster or card case in one to two hours.

  • Sado Night Enjoyment Tour (Local Dinner, Traditional Arts & Kitazawa Flotation Plant Illumination)

    佐渡満喫ナイトツアー「佐渡産食材の夕食＋伝統芸能＋北沢浮遊選鉱場ライトアップ見学」

    Sado, Niigata · 025-255-5101

    A package night tour on Sado with a local-ingredient dinner, traditional performing arts, hotel transfers, and night illumination viewing at the Kitazawa Flotation Plant.

    波面に青く光る無数の星　幻想的な佐渡のウミホタル観察会体験

    Night viewing of sea fireflies on Sado, the only place in Niigata where these nocturnal sea-floor creatures glow blue on moonless nights.

  • Night Sea Kayaking at Sawada

    Night kayaking on the gently lit shallow sea around Sawada Beach, starting near popular Ameya Pier and open to beginners and families.

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    Sado, Niigata · 0259-58-7285

    A half-day trial diving plan at Mushizaki Beach, 30 minutes by car from Ryotsu Port, with a short lesson so no permit is needed.
