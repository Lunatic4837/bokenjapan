Cafe in Sado, Niigata

Eat in Sado · 10 places

Cafe

  • Unsuke

    うんすけ

    Dining in Sado

    Serves Sadogohan, 10 seasonal dishes centered on Sado island ingredients, in a former folk house; a private plan for one group a day includes a meal and a building and storehouse tour, and vegetarian or vegan requests can be met.

  • Kominka Kukan Kyomachitei

    古民家空間　京町亭

    Dining in Sado

    A renovated old house in Aikawa serving dishes made with Sado ingredients; terrace seating is available on warm, clear days.

  • Oshokuji Dokoro Mitsuruka

    お食事処 満るか

    Dining in Sado

    Restaurant on Ryotsu Ebisu Honmachi-dori shopping street serving handmade meals made with seasonal ingredients and local sake.

  • Mereparekaiko

    メレパレカイコ

    Dining in Sado

    A couple who moved to Mushizaki, formerly a settlement of 17 people, serve menus using the island's seasonal ingredients and medicinal-cuisine knowledge.

  • Shikisai Kappo Den

    四季菜割烹　伝

    Dining in Sado

    A Japanese restaurant that uses local ingredients, serves Sado sake, and is also open for lunch on weekdays.

  • Aburiya Charakuragee

    炙り屋 ちゃらくらげぇ

    Dining in Sado

    Grilled-food restaurant named from a Sado dialect word meaning iikagen; seasonal Sado sashimi and local sake are also served.

  • Sumibiyaki Toki

    炭火焼 登貴

    Dining in Sado

    Restaurant serving charcoal-grilled seasonal local fish and steak, Sado regional dishes, original dishes, set meals, rice bowls and noodles, with local sake.

  • Shokusai Sobo Tan

    食彩創房 亶

    Dining in Sado

    Creative-cuisine restaurant using seasonal ingredients; lunch includes omurice and pasta, evening service includes meat and fish dishes, and there are over 100 cocktails.

  • Yajima Higashi-odori

    やじま 東大通店

    Dining in Sado

    Casual restaurant serving Sado-grown Koshihikari rice dishes such as kamameshi and oyakodon, noodles and fried foods, with charcoal-grilled Tsugaru jidori yakitori from the evening.

  • Yo Kitaya

    よう喜多屋

    Dining in Sado

    Old house from the gold-mining era serving a menu of seasonal wild plants and local ingredients; also sells Sado souvenirs and goods.
