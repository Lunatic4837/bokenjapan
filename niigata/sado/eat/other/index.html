Cafe in Sado, Niigata

Eat in Sado · 4 places

Cafe

  • Kato Restaurant

    かとうレストラン

    Dining in Sado

    Restaurant in central Kanai founded over 60 years ago, serving ramen, rice bowls, curry, set meals and Western dishes, including pork teki rice and homemade nuka sanma with Sado Koshihikari rice.

  • Family Restaurant Taigaa

    ファミリーレストラン たいがあ

    Dining in Sado

    Family restaurant on the Minamisen bus street serving hamburg steak, katsudon, fried rice, ramen, a pork stamina rice bowl with garlic and garlic chives, and omelette rice; a stage with a drum set can host live music.

  • Kitchen Kotoya

    キッチン ことうや

    Dining in Sado

    Meals are served in a dining room made by renovating about half of an old house; dogs have separate seating, phone reservations are accepted, and a Japanese-style room for up to 10 can be reserved.

  • Sosaku Ryori no Mise Taro

    創作料理の店 タロー

    Dining in Sado

    Creative cuisine restaurant serving tofu pizza, beer-battered karaage chicken, German-style potatoes, and mapo tofu, with shochu available.
