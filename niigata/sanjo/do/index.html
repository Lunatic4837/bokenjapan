Things to do in Sanjo, Niigata

Sanjo · Niigata · 7 places

Do

  • Yagigahana Onsen Iiyuratei

    八木ヶ鼻温泉 いい湯らてい

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-41-3011

    Day-trip hot spring overlooking Yagigahana, one of Niigata's 100 scenic spots, with open-air baths, a sauna and a restaurant.

  • Rankeiso (Echigo-Nagano Onsen)

    越後長野温泉 嵐渓荘

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-47-2211

    Single country inn in Sanjo with a sodium-chloride cold mineral spring and 42 Mbps lobby Wi-Fi.

  • CAPTAINSTAG Yagigahana Auto Campground

    CAPTAINSTAG®八木ヶ鼻オートキャンプ場

    Sanjo, Niigata · 090-7199-9547

    Auto campsite beside a river with views of Yagigahana, next to Ii Yuratei hot spring, with powered barbecue sites and two cottages.

  • Shitada Hot Springs

    しただ温泉郷

    Sanjo, Niigata

    Mountain hot-spring area in southeastern Sanjo, with a cool secluded mineral spring at Rankeiso and soft day-use baths facing the Yagigahana rock formation.

  • Yagigahana Onsen Iiyuratei

    八木ヶ鼻温泉 いい湯らてい

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-41-3011

    Day-trip onsen overlooking Yagigahana amid nature, with high- and low-temperature saunas that switch by gender on odd and even days.

  • Fukugao Sake Brewery (Ikarashigawa)

    福顔酒造（株）

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-33-0123

    Sake brewery in Sanjo that uses soft underground water from the Ikarashi River to make mellow sake.

  • Sanjo Octagonal Chopstick Making Experience (Marunao)

    今日から食卓にマイ箸！職人の町で八角箸作り体験（2膳）

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-45-7001

    Make two pairs of octagonal chopsticks from local cedar and ironwood outdoors in Sanjo, a craftspeople's town, and take them home the same day.
