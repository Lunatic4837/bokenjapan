Sights in Sanjo, Niigata

Sanjo · Niigata · 39 places

See

  • Tsubame-Sanjo Factory Festival 2026

    燕三条 工場の祭典2026

    Sanjo, Niigata · 080-1186-0484

    Open-factory event in Tsubame-Sanjo where 108 metalworking factories and companies usually closed to the public offer tours and activities.

  • KYOWA Classic Car & Life Station

    KYOWA クラシックカー＆ライフステーション

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-4440

    Retro facility in Sanjo displaying Showa-era domestic classic cars and motorcycles, with some test rides and a historical corner of period appliances and household goods.

  • Forest park campground in Sanjo

    中浦ヒメサユリ森林公園

    Romaji: Nakaura Himesayuri Forest Park

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-46-4997

    Forest park campground with 80 lawn auto campsites, five bungalows, a fishing pond, and an athletic course on 28 hectares.

  • Yagigahana Cliff

    八木ヶ鼻

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-5511

    A quartz-trachyte cliff over 200 meters high on the upper Ikarashi River, a prefectural natural monument and peregrine falcon breeding site.

  • Honjoji Temple

    法華宗総本山 本成寺

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-32-0008

    Head temple of the Hokke sect, founded in 1297 by priest Nichin, with carvings by late-Edo sculptor Ishikawa Uncho and his grave on site.

  • Sanjo Hachiman Shrine

    三条八幡宮

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-33-1278

    Shrine in Sanjo whose grounds include Kanayama Shrine for the metalwork deity and adjoin Hachiman Park, with festivals through the year.

  • Sanjo Blacksmithing Dojo

    三条鍛冶道場

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-8080

    Sanjo workshop offering courses in traditional blacksmithing, woodworking, and Buddhist altar making, including forging lessons by active blacksmiths.

  • Tsubame-Sanjo Jibasan Summer Fair 2026

    燕三条じばさんサマーフェア2026

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-32-2311

    Nine-day summer fair in Tsubame-Sanjo with maker demos, craft workshops, food stalls, and a century-old nail-clipper display.

  • Kita-Iogawa Rice Terraces

    北五百川の棚田

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-5511

    Terraced rice fields at the foot of Mount Awagatake in Sanjo, selected among Japan's 100 terraced rice fields, with mountain views and late-April dogtooth violets.

  • Shirasagi Forest Park

    しらさぎ森林公園

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-5717

    A forest park in Sanjo where Genji and Heike fireflies fly over about 20,000 Japanese irises on early summer nights.

  • Ichinokido Shinmei Shrine

    神明宮

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-33-1424

    Shrine whose grounds are illuminated by 300 lanterns from December 30 to January 3, and where Sanjo Kagura is performed on November 14.

  • Suwada Factory

    株式会社 諏訪田製作所

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-45-6111

    Century-old blade smithy where craftsmen hand-make nail clippers and bonsai shears, with factory tours available.

  • Snow Peak Headquarters Campfield

    スノーピークヘッドクォーターズキャンプフィールド

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-41-2222

    Campground beside Snow Peak headquarters in Sanjo, with free sites on natural terrain, gear rentals, and staff support.

  • Isurugi Shrine

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-5605

    Shrine in Sanjo, Niigata.

  • Hakucho no Sato Park

    白鳥の郷公苑

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-5511

    Tundra swans arrive on the Ikarashi River from early November, with up to about 400 birds visible from an observation building morning and evening.

  • Tsubame-Sanjo Wing

    燕三条Wing

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-7310

    Information and sales space in the JR Tsubame-Sanjo Station concourse, presenting local tourism and industry and selling products made in the area.

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-33-4446

    Museum showing Paleolithic and Jomon finds, old documents, festival tools and Showa-era goods; its building is a registered national tangible cultural property.

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-5511

    A 2-hectare pond in remote Yoshigahira, ringed by primeval beech forest and linked to a giant-snake legend marked by an August festival.

    Snow Peak FIELD SUITE SPA HEADQUARTERS

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-46-5650

    A nature-oriented bath, dining and lodging resort in Sanjo offering day-use bathing, with a sauna and outdoor cooling area overlooking Mt. Awagatake.

    A six-hour Honda CBX400F rental for touring from Tsubame-Sanjo along the Teradomari coast or to Yahiko Onsen.

  • Mount Awagatake

    粟ヶ岳

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-34-5511

    A 1,293 m mountain spanning Sanjo and Kamo; the summit takes about four hours and offers views toward Sado and Noto on clear days.

  • Green Sports Center Campground

    グリーンスポーツセンター

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-38-3968

    Campground on Eimeiji Mountain, also called Osaki Mountain, with over 20 dirt tent sites among greenery for campfires and tent stays.

  • Koshoji Temple

    光照寺

    Sanjo, Niigata · 0256-45-2508
