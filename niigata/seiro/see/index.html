Sights in Seiro, Niigata

Seiro · Niigata · 9 places

See

  • Ajirohama Beach

    網代浜海水浴場

    Seiro, Niigata · 0254-27-2111

    Family swimming beach in Seiro with a sandy, shallow shore, where visitors also fish from the coast or boats.

  • Seiju-en Orchard (Grape Picking)

    誠樹園

    Seiro, Niigata · 070-4109-6920

    Grape orchard in Seiro selling Kyoho, Shine Muscat and other varieties plus homegrown vegetables; reservations required.

  • Benten-gata Scenic Park

    弁天潟風致公園

    Seiro, Niigata · 0254-27-2111

    A year-round scenic park, once part of the Ninomiya family garden, with cherry blossoms, hydrangeas, lotuses and over 200 swans from late November.

  • Sakagami Orchard (Grape Picking)

    坂上ぶどう園

    Seiro, Niigata · 0254-27-3125

    Grape orchard in Seiro offering picking of over 20 varieties, grown with low-pesticide organic methods, for up to 100 people.

  • Amano Cherry-Picking Orchard

    天野さくらんぼ狩り園

    Seiro, Niigata · 090-5409-4159

    Cherry-picking orchard in Seiro, the first in the prefecture to grow cherries in greenhouses, with all-you-can-eat picking of several varieties even in the rain.

  • Ken's Grape Garden

    ケンズグレープガーデン

    Seiro, Niigata · 080-1078-4233

    A free-admission grape garden where visitors cut grapes with provided scissors and buy them by variety after weighing, with tastings offered.

  • Ninomiya Family Garden Seishoen and Rose Garden

    国登録有形文化財 二宮家庭園「静勝園」とバラ園

    Seiro, Niigata · 0254-27-2002

    The Ninomiya family garden Seishoen in Seiro, whose buildings were nationally registered in 2006, adjoins swan-visited Benten-gata and opens with the rose garden from late May to mid-June.

  • Tanaka Grape Orchard

    田中ぶどう園

    Seiro, Niigata · 090-3339-5907

    Eco-farmer-certified grape orchard in Seiro growing Benizuho and seedless Shine Muscat that can be eaten with the skin.

  • Sea Fishing at Niigata East Port 2nd East Breakwater

    防波堤で出来る海釣り～新潟東港第2東防波堤～

    Seiro, Niigata · 025-278-3061

    A breakwater fishing spot at Niigata East Port, opened in June 2011 under safety management, where various fish draw families and advanced anglers.
