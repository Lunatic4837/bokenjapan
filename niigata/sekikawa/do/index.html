Things to do in Sekikawa, Niigata

Sekikawa · Niigata · 7 places

Do

  • えちごせきかわ温泉郷

    Echigo Sekikawa Hot Spring Area

    Sekikawa, Niigata

    Hot-spring area of five springs along the Arakawa River in Sekikawa, traditionally dated to the Kamakura period and within Bandai-Asahi National Park.

  • 雲母温泉（えちごせきかわ温泉郷）

    Kirara Onsen (Echigo Sekikawa Hot Springs)

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Hot-spring area on the left bank of the Arakawa River, with views of the Iide and Asahi ranges and two inns near National Route 113.

  • 鷹の巣キャンプ場

    Takanosu Campground

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-0252

    Forest campground beside the clear Arakawa River, ringed by hinoki, zelkova, and oak, with dogtooth violets covering the slopes in spring.

  • 鷹の巣温泉（えちごせきかわ温泉郷）

    Takanosu Hot Spring (Echigo Sekikawa Hot Springs)

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Takanosu Hot Spring is one of five Echigo Sekikawa hot-spring areas in the Arakawa gorge within Bandai-Asahi National Park, with two villa inns across a suspension bridge.

  • 高瀬温泉（えちごせきかわ温泉郷）

    Takase Hot Spring (Echigo Sekikawa Hot Springs)

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Takase Hot Spring is an Echigo Sekikawa hot-spring area with six inns accommodating 365 guests.

  • 湯沢温泉（えちごせきかわ温泉郷）

    Yuzawa Onsen (Echigo Sekikawa Hot Springs)

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Hot spring in Sekikawa Village, said to have been found in the Kamakura period when a wounded bear was seen bathing.

  • 鷹の巣キャンプ場

    Takanosu Campground

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-0252

    A campsite beside the Arakawa River in hinoki, zelkova, and oak forest within Bandai-Asahi National Park, managed by Sekikawa Village.
