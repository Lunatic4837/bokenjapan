Sights in Sekikawa, Niigata

Sekikawa · Niigata · 25 places

See

  • Arakawa Gorge Momiji Line and Takanosu Suspension Bridge

    荒川峡もみじライン・鷹の巣吊り橋

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    A roughly 20 km autumn-foliage route from Sekikawa Village to Oguni in Yamagata, with scenery to enjoy in every season.

  • Kuni Shitei Juyo Bunkazai Watanabe Tei

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1002

    Sight in Sekikawa, Niigata.

  • Neko Chigura (Straw Cat Baskets)

    猫ちぐら

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-3311

    Handmade straw cat baskets from Sekikawa Village, modeled on traditional childcare baskets, with making demonstrations at the Sekikawa tourist information center.

  • Cosmos Park

    コスモスパーク

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Cosmos Park in Sekikawa, where about 200,000 cosmos bloom on 2.5 hectares from late September to mid-October, with paths for walking beside the Arakawa River.

  • Oishi Auto Camp Village

    大石オートキャンプ村

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-0252

    Auto campground at the foot of the Iide Mountains with 50 powered auto sites, birdwatching, and a wetland plant garden.

  • Old Yonezawa Highway: Echigo Three Passes (Takanosu, Enoki and Osato)

    旧米沢街道　越後三峠（鷹の巣峠・榎峠・大里峠）

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Historic passes linking Shibata and Yonezawa in Yamagata; Sekikawa’s three passes are among Japan’s 100 Historic Roads, with autumn foliage in late October and early November.

  • Arakawa-kyo Grape Orchard

    荒川峡ブドウ園

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-2199

    Grape orchard in Sekikawa Village near Takase and Takanosu hot springs, selling more than ten varieties by weight.

  • Sato Family Residence

    佐藤家住宅

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Thatched 1765 house of a former village headman, in mallet style on the old Yonezawa road; not open to the public.

  • Tokeien Historic Residence

    東桂苑

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-0252

    Built in 1905 and set back from the street, this Watanabe family branch residence offers mountain views from its tatami rooms.

  • Tōkeien Cafe

    東桂苑カフェ

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1349

    Cafe inside village-designated Tōkeien, a 1905 mansion by Michi-no-Eki Sekikawa, offering soba and other dishes on weekends.

  • Oishi Dam Lakeside Prefectural Recreation Area

    大石ダム湖畔県民休養地

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-0252

    A prefectural recreation park downstream of Oishi Dam with go-karts, mini trains, a small zoo, and autumn foliage.

  • Okura Shrine

    大蔵神社

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1490

    Shrine in Sekikawa founded before 806, dedicated to Izumo deities and keeping a biwa from the Osato Pass legend as a sacred treasure.

  • Yuzawa Kannon Park

    湯沢観音公園

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Hilltop park near Yuzawa Onsen with 33 life-size Kannon statues, historic monuments, and spring katakuri flowers.

  • Maruyama Ohashi and Maruyama Park

    丸山大橋 丸山公園

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    A reinforced-concrete fixed arch bridge linking Onodachi Park to Maruyama Park, with sunset views over flooded rice fields and a cherry-blossom park.

  • Takase Cherry Blossom Avenue

    高瀬の桜並木　（見頃：例年4月初旬）

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    About 90 cherry trees line some 600 meters from Yakushido at Takase Onsen and are lit by lanterns at night in early April.

  • Taki Shrine Spring

    滝神社の清水（たきじんじゃのしみず）

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    A cold spring at the shrine approach in central Kajikadani, surrounded by cedar forest and long enjoyed by locals and travelers.

  • Mount Koto (Nakatsuka Trailhead)

    光兎山（中束登山口）

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Nakatsuka trailhead for 966-meter Mount Koto in Sekikawa, where a rabbit-shaped snow pattern appears on the west face in spring.

  • Sekikawa Fureai Dome

    せきかわふれあいど～む

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-0252

    Indoor multi-purpose dome at Roadside Station Sekikawa for sports and events, displaying the 82.8-metre, two-tonne Daishitamon snake float.

  • Mount Tsuesashi (Oishi Trailhead)

    ※23年東俣林道通行禁止 杖差岳（大石登山口）

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    Oishi route to 1,636-meter Mount Tsuesashi, one of Japan's 200 famous mountains, with spring anemones and summer daylilies; both trails are closed after 2022 heavy rain.

  • Tōkeien Office

    東桂苑オフィス

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-0252

    Office space with dedicated Wi-Fi on the second floor of village-designated Tōkeien, open from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

  • Hozaka Mountain (Hozaka Trailhead)

    朴坂山（朴坂山登山口）

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    A 438 m beginner mountain in Sekikawa with one of Iwafune District's three first-order triangulation points and clear-day views to Sado.

  • Furusato Sekikawa Gozen (Local Set Meal)

    ふるさと関川 御膳

    Sekikawa, Niigata

    Local Sekikawa set meals are served twice a year at Meiji-era Tokeien, overlooking the garden, by advance reservation.

  • Tate-eboshi (Oishi Trailhead)

    立鳥帽子（大石登山口）

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    A 680 m beginner mountain in Sekikawa, said to be where Oishi women danced for rain, with views of the Iide range.

  • Sekikawa History and Road Museum

    せきかわ歴史とみちの館

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1288

    A museum in Sekikawa Village exhibiting village history, the old Yonezawa Kaido, and local nature, where armor can be tried on for free.

  • Arakawa River

    荒川

    Sekikawa, Niigata · 0254-64-1478

    The Arakawa River ranked first in national Class-A river water-quality inspections (2004–2006, 2008) and is a Niigata famous water.
