Things to do in Shibata, Niigata

Shibata · Niigata · 12 places

Do

  • Tsukioka Onsen

    月岡温泉

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-32-2975

    Sulfur hot-spring town in Shibata, opened in 1915, with emerald-green water, foot baths, and shops for local sake and sweets.

  • Tsukioka Onsen Direct Bus

    月岡温泉直行バス

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-26-6789

    Direct bus from JR Niigata Station and JR Shibata Station to Tsukioka Onsen for visitors arriving by train.

  • Ayame no Yu (Shibata Onsen)

    新発田温泉「あやめの湯」

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-26-1173

    Day-trip hot spring on a hill in Shibata, where the main bath overlooks the Ninoji mountain range and uses undiluted, unheated source water.

  • Hyakka no Sato (Shiroyama Onsen)

    百花の里「城山温泉」

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-21-2626

    Day-trip hot spring overlooking Shiroyama, site of a Sengoku battle between Shibata Shigeie and Uesugi Kagekatsu, with open-air baths and a sauna.

  • Kikusui Sake Culture Institute

    菊水酒造（株）

    Shibata, Niigata · 0120-23-0101

    Open to the public, this Shibata institute shows a small traditional brewery and part of 30,000 sake-related cultural materials.

  • Kenryu Gorge Campground

    剣龍峡キャンプ場

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-28-9960

    Campsite in Kenryu Gorge at the north end of Gozu Prefectural Natural Park, among rocky streams, cedars and broadleaf trees at 630 m.

  • Shinsoyu Onsen Shiun-no-Sato

    深層湯温泉「紫雲の郷」

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-41-1126

    Day-use hot spring in Shiunji Memorial Park, Shibata, with a custom outdoor bath carved from a huge stone.

  • Omon Sake Brewery / Gokaibishi Experience Resort

    王紋酒造（株）

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-22-5150

    This 1790s sake brewery produces Omon and wine-like Karen, and its 2022 resort offers tastings and projection mapping.

  • Niigata Prefectural Shiunji Memorial Park Auto Campground

    新潟県立紫雲寺記念公園オートキャンプ場

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-41-1010

    Auto campground inside Niigata Prefectural Shiunji Memorial Park beside Fujitsuka Beach, with powered sites, flush toilets, and a sanitary building.

  • Nioji Shrine Campground

    二王子神社キャンプ場

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-25-2536

    A campground on the grounds of Nioji Shrine, a nationally registered tangible cultural property, and the trailhead for Mount Nioji.

  • Kingyo Daiwa Clay Bell Painting Experience

    金魚台輪土鈴絵付け体験

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-26-6789

    Paint a one-of-a-kind Kingyo Daiwa clay bell, a traditional Shibata folk-toy charm, at Yasubei Chaya in front of Shibata Castle from April to November.

  • Beginner SUP Experience (60 Minutes)

    のんびり気ままに水上散歩！初心者歓迎のSUP体験60分

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-28-8337

    A 60-minute beginner SUP session on the sea at Fujitsuka Beach, taught by a water-rescue qualified instructor, with one Cafe Paradiso drink for Asoview bookings.
