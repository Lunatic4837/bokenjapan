Shibata · Niigata · 62 places

See

  • 共同浴場「美人の泉」

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-32-1365

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-22-3101

    Shibata Castle retains its original main gate and corner turret as Important Cultural Properties, plus a restored three-story turret chosen among Japan’s 100 Famous Castles.

  • 東赤谷連続洞門

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-28-9960

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-22-2659

    Daimyo-style stroll garden in Shibata, said to date from the Kanbun era, with three tea rooms and a former rice storehouse showing domain materials.

  • 藤塚浜海水浴場

    Shibata, Niigata · 0254-28-9960

  • 新潟地酒 premium SAKE 蔵
