Things to do in Tagami, Niigata

Tagami · Niigata · 9 places

Do

  • Yudakami Onsen

    湯田上温泉

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-2291

    Quiet countryside hot-spring area in Tagami with a drinkable sodium-chloride spring, seasonal local cuisine, and a nearby garden of about 30,000 hydrangeas.

  • Gomado Yuttarikan (Day-Use Hot Spring)

    日帰り温泉 ごまどう湯っ多里館

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6301

    Day-use hot spring in Yudakami Onsen with large, open-air, and medicinal baths on a hill with good views; one daily ticket allows unlimited entry.

  • Bamboo Shoot Festival

    たけのこまつり

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-47-0661

    Festival in Tagami selling morning-dug bamboo shoots and offering digging sessions and bamboo-shoot rice.

  • Tagami General Park YOU Yu Land Campground

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-5945

    Campground in Tagami, Niigata.

  • 47th Mount Gomado Yudagami Onsen Hydrangea Festival

    第47回護摩堂山湯田上温泉あじさいまつり

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    Early-summer hydrangea festival at the summit of Mount Gomado in Tagami, reached by a roughly 40-minute hike with views across the Echigo Plain.

  • Shigeno Tansu Shop (Factory Tour and Craft Experience)

    茂野タンス店 工場見学・小物づくり体験

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    In Tagami, visitors try traditional Kamo paulownia-chest techniques used to make durable kiri tansu.

  • 15th Yudakami Onsen Hot Spring Festival

    第15回 湯田上温泉 温泉まつり

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    The 15th Yudakami Onsen festival offers morning yoga, zazen, tea gatherings, mountain hikes, and town walks, praying for visitors' health.

  • Yudakami Onsen Sweets Project

    湯田上温泉SWEETSプロジェクト

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    Seasonal sweets project at Yudakami Onsen inns in Tagami, where original desserts use local produce such as peaches and Le Lectier pears.

  • Zazen Experience for Spiritual Healing

    心の癒し座禅体験

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-2291

    A one-hour zazen program for spiritual healing at Tōryū-ji, a Sōtō Zen temple beside Yudakami Onsen in Tagami.
