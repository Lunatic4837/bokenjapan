Sights in Tagami, Niigata

Tagami · Niigata · 10 places

See

  • たがみバンブーブー2026

    Tagami Bamboo Boo 2026

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-47-0661

    Bamboo-light art event in Tagami Town's bamboo groves, with viewing areas, a cafe, workshops, and a light parade on 10 September 2026.

  • 椿寿荘

    Chinjuso Villa

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-2040

    Detached residence of Tagami’s wealthy Tamaki family, built from 1914 over three and a half years with fine timber and temple-style architecture, plus a dry landscape garden.

  • 護摩堂山・護摩堂山登山口

    Mount Gomado (Gomado Trailhead)

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    A beginner-level 274 m mountain where about 30,000 hydrangeas bloom; the summit is about 45 minutes up a gentle path.

  • 護摩堂山あじさい園

    Gomadoyama Hydrangea Garden

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    Hydrangea garden at the summit of 274-meter Mount Gomado in Tagami, where about 30,000 plants bloom from mid-June to early July.

  • Tagami General Park YOU Yu Land

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-5945

  • 了玄寺のツナギガヤ

    Tsunagigaya at Ryogenji Temple

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-3422

    A kaya tree said to be over 700 years old, linked to a legend of Shinran, whose leaves reverse front and back each year.

  • 越後豪農の館 椿寿荘ランチ

    Tsubakijuso Lunch (Echigo Wealthy Farmer's Residence)

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-2040

    Lunch at Tsubakijuso in Tagami, a nail-free temple-style villa of Yoshino cedar and Aizu cypress beside a dry landscape garden.

  • たけのこフルコース

    Bamboo Shoot Full-Course Meal

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    Yudakami Onsen inn chefs in Tagami serve a full course of fresh local bamboo shoots, including simmered, fried, and sashimi dishes.

  • 護摩堂城跡

    Gomado Castle Ruins

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    Castle ruins where the main bailey, storehouse residence, archery ground, and dry moats remain clearly shaped, with hydrangeas from mid-June to mid-July.

  • 梅加工品・梅干し・梅酒・梅大福

    Ume Processed Products (Pickled Plums, Plum Wine and Ume Daifuku)

    Tagami, Niigata · 0256-57-6225

    Tagami ranks second in Niigata for ume output; locals process Koshi no Ume into pickled plums, plum wine, and ume daifuku.
