Things to do in Tainai, Niigata

Tainai · Niigata · 19 places

Do

  • Shio no Yu Hot Spring

    塩の湯温泉

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-45-3325

    Day-trip hot spring in Tainai serving sodium-chloride salt water from about 1,800 meters underground without added water, heating, or chlorine.

  • Tainai City Free Campground Natsui Riverside Park

    胎内市無料キャンプ場　夏井河川公園

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-43-6111

    A waterside park on the Tainai River floodplain with a lawn and space to pitch tents and tarps.

  • Shintarugabashi Onsen (Kurhaus Tainai)

    新樽ケ橋温泉「クアハウスたいない」

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-47-2660

    Day-trip hot spring beside a roadside station in Tainai, with eight baths, a 25-metre pool, a 30-metre waterslide, and a training room.

  • Tainai Claystone Doctor's House and Pottery Experience Hall

    胎内クレーストーン博士の館・胎内陶芸体験館

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-48-2011

    A museum of minerals collected in Tainai, Japan, and mines worldwide, with pottery and stonework experiences.

  • Tainai City Hina Doll Festival

    胎内市のひなまつり

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-47-2723

    Hina doll festival in Tainai displaying dolls donated by residents at Nakajo Grand Hotel and Royal Tainai Park Hotel.

  • Kurokawa Nensui Festival

    黒川燃水祭

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-47-3409

    An annual festival in Kurokawa, based on a Nihon Shoki account, in which petroleum is skimmed with a plant and offered to Omi Shrine in Shiga.

  • Tainai City Campground Matsubara Stables

    胎内市キャンプ場　牧場の一角にある松原ステーブルス

    Tainai, Niigata · 090-4920-2493

    Campsite in Tainai where guests may camp, picnic, or walk as they like, with open fires and pets allowed.

  • Tainai Resort

    胎内リゾート

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-43-6112

    Resort in Tainai centered on the Western-style Royal Tainai Park Hotel, set among mountains vividly colored in autumn.

  • Tainai City Free Campground Sekizawa Forest Park

    胎内市無料キャンプ場　関沢森林公園

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-43-6111

    Free campsite in Sekizawa Forest Park, Tainai, at the Kushigata Mountains trailhead, with a gazebo, tables, chairs, and toilets.

  • Tainai Onsen

    胎内温泉

    Tainai, Niigata

    Hot-spring area along the Tainai River where you can bathe in silky water with mountain views or use a pool and training facilities.

  • Tainai Cherry Blossom Festival

    たいない桜まつり

    Tainai, Niigata · 080-1064-4251

    Cherry-blossom festival in Tainai, begun in 2016, held on 12 April 2026 at B&G Sports Park with local food stalls and hands-on activities.

  • Tainai City Campground Nagaike Relaxation Forest Park

    胎内市キャンプ場　長池憩いの森公園

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-43-6111

    Forest and flower-field zones around Nagaike pond, intended for learning, play and rest, and for selling seasonal flowers and local vegetables.

  • Tainai Ski Resort

    胎内スキー場

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-28-1717

    Ski resort in Tainai City with 13 courses, seven lifts, night skiing, and a children's park viewed from the lodge.

  • Tainai Insect House (Butterfly Feeding Experience)

    胎内昆虫の家　チョウのエサやり体験

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-48-3300

    Visitors watch butterflies drink nectar up close and can see swallowtails lay eggs on mandarin trees in the garden.

  • Momozakihama Cultural Repository

    桃崎浜文化収蔵庫

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-43-6111

    Cultural repository in Momozakihama storing and displaying many ship votive plaques and models dedicated to Arakawa Shrine.

  • Tarugabashi Amusement Park (Animal Encounter)

    樽ケ橋遊園　動物ふれあい体験

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-47-3521

    An amusement park in Tainai with over 23 species and 100 animals, offering guinea pig and alpaca encounters on weekends and holidays.

  • Satoyama Aerial Walk (Paragliding Experience)

    ​里山を空中散歩！パラグライダー体験

    Tainai, Niigata · 090-2525-8171

    Instructors guide paragliding at Tainai Ski Resort, where you run down the slope and float about 5-20 meters.

  • Tree Climbing Experience at Tarugabashi Amusement Park

    樽ケ橋遊園でツリーイング体験

    Tainai, Niigata · 050-5372-8809

    Outdoor activity in which visitors climb trees wearing ropes and harnesses that support the body.

  • Snow Ride Park Popland (Tainai Ski Resort)

    Snow Ride Park ポプランド

    Tainai, Niigata · 0254-28-1717

    A snowmobile pulls a banana boat across the snow at Tainai Ski Resort, for riders aged three and up.
