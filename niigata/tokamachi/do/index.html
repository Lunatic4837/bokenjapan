Things to do in Tokamachi, Niigata

Tokamachi · Niigata · 59 places

Do

  • 78th Tokamachi Snow Festival

    第78回十日町雪まつり｜新潟・豪雪地で楽しむ冬の祭典

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-757-3100

    A three-day winter festival in Tokamachi with citizen-made snow sculptures, snow activities, and warm local food.

  • Matsunoyama Onsen

    日本三大薬湯・松之山温泉の観光ガイド│温泉街の歩き方

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-597-3442

    Historic hot spring in snowy Tokamachi, traditionally listed with Arima and Kusatsu among Japan's three medicinal hot springs.

  • Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale 2027

    大地の芸術祭 越後妻有アートトリエンナーレ 2027

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-761-7767

    The Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale, held once every three years, takes place in 2027 and includes tours, food, and lodging.

  • Matsudai Shibatoge Onsen Unkai

    まつだい芝峠温泉 雲海

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-597-3939

    Day-use hot spring in Tokamachi whose outdoor bath overlooks the Uonuma mountains, including Hakkaisan, Makihata and Naeba; the water is a strong sodium chloride spring.

  • Senju Hot Spring Sennen-no-Yu

    千手温泉 千年の湯

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-768-2988

    A free-flowing natural hot spring with a cypress bath, a Shinano River stone bath, outdoor baths of different sizes and temperatures, and a free foot bath.

  • Backcountry Bike Experience Tour, Echigo-Tsumari Daigonji Plateau Campground

    ▲越後妻有 大厳寺高原キャンプ場

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-596-2994

    A campsite at about 700 meters on Mount Tensui, with cottages, tents, and activities such as SUP on Fudo Pond.

  • Akashi no Yu (Echigo-Tsumari Exchange Hall)

    越後妻有交流館「明石の湯」

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-752-0117

    Day-use hot spring in Tokamachi, about a ten-minute walk from Tokamachi Station, in the same building as the MonET art museum.

  • Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale Official Tour

    越後妻有 大地の芸術祭オフィシャルツアー

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-761-7767

    Day bus tour from Echigo-Yuzawa Station of the Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale, with official guides and a seasonal vegetable lunch.

  • Echigo-Tsumari Seidayama Campground

    越後妻有 清田山キャンプ場

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 080-9415-1501

    Quiet mountain campsite in Tokamachi overlooking Mt. Myoko, Mt. Yoneyama, Mt. Kurohime and the Uonuma range, with tent sites, auto sites and cottages.

  • Taka-no-Yu (Matsunoyama Onsen Center)

    松之山温泉センター 鷹の湯

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-596-2221

    A day-use hot spring in central Matsunoyama Onsen, a saline sodium-calcium chloride spring said to be one of Japan's three medicinal baths.

  • Kiyotsukyo Hot Spring Village

    清津峡温泉郷

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-761-7261

    Hot-spring village at the foot of Kiyotsukyo Gorge, with two inns, a foot bath, a restaurant, and a souvenir shop.

  • Home of the Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale

    大地の芸術祭の里

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-761-7767

    The Echigo-Tsumari region of Tokamachi and Tsunan stages the art triennale every three years; the 2015 edition drew about 510,000 visitors.

  • Kiyotsu River Fish Festival

    清津川さかなまつり

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-763-3012

    A fish festival beside the Kiyotsu River with rainbow-trout fishing, a children's catching contest, and sales of grilled sweetfish, yamame, and char.

  • Matsunoi Sake Brewery

    （株）松乃井酒造場

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-768-2047

    Sake brewery in snow-country Tokamachi where junmai sake ages in a cave fed by spring water from a red-pine forest.

  • Echigo-Tsumari Matsunoyama Onsen Snow Park

    ▲越後妻有 松之山温泉スノーパーク（松之山温泉スキー場）

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-596-3133

    Snow park about five minutes by car from Matsunoyama Onsen, offering skiing, snowboarding, airboarding, snowbiking, and on-slope camping.

  • Mion Nakasato (Miyanakajima Hot Spring)

    宮中島温泉　ミオンなかさと

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-763-4811

    Hot spring facility with silky brown water, a large indoor bath, and an open-air bath where the Shinano River can be heard.

  • Haramachi Hot Spring Yukura Tsumari

    原町温泉　ゆくら妻有

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-763-2944

    A hot spring with source water drawn directly into the baths and an outdoor bath overlooking the clear Kiyotsu River.

  • Snow Play and Kamakura Experience

    雪遊び・かまくら体験

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-761-7433

    At Mion Nakasato in Tokamachi, enter snow kamakura huts to grill rice cakes and eat mandarins, with candle lighting at night.

  • Sasadango Making Experience (Easy Course)

    新潟の伝統食を作ろう！笹団子作り体験・お手軽コース

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-755-5080

    Make three or four sasadango, steamed mugwort dumplings wrapped in bamboo leaves, with local mothers in about one hour, then taste them fresh.

  • Nakasato Kiyotsu Ski Area

    なかさと清津スキー場

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-782-4501

    Ski area in Tokamachi whose operations are currently suspended.

  • Shichirin Pottery

    七輪陶芸

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-761-7433

    Hand-build sake cups and small plates, add color or stamped designs, and fire them the same day in a charcoal brazier.

  • Setguro Castle Ruins Campground

    節黒城跡キャンプ場

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-768-4419

    Campsite midway up the Setguro Castle ruins, with three log-style bungalows, three architect-designed cottages, a kitchen, and flush toilets, near trail artworks from Nakago Green Park.

  • Matsunoyama Onsen and Snowshoe Rice-Terrace Walk

    Tokamachi, Niigata

    Winter package in Tokamachi pairing a stay at Matsunoyama Onsen, a snowshoe walk on rice terraces, and a satoyama gastronomy lunch at MURA PUB.

  • Atema Gelande, Joetsu Kokusai Ski Resort

    上越国際スキー場 あてまゲレンデ

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-758-3291

    North-slope ski area of Joetsu Kokusai Ski Resort in Tokamachi, with powder snow, snowboarding, and lifts linking other zones.

  • Airboard at Matsunoyama Onsen Ski Area

    エアボード

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-596-3133

    Lie face-down on an inflatable board and slide the slope at Matsunoyama Onsen Ski Area, with beginner lessons offered.

  • Karamushi Stole Plant-Dyeing Experience

    からむしの布と葉を使用！ストール草木染め体験（1枚）

  • Matsudai Family Ski Area

    松代ファミリースキー場

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-597-3330

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 080-9033-9969

    Make salt from Matsunoyama Onsen’s 12-million-year-old fossil seawater, using locally thinned timber.
