Sights in Tokamachi, Niigata

Tokamachi · Niigata · 111 places

See

  • Kiyotsu Gorge (Tunnel of Light)

    清津峡

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-763-4800

    A V-shaped gorge of columnar-jointed cliffs on the Kiyotsu River, a national scenic site and natural monument, with the 2018 Tunnel of Light installation.

  • Hoshitoge Rice Terraces

    越後松代棚田群 星峠の棚田

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-597-3442

    About 200 hillside rice paddies in Tokamachi become water mirrors in spring and autumn and were certified a Connecting Rice Terrace Heritage site in 2022.

  • Bijinbayashi Beech Forest

    美人林

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-597-3442

    A hillside of roughly 100-year-old beech trees in Matsunoyama that regrew after the area was cleared for charcoal at the end of the Taisho era.

  • Atema Highlands Cosmos

    あてま高原のコスモス

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-758-4141

    Cosmos bloom across the Hanadaira field on the Atema Highlands Resort Bernatio grounds, in season from late September to mid-October.

  • Sekki Market (Chinkoro Market)

    節季市（チンコロ市）

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-757-3100

    Outdoor winter market in Tokamachi selling handmade rice-flour zodiac figures called chinkoro, which often sell out by morning.

  • Muko-nage and Sumi-nuri (Groom Throwing and Face Painting)

    むこ投げ・すみ塗り

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-597-3442

    A 15 January rite at Matsunoyama Onsen where new grooms are thrown onto a snowy slope and couples paint each other's faces with ash.

  • Tokamachi Jomon Tours

    十日町縄文ツアーズ

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-757-3345

    Guided Jomon tours in Tokamachi visit the Sasayama Ruins, where Japan's only National Treasure flame-style pottery was found, plus the museum and a one-night restaurant.

  • Nouland Kurosawa Chestnut and Hardy Kiwi Orchard

    株式会社のうランド　黒沢観光栗・さるなし園

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-752-2824

    Tourist orchard in Tokamachi where visitors pick ten kinds of chestnuts and sarunashi from about 1,200 vines.

  • Tashiro Nanatsugama (Seven Pools)

    田代の七ツ釜

    Tokamachi, Niigata

    Seven waterfall pools on the Kamagawa in Tashiro, Tokamachi, designated a national scenic site and natural monument for their unusual layered rock banks.

  • National Treasure Flame-Style Pottery

    国宝 火焔型土器

    Tokamachi, Niigata · 025-757-5531

    Flame-style pottery from about 5,000 years ago, excavated at the Sasayama Site in Tokamachi, designated a National Treasure in 1999 and shown at Tokamachi City Museum.
