Things to do in Tsubame, Niigata

Tsubame · Niigata · 15 places

Do

  • Temari no Yu

    てまりの湯

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-97-1755

    Temari no Yu is a day-use hot spring at the foot of Mount Kugami in Tsubame, with a simple sulfur cold mineral spring beside Michi-no-Eki Kugami.

  • Bunsui Oiran Procession (Tsubame Cherry Blossom Festival)

    つばめ桜まつり 分水おいらん道中

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-7630

    Three oiran performers and about 60 attendants parade along about 1,500 cherry trees planted in gratitude for the Ohkouzu Diversion.

  • Hammered Copperware Making Experience

    鎚起銅器製作体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    In Tsubame, shape copper by repeated hammering and annealing into a tumbler, sake cup, or spouted server.

  • Tin Sake Cup Making Experience

    錫ぐい呑み製作体験

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-63-7666

    At Tsubame City Industrial History Museum, shape a circular tin plate into a sake cup with a wooden mallet and molds.

  • Hammered Copperware Small Plate and Cup-Saucer Making

    鎚起銅器の小皿/カップソーサーづくり体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Craftsmen guide beginners as they hammer a round copper plate into a small dish at Tsubame City Industrial History Museum.

  • Beer Cup or Spoon Polishing Experience

    ビアカップ or スプーンの磨き体験

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-61-6701

    Polish your own beer cup with a Tsubame craftsman at Migakiya Ichibankan; children can polish spoons.

  • Knife Resharpening Experience (Currently Suspended)

    包丁研ぎ直し体験（休止中）

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-93-4195

    Try basic knife resharpening at Tojiro Open Factory in Tsubame, using knives provided by the shop for about 45 minutes.

  • Metal Engraving Experience

    彫金製作体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    In Tsubame, engrave bamboo-grass, plum, or dragonfly motifs on a copper pen case or tumbler with a chisel and hammer.

  • Tin Small Plate Making Experience

    錫(すず)の小皿作り体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Shape a small tin plate, round, square, hexagonal, or octagonal, with wooden or metal hammers at Tsubame City Industrial Materials Museum.

  • Tsuchime Hammering Experience

    鎚目（つちめ）入れ体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Add hammer marks to a pure copper cup at Tsubame City Industrial History Museum, one step in Tsubame hammered copperware making.

  • Small Vessel Making (Hammer-Mark Experience)

    「小さな器づくり」鎚目（つちめ）入れを体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Hammer marks into a prepared vessel shape and attach a handle to make a small vessel, with staff guiding each step.

  • Welding Art Experience

    溶接技術を使ったアート体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    At WAAS FACTORY in Tsubame, visitors sketch on a 10 cm stainless-steel plate and melt the design with welding, without sparks.

  • Handmade Soap Experience with Rice Oil and Rice Bran

    米油、米ぬかを配合した手作り石けん体験

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-93-0333

    Make a skin-friendly soap with rice oil and rice bran at Aromatica Lab in Tsubame, choosing colors and scents; a souvenir soap is included.

  • Tin Sake Cup Hammer-Marking Experience

    錫(すず)ぐい呑み 鎚目(つちめ)入れ体験

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Hammer a textured pattern into a spun tin sake cup or shot glass at Tsubame City Industrial Materials Museum and take it home.

  • Hammered Copperware Cylindrical Vase Making

    鎚起銅器の筒花入れ製作体験

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-63-7666

    At Tsubame Industrial History Museum, beginners make a sturdy hammered-copper vase whose copper helps keep flower water clean.
