Sights in Tsubame, Niigata

Tsubame · Niigata · 35 places

See

  • Sengando Suspension Bridge

    千眼堂吊り橋

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-77-8233

    A 124-meter red suspension bridge on Mount Kugami linking Asahiyama Observatory and Gogoan, about 35 meters above Sengando Valley, chosen among Niigata’s 50 bridges in 1993.

  • Kuni Uedera (Kokujoji)

    Tsubame, Niigata

  • Tsubame Back-Fat Ramen

    燕のソウルフード「燕背脂ラーメン」

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-7630

    Local ramen of extra-thick noodles in a mellow, fairly strong seafood-soy broth, covered with enough pork back fat to keep the soup hot.

  • Mount Kugami

    国上山

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-77-8233

    A 313-meter mountain in Tsubame whose summit overlooks the Bunsui district and Ohkouzu Diversion Channel, with a beech forest designated a prefectural natural monument.

  • Tsubame City Traffic Park

    燕市交通公園

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-4680

    A park in Tsubame with traffic signals and railroad crossings for learning road rules, large play equipment, and a 650-meter go-kart course with an overpass.

  • Gogo-an

    五合庵

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-97-3758

    Hermitage of Kokuji Temple where Ryokan lived for about 20 years from 1793, meditating, reading classics, and writing poetry.

  • Tsubame City Industrial History Museum

    燕市産業史料館

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Museum tracing Tsubame's 400-year metalworking history, with artisan works, kiseru pipes, spoons, and hands-on metalcraft workshops.

  • Omagari Riverside Park

    大曲河川公園

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Riverside park on the Nakanokuchi River’s left bank in Tsubame, where pheasants, harriers, and rare plants gather, with an Eco Clock and barbecue facilities.

  • Shinano River Ohkouzu Museum

    信濃川大河津資料館

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Museum marking a century of Shinano River works, with exhibits on the river and Ohkouzu Diversion and river views from a fourth-floor observatory.

  • Ohkouzu Diversion Cherry Blossom Avenue

    大河津分水桜並木

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-7630

    Someiyoshino cherry trees planted to honor the Ohkouzu Diversion project bloom along the river embankment in Tsubame.

  • Yoshida Fureai Plaza

    吉田ふれあい広場

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Multipurpose park in Tsubame with a dog run, playground, go-karts, barbecue area, and spring cherry blossoms.

  • Echigo Tsubame Tenjin-ko

    越後つばめの天神講

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-77-8231

    Tsubame's Tenjin-ko on February 25 honors Sugawara no Michizane with colorful sweets offered for children's growth and studies.

  • Gyokusendo

    株式会社 玉川堂

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-62-2015

    A Tsubame workshop where visitors can watch hammered-copperware making; Tamagawa Nobuo is a Living National Treasure, and the buildings are nationally registered cultural properties.

  • Titanium Spoon and Fork Oxidation Coloring

    チタン製各種スプーンやフォーク酸化発色

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-63-7666

    Visitors choose from 24 oxidation colors to make a titanium dessert spoon, fork, yogurt spoon, or coffee spoon at Tsubame City Industrial History Museum.

  • Saito Strawberry Farm

    斉藤いちご園

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-47-1519

    Strawberry farm in Tsubame growing fleshy, high-sugar Echigo-hime and selling jam, gelato, and sherbet made from them.

  • Kamameshi Popularized by Local Company Presidents

    地元の社長たちが広めた「釜めし」

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-7630

    In Showa-era Tsubame, pricey kamameshi eaten by company presidents was later popularized by restaurateurs for ordinary diners.

  • Otogo Shrine Hermitage

    乙子神社草庵（おとごじんじゃそうあん）

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-77-8233

    Rebuilt hermitage at Otogo Shrine in Tsubame, where the monk Ryokan lived for about ten years from 1816.

  • Kugami Health Forest Park

    国上健康の森公園

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-98-0770

    A park surrounded by greenery at the foot of Mount Kugami, across from Shuten-doji Shrine, with a carp-feeding stream, play equipment, and a trail to Mount Kugami.

  • Sharing Happiness Candle Making

    幸せのおすそ分けキャンドル作り

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Remake wedding-venue candles into an original candle at Tsubame Candle in Tsubame City, with weekday sessions from 500 yen.

  • Great White Wisteria of Hachioji

    八王寺の大白藤

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-7630

    A white wisteria about 300 years old stands in the grounds of Anryoji Temple in Tsubame, a prefectural natural monument since 1958.

  • Tsubame City Chozenkan Museum

    燕市長善館史料館

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Museum of the former Chozenkan private academy, with rooms on its history and teaching plus a Yonozu meteorite model and works by Yokoyama Misao.

  • Tsubame City Bunsui Ryokan Historical Museum

    燕市分水良寛史料館

    Tsubame, Niigata

    A museum presenting Zen monk Ryokan's calligraphy and related materials from his Gogo-an and Otogo Shrine hut periods.

  • Mount Kugami (Kokujō-ji Trailhead)

    国上山・国上寺脇登山口

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-7630

    A 313-metre mountain in Tsubame linked to the monk Ryokan, with beginner trails and summit views of the Sea of Japan, Sado, and the Echigo Plain.

  • Tsubame City Bunsui Visitor Service Center

    燕市分水ビジターサービスセンター

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Visitor center and base for climbing Mt. Kugami, with exhibits of Ryokan’s calligraphy and seasonal photos, plus a free rest area and café.

  • Tojiro Knife Gallery and Open Factory

    藤次郎ナイフギャラリー/オープンファクトリー

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-93-4195

    Tojiro’s knife gallery and open factory in Tsubame, where visitors can tour production, join workshops, and buy kitchen knives.

  • Tsubame–Yahiko Sightseeing Taxi Tour (Ryokan Course)

    燕・弥彦を観光タクシーでめぐる旅[良寛巡りコース]

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-38-2021

    A day taxi tour of Tsubame and Yahiko visiting sites linked to Ryokan, a late-Edo Zen monk, poet, and calligrapher.

  • Yonozu Meteorite Fall Site Monument

    米納津隕石落下地記念碑

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-63-7002

    Monument marking the 1837 fall of the Yonozu meteorite, a 31.65 kg specimen ranked third by weight among single meteorites in Japan.

  • Yugure Hill

    夕ぐれの岡

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-77-8233

    A hill on the east bank of the Ohkouzu Diversion Channel where the monk Ryokan is said to have stopped on his alms rounds and composed a poem.

  • Tsubame Ichibi (Street Markets)

    つばめのいちび

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-77-8231

    Periodic open-air markets in Tsubame, Yoshida, and Bunsui, held on fixed calendar days and selling vegetables, fish, and prepared foods.

  • One-of-a-Kind Brass Ruler Making

    世界にひとつの真ちゅう製定規作り

    Tsubame, Niigata

    In Tsubame, press scales into a brass ruler and stamp letters under a mold craftsman’s guidance to make a one-of-a-kind piece.

  • Brass Accessory and Teaspoon Making

    「真鍮アクセサリー」「ティースプーン」づくり

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Make a brass accessory or teaspoon at Factory Front in Tsubame, a session on the city's hammered-copperware and cutlery crafts.

  • Local sight in Tsubame

    写経会 写仏会

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Sutra-copying and Buddha-drawing sessions at Kokujō-ji Temple in Tsubame, where visitors write the Heart Sutra.

  • Tsubame and Yahiko Sightseeing Taxi (Highlights Course)

    燕・弥彦を観光タクシーでめぐる旅[いいとこ巡りコース]

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-38-2021

    A one-day taxi course around Tsubame, Yahiko Village, and Teradomari for day-trippers and guests near Tsubame-Sanjo, Yahiko Onsen, or Iwamuro Onsen.

  • Mt. Kugami Ryokan Historic Sites Volunteer Guide

    国上山良寛史跡ボランティアガイド

    Tsubame, Niigata · 0256-64-7630

    Volunteer guides lead visitors around Ryokan sites on Mt. Kugami, including Kokujo-ji, Gogo-an, and Shuten-doji's mirror well.

  • Tsubame City Bunsui Ryokan Museum

    燕市分水良寛史料館

    Tsubame, Niigata

    Museum about Zen monk Ryokan, showing calligraphy from his hermitage years and spring and autumn exhibits of Kera and Abe family works.
