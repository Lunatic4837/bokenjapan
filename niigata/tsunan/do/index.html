Things to do in Tsunan, Niigata

Tsunan · Niigata · 5 places

Do

  • 大地の芸術祭 越後妻有アートトリエンナーレ 2027

    Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale 2027

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-761-7767

    The Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale, held once every three years, takes place in 2027 and includes tours, food, and lodging.

  • 無印良品津南キャンプ場

    MUJI Tsunan Campground

    Tsunan, Niigata · 03-5950-3660

    A MUJI-managed campground next to 903-meter Mount Yamabushi, surrounded by beech, mizunara oak, and birch, with grass sites, three cottages, a bait-fishing pond, and a dog run.

  • ニュー・グリーンピア津南 キャンプ場

    New Greenpia Tsunan Campground

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-4611

    A highland campground in Tsunan with cottages, bungalows, lawn tent sites, a hot spring, and more than 16 outdoor activities.

  • 苗場酒造 株式会社

    Naeba Sake Brewery (Naebasan)

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-2011

    A sake brewery in Tsunan operating for over 100 years, offering paid tastings and brewery tours.

  • 津南町 農と縄文の体験実習館「なじょもん」

    Najomon (Tsunan Agriculture and Jomon Experience Hall)

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5511

    Experience hall in Tsunan for Jomon crafts and seasonal farming, with exhibits, reconstructed pit dwellings, and beech-forest walks.
