Sights in Tsunan, Niigata

Tsunan · Niigata · 28 places

See

  • Tsunan Lantern Night Sky Light Show

    津南ランタンと夜空を彩る光の共演

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-4611

    Winter lantern-launch experience in snowy Tsunan Town, where wish lanterns rise into the night sky, with fireworks on selected dates.

  • Ryugakubo Pond

    龍ヶ窪の池

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A 1.2-hectare spring pond renewed once daily by about 43,000 tons of flow, designated one of Japan's 100 Famous Waters in 1985.

  • Tsunan Sunflower Plaza 2026

    津南ひまわり広場2026

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A 4-hectare field in Tsunan with about 500,000 sunflowers, staggered bloom sections, a viewing platform, and a sunflower maze.

  • Akiyamago

    秘境秋山郷

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    Villages along the upper Nakatsu River gorge spanning Niigata and Nagano, retaining older ways amid steep mountains and deep snow.

  • Mount Naeba (Koakazawa and Oakazawa Trailheads)

    苗場山・小赤沢登山口

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A 2,145 m peak reached in about 3 hours 30 minutes from Koakazawa Trailhead or 6 hours 10 minutes from Oakazawa Trailhead, with no mountain hut.

  • Dankyu Cherry Blossoms

    段丘桜

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    More than 200 individually owned cherry trees line the river terraces in Tsunan, with views of distant mountains still holding snow.

  • Kurhaus Tsunan

    クアハウス津南

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-3711

    Swimwear hot-spring facility in Tsunan with a heated pool, ten bath types, a gym, and climbing, plus an indoor bath added in 2012.

  • Mitama Fudo Temple

    見玉不動尊

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-3886

    Temple in Mitama, Tsunan, enshrining Fudo Myoo and credited with benefits for eye ailments, where spring water forms waterfalls along the stone steps.

  • Machinaka Open Space Dandan

    まちなかオープンスペースだんだん

    Tsunan, Niigata · 080-3534-1296

    A community exchange space in Tsunan, created with residents to revitalize the shopping street, which won the 2023 Good Design Award and Wood Design Award.

  • 13th Niigata Prefecture Doburoku Fair

    第13回 新潟県どぶろく博覧会

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-4611

    A one-day gathering in Tsunan where visitors taste Niigata doburoku, talk with producers, and enjoy meals paired with the drink.

  • Snow Walk 2027: Views Shaped by the Land and People

    雪散歩2027 ～大地と人が築いた眺め～

    Tsunan, Niigata

    Snowshoe walk in Tsunan Town to river-terrace viewpoints, meeting at Tsunan Station or the town office.

  • Tsunan Snow-Buried Carrot Digging Contest

    津南の雪下にんじん掘り競争

    Tsunan, Niigata

    A timed contest in Tsunan Town to dig up as many snow-buried carrots as possible, a local specialty sweetened by storage under snow.

  • Tsunan Mitama Park

    津南見玉公園

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A park in Okubo, Mitama, overlooking Ishiotoshi columnar jointing cliffs across the Nakatsu River in Naeba Sanroku Geopark.

  • Tsunan Brewery (Kiri no To)

    津南醸造（株）

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5252

    Sake brewery in Tsunan Town making Kiri no To from Gohyakumangoku rice and ultra-soft Naeba snowmelt water.

  • Mikura Bridge

    見倉橋

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A wooden-deck suspension bridge over the clear Nakatsu River in Tsunan, with views of rock walls, forest, and autumn foliage.

  • Echigo Sorin Shrine Candle Night 2026 (Dream of the Short Night, 2nd Edition)

    越後双輪神社 candle night2026〜短夜の夢 2nd Edition〜

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-2823

    An outdoor candle night at Echigo Sorin Shrine in Tsunan on the short summer-solstice night, with food trucks.

  • Riverside Tsunan

    リバーサイド津南

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-4733

    Station complex at JR Tsunan Station with a shop, light cafeteria, rest area, and hot-spring baths overlooking the trains.

  • Mikura Katakuri Colony

    見倉

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A long-hidden katakuri colony in Mikura, Akiyamago, with trekking paths, giant horse-chestnut trees, and a bloom delayed by late snowmelt.

  • Tsunan Town Archaeological Center (Umorea)

    津南町埋蔵文化財センター“うもれあ”

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-755-7011

    Archaeological center in a former school in Tsunan, showing a reconstructed Okinohara pit dwelling, flame-shaped pottery, and finds from about 30,000 years of local history.

  • River Observatory

    川の展望台

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-1600

    A viewpoint at about 450 meters overlooks the meandering Shinano River, with the Nakatsu River and river terraces beyond, from a deck jutting over the cliff.

  • Tsunan Town Forest Therapy Base

    津南町森林セラピー基地

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-3115

    Forest therapy base in Tsunan with 300 hectares of beech forest that withstands heavy snow, between Mountain Park Tsunan and a MUJI campsite.

  • Nakago Cherry Blossoms

    中子の桜

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    Cherry blossoms in higher-elevation Nakago, seen with remaining snow and reflected on a lake except in low-snow years.

  • Jabuchi Falls

    蛇淵の滝

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A waterfall in remote Akiyamago straddling the border with Sakae Village in Nagano, seen from an observation deck among autumn foliage.

  • Sky Lookout

    空の展望台

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-1600

    A lookout about 700 meters above sea level in Tsunan, overlooking Shinano River terraces and, on clear days, Mount Naeba and the Echigo range.

  • Akeyama (Akiyamago Oakazawa Elementary School)

    アケヤマ －秋山郷立大赤沢小学校－

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-761-7767

    Former elementary school in Akiyamago, reopened in 2024 as a project to explore and share the region’s mountain living skills.

  • Shimofunato Honmura Housing Complex

    下船渡本村団地

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    Genji firefly habitat in the Shimofunato Honmura settlement of Tsunan, active from mid-June to early July.

  • Yamagen Woodcraft

    山源木工

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-767-2091

    Woodcraft workshop in Akiyamago making one-of-a-kind solid natural-wood pieces, including custom orders, since 1921.

  • Mikura Giant Horse Chestnut

    見倉の大栃

    Tsunan, Niigata · 025-765-5585

    A giant horse chestnut in Mikura, Tsunan, 25 meters tall with an 8.5-meter trunk and an estimated age of 500–800 years, listed among Japan’s 100 forest giants.
