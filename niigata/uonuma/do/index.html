Things to do in Uonuma, Niigata

Uonuma · Niigata · 19 places

Do

  • Uonuma Shibazakura Festival

    魚沼芝桜まつり

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-780-4560

    About 200,000 moss phlox usually peak in mid-May at Flower, Greenery and Snow Village Park, overlooking Uonuma city and the Echigo Sanzan.

  • Oyu Onsen

    大湯温泉

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 1,300-year-old hot spring in Uonuma, with purple katakuri colonies in spring and ski areas nearby in winter.

  • Tochiomata Onsen

    栃尾又温泉

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Hot-spring area in Uonuma called the child-blessing waters, a simple radium spring said to boost metabolism and aid calm, pain relief, and sleep.

  • Komami View Bath (Aoshima Onsen)

    青島温泉　見晴らしの湯こまみ

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-8001

    Day-use hot spring in Uonuma with a view through the steam and two spring types, beside Koide Park and the winter Koide Ski Area.

  • Ginzandaira Campground

    銀山平キャンプ場

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-2448

    Campground below Mt. Arasawa with riverside play, Lake Okutadami fishing, a hike to year-round snow, and a day-use hot spring.

  • Juwa Onsen

    寿和温泉

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-796-3033

    A quiet mountain hot spring in Uonuma, renewed in 2023 into a compact barrier-free facility with an indoor bath and sauna.

  • Yakushi Onsen Yu-Park Yakushi

    薬師温泉 ゆ～パーク薬師

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-5554

    Day-use hot spring with weakly alkaline Yunotani Yakushi Onsen baths at 37.4°C, handmade soba from slope-grown local flour, and a corner with over 2,000 comics.

  • Oritate Onsen

    折立温泉

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Hot spring at the gateway to the Okutadami Silver Line, known for homestyle hospitality and abundant hot water.

  • Okutadami Lake Sightseeing Boat Children's Captain Experience

    奥只見湖遊覧船こども船長体験

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-2750

    On the Okutadami Lake sightseeing boat, elementary-school children and younger learn steering in the wheelhouse and receive a children's maritime certificate.

  • Ginzandaira Onsen

    銀山平温泉

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Hot-spring area in Uonuma among peaks such as Mt. Echigo-Komagatake, with spring skiing, fishing on Lake Okutadami, hiking, camping, and morning waterfall clouds at Shiori Pass.

  • Suhara Ski Area

    須原スキー場

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-797-2059

    Ski area in Uonuma with ten courses, a 1.5 km run, night skiing and summit views of the Echigo Sanzan.

  • Okutadami Maruyama Ski Resort

    奥只見丸山スキー場

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-2750

    A ski resort with mountain and Okutadami Dam Lake views from a 1,242 m summit and a 4 km downhill run.

  • Koide Ski Resort

    小出スキー場

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-5320

    Family-oriented ski area in Uonuma with views of the Echigo Sanzan, seven courses, night skiing, and an adjacent hot spring.

  • Hajimete no Yuki Asobi Experience in Uonuma | Yuki Iku Monita Tour

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Experience tour in Uonuma, Niigata.

  • Yakushi Ski Area

    薬師スキー場

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-5554

    North-facing ski area in Uonuma popular with families, with beginner-to-advanced slopes and views along the Uono and Aburuma rivers.

  • Banana Boat at Yakushi Ski Area

    バナナボート

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-5554

    Snowmobile-towed banana boat for up to six people at Yakushi Ski Area, held on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays.

  • Uonuma Snowshoe Experience

    豪雪地『魚沼』で体感！静寂の世界を行くスノーシュー体験

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A snowshoe walk through the quiet, snow-covered landscape of Uonuma, a heavy-snowfall area in Niigata.

  • Snow-Viewing Walk, Teatime and Snow Tent Sauna Experience

    雪見散歩・ティータイム＆雪中テントサウナ体験

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-796-2331

    Snowshoe through fresh powder at Asakusa Sanso in Uonuma, then have hot sandwiches by a fire and a tent sauna with snow dives.

  • Lake Okutadami SUP Experience

    奥只見湖SUP体験

    Uonuma, Niigata

    Stand-up paddleboarding on Lake Okutadami at Ginzan-daira, where clear snowmelt from 2,000-meter mountains flows into the lake.
