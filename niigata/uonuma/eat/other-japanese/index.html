Eat in Uonuma · 10 places

Cafe

  • ORANGE Kitchen

    オレンジキッチン

    Dining in Uonuma

    Kitchen car from Irihirose serving dondon-yaki (Kansai-style okonomiyaki) with a choice of toppings; it travels to events in Niigata Prefecture and the menu changes with events.

  • Ichibaya

    いちば屋

    Dining in Uonuma

    Onigiri shop next to Koide Fish Market serving rice balls, seafood soup, pork soup, fried Kogane mochi and Tochio aburaage; 15 eat-in seats, usually open 10:00-15:00, closed irregularly.

  • Shunshokuya Koshu

    旬食や 香秀

    Dining in Uonuma

    Kappo restaurant serving seasonal dishes, with kaiseki courses by reservation; open 17:00-23:00, closed Mondays.

  • Miyuki no Sato

    深雪の里

    Dining in Uonuma

    Restaurant serving local cuisine with Uonuma products, including Koshihikari rice and kenchin-jiru, plus noodles; open 11:00-14:00 (varies by season and day), closed irregularly.

  • Marukichi Shokudo

    丸吉食堂

    Dining in Uonuma

    Diner in the Oritate Onsen area serving home-grown Koshihikari rice; open 17:00-23:00 (last order 22:30), closed Wednesdays.

  • Cafeteria Matsuzakaya

    松坂屋

    Dining in Uonuma

    Traditional cafeteria at Oyu Onsen serving rice bowls and ramen; open 11:00-20:00, closed Thursdays.

  • Bento Shop Omomo

    和洋酒・惣菜・弁当専門 おおもも

    Dining in Uonuma

    Shop for liquor, prepared foods and bento with 15-20 seats, offering homemade boxed meals and local dishes; open 7:00-19:00 (side dishes 11:00-19:00), closed Tuesdays except holidays.

  • Ginzan Chaya

    銀山茶屋

    Dining in Uonuma

    Serves seasonal vegetables, mountain vegetables, mushrooms and prefecture-produced fresh fish with alcohol; open 11:30-13:30 and 17:00-22:30, closed Sundays and public holidays.

  • Umenoya

    梅野屋

    Dining in Uonuma

    Restaurant serving meals for banquets, celebrations, memorial services and reunions, plus boxed meals; open 11:00-22:00 by reservation, irregular closures, 150 seats and private rooms.

  • Nihon Ryori Inaho

    日本料理 いな穂

    Dining in Uonuma

    Japanese restaurant serving kaiseki, sushi and soba made with 100% buckwheat flour; open 11:00-13:30 and 17:00-21:00, closed every Monday.
