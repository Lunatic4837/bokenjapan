Sights in Uonuma, Niigata

Uonuma · Niigata · 58 places

See

  • Uehara Cosmos Garden

    上原コスモス園

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A highland cosmos garden in Uonuma where flowers cover the plateau, overlooking Doko Highland fields and distant Mount Gongendo and the Echigo Sanzan.

  • Shiori Pass

    枝折峠（しおりとうげ）

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A pass along National Route 352 in Uonuma, where early-morning mist from Oku-Tadami and Ginzan-daira flows over the ridgeline as waterfall clouds.

  • Okutadami Lake

    奥只見湖

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-2750

    Man-made lake in Uonuma, also called Ginzan Lake, with about 600 million tonnes of storage behind the 157-metre Okutadami Dam and sightseeing cruises.

  • Saifuku-ji Temple (Founder's Hall)

    西福寺（開山堂）

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-3032

    Soto Zen temple in Uonuma founded in 1534; its founder's hall holds Ishikawa Uncho carvings designated as prefectural cultural properties.

  • Okutadami Lake Sightseeing Cruise

    奥只見湖遊覧船

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-2750

    Sightseeing cruises on Okutadami Lake, a reservoir storing over 600 million tons, including the 300-passenger paddle steamer Fantasia.

  • Shiori Pass Trailhead (Mount Echigo-Komagatake)

    越後駒ヶ岳・枝折峠登山口

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Trailhead for Mount Echigo-Komagatake, a 2,002.7 m peak among Japan's 100 Famous Mountains, with popular flower fields near the summit.

  • Takigumo Market in Ginzan-daira

    滝雲マルシェin銀山平

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-2611

    A market in Ginzan-daira with four to six food trucks and craft workshops, beside Hakugin no Yu, which offers morning baths on market days.

  • Rokujurigoe Pass

    六十里越峠

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A mountain pass on National Route 252 linking Niigata and Fukushima, where drivers see spring greenery, mountain cherries, autumn foliage, and the parallel JR Tadami Line.

  • Ginzan-daira

    銀山平

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Highland area popular for climbing Mount Echigo-Komagatake and fishing at Okutadami Lake, with spring skiing, autumn foliage, beech walks, and hot springs.

  • Eirinji Temple

    永林寺

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-794-2266

    Eirinji Temple is a Soto Zen temple founded over 500 years ago that holds over 100 Ishikawa Uncho carvings and served Matsudaira lords.

  • Echigo Yukikura-kan (Snow Sake Storehouse)

    越後ゆきくら館

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-797-2777

    Sake storehouse in Uonuma that stores sake using snow, with free guided tours and tastings.

  • Meguro Residence

    目黒邸

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-797-3220

    Nationally designated Important Cultural Property built in 1797 as a wealthy farmer's house and village official residence, retaining storehouses and an Edo-period garden.

  • Uono River

    魚野川

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-0261

    River in Uonuma with views of the Echigo Sanzan mountains, summer sweetfish fishing, and autumn salmon runs.

  • Mount Arasawa (Ginzandaira Trailhead)

    荒沢岳・銀山平登山口

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 1,969 m peak and one of Japan's 200 famous mountains, with chain and ladder sections suited to experienced climbers.

  • Koide Park

    小出公園

    Uonuma, Niigata

    Hillside park in Uonuma covering 10 hectares, with seasonal flowers, paths to the summit, views of Echigo Sanzan, and a dog run opened in 2026.

  • Uonuma Jozo

    魚沼醸造

    Uonuma, Niigata · 0120-060-070

    Fermentation factory in Uonuma producing rice koji and amazake, with reservation tours and an on-site cafe.

  • Enpuku-ji Temple

    円福寺

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-0871

    Ancient temple in Uonuma founded in the Nara period, preserving early Kamakura Buddhist statues and a Kyoto-style garden from 1704.

  • Snow Cherry Blossoms at Fukuyama Pass

    福山峠の雪上桜

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    About 100 cherry trees, including Somei-Yoshino and Oyama-zakura, bloom on remaining snow at Fukuyama Pass in Uonuma.

  • Aburuma River Valley

    破間川渓谷（あぶるまがわけいこく）

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A hidden autumn-foliage valley near the Uonuma–Fukushima border, with primeval beech forest, maples, and wheelchair-accessible boardwalks by the Asakusayama Foothills Eco-Museum.

  • Fukuyama Pass Hometown Plaza

    福山峠のふるさと広場

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-797-2366

    Outdoor experience site at Fukuyama Pass for camping, charcoal making, soba making, and fishing, next to a snow-storage cellar.

  • Hibiki-no-Mori Park (Okutadami Recreation Urban Park)

    奥只見レクリエーション都市公園 響きの森公園

    Uonuma, Niigata

    A 28.7-hectare park in Uonuma with the concept of a snow-country music forest, including a snow colosseum, culture hall, and visitor center.

  • Sato House

    佐藤家

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-5015

    A sturdy farmhouse in Uonuma, built in 1738, typical of snow-country houses.

  • Gongendo Mountain (Nagamatsu Trailhead)

    権現堂山・長松登山口

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 998-meter beginner hike in Uonuma, about 3 hours 10 minutes to the summit, with June rhododendrons and azaleas and autumn foliage.

  • Aburumagawa Dam Snow Flow

    破間川ダムの雪流れ

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    In spring, meltwater raises Aburumagawa Dam faster than the snow melts, so snow masses float on the surface like drift ice.

  • Uono River Cherry Blossom Embankment

    魚野川桜づつみ

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    About 230 cherry trees line roughly 2 km along the Uono River, a Niigata health walking route with views of the Echigo Sanzan.

  • Hiragatake (Takanosu Trailhead)

    平ヶ岳・鷹ノ巣登山口

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Trailhead for 2,140 m Hiragatake in the Yunotani district of Uonuma; an advanced July–November hike of about 6.5 hours to summit wetlands and egg-shaped stones.

  • Mount Asakusa (Mujinazawa Trailhead)

    浅草岳・ムジナ沢登山口

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 1,586 m peak in Echigo Sanzan-Tadami Quasi-National Park and one of Japan's 300 famous mountains, where rare himesayuri lilies bloom near the summit around July.

  • Togakushi Shrine

    戸隠神社

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Togakushi Shrine is a quiet shrine among dense cedars, said to date from 1583 and revered by farmers as a harvest deity.

  • Machi-no-Eki Uonuma (Local Specialties Shop)

    まちの駅魚沼

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-5006

    Uonuma community shop opened in May 2024, selling local specialties and serving rice-flour chiffon cake and strawberry milk at Nou Cafe Lily.

  • Miya Shuji Memorial Museum

    宮柊二記念館

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-794-3800

    Memorial museum in Uonuma for tanka poet Miya Shuji, displaying his belongings and about 300 materials related to his teacher Kitahara Hakushu.

  • Echigo Herb Garden Irihirose

    越後ハーブ香園入広瀬

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-796-2120

    Campsite in Uonuma with auto sites, herb, lavender, rose and rock gardens, a grass soccer field, tennis courts and barbecue space.

  • Uonuma City Natural Science Museum (House of Stars)

    魚沼市自然科学館 星の家

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-797-2360

    A natural science museum at 555 meters elevation with a 400mm reflecting telescope dome, hosting stargazing sessions from May to October.

  • Mount Sumon

    守門岳

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 1,538-meter peak in Uonuma within Echigo Sanzan-Tadami Quasi-National Park, counted among Japan’s 200 Famous Mountains.

  • Aoshima Education Shrine

    青島教育神社

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-2456

    Shrine to Sugawara no Michizane in Aoshima, built in 1993 for children's growth and academic success, with a large zelkova associated with Nitta Yoshisada.

  • Hirose Yana (Fish Trap)

    広瀬ヤナ

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-799-2093

    A seasonal fish trap on the clear Aburuma River, a fish-rich tributary of the Uono River, where visitors can eat sweetfish dishes.

  • Zazenso Colony

    ザゼンソウ群生地

    Romaji: Zazenso Colony

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-794-6073

    Zazenso colony in Uonuma, an aroid named for resembling meditating Daruma, with thousands of plants and mizubasho also visible.

  • Hayatsu Gallery

    早津ギャラリー

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-1451

    Gallery in Uonuma of artist Hayatsu, who paints rarely seen snow-country folk houses, with about 20 oil and ink works always displayed.

  • Oda Bracken Village (Mountain Vegetable Picking)

    小田わらびの里

    Uonuma, Niigata · 090-2881-0835

    Visitors pick mountain vegetables in snowy Uonuma, including on flat ground for adults and children, and guides explain how to eat them.

  • Okutadami Electric Power Museum

    奥只見電力館

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-795-2059

    A museum for learning about Okutadami Dam, power generation, nature, and energy, with LED displays, Otori Dam trick art, and an AR dam app.

  • Yunotani Handmade Village

    ゆのたに手づくり村

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-793-1000

    Local-products shop and restaurant in Uonuma selling wild-vegetable foods, rice mochi, tofu, and sweets.

  • Sari River

    佐梨川

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-0261

    Clear stream in Uonuma flowing into the Uono River, visited by anglers for char and cherry salmon.

  • Koide Park Katakuri Colony

    小出公園カタクリ群生地

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Katakuri grow in colonies on Koide Park’s slope and near the third lift, with cherry blossoms and views of Echigo Sanzan and Uonuma.

  • Autumn Cosmos Walk

    秋のコスモスウォーク

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-797-3566

    Autumn day-trip walk to a cosmos garden, with new Koshihikari onigiri, a sweets stop, and a bath at Kamiyu Onsen.

  • Oze Pass (Transit Ticket)

    尾瀬パス～トランジットチケット～

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Combined ticket from Uonuma that prepays the lake cruise and shared bus to Oze, a wetland designated a special natural monument and quasi-national park.

  • Nunobiki Falls

    布引の滝

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 30-meter waterfall reached in 30 minutes on foot from the Ohara trailhead of Mount Sumon.

  • Negoya Flowers, Greenery and Snow Village

    奥只見レクリエーション都市公園　根小屋（ねごや）花と緑と雪の里

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-780-4560

    Park area in Uonuma where 10,000 moss phlox bloom in spring and fireflies live by an entrance stream, with a free mid-June viewing event.

  • Oze National Park

    尾瀬国立公園

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Designated in 2007, it includes Ozegahara, a 1,400 m high moor about 6 km by 3 km with skunk cabbage, daylilies, and other alpine plants.

  • Uonuma City Historical Museum

    魚沼市歴史資料館

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-793-7782

    History museum in Uonuma with a permanent display of about 550 artifacts covering local history from the Paleolithic to the present.

  • Oritate Shaka Hall Wart Spring

    折立釈迦堂の疣水（しゃかどうのいぼみず）

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Clear spring at a Shaka hall overlooking Route 352 and Mt. Echigo-Komagatake, linked to a legend of curing warts; currently not flowing.

  • Nakaarasawa Perennial Snow

    万年雪

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Perennial snow at Nakaarasawa appears about 40 minutes from the trailhead; Honjo Falls may be visible in late August as the snow melts.

  • Fixed-Fare Sightseeing Taxi: Niigata–Uonuma Tour (from Urasa Station)

    定額観光タクシー 新潟･魚沼観光めぐり（浦佐駅発）

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Reserved fixed-fare taxi from Urasa Station visiting temples that display works by Ishikawa Uncho on two- or three-hour courses.

  • Natural Maple Sap Collection Snowshoe Tour

    春の息吹のおすそわけ　天然のメイプルサップ採取スノーシューツアー

    Uonuma, Niigata · 070-4415-0303

    Snowshoe at Echigo Herb Garden to collect sap from wild itaya maples and taste it as herb tea.

  • Mount Onigamen

    鬼が面山

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 1,465 m peak for advanced hikers, about 4 hours 30 minutes from the Rokujurigoe trailhead between June and November, with no mountain hut.

  • Autumn Gourmet Onigiri Walk

    秋のごちそうおにぎりウォーク

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-797-3566

    A walk to Echigo Herb Garden Irihirose for views and onigiri, with herb tea, a Juwa Onsen bath, and a Tadami Line ride.

  • Sanjo Falls

    三条の滝

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    A 73-meter waterfall on the Niigata-Fukushima border along the Tadami River, selected among Japan's 100 waterfalls, with a viewing platform.

  • Echigo Herb Kōen Irihirose Aroma Bath Bomb Making (1 Piece)

    お好きな香りでリフレッシュ！入浴剤作り（1個）

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-796-2120

    Make one original aroma bath bomb with your choice of essential oil in about 30 minutes and take it home the same day.

  • Echigo Herb Garden Irihirose (Original Soap Making)

    好きな香りと色を自分でチョイス！オリジナル石けん作り

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-796-2120

    Make a gentle handmade soap by choosing a scent from various essential oils, for hands and face, and take it home the same day.

  • Hiraname Falls

    平滑の滝

    Uonuma, Niigata · 025-792-7300

    Water flows over a single granite slab about 500 meters long, viewed from the hiking trail, and it is paired with Sanjo Falls on the Oze route.
