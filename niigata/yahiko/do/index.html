Things to do in Yahiko, Niigata

Yahiko · Niigata · 5 places

Do

  • 弥彦菊まつり

    Yahiko Chrysanthemum Festival

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Autumn chrysanthemum exhibition at Yahiko Shrine, with judged blooms and a themed flower bed of 30,000 small chrysanthemums.

  • やひこ桜井郷温泉 さくらの湯

    Sakura no Yu (Yahiko Sakurai-go Onsen)

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-1126

    Day-visit hot spring at the foot of Mount Yahiko, with a free-flowing outdoor bath and a bedrock bath.

  • 弥彦湯神社温泉（弥彦温泉郷）

    Yahiko Onsen

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Alkaline simple hot-spring village in the temple town of Yahiko Shrine, known for skin-beautifying waters and seasonal views.

  • 弥彦酒造（株）

    Yahiko Sake Brewery (Yahiko Kiwami)

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3100

    Sake brewery in Yahiko Village, founded in 1838, that hand-brews small winter batches using only Mount Yahiko underground water.

  • 弥彦温泉 ホタル観賞

    Yahiko Onsen Firefly Viewing

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Fireflies around Yahiko Onsen are viewed mainly in residential areas, with no special tourist setup; visitors should mind neighbors and traffic.
