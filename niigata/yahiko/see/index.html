Sights in Yahiko, Niigata

Yahiko · Niigata · 34 places

See

  • Yahiko Shrine

    越後一宮　彌彦神社

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-2001

    Niigata’s representative shrine, long revered as Echigo Ichinomiya, known for a rare two-bow, four-clap rite and matchmaking blessings.

  • Yahiko Park

    弥彦公園

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Natural park beside JR Yahiko Station, developed as Yahiko Shrine’s outer garden, with waterfalls, a Taisho-era tunnel, and the vermilion Kangetsu Bridge.

  • Mount Yahiko

    弥彦山

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A 634-meter mountain in Yahiko with hiking trails and a ropeway, offering views of the Echigo Plain, the Sea of Japan, and Sado Island.

  • Yahiko Park Momiji Valley Light-up

    弥彦公園もみじ谷ライトアップ

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Illumination of Yahiko Park's Momiji Valley, the vermilion Kangetsu Bridge, and a ginkgo over 300 years old and 20 metres high, from early to mid-November.

  • Mount Yahiko Skyline

    弥彦山スカイライン

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A roughly 16.8 km drive toward Mount Yahiko's summit, with a 100 m revolving Panorama Tower overlooking the Echigo Plain, Sea of Japan, and Sado Island.

  • Mount Yahiko Ropeway

    弥彦山ロープウェイ

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4141

    Ropeway to the 634 m summit of Mount Yahiko in about five minutes, with views of the Echigo Plain and the Sea of Japan.

  • Mt. Yahiko (Yahiko Shrine Trailhead)

    弥彦山・彌彦神社登山口

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Sacred mountain of Echigo Ichinomiya worshipped since the Manyo period; a beginner trail from Yahiko Shrine reaches the 634 m summit in about 90 minutes.

  • Yahiko Summit Park

    弥彦山頂公園

    Yahiko, Niigata

    Summit park at Yahiko's ninth station, with a roughly 100-metre rotating Panorama Tower overlooking the Sea of Japan and Sado.

  • Mount Yahiko Opening Ceremony

    弥彦山開き

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Public climb of Mount Yahiko starts after a purification rite at Yahiko Shrine; watch for remaining snow.

  • Great Torii (Yahiko)

    大鳥居

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A 30.16-metre ryobu torii built in 1982 to mark the Joetsu Shinkansen opening, modeled on Yahiko Shrine's first torii.

  • Yahiko Shrine Onikkusai (Morning Offering Ceremony)

    越後一宮 彌彦神社「御日供祭（おにっくさい）」

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A daily 7 a.m. rite at Yahiko Shrine offering rice, sake, and salt; visitors may attend and receive offered rice.

  • Yahiko Shrine Summit Sanctuary

    彌彦神社御神廟

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Summit sanctuary of Yahiko Shrine enshrining Ame-no-Kagoyama-no-Mikoto and his consort, with 360-degree views of Sado, the Echigo Plain, and the Noto Peninsula.

  • Tohashira Shrine Hall (Yahiko Shrine)

    彌彦神社境内末社十柱神社社殿

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-2001

    Subsidiary shrine hall at Yahiko Shrine, donated in 1694 by Nagaoka lord Makino Tadatsune, with late Momoyama-period styling.

  • Yahiko YY Park

    やひこ YY Park

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-78-8288

    Mixed clothed sauna in Yahiko with automatic löyly every 30 minutes, a cold-water slide pool, outdoor bathing at the foot of Mt. Yahiko, and glamping.

  • Mount Yahiko Observation Building

    弥彦山展望ビル

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4141

    Observation restaurant at the ninth station of Mount Yahiko, overlooking the Echigo Plain, the Sea of Japan, and Sado.

  • Manyo Path

    万葉の道

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A path from Yahiko Shrine to the Yahiko Ropeway foothill station, displaying over 60 Manyoshu plants native to Mount Yahiko with their poems.

  • Former Takeishi Family Residence

    旧武石家住宅

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4311

    About 300-year-old thatched hip-roofed farmhouse showing a typical mid-Edo farmer's home; the main house is a village-designated cultural property.

  • Gyokuto (Rabbit-shaped Confection)

    玉兎

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Traditional rabbit-shaped powdered sweet from Yahiko, also offered at Yahiko Shrine, with a light texture and faint sweetness.

  • Yahiko Octopus Zelkova

    弥彦の蛸ケヤキ

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    At Sumiyoshi Shrine in Yahiko, a zelkova over 1,000 years old, 30 meters tall and 8 meters around, is named for its octopus-like branches.

  • Great Ginkgo of Yahiko

    弥彦の大イチョウ

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A roughly 300-year-old ginkgo near Omotenashi Square in Yahiko Onsen turns gold in autumn and has been illuminated at night since 2023.

  • Yahiko Shrine Treasure Hall

    彌彦神社宝物殿

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-2001

    Treasure hall on the Yahiko Shrine grounds, displaying modern art by local artists and shrine treasures, including the 2.2 m Shida Odachi.

  • Basho Haiku Monument

    芭蕉句碑

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Haiku monument in the grounds of Hoko-in inscribed with Basho's poem about the rough sea stretching toward Sado.

  • Baba Cedar of Yahiko

    弥彦の婆々スギ

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A cedar in Yahiko said to be 1,000 years old, 40 meters tall and over 10 meters around, linked to a legend of a reformed demon hag.

  • Yu Shrine (Ishi Yakushi)

    湯神社（石薬師様）

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    A subsidiary shrine outside Yahiko Shrine's grounds, on Gotenyama's mid-slope, enshrining hot-spring and medicine deities linked to healing and prosperity.

  • Kurotaki Castle Ruins

    黒滝城址

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4311

    Medieval fortress of the Echigo Uesugi clan on the Hokuriku road, important in the Otate Rebellion that aided Uesugi Kagekatsu's victory.

  • Yahiko Shrine Sacred Grove

    彌彦神社社叢

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Visitors can walk the Man'yo Path through Yahiko Shrine's sacred grove, where about 60 plants named in the Man'yoshu grow among old cedars and zelkova.

  • Mt. Yahiko Night View and Starry Sky Cruise

    弥彦山 夜景＆星空クルーズ

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4141

    A night ropeway to the 634 m summit of Mt. Yahiko for Echigo Plain night views, Sea of Japan fishing fires, and stars.

  • Sakaya Yayoi Tasting Room

    酒屋やよい テイスティングルーム

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-5841

    A tasting room in a 200-year-old folk house restored by German architect Karl Bengs, serving Niigata sake from 400 yen with views of Yahiko Shrine.

  • Yahiko Shrine Cherry Garden

    彌彦神社桜苑

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Cherry blossom garden at Yahiko Shrine where over 50 varieties, from Somei Yoshino to May double blossoms, flower in succession for about a month.

  • Mt. Yahiko (Hachimaizawa Trailhead, Chubu-Hokuriku Nature Trail)

    弥彦山・八枚沢登山口・中部北陸自然歩道

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3154

    Beginner trail on sacred Mt. Yahiko from Hachimaizawa passes flower colonies to the deity’s mausoleum, about 90 minutes to the 634 m summit.

  • Kikyo Castle Ruins

    桔梗城址

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4311

    Ruins of Kikyo Castle, said to have been built in 1055 by Yoshikawa Munekata under Minamoto no Yorimitsu and never rebuilt after 1597.

  • Yahiko Summit Museum

    弥彦山頂資料館

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4141

    Museum displaying insects and plants that live on Mount Yahiko.

  • Yahagi Twin Pines

    矢作の二本松

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-4311

    Twin pines in Yahagi, about 700 years old, 10 meters tall and 6 meters around, where Yoshitsune and Benkei are said to have rested in 1187.

  • Mikopyon-go Autonomous Bus Experience Event

    自動運転バス「ミコぴょん号」まるわかり特別体験会

    Yahiko, Niigata · 0256-94-3138

    Free, reservation-only session at Yahiko Village Office where elementary and junior high students learn bus autonomy, obstacle detection, and try simple operation.
