Things to do in Yuzawa, Niigata

Yuzawa · Niigata · 40 places

Do

  • Yuzawa Kogen Panorama Park (Yuzawa Kogen Ski Resort)

    湯沢高原パノラマパーク（湯沢高原スキー場）

    Romaji: Yuzawa Kogen Panorama Park (Yuzawa Kogen Ski Resort)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-3326

    Highland park at about 1,000 m in Yuzawa, reached by a 1,300 m ropeway, with mountain views, a foot bath, and gardens.

  • Akayu Onsen Yamaguchi-kan

    赤湯温泉 山口館

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-772-4125

    Remote hot-spring inn at the fifth station southeast of Mt. Naeba (2,145 m), reached by a two-and-a-half-hour hike, with three spring baths.

  • Daigenta Lake

    大源太湖

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    Vast lake formed by Japan's first arch dam on the Daigenta River, ringed by mountains and seasonal scenery.

  • Echigo-Yuzawa Onsen

    越後湯沢温泉

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-4850

    Hot spring area in Yuzawa, setting of Yasunari Kawabata's Snow Country, with six public baths and ski resorts within walking distance of the station.

  • 2026 Yuzawa Nakazato Autumn Festival

    ２０２６　湯沢中里秋祭り

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3240

    Autumn festival in Yuzawa with local food and product sales, new Koshihikari rice tastings, a children's stamp rally, and pony feeding.

  • Naeba Ski Resort

    苗場スキー場

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-4117

    Ski resort in Yuzawa with 24 courses, two snow parks and three gondolas on about 134 hectares, including a World Cup slope.

  • Yuzawa Kogen Ski Resort

    湯沢高原スキー場

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-3326

    Ski resort in Yuzawa with a 166-person ropeway, family slopes, and a 5 km downhill from the summit.

  • GALA Yuzawa Ski Resort

    GALA湯沢スキー場

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-785-6543

    Ski resort beside Yuzawa Station, open through Golden Week, with beginner-to-advanced runs and an on-site hot spring.

  • 11th Iiji Mountain Climbing Festival

    第11回　飯士登山祭

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3476

    Cleanup hike on 1,111 m Mount Iiji from the mid-slope of Iwappara Ski Resort in Yuzawa; the fiscal 2026 event is cancelled.

  • Iwappara Ski Resort

    岩原スキー場

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3211

    Ski resort in Yuzawa, about 1.5 hours from Tokyo by shinkansen, with 20 courses and views of the Tanigawa range.

  • Kagura Ski Resort (Kagura and Mitsumata Areas)

    かぐらスキー場（かぐら・みつまたエリア）

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-788-9221

    The Kagura and Mitsumata areas of Kagura Ski Resort offer views from 1,845 m, steep powder slopes, and a 121-person ropeway.

  • Daigenta Canyon Campground

    大源太キャニオンキャンプ場

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3536

    Campsite among mountains and Daigenta Lake in Yuzawa, with summer kayaking, stream cooling, and craft activities.

  • Shirataki Sake Brewery

    白瀧酒造株式会社

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    Sake brewery in Yuzawa known for Jozen Mizunogotoshi, offering showroom tastings and reserved tours from November through March.

  • Yumoto Communal Bath (Yama no Yu)

    湯元共同浴場 「山の湯」

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-2246

    The oldest communal bath in Echigo-Yuzawa, near the inn where Yasunari Kawabata wrote Snow Country and is said to have bathed.

  • Kagura Ski Resort (Tashiro Area)

    かぐらスキー場（田代エリア）

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-3111

    Ski area in Yuzawa’s Tashiro highlands with Nordic-style views, gentle slopes, and a longest run of 2,100 metres.

  • Shimo-Yuzawa Communal Bathhouse Komako no Yu

    下湯沢共同浴場 「駒子の湯」

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-785-7660

    Communal bathhouse named after Komako from the novel Snow Country, with a tub for about 15 people, a display corner, and a small rest room.

  • Akihayama Snowshoe Experience

    秋葉山スノーシュー体験

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-785-5353

    A guided snowshoe tour a 10-minute walk from Echigo-Yuzawa Station, through snowy forest in search of animal tracks.

  • Tsuchitaru Public Bath (Iwa-no-Yu)

    土樽共同浴場 「岩の湯」

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-2787

    Public bath in Tsuchitaru, Yuzawa, with a stone tub for about 15 people, near Yuzawa Fishing Park and ski areas.

  • Snow Tubing, Sledding and Kids' Slope at Yuzawa Kogen Ski Resort

    スノーチュービング、そり遊び、キッズゲレンデ

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-3326

    Yuki Asobi Park at the foot of Yuzawa Kogen Ski Resort offers snow tubing, sledding, a gentle kids' slope, and a snow escalator.

  • Kandatsu Snow Resort

    神立スノーリゾート

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-788-0111

    Ski and snowboard resort in Yuzawa with a 3,500-meter run, park, powder and mogul areas, a day-use hot spring, and night skiing.

  • Egami Onsen Bathhouse

    江神温泉浴場

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-2727

    Hot spring bathhouse in Yuzawa whose tubs are continuously filled with unheated source water and are gentle on the skin.

  • Yuzawa Park Ski Resort

    湯沢パークスキー場

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-4111

    Hotel-linked ski resort in Yuzawa with beginner, intermediate, and advanced runs, a kids’ snow-play area, and a free shuttle from Echigo-Yuzawa Station.

  • Mitsumata Communal Bath (Kaido no Yu)

    三俣共同浴場 「街道の湯」

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-788-9229

    A communal bath surrounded by nature, with a rock open-air bath for about 10 people and a stone indoor bath for about 20.

  • Futai Public Bath (Shukuba-no-Yu)

    二居共同浴場 「宿場の湯」

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-5855

    Public bath beside a ski area in Yuzawa, with a sauna, a spacious rest area, and a permanent exhibition of children's pictures.

  • Daigenta Experience Workshop

  • Yuzawa Nakazato Snow Resort

    湯沢中里スノーリゾート

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3301

    Ski resort in front of JR Echigo-Nakazato Station with 17 courses, a kids park, sledding, snow tubing, skiing and snowboarding.

  • Naeba Plateau Auto Campground

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 080-1234-6793
