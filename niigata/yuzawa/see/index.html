Sights in Yuzawa, Niigata

Yuzawa · Niigata · 50 places

See

  • ぽんしゅ館　越後湯沢驛店

    Ponshukan Echigo-Yuzawa Station Store

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-3778

    Niigata specialty food shop selling local sake, wine, rice and sweets, with brewery tastings, rice balls and a sake bath.

  • 苗場ドラゴンドラ

    Naeba Dragondola

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-4117

    Gondola of 5,481 meters linking Naeba and Tashiro in about 25 minutes, operating for spring greenery, autumn leaves, and winter slope access.

  • 苗場ドラゴンドラ＆田代ロープウェー　紅葉営業

    Naeba Dragondola & Tashiro Ropeway (Autumn Foliage Season)

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    During autumn foliage season, the Naeba Dragondola travels 5.5 km with a 425 m elevation change, and the Tashiro Ropeway offers views from 230 m.

  • 滝沢公園「不動滝」

    Takizawa Park (Fudo Falls)

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    Two-tier Fudo Falls beyond Takizawa Park has a 20-meter upper fall that splits into male and female falls and cannot be reached in winter.

  • 毛渡沢橋梁

    Kewatasawa Railway Bridge

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-785-5505

    A stone railway bridge near secluded Tsuchitaru Station, 217.4 meters long and more than 10 meters high.

  • 足湯　かんなっくり

    Kannakkuri Foot Bath

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-785-5505

    Foot bath about a seven-minute walk from Echigo-Yuzawa Station, renovated and reopened in 2020 so visitors can rest at leisure.

  • 湯沢町歴史民俗資料館「雪国館」

    Yuzawa Town History and Folklore Museum (Yukiguni-kan)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-3965

    A museum introducing snow-country life and history in Yuzawa, and the world of Kawabata Yasunari's novel Snow Country.

  • 三国山・三国トンネル湯沢側登山口

    Mount Mikuni (Mikuni Tunnel Yuzawa Trailhead)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-4850

    A 1,636 m mountain on the Joetsu border known for alpine flowers, with a beginner trail of about 1 hour 30 minutes from Mikuni Tunnel.

  • 平標山・平標登山口

    Mount Tairappyo (Tairappyo Trailhead)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-4850

    One of Japan's 100 Famous Flower Mountains, this 1,983 m peak at the western end of the Tanigawa range has alpine flower fields near the summit.

  • 湯沢フィッシングパーク

    Yuzawa Fishing Park

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3911

    A no-gear stream-fishing spot for char, yamame and rainbow trout, with on-site charcoal grilling, hand catching and barbecue.

  • 清津峡渓谷トンネルへは楽々貸切りタクシーで

    Private Taxi Charter to Kiyotsukyo Gorge Tunnel

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-785-5353

    A private taxi charter in Yuzawa with local drivers experienced on snowy roads and customizable sightseeing courses.

  • 小説「雪国」の碑

    Snow Country Monument

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    A monument in a Yuzawa park is engraved with Yasunari Kawabata's handwriting of the opening line of his novel Snow Country.

  • 苗場山・祓川登山口

    Mount Naeba (Haraigawa and Motohashi Trailheads)

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    From the 2,145 m summit, a high moor of hundreds of ponds spreads out, with clear-day views toward Mount Fuji, the Northern Alps, and Sado Island.

  • 湯沢中央公園＆スポーツ施設

    Yuzawa Central Park and Sports Facilities

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3388

    A park with an athletics field, baseball ground, tennis courts and leisure pool, plus spring cherry blossoms and autumn ginkgo.

  • 山鳥原公園

    Yamadorihara Park

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-1666

    A nature park with a wildflower garden, birch and azalea forest, a stone path recreating the old Mikuni Road, and a stream fishing area.

  • 一本杉スノーパーク

    Ipponsugi Snow Park

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-4440

    Snow park in Yuzawa that uses a snow escalator instead of lifts, with separate ski and sled areas on a gentle slope of about 60 metres.

  • 大源太キャニオン　四十八滝

    Daigenta Canyon Forty-Eight Falls

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    A waterfall formed by clear water from Lake Daigenta, viewed from an observation deck looking down on the stream, and recommended in spring and autumn.

  • 雪国アグリパーク　湯沢いちご村

    Yukiguni Agri Park Yuzawa Strawberry Village

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-780-6011

    Strawberry-picking greenhouse in Yuzawa where visitors can pick Echigo-hime strawberries grown through heavy snow, even on snowy or rainy days.

  • 三国街道脇本陣跡 池田家

    Ikeda Family Waki-Honjin (Mikuni Kaido)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-2211

    This prefectural cultural property is the only surviving Edo-period waki-honjin at Mitsumata-juku, with rooms where high-ranking guests stayed.

  • GALA YUZAWA MOUNTAIN FIELD

    GALA Yuzawa Mountain Field

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-785-6543

    Activity area in Yuzawa with a panorama coaster, a giant electric seesaw, mountain-cart courses, and Obon night events including gondolas and fireworks.

  • NASPAスキーガーデン

    NASPA Ski Garden

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-780-6888

    Skier-only ski area in Yuzawa with eight courses, a longest run of nearly 2,200 metres, and a direct link to NASPA New Otani.

  • 浅貝水芭蕉群生地

    Asagai Skunk Cabbage Colony

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-2121

    About 20,000 white skunk cabbage plants grow across 1,260 square meters near Naeba Ski Resort, visible from the Naeba Independence Boardwalk from late April to mid-May.

  • レジャープール「オーロラ」

    Aurora Leisure Pool

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-6600

    Leisure pool in Yuzawa with one indoor and one outdoor flowing pool, plus water slides for families.

  • 苗場ボードウォークキャンプ

    Naeba Boardwalk Camp

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 03-6427-1085

    Volunteer program maintaining the Naeba Boardwalk, Japan's second wooden path, built by hand since 2002 to allow wheelchair nature walks.

  • 越後湯沢スノーシューツアーNightコース

    Echigo-Yuzawa Snowshoe Tour (Night Course)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 070-2836-8559

    Night snowshoe walk from Echigo-Yuzawa Station’s west exit across moonlit snowfields, with starts from 6 to 8 p.m.

  • NASPA CAMP GARDEN

    NASPA Camp Garden

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-780-6888

    Campground at NASPA New Otani in Yuzawa with glamping sites, free tent sites, an RV park, and use of the hotel hot-spring baths.

  • 田代ロープウェー

    Tashiro Ropeway

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-3111

    A 2,175-metre ropeway in Yuzawa with 230 metres of ground clearance, reaching a summit station overlooking Lake Tashiro and Mount Naeba.

  • 湯沢高原サマーフェア

    Yuzawa Kogen Summer Fair

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-3326

    Summer fair at Yuzawa Kogen with a mystery treasure hunt, gem hunting, water play, seasonal food, and longer ropeway hours on 13–15 August.

  • 2025湯沢高原サマーフェア

    2025 Yuzawa Kogen Summer Fair

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-3326

    Summer fair at Yuzawa Kogen during summer vacation, with a new free-research workshop, water play, gem hunting, and extended ropeway hours on 13–16 August.

  • 湯沢中里　カタクリの里

    Yuzawa Nakazato Katakuri-no-Sato (Dogtooth Violet Garden)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-787-3240

    Dogtooth-violet colony in Yuzawa Nakazato, improved over several years, with a roughly 500-metre viewing path.

  • 越後湯沢スノーシューツアー

    Echigo-Yuzawa Snowshoe Tour

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 070-2836-8559

    Snowshoe hike from Echigo-Yuzawa Station’s west exit, where families walk through a snow-covered forest.

  • 万太郎山・浅間平橋登山口

    Mount Mantaro (Asamadaira Bridge Trailhead)

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-784-4850

    A Tanigawa-range peak on the Niigata–Gunma border, with an advanced trail of about 5 hours 30 minutes from Asamadaira Bridge to the summit.

  • 蓬峠

    Yomogi Pass

    Yuzawa, Niigata

    Historic hiking pass on the Niigata–Gunma border at 1,540 m, with a mountain hut, bamboo-grass meadows, and summer daylilies.

  • HOSHIZORAテラス

    Hoshizora Terrace

    Yuzawa, Niigata · 025-789-4117

  • フォレストアドベンチャー・湯沢中里

    Forest Adventure Yuzawa-Nakazato

    Snow Activity Experience (Round-trip Bus from Echigo-Yuzawa Station)

  • 「瑞祥庵」の仁王像

    Two Niō statues by Ishikawa Unchō are enshrined in the tower gate of Zuishōan, a Sōtō Zen temple in the Yuzawa Tsuchitaru area.

  • 大源太山・大源太キャニオン登山口

    Trailhead for 1,598 m Mount Daigenta, a four-hour mid-level hike with Tanigawa and Uonuma range views from the summit.
