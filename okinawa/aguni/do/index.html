Do

  • Ugu Beach (Nagahama Beach)

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2258

    About 1 km natural white coral beach with blue sea, monument tree-heliotrope groves, and eastern sunrises.

  • Shima Ashibi-kan Aguni Experience Center

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-896-5151

    Aguni tourism base with experiences, assist-bicycle and electric-car rentals, and maps.

  • Coastal Walk

    Aguni, Okinawa

    Coast walk on volcanic Aguni past colorful strata and village scenic sites, best at low tide.

  • West Beach (Boja)

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Tiny beach used for the farewell scene in Nabbie's Love, with clear blue water.
