Sights in Aguni, Okinawa

Aguni · Okinawa · 13 places

See

  • Okinawa Sea Salt Research Institute

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2160

    Produces popular mineral-rich Aguni salt with a 10-meter tower and about 15,000 bamboo sticks.

  • Do-ji

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Maintained limestone cave near Aguni's north coast, where an exiled Naha monk once lived; revered as sacred.

  • Stone Walls and Fukugi Tree Rows

    Aguni, Okinawa

    Old red-tile houses, tall Fukugi trees, and hand-stacked stone walls remain in Aguni Island villages.

  • Yahija Coast

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Coastal cliffs with Okinawa's only large tuff layers, from blackish basalt and andesite to white walls tens of meters high.

  • Mahana Observatory

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Cliff-top observatory with panoramic East China Sea views, island vistas, sunsets, and birdwatching.

  • Aguni Island

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-896-5151

    Island known for Aguni salt, red-tile houses, coral walls, Nabbie's Love sites, white beaches, Tera cave, and stars.

  • Tuji

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Large stone tanks on water-poor Aguni stored rainwater; carved from coastal rock and handed down for generations.

  • Munjuru Bushi Song Monument

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2449

    Song monument in Doji Park for Munjuru Bushi, Aguni's intangible cultural property.

  • Higashi Yamatuga

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Sacred split-rock path to a spring once used as Aguni's water source, with waterworks remains.

  • Nishi Uganjo

    Aguni, Okinawa

    Sacred site at the West settlement's edge, untouched for over 100 years and a prefectural natural monument.

  • Taisho Pond Park

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Quiet green pond commemorating Emperor Taisho's enthronement, with waterbirds and an overlook.

  • Fudenzaki Cape

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Steep cliffs about 85 meters above the sea, with exceptional sunsets and volcanic rocks indicating past activity.

  • Terukinabaru Monpanoki Grove

    Aguni, Okinawa · 098-988-2016

    Village natural monument of coastal trees with white flowers that shade beaches and serve as windbreaks.
