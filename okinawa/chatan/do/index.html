Things to do in Chatan, Okinawa

Chatan · Okinawa · 14 places

Do

  • Araha Beach

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-936-0077

    Beach beside Araha Park, known for the Indian Oak ship play structure, with 600m of white sand and BBQ.

  • Chatan Park Sunset Beach

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-936-8273

    Casual town beach in central Okinawa and a leading sunset spot, next to Mihama American Village.

  • BLUE STEAK WONDER CHATAN

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-988-5505

    Stylish condominium in Chatan Sunabe, five minutes by car from American Village, with ocean-view upper floors.

  • Half-Day Whale Watching Tour from Chatan (Cerulean Blue)

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-941-6828

    Half-day Chatan whale tour, handy for Chatan and Onna guests, seeing humpbacks near Zamami from late December to early April.

  • Ryukyu Workshop Chura

    Chatan, Okinawa · 080-3001-7500

    Indoor resin art and candle workshop near American Village at Uminchu Wharf in Chatan, for ages four and up.

  • RAVINA Jewelry Workshop Studio

    Chatan, Okinawa

    Ocean-view jewelry workshop in Chatan's American Village for making original rings and accessories in silver, gold, or platinum.

  • Okinawa Marine Sports Chatan Fisheries Cooperative Information Center

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-923-5771

    Marine sports in Chatan, including Sea Park Chatan, clear kayaks, SUP, diving, and snorkeling.

  • Relaxation CORAN

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-989-9945

    Affordable relaxation space near American Village in Chatan; couples massage available and parking for eight cars.

  • Ryukyu Ninja Show at LEQUIOS THEATER in Mihama

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-921-5238

    Nonverbal Ryukyu ninja show of karate and kobudo from kingdom stories, plus shuriken, blowgun, and costume activities.

  • Chatan Umibito no Kai

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-936-2068

    Urban beach at Chatan Resort with 600 meters of white sand, clear water, coral, and marine activities.

  • Yambaru Wedding

    Chatan, Okinawa · 080-4270-1986

    Photo wedding studio run by a beauty salon so visitors can try wedding photos more casually.

  • Half-Day Whale Watching Tour from Chatan

    Chatan, Okinawa · 098-936-8292

    Half-day whale watching cruise from Chatan, limited to 30 of 60 passengers.

  • lab Beauty x Longevity

    Seafront total beauty salon in Chatan offering spa, slimming, and relaxation treatments.

  • Rolfing Office Okinawa
