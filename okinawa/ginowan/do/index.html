Things to do in Ginowan, Okinawa

Ginowan · Okinawa · 8 places

Do

  • Arc Dive

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 098-897-1234

    PADI 5-star shop for Okinawa and Kerama diving, with daily LEGEND trips and Blue Cave courses.

  • Thai Traditional Salon Yukuidoki

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 080-3314-9924

    Authentic Thai massage salon with stretching and kneading at a relaxed pace; men are welcome.

  • Parasailing Experience (Skywalker)

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 098-943-6399

    Ginowan parasailing shop offering sound parasailing with music, flight certificate, and photos.

  • Prince Ginowan Tropical Beach

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 098-897-2759

    Ginowan Bypass town beach for easy BBQ and marine sports, popular with locals, youth, and foreign tourists.

  • Nangoku Kurashi Goods Shop

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 090-3790-8722

    Resin art and painting experiences to make a one-of-a-kind piece as an Okinawa travel memory.

  • Ginowan Civic Hall

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 098-893-4433

    Opened in 1983 as a hall for music, theater, and art, with a 1,200-seat multipurpose hall.

  • Okinawa Fortune-telling Hall Aquamarine

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 0120-822-244

    Fortune-telling hall on Route 58 with tarot, astrology, numerology, and spiritual readers in private rooms by reservation.

  • ANGLER Fishing Gear Rental

    Ginowan, Okinawa · 080-6486-3035

    Backpack rental of complete fishing gear from 4,800 yen for one night two days at ports, rivers, and beaches.
