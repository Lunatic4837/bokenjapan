Things to do in Ginoza, Okinawa

Ginoza · Okinawa · 6 places

Do

  • Matsuda Limestone Cave

    Ginoza, Okinawa · 098-989-8100

    Untouched limestone cave unlike tourist caves. Local guides lead reserved adventure tours into an underground world over 100,000 years old.

  • Kanna Beach

    Ginoza, Okinawa · 090-3016-2723

    Quiet east-coast beach in Ginoza, gently arched with clear water, near Route 329.

  • Hipii Beach

    Ginoza, Okinawa · 090-3016-2723

    Hidden-gem private beach in Ginoza Village offering swimming, marine sports, and camping in clear, calm water.

  • Okinawa Watsu Center

    Ginoza, Okinawa · 098-968-5008

    Aqua therapy with gentle massage and stretching while floating in water.

  • GINOZA A5UMIPIOTO Diving and Photo Tour

    Ginoza, Okinawa · 080-5331-0552

    One-group-a-day diving and photo tour of Okinawa's sea and land beauty, in a small-group format.

  • Happiness Marine Club

    Ginoza, Okinawa · 090-3016-2723

    Beginner-friendly marine activities on an east-coast private beach, about 50 minutes by car from Naha Airport.
