Things to do in Higashi, Okinawa

Higashi · Okinawa · 5 places

Do

  • Higashi Villagers' Forest Tsutsuji Eco Park

    Higashi, Okinawa · 0980-43-3300

    Park in Yanbaru nature, as large as five Tokyo Domes, offering bungalows, camping, and river trekking.

  • Higashi Village Fureai Hirugi Park

    Higashi, Okinawa · 0980-51-2433

    Mangrove park in Yanbaru with boardwalks and canoe tours.

  • Fukuchigawa Seaside Park

    Higashi, Okinawa · 0980-51-2301

    Seaside outdoor field with habu jellyfish prevention nets, swimming, banana boats, canoe and SUP rentals, and camping.

  • Higashi Village Tourism Promotion Council

    Higashi, Okinawa · 0980-51-2655

    Second ecotourism association in Japan, guiding canoe trips in Gesashi Bay mangroves and walking tours of Higashi nature.

  • Uppama Beach

    Higashi, Okinawa · 0980-43-2265

    Unmanaged 1 km white-sand beach in Higashi Village, meaning large beach, retaining Okinawa's original scenery.
