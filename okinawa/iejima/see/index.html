Sights in Iejima, Okinawa

Iejima · Okinawa · 8 places

See

  • Mount Gusuku, Ie Village

    Iejima, Okinawa

    172m pointed rock mountain and symbol of Ie Island, with 360-degree panorama views from the summit.

  • Ie Island Hibiscus Garden

    Iejima, Okinawa · 0980-49-5850

    Garden growing over 1,800 hibiscus varieties; new types unique to Ie Island are bred here, enjoyable in rain.

  • Nyatiya Cave

    Iejima, Okinawa

    Sacred Ie Island cave with the child-granting Bijiru Stone, also a wartime shelter called the Thousand-Person Cave.

  • Wakide

    Iejima, Okinawa

    Fresh water springs from the northwest shoreline, still used for drinking, beside cliffs over 60 meters.

  • Shotai-ji

    Iejima, Okinawa · 0980-49-2128

    Zen temple founded in 1554 by King Sho Sei after an ancient mirror was discovered.

  • Shimamuraya Sightseeing Park

    Iejima, Okinawa · 0980-49-2422

    Park on Shimamuraya mansion ruins, stage of famous tragic play Iejima Handu-gwa, with statue, habu park, and folk museum.

  • Ernie Pyle Monument

    Iejima, Okinawa

    Memorial to US correspondent Ernie Pyle, killed on Ie Island on April 18, 1945, built by the US military.

  • Ara Utaki

    Iejima, Okinawa

    Worship site at the former island gateway where people prayed for safe journeys.
