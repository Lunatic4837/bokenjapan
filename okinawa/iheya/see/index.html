Sights in Iheya, Okinawa

Iheya · Okinawa · 11 places

See

  • Kumaya Cave

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Chert cave on Iheya Island, a prefectural natural monument called the southernmost Ama-no-Iwato site.

  • Nentohiramatsu Pine, Iheya Island

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Nationally designated natural monument: about 300-year-old Ryukyu pine with parasol-like branches, symbol of Iheya Island.

  • Noho Bridge

    Iheya, Okinawa

    A 320-meter curved bridge linking Iheya and Noho over emerald sea, ideal for driving.

  • Muzomizu Spring

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Undying spring on a rock about six meters around, where a wife awaited her husband.

  • Yahe-iwa

    Iheya, Okinawa

    About 280 million years old and about 50 meters offshore, walkable at low tide, with ancient castle traces.

  • Dana Kuba Mountain

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Prefectural natural monument on Iheya's northern tip, covered in kuba palms, with a lighthouse.

  • Noho Island Observatory

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Noho's best scenic point: high-ground views of Iheya Island, Noho Bridge, and the sea.

  • Koto-iwa

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Rock like a crouching tiger overlooking Maedomari Port; five-minute walk, 360-degree views of Iheya.

  • Iheya Village Shimajiri Kami Ashiage

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Thatched hall on eight stone pillars once used by noro in Iheya; Okinawa folk cultural property.

  • Iheya Village History and Folklore Museum

    Iheya, Okinawa · 0980-46-2384

    Museum on Iheya origins, geology, history, and annual events, three minutes from Maedomari Port.

  • Katakuma Shrine

    Iheya, Okinawa

    Iheya cherry-blossom spot enshrining Yagura Ufusu, Sho Hashi's great-grandfather; best in February.
