Sights in Ishigaki, Okinawa

Ishigaki · Okinawa · 18 places

See

  • NEI Museum of Art

    Ishigaki, Okinawa · 0980-83-6303

    Displays yuzen-dyed paintings of southern islands, plus silk stoles, handkerchiefs, and stationery.

  • Emerald Sea Observatory

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    360-degree views of Ishigaki town, lush plains, emerald sea, Taketomi, Iriomote, night views and stars.

  • Southern Gate Bridge

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Blue bridge linking Yashima and Painuhama, a town symbol with night lighting and projection mapping.

  • Yaeyama Peace Memorial Museum

    Ishigaki, Okinawa · 0980-88-6161

    Established in 1999 as a branch, showing Yaeyama war malaria and postwar malaria control through photos and materials.

  • Tojinbaka

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Tomb enshrining Chinese who died in the mid-19th-century Baun incident, built in Chinese style in 1972.

  • Miyara River Mangrove Forest

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Mangrove forest designated a national natural monument, spanning about 1,500m of the Miyara River estuary.

  • Kabira Bay

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Ishigaki's finest scenic inlet, with nine islets and black pearl cultivation since the Taisho era.

  • Hirakubo Cape Lighthouse

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Lighthouse at Ishigaki's northern tip, where cattle graze and Tarama Island is visible on clear days.

  • Cape Ogamizaki

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    North end of Yarabu Peninsula with a white lighthouse, cliffs, odd rocks, white spring lilies, and popular offshore diving.

  • Former Miyara Dunchi

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    1819 Yaeyama samurai residence modeled on Ryukyu houses, with a dry-landscape garden and national Important Cultural Property status.

  • Gongendo and Nio Statues

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Yaeyama's oldest Buddhist temple, founded in 1614, with Okinawa's oldest wooden Nio statues.

  • Tamatorizaki Observatory

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    At Hirakubo Peninsula's base, views run from Nobaru Cape to Hirakubo Cape over green hills and coral-reef sea.

  • Shiraho Coast

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Coral reef coast about 10 minutes by car from New Ishigaki Airport.

  • Ishigaki Island Astronomical Observatory

    Ishigaki, Okinawa · 0980-88-0013

    Kyushu-Okinawa's largest 105cm optical-infrared telescope, with public facility tours, a 4D2U theater, and stargazing.

  • Ishigaki Island Limestone Cave

    Ishigaki, Okinawa · 0980-83-1550

    Ishigaki's largest limestone cave, created over 200,000 years from coral, with stalactites, illumination, a water chime, and Totoro stalactite.

  • Ishigaki City Yaeyama Museum

    Ishigaki, Okinawa · 0980-82-4712

    Municipal Yaeyama museum in Ishigaki City.

  • Yonehara Yaeyama Palm Grove

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    National natural monument grove of endemic Yaeyama palms in Yonehara, Ishigaki, praised as one of the world's most beautiful palms.

  • Mount Nosoko

    Ishigaki, Okinawa

    Pointed northern mountain with summit panoramas and a signboard for the Nusukumape tragic love story.
