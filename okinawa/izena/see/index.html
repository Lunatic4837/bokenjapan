Sights in Izena, Okinawa

Izena · Okinawa · 14 places

See

  • Mekaru Family Residence

    Izena, Okinawa

    Well-preserved house of King Sho En's uncle's descendants that escaped war damage, showing Ryukyuan noble features.

  • Sea Gitara and Land Gitara

    Izena, Okinawa · 0980-45-2435

    Two towering sheer rocks, one in the sea and one on land, regarded as sacred and viewed from Gitara Observatory.

  • Izena Tamaudun

    Izena, Okinawa

    Second Sho Dynasty mausoleum on the slope of Izena Castle ruins, with Chinese diabase coffins.

  • Izena Kami Asagi

    Izena, Okinawa

    Thatched wall-less ritual hut on eight stone pillars in Izena settlement, designated a folk cultural property.

  • King Sho En Garden Park

    Izena, Okinawa · 0980-45-2435

    1996 park at King Sho En's birthplace, with his first-bath well and a bronze statue.

  • Izena Island

    Izena, Okinawa · 0980-45-2001

    Birthplace of King Sho En, a scenic island with sacred sites and historic remains.

  • Izena Castle Ruins

    Izena, Okinawa

    Steep coastal castle ruins said to be built by Samekawa, with stone walls, a gate, and sea views.

  • Samure Road

    Izena, Okinawa

    Roughly 2 km old road on Izena, also the approach for official Shiimii festivals.

  • Equestrian Statue of King Sho En

    Izena, Okinawa · 0980-45-2435

    Bronze equestrian statue of young King Sho En, erected for his 600th birth anniversary.

  • Izena Village Fureai Folk Museum

    Izena, Okinawa · 0980-45-2165

    Exhibits Izena history, folklore, and archaeology, plus Mekaru family royal gifts and colorful festival lacquerware.

  • Izena Settlement

    Izena, Okinawa

    Old Okinawan landscape of coral stone walls and fukugi rows, preserved because no habu live here.

  • Izena Village Moromi Kami Asagi

    Izena, Okinawa

    Ritual hall beside King Sho En's birthplace in Moromi, Izena; Okinawa tangible folk cultural property.

  • Serikyaku Kami Asagi

    Izena, Okinawa

    Thatched hipped-roof ritual hut on four stone pillars in Serikyaku, designated a folk cultural property.

  • Izena Village Nakada Kami Asagi

    Izena, Okinawa

    Four-pillar thatched hall in Nakada, Izena, used for Unjami and harvest festivals; Okinawa folk cultural property.
