Sights in Kitadaito, Okinawa

Kitadaito · Okinawa · 11 places

See

  • Kita-Daito Island

    Kitadaito, Okinawa

    Untouched nature with rare endemic plants, sugarcane farming, fishing, swimming, and cycling.

  • Kitadaito Island Phosphate Mine Ruins

    Kitadaito, Okinawa

    Phosphate mine ruins near West Port, worked from 1919 to 1950 mainly for chemical fertilizer ore.

  • Kita-Daito Phosphate Mining Cultural Landscape

    Kitadaito, Okinawa · 0980-23-4001

    Okinawa's first national Important Cultural Landscape, selected in 2018, with extensive remaining phosphate mining facilities.

  • Nagamaku (Cliff and Talus Plant Community)

    Kitadaito, Okinawa · 09802-3-4001

    Raised coral reef belt ringing inland Kitadaito; rare plants are a national natural monument.

  • Landing Park

    Kitadaito, Okinawa

    Centennial park overlooking the 1903 first landing site, with a steep sea slope and clear-water views.

  • Okinawa Easternmost Point Monument

    Kitadaito, Okinawa

    Monument by Kitadaito Airport for Okinawa's easternmost point, striking at sunrise, with toilets and barbecue space.

  • Minamidaito Fishing Port (Kitadaito District)

    Kitadaito, Okinawa

    Despite abundant fishing grounds, boats once needed cranes; the long-awaited fishing port was completed in 2019.

  • Typhoon Rock

    Kitadaito, Okinawa

    Rock 4.6 m wide and 3.3 m high, blown near Esaki Port by 2009 typhoon winds of 58.9 m/s.

  • Ufuagari People and Nature Museum

    Kitadaito, Okinawa · 09802-3-4350

    Museum on Kita-Daito origins, phosphate mines, sugarcane farming, and settlement, with e-bike rental and souvenirs.

  • Hamayu-so Observatory

    Kitadaito, Okinawa

    Observatory at Hamayu-so on Koganeyama, the island's highest point, viewing sugarcane fields and Nagamaku cliffs.

  • West Port

    Kitadaito, Okinawa · 09802-3-4001

    One of three ports, located on the west side of the island.
