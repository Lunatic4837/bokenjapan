Things to do in Kumejima, Okinawa

Kumejima · Okinawa · 6 places

Do

  • Hatenohama

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-985-7131

    A 7 km coral-sand sandbar east of Kume Island, with changing sea colors and popular sunset tours.

  • Eef Beach

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-985-7131

    Kume Island's representative natural beach of fine white sand about 2 km long, among Japan's 100 beaches.

  • Algae Farm FARMO

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-996-7172

    Japan's first algae farm on Kume Island uniting production, processing, and tourism, using deep ocean water for cultivation.

  • Shinri Beach

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-851-7973

    West Kume Island beach with superb sunsets, sea life, low-tide reef walks, camping, and airplane views.

  • Kumejima Tsumugi Village Yuimaru Hall

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-985-8333

    A Kumejima tsumugi theme park with a museum, loom viewing, coaster weaving, and plant-dyeing experiences.

  • Kumejima Horse Ranch

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 080-6491-1950

    Horseback riding on a white sand beach, or simply meeting and touching horses.
