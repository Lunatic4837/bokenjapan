Sights in Kumejima, Okinawa

Kumejima · Okinawa · 10 places

See

  • Ou Island Tatami-ishi

    Kumejima, Okinawa

    More than 1,000 pentagonal and hexagonal turtle-shell rocks, a national natural monument of columnar jointing.

  • Hiyajo Cliff

    Kumejima, Okinawa

    200-meter sheer cliff with an observatory overlooking distant islands on clear days.

  • Kumejima Sea Turtle Museum

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-985-7513

    Meet live sea turtles and learn about the ecology and environmental issues of globally endangered turtles.

  • Uezuke Residence

    Kumejima, Okinawa

    Mid-1700s Ryukyu Kingdom pechin house on Kume Island, a national Important Cultural Property amid limestone walls and fukugi trees.

  • Mifuga

    Kumejima, Okinawa

    Odd rock on Kume Island's Yamatodomari shore formed by wind and tide erosion; women pray for children.

  • Kumejima Town

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-985-7131

    Fifth-largest Okinawa island and prefectural natural park, with coral reefs, beaches, and weaving experiences.

  • Gushikawa Castle Ruins (Kume Island)

    Kumejima, Okinawa

    Scenic national historic site: early fifth-century lord's castle ruins on Kume Island's northwest coast.

  • Tenko-gu

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-851-7973

    Built in 1756 in Maja, Kumejima, enshrining Mazu after islanders rescued a shipwrecked envoy fleet.

  • Kumejima Firefly Museum

    Kumejima, Okinawa · 098-896-7100

    Facility to learn and protect nature linked to Kumejima fireflies and nurture a sustainable environment.

  • Former Nakazato Magiri Kuramoto Site

    Kumejima, Okinawa

    Arched gate and stone walls remain at a Ryukyu-era local office, a National Important Cultural Property.
