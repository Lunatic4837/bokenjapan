Things to do in Kunigami, Okinawa

Kunigami · Okinawa · 10 places

Do

  • Kunigami Village Environmental Education Center "Yanbaru Learning Forest"

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-41-7979

    Yanbaru forest facility with lodging, guided walks, night hikes, canoe tours, trails, and auto camping.

  • Kunigami Village Forest Park

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-43-0514

    Forest park with camping, bungalows, a 3 km trail, toy museum, and views of mountains and Haneji inland sea.

  • Course to Mount Yonaha's Ninth Station

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-41-2420

    Popular Yanbaru National Park course to Mount Yonaha's ninth station, certified for forest therapy.

  • Asumui Hikes

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-41-8117

    Audio-guided walk in a nature-rich grove of Ryukyu myth on Asumui, seen from Cape Hedo.

  • Okuma Beach

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-41-2222

    Fine white-sand private beach about 1 km long, with a clear emerald East China Sea and marine activities.

  • Yanbaru Kuina Ecological Exhibition Learning Facility Kuina Forest

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-41-7788

    Booths to observe active Yanbaru kuina, materials booths, a goods shop, kayak and trekking tours, and park golf.

  • Yambaru Forest Nature Guide Tour

    Kunigami, Okinawa

    About two-hour EV bus tour of Yambaru forest with a local guide near the national park.

  • Set Net Fishing Experience (OSC & Kunigami Fisheries Cooperative)

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-51-4100

    Join actual fishing to haul one of Okinawa's largest set nets near Ada Fishing Port.

  • Yanbaru East Coast Blue Tourism Base

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-51-4100

    Marine recreation base at Ada Fishing Port offering set-net fishing, snorkeling, and diving tours since April 2018.

  • Nanmei Shinshitsu

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 050-8884-4992

    Four private single-building lodgings in Ogimi and Kunigami using old houses and vacant lots.
