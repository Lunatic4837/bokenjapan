Sights in Kunigami, Okinawa

Kunigami · Okinawa · 5 places

See

  • Yanbaru Kuina Observatory

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-43-0977

    Giant Okinawa rail seen from Cape Hedo is an observatory viewing the cape, Yoron Island, and Pacific waves.

  • Asumuihaikusu Stone Museum

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-41-8117

    Touch rocks from all 41 municipalities and see rare stone folk tools while learning Okinawa's geological history.

  • Benoki Dam

    Kunigami, Okinawa

    Only northern dam with a lake loop road, starry skies, and a two-hour walk.

  • Yanbaru Forest Toy Museum

    Kunigami, Okinawa · 0980-50-1022

    Experience-based museum with many wooden toys from Yanbaru trees, including itajii and Ryukyu pine.

  • Sedakezaki Lighthouse

    Kunigami, Okinawa

    Lighthouse with views of the Okinawa-blue sea; facility routes and nearby tourist information can be checked.
