Things to do in Minamidaito, Okinawa

Minamidaito · Okinawa · 4 places

Do

  • Kaigunbo Pool

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Rock pool carved by islanders, one of two swim spots on beachless Minamidaito, where coral is visible.

  • Minamidaito Island Secret Exploration Tour

    Minamidaito, Okinawa · 090-5082-4949

    Cave, karst lake canoe, and night tours on coral-formed Minamidaito Island.

  • Minamidaito Island Local Meteorological Observatory / Radiosonde

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Watch observation balloon launches carrying radiosondes about 30 minutes before 9:00 and 21:00.

  • Shioya Pool

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Reef-carved seawater pool on cliff-bound Minamidaito, which has no sandy beaches; depth changes with the tide.
