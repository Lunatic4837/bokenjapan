  • Hoshino Cave

    Minamidaito, Okinawa · 098-022-2815

    Minamidaito Island's foremost limestone cave, 375 meters long, with paths, lighting, and varied milky-white stalactites.

  • Baribari Rock

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Walk through a huge rock fissure created by crustal movement on Minamidaito Island, born about 48 million years ago.

  • Akiba Underground Lake

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Underground lake beneath sugarcane fields on Minamidaito, entered through a sinkhole; guide required.

  • Minamidaito Fishing Port

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Minamidaito's only fishing port, where cranes lift whole boats because cliffs prevent docking.

  • Sugar Train

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Train that carried harvested sugarcane on Minamidaito for over 60 years until truck transport took over.

  • Furusato Cultural Center

    Minamidaito, Okinawa · 0980-22-2815

    Folk museum of Minamidaito pioneer history, mixing Hachijo and Ryukyu culture, with tools, specimens, and a sugar train.

  • Daito Shrine

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Shrine on Minamidaito enshrining Amaterasu, founded by devout Hachijo Island settlers.

  • Minamidaito Island Settlement Centennial Monument

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Monument in Frontier Park south of Nishi Port, with statues of Tamaki Han'emon and a settler, built in 2000.

  • Hinomaru-yama Observatory

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Highest observatory on basin-like Minamidaito, on a position site built by Japanese forces in wartime.

  • Ohirugi Grove at Oike

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Nationally designated natural monument: mangroves growing in freshwater inland at Oike, a globally rare ecology.

  • Railway Ruins

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Remains of the sugarcane railway whose sugar train once crossed Minamidaito Island.

  • Minami-Daito Island

    Minamidaito, Okinawa

    Raised coral island with sheer cliffs, an observatory, a large limestone cave, and local foods.
