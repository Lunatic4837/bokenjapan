  • Sabautsugaa (City-Designated Historic Site)

    Precious water source for Sarahama on Irabu Island for over 240 years until 1966; too salty to drink.

  • Sumurya Myaka

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    Stone tomb of the Sumurya (Nagama) family on Kurima Island, a prefectural historic site.

  • German Emperor's Benevolence Monument

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    1876 monument praising Miyako islanders who rescued crew from a wrecked German merchant ship.

  • Uehiyayama Ruins

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    Prefectural historic site: vast 14th-15th-century village ruins that yielded Chinese ceramics.

  • Shimoji Island

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    Northwest of Miyako, linked to Irabu by six bridges; Toriike, the Dragon's Eye, is a diving spot.

  • Shimoji Island Toriike Pond

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    Mysterious pond of mermaid legends that looks like two pools joined by a natural stone bridge.

  • Shimoji Ikeda Bridge

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    Japan's southernmost stone bridge, an arch of fitted Ryukyu limestone in Shimoji, a prefectural historic site.

  • Monument to the Five Heroes of Hisamatsu

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    Honors five Hisamatsu fishermen who rowed to Ishigaki to report the Russian Baltic Fleet in the Russo-Japanese War.

  • Head Tax Stone

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    1.43 m pillar; Miyako and Yaeyama people aged 15-50 taller than it paid head tax, 1637-1903.

  • Tomb of Nakasone Toyumiya

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    National important cultural property tomb built by Miyako ruler Nakasone Toyumiya to mourn his father.

  • Nakazato Tropical Fruit Orchard

  • Irabu Bridge

    Japan's longest free bridge, 3,540 meters, opened in 2015 between Miyako and Irabu islands.

  • Yabiji

  • Kisama Utaki

    Miyakojima, Okinawa

    Historic Miyako Island worship site recorded in 1705 and 1713 texts, a prefectural tangible folk cultural property.

  • Miyakojima City Traditional Crafts Center

  • Kurima Bridge
