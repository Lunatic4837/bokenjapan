Sights in Nakijin, Okinawa

Nakijin · Okinawa · 4 places

See

  • Nakijin Village

    Nakijin, Okinawa · 0980-56-2101

    Northern Okinawa village including Kouri Island, known for sweet watermelon and World Heritage castle ruins.

  • Nakijin Village History and Culture Center

    Nakijin, Okinawa · 098-056-5767

    Beside Nakijin Castle Ruins, displays excavated Chinese ceramics, village tools, and festivals.

  • Kouri Ocean Tower

    Nakijin, Okinawa · 0980-56-1616

    Scenic views from 82 m elevation over Kouri Bridge, sea, sky, and Yanbaru; pumpkin sweets sold only here.

  • Nakijin Castle Ruins

    Nakijin, Okinawa · 0980-56-4400

    Hokuzan king's main castle before Ryukyu unification, built on a 90-100m hill and listed as World Heritage in 2000.
