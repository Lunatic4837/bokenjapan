Things to do in Nanjo, Okinawa

Nanjo · Okinawa · 19 places

Do

  • Azama Sun Sun Beach

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-948-3521

    Okinawa's first year-round universal-design beach with wheelchair mats, amphibious wheelchairs, and staff trained for seniors and disabled visitors.

  • Umikaze Horse Farm

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 090-6869-5788

    Ranch at Yuinchi Hotel Nanjo for riding native Yonaguni horses, even into the sea.

  • Okinawa World

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-949-7421

    Leading Okinawa spot with Gyokusendo Cave, habu museum park, Ryukyu castle town, fruit garden, brewery, and Super Eisa.

  • Karin Relaxation and Spa

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 070-5411-4919

    Affordable relaxation spa inside the popular onsen Sarujin no Yu at Yuinchi Hotel Nanjo.

  • Wakuwaku Experience Natura Eco Tourism

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 050-3636-2270

    Sea kayaking, SUP, pottery, local food, and health tourism in southern Okinawa.

  • Gangala Valley

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-948-4192

    Reserved guided tours of a collapsed-cave valley, forest, caves, Ufushugajumaru tree, and ancient dwellings.

  • Komaka Island

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Small uninhabited island off Chinen Peninsula for snorkeling, diving, fishing, and windsurfing.

  • Komaka Uninhabited Island

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-948-3355

    Uninhabited island beach 15 minutes by boat from the main island, with clear sea and white sand.

  • Sports Culture Center Peare

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-947-6897

    Indoor and outdoor sports facilities at Yuinchi Hotel Nanjo, with gym, pools, field, billiards, and squash.

  • Sashiki no Sarujin no Yu Natural Onsen

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Golden natural onsen of blended ancient fossil seawater, with free-flowing baths and panoramic views from a 150 m hill.

  • Ou Island (Nanjo City)

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Small fisherman's island linked by a 100m bridge, with affordable seafood and a Hari sea-god festival.

  • Niihara Beach

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Unchanged natural beach loved by locals, with shallow emerald water, sand, white coral, and shells.

  • Nami Okinawa

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 080-7828-7776

    Private salon in Nanjo offering deep relaxation and original body care, with forest and Nakagusuku Bay views.

  • Ryukyu Glass Kingdom Workshop Ufuya

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Ryukyu glass workshop in Okinawa World offering glassmaking experiences plus display and sale of artisan works.

  • Kudaka Island Ishiki Beach First Sunrise Cruise

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-941-6828

    Limited 160-person cruise to sacred Kudaka Island to view the first sunrise from Ishiki Beach.

  • Amayama Soan Healing Salon and Gallery

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 080-8557-0311

    Reservation-only private salon near Ou Island offering singing bowl healing by a therapist with over 10 years' experience.

  • Hyakuna Beach

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    White-sand natural beach in southern Okinawa, said to be founding deity Amamikyo's first landing; no lifeguards.

  • Chinen Marine Leisure Center

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-948-3355

    Year-round marine leisure at Cape Chinen, with swimming, snorkeling, banana boats, glass boats, and island transfers.

  • Churaichigo Nanjo House

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Okinawa's largest strawberry-picking tourist farm, about 30 minutes from Naha Airport, with a farmer cafe.
