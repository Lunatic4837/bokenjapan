Sights in Nanjo, Okinawa

Nanjo · Okinawa · 19 places

See

  • Teda Spring

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Coastal spring where the sun deity is said to have descended, still visited for worship.

  • Nirai Bridge and Kanai Bridge

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    A 660m-long, 80m-high curving bridge with Pacific views of Chinen Cape and Kudaka Island.

  • Bridge of Happiness

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-948-4660

    Small bridge at Ganju Station in Nanjo; spreading your arms centers you between Sefa-utaki and Kudaka Island.

  • Yaharazukasa

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Sacred sea site where creation deity Amamikyo first landed, reachable only at low tide.

  • Kudaka Island

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Sacred island long called the Island of the Gods, about 25 minutes by ferry from Azama Port.

  • Nakandakari Spring

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Only spring in the prefecture with an attached goemon bath, rebuilt in Ryukyu limestone in 1912.

  • Sashiki Ue Gusuku

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Castle of Sho Hashi, unifier of the three kingdoms, and his father Sho Shisho, overlooking Nakagusuku Bay and Kudaka Island.

  • Fusozaki Coast Hamajincho Community

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Rare coastal hamajincho community, a prefectural natural monument mixed with mangroves in a wetland park.

  • Nanjo Art Museum

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-975-7616

    Art museum in the same mountains as World Heritage Sefa-utaki, overlooking blue sea and sky.

  • Ukinju-Hainju

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Two coastal springs told as Ryukyu's rice-farming birthplace and an eastern pilgrimage worship site.

  • Kakinohana Spring

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Spring selected as one of Japan's 100 Famous Waters, below castle ruins, with views of a cobalt-blue sea.

  • Ufugusuku Castle Ruins

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Castle ruins on steep limestone cliffs, built into the natural terrain around the 14th century.

  • Sefa-utaki

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Highest sacred site of the Ryukyu Kingdom, a World Heritage site since December 2000.

  • Hamagawa Utaki

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Green forest where creation deity Amamikyo rested at a spring, an eastern pilgrimage site.

  • Tomb of Momoto Fumiagari

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Tomb of a Ryukyuan princess sacrificed in men's power struggles.

  • Chinen Castle Ruins

    Nanjo, Okinawa · 098-917-5374

    Said to be Chinen Aji's castle, sung in Omoro Soshi, considered used since the 13th century.

  • Chinen Ukka

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Sacred spring where Amamikyo is said to have planted rice brought from heaven.

  • Chinen Cape Park

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    Scenic cape park overlooking the Pacific, Kudaka Island, and Komaka Island, with a gazebo and paths.

  • Itokazu Abuchiragama Guide Center

    Nanjo, Okinawa

    270m natural cave in Nanjo, a wartime shelter and hospital annex; about 150,000 peace-learning visitors yearly.
