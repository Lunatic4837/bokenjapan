Things to do in Okinawa, Okinawa

Okinawa · Okinawa · 11 places

Do

  • BBQ Station

    Okinawa · 050-5487-7384

    Reservation-only rooftop BBQ: 120-minute all-you-can-eat-and-drink for 4000 yen, plus private rooms.

  • Earth & Pure

    Okinawa · 050-5479-7877

    Reservation candle-making and perfume-making experiences in Koza using natural materials.

  • Live House Jambalaya M

    Okinawa · 090-8356-7809

    Koza live house, Okinawan rock's birthplace, open over 60 years with wide-ranging cover songs.

  • Eisa Hall

    Okinawa · 098-989-5066

    See Eisa tools and music, watch dance videos, drum and dance, and visit an archive corner.

  • Esthetic Salon mino Misato Odori

    Okinawa · 098-987-8746

    Okinawa-only Ryukyu Tinnadi treatment uses Okinawa plant oils and traditional music to refresh mind and body.

  • Okinawa Grand Mer Resort

    Okinawa · 098-931-1500

    Ryukyu-American style resort hotel in central Okinawa City, with balcony rooms, indoor pool, sauna, and fitness.

  • Honey Ray Marine

    Okinawa · 080-3340-7457

    Private boat tours, jet ski rentals, and marine sports to uninhabited island resorts.

  • Fourteen Marine Okinawa

    Okinawa · 080-8416-7253

    Private cruising on luxury boats to a secret private beach for sunbathing, snorkeling, and beachcombing.

  • Anko Shoten

    Okinawa · 090-5747-8932

    Okinawa City shop offering nerikiri making experiences, classes, and custom orders.

  • Okinawa Arena

    Okinawa · 050-5444-1478

    Multipurpose arena completed in 2021, seating 10,000, and home court of the Ryukyu Golden Kings.

  • Okinawa City Machimaai

    Okinawa · 098-989-5566

    Guided Koza walks of shopping streets and hidden spots; five courses, book two days ahead.
