See

  • art gallery soranoe

    Okinawa · 098-988-7848

    Katsuyuki Yogi's atelier and gallery in Koza, Okinawa City, selling works and goods.

  • Koza Crossroads Mural

    Okinawa · 098-929-3300

    A 170-meter mural painted across building walls, tracing Koza from the 15th-century Goeku Gusuku era to the future.

  • Bokunen Art Gallery

    Okinawa · 098-923-3719

    Permanent gallery featuring Okinawan printmaker Naka Bokunen, creator of world-project posters and commemorative stamp art. Free admission.

  • Okinawa Zoo & Museum

    Okinawa · 098-933-4190

    Zoo and Wonder Museum in central Okinawa with about 150 animal species and hands-on exhibits.

  • Okinawa City Postwar Culture Museum Histreet

    Okinawa · 098-929-2922

    Exhibition hall on Okinawa City postwar history, showing materials on distinctive Koza culture.

  • Chura Yashi Park Okinawa (Southeast Botanical Gardens)

    Okinawa · 098-939-2555

    One of Japan's largest outdoor botanical gardens, opened in 1968, with about 1,300 species and 50 animal species.
