Things to do in Taketomi, Okinawa

Taketomi · Okinawa · 12 places

Do

  • Iriomote Island Sunny Day

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 090-6860-9533

    Offers canoe, trekking, and wildlife observation tours on Iriomote Island.

  • Kayak & Request Iriomote Island Kurage

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-5493

    Small-group mangrove kayak and jungle trekking tours to Pinaisara Falls with veteran guides.

  • Iriomote Island Canoe Tour Kazaguruma

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 090-8915-0931

    Founded in 1999, it has guided over 28,000 people on Iriomote canoe and waterfall tours.

  • Tsukigahama Beach

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Fine white crescent beach at the mouth of Iriomote's largest river, Urauchi, with exceptional sunsets.

  • Kuina & Kayak Iriomote

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 080-9036-9027

    Specialist canoe and sea kayak shop on Iriomote, with waterfall tours, calm-sea plans, and early-morning trips.

  • TAKE Diving School

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-6871

    A couple has guided Iriomote seas for 33 years in small homey groups, with lively evening underwater video screenings.

  • Kondoi Beach

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Representative Taketomi Island beach with white sand and aqua-blue sea.

  • Kaiji Beach

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Taketomi beach beside Kondoi Beach, famous for star-shaped sand; fast currents require swimming caution.

  • Urauchi River Sightseeing

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-6154

    Sightseeing-boat jungle cruise on Iriomote's largest river, about 2 km trek to Mariyudu and Kanbire Falls, plus canoe tours.

  • Haemida Beach

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Quiet 2 km white-sand Iriomote beach with Hateruma views and a stone memorial to wartime malaria victims.

  • Nishi Beach

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Among Yaeyama's most beautiful beaches, with Hateruma Blue and popular sunsets; Nishi means north.

  • Aragusuku Island

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Coral-ringed islands Kamiji and Shimoji, called Panari, walkable at low tide; no scheduled boats.
