Sights in Taketomi, Okinawa

Taketomi · Okinawa · 18 places

See

  • Shinmori Family Residence

    Taketomi, Okinawa

    Said to be Okinawa's oldest surviving wooden thatched folk house, in Sonai, Iriomote Island, over 150 years old.

  • Iriomote Island

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Subtropical jungle island, Okinawa's second largest, registered as World Natural Heritage in July 2021.

  • Iriomote Wildlife Conservation Center

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-5581

    Protects and researches rare Iriomote wildlife, with nature exhibits, Iriomote cat taxidermy, and sighting records.

  • Taketomi Island Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings

    Taketomi, Okinawa

    Red-tiled Ryukyu houses, roof shisa, coral walls, and white-sand roads preserve the village townscape.

  • Taketomi Island

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Traditional townscape island 6 km southwest of Ishigaki, with coral-stone walls and red-tile houses.

  • West Pier

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    National tangible cultural property pier in Taketomi, about 105 meters long, popular for photos and sunsets.

  • Taketomi Island Yugafu-kan Visitor Center

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-2488

    Introduces Taketomi nature, rituals, performing arts, and traditional culture through panels, video, and audio.

  • Hateruma Island Star Observation Tower

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-8112

    Observe Hateruma's starry sky, little affected by artificial light or jet streams, via a 200mm telescope. Closed for aging.

  • Takanazaki

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Eroded Ryukyu limestone cape on southeast Hateruma, with about 1 km of sheer cliffs.

  • Hamashitan Grove

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-6191

    Centuries-old hamashitan trees rooted in uplifted coral on east Hateruma, a Taketomi Town natural monument.

  • Kotomori (National Historic Site)

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-6191

    Ryukyu Kingdom limestone fire lookout and national historic site overlooking Hateruma and Iriomote.

  • Hateruma Island

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Japan's southernmost inhabited island, 42 km southwest of Ishigaki, with Nishihama among Japan's best beaches.

  • Shimusuke (Town-Designated Historic Site)

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-6191

    Old well on northeast Hateruma Island; spring water still flows and once sustained islanders.

  • Kuroshima Research Institute

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-4341

    Southernmost small aquarium and museum displaying coral specimens, folk tools, and sea turtles.

  • Kuro Island

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Island with more cattle than people, heart-shaped, famed for coral and turtle nesting.

  • Kuroshima Visitor Center

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-4149

    Built on a Satsuma Domain office site, exhibiting farm tools, folk tools, household goods, and Harvest Festival boats.

  • Hatoma Island

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-82-5445

    Small island north of Iriomote with a 1948 lighthouse, untouched beaches, and emerald sea views.

  • Yubu Island

    Taketomi, Okinawa · 0980-85-5470

    Small sand island crossed slowly by water buffalo cart, a subtropical plant paradise with gardens and Manta Beach.
