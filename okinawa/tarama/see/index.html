Sights in Tarama, Okinawa

Tarama · Okinawa · 4 places

See

  • Yaeyama Lookout

    Tarama, Okinawa · 0980-79-2011

    National historic site on Tarama Island's highest point, built around the 17th century to watch ships and enemies.

  • Shiokawa Utaki Plant Community and Fukugi Avenue (Prefectural Natural Monument)

    Tarama, Okinawa

    A 650-meter fukugi avenue leads to revered Shiokawa Utaki; the plant community is a prefectural natural monument.

  • Tarama Village Hometown Folklore Museum

    Tarama, Okinawa · 0980-79-2223

    Museum of Tarama folk culture displaying old documents, Chinese ceramics, and August Dance costumes and scripts.

  • Upumeka Prefectural Historic Site

    Tarama, Okinawa

    Huge box-stone clan tomb honoring Ntabaru Tuyumiya, one of Miyako's five great tombs.
