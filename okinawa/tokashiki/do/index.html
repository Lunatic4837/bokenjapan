Things to do in Tokashiki, Okinawa

Tokashiki · Okinawa · 17 places

Do

  • Aharen Beach

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 098-987-2333

    Popular Tokashiki Island beach with 800 meters of white sand and Kerama Blue sea.

  • Tokashiku Beach

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 098-987-2333

    Bay beach in the island center where sea turtles come to feed.

  • Tokashiki Island

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Short boat ride from Naha, with white-sand beaches, clear blue sea, observatories, and prime whale-watching.

  • Tokashiki Island Trekking

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Hilly Tokashiki exceeds 200m, with plant-filled trails to observatories and a scenic 1km path completed in 2017.

  • Tokashiki Island Diving Service Relax Island

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 090-7986-6124

    Small-group diving service on Tokashiki Island in the Kerama Islands for beginners to advanced divers.

  • Tokashiki Island Whale Watching

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Wild whales visit Kerama waters late December to late March to birth and raise young; Tokashiki rates are high.

  • Tokashiki Village Youth Travel Village

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 098-987-2323

    Campground beside Aharen Beach with tent sites, cooking and shower buildings, auto camping, and a botanical garden.

  • Snorkeling

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Tokashiki's most popular Aharen Beach has calm clear shallows, coral, and colorful tropical fish.

  • Hanare Island

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Uninhabited island off Aharen Beach in the prefecture's clearest water, for snorkeling and marine leisure.

  • Mihanabaru Walking Trail

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    About 1 km trail completed in 2017 on eastern Tokashiki Island, with open sea and sky views.

  • National Okinawa Youth Exchange House Campground (Marine Training Site)

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 098-987-2306

    Tokashiki campground at turtle-inhabited Tokashiku Beach with canoe, kayak, and snorkeling programs.

  • Reef Inn Kuniyoshi & Water Kids

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 098-987-2206

    Small-group diving service guiding all levels through Tokashiki's clear Kerama seas and coral reefs.

  • Stand-Up Paddleboarding

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Paddle standing across Tokashiki's clear sea to spots with sea turtles and coral reefs.

  • Marine Club Kanaloa

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 070-3803-2071

    Marine activity shop on Tokashiki Island attached to a hotel, with underwater photo gifts.

  • Fishing

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Tokashiki's indented eastern coast suits shore fishing, offshore fishing, and trolling for marlin, bonito, and large squid.

  • Diving

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Tokashiki offers many dive spots in praised Kerama Blue waters, with coral colonies along the Kerama Strait.

  • Sea Kayaking

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Highly clear Tokashiki waters let kayakers view coral and tropical fish while paddling the Kerama Blue sea.
