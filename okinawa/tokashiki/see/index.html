Sights in Tokashiki, Okinawa

Tokashiki · Okinawa · 4 places

See

  • Mass Suicide Site

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 098-987-2306

    Over 300 islanders died here in a group suicide on March 28, 1945; monument built in 1993.

  • Tokashiki Village History and Folklore Museum

    Tokashiki, Okinawa · 098-987-2120

    Museum of Tokashiki life, history, and nature, displaying whale specimens and wartime clothing.

  • Aharen Park

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    South-end Tokashiki spot with 360-degree views, white tuff layers, sunrise, and sunset.

  • West Observatory

    Tokashiki, Okinawa

    Observatory on the island's highest peak, Akama Mountain, with views of Kerama and Aguni islands and Kerama Blue.
