Sights in Tomigusuku, Okinawa

Tomigusuku · Okinawa · 9 places

See

  • DMM Kariyushi Aquarium

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa · 098-996-4844

    Aquarium of Okinawan marine life and plants offering feeding and other experience programs.

  • Okinawa Crafts Forest

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa · 098-987-0467

    Facility promoting Okinawa crafts with a permanent exhibition, shared workshops, and rental studios for makers.

  • Former Japanese Navy Underground Headquarters

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa · 098-850-4055

    Partly open Navy bunker from the Battle of Okinawa where about 4,000 soldiers died; command rooms remain.

  • Okinawa Karate Kaikan

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa · 098-851-1025

    Facility preserving Okinawa karate and sharing its birthplace with visitors and tourists.

  • Manko Waterbird and Wetland Center

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa · 098-840-5121

    Education facility for Ramsar-registered Manko, with exhibits, telescopes, and a mangrove boardwalk to observe waterbirds.

  • Senaga Island

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa

    Small resort island south of Naha Airport for sunsets, night views, and close-up plane takeoffs and landings.

  • Senaga Island Sunset Park

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa

    Sea-view lawns on a small island near Naha Airport, famous for planes and sunsets.

  • Madan Bridge

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa

    Stone bridge over the Kokuba River built by King Sho Shin to link Shuri and Nanzan.

  • Tomigusuku City History and Folklore Exhibition Room

    Tomigusuku, Okinawa · 098-856-3671

    Free exhibition introducing Tomigusuku City history and culture from the Jomon period through postwar recovery.
