Do in Tonaki

Tonaki · Okinawa · 6 places

Do

  • Agari Beach

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2066

    Tonaki's only swimming beach and 100-year water sports meet venue, with sunrise and islanders cooling at dusk.

  • Shimajirimo Walking Trail

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2002

    A 2006 trail on southeastern Tonaki Island for trekking among seasonal native plants and sea views.

  • Stargazing and Coconut Crab Watching

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    Countless stars fill the sky; coconut crabs, called the largest land crustaceans, may appear along the quiet coast.

  • Angela Beach

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2066

    Sandy beach used for the film Gunjo; many sea turtles surface near high tide by coastal asa cultivation poles.

  • Sakashi Walking Trail

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    Trail north of the village to Watanji Cape; about 20 minutes one way, sea views, ending at Irisuna Island.

  • Yubuku Beach

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2066

    Quiet white-sand beach with blue gradient sea, odd rocks including a sacred one, and summer turtle tracks.
