Sights in Tonaki, Okinawa

Tonaki · Okinawa · 11 places

See

  • Tonaki Island Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2002

    Red-tile houses stand about one meter below white-sand roads, enclosed by fukugi and stone walls.

  • Tonaki Island Footlights

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    At night, footlights illuminate a 540-meter white-sand road and stone walls to Agari Beach.

  • Tonaki Island Village Walk

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    Designated May 2000 as Japan's 55th Important Traditional Building Preservation District, with red-tile houses and fukugi trees.

  • Sato Utaki

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    Faith center on a hill about 80 meters high, overlooking the village, sea, Okinawa, and Kume.

  • Ufunda Observatory

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2002

    165 m summit observatory in southern Tonaki with views of Kume, Aguni, and the Kerama Islands, plus free binoculars.

  • Nishimori Park Observatory

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    Observatory in northern Nishimori Park reached by 643 steps, viewing Tonaki village plus Irisuna, Kume, and Aguni islands.

  • Fukugi Grove at Former Tonaki Elementary and Junior High School

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2002

    Nine large fukugi trees said to exceed 200 years, a village cultural property since 1992.

  • Shunza

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2002

    A 200-meter screen-like sea cliff of rugged rock, with old stacked-stone road Amanjaki for high-tide passage.

  • Tonaki Island

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    Least populous village in Okinawa: fishing island with traditional buildings and shallow beaches.

  • Tonakizato Ruins

    Tonaki, Okinawa · 098-989-2002

    14th-15th century gusuku site on a hill, thought to be chieftains' worship hall; pottery, iron tools, and carbonized grain excavated.

  • Tida Plaza

    Tonaki, Okinawa

    Large plaza near the port for sunsets over Irisuna Island and stargazing with constellation guide boards.
