Things to do in Yonaguni, Okinawa

Yonaguni · Okinawa · 4 places

Do

  • Shikinajima Clinic (Dr. Koto's Clinic)

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 090-5294-4445

    You can tour the drama clinic set, with waiting and inpatient rooms left intact.

  • Nama Beach

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 0980-87-3577

    Japan's westernmost beach, about 10 minutes from Yonaguni Airport, where the last sunset is seen.

  • Yonaha Folk Tools

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 090-8292-1299

    Makes traditional living tools on Yonaguni Island using only native plants and kuba leaves.

  • Dannu Beach

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 0980-87-6252

    A lapis-blue sea spreads; facility routes and nearby sightseeing information can be checked.
