Sights in Yonaguni, Okinawa

Yonaguni · Okinawa · 7 places

See

  • Irizaki (Japan's Westernmost Point Monument)

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 0980-87-2402

    Japan's westernmost cape; Taiwan visible on clear days, with observatory, lighthouse, and monument.

  • Tachigami Rock

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 0980-87-2402

    Yonaguni's symbol, a rock rising in the sea south of Sanninu-dai.

  • Agarizaki

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 0980-87-2402

    Eastern cape of Yonaguni Island with sheer cliffs, a white lighthouse, and pastures of Yonaguni horses.

  • Yonagunisan Nature Interaction Plaza Ayamihabiru Hall

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 0980-87-2440

    Hall on the Yonaguni moth, one of the world's largest moths and an endangered Okinawa natural monument.

  • Kubura Bari

    Yonaguni, Okinawa

    Yonaguni scenic rock cleft about 3m wide and 7m deep, with a tragic head-tax legend.

  • Tindabana

    Yonaguni, Okinawa

    Sheer cliff at 100 meters in Sonai, Yonaguni, with an erosion cave used as an observatory.

  • Yonaguni Town Traditional Crafts Museum

    Yonaguni, Okinawa · 0980-87-2970

    Built in 1979 to revive postwar-lost Yonaguni weaving; production viewing and sales offered.
