Things to do in Zamami, Okinawa

Zamami · Okinawa · 13 places

Do

  • Furuzamami Beach

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Zamami Island main beach with two Michelin Green Guide Japan stars, white coral sand, and clear blue water.

  • Ama Beach

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2320

    Shallow beach west of Zamami Island facing Ramsar-registered coral seas, where sea turtles may be seen.

  • Kanusuba Zamami

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-996-3383

    Zamami's only five-room small-luxury auberge, serving Ryukyu Innovative dinner made with Okinawa ingredients.

  • Zamami Village Whale Watching Association

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-896-4141

    Association offering winter humpback whale watching tours around Zamami, founded in 1991 for whale protection.

  • Diving Team Ushio

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-3533

    Zamami Island shop offering diving, snorkeling, and whale watching in the Kerama Islands' world-class clear seas.

  • Kerama Kayak Center

    Zamami, Okinawa · 070-6488-7013

    Sea kayak and snorkeling tours on Zamami Island to uninhabited islands, private beaches, and coral seas.

  • Snorkeling

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    From the surface, see Zamami's seabed over 10 meters down, plus coral, fish, and maybe sea turtles.

  • Yuhina Beach

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Small beach on northern Zamami; couples who vow love on the central rock are said to be blessed. No swimming.

  • Cat's

    Zamami, Okinawa · 090-1943-2626

    Zamami dive shop with lodging offering guided dives, snorkeling, and sunset tours.

  • Nishibama Beach

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-3535

    Aka Island's main beach, easily reached from Okinawa mainland; coral snorkeling like Furuzamami.

  • Marine House Shisa Aka Island

    Zamami, Okinawa · 0120-10-2737

    Divers' pension on Aka Island in the Kerama Islands, 50 minutes by high-speed boat from Naha.

  • Hizushi Beach

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Quiet, calm, shallow non-swimming beach facing Kuba Island, with beautiful summer sunsets.

  • Kawai Diving

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Long-established Aka Island dive shop on a white beach offering courses, boat dives, and snorkel tours.
