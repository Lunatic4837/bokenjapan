Sights in Zamami, Okinawa

Zamami · Okinawa · 25 places

See

  • Kaminohama Observatory

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Spacious west-facing Zamami observatory with tables and benches, sunset views, and sacred Shirugusuku islet offshore.

  • Marilyn Statue

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Statue of dog Marilyn waiting for Shiro and looking toward his statue on Aka Island.

  • Mount Takatsuki

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Kerama's finest scenic spot at 137 m on southern Zamami, overlooking the Kerama Islands.

  • Mesenosaki Observatory

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Observatory on Zamami's west end with a cliff path, crashing waves, and beautiful sunsets.

  • Chishi Observatory

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Cliff observatory about a ten-minute walk from Asa, viewing surrounding islands and Aguni on clear days.

  • Inazaki Observatory

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Views Tonaki and Kume islands and rough outer-sea waves; called Whale Observatory for winter whale searches.

  • Zamami Island

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Popular for summer swimming and diving, winter whale watching, and many observatories.

  • Takara Family Residence

    Zamami, Okinawa

    House of a former official-ship captain on Geruma Island, a national important cultural property.

  • Aka Bridge

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-3535

    Bridge linking Geruma and Aka with Kerama Blue views, where sea turtles are sometimes seen.

  • Geruma Bridge

    Zamami, Okinawa

    240-meter 1989 bridge linking Geruma and Fukaji, with Kerama Blue views and a seaside school.

  • Shiro Statue

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Statue at Aka Port of Shiro, who swam from Aka to Zamami to meet Marilyn.

  • Peace Flame Collection Monument

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-3535

    Monument on Aka Island, the US forces' first Okinawa landing, where fire for the Peace Flame was collected.

  • Ie Villagers Internment Site Memorial

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Monument at a former camp where US forces interned Ie Island villagers on the Kerama islands in World War II.

  • Amagusuku Observatory

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Overlooks Tokashiki, Geruma, and Kuba islands with Kerama Blue near the harbor.

  • Kushibaru Observatory (Ushirohara Observatory)

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-3535

    Westernmost observatory on Aka Island, a sunset spot together with the beach below.

  • Takibaru Observatory (Nakadake Observatory)

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-3535

    Hilltop observatory in central Aka Island with 360-degree views of the island and nearby Kerama Islands.

  • Azanamui Observatory

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    The island's only observatory, a southeast pavilion viewing Geruma Bridge, Amuro, Agenashiku, and Zamami.

  • Kerama Deer Habitat Monument

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2278

    Habitat monument for Kerama deer, a natural monument living only in the Kerama Islands.

  • Aka Island

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Relaxed island 4.7 km south of Zamami, known for the Kerama deer natural monument.

  • Geruma Island

    Zamami, Okinawa

    Quiet island about 200 m south of Aka, with untouched coral reefs and Kerama deer.

  • Urun no Ki

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-3535

    Giant akatetsu on Aka Island, over 400 years old and about 13m tall, at a worship site.

  • World Peace Prayer Monument

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Peace monument by Geruma Bridge, built in 1999 where US forces first landed on Japanese soil.

  • Haru Stones

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Ryukyu survey reference stones from 1737-1750, remaining beside Kobato Tower on Geruma Island.

  • Ebisumaru Cooperative Memorial

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    Monument to a former bonito-flake manufacturing cooperative dissolved by depopulation in 1967.

  • Kobato Tower

    Zamami, Okinawa · 098-987-2277

    A memorial on a hill overlooking the Kerama sea, enshrining children killed in the Battle of Okinawa.
