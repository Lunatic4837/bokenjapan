Things to do in Chihayaakasaka, Osaka

Chihayaakasaka, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 3 places

Do

  • Chihaya Park Kongosan Campsite

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka · 0721-74-0056

    Chihaya Garden Kongosan Campground, located near the summit of Mount Kongo in Osaka. The high altitude provides a cool environment even in summer, allowing you to enjoy forest bathing and stargazing.

  • Chihaya River Trout Fishing Spot

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka · 0721-74-0116

    Chihaya River Trout Fishing Ground is where you can enjoy trout fishing in the clear streams of Mount Kongo in Osaka. It has four attractive zones, including weight fishing, which is perfect for beginners and families with children, authentic lure and fly fishing, and a group BBQ.

  • Prefectural Forest (Chihaya Park)

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka · 0721-74-005610

    Located near the summit of Mount Kongo, Osaka's highest point, Prefectural Forest Chihaya Park is a popular spot where you can experience the richness of nature, with spectacular views throughout the seasons, camping, BBQs, stargazing, and more.
