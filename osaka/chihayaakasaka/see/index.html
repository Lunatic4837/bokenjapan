Sights in Chihayaakasaka, Osaka

Chihayaakasaka, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 5 places

Sights

  • Nanko Tanjo Chi

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka · 0721-26-7128

    This is the birthplace of Kusunoki Masashige (Kusunoki-ko), a hero of the Northern and Southern Courts period. It is a spot where you can feel the weight of history, with sites such as the land survey conducted by Toyotomi Hideyoshi and the stone monument erected at the urging of Okubo Toshimichi.

  • Chihaya Star and Nature Museum

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka · 0721-74-0056

    Chihaya Museum of Stars and Nature is located on Mount Kongo (at an altitude of approximately 1,000 meters). It offers stargazing events using a 400mm astronomical telescope, exhibits on Mount Kongo's flora and fauna, and a wide range of nature experience programs.

  • Kongozan Katsuragi Shrine

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka · 0721-74-0005

    Located on the summit of Mount Kongo in Osaka, Kongozan Katsuragi Shrine is a 1300-year-old shrine founded by En no Gyoja and dedicated to Hitokotonushi no Okami. Known as a sacred mountain where Shinto and Buddhism are intertwined, surrounded by abundant nature, it is ideal for pilgrimages combined with hiking.

  • Kongo Yama Lookout

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka

    This observation deck is located near the summit of Mount Kongo in Osaka. On clear days, it offers a spectacular view of Kansai International Airport and the surrounding mountains. The adjacent grassy park is perfect for a picnic.

  • Takemikura Shrine

    Chihayaakasaka, Osaka · 0721-72-0534

    Located in Chihayaakasaka Village, Osaka Prefecture, Takemizumi Shrine is the guardian deity of Mount Kongo and an ancient shrine associated with Lord Kusunoki Masashige.
