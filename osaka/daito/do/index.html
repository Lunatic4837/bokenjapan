  • Experience sumo!

    Daito · 0570-001-962

    This tourist activity in Osaka allows you to watch sumo practice, experience it, and even make chanko nabe. Under the guidance of an amateur sumo wrestler, you can try on sumo wearing a mawashi and sumo suit.
