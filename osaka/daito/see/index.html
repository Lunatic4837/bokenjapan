Sights in Daito, Osaka

Daito, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

See

  • Morofuku Tenmangu Shrine

    Daito · 072-872-2576

    This shrine was established in 1644 and enshrines Sugawara no Michizane. It is known for its main hall, which retains the Momoyama architectural style, and for the enshrined "Tooth God," who is said to be effective against toothache. A danjiri float is pulled during the autumn festival in October.

  • Nozaki Kannon (Jigenji Temple)

    Daito · 072-833-8881

    This is the closest mountain temple to Osaka for the "Nozaki Pilgrimage." It is a Soto Zen temple with an eleven-faced Kannon Bodhisattva as its principal deity, and offers opportunities to experience Zen meditation and sutra copying.
