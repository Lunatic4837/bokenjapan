Sights in Fujiidera, Osaka

Fujiidera, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 3 places

See

  • Karakuni Shrine

    Fujiidera · 072-955-2473

    Located in Fujiidera City, Osaka Prefecture, this historic shrine was founded in the late 5th century. It is an ancient shrine associated with the Mononobe clan, and its lush, green approach has been selected as one of "Osaka's 100 Best Green Spaces."

  • Domyoji

    Fujiidera · 072-955-0133

    This temple has a history of 1,400 years and is associated with Sugawara no Michizane. It is also the birthplace of Domyoji flour, an ingredient in Kansai sakura mochi.

  • Domyoji Tenmangu Shrine

    Fujiidera · 072-953-2525

    Located in Fujiidera City, Osaka Prefecture, Tenmangu Shrine has a history of over 1,400 years. Enshrining Sugawara no Michizane, it is known as a famous plum blossom spot, with approximately 800 plum trees of 80 varieties in full bloom.
