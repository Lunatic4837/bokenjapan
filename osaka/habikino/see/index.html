Sights in Habikino, Osaka

Habikino, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 9 places

See

  • Yoshimura Residence (Yoshimura Family Residence)

    Habikino · 072-954-8022

    The Yoshimura Residence preserves the architectural style of the Momoyama period's Shoin-zukuri architecture.

  • Tsuboi Hachiman Shrine

    Habikino · 072-956-2824

    Tsuboi Hachiman Shrine, located at the southern end of Habikino City, is the guardian deity of the Kawachi Genji clan, the ancestors of Minamoto no Yoritomo, who founded the Kamakura Shogunate.

  • Daikokuji Temple

    Habikino · 072-956-0953

    Founded by En no Ascetic in 665, this is the first sacred site in Japan where Daikokuten appears. It is widely worshipped as the birthplace of Daikokuten, the god of fortune, and also as a sacred site for the Seven Lucky Gods and the Kawachi Saigoku Kannon Temple.

  • Morimoto Shrine

    Habikino · 072-921-2711

    Located in Habikino City, Osaka Prefecture, this shrine enshrines the husband and wife deities Futsunushi-no-Mikoto and Futsunushi-hime-no-Mikoto.

  • Kawachi Konda Haniwa Village Okuraya

    Habikino · 072-958-3344

    This is a haniwa-making experience facility located in Habikino City, Osaka Prefecture, where haniwa making was popular 1,500 years ago. There is also a shop and gallery selling kofun and haniwa goods, and you can also enjoy original haniwa bento lunches.

  • Suichoen Ruins Park

    Habikino · 072-958-1111

    This park was developed around the Suichoen archaeological site, where traces of stone tool making from the Paleolithic period, dating back 20,000 years, were discovered. Known as a stone tool making atelier, visitors can learn about the conditions of that time through videos and exhibits.

  • Sairinji Temple

    Habikino · 072-956-0603

    Located in Habikino City, Osaka Prefecture, this historic temple is said to have been founded by a descendant of the Baekje scholar, Dr. Wang In.

  • Konda Hachimangu Shrine

    Habikino · 072-956-0635

    Japan's oldest Hachiman Shrine is located south of the Konda Gobyoyama Kofun (the tomb of Emperor Ojin) in Habikino City, Osaka Prefecture. It is said to have been built by order of Emperor Kinmei in the late 6th century, and houses a portable shrine, a national treasure, among other items.

  • Nonakaji Temple

    Habikino · 072-953-2248

    Counted as one of the "Three Princes of Kawachi," this temple was built during the Asuka period and is associated with Prince Shotoku.
