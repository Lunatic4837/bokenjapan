  • Pichi Pichi Beach (Hakotsukuri Beach)

    Hannan, Osaka · 072-489-4508

    Pichi Pichi Beach (Hakotsukuri Beach) in Hannan City, Osaka Prefecture, is an artificial beach with attractive white sand and blue waters. In addition to swimming, you can also enjoy BBQs, beach volleyball, and the spectacular view of Awaji Island. There are also plenty of facilities, including showers.
