Sights in Hannan, Osaka

Hannan, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Sights

  • Wanpaku Kingdom

    Hannan, Osaka · 072-472-1890

    Wanpaku Kingdom, located in Hannan City, Osaka Prefecture, is a popular park where you can enjoy a 200m-long slide into the mouth of a giant dinosaur, athletic activities, and BBQs.

  • Yamanakadani Cherry Blossoms

    Hannan, Osaka · 072-471-5678

    Located right next to JR Yamanakakei Station! Yamanakakei is one of Osaka's most famous spots, with 1,000 cherry trees lining the river. Enjoy seasonal flowers, including hydrangeas in early summer and cluster amaryllis in autumn, as well as the spring illuminations. Get detailed information on access and attractions.
