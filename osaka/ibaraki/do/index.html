Do

  • Ai-jinja Shrine

    Ibaraki · 072-643-7778

    Located in Ibaraki City, Osaka Prefecture, this shrine is an Engishiki-listed shrine dedicated to the ancestral deity of the Nakatomi clan. Known for its connection to Fujiwara no Kamatari, it is a historical shrine where a mirror designated as an important work of art was unearthed.
