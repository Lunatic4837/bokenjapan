Sights in Ibaraki, Osaka

Ibaraki, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 8 places

See

  • Dainenji Temple (Ibaraki City)

    Ibaraki · 072-643-7678

    This Jodo sect temple boasts one of the oldest histories in Ibaraki City, Osaka Prefecture, and is associated with Fujiwara no Kamatari. It was founded in the Asuka period by Fujiwara no Kamatari and his eldest son, Joe.

  • Daimonji Temple

    Ibaraki · 072-649-2027

    This Shingon Buddhist temple boasts a history of over 1250 years. Its principal image, Nyoirin Kannon Bodhisattva, and the Four Heavenly Kings are designated as important national cultural properties. It is also famous as a spot for viewing autumn foliage.

  • Higashi Honganji Ibaraki Betsuin

    Ibaraki · 072-622-2903

    This temple belongs to the Otani branch of the Jodo Shinshu sect and is located in Ibaraki City, Osaka Prefecture. Founded in 1603 by Abbot Kyonyo, it boasts a history of 400 years. Highlights include the magnificent temple complex, the largest in Ibaraki City, and the 400-year-old "Kosei Pine."

  • Isora Shrine (also known as Iromizu Shrine)

    Ibaraki · 072-622-4815

    Known since ancient times as "wart water," it is renowned as sacred water with miraculous efficacy in curing not only warts but also various other illnesses. The sacred spring "Tama-no-i" in front of the shrine attracts worshippers seeking to cure warts.

  • Sojiji Temple

    Ibaraki · 072-622-3209

    This famous temple is the 22nd temple of the Thirty-three Kannon Temples of the Western Provinces.

  • Ibaraki City Christian Relics Museum

    Ibaraki · 072-649-3443

    The museum exhibits valuable Christian relics that were passed down in the Senjiji area of Ibaraki City, known as a village of hidden Christians, including the "Statue of St. Francis Xavier (replica)" and "The Fifteen Mysteries of Mary (replica)," which are featured in textbooks.

  • Ibaraki Municipal Kawabata Literature Memorial Hall

    Ibaraki · 072-625-5978

    Kawabata Yasunari, the first Japanese to win the Nobel Prize in Literature, spent his childhood in Ibaraki from the age of three until he graduated from junior high school under the old system.

  • Ibaraki Shrine

    Ibaraki · 072-622-2346

    Located in Ibaraki City, Osaka Prefecture, this is a historic shrine with an inner sanctuary boasting over 1200 years of history, and a main hall that celebrated its 400th anniversary.
