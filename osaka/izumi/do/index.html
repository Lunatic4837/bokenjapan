Things to do in Izumi, Osaka

Izumi, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Izumi City Youth Center (Izumi YMCA Camp Center)

    Izumi · 0725-92-0422

    This social education facility is located in the south of Kongo-Ikoma-Kisen Quasi-National Park, surrounded by the natural beauty of Mt. Makio (601m) and Mt. Yatsugamaru (422m). It offers hands-on activities through camping and outdoor activities.

  • Flowers, greenery and fresh produce - Kokkaen

    Izumi · 0725-92-2737

    Located in Izumi City in southern Osaka, this is the largest plant and fresh food store in Osaka and is known as one of the largest gardening centers in western Japan.
