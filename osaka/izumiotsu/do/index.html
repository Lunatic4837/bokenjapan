  • Ikegami-Sone Yayoi Cultural Workshop (Ikegami-Sone Historical Landmark Park)

    Izumiotsu · 0725-20-1841

    This learning facility is located next to the Ikegami-Sone archaeological site, one of the nation's leading Yayoi period sites. Through hands-on experiences such as pottery making and magatama (comma-shaped bead) making, visitors can learn about life in the Yayoi period, 2000 years ago.
