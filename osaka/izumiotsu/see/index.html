  • Izumianaishi Shrine

    Izumiotsu · 0725-32-2610

    This shrine has a history of 1,350 years as the second shrine in Izumi Province. The main hall was built by Toyotomi Hideyori and is an important cultural property, and the grounds are lined with giant camphor trees, designated as natural monuments.
