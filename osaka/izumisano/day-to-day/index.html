Day-to-day services in Izumisano, Osaka

Izumisano, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 4 places

Day to day

  • Rinku Premium Outlets Information Center/Welcome Center

    Izumisano · 072-458-4600

    This tourist information center is located inside the Rinku Premium Outlets, about a six-minute walk from Rinku Town Station. In addition to providing information in Japanese, English, and Chinese, it also has wheelchair rentals and a foreign currency exchange machine.

  • Tourism Hospitality Plaza "Izumisano Machidokoro"

    Izumisano · 072-469-0724

    This is a tourist information hub for the Izumisano City area, located across from Kansai International Airport. In addition to tourist information, it also offers local product sales, transportation ticket sales, and temporary luggage storage.

  • Tourism Exchange Plaza "Rinku Machidokoro"

    Izumisano · 072-474-2056

    This is a tourist information center for Izumisano City and Osaka, certified by the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO). It also sells local products, offers luggage storage, provides foreign currency exchange, and sells various transportation tickets.

  • Kansai International Airport

    Izumisano · 072-455-2500

    The world's first "offshore airport made of a completely artificial island" was built off the coast of Senshu in Osaka Bay. As a world-class airport that operates 24 hours a day, it connects many cities both in Japan and overseas.
