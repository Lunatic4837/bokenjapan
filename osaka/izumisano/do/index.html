Things to do in Izumisano, Osaka

Izumisano, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 9 places

Do

  • e-Stadium Izumisano

    Izumisano

    This e-sports facility is directly connected to Rinku Town Station, one stop away from Kansai Airport Station, and offers free access to professional gaming PCs. Beginners, families, and children can all enjoy themselves with peace of mind.

  • Rinku Pleasure Town SEACLE

    Izumisano · 072-461-4196

    Located near Kansai International Airport, this amusement facility is fun for the whole family. It features a large Ferris wheel called "Rinku no Hoshi" and a hot spring facility called "Rinku no Yu."

  • Rinku Premium Outlets

    Izumisano · 072-458-4600

    Located across from Kansai International Airport, this is one of the largest outlet malls in western Japan, where you can enjoy shopping at around 250 stores selling famous domestic and international brands.

  • Exciting Kansai Airport Tour Plan "Exploring the Behind the Scenes at Kansai Airport"

    Izumisano · 072-455-2082

    This bus tour features an official guide introducing the facilities and work at Kansai International Airport. You can also see security areas that are normally off-limits, such as the in-flight catering factory and the international cargo area, from the bus window.

  • Kitashoji Sake Brewery

    Izumisano · 072-468-0850

    Founded in 1921, this is the only sake brewery in Izumisano City, Osaka Prefecture. They produce local sake, including their flagship brand "Shono Sato," and offer brewery tours, a cafe, and tasting/sales.

  • Izumisano Fisheries Cooperative Open-Air Market

    Izumisano

    The open-air market inside Izumisano Fishing Port sells fresh seafood caught that day. There are also restaurants and sushi shops inside the market, so you can enjoy lively, fresh fish on the spot.

  • Kanku Ice Arena

    Izumisano · 072-463-6880

    Located in Rinku Town overlooking Kansai Airport, this year-round international standard ice skating rink has seating for up to 1,500 people.

  • Kansai Airport Observation Hall "Sky View"

    Izumisano · 072-455-2082

    An observation facility where you can enjoy a 360° panoramic view of the runway and passenger terminal building. This is the best vantage point for watching planes take off and land up close. It also houses one of Japan's largest aviation-related shops and a Sky Museum.

  • KIX DUTY FREE, a duty-free shop directly operated by Kansai Airport

    Izumisano · 072-455-4195

    This is a duty-free shop directly operated by Kansai International Airport. Boasting one of the largest selections in the airport, it offers a wide variety of products including global cosmetics, perfumes, alcohol, tobacco, watches, and souvenirs, including brands making their debut in Japan.
