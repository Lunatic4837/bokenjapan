Sights in Izumisano, Osaka

Izumisano, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 12 places

See

  • Rinku Town

    Izumisano

    Located across the water from Kansai International Airport, known as the "gateway to the world," this international city is a popular tourist spot in southern Osaka, where shopping, accommodation, gourmet food, parks, beaches and more are all gathered together.

  • Rinku Park

    Izumisano · 072-469-7717

    This park has an excellent location, overlooking planes taking off from Kansai International Airport and Awaji Island and the Akashi Kaikyo Bridge in the distance. The sunset from Marble Beach, which has been selected as one of the top 100 sunsets in Japan, is a must-see.

  • Oiseki Park

    Izumisano · 072-469-3131

    Located in Izumisano City, Osaka Prefecture, this park is one of the "100 Best Green Spots in Osaka." It is a naturally rich park known for the clear streams and strange rock formations of the Roji Valley, and for its cherry blossoms, Somei Yoshino, which bloom in the spring.

  • Nakami Shrine

    Izumisano · 072-462-7080

    Located in Izumisano City, Osaka Prefecture, this shrine was founded in the late Heian period.

  • okami Shrine (Izumisano City)

    Izumisano · 072-468-0540

    Located in Izumisano City, Osaka Prefecture, this shrine enshrines Takaokami, the goddess of rain. The main hall, built in 1442, is the oldest Kasuga-style building in Osaka Prefecture and is designated as an Important Cultural Property of Japan.

  • Jigen-in Temple

    Izumisano · 072-467-0092

    This is the oldest temple in Senshu, built at the request of Emperor Tenmu. The pagoda, built during the Kamakura period, is designated as a national treasure.

  • Hinone Shrine

    Izumisano · 072-467-1162

    Hine Shrine, located in Izumisano City, Osaka Prefecture, is a historic shrine known for its annual festival, the "Makura Matsuri," held in May.

  • Hibashiri Shrine

    Izumisano · 072-459-7511

    The enshrined deity is Kagutsuchi-no-kami, who is said to be the god who controls fire and water.

  • Mount Inunaki

    Izumisano · 072-469-3131

    Mount Inunaki is known as a sacred place where Japan's oldest Shugendo training center, Shipporyu-ji Temple, is located, and is also known as the Women's Omine.

  • Inunakiyama Shippo-ryuji Temple

    Izumisano · 072-459-7101

    Founded in 661 (the 7th year of the reign of Empress Saimei), this is the head temple of the Inunaki school of Shingon Buddhism. The principal image is Kurikara Dairyu Acala, known as the guardian deity of wish fulfillment and the protector of good fortune.

  • Arito Shrine

    Izumisano · 072-465-0897

    This ancient shrine in Izumisano City, Osaka Prefecture, is deeply connected to the history and culture of the Heian period poet Ki no Tsurayuki, the legend of the shrine's name in "The Pillow Book," and the Noh play "Ant Pass" by Zeami. It also hosts Noh performances.

  • Former Mukai Family Residence

    Izumisano · 072-447-6766

    This thatched-roof farmhouse is a well-preserved example of Edo-period architecture in the Sennan region. Believed to have been built around 1802 (Kyowa 2), it features distinctive architectural techniques such as a staggered four-room layout and high ceilings.
