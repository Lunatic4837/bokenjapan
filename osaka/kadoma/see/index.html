  • Panasonic Museum

    Kadoma · 06-6906-0106

    A corporate museum commemorating the 100th anniversary of Panasonic's founding, which aims to pass on the spirit of founder Konosuke Matsushita and Panasonic's "manufacturing philosophy" to future generations.

  • Shinshu Otaniha Komyozan Gantokuji Temple

    Kadoma · 06-6902-8753

    This Otani sect Shinshu temple in Kadoma City, Osaka Prefecture, originated as a Nembutsu training hall opened by the 8th head priest of Honganji, Rennyo, at the end of the 15th century.
