  • Kaizuka Town Station (Kaizuka City Tourist Information Center)

    Kaizuka · 072-432-1244

    This general information center handles all things related to tourism in Kaizuka City, including providing tourist information about the city, selling traditional crafts and local specialties, and accepting volunteer tourist guides. It also has a baby station (for breastfeeding and diaper changing).
