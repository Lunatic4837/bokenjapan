Sights in Kaizuka, Osaka

Kaizuka, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 7 places

See

  • Nishikinohama Park Seaside Green Space

    Kaizuka · 072-437-0202

    This seaside spot offers a variety of activities, including barbecues and jet skiing. You can also enjoy board sailing and jet skiing at the jet sports facility.

  • Nishikinohama Beach

    Kaizuka · 072-432-3022

    This Osaka beach is impressive for its beautiful green pine groves and white sand. You can enjoy clam digging from early April to early June, and swimming from mid-July to late August.

  • Nishikinohama Shellfish Digging Area

    Kaizuka · 072-432-3022

    Nishikinohama Beach Kaizuka City is a spacious beach stretching for about 1km, a 10-minute walk from Nishikinohama Station on the Nankai Main Line. Clam digging is permitted every year between April and June, and the beach is bustling with families.

  • Osaka Prefectural Nishikinohama Park

    Kaizuka · 072-422-0442

    The park's symbolic sailing ship mast monument stands out against the blue sky, and Nishiki Beach, with its white sand and pleasant sea breeze, is the closest swimming beach to central Kansai.

  • Kanden Shrine

    Kaizuka · 072-422-1446

    Located in Kaizuka City, Osaka Prefecture, this shrine is the tutelary deity (guardian deity of the land) of Kaizuka Jinanimachi. Formerly known as Kanda Kawara Daimyojin, it is a shrine with a rich historical background.

  • Mizumadera Temple

    Kaizuka · 072-446-1355

    This ancient temple is said to have been founded by Gyoki at the request of Emperor Shomu in 744. It is deeply revered as a Kannon statue that wards off evil spirits, and is the fourth temple on the New Saigoku Pilgrimage.

  • Wakihama Ebisu Taisha Shrine (Takaokami Shrine)

    Kaizuka · 072-422-5598

    Along with Imamiya Ebisu and Nishinomiya Ebisu, it is known as one of the three major Ebisu shrines and is the oldest Ebisu shrine in Senshu.
