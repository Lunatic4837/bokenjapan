Things to do in Kanan, Osaka

Kanan, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Katsuragi Onsen (Warudo Ranch Ichi no Yu)

    Kanan, Osaka · 0721-93-6655

    Located within World Ranch in Kanan-cho, Osaka, "Ichinoyu" is a natural hot spring. Equipped with an open-air bath, sauna, and waterfall shower, it's perfect for refreshing yourself after leisure activities or a round of golf. At a reasonable price of 600 yen for adults, it's also recommended for families.

  • Warudo Ranch

    Kanan, Osaka · 0721-93-6655

    World Ranch, located at the foot of Mount Kongo in Osaka, is a popular Western-style spot where you can interact with 1,500 animals of 140 different species.
