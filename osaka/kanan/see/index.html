Sights in Kanan, Osaka

Kanan, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 4 places

Sights

  • Kokawa-dera Temple

    Kanan, Osaka · 0721-93-2814

    Kokawa-dera Temple in Kawanami, Osaka Prefecture, is an ancient temple known as the final resting place of the poet Saigyo Hoshi. It houses the Saigyo Memorial Hall and his tomb, and is also famous as a scenic spot for its magnificent cherry blossoms in spring.

  • Kanayama Kofun

    Kanan, Osaka · 0721-93-2500

    Kanayama Kofun, located in Kawanami Town, Osaka Prefecture, is one of Japan's largest double-circular burial mounds, unique in its gourd shape. Believed to have been constructed in the 6th or 7th century, the horizontal stone chamber contains two stone coffins.

  • Osaka Prefectural Chikatsu Asuka Fudoki Hill

    Kanan, Osaka · 0721-93-8321

    Chikatsu Asuka Fudoki no Oka in Minamikawachi District, Osaka Prefecture, is a historic park that preserves the Isuka Kofun group of 102 tombs. It is possible to tour the interiors of 40 of the tombs.

  • Osaka Prefectural Chikatsu Asuka Museum

    Kanan, Osaka · 0721-93-8321

    The Osaka Prefectural Chikatsu Asuka Museum, located in Kawanami-cho, Osaka, is a museum themed around ancient burial mound culture. Its dynamic architecture, designed by Tadao Ando, is a must-see.
