  • Do Sumou!

    Kashiwara · 072-971-6334

    Founded in 1914, this is the oldest winery still standing in western Japan, and began using sake brewing techniques to make wine.

  • Kashiwara City Tourist Grape Center

    Kashiwara · 072-971-8308

    A tourist spot near Osaka city where you can enjoy all-you-can-eat fresh Kashiwara grapes (Kawachi grapes and Katsugata grapes) picking.
