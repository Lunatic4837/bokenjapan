Sights in Kashiwara, Osaka

Kashiwara, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 5 places

See

  • Kotokuji Temple

    Kashiwara · 072-979-0029

    This historic temple, which is also the origin of the place name Gantaobata, belongs to the Shinshu Otani sect and enshrines Amida Buddha as its principal image.

  • Tsuboi-dera Temple

    Kashiwara · 072-972-1090

    This temple of the Yuzu Nembutsu sect enshrines a Bodhisattva with a unique legend of confronting the god of thunder. The principal image is Amida Buddha. It houses a bronze standing Bodhisattva statue, which is a designated cultural property of Osaka Prefecture.

  • Kanayamahime Shrine

    Kashiwara · 072-979-0683

    In this region, where mining and industry have flourished since ancient times, Kanayamahime is said to have been worshipped as its guardian deity.

  • Kanayamahiko Shrine

    Kashiwara

    Kanayamahiko Shrine, located in Kashiwara City, Osaka Prefecture, has long been worshipped as the local guardian deity.

  • Takaida Cave Park

    Kashiwara · 072-976-3430

    Located north of JR Takaida Station, this park preserves and opens to the public the Takaida Cave Group, a national historic site. Visitors can see ancient tombs and murals dating from the mid-6th century to the early 7th century, as well as Takaidayama Kofun.
