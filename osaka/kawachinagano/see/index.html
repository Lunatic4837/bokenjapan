Sights in Kawachinagano, Osaka

Kawachinagano, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 11 places

See

  • Toothpick Reference Room

    Kawachinagano · 0721-52-2901

    This unique exhibition facility allows visitors to learn about the differences, history, and manufacturing methods of toothpicks from Japan and around the world.

  • Osaka Prefectural Flower Garden

    Kawachinagano · 0721-63-8739

    The park boasts an area three times larger than Koshien Stadium, and visitors can enjoy seasonal flowers in decorative flower beds, rose gardens, hydrangea gardens, etc.

  • Tenjin Shrine

    Kawachinagano

    Located in Kawachinagano City, Osaka Prefecture, this shrine is known as the guardian deity of the seven villages of Takihata.

  • Iwawaki Temple

    Kawachinagano · 0721-62-4000

    This sacred site of Katsuragi Shugendo is located on the mountainside of Mt. Iwawaki (500m above sea level). It houses the Tahoto Pagoda, an important cultural property, and a seated statue of Vairocana Buddha from the late Heian period.

  • Takihata Falls

    Kawachinagano · 0721-53-1111

    This scenic spot is located in the mountains of Kawachinagano City, near the border with Wakayama Prefecture. Home to numerous waterfalls, including Otaki, Gokotaki, and Gongentaki, it is popular as a hiking trail and outdoor spot.

  • Takihata Hometown Cultural Heritage Forest Center

    Kawachinagano · 0721-63-0201

    The museum preserves and exhibits many cultural assets, including folklore, arts and crafts, and traditional houses, from the Takihata area, which was to be submerged by the construction of the Takihata Dam.

  • Takihata Dam

    Kawachinagano · 0721-62-3672

    The affectionate "Mandarin Ducks of Takihata Dam" is one of the Eight Views of Shinkawachinagano. Enjoy a stroll around the lush dam lake. This multipurpose dam was completed in 1982, and the surrounding area is equipped with a campground, walking trails, fishing ponds, and more.

  • Yakujusan Enmeiji Temple

    Kawachinagano · 0721-62-2261

    Founded during the Konin era (Heian period), this temple is known for its 1,000-year-old "Sunset Maple Tree," a natural monument designated by Osaka Prefecture.

  • Kanshinji Temple

    Kawachinagano · 0721-62-2134

    Located on the border of Osaka, Nara, and Wakayama prefectures, Kanshinji Temple is an ancient temple of the Koyasan Shingon sect that was founded by En no Gyoja and rebuilt by Kukai (Kobo Daishi).

  • Nagano Park

    Kawachinagano · 0721-62-2772

    Comprised of five areas (Nagano, Kawai-ji, Kanshin-ji/Maruyama, Enmei-ji, and Amano-san) scattered at the foot of the Kongo Mountains, this park is rich in nature and allows visitors to experience the changing seasons. It is known as a famous spot for cherry blossoms and autumn leaves.

  • Mount Iwawaki

    Kawachinagano

    Located in Kawachinagano City, Osaka Prefecture, this 897.7m high mountain is covered in beautiful waves of Japanese pampas grass on its summit. The autumn "Japanese pampas grass" is a spectacular sight, and the mountain trails are well maintained, making it a popular hiking course.
