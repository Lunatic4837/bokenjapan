Where to stay in Kawachinagano, Osaka

Kawachinagano, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 6 places

Stay

  • 光滝寺キャンプ場

    Kotakiji Campsite

    Stay in Kawachinagano

    This lush green campsite is conveniently located about 1 hour and 30 minutes by car from downtown Osaka and about 30 minutes from downtown Kawachinagano. Located upstream from Takihata Dam, you can enjoy hiking to the scenic spot "Hikaritaki," auto camping, and fishing.

  • 天然温泉 憩の宿 河内長野荘

    Natural Hot Spring Ikoi no Yado Kawachinaganoso

    Stay in Kawachinagano

    This hot spring inn boasts a historic natural hot spring that is said to have been gushing out since the time of Empress Suiko, and seasonal kaiseki cuisine. Located about 30 minutes from Osaka city, it is easily accessible, and you can enjoy overnight stays, day trips to the baths, and meals.

  • 天見温泉

    Amami Onsen

    Stay in Kawachinagano

    This historic hot spring resort was established in the 14th century. It features a building designated as a Registered Tangible Cultural Property, designed by the renowned architect Kingo Tatsuno, and a 3,000-tsubo (approximately 10,000 square meter) Japanese garden.
