Things to do in Kishiwada, Osaka

Kishiwada, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 4 places

Do

  • Town Development Hall

    Kishiwada · 072-433-3511

    Located in Kishiwada City, Osaka Prefecture, this is a rest area for tourists and a community facility for local residents. It offers a tourist information guide system and tea service, and is used as a base for sightseeing in Kishiwada.

  • Nankai Namikiri Hall (Kishiwada City Namikiri Hall)

    Kishiwada · 072-439-4173

    The facility is fully equipped with a variety of large and small rooms, including a large hall with 1,558 seats, a small hall with 288 seats, a multipurpose hall, special conference rooms, and a communication hall.

  • Kishiwada Cancan Bayside Mall

    Kishiwada · 072-436-9955

    This large, locally-focused shopping mall is located in the Kishiwada Bay area.

  • Kishikan (Kishiwada Tourist Farm)

    Kishiwada · 072-479-2256

    This farm is located in Kishiwada City, Osaka Prefecture. From January to late May, you can enjoy all-you-can-eat 'Nyoho' strawberry picking with no time limit. A charcoal barbecue facility is also available, making it popular with families and groups.
