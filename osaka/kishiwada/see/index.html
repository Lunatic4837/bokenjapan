Sights in Kishiwada, Osaka

Kishiwada, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 11 places

See

  • Kishiwada Nature Museum

    Kishiwada · 072-423-8100

    The facility introduces the nature of southern Osaka, centered around Kishiwada, through a variety of specimens and dioramas.

  • Fugu Museum

    Kishiwada · 072-422-3929

    The museum displays numerous items related to pufferfish, including skeletal specimens and folk art. Founded in 1964 by Kiichi Kitahama, it boasts a collection of 2,500 items related to pufferfish.

  • Gofusou

    Kishiwada · 072-438-1152

    The former site of the new teahouse of the Okabe clan, the former lord of Kishiwada Castle, is a mansion built over a period of 10 years, and a beautiful strolling garden with a teahouse and snow-viewing lanterns.

  • Kitasaka Town Tourist Farm

    Kishiwada · 072-428-5300

    This tourist farm in Kishiwada City offers a panoramic view of Osaka Bay. You can experience mandarin orange picking and sweet potato digging, and after picking you can even enjoy sukiyaki. This is a spot where both adults and children can enjoy a day in nature.

  • Tenshoji Temple (Octopus Jizo)

    Kishiwada · 072-422-0773

    This temple is known for the legend that an octopus and a white monk (Jizo Bodhisattva) saved Kishiwada Castle from falling. The Jizo Bodhisattva is enshrined in one of Japan's largest Jizo halls.

  • Kishiwada Castle

    Kishiwada · 072-431-3251

    Kishiwada Castle served as the southern defense for Osaka Castle during the Edo period. The current castle tower was rebuilt in 1954, and in addition to being able to enjoy document exhibits and a watchtower, the "Hachijin Garden" designed by Mirei Shigemori is also a must-see.

  • Kishijo Shrine

    Kishiwada · 0724-22-0686

    Located east of the Kishiwada Castle keep, this shrine is the guardian deity of the castle and a sacred site for the Kishiwada Danjiri Festival. It is widely worshipped as the god of matchmaking.

  • Sengoji Temple

    Kishiwada · 072-427-5610

    This is the family temple of the Okabe family, lords of Kishiwada Castle in Izumi Province. It originated as a temple in the retirement residence of Okabe Minokami Yoshikatsu, the first lord of Kishiwada Castle, and has served as the family temple for successive generations of the Okabe family.

  • Tsumikawa Shrine

    Kishiwada · 072-479-0134

    Tsumikawa Shrine, one of the five shrines of Izumi Province, was founded during the reign of Emperor Sujin. Prayers for rain were offered here during a severe drought, and the main hall, rebuilt by Toyotomi Hideyori, is a designated Important Cultural Property of Japan.

  • Tomboike Park

    Kishiwada · 072-443-9671

    Located in Kishiwada City, Osaka Prefecture, this vast park is approximately the size of 16.5 Koshien Stadiums. You can enjoy seasonal flowers such as roses, hydrangeas, and daffodils, as well as large playground equipment with dragonfly and butterfly motifs, and a long slide.

  • Kishiwada Danjiri Kaikan

    Kishiwada · 072-436-0914

    This museum allows visitors to experience the history and excitement of the Kishiwada Danjiri Festival, which boasts a 300-year tradition, through actual exhibits and the latest video technology.
