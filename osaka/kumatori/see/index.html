Sights in Kumatori, Osaka

Kumatori, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 3 places

Sights

  • Eiraku Dam

    Kumatori, Osaka

    Eiraku Dam in Kumatori Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a spectacular spot where approximately 700 cherry trees bloom. The 2km circular path around the dam is perfect for walking or running. Along with the hiking trails in the adjacent Okuyama Ameyama Natural Park, this is a great spot to enjoy nature in all four seasons.

  • Nakaya House

    Kumatori, Osaka · 072-452-1001

    The Nakaya Residence in Kumatori Town, Osaka Prefecture, was the home of a village headman of the Kishiwada Domain, built in the early Edo period, and is designated as an Important Cultural Property of Japan.

  • Station Underground Community Center

    Kumatori, Osaka · 072-451-2572

    The "Ekishita Nigiwai-kan" on the first floor of JR Kumatori Station is the Kumatori Town tourist information center. It provides information on seasonal events, sells local products, offers bicycle rentals, and has a luggage storage service. It's easily accessible, just about 30 minutes by rapid train from Tennoji.
