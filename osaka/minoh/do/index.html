Things to do in Minoh, Osaka

Minoh, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Chusei-en Garden

    Minoh · 072-739-0895

    This tourist farm is located in Minoh City, Osaka Prefecture. From mid-September to early November, it is popular for its fruit-picking activities, such as chestnut gathering, shiitake mushroom picking, and potato digging, as well as barbecues where you can enjoy freshly picked shiitake mushrooms.

  • Kawanishi Garden

    Minoh · 072-739-0609

    This tourist farm is located in the naturally rich Kamitotoromi area of Minoh City. You can enjoy autumn fruit-picking experiences such as digging potatoes, gathering chestnuts, and picking shiitake mushrooms.
