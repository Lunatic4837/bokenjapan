Sights in Minoh, Osaka

Minoh, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 5 places

See

  • Katsuoji Temple: The Temple of Luck

    Minoh · 072-721-7010

    This temple is known as the "Temple of Luck" with a history of 1,300 years. Worshippers come here to pray for success in all aspects of life, including exams, protection from misfortune, business, and sports.

  • Meiji-no-mori Mino Quasi-national Park (Mino Waterfall)

    Minoh · 072-721-3014

    Attraction in Minoh.

  • Tokai Nature Trail

    Minoh

    Experience nature on a 1,697km long walk. Japan's first long-distance nature trail connects Tokyo, Tokyo, two metropolitan areas, and eight prefectures.

  • Ryuanji Temple

    Minoh · 072-721-3003

    Founded by En no Gyoja, this renowned temple houses a principal image considered to be one of Japan's four Benzaiten deities.

  • Minoh Park Insectarium

    Minoh · 072-721-7967

    Located within the nature-rich Meiji-no-Mori Minoh Quasi-National Park, this museum features a butterfly garden where butterflies fly all year round, as well as a wide variety of insect specimens and live displays.
