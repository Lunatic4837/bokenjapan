Things to do in Misakicho, Osaka

Misakicho, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 4 places

Do

  • Sennan Satoumi Park

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-494-2626

    Located at the southernmost tip of Osaka Prefecture, Sennan Satoumi Park is a seaside park featuring a swimming beach, BBQ area, and Japan's only permanent beach volleyball court.

  • Tokimeki Beach (Tanowa Beach)

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-494-2141

    Tokimeki Beach (Tanowa Beach) in Misaki Town, Osaka Prefecture, boasts white sand and crystal-clear water. In spring, you can enjoy clam digging, and in summer, swimming and beach volleyball. The stunning sunsets are also a major draw. A shuttle bus runs from Tanowa Station, making it easily accessible.

  • Fuka Fishing Port Fish Market

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-492-2052

    Fuke Fish Market in Misaki Town, Osaka Prefecture. Freshly caught fish are lined up, and you can enjoy the lively atmosphere of the auction. Be sure to try some exceptional fish, the flesh of which has been firmed by the influence of ocean currents.

  • Tottori Park Kojima

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-447-5126
