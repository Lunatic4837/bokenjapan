Sights in Misakicho, Osaka

Misakicho, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 6 places

Sights

  • Funamori Shrine (Misaki Town)

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-494-3333

  • Tendai Shu Kozen Tera

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-495-0001

    Kozenji Temple, located in Misaki Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a renowned Tendai sect temple founded by Jikaku Daishi Ennin. Its historical triad of statues, including the "Misaki Great Buddha (seated statue of Dainichi Nyorai)," a nationally designated Important Cultural Property, are a must-see.

  • Richiin Temple (Misakicho)

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-495-5162

    Richin Temple in Misaki Town, Osaka Prefecture, was founded by Gyoki at the behest of Emperor Shomu. It houses numerous treasures, including a statue of Fudo Myoo created by Kobo Daishi and artifacts associated with Toyotomi Hideyoshi.

  • Tannowa Yacht Harbor

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-494-2335

    Located at the southernmost tip of Osaka, Tannowa Yacht Harbor is a "sea station" where you can moor boats and enjoy fishing and cruising on rental boats. It also houses the Land Ho, a cafe with a spectacular view of Osaka Bay. We'll introduce the latest information on access, parking, opening hours, and more.

  • Atago Yama

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-492-2730

    Mount Atago in Misaki Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a scenic spot where 800 cherry trees and approximately 50,000 azaleas bloom in the spring. From the top of the mountain, you can enjoy a panoramic view of Osaka Bay, Kansai International Airport, and the Akashi Kaikyo Bridge.

  • Ubusuna Shrine

    Misakicho, Osaka · 072-495-5296

    Located in Misaki Town, Osaka Prefecture, Ubusuna Shrine is a historically significant shrine that has been revered as a sacred mountain since ancient times.
