Things to do in Neyagawa, Osaka

Neyagawa, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Neya Visitors' Farm

    Neyagawa · 072-821-0158

    A tourist farm in Neyagawa City, Osaka Prefecture, where you can enjoy the autumn sweet potato digging experience.

  • Naritasan Fudoson Temple

    Neyagawa · 072-833-8881

    This temple is famous for its devotion to the Fudo Myo-o, and is especially famous for its prayers for traffic safety. It is known as the first temple in Japan to offer prayers for both people (drivers) and vehicles, and is visited by over 2 million people a year.
