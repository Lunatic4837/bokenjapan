Sights in Neyagawa, Osaka

Neyagawa, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 4 places

See

  • Keihan Electric Railway Co., Ltd. Neyagawa Rolling Stock Factory

    Neyagawa · 072-823-6161

    This exhibit introduces the equipment used in the inspection and maintenance of railway vehicles. By observing how vehicles are inspected and maintained, visitors can think about the safety and convenience of railways.

  • Ninnaji Shrine

    Neyagawa

    This shrine is located in Neyagawa City, Osaka Prefecture. The name of the place comes from the fact that it was once the territory of Ninna-ji Temple in Kyoto. It is said to have been founded in the early Muromachi period and is also known as Tenmangu Shrine, which enshrines Sugawara no Michizane.

  • Kanda Tenmangu Shrine

    Neyagawa · 072-827-4640

    This shrine is located in Neyagawa City, Osaka Prefecture. It is famous for its hanging scroll of Sugawara no Michizane, whose face is said to turn red when offerings of sacred sake, and for its large camphor tree (a natural monument-class tree) that is over 800 years old.

  • Kayashima Shrine

    Neyagawa

    This shrine is famous for its gigantic camphor tree (O-kusu), which towers over the platform of Kayashima Station on the Keihan Main Line. It has also been selected as one of Osaka Prefecture's 100 Best Green Spaces.
