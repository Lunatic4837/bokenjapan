Things to do in Nose, Osaka

Nose, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Nose Onsen

    Nose, Osaka · 072-734-0041

    Nose Onsen in Nose Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a relaxing spot surrounded by the lush nature of Satoyama. Day trips to the baths are available from 1,000 yen on weekdays, and the restaurant is popular. Recommended for families and solo travelers.

  • Shionoyu Onsen

    Nose, Osaka · 072-734-0021

    Shionoyu Onsen in Nose, Osaka, is a historic hot spring with a history of over 1,000 years. Enjoy the carbonated cold mineral spring water directly from the source, the relaxing atmosphere of a sukiya-style building, and seasonal cuisine. It is also recommended for treating neuralgia and sensitivity to cold.
