Sights in Nose, Osaka

Nose, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 9 places

Sights

  • An'onji Temple (Nose Town)

    Nose, Osaka · 072-737-0604

    This is a tourist guide to An'onji Temple, located in Nose Town, Osaka Prefecture. Founded by the Heian period monk Genshin, this historic temple houses the Eleven-Faced Kannon Bodhisattva (an Osaka Prefecture designated cultural property), revered as a protector against fire.

  • Joruri Theater

    Nose, Osaka · 072-734-3241

    The Joruri Theater in Nose Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a cultural facility that preserves the Nose Joruri, a national intangible folk cultural asset, for future generations. You can enjoy traditional performing arts with 200 years of history, such as the Joruri Month in June every year and puppet Joruri performances.

  • Mizukoshi Kuri Sono

    Nose, Osaka · 072-734-0863

    Mizukoshi Chestnut Orchard in Nose Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a popular spot where you can enjoy picking fresh chestnuts that have fallen naturally. The vast chestnut fields are changed daily, so you can always find freshly fallen chestnuts.

  • Ichigo Heri Nakagawa

    Nose, Osaka · 090-5660-0928

    Ichigoen Nakagawa in Nose Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a strawberry farm with elevated cultivation systems that are stroller and wheelchair accessible. Enjoy a 30-minute all-you-can-eat buffet of Akihime and Benihoppe strawberries, as well as direct sales.

  • Yamabe Shrine

    Nose, Osaka · 072-734-0715

    Yamabe Shrine, located in Nose Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a historically significant shrine that was once integrated with Getsumoji Temple on Mount Kenbi. The main hall, built in 1668, is a must-see. We'll provide detailed information useful for sightseeing in Nose, including access methods and historical background.
