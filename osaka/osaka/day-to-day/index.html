Day-to-day services in Osaka, Osaka

Osaka, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 19 places

Day to day

  • Tourist Information OSAKA

    Kita Ward, Osaka · +81-6-6131-4550

    This tourist information center is located in front of the central ticket gates of JR Osaka Station, one of Japan's leading terminal stations. It offers multilingual support (English, Chinese, and Korean) and also houses a JR railway information center and a travel service center.

  • Tourist Information NAMBA

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6131-4550

    This tourist information center is located on the first floor of Nankai Namba Station, which is easily accessible from Kansai International Airport. It provides tourist information for the popular Minami area of Osaka.

  • Yodoyabashi Tourist Information Center

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    This convenient tourist information center is located next to Yodoyabashi Station on the Keihan Railway and Osaka Metro Midosuji Line. It provides multilingual information on Osaka's History / culture, as well as tourist spots in Kyoto, Shiga, and other areas.

  • DOTON PLAZA

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6212-6161

    A commercial facility that sells a variety of items, including miscellaneous goods, daily necessities, watches, and precious metals. It also has a tourist information center that provides service in English, Chinese, and Korean.

  • Pivot BASE Travel Cafe @ Tonbori

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 090-9864-2710

    A tourist exchange hub (cafe and pub) that digitally showcases local information rooted in the region and allows locals and tourists to enjoy interacting with each other.

  • ROAD BIKE RENTAL JAPAN

    Miyakojima Ward, Osaka · 050-1722-8801

    We are a specialty shop offering bicycle rentals, cycling tours, and cycling packages. Professional cyclists support your cycling experience throughout Japan, primarily in Osaka and Kyoto.

  • TKTS Tourist Information NAMBA

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 03-6228-1062

    Originally from Broadway in New York, this ticket booth offers discounted tickets to today's and tomorrow's entertainment, including musicals, plays, kabuki, bunraku, music, and sports.

  • WILLER Bus Terminal Osaka Umeda

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6131-4550

    This large bus terminal is located on the first floor of Osaka's landmark, Umeda Sky Building. As a tourist information hub, it is equipped with facilities such as free Wi-Fi, power outlets, coin lockers, and powder rooms.

  • Umeda International Clinic

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 080-6925-4560

    This clinic welcomes a wide range of people, including foreign tourists visiting Japan, who are experiencing health problems during their stay in Osaka. We offer services in English, Chinese, and Korean, and also accept consultations via WhatsApp.

  • Ee-an Osaka Tourist Information Cafe

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6641-1120

    This tourist information center is located right next to Nipponbashi Station in Osaka. It offers local Osaka information not found in guidebooks, luggage storage, reservation services, bicycle rentals, pocket Wi-Fi rentals, sightseeing tours, and more. There is also a cafe on-site.

  • Kirindo Pharmacy (4th floor, Herbis Osaka)

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6454-0602

    Located next to a clinic with various medical departments, this pharmacy can handle medications for a wide range of symptoms. In addition to prescription medications, they also stock over-the-counter drugs. English-speaking staff are also available.

  • Don Quijote Dotonbori store

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-4708-1411

    One of Japan's largest discount stores, it is marked by the yellow Ferris wheel, a landmark of Osaka Minami. It has a 24-hour tourist information center and a duty-free counter, and offers multilingual support.

  • Don Quijote Dotonbori Midosuji Store

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6214-3211

    This information center is located inside a large Don Quijote store facing Midosuji in Dotonbori. It is open 24 hours a day and offers convenient services for foreign visitors to Japan, including multilingual support and a duty-free counter.

  • Volunteer Tourist Guides

    Osaka · 090-3059-6923

    Members of the Osaka Tourist Volunteer Guide Association will convey the charm and excitement of Osaka through free introductions to its history, culture, and tourist attractions.

  • Tourist Information SHIN-OSAKA

    Yodogawa Ward, Osaka · +81-6-6131-4550

    This tourist information center is located on the 3rd floor concourse of JR Shin-Osaka Station. It provides tourist information about Osaka, sells tourist tickets, and provides multilingual support.

  • Shin-Umeda City General Information Center

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6440-3899

    This is the general information center for Shin-Umeda City, where the Umeda Sky Building (Floating Garden Observatory) is located. In addition to providing information about the facility, they also offer advice on sightseeing in Osaka.

  • Nipponbashi General Information Center

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6655-1717

    Tourist information center located at Nipponbashi (Denden Town) in Osaka, providing multilingual maps with information on over 500 stores, baggage storage, foreign currency exchange, and other services.

  • Abeno Harukas Kintetsu Main Store Foreign Customer's Salon

    Abeno Ward, Osaka · 06-6624-1111

    We provide a variety of services to foreign tourists visiting Japan, including tax exemption procedures, distribution of 5% discount coupons, overseas delivery, tourist information, foreign currency exchange, ATMs, and more.

  • Hankyu Tourist Center, Umeda, Osaka

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6131-4550

    This tourist information center is operated by Hankyu Railway, which connects Osaka with Kobe, Takarazuka, and Kyoto. They offer tourist and transportation information, ticket sales, Wi-Fi router rentals, and kimono photo shoot locations.
