    北浜レトロビルヂング

    幸せのフクロウカフェchouette

    Dining in Osaka

    Located in Shinsaibashi, Osaka, this is one of Japan's largest owl cafes, offering a relaxing experience where you can interact with 22 owls representing 17 different species.
