Sights in Osaka, Osaka

Osaka, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 195 places

See

  • Osaka Castle Main Keep (Osaka Castle Museum)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    A symbol of Osaka. 55 meters above ground, with 5 floors and 8 stories, this historic castle was started by Toyotomi Hideyoshi. The current building was rebuilt in 1931 and is a registered tangible cultural property of Japan.

  • Dotonbori

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6211-4542

    Dotonbori, lined with giant three-dimensional billboards such as Shochikuza, Kani Doraku, and the Glico sign, is a representative entertainment district in Osaka's Minami area. You can enjoy food and live entertainment.

  • Tsutenkaku Tower

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6641-9555

    Osaka's landmark tower is registered as a national tangible cultural property. Popular attractions include the golden observation deck, the Billiken statue, and the tower slider.

  • KAIYUKAN

    Minato Ward, Osaka · 06-6576-5501

    This is one of the world's largest aquariums, representing Osaka, and faithfully recreates the natural environment of the Pacific Ocean, the world's largest ocean, and its surrounding waters.

  • Shitennoji Temple

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-0066

    Founded by Prince Shotoku in 593, it was the first official temple of Buddhism in Japan. The temple has survived numerous war disasters and retains the Shitennoji style of temple layout, which conveys the oldest style in Japan.

  • Sumiyoshi Taisha Shrine

    Sumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6672-0753

    It is the head shrine of the approximately 2,300 Sumiyoshi shrines across the country and is the Ichinomiya of Settsu Province.

  • Umeda Sky Building KUCHU-TEIEN OBSERVATORY

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6440-3855

    One of the landmarks of Umeda, Osaka, the open-air rooftop observation deck is popular, offering a 360-degree view of Osaka's cityscape from a height of 173 meters above ground.

  • Shinsekai

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6643-6322

    This downtown area in the southern part of Osaka City is famous for Tsutenkaku Tower Janjan Yokocho, and a large puffer fish lantern sign. You can enjoy gourmet food such as kushikatsu and doteyaki, as well as a retro atmosphere.

  • Nakanoshima Park

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6312-8121

    This waterside park is sandwiched between the Dojima River and the Tosabori River. It has a rose garden with 3,700 roses of 310 different varieties, and its harmonious scenery with the surrounding retro buildings has earned it the title of one of Osaka's 100 best green spaces.

  • Osaka Castle Park

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6755-4146

    Located in the center of Osaka, this vast park covers a total area of 105.6 hectares. In addition to historical buildings such as Osaka Castle Museum, it is also home to numerous spots where you can enjoy seasonal flowers such as cherry blossoms and plum blossoms.

  • Osaka Tennoji Zoo

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-8401

    This zoo has a history of over 100 years. Its popular "ecological exhibits" recreate the landscapes of animal habitats, such as the African Savannah Zone.

  • Osaka Museum of History

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6946-5728

    This museum studies and introduces the history and culture of Osaka spanning nearly 1,400 years. It displays the history from ancient times to the present day through full-scale reconstructions and models.

  • Hozenji Yokocho Alley

    Osaka

    Located in the bustling Minami area of Osaka, this charming stone-paved alley is lined with renowned restaurants and eateries, centered around Mizukake Fudoson.

  • GLION MUSEUM

    Minato Ward, Osaka · 06-6573-3006

    This museum, housed in a renovated red brick warehouse built in 1923, displays approximately 80 precious classic cars from Japan and abroad.

  • OBP (Osaka Business Park)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6946-1048

    Osaka's new city center boasts a site of 26 hectares. In addition to corporate offices, the complex is home to Osaka Castle Hall, shopping facilities, hotels, and more.

  • OCAT (Osaka City Air Terminal)

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6635-3000

    Directly connected to JR Namba Station, this is the largest bus terminal in western Japan. In addition to being the departure and arrival point for airport limousine buses and express buses, this complex also features shopping, restaurants, and a rooftop garden.

  • hu+gMUSEUM

    Nishi Ward, Osaka · 06-6586-3789

    Located in the birthplace of Osaka Gas, this is an information hub for food and housing. It is a showroom where you can see, touch, taste, and experience the rich lifestyles that gas can make possible.

  • Gamoyon neighborhood (Gamo 4-chome)

    Joto Ward, Osaka

    This area is attracting attention for the "Gamoyon Nigiwai Project," a town development project that renovated old houses while preserving the atmosphere of a downtown area. It is popular as a food town lined with retro and modern restaurants.

  • Museum of Living (Ganko Hiranogo House)

    Hirano Ward, Osaka · 06-6796-0728

    This museum is located on the grounds of the Japanese restaurant "Ganko Hiranogoyashiki" in Hirano Ward, Osaka City. Housed in a renovated Edo period village headman's residence, it displays kimonos, pottery, hanging scrolls and other everyday items that evoke Kawachi culture.

  • Sakishima CosmoTower Observatory

    Suminoe Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6055

    252 meters above ground, this magnificent, 360-degree panoramic observation space offers unobstructed views of the Osaka Bay area, Awaji Island, the Akashi Kaikyo Bridge, Kansai International Airport, and more.

  • Tonbori River Walk

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6644-7565

    The Dotonbori waterfront promenade is impressive with its beautiful neon lights reflected on the water's surface. It's also a spot where the Tenjin Festival and Kabuki boat processions take place.

  • MIRACLE WORLD

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 050-1807-4118

    A 20-minute mini cruise of Dotonbori. Guided by an Osaka guide crew, you'll enjoy learning about the inside stories of famous spots and hidden highlights.

  • Namba Grand Kagetsu

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6641-0888

    The Hall of Comedy is a place where the best comedians in Japan gather. From veteran comedians who are the hall's flagship performers to popular young comedians, you can enjoy Yoshimoto Shinkigeki, manzai, and rakugo from morning to night, 365 days a year.

  • Thermos Memorial Museum

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6356-2340

    This museum showcases the 90-year history of Zojirushi Thermos' manufacturing and technological evolution. You can experience and learn about the evolution of the thermos and the wonders of vacuum.

  • Americamura

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    Osaka Minami is a unique town that is the most vibrant and dynamic part of the Kansai region, and a leader in youth culture.

  • Kids Plaza Osaka

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6311-6601

    This is Japan's first full-scale children's museum. It is filled with exhibits and workshops where children can learn while having fun, discovering the wonders of science, encountering world cultures and play.

  • Cosmo Square

    Suminoe Ward, Osaka

    This district in the Osaka Bay Area is home to a concentration of cutting-edge technology development companies, convention facilities such as Intex Osaka, WTC, and ATC, as well as cultural and amenity facilities, and is well-equipped with a variety of business support functions.

  • Knowledge Capital

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6372-6530

    Experience cutting-edge technology, products under development, and new ideas as you play with the "future." A large intellectual entertainment space that can be enjoyed by adults and children alike.

  • Peace Osaka (Osaka International Peace Center)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6947-7208

    This peace museum, which tells the story of the Osaka air raids, opened in a corner of Osaka Castle Park in 1991. It was renovated in 2015 and now commemorates the victims of the Osaka air raids and exhibits materials praying for peace.

  • Mizuno Sportology Gallery

    Suminoe Ward, Osaka · 06-6614-8000

    This gallery displays the history of Mizuno, known for its motto of "Let's make something great," as well as the history of sports and the latest technology. You can enjoy the exhibits of valuable baseball equipment and uniforms for free.

  • Miraiza Osaka Castle

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6755-4320

    This facility is located in a prime location right in front of the castle tower, allowing you to enjoy a leisurely meal and shopping. This historic building, originally built in 1931 as the headquarters of the 4th Army Division, has been renovated and reborn as a shop, restaurant, and museum.

  • LEGOLAND® Discovery Center Osaka

    Minato Ward, Osaka · 0800-100-5346

    This indoor family-friendly experience facility in Tempozan, Osaka, is home to millions of LEGO® blocks. There are plenty of attractions where you can experience the world of LEGO, including a 4D cinema and Miniland, which recreates the streets of Osaka.

  • Bandaiike Park

    Sumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6691-7200

    Bandai Pond, named after the legend of Prince Shotoku's defeat of a demon, is a peaceful park where migratory birds and humans can relax. The pond, which is about 700 meters in circumference, attracts cherry blossom viewers during the cherry blossom season and migratory birds in the winter.

  • Sanko Shrine

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6761-0372

    Sanko Shrine in Tennoji Ward, Osaka City, is known as the shrine for protecting against paralysis, and is a historic shrine where the "Sanada Loophole" remains as part of "Sanadamaru," a place associated with Sanada Yukimura.

  • Mitsudera Temple (Daifuku-in)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6211-1982

    This esoteric Buddhist temple was founded by Gyoki Bodhisattva in 744. Located along Midosuji in Shinsaibashi, Osaka, the main hall, rebuilt in 1808, is a valuable relic that escaped war damage.

  • Kamigata Ukiyo-e Museum

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6211-0303

    The world's only permanent ukiyo-e museum, which mainly houses and exhibits paintings of popular actors from the Bunka and Bunsei periods of the late Edo period, offers visitors the opportunity to enjoy Kamigata ukiyo-e, which are characterized by their realistic depictions.

  • Nakanoshima

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    The Nakasu area, surrounded by water and greenery between the Dojima River and Tosabori River, which flow through the center of Osaka, is home to a concentration of administrative, economic, and cultural facilities.

  • Nakanoshima Festival Tower/Nakanoshima Festival Tower West

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 0570-200-030

    A landmark of Nakanoshima. A complex containing offices, restaurants, shops, the Festival Hall, a hotel, and an art museum within its 200m-tall twin towers.

  • Nakaza Kuidaore Building

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    A gourmet and entertainment complex where you will be greeted by Kuidaore Taro, the symbol of Dotonbori, Osaka. It is scheduled to reopen in the spring of 2025.

  • Kyuhoji Bridge

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This is one of the oldest bridges in Osaka, spanning the Higashi-Yokobori River, dating back to the time of Toyotomi Hideyoshi. Currently, it is a Gerber-type reinforced concrete bridge characterized by its dark green railings and cleanly designed streetlights.

  • Imamiya Ebisu Shrine

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6643-0150

    This shrine is worshipped as the Osaka god of business, Ebessan. Every January, over one million people visit the shrine during the Toka Ebisu festival, and the place is bustling with activity.

  • Imahashi

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This historic bridge spans the Higashi-Yokobori River and even appears in the "Osaka Campaign Map." During the Edo period, the surrounding area was lined with money changers and flourished as a financial center.

  • Denpozan Saienji Temple

    Konohana Ward, Osaka · 06-6461-5602

    Founded by the hermit Hodo in the first year of the Taika era (645), this temple is said to be the oldest place in Japan where Buddhism was introduced.

  • Sumiyoshi Park

    Suminoe Ward, Osaka · 06-6671-2292

    Osaka's oldest park, opened in 1873. Adjacent to Sumiyoshi Taisha Shrine, it is home to the historic "Takatoro" lantern and a stone monument bearing a haiku poem by Matsuo Basho, and is a popular cherry blossom viewing spot in the spring, attracting many visitors.

  • Shinano Bridge

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This bridge once spanned the Nishiyokobori River, and is now a historical spot where a stone monument remains. There are records dating back to the Genroku period (1688-1704), and the surrounding area was once lined with lumber merchants and china merchants.

  • Senkoji Temple

    Hirano Ward, Osaka · 06-6791-2680

    Located in Hirano Ward, Osaka City, this temple is famous for allowing visitors to experience hell and paradise. Popular experiences include being judged by King Enma in the Jigoku-do Hall and meditating surrounded by mandalas in the Land of Buddha experience.

  • Hachikenyahama Pier

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    During the Edo period, this wharf flourished as a departure point for the 30-koku boats connecting Kyoto and Osaka. Today, it has been revived as a port where you can enjoy cruising and is a symbol of the water city of Osaka, serving as a place of relaxation for people.

  • Enjuan (Kama Hachiman)

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6761-3691

    This temple is famous for praying for victory, and it is said that during the Winter Siege of Osaka, Sanada Yukimura prayed for victory by driving a sickle into a sacred tree, achieving a great victory.

  • Kitamido (Tsumura Betsuin)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6261-6796

    This Jodo Shinshu Honganji temple is the origin of the name of Midosuji, a street that runs north and south through Osaka City. It was moved to its current location in 1597 and has now been rebuilt as a modern main hall.

  • Minamihorie and Kitahorie areas

    Nishi Ward, Osaka

    The Horie area is gaining attention as a fashionable part of Osaka. Centered around Tachibana Street, once known as a town of furniture, it is now a trendsetting area with cafes, general stores, and apparel shops.

  • Minamisemba

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    This trendsetting area in Osaka's Minami district is home to a unique lineup of shops, including stylish open-terrace cafes, designer shops, and hair salons that offer unique styles.

  • Kuchinawazaka

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka

    Located in Tennoji Ward, Osaka City, this is one of the "Seven Hills of Tennoji." The name comes from the fact that the undulating road resembles a snake (kuchinawa) when viewed from the bottom. This quiet, stone-paved slope is also known for its monument to the author, Sakunosuke Oda.

  • Manshozan Kichijoji (Temple of the Loyal Samurai)

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-4451

    This is the family temple of the Osaka Asano clan, and it is home to the graves of Asano Takumi-no-kami and the 47 loyal retainers, famous from the Chushingura story. The Osaka Gishi Festival is held here every year on the second Sunday of December.

  • Abiko-san Daisho Kannon-ji Temple (Abiko Kannon)

    Sumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6691-3578

    Japan's oldest temple of Kannon Bodhisattva. Founded in the 7th year of Emperor Kinmei's reign (546), it enshrines a statue of Kannon given as a gift by King Seong of Baekje.

  • Suomachi Street (Europe Street)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    The streets of Shinsaibashi in Osaka, lined with cobblestones and streetlights, exude an elegant atmosphere. Lined with stylish boutiques, restaurants, and galleries, it is a popular area for adults.

  • Sakuyakonohanakan

    Tsurumi Ward, Osaka · 06-6912-0055

    One of Japan's leading comprehensive botanical museums, it displays approximately 15,000 plants of 5,500 species that grow in various climates around the world, including rare flowers such as the Himalayan blue poppy.

  • Shitennoji Koshindo

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6772-9420

    This is the place where the first Koshin statue in Japan appeared, and the principal image is Shomen Kongo Doji (a secret Buddha). It has a history of 1,300 years, and it is revered as a place where if you visit on Koshin day or the day before, your wish will come true.

  • Kokubunji Temple, Settsu Province (Head temple of the Kokubunji branch of the Shingon sect)

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6351-5637

    Located in Kita-ku, Osaka City, this temple is the head temple of the Shingon sect of Buddhism's Kokubunji school. Founded 1,350 years ago, it became a Kokubunji temple during the reign of Emperor Shomu.

  • The National Museum of Art, Osaka

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6447-4680

    Located in Nakanoshima, Osaka, this completely underground art museum is a globally rare sight. It houses approximately 8,200 pieces of artwork, mainly contemporary art, and is characterized by its exterior design that evokes the vitality of bamboo.

  • Zama Shrine

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6251-4792

    The local deity of Senba, Osaka. Worshipped as the god of protection for homes, safe travels, and safe childbirth, the shrine is also known as the birthplace of the Watanabe family name.

  • Shirokita Park

    Asahi Ward, Osaka · 06-6928-0005

    This historic park in Osaka's Asahi Ward opened in 1934 on the banks of the Yodo River.

  • Johoku Iris Garden

    Asahi Ward, Osaka · 06-6928-0005

    This strolling iris garden, covering an area of approximately 1.3 hectares, has been selected as one of the "100 Best Green Areas in Osaka." It cultivates approximately 13,000 irises of 250 varieties, including Edo, Ise, and Higo varieties.

  • Horikawa Ebisu Shrine

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6311-8626

    This shrine is located in Kita-ku, Osaka City. It enshrines Hiruko-no-Okami, who is beloved as the god of prosperity in business. It is crowded with many worshippers during the Toka Ebisu festival.

  • Horikoshi Shrine

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-9072

    One of the seven shrines of Shitennoji Temple, built by Prince Shotoku in memory of his uncle, Emperor Sushun, the 32nd emperor. A power spot in Tennoji, Osaka, known for its deity who grants one wish in a lifetime.

  • Zofukuji Temple

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6772-5734

    This temple is associated with Usuda Hayato (Iwami Jutaro), a Toyotomi military commander who played an active role in the Battle of Osaka.

  • Dainenbutsuji Temple

    Hirano Ward, Osaka · 06-6791-0026

    It is the head temple of the Yuzunenbutsu sect, and its main hall is the largest wooden building in Osaka Prefecture. It is known for events such as the "Manbe Oneri," which has been designated an intangible folk cultural property of Osaka City.

  • Oebashi

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    One of the five bridges built over the Dojima River during the Genroku period, the current bridge was designed and completed in the early Showa period through a design competition, and is a beautiful arch bridge that represents modern Osaka architecture.

  • The Osaka Museum of Housing and Living

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6242-1170

    Japan's first housing museum, where you can see a life-size reproduction of Osaka's townscape from the late Edo period, and experience Osaka homes from the Meiji, Taisho, and Showa periods.

  • Osaka Cathedral (St. Mary's Cathedral)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6941-2332

    Built on the site of the Hosokawa family estate, this Catholic cathedral features stone statues of Hosokawa Gracia and Takayama Ukon. Its highlights include 100 stained glass windows, murals by Domoto Insho, and a 2,400-pipe organ.

  • Nakanoshima Museum of Art, Osaka

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6479-0550

    The museum aims to be a platform with communication and creativity, centered around a collection of modern and contemporary art and design from Osaka and around the world.

  • Osaka Entrepreneurs' Museum

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-4964-7601

    This museum displays and introduces the achievements of 108 entrepreneurs who were active in Osaka, along with related items. Operated by the Osaka Chamber of Commerce and Industry, it aims to promote entrepreneurial spirit and develop the next generation of people.

  • Osaka Club

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6231-8361

    This building is one of Osaka's three most famous buildings and has been used as a club for Osaka's business leaders for over 100 years. It was designed in Southern European style by Yasui Takeo in 1924 and is a registered tangible cultural property.

  • Osaka Exchange

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-4706-0800

    This retro building features a magnificent cylindrical white exterior and a beautiful entrance lobby with stained glass. It carries on the history of the Yodoya Rice Market of the Edo period and the Dojima Rice Exchange, the world's first futures exchange.

  • Osaka Castle Gozabune (Golden Ship)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    Take a 20-minute cruise around the inner moat of Osaka Castle on a golden Japanese boat, modeled after Toyotomi Hideyoshi's Houou Maru. You'll get a close-up look at Japan's tallest stone walls and castle tower.

  • Nishinomaru Garden of Osaka Castle

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6941-1717

    Located within Osaka Castle Park, which spreads out around Osaka Castle Museum, this approximately 6.5 hectare lawn garden is said to have been the residence of Toyotomi Hideyoshi's wife, Kita no Mandokoro.

  • Osaka Tenmangu Shrine

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6353-0025

    Located in the Tenma area near Osaka Station, this shrine is affectionately known as "Tenma no Tenjin-san." It enshrines Sugawara no Michizane, the god of learning and performing arts, and every July it hosts the Tenjin Festival, one of Japan's three major festivals.

  • Osaka City Sewerage Science Museum

    Konohana Ward, Osaka · 06-6468-1156

    This new Sewerage Science Museum will turn all your questions about sewers into questions! Everyone from children to adults can have fun learning about sewers, a lifeline we all rely on.

  • Osaka Science Museum

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6444-5656

    The theme of the museum is "Space and Energy," and there are about 200 interactive exhibits, as well as science shows and a planetarium that can be enjoyed by people of all ages.

  • Osaka Municipal Museum of Fine Arts

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-4874

    This long-established art museum in Tennoji Park has a history of over 85 years and a collection of over 8,500 pieces, including paintings, sculptures, and crafts from Japan and China.

  • Osaka Museum of Natural History

    Higashisumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6697-6221

    This museum allows you to learn about Osaka's nature, the evolution of living things, and the history of life on Earth. It features impressive exhibits such as a life-size replica of a Naumann's elephant, whale skeletons, and dinosaur skeletons.

  • Osaka Municipal Nagai Botanical Garden

    Higashisumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6696-7117

    Nagai Botanical Garden is one of Japan's leading botanical gardens, beloved as an "urban oasis" brimming with nature and greenery.

  • Osaka Regional Environmental Facilities Association Maishima Factory

    Konohana Ward, Osaka · 06-6463-4153

    This innovative and colorful waste disposal plant was designed by the Viennese artist Friedensreich Hundertwasser. It is a facility themed around environmental protection and coexistence with nature, and is open to the public for free.

  • Osaka Prefectural Kamigata Performing Arts Museum Wahha Kamigata

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6631-0884

    The only museum in the country with a laughter-themed theme where you can experience the charm of Kamigata performing arts such as manzai and rakugo.

  • Osaka Prefectural Nakanoshima Library

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6203-0474

    This stately building features three stone stories and a towering copper-roofed dome. It has been designated an Important Cultural Property of Japan and houses documents related to Osaka, classics, and books related to business.

  • Osaka Cultural Center, Tempozan

    Minato Ward, Osaka · 06-6586-3911

    In 2013, the former Suntory Museum was renamed "Osaka Culturarium Tempozan." The gallery is a facility that hosts a variety of exhibitions, including fascinating and topical special exhibitions, as well as natural history and art exhibitions.

  • Osaka Science and Technology Museum

    Nishi Ward, Osaka · 06-6441-0915

    This free science museum is run by Japan's leading cutting-edge companies and research institutes, who use hands-on exhibits and games to introduce the latest science and technology in everyday life in an easy-to-understand manner.

  • Tempozan Harbor Village

    Minato Ward, Osaka

    This comprehensive bay area leisure spot is centered around the world's largest aquarium, Kaiyukan. It also houses a marketplace where you can enjoy gourmet food, shopping, and entertainment, as well as the Santa Maria sightseeing boat.

  • Tempozan Park

    Minato Ward, Osaka · 06-6571-0552

    This park is home to Mount Tempo, famous for being the lowest mountain in Japan (4.5m above sea level). The beautifully maintained cherry blossoms and pine trees have made it a hidden cherry blossom viewing spot.

  • Tennoji Seven Hills

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka

    This is the collective name for the seven slopes in Tennoji Ward, Osaka City: Shingonzaka, Genshojizaka, Kuchinawazaka, Aizenzaka, Kiyomizuzaka, Tenjinzaka, and Osaka.

  • TEN-SHIBA(Tennoji Park Entrance Area )

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6773-0860

    In addition to the open lawn space (Ten-Shiba) and tenants (restaurants, soccer court, etc.), the park plaza is located near the Osaka Tennoji Zoo, Osaka City Museum of Fine Arts, Japanese garden (Keitakuen), etc.

  • Tenjinzaka

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6774-9910

    One of the Seven Hills of Tennoji, this historic slope leads to Yasui Shrine, which enshrines Sugawara no Michizane, and has long been known as a place with excellent water.

  • Tenjinbashi

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This bridge crosses over Nakanoshima's "Sword Stakes" and spans the Okawa River (formerly the Yodo River). Along with Tenmabashi and Naniwabashi, it is known as one of the "Three Great Bridges of Naniwa," and is a historic structure said to have been built around 1594.

  • Taiheiji Temple (Temple of the Thirteen Pilgrimages)

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6779-9133

    This temple in Tennoji, Osaka, is known for the tradition of "Jusan Mairi," where people receive wisdom and good fortune from Kokuzo Bosatsu at the age of thirteen (according to the Japanese age reckoning system).

  • Tairyuji Temple

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6311-5480

    This ancient temple in Umeda, Osaka was founded by Kobo Daishi, also known as Kukai. The principal image is a Thousand-Armed Kannon statue, and the temple grounds also contain the grave of Yodo-dono. It is also known as a place associated with the Freedom and People's Rights Movement.

  • Abe no Seimei Shrine

    Abeno Ward, Osaka · 06-6622-2565

    This is the legendary birthplace of Abe no Seimei, a popular onmyoji (exorcist) who has been gaining popularity in recent years.

  • Yasui Shrine

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-4932

    This ancient shrine enshrines Sukunahikona-no-kami and Sugawara no Michizane. It is known as the place where Sanada Yukimura died in battle during the Summer Siege of Osaka, and a monument to Yukimura has been erected within the grounds.

  • Soemoncho

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    Located in Osaka's Minami district, this historic entertainment district has flourished since ancient times. It is a popular tourist spot with a wide range of establishments, from high-end clubs and famous restaurants to more affordable eateries, and its beautiful neon lights at night.

  • Hoju-in Temple

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6351-4960

    This Shingon Buddhist temple was founded by Kukai in 825. The principal image of worship is Dainichi Nyorai. It has a history linked to Sugawara no Michizane, and also houses a statue of Maitreya Bodhisattva from the Kamakura period.

  • Jakkoji Temple (Kimido Hall at Eguchi)

    Higashiyodogawa Ward, Osaka · 06-6328-0504

    This temple was founded by Koso Bikuni, the daughter of Taira no Sukemori. It is widely known for the legend that the monk Saigyo and Lady Eguchi exchanged poems here.

  • Teranishi family Abeno tenement house

    Abeno Ward, Osaka · 06-6624-7618

    This historic building is the first row house in Japan to be registered as a national tangible cultural property. It is a two-story wooden row house with four houses built in 1932 and is currently used as a restaurant.

  • Sukunahikona Shrine

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6231-6958

    Located among the buildings of Doshomachi, Osaka's "medicine town," this shrine is known as the god of health and medicine. It enshrines Sukunahikona-no-Mikoto, the ancestor of Japanese medicine, and Shennong Yantei, the ancestor of Chinese medicine.

  • Kawaguchi Christian Church

    Nishi Ward, Osaka · 06-6581-5061

    This chapel was built in the foreign settlement established in the Kawaguchi area following the opening of Osaka Port in 1868. Completed in 1920, it is a Gothic-style brick building and is designated as a national tangible cultural property.

  • Kawasaki Bridge

    Miyakojima Ward, Osaka

    This cable-stayed bridge for bicycles and pedestrians crosses the Okawa River, connecting Tenma in Kita-ku and Amijima-cho in Miyakojima-ku, Osaka.

  • Kawabata Yasunari Literary Monument

    Sumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6672-0753

    This monument is inscribed with a passage from "Soribashi" (Soribashi Bridge), written by Osaka-born Nobel Prize-winning author Yasunari Kawabata. Located within the grounds of Sumiyoshi Taisha Shrine, it is a popular spot for literature fans.

  • Toyasu Bridge

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This bridge was built over the Tosabori River by Yodoya Tsuneyasu, a lumber merchant in the Edo period. It connects Kita-ku and Nishi-ku in Osaka City, and conveys the history of Yodoya, who was instrumental in the development of Nakanoshima.

  • Yae Shrine

    Ikuno Ward, Osaka · 06-6741-0900

    Yaei Shrine is a shrine in Ikuno Ward, Osaka City, that was founded during the Bunroku era by transferring a branch of the spirit from Kumano Shrine, a former national shrine in Shimane Prefecture.

  • Midosuji Sculpture Street

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6208-7854

    Along Osaka's main street, Midosuji, 29 sculptures by famous artists from Japan and abroad, including Rodin, Henry Moore, and Takamura Kotaro, are on display. Stretching approximately 4km from Umeda to Namba, this is a spot where you can casually stroll around and enjoy art appreciation.

  • Miyuki Street (Korea Town)

    Ikuno Ward, Osaka

    Japan's largest Koreatown is lined with approximately 150 Korean grocery stores, restaurants, and traditional clothing stores. You can enjoy local delicacies such as kimchi and chijimi, as well as hands-on Korean culture classes.

  • Mitsu Park (Triangle Park)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    This is a landmark and meeting spot in America-mura, a town that nurtured Kansai youth culture. It is a popular place of relaxation, with people enjoying street performances by young comedians and the surrounding takoyaki shops.

  • Mitsumiya (Mitsu Hachiman Shrine)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6211-8655

    Located in the busy Minami district of Osaka, this shrine has a history of over 1,000 years and is known as the guardian deity of the Shinsaibashi and Americamura areas.

  • Goryo Shrine

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6231-5041

    This shrine was revered as the tutelary deity of the former Tsumura district in Settsu Province, encompassing areas such as Senba, Nakanoshima, Kyomachibori, and Utsubo. It is also known as the birthplace of Bunraku puppetry.

  • Shinganji Temple

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6764-0630

    This temple stands on the site believed to be the location of "Sanada Maru," a fortress built by Sanada Yukimura during the Winter Siege of Osaka. It was rebuilt to commemorate Sanada Yukimura and his son, Daisuke.

  • Shikinagayoshi Shrine

    Hirano Ward, Osaka · 06-6709-1757

    It is a shrine listed in the Engishiki (an ancient Japanese legal code), and it is said to have been established around the early Heian period (794 AD), about 1200 years ago. The enshrined deities are Nagae no Sotsuhiko no Mikoto and Kotoshironushi no Mikoto.

  • Aizen-do Shoman-in Temple (Aizen-san)

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6779-5800

    Built by Prince Shotoku, this temple is dedicated to Aizen Myoo, a deity famous for bringing good luck in marriage and marital harmony. It is known for the Aizen Festival, one of Osaka's three major summer festivals, and the Tahoto Pagoda, an important cultural property.

  • Keitakuen Garden

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-7494-3583

    Located within Tennoji Park is a purely Japanese-style strolling garden with a forest and spring.

  • Oden-in Temple

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-7641

    As an open temple, people gather here for various cultural activities. The main hall is a hall called "Theatrical Oden-in," which is used by many artists as a gallery and performance stage.

  • Ogimachi Park

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6312-8121

    Conveniently located within walking distance of Umeda, this oasis in the city is always filled with cheers and cheers, with large playground equipment, a grassy area, and a pool.

  • Hiyoshi Shrine

    Asahi Ward, Osaka · 06-6925-6236

    Located in Asahi Ward, Osaka City, this historic shrine dates back to the Muromachi period. The summer festival in July features processions, lion dances, and kagura dances.

  • United Church of Christ in Japan Naniwa Church

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6231-4951

    Completed in 1930, this retro building features an exterior decorated with Gothic-style spire windows and yellow and green tinted glass, and a chapel with a clean and beautiful space. It was constructed under the design guidance of William Merrell Vories.

  • Japan Crafts Museum

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6641-6309

    This museum exhibits and preserves folk crafts (folk art), from ceramics from various regions such as Old Tamba and Old Seto to contemporary works. It is currently closed for reconstruction.

  • Nipponbashi

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    This bridge was built during the Edo period. Over the years, the area has transformed and developed into a post town, a used bookstore district, and an electronics district. Today, it is also known as the base of Den-Den Town, Osaka's leading electronics district.

  • Nipponbashi Denden Town

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6655-1717

    The largest electric town and otaku district in western Japan, this shopping district has over 150 stores selling highly specialized items such as anime, games, figurines, electronic parts, and audio equipment.

  • Bank of Japan Osaka Branch Old Building

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6206-7742

    Built in 1903 (Meiji 36), this authentic Western-style two-story stone building with a beautiful patina-colored cupola was modeled after the National Bank of Belgium and other buildings. It was designed by Tatsuno Kingo and others.

  • Former Konishi Family Residence Museum

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6203-3041

    This is the former office building of Konishi Co., Ltd., which manufactures and sells the synthetic adhesive "Bond." The building, completed in 1903 with a front-facing design, is designated as an Important Cultural Property of Japan as a culmination of modern Osaka townhouse architecture.

  • Meijiya Building

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    Located in Sakaisuji Hommachi, this neo-renaissance style retro Taisho era building was completed in 1924 and was known as a source of sophisticated Western food culture at the time.

  • Meiji Bridge

    Nishi Ward, Osaka

    This is the site of a bridge that was built over the former Tatsumichi Horikawa River in 1876. A monument now stands here, conveying the history of Osaka.

  • Hommachi Bridge

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6568-2715

    This bridge is said to have been built by Toyotomi Hideyoshi when he constructed Osaka Castle, as part of the excavation of the Higashi-Yokobori River. It is the oldest bridge still in use in Osaka City.

  • Kumata Shrine

    Hirano Ward, Osaka · 06-6791-0208

    Founded in 862 during the early Heian period, this shrine is the guardian deity of Hirano Ward, Osaka City, and was established by Sakanoue no Tamuramaro's grandson, Todo. The three shrine buildings are designated as Important Cultural Properties of Japan.

  • Sendannoki Bridge (Sandalwood Bridge)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    Located in the heart of Nakanoshima, Osaka, this viewpoint offers panoramic views of historical buildings such as the Osaka City Central Public Hall and the Osaka Prefectural Nakanoshima Library.

  • Umeda BIGMAN

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    A popular meeting spot in Osaka's Kita area. Powerful images and sounds are projected onto a large 200-inch screen.

  • Shorenji Temple

    Konohana Ward, Osaka · 06-6461-2845

    Shorenji Temple, located in Konohana Ward, Osaka City, is a Nichiren Buddhist temple known for its "Kawa Segaki," one of Japan's three major Segaki ceremonies. The ceremony, held annually on August 26th, is designated as an Intangible Folk Cultural Property of Osaka City.

  • Kema Sakuranomiya Park

    Miyakojima Ward, Osaka · 06-6312-8121

    A riverside park of water, greenery, and cherry blossoms that runs along the riverbed from Kemaaraizeki Weir to Tenmabashi Bridge.

  • Kema Lock

    Miyakojima Ward, Osaka

    This sluice gate connects the Yodo River and Okawa River, which have different water levels, allowing passage. The former No. 1 Lock Gate and the former Wash Weir, completed in 1907, are valuable brick structures and have been designated as Important Cultural Properties of Japan.

  • Suisho Bridge

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    This historic pedestrian bridge was originally built as a movable weir over the Dojima River. Its beautiful arch structure and nighttime illuminations make it a popular spot for strolling around the modern architecture of Nakanoshima, Osaka.

  • Esaki Memorial Hall

    Nishiyodogawa Ward, Osaka · 06-6477-8257

    The Ezaki Glico Museum was built in 1972 to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the founding of Glico. It displays past product packaging, advertisements, Glico toys, and items related to the company's founder, Riichi Ezaki.

  • Senpukan

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6469-5176

    Built in 1871 (Meiji 4) as a reception room for the Mint, this all-brick building is a masterpiece of veranda colonial style architecture. It was designated an Important Cultural Property by the Emperor Meiji.

  • Hoanji Temple

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6211-4585

    This historic temple is said to have been built by Prince Shotoku about 1,400 years ago. It enshrines Benzaiten, one of the Seven Lucky Gods of Osaka, and houses a standing statue of Shokannon, a nationally designated important cultural property.

  • Horakuji Temple

    Higashisumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6621-2103

    This temple was founded in 1178 by Taira no Shigemori, the eldest son of Taira no Kiyomori. It is known as an ancient temple with ties to the Taira clan, and is also the temple where the high priest Jion Sonja was ordained. The Kosaka Kiseki Memorial Museum is also located within the temple grounds.

  • Tsunami and Storm Surge Station

    Nishi Ward, Osaka · 06-6541-7799

    This disaster prevention education facility allows visitors to learn about the horrors of tsunamis and high tides through simulated experiences using video and audio, and to acquire knowledge such as proper evacuation methods.

  • Kaiyodo Figure Museum (Miraiza Osaka Castle)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6940-0835

    This museum displays over 3,000 figures created by Kaiyodo over the course of more than 40 years. You can enjoy the world of sculptures from a variety of genres, including special effects, anime, biology, and art.

  • Yodoyabashi

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    This famous bridge spans the Tosabori River and has been chosen as the most attractive bridge by Osaka residents. The original bridge was built by Yodoya, a wealthy merchant from the Edo period, and is now designated as an Important Cultural Property of Japan.

  • Yodogawa River Park

    Fukushima Ward, Osaka · 06-6994-0006

    This vast park stretches along the Yodo River, from Fukushima Ward to Oyamazaki Town in Kyoto Prefecture. Boasting an area approximately 1.9 times larger than Osaka Castle Park, it's a popular spot for locals, offering abundant nature and well-equipped sports facilities.

  • Yodogawa Shrine

    Miyakojima Ward, Osaka · 06-6921-5980

    Located in Miyakojima Ward, Osaka City, this shrine enshrines fifteen deities, including Amaterasu Omikami. It is a place associated with the haiku poet Yosa Buson, and its summer festival in July is a well-known summer tradition in Miyakojima Ward.

  • Minatomachi River Place

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-4397-0571

    A complex where you can enjoy the dynamic cityscape and waterfront atmosphere. It also houses the live music venue "Namba Hatch," restaurants, and an event plaza.

  • Yuki Art Museum

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6203-0188

    This museum houses and exhibits tea ceremony utensils, tea ceremony wares, art pieces, and more collected over a period of 50 years by Yuki Teiichi, the founder of the Japanese restaurant Kitcho.

  • Takimi Koji

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6440-3899

    Located on the first basement floor of Umeda Sky Building, this dining district allows you to enjoy meals in a retro townscape that recreates the early Showa period.

  • Tamatsukuri Inari Shrine

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6941-3821

    In ancient times, the surrounding area was called "Tamatsukurioka" and was inhabited by the Tamakuribe, who made magatama (comma-shaped beads), which is how the current name Tamatsukuri came about.

  • Ikutama Shrine

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-0002

    Engishiki Myojin Taisha Shrine is said to have originated as a shrine dedicated to the gods Ikushima and Tarushima at Ishiyamazaki (near present-day Osaka Castle). It is characterized by its main hall, the only one of its kind in Japan, built in the "Ikutama-zukuri" style.

  • Ikune Shrine (Nishinari Ward)

    Nishinari Ward, Osaka · 06-6659-2821

    This shrine is located in Nishinari Ward, Osaka City. It is known for its blessings in preventing strokes and warding off dementia, and every July it hosts the "Daigaku Festival," a tangible cultural property of Osaka Prefecture, and the "Kotsuma Pumpkin Festival" on the winter solstice.

  • Ikoma Building

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6231-0751

    The headquarters building of Ikoma Watch Shop overlooks Kitahama, the town of stock exchanges. It is a treasure trove of history, full of charm, with a clock tower, sculptures, stained glass, and more.

  • Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma Historical Museum

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6205-5100

    This exhibition introduces the wisdom and future of Japan's pharmaceutical industry, which was nurtured in Doshomachi, along with the history of Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma, which spans more than 345 years.

  • Nanba Betsuin Temple (South Mido Hall) of the Otani branch of Shinshu Buddhism

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6251-5820

    Affectionately known as Minami no Mido-san, this historic temple is the origin of the name Midosuji. There are historical sites such as a stone monument bearing a haiku poem by Matsuo Basho, and Buddhist lectures and events are also held here.

  • Statue of Sanada Yukimura

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6761-0372

    A bronze statue of Sanada Yukimura stands bravely in the grounds of Sanko Shrine. It was erected in 1987 and its base is made of Sanada stone taken from the Sanada family temple. The adjacent "Sanada's Secret Hole" is also a historical attraction.

  • Karabori area

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    This area retains the remnants of the castle town from the time when Toyotomi Hideyoshi built Osaka Castle. It's a popular strolling spot where historical streetscapes and new culture coexist, including "Ren," a complex facility that renovates old townhouses.

  • Tachibana Street (Orange Street)

    Nishi Ward, Osaka

    This street, which represents Minami-Horie in Osaka, is lined with stylish stores, including cafes, boutiques, general goods stores, and interior design shops, stretching for 800 meters. Formerly known as a furniture town, it is now a hub where new and old cultures blend together.

  • Umeda Sky Building Koji Kinutani Tenku Art Museum

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6440-3760

    This art museum is located on the 27th floor of Umeda Sky Building. It offers the world's first 3D visual experience that lets you jump into a painting, and exhibits works by Koji Kinutani, created using the fresco technique.

  • Midori Bridge

    Higashinari Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This is one of the bridges that spanned the Sengen River, which flowed through Higashi-Osaka City. Until it was filled in in 1967, it spanned the Koyakaido River, an irrigation canal that connected Nakatakano Kaido.

  • Oimatsu Street

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    Stretching about 1km across Nishitenma from east to west, this is Osaka's representative antique art and art district, with around 90 stores, including antique art dealers and galleries.

  • Higobashi Bridge

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    This ancient bridge spans the Tosabori River, and at its northern end was once the storehouse of the Higo and Kumamoto domains.

  • Maishima Seaside Promenade

    Konohana Ward, Osaka · 06-6460-9855

    This stylish promenade offers beautiful views of Minami-ko and Tempozan. The sunset seen from the stepped grass stands is very romantic, and at night, the night view is breathtaking.

  • Maishima Shin-Yuhigaoka

    Konohana Ward, Osaka · 06-6572-4050

    A 25m-high artificial hill in Maishima, Osaka. It is a famous spot selected as one of Japan's top 100 sunset spots, and is equipped with an observation plaza and walking paths offering panoramic views of Awaji Island and Kobe Port.

  • Semba Building

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6231-8531

    This building, completed in 1925, conveys the history of modern architecture in Senba, Osaka. It features a distinctive design with a long, patio-like courtyard in the center, an open atrium, and a corridor surrounding it.

  • Shibakawa Building

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    Completed on July 1, 1927, this historic reinforced concrete building is a representative example of Yodoyabashi, Osaka. It features a unique exterior with South American Mayan and Incan decorations and was once used as a bridal school.

  • Basho's haiku monument (Sumiyoshi Park)

    Suminoe Ward, Osaka · 06-6671-2292

    This monument is engraved with a poem that the haiku poet Matsuo Basho composed when he visited the Takara no Ichi Shinto ritual at Sumiyoshi Taisha Shrine in September 1694 (Genroku 7).

  • Hananoi Bridge

    Nishi Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This bridge was built in 1920 (Taisho 9) across the Edobori River. The bridge's name originates from "Konohana-no-i," a famous spring that Emperor Meiji used as a water source during his visit to Osaka. Currently, a monument stands on the grounds of Osaka Municipal Hananoi Junior High School.

  • Flower Expo Memorial Park Tsurumi Ryokuchi

    Tsurumi Ward, Osaka · 06-6911-8787

    This vast urban park, covering approximately 123 hectares, was redeveloped on the site of the 1990 International Garden and Greenery Exposition.

  • Chayamachi area

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    Osaka's Kita district is the number one youth district, and is a trendsetting area with fashion, miscellaneous goods, cafes, theaters and more.

  • Chausuyama (Tennoji Park)

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6761-1770

    This historical spot was the site of the battles between the Toyotomi and Tokugawa clans during the Winter and Summer Sieges of Osaka. At 26m above sea level, it is one of the five lowest mountains in Osaka and is also known as the headquarters of Sanada Yukimura.

  • Manpukuji Temple

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6771-2183

    This temple in Tennoji Ward, Osaka City, was founded by Maeda Jirobei Toshinobu, the younger brother of the Sengoku period warlord Maeda Toshiie.

  • Fujita Museum of Art

    Miyakojima Ward, Osaka · 06-6351-0582

    This is a top-class private art museum that houses the collection of Meiji era businessman Fujita Denzaburo, with a collection of approximately 2,000 pieces of ancient Oriental art, including 9 national treasures and 53 important cultural properties.

  • Dotonbori Konamon Museum

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6214-6678

    At the Konamon Food Museum, you can eat, learn, and experience Osaka's soul food, takoyaki. The food sample making experience is particularly popular.

  • Tekijuku

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6231-1970

    This school for Dutch studies was run by Ogata Koan from 1838 to 1862. It produced many great figures of the late Edo period, including Fukuzawa Yukichi, and the building is designated as an Important Cultural Property of Japan.

  • Nozato Sumiyoshi Shrine

    Nishiyodogawa Ward, Osaka · 06-6471-0277

    The shrine is said to have been founded by Ashikaga Yoshimitsu in 1382. It is known for the "One Night Court Lady" ritual, an intangible cultural property of Osaka Prefecture, and the legend of Iwami Jutaro's slaying of a baboon.

  • Hokonagashi Bridge

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    This bridge spans the Dojima River, connecting the north bank of Nakanoshima with Nishitenma. It is known for the Hokonagara Shinto ritual held every year on the morning of July 24th as part of the Tenjin Festival.

  • Ginzanji Temple

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka

    Founded in 1591, this Jodo sect temple is located in Tennoji Ward, Osaka City.

  • Nishiki Bridge

    Kita Ward, Osaka

    This pedestrian-only bridge is located on the south side of the Asahi Festival Hall. On top of the bridge, you can see tiled prints of Nishikie art, which remind you of the history of Nakanoshima.

  • Nagahoribashi

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This was the first bridge built over the Nagahori River, which was excavated in 1625 to connect the Higashiyokobori River and the Kizu River. It once played an important role as a government bridge, and a monument has now been erected there.

  • Nagai Park

    Higashisumiyoshi Ward, Osaka · 06-6694-9007

    Osaka's representative comprehensive park, covering an area of 66.3 hectares, is home to a variety of facilities, including Yanmar Stadium Nagai and Nagai Botanical Gardens, and is popular with people of all ages.

  • Abeno Oji Shrine

    Abeno Ward, Osaka · 06-6622-2565

    Located south of Tennoji, this shrine was known as the fourth of the 99 Kumano Oji shrines during the Heian period when pilgrimages to Kumano were popular. It is known for its blessings of warding off evil, good health, and recovery from illness.

  • Abeno Shrine

    Abeno Ward, Osaka · 06-6661-6243

    Located in Abeno-ku, Osaka City, this shrine enshrines Kitabatake Akiie, a famous general during the Nanboku-cho period, and his father, Kitabatake Chikafusa. Founded in 1885, the shrine hosts a procession ceremony to Kitabatake Park during the summer festival.

  • Namba Yasaka Shrine

    Naniwa Ward, Osaka · 06-6641-1149

    This shrine, representing Namba, Osaka, is famous for its 12m-tall emaden (votive plaque hall) in the shape of a giant lion's head. It is known as the god of protection from evil and victory, and its tug-of-war ritual has been designated an intangible folk cultural asset by Osaka City.

  • Naniwa Palace Ruins Park

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    The remains of the palaces from the early and late periods of Osaka, which was the capital of Japan from the Asuka to Nara periods, are evidence that Osaka was the capital of Japan.

  • Namba Shrine

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6251-8000

    This ancient shrine in Osaka is said to have been founded by Emperor Hanzei to enshrine his father, Emperor Nintoku. It is known for the distribution of kachiwari ice at the Himuro Festival in July.

  • Tsuyuten Shrine (Ohatsutenjin Shrine)

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6311-0895

    Founded over 1,300 years ago, this shrine is known as the setting for Chikamatsu Monzaemon's "Sonezaki Shinju." It is a power spot in Umeda, Osaka, visited by people praying for fulfillment in love.

  • Utsubo Park

    Nishi Ward, Osaka · 06-6441-6213

    This park stretches across approximately 9.7 hectares in Utsubo Honmachi, Osaka's business Hommachi. You can enjoy the city's oldest rose garden, the Utsubo Tennis Center, which is used for international tournaments, and rows of zelkova trees that change with the seasons.

  • Takatsu Shrine

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6762-1122

    This historic shrine in Osaka is dedicated to Emperor Nintoku and is also known as the setting for classic rakugo.

  • Koma Bridge

    Chuo Ward, Osaka

    Koraibashi Bridge, which spans the Higashi-Yokobori River, is known as Osaka's first iron bridge, and is a historic bridge where a milepost was placed during the Meiji period, marking the starting point for calculating road distances in western Japan.

  • Fishing Park Embankment (Osaka South Port Fishing Park)

    Suminoe Ward, Osaka · 06-6612-2020

    The only sea fishing park in Osaka Prefecture. A fishing spot using the breakwater of Nanko Port, you can target a variety of fish species such as horse mackerel, mackerel, and black porgy. Free fishing lessons are also held on weekends.

  • Reienji Temple

    Tennoji Ward, Osaka · 06-6772-0065

    This temple, located in Tennoji Ward, Osaka City, was founded in 1623.

  • Kitashinchi

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6345-0006

    This high-end entertainment district, representing Osaka's Kita area, has flourished as a place for business people to exchange information. It is an area where you can find a variety of restaurants, including bars, clubs, snack bars, small restaurants, and Japanese and Western cuisine.

  • The Museum of Oriental Ceramics, Osaka

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6223-0055

    The museum houses approximately 5,700 pieces of Oriental ceramics, including the Ataka Collection. This world-class collection, including two national treasures and 13 important cultural properties, can be viewed in beautiful exhibition rooms with natural lighting.

  • Himeshima Shrine

    Nishiyodogawa Ward, Osaka · 06-6471-5230

    This shrine enshrines Akarubihime-no-Mikoto, who is loved as the "goddess of decision-making and action." It also attracts believers as a "start-over shrine" that supports new beginnings and women's independence.

  • Toyokuni Shrine

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6941-0229

    Located on the south side of the main citadel in Osaka Castle Park, this shrine enshrines Hideyoshi, Hideyori, and Hidenaga, and is known as a god for praying for success and good fortune.

  • Japan Mint Museum

    Kita Ward, Osaka · 06-6351-8509

    The Mint Museum is housed in a Western-style brick building from the Meiji period, originally constructed as a thermal power plant in 1911. It displays precious coins, orders, medals, and other items from Japan and abroad, and provides a detailed introduction to the minting business.

  • Naniwa Bridge (Lion Bridge)

    Chuo Ward, Osaka · 06-6615-6818

    This bridge, which dates back to the Edo period and is affectionately known as "Lion Bridge," is one of Osaka's most iconic bridges.
