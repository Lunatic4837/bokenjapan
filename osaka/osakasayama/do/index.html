Things to do in Osakasayama, Osaka

Osakasayama, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Citizens' Community Village Squirrel Garden

    Osakasayama · 072-366-1616

    This popular tourist spot in Sayama City, Osaka, allows you to interact with Formosan squirrels. You can feed them directly, so it's recommended for families.

  • Citizens' Community Village Youth Outdoor Activities Area

    Osakasayama · 072-366-1616

    Located about a 20-minute walk east from Sayama Station on the Nankai Koya Line, this facility features a "Youth Outdoor Activity Area" where you can enjoy athletics and camping, and a "Flower and Green Plaza" with a botanical garden and squirrel enclosure.
