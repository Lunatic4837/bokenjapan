Sights in Osakasayama, Osaka

Osakasayama, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 4 places

See

  • Mito Shrine

    Osakasayama · 072-365-2416

    It is also called Kumano Shrine because people who traveled along the Amano Kaido road to Kumano in Kishu used to make pilgrimages there.

  • Osaka Prefectural Sayamaike Museum

    Osakasayama · 072-367-8891

    The museum displays 1,400 years of civil engineering and flood control history, with a focus on cultural assets excavated from Sayama Pond, Japan's oldest dam-type reservoir. The building, designed by Tadao Ando, is also a highlight.

  • Osaka Sayama City Local History Museum

    Osakasayama · 072-367-8891

    This museum introduces the historic town of Sayama, showing its transition from ancient times to the present through stone tools, weapons, ancient documents, and more.

  • Sayama Pond

    Osakasayama

    Built around 616 AD, it is Japan's oldest dam-type reservoir. Boasting an area of approximately 400,000 square meters, historical figures such as Gyoki and Chogen have been involved in its renovation.
