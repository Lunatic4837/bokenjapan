Things to do in Sakai, Osaka

Sakai, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 6 places

Do

  • Kanbukuro (walnut rice cakes)

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-233-1218

    This long-established store was founded in the late Kamakura period (1st year of Gentoku). Its specialty "Kurumi Mochi" is a traditional Sakai confection made by wrapping mochi in a secret bean paste.

  • Fenice Sakai (Sakai City Arts and Culture Hall)

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-223-1000

    This arts and culture hub boasts one of the largest halls in southern Osaka, with a capacity of 2,000 seats. It is renowned for its excellent acoustics and accommodates a wide range of performing arts, including music, opera, ballet, and theater.

  • Sakai City Hall 21st floor observation lobby

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-228-7493

    The observation floor is located about 80 meters above ground. There are large observation windows in all directions, and you can enjoy a 360-degree panoramic view, including the Emperor Nintoku Tomb, the largest in the world, for free.

  • Sakai City Fisheries Association Toretoreichi

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-221-7272

    This market is held every Saturday and Sunday at Sakai Dejima Fishing Port. You can buy fresh seafood and enjoy a seafood barbecue on a charcoal grill.

  • Sakai Incense (Baieido Showroom)

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-229-4545

    This showroom allows you to learn about the manufacturing process of incense sticks and their effects, and experience incense, a familiar part of Japanese life, through the spirit of the art of incense.

  • Shrine maiden experience

    Sakai Ward, Sakai

    Learn about Japanese Shinto and spiritual culture through the "Miko Experience" at Aikachi Shrine, which has protected this land for over 1,700 years.
