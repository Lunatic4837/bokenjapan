Sights in Sakai, Osaka

Sakai, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 46 places

See

  • Strawberry Sakai

    Kita Ward, Sakai · 090-3275-4115

    This urban farm is easily accessible from the nearest station, just a 10-minute walk away. You can enjoy plenty of ripe, fragrant, delicious strawberries.

  • Osaka Sakai Balloon

    Sakai Ward, Sakai

    This is a hot air balloon experience attraction that allows you to enjoy a panoramic view of the Mozu Tombs, Osaka Prefecture's only World Heritage Site, including the Emperor Nintoku Tomb, as well as the Sakai cityscape from about 100 meters above the ground.

  • “Sakai Risho no Mori”

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-260-4386

    This museum allows you to experience History / culture of Sakai through the lives and achievements of Sen no Rikyu, the master of tea ceremony born in Sakai, and the poet Yosano Akiko. You can also experience authentic tea ceremony and tour the Sakai Tai-an.

  • Compeito Museum Sakai

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-282-2790

    This facility allows visitors to learn about the history and culture of konpeito (sugar candy), which was introduced from Portugal, and to experience making their own original konpeito in a miniature 30cm diameter pot.

  • Xavier Park

    Sakai Ward, Sakai

    This park was built on the site of the residence of Hibiya Ryokei, a wealthy merchant who lavished hospitality on Francis Xavier when he visited Sakai in 1550. It was named to commemorate the 400th anniversary of Xavier's arrival in Japan.

  • Shimano Bicycle Museum

    Sakai · 072-221-3196

    This museum uses actual exhibits and videos to introduce the history of bicycles around the world, the dreams of inventors, science, and the future. It also offers test rides and bicycle riding classes for children.

  • Sofia Sakai (Sakai City Education and Culture Center) Planetarium Sakai Starry Sky Museum, where a thousand dreams sparkle

    Naka Ward, Sakai · 072-270-8110

    Sakai City's only planetarium. Popular attractions include family programs that even families with babies can enjoy, and astronomical observation sessions using one of Osaka Prefecture's largest 60cm reflecting telescopes.

  • Tomb of Emperor Nintoku

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-241-0002

    It is the largest keyhole-shaped tumulus in Japan, measuring approximately 486 meters in length. It is one of the components of the UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Mozu-Furuichi Kofun Group, and is managed by the Imperial Household Agency as the mausoleum of the 16th Emperor, Emperor Nintoku.

  • Nanshuji Temple

    Sakai · 072-232-1654

    Buddhist temple in Sakai.

  • clay pagoda

    Naka Ward, Sakai

    This pagoda was built southeast of the Oono-ji temple complex, which was built by the renowned monk Gyoki. It is the only pyramid-shaped, tiled earthen pagoda in Japan. It was designated a national historic site in 1953.

  • Sakai Alphonse Mucha Museum

    Sakai · 072-222-5533

    The museum houses works by Alphonse Mucha, a leading Art Nouveau artist, painted throughout his life. It also features works by Akiko Yosano, such as "Myojo" and "Madaregami," and is a museum where you can experience the history of East-West cultural exchange.

  • Sakai Green Museum Harvest Hill

    Minami Ward, Sakai · 072-2966-9911

    Located in Sakai City, Osaka Prefecture, this nature-rich agricultural park offers a year-round opportunity for adults and children to interact with plants and animals. Enjoy seasonal flower fields, animal feeding experiences, a Sylvanian park, athletics, and more.

  • Sakai Densho Museum

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-227-1001

    This facility brings together Sakai's traditional industries under one roof, offering exhibitions, sales, and hands-on experiences.

  • Sakai City Museum

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-245-6201

    This museum collects, preserves, and exhibits materials related to the history, art, archaeology, and folklore of Sakai City.

  • Sakai City Big Bang

    Minami Ward, Sakai · 072-294-0999

    This completely indoor facility is full of fun activities, where you can create, learn, move your body, and more. The main building, which is modeled after a landed spaceship, and the "Tower of Playground Equipment," a giant jungle gym 53 meters above ground, are popular.

  • Sakai City Tea Houses Shinan and Obaian

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-247-1447

    Located in Sakai, the hometown of the tea master Sen no Rikyu, this tea house was designed by the Meiji era master Ogi Rodo in the sukiya style. It is designated as a national registered tangible cultural property, and you can enjoy matcha tea in a ryureiseki style setting.

  • Tajihayahime Shrine

    Minami Ward, Sakai · 072-297-0726

    Founded around 580, Tajihayahime Shrine enshrines Tajihayahime, Susanoo, and Sugawara no Michizane, and is said to grant blessings for safe childbirth, matchmaking, and protection from evil. The main hall is designated as an Important Cultural Property of Japan.

  • Daisen Park

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-241-0291

    Located between the tombs of Emperor Nintoku and Emperor Richu, this vast park of approximately 35 hectares includes a lawn plaza, a Japanese garden, and the Sakai City Museum.

  • Daisen Park and Japanese Garden

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-247-3670

    The garden, which makes full use of traditional Japanese garden design techniques and is designed as a strolling garden with a hill, forest and spring, conveys the cultural atmosphere of Sakai.

  • Daianji Temple

    Sakai Ward, Sakai

    This Rinzai Zen temple of the Tofuku-ji school was founded in the early Muromachi period by Tokuhide Shigeaki.

  • Mt. Daiho Hounzenji Temple (Hounji Temple)

    Mihara Ward, Sakai · 072-361-4419

    This ancient Obaku sect temple was founded by Zen Master Egoku Domyo in 1672. It is famous for its Daibutsuden Hall, which houses 3,333 Buddha statues, as well as events such as the azaleas in April and the Manto-e festival in August.

  • Kobayashi Museum of Art

    Kita Ward, Sakai · 072-259-0316

    Oizumi Ryokuchi, which straddles Sakai City and Matsubara City, is one of Osaka's four major green spaces and is decorated with approximately 320,000 flowers and trees of 200 different species.

  • Ohama Park

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-232-1489

    This historic park opened in 1879 (Meiji 12). It features a baseball field, sumo wrestling ring, gymnasium, swimming pool, and monkey island, making it a popular spot for sports and recreation for local residents.

  • Otori Taisha Shrine

    Nishi Ward, Sakai · 072-262-0040

    Known as the Ichinomiya of Izumi Province, this historic shrine enshrines Emperor Yamato Takeru as its main deity.

  • Yamaguchi Family Residence

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-224-1155

    The Yamaguchi Residence is a valuable building, designated as a National Important Cultural Property, as it is one of the few remaining townhouses built in the early Edo period, shortly after the town of Sakai was completely destroyed by fire during the Siege of Osaka.

  • Hochigai Shrine

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-232-1216

    Located on the border of the three countries of Settsu, Kawachi, and Izumi, this shrine has long been worshipped as a god who wards off disasters, and is known for praying for protection from misfortune when building a new house, moving, or traveling.

  • Old Sakai Lighthouse

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-228-7198

    Built in 1877, this lighthouse is one of the oldest Western-style wooden lighthouses still standing in Japan. It has been designated a national historic site and is a beloved symbol of Sakai.

  • Honganji Sakai Betsuin

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-232-4417

    Honganji Sakai Betsuin Temple is the largest wooden building in Sakai City. It was once used as the Sakai Prefectural Office and is now known as the "Sakai Prefectural Office Ruins," a designated historic site of Osaka Prefecture.

  • Sakurai Shrine

    Minami Ward, Sakai · 072-297-0043

    This shrine is listed in the Engishiki-naisha (Engishiki-listed shrine) in Sakai City, Osaka Prefecture.

  • Hodoji Temple

    Minami Ward, Sakai · 072-297-0705

    This temple was founded in 670 AD (the 9th year of Emperor Tenji's reign) at the behest of Emperor Tenji.

  • Hamadera Park

    Nishi Ward, Sakai · 072-261-0936

    Designated as a public park in 1873, this is one of Osaka's oldest parks. It is a comprehensive park featuring a beautiful pine forest that has been selected as one of Japan's 100 most famous pines, a pool with a giant slide, a rose garden, and more.

  • Hamadera Park Rose Garden

    Nishi Ward, Sakai · 072-261-0936

    Hamadera Park is one of Osaka's oldest prefectural parks, and is home to a vast rose garden covering approximately 2.7 hectares. Approximately 6,000 roses of 500 varieties bloom in a variety of Japanese and Western-style landscapes.

  • Hamadera Park Old Station Building

    Nishi Ward, Sakai · 072-261-0033

    The half-timbered wooden station building was designed by Tatsuno Kingo and Kataoka Yasushi in 1907. It is designated as a national tangible cultural property.

  • Seigakuin

    Sakai · 072-228-1501

    It has a history as a training center for Shugendo sects, and is a registered tangible cultural property of Japan.

  • Shirasagi Park Iris Garden

    Higashi Ward, Sakai · 072-276-6818

    This is a famous spot where the iris, the official flower of Sakai City, beautifully colors the cloudy skies of the rainy season. Approximately 150 varieties and 10,000 irises bloom in the 90,000 square meter grounds from late May to early June.

  • Mozu Hachiman Shrine

    Kita Ward, Sakai · 072-252-1089

    Mozu Hachimangu Shrine is located in the heart of the Mozu Kofun Group and was founded approximately 1,400 years ago during the reign of Emperor Kinmei.

  • Mozu Kofun Group

    Sakai Ward, Sakai

    This World Heritage Site is located in Sakai City, Osaka Prefecture. It is a group of 44 ancient tombs, including the Emperor Nintoku Tomb, and huge keyhole-shaped tombs built between the end of the 4th century and the second half of the 6th century.

  • Shounji Temple

    Sakai · 072-232-2131

    This temple boasts a beautiful dry landscape garden with white sand arranged to resemble flowing water. Founded by Takuan Soho, it features a nationally designated Important Cultural Property and a scenic garden designated by Osaka Prefecture.

  • Sugawara Shrine

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-232-2450

    Sugawara Shrine is affectionately known as "Sakai's Tenjin-san." It enshrines Sugawara no Michizane as its deity, and within its grounds are Sakai Ebisu Shrine and Japan's oldest Yakuso Shrine. The Niomon Gate is a tangible cultural property designated by Osaka Prefecture.

  • Sugao Shrine

    Mihara Ward, Sakai · 072-361-0323

    This shrine is one of the shrines listed in the Engishiki (an ancient Japanese legal code). It enshrines Tenjin (Sugawara no Michizane), and its grounds contain "Sugazawa," which is said to be the birthplace of Michizane.

  • Hagiwara Tenjin (Hagiwara Shrine)

    Higashi Ward, Sakai · 072-285-0295

    This historic shrine in Sakai City is said to have originated over 1,000 years ago as a shrine dedicated to Sugawara no Michizane. Within the shrine grounds, the former main hall, which dates back approximately 350 years and faithfully retains the Momoyama style, is preserved and open to the public.

  • Tourist Farm Minami Rakuen

    Minami Ward, Sakai · 072-298-5037

    Located in Sakai City, Osaka Prefecture, this vast tourist farm was reclaimed in 1931. In addition to experiencing seasonal fruit and vegetable harvesting, you can also enjoy barbecues, a fishing pond, and athletics.

  • Gunsmith House

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-228-1501

    The only remaining workshop and residence of a gunsmith from the Edo period in Japan. Designated a tangible cultural property by Sakai City.

  • Akaguchi Shrine

    Sakai Ward, Sakai · 072-221-0171

    This shrine is said to have been built by order of Empress Jingu to protect the entrance to Osaka Bay and is the guardian deity of Izumi Sakai Minami-sho (Sakai's main guardian deity). It is also known as the inner sanctuary of Sumiyoshi.

  • Takabayashi Family Residence

    Kita Ward, Sakai

    It is one of the oldest private houses in Osaka Prefecture. Its current form was completed over 200 years ago during the Kansei era. It is a valuable historical building that retains the layout of a village headman's residence from the Edo period in the Kinki region.

  • Kurohimeyama Tomb

    Mihara Ward, Sakai · 072-362-2737

    The Kurohimeyama Kofun is a keyhole-shaped burial mound measuring 114 meters in length, 64 meters in width at the front, and 11 meters in height.
