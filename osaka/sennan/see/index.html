Sights in Sennan, Osaka

Sennan, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 7 places

See

  • Tarui Southern Beach

    Sennan · 072-447-8811

    This beautiful artificial beach features white sand and palm trees. It is a swimming spot with a panoramic view of Rinku Town, Kansai International Airport, and the connecting bridge.

  • David Austin English Rose Garden and Plant Center

    Sennan · 072-480-0031

    Located at Hanasaki Farm in Sennan, Osaka, this garden boasts one of the world's largest collections of English roses and features a state-of-the-art display of roses. In spring, 3,000 roses bloom in its full glory.

  • Ichioka Shrine

    Sennan · 072-484-0644

    Ichioka Shrine, affectionately known as "Kaiei-gu" or "Gion-san," is a spot dedicated to academic success, worshiping the god of wisdom, Takehaya Susanoo-no-Mikoto, as its main deity. Within the shrine grounds are the ruins of Kaikai-ji Temple, a nationally designated historic site.

  • Sennan Marble Beach

    Sennan · 072-483-8191

    This white-marbled beach was the first-ever officially certified “Lovers Sanctuary” by Osaka prefecture.

  • Otoko Shrine (Ono Shrine)

    Sennan · 072-483-2266

    Otoko Shrine, located in Sennan City, Osaka Prefecture, is a former Engishiki shrine with a history dating back to before Christ. It enshrines Itsutsu no Mikoto and is also known as Otakebi no Miya.

  • Tanekawa Shrine

    Sennan · 072-483-5041

    This shrine is located in Sennan City, Osaka Prefecture. It has a history of calming a smallpox epidemic that swept through prayers in 1545, and is now cherished as the main shrine of the region. It is also famous as a cherry blossom viewing spot in spring, when approximately 100 cherry trees bloom.

  • Konkumaji plum grove

    Sennan · 072-447-8811

    Located in the Kinyuji district in the mountainous area of Sennan City, this famous plum blossom spot has been in existence since the Edo period.
