Settsu, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 1 place

Do

  • Osaka Ranma Carving

    Settsu · 072-646-8470

    This spot continues to pass on the traditional Osaka craft of carving techniques known as "Osaka Ranma." In addition to being able to observe the delicate techniques used in making cedar and cypress, the museum also offers hands-on workshops where you can try making a mini ranma.
