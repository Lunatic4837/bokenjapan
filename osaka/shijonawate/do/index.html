  • Shijonawate Shrine

    Shijonawate · 072-876-0044

    This shrine enshrines Kusunoki Masayuki and 24 of his family members, who were defeated by the Ashikaga army in the Battle of Shijonawate during the Nanboku-cho period. This is a place associated with the famous general who is revered as Lord Konan.
