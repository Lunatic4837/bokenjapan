Sights in Shijonawate, Osaka

Shijonawate, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

See

  • Shijonawate City History and Folklore Museum

    Shijonawate · 072-878-4558

    This museum is designed to help children enjoy learning about history. With the quiz program "The Knowledgeable Doctor" and model exhibits, even beginners can learn about history in an easy-to-understand way.

  • Prefectural Forest (Muroike Green Cultural Park)

    Shijonawate · 072-879-6362

    This natural park has two walking trails, the "Family Trail" and the "Nature Trail," centered around Muroike Pond. You can also enjoy observing wetland plants and wild birds.
