  • Suntory Yamazaki Distillery

    Shimamoto, Osaka · 075-962-1423

    A comprehensive introduction to Suntory Yamazaki Distillery, Japan's oldest malt whisky distillery. Enjoy a tour of the manufacturing process (lottery system), exhibitions at the whisky museum, and paid tastings. It's a 10-minute walk from JR Yamazaki Station or Hankyu Oyamazaki Station.
