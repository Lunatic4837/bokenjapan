Sights

  • Minase Shrine

    Shimamoto, Osaka · 075-961-0078

    Located in Hommachi, Osaka Prefecture, Minase Shrine is a historic spot dedicated to Emperor Gotoba. The "Rikyu no Mizu" (Water of the Imperial Villa), certified by the Ministry of the Environment, has been selected as one of Japan's 100 best waters, and is famous as a water source favored by Sen no Rikyu.
