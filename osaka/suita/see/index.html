Sights in Suita, Osaka

Suita, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 13 places

See

  • EXPOCITY

    Suita · 06-6170-5590

    The fun of "playing, learning, and discovering" all in one place! This large-scale complex includes the NIFREL Museum of Living Creatures, Japan's tallest Ferris wheel, and Mitsui Shopping Park LaLaport EXPOCITY, which has around 300 stores.

  • OSAKA WHEEL

    Suita · 06-6170-3246

    At 123 meters tall, it's the largest Ferris wheel in Japan. All gondolas have see-through floors, and you can enjoy the view of Osaka from a comfortable, air-conditioned space.

  • Asahi Beer Museum

    Suita · 06-6388-1943

    This experiential museum, based on the concept of "a place where you can encounter the best, most memorable beer," allows you to experience the world of Asahi Beer with all five senses. You can enjoy VR experiences, videos of the manufacturing process, tastings, and more.

  • Duskin Museum

    Suita · 06-6821-5000

    The facility consists of two spaces: the Cleaning Hall, where you can learn about the history of cleaning, and the Mister Donut Museum, where you can learn about the history of Mister Donut and even try making donuts.

  • The Living Museum: NIFREL

    Suita · 0570-022-060

    This "living museum" is a unique genre that combines an aquarium, zoo, and art museum, with the concept of "touching the senses."

  • Expo ’70 Commemorative Park

    Suita · 06-6877-7387

    This cultural park was built on the site of the 1970 World Exposition (Osaka Expo). You can enjoy the Tower of the Sun, seasonal flowers, athletic facilities, the EXPO '70 Pavilion, and more.

  • Saidera Temple

    Suita · 06-6388-2413

    This historic temple was founded by Gyoki. It is known for its bell tower, which retains the style of the early Edo period, the holy water "Sai no Shimizu" that is said to be effective for eye ailments, and the "Cucumber Kaji" ceremony held on the day of the Ox during the Dog Days.

  • Kishibe Shrine

    Suita · 06-6388-5735

    Located on Mt. Shikinzan in Suita City, Osaka Prefecture, this shrine is known for its magnificent Momoyama-style shrine building, a restoration of an important cultural property, and for the "Donji" ritual, a city-designated intangible folk cultural property.

  • National Museum of Ethnology

    Suita · 06-6876-2151

    Located in Osaka's Expo'70 Commemorative Park, this is one of the world's largest ethnological museums, with a focus on cultural anthropology and ethnology. It houses approximately 345,000 items, including everyday items and ethnic costumes collected by researchers from around the world.

  • Tarumi Shrine

    Suita · 06-6384-1526

    Tarumi Shrine is located in Suita City, Osaka Prefecture. The shrine's name, "Tarumi," means "water flowing down a cliff," and it has been worshipped as the god of water since ancient times.

  • Japan Folk Crafts Museum, Osaka

    Suita · 06-6877-1971

    Located within Expo'70 Commemorative Park, this museum displays exquisite crafts from Japan and around the world, including ceramics, dyed textiles, and paintings. It occupies the space of a pavilion from the 1970 World Exposition in Japan.

  • Former Nishio Residence (Suita Cultural Creation Exchange Center)

    Suita · 06-6381-0001

    This modern Japanese-style building was once used by village headmen during the Edo period.

  • Izumidono Shrine

    Suita · 06-6388-5680

    This shrine in Suita City, Osaka Prefecture, was founded in 869.
