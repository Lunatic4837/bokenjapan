  • Eifuku-ji Temple (Tadaoka Town)

    Tadaoka, Osaka · 0725-32-3731

    Eifuku-ji Temple, located in Tadaoka-cho, Osaka Prefecture, is a historic temple built to commemorate Kusunoki Masashige, a hero of the Nanboku-cho period. Within its grounds stand five giant juniper trees, designated as natural monuments by Osaka Prefecture, preserving the temple's long history dating back to 1198.
