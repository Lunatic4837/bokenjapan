Do

  • Taishi Onsen

    Taishicho, Osaka · 0721-98-4126

    Taishi Onsen is located in Taishi Town, Osaka Prefecture, which is associated with Prince Shotoku. This natural hot spring boasts an open-air cypress bath and rock bath surrounded by the nature of Mount Nijo. It is the perfect place to refresh after touring the surrounding historical sites or hiking.
