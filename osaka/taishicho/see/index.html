Sights in Taishicho, Osaka

Taishicho, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 5 places

Sights

  • Eifuku-ji Temple

    Taishicho, Osaka · 0721-98-0019

    Eifuku-ji Temple in Taishi-cho, Osaka, is a famous temple known as "Kami no Taishi" (Upper Prince), home to the tomb of Prince Shotoku (Sankoichibyo).

  • Ue no Taishicho Kanko Mikan Sono

    Taishicho, Osaka · 0721-98-2552

    "Kami no Taishi Kanko Mikan Orchard," one of the largest in the Kansai region, located in Taishi Town, Osaka. You can enjoy mandarin orange picking and sweet potato digging during October and November.

  • Futagamiyama Manyo Forest

    Taishicho, Osaka

  • Kacho Shrine

    Taishicho, Osaka · 0721-98-0136

  • Takeuchi Kaido Historical Museum

    Taishicho, Osaka · 0721-98-3266

    This museum offers a deep dive into the history of the Takeuchi Kaido, Japan's oldest national highway. It showcases the changes along the road, dating back to the Asuka period, through exhibits of stone tools, pottery, and artifacts related to Prince Shotoku.
