  • Tajiri Marine Exchange Center

    Tajiri, Osaka · 072-465-0099

    Tajiri Marine Exchange Center in Tajiri Town, Osaka Prefecture, is a popular tourist hub known for its Sunday morning market, offshore fishing pond, and winter oyster huts. Visitors can enjoy purchasing fresh seafood, fishing, and the stunning views of the marina.
