Sights in Tajiri, Osaka

Tajiri, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 3 places

Sights

  • Kasho Shrine

    Tajiri, Osaka · 0724-65-4518

    Kasho Shrine, located in Tajiri-cho, Osaka Prefecture, boasts a main hall that preserves the architectural beauty of the Momoyama period and is designated as an Osaka Prefecture Tangible Cultural Property.

  • Kasuga Shrine (Tajiri)

    Tajiri, Osaka · 072-465-0465

    Kasuga Shrine in Tajiri-cho, Osaka Prefecture, is a historic shrine founded during the Hoki era. It enshrines four deities, including Takemikazuchi-no-Mikoto, and within its grounds stands a monument to the ancestors of onion cultivation, a rare sight nationwide.
