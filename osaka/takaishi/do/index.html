Things to do in Takaishi, Osaka

Takaishi, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Hamadera Park Transportation Amusement Park

    Takaishi · 072-261-8696

    This transportation amusement park in Takaishi City, Osaka Prefecture, is a must-see for railway enthusiasts, and features exhibits of the steam locomotive D51 and Hankai Electric Railway No. 130, as well as go-karts and children's trains.

  • Osaka Prefectural Rinkai Sports Center

    Takaishi · 072-268-8351

    This multi-purpose sports facility includes gymnasiums and conference rooms. You can enjoy a variety of activities, including an ice skating rink that is open all year round, as well as tennis, basketball, and futsal.
