Things to do in Takatsuki, Osaka

Takatsuki, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Kashida Bath (Takatsuki Forest Tourism Center)

    Takatsuki · 072-688-9400

    This hot spring is located within a nature experience facility directly operated by the Osaka Prefectural Forestry Cooperative. You can enjoy a forest bathing experience in a bathroom made of natural stone.

  • Takatsuki Shiitake Center

    Takatsuki · 072-688-9359

    Admission is free, and you can purchase the shiitake mushrooms you picked for 330 yen per 100g. You can grill and eat them fresh at the adjacent barbecue house.
