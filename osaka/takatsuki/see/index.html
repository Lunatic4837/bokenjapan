Sights in Takatsuki, Osaka

Takatsuki, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 8 places

See

  • Mount Pompon

    Takatsuki

    This 678.8m-high mountain was named after the "ponpon" sound that can be heard when you stomp on its summit. Located on the border between Takatsuki City, Osaka Prefecture and Kyoto Prefecture, the hiking trail along the Tokai Nature Trail is popular.

  • Shinike Haniwa Factory Park Historic Site

    Takatsuki · 072-695-8274

    This park preserves and maintains the Shinike ruins, the site of Japan's oldest and largest haniwa factory. This is where the haniwa for the Imashirozuka Tomb were manufactured, and visitors can tour the restored kilns and workshops for free.

  • Settsukyo Park

    Takatsuki · 072-674-7516

    This 42.65 hectare scenic park stretches across the upper reaches of the Akutagawa River in the northern part of Takatsuki City. The valley, lined with oddly shaped rocks and cliffs, is known as "Settsu Yabakei" and has been selected as one of Osaka's 100 best green spaces.

  • Honzanji Temple

    Takatsuki · 072-687-9921

    This ancient Tendai sect temple boasts numerous treasures, and the clear mountain air and tranquility cleanse the souls of visitors. It is said to have been founded by En no Gyoja and later established by Prince Kaisei in the 8th century.

  • Kabusanji Temple

    Takatsuki · 072-688-0788

    This ancient temple has a history of 1,300 years and is the first in Japan to house Bishamonten. Homa prayers are held every day without fail, and it is a famous spot where you can see fresh greenery in the spring and vibrant colors of the leaves in the fall.

  • Basho's haiku monument (Tamagawa Village)

    Takatsuki · 072-674-7411

    This monument, inscribed with the haiku of the haiku poet Matsuo Basho, reads, "Unohana ya akuraki willow no morigoshi." Located in a scenic spot known as Tamagawa in Mishima, Settsu Province, the unohana flowers bloom in full glory in early summer.

  • Nomi Shrine

    Takatsuki · 072-675-1316

    This historic shrine in Takatsuki City, Osaka Prefecture, was founded during the Heian period to quell epidemics. It was revered by successive lords of Takatsuki Castle as its guardian deity.

  • Takatsuki City Natural History Museum Aquapia Akutagawa

    Takatsuki · 072-692-5041

    This museum introduces the waterfront of the Akutagawa River, which flows through the center of Takatsuki City, as well as the local nature and history. It features a large aquarium displaying freshwater fish, and a wide selection of stuffed and specimen birds, plants, and insects.
