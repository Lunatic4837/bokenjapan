Things to do in Tondabayashi, Osaka

Tondabayashi, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Subaru Hall

    Tondabayashi · 0721-25-0222

    This multi-purpose facility accommodates a variety of uses, from large and small conference rooms to Japanese-style rooms and an impressive planetarium. The main hall has 806 seats, and the planetarium boasts a dome diameter of 20 meters and 200 seats.

  • Natural hot spring Tondabayashi Gakusan Onsen No. 2 spring

    Tondabayashi · 0721-33-0700

    This hot spring facility boasts an open-air bath with a panoramic view of the idyllic rural landscape and Mount Kongo and Mount Katsuragi in Yamato.
