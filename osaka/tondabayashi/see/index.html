Sights in Tondabayashi, Osaka

Tondabayashi, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 11 places

See

  • Yamabikoen

    Tondabayashi · 0721-34-5590

    This tourist farm is nestled in the quiet mountains of Tondabayashi City, Osaka Prefecture. In addition to mandarin orange picking, sweet potato digging, and chestnut gathering in the fall, you can enjoy barbecues year-round.

  • Nakamura Family Residence

    Tondabayashi · 0721-25-1000

    This townhouse was built around 1782 in Jinanimachi. It once operated as a sake brewery called "Sadoya," and Yoshida Shoin also stayed there. It is now a designated cultural property of Osaka Prefecture, and still retains the merchant style of the time. *The interior is not open to the public.

  • Shiroyama Orange Garden

    Tondabayashi · 0721-35-6943

    This is an experiential farm where people of all ages can enjoy the whole day, with activities such as fruit picking (mandarin oranges, sweet potato digging, and chestnut gathering), handmade play equipment, barbecues, crayfish fishing, and mini golf.

  • Tondabayashi Jinanimachi

    Tondabayashi · 0721-25-1000

    This area is designated as an Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings by the national government, and retains the town layout of the late Middle Ages. It is lined with historic houses, retaining the appearance of a religious autonomous city from the Sengoku period.

  • Tondabayashi City Agricultural Park (Savor Farm)

    Tondabayashi · 0721-35-3500

    This vast agricultural park in Tondabayashi City, Osaka Prefecture, is scheduled to reopen on August 2, 2025, where you can enjoy seasonal fruit and vegetable harvesting and BBQs.

  • Important Cultural Property Former Sugiyama Residence

    Tondabayashi · 0721-23-6117

    This merchant's residence is the oldest townhouse in Tondabayashi Jinanimachi and is designated as an Important Cultural Property of Japan. During the Edo period, it developed as a sake brewery and is also known as the birthplace of the poet Ishigami Tsuyuko.

  • Nansoan Kannonji Temple

    Tondabayashi · 0721-35-5161

    This temple is said to have been built by Kusunoki Masashige's wife, Hisako (Haikyouni), to commemorate the souls of her family. It is a historic spot where you can enjoy the magnificent autumn foliage.

  • Takitani Fudo Myo-o Temple

    Tondabayashi · 0721-34-0028

    This historic prayer hall was founded by Kobo Daishi during the Heian period. It is counted as one of Japan's three most important Fudo temples and is known for its blessings of business prosperity and protection from evil.

  • Migukurumitama Shrine

    Tondabayashi · 0721-23-3007

    The name "Migukurumitama" comes from the fact that the shrine enshrines "Mikukurumitama," the god who controls water and distributes water from the mountains to the land. It was founded by Emperor Sujin and the enshrined deity is Okuninushi no Mikoto.

  • Nishikiori Shrine

    Tondabayashi · 0721-25-2770

    The main hall, believed to have been founded in 1363, is a nationally designated Important Cultural Property. Situated in a historic area once known as Baekje Village, it preserves the culture and weaving techniques of immigrants to the present day.

  • Ryusenji Temple

    Tondabayashi · 0721-34-3134

    This ancient Shingon Buddhist temple is associated with the story of Soga no Umako's slaying of an evil dragon, and it also has a beautiful strolling garden with a pond decorated with seasonal flowers.
