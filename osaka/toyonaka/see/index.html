Sights in Toyonaka, Osaka

Toyonaka, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 6 places

See

  • Sumiyoshi Shrine (also known as Wakamiya Sumiyoshi Shrine)

    Toyonaka · 06-6863-0025

    This shrine originated from Itsukushima Shrine, founded by Taira no Kiyomori, on the site of Ishirenji Temple, which was established by Gyoki during the Tenpyo era. It is revered as the guardian shrine of Kosone-go in Toyonaka City.

  • Senri Tenjin (Kamishinden Tenjin Shrine)

    Toyonaka · 06-6834-5123

    The local deity of Senri. The main deity is Tenjin-sama (Michizane Sugawara), the god of academics, and the co-enshrined deity is Inari-o-kami, the god of business prosperity.

  • Museum of Osaka University

    Toyonaka · 06-6850-6284

    This museum displays academic specimens from Osaka University, housed in the Machikaneyama Shugakukan building completed in 1931. Its highlights include one of the world's largest crocodile fossils and materials related to Tekijuku and Kaitokudo.

  • Open-Air Museum of Old Japanese Farm Houses

    Toyonaka · 06-6862-3137

    Located within Hattori Ryokuchi Park in Toyonaka City, this is Japan's first open-air museum. It displays 12 representative Edo-period houses from all over Japan that have been relocated and restored.

  • Hattori Ryokuchi Park

    Toyonaka · 06-6862-4945

    This is a sports and recreation hub in Hokusetsu, with a variety of facilities including an urban greening botanical garden, an outdoor music hall, a Japanese folk house museum, a horse riding center, and a swimming pool.

  • Toko-in Temple of Hagi

    Toyonaka · 06-6852-3002

    This temple has a history of 1,300 years, and was founded by Gyoki in 735. It is known as the "Temple of Hagi" because of the approximately 3,000 bush clovers that bloom in the fall.
