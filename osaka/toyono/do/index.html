Do

  • Toyono Cosmos Village

    Toyono, Osaka · 072-739-1363

    About one million cosmos flowers bloom across roughly one hectare, with a walking path through the garden. Open from mid-September to late October (usually at their best from around 20 September to 10 October), 9:00 to 17:00, with free parking. Admission is 500 yen for adults and 300 yen for elementary and junior high school students.
