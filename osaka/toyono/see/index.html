Sights in Toyono, Osaka

Toyono, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 3 places

Sights

  • Hatsutani River

    Toyono, Osaka

    A scenic gorge chosen as one of the Osaka Midori 100 green sites. It forms part of the hiking route towards Mount Myoken and draws visitors through all four seasons.

  • Takayama Ukon Birthplace Monument

    Toyono, Osaka

    A monument marking the birthplace of Takayama Ukon, a Sengoku-period daimyo.

  • Takayama Terraced Rice Fields

    Toyono, Osaka

    Terraced rice fields in the Takayama district, selected as a View Spot Osaka for their rural scenery. They are at their most beautiful from rice planting in May until the harvest in September.
