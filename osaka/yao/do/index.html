Things to do in Yao, Osaka

Yao, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 2 places

Do

  • Kompeitou Museum Yao

    Yao · 072-948-1339

    This facility allows you to learn about the history and culture of Konpeito, a sweet that originated in the South Seas and was introduced from Portugal, and experience making your own original Konpeito. The coloring and flavoring experience using a mini pot is popular.

  • Kyuhoji Green Space

    Yao · 072-992-2489

    One of Osaka's four major green spaces, it boasts an area roughly 10 times larger than Koshien Stadium. It is a vast park equipped with large playground equipment, sports facilities, a BBQ area, and a summer-only pool.
