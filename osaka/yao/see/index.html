Sights in Yao, Osaka

Yao, Osaka: things to do, food and stays · Osaka · 4 places

See

  • Kyuhoji Jinanimachi

    Yao · 072-924-6371

    This historic townscape still retains the layout from 470 years ago. Jinanimachi was formed around Kenshoji Temple, and the grid-like roads remain as they were back then.

  • Jokoji Temple

    Yao · 072-922-7749

    Jokoji Temple in Yao City, Osaka Prefecture, is known as the birthplace of Kawachi Ondo and has been selected as one of the "Top 100 Soundscapes of Japan." It is famous for its bloody ceiling, which tells the story of the Summer Siege of Osaka, and for its connection to Ashikaga Yoshimitsu.

  • Kusumoto Shrine

    Yao

    This shrine is one of the shrines listed in the Engishiki (an ancient Japanese legal code), and it is said that Mononobe no Moriya enshrined it as the guardian deity of Inamura Castle. It has been revered since ancient times as a god of healing from illness, agriculture, and martial arts.

  • Prefectural Forest (Mizunomi Park)

    Yao · 072-988-4184

    This park is rich in nature and offers a panoramic view of the Osaka Plain, as well as one of the prefecture's largest hydrangea and azalea gardens.
