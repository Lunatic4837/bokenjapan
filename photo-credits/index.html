Photo credits

Who took the photos on BokenJapan, under which licence, and where to find the original.

How credits work here

Our cards stay clean: a card never carries a credit line, and every photo links to the place it shows. Where a photo’s licence asks for attribution (for example Creative Commons BY or BY-SA), the author, licence and source are listed on this page and on the prefecture’s own credit page below.

Data and map credits

Places and addresses. Some dining listings (marked “Dining in”) use data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0. Pages that carry these listings show a short “Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors” line in the footer and link here.

Prefecture maps. Municipality boundaries come from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) National Land Numerical Information, Administrative Area Data (N03, 2026), and have been simplified by BokenJapan for display. Corrections and removals

If a photo is credited wrongly, or you are the rights holder and want it changed or removed, write to boken_japan@agentmail.to and we will fix it.
