Do in Arita

Arita · Saga · 12 places

Do

  • Mount Kurokami

    Mountain of rock walls and deep forest; the Ontake and Medake crags from Chimachibo Park are a highlight.

    Arita, Saga · 080-2157-0357 · Official / source link

  • Munemasa Shuzo

    Sake brewery in Arita.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-41-0020 · Official / source link

  • Arita Porcelain Park

    Arita ware theme park centred on a replica of Dresden's Zwinger Palace.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-41-0030 · Official / source link

  • Ryumon Gorge

    Gorge above Ryumon Dam with a riverside path leading up to Mount Kurokami.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-46-5616 · Official / source link

  • Arita Onsen

    Wooden hot-spring building with large baths, sauna, open-air bath and private baths.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-42-6988 · Official / source link

  • Akae-za Independent Artist Workshop

    Incubator workshop for young Arita potters; watch them work, try pottery and buy their pieces.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-41-1310 · Official / source link

  • Daikichi Village

    Reservation-only outdoor spot for vegetable picking, bouldering and trampoline; activities vary by season.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-25-9003 · Official / source link

  • Ryumon Campground

    Year-round campground at the foot of Mount Kurokami beside Ryumon Gorge.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-46-4022 · Official / source link

  • Rokuro-za Pottery Workshop

    Try wheel-throwing and porcelain painting in 30–60 minutes with an instructor.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-41-1302 · Official / source link

  • Koraku Kiln Factory Tour & Treasure Hunting

    Kiln founded in 1865 on a 10,000-tsubo site; factory tour and porcelain treasure hunt.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-42-4121 · Official / source link

  • Kimono de Arita Sanpo (kimono walk)

    Rent one of about 80 kimono and stroll Arita's historic streets; sizes for men and children too.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2121 · Official / source link

  • Rekishi to Bunka no Mori Park

    Main venue of the 1996 World Ceramics Expo, with eight zones and a children's play area.

    Arita, Saga · 0955-46-5010 · Official / source link
