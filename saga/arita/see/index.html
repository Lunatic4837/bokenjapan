Sights in Arita, Saga

Arita · Saga · 18 places

See

  • Great Ginkgo of Izumiyama Benzaiten

    Arita, Saga · 0955-46-2500

    1,000-year-old, 30.5 m ginkgo, a national natural monument that turns gold in late November.

  • Uchiyama Streetscape

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2121

    Townhouses from four eras, Edo to Showa, in a national preservation district.

  • Tozan Shrine

    Arita, Saga · 0955-42-3310

    1658 shrine above Arita with a porcelain torii gate.

  • Koransha

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2132

    Japan's first porcelain maker and trader, founded in 1879; showroom and historic porcelain gallery.

  • Fukagawa Seiji China on the Park

    Arita, Saga · 0955-46-3900

    Showroom park of Fukagawa Seiji, an Arita porcelain maker founded in 1894 and Imperial Household purveyor from 1910.

  • Former Tashiro Family Western-style House

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-4866

    1876 house built by a porcelain trader's family to host foreign buyers.

  • Noritoh Kiln

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2890

    Kiln and gallery in Nangawara focusing on early-1600s Arita ware with natural glazes.

  • Yi Sam-pyeong Monument

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2121

    1917 memorial to Arita ware's founder, with a view over the town.

  • Arita History and Folklore Museum East Wing & Arita Porcelain Reference Hall

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2678

    Museum of Arita's history and folk life, with Edo-period kiln licences and wartime porcelain grenades and coins.

  • Izumiyama Quarry

    Arita, Saga · 0955-46-2500

    Birthplace of Japanese porcelain, where Yi Sam-pyeong found porcelain stone in the early 17th century.

  • Gen-emon Kiln

    Arita, Saga · 0955-42-4164

    Kiln with more than 260 years in Arita, known for modern takes on Ko-Imari designs.

  • Kyushu Ceramic Museum

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-3681

    Prefectural museum of Arita, Imari and other Kyushu ceramics.

  • Imaemon Kiln

    Arita, Saga · 0955-42-3101

    370-year-old kiln that inherited the Iro-Nabeshima overglaze tradition; holder of an Important Intangible Cultural Property.

  • Gaku Rice Terraces

    Arita, Saga · 0955-46-5616

    Curving terraced rice paddies in Arita's mountain district.

  • Arita Uchiyama Preservation District

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2121

    About 2 km of Edo- to Showa-era Japanese and Western buildings, a national preservation district for historic buildings.

  • Tonbai Walls

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2121

    Walls built from old kiln firebricks and kiln tools set in red clay.

  • Arita Ceramic Art Museum

    Arita, Saga · 0955-42-3372

    Saga's oldest art museum, in an 1874 stone porcelain warehouse in central Uchiyama.

  • Kakiemon Kiln

    Arita, Saga · 0955-43-2267

    Kiln that developed Japan's first overglaze enamel technique, the origin of the Kakiemon style.
