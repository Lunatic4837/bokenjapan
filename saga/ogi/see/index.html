Sights in Ogi, Saga

Ogi · Saga · 10 places

See

  • Eriyama Rice Terraces (Red Spider Lilies)

    Ogi, Saga

    About 600 terraced paddies at 250 m, lined with red spider lilies in late September.

  • Kiyomizu Falls

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-37-6129

    75 m waterfall also called "Tamasudare no Taki".

  • Suga Shrine

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-72-7115

    Shrine founded in 1316; 153 steep steps lead to a view over Ogi.

  • Ashikari Coast Mudskipper Reserve

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-37-6129

    Reserve for mudskippers and fiddler crabs; courtship in May–June.

  • Ogi Park

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-73-8813

    Garden built by the first two Ogi domain lords; one of Japan's 100 best cherry-blossom spots.

  • Muraoka Sohonpo Yokan Museum

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-72-2131

    Yokan museum in a 1941 brick building.

  • Ushio Plum Grove

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-37-6125

    About 5,000 plum trees on Mount Ushio, best from late February to early March.

  • Gochiku Kannon-do

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-73-8809

    Kannon hall built in 1908 by the calligrapher Nakabayashi Gochiku.

  • Seigan-ji

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-37-6129

    Family temple of the Ogi Nabeshima lords, founded in 1684, with a Chinese-style gate from 1852.

  • Nakabayashi Gochiku Memorial Museum

    Ogi, Saga · 0952-71-1132

    About 700 works by Gochiku, one of the three great Meiji calligraphers.
