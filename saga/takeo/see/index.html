  • Takeo Onsen Shinkan

  • Takeo Children's Library

  • Great Camphor of Tsukazaki

  • Hiryu Kiln

    Takeo, Saga · 0954-27-3383

  • Mifunegaoka Plum Garden

    Takeo, Saga · 0954-22-2010

  • Baba Mountain Cherry

    Takeo, Saga · 0954-27-2001

  • Daisho-ji (Hydrangea Temple)

    Takeo, Saga · 0954-36-4934

    Temple founded in 709 by Gyoki, one of Japan's three great Fudo temples; hydrangeas in June–July.
