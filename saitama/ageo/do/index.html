Things to do in Ageo, Saitama

Ageo · Saitama · 5 places

Do

  • Enomoto Farm

    Ageo, Saitama · 048-726-1306

    Farm experiences and gelato and yogurt from fresh milk. Cows, chickens, bantams, mini pigs, rabbits and guinea pigs live there.

  • Kamihira Park

    Ageo, Saitama · 048-776-8986

    Comprehensive park using good natural forest, with seasonal walks, waterside plaza and play equipment for citizen rest and exchange.

  • Ageo Maruyama Park

    Ageo, Saitama · 048-781-0163

    Comprehensive park opened in 1978 themed harmony of water and greenery, with animal corner, playground, barbecue, nature hall and pond.

  • Tozaki Park

    Ageo, Saitama · 048-783-0577

    District park centered on an 18-hole park golf course, with children's and multi-purpose plazas and a jogging loop.

  • Ageo Sports Park

    Ageo, Saitama · 048-771-4245

    1.5 km southeast of Ageo Station; athletics field and gym for 1967 National Sports Festival; prefecture's first leisure pool in 1971.
