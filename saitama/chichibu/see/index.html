Sights in Chichibu, Saitama

Chichibu · Saitama · 3 places

See

  • Banba Street

    Chichibu, Saitama · 0494-25-5209

    Stone-paved street from Ohanabatake Station to Chichibu Shrine, with many remaining Taisho romantic buildings and shops.

  • Mitsumine Shrine

    Chichibu, Saitama · 0494-55-0241

    Shrine said to date to Emperor Keiko about 2000 years ago, revered by the shogun and Kishu families.

  • Nakatsu Gorge

    Chichibu, Saitama · 0494-55-0707

    About 10 km of gorge between former Shiozawa and Nakatsugawa, a prefectural scenic site with cliffs up to 100 m.
