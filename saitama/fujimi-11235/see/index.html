Sights in Fujimi, Saitama

Fujimi · Saitama · 4 places

See

  • Cosmos Road

    Fujimi, Saitama · 049-251-2711

    Cosmos flowers line about 600 meters of the Fujimi cycling course and peak from late September to early October.

  • Nambata Castle Museum and Park

    Fujimi, Saitama · 049-253-4664

    Long park split by the central museum: east restored castle zone, west old houses relocated from the city.

  • Mizuko Shell Mound Museum and Park

    Fujimi, Saitama · 049-251-9686

    National historic site park, about 40,000 square meters, preserving an Early Jomon shell mound from 5500-6500 years ago.

  • Yamazaki Park

    Fujimi, Saitama · 049-251-2711

    Developed to mark 20 years of city status and nicknamed Seseragi Iris Garden. Many citizens visit for June irises.
