Things to do in Fukaya, Saitama

Fukaya · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Hanazono Forest

    Fukaya, Saitama · 048-579-0101

    Sweets theme park two minutes by car from Hanazono IC, modeled on a quiet European mansion.

  • Fukaya Terrace Park

    Fukaya, Saitama · 048-579-2022

    Circular event plaza, large play equipment themed on vegetables and flowers, city digital signage, and a splash pool.

  • Saitama Prefecture Agriculture and Forestry Park

    Fukaya, Saitama · 048-583-2301

    Set up to experience agriculture and forestry and train related workers. Harvest representative Saitama crops and try woodworking.

  • Fukaya Green Park and Aqua Paradise Patio

    Fukaya, Saitama · 048-574-5000

    54,000-square-meter park with an indoor heated pool using cleaning-center waste heat, produce sales, a restaurant, and display fields.
