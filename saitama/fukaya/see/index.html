Sights in Fukaya, Saitama

Fukaya · Saitama · 3 places

See

  • Fukaya Station

    Fukaya, Saitama

    Fukaya's symbol, a brick town since early Meiji. Western-style building references Tokyo Station, which shipped about 8.83 million Fukaya bricks.

  • Former Shibusawa Residence Naka no Ie

    Fukaya, Saitama · 048-587-1100

    Eiichi Shibusawa's birthplace. Main house built in 1895 by his sister and her husband; his upper parlor can be viewed.

  • Fukaya Green Kingdom

    Fukaya, Saitama · 048-551-5551

    Base for Garden City Fukaya citizen activities; a citizens' forest with plum, camellia, maple, and volunteer-tended seasonal flowers.
