Sights in Gyoda, Saitama

Gyoda · Saitama · 8 places

See

  • Oshi Castle Ruins and Gyoda City Local Museum

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-554-5911

    One of Kanto's seven famous castles, it withstood Ishida Mitsunari's water attack and inspired Nobo no Shiro.

  • Sakitama Kofun Park

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-559-1111

    Park of nine large burial mounds, a national special historic site; a national treasure sword was unearthed at Inariyama Kofun.

  • Gyoda City World's Largest Rice Paddy Art (2026 Minecraft Collaboration) and Ancient Lotus Village

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-559-0770

    About 1,000 people planted rice June 13-14; four colorful varieties grow well and are at peak viewing.

  • Gyoda City Mizuki Park

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-556-1111

    Park in central Gyoda developed from remaining Oshi Castle moats and marshes, with lawn plaza, garden, and ponds.

  • Ozeki Nature Observation Room

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-557-1501

    See fish climbing the Tone Ozeki fishway through windows, with sweetfish in early summer and salmon in autumn.

  • Gyoda Hachiman Shrine

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-554-5926

    Famous as the birthplace of Gyoda Hana-temizu, hosting monthly Light of Hope light-up events.

  • Sakitama Shrine

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-559-0464

    Solemn ancient shrine over a thousand years old, origin of Saitama's name, on 8.7-meter-high Sengen-zuka.

  • Cafe Gallery Takazawa Memorial Hall

    Gyoda, Saitama · 048-556-1774

    Edo-period national registered cultural property and garden cafe serving keyhole-shaped ancient rice curry and handmade cakes.
