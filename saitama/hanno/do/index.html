Things to do in Hanno, Saitama

Hanno · Saitama · 5 places

Do

  • Moominvalley Park

    Hanno, Saitama · 0570-03-1066

    Meet the Moomin family, friends, and new discoveries at Moomin House, bathing hut, lighthouse, and experience facilities.

  • Tove Jansson Akebono Children's Forest Park

    Hanno, Saitama · 042-972-7711

    Nordic fairy-tale park with greenery and unique imaginative buildings, where anyone can play freely and relax.

  • Hanno Riverbed

    Hanno, Saitama · 042-980-5051

    Fifteen-minute walk from the station; crowded Golden Week and summer river-play spot with green trees and red Wariiwa Bridge.

  • OH!!! The Magic of Fermentation, Health, and Food

    Hanno, Saitama · 042-975-7001

    Fermentation theme park operated by pickle maker Pickles Corporation, with a shop, bakery, and restaurant.

  • Miyazawa Lake Onsen Kirari Bettei

    Hanno, Saitama · 042-983-4126

    Weakly alkaline natural onsen (water added, heated) said near beauty-bath effects, plus popular mixed bedrock bath and seasonal restaurant.
