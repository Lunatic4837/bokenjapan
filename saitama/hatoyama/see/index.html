Sights in Hatoyama, Saitama

Hatoyama · Saitama · 4 places

See

  • Ishizaka Forest

    Hatoyama, Saitama · 049-296-7887

    Woodland where people easily feel nature, build life purpose and health, and conserve ecosystems.

  • Akanuma Ancient Roof Tile Kiln Ruins

    Hatoyama, Saitama · 049-296-3862

    Seventh-century kiln remains, said among Saitama's oldest and designated a prefectural historic site, once thought built for Musashi Kokubunji Temple.

  • Takanokura Fureai Nature Park

    Hatoyama, Saitama · 049-296-7887

    Hatoyama park with one large designated landscape fir near Midoyama, 88 sites, and Hachiman Shrine's natural monument oak.

  • Oshamojiyama Park

    Hatoyama, Saitama · 049-296-5893

    Southern gateway park covered in spring cherries and azaleas, busy with blossom viewers, named for a rice-paddle goddess shrine below.
