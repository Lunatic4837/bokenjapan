Things to do in Hidaka, Saitama

Hidaka · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Mount Hiwada

    Hidaka, Saitama · 042-989-2111

    305-meter mountain loved as Hidaka City's symbol, with hiking courses, gentle Onnazaka, rocky Otokozaka, and a summit pagoda.

  • Saiboku

    Hidaka, Saitama · 0120-112-922

    A pork theme park in Hidaka drawing 4 million visitors yearly, offering ham, sausage, dining, athletics, and natural onsen.

  • Kachofugetsu Natural Onsen

    Hidaka, Saitama · 042-919-2626

    Salt-rich spring water from 2000 meters underground warms the body to the core; also called the warming bath.

  • Soy Sauce Kingdom Hidaka Main Store

    Hidaka, Saitama · 042-985-8011

    Long-established soy sauce brewery with factory tours and two pressing experiences, including paid wooden-barrel pressing.
