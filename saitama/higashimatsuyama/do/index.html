Do

  • Saitama Children's Animal Nature Park

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-35-1234

    Vast 46-hectare grounds in the Hiki Hills feature giraffes, red pandas, koalas, and Penguin Hills for Humboldt penguins.

  • Higashimatsuyama City Agriculture and Forestry Park

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-39-0150

    Theme park to experience farming via strawberry picking and vegetable harvests. Popular Hilltop Cafe local light meals and seasonal desserts.

  • Higashimatsuyama Kurakake Clear Stream Village

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-81-6868

    River spot near Tokigawa Kurakake Bridge developed with Saitama Prefecture for river play and hands-free barbecue amid greenery.

  • Fossil and Nature Experience Hall

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-35-3892

    Nearby Iwadono Hills are Tokigawa Group sea strata from 15 to 10 million years ago, known for fossils.
