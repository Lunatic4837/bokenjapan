Higashimatsuyama · Saitama · 5 places

See

  • Saitama Peace Museum

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-35-4111

    In Monomiyama Park, it aims to pass on fading war experiences and convey war's misery and peace's value.

  • Yakyu Inari Shrine

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-22-2104

    Founded 712. Minamoto no Yorinobu donated the hall after victory from a dream of a god riding a white fox.

  • Maruki Gallery for the Hiroshima Panels

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-22-3266

    Art museum built by painters Iri and Toshi Maruki so anyone can always see their joint Hiroshima Panels.

  • Iwadono Kannon Shobo-ji

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-34-4156

    Tenth Bando 33 Kannon temple, long revered with a temple town. Takeda forces placed their headquarters here attacking Matsuyama Castle.

  • Higashimatsuyama Peony Garden

    Higashimatsuyama, Saitama · 0493-81-7607

    Opened 1990 to promote peonies, the city flower. Colorful blooms cover about 30,000 square meters mid-April to early May.
