Sights in Honjo, Saitama

Honjo · Saitama · 7 places

See

  • Marigold Hill Park

    Honjo, Saitama

    Park east of Honjo-Waseda Station; a sundial hill views Mount Akagi, Mount Haruna, seasonal flowers, and illumination.

  • Joshin-in Hyakutai Kannon Hall

    Honjo, Saitama · 0495-72-6742

    Built to mourn 1783 Shinshu Mount Asama eruption victims, called Sazaedo. Unusual two-tier exterior, three-tier spiral corridor inside.

  • Maze Lake

    Honjo, Saitama · 0495-72-1334

    Eastern Japan's oldest gravity concrete agricultural dam; embankment and management bridge are registered cultural properties, known for fishing.

  • Honjo Waseda no Mori Museum

    Honjo, Saitama · 0495-71-6878

    Jointly opened by Honjo City and Waseda University to trace Honjo history from the Paleolithic via archaeological materials.

  • Former Honjo Commercial Bank Brick Warehouse

    Honjo, Saitama · 0495-71-6685

    1896 brick warehouse stored cocoons and raw silk, supporting Honjo's silk industry; a registered tangible cultural property.

  • Kotsuhata Wisteria (Chosen-ji)

    Honjo, Saitama · 0495-72-1331

    About 650-year-old Kotsuhata Wisteria at Chosen-ji, a prefectural natural monument among 100 eastern flower temples.

  • Hanawa Hokiichi Memorial Museum

    Honjo, Saitama · 0495-72-6032

    Hanawa Hokiichi memorial museum for the blind mid-Edo scholar. About 200 items include Gunsho Ruiju and his mother's hand-sewn pouch.
