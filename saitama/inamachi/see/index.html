Sights in Inamachi, Saitama

Inamachi · Saitama · 5 places

See

  • Ina Town Establishment Memorial Park

    Inamachi, Saitama · 048-721-2111

    Saitama's largest rose garden blooms in spring and autumn, with 400 varieties and 5,000 bushes, plus a hydrangea garden.

  • Ina Clan Residence Site

    Inamachi, Saitama · 048-721-2111

    Base of Ina Tadatsugu, ancestor of the Kanto deputy Ina clan, who did flood control, irrigation, and new-field development.

  • Musen-yama and KDDI Forest

    Inamachi, Saitama · 048-721-2111

    Green Trust Site No. 13 on the Omiya Plateau's east edge, with konara oak woods and spring cherry rows.

  • Ina Town Local History Museum

    Inamachi, Saitama · 048-721-2111

    Comprehensive exhibits on Ina Town's nature, geography, history, and folklore, and its cultural properties.

  • Kogaido Shell Mound

    Inamachi, Saitama · 048-721-2111

    Early Jomon shell mound about 7,000 years old, when the sea reached inland, mainly brackish Japanese corbicula, behind Komuro Kannon.
