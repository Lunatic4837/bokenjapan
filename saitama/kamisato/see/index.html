    Kamisato, Saitama · 0495-33-8255

  • Kamisato Town Local History Museum

    Kamisato, Saitama · 0495-34-0455

    Shows Japan's only castanet-playing haniwa, a dancing female haniwa, pottery, stone tools, and Japan's first female seaplane pilot.

  • Ambassador Lotus at Fukusho-ji

    Kamisato, Saitama · 0495-33-3465

    June-August, lotuses bloom in two Fukusho-ji ponds. Ancient Chinese and Ohga hybrid gifted by China's ambassador for normalization's 25th anniversary.

  • Kanakubo Castle Ruins

    Kamisato, Saitama · 0495-35-1232

    Built in 1177-80, fell in the 1582 Kanna River battle, abandoned in 1698; some earthworks and moat remains survive.
