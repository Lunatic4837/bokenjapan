Things to do in Kasukabe, Saitama

Kasukabe · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Metropolitan Area Outer Underground Discharge Channel

    Kasukabe, Saitama · 048-747-0281

    Tours explain this regional flood-control role, including the popular underground temple pressure tank and third shaft.

  • Kasukabe Onsen Yura no Sato

    Kasukabe, Saitama · 048-755-4126

    Onsen from 1,500 m underground scored perfect fives on all six review items; salty like seawater and slow to cool.

  • Kasukabe Yumoto Onsen

    Kasukabe, Saitama · 048-733-1126

    Salt-free alkaline simple hot spring rare in Kanto; tea-brown water glows golden in light, smooths skin, spacious facility.

  • Toyoharu-kai Kasukabe Dream Forest Park

    Kasukabe, Saitama · 048-796-3787

    Prefectural park creating greenery with residents in an urban area lacking green space, through seedlings, planting, and tree growing.
