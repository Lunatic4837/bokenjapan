Sights in Kawagoe, Saitama

Kawagoe · Saitama · 6 places

See

  • Toki no Kane

    Kawagoe, Saitama · 049-222-5556

    Kawagoe lord Sakai Tadakatsu built the first; the current 16 m three-story tower was rebuilt after the Meiji 26 fire.

  • Kawagoe Hikawa Shrine

    Kawagoe, Saitama · 049-224-0589

    Said to be a 6th-century branch of Omiya Hikawa Shrine; the area's chief shrine since Ota Dokan built Kawagoe Castle.

  • Kawagoe Daishi Kitain Temple

    Kawagoe, Saitama · 049-222-5556

    Prospered from 1612 under head priest Tenkai, trusted by Ieyasu. Most burned in 1638; Iemitsu transferred rooms from Edo Castle.

  • Kurazukuri Townscape

    Kawagoe, Saitama · 049-222-5556

    Kawagoe still preserves kurazukuri townscape: clever fireproof Edo townhouse architecture with an Edo atmosphere unseen in today's Tokyo.

  • Five Hundred Arhats of Kitain

    Kawagoe, Saitama · 049-222-0859

    Arhats are enlightened high priests. Kitain's 500, one of Japan's three great groups, took 50 years to finish.

  • Former Yamazaki Family Villa

    Kawagoe, Saitama · 049-224-5940

    Retirement home of fifth Kameya owner Yamazaki Kashichi. City cultural property from 2000; buildings donated to the city in 2006.
