Do in Kawaguchi

Kawaguchi · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Kawaguchi Municipal Green Center

    Kawaguchi, Saitama · 048-281-2319

    A flower-rich park ranked Kawaguchi's favorite place in a citizen survey, covering about 158,000 square meters.

  • Kawaguchi City Ukima Golf Course

    Kawaguchi, Saitama · 048-253-1883

    Kawaguchi Ukima Golf Course has a 9-hole Sazanka Course and 3-hole Yuri short course; longest Sazanka hole is 500 yards.

  • Iina Park Kawaguchi (Akayama History and Nature Park)

    Kawaguchi, Saitama · 048-242-6337

    Oasis-themed park using nature and heritage, with a museum of Kawaguchi history, culture and nature, plus a play dome.

  • Gorilla Park

    Kawaguchi, Saitama · 048-256-2510

    Under the Gaikan Expressway, a park with a giant gorilla statue and a full-scale dirt BMX course for holiday races.
