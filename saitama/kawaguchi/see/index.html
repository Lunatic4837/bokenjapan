Sights in Kawaguchi, Saitama

Kawaguchi · Saitama · 4 places

See

  • Kawaguchi Municipal Science Museum (Science World)

    Kawaguchi, Saitama · 048-262-8431

    Hands-on science exhibits, a planetarium, and an observatory for the sun, planets, nebulae, and star clusters.

  • Kawaguchi Municipal Cultural Properties Center Local Museum

    Kawaguchi, Saitama · 048-283-3552

    Displays Kawaguchi history from the Nikko Onari Road and Shiba River, plus about 1,200-year-old wooden tablets.

  • Kizoro Fujizuka

    Kawaguchi, Saitama

    Built in 1800 on the Omiya Plateau, 5.4 meters high, among Saitama's oldest, a national folk cultural property.

  • Akayama Jinya Ruins

    Kawaguchi, Saitama

    Ina Tadaharu built this magistrate jinya in 1629 and directed the Tone eastward and Arakawa westward shifts.
