Sights in Kazo, Saitama

Kazo · Saitama · 5 places

See

  • Three-Prefecture Border

    Kazo, Saitama

    Administrative boundary in rice paddies about 500 meters southeast of Roadside Station Kazo Watarase, spanning Tochigi, Itakura and Kazo.

  • Kazo Future Hall

    Kazo, Saitama · 0480-69-2160

    Learning facility with a unique exterior, planetarium, astronomy room, large video systems, hands-on rooms and a workshop.

  • Great Wisteria of Tamashiki Park

    Kazo, Saitama · 0480-73-1111

    Park in Kisai, Kazo beside Tamashiki Shrine; wisteria over 400 years old, a prefectural natural monument, blooms pale purple.

  • Kisai Castle (Local History Exhibition Room)

    Kazo, Saitama · 0480-62-1223

    Restored with a keep from a single-story residence; displays many excavated artifacts and historical materials.

  • Saitama Prefectural International Center for Environmental Science

    Kazo, Saitama · 0480-73-8331

    Core environmental-science institution combining a learning facility and research institute, with an exhibition hall and events.
