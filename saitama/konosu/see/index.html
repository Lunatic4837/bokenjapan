See in Konosu

Konosu · Saitama · 7 places

See

  • Cosmos Arena Fukiage Area

    Konosu, Saitama · 048-541-1321

    About 10 million cosmos on 8.8 ha by the Arakawa; Chichibu and Fuji views. Peak mid-late October; poppies in May.

  • Flower Oasis

    Konosu, Saitama · 048-541-1321

    Park in Teraya, Konosu flower-production birthplace, with a spring tulip festival among 40,000 tulips and autumn sunflowers.

  • Kakyu no Sato

    Konosu, Saitama · 048-569-3811

    Old-house space beyond a longhouse gate, with blooming roses, an udon restaurant, and fresh local vegetables.

  • Kojinja Shrine

    Konosu, Saitama · 048-542-7293

    Stork-legend guardian shrine of Konosu, merging Raiden, Kumano and Hikawa shrines in 1873; year-end Tori no Ichi.

  • Konosu City Industry and Tourism Hall Hina no Sato

    Konosu, Saitama · 048-540-3333

    Hina dolls, red crafts, a designated Meiji storehouse, mosaic stage, tourist information, and specialty sales.

  • Poppy Happy Square

    Konosu, Saitama · 048-541-1321

    Japan's largest poppy field, about 12.5 ha, planted to prevent illegal dumping. Festival in mid-May: stalls, produce, picking.

  • Shogan-ji Temple

    Konosu, Saitama · 048-541-0227

    Restored by Priest Seigon in the late 16th century, a Jodo Kanto training temple granted the hollyhock crest.
