Things to do in Koshigaya, Saitama

Koshigaya · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Koshigaya Natural Onsen Bijin no Yu Yunohana

    Koshigaya, Saitama · 048-985-4126

    Black Kanto spring along National Route 4: free-flowing baths, jet bath, salt sauna, hot sauna, medicinal and cascade baths.

  • Takamoto Health Park (Koshigaya Park)

    Koshigaya, Saitama · 048-963-7111

    Urban park opened April 1, 2026 from the former prefectural health and welfare village for rest, scenery, and exercise.

  • Alnet Home Smile Park Shirakobato (Shirakobato Water Park)

    Koshigaya, Saitama · 048-977-5151

    Prefectural park opened June 1979, named for local bird Shirakobato, with summer pool, winter trout fishing, bicycle plaza, barbecue.

  • Koshigaya Strawberry Town

    Koshigaya, Saitama · 048-965-1514

    One of Kanto's largest strawberry-picking farms, with 8 greenhouses and about 10 varieties, roughly 64,000 plants.
