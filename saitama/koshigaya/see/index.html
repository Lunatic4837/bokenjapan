Sights in Koshigaya, Saitama

Koshigaya · Saitama · 3 places

See

  • Hanada-en

    Koshigaya, Saitama · 048-962-6999

    A 2.1-hectare Japanese stroll pond garden with wooden bridges, a sukiya-style tea house, and stone lanterns.

  • Koshigaya Rice Paddy Art

    Koshigaya, Saitama · 048-971-9002

    Giant art viewed from 80 meters, with pictures and letters in different-colored rice; started in 2010, design changes yearly.

  • Osawa Katori Shrine

    Koshigaya, Saitama · 048-975-7824

    Guardian shrine of Osawa, said transferred from Sagigo's Katori Shrine. Records list founding in the Oei era, 1394-1428.
