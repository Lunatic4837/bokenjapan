Things to do in Kuki, Saitama

Kuki · Saitama · 3 places

Do

  • Hyaku Kannon Onsen

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-59-4126

    A 100 percent free-flowing natural hot spring offering skin-beautifying effects plus relief from eczema, allergies, and fatigue.

  • Tetsuo-kun Park Kuki (Kuki Shobu Park)

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-23-1366

    Once wet paddies full of creeks, consolidated into a 31.3 ha pond; opened as a park in July 1977.

  • Kuki City General Sports Park

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-21-3611

    About 13.3 hectares with gyms, a civic ground, gateball field, multipurpose plaza, and tennis courts.
