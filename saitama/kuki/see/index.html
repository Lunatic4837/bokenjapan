Sights in Kuki, Saitama

Kuki · Saitama · 7 places

See

  • Cosmos Fureai Road

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-85-1111

    Spring poppies and autumn cosmos cover 10 km of Kasai Canal in Washinomiya, Kuki, also a running course.

  • Washinomiya Shrine

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-58-0434

    Said to be Kanto's oldest grand shrine and main Otori shrine, revered by Kanto warriors and cited in Azuma Kagami.

  • Kurihashi Red-Flower Buckwheat

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-53-1111

    Ornamental red-flower buckwheat from Nepal's Himalayas, grown in Kurihashi. About 0.6 hectares turn ruby red in October.

  • Grave of Shizuka Gozen

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-58-1111

    Grave of Yoshitsune's concubine Shizuka Gozen, who died in Kurihashi. A gravestone was built in 1803.

  • Shirasagi Park

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-85-1111

    April cherry blossoms bloom; June lavender peaks. About 1,000 plants dye the Lavender Mountain slope purple with fresh scent.

  • Shobu Castle Ruins Iris Garden

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-85-1111

    One June venue for the Iris and Lavender Blue Festival. A castle is said to have once stood here.

  • Wisteria at Shobu Shrine

    Kuki, Saitama · 0480-85-1111

    Shobu Shrine has Noda wisteria, a Saitama Prefecture natural monument said to exceed 300 years.
