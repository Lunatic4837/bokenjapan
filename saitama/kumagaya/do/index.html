Do in Kumagaya

Do

  • Beppu-numa Park

    Kumagaya, Saitama · 0493-39-4806

    Northwest Kumagaya park around Beppu-numa, with multipurpose field, jogging course, Japanese and iris gardens, playground, picnic area.

  • Kumagaya Onsen Yura no Sato

    Kumagaya, Saitama · 048-525-4126

    Simple spring common at famous onsen, in Japanese-style rock baths, plus healing space with bedrock bath and massage machines.

  • Kumagaya Sports Culture Park

    Kumagaya, Saitama · 048-526-2004

    2004 National Sports Festival main venue and 2019 Rugby World Cup site, with dome, athletics field, and rugby ground.

  • Arakawa Oaso Park

    Kumagaya, Saitama · 048-645-0570

    Prefectural riverbed park in Kumagaya, a leading wild-bird site with nearly 50 rare species.
