Sights in Kumagaya, Saitama

Kumagaya · Saitama · 4 places

See

  • Menuma Shodenzan

    Kumagaya, Saitama · 048-588-1644

    Known as one of Japan's three great Shoten, said to bless relationships, household safety, fortune, and studies.

  • Katakura Silk Memorial Hall

    Kumagaya, Saitama · 048-522-4316

    Memorial hall using the cocoon warehouse of Katakura's last silk mill in Kumagaya, certified a modernization industrial heritage in 2007.

  • Nogo-ji (Hydrangea Temple)

    Kumagaya, Saitama

    Said founded by Gyoki in 743, later rebuilt by Kukai. About 50 kinds, 800 hydrangeas bloom early to late June.

  • Kumagaya Cherry Blossom Embankment

    Kumagaya, Saitama

    Edo-period cherry spot, a Japan 100 site; about 500 Someiyoshino bloom 2km on the Arakawa, late March to early April.
