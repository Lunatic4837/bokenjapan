Things to do in Nagatoro, Saitama

Nagatoro · Saitama · 2 places

Do

  • Nagatoro Gorge and Boat Ride

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-3311

    Leisurely, sometimes thrilling Arakawa boat ride through Nagatoro's Special Natural Monument rock terraces, from young children to the elderly.

  • Hodosan Ropeway

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-0258

    Ropeway on 497-meter Mount Hodo. Two gondolas link foothill and summit, 832 meters in about five minutes.
