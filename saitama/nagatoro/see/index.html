Sights in Nagatoro, Saitama

Nagatoro · Saitama · 7 places

See

  • Tsuki no Ishi Maple Park

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-3311

    Park named after Takahama Kyoshi's poem monument, famous for autumn leaves; about 50 maples are illuminated in November.

  • Saitama Prefectural Museum of Nature

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-0404

    Shows giant shark Carcharodon megalodon and mysterious Paleoparadoxia fossils found in Saitama, plus Nagatoro nature.

  • Nagatoro Flower Village

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-3111

    Behind the local history museum, about 10,000 square meters. California poppies bloom over the hill in early summer.

  • Hodosan Shrine

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-0042

    Said to be founded by Yamato Takeru over 2000 years ago. Gongen-style halls rebuilt late Edo to early Meiji.

  • Nagatoro Cherry Blossoms (Kita Sakura Street, Walk-through Cherries)

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-0262

    Kita Sakura Street has about 400 cherries along about 2.5 km; Mt. Hodo's foot has over 30 double-flowered cherry varieties.

  • Hodosan Plum Garden

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-3311

    Plum planting began in 1986. About 170 varieties, known as the plum garden with the most varieties in Kanto.

  • Mount Hodo Wintersweet Garden

    Nagatoro, Saitama · 0494-66-3311

    About 2,000 wintersweet trees at the summit of Mount Hodo, Nagatoro's symbol, richly fragrant from mid to late February.
