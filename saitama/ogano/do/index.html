Things to do in Ogano, Saitama

Ogano · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Ryokami Onsen Yakushi-no-Yu Roadside Station

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-79-1533

    Quality onsen for quiet relaxation in rich nature, plus a shop selling seasonal vegetables and handmade crafts.

  • Echigoya Ryokan

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-75-0048

    Family-run small inn offering tailored hospitality for sightseeing, go and shogi camps, sports camps, and business.

  • Suzaki Ryokan

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-75-0024

    Meiji-founded inn with period safe, phone box, free private open-air bath, local cuisine, and Chichibu Ogano culture.

  • Climbing Park Shin'ikan

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-26-7805

    Largest-class Chichibu bouldering facility with kids space, from elementary age up. Ogano's Mt. Futago is a climbing mecca.
