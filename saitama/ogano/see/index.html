Ogano · Saitama · 7 places

See

  • Mount Ryokami Foothills Dahlia Garden

    One of Kanto's largest dahlia gardens at Mount Ryokami's foot, with about 350 varieties and 5,000 plants grown by volunteers.

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-79-1100 · Official / source link

  • Marugami Falls

    Saitama's only falls chosen for Japan's 100 Best Waterfalls. About 1.5 km trail; 76 m drop in three tiers.

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-79-1100 · Official / source link

  • Onouchi Valley

    Gorge from Mount Ryokami. Onouchi Icicles, one of Chichibu's three great ice pillars, peak early January to late February.

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-75-5060 · Official / source link

  • Hannya no Oka Park

    Park in Ogano, a treasure house of precious fossils. The area was seabed about 15 million years ago.

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-75-5060 · Official / source link

  • Setsubunso Garden

    Native habitat of about 5,000 square meters, among Japan's largest. White 2 cm flowers bloom like accumulated snow.

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-75-5060 · Official / source link

  • Ogano Fossil Museum

    Displays Ogano Town and nearby fossils plus Miyazawa Kenji and geopark materials; national monument Yobake is visible.

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-75-4179 · Official / source link

  • Ryokami National Recreation Area Iris Garden

    About 100 Japanese iris varieties bloom amid nature, mountain greenery, and a murmuring stream.

    Ogano, Saitama · 0494-75-5060 · Official / source link
