Day-to-day services in Ogawamachi, Saitama

Ogawamachi · Saitama · 4 places

Day to day

  • Ogawamachi Roadside Station

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-72-1220

    Reopened May 30, 2025. Handmade-washi and organic-village exchange hub with papermaking, fan and lantern experiences, plus a washi display room.

  • Teisho Matsuoka Brewery

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-72-1234

    Eight straight gold awards at national appraisal, most in the prefecture. Founder moved from Niigata to Ogawa for better water.

  • Ogawa Town Tourist Information Center Musubime

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-74-1515

    Ask about directions, meals, souvenirs, and events. Bus-waiting and planning space. Local vegetables, washi, and handmade goods sold.

  • Seiun Sake Brewery

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-72-0055

    Historic Ogawa sake brewery that polishes rice in-house under the makers' eyes. Groups of 10 or more may reserve tours.
