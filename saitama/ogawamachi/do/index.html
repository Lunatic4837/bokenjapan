  • Kannokurayama Hiking Course

    Nature park on the mid-slope of 299-meter Mount Sengen, with a 203-meter roller slide for all ages.

  • Coworking Lobby NESTo

    Renovated 100-year stone storehouse, formerly a silk and tobacco warehouse, with Oya stone and a single-cedar table.

  • Ogawa Town Washi Experience Learning Center

    Reserved year-round handmade washi lessons from material preparation to papermaking, with unusually complete equipment, rare in Japan.

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-72-7262 · Official / source link

  • Kubo Seishi Papermaking Village

    Traditional papermaking workshop using bamboo supports. Watch tree bark boiled and tororo-aoi added as materials become washi. Reservation required.

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-72-0436 · Official / source link
