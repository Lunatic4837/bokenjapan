Sights in Ogawamachi, Saitama

Ogawamachi · Saitama · 6 places

See

  • Former Shimozato Branch School

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-81-3015

    Surviving 1964 schoolhouse feels nostalgic amid matching scenery. Partly renovated Cafe MOZART centers on local organic farmers' vegetables.

  • Tochimoto Waterside Park

    Ogawamachi, Saitama

    Tsuki River flows through central Ogawa. Tochimoto Weir, built over 300 years ago, long linked town life and industry.

  • Yoshida Family Residence

    Ogawamachi, Saitama · 0493-73-0040

    Built in 1721, the prefecture's oldest dated thatched hip-and-gable private house, a national Important Cultural Property, 21.8 by 10.5 meters.

  • Katakuri Colony

    Ogawamachi, Saitama

    Large katakuri colony south of Roadside Station Ogawamachi, protected by residents and seen at three sites including Saiko-ji.

  • Tanaka Family Row House, National Registered Tangible Cultural Property

    Ogawamachi, Saitama

    Presumed early Meiji row house south of the old Chichibu highway, bending with Minamiura-dori, once a waterway.

  • Hemerocallis Garden

    Ogawamachi, Saitama

    Lily-family perennial Hemerocallis means one-day beauty in Greek. Morning flowers wilt by evening, yet bloom in turn nearly two weeks.
