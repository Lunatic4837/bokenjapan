Sights in Ogose, Saitama

Ogose · Saitama · 9 places

See

  • Godaison Azalea Park

    Ogose, Saitama · 0493-65-1455

    Trees said planted over 350 years ago bloom beside five Myoo statues. About 10,000 azaleas nearby form a leading Kanto spot.

  • Kuroyama Three Falls

    Ogose, Saitama

    Collective name for Otoko, Onna, and Tengu Falls. Scenic spot chosen for Japan's top 100 sightseeing selections.

  • Ogose Plum Grove

    Ogose, Saitama · 049-292-1451

    Considered one of Kanto's three great plum groves, about 2 hectares, with trees over about 670 years old.

  • Yamabuki no Sato Historical Park

    Ogose, Saitama · 049-292-1451

    Ota Dokan-related site famous for a waka poem. About 2,500 golden kerria match a waterwheel hut in the spring breeze.

  • Hydrangea Road

    Ogose, Saitama · 049-292-1451

    About 1,500 hydrangeas bloom along roughly 3 km, where clean air and birdsong can be enjoyed in nature.

  • Sakuranoyama Park

    Ogose, Saitama · 049-292-1451

    About 300 Someiyoshino cherries bloom in early April. Summit overlooks Saitama Shintoshin. Fireworks are held the second Saturday in May.

  • Kamiya Great Camphor Tree

    Ogose, Saitama

    1988 survey: nationally 16th, prefecture 1st. Girth 15m, height 30m, said over 1000 years. Prefectural natural monument.

  • Shibusawa Heikuro Suicide Site

    Ogose, Saitama · 049-292-1451

    Defeated at Hanno, Shibusawa Heikuro fled over Kaoburi Pass, fought Geishu scouts, and sat wounded by the river.

  • Mitsuba Azalea

    Ogose, Saitama · 049-292-1451

    A person cleared forest, cultivated land, and planted Mitsuba azaleas over about ten years. About 2,500 bloom in early April.
