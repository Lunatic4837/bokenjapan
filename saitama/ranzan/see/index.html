See in Ranzan

Ranzan · Saitama · 8 places

See

  • Ranzan Gorge

    Representative Saitama scenic spot with rock terraces, the Tsukigawa's clear stream, and rich wooded surroundings.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-81-4511 · Official / source link

  • Kijin Shrine

    Said to begin in 1182 when Hatakeyama Shigetada dedicated a demon statue; a rare oni-enshrining shrine.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-62-2131 · Official / source link

  • Sennen no Sono Lavender Garden

    About 20,000 lavenders cover about 4.7 ha, Tokyo Dome-sized, including fragrant Grosso, Furano Blue, and Hidcote.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-81-4511 · Official / source link

  • Sugaya Yakata Ruins

    National historic site overlooking the Tokigawa, traditionally Hatakeyama Shigetada's early Kamakura residence. Ranzan Historic Site Museum is inside.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-62-2150 · Official / source link

  • Kyogaku Training Institute and Yasuoka Masahiro Memorial Hall

    Established in 1970 on the site of Japan Noshi School, founded by Yasuoka Masahiro in the early Showa period.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-62-3375 · Official / source link

  • Saitama Prefectural Ranzan Historic Site Museum

    On the site of Sugaya Residence, later Sugaya Castle; opened in 1976 as a prefectural history museum.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-62-5896 · Official / source link

  • Fuefuki Pass Cherry Blossom Avenue

    Ranzan cherry-blossom spot on the Hatoyama-Ranzan border, in full bloom late March to early April, a Nanboku-cho battle site.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-62-2150 · Official / source link

  • Tokigawa Cherry Blossom Embankment

    About 250 Someiyoshino cherries line 2 km of Tokigawa's right bank, peaking from late March to early April.

    Ranzan, Saitama · 0493-81-4511 · Official / source link
