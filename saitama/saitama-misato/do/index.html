Things to do in Misato (Kodama), Saitama

Misato (Kodama) · Saitama · 3 places

Do

  • Iseki-no-Mori Comprehensive Park

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · 0495-76-3431

    Health and culture park in Kibe and Amakasu with hall, multipurpose ground, tennis courts, and community center.

  • Misato Town Blueberry Tourist Farm

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · 0495-76-5133

    One of Japan's top blueberry areas; picking in Misato from early June to late August, with early and late varieties.

  • Inomata 108 Lanterns

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama

    Bon festival event every August 15 for over 400 years, designated a national important intangible folk cultural property.
