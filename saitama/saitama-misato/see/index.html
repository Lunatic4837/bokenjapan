  • Inomata 108 Lantern Mounds

    108 mounds on the Domaeyama ridge at the Misato-Yorii border; clear days reveal Mt. Akagi and Mt. Asama.

  • Mizudono Tile Kiln Ruins (National Cultural Property)

    Kamakura-period kiln site in Numakami with four confirmed tile kilns. Tiles resemble those from Yofuku-ji, built by Yoritomo in 1189.

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · Official / source link

  • Taishi Lotus

    Lotus at Nagaoka Pond, given by China in 1997 as a Japan-China friendship symbol, in bloom mid-July to mid-August.

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · Official / source link

  • Manyo Site Attributed to Otomobe no Matarime

    A 90-meter-square Hiroki site ringed by moat-shaped fields, said to be chieftain Hinokuma no Toneri Iwasaki's mansion site.

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · Official / source link

  • Rensho-do Weeping Cherry

    Small hall said to hold remains of Kumagai Naozane, called Japan's strongest by Yoritomo, beside a grand weeping cherry.

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · Official / source link

  • Nagasaka Shotenzuka Kofun

    Early 5th-century round kofun, 4.5 m high, 50 m in diameter, in Seki's Suwayama group; prefecture-designated.

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · Official / source link

  • Grave of Inomata Koheiji (Prefectural Cultural Property)

    Inomata Koheiji Noritsuna, late Heian-Kamakura Musashi warrior of the Inomata clan, served the Minamoto in the Hogen and Heiji rebellions.

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · Official / source link

  • Sarashi Well (Prefectural Cultural Property)

    Rock-enclosed well in Hiroki whose spring water once bleached cloth often presented to the imperial court.

    Misato (Kodama), Saitama · Official / source link

  • Shinto-ji

  • Jujo Jori Site
