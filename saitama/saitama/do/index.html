Things to do in Saitama, Saitama

Saitama · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Iwatsuki Castle Ruins Park

    Saitama · 048-757-9122

    Park on the former castle site with paths, lawns, plazas, tennis courts, baseball field, playground, and fountain.

  • Gas One Green Park Akigase

    Saitama · 048-865-7966

    Arakawa riverbed park between Akigase and Hanekura bridges, with sports grounds, barbecue areas and woods.

  • Omiya Park

    Saitama · 048-641-6391

    Opened beside Hikawa Shrine in 1885, the longest-running prefectural park, known for cherries and sports.

  • Saitama City Iwatsuki Nigiwai Exchange Center

    Saitama · 048-757-2981

    Annex to Iwatsuki Doll Museum offering history learning, craft experiences, events, cafe food, and local produce.
