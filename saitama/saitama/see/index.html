Sights in Saitama, Saitama

Saitama · Saitama · 6 places

See

  • The Railway Museum

    Saitama · 048-651-0088

    Opened October 14, 2007 in Omiya, Saitama City as JR East's 20th anniversary main project. South wing opened July 2018.

  • Omiya Hana-no-Oka Agriculture and Forestry Park

    Saitama · 048-622-5781

    Four-season flowers: cherry blossoms, tulips, roses, salvia, begonias, cosmos, spider lilies, violas, wintersweet, and plums.

  • Musashi Ichinomiya Hikawa Shrine

    Saitama · 048-641-0137

    Head shrine of over 280 Hikawa shrines in Saitama, Tokyo, and Kanagawa, said to be over 2,000 years old.

  • Saitama City Omiya Bonsai Art Museum

    Saitama · 048-780-2091

    Garden renovation prepared exhibition space to showcase bonsai for Omiya Bonsai Village's 100th anniversary.

  • Saitama City Iwatsuki Doll Museum

    Saitama · 048-749-0222

    Opened in 2020 in Iwatsuki Ward to explore the history of people and dolls and pass on doll culture.

  • Saitama Prefectural Museum of Modern Art

    Saitama · 048-824-0111

    Opened in 1982 in lush Kita-Urawa Park. Exhibits works from Monet, Chagall, Picasso, and contemporary Japanese artists.
