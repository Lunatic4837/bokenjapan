See

  • Seitenkyu of Five Thousand Rising Dragons

    Sakado, Saitama · 049-281-1161

    One of Japan's largest Taiwanese shrines, with 5,000 dragon-motif decorations and carvings. Worship and fortune slips offered.

  • Shimada Bridge

    Sakado, Saitama

    Atmospheric wooden submersible bridge blending into beautiful natural scenery, sometimes used for drama filming.

  • Josai University Mizuta Art Museum

    Sakado, Saitama

    Shows ukiyo-e collected by founder Mikio Mizuta and special exhibitions. Over 200 works trace ukiyo-e origins to modern Japanese painting.

  • Whooper Swan Arrival

    Sakado, Saitama

    Whooper swans arrive November to March near the Oppe and Iimori confluence, where their elegant forms can be seen.

  • Angyo Kanzakura at Kitaasaba Cherry Embankment Park

    Sakado, Saitama

    About 200 Angyo winter cherries bloom pale pink along 1.2 km of the Oppe River from early to mid-March.

  • Sakado City History and Folklore Museum

    Sakado, Saitama

    Displays archaeological finds from city sites and folklore materials collected elsewhere, plus folk-tool special exhibitions.
