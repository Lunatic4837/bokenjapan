Do in Tokigawa

Tokigawa · Saitama · 8 places

Do

  • Showa Retro Tamagawa Onsen

    Large indoor and wood-scented outdoor baths; men's and women's baths switch weekly. Natural onsen in Tokigawa, nearest satoyama to Tokyo.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 0493-65-4977 · Official / source link

  • Dodaira Observatory Stars and Greenery Creation Center

    Renewed former National Dodaira Observatory lodging with log houses, Mongolian tents, stargazing, and Kanto night views.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 080-2373-8682 · Official / source link

  • Satoyama Village Shikisai-kan

    Relocated old-house day-trip onsen by the Tokigawa with a veranda foot bath and empty-handed barbecue.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 0493-65-5515 · Official / source link

  • River Square Barbecue Site

    Opened in 2013 as Kanto's first privately operated barbecue site on river land beside the Tokigawa.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 0493-65-1171 · Official / source link

  • Kinomura Campground

    Tree-lined campsite along the upper Tokigawa gorge, with cookhouse, showers, and toilets. Cherry salmon and char swim in shallows.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 0493-67-0850 · Official / source link

  • Kunugimura Experience Exchange Hall

    400m mountainside former elementary school experience site for wooden chairs, bamboo dragonflies, and himokawa udon.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 0493-67-1571 · Official / source link

  • Yasuragi House

    Relocated farmhouse over 100 years old for rural exchange. Second-floor gallery and local udon-making; meal-only visits welcome.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 0493-67-0800 · Official / source link

  • Ikoi-no-Sato Otsuki

    Make soba from mountain buckwheat flour inside a fragrant Tokigawa-timber hall. Sells Fukumikan and other produce and crafts.

    Tokigawa, Saitama · 0493-65-0322 · Official / source link
