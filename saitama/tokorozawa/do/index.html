Do

  • Tokorozawa Aviation Memorial Park

    Tokorozawa, Saitama · 04-2998-4388

  • Seibuen Amusement Park

    Tokorozawa, Saitama · 04-2929-5354

    Centered on 1960s-style Sunset Hill Shopping Street, with attractions, shows, resident encounters, and nostalgic food experiences.

  • Belluna Dome

    Tokorozawa, Saitama · 04-2925-1141

    Home stadium of the Saitama Seibu Lions, also used for prefectural events and concerts.

  • Tokorozawa Onsen Yura no Sato

    Tokorozawa, Saitama · 04-2943-0126

    Spring from 1,700m underground, rich in sodium and calcium, said to benefit health and beauty; also carbonated and bedrock baths.

  • Tokorozawa Civic Cultural Center Muse

    Tokorozawa, Saitama · 04-2998-6500

    A cultural complex with one of Japan's largest Austrian pipe organs and three halls for concerts and theater.

  • Arai-en Main Store

    Tokorozawa, Saitama · 04-2948-1892

    Tea factory tours and tea-picking in the new-tea season. Direct shop sells products cheaper than regular stores.
