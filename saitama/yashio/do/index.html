Things to do in Yashio, Saitama

Yashio · Saitama · 6 places

Do

  • Yashio Station Front Park

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-996-2111

    Urban park at TX Yashio Station south exit with lawn plaza and play equipment, a family rest place.

  • Yashio Citizens Festival

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-996-2111

    Parade, kids sumo tournament, product stalls and character shows are held grandly for a wide age range.

  • Hachijo Waterside Park

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-996-2111

    Spring cherries span the park like a flower tunnel, busy with viewers yearly. A promenade with tennis courts.

  • Oze Lion Dance

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-997-6666

    Offered at Sengen and Hikawa festivals July's first Saturday and next day. Called Doronko Lion as rain muddies the feet.

  • Nakagawa Yashio Waterside School

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-996-2111

    MLIT-developed for local waterside learning and nature activities; a 540m loop rings a Nakagawa-fed channel.

  • Nichome Lion Dance

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-997-6666

    Started around 1655; old-style festival rites continue. Offered yearly at Hikawa Shrine on July's third Sunday.
