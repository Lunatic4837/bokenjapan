Sights in Yashio, Saitama

Yashio · Saitama · 3 places

See

  • Nakagawa Yashio Flower Park

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-951-0323

    Pink flowering peaches and yellow rapeseed from March to April, spider lilies and cosmos in autumn, on the Nakagawa riverbed.

  • Ota Family Residence

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-997-6666

    A 1999 city-designated tangible cultural property: rare wooden two-story tiled machiya about 150 years old, once a rice wholesaler.

  • Yashio City Museum

    Yashio, Saitama · 048-997-6666

    Permanent exhibits introduce Yashio City history themed on water and life, plus 2012 Hachijo site artifacts.
