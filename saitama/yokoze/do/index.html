Things to do in Yokoze, Saitama

Yokoze · Saitama · 13 places

Do

  • Chichibu Yumoto Buko Onsen

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-5151

    Day-trip alkaline beautiful-skin onsen with an open-air cypress bath. Effects on neuralgia, muscle pain, joint pain, frozen shoulder, and chilliness.

  • Komatsuzawa Leisure Farm

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-24-0412

    Year-round seasonal farm experiences; mountain views and meals can all be enjoyed in one day. Strawberries and grapes.

  • Saitama Prefectural Citizens' Forest

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-23-8340

    Forest recreation area north of Mount Maruyama (960 m) with seasonal forests, wild plants, flowers, trees, and animals.

  • Buko Campground

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-5151

    Advance reservation required. Riverside campground viewing Mount Buko, with quiet forest sites and riverbank sites.

  • Chichibu and Yokoze Town Grape Picking

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-0450

    Grape picking with 30-minute all-you-can-eat, harvesting, and direct sales.

  • Roadside Station Fruit Park Ashigakubo Barbecue Area

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-21-0299

    Reservation-only rental BBQ using your own gear and food on the Yokoze River bank by Ashigakubo Station.

  • Okiune Farm

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-23-2712

    Log house marks this farm at Mount Buko's foot. Seasonal all-you-can-eat strawberry picking and additive-free handmade gelato.

  • Ashigakubo Valley International Fishing Ground

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-24-3812

    Fishing spot using a natural river amid rich Chichibu nature. Ashigakubo is known as Yokoze's best summer retreat.

  • Kotohira Farm

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-24-1497

    Fruit picking on a south slope with good views, including Mount Buko in front.

  • Yokoze Family Farm Village

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-0450

    About six farms, including Komatsuzawa Leisure Farm, cluster near Yokoze Station. Seasonal strawberry, grape, sweet potato, and shiitake picking.

  • Chichibu Ashigakubo Cycle Station

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-0450

    Ashigakubo rental cycle station offers same-day and advance reservations; call 0494-25-0450.

  • Chichibu Yokoze Cycle Station

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-24-0898

    Cycle rentals near Yokoze Station, convenient for sightseeing. Ages 13 and up; photo ID required.

  • Kaminohara Farm

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-23-8975

    Picking available; take-home only also possible. May end early depending on berries. All-you-can-eat: Benihoppe, Tochiotome, Yayoihime.
