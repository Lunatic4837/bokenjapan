See in Yokoze

Yokoze · Saitama · 12 places

See

  • Terasaka Rice Terraces

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-0450

    Saitama's largest-class rice terraces with seasonal rural views. About 250 plots restored. Summer event lights about 600 bonfires.

  • Ashigakubo Icicles

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-0450

    One of Chichibu's three great icicles, a winter-only sight about 30 meters high and 200 meters wide.

  • Akechi-ji

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-24-3125

    Temple for safe childbirth, child-rearing, and evil-warding. Nyoirin Kannon is called a Kannon of fortune and wisdom.

  • Daiji-ji Temple

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-23-4124

    Obinzuru-sama is said to be good to stroke on ailing spots. Rare Buko bean cherries bloom in spring.

  • Yokoze Town History and Folklore Museum

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-24-9650

    Exhibits Yokoze heritage, puppet-theater stage model, Mount Buko Ontake Shrine palace model, specimens, and historical materials.

  • Hocho-ji

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-22-1921

    Seventh site, called Ushibuse-do, has an Eleven-faced Kannon attributed to Gyoki. Moved here after the 1782 disaster.

  • Bokuun-ji

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-24-6236

    Also called Ogino-do. Principal Kannon was once on Mt. Buko; rare offerings include a Buddha image and a mountain-witch tooth.

  • Gokado Hall

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-23-4701

    Ogawayama hall with purple hanging wisteria in season, named after its builder mastered poetry secrets overnight with a monk.

  • Kisshozan Daichu-in

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-23-2323

    Said to be founded by Zenan Ryochi. Town-designated bronze Shogun Jizo; spring cherries surround the Nio statues.

  • Saizen-ji Temple

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-23-3413

    The Komine maple, said to be about 600 years old, is an elegant Saitama natural monument changing with the seasons.

  • Hanasakiyama Park

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-25-0450

    Low mountain of about 3.9 hectares in Yokoze, being developed with volunteers into a flower-filled gathering place.

  • Torin-ji Une Ebisu

    Yokoze, Saitama · 0494-22-4394

    One of the Chichibu Seven Lucky Gods. Ebisu is enshrined for harvests, big catches, and business prosperity.
