Things to do in Yorii, Saitama

Yorii · Saitama · 4 places

Do

  • Tamayodo Lake

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-2121

    Artificial lake of Tamayodo Dam on the Arakawa; clear, quiet, forest-ringed, for canoeing, rafting, and strolls.

  • Tsuburata Lake

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-8511

    Artificial lake built in 1955 as an irrigation reservoir; year-round crucian fishing, winter smelt fishing, and nearby hiking.

  • Yorii Hojo Festival

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-2121

    Festival recreating the Sengoku age with armored processions and cannon battles under cherry blossoms, held mid-May.

  • Kawasemi Kawara

    Yorii, Saitama · 080-2634-1112

    Year-round outdoor spot for camping, barbecue, and canoeing, busy with families near a river museum.
