Sights in Yorii, Saitama

Yorii · Saitama · 9 places

See

  • Hachigata Castle Ruins

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-586-0315

    A representative Sengoku castle, one of Japan's 100 Famous Castles. Nagao Kageharu is said to have built it in 1476.

  • Nakamaidaira Green Park

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-2121

    Five-hectare park: May mountain azaleas, autumn leaves, 180-degree views of Yorii, Tsukuba, Nantai, and Tokyo Skytree.

  • Saitama Prefectural Museum of Rivers

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-7333

    Hands-on museum on a spacious Arakawa right-bank site where you enjoy and learn about rivers and water.

  • Jakugu Park

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-2121

    Former villa site of performer Matsumoto Koshiro VII, over 10,000 square meters, maples redden in autumn.

  • Hachigata Castle Park

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-586-0315

    Stone earthworks, gates, and ponds restored to show Sengoku castle techniques; Edohigan cherries nicknamed Ujikuni Cherry bloom in spring.

  • Kanao Azalea Mountain Park

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-3012

    1959 imperial tree-planting festival park; about 5,000 azaleas bloom mid-April to early May; hiking and observatory views.

  • Meoto Falls

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-2121

    Two falls about 3m high drop side by side before Fuppu River meets Tamayodo Lake, scenic in all seasons.

  • Hachigata Castle History Museum

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-586-0315

    Museum to learn and experience national historic site Hachigata Castle, with video on its history and structure.

  • Hachigata Castle Edohigan Cherry

    Yorii, Saitama · 048-581-2121

    Town natural monument in Hachigata Castle Park, estimated over 150 years old, blooming fully around late March with illumination.
