Things to do in Yoshikawa, Saitama

Yoshikawa · Saitama · 5 places

Do

  • Minami Central Park

    Yoshikawa, Saitama

    Widest park in Yoshikawa, a five-minute walk from Yoshikawa-Minami Station, with a viewing hill and fitness equipment.

  • Aqua Ignis Musashino Onsen

    Yoshikawa, Saitama · 048-973-7900

    Opened June 2021 at Aeon Town Yoshikawa-Minami; natural 1500-meter spring water, indoor baths, and an open-air bath.

  • Azcom Momotaro Bin Yoshikawa Park

    Yoshikawa, Saitama · 048-955-2067

    Prefectural park on the Edo River right bank at the Chiba border, for family recreation, outdoor sports, and events.

  • Hanayoi Glass Studio

    Yoshikawa, Saitama · 048-915-7951

    Tamami Sudo's studio offers glassblowing and sandblasting by reservation and teaches beginners carefully.

  • Aqua Park

    Yoshikawa, Saitama

    Unusual park in the prefecture with skateboard section, basketball court, outdoor stage and a flood-reducing retention basin.
