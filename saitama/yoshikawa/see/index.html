Sights in Yoshikawa, Saitama

Yoshikawa · Saitama · 4 places

See

  • Nagata Park

    Yoshikawa, Saitama

    About 16-meter Yoshikawa Fuji, also called Kiyomino Fuji, overlooks the city; clear days show Skytree, Mount Fuji and Mount Tsukuba.

  • Golden Catfish

    Yoshikawa, Saitama

    Golden catfish monument, Yoshikawa's symbol, in the Musashino Line Yoshikawa Station south exit rotary, welcoming catfish-town visitors.

  • Yoshikawa Shrine

    Yoshikawa, Saitama · 048-982-2034

    Reestablished in 1187 by the Yoshikawa clan as Suwa Shrine; subsidiary shrines and a tabu tree in front.

  • Kibo Cherry Tree at Seki Park

    Yoshikawa, Saitama

    Planted April 6, 2019 from Jindai-zakura seeds that traveled to space with astronaut Koichi Wakata in 2008.
