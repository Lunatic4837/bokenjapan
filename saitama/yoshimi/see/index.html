Sights in Yoshimi, Saitama

Yoshimi · Saitama · 5 places

See

  • Yoshimi Hundred Caves

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-54-4541

    Late to final Kofun-period cave tombs, designated a National Historic Site in 1923, with 219 caves confirmed.

  • Hatcho Lake

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-63-5018

    An artificial irrigation pond in Yoshimi built for rice farming, covering about 52,000 square meters.

  • Ponpon Mountain

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-54-5027

    A rocky hill with fine views behind Takaohikone Shrine; stomping midway up makes a ponpon sound.

  • Anraku-ji (Yoshimi Kannon)

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-54-2898

    Eleventh Bando pilgrimage temple, long called Yoshimi Kannon. About 1200 years ago Gyoki placed a carved Kannon in a cave.

  • Matsuyama Castle Ruins

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-54-9111

    One of northern Musashi's finest hill-and-plain castles, at the tip of the Hiki Hills; a prefectural historic site since 1925.
