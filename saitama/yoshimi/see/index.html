    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-63-5018

  • Ponpon Mountain

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-54-5027

  • Anraku-ji (Yoshimi Kannon)

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-54-2898

    Eleventh Bando pilgrimage temple, long called Yoshimi Kannon. About 1200 years ago Gyoki placed a carved Kannon in a cave.

  • Matsuyama Castle Ruins

    Yoshimi, Saitama · 0493-54-9111
