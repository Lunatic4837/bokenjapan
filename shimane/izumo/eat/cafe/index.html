  • Fujihiro Coffee

    ふじひろ珈琲

    Dining in Izumo

    Coffee shop in Izumo, about six minutes by car from JR Izumoshi Station. Open 8:00-19:00, closed Sundays, with parking for 35 cars and coffee from around 600 yen.

  • Soba Cafe Isatoya

    SOBA-CAFE-いさと家

    Dining in Izumo

    Old-house cafe in Hishine, Taisha-cho, Izumo, specialising in coarsely ground juwari (100% buckwheat) soba made from naturally grown, snow-room-aged buckwheat with no binder. Also gluten-free buckwheat sweets, homemade drinks and natural shaved ice; reservations accepted.

    Agar-jelly workshop and gift shop in Taisha-cho, Izumo, making jellies from Shimane figs, blueberries, ginger and matcha, plus dice-shaped five-colour agar and a red-and-white lucky-knot jelly. Samples on offer; open 11:00-16:00, closed Wednesdays.
