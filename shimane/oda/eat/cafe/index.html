Cafe

  • La Pigna Cafe & Sweets

    La Pigna Cafe & Sweets

    Dining in Oda

    Homemade-sweets cafe on National Route 9 in Nima, Oda, close to the Nima Sand Museum. Signature En-musubi chiffon cake, baked pudding, tiramisu and french toast; open 11:00-18:00, closed Thursdays and Fridays, and children are welcome.

  • doma cafe gallery

    doma cafe gallery

    Dining in Oda

    Old-house cafe at the foot of Mount Sanbe, with weekly-changing lunches and homemade bagel plates. A gallery upstairs shows work by local and visiting artists. Open Saturdays and Sundays 12:00-18:00, closed in winter.

  • Kissa Yamabuki

    喫茶やまぶき

    Dining in Oda

    Log-house-style coffee shop in Omori, near the Iwami Ginzan silver-mine town, serving freshly ground coffee with homemade sweet-potato doughnuts and sweet potatoes. Open 10:00-17:00, closed Tuesdays to Thursdays.

  • Riraku &amp; Kafe Luck Luck

    リラク＆カフェ Luck Luck

    Dining in Oda

    Cafe in a renovated 140-year-old farmhouse in central Oda, with exposed beams and soft daylight. Thai dishes, sweets and Japanese food, plus Thai traditional massage; open 10:00 (last order 19:30), closed Tuesdays, five parking spaces.

More places in Oda

  • Kotogahama no Chinakamame

    琴ヶ浜の『チーナカ豆』

    Dining in Oda

    Beach cafe beside Kotogahama, one of Japan's singing-sand beaches, in a self-renovated old beach hut built with reused school timber. Wood-fired pizza, house-cured bacon and ham sandwiches and spice curry, nearly all available to take away.

  • Cafe Asbach

    Cafe Asbach

    Dining in Oda

    Coffee shop on the Ota Sakurae Line in the Kuri area of Oda, listed in OpenStreetMap. No website is published; check locally for opening hours.

  • Kissa Keishoku Chatei

    喫茶・軽食 チャティ

    Dining in Oda

    Coffee shop and light-meal spot on National Route 9 in the Nagahisa area of Oda. OpenStreetMap lists curry, pasta, sandwiches, juice and breakfast; check locally for opening hours.
