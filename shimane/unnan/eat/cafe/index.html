  • Multicultural Exchange Cafe Soban

    多文化交流カフェSoban

    Dining in Unnan

    Tatami-room cafe in Kisuki, Unnan, run by a Seoul-born owner. Korean shaved-ice sweets at weekends and holidays, Korean set meals on the last Friday evening of the month (booking only), and homemade Korean seasonings. Opening days vary monthly.

  • Shitaueya Old House Cafe

    山ん中の古民家Cafe 下上屋

    Dining in Unnan

    Old-house cafe in the grounds of the Hosoda pickle maker in Daito, Unnan, with fine views, about 2 km from the Sanno-ji terraced rice fields. Lunch sets include dessert and a drink; open 11:00-16:00 from March to November, closed Mondays, Tuesdays and Sundays.
