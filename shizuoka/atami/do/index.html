Things to do in Atami, Shizuoka

Atami, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • 熱海芸妓見番歌舞練場

    Atami Geigi Kenban Kaburenjo

    #1 ranked in Atami

    The usually exclusive Atami geisha kenban opens for Yumemachi Odori Hana no Mai, featuring kimono dancers and shamisen players.

  • PICA初島

    PICA Hatsushima

    #2 ranked in Atami

    PICA Hatsushima is a resort on the remote island closest to greater Tokyo, 30 minutes by high-speed boat from Atami Port.

  • オーシャンスパ Fuua

    Ocean Spa Fuua

    #14 ranked in Atami

    A day-use hot spring at Atami Bay Resort Korakuen facing Sagami Bay, with an outdoor standing bath about 25 meters long.
