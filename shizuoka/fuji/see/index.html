Sights in Fuji, Shizuoka

Fuji, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 8 places

See

  • Hiromi Park

    広見公園

    #1 ranked in Fuji

    Fuji City's foremost relaxation park, with a vast lawn, about 300 cherry trees, and a rose garden of about 2,200 bushes.

  • Chuo Park

    中央公園

    #2 ranked in Fuji

    An urban park in central Fuji City, split by the Urui River, with lawns, roses, and Mount Fuji views.

  • Karigane Embankment

    かりがね堤

    #3 ranked in Fuji

    A historic Edo-period embankment built to control Fuji River floods, about 4.7 kilometers long and now a flower-lined walk.

  • Bishamonten Myoho Temple

    毘沙門天　妙法寺

    #4 ranked in Fuji

    A temple said to have been founded in 1627, known for a three-day lunar New Year festival counted among Japan's three great daruma markets.

  • Obuchi Sasaba

    大淵笹場

    #7 ranked in Fuji

    Green tea fields in Obuchi with an unobstructed view of Mt. Fuji, widely known as a Shizuoka photo spot.

  • Fujinokuni Tagonoura Port Park

    ふじのくに田子の浦みなと公園

    #9 ranked in Fuji

    A park offering an unobstructed front view of Mt. Fuji from sea level to the 3,776-meter summit.

  • Iwamotoyama Park

    岩本山公園

    #10 ranked in Fuji

    A nature park on hills about 193 meters high, with plum and cherry blossoms against Mt. Fuji from early spring.

  • Ryuganbuchi

    龍巌淵

    #11 ranked in Fuji

    Cherry trees and the Urui River against Mt. Fuji, with about 250 meters turning pink from late March to early April.
