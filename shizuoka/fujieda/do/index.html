Things to do in Fujieda, Shizuoka

Fujieda, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 2 places

Do

  • 瀬戸谷温泉　ゆらく

    Setoya Onsen Yuraku

    #6 ranked in Fujieda

    A day-trip natural hot spring amid Fujieda's nature, with sodium-bicarbonate-chloride water, a carbonated bath, local dishes, and a produce shop.

  • 明治なるほどファクトリー東海

    Meiji Naruhodo Factory Tokai

    #10 ranked in Fujieda

    A reservation-only tour of Meiji sweets making, with almond-chocolate and fruit-gummy exhibits, tastings, souvenirs, and photo spots.
