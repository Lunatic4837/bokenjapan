Sights in Fujieda, Shizuoka

Fujieda, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 7 places

See

  • Okabe-juku Daihatago Kashiwaya

    岡部宿大旅籠柏屋

    #2 ranked in Fujieda

    An Edo-period inn in Okabe-juku, the 21st post town of the Tokaido, with life-size dolls of travelers.

  • Akunami Shrine

    飽波神社

    #3 ranked in Fujieda

    An Engishiki-listed shrine dating from the Heian period, long revered for warding off evil and safe travel.

  • Hanakura Castle Ruins

    花倉城跡

    #4 ranked in Fujieda

    Castle ruins said to have been built by the Imagawa, Suruga's Sengoku lords, with a main bailey and dry moats near the 296-meter summit.

  • Taki no Tani Fudo Kyo

    Takinotani Fudokyo

    #5 ranked in Fujieda

    Sightseeing spot in Fujieda, Shizuoka.

  • Rengeji-ike Park

    蓮華寺池公園

    #7 ranked in Fujieda

    A Fujieda park of flowers, water, birds, and smiles, with 1,300 cherry trees, a wisteria trellis about 500 meters long, and a Japanese garden.

  • Umine Falls

    宇嶺の滝

    #8 ranked in Fujieda

    A famed Tokai waterfall dropping 70 meters from a cliff at Mount Takane's foot; only the viewpoint trail is open after a landslide.

  • Konpirayama Green Space

    金比羅山緑地

    #9 ranked in Fujieda

    A scenic green space overlooking the Seto River, with Mount Fuji and Southern Alps views, plum blossoms, and a cherry-blossom festival.
