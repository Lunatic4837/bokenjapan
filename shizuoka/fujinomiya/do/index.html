Things to do in Fujinomiya, Shizuoka

Fujinomiya, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 5 places

Do

  • あさぎりフードパーク

    Asagiri Food Park

    #3 ranked in Fujinomiya

    An experiential food park on Asagiri Highlands offering tours, tastings, and hands-on dairy, sake, and sweets making.

  • 田貫湖ふれあい自然塾

    Lake Tanuki Fureai Nature School

    #8 ranked in Fujinomiya

    Beside Lake Tanuki, this nature school offers daily eco programs, crafts, and a simulated Mount Fuji lava-cave exploration.

  • 富士ミルクランド

    Fuji Milk Land

    #10 ranked in Fujinomiya

    At Asagiri Highlands, this complex offers milking, butter-making, animal encounters, gelato, a cafe, a restaurant, and lodging.

  • 朝霧高原もちや

    Asagiri Highlands Mochiya

    #11 ranked in Fujinomiya

    Facing Mount Fuji, this drive-in has a giant wooden-horse slide, play equipment, and kusa daifuku made unchanged for over 60 years.

  • 富士山麓朝霧高原 まかいの牧場

    Makai no Ranch

    #17 ranked in Fujinomiya

    A hands-on ranch on Asagiri Highlands offering goat walks, milking, horse leading, and other activities.
