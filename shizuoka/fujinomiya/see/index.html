Sights in Fujinomiya, Shizuoka

Fujinomiya, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 11 places

See

  • Hitoana Fuji-ko Historic Site

    人穴富士講遺跡

    #1 ranked in Fujinomiya

    A historic site around the Hitoana lava cave, where Fuji-ko founder Hasegawa Kakugyo practiced, with more than 200 stone monuments.

  • Asagiri Highlands

    朝霧高原

    #4 ranked in Fujinomiya

    Highlands at about 700 to 1,000 meters on Mount Fuji's west foot, in Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park since 1936, with open views and pastures.

  • Kariyado no Geba-zakura and Ide-kan

    狩宿の下馬桜と井出館

    #5 ranked in Fujinomiya

    Ide-kan, a surviving mid-Edo farmhouse, shares its grounds with 800-year-old Geba-zakura, a national special natural monument.

  • Kiseki Museum

    奇石博物館

    #6 ranked in Fujinomiya

    This hands-on museum shows about 2,000 of some 20,000 rare stones and minerals, including ones that bend or display images.

  • Fuji Harness Guide Dog Village

    日本盲導犬総合センター「盲導犬の里　富士ハーネス」

    #9 ranked in Fujinomiya

    Japan's only guide-dog training center open without reservation, offering daily demonstrations at the foot of Mount Fuji.

  • Shizuoka Prefecture Mount Fuji World Heritage Center

    静岡県富士山世界遺産センター

    #12 ranked in Fujinomiya

    An inverted-cone wooden lattice reflected in water forms Mount Fuji; inside, a 193-meter spiral slope simulates the climb.

  • Fujisan Hongu Sengen Taisha

    富士山本宮浅間大社

    #13 ranked in Fujinomiya

    Head shrine of over 1,300 Sengen shrines; Ieyasu donated its vermilion hall in thanks for his Sekigahara victory.

  • Shiraito Falls

    白糸ノ滝（白糸の滝）

    #14 ranked in Fujinomiya

    Hundreds of clear springs of Mount Fuji snowmelt fall from a curved cliff 20 meters high and 150 meters wide.

  • Lake Tanuki

    田貫湖

    #15 ranked in Fujinomiya

    A calm lake 3.3 kilometers around, at 660 meters on Asagiri Highlands, mirrors Mount Fuji when winds stop.

  • Murayama Sengen Shrine

    村山浅間神社

    #16 ranked in Fujinomiya

    A Mount Fuji World Heritage shrine said to have been founded in 806 by Sakanoue no Tamuramaro.

  • Yamamiya Sengen Shrine

    山宮浅間神社

    #18 ranked in Fujinomiya

    The oldest Sengen shrine, predecessor of Fujisan Hongu Sengen Taisha, and part of Mount Fuji's World Heritage.
