Sights in Fukuroi, Shizuoka

Fukuroi, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 5 places

See

  • Kuno Castle Ruins

    久野城址

    #2 ranked in Fukuroi

    Castle ruins in Fukuroi known for repelling Takeda Shingen, with moats, earthworks, and a main-bailey view.

  • Kasuisai Temple

    秋葉総本殿　可睡斎

    #3 ranked in Fukuroi

    A Soto Zen Enshu Sanzan temple over 600 years old, named when Ieyasu allowed his teacher to nap.

  • Kasui Lily Garden

    可睡ゆりの園

    #5 ranked in Fukuroi

    A seasonal garden open from late May to early July, with 150 lily varieties across 30,000 tsubo of hills.

  • Yusanji Temple

    目の霊山　油山寺

    #6 ranked in Fukuroi

    Founded by Gyoki in 701, this Enshu Sanzan temple became an imperial prayer temple after Ruri Falls water healed Empress Koken's eyes.

  • Hattasan Soneiji Temple

    法多山　尊永寺

    #7 ranked in Fukuroi

    Opened by Gyoki in 725 on Emperor Shomu's order, this Enshu Sanzan temple is worshipped for its misfortune-warding Kannon.
