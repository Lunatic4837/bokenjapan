Things to do in Gotenba, Shizuoka

Gotenba, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • Gotenba Otainai Onsen Health Center

    御殿場市御胎内温泉健康センター

    #1 ranked in Gotenba

    A day-trip hot spring at Mt. Fuji's foot, with Fuji views, lava and hinoki outdoor baths, a sauna, and a cafeteria.

  • Kirin Fuji Gotenba Distillery

    キリンディスティラリー株式会社富士御殿場蒸溜所（工場見学可）

    #4 ranked in Gotenba

    A rare distillery that makes both malt and grain whisky from mashing through bottling, offering an immersive tour and tastings.

  • Gotenba Highlands Tokinosumika

    御殿場高原時之栖

    #10 ranked in Gotenba

    A large eastern Fuji foothills resort with varied lodging, hot springs, dining, activities, and the Hikari no Sumika illumination.
