Sights in Gotenba, Shizuoka

Gotenba, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 7 places

See

  • Futagoyama

    双子山

    #2 ranked in Gotenba

    Twin peaks at about 2,000 meters near the Gotenba trail's fifth station, with views of Suruga Bay and Lake Yamanaka.

  • Fuji Bussharito Peace Park

    富士仏舎利塔平和公園

    #3 ranked in Gotenba

    A peace park facing Mt. Fuji, centered on a white stupa enshrining Buddha relics gifted by India's Prime Minister Nehru.

  • Komakado Wind Cave

    駒門風穴

    #5 ranked in Gotenba

    A roughly 400-meter lava cave from a Fuji eruption about 10,000 years ago, a national natural monument since 1922.

  • Chichibunomiya Memorial Park

    秩父宮記念公園

    #6 ranked in Gotenba

    A park created from the Gotenba villa where Prince Chichibu and Princess Setsuko stayed in wartime and after, per her will.

  • Kamibayama Shrine

    神場山神社

    #7 ranked in Gotenba

    This Gotenba shrine to Oyamazumi enshrines Japan's largest scissors, about three meters high and 60 kilograms, believed to cut illness and misfortune.

  • Mountain in Gotenba

    富士山樹空の森

    Romaji: Fujisan Ki Sora no Mori

    #8 ranked in Gotenba

    A nature and Fuji-information park with a theater, a former Self-Defense Forces helicopter, lawns, park golf, and a nearby hot spring.

  • Higashiyama Former Kishi Residence

    東山旧岸邸

    #9 ranked in Gotenba

    Former home of the 56th and 57th prime minister, Nobusuke Kishi, designed by architect Isoya Yoshida in modern sukiya style.
