Things to do in Hamamatsu, Shizuoka

Hamamatsu, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 8 places

Do

  • 浜北森林アスレチック

    Hamakita Forest Athletic

    #24 ranked in Hamamatsu

    This field-athletic facility next to the prefectural forest park in Hamakita has 40 courses, including a human ropeway and suspension bridge.

  • 浜名湖遊覧船

    Lake Hamana Sightseeing Boat

    #46 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Cruises on Lake Hamana, which has Japan's longest brackish-lake shoreline, offer seasonal views from the water.

  • 浜名湖かんざんじ温泉　華咲の湯

    Hanasaki Bath, Lake Hamana Kanzanji Onsen

    #48 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Rock and open-air baths of a heat-retaining strong chloride salt spring, plus meals and relaxation.

  • 浜名湖マリーナ　ファントムマリンコダマ

    Phantom Marine Kodama, Lake Hamana Marina

    #51 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Parasailing about 40 meters up, plus speedboats and charter yachts, all insured for beginners.

  • 浜松市渚園

    Hamamatsu City Nagisaen

    #59 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Vast leisure facility on an island-like site in Lake Hamana, offering camping, sports, and BBQ.

  • はままつフルーツパーク時之栖

    Hamamatsu Fruit Park Tokinosumika

    #61 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Fruit park where you can eat, play, and stay, and pick about 15 kinds of fruit yearly.

  • 浜名湖パルパル

    Hamanako Palpal

    #62 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Hamanako amusement park greeted by Yanase's Palpal Family, with many rides for children up to age three.

  • かんざんじロープウェイ

    Kanzanji Ropeway

    #71 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Japan's only ropeway crossing a lake, a 723-meter ride from Hamanako Palpal to Mount Okusa.
