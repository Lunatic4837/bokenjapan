Sights in Hamamatsu, Shizuoka

Hamamatsu, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 58 places

See

  • Nukumori Forest

    ぬくもりの森

    #1 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A quiet forest near Lake Hamana whose rounded buildings suggest a medieval European village, with select shops, cafes, and restaurants.

  • Iinoya Castle Ruins

    井伊谷城跡

    #2 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Ii family Sengoku castle on a 115-meter hill, now Shiroyama Park overlooking Iinoya and Lake Hamana.

  • Meizen Memorial Museum

    明善記念館

    #3 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Memorial museum on Hamamatsu entrepreneur Kinpara Meizen, showing his Tenryu River flood control and tree-planting work through relics and documents.

  • Seirai In

    西来院

    #4 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Founded in 1428, this temple holds the mausoleum of Tsukiyama-dono, Tokugawa Ieyasu's wife, who was later killed on his orders.

  • Shijimizuka Site

    蜆塚遺跡

    #5 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A late-to-final Jomon village from about 3,000-4,000 years ago, with five reconstructed houses, a preserved shell layer, and relocated old homes.

  • Hikima Castle Ruins (Hamamatsu Motoshirocho Toshogu)

    引間城跡（浜松元城町東照宮）

    #6 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A Sengoku stronghold on the present Hamamatsu Castle site, taken by Ieyasu in 1568 and now home to Motoshirocho Toshogu.

  • Shizuoka University Takayanagi Memorial Future Technology Creation Hall

    静岡大学 高柳記念未来技術創造館

    #7 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Memorial hall exhibiting materials on Kenjiro Takayanagi, who in 1926 first displayed a character on a cathode-ray tube.

  • Hamamatsu Hachimangu Shrine

    浜松八幡宮

    #8 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Ancient shrine linked to Ieyasu's victory prayers, preserving a thousand-year camphor tree designated a city natural monument.

  • Tsubakihime Kannon

    椿姫観音

    #9 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Memorial to Lady Otazu, Hikima Castle's lord who died fighting Tokugawa forces, now marked by camellias and Tsubakihime Kannon.

  • Soichiro Honda Craftsmanship Heritage Hall

    本田宗一郎ものづくり伝承館

    #10 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Memorial hall honoring Tenryu-born Soichiro Honda, exhibiting early products he made and valuable materials.

  • Tenryu-Futamata Station

    天竜二俣駅

    #11 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Headquarters station of the Tenryu Hamanako Railway, preserving a turntable and roundhouse registered as national tangible cultural properties.

  • Takane Castle Ruins

    高根城址

    #12 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Mountain castle ruins at 420 meters, later a Takeda base, with bailey, turret, and gate restored based on excavations.

  • Zudaji Castle Ruins and Matsushita Residence Site

    頭陀寺城跡・松下屋敷跡

    #13 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Old temple and former Matsushita residence where young Hideyoshi reportedly served and Ii Naomasa first entered Ieyasu's service.

  • Hosoe Shrine

    細江神社

    #14 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Shrine said to ward off earthquakes, begun in 1510 after a tsunami, holding a July boat ritual on Lake Hamana.

  • Mikatagahara Old Battlefield Monument

    三方原古戦場碑

    #15 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Monument to the 1572 battle where young Tokugawa Ieyasu suffered his only and greatest defeat to Takeda Shingen.

  • Hamamatsu City Art Museum

    浜松市美術館

    #16 ranked in Hamamatsu

    This art museum centers on Oriental arts and crafts, especially 18th- and 19th-century glass paintings, folk pictures, ukiyo-e, ceramics, and Buddhas.

  • Hachimae Shrine

    蜂前神社

    #17 ranked in Hamamatsu

    An ancient shrine in Hoda near Mikatagahara stands where Takeda forces supposedly ambushed Tokugawa troops and once held Ii Naotora's signed document.

  • Grave of Ii Naochika

    井伊直親の墓

    #18 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Ii Naochika, betrothed of Ii Naotora and father of Ii Naomasa, was murdered at 27 in 1562; Ii Naosuke donated the lantern before the grave.

  • Inohana Lake

    猪鼻湖

    #19 ranked in Hamamatsu

    This quiet branch lake connects to Lake Hamana via the Seto channel, with mandarin slopes and pleasure boats from Seto to Kanzanji Onsen.

  • Tsubute Island

    礫島

    #20 ranked in Hamamatsu

    This small pine-covered island of about 20 ares in Lake Hamana is said to have formed from a stone thrown by the giant Daidaraboshi.

  • Nakamura Family Residence

    中村家住宅

    #21 ranked in Hamamatsu

    In 1574 Ieyasu's concubine Oman bore Yuki Hideyasu in this Minamoto-line warrior residence, where the placenta mound still remains.

  • Okusayama Shoryu Weeping Plum Garden

    大草山昇竜しだれ梅園

    #22 ranked in Hamamatsu

    About 300 weeping plums, including trees over 100 years old, are trained here like dragons rising to heaven, a nationally rare style.

  • Mount Okusa

    大草山

    #23 ranked in Hamamatsu

    This 113-meter scenic spot overlooking Lake Hamana is reached from Kanzanji Onsen by the Kanzanji Ropeway, Japan's only over-lake ropeway.

  • Bentenjima Seaside Park Beach

    弁天島海浜公園海水浴場

    #25 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Near Imagireguchi, where Lake Hamana meets the sea, this shallow beach has clear water, roofed wooden porches, and views of a red torii.

  • Well of Tokugawa Hidetada's Birth

    徳川秀忠公誕生の井戸

    #26 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A well created from the legend that nearby well water was used for Tokugawa Hidetada's first bath at birth.

  • Saigagake Historic Battlefield

    犀ヶ崖古戦場

    #27 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Cliff where Ieyasu's night raid across a cloth bridge in the Battle of Mikatagahara sent many Takeda troops falling.

  • Iinoya Shrine

    井伊谷宮

    #28 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Shrine to Prince Munenaga, revered as a god of learning, beside the Ii family temple Ryotanji.

  • Kiga Checkpoint

    気賀関所

    #29 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Historic facility recreating Edo-period inspections at Kiga Checkpoint on the Hime Kaido, with costume experiences.

  • Hamamatsu City Hime Kaido and Dotaku Folk History Museum

    浜松市姫街道と銅鐸の歴史民俗資料館

    #30 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Museum of Hime Kaido relics and a locally excavated Yayoi bronze bell, with a relocated birth hut outdoors.

  • Kiyotaki Temple

    清瀧寺

    #31 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Temple built by Tokugawa Ieyasu to mourn his eldest son Nobuyasu, named for a clear waterfall on the grounds.

  • Shibukawa Azalea Park

    渋川つつじ公園

    #32 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Park with about 4,000 wild Shibukawa azaleas, a prefectural natural monument, at their best from mid to late May.

  • Hamamatsu City Akino Fuku Art Museum

    浜松市秋野不矩美術館

    #33 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Wooden art museum of Akino Fuku's Japanese paintings of India, entered shoeless to sit on rattan mats.

  • Hamamatsu Flower Park

    はままつフラワーパーク

    #34 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A 300,000-square-meter botanical garden by Lake Hamana displaying 3,000 plant species throughout the year.

  • Hamamatsu City Museum

    浜松市博物館

    #35 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Museum tracing Hamamatsu history, highlighted by the only letter bearing Ii Naotora's signature.

  • Hamamatsu Science Museum Miraira

    浜松科学館　みらいーら

    #36 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A hands-on science park with five zones, over 100 exhibits, and one of the prefecture's largest planetariums.

  • Hirano Art Museum

    公益財団法人　平野美術館

    #37 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A private art museum in central Hamamatsu holding about 2,000 works, from Japanese painting to contemporary art.

  • Hamanako Music Box Museum

    浜名湖オルゴールミュージアム

    #38 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A specialist museum on Ogusayama displaying about 70 antique music boxes and automatic instruments over 100 years old.

  • Inohanako Shrine

    猪鼻湖神社

    #39 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A shrine on Shishi Rock between Lake Hamana and Lake Inohana, reached by a vermilion drum bridge.

  • Daifuku Temple

    大福寺

    #40 ranked in Hamamatsu

    An Okuhamanako temple with an Edo stroll garden, traditional natto, and nationally designated cultural properties.

  • Makaya Temple

    摩訶耶寺

    #41 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Founded in 726, this Okuhamanako temple has a rare Heian-style garden and nationally designated Buddha statues.

  • Sakuma Damu

    佐久間ダム

    #42 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A 150-meter dam on the Tenryu River, among Japan's largest, with an observation deck and a power museum.

  • Futamata Castle Ruins

    二俣城址

    #43 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Castle ruins on a Tenryu River cliff, contested by the Takeda and Tokugawa, now a quiet park with stone walls.

  • Hamanako Experience Learning Facility Wotto

    浜名湖体験学習施設 ウォット

    #44 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A hands-on aquarium where visitors can touch Lake Hamana life, with a 120-ton tank and eel feeding.

  • Shirakura Gorge

    白倉峡

    #47 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A celebrated gorge on a Tenryu River tributary, with emerald streams, cliffs, and waterfalls along a 1 km path.

  • Hamamatsu City Zoo

    浜松市動物園

    #50 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A lakeside zoo keeping about 100 or more species, including Japan's only golden lion tamarins.

  • Yamahai no Beshonrodo

    Yamahai no Beshon Road

    #52 ranked in Hamamatsu

  • Ryuun Tera

    龍雲寺

    #53 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A historic Zen temple on the shore of Lake Sanaru, displaying a 4-by-16-meter Heart Sutra, the world's largest.

  • Shointei Teahouse

    茶室 松韻亭

    #54 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A Hamamatsu Castle Park teahouse designed by Yoshio Taniguchi, serving matcha or sencha with seasonal sweets.

  • Hamamatsu Festival Hall

    浜松まつり会館

    #60 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Exhibition hall where Hamamatsu Festival's full-size giant kites and ornate floats can be seen year-round.

  • Hamanako Garden Park

    浜名湖ガーデンパーク

    #63 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Park on a Lake Hamana peninsula, whose 50-meter tower views the lake and Mount Fuji on clear days.

  • Hamamatsu City Museum of Musical Instruments

    浜松市楽器博物館

    #64 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Japan's only public musical instrument museum, displaying about 1,500 instruments from around the world.

  • Hamamatsu Castle Park

    浜松城公園

    #65 ranked in Hamamatsu

    A green central park in downtown Hamamatsu, famous for about 270 cherry trees and a children's adventure plaza.

  • Hamamatsu Castle

    浜松城

    #66 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Where Tokugawa Ieyasu built his foothold toward unification, later nicknamed the Success Castle.

  • Nakatajima Sand Dunes

    中田島砂丘

    #67 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Called one of Japan's three great sand dunes, stretching about 4 km along Enshu-nada with wind-rippled sand.

  • Bentenjima Seaside Park

    弁天島海浜公園

    #68 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Seaside park on Lake Hamana with a red 18-meter tower, palms, and a calm AA-rated summer beach.

  • Akihasan Hongu Akiha Shrine Kamisha

    秋葉山本宮秋葉神社 上社

    #69 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Considered the head of over 400 Akiha shrines, enshrining the fire god atop 866-meter Mount Akiha.

  • Tenryu Hamanako Railway Depot

    天竜浜名湖鉄道 車両基地（転車台）

    #70 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Nationally registered 1940 railway facilities at Tenryu-Futamata Station, with a turntable still used daily.

  • Hokoji Temple

    方広寺

    #72 ranked in Hamamatsu

    Shizuoka's oldest building, a 600-year-old Rinzai head temple in Okuyama forest with 500 stone arhats.
