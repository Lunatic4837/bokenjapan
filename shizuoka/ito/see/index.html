Sights in Ito, Shizuoka

Ito, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 11 places

See

  • Lake Ippeki

    一碧湖

    #2 ranked in Ito

    A crater lake formed in an eruption about 100,000 years ago, called the Eye of Izu, among the Izu Peninsula's largest.

  • Ito Orange Beach

    伊東オレンジビーチ

    #3 ranked in Ito

    A palm-lined beach within walking distance of Ito Station, popular for summer swimming and year-round strolls.

  • Usami Beach

    宇佐美海水浴場

    #4 ranked in Ito

    A spacious long beach in Ito, about 1,500 meters of shallow shore with gentle waves, suited to families.

  • Izu Teddy Bear Museum

    伊豆テディベア・ミュージアム

    #5 ranked in Ito

    A brick museum in Izu-kogen conceived as a house of teddy bears, featuring the 1904 antique Teddy Girl.

  • New York Lamp Museum & Flower Garden

    ニューヨークランプミュージアム＆フラワーガーデン

    #7 ranked in Ito

    A museum of about 120-year-old Tiffany lamps and stained glass beside Jogasaki Coast cliffs, with year-round flowers.

  • Otonashi Shrine

    音無神社

    #8 ranked in Ito

    A quiet Matsukawa riverside shrine enshrining a safe-birth deity, famous for its November 10 Shiritsumi Festival.

  • Mount Omuro

    大室山

    #9 ranked in Ito

    A 580-meter scoria cone formed about 4,000 years ago, a national natural monument with a crater about 300 meters across.

  • Kawana Beach

    川奈海水浴場

    #10 ranked in Ito

    A pebble beach in Ito with clear, calm water, known as a quieter spot well suited to snorkeling.

  • Jogasaki Coast

    城ヶ崎海岸

    #12 ranked in Ito

    Lava cliffs and deep-blue sea formed by Mount Omuro's eruption about 4,000 years ago, with a 48-meter suspension bridge.

  • Komuroyama Park

    小室山公園

    #13 ranked in Ito

    A park with about 100,000 azaleas, 4,000 camellias, a life-size dinosaur plaza, and a lift to its 321-meter summit.

  • Izu Shaboten Zoo

    伊豆シャボテン動物公園

    #14 ranked in Ito

    At the foot of Mount Omuro, a park with about 1,500 cactus and succulent varieties and about 140 kinds of animals.
