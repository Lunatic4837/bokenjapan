Things to do in Iwata, Shizuoka

Iwata, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • 磐田市竜洋海洋公園

    Ryuyo Marine Park

    #1 ranked in Iwata

    A spacious Iwata park with broad lawns, a long roller slide on Ryuyo Fuji hill, and a nearby campground and bath.

  • つつじ公園

    Tsutsuji Park

    #2 ranked in Iwata

    A green hilltop park behind Mitsuke Tenjin, with spring azaleas, cherry blossoms, wisteria, and a pond-side path.

  • ヤマハ発動機　コミュニケーションプラザ

    Yamaha Motor Communication Plaza

    #5 ranked in Iwata

    A modern museum displaying Yamaha motorcycles, boats, race vehicles, outboard motors, and industrial robots.
