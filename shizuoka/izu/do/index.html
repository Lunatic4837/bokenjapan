Things to do in Izu, Shizuoka

Izu, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • Bicycle Country Cycle Sports Center

    自転車の国サイクルスポーツセンター

    #2 ranked in Izu

    Japan's largest bicycle theme park in Izu, with the widest variety of fun bikes plus coaster and flying rides.

  • Nakaizu Winery Chateau T.S

    中伊豆ワイナリーシャトーT.S

    #4 ranked in Izu

    A 10-hectare Izu vineyard winery offering cellar tours, Fuji-view tastings, and horseback riding.

  • Shuzenji Niji no Sato

    修善寺虹の郷

    #10 ranked in Izu

    A theme park about ten Tokyo Domes in area, with British and Canadian streets, Japanese gardens, and a 15-inch railway.
