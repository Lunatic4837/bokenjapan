Sights in Izu, Shizuoka

Izu, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 8 places

See

  • Shuzenji Temple

    修禅寺

    #1 ranked in Izu

    Said to be founded by Kukai in 807, this temple gave Shuzenji its name and saw Noriyori and Yoriie die.

  • Namesawa Gorge

    滑沢渓谷

    #3 ranked in Izu

    North of Amagi Pass, this forest gorge has a smooth andesite bed, Ryushi Falls, and 450-year-old Taro Cedar.

  • Kyu Amagi Tonneru

    Kyu Amagi Tonneru

    Former Amagi Tunnel

    #5 ranked in Izu

    Tunnel in Izu, Shizuoka.

  • Tsukigase Plum Grove

    月ヶ瀬梅林

    #6 ranked in Izu

    Originally a production plum grove, with about 1,500 white trees blooming on Amagi slopes from mid-February.

  • Toi Gold Mine

    土肥金山

    #7 ranked in Izu

    Historic Edo-period gold mine ranked with Sado, with recreated tunnels, gold panning, and a 2026 garden of about 850 golden bamboos.

  • Terrasse Orange Toi

    テラッセオレンジトイ

    #9 ranked in Izu

    Opened in 2024 as Japan's first tsunami-evacuation complex, with a local shop, cafe, seaside footbath, and seafood restaurant.

  • Joren Falls

    浄蓮の滝

    #11 ranked in Izu

    One of Japan's 100 waterfalls: 25 meters high, 7 meters wide, with volcanic columnar jointing in Amagi forest.

  • Darumayama Highland Rest House

    だるま山高原レストハウス

    #13 ranked in Izu

    A viewpoint of Mount Fuji across Suruga Bay, where a photo shown at the 1939 New York World's Fair was taken.
