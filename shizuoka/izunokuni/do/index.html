Things to do in Izunokuni, Shizuoka

Izunokuni, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • 江間いちご狩りセンター

    Ema Strawberry Picking Center

    #4 ranked in Izunokuni

    Over 300 greenhouses make this one of Izu's largest strawberry-picking spots, with in-season varieties and free milk refills.

  • 蔵屋鳴沢

    Kura-ya Narusawa

    #5 ranked in Izunokuni

    A tea and craft-beer complex beside the Nirayama Reverberatory Furnace, with seasonal tea picking and views of the furnace and Mount Fuji.

  • 小坂みかん共同農園

    Kosaka Mikan Cooperative Farm

    #6 ranked in Izunokuni

    This five-hectare farm at Mount Katsuragi's foot grows about 8,000 mikan trees and offers all-you-can-eat picking.
