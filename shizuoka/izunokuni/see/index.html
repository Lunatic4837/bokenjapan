  • Izu Pa no Rama Park

    Izu Panorama Park

    #1 ranked in Izunokuni

    Park in Izunokuni, Shizuoka.

  • Egawa Residence

    江川邸

    #2 ranked in Izunokuni

    Early-Edo Egawa family residence, a National Important Cultural Property, with a monument to Egawa Hidetatsu, who first had bread made in Japan.

  • Hirugashima Park

    蛭ヶ島公園

    #3 ranked in Izunokuni

    Exile site where Minamoto no Yoritomo spent about 20 years after the Heiji Rebellion, with statues of him and Hojo Masako facing Mount Fuji.

  • Nirayama Reverberatory Furnace

    史跡 韮山反射炉

    #8 ranked in Izunokuni

    Completed in 1857 to cast cannons, this World Cultural Heritage furnace is the world's only near-original reverberatory furnace.
