Things to do in Kakegawa, Shizuoka

Kakegawa, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • キウイフルーツカントリーJapan

    Kiwi Fruit Country Japan

    #1 ranked in Kakegawa

    One of Japan's largest kiwi farms, researching about 80 varieties and offering year-round all-you-can-eat ripe fruit.

  • つま恋リゾート彩の郷

    Tsumagoi Resort Sai no Sato

    #5 ranked in Kakegawa

    A hillside resort with go-karts, archery, horseback riding, tennis, putter golf, a large summer water park, winter illuminations, and a natural hot spring.

  • 旅ノ舎

    Tabi No Sha

    #12 ranked in Kakegawa

    An experiential old folk-house inn below Mount Awagatake offering tea picking, factory visits, tea rolling, and seasonal countryside activities.
