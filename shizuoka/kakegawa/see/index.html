Sights in Kakegawa, Shizuoka

Kakegawa, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 10 places

See

  • Kapposhi Terrace

    かっぽしテラス（粟ヶ岳世界農業遺産茶草場テラス）

    #2 ranked in Kakegawa

    On Mount Awagatake's summit, this terrace overlooks World Agricultural Heritage tea fields, Mount Fuji, and Suruga Bay.

  • Mikumano Shrine

    三熊野神社

    #3 ranked in Kakegawa

    Founded in 701 and long worshipped for safe childbirth and matchmaking, it holds an April festival of Edo-style floats.

  • Sayo no Nakayama Pass

    小夜の中山峠

    #4 ranked in Kakegawa

    Once praised as one of the Tokaido's three difficult spots, this historic pass is home to the Yonaki-ishi legend and Kuonji Temple.

  • Sakae Soy Sauce Brewery

    栄醤油醸造

    #7 ranked in Kakegawa

    Founded in 1795 in Yokosuka, this brewery naturally ages domestic whole soybeans and wheat for over a year in wooden barrels.

  • Ryubi Shrine

    龍尾神社

    #8 ranked in Kakegawa

    An ancient shrine northeast of Kakegawa Castle, revered by successive lords, with about 300 weeping plums in bloom from mid-February to early March.

  • Kakegawa City Take no Maru

    掛川市竹の丸

    #9 ranked in Kakegawa

    A city-designated residence by Kakegawa Castle's north gate, built in 1903 by the Matsumoto family, with a guest room viewing the keep.

  • Kakegawa Flower and Bird Garden

    掛川花鳥園

    #10 ranked in Kakegawa

    A barrier-free all-weather greenhouse housing about 100 species and 600 birds, with close encounters, feeding, and bird shows.

  • Kotomama Hachiman Shrine

    事任八幡宮

    #11 ranked in Kakegawa

    Totomi's chief shrine, named in The Pillow Book, where travelers prayed for safety, with camphor and cedar trees designated city monuments.

  • Kakegawa City Stained Glass Art Museum

    掛川市ステンドグラス美術館

    #14 ranked in Kakegawa

    In Kakegawa Castle Park, it displays about 70 Victorian British stained-glass works, chiefly by Charles Kemp.

  • Kakegawa Castle

    掛川城

    #15 ranked in Kakegawa

    The keep, Japan's first authentic wooden reconstruction in 1994, overlooks the castle town and Mount Fuji on clear days.
