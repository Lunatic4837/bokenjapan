Sights in Kikugawa, Shizuoka

Kikugawa, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

See

  • 西富田ほたるの里

    Nishitomida Firefly Village

    #2 ranked in Kikugawa

    Genji fireflies inhabit a stream about one kilometer southwest of Kakkenzan, with a local viewing gathering in early June.

  • 永寳寺

    Eihoji Temple

    #3 ranked in Kikugawa

    A yamabushi temple opened in 1616 that conveys Shugendo history, with a free human-powered ropeway over its ponds.

  • 黒田家代官屋敷

    Kuroda Family Magistrate's Residence

    #4 ranked in Kikugawa

    A National Important Cultural Property preserving the Kuroda family's Edo-period magistrate residence, surrounded by about 13 plum varieties.
