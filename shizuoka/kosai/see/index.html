Sights in Kosai, Shizuoka

Kosai, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 4 places

See

  • Onyado Shirasuka

    おんやど白須賀

    #2 ranked in Kosai

    A museum on Shirasuka-juku, the Tokaido's 32nd post town, covering the 1707 tsunami and the town's move uphill.

  • Arai Checkpoint Museum

    特別史跡新居関跡　新居関所史料館

    #3 ranked in Kosai

    Japan's only checkpoint where Edo-period buildings still stand, rebuilt late in the period after earthquakes and tsunamis.

  • Honkoji Temple

    本興寺

    #4 ranked in Kosai

    A historic temple revered by the Imagawa clan and Tokugawa Ieyasu, whose main hall is a National Important Cultural Property.

  • Sakichi Toyoda Memorial Museum

    豊田佐吉記念館

    #5 ranked in Kosai

    Memorial to Toyota Group founder Sakichi Toyoda, displaying looms he invented, including the Model G, and his restored thatched birthplace.
