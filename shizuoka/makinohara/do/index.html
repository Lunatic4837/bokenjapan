Things to do in Makinohara, Shizuoka

Makinohara, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 2 places

Do

  • Sagara Komare Hot Spring Hall

    さがら子生れ温泉会館

    #4 ranked in Makinohara

    A natural hot spring linked to the Komare Stone legend, known for silky free-flowing water called a beauty-skin bath.

  • Activity spot in Makinohara

    グリンピア牧之原

    Romaji: Gurinpia Makinohara

    #12 ranked in Makinohara

    A tea theme park on the Makinohara Plateau, run by long-established tea wholesaler Kisakuen, offering seasonal tea picking.
