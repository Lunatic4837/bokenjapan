Sights in Makinohara, Shizuoka

Makinohara, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 9 places

See

  • Fujisan Shizuoka Airport

    富士山静岡空港

    #1 ranked in Makinohara

    The airport closest to Mt. Fuji, with observation decks for viewing takeoffs, landings, and the mountain.

  • Ogane House

    大鐘家

    #2 ranked in Makinohara

    Enshu Mineda domain headman's house with a 300-year-old look, Important Cultural Property buildings, and 10,000 hydrangeas.

  • Kyukozan Hirata Temple

    吸江山 平田寺

    #3 ranked in Makinohara

    A Kamakura-period Rinzai temple; its main hall was rebuilt in 1786 by Tanuma Okitsugu and it holds a national treasure.

  • Shizunami Coast

    静波海岸

    #5 ranked in Makinohara

    A broad sandy beach with gentle shallows for family swimming and steady waves that draw surfers from across Japan.

  • Sagara Sanbichi

    相良サンビーチ

    #6 ranked in Makinohara

    A swimming beach of blue sea, white sand, and pines, with Mt. Fuji visible beyond the horizon on clear days.

  • Yuden no Sato Park and Yuden Museum

    油田の里公園　油田資料館

    #7 ranked in Makinohara

    Museum and park on the former Sagara Oil Field, the Pacific side's only oil site, with hand-dug well models.

  • Komare Stone

    子生れ石

    #8 ranked in Makinohara

    A stone in Nishihagima that seems to grow from a wall and fall like a child being born, one of Enshu's Seven Wonders.

  • Makinohara City Historical Museum

    牧之原市史料館

    #9 ranked in Makinohara

    A local history museum on Sagara Castle's main-bailey site, displaying over 200 Tanuma family materials in a castle-like building.

  • Shizunami Beach

    静波海水浴場

    #11 ranked in Makinohara

    A famous beach with a long sandy shore, gentle shallow waves, excellent water, and year-round surf swells.
