Sights in Mishimashi, Shizuoka

Mishimashi, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 4 places

See

  • Mishima Skywalk

    三島スカイウォーク

    #1 ranked in Mishima

    A 400-meter pedestrian suspension bridge offering simultaneous views of Mount Fuji, Suruga Bay, and Izu's mountains.

  • Rakujuen

    楽寿園

    #2 ranked in Mishima

    A nationally designated strolling garden around spring-fed Kohama Pond, created in 1890 by Prince Komatsu Akihito.

  • Yamanaka Castle Ruins Park

    山中城跡公園

    #3 ranked in Mishima

    Rare stone-free earthwork castle ruins of the Hojo clan, selected as one of Japan's Top 100 Castles.

  • Mishima Taisha

    三嶋大社

    #6 ranked in Mishima

    Izu Province's ichinomiya for more than a thousand years, known as Izu's foremost power spot for victory and luck.
