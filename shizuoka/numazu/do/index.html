Do

  • Awashima Marine Park

    あわしまマリンパーク

    #6 ranked in Numazu

    A marine park using all of Awashima in Suruga Bay, reached by ferry in about three minutes, with shows and a frog hall.

  • Izu-Mito Sea Paradise

    伊豆・三津シーパラダイス

    #7 ranked in Numazu

    Nicknamed Mitoshi, this experience aquarium popular with families offers close animal encounters, shows, and Suruga Bay fish.
