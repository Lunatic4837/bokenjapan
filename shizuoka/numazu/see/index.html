Sights in Numazu, Shizuoka

Numazu, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 4 places

See

  • 沼津港深海水族館　シーラカンス・ミュージアム

    Numazu Port Deep-Sea Aquarium Coelacanth Museum

    #1 ranked in Numazu

    Japan's only aquarium specializing in deep-sea life, with a frozen coelacanth specimen and rare Suruga Bay fish and crabs.

  • 沼津港大型展望水門びゅうお

    Numazu Port Byuo Observation Floodgate

    #2 ranked in Numazu

    One of Japan's largest floodgates, built to protect Numazu Port from tsunamis, has a 30-meter facility with 360-degree views including Mount Fuji.

  • 香貫山

    Mount Kanuki

    #3 ranked in Numazu

    Southeast of Numazu, this 193-meter mountain has road access, Mount Fuji views, night scenery, and about 2,000 spring cherries.

  • 大瀬崎

    Osezaki

    #4 ranked in Numazu

    A cape jutting about one kilometer into Suruga Bay, sacred to Ose Shrine, with a national-monument byakushin forest and freshwater pond Kamiike.
