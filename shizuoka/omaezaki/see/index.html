Sights in Omaezaki, Shizuoka

Omaezaki, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

See

  • Omaesaki Lighthouse

    御前埼灯台

    #2 ranked in Omaezaki

    A white lighthouse and Important Cultural Property at mainland Shizuoka's southern tip, one of 16 in Japan you can climb.

  • Hamaoka Nuclear Energy Hall

    浜岡原子力館

    #3 ranked in Omaezaki

    Public exhibition hall with a full-scale reactor model, an 18-meter-diameter energy dome, and a lookout over Enshu-nada.

  • Hamaoka Sand Dunes

    浜岡砂丘

    #6 ranked in Omaezaki

    Dunes of Tenryu River sand piled by the strong west wind Enshu no Karakkaze, with wind ripples from winter to spring.
