Things to do in Shimoda, Shizuoka

Shimoda, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • Kannon Onsen

    飲泉、自家源泉かけ流しの秘湯　観音温泉

    #1 ranked in Shimoda

    A pH 9.5 alkaline spring hidden in Oku-Shimoda forest, also approved as a drinkable onsen.

  • Shimoda Ropeway

    下田ロープウェイ

    #2 ranked in Shimoda

    A 3.5-minute ride from Izukyu-Shimoda Station to Nesugatayama, whose summit views rank among Izu's three scenic sights.

  • Izu Cruise Shimoda Port Sightseeing Boat

    伊豆クルーズ 「下田港内めぐり」遊覧船

    #24 ranked in Shimoda

    About a 20-minute cruise of Shimoda Port on a black ship recreating Perry's flagship Susquehanna.
