Sights in Shimoda, Shizuoka

Shimoda, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 20 places

See

  • Shirahama Ohama Beach

    白浜大浜海水浴場

    #3 ranked in Shimoda

    A 700-meter stretch of some of the Izu Peninsula's whitest sand, facing an emerald sea.

  • Shimoda Park

    下田公園

    #4 ranked in Shimoda

    A cape park on the site of Ushima Castle, known for Japan's largest hydrangea display in June.

  • Sotoura Beach

    外浦海水浴場

    #5 ranked in Shimoda

    A quiet cove beach on Route 135 with calm, clear shallows, also known for sea kayaking.

  • Nabetahama Beach

    鍋田浜海水浴場

    #6 ranked in Shimoda

    The beach nearest Izukyu-Shimoda Station, with extremely calm water safe for small children.

  • Tatadohama Beach

    多々戸浜海水浴場

    #7 ranked in Shimoda

    A 450-meter white-sand beach with a surfing area, swimming zone, and year-round heated showers.

  • Kujuhama Beach

    九十浜海水浴場

    #8 ranked in Shimoda

    A 50-meter beach beside Suzaki Imperial Villa, with calm clear water good for snorkeling.

  • Nyutahama Beach

    入田浜海水浴場

    #9 ranked in Shimoda

    A cycad-lined beach with fine white sand and clear water, often chosen for films and commercials.

  • Shirahama Chuo Beach

    白浜中央海水浴場

    #10 ranked in Shimoda

    North of Shimoda, the only local beach allowing designated barbecue, and listed among Japan's best bathing spots.

  • Toji Beach

    田牛海水浴場

    #11 ranked in Shimoda

    A southern Shimoda beach with clear water, relatively calm waves, and a neighboring sand-sled slope.

  • Kisami Ohama Beach

    吉佐美大浜海水浴場

    #12 ranked in Shimoda

    A white-sand beach about 750 meters long, popular with surfers, families, and foreign visitors.

  • Izu Shirahama Shrine

    伊豆白濱神社

    #13 ranked in Shimoda

    Izu's oldest shrine, over 2,400 years old, beside Shirahama Beach and known for matchmaking.

  • Hofukuji Temple

    宝福寺

    #14 ranked in Shimoda

    Historic temple on the former Shimoda magistrate's office site, known as Tojin Okichi's family temple.

  • Yoshida Shoin Hideout

    吉田松陰寓寄所

    #15 ranked in Shimoda

    Hideout where Yoshida Shoin hid while seeking a chance to stow away, with a secret room preserved.

  • Uehara Art Museum

    公益財団法人 上原美術館

    #16 ranked in Shimoda

    Founded from donations of Uehara Shoji's modern paintings and his parents' Buddhist statues.

  • Shimoda Undersea Aquarium

    下田海中水族館

    #18 ranked in Shimoda

    Floating aquarium in a natural inlet where visitors can see 10,000 Izu creatures and dolphin shows.

  • Ryugu Cave

    龍宮窟

    #19 ranked in Shimoda

    Wave-eroded sea cave in Toji whose partly collapsed ceiling forms a skylight about 50 meters across.

  • Perry Road

    ペリーロード

    #20 ranked in Shimoda

    About 400 meters of stone paving along the Hiraname River, a path Admiral Perry is said to have walked in 1854.

  • Tsume Kizaki

    爪木崎

    #21 ranked in Shimoda

    Scenic cape at the eastern tip of Shimoda's Suzaki Peninsula, with a white lighthouse, three million winter daffodils, and columnar joints.

  • Shimoda Kaikoku Museum

    下田開国博物館

    #22 ranked in Shimoda

    Museum of Shimoda's role in Japan's opening, rotating about 1,000 of some 2,000 artifacts, with AR exhibits.

  • Ryosen Temple

    了仙寺

    #23 ranked in Shimoda

    National historic temple founded in 1635, where Perry signed the 1854 Shimoda Treaty; about 1,000 American jasmine plants bloom each May.
