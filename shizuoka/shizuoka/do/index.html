Things to do in Shizuoka, Shizuoka

Shizuoka, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 14 places

Do

  • Ribaweru Ikawa Ski Area

    リバウェル井川スキー場

    #19 ranked in Shizuoka

    Beginner and family ski area at the Southern Alps' foot: wide slopes average 8 degrees, with Fuji views and sledding.

  • Fujisan Shimizu Minato Kuruzu

    Fujisan Shimizu Port Cruise

    #20 ranked in Shizuoka

    Activity in Shizuoka, Shizuoka.

  • Suruga Kenko Land

    駿河健康ランド

    #21 ranked in Shizuoka

    A natural hot-spring facility with 20 bath types, including saunas and herbal baths, an ocean-view outdoor bath, dining, and lodging.

  • Suruga Bay Ferry

    駿河湾フェリー

    #22 ranked in Shizuoka

    A ferry linking Shimizu Port and Toi Port in about 90 minutes, designated Prefectural Road 223, with Mt. Fuji views across Suruga Bay.

  • Miho Uchihama Beach

    三保内浜海水浴場

    #30 ranked in Shizuoka

    A calm, family-friendly swimming beach near Miho Peninsula's tip in Miho no Matsubara, with views of Mt. Fuji.

  • Miho Masaki Beach

    三保真崎海水浴場

    #31 ranked in Shizuoka

    A popular swimming beach at the tip of Miho Peninsula, beside Masaki Lighthouse, facing Mount Fuji.

  • Mochimune Beach

    用宗海岸海水浴場

    #32 ranked in Shizuoka

    A calm beach about a five-minute walk from JR Mochimune Station, with lifeguards on duty in summer.

  • Liberty Resort Kunozan

    リバティーリゾート久能山

    #35 ranked in Shizuoka

    A large hot-spring resort at the foot of Kunozan Toshogu, themed on an Edo castle town.

  • Sunpu Workshop Takumiyado

    駿府の工房 匠宿

    #38 ranked in Shizuoka

    One of Japan's largest craft experience centers, and the world's only place to try Suruga bamboo craft.

  • Sauna Shikiji

    サウナしきじ

    #39 ranked in Shizuoka

    A nationally known sauna offering Finnish and Korean-style rooms plus a drinkable natural-water cold bath.

  • Nihondaira Ropeway

    日本平ロープウェイ

    #45 ranked in Shizuoka

    A ropeway linking Nihondaira summit to Kunozan Toshogu in about five minutes, overlooking Suruga Bay.

  • Kuchisakamoto Onsen Yokujo

    口坂本温泉浴場

    #47 ranked in Shizuoka

    A mountain onsen along the Nakakawachi River, with a pH 8.9 sodium bicarbonate spring.

  • Shimizu Nishisato Onsen Yamasemi no Yu

    清水西里温泉「やませみの湯」

    #48 ranked in Shizuoka

    A day-trip hot spring in Shimizu Forest Park Yasuragi no Mori, with three outdoor baths for each gender and sodium-calcium chloride water.

  • Yunoshima Onsen Yokujo

    湯ノ島温泉浴場

    #49 ranked in Shizuoka

    A city-run day-trip onsen along the Warashina River, with sodium bicarbonate water and indoor baths facing the river.
