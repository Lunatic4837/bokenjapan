Sights in Shizuoka, Shizuoka

Shizuoka, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 33 places

See

  • Yui Honjin Park

    由比本陣公園

    #1 ranked in Shizuoka

    Park preserving the look of Yui-juku, the Tokaido's 16th post town, including a space that once received daimyo.

  • Hamaishidake

    浜石岳

    #2 ranked in Shizuoka

    A 707-meter peak north of Yui, with summit views of Suruga Bay, Mount Fuji, and on clear days the Southern Alps.

  • Shosetsu Indigo Dye Shop

    正雪紺屋（由井正雪生家）

    #3 ranked in Shizuoka

    Indigo dye shop along old Tokaido in Yui, said to be Yui Shosetsu's birthplace, in a roughly 200-year-old building.

  • Shizuoka City Tokaido Hiroshige Art Museum

    静岡市東海道広重美術館

    #4 ranked in Shizuoka

    Art museum in Yui Honjin Park named for ukiyo-e artist Utagawa Hiroshige, holding about 1,400 works and changing exhibits monthly.

  • Shimizu Port Boat Inn Memorial Museum Suehiro

    清水港船宿記念館「末廣」

    #6 ranked in Shizuoka

    Restored boat inn opened in 1886 by Shimizu Jirocho late in life, with exhibits on Katsu Kaishu and an English school.

  • Okitsu Zagyo-so

    興津坐漁荘

    #7 ranked in Shizuoka

    Faithfully restored villa built for Meiji elder statesman Saionji Kinmochi's later years, with bamboo sukiya-zukuri design.

  • Togeppo Saiokuji Temple

    吐月峰柴屋寺

    #8 ranked in Shizuoka

    A temple said to have begun as renga poet Socho's retreat, with a national scenic garden modeled on Kyoto's Ginkakuji.

  • Shizuoka Prefectural Art Museum

    静岡県立美術館

    #9 ranked in Shizuoka

    Art museum at the foot of Nihondaira, housing 32 Rodin works including the over-six-meter Gates of Hell.

  • S-Pulse Dream Plaza

    エスパルスドリームプラザ

    #10 ranked in Shizuoka

    An orange Shimizu bay-area complex with Mount Fuji views, Chibi Maruko-chan Land, dining, shopping, and sushi.

  • Shizuoka Prefectural Earthquake Preparedness Education Center

    静岡県地震防災センター

    #11 ranked in Shizuoka

    A hands-on disaster-prevention center with earthquake simulators and exhibits on river disasters, landslides, and volcanic eruptions.

  • Verkehr Museum

    フェルケール博物館

    #12 ranked in Shizuoka

    Shows elaborate ship models and port tools linked to Shimizu Port, tracing its history and people's ties with the sea.

  • Shizuoka Science Museum Ru-Ku-Ru

    静岡科学館る・く・る

    #13 ranked in Shizuoka

    A hands-on science museum a one-minute walk from JR Shizuoka Station, with illusion sliders, magnet walls, and weekend shows.

  • Special Historic Site Toro Ruins

    特別史跡 登呂遺跡

    #14 ranked in Shizuoka

    A revived Yayoi village from about 2,000 years ago, with raised storehouses, dwellings, restored paddies, and fire-making.

  • Nihondaira

    日本平

    #15 ranked in Shizuoka

    A 307-meter hill once ranked No. 1 among 100 Japanese tourist spots, with Suruga Bay, Shimizu Port, and Fuji views.

  • Tesshu Tera

    Tesshu Tera

    Tesshu Temple

    #16 ranked in Shizuoka

    Temple in Shizuoka, Shizuoka.

  • Seikenji Temple

    清見寺

    #17 ranked in Shizuoka

    A Shimizu temple over 1,300 years old where young Ieyasu studied; Korean envoys lodged here, and JR tracks cross the grounds.

  • Ryugeji Temple

    龍華寺

    #18 ranked in Shizuoka

    An old temple whose Kanpuen garden likens its pond, mound, and hall roof to Suruga Bay, Mount Udo, and Mount Fuji.

  • Shimizu Funakoshi Tsutsumi Park

    清水船越堤公園

    #23 ranked in Shizuoka

    A nature park at the eastern foot of Nihondaira, overlooking Mt. Fuji, Shimizu, and the port, with spring cherry blossoms.

  • Jirocho Seika

    次郎長生家

    #24 ranked in Shizuoka

    Birthplace of Shimizu Jirocho (1820-1893), preserving his first-bath well and an Edo-Meiji townhouse registered as a tangible cultural property.

  • Otatara Fudoson

    大鈩不動尊

    #25 ranked in Shizuoka

    A Fudoson above a waterfall deep in Mariko, enshrining Atago Daigongen and Fudo Myoo, with a morning market on the 28th.

  • Kuju Yama Hora Yoshi In

    久住山　洞慶院

    #26 ranked in Shizuoka

    A Soto Zen temple in Hatori with about 400 plum trees and a late-July festival awarding the traditional okanjake snake carving.

  • Kyu Igarashi Shikaiin

    Kyu Igarashi Shikaiin

    Former Igarashi Dental Clinic

    #27 ranked in Shizuoka

    Sight in Shizuoka.

  • Shizuoka City Nihondaira Zoo

    静岡市立日本平動物園

    #28 ranked in Shizuoka

    A zoo at the foot of Mt. Fuji where visitors can watch red pandas up close, plus lions and polar bears.

  • Shizuoka Prefectural Buried Cultural Properties Center

    静岡県埋蔵文化財センター

    #33 ranked in Shizuoka

    A center preserving excavated finds, with free displays of about 750 artifacts from the Paleolithic to Edo periods.

  • Fujinokuni Museum of Earth Environmental History

    ふじのくに地球環境史ミュージアム

    #34 ranked in Shizuoka

    A prefectural museum in a former high school, showing Shizuoka nature specimens from Suruga Bay to Mount Fuji.

  • Fugetsuro

    浮月楼

    #36 ranked in Shizuoka

    The former residence site of Tokugawa Yoshinobu, a three-minute walk from Shizuoka Station North Exit.

  • Nihondaira Yume Terrace

    日本平夢テラス

    #37 ranked in Shizuoka

    A Kengo Kuma observatory opened in 2018 atop Nihondaira, with views of Mount Fuji and Suruga Bay.

  • Rinzaiji Temple

    臨済寺

    #40 ranked in Shizuoka

    Family temple of the Imagawa clan, where Tokugawa Ieyasu studied under the monk Taigen Sessai as a hostage.

  • Satta Pass

    薩埵峠

    #41 ranked in Shizuoka

    Once known as a difficult spot on the Tokaido, it appears in Hiroshige's ukiyo-e and is now an easy hiking course.

  • Kunozan Toshogu Shrine

    久能山東照宮

    #42 ranked in Shizuoka

    The first Toshogu shrine in Japan, enshrining Tokugawa Ieyasu on a steep mountain overlooking Suruga Bay.

  • Sunpu Castle Park

    駿府城公園

    #43 ranked in Shizuoka

    A park enclosed by Sunpu Castle's moat and stone walls, where Tokugawa Ieyasu spent his final years.

  • Miho no Matsubara (Hagoromo Pine)

    三保松原（羽衣の松）

    #44 ranked in Shizuoka

    A pine-lined coast about five kilometers long, with Mount Fuji views, named a World Cultural Heritage site in 2013.

  • Shizuoka Sengen Shrine

    静岡浅間神社

    #46 ranked in Shizuoka

    A complex of shrines long called O-Sengen-san, where young Tokugawa Ieyasu held his coming-of-age ceremony.
