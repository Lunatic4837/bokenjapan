Things to do in Susono, Shizuoka

Susono, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 3 places

Do

  • Snow Park Yeti

    スノーパーク　Yeti

    #1 ranked in Susono

    Japan's earliest outdoor ski area, opening in late October at Mt. Fuji's second station, with skiing, snowboarding, and night sessions.

  • Fuji Safari Park

    富士サファリパーク

    #5 ranked in Susono

    A Mt. Fuji safari park where you enter by car and feed lions and bears from a wire-mesh jungle bus.

  • Grinpa

    Grinpa（ぐりんぱ）

    #6 ranked in Susono

    An amusement park at about 1,200 meters on Mt. Fuji's second station, with Sylvanian Village and large athletic courses.
