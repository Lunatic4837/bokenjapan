Sights in Susono, Shizuoka

Susono, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 4 places

See

  • 五竜の滝

    Goryu Falls

    #2 ranked in Susono

    A Shizuoka Prefecture natural monument: five falls about 100 meters wide and 12 meters high over a lava cliff on the Kise River.

  • 富士山すそのパノラマロード

    Fujisan Suso no Panoramarodo

    #3 ranked in Susono

    A road of about 10 kilometers from Tomei Susono IC to Suyama, with close views of Mt. Fuji and seasonal flower fields.

  • 須山浅間神社

    Suyama Sengen Shrine

    #4 ranked in Susono

    A shrine at the Suyama trailhead on Mt. Fuji, said to be founded by Yamato Takeru, among cedars over 500 years old.

  • 水ケ塚公園

    Mizugatsuka Park

    #7 ranked in Susono

    A park at 1,450 meters on Mt. Fuji's second station, with an unobstructed view of the mountain, including the Hoei Crater.
