Things to do in Yaizu, Shizuoka

Yaizu, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 2 places

Do

  • Discovery Park Yaizu

    ディスカバリーパーク焼津

    #6 ranked in Yaizu

    A Suruga Bay space-and-nature park with Shizuoka's largest 80-centimeter telescope for day and night viewing, plus a planetarium.

  • Ekichika Onsen Kuroshio

    エキチカ温泉・くろしお

    #8 ranked in Yaizu

    A mildly alkaline hot spring from 1,500 meters down, two minutes from JR Yaizu Station, with lounges and seafood dining.
