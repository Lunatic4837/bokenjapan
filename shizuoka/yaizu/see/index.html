Yaizu, Shizuoka: things to do, food and stays · Shizuoka · 5 places

  • 浜当目海水浴場

    Hamatome Beach

    #2 ranked in Yaizu

    The Shida area's only swimming beach has a roughly 400-meter shore, relatively calm waves, free parking, changing rooms, and showers.

  • 花沢の里

    Hanazawa Village

    #3 ranked in Yaizu

    A quiet old-Tokaido mountain village of about 30 Edo-period houses along the Manyoshu's Yakitsubeno path.

  • 深層水ミュージアム

    Deep Seawater Museum

    #4 ranked in Yaizu

    A free museum on Suruga Bay deep seawater, with deep-sea tanks, tastings, and clear-day rooftop views of Mount Fuji.

  • 満観峰

    Mankanho Peak

    #5 ranked in Yaizu

    A 470-meter peak popular for easy family hikes, with a grassy summit and 360-degree views of Mount Fuji and Suruga Bay.
