    Ichikai, Tochigi · 0285-68-3483

  • Tatara Pond

    Ichikai, Tochigi · 0285-68-1118

    This two-hectare irrigation pond and surrounding forest feature water lilies in early summer.

  • Irino Family Residence (Peach Mansion)

    Ichikai, Tochigi · 0285-68-0020

    Built in 1836-1841 as famine relief, this headman's house is a National Important Cultural Property.

  • History and Folklore Museum

    Ichikai, Tochigi · 0285-68-4380
