Sights in Kaminokawa, Tochigi

Kaminokawa · Tochigi · 10 places

See

  • Shirasagi Shrine

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi

    Shrine to Yamato Takeru, trusted for protection from misfortune and for traffic safety.

  • Kamikonushi-Mobara Government Office Site

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi

    Nara-period Kawachi District office site, famed for named tiles and the prefecture's largest round tomb.

  • Kabutozuka Burial Mound

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 0285-56-9160

    A round burial mound about 40 meters across, named for its helmet shape, with an exposed chamber.

  • Atago Shrine Burial Mound

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 0285-56-3510

    This sixth-century round burial mound, centered on Atago Shrine, holds children's sumo yearly.

  • Enmei-in Temple

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 0285-53-7862

    Temple founded in the Oan period, enshrining Dainichi Nyorai, Yakushi, Jizo, and other Buddhist images.

  • Miyaoka Bridge

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 028-626-3146

    Completed in 2009, this 556.5-meter bridge crosses the Kinugawa River.

  • Keno Purification Center

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 0285-53-5694

    Beneath its solar panels, this plant treats water and generates digester-gas power.

  • Kenshoji Temple

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 0285-53-0808

    A retired Taira general founded it in 1212; it later became a Soto Zen temple.

  • Kaminokawa Seven Lucky Gods Pilgrimage

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 0285-56-2549

    A roughly three-hour pilgrimage of seven temples honoring the Seven Lucky Gods, with open main halls.

  • Chosenji Temple

    Kaminokawa, Tochigi · 0285-56-2549

    Founded in 1504, it houses a prefecture-designated wooden Eleven-faced Kannon statue.
