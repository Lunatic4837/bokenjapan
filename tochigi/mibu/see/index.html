Sights in Mibu, Tochigi

Mibu · Tochigi · 8 places

See

  • Mibu Town History and Folklore Museum

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-8544

    A museum on the former Mibu Castle site, with three galleries on local history and culture.

  • Ushizuka Burial Mound

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-8544

    This national historic site is a scallop-shaped keyhole tomb about 47 meters long.

  • Kurumazuka Burial Mound

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-8544

    A three-tier round burial mound over 80 meters in diameter, with a moat and stone chamber.

  • Jorakuji Temple

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-0225

    Zen temple founded in 1462 by Mibu Tanenari and kept as the Mibu family temple.

  • Mibuji Temple

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-0811

    Birthplace of Jikaku Daishi Ennin, with a birth-bath well and a giant ginkgo tree.

  • Chausuyama Burial Mound

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-8544

    A nationally designated keyhole tomb, 91 meters long, where large haniwa figures were excavated.

  • Fujisan Tumulus

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-8544

    Tochigi's largest round tumulus, a prefectural historic site with clay figures over 150 centimeters tall.

  • Ogoto Shrine

    Mibu, Tochigi · 0282-82-0430

    Founded in 1091, Mibu's tutelary shrine has a prefecture-designated copper torii.
