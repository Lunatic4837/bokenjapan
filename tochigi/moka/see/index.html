Sights in Moka, Tochigi

Moka · Tochigi · 11 places

See

  • Igashira Park

    Moka, Tochigi · 0285-83-3121

    Chosen among Japan's 100 Urban Parks, this leading Tochigi park offers birds and seasonal nature across vast grounds.

  • Omae Ebisu Shrine

    Moka, Tochigi · 0285-82-2012

    Completed on December 10, 1989, this subsidiary shrine of Engishiki-listed Omae Shrine is called Japan's greatest Ebisu.

  • Omae Shrine

    Moka, Tochigi

    This Engishiki-listed shrine, over 1,500 years old, has an ornately carved polychrome late Azuchi-Momoyama main hall.

  • Samurai Sword Museum

    Moka, Tochigi

    Founded in 1994, it publicly displays about 100 Japanese swords, plus armor, firearms, and foreign blades.

  • Igashira Motor Park

    Moka, Tochigi

    A Tochigi racing-kart track where adults and children, from aspiring racers to families, can enjoy casual races.

  • Ten-Thousand-Person Pool

    Moka, Tochigi · 0285-83-3121

    Named for a 10,500-square-meter water area that could hold 10,000 swimmers, it has seven pools.

  • Ninomiya Sontoku Museum and Sakuramachi Jinya Site

    Moka, Tochigi

    Ninomiya Sontoku was posted to Sakuramachi Jinya in 1823 to lead waterway, weir, and bridge repairs.

  • SL Kyuroku Hall

    Moka, Tochigi · 0285-83-9600

    Japan's only compressed-air self-propelled 9600-class steam locomotive is displayed with other rolling stock.

  • Mount Nemoto

    Moka, Tochigi

    This 165-meter satoyama in eastern Moka has mixed woods, ponds, grassland, and a 3,000-tree cherry avenue.

  • Okabe Memorial Hall Kinreiso

    Moka, Tochigi

    A prefecture-designated two-story wooden building with a stroll garden, Isoyama stone walls, and fireproof storehouse construction.

  • Takadayama Senju-ji Temple

    Moka, Tochigi

    Founded by Shinran in 1225, this Jodo Shinshu temple preserves many nationally designated Important Cultural Properties.
