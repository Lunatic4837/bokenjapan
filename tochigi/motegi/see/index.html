Sights in Motegi, Tochigi

Motegi · Tochigi · 11 places

See

  • Ose Tourist Fish Weir

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-63-2885

    A Naka River fish weir among the largest, popular for catching fish and salt-grilled fresh ayu.

  • Motegi Castle Hill Park

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-63-5644

    A symbolic park on the Kamakura-period Motegi Castle site, with staggered cherries and 300,000 spider lilies.

  • Unfinished Nagakura Line Tour

    Motegi, Tochigi

    Seasonal guided tours of the unfinished Nagakura Line include the normally closed Omineyama Tunnel.

  • Former Kotado Gado Residence

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-64-1023

    A rare two-by-four Western-style house called the White House is a Motegi tangible cultural property.

  • Fuminomori Motegi Cultural Exchange Hall

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-64-1023

    A wooden facility five minutes from Motegi Station with a library, gallery, history room, and plaza.

  • Mount Kamakura

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-63-5644

    216-meter cliff viewpoint famed for late-autumn seas of cloud and foliage above the Naka River.

  • Mount Yakemori (Mitsumata Colony)

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-63-5644

    On the Ibaraki border, this mountain offers a varied hike and a March mitsumata flower colony.

  • Yakumo Shrine (Motegi)

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-63-0479

    Founded in 1192 for Susanoo, it draws visitors seeking health and protection from misfortune.

  • Motegi Showa Hall

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-63-5644

    A mini theme park of Showa nostalgia in renovated downtown shops, displaying tobacco goods and old posters.

  • Motegi Showa Hall No. 2

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-63-5644

    Opened in 2025, this Showa-retro hall themed as a candy shop displays about 3,000 nostalgic items.

  • Flower Mountain at Kinohana Nature Garden

    Motegi, Tochigi · 0285-65-1187

    Nobuo Sugino's Motegi garden holds over seventy flowering trees that bloom nearly year-round.
