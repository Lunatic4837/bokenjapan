    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    This day-trip hilltop bath overlooks the Naka River and Nikko peaks and is called a silky beauty spring.

  • Roadside Station Bato

    Nakagawa, Tochigi · 0287-92-5711

    This Route 293 roadside station offers local produce, a restaurant, brochures, and handmade gelato.

  • Kaneko Store (Tamari Pickles)

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    This hundred-year-old handmade miso shop is popular for local-vegetable pickles and carefully aged miso.

  • Nisshindo Confectionery

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    Since 1931, this local shop has sold Kabochakko, a Nakagawa-brand Minami-chan pumpkin pie.
