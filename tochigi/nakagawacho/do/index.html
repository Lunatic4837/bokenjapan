Things to do in Nakagawacho, Tochigi

Nakagawacho · Tochigi · 4 places

Do

  • Koisago-yaki Experience Center Toyukan

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    Visitors can try the wheel, hand-building, and painting with local clay for traditional Koisago-yaki.

  • Nanpeidai Hot Spring Hotel

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    Open-air beauty baths overlook the Nasu-Nikko mountains and Naka River, with local kaiseki meals.

  • Isamikan Irori Hot Spring Inn

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    This retro folk-craft inn serves creative country cuisine of freshwater fish, wild plants, and organic vegetables.

  • Nakagawa Town Tourism Association

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-92-5757

    A center inside Roadside Station Bato offering Nakagawa sightseeing and event information.
