See in Nakagawacho

Nakagawacho · Tochigi · 14 places

See

  • Hometown Lodge at Mahoroba no Yu

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    This five-guest lodge costs 15,000 yen a night and includes free bathing until 21:00.

  • Kazuo Iwamura Picture Book Hill Art Museum

    Nakagawa, Tochigi · 0287-92-5514

    It opened in April 1998 in Nakagawa and displays Kazuo Iwamura originals, sketches, and overseas editions.

  • Torinoko Sanjo Shrine

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    Founded in 807 astride the prefectural border, it is famed for one of Japan's largest owl statues.

  • Gozen Rock

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    This odd rock linked to Tokugawa Mitsukuni is prayed to for children and safe birth amid autumn foliage.

  • Hometown Forest Park

    Nakagawa, Tochigi · 0287-92-1116

    This family-friendly Nakagawa park has historic buildings, a folklore museum, a pond, and a children's area.

  • Koisago District, Most Beautiful Villages in Japan

    Nakagawa, Tochigi · 0287-92-5757

    It is Tochigi's only member of Japan's Most Beautiful Villages, known for Koisago-yaki kilns and terraces.

  • Mount Mumo Jurinji Bato-in

    Nakagawa, Tochigi

    Founded in 1217, it was renamed by Tokugawa Mitsukuni and enshrines Bato Kannon.

  • Nakagawa Town Bato Local History Museum

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-92-1103

    Exhibits Nakagawa history and culture from Jomon times to the present, including pottery and gold production.

  • Nakagawa Town Bato Hiroshige Museum of Art

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-92-1199

    Displays Utagawa Hiroshige paintings and prints, reopening after renewal in March 2026.

  • Jiganji Temple (13th of the Twenty-Four Disciples)

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-92-2326

    A Jodo Shinshu Honganji temple founded by Shinganbo, one of Shinran's twenty-four direct disciples.

  • Nakagawa Town Nasu Fudoki-no-Oka Museum

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-96-3366

    Introduces Nasu history from Jomon through Heian times around the Naka and Hoki river confluence.

  • Nakagawa Town Artisans' Hall Folk Museum

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-96-4580

    An old house in Hometown Forest Park where visitors can experience Meiji-era daily life.

  • Kara-no-Gosho Cave Tombs

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-92-5757

    Nationally designated ancient cave tombs of late Kofun chieftains, with house-like inner chambers.

  • Tomiyama Iwauchiwa

    Nakagawacho, Tochigi · 0287-92-5757

    Large colonies of small pale-pink iwauchiwa bloom deep in the mountains for two or three weeks.
