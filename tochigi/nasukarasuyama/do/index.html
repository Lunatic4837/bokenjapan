Things to do in Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi

Nasukarasuyama · Tochigi · 4 places

Do

  • Nasukarasuyama Ogane Hot Spring Grand Hotel

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-88-2211

    Ancient seawater from 1,200 meters underground flows freely and ranks among Japan's highest in calcium.

  • Yamaage Hall

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-84-1977

    The hall presents Karasuyama Yamaage materials, three floats, and an eight-minute festival recreation.

  • Shimazaki Sake Brewery

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-83-1221

    Founded in 1849, the city's only sake brewery offers tours and ages sake in a cave.

  • Minaminasu Auto Campground

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-88-0064

    A hilltop woodland campground in Nasukarasuyama under skies ranked third in a national starry-town contest.
