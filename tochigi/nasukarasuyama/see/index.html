Sights in Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi

See

  • Ryumon Falls

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-83-2765

    This waterfall is about 20 meters high and 65 meters wide, with a giant-serpent legend.

  • Karasuyama Castle Country Club

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-83-1100

    Master designer Seiichi Inoue created this 27-hole club with Honmaru, Ninomaru, and Sannomaru courses.

  • G7 Country Club

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-88-7711

    Pro Isao Aoki designed this low-relief course, which has hosted the Japan Women's Pro Championship.

  • Ryumon Hometown Folk Craft Museum

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-83-2765

    Reopened in 2021, it offers local cafe food, cider, and views of Ryumon Falls.

  • Karasuyama Castle Ruins

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-88-6223

    Built in 1418, this mountain castle is nicknamed Lying Cow Castle for its shape.

  • Cave Sake Brewery

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-83-1221

    Built late in World War II to make tanks, this cave now houses a sake brewery.

  • ACCUM

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi

    Japan's first battery-powered train has run on the JR Karasuyama Line since March 2014.

  • Sakai Bridge

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-83-1321

    Built in 1937, this rare three-span concrete arch bridge crosses the Nakagawa River.

  • Hanadate Pass Forest Park

    Nasukarasuyama, Tochigi · 0287-83-1115

  • Restaurant Kasai

  • Slive Eves Western Kitchen

  • Karasuyama Washi Hall
