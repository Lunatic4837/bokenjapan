Things to do in Nikko, Tochigi

Nikko · Tochigi · 6 places

Do

  • Kinu Tateiwa Suspension Bridge

    Nikko, Tochigi · 0288-22-1525

    A 140-meter pedestrian bridge about 40 meters high, linking Kinugawa Onsen with the scenic Tateiwa rock.

  • Nikko Yuba

    Nikko, Tochigi · 0288-22-1525

    A signature Nikko food of soy-milk skin, once vital protein for ascetic mountain monks.

  • Kanaya Confectionery

    Nikko, Tochigi · 0288-77-1180

    A gift shop and cafe opposite Kinugawa Onsen Station selling original baked sweets.

  • Katakuri no Yu Hot Spring Health Center

    Nikko, Tochigi · 0288-21-8671

    Closed from April 1, 2026; an alkaline simple hot spring in former Imaichi, opened October 1993.

  • Fishing in Oku-Nikko

    Nikko, Tochigi · 0288-62-2524

    Experience trout and fly fishing at Lake Yunoko and the Yukawa, a birthplace of fly fishing.

  • Kawaji Onsen Yakushi no Yu Campground

    Nikko, Tochigi · 0288-78-0229

    An auto campground beside Yakushi no Yu bath, with Yagan Railway trains passing overhead.
