  • Aburagen

    油源

    Dining in Nikko

    Ekiben (station-bento) shop in Nikko, founded in Ansei 6 of the Edo period, selling yuba chirashi bento and simmered young sansho leaves, with in-shop dining; closed Wednesdays.

  • Japanese-style Restaurant Kodaka

    和風レストラン こだか

    Dining in Nikko

    Japanese restaurant serving yuba gozen, soba, and salt-grilled river fish, about 3 minutes on foot from Kinugawa-Onsen Station, with irregular closing days.

  • Yuba Gozen Miyazaki

    ゆば御膳みやざき

    Dining in Nikko

    Yuba-gozen restaurant along the Kinugawa gorge serving the proprietress's handmade yuba dishes made with selected Nikko yuba.

  • Tsuruya

    つるや

    Dining in Nikko

    Japanese restaurant about 1 minute on foot from Kinugawa-Onsen Station serving the Kinuhime set of maitake tempura and mixed rice, plus yuba sashimi and soba, closed on Tuesdays.

  • Meshidokoro Ryuo

    めし処 龍王

    Dining in Nikko

    Japanese restaurant at the entrance to the Ryuokyo nature walking path serving ayu set meals, fresh yuba, yuba soba, homemade motsu stew, and shaved ice made with Nikko natural ice.

  • Japanese Cuisine Nikko Takaiya

    日本料理 日光 高井家

    Dining in Nikko

    Japanese restaurant in Nikko serving local yuba dishes in private rooms by an Edo-period moss garden near the World Heritage shrines and temples; founded in 1805, reservation only.
