Sights in Nogi, Tochigi

Nogi · Tochigi · 8 places

See

  • Crane Tochigi Riding Club

    Nogi, Tochigi

    Visitors can spend a relaxing holiday with horses, and beginners may come to watch.

  • Nogi Town Local History Museum

    Nogi, Tochigi · 0280-57-4188

    The museum covers Nogi Town history from the Jomon period to the present.

  • Kumano Shrine

    Nogi, Tochigi · 0280-57-4153

    Nowatari's guardian shrine holds a spring children's sasara lion dance.

  • Nirinso at Nogi Shrine

    Nogi, Tochigi · 0280-55-0208

    This colony of buttercup-family nirinso bears two coin-sized white flowers from mid-March to mid-April.

  • Nogi Shrine

    Nogi, Tochigi · 0280-55-0208

    It was founded about 1,600 years ago and has a prefecture-listed ginkgo and spring owls.

  • Nogi Town Brick Kiln and Nogi Hoffman Hall

    Nogi, Tochigi · 0280-33-6667

    This Hoffman ring kiln, the only one retaining its original form, became an Important Cultural Property in 1979.

  • Nogi Waterside School

    Nogi, Tochigi · 0280-57-4168

    Visitors can see Watarase basin plants up close and fireflies in mid-June.

  • Matsubara Bridge

    Nogi, Tochigi · 0282-23-3433

    This southernmost prefecture-managed Omoigawa bridge is a 694-meter Nielsen arch.
