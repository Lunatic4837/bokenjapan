Things to do in Oyama, Tochigi

Oyama · Tochigi · 12 places

Do

  • Omoigawa Cherry Embankment East of Hakuoh University Daigyoji Campus

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-22-9273

    About eighty pale-pink Omoigawa cherries form a tunnel on the levee below Kanko Bridge.

  • Mamada-himo Braiding Experience

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-45-0343

    Visitors braid an original strap from plant-dyed silk using traditional Mamada hand-braiding.

  • AOYA CAMPAGNE

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-39-7555

    The world's first anko bar, designed by Kengo Kuma, combining bean paste with Western ingredients.

  • Honba Yuki Tsumugi Fitting Experience (Oyama Craft Hall)

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-32-6477

    Visitors may wear Honba Yuki Tsumugi free, with kimono, obi, and dressing included.

  • Oyama City Tourism Association

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-32-6477

    An information base guiding visitors to Oyama sights, festivals, crafts, and restaurants.

  • Wakakoma Sake Brewery

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-37-0429

    Oyama's oldest sake brewery, founded in 1860, is a national tangible cultural property.

  • Sugita Sake Brewery (Yuto Masamune)

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-38-0005

    Oyama-brand sake brewery using Nikko groundwater and aging its sake for one year.

  • Sanpuku Brewery (Hage Oyaji no Kakushizake)

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-38-0003

    A quality-first sake brewery known for a crisp, dry junmai ginjo with light aroma.

  • Kobayashi Sake Brewery (Phoenix Gold Award)

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-37-0005

    Offers fragrant, clean, elegant unfiltered draft sake from Oyama's rice-growing district.

  • Nishibori Sake Brewery

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-45-0035

    Founded in 1872, this Oyama sake brewery holds a clear-tank brewing patent.

  • Omoi Bell

    Oyama, Tochigi · 0285-38-0201

    A bell telling time at Roadside Station Omoigawa, promoting Oyama as a handbell town.

  • Slow Life Resort Strawberry Village

    Oyama, Tochigi

    Visitors can enjoy year-round fruit picking, an all-you-can-eat restaurant, and a cafe.
