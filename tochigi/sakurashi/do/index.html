Things to do in Sakurashi, Tochigi

Sakurashi · Tochigi · 12 places

Do

  • Kamenoi Hotel Kitsuregawa

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-2822

    A hotel for enjoying Kitsuregawa Onsen, one of Japan's three beautiful-skin springs.

  • Senkin (Sakura Brand)

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-681-0011

    Sakura City's celebrated sake brewery, making award-winning Senkin from local water and rice.

  • Kitsuregawa Onsen

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-6627

    Said to be one of Japan's three beautiful-skin hot springs, rich in sulfur, salt, and iron.

  • Motoyu Onsen

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-3284

    A riverside public bath with indoor and rock-style outdoor baths, opening at 7 a.m.

  • Kitsuregawa Saotome Onsen

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-4126

    A sulfur spring that shifts from vivid green to milky white, constantly overflowing with fresh water.

  • Omaruyama Hotel (Kitsuregawa Onsen)

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-6688

    A local hotel at Kitsuregawa Onsen, ranked among Japan's three beautiful-skin hot springs.

  • Kitsuregawa Onsen Open-air Bath

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-5047

    A large free-flowing rock bath for about 50 people, filled with a concentrated tea-brown spring.

  • Shizuka Golf Club

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-3011

    This strategic 27-hole course has little elevation change, wide fairways, and a natural hot spring.

  • Kitsuregawa Family Campground

    Sakurashi, Tochigi

    A quiet nature campground intended for families and couples.

  • Onsen Pan Bakery

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-686-1858

    Bakery founded in 1941, famous for dense, chewy Onsen Pan devised from leftover dough around 1955.

  • Waterside Park (Joyful Blue Park)

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-681-1120

    Park beside Kitsuregawa Roadside Station offering water play, athletics, and barbecue.

  • Matsushima Onsen Otome no Yu

    Sakurashi, Tochigi · 028-682-2266

    A lukewarm free-flowing alkaline spring said to leave skin smooth.
