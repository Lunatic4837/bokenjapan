Things to do in Shimotsuke, Tochigi

Shimotsuke · Tochigi · 4 places

Do

  • Tenpyo Hill Park

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi · 0285-32-8907

    A large park of natural forest and historic sites, with about 500 cherry trees and cultural facilities.

  • Grimm Forest Grimm Hall

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    A German-themed woodland park whose Grimm Hall evokes Rechingen's town hall and holds Grimm exhibits and books.

  • Sugatagawa Amenity Park

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    More than 100 cherries along the Sugata River bloom beside rapeseed, with seasonal stalls and lighting.

  • Shimotsuke Kokubun Nunnery Site

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    About 600 meters east of Kokubunji, this imperial nunnery site, Todaiji-style without a pagoda, is a cherry-blossom park.
