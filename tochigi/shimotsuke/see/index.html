Sights in Shimotsuke, Tochigi

Shimotsuke · Tochigi · 11 places

See

  • Tochigi Prefectural Buried Cultural Properties Center

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi · 0285-44-8441

    Opened in 1991 to protect, research, preserve, and promote Tochigi's buried cultural properties.

  • Shimotsuke Kokubunji Temple Site

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    Built in 741 under Emperor Shomu's decree, this national temple site follows the same layout as Todaiji.

  • Shimotsuke Yakushiji Temple Site

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi · 0825-47-3121

    One of Japan's three ordination platforms, founded around the late seventh century and later renamed Ankoku-ji.

  • Shimotsuke Yakushiji (Former Ankoku-ji)

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    This temple, once Ankoku-ji, carries Shimotsuke Yakushiji's lineage and restored the name Yakushiji in 2018.

  • Monument to Priest Ganjin

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    A roughly two-meter memorial tower said to have been raised by Ganjin's disciples to honor their teacher.

  • Dokyo Mound

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    A city-designated mound in Ryuko-ji grounds, traditionally the reburial tomb of Nara priest Yuge no Dokyo.

  • Koganei Ichirizuka

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    A national historic site on the Nikko Kaido, these mounds mark 22 ri, about 88 kilometers, from Nihonbashi.

  • Shimotsuke City Shimotsuke Fudoki Hill Museum

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    Transferred to the city in 2015, it displays finds including the nationally designated Kabuzuka Kofun artifacts.

  • Yakushiji Hachimangu Shrine

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    Traditionally founded in 875, this shrine guarding Yakushiji has a worship hall and main hall rebuilt in 1662.

  • Two Loom-Shaped Haniwa from Kabuzuka Kofun

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    Japan's first two loom-shaped haniwa, from Kabuzuka Kofun, resemble the ground loom used for Yuki tsumugi.

  • Seiunzan Ryuko-ji Temple

    Shimotsuke, Tochigi

    Traditionally a Shimotsuke Yakushiji branch, it has a designated bronze Shaka and a shirakashi over 20 meters tall.
