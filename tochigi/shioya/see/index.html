Sights in Shioya, Tochigi

Shioya · Tochigi · 8 places

See

  • Higashigoya Lake

    Shioya, Tochigi

    From the March opening day, many anglers visit to fish for large rainbow trout.

  • Funyu Kabuki Village Theater Teahouse

    Shioya, Tochigi

    A popular theater performed by the Misaki Tetsuya school and successive Misaki leaders.

  • Higashiarakawa Dam

    Shioya, Tochigi

    A 70-meter concrete gravity dam completed in 1990 for flood control, water supply, and power.

  • Abandoned Tobu Yaita Line

    Shioya, Tochigi

    The disused Tobu Yaita Line continues as a pedestrian path with traces of old stations.

  • Kurosawa Bridge

    Shioya, Tochigi · 0287-44-2185

    Built in 1983 as a Higashi-Arakawa Dam replacement road, this 165-meter bridge has three continuous steel spans.

  • Sanuki Stone Buddha

    Shioya, Tochigi · 0287-45-2211

    An 18-meter seated Dainichi Nyorai is carved into a 64-meter cliff along the Kinugawa River.

  • Shoninzawa Spring

    Shioya, Tochigi · 0287-45-2211

    This Mount Takahara spring, a Hundred Famous Waters site, stays about 11°C and never freezes.

  • Wake Memorial Hall

    Shioya, Tochigi · 0287-45-1010

    An art museum in Tamanyu, Shioya, converted from Wake Shiro's family stone storehouse and showing about 100 works.
