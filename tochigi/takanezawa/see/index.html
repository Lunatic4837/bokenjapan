Takanezawa · Tochigi · 5 places

See

  • Utsunomiya Garden Golf Club

    Takanezawa, Tochigi

  • Saginoya Komorebi Forest

    Takanezawa, Tochigi · 028-680-2080

    Volunteers tend this nature forest, which opens a cyclocross course in winter.

  • Chokkura Plaza

    Takanezawa, Tochigi · 028-675-0004

  • Utsu Kyumeigan

    Takanezawa, Tochigi · 028-675-0361

    This is the birthplace of children's medicine Utsu Kyumeigan, with historic halls.

  • Azumi Shrine

    Takanezawa, Tochigi · 028-675-0357

    It has Kanto's largest torii, enshrines safe childbirth, and welcomes many motorcyclists.
