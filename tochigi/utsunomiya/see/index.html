Sights in Utsunomiya, Tochigi

See

  • Oya Museum

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-652-1232

    A museum on Oya stone mining, highlighted by vast underground quarry ruins.

  • Utsunomiya City Guide Association

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi

    Reservation-only volunteer guides offer model courses and accept time and place requests.

  • Matsugamine Catholic Church

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-635-0405

    A modern Romanesque Oya-stone church, a nationally registered tangible cultural property.

  • Peace Kannon

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-632-2445

    A roughly 27-meter Kannon hand-carved into an Oya stone wall for peace and war dead.

  • Oya-ji Temple (Oya Kannon)

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-652-0128

    An unusual cave temple in Oya housing Japan's oldest stone Buddha, the Oya Kannon.

  • Former Shinohara Family Residence

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-624-2200

    This 1895 merchant house, a nationally designated Important Cultural Property, remains closed until April 19, 2027.

  • Tobiyama Castle Ruins (National Historic Site)

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-632-2764

    Late Kamakura castle ruins, a national historic site said to have been built by Haga Takatoshi.

  • Ujiie Ohashi Tourist Fish Weir

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi

    Visitors can catch sweetfish by hand at this Kinugawa weir in northern Utsunomiya.

  • Oya Connect (Oya Sightseeing Hub)

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-652-4800

    An Oya sightseeing hub using the nationally registered former Oya Public Hall and a visitor center.

  • Cherry, Mango, and Peach Picking (Komaki-en)

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi

    An orchard where visitors can pick various cherries, peaches, and ripe mangoes.

  • Hachimanyama Park

    Utsunomiya, Tochigi · 028-624-0642

    Opened in 1927, this central park has Utsunomiya Tower, playgrounds, animals, and flowers.
