Things to do in Yaita, Tochigi

Yaita · Tochigi · 6 places

Do

  • Yaita Onsen Glamping Yulampi

    Yaita, Tochigi · 0287-43-8800

    Private glamping units, each with a free-flowing open-air hot-spring bath available anytime.

  • Takahara Mountain Station

    Yaita, Tochigi · 0287-43-1515

    It is a base for Happogahara and Mount Takahara hiking and waterfall tours.

  • Mountain Station Takahara Star Light Camp Base

    Yaita, Tochigi · 0287-43-1515

    A 1,049-meter campsite with air clear enough to see the Milky Way year-round.

  • Citizens' Forest Campground (Temporarily Closed)

    Yaita, Tochigi · 0287-43-0479

    Visitors can enjoy camping, hiking, nature observation, outdoor cooking, and campfires.

  • Morito Sake Brewery

    Yaita, Tochigi · 0287-43-0411

    One of Yaita's two major breweries, it brews balanced sake that does not grow tiresome.

  • Tomikawa Sake Brewery

    Yaita, Tochigi · 0287-48-1510

    Founded in 1913, this brewery produces the sakes Chuai and Fumikawa.
