Things to do in Funahashi, Toyama

Funahashi, Toyama: things to do, food and stays · Toyama · 5 places

Do

  • Funahashi-Tateyama Natural Hot Spring Yumegokochi

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Hot-spring facility with garden open-air baths, towels and robes provided and the Ikkyuan restaurant. Open 10:00-24:00 on weekdays and 9:00-24:00 on weekends and holidays; closed Thursdays (open if a holiday).

  • Orange Park Funahashi (Kyotsubogawa Riverside Park)

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Park in the middle of the village with a lawn, composite and wooden play equipment and a basketball court; cherry trees line the Kyotsubo River through it.

  • Funahashi Village Library

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Village library built into the Etchu-Funahashi Station building.

  • Funahashi Village Children's Park

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Public children's park listed among the village's city parks.

  • Funahashi Festival (Funahashi Matsuri)

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Village festival held on the first Saturday of August.
