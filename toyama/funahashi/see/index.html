Sights in Funahashi, Toyama

Funahashi, Toyama: things to do, food and stays · Toyama · 4 places

See

  • Muryo-ji Temple

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Temple in Funahashi, noted among the village sights for its lotus flowers.

  • Etchu-Funahashi Station

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Station on the Toyama Chiho Railway Main Line; the village library is built into the station building and Funahashi Post Office stands opposite.

  • Kyotsubo River Cherry Blossom Row

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Row of cherry trees along the Kyotsubo River; in spring 2009 the village road beside the junior high school was closed to cars in the evenings for cherry viewing.

  • Takeuchi Tenjindo Kofun

    Funahashi, Toyama

    Ancient burial mound (kofun) in the Takeuchi district, listed among the village historic sites.
