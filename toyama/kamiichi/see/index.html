Sights in Kamiichi, Toyama

Kamiichi, Toyama: things to do, food and stays · Toyama · 14 places

See

  • Shakuizumi-ji Cylindrical Water-Distributor

    Kamiichi, Toyama, Toyama

    Historic 9.3-meter cylindrical weir built in 1954 to divide irrigation water accurately.

  • Medicinal Plant Research Center

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-472-0801, Toyama

    Garden and exhibition hall with about 1,000 medicinal plants and 170 crude-drug specimens.

  • Oiwa River Waterside Park

    Kamiichi, Toyama, Toyama

    Riverside park in Oiwa offering cherry-blossom viewing and night illumination in early April.

  • Sengan Kei

    Kamiichi, Toyama, Toyama

    Scenic gorge along Oiwa River featuring a series of waterfalls and distinctive rock formations.

  • Tsurugi Youth Travel Village (Baba Island Campground)

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-472-3080, Toyama

    Campground at the Tsurugi trailhead in Baba Island, Kamiichi, with tent sites, lawn area, cooking facilities and restrooms.

  • Iori Bridge and Iori Bridge Left-Bank Plaza

    Kamiichi, Toyama, Toyama

    Bridge spanning the Hayatsuki River nine kilometres before Baba Island, offering a panorama of Tsurugi, Okudainichi and Dainichi peaks.

  • Uegyoden Central Park

    Kamiichi, Toyama, Toyama

    Neighborhood park in Uegyoden, Kamiichi, with playground equipment, restrooms and cherry blossoms in early to mid-April.

  • Baba Island Forest Therapy Road, Nakayama Walk

    Kamiichi, Toyama, Toyama

    Forest-therapy walking course from the Tsurugi trailhead, best for autumn foliage from mid-October to early November.

  • Yumi no Sato History and Culture Museum

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-473-8100, Toyama

    Museum in Tate, Kamiichi, displaying local artefacts from the Paleolithic period to the Middle Ages.

  • Nishida Art Museum

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-472-4352, Toyama

    Art museum in Kamiichi with ceramics from the Orient and a notable collection of Russian icons and Central-European paintings.

  • Kobo Daishi Spring (Gomado)

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-472-2505, Toyama

    Spring near Gomado, Kamiichi, said to improve intellect, with a viewpoint overlooking Toyama Bay and the Toyama Plain.

  • Ana-no-Tani Spring

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-472-4711, Toyama

    Renowned spring in Kurokawa, Kamiichi, reached by 108 stone steps and believed to have healing properties.

  • Tateyama-ji Temple

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-472-0699, Toyama

    Soto Zen temple founded in 1370 with a 400-year-old cedar avenue.

  • Nisseki-ji Temple

    Kamiichi, Toyama · 076-472-2301, Toyama

    Temple known for cliff-carved Buddha and six-fall ascetic training.
