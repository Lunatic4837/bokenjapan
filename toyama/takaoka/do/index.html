Things to do in Takaoka, Toyama

Takaoka, Toyama: things to do, food and stays · Toyama · 8 places

Do

  • Roadside Station Ameharu

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-53-5661, Toyama

    Roadside station on the Ameharu coast with cafe and observation decks overlooking the Tateyama mountains.

  • Roadside Station Manyo Village Takaoka

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-30-0011, Toyama

    Roadside station on National Route 8 near Takaoka Interchange with food court and extensive local products.

  • Ameharu Shotaehama Campground

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-44-6200, Toyama

    Campground beside a long sandy beach selected among Japan’s 100 beaches, with views of Tateyama peaks.

  • Nosa Casting Experience

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-63-0001, Toyama

    Workshop in Takaoka Office Park where visitors cast tin objects using traditional sand-mold methods.

  • Otera Tin Jewelry Workshop

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-25-1911, Toyama

    Jewelry-making workshop in Kanaya-machi where participants craft original tin accessories from nine designs.

  • Yodamari-no-Yu

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-27-8800, Toyama

    Large public bathhouse in Takaoka offering multiple baths, daily sauna events and yoga classes.

  • Momentum Factory Orii

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-23-9685, Toyama

    Workshop in Nagae offering patina coloring experiences on copper coasters and trays on Saturdays.

  • Takaoka Copperware Exhibition Hall

    Takaoka, Toyama · 0766-30-0850, Toyama

    Showroom and shop inside Roadside Station Manyo Village Takaoka displaying traditional Takaoka copperware.
