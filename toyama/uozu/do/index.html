Things to do in Uozu, Toyama

Uozu, Toyama: things to do, food and stays · Toyama · 10 places

Do

  • Kintaro Onsen

    Uozu, Toyama · 0765-24-1220, Toyama

    Hot spring in Uozu with chloride and sulfur waters, facilities include day-use baths and lodging.

  • Katagai Yamanomori Campground

    Uozu, Toyama · 0765-32-7755, Toyama

    Campground in Uozu beside the Katagai River with auto sites, fishing and water play.

  • UO! JAZZ 2026 (Jantokoi Uozu Festival)

    Uozu, Toyama · 0765-22-1200, Toyama

    Annual summer festival held in Uozu each year.

  • Uozu Shuzo

    Uozu, Toyama · 0765-22-0134, Toyama

    Sake brewery in Uozu founded in 1925 producing sake named Hokuyo and Shinkiro.

  • Mochitsuki Experience (Genshichi)

    Uozu, Toyama · 0120-224-857, Toyama

    Hands-on mochi pounding using traditional mortar and pestle at Genshichi Honten in Uozu.

  • ZAXFOX Confectionery Laboratory

    Uozu, Toyama

    ZAXFOX Confectionery Laboratory is a pastry workshop creating fresh sweets overlooking the Tateyama mountains.

  • Aizome Experience Aiya

    Uozu, Toyama

    Aizome Experience Aiya offers indigo dyeing using a traditional natural-fermentation method in Uozu City.

  • Uozu Miracle Rental Cycles

    Uozu, Toyama

    Uozu Miracle Rental Cycles provides bicycles for exploring coastal and countryside routes around Uozu City.

  • Mukaisanchi Farm Blueberry Picking Garden

    Uozu, Toyama

    A blueberry picking farm in Uozu where visitors can harvest berries from late June to August.

  • Aizome-ya aiya

    Uozu, Toyama

    Aizome-ya aiya cultivates indigo plants and conducts natural indigo dyeing and workshops.
