Things to do in Mihama, Wakayama

Mihama, Wakayama: things to do, food and stays · Wakayama · 3 places

Do

  • Saori Weaving at Saori Kobo Saishoku

    Mihama, Wakayama · 0738-62-2004

    Weave coasters, table runners, place mats or scarves freely by hand in an ocean-view studio in Mihama; no experience needed.

  • Driftwood Interior Goods Workshop at Atelier Bianco

    Mihama, Wakayama · 0738-20-5093

    Choose driftwood from the beach in front of the studio and turn it into a clock or hook-style interior piece.

  • Travel Postcard Art at Atelier Bianco

    Mihama, Wakayama · 0738-20-5093

    Make a one-off postcard from tickets, photos and brochures from your trip; take home a copy and send the original to someone special.
