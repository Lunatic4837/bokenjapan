  • Michi no Eki Asahimachi

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-85-0623

    Asahi is known as the birthplace of bagless-grown Fuji apples. From August to around February the farm-direct corner stocks a changing range of seasonal apples, plus a wide choice of freshly picked vegetables.

  • Wago Midori no Eki

    産直「和合みどりの駅」（秋・冬のみ）

    Asahi, Yamagata

    A seasonal direct-sales stall run by farmer colleagues, open only in autumn and winter. It mainly sells apples, pears and peaches, plus figs, taro and daikon when available.

  • Oishii Dewa Direct Sales Market

    産直「おいしい出羽産直市場」

    Asahi, Yamagata

    A direct-sales market in Asahi selling apples, La France pears, cherries, mountain vegetables, handmade wooden goods, cold-dried rice cakes, pickles and seasonal vegetables.
