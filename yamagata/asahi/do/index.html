Things to do in Asahi, Yamagata

Asahi · Yamagata · 8 places

Do

  • Asahimachi Wine Castle

    ワイナリー「朝日町ワイン城」

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-68-2611

    Winery based on Yamagata’s climate and long experience, using modern brewing techniques and a one-hectare vineyard of European wine-grape varieties.

  • Ringo Onsen

    りんご温泉

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-67-7888

    Day-use onsen with strongly alkaline water, uncommon in Tohoku, and a seasonal outdoor bath.

  • Sawaradaira Rice Terraces

    椹平の棚田

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-67-2114

    Terraced paddies on a gentle fan-shaped slope, with reflective water in spring, green fields in summer, golden rice and traditional stake-drying in autumn, and snow in winter. Vehicles cannot pass during snowfall.

  • Imogawa Onsen

    いもがわ温泉

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-67-7249

    Day-trip onsen along the clear Asahi River, with free-flowing spring water said to help skin troubles such as heat rash. The owner, who also runs a fishery, serves seafood rice bowls.

  • Mogami River Canoe Land

    最上川カヌーランド

    Asahi, Yamagata · 090-3367-5367

    Iogawa Gorge, once a difficult section for boat transport on the Mogami River, is now used for canoeing. From April to around November, canoeists use launch points at Tamanai and Yukitani.

  • Hachimitsu no Mori Candle Workshop

    蜜ロウソク工房「ハチ蜜の森キャンドル」

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-67-3260

    Beeswax candle making and beekeeping experiences are offered year-round by reservation, including on weekdays. Off-site classes are also available for children's group events.

  • Asahi Town Tourist Information Center

    朝日町観光案内所

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-85-0644

    Inside Roadside Station Asahimachi, pamphlets on events and facilities are available and tourism association staff provide guidance. Roadside-station stamp-rally stamps can be collected here.

  • Asahi Shizenkan Ski Area and Cottages

    Asahi自然観/スキー場・コテージ

    Asahi, Yamagata

    Green-season options include barbecues, hiking, trekking, and stream fishing; winter adds skiing and snowboarding at the snow park. The Air Shrine stands in beech forest, and the hotel closed at the end of March 2025.
