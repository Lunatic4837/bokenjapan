Sights in Asahi, Yamagata

Asahi · Yamagata · 3 places

See

  • Kuki Shrine (Air Shrine)

    Asahi, Yamagata · 0237-67-2134

    The only shrine in the world whose object of worship is air itself. It was built in July 1990 to give thanks for the blessings of air and to pass a clean Earth on to future generations; this environmental monument stands in a beech forest.

  • Ostrich Exhibition Field

    オーストリッチ展示圃

    Asahi, Yamagata

    Visitors can meet ostriches raised in Asahi, a town known for them.

  • Satake Family Residence

    佐竹家住宅

    Asahi, Yamagata

    A preserved upper-class farmhouse of a village headman who administered an exclave of the Matsuyama Domain Sakai family. It is a National Important Cultural Property, and the Satake family still lives there.
