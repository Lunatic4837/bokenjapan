Things to do in Funagata, Yamagata

Funagata · Yamagata · 9 places

Do

  • Ayu Park Funagata

    アユパークふながた(河川公園)

    Funagata, Yamagata · 0233-32-2111

    This riverside park has a large fountain, an imoni plaza, an ayu-shaped channel, rope climbing, and spring play equipment. Shallow areas suit small children, and ayu catching is offered in the channel.

  • Ayukko Village

    あゆっこ村

    Funagata, Yamagata · 0233-32-3655

    Offers 13 cottages, bungalows, and a ground-tent campsite with space for about eight sites, plus four all-weather tennis courts, two gateball courts, a ground golf course, and a children's play plaza.

  • Funagata Town History and Folklore Museum

    舟形町歴史民俗資料館

    Funagata, Yamagata · 0233-32-3495

    Collects, preserves, and displays a replica of the National Treasure clay figurine Jomon no Megami, plus many old documents, folk tools, and folklore materials.

  • Funagata Wakaayu Onsen

    舟形若あゆ温泉

    Funagata, Yamagata · 0233-32-3655

    This onsen emerged in August 1992 and has a large sweetfish sculpture at the entrance. The water contains salt, sodium, and magnesium, and the open-air bath overlooks the town toward Mount Gassan and Hayama.

  • Kenmin Golf Course

    県民ゴルフ場

    Funagata, Yamagata · 0233-32-3344

    Eighteen-hole hilly course that uses the land's ups and downs. On clear days, fresh greenery and autumn foliage can be seen.

  • Saruhaneyama Park

    猿羽根山公園

    Funagata, Yamagata · 0233-32-0844

    Seasonal flowers and scenery fill this nature-rich park. Saruhaneyama Jizoson is associated with matchmaking and fertility, and the grounds include a soba restaurant and places to learn history.

  • Kunpukama Pottery Experience

    薫風窯 陶芸体験

    Funagata, Yamagata · 0233-32-0660

    Pottery experience using only clay from Funagata, where the national treasure dogu Jomon no Megami was excavated. Hand-building and electric-wheel courses cover cups, plates, nameplates, and dogu replicas.

  • Activity spot in Funagata

    ブナの実塾

    Romaji: Buna no Mi Juku

    Funagata, Yamagata

    Said to have been built about 160 years ago as a villa for a sake brewer living in Funagata. The facility can be used for day visits.

  • Tekuramori Wetland

    手倉森湿原

    Funagata, Yamagata

    Walk 30 minutes on a paved forest road, then about 10 minutes on a side path, to reach a wetland hidden in the forest. Endangered hatcho dragonflies, tokiso, kakiran, and other plants grow there.
