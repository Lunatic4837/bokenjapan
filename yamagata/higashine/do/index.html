Do in Higashine

Higashine · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • 山形空港

    Yamagata Airport

    Higashine, Yamagata

    Yamagata's air gateway, nicknamed Oishii Yamagata Airport. The name reflects the idea that the food, scenery, festivals, and hot springs are all delicious.

  • (有)文四郎麩

    Bunshiro Fu

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-42-0117

    Long-established fu maker, one of 400 Yamagata companies operating for over 100 years. Founded in the Bunkyu era, it still makes traditional fu with Rokuda Water in a Japanese-style shop.

  • ひがしねあそびあランド

    Higashineasobia Land

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-43-5551

    Park in Higashine, Yamagata.

  • 堂ノ前公園

    Donomae Park

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-42-1111

    Urban park east of Higashine Onsen, at the foot of Hebiyama, developed since 1972. About 200 cherry trees and other plants line the path around the pond; an observatory overlooks the Murayama Basin.

  • 黒伏高原スノーパーク ジャングルジャングル

    Kurobushi Plateau Snow Park Jungle Jungle

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-41-5555

    34-hectare ski area facing the cliffs of Mount Kurobushi, with terrain for beginners through advanced. Lessons and rentals are available, snowboarding is allowed on all slopes, and day lift tickets are free through sixth grade.

  • 東の杜

    Higashi no Mori

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-48-7211

    Local history museum in Higashine, Yamagata.

  • 滝口観光果樹園

    Takiguchi Tourist Orchard

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-44-2881

    Seasonal fruit picking runs from cherry season and includes cherries, peaches, grapes, and apples. The shop also sells fruit, homemade processed goods, fruit wine, and gift La France pears.

  • 神町観光果樹園

    Jinmachi Tourist Orchard

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-48-1888

    Fruit is grown with organic fertilizer. Cherry and apple picking are offered, and gift La France pears are also sold.

  • 阿部果樹園

    Abe Kajuen

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-47-1827

    Fruit-picking farm in Higashine, Yamagata.

  • 小山田マルサン果樹園

    Oyamada Marusan Orchard

    Higashine, Yamagata · 0237-42-3248

    Orchard nearest Sakuranbo Higashine Station. It practices low-pesticide cultivation.
