  • Yamagata Airport

  • Higashine Great Zelkova

    東根の大ケヤキ

    Higashine, Yamagata

    A National Special Natural Monument zelkova stands in Higashine Elementary School grounds. Said to exceed 1,500 years and planted in Higashine Castle in 1347, it is 28 m tall with a 16 m eye-level girth.

  • Lake Pia Shiromizu

    レークピア白水

    Higashine, Yamagata

    A leisure area around a dam built for flood control and irrigation, with seven plazas including a constellation square, a water square, a viewing square, sports grounds, and a stream square with a campground.

  • Osanagiyama

    若木山

    Higashine, Yamagata

    A low mountain in central Jinmachi has a maintained trail that children can climb. The south-side air-raid shelter is closed but opened once every three years, and the park has fitness equipment.

  • Mount Kurobushi

    黒伏山

    Higashine, Yamagata

    A mountain with a cliff about 300 m high. Its scenery changes with the seasons, particularly during autumn foliage.

  • Mountain in Higashine

    大森山/磨崖仏

    Higashine, Yamagata

    Said to be the prefecture's oldest cliff-carved Buddha, dated to the late Kamakura period. The rock is 5 m high, about 6.5 m wide and 3.5 m thick, with line-carved Buddha images.

  • Funagata Range (Mount Gosho)

    船形連峰(御所山)

    Higashine, Yamagata

    Centered on Mount Gosho, this range spans Miyagi and Yamagata. Higashine climbs include the Kannonji course, with Yanagisawa Hut mid-slope, a summit shelter, spring alpine plants, and autumn foliage.
