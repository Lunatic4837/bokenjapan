Sights in Iide, Yamagata

Iide · Yamagata · 8 places

See

  • Iide Michi-no-Eki

    Iide, Yamagata · 0238-86-3939

    A products hall on Route 113, midway between the Sea of Japan and the Pacific, selling local souvenirs, folk crafts, sake and fresh vegetables and fruit. The restaurant is popular for its Yonezawa beef bowl and Yonezawa ramen.

  • Shirakawa Dam Kogan Park

    Iide, Yamagata

    This is a park around Lake Shirakawa formed by Shirakawa Dam.

  • Donden-daira Snow Park

    Iide, Yamagata · 0238-86-2411

    A snow park with snowmobile rides, thrilling banana-boat runs and a giant snow slide, plus snowshoe walks and target-throwing games; guided snowmobile forest rides are available for an extra fee.

  • Shirakawa Lake Submerged Forest

    白川湖の水没林

    Iide, Yamagata

    For two months in spring, snowmelt fills Shirakawa Lake and willows seem to grow out of the water. From Shirakawa Dam Lakeside Park, early spring looks white and the new-leaf season looks green.

  • Iide Donden-daira Lily Garden

    いいでどんでん平ゆり園

    Iide, Yamagata

    More than 500,000 lilies of many varieties grow here, and a lily festival runs from mid-June to mid-July. The grounds also have seasonal flowers, trees, and a large slide; leashed pets are allowed.

  • Shirakawa Dam

    白川ダム

    Iide, Yamagata

    A rockfill dam completed in 1981 and standing 66 meters high. Visitors can view the exhibition room, closed in winter, and the crests of the dam and spillway.

  • Hagyu Kannon

    大行院 瑞雲寺（真言宗）/ 置賜三十三観音 第11番 萩生観音

    Iide, Yamagata

    A Shingon temple and the 11th stop of the Okitama Thirty-three Kannon pilgrimage. It enshrines a wooden seated Eleven-faced Kannon, and a festival is held on July 10.

  • Nagameyama Mizubasho Colony

    眺山水芭蕉群生地

    Iide, Yamagata

    Mizubasho grow wild in a wetland about 500 m long. A walking path follows a small stream, with peak viewing from early April to early May.
