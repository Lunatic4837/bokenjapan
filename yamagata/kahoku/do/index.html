Things to do in Kahoku, Yamagata

Kahoku · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • Kahoku Town Children's Zoo

    河北町児童動物園

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-73-5162

    More than 30 kinds of animals are kept, including a toco toucan, hedgehogs, deer, ponies, monkeys, goats, sheep, rabbits, and owls. Open year-round, 24 hours, with free admission.

  • Kahoku Central Park (Imoko Train)

    河北中央公園（いもこ列車）

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-73-2111

    Set near the center of Kahoku on about 5 hectares, with water and land field athletics, wooden play equipment, and tennis courts.

  • Benibana Onsen Hinano-yu

    べに花温泉ひなの湯

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-71-0333

    Opened in June 1999, with a sauna and access for people with disabilities. Restaurant Yuratei offers meals and takeout, and neighboring Hinano-yado has overnight stays with two meals.

  • Benibana Museum / Former Horigome Residence

    紅花資料館/旧堀米邸

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-73-3500

    The former Horigome Residence of a wealthy safflower merchant was restored and opened as a museum in May 1984. Edo-period Mogami safflower petals were made into flower cakes and sent to Kyoto.

  • Kahoku Roadside Station Buratto Pia

    道の駅 河北 ～ ぶらっとぴあ ～

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-72-2870

    On National Route 287 at Kahoku's eastern entrance, facing the Mogami River. The first floor offers tourist information and sells cold meat soba, slippers, local sake, and light meals.

  • Kahoku Town General Exchange Center Sahato Benibana

    河北町総合交流センター サハトべに花

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-72-6555

    Named Sahato, Arabic for plaza, because safflower is said to originate in Egypt. The town is known as the home of hina dolls and safflower.

  • Mogami River Green Park

    最上川グリーンパーク

    Kahoku, Yamagata

    Near Yachi Bridge on the Mogami River, with a golf course and several plazas. Wanpaku Plaza sits just below Roadside Station Buratto Pia, along with Fureai Plaza, Yasuragi Plaza, and a multipurpose sports plaza.

  • Ebitsuru Onsen

    海老鶴温泉

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-72-5151

    Hot spring linked to a Kahoku legend, just below the embankment after Kahoku Bridge across the Mogami River. The site once farmed edible tilapia, an introduced fish, and photos are displayed inside.

  • Sun Sport Land Kahoku

    サン・スポーツランド河北、（楽天イーグルスべに花スタジアム河北）

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-72-6123

    Baseball field called Rakuten Eagles Benibana Stadium Kahoku, four tennis courts, and Fureai Plaza on a 45,910-square-meter site. Spectator capacity is 5,000, and the facilities are used from April to November.

  • Kahoku Municipal Pool

    河北町民プール

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-85-1858

    Indoor pool for aqua walking that uses water resistance, with jets for the back, waist, and shoulders expected to promote circulation. A training room has treadmills and fitness bikes adjusted to each person's fitness.
