See

  • Benibana Museum (Former Horigome Residence)

    Kahoku, Yamagata · 0237-73-3500

    The restored residence of the Horigome family, wealthy safflower merchants, opened as a safflower museum in 1984. In the Edo period the local Mogami safflower was made into dried petal cakes and shipped all the way to Kyoto.

  • Enichizan Jigen-in Temple

    恵日山 慈眼院（天台宗）/ 最上三十三観音 第18番 岩木観音

    Kahoku, Yamagata

    A Tendai temple, the 18th Mogami Thirty-three Kannon stop, known as Iwaki Kannon. A Jibo Kannon for fertility and safe childbirth stands by the main image, with a hand pump for well water.
