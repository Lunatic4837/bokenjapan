Day to day

  • Takeda Winery

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata · 023-672-0040

    A winery whose 15-hectare south-facing vineyard is farmed with reduced pesticides and no chemical fertiliser, following natural cycles. Its wines are made with long experience and high technical skill.

  • Kaminoyama Onsen Tourist Information Center

    かみのやま温泉観光案内所

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Opened on 25 April 2018 in front of JR Kaminoyama Onsen Station to share city sightseeing and product information. Tenants include Yamagata Wine Cave and 85cafe, due to open on 5 August 2026.

  • Hayama Public Bath

    葉山公衆浴場

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A public bath on a hill in Hayama Onsen, with glass windows on two sides and an open-feeling outdoor bath.

  • Yamagata Wine Cave

    山形ワインカーヴ

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A shop in front of Kaminoyama Onsen Station selling and serving wines from Yamagata wineries, with local snacks for pairing.
