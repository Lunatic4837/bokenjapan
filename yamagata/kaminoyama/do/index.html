Things to do in Kaminoyama, Yamagata

Kaminoyama · Yamagata · 12 places

Do

  • Kaminoyama Onsen

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata · 023-672-0839

    https://yamagatakanko.com/en/attractions/detail_2533.html

  • Lina World

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata · 023-672-1614

    One of the largest amusement parks in Tohoku, about 10 minutes by car from Kaminoyama Onsen and 20 minutes from Zao Onsen, with thrill rides such as a log flume and pirate ship as well as Sanrio-themed attractions.

  • Zao Raiza World Round-Trip Lift Ticket

    【樹氷鑑賞にオススメ】蔵王ライザワールド往復リフト券

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A round-trip lift ticket at Zao Raiza World on Zao Bodaira Plateau for viewing winter juhyo on Maries' fir. Riders take two connecting lifts to the ice-tree field and descend between 13:00 and 14:30.

  • Yamagata Cherry Land (Kaminoyama Sightseeing Fruit Orchard)

    山形チェリーランド（上山観光フルーツ園）

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A hilltop sightseeing orchard in Kaminoyama where visitors pick seasonal fruit, including cherries, plums, peaches, grapes, apples, and Japanese and European pears. The shop offers tastings and shipping.

  • Zao Raiza World

    蔵王ライザワールド

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A family ski area near a rime-ice forest, with a wide 570 m course plus beginner, intermediate, and advanced runs. Weekend sledding beside the restaurant is planned when snow allows.

  • Kimura Tourist Orchard

    木村観光果樹園

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Visitors can pick cherries, grapes, and apples in season. The orchard also grows and sells those fruits, along with La France pears.

  • Woody Farm & Winery

    ウッディファーム＆ワイナリー

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A domaine winery on the Zao foothills grows nine grape varieties and handles winemaking and bottling. Wine tastings need no reservation, while winery tours with tastings are offered by reservation.

  • DROP

    DROP

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A winery opened in town in 2023 by a grower who began cultivating wine grapes in 2019. An attached wine stand lets visitors taste natural wines expressing the vineyard's four seasons.

  • Saito Tourist Fruit Orchard

    斎藤観光果樹園

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Cherries and apples can be picked on a spacious flat orchard accessible to older visitors and people with limited mobility. Cherries, apples, and European pears are also sold, and groups are welcome.

  • ZAO Tairagura

    ZAOたいらぐら

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A comprehensive exchange facility at Zao Bodaira with a training room used for camps in many sports. It also has a farm-produce direct-sales shop, an experience room, and a cooking practice room.

  • Himawari Farm

    ひまわり農園

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Cherries can be picked, and La France pears are also grown. Cherries, apples, and European pears are sold, with a shop for purchases after picking.

  • Zao Sarukura Ski Area

    蔵王猿倉スキー場

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Snowboarding is prohibited at this ski area, close to downtown Kaminoyama and Yamagata City. Practice courses, a sled run with free sled rental, and ski gear rentals are available.
