  • Kaminoyama Castle Local Museum

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata · 023-673-3660

    Kaminoyama Castle, also called Tsukioka Castle, is said to have been built in 1535. It was the southernmost stronghold of the Mogami clan and saw fighting with the Date and Uesugi of Yonezawa; today it houses the local history museum.

  • Takahashi Fruits Land HATAKE STYLE

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata · 023-673-4706

    An orchard growing seasonal fruit 100% organically, with greenhouse cherries for early cherry picking. The adjoining HATAKE STYLE café serves parfaits, sundaes and soft-serve.

  • Zao Bodaira Highlands

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata · 023-672-1111

    https://yamagatakanko.com/en/attractions/detail_346.html

  • Narage-juku (Ushu Kaido Post Town)

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata · 023-672-0839

    A post town on the Ushu Kaido that once bustled with daimyo processions, with a main inn, sub-inn and lodging houses. Old houses and stone bridges line the river, and some of the old houses are open to visitors.

  • Kaminoyama Domain Samurai Residences

    上山藩　武家屋敷

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Four thatched samurai houses of the Kaminoyama domain, about 200 years old, stand in a row. The Miwa interior is open to visitors; Morimoto, Yamada, and the former Sogabe show exteriors and gardens.

  • Zao Katta Lift

    蔵王刈田リフト

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A lift to Okama, a crater lake among Mount Katta, Mount Kumano, and Mount Goshiki. Its emerald water changes color with the light; at 10 meters the water is about 2°C and warms with depth.

  • Shimo-Oyu Communal Bath

    下大湯共同浴場

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A communal bath in Kaminoyama Onsen, opened in 1624 by domain lord Matsudaira and said to have been used by Zen master Takuan. Hot and lukewarm tubs remain, and 2024 marked 400 years since opening.

  • Hanasakiyama Observatory

    花咲山展望台（恋人の聖地）

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A viewpoint on the Hayama course near Hayama Onsen, with views of the town and the Zao range through the seasons. Hydrangeas bloom in summer, and the walk from the trailhead takes about 30 minutes.

  • Harusame Hermitage

    春雨庵

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Priest Takuan, known for takuan pickles, spent three years here after exile to Kaminoyama in 1629. The restored building is a prefectural Important Cultural Property, with the Harusame well and Choutei tea room.

  • Tsukioka Park

    月岡公園

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A cherry-blossom park in Kaminoyama centered on Kaminoyama Castle, with about 80 trees including old Somei-yoshino, weeping cherries, and a few Keio-zakura. A nearby foot bath looks toward the Zao range.

  • Former Ogata Family Residence

    旧尾形家住宅

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Built around the mid-17th century for the Ogata family, hereditary village headmen, this farmhouse uses nail-free construction and is a National Important Cultural Property. It is temporarily closed.

  • Local sight in Kaminoyama

    ヴェンテンガルテン　菜の花畑

    Romaji: Ventengarten Rapeseed Flower Field

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A citizen farm near JR Mokichi Memorial Hall Station has a heart-shaped rapeseed field, heart-shaped photo frames, and views of the Zao range. Flowers usually bloom from late April to early May.

  • Zao Senninzawa Ice Garden Icefall

    蔵王　仙人沢アイスガーデンの氷瀑

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Ice formations appear only in midwinter at Senninzawa on Bodaira Plateau in Kaminoyama. The main icefall is about 30 meters high and tens of meters wide, seen from January to mid-March.

  • Kurikawa Inari Shrine

    栗川稲荷神社

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    It enshrines Ukanomitama-no-Mikoto, guardian deity of the Fujii Matsudaira family and long worshipped by successive castle lords. Donated torii form a tunnel-like approach; the annual festival is held on April 23.

  • Kurort Hayama Course

    クアオルト　葉山コース

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Locals have planted cherries, autumn leaves, and hydrangeas along this walking course near the onsen district since 2007. Hanasakiyama Observatory, designated a Lover's Sanctuary, overlooks the town and the Zao range.

  • Tsukioka Shrine

    月岡神社

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    It enshrines members of the Matsudaira domain family. The annual festival is on April 25, a special festival is held with Hachiman Shrine in mid-September, and shrine seals are available.

  • Kaminoyama Castle Foot Bath

    上山城の足湯

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A foot bath beside Kaminoyama Castle overlooks the city and the Zao mountain range. In spring it can be used while viewing cherry blossoms.

  • Maekawa Foot Bath

    前川足湯

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A foot bath at the base of Mayukawa Bridge and the closest to JR Kaminoyama-Onsen Station. It is a place to rest while waiting for a train or shinkansen.

  • Shinyu Foot Bath

    新湯の足湯

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A foot bath in the Shinyu Onsen town, opened in 1953 and used by tourists and local residents. A foot-bath stamp is available.

  • Yumachi Foot Bath (Crane's Resting Stone)

    湯町の足湯（鶴の休石）

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A foot bath at the birthplace of Kaminoyama Onsen, in the quiet Yumachi hot-spring district. The Crane's Resting Stone stands on the grounds, and a hand bath is also available.

  • Saito Mokichi Poem Monument at Zao Kumanodake Summit

    斎藤茂吉歌碑/蔵王熊野岳山頂

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A poem stone erected in August 1934 atop Zao Kumanodake, the only monument Mokichi approved in his lifetime. It faces his birthplace in Kanakame, Kaminoyama; he first saw it on 8 July 1939.

  • Kurort Nishiyama Course

    クアオルト　西山コース

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A satoyama course about 15 minutes' walk from the station, passing mountains around 300 meters with views of the Zao range and town, poet monuments, and traces of old terraced fields.

  • DEEP ZAO: Juhyo Field of Zao Bodaira Plateau

    DEEP ZAO　蔵王坊平高原の樹氷原

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    On Kaminoyama's Bodaira Plateau in the Zao range, summer brings alpine plants at Otanokami Marsh, and winter winds from the Iide and Asahi ranges form juhyo, with beech forest beyond.

  • Hosenji Temple

    宝泉寺

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    The family temple of the poet Saito Mokichi, in Kanakame, Kaminoyama, his hometown.

  • Born here in Kaminoyama

    斎藤茂吉の生家

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Born here on 14 May in Meiji 15 (1882) as the third son of Moriya Den'emon. The house is private, and only the exterior may be viewed.

  • Hayama Foot Bath (Yuami Jizo)

    葉山足湯（湯あみ地蔵）

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A foot bath on a hill in the Hayama Onsen district, which opened in 1936. It overlooks the town and the Zao range, and a foot-bath stamp is available.

  • Saito Mokichi Poem Monuments / Tsukioka Park

    斎藤茂吉歌碑/月岡公園

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    Poem stones by Saito Mokichi stand in Tsukioka Park, raised in 1961 for the city's seventh anniversary and in 1972 by Kaminoyama Rotary Club. One recalls his mother; one followed a climb of Mt. Kokuzo.

  • Takamatsu Kannon

    高松山 光明院（真言宗）/ 最上三十三観音 第11番 高松観音

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    A Shingon temple and the 11th stop of the Mogami Thirty-three Kannon pilgrimage. It enshrines a tiny seated Sho-Kannon attributed to Gyoki, and naked rice-cake pounding is offered each December.

  • Kannon Falls

    観音滝

    Kaminoyama, Yamagata

    An 80-meter straight waterfall in Senninzawa Gorge on the Zao Bodaira plateau. Entry is currently prohibited because part of the trail and cliff has collapsed; the stated season is mid-May to early November.
