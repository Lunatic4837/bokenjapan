Do in Kaneyama

Kaneyama · Yamagata · 6 places

Do

  • Kurashi Kobo

    暮らし考房

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-7132

    Log house for considering what a rich life in a mountain village means. Traditional indigo dyeing, maple-sap collection, forest play, and forest making convey forest living, and wild itaya maple sap is boiled down.

  • Kamuro Village

    神室ヴィレッジ

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-7725

    The grounds cover about 25,000 tsubo, with a horse ranch, fishing, lawn activities, family visits, and forest-road walks. Former Kamuro training-farm buildings include circular wooden architecture.

  • Yugaku Forest

    遊学の森

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-64-3305

    Yamagata Prefecture's fourth prefectural forest, it draws on the Mogami region's nature and local culture. The guiding idea is a model area where forests and people coexist, with nature-learning facilities planned.

  • Yawata Park

    八幡公園

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    Redeveloped in 1995 beside Yawata Shrine, it replaced a little-used children's park with a design that uses water. Gently rolling lawns lie inland, reached by a pond-side path from the road.

  • Green Valley Kamuro

    グリーンバレー神室

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-29-5640

    Centered on stay hotel Schenesheim Kaneyama, the mini resort area has camping, skiing, and snowboarding year-round. Hot House Kamuro onsen is attached, with day bathing available.

  • Hobby House Tobei

    趣味の家・藤兵衛

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-7386

    Fabric, needle, and thread are used to design pouches, wall hangings, and other small items.
