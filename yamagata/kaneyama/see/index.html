Sights in Kaneyama, Yamagata

Kaneyama · Yamagata · 10 places

See

  • Ozeki and Ozeki Park

    大堰・大堰公園

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    Irrigation canal around central Kaneyama, built over seven years from 1977 to 1983. Rough-cut stone forms the bed and sides, and perforated blocks let water seep underground.

  • Daimiwa Giant Cedar

    大美輪の大杉

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    Said to have been planted since the domain era. Nearly 300 years old, the giant cedar at Daimiwa in Ariya stands in 0.87 hectares of Kaneyama cedar with 115 trees.

  • Kaneyama Three Peaks

    金山三峰（薬師山・中の森・熊鷹森）

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    Three pyramid-shaped peaks seen from Kamiage Pass on National Route 13, listed from the west: Yakushi-yama (436.7 m), Naka-no-Mori (415 m), and Kumataka-mori (390 m).

  • Townscape of Gabled Roofs and White Walls

    切妻屋根と白壁の町並み

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    Local Kaneyama cedar is used in Kaneyama houses, forming streets of white walls and gabled roofs. Materials suited to the climate age beautifully and give a nostalgic, refined look.

  • Megane Weir

    めがね堰

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    Water from the Kamuro mountains flows through this weir. Residents work on shaping the surrounding landscape.

  • Kigokoro Hashi

    きごころ橋

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    Historic building or streetscape in Kaneyama, Yamagata.

  • Kurashikan

    蔵史館

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2111

    This storehouse serves as a base for residents' cultural activities. Exhibitions and tea gatherings are held here.

  • Taniguchi Silver Mine Site

    谷口銀山跡

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-3192

    The mine peaked in the early Shinjo domain years, about 1624-1651, with 66 tunnels. Records say seven cattle carried 120 kg of silver daily for seven months to refining, until flooding in 1662.

  • Ogiri Falls

    大切り滝

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-3192

    Waterfall beside the Taniguchi Silver Mine ruins that slides down among trees. The drop is about 20 meters. The beginner route takes five minutes from the parking area and includes a steep slope.

  • Fureaino Mori

    ふれあいの森

    Kaneyama, Yamagata · 0233-52-2240

    Mountain and highland area in Kaneyama, Yamagata.
