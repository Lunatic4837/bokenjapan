Things to do in Kawanishi, Yamagata

Kawanishi · Yamagata · 4 places

Do

  • Kawanishi Dahlia Garden

    川西ダリヤ園

    Kawanishi, Yamagata · 0238-42-2112

    Dahlia garden on 4 hectares with 650 varieties and 100,000 plants. Flowers of many colors, sizes, and shapes usually peak from mid-September to mid-October.

  • Kawanishi Onsen Madoka Bath Center

    川西温泉 川西町浴浴センターまどか

    Kawanishi, Yamagata · 0238-42-4126

    Hot-spring facility with day-use bathing, lodging, and a restaurant. The water is a salt spring. While Kawanishi Dahlia Garden is open, a combined ticket covers the day bath and the garden.

  • Kawanishi Municipal Komatsu Ski Area

    川西町営小松スキー場

    Kawanishi, Yamagata · 0238-27-1169

    Small ski area. Visitors climb by grabbing a rope rather than using a lift. Skiing, snowboarding, sledding, snow play, and yukita boards are available.

  • Ryuzosakura

    龍蔵桜

    Kawanishi, Yamagata

    Park in Kawanishi, Yamagata.
