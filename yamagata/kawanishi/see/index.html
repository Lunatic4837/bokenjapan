    Kawanishi, Yamagata · 0238-42-2111

  • Suwa Shrine

    諏訪神社（川西町）

    Kawanishi, Yamagata · 0238-42-4612

    Said to have been enshrined here early, as a branch of Suwa Taisha, when the area became Dewa Province. It is one of over 7,000 branch shrines and was first a guardian deity of Dewa.
