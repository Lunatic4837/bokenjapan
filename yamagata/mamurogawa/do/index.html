    Mamurogawa Park

    Akiyama Ski Area

    Mamurogawa, Yamagata

    Mamurogawa Town Museum of History and Folklore

    Mamurogawa, Yamagata · 0233-62-3511

    Preserves and displays tools of daily life and production long used by earlier residents, gathered with local cooperation to support education and culture.
