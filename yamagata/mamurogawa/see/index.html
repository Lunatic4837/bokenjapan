    野々村ため池

    Mamurogawa, Yamagata · 0233-62-2111

    Originally built for irrigation. After ten years of feeding, more than 300 swans come each winter to overwinter, and Mount Chokai, called Dewa Fuji, is visible on clear days.

  • Kamuyama

    加無山

    Mamurogawa, Yamagata · 0233-62-2111

    Views of Mt. Chokai and the Dewa Hills, unusual rocks, and alpine plants. The trail to the col between Otoko and Onna Kamuyama has a narrow steep section called Tainai-kuguri, then a difficult summit route.

  • Sangai Falls

    三階滝

    Mamurogawa, Yamagata · 0233-62-2111

    Tiered waterfall about 5 meters high, with a broad, cool, pool-like basin. Beginner difficulty; about one minute from beside a forest road, viewed from below the falls.

    渓流釣り/真室川一帯

    Mamurogawa, Yamagata · 0233-62-2078

    Clear streams include the Mamuro, Kaneyama, and Sakegawa rivers, with seasonal catches and waterside scenery. Fishing-permit inquiries go to the Mogami Fisheries Cooperative.
