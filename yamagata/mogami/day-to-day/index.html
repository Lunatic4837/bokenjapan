  • Wakuwaku Farm Maemori Kogen

    Mogami, Yamagata · 0233-43-3522

    Maemori Kogen spreads over 200 hectares, with cottages, horse-riding and other outdoor activities in open grassland, plus a soba restaurant serving hand-made soba.

  • Semi Onsen Communal Bath Semi no Yu

    瀬見温泉共同浴場　せみの湯

    Mogami, Yamagata

    Indoor, steam-heated, and outdoor baths can be used together. The indoor pools are hot and moderate with little added water, and a covered foot bath and hot-spring egg space are available.

  • Station in Mogami

    道の駅もがみ「あっつぇ」

    Mogami, Yamagata

    Opened on 26 November 2023, it shares tourism, event, and road information for Mogami Town and nearby municipalities. Attse is Mogami dialect meaning that something is available, used when offering tea, food, or rest.
