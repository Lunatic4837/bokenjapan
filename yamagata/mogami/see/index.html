  • 堺田 分水嶺

    Sakaida Watershed

    Mogami, Yamagata

    In the plaza before JR Rikuu East Line Sakaida Station, water from the north splits east to the Pacific and west to the Sea of Japan. This flatland divide is closed November to March.

  • 赤倉温泉

    Akakura Onsen

    Mogami, Yamagata

    A hot-spring area in Mogami with free-flowing source water, traditionally opened in 863 by the priest Jikaku Daishi. The water is a sodium-calcium-sulfate spring, and each inn uses its own source.

  • 山刀伐峠

    Natagiri Pass

    Mogami, Yamagata

    Matsuo Basho and Kawai Sora crossed this pass in 1689 toward Obanazawa on the Oku no Hosomichi route. Views include the Kamuro range and Mount Gassan, and a winding beech-forest path follows Basho's route.

  • 旧有路家住宅（封人の家）

    Former Ariji Family Residence (Hojin no Ie)

    Mogami, Yamagata

    This thatched hipped-roof hall-type farmhouse was designated a National Important Cultural Property in 1969 and restored for public viewing. It is regarded as the border official’s house in Sakaida where Matsuo Basho stayed.

  • 前森高原

    Maemori Plateau

    Mogami, Yamagata

    A 200-hectare plateau with cottages, horseback-riding facilities, and a place serving handmade soba, set in open grassland.

  • 瀬見渓谷

    Semi Gorge

    Mogami, Yamagata

    Runs about 4 km up and down National Route 47 along the Mogami Oguni River, centered on Semi Onsen. Spring greenery, autumn leaves, and year-round fishing, especially Matsubara ayu, are noted.

  • 最上白川渓流・白川ダム公園

    Mogami Shirakawa Stream and Shirakawa Dam Park

    Mogami, Yamagata

    Shirakawa Dam is 273 meters long and 12 meters high on the upper Shirakawa, at the foot of the Kamuro range. Nearby are autumn foliage, streamside forest walks, and a downstream park used for camping.

  • 愛宕山公園

    Atagoyama Park

    Mogami, Yamagata

    200 Somei-Yoshino and mountain cherry trees bloom here and are illuminated during the cherry festival. Azaleas, bush clover, and hydrangeas flower at different times through the year.
