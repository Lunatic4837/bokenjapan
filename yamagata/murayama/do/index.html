Things to do in Murayama, Yamagata

Murayama · Yamagata · 11 places

Do

  • Activity spot in Murayama

    最上川三難所舟下り

    Romaji: Mogami Kawa Sannanshofuneshita Ri

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-56-3535

    Lake, river, gorge or waterfall in Murayama, Yamagata.

  • Higashisawa Rose Park

    東沢バラ公園

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-55-2111

    Colorful roses bloom across the grounds beyond the entrance. About 20,000 rose bushes fill the park, and the Rose Exchange Hall sells soft-serve ice cream made with roses.

  • Kurhaus Goten

    クアハウス碁点

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-56-3351

    Ten baths can be chosen by their effects, and a Western-style outdoor spa pool is available. The restaurant serves country soba made with Murayama-grown Dewakaori buckwheat flour.

  • Yubunezawa Onsen

    湯舟沢温泉

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-58-2439

    Surrounded by greenery, this single mountain inn has warming water called beauty water. Meals use natural ingredients year-round, fireflies appear in early summer, and a thatched-roof building dates from 1875.

  • Soba-Making Experience / Rural Tradition House

    そば打ち体験/農村伝承の家

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-53-3277

    At the Rural Culture Preservation Hall, visitors make soba with a soba master's guidance and can eat what they prepare. Folk tools and crafts from the countryside are displayed.

  • Natural Junsai Picking Experience

    天然ジュンサイの摘みとり体験

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-53-1351

    Natural junsai is picked at Oyachi Marsh, commonly called Junsai Marsh. Plants here are thick, rich in jelly-like nuru, and very soft, and picking is limited to early summer.

  • Horse Garden Murayama

    ホースガーデンむらやま

    Murayama, Yamagata

    Set in quiet countryside north of Murayama, horseback riding is available among green fields. Professional instructors are present, so beginners can ride.

  • Mogami River Art Museum

    最上川美術館

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-52-3195

    Paintings by Keiji Mashimo, who loved and continued to paint the Mogami River, can be viewed with the river. The permanent exhibition room uses natural light and wire-free displays.

  • Cloisonne Experience

    七宝焼体験

    Murayama, Yamagata · 090-4048-8772

    Glass glaze is applied to metal such as copper plate and fired at about 800 degrees. Wired cloisonne brought from Tang China in the Nara period was treasured as a secret heirloom.

  • Higashizawa Boat House

    東沢ボートハウス

    Murayama, Yamagata

    A boat house in Higashizawa Park offers rental boats and light meals. Seasonal scenery can be viewed along the lakeshore.

  • Toshu Kiln Goten-yaki

    陶修窯 碁点焼

    Murayama, Yamagata

    A pottery kiln a two-minute walk from Goten, one of three difficult points on the Mogami River. Visitors from beginners to experienced makers can create their own pieces, including bluish Ai and iron-glaze Ginsuna ware.
