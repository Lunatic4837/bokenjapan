Sights in Murayama, Yamagata

Murayama · Yamagata · 5 places

See

  • Higashizawa Rose Park

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-55-2111 / 0237-53-1351

    Beyond the entrance, beds of colourful roses bloom across the park: about 20,000 rose bushes. The Rose Exchange Hall sells a popular rose soft-serve ice cream.

  • Hayashizaki Iai Shrine

    Murayama, Yamagata · 0237-53-1351

    The only shrine in Japan dedicated to iai, enshrining Hayashizaki Jinsuke Shigenobu, founder of iaido. Each September practitioners of many schools give demonstrations, and visitors can join an iaido experience programme.

  • Shorenzan Seijoin (Komatsuzawa Kannon)

    青蓮山 清浄院（真言宗）/ 最上三十三観音 第20番 小松沢観音

    Murayama, Yamagata

    A Shingon temple, the 20th of the Mogami 33 Kannon pilgrimage. The approach climbs about 1.5 km and 164 stone steps; giant straw sandals hang at the gate, and mukasari wedding plaques fill the hall.

  • Yuki no Kannon / Fubo Hoonji Temple

    雪の観音/父母報恩寺

    Murayama, Yamagata

    A temple hall built by Diet member Matsuoka Shunzo and named for prayers connected to his snow-damage relief campaign. An account says prayer to the Kannon he carried made a blizzard near Aomori subside.

  • Fubo Hoonji Temple

    父母報恩寺

    Murayama, Yamagata

    A Jodo sect temple built to guard Suwayama Cemetery and named to honor deceased parents. Its copper seated Amida, made in 1715, is a Murayama City cultural property.
