Things to do in Nagai, Yamagata

Nagai · Yamagata · 14 places

Do

  • Mibuchi Gorge Through-Passage Worship

    絶景・三淵渓谷通り抜け参拝

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-87-0605

    Mibuchi Gorge on Nagai Hyakushuko can be seen only by boat, on a passage of about one hour. It also draws visitors as a power spot.

  • Nagai Dam / Nagai Hyakushuko

    長井ダム／ながい百秋湖

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-88-5279

    Completed in 2011 a short climb west of central Nagai, the dam created Nagai Hyakushuko. The lake scenery is especially beautiful in the fresh-green and autumn-foliage seasons.

  • Nagai Hyakushuko Matsuri

    ながい百秋湖まつり

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-82-8005

    Observation point in Nagai, Yamagata.

  • Nogawa Manabi-kan

    野川まなび館

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-87-0605

    Lake, river, gorge or waterfall in Nagai, Yamagata.

  • Kodai no Oka

    古代の丘

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-88-9978

    At the foot of the Asahi mountains in Kusaoka, sites span the Paleolithic to the Sengoku period. Dogu Plaza has enlarged, restored clay figurines, plus Choja Yashiki materials and city excavation finds.

  • Nagai Ayame Festival

    長井あやめまつり

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-88-5279

    More than several hundred flower varieties bloom in the 3.3-hectare park, including Nagai koshu irises seen only there. The season runs from mid-June to early July.

  • Nagai Ayame Onsen New Sakurayu

    長井あやめ温泉「ニュー桜湯」

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-88-7280

    About 5 km west of Route 287 (Nagai Ohashi) at 265 m, it overlooks Nagai and the Okitama Basin from the Asahi mountains. The free-flowing, iron-rich water is faintly brown and leaves the body warm.

  • Nagai Ayame Park

    長井あやめ公園

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-84-2111

    More than several hundred iris varieties bloom in a park open for over 110 years; ayame is a symbol of Nagai. Flowers are in season from mid-June to early July.

  • Unohana Onsen Haginoyu

    卯の花温泉 はぎ乃湯

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-84-1387

    Spring water from the source supplies the large bath and an unusual outdoor bath. A bedrock bath, shop, lounge, rest room, dining area, and massage corners are also on site.

  • Doshojidaira Ski Area

    道照寺平スキー場

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A ski area in Hirayama, Nagai. The 2025-26 season is scheduled from December 25 to March 1, and dates may change with snowfall.

  • Nagai Hyakushuko Sightseeing Boat

    【2026年中止】ながい百秋湖　遊覧船

    Nagai, Yamagata

    Boat cruises for autumn foliage on Nagai Hyakushuko at Nagai Dam are not operating in 2026. An amphibious bus runs from September to early November instead.

  • Ishibuchi Buggy Challenge

    いしぶちバギーチャレンジ

    Nagai, Yamagata

    An off-road buggy activity based at Nogawa Manabikan. Drivers must be of junior high school age or older, younger children need a parent, and the route pairs a short safety course with a longer course.

  • Yamagata Morimori Forest

    やまがたもりもりの森

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A satoyama forest base centered on a colorful treehouse, offering forest play, countryside experiences, and day camping. Seasonal activities include meeting chickens and gathering wild plants, mushrooms, and maple sap.

  • Kendama Plaza SPIKe

    けん玉ひろばSPIKe

    Nagai, Yamagata

    In Nagai, which produces about 70 percent of Japan's competition kendama, visitors can try them, watch technique videos, and receive grade or rank certificates. Kendama, holders, and a painting experience are also available.
