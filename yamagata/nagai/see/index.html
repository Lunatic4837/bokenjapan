Sights in Nagai, Yamagata

Nagai · Yamagata · 18 places

See

  • Mifuchi Keikoku Valley

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-87-0605

    Mifuchi Keikoku Valley in the upper part of Nagai Dam can be only reached by a rescue

  • Nagai Ayame Park

    Nagai Ayame Park

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-84-2111

    Iris park run for over 110 years, home to 1 million irises. Ayame is a symbol of Nagai.

  • Isazawa Kubo Cherry Blossom Tree

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-88-5279

    Edohigan, Cherry Blossom tree, a nationally designated natural monument

  • Senbonzakura(Thousand of Cherry blossoms) at Mogami River Embankment

    Nagai, Yamagata · 0238-88-5279

    Senbonzakura(Thousand of Cherry blossoms) at Mogami River Embankment

  • Former Nagai Elementary School First Building

    旧長井小学校第一校舎

    Nagai, Yamagata

    Built in 1933 beside Nagai City Hall, this two-story wooden schoolhouse was registered as a national tangible cultural property in 2009. It has a reddish exterior, large central staircase, and long boat-bottom-ceiling corridor.

  • Sangai Falls (Fudo Falls)

    三階滝＜不動滝＞（長井市）

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A three-tier waterfall in Kuwazawa, Teraiizumi, Nagai, on a former pilgrimage shortcut toward Mount Iwaigame. The surroundings have fresh greenery and autumn foliage, and the narrow access road closes in winter.

  • Local sight in Nagai

    最上川ビューポイント（最上川発祥の地）

    Nagai, Yamagata

    The Matsukawa and Oitama Shirakawa meet here, and the river takes the name Mogami. It is the first prefecture-selected Mogami River Viewpoint, with a footpath, cherry trees and a trolley path.

  • Kotai Shrine

    皇大神社

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A shrine beside White Azalea Park enshrining Amaterasu, with a hall from Ise Grand Shrine, as Okitama's tutelary shrine. Founding records burned in 1864. The Koide lion dance here is a city intangible cultural property.

  • Saito Family Residence

    齋藤家住宅

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A late-Edo thatched two-story wooden townhouse with a Meiji-period storehouse, preserved along the old street. It is a nationally registered tangible cultural property and is not open to the public.

  • Kodai no Oka Museum

    古代の丘資料館

    Nagai, Yamagata

    Displays finds from the mid-Jomon Chojayashiki site and other city ruins. Nearby are a Jomon soba hall, a dogu plaza, and a sun plaza, with a Jomon festival each October.

  • Hayama Forest Park

    葉山森林公園

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A forest campsite among cedar woods, with a stream for water play and shared bungalows. Views open toward Asahi, Iide, Zao, and Azuma, and autumn brings colored foliage.

  • Mogami River Footpath

    最上川フットパス

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A path for strolling along the Mogami River and town waterside lanes, completed in June 2005. Wooden bridges, stepping stones, and signs were added on the left bank between the Shirakawa and Nogawa confluences.

  • Hagi Park

    はぎ公園

    Nagai, Yamagata

    White and red bush clover flower from early August to mid-September. Irises, azaleas, maples, roses, bamboo, cedar, and Japanese cypress also grow here, next to Unohana Onsen Haginoyu.

  • Former Marudai Ogiya (Bunkyo-no-Mori)

    旧丸大扇屋（文教の杜）

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A merchant house that prospered on Mogami River boat transport from the Edo through Showa periods, spanning about 330 years. Seven buildings remain and it was designated a Yamagata Prefecture tangible cultural property in 2003.

  • Kuwajima Memorial Hall (Former Kuwajima Eye Clinic)

    桑島記念館（旧桑島眼科医院）

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A wooden two-story Western-style building constructed in 1927 as an eye clinic and designated a city tangible cultural property. After a citizen campaign, it was moved to a corner of Honmachi parking lot and preserved.

  • Unnan Shrine

    蘊安神社

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A shrine honoring Fujiwara Unnan, who settled the Gangyo Rebellion of 878 without forcing military service. Two cedars estimated at about 600 years old, designated a natural monument in 1978, stand along the approach.

  • Mount Iwaigame

    祝瓶山

    Nagai, Yamagata

    A steep mountain of 1,417 m, counted among Japan's 300 famous mountains and called Tohoku's Matterhorn. Its northern ridge is in Bandai-Asahi National Park, and citizen climbs are held in the fresh-green and autumn-foliage seasons.

  • Kusaoka no Daimyojin Zakura

    草岡の大明神ザクラ

    Nagai, Yamagata

    Nationally designated natural monument cherry about 1,200 years old, 14 m tall with a 27 m branch spread, growing at a private home. Locally called the sowing cherry because it blooms near spring planting time.
