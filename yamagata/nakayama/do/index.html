Things to do in Nakayama, Yamagata

Nakayama · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • Activity spot in Nakayama

    芋煮会発祥の地 中山町

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-2114

    The Mogami River flows north through Yamagata to the Sea of Japan. Edo-period boats carried rice, safflower, and ramie via Sakata toward Kyoto and Osaka, returning with sugar, dried fish, clothing, and hina dolls.

  • Himawari Onsen Yurara

    ひまわり温泉 ゆ・ら・ら

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-5780

    Public lodging opened in December 1993. The JR Aterazawa Line Mogami River Bridge to the north, once one bridge with the Flower Nagai Line Arato Bridge, is Japan's oldest railway bridge still in use.

  • Nakayama Park

    中山公園

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-5361

    Sports park centered on Yamaryo Stadium Yamagata, the prefectural baseball field, plus a second baseball field and multipurpose plaza. Farmland surrounds it, and the stadium is visible from National Route 112.

  • Himawari Ground Golf Course

    ひまわりグラウンド・ゴルフ場

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-7577

    Dedicated ground-golf course in Mogami River Nakayama Green Space, with six courses and 48 holes, certified by the Japan Ground Golf Association. Mount Gassan and Mount Zao can be seen, and national tournaments are held.

  • Former Nagasaki School Entrance

    旧長崎学校の玄関

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-2175

    Early Meiji entrance, relocated to the town history and folklore museum. Saigo Tsugumichi, then navy vice admiral and Minister of Agriculture and Commerce, addressed the Nagasaki Elementary opening from this white balcony.

  • Mogami River Nakayama Green Space

    最上川中山緑地(運動広場など）

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-2116

    West of Nagasaki Bridge are a sports ground for softball, soccer and baseball, tennis courts and a children's plaza. A cycling path, open plaza, flower beds and picnic areas are also here.
