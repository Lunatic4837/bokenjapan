  • 金剛山 正法寺（真言宗）/ 最上三十三観音 第14番岡村観音

    Kongozan Shoboji Temple (Okamura Kannon)

    Nakayama, Yamagata

    Fourteenth of the Mogami 33 Kannon, this Shingon temple's origin story tells that priest Gyoki carved a sandalwood Kannon in the Nara period and prayed for rain during a drought.

  • 中山町立歴史民俗資料館

    Nakayama Town History and Folklore Museum

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-2175

    About 3,000 items cover inland farming, Mogami River shipping, daily life, and Important Cultural Property materials on Iwaya Juhachiya Kannon folk belief. Nagasaki Elementary School's early Meiji entrance, a town cultural property, was moved here.

  • 達磨寺

    Darumadera Temple

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-3194

    Soto Zen temple founded in 1616. The gate, received from Tendo Atago Jizo Gongen under the Shinto-Buddhist separation edict, shows late Edo features. Inside are an Iki Daruma scroll and a Tsunbuku Daruma figure.

  • 楯稲荷神社

    Tate Inari Shrine

    Nakayama, Yamagata

    Founded in 1472 from a divided spirit of Kyoto's Fushimi Inari, said to be a prayer for residents' prosperity. The festival is on September 9.

  • 旧柏倉惣右衛門家住宅

    Former Kashiwagura Soemon Family Residence

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-2175

    The main house, built from 1920 to 1922 on about 1,600 tsubo, belongs to a 1724 branch of the Kashiwagura Kyuzaemon family that served as village head. Nakayama Town designates it a tangible cultural property.

  • 光秀院（大日如来坐像）

    Koshuin Temple (Seated Dainichi Nyorai)

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-662-3181

    Founded in 1560 by shugen ascetic Samon, this Tendai Jimon temple is a town tangible cultural property. Its bronze seated Dainichi Nyorai, cast in 1768, stands 2.8 meters high.

  • 光照寺

    Koshoji Temple

    Nakayama, Yamagata

    Established in 1667, this Jodo Shinshu Honganji sect temple enshrines a wooden Amida Nyorai carved by Koun as its principal image.

  • 重要文化財 旧柏倉家住宅（九左衛門）

    Important Cultural Property Former Kashiwagura Family Residence (Kyuzaemon)

    Nakayama, Yamagata · 023-687-1778

    This large residence inherits the form of an early-modern upper-class farmhouse. The present main house comes from a predecessor built in 1783, after a major renovation in 1898, with precious-wood rooms, lacquer, and gold leaf.

  • 月山神社（土橋）

    Gassan Jinja Dobashi

    Nakayama, Yamagata

    Shinto shrine in Nakayama, Yamagata.

  • 鍋掛松

    Nabekake Pine

    Nakayama, Yamagata

    This fifth-generation pine marks where boatmen and merchants began imoni gatherings, cooking salted cod and taro in pots hung from branches while waiting to transfer cargo at the former Mogami River terminus.
