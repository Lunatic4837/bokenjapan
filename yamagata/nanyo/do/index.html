Things to do in Nanyo, Yamagata

Nanyo · Yamagata · 8 places

Do

  • Nanyo Sky Park (Experience on Paraglider)

    Nanyo, Yamagata · 0238-40-2149

    Nanyo Sky Park (Experience on Paraglider)

  • Eboshiyama Park

    Nanyo, Yamagata · 0238-40-2002

    The "100 Best Cherry Blossom Spots in Japan" spread out in the mountains behind Akayu Hot Springs.

  • Balloon Flight Experience at Jubuichiyama Famous for Sea of Clouds

    雲海で有名な十分一山での気球フライト体験

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A rope-tethered balloon rises about 15-20 meters at Jubuichiyama in Nanyo, overlooking the Okitama Basin, nearby 2,000-meter peaks, and sometimes a sea of clouds. Boarding-only and full-participation options are available.

  • Suto Wine Factory

    須藤ぶどう酒工場

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A family winery handles cultivation, harvest, brewing, and bottling itself. Its Shikin-en vineyard offers grape picking from early August to mid-October, and a harvest-to-wine experience from mid-August to early October.

  • Oura Wine

    大浦葡萄酒

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    Wine production began here in 1939 in Nanyo, called the birthplace of Yamagata grapes. Wines range from sweet, fruity types to richer aged ones, with attention to each grape variety's character.

  • Matsuda Tourist Fruit Orchard

    （ヤマ松） 松田観光果樹園

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A tourist fruit orchard in Nanyo. The city grows cherries such as Sato Nishiki and Benishuho, and its tourist orchards and farm shops are busy from June to early July.

  • Daimaru Farm

    ダイマル農園

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    In Nanyo, one of Yamagata’s main cherry-producing areas, varieties such as Sato Nishiki and Benishuho are grown. Tourist cherry orchards and direct-sales shops are busy from June to early July.

  • Maruen Endo Farm

    （マル遠）遠藤農園

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A fruit farm in Nanyo, a city that grows cherries such as Sato Nishiki and Benishuho. Tourist cherry picking and direct sales there run from June to early July.
