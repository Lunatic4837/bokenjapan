See in Nanyo

Nanyo · Yamagata · 8 places

See

  • Kumano Taisha Shrine

    Nanyo, Yamagata · 0238-47-7777

    Nanyo Chrysanthemum Festival (Miyauchi venue)

  • Sosho Rose Park

    Nanyo, Yamagata · 0238-40-2002

    Set on a hill overlooking the Yonezawa Basin and known as the 'rose garden of fragrance', with 6,000 roses of 340 varieties, including many rare old roses. Kumano Taisha shrine is nearby.

  • Kuguri Fall

    Nanyo, Yamagata · 0238-40-8295

    A 14 m waterfall in Nanyo's Kotaki district that pours through a hole in the rock 5 m high and 4.6 m wide, hence the name. It is surrounded by natural beech forest, with skunk cabbage in late spring and autumn colours.

  • Akayu Onsen

    赤湯温泉

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A hot-spring town opened more than 930 years ago and once visited by Uesugi lords. Six wineries, some offering tastings, stand in the grape-growing district, and the town is known for spicy miso ramen.

  • Eboshiyama Hachimangu Shrine

    烏帽子山八幡宮

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A shrine in Akayu Onsen, Nanyo, with a jointless single-stone torii. Its sacred rope is replaced each April 18, foliage lasts from mid-October to early November, and a monument honors Suishinshi Masahide.

  • Sosho Park

    双松公園

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    Known for roses and azaleas, and on the Okitama Sakura Corridor. Choyo-zakura and Keikai-zakura are weeping cherries over 150 years old; Choyo was named for the Nanyo view, and Keikai is an old place name.

  • Hase Kannon

    珠宮山 宝積坊（真言宗）/ 置賜三十三観音 第30番 長谷観音

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A Shingon temple and the 30th stop of the Okitama Thirty-three Kannon pilgrimage. The Kannon hall was rebuilt in 1827, and its hidden principal image is a six-shaku standing Sho-Kannon.

  • Yuzuru no Sato Museum

    夕鶴の里資料館

    Nanyo, Yamagata

    A museum in Nanyo's Urushiyama district created to pass on local folktales, including Crane's Gratitude, transmitted as a temple founding story. Displays and oral recitals show how the tales were told in daily life.
