Things to do in Nishikawa, Yamagata

Nishikawa · Yamagata · 7 places

Do

  • Gassan Shizu Onsen

    Nishikawa, Yamagata · 0237-74-4119

    A hot spring full of nature that springs in the Mt. Gassan!

  • Mt. Gassan Ski Resort

    Nishikawa, Yamagata · 0237-75-2025

    While other ski resorts are closed, the Mt. Gassan Ski Resort opens in April until July.

  • Gassan Toraya Winery, Toraya Nishikawa Factory

    月山トラヤワイナリー　虎屋西川工場

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    A winery at the foot of 1,980-meter Mt. Gassan that uses local grapes grown in a basin with wide day-night temperature swings. It also makes cherry and La France fruit wines and apple sparkling cider.

  • Sagae Dam Sports Plaza

    寒河江ダムスポーツ広場

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    This sports ground has five kinds of light sports and a barbecue area. It is promoted for children’s group outings and company trips.

  • Gassan Yumiharidaira Auto Campground

    月山・弓張平オートキャンプ場

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    An auto campground on hilly land at the foot of Mount Gassan, in an area with highland character.

  • Nishikawa Town Minmazawa Ski Area

    西川町民間沢スキー場

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    A ski area offering beginner to intermediate runs for both skiing and snowboarding, with night operations. It stands within sight of National Route 112.

  • Yamagata Prefecture Shizu Campground

    山形県志津野営場

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    Old-style campground beside Jizo-numa, an autumn-foliage spot in Nishikawa, at the foot of Mt. Gassan amid beech forest.
