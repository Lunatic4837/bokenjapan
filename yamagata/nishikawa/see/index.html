Sights in Nishikawa, Yamagata

Nishikawa · Yamagata · 6 places

See

  • Nishikawa Michi-no-Eki

    Nishikawa, Yamagata · 0237-74-2100

    A roadside station selling fresh local vegetables, fruit, mountain vegetables and mushrooms, with a restaurant using local ingredients. It also has Yamagata's only craft beer brewery and the Mizusawa Onsen bath house.

  • Sagae Dam (Gassan Lake) and Gassan Lake Great Fountain

    寒河江ダム（通称：月山湖）・月山湖大噴水

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    An artificial lake and multipurpose dam on Route 112, supplying six Murayama cities and six towns and also used for flood control and power. A fountain rises 112 meters on a seasonal schedule.

  • Jizo-numa

    地蔵沼

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    A pond at the foot of Mount Gassan, with a walking path and a campsite with toilets. Visitors come for beech greenery, autumn leaves, summer skiing, wild plants, and the Buna no Izumi spring.

  • Yamagata Prefectural Natural History Park

    山形県立自然博物園

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    A prefectural facility in Nishikawa categorized as a park, reference hall and museum.

  • Hondoji Kuchinomiya Yudonosan Shrine

    本道寺口ノ宮湯殿山神社

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    A shrine enshrining Onamuchi, Sukunahikona, and Oyamatsumi. The hall is 11.5 ken wide and 7 ken 5 shaku deep, with a Buddha's footprint stone said to exist at only five sites in Japan.

  • Kaishu Onsen

    海味温泉

    Nishikawa, Yamagata

    A hot-spring area in Nishikawa.
