Day-to-day services in Obanazawa, Yamagata

Obanazawa · Yamagata · 4 places

Day to day

  • Michi-no-Eki Obanazawa

    Obanazawa, Yamagata · 0237-24-3535

    A busy roadside station with places to eat, souvenirs and a farm stand. As the birthplace of the Hanagasa dance it sells flower hats, along with traditional crafts, local sweets and same-day-harvested produce.

  • Hanagasa Onsen Fukunoyu

    日帰り温泉「花笠温泉ふくの湯」

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    A day-trip hot spring along National Route 347 in Obanazawa. The water is a sodium chloride spring with a pH of 10.

  • Ginzan Onsen Taisho Roman Hall

    銀山温泉「大正ろまん館」

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    A souvenir shop and restaurant in Ginzan Onsen, opened in October 2015, that sells the hot spring's curry bread. In winter, visitors who are not staying overnight transfer to a shuttle bus here.

  • Tokura Lake Onsen Hanagasa no Yu

    徳良湖温泉　花笠の湯

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    A day-use hot spring in Obanazawa, part of Tokura Lake Onsen.
