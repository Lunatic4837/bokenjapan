See in Obanazawa

Obanazawa · Yamagata · 8 places

See

  • Lake Tokura

    Obanazawa, Yamagata · 0237-22-1111

    A man-made irrigation lake built in the Taisho era; the famous Hanagasa dance is said to have grown from the work songs sung during its construction. Cherry trees line the shore in spring, and migrating birds winter here.

  • 白銀の滝

    Shirogane Falls

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    A 22-meter waterfall marks the entrance to Shirogane Park at the end of Ginzan Onsen. It can appear as two or three streams. A vermilion bridge spans the lip, with an observation deck opposite.

  • 延沢銀山遺跡

    Nobesawa Silver Mine Ruins

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    A nationally designated historic site of a silver mine worked from the Muromachi period and abandoned after a collapse in 1689. A lit walkway runs through the tunnel, about 15 minutes from Ginzan Onsen.

  • しろがね湯

    Shirogane-yu

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    A day-trip communal bath in Ginzan Onsen, housed in a modern building by Kengo Kuma and Associates. It recalls the feel of the town's former hot-spring cure days.

  • 銀山温泉(紅葉)

    Ginzan Onsen (Autumn Foliage)

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    From the hot-spring street, a walking course to Shirogane Falls and the silver-mine cave can also be done in yukata.

  • 白銀公園

    Shirogane Park

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    A park in Ginzan Onsen that includes Yoshino cherries, a mountain-cherry park area, Omokage Garden, a rest house, and toilets.

  • 芭蕉、清風歴史資料館(鈴木家住宅)

    Basho and Seifu Historical Museum (Suzuki Family Residence)

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    Opened in July 1983, this Edo-period townhouse has hanging fireproof lattice doors and a snow-country central-gate plan with a through earthen floor. Permanent and special exhibitions, lectures, and gallery talks are held.

  • 昭和館

    Showakan

    Obanazawa, Yamagata

    Located in Ginzan, Obanazawa City, in the Murayama area.
