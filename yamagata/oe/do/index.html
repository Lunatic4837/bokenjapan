  • Oku-Oe Yanagawa Onsen

    奥おおえ柳川温泉

    Oe, Yamagata · 0237-64-2151

    Also called Netsu no Yu, this spring retains heat well and is used for arteriosclerosis and chronic skin conditions. Drinking, day bathing, and a foot bath are possible, with hydrangeas beside the open-air bath.

  • Therme Hakuryo Health Onsen Hall

    テルメ柏陵健康温泉館

    Oe, Yamagata · 0237-83-4126

    Hot-spring health facility on the Mogami River bank in Oe, sourced from Funauta Onsen. The water is a sulfur-bearing sodium-calcium chloride spring, used for diabetes, neuralgia, and muscle pain.

  • Daisen Nature Park Cottages and Campground

    大山自然公園コテージ・キャンプ場

    Oe, Yamagata · 0237-62-5421

    Children can play on the lawn plaza. Seasonal plants include satozakura in early to mid-May, hime-sayuri lilies from late May to early June, and hydrangeas from late June to late July.

  • Daisen Nature Park

    大山自然公園

    Oe, Yamagata · 0237-62-5421

    Satoyama park on the 317 m summit of Mt. Daisen, southwest of JR Aterazawa Station. Plaza and observation building views include the Asahi range, Mt. Gassan, the Ou Mountains, and the Murayama Basin.

  • Jindai Kaya

    神代カヤ

    Oe, Yamagata

    About 1,500 years old, this kaya has a legend that its spirit and a cedar 8 m around, before nearby Miroku-do, traveled together to Ise. Tradition forbids cutting it even if dead.

  • Filming Location of "Isha Sensei" (Fujita, Oe)

    映画「いしゃ」先生ロケ地 （大江町藤田）

    Oe, Yamagata · 0237-62-2139

    One filming location for the movie Isha Sensei, which portrays female doctor Shida Chikako and is set in Oisawa, Nishikawa. A later-life scene of Chikako thanking Sojiro was filmed here.

  • Cherry Blossom Light-up at Terme Hakuryo Health Onsen

    テルメ柏陵健康温泉館桜ライトアップ

    Oe, Yamagata

    Cherry trees planted around the facility are lit up in mid-April at full bloom. The baths are 100 percent free-flowing source water, where visitors can ease daily fatigue as spring arrives.
