Do in Oguni

Oguni · Yamagata · 9 places

Do

  • Roadside Station Shiroi Mori Oguni

    道の駅「白い森おぐに」

    Oguni, Yamagata

    Located 5 km east of the Niigata border along Route 113, with a light-meal and rest corner, restaurant, and local-products shop. It adjoins Yokone Ski Area, with skiing and snowboarding in winter.

  • Wakasagi Fishing Experience

    わかさぎ釣り体験

    Oguni, Yamagata

    Fish for wakasagi on snow-covered Shiroi Mori Oguni Lake. Rods can be rented from a hut, and about 2,000 people of all ages visit during the season.

  • Mamino-taira Chestnut Orchard

    “まみの平”自然観光栗園

    Oguni, Yamagata · 0238-62-4918

    In the Kaname district, this 4-hectare orchard has 1,000 chestnut trees. Visitors gather chestnuts in autumn from late September to late October; falling time can shift with the weather.

  • Kurosawa-toge Stone-Paved Road

    黒沢峠敷石道

    Oguni, Yamagata · 0238-62-4496

    Mossy paving stones laid in 1512 as part of the Echigo Kaido served transport and trade. About 3,600 remain nearly intact over 2.7 km to Shinono; the road is a town cultural property.

  • Nukumidaira Beech Forest Mini Tour

    ブナの森 温身平（ぬくみだいら）ミニツアー（8月～10月の日・祝）実施

    Oguni, Yamagata · 0238-62-5130

    Nukumidaira stands at the foot of the Iide Range. White beech trunks and snow give the town its White Forest name. A forest-therapy attendant is stationed at the entrance on Sundays and holidays, August to October.

  • Shiroimori Auto Campground

    白い森オートキャンプ場

    Oguni, Yamagata · 0238-67-2778

    Campground among Asahi range greenery and the Arakawa River, with showers and toilets. Guests can use indoor baths at adjacent Rifure, play in the river, or walk the forest.

  • Matagi no Sato Exchange Hall

    マタギの郷 交流館

    Oguni, Yamagata · 0238-64-2525

    Exhibition room on matagi history and culture, in the Kodamagawa district where that culture has long been passed down. A restaurant serves meals made with local ingredients.

  • Buna no Mori Nukumidaira Retreat Tour: Forest Nap and Tea Time

    ブナの森 温身平リトリートツアー 森の中でお昼寝・ティータイム

    Oguni, Yamagata · 0238-62-5130

    Forest therapy attendants guide visitors through a beech forest of trees over 200 years old, on seasonal courses that include a nap and tea time.

  • Yokone Health Forest

    健康の森横根

    Oguni, Yamagata · 0238-62-5584

    Broadleaf woods of beech, oak, chestnut, magnolia and maple cover about 180 hectares. Walking trails total 8.6 km, and bungalows and an exchange center stand in the forest.
