  • Hijiori Morning Market

    肘折名物「肘折朝市」

    Ohkura, Yamagata · 0233-34-6106

    This morning market is one of Hijiori Onsen's local specialties. It runs daily from April 20 to early December, selling seasonal wild vegetables and produce, and many visitors come to talk with local women.

  • Okura Village Tourism Association (Hijiori Ideyu-kan)

    大蔵村観光協会（肘折いでゆ館内）

    Ohkura, Yamagata · 0233-34-6106

    Inside Hijiori Ideyu-kan, the association introduces lodgings suited to each visitor and answers sightseeing and other questions.

  • Hijiori Ideyu Hall (Okura Village Tourism Association)

    肘折いでゆ館（大蔵村観光協会）

    Ohkura, Yamagata

    A core facility in Hijiori Onsen providing tourism and culture information for Okura Village. It includes a cafeteria, rest rooms, coworking space, a Pokemon manhole cover, and a kokeshi and history museum opened in 2026.
