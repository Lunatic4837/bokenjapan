See in Ohkura

Ohkura · Yamagata · 8 places

See

  • Shikamura Rice Terraces

    Ohkura, Yamagata · 0233-75-2105

    Four mountain hamlets of Okura are known together as Shikamura. Their rice terraces cover about 120 hectares, among the largest in Tohoku, and are listed among Japan's 100 best rice terraces.

  • Okura Yuki Monogatari

    Okura Yuki Monogatari

    Ohkura, Yamagata · 0233-34-6106

    This snow festival is a winter tradition in Okura Village. The 28th edition includes the Fuyu Hanabi Uchiage, a winter fireworks launch.

  • Jizogura

    Ohkura, Yamagata · 0233-34-6106

    A place luck in marriage and good business

  • Kaminoyu

    上の湯

    Ohkura, Yamagata

    A communal bath in central Hijiori Onsen using its oldest spring. Separate baths have blue stones on the floor, and a Jizo linked to an onsen legend stands in one corner.

  • Former Hijiori Post Office Building

    旧肘折郵便局舎

    Ohkura, Yamagata

    A small landmark in the center of Hijiori Onsen, with window lattices shaped like the postal symbol. The interior cannot currently be viewed, but the building is used for mini-concerts and events.

  • Komatsubuchi

    小松渕

    Ohkura, Yamagata

    A scenic gorge on the Dozan River linked to a legend that samurai Hachiro Koyama of the Shinjo domain slew a giant serpent. Seasonal foliage, including fresh green and autumn leaves, reflects on the water.

  • 国道458号のブナ林

    Beech Forest on National Route 458

    Ohkura, Yamagata

    A beech forest about four kilometers south of Hijiori on National Route 458 forms a tunnel-like passage. Bright new leaves appear in spring, and the landscape turns golden in autumn.

  • 男沼・長沼・馬頭観音

    Onuma, Naganuma and Bato Kannon

    Ohkura, Yamagata

    Ponds here shelter Ruriitotonbo damselflies and precious aquatic plants and form a designated Yamagata satoyama conservation area. Nearby Bato Kannon, founded in 1727, draws local worship; Naganuma's water level hardly changes through the year.
