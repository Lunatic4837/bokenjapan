  • Josen-ji Temple

    乗船寺

    Oishida, Yamagata · 0237-35-2111

    Founded in 1596 as Lord Mogami Yoshiaki's military deputy temple. The grounds include Shiki's verse monument and Mokichi's grave and poem monument.

  • Shiozawayama Sogen-in (Soto Sect) / Mogami Thirty-three Kannon No. 28 Shionosawa Kannon

    塩沢山 曹源院（曹洞宗）/ 最上三十三観音 第28番 塩ノ沢観音

    Oishida, Yamagata

    Said to have begun when a fourth-rank court noble built a hall and enshrined a Thousand-Armed Kannon. His descendant, Hino Bitchu no Kami, later ruled the area from a residence on the mountain.

  • Sekisui-zan Saiko-ji (Ji Sect) / Mogami Thirty-three Kannon No. 29 Oishida Kannon

    石水山 西光寺（時宗）/ 最上三十三観音 第29番 大石田観音

    Oishida, Yamagata

    Long ago, Kannon devotee Hosokawa Jinbei lived in Oishida. His son Senta, fishing in the Mogami River, reeled in a dazzling light and found a Kannon statue.

    Oishida, Yamagata · 0237-35-2111
