Do in Sagae

Sagae · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Sagae Cherry Land

    道の駅「寒河江」チェリーランド

    Sagae, Yamagata

    Along the Sagae River, the grounds include shops for Yamagata products, sweets made with seasonal fruit, and meals using branded beef. Sakuranbo Kaikan on the same site features local cherries and other produce.

  • Sagae Hanasaka Onsen Yu Cherry

    寒河江花咲か温泉 ゆ～チェリー

    Sagae, Yamagata · 023-783-1414

    Men and women each have three baths of differing spring quality, all using 100% free-flowing source water. The day-trip onsen also has an outdoor bath, sauna, shop, restaurant, and fee-based rest room.

  • Sagae Onsen

    寒河江温泉

    Sagae, Yamagata · 0237-86-8866

    Roses, a Sagae specialty, float in bath water described as good for the skin. Day-trip bathing includes a rose bath.

  • Park in Sagae

    最上川ふるさと総合公園

    Sagae, Yamagata · 0237-83-5195

    Prefecture-run urban park south of Sagae, opened in April 2000 as Sagae Highway Oasis. It is laid out along the Mogami River and includes cherries, the prefectural tree.

  • Furusawa Shuzo Museum

    古澤酒造資料館

    Sagae, Yamagata · 0237-86-5322

    The Furusawa family, said to have been safflower merchants, have long brewed sake in Sagae. Furusawa Shuzo, known for Sawa Masamune, was founded in 1836. Sake was made in the brewery from 1917 to 1983.

  • Grape Picking

    ぶどう狩り

    Sagae, Yamagata · 0237-86-1811

    All-you-can-eat large-grape picking for 60 minutes runs from August 28 until around the end of September. Varieties include Sunny Rouge and Pione.

  • Dewa Three Noted Temples Pilgrimage

    出羽名刹三寺まいり

    Sagae, Yamagata · 023-695-2816

    The three temples are Risshaku-ji in Yamagata City, Jakusho-ji in Tendo City, and Jion-ji in Sagae.

  • Sagae Onsen Foot Bath

    寒河江温泉 足湯

    Sagae, Yamagata

    Seseragi Foot Bath has a hand bath, so hands can be warmed together with the feet.

  • Ninoseki Waterside Park

    二の堰親水公園

    Sagae, Yamagata · 0237-86-5112

    Waterside park next to the urban area, familiar to local residents. In spring, cherry trees along the promenade bloom and it is a blossom-viewing spot in Sagae.

  • Strawberry Picking

    いちご狩り

    Sagae, Yamagata

    Visitors pick strawberries in warm tourist-garden greenhouses, even on cold days. Hydroponic growing avoids soil mess, and reservation by the previous day is required.
