  • Michi-no-Eki Sagae Cherry Land

    Sagae, Yamagata · 0237-86-1818 / 0237-86-3111

    A large site on the Sagae River with Yamagata specialities, sweets made from seasonal fruit and dishes using brand-name beef; the Cherry Hall on the same grounds sells local cherries and produce.

  • Furusawa Brewery Museum

    Sagae, Yamagata · 0237-86-5322

    The Furusawa family, said to have been safflower merchants, have brewed sake in Sagae since 1836 and are known for Sawamasamune. The museum is in the brewery building used from 1917 to 1983.

  • 本山慈恩寺

    Honzan Jionji Temple

    Sagae, Yamagata

    According to temple tradition, Gyoki reported the scenery to Emperor Shomu and Baramon Sojo founded it by imperial order. A 2014 national historic site, it keeps Important Cultural Property halls, statues and 5 May bugaku.

  • 寒河江神輿会館

    Sagae Mikoshi Hall

    Sagae, Yamagata

    An information center in front of JR Sagae Station that permanently displays Sagae mikoshi, serving as a symbol of the town known for its portable shrines.
