Do in Sakata

Sakata · Yamagata · 11 places

Do

  • Sakata Seafood Market

    さかた海鮮市場

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-23-5522

    Seafood market beside Sakata Port stocking fish landed from the Sea of Japan. The first-floor Sugawara fish shop focuses on local fish and offers cooking advice and takeaway packing.

  • Chokai Kogen Family Travel Village

    鳥海高原家族旅行村

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-64-4111

    Leisure facility at 500 meters on the Chokai highlands, with a campsite, cabins, and treehouses for camping-style stays and outdoor barbecues. A ground-golf course in the wide lawn has 48 permanent holes.

  • Historic building or streetscape in Sakata

    雛の飾り菓子制作体験

    Romaji: Hina no Shoku Ri Kashi Seisaku Taiken

    Sakata, Yamagata · 090-6222-9007

    Historic building or streetscape in Sakata, Yamagata.

  • Tatenokawa Brewery

    楯の川酒造株式会社

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-52-2323

    Sake brewery founded in 1832 that has contributed to the development of Japan's sake, food, and agriculture culture. Brewery tours are available.

  • Miraini, Sakata Station-Front Exchange Hub

    酒田駅前交流拠点施設 ミライニ

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-24-2454

    Miraini is a public facility comprising Sakata City Central Library, the station-front tourist information center, a plaza, municipal parking, and a bus bay.

  • Hiyoriyama Park

    日和山公園（酒田市）

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-26-5745

    Sea of Japan sunsets can be seen here. In mid-April, Sakata Hiyoriyama Cherry Blossom Festival features about 400 blooming trees and bonbori night lighting. A wooden hexagonal lighthouse and direction stones stand in the park.

  • Iesaka-tei, Former Sakata Port Shipping Wholesaler

    酒田湊旧廻船問屋・家坂亭

    Sakata, Yamagata · 080-8543-5591

    The Iesaka family traded rice and tung oil by kitamaebune from late Edo through mid-Meiji, and made paper raincoats coated with tung oil for sailors. The present building was constructed after the 1894 Shonai Earthquake.

  • Tobishima Seafood Donya

    海鮮どんや とびしま

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-26-6111

    On the second floor of Sakata Seafood Market, it serves seafood bowls, limited-quantity boat platters, salmon-roe bowls, and sea-urchin and salmon-roe bowls using seasonal Shonai coast seafood.

  • Sakata Port Cruise

    酒田港クルーズ

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A pleasure-boat cruise at Sakata Port. All sailings for the current year were cancelled after an engine failure on the regular ship Tobishima.

  • Hatsumago Sake Brewery Museum Kura Tanbokan

    初孫酒造資料館「蔵探訪館」

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A museum run by Tohoku Meijo, maker of Hatsumago sake, in a black-and-white storehouse amid pines outside Sakata. Exhibits cover brewing history and process, with a tasting corner for seasonal and brewery-only sake.

  • 八森キャンプ場

    Hachimori Campground

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A campground in Hachimori Nature Park, with areas for viewing wildflowers, nature observation, and forest bathing. Tents, bungalows, and a cooking area are available, with sports and hot-spring facilities nearby.
