  • 旬味 井筒

    Junaji Izutsu

    #24 ranked in Sakata

    Local-cuisine restaurant in Sakata, Yamagata.

  • 食彩旬味　芳香亭（令和8年7月29日に本店「香梅咲」で営業再開）

    Shokusai Shunmi Hokotei

    Dining in Sakata

    Reopened on July 29, 2026 at the main shop after ending operations at Sankyo Warehouse. Menu centers on shokado bento, bamboo-basket bento, and Yamagata beef steak meals.

  • 割烹　さわぐち

    Kappo Sawaguchi

    Dining in Sakata

    Kappo restaurant offering set meals and banquet dishes, focused on local cuisine. Sashimi, grilled fish, tempura, and house-made fish-sauce shiokara use ingredients from Tobishima.

  • 割烹 よしのや

    Kappo Yoshinoya

    Dining in Sakata

    Japanese cuisine is served in quiet, fully private tatami rooms with views of Hiyoriyama Park and the sea. Every course includes thin handmade soba made from 100 percent buckwheat.

  • 料亭 香梅咲

    Ryotei Kamezaki

    Dining in Sakata

    Kyoto-style cuisine with the current chef's own touches is served at a ryotei founded in 1854, recalling Sakata's kitamaebune port trade. The namesake old plum tree still blooms in spring.

  • 味工房　八月

    Ajikobo Hazuki

    Dining in Sakata

    Fresh fish from Shonai beach is served as creative dishes, with banquet menus of charcoal-grilled seafood and yakiniku. Catering and boxed meals are also offered.

  • 割烹食堂 伊豆菊・寿司処 武蔵

    Kappo Shokudo Izugiku and Sushi Dokoro Musashi

    Dining in Sakata

    Run by a fish shop, Izugiku serves seasonal Japanese cuisine and Musashi serves sushi. Local sashimi is recommended, and sweet-savory Sangen pork katsudon is the specialty.

  • うなぎ割烹　玉勘

    Unagi Kappo Tamakan

    Dining in Sakata

    Founded in the late Edo period and focused on eel. A mildly sweet thick omelette is also a specialty.

  • 割烹 治郎兵衛

    Kappo Jirobee

    Dining in Sakata

    Private rooms serve kappo meals, including Mezuru shumai made with Sakata meat, vegetables, and Mezuru glutinous rice, plus Shonai Tsuyahime rice. Opened in 1877.

  • 若葉旅館　お食事処　新芽

    Wakaba Ryokan Oshokujidokoro Shinme

    Dining in Sakata

    Serves local seasonal ingredients from the sea, mountains, and rivers. Evening sets include grilled fish, sashimi, meat, rice, miso soup, pickles, and a sweet, with about 50 snacks at night.

  • くつろぎ割烹 志幡

    Kutsurogi Kappo Shibata

    Dining in Sakata

    Serves omakase dishes based on Japanese cuisine, overlooking the Sankyo Warehouse, and closes with mukisoba, Benkei rice, and fried manju. Women receive handmade cake.
