See in Sakata

Sakata · Yamagata · 15 places

See

  • Tobishima Island

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-26-5759

    A remote island "Hishima" where you can interact with nature and people.

  • Tamasudare Falls

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-64-3111

    These dramatic vertical falls over a sheer cliff face were a location for ascetic Zen training

  • Sankyo Soko Storehouse

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-24-2994

    These functioning rice storehouses form a picturesque view with their black wooden walls lined with a row of Keyaki trees

  • Kaikoji Temple

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-22-4264

    https://yamagatakanko.com/en/attractions/detail_11841.html

  • Somaro (Maiko Teahouse and Takehisa Yumeji Museum)

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-21-2310

    The renovated Somaya, Sakata's leading restaurant since the Edo period, reopened in 2000. Its wooden main house was built around surviving storehouses after the 1894 Shonai earthquake fire and is a registered cultural property.

  • Hiyoriyama Park

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-26-5745

    Learn about the historic port town of Sakata at this park with an iconic white lighthouse and traditional white sailing ship!

  • Homma Art Museum

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-24-4311

    The museum's main building, Seienkaku, was built in 1813 and faces the Kakubuen scenic garden, with a new wing completed in 1968. It once hosted the Shonai domain lords and shogunate officials, and later imperial family members.

  • Former Honma Family Residence

    Sakata, Yamagata · 0234-22-3562

    Feel the spirit of simplicity and fortitude at the residence of Japan’s richest merchant!

  • Sankyo Warehouses Light-Up

    山居倉庫のライトアップ

    Sakata, Yamagata

    Rice storehouses built in 1893, a symbol of rice-growing Shonai, were designated a national historic site in March 2021 and ceased storage in September 2022. Illumination, including under snow, differs from the daytime view.

  • Tsuruma Pond

    鶴間池

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A pond on the way to Mount Chokai's Yunodai trailhead. May greenery and October foliage attract photographers and trekkers, and it is a prefectural natural monument breeding ground for forest green tree frogs.

  • Juni Falls

    十二滝

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A group of 12 falls in stepped form, with a 30 m drop and a red suspension bridge. The route is fully closed after forest-road damage and landslides as of July 2026.

  • Ken Domon Photography Museum

    土門拳写真美術館

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A photography museum opened in 1983 after Ken Domon donated his works to Sakata. Yoshio Taniguchi designed the building, and exhibitions rotate through themes such as temples, Hiroshima, and portraits.

  • Fudo Falls

    不動の滝（酒田市）

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A roughly 15-meter waterfall behind Mitaki Shrine in Shimokurokawa, Sakata, splitting over a projecting rock face. Also called Kaiun Shusse Falls; passage has been closed since heavy rain on 25 July 2024.

  • Kaianji Temple

    海晏寺

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A Soto Zen temple founded in 1394, with Shakyamuni as its principal image. Its three-story pagoda, rebuilt in recent years in traditional style, is one symbol of the town.

  • Daishinji Temple

    大信寺

    Sakata, Yamagata

    A Jodo Shinshu Honganji-ha temple whose principal image is Amida Nyorai. Founded by Josenbo Ryogen, it moved to the present site in 1624.
