Sakegawa · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • Hanesawa Onsen

    羽根沢温泉

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-2111

    Quiet mountain onsen about 30 minutes by car from JR Shinjo Station, surrounded by primeval forest. The spring emerged in Taisho 8 during oil test drilling; the setting is small and traditional.

  • Autumn Leaves at Sakegawa Village Eco Park

    秋の紅葉・鮭川村エコパーク

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-4455

    Nature park for overnight stays covering 30 hectares of mixed woodland, themed on coexistence with nature. Auto campground among broadleaf trees includes free sites, cottages, and Kinoko no Mori lodging and training facilities.

  • Nozaki Cold Sweets Shop

    野崎冷菓店

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-2074

    Eat-in is available, though takeout may be requested when the shop is crowded.

  • Sakegawa Village Eco Park / Year-round Activities

    鮭川村エコパーク／各種体験メニュー（通年）

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-4455

    Year-round activities include stone-oven pizza baked from scratch in the park’s kiln, taking about 2.5 hours for groups of six or more.

  • Yone Wetland

    米湿原

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-2111

    Water source of Yone settlement, once used for rice fields and reed cutting. Residents and the village nature conservation committee restored the abandoned wetland, bringing back plants of earlier times; Gifu butterflies appear after snowmelt.

  • Activity spot in Sakegawa

    さけがわトレッキング

    Romaji: Sakegawa Trekking

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-2111

    Yozura Pass trekking follows an old beech-forest road between Mogami and Shonai, from the Hanezawa trailhead to Oashizawa via Yozura Pond, a tree-frog pond, and Maboroshi Falls.

  • Park in Sakegawa

    鮭の子公園

    Romaji: Sake no Ko Park

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-4460

    Along the Izumida River on Route 458 from Shinjo, with produce shop Sanchoku Sakemarukun and Sake no Ko Kan, where ramen and coffee are served.

  • Campground in Sakegawa

    鮭川村エコパーク／栗の木オートキャンプ場

    Romaji: Sakegawa Village Eco Park / Kuri no Ki Auto Campground

    Sakegawa, Yamagata · 0233-55-4455

    Camping here includes the reservation-only Kuri no Ki Auto Campground and free sites. Auto sites measure 11 by 11 meters and have power outlets, with a sanitary building offering showers, toilets, and changing rooms.
