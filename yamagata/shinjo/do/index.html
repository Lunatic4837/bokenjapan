Do in Shinjo

Shinjo · Yamagata · 7 places

Do

  • Mogami Central Park (Kamuten Park)

    最上中央公園（かむてん公園）

    Shinjo, Yamagata · 0233-22-2111

    Beside Shinjo Station’s east exit, a low hill looks out toward Mount Gassan and Mount Chokai. The park has a lawn and play equipment, and rents snowshoes and kanjiki in winter.

  • Shinjo Castle Ruins / Mogami Park

    新庄城址・最上公園

    Shinjo, Yamagata · 0233-22-2111

    The former flatland castle stood within a triple moat; moats and stone walls remain, with Tozawa Shrine, Tenmangu, and Gokoku Shrine. The Tozawa clan ruled the 68,200-koku domain for about 243 years.

  • Yuki no Sato Information Center

    雪の里情報館

    Shinjo, Yamagata · 0233-22-7891

    Exhibits review Japan’s first snow-country rural economy institute, founded in Shinjo in 1933 through Matsuoka Shunzo’s efforts, plus snow crystals, snow properties, and life in snow country.

  • Jingamine Citizens' Forest

    陣峰市民の森

    Shinjo, Yamagata · 0233-22-2111

    Forest of about 50 hectares at the foot of Mount Jingamine. The summit is a 50-minute walk from parking, and the path to Higashiyama Gymnasium is part of the Tohoku Nature Trail.

  • Yukiguni Wonderland

    雪国ワンダーランド（新庄市）

    Shinjo, Yamagata · 0233-22-2340

    Snow theme park with snow activities not usually experienced, including snowmobile rides and banana boats on the snow.

  • Mogami Joho Annai Center

    もがみ情報案内センター

    Shinjo, Yamagata · 0233-28-8881

    Visitor facility in Shinjo, Yamagata.

  • Shinjo Municipal Ski Area

    新庄市民スキー場

    Shinjo, Yamagata

    Family slope 15 minutes by car from Shinjo Station, used by skiers, snowboarders, and city elementary school ski classes. Includes a jump, a wide box, and other snow features.
