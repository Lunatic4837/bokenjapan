  • Higashiyama Park Hydrangea Forest

    Shinjo, Yamagata · 0233-22-2111

    Hydrangea is enacted in the flowers of Shinjo City as a symbol of the character of the snow country people with its powerful vitality.

  • Former JNR Shinjo Station Engine Shed

    旧国鉄新庄駅機関庫

    Shinjo, Yamagata

    Brick engine shed attached to Shinjo Station since the station opened in 1903. The building and its turntable are included in Yamagata's modernization industrial heritage.

  • Tozawa Shrine

    戸澤神社

    Shinjo, Yamagata

    Founded in 1893, it enshrines Tozawa founder Hiramori and Shinjo Domain founder Masamori on the former main bailey of Shinjo Castle, with the 11th lord Masazane added later.

  • Mount Kamuro

    神室山

    Shinjo, Yamagata

    A mountain of ascetic practice in the Kamuro range of northeastern Yamagata, inside Kurikoma Quasi-National Park, with a tengu legend. Its long ridge appears alpine in winter, and climbing routes are rated intermediate or advanced.
