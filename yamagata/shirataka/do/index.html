Things to do in Shirataka, Yamagata

Shirataka · Yamagata · 3 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Shirataka Yana Park

    Shirataka, Yamagata · 0238-85-5577

    A roadside station with a traditional fish weir on the Mogami River, popular for salt-grilled sweetfish and other ayu dishes; the Shirataka Ayu Festival is held in autumn.

  • Washi Papermaking and Shikishi Making

    和紙漉き、色紙づくり/白鷹町

    Shirataka, Yamagata

    Paper fibers are scooped from a vat with a screen to form washi sheets. Because drying takes time, finished pieces are shipped later.

  • Pottery Class (Miyama-yaki, Miyama Kobo Tsuchidango)

    陶芸教室（深山焼　深山工房つち団子）

    Shirataka, Yamagata

    In Miyama, visitors can hand-build with 500 kg of clay, use an electric potter's wheel, or paint pictures and characters on unglazed cups and plates.
