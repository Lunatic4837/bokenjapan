Things to do in Takahata, Yamagata

Takahata · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • Uriwari Stone Garden Park

    瓜割石庭公園

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-52-4482

    Past a cave, a stone wall about 30 m high stands at a Takahata-stone quarry. The site is also used for live performances and photo shoots.

  • Takahata Winery

    高畠ワイナリー

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-40-1840

    Uses Chardonnay and Delaware grapes from its own vineyards and local farmers, along with Yamagata fruit such as cherries, La France pears, and peaches.

  • Mahoroba Ancient Village Historical Park

    まほろば古の里歴史公園

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-57-3844

    Recreates Jomon living spaces and landscapes in an area inhabited since the Jomon period, where many ruins have been excavated.

  • Mahoroba Tourist Orchard

    まほろば観光果樹園

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-52-4366

    Cherry and grape picking with direct sales, and no reservation needed. Flat ground suits children, older visitors, and wheelchair users.

  • Hinata Cave

    日向洞窟

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-52-1111

    Treated as an ogre's rock shelter and kept off-limits in the Edo period; later visitors found stone arrowheads. The cave lies at 230 m on the foothills of a mountain commonly called Tateishi.

  • Yuki no Sato Sansan

    ゆうきの里さんさん

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-58-3060

    Log-house-style lodging built with Wada cedar by local craftspeople. Stay essentials are provided, and an agricultural-processing experience facility is also on site.

  • Hamada Hirosuke Memorial Museum

    浜田広介記念館

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-52-3838

    Memorial museum for Hamada Hirosuke, a Yamagata children's writer called Japan's Andersen. He published about 1,000 tales and songs over some 50 years, including Naita Aka Oni.

  • Nukanome Waterside School

    糠野目 水辺の楽校

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-57-3505

    Riverbed park in Nukanome, once developed by Mogami River boats. A pond about 30 cm deep has stepping stones and a wooden dam for young children, plus a stream and kappa pool from local tales.

  • Yamagata Prefectural Ukitamu Fudoki Hill Archaeological Museum

    山形県立うきたむ風土記の丘考古資料館

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-52-2585

    Displays Okitama finds centered on the Oshidashi site. Ukitamu is Okitama's old name in the Nihon Shoki; the area is a Jomon-to-Kofun park and a base for nationally designated caves such as Hinata.

  • Safflower Dyeing Experience

    紅花染め体験/高畠町

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-57-2140

    Safflower dyeing and Yonezawa weaving experiences, including dyeing an original handkerchief.
