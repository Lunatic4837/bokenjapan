See

  • Kameoka Monjudo

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-52-0444

    One of Japan's three great Monju temples. A Nio gate with guardian statues stands at the entrance to a cedar-lined approach, and statues of the Sixteen Arhats line the stone path to the main hall.

  • Takahata Winery

    Takahata, Yamagata · 0238-40-1840

    Among the fruit kingdoms of Yamagata, Takahata Town, where Takahata Winery is located,

  • Former Takahata Station

    旧高畠駅

    Takahata, Yamagata

    The station building was rebuilt in 1934 in local Takahata stone, unusual for a station. Period trains, tracks, and tools remain on display.

  • Takahata Station

    高畠駅

    Takahata, Yamagata

    A station on the Ou Main Line (Yamagata Line) and Yamagata Shinkansen, unusual for hot-spring facilities in the station building.

  • Akutsu Hachiman Shrine

    安久津八幡神社

    Takahata, Yamagata

    Yamagata's only three-story pagoda, a bugaku hall, and the main hall are prefecture-designated cultural properties along a mossy stone approach. Tradition dates an Amida hall here to 860, but the origin is uncertain.

  • Gyokuryuin Five Hundred Arhat Statues

    玉竜院五百羅漢像

    Takahata, Yamagata

    500 Arhat statues made in Tenpo 6 by Kyoto sculptors fill a Soto Zen hall, with 16 Arhats and 33 Kannon images. They are a town-designated cultural property, each said to have a distinct face.

  • Ichinosawa Cave

    一の沢洞窟

    Takahata, Yamagata

    A nationally designated historic site on a mountainside at 400 meters, northeast of Hiruzawa Lake. Finds show long use and a clear transition from the Incipient Jomon period to the Yayoi period.

  • Shifukuji Temple Site

    資福寺跡

    Takahata, Yamagata

    Founded in the Koan era (1278-1287) as a Date academic temple, where young Date Masamune is thought to have studied. After it moved to Sendai in 1591, moats, earthworks, and Date family graves remain.

  • Hatomine Plateau

    鳩峰高原

    Takahata, Yamagata

    An 800 m pass on the Fukushima border has an 86-hectare pasture where about 100 dairy cows graze each June. Ryugatake (994 m) enshrines a dragon deity, and Hirosuke Hamada's poem monument stands here.

  • Biruzawa Lake

    蛭沢湖

    Takahata, Yamagata

    A 25-hectare irrigation reservoir begun in 1936 and completed after 13 years. Spring snowmelt fills the green lake, a forest park and Kannon-iwa trail lie nearby, and the Yonezawa-Takahata cycling road ends here.

  • Mahoroba Greenway

    まほろばの緑道　(高畠駅～まほろば古の里歴史公園)

    Takahata, Yamagata

    Built on the former Takahata Railway and Yamagata Kotsu Takahata Line, discontinued in 1974, this greenway runs about 6 km and is planted with about 700 cherry trees and seasonal flowers and fruit trees.
