  • Original Tendo Sightseeing Orchard

    元祖　天童観光果樹園

    Tendo, Yamagata

    Guests pick seasonal fruit and may eat as much as they like while viewing a Japanese garden.

  • Tendo Wine Co., Ltd.

    天童ワイン株式会社

    Tendo, Yamagata

    In the grape-growing Yamagata Basin, this winery also makes wine from La France pears and cherries. Free 45-minute factory tours, for groups of 10 to 30 by reservation, include production talks and about five tastings.

  • Tendo Kogen Ski Area and Campground

    天童高原　スキー場　 /　 キャンプ場

    Tendo, Yamagata

    A ski area and campground where skiing is available in winter, with views of mountain ranges including Gassan, Hayama, and sometimes Chokaisan.

  • TENDO BREWERY

    Tendo Brewery

    Tendo, Yamagata

    A craft-beer brewery opened in 1999 inside Yubo Ichiraku at Tendo Onsen, using local groundwater and Yamagata ingredients. Beer is served at the inn and sold at nearby shops.

  • Dewazakura Sake Brewery

    出羽桜酒造株式会社

    Tendo, Yamagata

    Founded in 1892, this sake brewery has long made ginjo and released Oka Ginjo in 1980. Brewery tours are offered, and the sake is exported to 35 countries.
