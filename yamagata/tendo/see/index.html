Sights in Tendo, Yamagata

Tendo · Yamagata · 8 places

See

  • Wakamatsu-ji Temple

    Tendo, Yamagata · 023-653-4138

    Temple No. 1 of the Mogami 33 Kannon pilgrimage (Tendai sect). Legend says the Nara-period priest Gyoki heard a bell on the mountain, climbed it and saw a shining image of Kannon, and founded the temple there.

  • Nakano Fruits (Nakano Tourist Orchard)

    Tendo, Yamagata · 023-656-2775

    A popular Tendo orchard offering seasonal fruit picking, with an orchard-run café serving sweets made with fresh fruit; its fruit parfaits and shakes are especially popular.

  • Osho Orchard

    Tendo, Yamagata · 023-657-3211

    A fruit-picking orchard with a café, and the only place in Tendo offering greenhouse cherry picking. About 10 hectares of orchards, plus a fruit vending stand open 24 hours a day, all year.

  • Tendo Park Momiji-en Light-up

    天童公園もみじ園　ライトアップ

    Tendo, Yamagata

    A walking-path area linking the Atago-numa waterfront to near Mount Maizuru's summit, lit when cherries and maples are in season. The route is 250 meters long with a 52.4-meter rise and two shelters.

  • Shogi Village Tendo Tower

    将棋むら 天童タワー

    Tendo, Yamagata

    Marked by a giant shogi king piece, it sells decorative pieces and Yamagata specialties and offers shogi writing, soba making, pottery, safflower dyeing and painting. Basho Garden recreates scenic spots visited by Matsuo Basho.

  • Tendo City Museum of Art

    天童市美術館

    Tendo, Yamagata

    The collection of Japanese paintings is substantial. About five special exhibitions are held each year, with experience and collection exhibitions between them.

  • Jakushoji Temple

    若松寺

    Tendo, Yamagata

    Opened by Gyoki in 708 and later rebuilt by Ennin, it is first among the Mogami 33 Kannon temples. A matchmaking rite is held on the first Sunday from April to December, with no reservation needed.

  • Gokurakuen

    御苦楽園

    Tendo, Yamagata

    A Japanese garden was built over seven years from 1928 by the late Mitobe Yasaku as unemployment relief. Stone pillars carry sayings on life's joys and sorrows, and the scenery changes by season.
