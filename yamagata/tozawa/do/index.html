Do in Tozawa

Tozawa · Yamagata · 8 places

Do

  • Mogami Gorge Basho Line Boat Cruise

    （最上川舟下り）最上峡芭蕉ライン観光

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 0233-72-2001

    Boat cruise down the Mogami River through Mogami Gorge, with views from the boat and the boatman's song. Runs in all seasons; kotatsu boats operate from December to the end of March.

  • Mogami Gorge

    最上峡

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 0233-72-2110

    Sixteen-kilometer gorge on the middle Mogami River, from Furukuchi in Tozawa to Kiyokawa in Shonai. Mountains crowd both banks, and the scenery changes with the seasons.

  • Roadside Station Tozawa Koma-kan

    道の駅「とざわ」高麗館

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 0233-72-3303

    Roadside station in Tozawa, known for Mogami River boat cruises. Views from a low hill take in the Mogami River and fields; Korean-style buildings offer Korean ingredients, cosmetics, bibimbap, and cold noodles.

  • Mogami River Yoshitsune Romance Boat Cruise

    最上川舟下り義経ロマン観光

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 0234-57-2148

    Course that goes upstream through Mogami Gorge, then downstream, and on to Sennindo.

  • Ikiiki Land Ponpo-kan

    いきいきランド ぽんぽ館

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 0233-72-3600

    Large, medicinal, and sand baths plus a sauna are available. The sodium-chloride and bicarbonate spring is said to warm well and leave skin smooth. The second floor has heated pools and a 50-meter waterslide.

  • Activity spot in Tozawa

    農家レストラン安食

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 090-4046-3724

    The Ajiki family, local farmers for generations, opened this restaurant in their old home. Meals are cooked by the mother in a traditional folk-house space.

  • Sanzaemon Soba-Making Experience

    そば打ち体験(三左衛門そば)

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 0233-73-2182

    Made with 100% buckwheat grown in Tozawa. Beginners receive careful guidance and can make soba to their own taste.

  • Tozawa Village Youth Center

    戸沢村若者センター

    Tozawa, Yamagata · 0233-72-3594

    Visitors can play various sports on the baseball field and grounds. Battery-powered cars are available for children. Cherry trees around the site reach full bloom in spring.
