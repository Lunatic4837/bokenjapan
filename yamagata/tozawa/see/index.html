  • Shiraito Falls

    白糸の滝

    Tozawa, Yamagata

    A tiered waterfall dropping about 120 meters into the Mogami River, one of Japan's 100 selected waterfalls. Matsuo Basho wrote of it in Oku no Hosomichi, and a Fudo hall stands at its base.

  • Genso no Mori

    幻想の森

    Tozawa, Yamagata

    A grove of Tsuchiyu cedars, a type seen throughout the Mogami Gorge area, has trunks over 15 meters around thought to be more than 1,000 years old. Branches split from the base and rise upward.

  • Waterfall in Tozawa

    浄の滝

    Romaji: Jo Falls

    Tozawa, Yamagata

    An 80-meter stepped waterfall, long a symbol of mountain worship for ascetics bound for Dewa Sanzan. It can be reached by a 90-minute walk from early June to November, when daylilies and lilies may bloom.

  • Sannotaki Falls

    三ノ滝（戸沢村）

    Tozawa, Yamagata

    A waterfall of about 25 meters that slides down in many tiers. After leaf fall it can be seen through the trees without descending to the stream, and in winter from the bridge.
