  • Gassan Visitor Center

    月山ビジターセンター

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Exhibits and provides the latest information on the plants, animals, and hiking of Mount Haguro and Mount Gassan. Trekking, nature observation, and craft classes are held, and it has been open year-round since April 2013.

  • Katakuri Onsen Bonbo

    【かたくり温泉ぼんぼ】リフレッシュオープン！！

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A day-trip hot spring reopened by an NPO of Asahi-area residents, with a sauna and a 50-tatami hall. The water is said to help cuts, burns, chronic skin disease, neuralgia, and cold sensitivity.

  • Tsuruoka Bicycle Rental

    レンタサイクルつるおか

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Fifteen sightseeing bicycles for city-center touring are based at Tsuruoka City Tourist Information Center and must be returned the same day.

  • Choju Onsen

    長寿温泉

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A public hot-spring bath in a residential district beside Komagihara Park, available in the manner of a neighborhood sento.
