Do in Tsuruoka

Tsuruoka · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • Atsumi Onsen

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-43-3547

    Atsumi Onsen is a hot spring resort town with a pleasant atmosphere.

  • Kushibiki Taranokidai Ski Resort

    櫛引たらのきだいスキー場

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A ski area with courses from beginner to intermediate and advanced levels. Daytime views take in the Shonai Plain and the Sea of Japan, and night skiing overlooks the lights of Tsuruoka.

  • Yudonosan Ski Resort

    湯殿山スキー場

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Four courses range from a 300 m advanced run (35-degree maximum slope) to a gentle 2,000 m forest course. The slopes include a terrain park and R-shaped walls, plus a ski and snowboard school.

  • Hachimoriyama Ski Area

    八森山スキー場

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Tsuruoka's ski area closest to the Sea of Japan, with a T-bar lift. Its hut is open free as a rest stop, and the slope can be seen from the Nihonkai-Tohoku Expressway.

  • Picture Candle Painting Experience

    絵ろうそく絵付け体験

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    At Togashi Candle Shop, visitors paint historic picture candles once presented to the Tokugawa shogunate, also called flower-crest candles. Staff teach delicate hand-painted pigment patterns, and a candle can be finished in about one hour.

  • Hagurosan Ski Area

    羽黒山スキー場

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A ski area in Tsuruoka with views of Mount Gassan. It has beginner, intermediate and advanced courses and a separate slope for small children.

  • Stained Glass Making Experience

    ステンドグラス製作体験

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    At a workshop opposite Tsuruoka Catholic Church, visitors make stained-glass lampshades and kaleidoscopes with a soldering iron. Sessions take about two hours and accept elementary-school age and up.

  • Soba-Making Experience at Furusato Mura Takaradani

    そば打ち体験／ふるさとむら宝谷

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Visitors make and eat fresh handmade soba from locally grown buckwheat milled on a stone mill. Local farmers guide the session, so beginners can take part, and pickles and tempura use local ingredients.

  • Sososo Camp Nezugaseki Campground

    そそそCAMP～鼠ヶ関キャンプ場

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A seaside campground with the sea in front, where visitors can enjoy rock-pool play and rock fishing. Nearby are Marine Park Nezugaseki beach, Benten Island, Nezugaseki Lighthouse, and Nezugaseki fishing port.

  • Yamabushi Ascetic Training Experience

    山伏修行体験塾

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Wearing white robes, participants leave daily life to try part of yamabushi training and learn about Dewa Sanzan—Mt. Haguro, Mt. Gassan, and Mt. Yudono—and Shugendo.
