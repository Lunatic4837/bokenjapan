See in Tsuruoka

Tsuruoka · Yamagata · 45 places

See

  • Mt. Haguro

    Mt. Haguro

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-62-4727

    One of the Three Mountains of Dewa, with Mount Gassan and Mount Yudono, revered as sacred mountains for hundreds of years. Travel to them is said to be a journey of reincarnation.

  • Kamo Aquarium

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-33-3036

    Awarded a Guinness World Record for the world’s largest jellyfish collection

  • Mt. Gassan

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-62-4321

    Enjoy breath-taking views while trekking on this mountain judging reincarnation

  • Mt. Haguro Dewa Sanzan Sanjin Gosaiden

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-62-2355

    The Sanjin Gosaiden on the summit of Mt. Haguro enshrines the deities of all three Dewa Sanzan mountains: Gassan, for peace after death (the past); Haguro, for happiness in this life (the present); and Yudono, for rebirth (the future).

  • Yudonosan Shrine

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-54-6133

    This mysterious sacred location is the last stop of the Three Mountains of Dewa

  • Yunohama Onsen

    Yunohama Onsen

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-75-2258

    Hot spring hotels face the Sea of Japan, with sunset views from open-air baths.

  • Mt. Yudono

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-53-3411

    At 1,504 m and linked to Mt. Gassan, Mt. Yudono ranks with Ise and Kumano as one of Japan's three great sacred sites. In a gorge carved by the Bonji River on its northern slope stands the main sanctuary of Yudonosan Shrine, revered as a guardian of good harvests and household safety.

  • （国宝）羽黒山 五重塔 【12年分のご利益！ 令和８年「羽黒山午年御縁年」】

    Haguro-san Five-Story Pagoda

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A 29-meter unpainted five-story pagoda in a cedar avenue on the Mount Haguro approach, designated a National Treasure in 1966. Said to have been rebuilt about 600 years ago, it stands near giant cedar Jiji-sugi.

  • 月山八合目　弥陀ヶ原

    Gassan Eighth Station Midagahara

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    An alpine wetland at 1,400 m on Gassan, with more than 130 alpine plant species, including Nikko daylilies and skunk cabbage. A boardwalk loop of about 60 minutes can be reached by bus.

  • 本明寺

    Honmyoji Temple

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    It enshrines self-mummified monk Honmyo Kai Shonin, the oldest of six in Shonai, preserved with little damage and said to reflect strict wood-eating asceticism. A meditation mound and believers' monuments remain beside the hall.

  • 善宝寺

    Zenpoji Temple

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A Soto Zen dragon-god temple with Japan's only fish-scale memorial five-story pagoda. It keeps an 1833 Dragon King Hall, 1855 arhat hall, 1862 gate and Hishida Shunso's Important Cultural Property painting.

  • 出羽三山神社

    Dewa Sanzan Shrine

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A shrine on the summit of Mount Haguro jointly enshrining the deities of Mount Gassan, Mount Haguro, and Mount Yudono. Year-round rites for the three shrines are held here together.

  • 七ツ滝（鶴岡市田麦俣）

    Nanatsu Falls

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A 90-meter tiered waterfall on the Rokujurigoe Road in Bandai-Asahi National Park, one of Japan's 100 Waterfalls. Full view is visible from Nanatsu Falls Park, with fresh greenery and autumn leaves.

  • 致道博物館

    Chido Museum

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A museum in the former Sakai residence at Tsuruoka Castle's third bailey. Relocated buildings include the old Nishitagawa County Office, and the Sakai Clan Garden is a national Place of Scenic Beauty.

  • 随神門

    Zuishinmon Gate

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    The front entrance to the sacred precinct of Dewa Sanzan Shrine, enshrining gate deities that keep evil out. Called the Niomon until the Meiji era, it was donated in 1695 by the lord of Yashima.

  • 湯殿山 注連寺（真言宗 智山派）/ 庄内三十三観音 第31番

    Yudonosan Churenji Temple

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Founded by Kobo Daishi as a distant-worship site for women once barred from Yudonosan. This Shingon Chizan temple enshrines the self-mummified monk Tetsumonkai Shonin and is the 31st of the Shonai Thirty-Three Kannon.

  • 鶴岡公園

    Tsuruoka Park

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A park on the former Tsurugaoka Castle site, seat of the Sakai family for about 250 years. Moats, stone walls, and centuries-old cedars remain, with 730 cherry trees among Japan's 100 cherry sites.

  • 月山花笠ライン

    Gassan Hanagasa Line

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A scenic drive along the former Rokujurigoe Kaido, once a pilgrimage road to Dewa Sanzan. Fresh greenery and autumn leaves are beautiful, with viewpoints and sights along the route.

  • 鼠ヶ関

    Nezugaseki

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A coastal area in southwestern Tsuruoka on the Niigata border, said to be where Basho passed a checkpoint. The prefectural boundary runs through the built-up area, and fishing-town events are held.

  • 月山ダム

    Gassan Dam

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    The dam office information hall on Lake Asahi Gassan shows fish from the Aka River and models of the dam. An observation deck overlooks the lake, and interior dam tours are offered.

  • 山五十川の玉スギ

    Yamairagawa Tamasugi Cedar

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    On the grounds of Kumano Shrine in Yamairagawa, this cedar is said to be over 1,500 years old. About 36 m tall with a hemispherical crown, it was designated a national natural monument in 1951.

  • 出羽三山歴史博物館

    Dewa Sanzan Historical Museum

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Displays many materials on Haguro-san ascetic practice and faith, including 190 of 500 bronze mirrors unearthed from Kagami-ike and Buddhist statues, plus old documents related to Matsuo Basho.

  • 大山公園

    Oyama Park

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A park on the site of Oura Castle, seat of the Muto clan, completed in 1940. It has two lookouts and eight monuments, and is known for cherry blossoms and seasonal hydrangeas.

  • 熊野神社（鶴岡市）

    Kumano Shrine (Tsuruoka)

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Said to date from 672, when En no Gyoja enshrined a deity atop Mount Atsumi. Moved here in 1916, it was rebuilt in 1958 after the 1950 Atsumi Onsen fire.

  • 月山中の宮　御田原参篭所

    Gassan Nakanomiya Mitahara Sanrojo

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A prayer facility a 10-minute walk from the parking area at the eighth station of Mount Gassan. It accepts prayers and memorial services, but not lodging or meals.

  • スタジオセディック庄内オープンセット

    Studio Sedic Shonai Open Set

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A film studio open to the public where many movies and dramas were shot. A 2 km site holds post-town, fishing-village, farming-village, and mountain-settlement sets, plus photo displays and play areas.

  • 由良温泉

    Yura Onsen

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A coastal onsen area. Hakusan Island, its symbol, is reached by a vermilion bridge and has a fishing pond and a 40- to 60-minute loop; the bridge is lit in summer.

  • 羽黒山 正善院（羽黒山 修験本宗）/ 庄内三十三観音 第1番　

    Hagurozan Shozen-in Temple

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A Haguro Shugendo temple, said to have been founded by Jikaku Daishi, and the first stop on the Shonai 33 Kannon pilgrimage. Its Golden Hall, a national Important Cultural Property, houses 33 gilded Kannon statues.

  • マリンパークねずがせき海水浴場

    Marine Park Nezugaseki Beach

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A low-turbidity gravel beach with calm waves, listed by the Environment Ministry among 100 bathing beaches. It includes an artificial rocky shore, lawn and dusk views of Benten Island and the Japan Sea.

  • 念珠の松庭園

    Nenju no Matsu Garden

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A black pine in a 20-meter reclining-dragon form was planted about 400 years ago. Designated a Yamagata Prefecture natural monument in 1955, the present garden was completed in 1994 to a design by Ken Nakajima.

  • 横綱柏戸記念館

    Yokozuna Kashiwado Memorial Hall

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Built to honor Kashiwado, Tsuruoka honorary citizen and 47th yokozuna of the Showa Kashiwado-Taiho era. The former Kagamiyama stable practice ring and raised room from Tokyo were relocated here, with memorabilia and match videos.

  • 春日神社

    Kasuga Shrine

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Said to have been founded in 806, early in the Heian period. It is known as the shrine where Kurokawa Noh, a nationally designated Important Intangible Folk Cultural Property, is performed.

  • 由豆佐売神社

    Yuzusame Shrine

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Worshipped as Yudagawa Onsen's guardian and as a deity of hot springs and water. A prefecture-designated natural monument, a giant milk ginkgo, stands by the stone steps, and the grounds hold memorial poem stones.

  • 菅家庭園

    Sugake Garden

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Garden of the house granted to Sanehide Suge, a late-Edo to Meiji Shonai retainer who knew Takamori Saigo. Mid-Edo style remains, with a 350-year-old pine, weeping cherry, azaleas, and a shrine to Sugawara no Michizane.

  • 荘内神社

    Shonai Shrine

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Founded in 1877 on the former main bailey of Tsurugaoka Castle by people who revered the Sakai lords. A treasure hall shows related arms, crafts, and seasonal dolls, and seasonal flowers decorate a hand-water basin.

  • 朝日連峰

    Asahi Mountain Range

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Ito-dake (1,771.4 m) is the northern start and end of the traverse and overlooks Otori Pond, a 963 m granite lake up to 68 m deep, linked to the legendary fish Takitaro.

  • 大鳥居

    Great Torii

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A vermilion ryobu torii, 20 m high and 15 m wide, marks entry to Dewa Sanzan. Donated in 1929 by Tetsutaro Yoshioka of Yamagata City, the slope beyond is called Shinjizaka.

  • 金峯山 青龍寺（真言宗 豊山派）/ 庄内三十三観音 第33番

    Kinbozan Shoryu-ji Temple

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A Shingon Buzan-school temple and the 33rd stop of the Shonai Thirty-three Kannon pilgrimage. It enshrines Nyoirin Kannon, and Kinbozan is a prefecture-designated scenic site with a shrine.

  • あつみ温泉ばら園

    Atsumi Onsen Rose Garden

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A rose garden on high ground overlooking the Atsumi Onsen town, planted with about 90 varieties and 3,000 bushes that bloom from June to October. A rose festival is held each June.

  • 小国城跡

    Oguni Castle Ruins

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Mountain-castle ruins on Tateyama (348.5 m), about 10 km southwest of JR Atsumi Onsen Station, estimated to date from the 14th century and abandoned in 1615. Earthwork ramparts on the summit are considered unusual.

  • 山形県・新潟県境標

    Yamagata-Niigata Prefecture Border Marker

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A stone monument on the border between Yamagata and Niigata prefectures. It blends into a residential street so fully that the border is easy to miss, and it has appeared on Tetsuwan DASH.

  • 三瀬海水浴場

    Sanze Beach

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    A swimming beach near Route 7, with varied strange rocks and a natural rock diving platform. It is popular with families, photographers visit for the Tateiwa sunset, and it was a film location.

  • 弁天島

    Benten Island

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Tradition says Minamoto no Yoshitsune landed by boat at Nezugaseki while fleeing to Ou. Itsukushima Shrine stands on the island, and a promenade overlooks the Sea of Japan.

  • 鶴岡市立藤沢周平記念館　

    Tsuruoka City Shuhei Fujisawa Memorial Museum

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    Exhibits manuscripts, materials, and personal items of historical novelist Shuhei Fujisawa and recreates his Tokyo study. It also presents the Kaisaka Domain, said to model Edo-period Shonai, plus local nature and history in his works.

  • 鼠ヶ関　厳島神社

    Nezugaseki Itsukushima Shrine

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata

    The shrine stands on Benten Island, a scenic spot in Nezugaseki, Tsuruoka, and enshrines Ichikishima-hime. Its mikoshi-nagashi festival is held every April 15, and a path leads to Nezugaseki Lighthouse.
