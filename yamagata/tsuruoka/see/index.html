  • Mt. Haguro Dewa Sanzan Sanjin Gosaiden

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-62-2355

    The Sanjin Gosaiden on the summit of Mt. Haguro enshrines the deities of all three Dewa Sanzan mountains: Gassan, for peace after death (the past); Haguro, for happiness in this life (the present); and Yudono, for rebirth (the future).

  • Mt. Yudono

    Tsuruoka, Yamagata · 0235-53-3411

    At 1,504 m and linked to Mt. Gassan, Mt. Yudono ranks with Ise and Kumano as one of Japan's three great sacred sites. In a gorge carved by the Bonji River on its northern slope stands the main sanctuary of Yudonosan Shrine, revered as a guardian of good harvests and household safety.
