Things to do in Yamagata City, Yamagata

Yamagata City · Yamagata · 12 places

Do

  • Zao Onsen / Zao Hot Springs / Zao Onsen Mountain & Snow Resort

    Yamagata City, Yamagata · 023-694-9328

    Zao Onsen / Zao Hot Springs / Zao Onsen Mountain & Snow Resort

  • Omoshiroyama Highlands

    Yamagata City, Yamagata · 023-641-1212

    Spreading below Mt. Omoshiro on the Yamagata–Miyagi border, east of Yamadera, with trekking and hiking from spring to autumn and the Cosmos Berg cosmos field, at its best around September.

  • Zao Onsen Snow Resort

    Yamagata City, Yamagata · 023-694-9617

    https://yamagatakanko.com/en/attractions/detail_7455.html

  • MINT Pairing Workshop

    【MINT】ペアリングワークショップ

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    Visitors make silver or brass rings, choosing width and texture, in a session of about two hours and can usually take them home the same day. Craftsmen who make wedding rings provide support.

  • Otokoyama Sake Brewery

    男山酒造

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    Founded in the Kansei era, this sake brewery is known for brands such as Uyo Otokoyama. Brewery visits are available.

  • Super Slider and Paintball

    スーパースライダー・ペイントボール

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A green-season activity at Zao Onsen. Visitors ride a summer mountain lift to the mid-slope, then slide down to the base on the Super Slider.

  • Yamagata Maiko and Yamagata Geiko

    やまがた舞子・やまがた芸子

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    They train in dance, song, and shamisen and appear at restaurant banquets, hotel parties, and tourism events. Requests are made through Yamagata Beni no Kai or sponsoring venues.

  • Yamadera-yaki Nagase Pottery Studio

    陶芸山寺焼 ながせ陶房

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A pottery studio at the entrance to the Yamadera approach path, offering hands-on pottery classes.

  • Safflower Dyeing Experience at Kameya Plant Dye Workshop

    【草木染工房瓶屋】紅花染め体験

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A safflower dyeing experience held at a plant dye workshop in Hiraishimizu, Yamagata City.

  • Kameya Plant Dye Workshop

    草木染工房瓶屋

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A plant dye workshop in Hiraishimizu, Yamagata City, offering craft experiences and souvenir items.

  • Zao Country Club

    蔵王カントリークラブ

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A golf course at the foot of Zao with a 60-year history. From mid-April through Golden Week, cherry blossoms color the entire course.

  • Koryuko Campground

    古竜湖キャンプ場

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    Campground in the Zao Yamada district of Yamagata City.
