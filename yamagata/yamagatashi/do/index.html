    Yamagata City, Yamagata

  • Super Slider and Paintball

    スーパースライダー・ペイントボール

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A green-season activity at Zao Onsen. Visitors ride a summer mountain lift to the mid-slope, then slide down to the base on the Super Slider.
