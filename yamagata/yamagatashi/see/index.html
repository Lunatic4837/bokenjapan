  • 山形県総合文化芸術館

    Yamagata Prefectural Cultural and Arts Hall

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A cultural complex at JR Yamagata Station's west exit, centered on a 2,001-seat hall and finished with local crafts. A shop sells goods from 35 municipalities, and a restaurant uses prefectural ingredients.

  • 蔵王温泉大露天風呂

    Zao Onsen Large Open-Air Bath

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A rustic streamside open-air bath in Zao Onsen, using a strongly acidic sulfur spring. Showers, soap, and shampoo are not available, and metal jewelry should be removed because the water corrodes it.

  • 面白山紅葉川渓谷

    Omoshiroyama Momijigawa Gorge

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A gorge below Mount Omoshiroyama on the Yamagata-Miyagi border, with a roughly 2 km trail, waterfalls, and a suspension bridge. Autumn colors the trees red and yellow; the path is narrow and can be slippery.

  • 山寺芭蕉記念館

    Yamadera Basho Memorial Museum

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A Japanese-style museum on a hill overlooking Yamadera, built to mark 300 years since Basho's visit. It displays his original writings; the rear lookout shows Risshaku-ji in cherry blossom, summer green, autumn leaves, and snow.

  • 黒沢温泉

    A quiet hot-spring district that opened in 1970 among farmland.

  • 霞城セントラル

    Kajo Central

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

  • 蔵王温泉共同浴場

    Zao Onsen Communal Baths

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    Three communal baths, Kamiyu, Shimoyu, and Kawarayu, are located in the Zao Onsen district. Shimoyu has a foot bath.

  • 山形県立博物館

    Yamagata Prefectural Museum

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    Opened in 1971 inside Kajo Park on the Yamagata Castle site, it presents prefectural nature, history, and culture through geology, plants, animals, archaeology, history, and folklore. An education-materials annex and nature study garden are attached.

  • 蔵王中央高原

    Zao Central Highlands

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A highland plateau in the Zao Onsen area of Yamagata City, with marshes such as Dokko Marsh, Mount Torikabuto, and a primeval beech forest.

  • 山形酒のミュージアム＆湯けむり屋台つまみ

    Yamagata Sake Museum and Yukemuri Yatai Tsumami

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    In Zao Onsen, visitors compare sakes from Yamagata breweries and look for a bottle that suits them. An attached stall serving imoni and other dishes is currently closed.

  • 長谷堂城跡公園

    Hasedo Castle Ruins Park

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    A park at the Hasedo Castle ruins and castle mountain in Yamagata City. Volunteer guides cover historic sites in the park.

  • 山形県産業科学館

    Yamagata Prefectural Industrial Science Museum

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    Exhibits introduce advanced technology from prefectural companies and let visitors try scientific principles. Hands-on devices cover the environment, a corner offers simple crafts, and panels on a spiral staircase trace the prefecture's industrial history.

  • 山形美術館

    Yamagata Museum of Art

    Yamagata City, Yamagata

    Beside the East Otemon Gate of Kajo Park, it shows special exhibitions plus permanent displays of Edo-to-modern Japanese art, local art, and French paintings. It is also used for presentations by local cultural groups.

    About 200 cherry trees line the embankment of the Mamigasaki River, forming a blossom tunnel along the riverside path in spring. Part of the row is illuminated at night.
