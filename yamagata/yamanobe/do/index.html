Things to do in Yamanobe, Yamagata

Yamanobe · Yamagata · 7 places

Do

  • Yamanobe Onsen Recreation Center

    山辺温泉保養センター

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-664-7777

    Opened in 1983 and renovated to be fully barrier-free in 2005. Baths use 100% free-flowing water from a sodium chloride spring and a simple hot spring.

  • Kitsune Hitomeguri Cycling Road

    きつね一巡りサイクリングロード

    Yamanobe, Yamagata

    Regional cycling model route of the Yamagata Prefecture Bicycle Network Plan along the Kitsune Hitomeguri road.

  • Kenmin no Mori

    県民の森

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-666-2116

    Opened in 1981 for the prefecture's centenary, to promote health, rest, and nature conservation. Onuma, Aranuma, and smaller lakes lie among a learning hall, woodcraft workshop, field athletics, a campground, and trails.

  • Furusato Exchange Center Agarasshai

    ふるさと交流センター「あがらっしゃい」

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-665-8582

    Relocated and renovated from the Meiji-era inn Kakuni Ryokan, it stands beside the town furusato museum and keeps the old appearance. The first floor is a casual space for shopping, walks, and meeting up.

  • Yamanobe Town Furusato Museum

    山辺町ふるさと資料館

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-664-5033

    The restored storehouse of the Sato Seigoro family, merchants in safflower and ramie since the Edo period, opened in 1994. Three storehouses and a Japanese garden hold displays of local history and special exhibitions.

  • Yamagata Golf Club

    山形ゴルフ倶楽部

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-666-2221

    Golf course in Yamanobe, Yamagata.

  • Oriental Carpet Co., Ltd.

    オリエンタルカーペット株式会社

    Yamanobe, Yamagata

    A carpet maker producing Yamagata Dantsu, handwoven carpets developed from techniques passed directly by Chinese craftspeople, with in-house design, dyeing, weaving, and aftercare. Factory tours to see the making process require a reservation.
