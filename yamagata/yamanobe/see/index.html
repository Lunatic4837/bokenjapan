  • 玉虫沼

    Tamamushi Pond

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-667-1110

    Irrigation pond in Owarabi, built in the late Muromachi period by Takeda Nobuyasu for villagers short of water. Kept as a rural park, it joined a national list of 100 irrigation ponds in 2010.

  • 畑谷城址

    Hataya Castle Ruins

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-667-1110

    Mountain castle west of Yamagata Castle, on the Kitsunegoe Road from Okitama. In 1600, lord Eguchi Gohei Mitsukiyo died fighting Naoe Kanetsugu’s Uesugi force of 20,000.

  • 龍神水

    Ryujin Water

    Yamanobe, Yamagata · 023-667-1110

    Spring water flows without stopping from the mouth of a stone dragon statue at Koyasu Shrine in Yanazawa. Tradition says a dragon god flew to the shrine, linked with prayers for children and safe birth.

  • 山野辺城址

    Yamanobe Castle Ruins

    Yamanobe, Yamagata

    Completed in 1601 as a low hill castle with four moats and outworks for Yamanobe Yoshitada, Mogami Yoshiaki’s fourth son. Little remains; nearby roads were staggered to prevent a straight approach.
