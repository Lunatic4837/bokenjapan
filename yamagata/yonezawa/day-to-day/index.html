Day to day in Yonezawa

Yonezawa · Yamagata · 3 places

Day to day

  • Michi-no-Eki Yonezawa

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-40-8400

    A roadside station styled on the castle town of Yonezawa, with tourist exhibits, a nine-screen video wall of local films and a general tourist information desk that can help with sightseeing and accommodation bookings.

  • 道の駅「田沢」なごみの郷

    Roadside Station Tazawa Nagomi no Sato

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A roadside station in Tazawa, Yonezawa, on National Route 121 before the Otoge Rainbow Line, with a 200-year-old traditional house. It sells local produce, Yonezawa beef products, and Sasano carvings, and serves handmade soba.

  • 米沢織物直売所おりじん

    Yonezawa Textile Direct Sales Shop Orijin

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A Yonezawa-textile direct-sales shop that operated at Yoneori Kaikan until autumn 2025 and reopened near Minami-Yonezawa Station in March 2026.
