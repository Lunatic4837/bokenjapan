  • Mugiwara Boshi (Tourist Chestnut Orchard)

    麦わらぼうし（観光くり園）

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Visitors can see herbs, flowers, and animals in the garden and greenhouse, and eat handmade sweets on the terrace. In autumn, chestnut picking is offered in an orchard on the back hill.

  • Yottette Caffe (Nakayama Orchard)

    よってってCAFFE（中山果樹園）

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    An orchard growing cherries, apples, and La France pears for counter sales and shipping. Cherry picking is offered from mid-June to late June.

  • Magariya Nadera Soba-Making Experience

    曲家なでら（そば打ち体験）

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A soba-making experience in Yonezawa City, in the Okitama region.

  • Onogawa Onsen Ski Area

    小野川温泉スキー場

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A ski area in Yonezawa with wide gentle slopes for beginners and a dedicated sledding space popular with children. Many families visit, and the hot-spring town offers a place to relax.

  • Yonezawa Ski Area

    米沢スキー場

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Winter sports such as skiing and snowboarding can be enjoyed here. Ski experiences and forest-school programs are also possible.

  • Wagatsuma Tourist Fruit Orchard

    我妻観光果樹園

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Reduced-pesticide cherries and grapes can be picked without a reservation, with all-you-can-eat, including on rainy days. Dogs are welcome; the 2026 cherry picking opens on June 7.
