Yonezawa · Yamagata · 21 places

  • Uesugi Jinja Shrine

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-22-3189

    Receive some good luck at the shrine of Kenshin Uesugi, one of the most powerful warriors of the civil war period

  • Yonezawa Castle Ruins / Matsugasaki Park

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-21-6226

    Travel back to Japan’s civil war period at this park with beautiful moats in the hometown of the Uesugi clan!

  • Namegawaotaki Fall

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-22-5111

    One of Japan's 100 best waterfalls: 80 m high and 40 m wide, among the largest in Tohoku. A viewing platform is about 30 minutes' climb up the trail from Namegawa Onsen.

  • Kinoene Daikokuten Honzan Hoju-ji Temple

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-32-2929

    https://yamagatakanko.com/en/attractions/detail_2863.html

  • Denkoku No Mori Yonezawa City Uesugi Museum

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-26-8001

    Denkoku No Mori Yonezawa City Uesugi Museum

  • Shirabu Falls

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-22-5111

    A famous waterfall in Shirabu Onsen(Shirabu Hot spring). The majestic appearance with a height of 30m is quite spectacular.

  • Kasugasan Rinsenji Temple

    Yonezawa, Yamagata · 0238-23-0601

    The Uesugi family temple of the Yonezawa domain, founded by Nagao Yoshikage, grandfather of Uesugi Kenshin. It was first built at the foot of Kasugayama Castle in present-day Joetsu, Niigata, and moved to Yonezawa with the clan.

  • Toko no Sakagura Sake Brewing Museum

    酒造資料館　東光の酒蔵

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A sake museum in Yonezawa at Kojima Sohonten, founded in 1597 as a purveyor to the Yonezawa domain's Uesugi family. The brewery shop offers tastings, including higher-grade and award-winning sake.

  • Tengendai Plateau

    天元台高原

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A 1,300-meter plateau in Yonezawa with clear-day views toward Zao, Chokai, Gassan, Iide, and Asahi, and a short approach to Mount Azuma. Trekking, autumn foliage, and skiing from late November into Golden Week are available.

  • Uesugi Memorial Hall (Former Uesugi Count's Residence)

    上杉記念館（旧上杉伯爵邸）

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    First built in 1896 and rebuilt in cypress in 1925 after the Yonezawa fire, this former count's residence is a registered cultural property. It serves the local dish katemono beside a garden modeled on Hama-rikyu.

  • Uesugi Family Mausoleum

    上杉家廟所

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Tombs of Yonezawa's successive Uesugi lords, from Kenshin to the 12th lord Narisada, stand among cedars. Kenshin's remains were moved here in 1876; snow lanterns are set out in mid-February.

  • Mount Nishi-Azuma

    西吾妻山

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    The highest peak of the Azuma range stands over 2,000 m on the Fukushima-Yamagata border and is one of Japan’s 100 Famous Mountains. The Tengendai Ski Area ropeway and lift make the ascent relatively easy.

  • Domori Zenkoji Temple

    堂森善光寺

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A temple linked to Maeda Keiji, said to have kept a hermitage here late in life, with a memorial tower on the grounds. It holds prefecture-designated Buddhist statues and is planted with hydrangeas.

  • Onariyama Park

    御成山公園

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A hilltop park with 1,000 cherry trees in spring. An observation deck looks over Yonezawa and mountains including Azuma, Zao, and Oasahi, and the grounds include a ski area and ski jumps.

  • Soryukyo Lookout (Aka Falls and Kuro Falls)

    双竜峡展望台（赤滝・黒滝）

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Aka Falls and Kuro Falls are seen at a distance from a lookout just past the Nishi-Azuma Tunnel. Both are 100-meter branching falls, viewable from June to early November.

  • Onogawa Onsen Firefly Park

    小野川温泉ほたる公園

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A park about 200 meters from the onsen district where fireflies fly from mid-June to mid-July. Viewing is most suitable around 8 to 9 p.m.

  • Local sight in Yonezawa

    宮坂考古館

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    A museum of over 700 archaeological, historical, and folk items from Yonezawa and Okitama, gathered by former director Miyasaka Zensuke. It includes 56 armor suits, some attributed to Uesugi, Naoe, and Maeda lords.

  • Former Yonezawa Higher Technical School Main Building

    旧米沢高等工業学校本館

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Opened in 1910 and designated an Important Cultural Property in 1973, it is a Renaissance-style wooden two-story building. It is the only structure on the Yamagata University engineering campus that retains the school's original appearance.

  • Shirako Shrine

    白子神社

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    It enshrines two kami, a fire deity and Haniyamahime. City-designated tangible cultural properties here include two Heian-period oni-gawara tiles, two copies of Uesugi Yozan's frugality oath, and a Shirako Daimyojin plaque.

  • 小野川温泉共同浴場 「尼湯」 「滝の湯」

    Onogawa Onsen Communal Baths Ama-yu and Taki-no-yu

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Communal baths Ama-yu and Taki-no-yu in Onogawa Onsen, Yonezawa. The hot-spring area is called Yonezawa's inner retreat and was discovered by Ono no Komachi.

  • 普門院

    Fumon-in

    Yonezawa, Yamagata

    Shingon temple where Uesugi Yozan rested with his teacher Hosoi Heishu after welcoming him at Haguro Shrine eight kilometers from the castle. The room remains; the site was designated a national historic site in 1935.
