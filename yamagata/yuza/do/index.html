Things to do in Yuza, Yamagata

Yuza · Yamagata · 10 places

Do

  • Michi-no-Eki Chokai Flat

    道の駅鳥海 ふらっと

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-71-7222

    Roadside station on National Route 7 with fast food, souvenirs, ramen, and a bakery. The direct-sales shop offers vegetables and processed foods, including Yuza melon, negi, yam, daikon, white eggplant, and kolinky.

  • Kamaiso Beach

    釜磯海水浴場

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-5886

    Fresh water, not seawater, springs from the sand and also wells up on the seabed. Visitors can watch the springs up close and swim in both the sea and the cold spring water.

  • Hands-on craft workshop in Yuza

    農林漁業体験実習館「さんゆう」 鳥海山の湧水を堪能♪

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-4500

    Hands-on craft workshop in Yuza, Yamagata.

  • Takase Gorge

    高瀬峡

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-5666

    Route at the foot of Mount Chokai spanning Hinokizawa, Babazawa, and Karasawa, with waterfalls and streams. From Fujii, five minutes by car on a forest road reaches parking with a mountain-god shrine.

  • Nishihama Beach

    西浜海水浴場

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-5666

    Swimming beach on the northern Shonai coast, backed by Mount Chokai, with a straight north-south shoreline, wide white sand, and pine woods. A campground and cottages stand nearby.

  • Nishihama Cottage Village

    西浜コテージ村

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-77-3600

    Pots and tableware are provided, so bring personal items, a change of clothes, and food. Families and friends use the cottages for overnight stays, barbecues, and parties, with a pine-forest campsite in front.

  • Salmon Grabbing Contest (2025 Cancelled)

    【2025年中止】鮭のつかみどり大会

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-5666

    The 30th salmon grabbing contest, scheduled for October 26, was cancelled after a mother bear and cubs were confirmed nearby. Visitor safety was given priority.

  • Nishihama Campground

    西浜キャンプ場

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-77-3600

    Set in a pine forest, so sun and wind are not a concern. A beach and day-trip onsen are nearby, and families and children's groups use it in summer.

  • Soba-Making Experience at the Foot of Mount Chokai

    鳥海山の麓で体験するおもしろ自然塾「そば打ち体験」

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-2069

    Soba making with flour grown in the Kanamata district, from mixing water through kneading, rolling, and cutting. Afterward, visitors eat soba that is freshly milled, made, and boiled.

  • Yuza Traditional Sasamaki Making Experience

    遊佐の伝統郷土食『笹巻き』づくり体験

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-2069

    Sasamaki is a traditional local food passed down in Yuza and made in various shapes. Participants try Yuza’s takenoko-maki form while residents teach each wrapping step.
