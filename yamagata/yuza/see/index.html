Sights in Yuza, Yamagata

Yuza · Yamagata · 13 places

See

  • Mt. Chokai

    Yuza, Yamagata · 0234-72-5886

    Admire the graceful snow-capped mountain from below and enjoy uninterrupted views from the summit!

  • Ichinotaki Falls

    Yuza, Yamagata

    From a red torii across from the car park, a walk through beech forest leads to Ichinotaki Shrine; iron steps nearby descend to a close view of the roughly 20 m falls, and the trail continues to Ninotaki Falls.

  • Juroku Rakan Rocks

    十六羅漢岩

    Yuza, Yamagata

    Twenty-two Buddhist figures carved into rocks washed by the Japan Sea, completed in 1868 after five years of work begun in 1864. Sixteen arhats are accompanied by Shakyamuni, Manjusri, Samantabhadra, Kannon, and two disciples.

  • Chokai Blue Line

    鳥海ブルーライン　2025年4月25日開通

    Yuza, Yamagata

    A 34.9 km road climbs from sea level to 1,100 m, overlooking Mt. Chokai, the Sea of Japan and the Shonai Plain. Closed early November to late April; snow corridors last late April to mid-May.

  • Dohara Falls

    胴腹滝

    Yuza, Yamagata

    Spring water from Mt. Chokai gushes from the rocky slope in two streams said to taste different. The fall drops about 3 m, does not freeze in winter, and the water is untreated.

  • Chokaisan Omonoimi Shrine Fukura Kuchi-no-Miya

    鳥海山大物忌神社吹浦口ノ宮

    Yuza, Yamagata

    Said to date from Emperor Kinmei's 25th year, this is a village shrine of Dewa Province's ichinomiya, whose main sanctuary is on the Mount Chokai summit. Fukura Dengaku is offered on 5 May.

  • Ushiwatari River

    牛渡川

    Yuza, Yamagata

    Spring water from Mount Chokai wells up along the banks of this clear river. On windless days dark green baikamo sways in the current, and salmon run upstream in autumn.

  • Chokai Sanshin Spring

    鳥海三神の水

    Yuza, Yamagata

    A long-used spring on the upper Gekko River in Yuza, reachable by car. Water was piped into the San'yu experience hall, which has a direct-sales shop; Gekko Dam lies along the approach.

  • Chokaisan Omonoimi Shrine Main Hall

    鳥海山大物忌神社　本殿

    Yuza, Yamagata

    The main hall stands at the summit of Mount Chokai, an active volcano revered as Dewa Province's ichinomiya. Tradition dates the shrine to 564, and a 20-year rebuilding, like Ise's, was last held in 1997.

  • Chokaisan Omonoimi Shrine Warabioka Kuchinomiya

    鳥海山大物忌神社蕨岡口ノ宮

    Yuza, Yamagata

    One of two foothill shrines of the summit sanctuary, said to date from Emperor Kinmei's 25th year, more than 1,400 years ago. It enshrines Omonoimi Okami; the May 3 festival is the Omitegura Festival.

  • Sato Seiyo Monument

    佐藤政養碑

    Yuza, Yamagata

    It honors Sato Seiyo (1821–1877) of Yuza, the first Assistant of Railways. He recommended Yokohama for a new port and in 1872 ran Japan's first train from Shimbashi to Yokohama.

  • Former Aoyama Main Residence

    旧青山本邸

    Yuza, Yamagata

    A Meiji-era mansion built in his hometown of Yuza by Aoyama Tomekichi, who prospered in Hokkaido fisheries. Designated a national Important Cultural Property in 2000, it preserves characteristic Meiji architecture.

  • Kamiko no Mizu

    神泉の水

    Yuza, Yamagata

    Spring water that rises in mountains east of the settlement and was piped in for daily life. It is divided into six tiers, used from drinking water down to washing farm tools.
