Things to do in Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi

Fujiyoshida · Yamanashi · 8 places

Do

  • Fuji-Q Highland

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-23-2111

    Since summer 2018, this free-entry amusement park has offered thrill rides and themed areas.

  • Mount Fuji Radar Dome Museum

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-20-0223

    A museum in the Fujiyoshida Roadside Station area displaying the Mount Fuji weather radar and a summit cold experience.

  • Mount Fuji

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-1111

    Mount Fuji is a mountain.

  • Kaneyama Falls

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-21-1000

    This Katsura River waterfall among Fuji lava rocks stays cool even in summer.

  • Tsutsujigahara

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-1111

    Mixed azalea and Fuji cherry communities along Fuji's Yoshida Trail form a national natural monument.

  • Mount Shakushi

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-21-1000

    Mount Shakushi, a Yamanashi 100 mountain in the Daibosatsu-Doshi range, offers open views of Mount Fuji.

  • Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Shrine

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-0221

    A Fuji World Heritage site and northern trailhead, with eleven precinct buildings designated Important Cultural Properties.

  • Hasu Pond (Myoken Lake)

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-1111

    Hasu Pond is also known as Myoken Lake.
