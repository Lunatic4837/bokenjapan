  • Mika

    美香

    Dining in Fujiyoshida

    Near the Showa University entrance on Route 139, it serves meat-and-kinpira udon for 550 yen in a broth that includes shrimp, with garden-view rooms; open 11:00-16:00 with no closing day.

  • Kaika

    開花

    Dining in Fujiyoshida

  • Akasaka Udon

    あかさかうどん

    Dining in Fujiyoshida

    This Yoshida udon shop looks toward Fuji-Q Highland and Mt. Fuji; meat udon is listed as the most popular dish, bamboo-charcoal black noodles are served, and a high-school collaboration menu is 500 yen; open 11:00-15:00.

  • Genji

    源氏

    Dining in Fujiyoshida

  • Tenseiya

  • Ono Udon

  • Mendokoro Ikkyu

    めん処　一休

    Dining in Fujiyoshida

    It serves handmade Yoshida udon with medium-thick noodles in a light soy broth, seating about 40 at tatami and tables; open 11:00-15:00 and 17:00-21:00, closed on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Sundays.

  • Hoshiya

    奉仕や

    Dining in Fujiyoshida

    Meat tsuke-udon is 500 yen, horse meat is sweet and tender, and there are 52 tatami, counter and table seats; open 11:00-14:00 and closed Tuesdays.

  • Teuchi Udon Hanaya

    手打ちうどん　はなや

    Dining in Fujiyoshida

    Founded around Meiji 25 (1892), it is the oldest udon shop in the Yoshida area and serves thin noodles, including yumori for 350 yen, with a glass-fronted noodle workshop.
