Sights in Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi

Fujiyoshida · Yamanashi · 18 places

See

  • Yoshida Tainai Lava Tree Molds

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-1111

    Yoshida Tainai Lava Tree Molds is a lava tree-mold site.

  • Arakurayama Sengen Park

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-21-1000

    A world-famous view pairs Mount Fuji with a vermilion five-story pagoda.

  • Fuji Hokuroku Park

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-24-3651

    It has athletics, ball-game and baseball fields, gymnasiums, an indoor track, and training rooms.

  • Fujiyama Museum

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-8223

    Art museum, completed in July 2003, displaying Mt. Fuji paintings collected over 40 years by Fuji Kyuko and the Horiuchi Koankai foundation; open 10:00-17:30, last entry 17:00.

  • Onshirin Garden

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-3355

    Onshirin Garden features a Western-style flower garden and a maintained lawn.

  • Rural Park

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-1111

    Visitors can see Mount Fuji and its reflection in rice paddies near planting time.

  • Biodiversity Center

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-72-6031

    This Environment Ministry center on Mount Fuji's north side explains Japan's biodiversity.

  • Fuji-ko Prayer Burning

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi

    Fuji-ko, founded by Hasegawa Kakugyo, still worships Mount Fuji and flourished in Edo times.

  • Fujisangogome Tembo Plaza

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 055-223-1316

    Observation plaza at the Fuji Subaru Line fifth station, behind Komitake Shrine and set back from the mountain so the summit side and eastern sunrise are visible; hours match the Subaru Line.

  • Fujiyoshida Municipal Suwa-no-Mori Nature Park

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-24-0292

    Seasonal hours run from 7:30 to 19:00 in summer and 8:30 to 17:00 from October to mid-May.

  • Yamanashi Mount Fuji Research Institute

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-72-6211

    Wheelchair access, first aid, step-free routes, accessible toilets, elevators, and an AED are available.

  • Former Togawa Family Oshi House

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi

    The Former Togawa Family Oshi House is a historic residence.

  • Fujisan Museum

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-24-2411

    A 2023 VR theater, Fuji model mapping, and faith materials present Fuji history.

  • Fuji Sansaku Park

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-1111

    Park facing Mt. Fuji with slope walking paths and a lawn plaza, open 8:30-17:00, until 19:00 from May 15 to August 31 and until 18:30 in September.

  • Mount Fuji Komitake Shrine

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-72-1475

    Founded in 937 at Fuji's fifth station, the shrine honors a tengu as a path-opening god.

  • Wild Goose Cave

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-21-1000

    Designated a national natural monument in 1932, it is a scattered group of tree molds.

  • Yoshida Kuchi Tozando

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-22-1111

    Park facing Mt. Fuji with slope walking paths and a lawn plaza, open 8:30-17:00, until 19:00 from May 15 to August 31 and until 18:30 in September.

  • Suwa no Mori Nature Park [ Fuji Painzu Park ]

    Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi · 0555-24-0292

    Municipal nature park a five-minute walk from Kitaguchi Hongu Fuji Sengen Shrine, with walking paths, a forest-bathing course, lawns, and sports fields; parking holds 40 cars.
