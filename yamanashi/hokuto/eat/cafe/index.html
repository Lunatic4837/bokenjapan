  • Dog Run Cafe Fleur

  • TeamSherpa

    TeamSherpa

    Dining in Hokuto

    A cafe by a log house below Yatsugatake serves vegetable curry with 12-plus vegetables and ancient rice, rock-salt onigiri and seasonal noodles, open 10:00 until the sun sets behind the Minami Alps, closed Tue-Thu.

  • Seisenryo Farm Shop

    清泉寮ファームショップ

    Dining in Hokuto

    A cafe-restaurant in a pasture serving Harvest Curry with over 10 vegetables, organic Jersey-butter chicken doria, pancakes and Seisenryo soft cream; open 9:00-17:00 (L.O. 15:30), with temporary winter closures.
