  • フランス菓子・喫茶　ドゥ・ミール

    Furansu-gashi Kissa Du Miru

    Dining in Hokuto

    It serves an Akeno Kintoki tart and seasonal cakes, with coffee in the cafe space; it is open 10:00-18:30 and closed on Tuesdays.

  • タルト工房 アルウェット

    Taruto Kobo Aruwetto

    Dining in Hokuto

    It serves strawberry, Akeno Kintoki, and dekopon-hyuganatsu tarts, plus mont blanc, pudding and cream puffs, and has eat-in seating; it is open 11:00-18:00 and closed Tuesday to Thursday.

  • ぱんの店 虹

    Pan no Mise Niji

    Dining in Hokuto

    A Kobuchisawa bread shop making natural-yeast bread from wheat, salt and yeast, with self-service coffee; open 10:00-17:00 on Saturdays, order production only on Sundays, and closed Monday-Friday.
