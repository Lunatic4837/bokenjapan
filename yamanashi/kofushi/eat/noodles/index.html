  • Fujihara-an

    藤原庵

    Dining in Kofushi

    A renovated house beside a river in Kurobera, at 1,000 m, serves handmade hoto with light miso and mountain vegetables, plus handmade soba and konnyaku; open about 11:00-17:00 and closed December-March.

  • Sobadokoro Okuyoshi

    そば処　奥義

    Dining in Kofushi

    Serves ankake soba, udon, and tori motsu-ni, including a mini portion of chicken offal with lettuce, within walking distance of Kofu Station; open 11:00-20:00 (last order 19:30), closed Sundays, sometimes shifted.

  • Soba Kanmidokoro Mitsuya

    そば・甘味処 みつや

    Dining in Kofushi

    Serves nihachi soba made with Shosenkyo water listed in the Heisei Meisui 100, plus anmitsu and ice cream; open 11:00-16:00, with irregular closures and a winter break.

  • Okuto Honten Kofu Ekimae-ten

    奥藤本店 甲府駅前店

    Dining in Kofushi

    Serves hand-cut soba using Horokanai flour and Fuji-system well water, Kofu tori motsu-ni begun at Okuto around 1950, and hoto finished by customers at the table; open 11:00-14:30 and 17:00-20:30, closed Thursdays.

  • Kawada Okuto Daini Bunten

    川田奥藤第二分店

    Dining in Kofushi

    It serves handmade medium-thick soba and Okuto's torimotsu-ni (simmered chicken offal); the soba set meal is 1,607 yen with free soba refills, and it is closed on Wednesdays unless that day is a holiday.

  • Koshu Hoto Kosaku Kofu Kitaguchi Ekimae

    甲州ほうとう小作　甲府北口駅前店

    Dining in Kofushi

    Serves hoto cooked individually in an iron pot; pumpkin hoto from 1,400 yen, pork hoto from 1,800 yen and ozara from 1,000 yen. Open 11:00-22:00, until 21:00 on Sundays and the last day of holidays, with no fixed closing day.

  • Chiyoda

    ちよだ

    Dining in Kofushi

    Said to be the shop that originated ozara; serves hoto from 1,240 yen, ozara from 740 yen and horse sashimi. Closed Mondays and Tuesdays.

  • Kisoba Kiri

    生そば　きり

    Dining in Kofushi

    Serves hand-made soba and Kofu-style chicken offal stew, plus sashimi and fried dishes; open 11:00-14:00 and 17:00-2:00, closed Wednesdays.

  • Okuto Honten

    奥藤本店

    Dining in Kofushi

    Opened in 1913 and described as the originator of chicken offal stew; also serves hand-made soba. Lunch 11:30-14:30, dinner 17:00-19:30, closed Wednesdays.

  • Keiryu Nagashi Somen Hoto En'emon

    渓流流しそうめん・ほうとう円右衛門

    Dining in Kofushi

    Restaurant in the Shosenkyo Takigami area serving hoto and nagashi somen using the stream, plus vegetable tempura, handmade konnyaku and salt-grilled river fish; open 10:30-16:00.

  • Tsukesoba Ichifuku Rei

    つけそば一福　澪～Rei～

    Dining in Kofushi

    Tsukesoba shop with soy-sauce soup and a choice of thick or thin noodles, including niboshi osakana tsukesoba; open 11:30-15:00, closed Sundays and the third Thursday.
