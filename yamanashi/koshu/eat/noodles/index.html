  • 番屋茶屋

    Dining in Koshu

  • ほうとう・生そば 七福 本店

    Hoto Kisoba Shichifuku Honten

    Dining in Koshu

    On the Enzan Bypass, it serves hoto hot pot with 14-16 vegetables and mushrooms, three blended misos, and stock from bonito, kelp and ago, plus large-shrimp tempura zaru soba; open 10:00-19:00, closed Mondays.

  • 銀月食堂

    Gingetsu Shokudo

    Dining in Koshu

    One minute from Katsunuma-Budokyo Station, it serves pumpkin hoto with homemade miso and about 10 vegetables; the hoto set is weekends and holidays only; weekdays 11:30-14:00 and 17:00-21:00, weekends 11:30-21:00.

  • 蕎麦街道 砥草庵

    Sobakaido Tokusaan

    Dining in Koshu
