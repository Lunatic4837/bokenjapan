  • Minaki

    皆吉

    Dining in Koshu

    Vegetable hoto costs 1,260 yen among five hoto types, and horse sashimi, horse sinew stew, local beer and Katsunuma wine are also served; open 11:00-18:00, closed Wednesdays and the third Tuesday.

  • Banya Chaya

    番屋茶屋

    Dining in Koshu

    Ten-ingredient hoto with homemade miso and local vegetables is served in front of the Daibosatsu-toge trailhead bus stop, and yomogi dumplings are available on weekends; open 8:00-18:00, irregular closures.

  • Hoto Kisoba Shichifuku Honten

    ほうとう・生そば 七福 本店

    Dining in Koshu

    On the Enzan Bypass, it serves hoto hot pot with 14-16 vegetables and mushrooms, three blended misos, and stock from bonito, kelp and ago, plus large-shrimp tempura zaru soba; open 10:00-19:00, closed Mondays.

  • Gingetsu Shokudo

    銀月食堂

    Dining in Koshu

    One minute from Katsunuma-Budokyo Station, it serves pumpkin hoto with homemade miso and about 10 vegetables; the hoto set is weekends and holidays only; weekdays 11:30-14:00 and 17:00-21:00, weekends 11:30-21:00.

  • Sobakaido Tokusaan

    蕎麦街道 砥草庵

    Dining in Koshu

    Serves soba made entirely by hand and milled with a Koshu millstone, including tokuseiro juwari soba limited to 20 servings a day, and tori motsu miso-ni; open 11:30-19:00, closed Wednesdays.
