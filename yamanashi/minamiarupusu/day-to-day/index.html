    Minamiarupusu, Yamanashi · 090-5801-7728

    Minamiarupusu, Yamanashi · 055-285-0020

    Cherry and grape picking orchard run by a couple, open 9:00-16:00, with a wheelchair-accessible toilet; visitors with disabilities who have caregivers are free.
