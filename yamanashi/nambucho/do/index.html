Things to do in Nambucho, Yamanashi

Nambucho · Yamanashi · 5 places

Do

  • Fukushi River Gorge

    Nambucho, Yamanashi · 0556-66-2111

    The Fukushi River from the Southern Alps carved this gorge, where seasonal stream fishing is offered.

  • Rokujizo Park

    Nambucho, Yamanashi · 0556-66-2111

    The park's Jizo statues are said to guide people through six realms before they become divine.

  • Utsubuna Park

    Nambucho, Yamanashi · 0556-64-4838

    This hilltop park holds 90 hydrangea varieties, about 30,000 plants.

  • Mount Chojagatake

    Nambucho, Yamanashi · 0556-64-3111

    Chojagatake, on the Shizuoka border, has a sunny summit viewing Mount Fuji and Lake Tanuki.

  • Mount Jumai

    Nambucho, Yamanashi · 0556-64-3111

    A southwestern Yamanashi peak with quiet eastern trails and views toward Fuji and Suruga Bay.
