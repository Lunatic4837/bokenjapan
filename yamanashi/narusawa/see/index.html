Sights in Narusawa, Yamanashi

Narusawa · Yamanashi · 7 places

See

  • Okuniwa, Oniwa, and Ochudo

    Narusawa, Yamanashi · 0555-72-5244

    Okuniwa, Oniwa, and Ochudo are walkable routes near Fuji's fifth station, with lava and dwarf trees.

  • Forest Adventure Fuji

    Narusawa, Yamanashi · 090-3345-0970

    A forest adventure park from France; the Fuji location was Japan's first.

  • Mount Fuji Fifth Station Gogoen Rest House

    Narusawa, Yamanashi · 0555-72-2121

    A log mountain hut at 2,305 meters serves as a summer climbing base with showers and lockers.

  • Narusawa Ice Cave

    Narusawa, Yamanashi · 0555-85-3089

    Narusawa Ice Cave is a natural ice cave designated a natural monument.

  • Koyodai

    Narusawa, Yamanashi · 0555-85-2252

    Koyodai is a mountain viewpoint with autumn foliage and walking trails.

  • Mount Fuji Nature School

    Narusawa, Yamanashi · 0555-85-2386

    The school runs activities exploring Fuji's nature, greatness, history, and future responsibility.

  • Narusawa Mount Fuji Museum

    Narusawa, Yamanashi · 0555-20-5600

    Listed facilities include wheelchair ramps, accessible toilets, parking, elevators, rental wheelchairs, and an AED.
