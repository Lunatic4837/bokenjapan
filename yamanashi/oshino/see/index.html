Sights in Oshino, Yamanashi

Oshino · Yamanashi · 8 places

See

  • Fuji Spring Water Village Aquarium

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-20-5135

    It is an aquarium and recreation park in a spring-water village.

  • Oshino Sukaisupotsu Kurabu (Oshi no Sukaisupotsukurabu)

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-84-7561

    Hang-gliding school on a grass airfield with tandem flights from 360 m (elementary age and up, under 80 kg) and short towing flights (age 4 and up, under 90 kg).

  • Nijumagari Pass

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-84-7794

    Nijumagari Pass is a mountain pass.

  • Cherry Blossoms along the Shinmeisho River, Oshino Village

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-84-7794

    Someiyoshino cherries line about 400 meters of the Shinmeisho River near Oshino Hakkai.

  • Oshino Hakkai

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-84-4222

    Oshino Hakkai means eight seas.

  • Okada Koyo Photography Museum

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-84-3222

    The museum displays about 50 works by Mount Fuji photographer Okada Koyo, including the 1,000-yen note image.

  • Kunio Koike Picture Letter Museum

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-84-3222

    Kunio Koike's museum permanently displays about 300 works and holds an autumn open exhibition.

  • Oshino Ninja Village

    Oshino, Yamanashi · 0555-84-1122

    Wheelchair access, rental wheelchairs, accessible parking and toilets, automatic doors, an infant room, service dogs, and an AED are available.
