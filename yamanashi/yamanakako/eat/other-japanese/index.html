  • Oshokujidokoro Taiho

    Dining in Yamanakako

    Serves Fuji sakura trout sushi and salt hoto, with lunch 11:30-14:00 and course-only dinner 17:30-20:00; closed Mondays, or Tuesdays when Monday is a holiday.

  • Kyodo Ryori Seahorse

    郷土料理 海馬

    Dining in Yamanakako

    Serves hoto, highland soba, Fuji lava grill, wakasagi, Oshino tofu and Koshu beef steak, with some seats overlooking the lake; usually 10:00-21:00, closed irregularly about four weekdays a month.

  • Washoku Dining Nagomi

    A Japanese restaurant serving seasonal ingredients with Yamanashi sake, and delivering a hot-pot set with free stove, pot and tableware rental in Oshino and Yamanakako.
